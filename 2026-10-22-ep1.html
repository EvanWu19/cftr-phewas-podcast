Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): Does an open-source SpliceAI actually hold up?

Fortier, Rudy, and Scherer (2026, PLoS One) run the first independent benchmark of two open-source SpliceAI reimplementations, OpenSpliceAI and CI-SpliceAI, against the original licensed SpliceAI and a four-tool "Legacy Ensemble" baseline, across six datasets spanning 1,316 to 99,601 variants. On canonical and near-splice-site variants all three deep learning tools are statistically indistinguishable and all comfortably beat the legacy tools, but on deep intronic variants — exactly the mechanism behind several known CFTR pseudoexon-activating pathogenic alleles — SpliceAI holds a real edge, and critically, the optimal flagging threshold on deep intronic variants is an order of magnitude lower (0.01–0.04) than the textbook 0.5 cutoff. Licensing, computational throughput, and genome-build liftover quality turn out to matter as much as raw accuracy when choosing a tool. Buildable artifact: a CFTR Deep-Intronic Splice Score Triager that applies a calibrated threshold and flags cross-tool disagreement.

Plain-English glossary

  • Balanced accuracy — the average of sensitivity and specificity, used here instead of raw accuracy because several benchmark datasets have extreme class imbalance.（中文：敏感度与特异度的平均值，用于替代原始准确率，因为本文中多个基准数据集的正负样本比例极不均衡。）
  • Canonical splice site — the exact sequence boundary marking the start or end of an intron, where most disruptive splice variants are found and are relatively easy to flag.（中文：标记内含子起点或终点的精确序列边界，大多数破坏性剪接变异位于此处，相对容易被识别。）
  • Deep intronic variant — a variant located well inside an intron, often hundreds of base pairs from any splice-site boundary, that can still disrupt splicing by activating a cryptic site.（中文：位于内含子内部较深位置的变异，常常距离任何剪接位点边界有几百个碱基，但仍可通过激活隐蔽位点破坏剪接。）
  • Delta score (DS_AG / DS_AL / DS_DG / DS_DL) — SpliceAI-family output representing the predicted probability that a variant causes acceptor gain, acceptor loss, donor gain, or donor loss, respectively.（中文：SpliceAI系列工具输出的差异分数，分别表示变异导致受体位点新增、受体位点丢失、供体位点新增或供体位点丢失的预测概率。）
  • GENCODE / RefSeq MANE — two competing reference annotation systems defining which transcripts count as a gene's canonical exon-intron structure; SpliceAI, OpenSpliceAI, and CI-SpliceAI were each trained on different versions of these.（中文：两套相互竞争的参考基因注释体系，用于界定哪些转录本算作基因的典范外显子-内含子结构；SpliceAI、OpenSpliceAI和CI-SpliceAI分别基于这两套体系的不同版本进行训练。）
  • Liftover — the process of translating variant coordinates from one genome assembly (e.g. GRCh37) to another (e.g. GRCh38), which can introduce errors for precomputed score tables.（中文：将变异坐标从一个基因组组装版本（如GRCh37）转换到另一个版本（如GRCh38）的过程，该过程容易为预计算评分表带来误差。）
  • Masking — a SpliceAI-family setting restricting predictions to biologically plausible splice alterations, avoiding spurious hits at positions that could never plausibly splice.（中文：SpliceAI系列工具的一项设置，将预测结果限制在生物学上合理的剪接改变范围内，避免在不可能参与剪接的位置报出虚假结果。）
  • Pseudoexon — a stretch of intronic sequence mistakenly spliced into the mature mRNA as if it were an exon, typically caused by a deep intronic variant activating a cryptic splice site.（中文：一段内含子序列被错误地像外显子一样拼接进成熟mRNA，通常由深度内含子变异激活隐蔽剪接位点所致。）

Full citation

Fortier N, Rudy G, Scherer A. "Analyzing the performance of deep learning splice prediction algorithms." PLoS One. 2026. PMID: 42127163 | PMC13170886 | DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0348885

Methods / tools to lift

  • OpenSpliceAI — PyTorch reimplementation of SpliceAI trained on RefSeq MANE v1.3, open-source and redistributable.
  • CI-SpliceAI — SpliceAI reimplementation trained only on curated GENCODE HAVANA splice sites, excluding RNA-seq-derived junctions.
  • Legacy Ensemble (via VarSeq) — N-of-4 majority vote across MaxEntScan, NNSplice, GeneSplicer, and a SpliceSiteFinder-based position-weight-matrix method; the pre-deep-learning baseline.
  • Barbosa deep intronic benchmark — 242 manually curated pathogenic deep intronic variants matched against gnomAD common-variant negatives, the sharpest test of algorithm divergence in the paper.
  • Two-proportion Z-test — the significance test used for every head-to-head comparison against original SpliceAI, threshold P < 0.05.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / CFTR variant interpretation, specifically deep-intronic VUS triage. MVP: given a list of CFTR intronic VUS, run available splice predictors, apply the deep-intronic-calibrated threshold from this paper instead of the standard 0.5 cutoff, and output a triage table flagging cross-tool disagreement for manual review.

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR's known deep intronic pathogenic variants fall in regions where GENCODE and RefSeq MANE annotate the gene consistently, since annotation differences partly explained the Riepe-benchmark divergence at MYBPC3.
  • Whether any CFTR-specific validated deep intronic variant set exists to serve as a local calibration check before borrowing a threshold derived from ABCA4/Barbosa.
  • Whether precomputed genome-wide scores can actually be obtained for whichever algorithm gets chosen, since neither open-source tool has an official precomputed table yet.
  • How much weight the Riepe-benchmark divergence should carry given its small size (213 variants, two genes).

ALEX Today's paper is from PLoS One, 2026 — Fortier, Rudy, and Scherer, "Analyzing the performance of deep learning splice prediction algorithms." 今天要聊的论文发表在2026年的《PLoS One》上——作者是Fortier、Rudy和Scherer，题目是《深度学习剪接预测算法性能分析》。 This is a Track A pick, straight into variant interpretation, the Karen lane. 这是Track A的选题，直接落在变异解读这条线上，也就是Karen的方向。 The question is simple but consequential: SpliceAI is the dominant tool for predicting splice-altering variants, but its license restricts clinical and commercial use, so two open-source reimplementations — OpenSpliceAI and CI-SpliceAI — have been built to replace it, and this paper is the first independent benchmark of whether they actually hold up. SpliceAI（剪接人工智能）是预测剪接改变型变异的主流工具，但其许可协议限制了临床和商业用途，于是出现了两个开源的重新实现版本——OpenSpliceAI和CI-SpliceAI，用来替代它；而这篇论文是第一次对它们是否真正站得住脚进行的独立基准测试。

JORDAN Let's set up vocabulary before we get into it. 在深入之前，我们先把术语讲清楚。 A splice donor site and splice acceptor site (剪接供体位点与受体位点) are the sequence boundaries at the start and end of an intron, the stretch of RNA that gets cut out before the mature messenger RNA is made; a variant near either boundary can disrupt normal splicing. 剪接供体位点和受体位点（splice donor and acceptor sites）是内含子两端的序列边界——内含子是在成熟信使RNA生成之前被剪掉的那段RNA；靠近这两个边界的变异可能破坏正常的剪接过程。 Canonical splice-site variants sit right at that boundary and are relatively easy to flag; deep intronic variants (深度内含子变异) sit much further into the intron, sometimes hundreds of base pairs away, and work by activating a cryptic splice site that creates a pseudoexon — a stretch of intronic sequence that gets mistakenly spliced in as if it were an exon. 典型剪接位点变异正好位于这个边界上，相对容易被识别；而深度内含子变异（deep intronic variants）则位于内含子内部更深的位置，有时距离边界有几百个碱基，其作用机制是激活一个隐蔽剪接位点，从而产生一个假外显子（pseudoexon）——也就是一段内含子序列被错误地当作外显子拼接进了成熟RNA。 CFTR has several well-documented deep intronic pathogenic variants that work exactly this way, which is why this benchmark matters directly for CFTR VUS resolution. CFTR基因就有若干个记录充分的深度内含子致病变异，其作用机制正是如此，这也是为什么这项基准测试直接关系到CFTR意义不明变异（VUS）的解读。 The paper also uses "masking," a SpliceAI-family setting that restricts predictions to biologically plausible splice alterations — gains of genuinely novel splice sites and losses of sites already annotated as functional — so the model doesn't report spurious hits at positions that could never plausibly splice. 论文中还用到"masking"（遮蔽）这一设置，这是SpliceAI系列工具的一个参数，用于把预测结果限制在生物学上合理的剪接改变范围内——即真正新获得的剪接位点和已标注为功能位点的丢失——从而避免模型在那些根本不可能参与剪接的位置报出虚假结果。

ALEX One more piece of vocabulary worth flagging: the two open-source tools differ in which reference annotation set they were trained on. 还有一个值得提一下的术语：这两个开源工具在训练时所依据的参考基因注释集不同。 GENCODE and RefSeq MANE (基因组注释集) are two competing systems for defining which transcripts count as a gene's "real" exon-intron structure — GENCODE HAVANA is a manually curated, conservative annotation, while RefSeq MANE is a curated one-to-one mapping between RefSeq and Ensembl transcripts. GENCODE和RefSeq MANE是两套相互竞争的基因注释体系，用来界定哪些转录本算作一个基因"真正的"外显子-内含子结构——GENCODE HAVANA是人工精心整理、相对保守的注释，而RefSeq MANE则是RefSeq与Ensembl转录本之间经过整理的一对一映射。 SpliceAI itself was trained on GENCODE v24 with additional GTEx RNA-seq-derived junctions; OpenSpliceAI was trained on RefSeq MANE v1.3; and CI-SpliceAI excluded RNA-seq-derived junctions entirely, training only on the curated GENCODE HAVANA splice sites. 原始SpliceAI的训练数据是GENCODE v24加上GTEx RNA测序衍生的剪接位点；OpenSpliceAI的训练数据是RefSeq MANE v1.3；而CI-SpliceAI则完全排除了RNA测序衍生的剪接位点，只用经过整理的GENCODE HAVANA剪接位点进行训练。 That annotation choice turns out to matter a lot for exactly the variant class we care about. 这个注释选择的差异，恰恰对我们最关心的那一类变异产生了重大影响。

JORDAN So why did this benchmark need to happen at all — hadn't the open-source authors already reported their own numbers? 那为什么还需要做这个基准测试呢——开源工具的作者不是已经报告过自己的测试结果了吗？ They had, but author-reported comparisons are exactly the kind of result that benefits from independent replication, especially when the stakes are a license that blocks clinical labs from using the original tool at all. 确实报告过，但作者自评的结果恰恰是最需要独立复现验证的那种结果，尤其是在原始工具的许可协议完全禁止临床实验室使用它的情况下，风险更高。 SpliceAI's model weights and precomputed genome-wide scores are restricted to academic, non-commercial use, which is a hard wall for any commercial clinical laboratory wanting to run splice prediction at scale. SpliceAI的模型权重和预先计算好的全基因组评分仅限学术性、非商业用途使用，这对任何想要大规模运行剪接预测的商业临床实验室来说都是一道硬性障碍。 So the authors assembled six independent benchmark datasets spanning very different variant populations: a curated set of 1,316 functionally validated variants published by the CI-SpliceAI team; 213 variants from Riepe et al. with real splice-assay functional data in ABCA4 and MYBPC3, including 81 deep intronic ABCA4 variants; 99,601 variants from the SPiP splicing-prediction study, with an extreme 2% positive rate that mimics realistic genome-wide screening; 242 manually curated deep intronic pathogenic variants from Barbosa et al., paired with matched gnomAD common-variant negatives; 53,600 ClinVar-derived intronic SNVs with at least one-star review confidence; and 58,064 ClinVar Assessments variants pre-filtered to keep only benign variants that at least one legacy tool had flagged as a plausible splice disruptor, specifically to construct a harder negative set. 于是作者组建了六个独立的基准数据集，覆盖了差异很大的变异群体：CI-SpliceAI团队发表的1,316个经过功能验证的变异集；Riepe等人发表的213个具有真实剪接检测功能数据的ABCA4和MYBPC3变异，其中包括81个深度内含子ABCA4变异；来自SPiP剪接预测研究的99,601个变异，阳性率极低（仅2%），模拟真实的全基因组筛查场景；Barbosa等人发表的242个人工整理的深度内含子致病变异，并配以来自gnomAD的常见变异作为对照阴性样本；53,600个至少获得一星审核可信度的ClinVar内含子单核苷酸变异；以及58,064个经过预筛选的ClinVar评估变异，只保留了至少被一种传统工具标记为可能破坏剪接的良性变异，专门用来构建更难区分的阴性样本集。

ALEX That last dataset deserves a pushback, because the pre-filtering step is doing something subtle. 最后那个数据集值得提出质疑，因为这个预筛选步骤做了一件颇为微妙的事情。 By only keeping benign variants that at least one legacy algorithm — MaxEntScan, NNSplice, GeneSplicer, or a position-weight-matrix method — flagged as plausible, the authors deliberately excluded any benign variant that only a deep-learning model would have falsely flagged. 通过只保留那些至少被一种传统算法——MaxEntScan、NNSplice、GeneSplicer或基于位置权重矩阵的方法——标记为可疑的良性变异，作者实际上刻意排除了那些只有深度学习模型才会误判的良性变异。 That's a reasonable way to build a harder negative set, but it also means specificity estimates on this dataset conservatively underestimate the deep learning models' true false-positive rate relative to a fully unfiltered population — the authors acknowledge this themselves, and it's the kind of design choice worth flagging rather than quietly accepting. 这是构建更难阴性样本集的一种合理方式，但也意味着这个数据集上的特异性估计会相对于完全未经过筛选的真实总体，保守地低估深度学习模型的真实假阳性率——作者自己也承认了这一点，这正是那种需要明确指出而不能悄悄接受的设计选择。

JORDAN And the "Legacy Ensemble" baseline — what exactly is that? 那"传统集成"（Legacy Ensemble）基线又是什么呢？ It's an N-of-4 majority vote across four pre-deep-learning splice tools bundled in the VarSeq genomic analysis software — MaxEntScan, NNSplice, GeneSplicer, and a SpliceSiteFinder-based position-weight-matrix method — representing the standard clinical pipeline before deep learning arrived. 它是打包在VarSeq基因组分析软件中的四种深度学习出现之前的剪接工具——MaxEntScan、NNSplice、GeneSplicer和基于SpliceSiteFinder的位置权重矩阵方法——所组成的"N取4"多数投票集成，代表了深度学习出现之前的标准临床流程。 Including it lets the paper answer a second question beyond just "do the open-source clones match SpliceAI": does deep learning actually buy you anything over the tools clinical labs were already running. 把它纳入分析，能让论文回答第二个问题，而不仅仅是"开源克隆是否匹配SpliceAI"：深度学习相比临床实验室原本就在用的工具，到底带来了多大的实际提升。

ALEX On the results, the headline is reassuring on the easy cases and sobering on the hard ones. 从结果来看，在容易的情形下是令人放心的，但在困难的情形下则令人警醒。 Across the four benchmarks dominated by canonical and near-splice-site variants — the CI-SpliceAI set, the SPiP set, and both ClinVar sets — all three deep learning algorithms landed within a tight band, balanced accuracies from 0.889 to 0.977, with no statistically significant difference between either open-source tool and the original SpliceAI in most comparisons. 在以典型剪接位点及邻近变异为主的四个基准数据集——CI-SpliceAI数据集、SPiP数据集和两个ClinVar数据集——上，三种深度学习算法的表现都落在一个较窄的区间内，平衡准确率在0.889到0.977之间，而且在大多数比较中，两种开源工具与原始SpliceAI之间都没有统计学显著差异。 The Legacy Ensemble, meanwhile, trailed every deep learning algorithm on every single dataset, from a balanced accuracy of 0.659 on the Riepe benchmark up to 0.942 on the easiest ClinVar set, and on the realistic-class-imbalance SPiP benchmark its false-positive count was roughly fifty times higher than any deep learning model's. 相比之下，传统集成方法在每一个数据集上都落后于所有深度学习算法，从Riepe基准上的0.659平衡准确率，到最容易的ClinVar数据集上的0.942，而在贴近真实类别不平衡情形的SPiP基准上，其假阳性数量大约是任何深度学习模型的五十倍。 But on the two benchmarks that specifically target deep intronic variants — Riepe and Barbosa — the picture changes: original SpliceAI held a real, statistically significant edge over OpenSpliceAI on both, and the gap on the Barbosa deep intronic set was the widest divergence in the entire study, 0.940 for SpliceAI versus 0.890 for CI-SpliceAI and 0.841 for OpenSpliceAI. 但在专门针对深度内含子变异的两个基准——Riepe和Barbosa——上，情况发生了变化：原始SpliceAI在两者上都对OpenSpliceAI保持着真实且具有统计学显著性的优势，而在Barbosa深度内含子数据集上的差距是整项研究中最大的分歧，SpliceAI为0.940，而CI-SpliceAI为0.890，OpenSpliceAI仅为0.841。

JORDAN And the threshold finding — that's the part that would trip someone up in practice. 还有阈值方面的发现——这正是实际操作中容易让人栽跟头的地方。 The standard recommended cutoff for calling a variant splice-altering is a delta score above 0.5, but on the deep intronic benchmark the optimal thresholds that actually maximized balanced accuracy were 0.04 for SpliceAI and just 0.01 for both open-source tools — an order of magnitude lower. 判定变异是否改变剪接的标准推荐阈值是差异分数（delta score）超过0.5，但在深度内含子基准上，真正能让平衡准确率最大化的最佳阈值却是：SpliceAI为0.04，两个开源工具均为0.01——低了整整一个数量级。 Using the textbook 0.5 cutoff on a deep intronic variant means missing the majority of true pathogenic calls, regardless of which algorithm generated the score. 如果对深度内含子变异套用教科书式的0.5阈值，就意味着会漏掉大多数真正致病的变异，无论这个评分是哪种算法算出来的都是如此。 The correlation analysis adds one more wrinkle: CI-SpliceAI tracked SpliceAI's four delta-score types — acceptor gain, acceptor loss, donor gain, donor loss — fairly evenly, Spearman's rho between 0.786 and 0.883, while OpenSpliceAI was lopsided, strongly correlated on loss events (rho up to 0.940) but much weaker on gain events (rho as low as 0.668), even though both open tools agreed with SpliceAI on the exact splice-site location over 90% of the time whenever they flagged a variant at all. 相关性分析还揭示了另一个细节：CI-SpliceAI在SpliceAI的四种差异分数类型——受体位点新增、受体位点丢失、供体位点新增、供体位点丢失——上表现得相当均衡，斯皮尔曼相关系数在0.786到0.883之间；而OpenSpliceAI则不均衡，在"丢失"类事件上相关性很强（相关系数最高达0.940），但在"新增"类事件上则明显较弱（相关系数低至0.668），不过只要这两个开源工具标记出了某个变异，它们与SpliceAI判定的确切剪接位点位置的吻合率都超过90%。
