Identifying and categorizing spurious weight data in electronic medical records

Chen S, Banks WA, Sheffrin M, Bryson W, Black M, Thielke SM. American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2018.

Abstract-only this run — no PMCID, closed access; full text not retrievable via any fetch route tried. This VA electronic-medical-record study looked at 10,000 randomly selected patients aged 65+ and examined percent weight change across time intervals ranging from 1 to 3,000 days, building three simple, reference-free algorithms that flag values sitting furthest into the tail of the observed percent-change distribution as likely spurious. Roughly 0.8% of all recorded weight values were flagged as spurious, and about one in five patient charts contained at least one such value; the dominant identified error mechanism was single-digit misentry (e.g. 148 recorded instead of 178). The flagged cases were manually reviewed and categorized — but as far as the abstract indicates, this was a review of the algorithm's own flagged cases, not an independently, randomly sampled stratum of the whole cohort, so no true sensitivity/specificity figure can be reported from what's available. It lands as the simplest possible candidate generator in our growing catalog: no growth reference, no fitted trajectory model, just percent-change-over-interval binning — useful precisely because it shows what's catchable with almost no infrastructure, though its adult (elderly, weight-stable) population may not transfer cleanly to a pediatric or CF-registry weight series where real rapid change is expected.

Plain-English glossary

  • percent-change-over-interval statistic — the core flagging statistic here: weight change scaled as a percentage of the starting value, itself scaled to the time elapsed between two measurements, rather than an absolute weight difference.（中文：本文核心的标记统计量：将体重变化按起始值的百分比表示，并进一步按两次测量间隔的时间进行缩放，而非直接用绝对体重差值。）
  • single-digit misentry — a data-entry error where one digit is swapped or mistyped (e.g. 148 recorded instead of 178); the dominant error mechanism identified in this study.（中文：一种数据录入错误，即某一位数字被换错或打错（例如把 178 记成 148）；是本研究中识别出的主要错误机制。）
  • spurious value — here, a recorded value almost certainly caused by a data-entry or measurement error rather than a real physiological change, distinct from a growth-chart-referenced "biologically implausible value" used in pediatric contexts.（中文：本文中指几乎肯定由数据录入或测量错误造成、而非真实生理变化的记录值，区别于儿科语境中依据生长图表定义的"生物学不合理值"。）
