Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): RS1-specific ACMG/AMP criteria

Paper: Hull S, et al. (ClinGen X-Linked Inherited Retinal Disease VCEP). "Development of RS1-specific ACMG/AMP variant classification criteria with pilot variant curation." Human Genetics, 2026.

Blurb

A ClinGen Variant Curation Expert Panel (VCEP) took the generic ACMG/AMP variant-classification framework — 28 evidence codes across five domains, each with four possible strength levels — and built an RS1-specific rule specification, the same kind of gene-specific calibration CFTR2 already runs for CFTR. Piloted on 54 variants (47 already in ClinVar), the new rules reclassified 21: three calls moved toward uncertainty, four moved out of uncertainty into a definitive call, and the rest resolved submitter conflicts. It's abstract-only (full text wasn't retrievable), so the per-code breakdown of what drove each change isn't available yet. The buildable artifact is a VCEP Rule-Diff Tool: recompute ACMG/AMP classifications under two rule-weighting versions and surface every variant whose call changes plus which code drove it — a sensitivity-analysis script for auditing proposed changes to CFTR-specific evidence weighting before adopting them.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP framework — the 2015 Richards et al. standard most clinical labs use to classify a variant as pathogenic, likely pathogenic, uncertain significance (VUS), likely benign, or benign by combining weighted evidence codes.（中文:大多数临床实验室使用的变异分类标准,通过组合加权的证据代码,把变异归为致病、可能致病、意义不明、可能良性或良性。）
  • ClinVar — the public NCBI database where labs submit and share variant classifications, often producing conflicting calls for the same variant across submitters.（中文:NCBI的公共数据库,实验室在此提交和共享变异分类,常常出现不同提交方对同一变异给出相互矛盾的结论。）
  • Evidence code (PVS1, PS3, PM1, PP1, PP3, etc.) — a labeled piece of ACMG/AMP evidence (null variant, functional assay, mutational hot spot, segregation, computational prediction) with an assigned strength: very strong, strong, moderate, or supporting.（中文:ACMG/AMP框架中带标签的证据类型,如无效变异、功能实验、突变热点、家族共分离、计算预测,各自带有强度等级:极强、强、中等或支持。）
  • Retinoschisin (RS1 gene product) — a secreted photoreceptor protein needed to hold the retina's layers together; loss of function causes the layers to split (schisis).（中文:一种由光感受器细胞分泌的蛋白质,负责维持视网膜各层的粘连;功能丧失会导致各层分离即"劈裂"。）
  • Segregation (evidence code PP1) — evidence that a variant co-occurs with disease status across family members, used to support pathogenicity.（中文:一种证据类型,指变异在家族成员中与疾病状态共同出现的情况,用于支持致病性判断。）
  • VCEP (Variant Curation Expert Panel) — a ClinGen-convened group of clinicians and scientists that adapts the generic ACMG/AMP rules into a gene-specific rule specification.（中文:由ClinGen召集的专家组,负责把通用ACMG/AMP规则改造成针对某一基因的具体规则说明。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant for which current evidence doesn't clearly support either a pathogenic or benign call.（中文:现有证据不足以明确判定为致病或良性的变异,即"意义不明变异"。）
  • X-linked retinoschisis — an X-linked retinal disease caused by RS1 variants, characterized by splitting of the retina's layers.（中文:由RS1基因变异引起的X连锁视网膜疾病,特征是视网膜各层发生分离。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • The ClinGen VCEP process itself — a repeatable template for taking the generic ACMG/AMP evidence-code set and formally documenting gene-specific modifications across the five evidence domains (phenotype, population, computational, functional, segregation).
  • The pilot-and-report pattern: re-score a curated set of variants already in ClinVar under the new rules and publish a reclassification table showing direction of change (toward uncertainty vs. out of uncertainty vs. conflict resolution).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / CFTR variant interpretation — a worked example of exactly the kind of gene-specific ACMG/AMP calibration CFTR2 already runs for CFTR, directly useful as an audit template for domains where CFTR2's coverage may be thinner (e.g., regulatory/non-coding variants).

Buildable artifact — VCEP Rule-Diff Tool (S–M). One-line MVP: given a variant table with ACMG/AMP evidence codes assigned under two rule-weighting versions, recompute the classification under each and output a diff listing every variant whose call changes plus which code drove it.

What to verify first

  • Whether the ~39% reclassification rate (21/54) holds up on a larger, prospectively curated variant set rather than mostly re-scoring known ClinVar entries.
  • The per-evidence-code breakdown of which specific code changes drove each of the 21 reclassifications (not available in the abstract).
  • Reproducibility: whether a second group of curators applying the same written rules to the same 54 variants would reach the same 21 reclassifications.
  • How much of the RS1-specific calibration logic is gene-agnostic methodology versus retinoschisin-specific biology that wouldn't transfer to CFTR.

ALEX VCEP stands for Variant Curation Expert Panel — it's a ClinGen-convened group of clinicians, molecular biologists and geneticists chartered to take the generic ACMG/AMP variant-classification guidelines and adapt them to one specific gene or gene family. VCEP的全称是Variant Curation Expert Panel(变异审定专家组)——是由ClinGen召集的一个专家组,成员包括临床医生、分子生物学家和遗传学家,他们的任务是把通用的ACMG/AMP变异分类指南,改造成适用于某一个具体基因或基因家族的版本。

JORDAN And ACMG/AMP itself is...? 那ACMG/AMP本身指的是……?

ALEX The 2015 Richards et al. framework that most clinical labs use to classify a variant as pathogenic, likely pathogenic, uncertain significance, likely benign, or benign. 这是Richards等人在2015年提出的框架,大多数临床实验室都用它把一个变异归类为致病(pathogenic)、可能致病(likely pathogenic)、意义不明(uncertain significance,即VUS)、可能良性(likely benign)或良性(benign)。 It works by combining evidence codes — things like PVS1 for a null variant in a gene where loss-of-function is a known mechanism, PS3 for functional-assay data, PP1 for segregation with disease in a family — each carrying a strength level: very strong, strong, moderate, or supporting. 具体做法是组合各种证据代码(evidence code)——比如PVS1对应"在已知功能丧失致病机制的基因中出现的无效变异",PS3对应"功能实验数据",PP1对应"家族中与疾病的共分离(segregation)"——每条证据都带有一个强度等级:极强(very strong)、强(strong)、中等(moderate)或支持(supporting)。

JORDAN So a VCEP's job is to say exactly which codes apply to this gene, and how strong they should be? 所以VCEP的工作就是具体规定:哪些证据代码适用于这个基因,以及它们各自应该有多强的强度?

ALEX Exactly. 正是如此。 The generic framework is deliberately gene-agnostic, which makes it usable everywhere but calibrated nowhere. 这个通用框架是故意设计成"基因无关"的,这让它可以用在任何基因上,但代价是对任何一个基因都没有经过专门校准。 A VCEP's whole output is a gene-specific rule specification — which codes apply, what thresholds trigger them, and how strongly each one should count for this particular gene. VCEP的全部产出,其实就是一份针对特定基因的"规则说明书"——哪些代码适用、触发它们的具体阈值是什么、以及每条证据对这个基因应该算多强的权重。

JORDAN And RS1 causes X-linked retinoschisis — a splitting of the retina's layers, correct? RS1导致的是X连锁视网膜劈裂症——也就是视网膜各层发生分离,对吗?

ALEX Right, a retinal disease, and the reason it matters today is that gene-therapy trials for RS1-related X-linked retinoschisis are enrolling patients right now, which makes getting the variant calls right a prerequisite for the whole program, not an academic nicety. 没错,这是一种视网膜疾病,而它今天之所以重要,是因为针对RS1相关X连锁视网膜劈裂症的基因治疗试验正在招募患者,这就使得"把变异判读做对"成了整个项目能否成立的前提,而不只是学术上的锦上添花。

ALEX So why does a gene need its own rule set in the first place? 那么,一个基因究竟为什么需要属于自己的一套规则呢? Before ACMG/AMP standardized the evidence-code vocabulary in 2015, labs classified variants with a real mix of local conventions, and even after standardization, each lab still had latitude in how it applied codes like PP3 — computational evidence — or PM1 — a variant located in a mutational hot spot or critical functional domain — to any particular gene. 在2015年ACMG/AMP把证据代码的词汇统一起来之前,各个实验室其实是用各自不同的本地惯例来做变异分类的;而即便统一了之后,每个实验室在把像PP3(计算预测证据)或PM1(位于突变热点或关键功能域内的变异)这类代码,具体应用到某个基因上时,仍然拥有相当大的自由裁量空间。 That latitude produces exactly the kind of inter-laboratory disagreement ClinVar is full of: the same variant submitted as pathogenic by one lab and uncertain by another. 这种裁量空间,正是ClinVar里到处可见的那种实验室间分歧的根源:同一个变异,一家实验室提交为致病,另一家却提交为意义不明。

JORDAN Is that disagreement actually common? 这种分歧真的很常见吗?

ALEX Common enough that ClinGen built an entire VCEP infrastructure to fix it gene by gene, because trying to fix it globally in one pass isn't tractable. 常见到足以让ClinGen专门搭建整套VCEP体系,逐个基因地去解决这个问题——因为试图用一次性的全局方案去解决它,根本行不通。 The right computational threshold for PP3 in a tumor-suppressor gene isn't the right threshold in an ion channel gene, and the right functional-assay evidence for a channel gene isn't the right evidence for a retinal structural protein. 在一个肿瘤抑制基因上,PP3所需的恰当计算阈值,放到一个离子通道基因上就不对;同样,适用于通道基因的恰当功能实验证据,用在视网膜结构蛋白上也不合适。

RS1编码的是retinoschisin(视网膜劈裂蛋白),这是一种由光感受器细胞分泌的蛋白质,其作用是把视网膜各层"粘"在一起——功能丧失到一定程度,各层就会分离,这正是"劈裂症(schisis)"这个名字的来源。 Before this VCEP, RS1 variants were being classified under the generic ACMG/AMP rules without gene-specific calibration: no agreed hot-spot domains for PM1, no agreed functional-assay evidence threshold for PS3, no agreed computational cutoffs for PP3. 在这个VCEP成立之前,RS1的变异都是按照未经基因特异性校准的通用ACMG/AMP规则来分类的:PM1没有公认的热点结构域,PS3没有公认的功能实验证据阈值,PP3也没有公认的计算预测截断值。 That's the gap the panel set out to close, and the stakes rose considerably once gene-replacement and gene-therapy approaches for RS1-related disease moved from bench work into actual clinical trials, because a misclassified variant can mean enrolling — or wrongly excluding — the wrong patient. 这正是这个专家组要填补的空白,而一旦针对RS1相关疾病的基因替代和基因治疗方法从实验台走向实际临床试验,风险就显著上升了,因为一个被误分类的变异,可能意味着招募了不该入组的患者,或者错误地排除了本该入组的患者。

VCEP这种机制大约从2010年代中期就开始运行了,到现在已经有数十个专家组,覆盖的基因从BRCA1到各种心脏离子通道病基因都有,所以这个机制本身已经相当成熟了——真正因基因而异的,只是用于校准的证据量有多少,以及现有的ClinVar记录本身已经存在多大的争议。 RS1 isn't a first-of-its-kind pilot for the method; it's a fairly routine application of a well-established process to a gene that simply hadn't gotten its own rule set yet. ALEX Mechanically, the panel took the full ACMG/AMP evidence-code set — 28 distinct codes, each with up to four strength levels: very strong, strong, moderate, supporting — and modified them for RS1 across five evidence domains: phenotype, population data, computational assessment, functional impact, and segregation. 具体操作上,这个专家组拿出了完整的ACMG/AMP证据代码体系——一共28个不同的代码,每个代码最多有四档强度:极强、强、中等、支持——然后针对RS1,在五个证据维度上对它们逐一做了修改:表型(phenotype)、人群数据(population data)、计算评估(computational assessment)、功能影响(functional impact),以及共分离(segregation)。

JORDAN And in the other direction? 那另一个方向呢?

ALEX Four previously unclassified variants moved to pathogenic, likely pathogenic, or likely benign once the RS1-specific codes were applied — meaning the calibrated rules resolved ambiguity that the generic framework had left open. 有四个此前未分类的变异,在套用RS1特异性代码后,变成了致病、可能致病或可能良性——也就是说,经过校准的规则,解决了通用框架留下的那部分模糊地带。 The rest of the 21 reclassifications resolved conflicts: cases where multiple ClinVar submitters disagreed, and the new RS1-specific scoring broke the tie one way or the other. 剩下的重新分类则是用来解决冲突的:也就是多个ClinVar提交方彼此意见不一致的那些案例,而新的RS1特异性打分帮它们打破了平局,朝某一个方向定了案。

JORDAN Net-net, did gene-specific calibration make things stricter or looser? 总的来看,基因特异性校准是让判定变得更严格了,还是更宽松了?
