Ep4 — Shared Parameter Modeling of an Informative Visiting Process and Terminal Event

Blurb

Longitudinal registries assume, usually silently, that when a patient gets measured has nothing to do with what the measurement would show. This paper (Thomadakis et al., Biostatistics, 2025) shows that assumption can fail in a chronic-disease EHR cohort — using HIV/CD4 data from the Athens Multicenter AIDS Cohort Study (AMACS) — and proposes a shared-parameter joint model that links the longitudinal marker, the visiting process, and a competing-risks terminal event (death/AIDS progression vs. disengagement from care) through the marker's own latent baseline value and slope, without assuming the three processes are conditionally independent given random effects. It also lets the visiting process be modeled on a gap-time scale (time since last visit) instead of only calendar time. Simulations show that ignoring an informative visiting process seriously biases estimated trajectories (and that gap-time is more robust to misspecification); the AMACS application confirms visiting was informative there. For Track C, the relevance is upstream: it's a substrate-model concern for any spline/mixed-effects growth baseline used before outlier flagging ever happens.

Glossary

  • Competing risks — A time-to-event setup with more than one possible terminal event (here: death/AIDS progression vs. disengagement from care), where only the first one to occur is observed.（中文：竞争风险,一种生存分析设定,存在多种可能的终末事件,只能观察到最先发生的那一个。）
  • Disengagement from care — A patient effectively leaving a cohort/registry's active follow-up (here operationalized as 18 months with no visit, backdated to 9 months after the last visit); a real clinical/administrative event, not a data error.（中文：脱离诊疗,患者实际退出了队列或登记系统的主动随访,本文将其操作化定义为18个月无就诊记录,并将脱离时间回溯到最后一次就诊后9个月;这是一个真实的临床/行政事件,不是数据错误。）
  • Informative visiting process — When the timing of a patient's visits is statistically related to their underlying (observed or latent) marker trajectory, rather than being schedule-driven or independent of it.（中文：信息性就诊过程,指患者的就诊时间在统计上与其潜在的标志物轨迹相关,而不是单纯由排班决定或与轨迹无关。）
  • Terminal event — An absorbing event that permanently ends a patient's longitudinal measurement series; in a chronic-disease registry this can be death, but also a non-death absorbing event like disengagement from care.（中文：终末事件,一个会永久终止患者纵向测量序列的吸收性事件;在慢性病登记系统中,除死亡外,脱离诊疗等非死亡事件也可以是终末事件。）
  • VCAR / VAR / VNAR — "Visiting completely at random," "visiting at random," and "visiting not at random": the visit-timing analogs of the MCAR/MAR/MNAR missing-data taxonomy, describing whether visit timing depends on marker values only through observed history (VCAR/VAR) or also on unobserved/latent marker values (VNAR, informative).（中文：分别对应"完全随机就诊""随机就诊""非随机就诊",是缺失数据分类MCAR/MAR/MNAR在"就诊时间"上的类比,用于描述就诊时间是否仅依赖于已观测的标志物历史,还是也依赖于未观测的潜在标志物值(VNAR,即具有信息性)。）

Citation

Thomadakis C, Meligkotsidou L, Pantazis N, Touloumi G. Shared parameter modeling of longitudinal data allowing for possibly informative visiting process and terminal event. Biostatistics (Oxford, England). 2025;26(1). PMID: 39449049 | PMCID: PMC11911807 | DOI: 10.1093/biostatistics/kxae041

Methods/tools to lift

  • Gap-time (proportional-hazards, time-since-last-visit) parameterization of a visiting process, as an alternative to the near-universal calendar-time intensity model — and found more robust to misspecification in this paper's simulations.
  • Shared-parameter linkage via latent baseline value and latent current slope (not the noisy current observed value) feeding both the visiting hazard and each cause-specific terminal-event hazard — a concrete way to connect "how fast is this trajectory really moving" to "how often does this person get measured."
  • An optional frailty term on the visiting process alone, independent of the marker's random effects, to absorb leftover per-patient visiting tendency not explained by the latent slope or visit history.
  • A cheap empirical screen the paper's own ablations suggest: regress/correlate inter-visit gap length (or visit count) against the baseline model's residuals or random-slope estimates, as a first-pass check for VNAR-like dependence, without fitting the full joint likelihood.
  • Open-source R implementation: https://github.com/cthomadak/JointModelVisCompRisk

Where it lands + buildable artifact

  • Relevant to Runyu's flagging task as a substrate-model concern: any spline/mixed-effects growth baseline computed before outlier flags are raised implicitly assumes visit timing is ignorable; if it isn't, the "expected" trajectory itself shifts, not just its uncertainty band, which can mis-calibrate what counts as an outlier for frequently- vs. infrequently-seen patients in either direction.
  • Buildable artifact: a visiting-informativeness diagnostic — MVP: take encounter-level visit timestamps plus the residuals or random-slope estimates the current baseline growth model already produces per patient, and test whether inter-visit gap length correlates with those residuals/slopes as a low-cost proxy for VNAR-like visiting in this registry, without fitting the full joint model. Input: encounter-level timestamps + existing baseline-model residuals/EB slope estimates. Difficulty: S–M (a correlation/regression screen; doing it with proper empirical-Bayes random-effects estimates rather than naive residuals pushes it toward M).

What to verify first

  • Whether CF registry visit timing behaves like AMACS's symptom/care-driven, highly irregular HIV-clinic visits, or more like scheduled protocol visits — pushing toward VAR rather than VNAR; this is an empirical question, not something to assume.
  • Whether the paper's estimation machinery (numerical integration per gap time, Gauss-Hermite/Monte Carlo integration over random effects, direct maximum likelihood via BFGS), built and tested on ~2,315 people, is computationally feasible at millions of encounters, or needs a lighter-weight proxy.
  • Whether an analogous "terminal event"/disengagement definition is even needed for a QC use case, and if so, what a defensible working definition would be — the paper's own disengagement rule was a working definition set with study physicians, not a ground truth.

Pipeline stage

(a) Candidate generator — this is a substrate-model concern affecting the baseline spline/mixed-effects trajectory model used before any outlier flag is raised, not the adjudication step, the review-budget sizing, or a no-gold-standard evaluation method.

Error types covered / not covered

  • Not covered: unit conversion errors, digit transposition, decimal-place shifts, carry-forward/stuck-value errors — this paper is not an error-detection method at all; it says nothing about individual erroneous values.
  • Indirectly relevant: real-clinical-event vs. data-entry-error confusion — by modeling how visit timing and terminal events relate to the true latent trajectory, it offers a way to reason about why an unusual value's surrounding visit pattern might reflect a real clinical trajectory shift (e.g., a patient being seen more often while declining) rather than a measurement error, but it does not itself classify any single value.
  • Core contribution: making the baseline that any outlier-detection step compares against more honest when who-gets-measured-when is not independent of the underlying trajectory.

ALEX Right — if sicker patients get seen more often, or people who feel fine stop showing up, the pattern of visit timing becomes correlated with the very thing you're trying to measure. 没错——如果病得更重的患者被更频繁地看诊,或者感觉良好的人干脆不再来了,那么就诊时间的模式就会和你想测量的东西本身产生相关性。 The paper borrows the missing-data vocabulary for this: visiting completely at random, visiting at random, and visiting not at random — VCAR, VAR, and VNAR — as the visit-timing analogs of MCAR, MAR, and MNAR. 这篇论文借用了缺失数据的那套词汇:完全随机就诊、随机就诊、非随机就诊——VCAR、VAR、VNAR(分别是"就诊过程"版本的MCAR、MAR、MNAR)。 You know that taxonomy cold for missingness; the news is just that it now applies to whether someone is observed at all, not just whether one value is missing. 这套分类你对缺失数据早就很熟了;新鲜的地方只是,它现在用来描述"这个人有没有被观察到"本身,而不只是"某个数值是否缺失"。

JORDAN Second idea: "terminal event" here isn't only death. 第二个概念:这里的"终末事件"(terminal event)不只是死亡。 It's a competing-risks setup with two absorbing events — death or AIDS progression, and disengagement from care. 这是一个竞争风险(competing risks)设定,有两个终止性事件——死亡或进展为AIDS,以及脱离诊疗(disengagement from care)。 And disengagement matters for registry thinking generally: it's not a data error, it's a real clinical-administrative event, defined in this paper by a working rule — eighteen months with no visit, backdated to nine months after the last one. 脱离诊疗这一点对登记系统的思路特别重要:它不是数据错误,而是一个真实的临床兼行政事件,这篇论文用一个操作性规则来定义它——十八个月没有就诊,把脱离时间回溯到最后一次就诊后的九个月。

ALEX So a "terminal event" can quietly end someone's longitudinal series for reasons tangled up with the very trend you're modeling — and the paper's whole point is that ignoring either an informative visiting process or an informative terminal event can bias a trajectory, even fit through an otherwise correct mixed model. 所以"终末事件"可能会悄悄终结一个人的纵向数据序列,而原因恰恰和你正在建模的趋势本身纠缠在一起——这篇论文的核心论点就是,无论忽略信息性就诊过程,还是忽略信息性终末事件,都可能让轨迹估计产生偏倚,就算用的混合模型本身是对的也一样。

ALEX So why write this paper. 那为什么要写这篇论文呢。 The motivating data is the Athens Multicenter AIDS Cohort Study, AMACS — electronic health records tracking CD4 cell counts, the standard immune marker for HIV progression, after people start antiretroviral therapy. 促成这篇论文的数据来自雅典多中心AIDS队列研究(Athens Multicenter AIDS Cohort Study,AMACS)——电子健康记录追踪的是CD4细胞计数,也就是HIV进展的标准免疫标志物,记录的是患者开始抗逆转录病毒治疗之后的情况。

JORDAN And CD4 isn't measured on a fixed calendar. 而CD4并不是按固定的日程来测量的。

ALEX Right — visit frequency varies a lot across individuals in that cohort, which immediately raises the question of whether tools like linear mixed models or GEE, which quietly assume the visiting process is ignorable, are giving an honest trajectory. 没错——这个队列里不同个体的就诊频率差异很大,这立刻带来一个问题:像线性混合模型或GEE这类默认就诊过程是可忽略的工具,给出的轨迹到底靠不靠谱。 So earlier authors turned to counting-process machinery instead for arbitrary visit times. 所以早先的作者转而使用计数过程(counting process)这套工具来处理任意的就诊时间。

JORDAN And where joint models of a marker and a visiting process did exist? 那在已有的、把标志物和就诊过程联合建模的方法里,情况又如何呢?

ALEX Almost all of them leaned on a conditional-independence assumption — that the marker process, the visiting process, and the time-to-event process are independent once you condition on a shared or correlated set of random effects. 几乎所有这类方法都依赖一个条件独立性假设——也就是在给定一组共享或相关的随机效应之后,标志物过程、就诊过程和事件发生时间过程彼此独立。 Which sounds reasonable until you integrate those random effects back out: marginally, conditional independence quietly induces dependence between visiting and the marker values you never observed. 这听起来挺合理,但一旦你把随机效应积分积掉就不对了:从边际上看,条件独立性其实悄悄地在"就诊"和你从未观测到的标志物值之间引入了依赖关系。 The authors cite their own earlier work showing that violating this assumption in a joint longitudinal-survival model can seriously bias the marker estimates. 作者引用了他们自己之前的研究,表明在纵向-生存联合模型中违反这个假设,会让标志物估计产生严重偏倚。 Nobody had really tested whether that choice matters once you also have a terminal event. 而且没人真正测试过,一旦同时存在终末事件,这个选择到底有没有影响。 So the gap this paper fills is specific: one framework that relaxes conditional independence, allows either time scale for visiting, and jointly handles competing terminal events. 所以这篇论文填补的空白很具体:一个放宽条件独立性假设、就诊过程可以用任意一种时间尺度、并能同时处理竞争性终末事件的统一框架。

JORDAN Okay, walk me through what they actually built. 好,带我看看他们到底搭建了什么。

ALEX Three linked pieces. 三个相互关联的部分。 First, a standard linear mixed model for the marker — nothing new, random intercept and slope. 第一,标志物用标准的线性混合模型——没什么新鲜的,随机截距加随机斜率。 Second, the visiting process: they model the hazard of the next visit, either as a function of time since the last visit — the gap-time model, proportional hazards with a cubic B-spline baseline — or as an intensity model on calendar time, same spline idea but indexed by time since baseline. 第二,就诊过程:他们把下一次就诊的风险建模成距上次就诊时间的函数——这是间隔时间模型(gap-time model),用比例风险加三次B样条基线;或者建模成日历时间上的强度模型(intensity-based model),同样用样条的思路,但按从基线起算的时间来索引。

JORDAN And the "shared parameter" part — what's actually shared? 那"共享参数"(shared parameter)这部分——到底共享的是什么?

ALEX Two specific pieces of the latent marker trajectory get plugged directly into the visiting hazard and into each cause-specific hazard for the terminal events: the patient's true baseline marker value, and their true current rate of change — the latent slope — both computed from the mixed model's random effects, not the noisy observed values. 潜在标志物轨迹中有两个具体的量,被直接代入就诊风险和每个终末事件的病因特异性风险中:患者真实的基线标志物值,以及他们真实的当前变化率——也就是潜在斜率——这两个量都来自混合模型的随机效应,而不是带噪声的观测值。 So a patient whose true trajectory is declining faster can have a different hazard of coming in for the next visit and a different hazard of hitting a terminal event, with those two hazards sharing that same latent-slope term. 所以,真实轨迹下降更快的患者,下一次就诊的风险和遭遇终末事件的风险都会不同,而这两个风险共享着同一个潜在斜率项。 They also let you add a frailty — an extra random effect for the visiting process alone, independent of the marker's random effects — to soak up leftover individual tendency to visit often or rarely that the latent slope and visit history don't already explain. 他们还允许加入一个脆弱项(frailty term)——一个只作用于就诊过程、独立于标志物随机效应的额外随机效应——用来吸收潜在斜率和就诊历史都解释不了的、个体本身"爱来或不爱来"的剩余倾向。

JORDAN Where's your pushback? 你要吐槽的点在哪?

ALEX Identifiability. 可识别性(identifiability)问题。 They explicitly chose not to let the current true marker value drive the hazards, only the baseline value and the slope, because — in their own words — separately estimating that against the already-included history of observed past values would need an enormous sample or risk breaking the model's stability. 他们明确选择不让"当前"的真实标志物值直接驱动风险,只用基线值和斜率,因为——用他们自己的话说——要把这个量和已经纳入模型的既往观测值历史分开估计,需要极大的样本量,否则就有可能破坏模型的稳定性。 That's a real constraint built into the structure: it can't test every version of "informative visiting" you might worry about, only the ones expressible through baseline-plus-slope. 这是写进模型结构里的一个真实限制:它没法检验你能想到的所有版本的"信息性就诊",只能检验能用"基线加斜率"表达出来的那些。 And computationally it's heavy — every hazard needs numerical integration, the random effects need Gauss-Hermite or Monte Carlo integration, and the whole thing is fit by direct maximum likelihood with the BFGS algorithm — fine for twenty-three hundred people, worth asking whether it holds at registry scale. 而且计算量很大——每个风险函数都需要数值积分,随机效应需要用Gauss-Hermite积分或蒙特卡洛积分(Monte Carlo integration)来近似,整个模型用BFGS算法(一种数值优化算法)直接做最大似然拟合——对两千三百人没问题,但值得问一问,放到登记系统的规模上还撑得住吗。

JORDAN Did it deliver? 它达到预期效果了吗?

ALEX Badly biased. 偏倚很严重。 Under the gap-time scenario, the slope parameters' coverage dropped to seventy-six and forty-nine percent instead of ninety-five, and the estimated marker value at ten years came back as 27.06 against a true value of 25.98, with coverage of just seventeen percent. 在间隔时间场景下,斜率参数的覆盖率从本该的95%掉到了76%和49%,而估计出的十年时标志物值是27.06,真实值是25.98,覆盖率只有17%。 Same pattern under the calendar-time scenario: the marker overestimated at 26.79 against 25.98, with coverage near thirty-two percent. 日历时间场景下的模式一样:标志物被高估为26.79,真实值25.98,覆盖率只有约32%。 Even getting the visiting process into the model but on the wrong time scale still left real bias, coverage around seventy percent for the fixed-effect slopes, though adding visit-history covariates like the previous gap time clawed some of it back. 就算把就诊过程纳入模型,但用错了时间尺度,依然留下了实实在在的偏倚,固定效应斜率的覆盖率大约只有70%,不过加入像"上一次间隔时间"这样的就诊历史变量,能挽回一部分。

JORDAN Was the gap-time formulation actually more robust, the way the abstract claims? 间隔时间这个建模方式,真的像摘要里说的那样更稳健吗?

ALEX Yes. 是的。 Fitting a gap-time model to calendar-time-generated data recovered the true ten-year marker value almost exactly, 25.99 against 25.98. 用间隔时间模型去拟合由日历时间机制生成的数据,恢复出的十年时标志物值几乎和真实值完全一致,25.99对25.98。 The reverse — a calendar-time model fit to gap-time-generated data — did not. 反过来,用日历时间模型去拟合由间隔时间机制生成的数据,就做不到这一点。

JORDAN And the real data, AMACS? 那真实数据,也就是AMACS呢?

ALEX Twenty-three hundred fifteen people: about nine-and-a-half percent died or progressed to AIDS, almost fifteen percent disengaged from care. 两千三百十五人:大约9.5%死亡或进展为AIDS,将近15%脱离了诊疗。 The gap-time-plus-frailty model fit dramatically better by AIC than the calendar-time version, and it found real signal — a one-unit increase in the latent CD4 slope tied to a thirteen percent higher hazard of showing up for the next visit, even after adjusting for the most recent observed CD4 and visit history. 按AIC(一种模型选择准则)来看,"间隔时间加脆弱项"模型的拟合效果比日历时间版本好得多,而且它找到了真实信号——潜在CD4斜率每增加一个单位,下一次就诊的风险就提高13%,即便已经调整了最近一次观测到的CD4值和就诊历史。

JORDAN So visiting really was informative there. 所以在那个队列里,就诊真的是有信息性的。

JORDAN Okay — does this change anything about the outlier-flagging work? 好——这对异常值标记工作有什么影响吗?
