Ep4 (Track C) — Frequency of anthropometric implausible values: a systematic review and meta-analysis

Abstract-only. Full text was not retrievable — Europe PMC core marks this paper closed access with no PMCID, consistent with the sheet's own OA=N flag; the PubMed MCP fallback returned metadata/abstract only. Everything in this episode is drawn from the published abstract alone.

Blurb. Santana Dos Santos, Borges Dos Santos Pereira, Cumpian Silva, and colleagues (University of São Paulo; Nutrition Reviews, 2024) run a PROSPERO-registered systematic review and meta-analysis of anthropometric data-cleaning methods, pooling 104 studies (123 reports, 1980–2022) in qualitative synthesis and 23 weight/14 height studies in quantitative meta-analysis. The pooled frequency of biologically implausible values (BIVs) was 0.55% for weight (95% CI 0.29–0.91) and 1.20% for height (95% CI 0.44–2.33) — height at roughly twice weight's rate. Heterogeneity was not explained by study methodological quality, and publication bias was ruled out by funnel plot. The paper's central methodological conclusion: using a set of quality indicators beats relying on any single indicator alone. For CFFPR work, this is the external, literature-wide benchmark against which any observed flag rate can be sanity-checked, and independent support for combining multiple detection signals at candidate generation — serving Liz Cromwell's registry data-quality assignment as a reference table rather than a specific rule to adopt.

Plain-English glossary

  • Biologically implausible value (BIV) — A measurement outside the range a human body could plausibly have, flagged by a fixed cutoff, standard-deviation rule, or percentile/z-score rule against a reference population.（中文：落在人体生理上不可能达到范围之外的测量值，可通过固定阈值、标准差规则或相对于参照人群的百分位数/z分数规则来标记。）
  • Systematic review / meta-analysis — A structured, pre-specified literature synthesis (systematic review) combined with statistical pooling of comparable numeric results across studies (meta-analysis).（中文：一种结构化、预先设定方案的文献综合方法（系统综述），并对多项研究中具有可比性的数值结果进行统计学汇总（荟萃分析）。）
  • PROSPERO registration — A public registry where systematic review teams pre-specify their methods and outcomes before starting the review, to prevent post-hoc changes to the analysis plan.（中文：一个公开登记平台，系统综述团队在开始综述前预先登记研究方法和结局指标，以防止事后修改分析计划。）
  • Qualitative vs. quantitative synthesis — Qualitative synthesis describes all included studies narratively; quantitative synthesis (meta-analysis) statistically pools numeric estimates from the subset of studies with comparable data.（中文：定性综合以叙述方式描述全部纳入研究；定量综合（荟萃分析）则对具有可比数据的研究子集进行数值上的统计汇总。）
  • Funnel plot — A scatter plot of study effect size against precision, used to visually assess whether smaller studies with unfavorable results are systematically missing from the literature (publication bias).（中文：一种以研究效应量为横轴、精度为纵轴的散点图，用于直观评估结果不利的小型研究是否在文献中被系统性遗漏（发表偏倚）。）
  • Heterogeneity — The degree to which individual study estimates disagree with each other beyond what chance alone would predict.（中文：各研究估计值之间超出偶然因素预期的分歧程度。）
  • Single-indicator vs. multi-indicator screening — Flagging a value using one rule alone vs. requiring agreement (or scoring) across several independent detection rules.（中文：仅用一条规则来标记数值，与要求多条独立检测规则达成一致（或综合评分）之间的区别。）
  • Stadiometer — A calibrated device for measuring standing height, whose accuracy depends on equipment calibration and measurer technique.（中文：一种用于测量站立身高的校准设备，其准确性取决于设备校准和测量者的操作技巧。）
