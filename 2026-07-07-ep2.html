Show notes — Track B — Target Trial Emulation (Hernan, Wang, Leaf, 2022)

Sourcing flag: This paper (JAMA Guide to Statistics and Methods, pp.2446-2447) is not open-access; no abstract or full text was retrievable via PubMed or Europe PMC. The episode teaches the established target-trial-emulation (TTE) framework as documented across Hernan and Robins' broader work, and claims no specific numbers or quotes from this paper. Read the JAMA piece directly for its exact emphasis.

Blurb. Target trial emulation is the canonical framework for extracting RCT-grade causal estimates from observational data such as a patient registry. You explicitly write the protocol of the randomized trial you would run — seven components: eligibility, treatment strategies, assignment procedure, follow-up period, outcome, causal contrast, and analysis plan — then imitate each with observational data. The load-bearing idea is "time zero": eligibility, treatment assignment, and start of follow-up must coincide, or you invite immortal time bias. Reanalyses of famous observational-vs-RCT disagreements (e.g., hormone replacement therapy) showed the gap often came from avoidable design errors, not mysterious confounding — and shrank once the analysis emulated the trial's protocol. Crucially, TTE removes design-induced bias but does not remove unmeasured confounding; it makes your reliance on the no-unmeasured-confounders assumption explicit and critiquable. Where it lands: the AI-tooling / reproducible-RWE-software career lane (and the same template feeds a future Liz-sponsored effectiveness question). Buildable artifact: a parametrized TTE protocol template over OMOP-mapped data with a clone-censor-weight skeleton for grace periods.

Plain-English glossary

  • Clone-censor-weight — for grace-period strategies, copy each person into every strategy compatible with their baseline, censor them when their behavior diverges, and use weights to correct the censoring.（中文：克隆-删失-加权，处理宽限期策略的技巧：把每人复制进所有相容策略，偏离时删失，再用权重校正。）
  • Conditional exchangeability — the assumption that, within levels of measured baseline covariates, treatment assignment is as-if random; the observational stand-in for randomization.（中文：条件可交换性，假设在测得的基线协变量各水平内治疗分配"近似随机"，是随机化的观察性替身。）
  • Grace period — a window in which a person can start the assigned strategy and still count as adherent.（中文：宽限期，允许在其内开始所分配策略、仍算依从的时间窗。）
  • Immortal time bias — follow-up time during which the outcome could not occur by the design's logic, wrongly credited to treatment.（中文：不死时间偏倚，按设计逻辑不可能发生结局的一段随访时间被错误记到治疗头上。）
  • Intention-to-treat vs per-protocol — ITT is the effect of being assigned a strategy; per-protocol is the effect of actually following it.（中文：意向性治疗 vs 符合方案，ITT 是被分配到某策略的效应，符合方案是真正遵循它的效应。）
  • New-user (incident-user) vs prevalent-user design — new-user starts everyone at first exposure (matches trial randomization of naive patients); prevalent-user enrolls existing users and selects for tolerant survivors.（中文：新使用者 vs 既有使用者设计，前者从首次暴露开始，后者纳入已在用者、筛出耐受幸存者而产生偏倚。）
  • OMOP CDM — the common data model that standardizes clinical tables so one analysis runs on any mapped source.（中文：OMOP CDM，把临床数据表标准化的通用数据模型，让同一分析能跑在任何已映射数据源上。）
  • Positivity — every eligible person must have a nonzero probability of each strategy, or inverse-probability weights become unstable.（中文：正性，每个合格者对每种策略都须有非零概率，否则逆概率权重会失稳。）
  • Target trial — the hypothetical RCT you would run to answer the question; emulation imitates its protocol with observational data.（中文：目标试验，为回答问题而假想的 RCT；模拟用观察性数据模仿其方案。）
  • Time zero — the aligned instant at which eligibility is met, strategy is assigned, and follow-up begins.（中文：时间零点，纳入满足、策略分配、随访开始三者对齐的瞬间。）

Full citation

Hernan MA, Wang W, Leaf DE. Target Trial Emulation: A Framework for Causal Inference From Observational Data. JAMA. 2022;328(24):2446-2447. PMID 36508210 · DOI 10.1001/jama.2022.21383. Not OA; metadata via PubMed (abstract not available).

Methods / tools to lift

  • Seven-component protocol table — force eligibility, treatment strategies, assignment, follow-up, outcome, causal contrast, and analysis plan to be written before analysis.
  • Time-zero alignment — the single most bias-preventing step; align eligibility assessment, assignment, and start of follow-up.
  • New-user design — start follow-up at first exposure to avoid prevalent-user survivor selection.
  • Clone-censor-weight — the estimator machinery for grace-period "start-now vs delay" strategies.
  • Inverse-probability weighting — for per-protocol effects, weight to handle non-adherence and censoring; pairs naturally with flexible (doubly-robust) nuisance models.
  • Negative-control outcomes — a residual-confounding detector.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

  • Serves: AI-tooling / career (reproducible-RWE-software lane); the same template later feeds a Liz-sponsored effectiveness question (method-only).
  • MVP (paste-ready): tte-protocol-template — a parametrized notebook/package that ingests an OMOP-CDM cohort + a YAML protocol spec (the 7 components), enforces time-zero alignment, applies eligibility using only baseline-available fields, and emits (a) a trial-ready analytic table and (b) a clone-censor-weight skeleton for grace-period strategies + a printable protocol summary. Difficulty: M/L (estimator math is standard; correctness lives in time-zero alignment and cloning logic).

What to verify first

  • No unmeasured confounders is load-bearing: enumerate the confounders the target trial would balance and confirm each is captured at baseline (method-side; no claim about any registry's internal completeness).
  • Positivity — every eligible person needs nonzero probability of each strategy.
  • Time-zero correctness — audit that eligibility, assignment, and follow-up truly coincide (commonest failure).
  • Adherence measurement for the per-protocol contrast; pre-declare ITT vs per-protocol before seeing results.
  • Negative-control outcomes to surface residual confounding.
  • Method template on a generic mapped cohort only — no CFFPR-internal genotype/data claims.

ALEX Today's Track B pick is "Target Trial Emulation: A Framework for Causal Inference From Observational Data," by Miguel Hernan, Wei Wang, and David Leaf, a JAMA Guide to Statistics and Methods from 2022. 今天 Track B 选的是 Miguel Hernan、Wei Wang 和 David Leaf 于 2022 年在《JAMA》"统计与方法指南"栏目发表的《目标试验模拟：从观察性数据进行因果推断的框架》。 One honesty note up front: I could not retrieve this paper's text, or even an abstract, through PubMed or Europe PMC — it's a paywalled two-page methods guide. 先说一句实话：我无法通过 PubMed 或 Europe PMC 取到这篇论文的正文，甚至连摘要都没有——它是一篇付费的两页方法指南。 So today I'm teaching the target-trial-emulation framework as it is established across Hernan and Robins' broader body of work, and Runyu should read the actual JAMA piece for its exact emphasis; I'll claim no specific numbers or quotes from this paper. 所以今天我讲的是 Hernan 与 Robins 更广泛的著作中已确立的目标试验模拟框架，Runyu 应去读那篇 JAMA 原文以获取其确切侧重；我不会声称任何来自这篇论文的具体数字或引文。 It's in Track B because it's the canonical framework for turning registry data into RCT-grade effect estimates. 它属于 Track B，因为它是把登记库数据转化为随机对照试验级别效应估计的经典框架。

JORDAN Let's define the vocabulary first, because this is specialized even for someone comfortable with causal inference. 我们先把术语定义清楚，因为即便对熟悉因果推断的人来说，这些也很专门。 What is a "target trial"? 什么是"目标试验" (target trial)？

ALEX The target trial is the hypothetical randomized controlled trial you would run to answer your question, if ethics and money were no object. 目标试验就是——假如伦理和经费都不成问题——你为回答问题而会去做的那个假想随机对照试验 (RCT，randomized controlled trial)。 Emulation means you write that trial's protocol down explicitly, then use observational data to imitate each component of it. 模拟 (emulation) 的意思是：你把那个试验的方案明确写下来，然后用观察性数据去模仿它的每一个组成部分。 The single most important idea is "time zero" — the moment eligibility is met, the treatment strategy is assigned, and follow-up starts, all aligned to the same instant. 最重要的一个概念是"时间零点" (time zero)——纳入条件被满足、治疗策略被分配、随访开始，这三者对齐到同一个瞬间。 When those three fall out of alignment, you get immortal time bias. 当这三者错位时，你就会得到不死时间偏倚 (immortal time bias)。

JORDAN Unpack immortal time bias for me. 帮我拆解一下不死时间偏倚。

ALEX Immortal time is a stretch of follow-up during which, by the design's own logic, a patient could not have had the outcome. 不死时间是一段随访期，在这段时间里，按照设计本身的逻辑，患者不可能发生结局事件。 Classic example: if you define the "treated" group as people who filled a prescription, then the time between study entry and that prescription is time they had to survive to be classified as treated. 经典例子：如果你把"接受治疗"组定义为"领了处方药的人"，那么从入组到领药之间的这段时间，是他们必须存活下来才能被归为"已治疗"的时间。 That guaranteed survival gets wrongly credited to the treatment, making it look protective when it isn't. 这段被"保证"的存活被错误地记到了治疗头上，让治疗看起来有保护作用，其实并没有。 Target trial emulation kills this by forcing assignment and follow-up to start at the same time zero. 目标试验模拟通过强制"分配"与"随访"从同一个时间零点开始，来消除这种偏倚。

JORDAN And "new-user" versus "prevalent-user"? 那"新使用者"和"既有使用者"呢？

ALEX A prevalent-user design enrolls people already on a drug, which selects for survivors who tolerated it — a stacked deck. 既有使用者 (prevalent-user) 设计纳入的是已经在用某药的人，这就筛选出了能耐受该药的幸存者——一副做过手脚的牌。 A new-user, or incident-user, design starts everyone at their first exposure, matching how a real trial randomizes treatment-naive patients. 新使用者 (new-user，或称 incident-user) 设计让每个人都从其首次暴露开始，这与真实试验对未经治疗的患者进行随机化的方式相匹配。 Then two more terms: the grace period is a window during which a person can start the assigned strategy and still count as adherent. 再讲两个术语：宽限期 (grace period) 是一段时间窗，人们在此期间开始所分配的策略仍算作依从。 And there are two causal contrasts — intention-to-treat, the effect of being assigned a strategy, versus per-protocol, the effect of actually following it. 还有两种因果对比——意向性治疗 (ITT，intention-to-treat)，即被分配到某策略的效应，对比符合方案 (per-protocol)，即真正遵循该策略的效应。

JORDAN Quick clarification — why is adjusting for baseline confounders a stand-in for randomization? 快速澄清一下——为什么对基线混杂做调整可以替代随机化？ ALEX: Randomization works because it makes treatment groups exchangeable — comparable on everything, measured or not. 随机化之所以有效，是因为它让各治疗组可交换 (exchangeable)——在一切因素上都可比，无论测没测到。 You cannot randomize in a registry, so you buy a weaker version of exchangeability by measuring the variables that drive both treatment choice and outcome and adjusting them away. 在登记库里你无法随机化，于是你退而求其次：测量那些同时驱动治疗选择和结局的变量，再把它们调整掉，换来一个较弱版本的可交换性。 It only equals randomization if you measured all such variables — that is the conditional-exchangeability assumption, and it is an assumption, not a guarantee. 只有当你测全了所有这类变量，它才等同于随机化——这就是条件可交换性假设，而它是一个假设，不是保证。

JORDAN Why does the field need this framework at all? 这个领域到底为什么需要这个框架？

ALEX Because observational studies and randomized trials have a long history of disagreeing, and people blamed unmeasured confounding by default. 因为观察性研究与随机试验长期存在分歧，而人们习惯性地把锅甩给未测量的混杂 (confounding)。 The famous case is hormone replacement therapy: observational studies suggested it protected the heart, but randomized trials found harm. 最著名的案例是激素替代疗法 (HRT，hormone replacement therapy)：观察性研究提示它保护心脏，随机试验却发现有害。 Reanalyses showed much of that gap came not from mysterious confounding but from avoidable design errors — misaligned eligibility and start of follow-up, prevalent users, immortal time. 重新分析表明，那道鸿沟很大程度上并非来自神秘的混杂，而是来自可避免的设计错误——纳入条件与随访起点错位、既有使用者、不死时间。 When the observational analysis was redesigned to emulate the trial's protocol, the estimates moved much closer together. 当把观察性分析重新设计成模拟试验方案时，两者的估计就靠近了许多。 So the framework's purpose is to make you specify the trial you're imitating, which surfaces those errors before they poison the result. 所以这个框架的目的，是逼你把你正在模仿的那个试验写清楚，从而在错误毒害结果之前把它们暴露出来。

JORDAN So concretely, what does "writing down the protocol" mean? 那具体来说，"把方案写下来"是什么意思？

ALEX And notice what that reframing does — it turns an unfalsifiable excuse into a checklist. 而且请注意这种重构起到的作用——它把一个无法证伪的借口变成了一份清单。 "Unmeasured confounding" can explain away any discrepancy and can never be checked; "your follow-up started before your eligibility was assessed" can be pointed at and fixed. "未测量的混杂"可以搪塞掉任何分歧，且永远无法核查；而"你的随访在纳入条件评估之前就开始了"是可以被指出并修正的。 That shift from blame to audit is the whole reason the framework caught on. 从"甩锅"到"审计"的这一转变，正是这个框架流行起来的全部原因。

ALEX You specify seven components, the same seven any trialist writes. 你要明确七个组成部分，和任何试验设计者要写的七个是一样的。 One, eligibility criteria — who's in, assessed only on information available at time zero. 一，纳入标准——谁入组，且只依据时间零点时可获得的信息来判定。 Two, treatment strategies — the arms being compared, defined precisely enough to be actionable. 二，治疗策略——被比较的各组，定义得足够精确以可执行。 Three, the assignment procedure — a real trial randomizes; the emulation instead adjusts for the confounders measured at baseline and argues that assignment is as-if random within levels of those covariates. 三，分配程序——真实试验做随机化；模拟则改为对基线测得的混杂因素做调整，并论证在这些协变量各水平内分配"近似随机"。 Four, the follow-up period — when the clock starts and stops. 四，随访期——计时何时开始、何时结束。 Five, the outcome. 五，结局。 Six, the causal contrast — intention-to-treat or per-protocol. 六，因果对比——意向性治疗或符合方案。 Seven, the analysis plan, which for per-protocol effects usually means inverse-probability weighting to handle adherence and censoring. 七，分析计划，对于符合方案效应，通常意味着用逆概率加权 (IPW，inverse-probability weighting) 来处理依从性与删失。 For grace-period strategies there's a specific trick — cloning, censoring, and weighting — where each person is copied into every strategy compatible with their baseline, then censored when they diverge, with weights correcting the censoring. 对于带宽限期的策略，有一个特定技巧——克隆、删失、加权 (clone-censor-weight)——把每个人复制进所有与其基线相容的策略里，等他们偏离时再删失，并用权重校正删失。

JORDAN Push back on one thing. 挑一处质疑一下。

ALEX Here's the honest limit: target trial emulation removes design-induced bias, but it does not remove unmeasured confounding. 这是诚实的局限：目标试验模拟消除的是设计导致的偏倚，但它并不能消除未测量的混杂。 Step three still rests on a "no unmeasured confounders" assumption — that everything driving both treatment and outcome was measured at baseline. 第三步仍然依赖"无未测量混杂"的假设——即所有同时驱动治疗和结局的因素都在基线被测量了。 In registry data that assumption is heroic, and the framework doesn't verify it; it just makes your reliance on it explicit. 在登记库数据中，这个假设是很勇的，而框架并不验证它；它只是让你对该假设的依赖变得明确。 The value is that it converts a vague "we adjusted for confounders" into a falsifiable protocol you can critique component by component. 它的价值在于，把含糊的"我们调整了混杂"变成一份可证伪的方案，让你能逐个部件地批评它。

JORDAN Make the treatment-strategy piece concrete for me. 帮我把"治疗策略"那部分讲具体点。 ALEX: Take a modulator-initiation question, framed purely as method. 拿一个"何时启动调节剂"的问题为例，纯粹作为方法演示。 One strategy might be "start the therapy at the first visit you become eligible"; the comparator might be "delay initiation." 一种策略可能是"在你首次符合条件的那次就诊就开始治疗"；对照可能是"推迟启动"。 A real trial would randomize patients between those; the emulation instead identifies, at each patient time zero, everyone who was eligible and classifies their observed strategy. 真实试验会在两者间随机分配患者；模拟则改为在每位患者的时间零点识别所有合格者，并对其观测到的策略分类。 The grace period matters here — a patient who starts within, say, ninety days can still be counted under the "start now" strategy, which is why the clone-censor-weight machinery exists. 宽限期在这里很关键——一个在比如九十天内开始治疗的患者仍可算作"立即启动"策略，这正是克隆-删失-加权机制存在的原因。 ALEX: The reason registries are attractive for this is that they are longitudinal, so time zero, exposure, and outcomes are all timestamped — which is exactly the raw material the framework needs. 登记库之所以适合做这个，是因为它们是纵向的 (longitudinal)，所以时间零点、暴露和结局都有时间戳——这正是这个框架所需要的原材料。 The flip side is that a registry records care as it happened, not as a protocol dictates, so eligibility and adherence have to be reconstructed carefully rather than read off a field. 另一面是，登记库记录的是"实际发生的医疗"，而非"方案规定的医疗"，所以纳入条件和依从性必须被小心地重建，而不能直接从某个字段读出。

JORDAN Does emulation actually recover trial results when you test it? 当你去检验时，模拟真的能还原试验结果吗？

ALEX In the broader literature — and I'm flagging that I'm not sourcing this specific paper — emulations of completed randomized trials have repeatedly recovered the trial's own effect estimate when the target trial was well specified, across statins, hormone therapy, and other benchmarks. 在更广泛的文献中——我要标明这不是从这篇论文取材的——当目标试验被良好设定时，对已完成随机试验的模拟屡屡还原出试验本身的效应估计，涵盖他汀、激素疗法等多个基准。 The failures are informative too: where emulations diverged, the divergence usually traced back to a specific, nameable protocol mismatch, not to hand-waving about confounding. 失败案例同样有信息量：当模拟出现分歧时，那分歧通常可追溯到某个具体的、能点名的方案不匹配，而非对混杂的含糊搪塞。 That is the framework's real contribution — it makes disagreements diagnosable. 这才是这个框架真正的贡献——它让分歧变得可诊断。

JORDAN Does this change how I should think about throwing ML at registry effectiveness questions? 这会改变我"把机器学习砸向登记库效果评估问题"的思路吗？ ALEX: It reorders the priorities. 它重排了优先级。 A fancier outcome model cannot rescue an analysis whose time zero is misaligned or whose eligibility peeks at the future — the bias is baked in before any model runs. 更花哨的结局模型救不了一个时间零点错位、或纳入条件"偷看未来"的分析——偏倚在任何模型运行之前就已铸进去了。 So the framework says: get the design right first, then machine learning has a legitimate, bounded job — estimating the nuisance pieces like the treatment and censoring weights. 所以这个框架说：先把设计做对，然后机器学习才有一个正当且有界的活儿——估计那些"讨厌参数"，比如治疗权重和删失权重。 That is the doubly-robust idea: flexible models for the weights, sitting inside a causal contrast whose validity comes from the design, not the model. 这就是双重稳健 (doubly-robust) 的思路：用灵活的模型去估权重，而它坐落在一个因果对比之内，这个对比的有效性来自设计，而非模型。 For a senior data scientist, that is the mature move — it is why RWE teams value causal design literacy over another gradient-boosted classifier. 对一名资深数据科学家而言，这才是成熟的做法——这也是为什么真实世界证据团队更看重因果设计素养，而不是再多一个梯度提升分类器。

JORDAN Where does this land for us, and what's the tool? 那这对我们落在哪里，工具是什么？
