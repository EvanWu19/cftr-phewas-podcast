Show notes — SLC6A14 / Mercier 2022 (Track C, CF modifier)

Blurb. Mercier et al. (Front Mol Biosci, 2022) take the CF lung-modifier gene SLC6A14 from genetic association down to mechanism. In a French cohort of 3,257 pancreatic-insufficient people with CF (aged 6–40), the rs3788766 promoter minor allele G was associated with worse lung function (p=0.020; ~−0.038 SaKnorm Z per G allele, ~−1.5% ppFEV1 for ≥1 G carriers; MAF 0.38). A luciferase reporter showed the G-allele promoter is 12.1% weaker in CFTR-deficient bronchial cells — i.e., the deleterious allele lowers SLC6A14 expression. Notably, GTEx shows no significant lung eQTL for rs3788766, which the authors attribute to GTEx sampling deep lower-lobe tissue and avoiding the bronchi, whereas immunohistochemistry localizes SLC6A14 to bronchial epithelium. Pharmacological blockade (α-MT) cut arginine transport 54%, reduced mTOR phosphorylation, and slowed scratch-wound repair dose-dependently (8/27/39%), replicated in primary F508del-homozygous CF cells. Proposed chain: weaker promoter → less transporter → less arginine → lower mTOR signaling → slower airway epithelial repair. Where it lands: summary-stats colocalization. Buildable artifact: a tissue-aware eQTL-coverage checker that flags CF modifier loci where GTEx lung is likely uninformative because the disease-relevant airway cells aren't sampled.

Plain-English glossary

  • eQTL (expression quantitative trait locus) — a genetic variant statistically associated with how much a gene is expressed; GTEx is the main public cross-tissue atlas.（中文：eQTL，与某基因表达水平在统计上相关的遗传变异位点；GTEx 是主要的跨组织公开图谱。）
  • F508del — the most common CFTR pathogenic variant; "F508del-homozygous" patients carry two copies.（中文：F508del，最常见的 CFTR 致病变异；纯合患者携带两个拷贝。）
  • Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium — the expected balance of genotype frequencies in a population; here checked only in females because the gene is X-linked.（中文：哈迪-温伯格平衡，人群中基因型频率的预期平衡；因基因 X-连锁，此处仅在女性中检验。）
  • Luciferase reporter assay — cloning a promoter (one allele or the other) in front of a luciferase gene so promoter activity is read as emitted light.（中文：荧光素酶报告基因实验，把启动子克隆到荧光素酶基因前，把启动子活性读成发光强度。）
  • Meconium ileus — neonatal bowel obstruction present at birth in some CF infants; a CF GI phenotype.（中文：胎粪性肠梗阻，部分 CF 新生儿出生时即有的肠道梗阻，一种 CF 消化道表型。）
  • Modifier gene — a gene that doesn't cause CF but tunes its severity, explaining variability among same-CFTR-genotype patients.（中文：修饰基因，不引起 CF 但调节其严重程度，解释相同 CFTR 基因型患者间的差异。）
  • mTOR — a master signaling kinase balancing cell growth vs breakdown, highly sensitive to arginine and leucine availability.（中文：mTOR，调控细胞生长与分解平衡的主控信号激酶，对精氨酸和亮氨酸的可用性高度敏感。）
  • ppFEV1 / SaKnorm — CF lung-severity measures: percent-predicted forced expiratory volume in 1 s, and a normalized age-comparable severity Z-score.（中文：ppFEV1 / SaKnorm，CF 肺部严重程度度量；前者为占预计值百分比的一秒用力呼气量，后者为可跨年龄比较的标准化严重程度 Z 分数。）
  • Promoter — the regulatory DNA upstream of a gene that controls its transcription level; a promoter variant tunes expression, not protein sequence.（中文：启动子，基因上游控制其转录量的调控 DNA；启动子变异调节表达量，而非蛋白质序列。）
  • Pseudomonas aeruginosa — a bacterium whose chronic airway infection drives CF lung decline; SLC6A14 is tied to age at first acquisition.（中文：铜绿假单胞菌，其慢性气道感染驱动 CF 肺功能下降；SLC6A14 与首次感染年龄相关。）

Full citation. Mercier J, Calmel C, Mésinèle J, et al. SLC6A14 Impacts Cystic Fibrosis Lung Disease Severity via mTOR and Epithelial Repair Modulation. Front Mol Biosci. 2022;9:850261. PMID 35372502 · PMC PMC8965518 · DOI 10.3389/fmolb.2022.850261. According to PubMed; full text via BioC PMC-OA.

Methods / tools to lift

  • Allele-specific luciferase reporter to convert a promoter eQTL hypothesis into a direct functional measurement of promoter strength.
  • Tissue-collection-aware reading of GTEx: cross-check that the disease-relevant cell type is actually represented before trusting a null eQTL.
  • Pharmacological blockade (α-MT) + scratch-wound assay + Western-blot mTOR phosphorylation as a triad linking transporter function → signaling → repair phenotype.
  • Replication of cellular effects in primary F508del-homozygous patient cells, not only an engineered line.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Summary-stats colocalization. MVP (paste-ready): take lead SNPs at each catalogued CF modifier locus → pull GTEx eQTL effects across all tissues → emit per-locus flags for which CF-relevant tissues (bronchial / nasal airway epithelium) are absent or under-sampled, and whether the GTEx lung eQTL is therefore likely uninformative or misleading. Difficulty S–M. Serves as a guardrail before any modifier-locus colocalization against GTEx lung.

What to verify first

  • Association strength: p=0.020 in a single cohort is nominal, not genome-wide; confirm it within the consortium replication record rather than as standalone proof.
  • Mechanism specificity: effects rest on a pharmacological blocker (α-MT); confirm specificity and whether a genetic knockdown reproduces the mTOR/repair phenotype.
  • eQTL-discordance claim: check against a CF-airway-specific (nasal-epithelium) eQTL panel rather than accepting the GTEx-avoids-bronchi argument alone.
  • X-linkage: sex-stratified power/effect estimation differs for an X-linked locus; verify how males vs females were modeled.

Gap-map / catalog

  • Closes: adds a concrete molecular mechanism (promoter-strength → arginine → mTOR → epithelial repair) and the first French-cohort lung-severity confirmation for rs3788766, plus a worked GTEx tissue-sampling caution for this locus.
  • Leaves open: causal-gene resolution at the AGTR2/SLC6A14 chrX locus; whether the genetic G-allele dose change (vs chemical blockade) drives the same mTOR/repair effect; how mTOR, repair, and SLC6A14 are wired (Wnt-signaling lead noted); modulator-era persistence of the effect.

ALEX It sits in the promoter of SLC6A14 — the regulatory stretch of DNA upstream of the gene that controls how much it's transcribed. 它位于 SLC6A14 的启动子 (promoter) 里——基因上游那段控制其转录量的调控 DNA。That's a key clue: a promoter variant is a dial on expression level, not a change to the protein's amino-acid sequence. 这是个关键线索：启动子变异是一个调节表达量的旋钮，而不是对蛋白质氨基酸序列的改变。So the natural hypothesis is that rs3788766 changes how much SLC6A14 protein gets made, rather than making a broken protein. 所以自然的假设是 rs3788766 改变了 SLC6A14 蛋白的产量，而不是造出一个坏掉的蛋白。

JORDAN A couple more terms before the findings — "eQTL" and "luciferase reporter" and the lung-severity measures. 在讲发现之前再来几个词——"eQTL"、"荧光素酶报告基因"，还有肺部严重程度的度量。

JORDAN So where does this land for us, concretely? 那么具体来说，这对我们落在哪里？
