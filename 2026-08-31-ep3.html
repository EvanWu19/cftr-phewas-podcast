Ep3 — Track C (1/2): Utilization of locally estimated scatterplot smoothing (LOESS) regression to estimate missing weights in a longitudinal cohort of breast cancer patients

Abstract-only this episode — Europe PMC lists this record as not open access; both the fullTextXML route and the BioC PMC-OA fallback failed despite a PMCID existing. Zimbalist and colleagues used LOESS regression to estimate outpatient weight at seven fixed timepoints (baseline plus 6/12/24/48/72/96 months post-diagnosis) for 10,778 women with invasive breast cancer in a Kaiser Permanente EHR cohort (2005–2013), comparing it against the incumbent "closest-available measurement" convention. LOESS identified fewer weight estimates at baseline/6 months but significantly more at later follow-up, where actual nearby measurements grow sparse; more than 80% of estimates from both methods agreed within 2.5 kg at every timepoint. This is a missing-data imputation paper, not an outlier-flagging method — but the underlying machinery (a smoothed per-person expected-weight curve from irregular longitudinal data) is the same machinery a residual-based outlier flag needs, making it a candidate addition to the same method family as the spline- and SITAR-based residual approaches already catalogued, pending confirmation of methodological details the abstract doesn't specify.

Plain-English glossary

  • Outpatient weight — a weight measurement taken during a clinic visit rather than a hospital admission; more stable for tracking baseline body weight since inpatient weights can be distorted by acute illness, IV fluids, or clothing.（中文：门诊体重，指门诊就诊而非住院期间测得的体重；由于住院体重可能因急性疾病、静脉输液或衣着而失真，门诊体重更适合追踪基线体重变化。）
  • Closest-available method — the incumbent EHR longitudinal-research convention: estimate a person's value at a target timepoint using whichever actual measurement falls nearest to it within a specified window.（中文：最近可用值法，EHR纵向研究中现有的惯用做法：用落在指定时间窗口内、离目标时间点最近的一次实际测量值来估计该时间点的数值。）
  • Informative missingness (informative visit process) — the concern that whether a measurement exists at all may carry information about what it would show, because EHR visits happen for clinical reasons rather than on a research schedule.（中文：信息性缺失（信息性就诊过程），指某次测量是否存在这一事实本身可能携带关于其结果的信息，因为EHR就诊是出于临床原因而非按研究时间表安排的。）
  • LOESS (locally estimated scatterplot smoothing) — a nonparametric curve-fitting method that produces a smoothed estimate at any point using locally weighted neighboring data, applied here per longitudinal weight series instead of the DS-standard cross-sectional use.（中文：局部加权散点平滑法，一种非参数曲线拟合方法，利用局部加权的邻近数据在任意点给出平滑估计，此处应用于纵向体重序列而非典型的横截面场景。）
  • Span/bandwidth — the LOESS tuning parameter controlling how much neighboring data influences each local estimate; not specified in this paper's abstract.（中文：窗宽参数，控制LOESS每个局部估计受多少邻近数据影响的调节参数；本文摘要未说明具体取值。）
