Proteome-wide prediction of the functional impact of missense variants with ProteoCast — Abakarova M, Freiberger MI, Liehrmann A, Rera M, Laine E. Nature Communications, 2026.

Blurb

ProteoCast wraps GEMME — a fast, fully unsupervised, evolutionary-conservation-based mutation-effect predictor — into an automated pipeline that classifies every possible missense substitution in a proteome as neutral, mild, or impactful, using a per-protein adaptive threshold (a three-component Gaussian mixture fit to that protein's own score distribution) rather than one global cutoff. Applied to the complete Drosophila melanogaster proteome (22,169 proteoforms, ~293M substitutions), it correctly flagged 85% of known lethal mutations and 88%/82% of DGRP/DEST2 natural population variants as neutral, then went five-for-five on live CRISPR validation in the gene Naprt, out-resolving the older tool SIFT on a borderline variant SIFT scored as ambiguous. It also maps sensitivity onto AlphaFold structures to find regulatory sites hiding in disordered regions with no defined 3D fold — validated on Yorkie/YAP1's TEAD-binding domain. For us this lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation: ProteoCast's own Docker image and GitHub repo are explicitly runnable on any organism's protein, so the buildable move is a CFTR-specific GEMME landscape with the same adaptive per-protein classification, including a disordered-region sensitivity scan of CFTR's R-domain.

Plain-English glossary

  • Multiple sequence alignment (MSA) — aligning the same protein across many species or individuals, column by column, so each column represents one protein position.（中文：多序列比对——把同一蛋白质在多个物种或个体间逐列对齐，每一列代表蛋白质中的一个位置。）
  • Evolutionary conservation — how unchanged a position stays across evolutionary time; highly conserved positions are more likely functionally essential.（中文：进化保守性——一个位置在进化历程中保持不变的程度；高度保守的位置更可能在功能上至关重要。）
  • Mutational landscape — a full scorecard of predicted effect for every possible amino-acid substitution at every position of a protein (length L × 20 substitutions).（中文：突变图谱——对蛋白质每个位置上所有可能氨基酸替换的预测效应打分表（长度 L × 20 种替换）。）
  • GEMME — a fast, unsupervised, MSA-based method that scores a mutation's effect by how it reshapes a protein's position on its evolutionary tree; no GPU required.（中文：GEMME——一种快速、无监督、基于 MSA 的方法，通过突变对蛋白质在进化树上位置的重塑程度来打分，不需要 GPU。）
  • pLDDT — predicted local distance difference test; AlphaFold's per-residue confidence score (0–100) for its own 3D structure prediction.（中文：预测局部距离差异检验——AlphaFold 对其自身 3D 结构预测中每个残基的置信度评分，0 到 100。）
  • Proteoform — a specific protein isoform arising from alternative splicing of a given gene.（中文：蛋白质异构体——由同一基因经可变剪接产生的特定蛋白质形式。）
  • Protein language model (pLM) — a deep-learning model trained across huge swaths of protein sequence space (e.g. ESM-2); generalizes well but is computationally heavy and largely a black box.（中文：蛋白质语言模型——在海量蛋白质序列空间上训练的深度学习模型（如 ESM-2）；泛化能力强，但计算开销大且基本是黑箱。）
  • Deep mutational scanning (DMS) — large wet-lab experiments that directly measure a mutation's effect on protein function, used as ground truth for benchmarking predictors.（中文：深度突变扫描——直接在湿实验室中测量突变对蛋白质功能影响的大规模实验，用作预测工具的基准真值。）
  • RNAi (RNA interference) — a technique for knocking down a gene's expression, used here to test whether a candidate gene is essential before CRISPR point-mutation validation.（中文：RNA 干扰——一种基因敲低技术，本文用它在 CRISPR 点突变验证之前测试候选基因是否为必需基因。）
  • R-domain — CFTR's regulatory domain, substantially intrinsically disordered and central to channel gating control.（中文：R 结构域——CFTR 的调控结构域，高度天然无序，是通道门控调控的核心。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • GEMME — fast unsupervised MSA-based mutational-landscape predictor; no training labels, no GPU.
  • ColabFold (MMseqs2) — cheap, fast MSA generation against UniRef + environmental sequence databases, the step that makes proteome-scale GEMME runs feasible.
  • Per-protein adaptive Gaussian-mixture classification — fit a 3-component mixture to each protein's own score distribution rather than importing one universal REVEL/CADD-style cutoff.
  • Confidence filtering — protein-level (MSA depth) and residue-level (score dispersion + conservation + MSA coverage) flags for unreliable predictions.
  • FPOP segmentation — changepoint algorithm applied to per-residue sensitivity profiles to find regulatory/binding hotspots even in AlphaFold-disordered regions.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation. MVP (paste-ready): Run the ColabFold-then-GEMME pipeline on human CFTR to generate a full single-site mutational landscape (~1480 residues × 19 substitutions), fit ProteoCast's 3-component Gaussian-mixture classification to CFTR's own score distribution, map onto CFTR's cryo-EM/AlphaFold structure, and run FPOP segmentation over the R-domain to flag high-sensitivity disordered stretches. Input: CFTR reference sequence + structure. Output: full landscape table with neutral/mild/impactful calls, per-residue confidence flags, ranked disordered-region hotspots. Difficulty: L.

What to verify first

  • Cross-species transfer — all confidence and classification thresholds were calibrated on a fly proteome against fly-specific benchmarks (DGRP/DEST2/FlyBase); whether the same recipe transfers to a single well-studied human gene needs its own check, not an assumption.
  • Mild/impactful boundary — the paper never rigorously validated this split, only neutral-vs-functionally-altering; a CFTR VUS landing in "mild" is a weaker signal than a clean impactful/neutral call.
  • R-domain hotspot cross-check — any high-sensitivity segment found by segmentation should be cross-checked against known R-domain phosphorylation sites and regulatory motifs (PTM databases, PDB structures) before being read as biologically meaningful, the same way the authors validated Yorkie's segment.
