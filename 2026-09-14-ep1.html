Ep1 (Track A) — Deep mutational scanning of CYP2C9, CYP2C19, and NUDT15: pharmacogene variant interpretation requires assay-specific functional data

Blurb. General-purpose pathogenicity predictors like AlphaMissense output a single benign-to-pathogenic scalar for any missense variant, calibrated on Mendelian disease-gene data. This paper shows that scalar breaks down for pharmacogenes, where a variant's clinical effect depends on which biochemical dimension it perturbs — protein stability, catalytic competence, or substrate-specific handling — not just whether it's "damaging" in some generic sense. Using five deep mutational scanning (DMS) datasets from MaveDB spanning CYP2C9, CYP2C19, and NUDT15 (26,198 missense variants total), the authors show that 28% of CYP2C9 variants and 48% of NUDT15 variants decouple one functional axis from another, and that AlphaMissense systematically mis-scores a meaningful share of these discordant cases — calling stable-but-catalytically-dead CYP2C9 variants benign, and destabilized-but-thiopurine-resistant NUDT15 variants pathogenic. A supervised ESM-2 baseline matched or marginally beat AlphaMissense on raw functional scores, but struggled to recover the discordance signal itself (AUROC 0.60 for CYP2C9, 0.51 for NUDT15 — near chance). CFTR isn't a pharmacogene in this dial-shifting sense, but it has the same underlying structure (destabilization/trafficking-defect vs. intact-but-impaired gating/conductance), making this a direct methodological caution for the Karen Raraigh CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration: don't trust a single VEP score without knowing which functional dimension it was actually validated against.

Plain-English glossary

  • Deep mutational scanning (DMS) — an experimental method that systematically introduces nearly every possible amino-acid substitution in a protein and measures a functional score for all of them in one pooled assay.（中文：一种系统性地对蛋白质产生几乎所有可能氨基酸替换，并在一次汇总检测中同时测量所有变异体功能评分的实验方法。）
  • MaveDB — a public repository hosting DMS datasets ("multiplexed assays of variant effect"), enabling researchers to pool and compare scanning data across labs and genes.（中文：收录DMS数据集的公开数据库，全称"多重变异效应检测"，使研究者能够跨实验室、跨基因汇总比较扫描数据。）
  • Pharmacogene — a gene whose variants alter how a person metabolizes or responds to a specific drug, rather than causing disease on their own.（中文：药物基因，指其变异会改变个体对某种特定药物的代谢或反应方式、而非自身直接致病的基因。）
  • Missense variant — a single-nucleotide change that alters the amino acid encoded at that position in the protein.（中文：错义变异，指使蛋白质对应位置编码氨基酸发生改变的单核苷酸变化。）
  • Thiopurine — a class of immunosuppressant drugs (used in inflammatory bowel disease, some leukemias) whose safe dosing depends on NUDT15 and TPMT variant status.（中文：硫嘌呤类药物，一类免疫抑制剂，用于炎症性肠病和部分白血病治疗，其安全剂量取决于NUDT15和TPMT的变异状态。）
  • AlphaMissense — a deep-learning model that outputs a single likely-benign-to-likely-pathogenic score for any missense variant, calibrated on clinical pathogenicity data.（中文：一种深度学习模型，基于临床致病性数据校准，为任意错义变异输出一个从"可能良性"到"可能致病"的单一评分。）
  • ESM-2 / ESM1v (protein language models) — transformer models pretrained on large protein sequence databases; ESM-2 here is fine-tuned (supervised) on DMS labels, ESM1v is used zero-shot (no task-specific fine-tuning).（中文：在大规模蛋白质序列数据库上预训练的transformer模型；此处ESM-2以监督方式用DMS标签微调，ESM1v则以零样本方式使用，不做任务专属微调。）
  • VEP (variant effect predictor) — any computational tool that scores a genetic variant's likely functional or clinical impact.（中文：变异效应预测工具，指对遗传变异的功能或临床影响进行评分的计算工具的统称。）
  • Substrate-channel residues — the specific amino acid positions in an enzyme that physically contact the substrate molecule during catalysis.（中文：底物通道残基，指酶在催化过程中与底物分子发生物理接触的特定氨基酸位点。）
  • Gating / conductance (CFTR-specific framing) — for a channel protein like CFTR, gating refers to opening/closing of the channel and conductance to how much ion flow passes through when open; these are functionally distinct from whether the protein folds and traffics to the membrane at all.（中文：门控与电导——对CFTR这类通道蛋白而言，门控指通道的开合，电导指通道打开时离子通过的量；这两者与蛋白质是否能正确折叠并运输到细胞膜是不同的功能维度。）

Full citation

Kırboğa KK. "Deep mutational scanning of CYP2C9, CYP2C19, and NUDT15 shows that pharmacogene variant interpretation requires assay-specific functional data." G3 (Bethesda, Md.), 2026. PMID: 42667690 | no PMCID on record | DOI: 10.1093/g3journal/jkag248

Methods / tools to lift

  • MaveDB paired-assay design — using two independent functional readouts on the same variant set to directly test whether a computational score captures mechanism, not just aggregate "damaging-ness."
  • Position-based k-fold cross-validation — holding out variants by protein position rather than randomly, to avoid leaking position-level information across folds.
  • Discordance-recovery evaluation — scoring a model not just on raw functional-score correlation but on whether it recovers the difference between paired assay readouts, a stricter and more clinically relevant test.
  • ESM-2 supervised fine-tuning on DMS labels — a comparatively simple sequence-based baseline that matched or beat more complex structure-aware and multi-task extensions in this paper.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation collaboration. MVP: build a small annotation layer (not a new model) for a shortlist of CFTR VUS or discordant-classification variants, noting which functional dimension (folding/trafficking vs. gating/conductance) any existing assay evidence in CFTR2 or the literature actually speaks to, and treating a VEP score as informative only for the dimension it was validated against. Difficulty: S-to-M (the work is CFTR-specific literature curation, not new modeling).

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR's functional-effect landscape actually decomposes cleanly into folding/trafficking vs. gating/conductance the way CYP2C9's abundance-vs-catalysis split does — untested here, an analogy not a finding.
  • Whether existing CFTR functional assay data (e.g., from CFTR2's characterization efforts) is structured as a paired-assay dataset, or would need to be assembled from separate studies.
  • That this paper's own discordance-recovery numbers were modest (AUROC 0.60 and 0.51) even with a purpose-built model and full DMS label access — no assumption that a similar approach would perform meaningfully better on CFTR without dedicated training data.
  • No claims made here about CFFPR or CFTR2 data internals, current variant-classification rules, or what evidence currently backs any specific CFTR VUS.

ALEX Deep mutational scanning, DMS, is an experimental method where you systematically make almost every possible amino-acid substitution in a protein, then run all of those variants through a functional assay at once, so you get a measured functional score for thousands of variants in a single experiment, rather than testing them one at a time in a wet lab. 深度突变扫描（deep mutational scanning，DMS）是一种实验方法：系统性地对一个蛋白质产生几乎所有可能的氨基酸替换，然后把这些变异体一次性放入功能检测（functional assay）中，这样就能在一次实验里对成千上万个变异体获得实测的功能评分，而不需要在湿实验室里逐一检测。
