Ep4 — Track C: classifying a child's whole BMI trajectory, not just the latest number

Ebrahim N, Khadegi A, Deng S, Qian K, Yao Z, Thaker S, May B, Patibandala N, Lopez-Pintado S, Thaker VV. (2025). Classification of childhood obesity using longitudinal clinical body mass index and its validation. International Journal of Obesity.

Note: abstract-only. Full text was not retrievable through Europe PMC's fullTextXML (MED and PMC routes) or the BioC PMC-OA fallback, despite the paper having a PMCID. Everything below is grounded strictly in the published abstract.

For children with three or more BMI measurements, the authors classify longitudinal obesity status using the median BMI percentile across visits plus whether the child persisted in the same obesity class rather than fluctuating — a way of filtering single-visit noise (measurement error, illness, growth spurts) without a formal statistical noise model. Validated in a primary cohort of 22,352 children (2014–2018) with independent replication in 24,444 children — real external replication, a notable strength. Obesity (≥95th percentile) was 24.1%, severe obesity (≥120% of 95th percentile) 10.6%; early-onset obesity (≤6y) predicted persistence; higher SES predicted lower odds. The longitudinal classification predicted cardiometabolic risk better than cross-sectional BMI alone (AUC 80.0% vs. 75.8%, p<0.001).

Plain-English glossary

  • BMI percentile — a child's BMI compared to a reference distribution of same-age, same-sex children, rather than a fixed adult-style cutoff, since a healthy weight-for-height changes as children grow.（中文：将某个儿童的 BMI 与同龄同性别儿童的参考分布进行比较得出的结果，而不是像成人一样使用固定界值，因为儿童的健康身高体重范围会随生长而变化。）
  • CDC vs. WHO growth references — two different reference-population standards used to compute pediatric growth percentiles; they can classify the same child slightly differently near threshold boundaries.（中文：两套用于计算儿科生长百分位数的不同参考人群标准；在界值附近，两者可能对同一儿童给出略有不同的分类结果。）
  • Obesity / severe obesity thresholds — obesity is defined as BMI at or above the 95th percentile for age and sex; severe obesity as at or above 120% of that 95th-percentile value.（中文：肥胖定义为 BMI 达到或超过同年龄同性别第 95 百分位；重度肥胖定义为达到或超过该第 95 百分位数值的 120%。）
  • Persistence (in an obesity class) — remaining in the same classification category across multiple longitudinal visits, used here as evidence of a real, sustained pattern rather than single-visit noise.（中文：在多次纵向就诊中持续保持在同一分类类别，此处用作证明存在真实、持续模式（而非单次就诊噪声）的证据。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Median-percentile-plus-persistence classification — a simple, non-model-based way to define a child's "expected class" from ≥3 longitudinal measurements.
  • External replication cohort design — validating a classification rule in a fully independent cohort rather than only via internal cross-validation.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (d) evaluation — primary; offers a per-child "expected class" reference (median percentile + persistence) against which a single new encounter's plausibility could be judged. Secondarily touches (a) candidate generation — deviation from a child's own established class as a candidate-flagging feature distinct from population-percentile-based flags. Buildable artifact: a per-child rolling median-percentile + class-persistence feature, surfacing new encounters that deviate from the child's own established class. Difficulty: Small-to-Medium.

What to verify first

  • Full methods/results (currently abstract-only) — the exact statistical model, and sensitivity of findings to the ≥3-measurement threshold and the persistence definition, are unknown from the abstract alone.
  • How the classification handles children whose obesity class shifts across visits without settling into a stable pattern — not addressed in the abstract.
  • Population mismatch: thresholds are calibrated to a general pediatric population's overnutrition risk, not a CF-specific population, where undernutrition has historically been at least as much a concern, and where modulator-era weight trajectories may not resemble the general population's.
  • Whether "severe obesity" persistence is even the relevant end of the distribution to track for a CF cohort, versus other trajectory features.

Error types covered / not covered

Covered: distinguishing a real, sustained obesity pattern from single-visit measurement noise via a persistence rule. Not covered: unit conversion errors, digit transposition, decimal-shift errors, carry-forward errors, or any explicit data-entry-error detection — this paper is a phenotype classification method, not an error-flagging method.
