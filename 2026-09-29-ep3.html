Ep3 (Track C, 1 of 2) — Measurement error and misclassification in EMR: a taxonomy and mitigation review

Young JC, Conover MM, Funk MJ (2018). Measurement error and misclassification in electronic medical records: methods to mitigate bias. Current Epidemiology Reports.

A narrative review, not a new method — read for vocabulary and a map of what's already been tried. The authors organize EMR measurement error into eight domains (care fragmentation across institutions, unfilled prescriptions, ambiguous treatment-episode boundaries, stale carried-forward data, NLP-pipeline extraction error, cross-system algorithm-performance drift, recording-practice drift over time, and linkage bias) with a citation-backed summary table. Two directly relevant candidate-generation pointers surface inline: the conditional growth percentile method (Yang et al. 2016), which flags outliers against a time-dependent hierarchical model of a patient's own trajectory, and a 2018 extension (Shi et al.) that adjudicates which of two consecutive implausible-jump measurements is the erroneous one — at the cost of requiring investigators to pre-define what counts as "clinically implausible." For Runyu, this paper's value is mainly taxonomy and two primary-source leads to chase, not a ready-made pipeline component.

Plain-English glossary

  • Alloyed gold standard — An imperfect but useful reference (most often manual chart abstraction) used when a true gold standard for a value isn't available.（中文:合金金标准,在无法获得真正金标准时使用的次优但仍有用的参考,最常见的是人工病历摘录。）
  • Look-back window — The fixed period (often one year) a study scans backward from a given date to assess medical history; too short a window can discard genuinely relevant history.（中文:回溯窗口,研究向前扫描以评估病史的固定时间段,过短会丢弃相关病史。）
  • Vertical linkage — Connecting the same patients across different data sources (e.g. EMR joined to insurance claims) to surface care invisible to the original source.（中文:垂直链接,把同一批患者跨不同数据源连接起来。）
  • Horizontal linkage — Connecting different populations captured by separate EMR systems into one combined cohort, which can introduce systematic classification differences between sources.（中文:水平链接,把不同电子病历系统各自覆盖的人群连接成一个合并队列。）
  • Conditional growth percentile method — Flags an outlier by comparing an observed value to an expected value estimated from a time-dependent hierarchical model of the patient's own history.（中文:条件生长百分位法,通过对比观测值与基于患者自身历史轨迹估计的期望值来标记离群值。）
  • Multiple imputation (chained equations / MICE) — Generating several plausible replacement datasets and pooling results across them, rather than deleting or blindly trusting a flagged value.（中文:多重插补(链式方程),生成多个可信替代数据集并汇总结果,而非直接删除或盲目信任被标记的值。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Eight-domain measurement-error taxonomy — a reusable checklist for precisely describing any EMR measurement-error problem.
  • Conditional growth percentile method (Yang et al. 2016) — outlier flagging against a patient-specific expected trajectory, no external gold standard required.
  • Implausible-change adjudication (Shi et al. 2018) — determines which of two consecutive measurements is erroneous, given investigator-defined implausibility rules.
  • Vertical/horizontal linkage framing — distinguishes cross-source validation linkage from cross-population cohort-pooling linkage.
  • Lesko et al. censoring guidance — rules for handling right-censoring when events can only be recognized during an observed encounter.
