Ep1 (Track A) — Are Pathogenic Variants Concentrated in a Protein's Tunnels?

Špačková et al. (2025, Bioinformatics Advances) ask a structural-genomics question with an obvious CFTR echo: within a protein family, are variants sitting inside a substrate-transport tunnel, or right next to an essential cofactor, more likely to be pathogenic than variants elsewhere? Using 60 canonical human cytochrome P450 (CYP) proteins — modeled via AlphaFill since only 27 have solved structures — the authors traced substrate tunnels with the MOLE algorithm and scored every residue's pathogenicity three independent ways (AlphaMissense, SIFT, PrimateAI-3D). All three tools showed a statistically significant increase in average pathogenicity moving from whole-protein, to tunnel-lining, to cofactor-proximal residues, with AlphaMissense following the pattern in 93.1% of residues tested. The mechanistic story holds up: the single most pathogenic residue class, cysteine, is the heme's axial ligand; glycines cluster in tight turns needed for the fold; phenylalanines and arginines gatekeep the substrate channel and mediate the CYPOR redox-partner interaction. CFTR is structurally the same kind of object — a chloride-conduction pore (the tunnel) gated by two nucleotide-binding domains (the cofactor-pocket analogue) — and already has solved cryo-EM structures, making the buildable artifact a direct cross-reference rather than a new structural computation: score CFTR VUS by proximity to the pore/NBD and test whether the same gradient holds.

Plain-English glossary

  • AlphaFill — extends AlphaFold's predicted (ligand-free) protein structures by transplanting cofactors and ligands from homologous solved structures onto the model. （中文：AlphaFill，在AlphaFold预测的无配体蛋白结构基础上，将辅因子和配体从同源的已解析结构"移植"到模型上的方法。）
  • Cofactor (heme/HEM) — a non-protein, iron-containing molecule that a CYP enzyme must bind to perform any catalytic chemistry. （中文：辅因子（血红素/HEM），一种含铁的非蛋白质分子，CYP酶必须结合它才能进行任何催化化学反应。）
  • CYP (cytochrome P450) — a 57-gene human enzyme family central to drug metabolism, foreign-chemical processing, and vitamin D3 synthesis. （中文：细胞色素P450（CYP），人类一个包含57个基因的酶家族，在药物代谢、外源化学物质处理和维生素D3合成中起核心作用。）
  • MOLE — a computational tool that traces tunnels (paths connecting a buried active site to the protein surface) through 3D protein structures. （中文：MOLE，一种通过三维蛋白质结构追踪"通道"（连接埋藏活性位点与蛋白表面的路径）的计算工具。）
  • PrimateAI-3D — a 3D-convolutional-neural-network-based variant pathogenicity predictor incorporating evolutionary conservation and AlphaFold structure. （中文：PrimateAI-3D，一种基于三维卷积神经网络的变异致病性预测工具，结合了进化保守性和AlphaFold结构信息。）
  • Tunnel-lining residue — an amino acid position identified as forming the wall of a protein's substrate-access tunnel. （中文：通道内壁残基，被识别为构成蛋白质底物通道壁的氨基酸位点。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • MOLE tunnel detection — computationally traces substrate-access tunnels through a 3D protein structure and identifies lining residues.
  • AlphaFill — supplies cofactor/ligand-enriched structural models for proteins lacking a solved structure.
  • Three-way pathogenicity cross-check — scoring the same residues with a structure+PLM tool (AlphaMissense), a conservation-only tool (SIFT), and a 3D-CNN tool (PrimateAI-3D) to test whether a structural gradient is convergent-valid across independent methodologies.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen collaboration (CFTR variant interpretation / VUS triage). MVP: cross-reference CFTR's published pore-lining and NBD1/NBD2 nucleotide-contact residue lists (from existing cryo-EM structural annotation) against currently-uncertain CFTR VUS, score those positions with AlphaMissense, and test whether VUS inside the pore or nucleotide-binding pocket score systematically more pathogenic than VUS elsewhere — the same protein-vs-tunnel-vs-cofactor gradient this paper found for CYPs, applied to CFTR's own analogous structures. Difficulty: S — a data join against existing residue lists and AlphaMissense scores, not new structural computation.

What to verify first

  • Small N (56–60 CYP proteins, only 41 with PrimateAI-3D coverage) — check whether CFTR's own pore/NBD residues reproduce the pattern rather than assuming a family-level average transfers.
  • AlphaMissense reliability is known to drop in intrinsically disordered regions; CFTR's R-domain is intrinsically disordered, so the structural-gradient framework may not extend cleanly there even if it holds for the ordered pore/NBD regions.
  • Check whether "distance from the pore" is genuinely new signal or a proxy for "buried vs. exposed," a feature AlphaMissense's own structural inputs may already capture.
  • SIFT only followed the full protein-tunnel-cofactor gradient on 45% of residues — for any single CFTR VUS, check which specific predictors agree on that residue rather than citing family-level convergence alone.

ALEX Track A today is a 2025 paper in Bioinformatics Advances by Špačková and colleagues, "Pathogenicity patterns in cytochrome P450 family." 今天A轨道的论文来自2025年发表在《生物信息学进展》（Bioinformatics Advances）上的研究，作者是Špačková等人，题目是《细胞色素P450家族中的致病性模式》。 It's a structural-genomics paper, not a CFTR paper directly, but the question it asks has an obvious echo in CFTR's own architecture: are variants sitting inside a protein's substrate-transport tunnel, or right next to its essential cofactor, more likely to be pathogenic than variants scattered anywhere else in the protein? 这是一篇结构基因组学论文，不是直接讲CFTR的，但它提出的问题在CFTR自身的结构里有明显的对应：位于蛋白质底物转运通道内部、或紧邻其必需辅因子的变异，是否比散布在蛋白质其他位置的变异更可能致病？ CFTR is, structurally speaking, built around exactly this kind of tunnel — its chloride-conduction pore — so this is a direct methods borrow for the Karen collaboration on variant interpretation. 从结构上讲，CFTR本身正是围绕这样一条通道构建的——它的氯离子传导孔道——所以这是一次可以直接借用到Karen变异解读合作中的方法迁移。

JORDAN Before we get into the pathogenicity scoring, what exactly is a "tunnel" in a protein, as distinct from just a binding pocket? 在讲致病性评分之前，蛋白质里的"通道"（tunnel）到底是什么，它和单纯的结合口袋（binding pocket）有什么区别？

ALEX A tunnel is a path that connects a buried active site to the outside of the protein, so that substrates, products, and cofactors can move in and out without the whole structure having to be open to solvent. 通道是连接埋藏在蛋白质内部的活性位点与蛋白质外部的一条路径，它使得底物、产物和辅因子能够进出，而不需要整个结构对溶剂完全开放。 For cytochrome P450 enzymes, or CYPs, the active site sits deep inside a mostly closed protein core, so a tunnel is what lets a drug molecule reach the reactive iron center and lets the metabolized product leave again. 对于细胞色素P450酶（简称CYP）来说，活性位点深埋在一个大部分封闭的蛋白核心内部，因此正是这条通道让药物分子能够到达具有反应活性的铁中心，也让代谢产物能够再离开。 This paper used a tool called MOLE to computationally trace those tunnels through 3D protein structures, identifying which residues line the walls of the path. 这篇论文使用了一个叫MOLE的工具，通过计算方式在三维蛋白质结构中追踪这些通道，识别出哪些残基构成了通道壁。 The cofactor here is heme, sometimes written HEM — an iron-containing, non-protein molecule that every CYP absolutely requires bound in its core to do any chemistry at all, the way an enzyme's own built-in reactive center. 这里的辅因子是血红素，有时写作HEM——一种含铁的非蛋白质分子，每个CYP都必须在其核心结合这个分子才能进行任何化学反应，相当于酶自带的一个反应中心。 And since most CYPs don't actually have a solved crystal structure — only 27 of 60 do — the authors leaned on AlphaFill, which extends AlphaFold's predicted structures by transplanting cofactors and ligands from homologous solved structures onto the model, since plain AlphaFold predicts an empty, ligand-free shape. 而且由于大多数CYP实际上并没有解出晶体结构——60个里只有27个有——作者们借助了AlphaFill，它在AlphaFold预测结构的基础上，把辅因子和配体从同源的已解析结构中"移植"到模型上，因为普通的AlphaFold预测出的是一个空的、不含配体的形状。

JORDAN So the setup is: find the tunnel, find the cofactor, and see if pathogenicity is worse near either one. 所以整个设计是：找到通道，找到辅因子，然后看致病性在这两者附近是否更高。 Why did anyone think that would be true in the first place? 一开始为什么有人会认为这是成立的？

ALEX CYPs are a big, medically important family — 57 genes in humans, involved in drug metabolism, foreign-chemical processing, vitamin D3 synthesis, and calcium and phosphorus homeostasis. CYP是一个庞大且具有重要医学意义的家族——人类有57个基因，参与药物代谢、外源化学物质处理、维生素D3合成，以及钙磷稳态调节。 Specific CYP variants are already tied to real disease: loss-of-function mutations in CYP1B1 cause primary congenital glaucoma, a CYP2C19 poor-metabolizer allele is linked to endometriosis risk, a CYP2E1 promoter variant is tied to alcoholic liver disease risk, and a CYP2J2 variant has been associated with cardiovascular complications. 特定的CYP变异已经被证实与真实疾病相关：CYP1B1的功能丧失突变导致原发性先天性青光眼，CYP2C19的慢代谢型等位基因与子宫内膜异位症风险相关，CYP2E1启动子区的一个变异与酒精性肝病风险相关，CYP2J2的一个变异则与心血管并发症相关。 A prior study had already shown that pathogenic CYP mutations are enriched in buried residues generally, compared to surface-exposed ones, which is a familiar finding across many protein families. 此前已有研究表明，致病性CYP突变在整体上更富集于埋藏残基，相较于暴露在表面的残基，这是许多蛋白质家族中都常见的发现。 What this paper adds is finer resolution: not just "buried is worse," but specifically, is the tunnel — which has to stay open and precisely shaped to let the right molecules through — and the cofactor pocket, even more sensitive than the buried interior generally? 而这篇论文补充的是更精细的分辨率：不只是"埋藏就更差"，而是更具体地问——通道（它必须保持开放并具有精确的形状，才能让正确的分子通过）以及辅因子结合口袋，是否比整体埋藏区域更加敏感？ The hypothesis is that these are the most functionally constrained real estate in the whole protein, so a substitution there should be disproportionately more likely to break the enzyme than a substitution somewhere structurally unimportant. 假设是，这些区域是整个蛋白质中功能约束最强的"地段"，因此在这里发生的替换应该比在结构上不重要的位置发生的替换，更不成比例地容易破坏酶的功能。

ALEX To test it, they queried UniProt for reviewed, canonical human CYP entries, which gave 60 proteins, pulled AlphaFill models enriched with heme for each, and ran MOLE with default settings to detect tunnels — which worked for 56 of the 60; four proteins had no detectable tunnel under those settings and were dropped from the tunnel analysis. 为了检验这一点，他们在UniProt中检索经过审核的、规范的人类CYP条目，得到60个蛋白，为每一个都提取了富含血红素的AlphaFill模型，并用默认参数运行MOLE来检测通道——这在60个中的56个里成功了；有4个蛋白在这些参数下未检测到通道，被排除在通道分析之外。 Tunnel-lining residues were defined by sampling points along the tunnel's centerline and taking the five closest protein atoms at each point; cofactor-proximal residues were defined the same way, but centered on each heme atom instead. 通道内壁残基的定义方法是：沿通道中心线取样若干点，在每个点取最近的五个蛋白原子；辅因子邻近残基的定义方式相同，只是中心点改为血红素的每个原子。 Pathogenicity was then scored three separate ways for every residue — AlphaMissense, SIFT, and PrimateAI-3D — averaged across all possible non-synonymous substitutions at that position, and then compared across the whole-protein, tunnel, and cofactor subsets. 接下来，每个残基的致病性用三种独立方法分别打分——AlphaMissense、SIFT和PrimateAI-3D——并在该位置所有可能的非同义替换上取平均值，然后在整个蛋白、通道和辅因子这三个子集之间做比较。 I'd push back a little on that averaging step: a missense effect is highly substitution-specific — proline to glycine and proline to tryptophan are very different changes — so averaging over every possible substitution at a residue smooths that away, and the real question is whether that's the right summary for "how sensitive is this position overall," or whether it's hiding substitution-level signal that would matter more for any one real CFTR variant. 我对这个求平均的步骤有些保留：错义突变的效应高度依赖具体的替换方式——脯氨酸变甘氨酸和脯氨酸变色氨酸是非常不同的变化——所以对某个残基上所有可能的替换取平均，会把这种差异抹平，真正的问题是，这对于"这个位置整体上有多敏感"这个问题来说是不是合适的概括方式，还是说它掩盖了对任何一个具体真实CFTR变异更重要的替换层面的信息。

JORDAN And did the pattern actually hold up across all three pathogenicity tools? 这个模式在三种致病性工具上都成立吗？

ALEX Yes, and fairly convincingly. 是的，而且相当令人信服。 All three tools showed a statistically significant increase in average pathogenicity moving from whole protein, to tunnel-lining residues, to cofactor-proximal residues — paired t-tests confirmed this for nearly every comparison, with the sole exception of SIFT's protein-versus-tunnel comparison, which landed at p equals point-zero-five-five, just above the conventional significance threshold. 三种工具都显示出，从整个蛋白到通道内壁残基，再到辅因子邻近残基，平均致病性呈现出统计学显著的递增——配对t检验证实了几乎所有比较都成立，唯一的例外是SIFT在"整个蛋白 vs 通道"这一比较上，P值为0.055，刚好高于常规显著性阈值。 AlphaMissense adhered to the full protein-tunnel-cofactor gradient in 93.1% of amino acids tested, PrimateAI-3D in 63.4%, and SIFT — which uses an inverted, sequence-conservation-based scale and is generally more conservative — only 45.0%, though even SIFT showed the same overall directional trend. AlphaMissense在93.1%的被测氨基酸上都符合"蛋白-通道-辅因子"这一完整梯度，PrimateAI-3D是63.4%，而SIFT——它使用一种反向的、基于序列保守性的量表，通常更保守——只有45.0%，不过即便是SIFT，也显示出同样的整体方向性趋势。 PrimateAI-3D gave a particularly clean separation: benign substitutions clustered around a mean score of 0.47, pathogenic ones around 0.77. PrimateAI-3D给出了一个特别干净的区分：良性替换的平均分聚集在0.47左右，致病性替换聚集在0.77左均。 And critically, all three independently-built tools — one leaning on structure and a protein language model, one on pure sequence conservation, one on a 3D convolutional network — converged on the same small set of most-pathogenic amino acids in tunnels and cofactor sites: cysteine, glycine, phenylalanine, and tryptophan. 关键的是，这三种独立构建的工具——一种依赖结构和蛋白质语言模型，一种依赖纯序列保守性，一种依赖三维卷积网络——都收敛到了通道和辅因子位点上同一小组最具致病性的氨基酸：半胱氨酸、甘氨酸、苯丙氨酸和色氨酸。

ALEX That convergence has a clean mechanistic explanation. 这种收敛有一个清晰的机制性解释。 The cysteine is the single most critical residue in any CYP — it's the axial thiolate ligand that coordinates the heme iron directly, essential for electron transfer and oxygen activation, so a substitution there essentially kills the enzyme outright. 半胱氨酸是任何CYP中最关键的单个残基——它是直接配位血红素铁的轴向硫醇盐配体，对电子转移和氧激活至关重要，因此在这里发生替换基本上会直接使酶失活。 Glycines cluster in tight turns around the heme-binding loop, where their lack of a side chain is what allows the sharp backbone angles the fold needs; phenylalanines form part of a motif contributing to the substrate channel's hydrophobic walls and act as gatekeeping residues shaping what can pass through; and arginines mediate the protein-protein interaction with CYP's redox partner, the reductase enzyme CYPOR, that supplies the electrons for catalysis. 甘氨酸聚集在血红素结合环周围的紧密转角处，正是因为它没有侧链，才允许蛋白骨架形成折叠所需的尖锐角度；苯丙氨酸构成了底物通道疏水壁的一部分基序，并充当"看门"残基，塑造哪些分子能够通过；而精氨酸则介导与CYP的氧化还原伙伴——还原酶CYPOR——之间的蛋白质相互作用，后者为催化反应提供电子。 As one concrete example tying this back to real disease, two tunnel-lining variants in CYP1B1, A330F and V364M, are already ClinVar-annotated as causing primary congenital glaucoma and related anterior-segment disorders — exactly the pattern this paper's statistics predict. 举一个把这一切与真实疾病联系起来的具体例子，CYP1B1中的两个通道内壁变异A330F和V364M，已经在ClinVar中被注释为导致原发性先天性青光眼及相关前节疾病——这正是本文统计结果所预测的模式。 So yes, on the question of whether it delivered: three independent methodologies, converging on the same structural gradient and the same hot residues, for a reasonably well-understood biochemical reason. 所以，回到这项工作是否达到了预期目标这个问题：三种独立的方法，收敛到同一个结构梯度和同一组热点残基，背后还有一个相当清楚的生化学原因。

JORDAN Before we get to CFTR — did the amino-acid composition itself differ between the tunnel and the rest of the protein, separate from the pathogenicity scores? 在讲到CFTR之前——通道和蛋白质其他部分之间，氨基酸组成本身是否有区别，这是独立于致病性分数之外的一个问题？

ALEX Yes, and that composition shift is part of why the pathogenicity gradient makes sense in the first place. 是的，而这种组成上的变化，正是致病性梯度之所以成立的部分原因。 Across whole CYP structures, leucine is the single most common residue, which isn't surprising for a membrane-associated, largely hydrophobic protein family. 在整个CYP结构中，最常见的单一残基是亮氨酸，这对于一个膜结合、整体高度疏水的蛋白家族来说并不意外。 Moving into the tunnel, phenylalanine and isoleucine both become relatively more frequent than they are in the protein overall, consistent with a channel that needs a tightly packed hydrophobic wall. 进入通道内部后，苯丙氨酸和异亮氨酸的相对频率都比在整个蛋白中更高，这与一条需要紧密堆积疏水壁的通道相符。 But around the cofactor specifically, the pattern flips: arginine becomes the most frequent residue, alongside increases in threonine and glycine — a shift toward residues that can hydrogen-bond or electrostatically interact with the charged heme-propionate groups, rather than just pack tightly against a hydrophobic substrate. 但在辅因子周围，这个模式发生了反转：精氨酸成为最常见的残基，同时苏氨酸和甘氨酸的比例也上升——这是向能够与带电的血红素丙酸基团形成氢键或静电相互作用的残基转变，而不仅仅是与疏水底物紧密堆积。 The authors also found that pathogenicity correlated with position in the CYP phylogenetic tree, especially for SIFT, where entire branches of closely related CYPs shared similar pathogenicity ranges — which makes sense given SIFT is itself built directly on sequence conservation and multiple-sequence alignment. 作者们还发现，致病性与CYP系统发育树上的位置相关，尤其是在SIFT的结果中，关系密切的CYP在同一分支上往往共享相似的致病性范围——考虑到SIFT本身就是直接建立在序列保守性和多序列比对之上的，这个结果是合理的。

JORDAN So does this change anything for how we'd think about CFTR variants specifically? 那么，这对我们具体思考CFTR变异会带来什么改变吗？

CFTR在结构上正是与带有通道的CYP同一类对象——它是一个离子通道，它的全部工作就是充当一条形状精确的路径，只不过在这里传导的是氯离子而不是药物分子，这条路径贯穿两个跨膜结构域。 It also has two nucleotide-binding domains, NBD1 and NBD2, which gate that pore open and closed via ATP binding and hydrolysis — structurally playing a role analogous to the cofactor pocket in this paper's framework, a small, essential, precisely-shaped site that the whole protein's function depends on. 它还有两个核苷酸结合结构域，NBD1和NBD2，通过ATP的结合与水解来控制这条孔道的开闭——在结构上，其角色类似于本文框架中的辅因子口袋：一个微小、必需、形状精确的位点，整个蛋白质的功能都依赖于它。 Unlike most CYPs, CFTR already has high-resolution cryo-EM structures solved directly, so
