Track A — Predicting Splicing from Primary Sequence with Deep Learning (SpliceAI)

Blurb. Jaganathan et al. (Cell, 2019) introduce SpliceAI, a deep dilated residual convolutional network that predicts, base by base, whether a position in a pre-mRNA is a splice donor, acceptor, or neither — using primary sequence alone, with a 10,000-nucleotide receptive field. From the abstract: synonymous and intronic "splice-altering" predictions validate at a high rate on RNA-seq, are strongly depleted by natural selection, and are enriched among de novo mutations in autism/intellectual-disability patients; the authors estimate 9–11% of pathogenic mutations in rare-disease patients act through this under-appreciated splice mechanism. This paper is not open access, so the deep dive draws on the abstract plus established public facts about the SpliceAI architecture; numeric claims are restricted to the abstract. Where it lands: the Karen Raraigh variant-interpretation collaboration. Buildable artifact: a CFTR splice-variant evidence/discordance table that flags variants currently labeled benign/VUS on coding grounds but carrying a high splice-delta.

Plain-English glossary

  • Acceptor site — the sequence signal at the 3' end of an intron where the spliceosome cuts and joins to the next exon.（中文：受体位点，内含子末端、剪接体进行切割并连接下一个外显子的序列信号。）
  • Cryptic splicing — a new or destroyed splice site caused by a variant, producing an aberrant transcript not normally made.（中文：隐匿剪接，变异制造出新的或破坏掉原有的剪接位点，产生异常转录本。）
  • Donor site — the sequence signal at the 5' start of an intron where the spliceosome cuts.（中文：供体位点，内含子起点、剪接体进行切割的序列信号。）
  • De novo mutation — a variant present in a child but neither parent; newly arising.（中文：新发突变，子代有而父母都没有的、新出现的变异。）
  • Exon / Intron — exons are coding stretches retained in mature mRNA; introns are removed during splicing.（中文：外显子是保留在成熟 mRNA 中的编码片段；内含子在剪接时被去除。）
  • HGVS — the standardized written nomenclature for naming variants.（中文：HGVS，变异命名的标准化书写规范。）
  • Isoform — one of several alternative mature transcripts a gene can produce via different splicing.（中文：异构体，一个基因通过不同剪接方式产生的多种成熟转录本之一。）
  • Missense / Synonymous / Nonsense — missense changes an amino acid; synonymous does not; nonsense creates a premature stop.（中文：错义改变氨基酸；同义不改变氨基酸；无义产生提前终止密码子。）
  • Pre-mRNA — the initial transcript, before introns are removed.（中文：前体 mRNA，内含子尚未去除的初级转录本。）
  • Receptive field — the span of input sequence a convolutional model uses to score one position (10,000 nt for SpliceAI-10k).（中文：感受野，卷积模型为某一位置打分时所用的输入序列跨度，SpliceAI-10k 为一万个核苷酸。）
  • RNA-seq — sequencing of transcripts; the ground-truth readout for whether a predicted aberrant junction actually occurs.（中文：RNA-seq，转录本测序，用于验证预测的异常剪接是否真实发生的真值参照。）
  • Spliceosome — the molecular machine that removes introns and joins exons.（中文：剪接体，去除内含子、连接外显子的分子机器。）
  • Splice delta score — the change in donor/acceptor probability between reference and mutated sequence; ≥0.5 commonly calls a variant splice-altering.（中文：剪接 delta 分数，参考序列与突变序列间供体/受体概率的变化量；通常 ≥0.5 判定为改变剪接。）
  • VUS — variant of uncertain significance; a variant not confidently classified benign or pathogenic.（中文：VUS，意义未明变异，尚不能确定为良性或致病的变异。）

Full citation

Jaganathan K, Kyriazopoulou Panagiotopoulou S, McRae JF, et al. Predicting Splicing from Primary Sequence with Deep Learning. Cell. 2019;176(3):535–548.e24. PMID: 30661751 · PMC: n/a (not OA) · DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2018.12.015

Methods / tools to lift

  • SpliceAI — deep dilated residual CNN; takes ±5 kb of sequence context (SpliceAI-10k) and outputs per-base donor/acceptor probabilities; variant scored as the donor/acceptor probability delta between ref and alt.
  • Delta-score variant scoring — run reference vs. mutated sequence through the same network; max change near the variant is the actionable score.
  • Pangolin (successor predictor, lift candidate) — splice predictor that adds tissue-aware usage and often improves recall over SpliceAI; worth running alongside.
  • Orthogonal validation pattern — pair predictions with (1) RNA-seq junction evidence, (2) population depletion/selection, (3) disease-cohort enrichment.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP: Notebook that ingests every CFTR variant from CFTR2 + gnomAD (coding and flanking-intronic), computes SpliceAI and Pangolin donor/acceptor gain/loss deltas, and emits a ranked discordance table — variants labeled benign/VUS on coding grounds but with splice-delta ≥0.5. Input: CFTR-region VCF/variant list. Difficulty: S–M (predictors are pre-computed/downloadable; effort is HGVS↔coordinate harmonization + discordance logic).

What to verify first

  • Locus-specific calibration: does SpliceAI recover known, functionally confirmed CFTR splice variants?
  • "Alters splicing" ≠ "causes CF" — needs functional or phenotype evidence to bridge; do not assume.
  • Tissue specificity — airway epithelium may differ from training tissues; a variant may be context-dependent.
  • HGVS↔genomic-coordinate harmonization for intronic variants is the silent-error hotspot; confirm it's airtight before trusting any discordance call.
  • (Source caveat) Full text not OA — re-confirm any architecture/threshold detail against the paper before building.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读paper时间。 Today's Track A paper is "Predicting Splicing from Primary Sequence with Deep Learning," by Kishore Jaganathan and colleagues, published in Cell in 2019. 今天 Track A 的论文是 Jaganathan 等人 2019 年发表在 Cell 上的《用深度学习从一级序列预测剪接》。 The senior author is Kyle Farh's group at Illumina, and the tool this paper introduces is the one everyone now calls SpliceAI. 通讯作者是 Illumina 的 Kyle Farh 团队，这篇论文提出的工具就是大家现在都叫的 SpliceAI。 One housekeeping note up front: this paper is not open access, so we are reading from the abstract plus the well-established public facts about SpliceAI's architecture. 先说一句：这篇论文不是开放获取的，所以我们是基于摘要再加上 SpliceAI 架构里那些已经广为人知的公开事实来讲的。 Any specific number we quote comes straight from the abstract, and I'll flag when we step beyond it. 我们引用的任何具体数字都直接来自摘要，超出摘要的地方我会特别标注。 We're reading it because splicing is the single most under-served axis of CFTR variant interpretation. 我们读它，是因为剪接是 CFTR 变异解读里最被忽视的一个维度。

JORDAN Okay, before we go further, I want the biology straight. 好，在继续之前，我想先把生物学搞清楚。 What is splicing, and why does a data scientist working on a CF registry need to care? 什么是剪接 (splicing)，一个做囊性纤维化登记数据的数据科学家为什么要在意它？

ALEX Splicing is the editing step between DNA and protein. 剪接是 DNA 到蛋白质之间的剪辑步骤。 Your gene is transcribed into a pre-mRNA that contains coding stretches called exons and intervening stretches called introns. 你的基因先被转录成前体 mRNA（pre-mRNA，即未经剪辑的初级转录本），里面有叫外显子 (exon) 的编码片段，和夹在中间的内含子 (intron)。 The spliceosome cuts out the introns and stitches the exons together to make the mature message that actually gets translated. 剪接体 (spliceosome，负责剪接的分子机器) 把内含子切掉，再把外显子拼接起来，形成真正会被翻译的成熟 mRNA。 It decides where to cut using sequence signals: a donor site at the start of an intron and an acceptor site at the end. 它依靠序列信号来决定在哪里切：内含子起点的供体位点 (donor site) 和终点的受体位点 (acceptor site)。 Here is the part Runyu should hold onto: a mutation does not have to change an amino acid to wreck the protein. 这里有一点 Runyu 要记住：一个突变不需要改变氨基酸也能毁掉蛋白质。 A so-called synonymous or deep-intronic change can create a brand-new splice site, or destroy a real one, producing what we call cryptic splicing. 一个所谓的同义 (synonymous，不改变氨基酸的) 变异或深内含子变异，可以制造出一个全新的剪接位点，或者破坏一个真正的位点，产生我们说的隐匿剪接 (cryptic splicing)。 In CFTR that matters enormously, because some of the most consequential CF alleles are splice variants, not classic missense or nonsense changes. 在 CFTR 里这非常关键，因为一些后果最严重的 CF 等位基因是剪接变异，而不是经典的错义 (missense) 或无义 (nonsense) 突变。

JORDAN So the worry is that a variant looks harmless in the coding readout but is actually breaking the splicing machinery. 所以担心的是：一个变异在编码层面看起来无害，实际上却在破坏剪接机制。

ALEX Exactly, and that brings us to the background. 正是如此，这就引出了研究背景。 Before SpliceAI, predicting splice effects was the weak link in variant interpretation. 在 SpliceAI 之前，预测剪接效应一直是变异解读中最薄弱的一环。 We had decent tools for missense pathogenicity, but splicing predictors were mostly built on short, hand-crafted motif models around the canonical donor and acceptor. 我们对错义致病性有不错的工具，但剪接预测工具大多基于围绕经典供体、受体位点的短的、人工设计的基序 (motif，反复出现的序列模式) 模型。 They captured the obvious cases at the exon boundary and missed almost everything deep in introns. 它们能抓住外显子边界上明显的情况，却几乎漏掉所有深藏在内含子里的变异。 The motivating question of this paper was deceptively simple: can a model read raw pre-mRNA sequence and predict, base by base, whether each position is a splice donor, a splice acceptor, or neither? 这篇论文的核心问题看似简单：一个模型能不能读取原始 pre-mRNA 序列，逐个碱基地预测每个位置是供体、受体、还是都不是？ The contested belief at the time was that you needed experimentally measured features, conservation scores, or expression data to do this well. 当时有争议的看法是，要做好这件事你需要实验测得的特征、保守性分数 (conservation score) 或表达数据。 Farh's group bet that the primary sequence alone, given enough context, contained the answer. Farh 团队押注：只要给足上下文，单凭一级序列本身就包含答案。 The deeper scientific claim is about specificity: the spliceosome achieves astonishing precision, so the rules must be encoded locally enough that a network can learn them. 更深层的科学主张是关于特异性的：剪接体能达到惊人的精确度，所以这些规则一定足够局部地编码在序列里，让一个网络能学到。 If true, that reframes a huge class of "variant of uncertain significance" calls as something a model can actually adjudicate. 如果成立，这就把一大类"意义未明变异 (VUS)"的判定，重新定义成模型真正能裁决的问题。 And for a registry person, it means a class of CFTR variants currently parked as VUS might be re-readable computationally. 而对一个做登记数据的人来说，这意味着一类目前被搁置为 VUS 的 CFTR 变异，也许能用计算重新解读。

JORDAN Give me a concrete CFTR example, so this isn't abstract. 给我一个具体的 CFTR 例子，别让它停在抽象层面。 What does a splice variant in CFTR actually look like? CFTR 里的剪接变异实际长什么样？

ALEX The textbook one is 3849+10kbC>T, a change sitting ten thousand bases deep inside an intron. 教科书级别的例子是 3849+10kbC>T，一个坐落在内含子内部、深达一万个碱基处的变异。 By the coding sequence it changes nothing — it's nowhere near an exon — yet it creates a brand-new cryptic splice acceptor that pulls an extra pseudo-exon into the message, and it's a well-documented CF-causing allele. 从编码序列看它什么都没改——它离外显子很远——但它制造了一个全新的隐匿剪接受体，把一段额外的假外显子 (pseudo-exon) 拉进 mRNA，而它是一个有充分记录的致病 CF 等位基因。 A coding-only annotation pipeline would shrug at that variant; a splice-aware model is the only computational thing that flags it. 一个只看编码的注释流程对这个变异会无动于衷；一个懂剪接的模型是唯一能标记它的计算工具。 Another is 2789+5G>A, which weakens a donor site and produces partial exon skipping, giving milder, residual-function disease. 另一个是 2789+5G>A，它削弱一个供体位点、产生部分外显子跳跃 (exon skipping)，导致较轻的、有残余功能的疾病。 That residual-function angle is why splicing isn't just a yes/no question — the degree of splice disruption tracks with disease severity, and that gradient is what makes it hard. 这个"残余功能"的角度正说明剪接不是一个是/否的问题——剪接破坏的程度和疾病严重度相关，而这个梯度正是它困难之处。 So when we say SpliceAI matters for CFTR, this is the concrete payoff: a class of deep-intronic and donor-weakening alleles that only a sequence model can systematically surface. 所以当我们说 SpliceAI 对 CFTR 重要时，这就是具体的回报：一类深内含子和削弱供体的等位基因，只有序列模型才能系统地把它们浮现出来。

JORDAN Let's get into what they actually built. 我们来看他们到底建了什么。 How does a network predict splice sites from sequence? 一个网络怎么从序列预测剪接位点？

ALEX The published SpliceAI architecture is a deep dilated residual convolutional network. SpliceAI 公开的架构是一个深层的扩张残差卷积网络 (dilated residual convolutional network)。 Let me unpack only the genomics-relevant parts, since Runyu knows convolutions and residual blocks cold. 我只拆解和基因组学相关的部分，因为卷积和残差块 Runyu 早就懂了。 The key design choice is the receptive field: the flagship model, SpliceAI-10k, looks at ten thousand nucleotides of context to score the position in the middle. 关键的设计选择是感受野 (receptive field)：旗舰模型 SpliceAI-10k 会看一万个核苷酸的上下文，来给中间那个位置打分。 That is the genomics insight worth dwelling on. 这正是值得停下来体会的基因组学洞见。 Splice decisions are not purely local; the strength of an exon depends on sequence far upstream and downstream, so a 40-base motif window was always going to fail. 剪接决定不是纯局部的；一个外显子的强度取决于上下游很远的序列，所以一个 40 碱基的基序窗口注定会失败。 The training labels come from annotated transcripts: for each position they assign donor, acceptor, or neither, and the network outputs three probabilities per base. 训练标签来自注释好的转录本：每个位置标为供体、受体或都不是，网络对每个碱基输出三个概率。 ALEX's pushback on the design: training on canonical GENCODE annotations means the model's notion of "truth" is the reference transcript catalog, which is itself incomplete for tissue-specific and minor isoforms. 我对这个设计的质疑是：用经典的 GENCODE 注释来训练，意味着模型对"真相"的定义就是参考转录本目录，而这个目录本身对组织特异和次要异构体 (isoform) 是不完整的。 To score a variant, you run the reference sequence and the mutated sequence through the same network and take the change in donor and acceptor probabilities near the variant. 要给一个变异打分，你把参考序列和突变序列分别送进同一个网络，取变异附近供体和受体概率的变化量。 That delta is the now-familiar SpliceAI delta score, and a common threshold of 0.5 is used to call a variant splice-altering. 这个差值就是现在大家熟悉的 SpliceAI delta 分数，常用 0.5 作为判定变异是否改变剪接的阈值。 The elegant part is that no allele frequency, no conservation, no expression data enters the model — it is sequence in, splice probability out. 优雅之处在于：没有等位基因频率、没有保守性、没有表达数据进入模型——序列进去，剪接概率出来。

JORDAN Why was the sequence-only idea so contested? 为什么"只用序列"的想法这么有争议？ It sounds obvious in hindsight. 事后看起来很显然。

ALEX It always does in hindsight, but think about what people had tried. 事后总是这样，但想想人们之前试过什么。 The earlier splice tools leaned on position weight matrices around the canonical sites, plus bolt-on features like evolutionary conservation and known expression. 早期的剪接工具依赖经典位点周围的位置权重矩阵 (position weight matrix，描述每个位置碱基偏好的打分表)，再加上演化保守性、已知表达这类附加特征。 Those extra features were doing real work, so the prevailing intuition was that primary sequence alone was too impoverished a signal — that you needed the evolutionary and functional annotations to disambiguate true sites from the millions of sequence motifs that look like sites but aren't used. 那些额外特征确实在起作用，所以主流直觉是：单凭一级序列信号太贫乏——你需要演化和功能注释，才能把真正的位点和数百万个看起来像位点、却没被使用的序列基序区分开。 The genome is full of sequences that resemble splice sites; the spliceosome ignores almost all of them. 基因组里充满了像剪接位点的序列；剪接体几乎忽略它们全部。 The puzzle was: how does the cell know which ones are real? 谜题是：细胞怎么知道哪些是真的？ Farh's group's insight was that the answer is in the long-range context — the surrounding ten thousand bases tell you whether a candidate site is actually used, and a deep enough network can learn that context dependence directly from sequence. Farh 团队的洞见是：答案在长程上下文里——周围那一万个碱基会告诉你一个候选位点是否真的被使用，而一个足够深的网络能直接从序列里学到这种上下文依赖。 That's why the receptive-field size wasn't a hyperparameter detail; it was the whole hypothesis. 这就是为什么感受野的大小不是一个超参数细节；它就是整个假说。 When the short-context version of the model underperforms the ten-kilobase version, that gap is the proof that splicing is a long-range code. 当短上下文版本的模型表现不如十千碱基版本时，那个差距就是剪接是一种长程编码的证明。 And the practical upshot for variant interpretation is large: a sequence-only model can score any possible variant, including ones never seen in any database, because it needs no precomputed annotation for the position. 而对变异解读的实际意义很大：一个仅凭序列的模型可以给任何可能的变异打分，包括任何数据库里从未见过的变异，因为它不需要该位置的任何预计算注释。 That generalization to the unseen is exactly what you need for rare CFTR alleles. 这种对未见情况的泛化，正是你应对罕见 CFTR 等位基因所需要的。

JORDAN And does the sequence-only bet actually pay off? 那这个"只用序列"的赌注真的成功了吗？

ALEX According to the abstract, yes, and convincingly. 根据摘要，是的，而且很有说服力。 The headline number is that they estimate 9 to 11 percent of pathogenic mutations in patients with rare genetic disorders are caused by this previously underappreciated class of splice-altering variation. 最重磅的数字是：他们估计在罕见遗传病患者中，有 9% 到 11% 的致病突变是由这类此前被低估的剪接改变变异引起的。 Sit with that figure. 体会一下这个数字。 Roughly one in ten pathogenic variants may act through splicing, and many would be invisible to a coding-only interpretation pipeline. 大约每十个致病变异里就有一个可能通过剪接起作用，而它们中的很多在只看编码的解读流程里是隐形的。 The abstract reports that synonymous and intronic mutations with a predicted splice-altering consequence validate at a high rate on RNA-seq. 摘要报告说，被预测会改变剪接的同义和内含子突变，在 RNA-seq（测量实际转录本的测序方法）上以很高的比例得到验证。 RNA-seq here is the ground-truth readout: if the model says a variant creates a cryptic splice, you should see the aberrant junction in actual transcript reads. 这里 RNA-seq 是真值参照：如果模型说某变异制造了隐匿剪接，你应该在真实的转录本读段里看到异常的剪接连接。 They also report that these predicted splice-altering variants are strongly deleterious in the human population, meaning they are depleted by natural selection — an orthogonal line of evidence. 他们还报告，这些被预测会改变剪接的变异在人群中是强烈有害的，也就是被自然选择清除了——这是一条正交的证据线。 And de novo splice-altering mutations are significantly enriched in patients with autism and intellectual disability versus healthy controls, validating against RNA-seq in 21 of 28 such patients. 而新发 (de novo，父母没有、子代新出现的) 剪接改变突变，在自闭症和智力障碍患者中相比健康对照显著富集，并在 28 名这样的患者中有 21 名经 RNA-seq 验证。 So three independent validations — RNA-seq, population selection, and disease enrichment — all point the same way. 所以三条独立验证——RNA-seq、群体选择、疾病富集——都指向同一个方向。 That is why SpliceAI became the de facto splicing oracle the field standardized on. 这就是为什么 SpliceAI 成了整个领域默认采用的剪接判定标准。

JORDAN One more before we map it over — how confident should we be that a delta score means real disruption? 在我们把它映射过去之前再问一个——一个 delta 分数意味着真实破坏，我们该有多大把握？

ALEX That's the right instinct, and the abstract gives us the honest answer: predictions validate at a high rate, not a perfect one. 这个直觉是对的，而摘要给了我们诚实的答案：预测以很高的比例验证，而不是完美。 In the autism and intellectual-disability cohort, 21 of 28 predicted splice-altering de novo mutations were confirmed against RNA-seq. 在自闭症和智力障碍队列里，28 个被预测会改变剪接的新发突变中有 21 个经 RNA-seq 确认。 That's a strong hit rate, but it also means roughly a quarter were not confirmed in that readout, which could be tissue availability, expression level, or genuine false positives. 这是很强的命中率，但也意味着大约四分之一在那个读出里没被确认，原因可能是组织可得性、表达水平，或真正的假阳性。 The population-genetics evidence is the part I trust most: if these predicted variants are depleted by natural selection, then selection itself is acting as an independent labeler agreeing with the model. 我最信任的是群体遗传学的证据：如果这些被预测的变异被自然选择清除，那么选择本身就在充当一个独立的标注者，和模型达成一致。 For Runyu the lesson is to treat the delta as a strong prior that demands orthogonal confirmation, not a verdict. 对 Runyu 来说，教训是把 delta 当作一个需要正交确认的强先验，而不是一锤定音的判决。
