How did this episode land for you?

Ep1 (Track A) — Bayesian integration of tumor mutational signatures refines pathogenicity assessment of germline mismatch repair variants

Blurb. Variants of uncertain significance (VUS) in mismatch repair (MMR) genes are a persistent bottleneck in Lynch syndrome genetic testing. This paper builds a quantitative Bayesian likelihood-ratio framework that folds tumor-derived evidence — MMR-deficiency mutational signatures, somatic second hits, allele-specific expression, immunohistochemistry, and methylation status — into ACMG/AMP-style germline variant classification, rather than treating tumor context as a qualitative side note. Using paired germline and tumor sequencing from 1110 tumors across 1073 colorectal/endometrial cancer patients, the authors calibrated a likelihood ratio of about 8 for the presence of an MMR-deficiency signature, then applied a full multi-evidence Bayesian model to 45 germline MMR VUS, reclassifying 38 of them (84.4%) — 3 to pathogenic/likely pathogenic, 35 to likely benign — with 16 of the downgrades independently corroborated by an external clinical testing lab. CFTR isn't a cancer gene and has no tumor-signature analog, but CFTR2 already runs on the same underlying Bayesian ACMG/AMP point system, making the transplantable lesson a discipline, not a biology: assign every orthogonal evidence type a defensible likelihood ratio, flag which ones are cohort-calibrated versus literature-borrowed, and multiply them into one posterior instead of stacking evidence codes as a checklist. Buildable artifact: a small Bayesian posterior calculator for a shortlist of CFTR VUS, built on the same structure as this paper's Table 1.

Plain-English glossary

  • Variant of uncertain significance (VUS) — a genetic variant that current evidence cannot confidently classify as pathogenic or benign under ACMG/AMP guidelines.（中文：意义未明变异，指现有证据尚不足以在ACMG/AMP指南下确信判定为致病或良性的遗传变异。）
  • ACMG/AMP guidelines — the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology framework that combines weighted evidence codes into one of five variant classification categories.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学院/分子病理学会联合制定的框架，将带权重的证据代码综合为五个变异分类类别之一。）
  • Bayesian ACMG/AMP point system (Tavtigian calibration) — a formal mapping from evidence strength (supporting/moderate/strong/very strong) to log-scale likelihood-ratio thresholds (0.32/0.64/1.27/2.54), allowing evidence to be combined multiplicatively into a posterior probability.（中文：Tavtigian等人提出的正式贝叶斯计分体系，将证据强度——支持/中等/强/极强——映射为对数尺度的似然比阈值，使证据可以乘法方式结合为后验概率。）
  • Lynch syndrome / mismatch repair (MMR) genes — an inherited cancer-predisposition syndrome caused by a pathogenic germline variant in MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, or PMS2, genes that normally correct DNA replication errors.（中文：Lynch综合征/错配修复基因——一种遗传性癌症易感综合征，由MLH1、MSH2、MSH6或PMS2中的致病性germline变异引起，这些基因正常负责修复DNA复制错误。）
  • Microsatellite instability (MSI) — a detectable pattern of accumulated errors in short repetitive DNA sequences, caused by MMR deficiency.（中文：微卫星不稳定性，指因MMR功能缺陷而在短重复DNA序列中积累的、可检测的错误模式。）
  • Tumor mutational burden (TMB) — the overall number of mutations detected per megabase of a tumor's genome.（中文：肿瘤突变负荷，指肿瘤基因组每百万碱基检测到的突变总数。）
  • Immunohistochemistry (IHC) — a staining technique that detects whether a specific protein (here, an MMR protein) is present in tumor tissue; loss of staining points to which gene failed.（中文：免疇组化，一种染色技术，用于检测特定蛋白（此处为MMR蛋白）在肿瘤组织中是否存在；染色缺失提示哪个基因发生了故障。）
  • COSMIC mutational signatures — catalogued patterns of single-base substitutions across a genome, each linked to a specific mutagenic process (e.g., MMR deficiency).（中文：COSMIC突变signature，指基因组中单碱基替换模式的编目集合，每种模式对应一种特定的致突变过程，例如MMR功能缺陷。）
  • Allele-specific expression (ASE) — whether the mutant vs. wild-type copy of a gene is expressed at a different level in RNA than expected from DNA, used as evidence a variant is being silenced.（中文：等位基因特异性表达，指基因的突变拷贝与野生型拷贝在RNA中的表达水平，是否偏离DNA层面预期比例，可作为该变异正在被沉默的证据。）
  • MLH1 promoter hypermethylation — a somatic, non-genetic silencing mechanism for MLH1 that must be ruled out as an alternative explanation for MMR loss before crediting a germline variant.（中文：MLH1启动子高甲基化，一种体细胞、非遗传性的MLH1沉默机制，在将MMR功能丧失归因于某个germline变异之前，必须先排除这一替代解释。）
  • Somatic second hit — a second, tumor-only mutation in the same gene as the germline variant, consistent with the two-hit model of tumor suppressor inactivation.（中文：体细胞second hit，指与germline变异同一基因上，仅存在于肿瘤中的第二次突变，符合肿瘤抑制基因失活的"二次打击"模型。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Cohort-calibrated likelihood ratio for a single evidence type — deriving sensitivity/specificity from a 2x2 contingency table (evidence present/absent x pathogenic variant present/absent) before assigning a likelihood ratio, rather than asserting evidence strength qualitatively.
  • Multi-evidence Bayesian posterior computation — multiplying several independently-derived likelihood ratios together with a stated prior, following the Tavtigian ACMG/AMP Bayesian calibration.
  • Non-independence handling for confounded evidence — explicitly excluding shared tumor-derived evidence when two candidate variants' phenotypes could stem from the same underlying mechanism (e.g., MutS-alpha destabilization), rather than mechanically multiplying correlated evidence.
  • External concordance check — cross-referencing internal reclassifications against an independent clinical testing lab's own reclassifications as a validity check.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation collaboration. MVP: for a shortlist of CFTR VUS, enumerate the independent evidence types available (computational score, functional assay result if any, population frequency, splice prediction), assign each a likelihood ratio anchored in CFTR-specific literature or a clearly-flagged provisional estimate, and compute one posterior probability per variant using the same multiplicative Bayesian structure as this paper's Table 1. Difficulty: M (the math is simple multiplication; curating defensible, CFTR-specific likelihood ratios is the real work).

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR has any orthogonal evidence category as quantitatively strong as an LR of ~8 — CFTR variants have no tumor-signature analog, so the specific high-value evidence type for CFTR, if one exists, is unverified here.
  • Which likelihood ratios in any CFTR version would be cohort-calibrated versus borrowed from the literature (as this paper's own second-hit and ASE LRs were) — borrowed values should be treated as provisional, not equally trustworthy.
  • How to replicate the non-independence handling for any CFTR case involving more than one candidate variant with potentially confounded evidence.
  • No claims are made here about CFTR2's current classification rules or the evidence already backing any specific CFTR VUS — this paper supplies a combination method, not CFTR-specific inputs.
