How did this episode land for you?

Dai, Haque, Xi, and Costain (Genome Medicine, 2026) pulled together 45 MAVE (Multiplexed Assay of Variant Effect) datasets covering 39 disease genes and asked a simple but unanswered question: if one missense variant at a given amino acid residue is pathogenic, how often is a different missense variant at that same residue also damaging? That's the exact assumption behind PM5, a Moderate-level pathogenicity rule in the 2015 ACMG/AMP variant classification guidelines. The answer, using functional data instead of circular clinical datasets: most residues with one loss-of-function (LOF) variant have at least one other LOF variant nearby, but only a minority clear the likelihood-ratio bar needed to justify Moderate-level evidence as currently applied. This lands on the Karen Raraigh CFTR variant-interpretation priority — not because CFTR was studied here, but because PM5 is routinely applied to CFTR missense ClinVar submissions, and this paper is a reason to check how well-supported those specific applications are. The buildable artifact: a PM5-audit tool that re-scores CFTR missense ClinVar submissions citing PM5 against whatever CFTR-relevant functional/MAVE-style data is available, and flags the ones resting on thin intra-residue support.

Glossary

  • ACMG/AMP — The 2015 variant classification framework from the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology, used to combine evidence into pathogenic/benign calls.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会和分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类框架，用于综合证据得出致病性或良性判断。）
  • ClinGen — A clinical genome resource / expert-panel network that maintains gene-specific variant classification specifications.（中文：一个致力于变异分类标准化的专家协作网络，维护各基因特异性的分类规范。）
  • ClinVar — The public database of clinically submitted and classified human genetic variants.（中文：一个公开的人类基因变异临床分类数据库。）
  • CFTR2 — A database aggregating functional and clinical data on CFTR gene variants.（中文：一个汇总CFTR基因变异功能与临床数据的数据库。）
  • DMS (deep mutational scanning) — A MAVE method that tests many variants of a gene in a model system (e.g., yeast, reporter cell line) and reads out a fitness/function score.（中文：一种MAVE方法，在模型系统（如酵母、报告基因细胞系）中检测大量基因变异并读出适应度或功能评分。）
  • Grantham score — A metric quantifying how chemically different two amino acids are; larger values indicate a more drastic substitution.（中文：量化两个氨基酸化学性质差异程度的评分，数值越大代表替换越剧烈。）
  • IMP_MSS — The working name for a draft revised ACMG missense-variant scoring workflow that adds Grantham-score and in silico predictor points to the PM5 concept.（中文：草案中修订版ACMG错义变异评分流程的代号，在PM5概念基础上加入了Grantham score和计算机预测工具的加减分。）
  • LOF (loss-of-function) — A MAVE functional classification meaning the variant's protein product does not work as intended.（中文：MAVE功能分类之一，指变异对应的蛋白质产物不能正常发挥功能。）
  • FUNC — A MAVE functional classification meaning the variant behaves like wild-type/normal.（中文：MAVE功能分类之一，指变异表现类似野生型，功能正常。）
  • INT — A MAVE functional classification for an intermediate, not clearly LOF-or-FUNC, functional range.（中文：MAVE功能分类之一，指介于LOF和FUNC之间、难以明确归类的中间状态。）
  • Likelihood ratio (LR), in ACMG/AMP context — A statistic mapped to specific cutoffs (2.08/4.33/18.7/350) that correspond to Supporting/Moderate/Strong/Very Strong evidence tiers for pathogenicity.（中文：似然比，在此文中被对应到具体的临界值（2.08/4.33/18.7/350），分别对应支持级/中等级/强/极强的致病性证据等级。）
  • MAVE (Multiplexed Assay of Variant Effect) — An umbrella term for high-throughput experimental methods (including DMS and SGE) that measure the functional impact of nearly all possible variants in a gene.（中文：一类高通量实验方法的统称，用于测量某基因几乎所有可能变异的功能影响，包括DMS和SGE等。）
  • Missense variant — A DNA variant that changes a single amino acid in the encoded protein.（中文：导致编码蛋白中单个氨基酸发生改变的DNA变异。）
  • PM5 — A Moderate-level pathogenicity criterion in the 2015 ACMG/AMP guidelines: a novel missense variant at a residue already known to harbor a pathogenic missense variant gets Moderate-level evidence of pathogenicity.（中文：2015年ACMG/AMP指南中的一条中等级别致病性证据规则：若某残基已知存在致病性错义变异，则该残基上的新错义变异可获得中等级别的致病性证据。）
  • Protein domain — A structurally or functionally distinct, annotated region of a protein.（中文：蛋白质中具有特定结构或功能、被标注出来的区域。）
  • Residue — A single amino acid position within a protein sequence.（中文：蛋白质序列中的一个氨基酸位置。）
  • SGE (saturation genome editing) — A MAVE method that edits the actual genomic locus in human cells to assess nearly all possible variants at that site.（中文：一种MAVE方法，直接编辑人类细胞中真实的基因组位点，评估该位点几乎所有可能的变异。）
  • VEP (in silico variant effect predictor) — Computational tools (e.g., AlphaMissense, REVEL, BayesDel, MutPred2, VARITY, VEST4) that predict a variant's functional impact from sequence/structure features.（中文：根据序列或结构特征预测变异功能影响的计算工具，例如AlphaMissense、REVEL、BayesDel、MutPred2、VARITY、VEST4等。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • MaveDB and MaveRegistry searches — systematic ways to find existing MAVE datasets for a gene of interest rather than starting from scratch.
  • Standardized LOF/FUNC/INT functional-classification scheme — a reusable template for harmonizing disparate MAVE studies' output nomenclature.
  • Residue-level conditional-LOF-likelihood and likelihood-ratio computation — a direct, reusable method for turning raw MAVE scores into ACMG/AMP-style evidence-strength tiers.
  • VEPerform — a tool used here to benchmark in silico predictor performance against MAVE-derived functional classifications.
  • ANNOVAR annotation pipeline — used to attach VEP scores (AlphaMissense, BayesDel, MutPred2, REVEL, VARITY, VEST4) and Grantham distances to variants.
  • Sensitivity-analysis pattern (reassigning a fraction of FUNC↔LOF calls and re-running the pipeline) — a quick way to stress-test any binarization-dependent result.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority: Karen Raraigh collaboration — CFTR variant interpretation and variant-notation harmonization.

Buildable artifact — PM5-audit tool for CFTR: Pull CFTR missense ClinVar submissions that cite PM5, cross-reference against whatever CFTR-specific functional or MAVE-style data is publicly available, compute residue-level intra-residue LOF proportion and likelihood ratio using this paper's method, and flag submissions whose PM5-based Moderate-level evidence is only Supporting-level or weaker. Output is a review worklist, not an automatic reclassification. Difficulty: M (the scoring logic is simple once inputs are in hand; sourcing and harmonizing CFTR-specific residue-level functional data is the hard part).

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR is among genes with usable MAVE or MAVE-style functional coverage at the residue level — CFTR is not named among the 39-gene examples given in the available text.
  • How far the 39-gene findings generalize beyond this dataset, especially for genes/diseases with gain-of-function or dominant-negative mechanisms, which were underrepresented.
  • How sensitive the Moderate-evidence proportions (21.0% of residues) are to the LOF/FUNC binarization threshold, since many thresholds were study-specific or inferred rather than uniformly defined.
  • Whether the draft revised ACMG workflow (IMP_MSS) has moved beyond conference presentations toward an adopted guideline update.
