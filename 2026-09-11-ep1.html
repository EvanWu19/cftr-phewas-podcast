Ep1 (Track A) — Protein Structure Graph Embeddings for Missense Pathogenicity

Danner and colleagues (2025, NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics) test whether an actual predicted 3D protein structure — not just sequence-derived scores like CADD — carries useful signal for classifying missense variants as pathogenic or benign. They fold each variant and its wild type with ESMFold, represent the resulting structure as a graph (atomic-scoped or residue-scoped), compress it into a fixed-length vector with a graph autoencoder (GAE), and feed that embedding — alone or combined with CADD — into an XGBoost classifier. On a family-leakage-controlled held-out set of 12,590 variants from 95 protein families, the best combination (residue-level, 128-dim, structure + node embeddings, CADD included) reaches AUROC 0.902 versus CADD alone's 0.883, and swapping in AlphaFold2 wild-type embeddings pushes that to 0.908 — the paper's best result, despite using no variant-specific structure at all. Embeddings alone (no CADD) top out around 0.72, so this is an add-on to CADD, not a replacement. Lands on the Karen Raraigh collaboration: CFTR already has a solved cryo-EM structure, making it a strong candidate for a structure-aware VUS classifier that skips the folding-prediction step entirely.

Plain-English glossary

  • Missense variant — a single-nucleotide change that swaps one amino acid for another in the resulting protein.（中文:导致蛋白质中一个氨基酸被替换成另一个的单核苷酸变化。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant observed in sequencing where the evidence doesn't clearly support pathogenic or benign classification.（中文:已被测序观察到,但现有证据不足以判定为致病或良性的变异。）
  • Protein structure prediction (ESMFold / AlphaFold2) — computational methods that predict a protein's folded 3D shape from its amino-acid sequence; ESMFold is faster and more portable, AlphaFold2 is generally more accurate.（中文:根据蛋白质氨基酸序列预测其折叠后三维形状的计算方法;ESMFold速度更快、更便携,AlphaFold2通常更准确。）
  • Protein language model (pLM) — a model trained on large numbers of protein sequences the way a language model is trained on text, learning amino-acid co-occurrence and substitution patterns.（中文:像语言模型在文本上训练一样,在大量蛋白质序列上训练的模型,学习氨基酸的共现与替换规律。）
  • Graph embedding / graph autoencoder (GAE) — representing a structure as nodes (atoms or residues) and edges (physical interactions), then compressing that graph into a fixed-length numeric vector by training a model to reconstruct it.（中文:把结构表示为节点(原子或残基)和边(物理相互作用),再通过训练一个能重建该图的模型,将其压缩为固定长度的数字向量。）
  • CADD — a meta-predictor combining conservation, epigenetic, and other sequence-level annotations into one deleteriousness score; never directly uses 3D structure.（中文:整合保守性、表观遗传等序列层面注释信息、输出统一有害性评分的元预测工具,不直接使用三维结构。）
  • AUROC / MCC — standard classifier performance metrics (area under the ROC curve; Matthews correlation coefficient) (not re-explained on air — assumed known).（中文:标准分类器评估指标——ROC曲线下面积和马修斯相关系数。）
  • SHAP — a game-theory-based method that assigns each input feature a contribution score toward a specific prediction.（中文:一种基于博弈论、为每个输入特征分配其对具体预测结果贡献度的方法。）
  • Protein-family-aware data splitting — holding out entire UniProt protein families (not just individual variants) from training, to prevent near-identical proteins from leaking between train and test sets.（中文:按UniProt蛋白质家族整体划分训练/测试集,而非按单个变异划分,以防止高度相似的蛋白质在训练集和测试集之间泄漏。）

Full citation

Danner M, Begemann M, Elbracht M, Kurth I, Krause J. Utilizing protein structure graph embeddings to predict the pathogenicity of missense variants. NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics. 2025;7(3):lqaf097. PMID: 40708850 | PMC12288871 | DOI: 10.1093/nargab/lqaf097

Methods / tools to lift

  • ESMFold-based variant/wild-type structure prediction — fast, portable in silico folding for both a variant and its wild-type counterpart.
  • Atomic-scoped vs. residue-scoped protein graphs — two levels of structural granularity, with residue-scoped graphs losing almost no performance while being cheaper to build.
  • GCEncoder (graph convolutional) / MPNNEncoder (message-passing) — the two graph-autoencoder architectures used for residue- vs. atomic-scoped graphs respectively.
  • Optuna-tuned XGBoost with 5-fold CV — automated hyperparameter search (100 trials/fold), averaged into one meta-optimized model before touching held-out data.
  • SHAP-based per-variant explanation — a feature-attribution breakdown (CADD vs. pooled/node structure embeddings vs. cosine distances) usable as an interpretable output.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh collaboration (CFTR variant interpretation / VUS pathogenicity prediction). MVP: take CFTR2's expert-reviewed missense variants → build a residue-scoped graph from CFTR's existing cryo-EM structure (falling back to ESMFold/AlphaFold2 only for poorly-resolved flexible regions) → train/reuse a graph autoencoder for CFTR-specific embeddings → test whether adding the embedding to CADD (or AlphaMissense) improves separation of CFTR2's own pathogenic/benign calls, with SHAP output as a ready-made per-variant explanation. Difficulty: M (modest modeling code; the CFTR-specific graph construction and CFTR2-labeled test set are the real lift).

What to verify first

  • Whether membrane transporters like CFTR are well represented in ESMFold's/AlphaFold2's training data — both tools are known to be weaker on membrane proteins than soluble, well-crystallized ones.
  • Whether "wild-type structure matters more than variant-specific structural change" (this paper's most counter-intuitive finding) reflects real biology or ESMFold's difficulty modeling small point mutations accurately.
  • Whether this paper's many-gene cross-validation and leakage control generalizes to a single-gene, CFTR-only application, or whether a CFTR2-specific validation split is needed before trusting any performance number.
  • Whether CADD's own calibration holds for CFTR's specific amino-acid composition and constraint pattern, given the combined score only adds structure on top of CADD rather than replacing it.
