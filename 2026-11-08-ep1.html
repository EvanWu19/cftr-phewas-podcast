Blurb

Gogal, Schnieders, and colleagues (PLoS Genetics, 2026) tackle the 303,577-variant VUS backlog in the Deafness Variation Database (DVD, 381,924 missense variants across 224 deafness genes) by building and comparing Bayesian ACMG/AMP pathogenicity models. A REVEL-based model calibrated on DVD's own expert labels outperforms one calibrated on a 2019 ClinVar snapshot; adding gene-level missense-tolerance bins and, finally, a computational estimate of each variant's protein-folding-stability shift (ΔG, in kcal/mol) further improves the model while supplying a mechanistic rationale for pathogenicity. The folding-informed model flags 28,866 VUS above a 98% posterior-probability-of-pathogenic threshold, reclassifies twelve probands to likely pathogenic/pathogenic, and highlights two variants with visually unambiguous structural disruption. It lands on Karen Raraigh's CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration as a template: a folding-stability-informed Bayesian recalibration of CFTR2's own VUS list, complementary to (not a replacement for) existing structure-aware predictors like AlphaMissense.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP — The American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology rule framework that sorts a variant into one of five classes (pathogenic, likely pathogenic, uncertain significance, likely benign, benign) by combining weighted pieces of evidence.（中文:美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会与分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类规则体系,通过整合多条加权证据,把变异归入五个类别之一。）
  • Bayesian point/odds system — The Tavtigian-style framework that converts qualitative ACMG/AMP evidence categories into quantitative likelihood ratios, which multiply together into a single posterior probability of pathogenicity.（中文:Tavtigian提出的框架,把ACMG/AMP中定性的证据类别转化为定量的似然比,这些似然比相乘后得出一个致病性后验概率。）
  • ClinVar — The NIH-hosted public archive of reported variant-classification submissions, used here as a generic (non-disease-specific) comparison training set.（中文:由美国NIH维护的公共变异分类提交记录档案库,本集中作为一个通用、非疾病特异的对照训练集使用。）
  • Deafness Variation Database (DVD) — A public, deafness-gene-specific variant catalog (381,924 missense variants across 224 genes) maintained by the same Iowa-based research group, functionally a disease-specific analogue of CFTR2.（中文:一个公共的、专门针对耳聋基因的变异数据库,收录224个基因的381,924个错义变异,由同一个爱荷华研究团队维护,功能上类似于CFTR2在耳聋领域的对应版本。）
  • Gene-level missense tolerance — A per-gene classification (tolerant/average/intolerant) of how well a gene generally tolerates missense changes, used to set a gene-specific prior probability of pathogenicity before looking at the specific variant.（中文:按基因划分的分类(耐受型/中等型/不耐受型),反映某个基因对错义变异的总体耐受程度,用于在查看具体变异之前设定基因特异的致病先验概率。）
  • In silico predictor — A computational tool (e.g., CADD, REVEL, AlphaMissense) that estimates a variant's likely pathogenicity from sequence, annotation, or structural features, without a wet-lab experiment.（中文:一种计算工具(例如CADD、REVEL、AlphaMissense),无需湿实验,仅通过序列、注释或结构特征来估算变异的致病可能性。）
  • Missense variant — A single-nucleotide change that alters the amino acid encoded at that position in the protein.（中文:错义变异,指单个核苷酸的改变导致蛋白质对应位置的氨基酸发生变化。）
  • Posterior probability of pathogenicity — The final, evidence-combined probability that a variant is disease-causing, produced by the Bayesian model after multiplying together all included likelihood ratios.（中文:致病性后验概率,是贝叶斯模型把所有纳入的似然比相乘后得出的、综合各类证据的最终致病概率。）
  • Proband — The first affected family member through whom a genetic condition is identified in a family.（中文:先证者,指家族中最先被发现患有某种遗传病、并由此引出家系调查的个体。）
  • Protein folding stability (ΔG) — The free-energy difference between a protein's folded and unfolded states, usually reported in kcal/mol; a large destabilizing shift from a variant can mean the protein degrades or never folds correctly.（中文:蛋白质折叠稳定性(ΔG),指蛋白质折叠态与未折叠态之间的自由能差,通常以kcal/mol表示;变异造成的大幅去稳定化,可能导致蛋白质被降解或无法正确折叠。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant for which the accumulated evidence isn't strong enough, in either direction, to classify it as pathogenic/likely pathogenic or benign/likely benign.（中文:意义不明确变异,指积累的证据不足以将其明确归为致病/可能致病,或良性/可能良性。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • Bayesian ACMG/AMP point system (Tavtigian-style) — converts multiple computational evidence lines into a single posterior probability of pathogenicity.
  • REVEL, trained/calibrated against disease-specific labels — outperformed a generic ClinVar-trained version, a caution against borrowing an off-the-shelf calibration wholesale.
  • Gene-level missense-tolerance binning (tolerant/average/intolerant) — sets a gene-specific prior before the variant-level score is applied.
  • Computational protein-folding-stability (ΔG/ΔΔG) estimation — adds a physically interpretable, mechanism-grounded evidence term; the specific structure/ΔΔG tool used is not named in the retrievable abstract.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority: Karen Raraigh's CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration (method only).

Buildable artifact: CFTR Folding-Stability VUS Recalibrator. MVP — take CFTR2's current missense-VUS list, score each variant with a structure-based ΔΔG estimator against an existing CFTR structure, combine that with existing REVEL/CADD scores and a CFTR-specific missense-tolerance estimate into a Bayesian posterior on this paper's model, and flag VUS crossing a chosen high-confidence threshold for human review. Difficulty: Medium (the Bayesian combination is straightforward; a validated ΔΔG estimator and sensible tolerance bins are the real work).

What to verify first

  • Which specific structure-prediction or ΔΔG-estimation tool was used, and whether it has been separately validated against measured ΔG values for any CFTR-relevant protein family.
  • Whether the 98% posterior-probability threshold was itself validated against an independent, outcome-labeled variant set, or chosen post hoc.
  • How sensitive the twelve proband reclassifications are to the exact Bayesian weighting chosen.
  • Whether gene-level missense tolerance, as defined across 224 genes here, transfers cleanly to a single-gene setting like CFTR, which has no comparable multi-gene distribution to calibrate against.
  • Note: none of the above implies a specific research design has already been chosen for the Karen collaboration — these are open questions, not settled plans.
