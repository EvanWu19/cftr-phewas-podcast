Ep1 (Track A) — DisoPatho: predicting disease variants in intrinsically disordered regions

2026-09-04 · Wang X, Zhang S, Jiang H, Liang X, Xue W, Hou H, Liang G (2026) · Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling · Abstract-only

DisoPatho is a deep learning framework purpose-built to score disease-associated missense variants that fall inside intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs) — protein stretches with no fixed 3D shape, where structure-anchored predictors like AlphaMissense are known to be least reliable. Its cross-view, feature-adaptive interaction mechanism fuses IDR-specific energy representations with embeddings from two protein language models, xTrimoPGLM and ESM, deliberately skipping structural descriptors, multiple sequence alignments, and hand-crafted conservation scores. On a deliberately "confounded" independent test set designed to strip out cases where conservation alone gives the game away, DisoPatho beat AlphaMissense by a 50.2% relative improvement in Matthews correlation coefficient, with broader variant coverage. CFTR's R-domain — the regulatory domain between the transporter's two halves, disordered until PKA phosphorylation — is exactly the kind of region this tool targets, making it a direct candidate for triaging ambiguous CFTR VUS calls for the Karen Raraigh collaboration. Buildable artifact: run the public model against CFTR R-domain VUS and flag disagreements with AlphaMissense as a short priority-review list.

Plain-English glossary

  • Intrinsically disordered region (IDR) — a stretch of a protein that never folds into one fixed 3D shape, sampling many conformations instead.（中文：蛋白质中始终不会固定成一种三维形状的区域，会在多种构象间切换。）
  • R-domain — CFTR's regulatory domain, an IDR between the two transmembrane halves that stays unstructured until PKA phosphorylates it and enables channel gating.（中文：CFTR的调节结构域，是位于两个跨膜半区之间的无序区域，需经PKA磷酸化后才具备通道门控功能。）
  • Protein language model (PLM) — a model like ESM or xTrimoPGLM trained on huge sequence corpora to predict masked amino acids, encoding evolutionary/structural patterns implicitly from sequence alone.（中文：如ESM或xTrimoPGLM等，在海量序列上训练、用于预测被遮盖氨基酸的模型，仅凭序列即可隐式编码进化与结构规律。）
  • Matthews correlation coefficient (MCC) — a balanced classification metric (headword kept in English); here used to compare DisoPatho against AlphaMissense on a deliberately hard, conservation-poor test set.（中文：一种均衡的分类评价指标，本文用于在保守性信号较弱的高难度测试集上比较DisoPatho与AlphaMissense。）
  • Multiple sequence alignment (MSA) — aligning a protein's sequence across many species to derive conservation signal; DisoPatho deliberately omits this step.（中文：将一个蛋白质序列与多个物种的同源序列对齐以获得保守性信号；DisoPatho刻意省略了此步骤。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Cross-view feature-adaptive interaction encoding — adaptively fuses two feature "views" (energy representation + PLM embedding) rather than simple concatenation.
  • xTrimoPGLM / ESM embeddings — protein language model embeddings used in place of explicit MSA-derived conservation.
  • Confounded independent test-set construction — a benchmark design principle worth borrowing: strip out cases where conservation alone solves the problem, to stress-test a predictor's non-conservation signal.
