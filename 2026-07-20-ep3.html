Ep3 — Track C: residual disease activity, or what modulators left behind

⚠️ ABSTRACT-ONLY. Not open access, no PMCID. Everything attributed to the paper comes from the PubMed abstract. It is also a review, not a primary study — no cohort, no effect sizes, no named genes.

Blurb. Gramegna, Putti, Alicandro, Mall and Blasi (AJRCCM, 2026) propose residual disease activity (RDA) as a conceptual framework for the ongoing, potentially modifiable disease processes that persist in people with cystic fibrosis who are already receiving CFTR modulators. The abstract names six domains where abnormalities persist — CFTR function itself, mucus properties, mucociliary clearance, airway infection, inflammation, and symptom burden — and argues that modulation does not fully normalize airway physiology. The review examines candidate measures across these domains, identifies evidence gaps, and outlines a stepwise pathway for defining and validating RDA (i.e. the operational definition does not yet exist), with implications for sensitive endpoint selection, study-population enrichment, and therapies targeting processes that persist despite modulation. Read through the Track C lens, this is a phenotype-definition paper in clinical-review clothing: modulators compressed lung function toward a ceiling, so the variance a modifier study needs to explain has moved somewhere else, and RDA is a proposal for where. Where it lands: modifier / WGS long game (Track C). Buildable artifact: an RDA domain-concordance panel that asks whether residual disease activity is one construct or several.

Plain-English glossary

  • Bronchiectasis — permanent widening and scarring of the airways, the structural end-result of chronic infection and inflammation.（中文：支气管扩张——气道永久性扩张与瘢痕化，慢性感染与炎症的结构性后果。）
  • Ceiling effect — when most of a population sits near the top of a measurement scale, so the measure stops discriminating between them; a measurement crisis, not a clinical victory.（中文：天花板效应——群体大多聚集在量表顶端，导致该指标失去区分力。）
  • Corrector — a modulator that helps a misfolded CFTR protein fold correctly and reach the cell surface.（中文：矫正剂——帮助错误折叠的 CFTR 蛋白正确折叠并到达细胞表面的调节剂。）
  • CFTR modulator — a small molecule acting on the defective CFTR protein itself rather than on downstream consequences.（中文：CFTR 调节剂——直接作用于缺陷 CFTR 蛋白本身、而非其下游后果的小分子药物。）
  • Modifier gene — a gene outside CFTR whose variation shifts disease severity for a given CFTR genotype; discovery is rate-limited by phenotype definition, not genotyping.（中文：修饰基因——CFTR 之外、其变异会改变同一 CFTR 基因型下疾病严重程度的基因。）
  • Mucociliary clearance — the airway "escalator": cilia beating in a watery surface layer sweep mucus and trapped particles up and out of the lung.（中文：黏液纤毛清除——纤毛在气道表面液层中摆动，把黏液和颗粒推出肺部的清除机制。）
  • Potentiator — a modulator that increases the probability that a CFTR channel already at the cell surface actually opens.（中文：增效剂——提高已到达细胞表面的 CFTR 通道开放概率的调节剂。）
  • Residual disease activity (RDA) — the paper's proposed term for ongoing, potentially modifiable disease processes persisting despite CFTR modulation; currently a framework, not an operational score.（中文：残余疾病活动——本文提出的概念，指在 CFTR 调节治疗下仍持续、且可能可干预的疾病过程；目前只是框架，尚无可操作评分。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Multidomain phenotype framing — six named domains (CFTR function, mucus properties, mucociliary clearance, infection, inflammation, symptom burden) as a checklist for what a post-modulator severity phenotype should span.
  • Observed-minus-expected residualization (our extension, not the paper's method) — express each domain relative to an expected value modelled from CFTR functional class, modulator exposure, treatment duration and age, so "residual" has an explicit reference state.
  • Endpoint-sensitivity and enrichment reasoning — the review's stated motivation: pick endpoints that still vary, and enrich trial populations with patients who have something left to improve.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority served: modifier / WGS long game (Track C).

rda-domain-concordance-panel (S–M) — for each patient, z-score every available domain proxy (infection, inflammation, symptom burden, lung function) against an expected value modelled from CFTR functional class + modulator exposure + duration + age, so each domain reads as observed−expected. Deliver (a) a cross-domain correlation matrix and (b) the subgroup sitting high across multiple domains simultaneously. This answers the question the review poses but does not resolve: is RDA one latent construct or several? Correlated domains → a composite score is legitimate. Independent domains → a composite averages away the signal and each domain needs its own endpoint. The multi-domain-high subgroup is the enrichment population a modifier-discovery study would want to sequence.

What to verify first

  • Abstract-only, and a review. Nothing here is a measurement; it is a proposal for how measurement should be organized. The paper explicitly says the definition still needs a stepwise pathway to be built.
  • Define the reference state before computing any residual. "Residual relative to what?" is the load-bearing question — damage that predates treatment is not disease activity, and a naive residual will conflate them.
  • Regression to the mean — patients selected as extreme on a residual score at one time point drift back toward average at the next; any persistence claim needs a second independent time point.
  • Missingness is not random across domains — a cross-domain correlation may be measuring who gets measured rather than who is sick.
  • Modulator era is a time-varying exposure, not a fixed group label; treating it as fixed manufactures differences.
  • Ascertainment/survivorship — the population now on modulators is not the population the pre-modulator severity literature describes.

Gap-map / catalog

Closes:

  • Names and taxonomizes the post-modulator phenotype problem: six domains where disease activity persists despite modulation, and an explicit statement that modulation does not normalize airway physiology.
  • Supplies the field-level argument for why conventional severity measures are losing discriminative power — the endpoint-ceiling thread, which reinforces the phenotype-definition line running through Freitas 2026 and Majumder.

Leaves open:

  • No operational RDA definition, no thresholds, no validation cohort — the paper proposes the pathway, not the score.
  • No modifier gene, no locus, no genetic content whatsoever — the connection to modifier discovery is ours, by inference from the variance argument, not the paper's claim.
  • Whether the six domains are correlated (one construct) or independent (several) — unresolved, and the target of the buildable artifact.
  • Whether residual activity is separable from pre-treatment structural damage.

Catalog action: no new gene. CFTR entry updated with the post-modulator residual-disease-activity / endpoint-ceiling framing.

ALEX Today's Track C paper is Gramegna, Putti, Alicandro, Mall and Blasi, twenty twenty-six, in the American Journal of Respiratory and Critical Care Medicine. 今天的 Track C 论文是 Gramegna、Putti、Alicandro、Mall 和 Blasi 于 2026 年发表在《美国呼吸与危重症医学杂志》上的文章。 The title is "Residual disease activity in cystic fibrosis lung disease: implications for clinical management and research." 标题是《囊性纤维化肺病的残余疾病活动：对临床管理与研究的意义》。 One flag before anything else. 在开始之前先说明一点。 This is not open access, there is no PubMed Central identifier, and everything we say about the paper's own content comes from the abstract. 这篇论文不是开放获取的，没有 PubMed Central 编号，我们关于论文本身内容的所有陈述都来自摘要。 It is also a review, not a primary study, so there are no cohort numbers, no effect sizes, and no named genes to report. 它同时是一篇综述而非原始研究，所以没有队列数量、没有效应量，也没有可报告的具体基因。 We're reading it anyway because it names a gap, and naming a gap well is a real contribution. 我们仍然要读它，因为它命名了一个空白，而把空白命名清楚本身就是真正的贡献。

JORDAN Then give me the biology first, so I know what "residual" means here. 那先给我讲生物学背景，好让我知道这里的"残余"指的是什么。

ALEX Cystic fibrosis is caused by variants in CFTR, which encodes a chloride channel at the surface of epithelial cells. 囊性纤维化是由 CFTR 基因的变异引起的，该基因编码上皮细胞表面的一个氯离子通道。 When the channel does not work, the thin layer of liquid on the airway surface becomes depleted, mucus becomes concentrated and sticky, and mucociliary clearance fails. 当通道功能失常时，气道表面的液体层变薄，黏液变得浓稠黏滞，黏液纤毛清除功能失效。 Mucociliary clearance is the escalator: cilia beating in a watery layer sweep mucus and trapped particles up and out of the lung. 黏液纤毛清除 (mucociliary clearance) 就像一部自动扶梯：纤毛在水样液层中摆动，把黏液和被困住的颗粒向上推出肺部。 When that escalator stalls you get chronic airway infection, chronic neutrophilic inflammation, and progressive airway destruction — bronchiectasis, the permanent widening and scarring of airways. 当这部扶梯停摆，就会出现慢性气道感染、慢性中性粒细胞性炎症和进行性气道破坏——也就是支气管扩张 (bronchiectasis)，气道永久性扩张与瘢痕化。

JORDAN And modulators changed that. 而 CFTR 调节剂改变了这一点。

ALEX Substantially. CFTR modulators are small molecules that act on the defective protein itself rather than on downstream consequences. 改变得相当显著。CFTR 调节剂 (CFTR modulators) 是直接作用于缺陷蛋白本身、而非其下游后果的小分子药物。 Two mechanisms matter here. 这里有两种机制值得区分。 A corrector helps a misfolded CFTR protein fold properly and reach the cell surface. 矫正剂 (corrector) 帮助错误折叠的 CFTR 蛋白正确折叠并到达细胞表面。 A potentiator increases the probability that a channel already at the surface actually opens. 增效剂 (potentiator) 提高已经到达细胞表面的通道真正开放的概率。 The abstract's own framing is that modulators are fundamentally reshaping care and research, with unprecedented improvements in lung function and predicted survival. 摘要自己的表述是，调节剂正在从根本上重塑诊疗与研究，带来了肺功能和预期生存期的空前改善。 That is the premise. 这是前提。 The paper's claim is what follows the premise. 而论文的主张在于前提之后的部分。

JORDAN Which is? 也就是？

ALEX That a substantial proportion of people with cystic fibrosis continue to exhibit what the authors call residual disease activity, across multiple biological and clinical domains. 也就是：相当一部分囊性纤维化患者仍然表现出作者所称的"残余疾病活动"，并且横跨多个生物学与临床领域。 The abstract names six domains explicitly: persistent abnormalities in CFTR function itself, in mucus properties, in mucociliary clearance, in airway infection, in inflammation, and in symptom burden. 摘要明确列出了六个领域：CFTR 功能本身、黏液性质、黏液纤毛清除、气道感染、炎症，以及症状负担方面的持续异常。 Their conclusion from that list is that CFTR modulation does not fully normalize airway physiology or eliminate the pathological processes underlying the disease. 他们由此得出的结论是：CFTR 调节并没有让气道生理完全恢复正常，也没有消除疾病背后的病理过程。 And the word doing the work in the abstract is "modifiable." 而摘要里承担关键分量的词是"可修正的" (modifiable)。 They propose residual disease activity as a conceptual framework for identifying ongoing, potentially modifiable disease processes in people already receiving modulators. 他们把残余疾病活动作为一个概念框架提出，用于识别已经在使用调节剂的患者体内仍在进行、且可能可以干预的疾病过程。

JORDAN Why does that need a framework? Isn't "some patients are still sick" obvious? 为什么这需要一个框架？"有些患者仍然生病"难道不是显而易见的吗？

ALEX The observation is obvious. 观察本身是显而易见的。 What is not obvious is how to measure it, and that is the actual problem. 不显而易见的是如何测量它，而这才是真正的问题。 Before modulators, disease severity had a workhorse measure — lung function — and it moved enough to be useful. 在调节剂出现之前，疾病严重程度有一个主力指标——肺功能——而且它的变化幅度足以有用。 After modulators, a large share of the population sits near the top of that scale, so the measure stops discriminating. 在调节剂之后，很大一部分人群都聚集在这个量表的顶端，于是该指标失去了区分力。 That is a ceiling effect, and it is a measurement crisis, not a clinical victory. 这就是天花板效应 (ceiling effect)，它是一次测量危机，而不是一场临床胜利。 So the field needs measures that still vary when the old one does not, and the review's proposal is that you cannot find those by looking harder at one domain — you have to look across several at once. 因此这一领域需要在旧指标失去变异时仍能变化的新指标，而这篇综述的提议是：你无法靠在单一领域里看得更仔细来找到它们——你必须同时横跨多个领域来看。

JORDAN Walk me through why those six domains are six domains and not one. 给我讲讲为什么那是六个领域，而不是一个。

ALEX Because they sit at different points on the causal chain, and correcting the top of the chain does not automatically unwind the bottom. 因为它们位于因果链条的不同位置，而修正链条顶端并不会自动解开底端。 CFTR function is the top: it is the channel defect itself, and it is what a modulator acts on directly. CFTR 功能位于顶端：它就是通道缺陷本身，也是调节剂直接作用的对象。 Mucus properties are one step down — how concentrated and how mechanically stiff the airway mucus is. 黏液性质是下一步——气道黏液的浓度和机械硬度。 Mucociliary clearance is a step further, an integrated physiological output that depends on both the liquid layer and the mucus. 黏液纤毛清除更下一步，是一个综合性的生理输出，同时取决于液体层和黏液本身。 Airway infection is next, because chronic bacterial colonization of a poorly-cleared airway establishes structures — biofilms, remodelled tissue — that do not disappear when clearance partly improves. 气道感染再往下，因为清除功能不佳的气道中的慢性细菌定植会建立起某些结构——生物膜、重塑的组织——它们不会因为清除功能部分改善就消失。 Inflammation is next again, and it is the one most likely to be self-sustaining, because chronic neutrophilic inflammation damages tissue in ways that promote further inflammation independent of the original trigger. 炎症再往下，而它最有可能是自我维持的，因为慢性中性粒细胞性炎症以某种方式损伤组织，从而在脱离原始触发因素的情况下促成进一步炎症。 And symptom burden is what the patient actually experiences, which correlates with all of the above imperfectly. 而症状负担是患者真正体验到的东西，它与上述所有指标的相关性都不完美。 So the design intuition is that a modulator raises the top of the chain a long way and each subsequent link recovers less. 因此设计上的直觉是：调节剂把链条顶端提升了很多，而其后的每一环恢复得越来越少。 The empirical question — how much less, and for whom — is what the review says is still open. 而经验性的问题——少多少、对谁而言——正是综述所说仍然悬而未决的部分。

JORDAN Why does the word "modifiable" matter so much to you? 为什么"可修正的"这个词对你这么重要？

ALEX Because it is the difference between a description and a research programme. 因为它是"描述"与"研究计划"之间的分界。 If residual abnormality is just accumulated damage, the correct response is earlier treatment in the next generation and nothing else. 如果残余异常只是累积的损伤，那么正确的应对就是让下一代更早接受治疗，除此之外别无他法。 If some of it is an active, ongoing process, then there is something to intervene on right now in people already on modulators. 但如果其中一部分是仍在进行的活跃过程，那么对已经在用调节剂的人来说，眼下就有可以干预的对象。 The abstract commits to the second reading — it says potentially modifiable — and that commitment is the whole reason to build a framework rather than write a survey. 摘要选择了第二种解读——它写的是"可能可以修正的"——而正是这个选择，构成了要建立框架而非仅仅写一篇综述的全部理由。 But committing to it is not demonstrating it. 但选择这种解读并不等于证明了它。 Separating active process from fixed damage requires either a longitudinal measure that moves, or a treatment that moves it, and the abstract offers neither. 要把活跃过程与固定损伤区分开，要么需要一个会变动的纵向指标，要么需要一种能让它变动的治疗，而摘要两者都没有提供。

JORDAN What does the review actually deliver? 这篇综述实际交付了什么？

ALEX Three things, per the abstract. 根据摘要，有三样。 First, it examines candidate measures across complementary mechanistic and clinical domains, and discusses their potential multidimensional interpretation as a way to support more individualized monitoring and treatment decisions. 第一，它考察了机制层面与临床层面上互补的候选指标，并讨论了对它们做多维度解读的可能性，以支持更加个体化的监测与治疗决策。 Second, it identifies key evidence gaps and outlines a stepwise pathway for defining and validating residual disease activity. 第二，它指出了关键的证据空白，并勾勒出一条分步骤的路径，用于定义和验证残余疾病活动。 That second point is the one I want to be precise about: the paper says the definition does not yet exist and proposes a route to building it. 第二点我想说得精确一些：论文说的是这个定义尚不存在，并提出了构建它的路线。 It is not handing anyone an operational score. 它并没有交给任何人一个可操作的评分。 Third, it explores implications for clinical research — sensitive endpoint selection, enriching study populations, and guiding development of therapies aimed at processes that persist despite modulation. 第三，它探讨了对临床研究的意义——敏感终点的选择、研究人群的富集，以及指导针对调节后仍持续存在的病理过程的疗法开发。 And there are no numbers to report, because there is no primary data and because we only have the abstract. 而没有数字可报告，因为它没有原始数据，也因为我们只拿到了摘要。

ALEX My pushback is on the word "residual." 我的质疑针对"残余"这个词。 Residual relative to what? 相对于什么而言的残余？ Residual disease activity is only definable against an expected post-treatment state, and that expectation depends on the person's CFTR genotype, which modulator they are on, how long they have been on it, their age, and how much structural lung damage predates treatment. 残余疾病活动只有相对于一个"治疗后应有状态"才可定义，而这个预期取决于个体的 CFTR 基因型、正在使用哪种调节剂、用了多久、年龄，以及治疗前已经存在多少结构性肺损伤。 If you define residual as simply "abnormal on some measure," you will mostly rediscover pre-existing structural damage and call it activity. 如果你把残余简单定义为"某项指标异常"，那你多半只是重新发现了既有的结构性损伤，然后管它叫活动。 Damage is not activity. 损伤不等于活动。 The framework is only useful if the reference state it subtracts from is explicit, and that is exactly the part the abstract says still needs to be built. 这个框架只有在被减去的参照状态被明确写出来时才有用，而这恰恰是摘要说仍需构建的部分。

JORDAN So where does this land for the project? 那这对项目意味着什么？

ALEX One priority: the modifier and whole-genome-sequencing long game — Track C. 一个优先级：修饰基因与全基因组测序的长线目标，也就是 Track C。 Here is why it lands there rather than anywhere else. 这里说明为什么它落在这里而不是别处。 A modifier gene is a gene outside CFTR whose variation shifts how severe cystic fibrosis is for a given CFTR genotype. 修饰基因 (modifier gene) 是指 CFTR 之外的基因，其变异会改变在同一 CFTR 基因型下疾病的严重程度。 Modifier discovery has always been rate-limited by phenotype definition, not by genotyping. 修饰基因发现的限速环节从来都是表型定义，而不是基因分型。 If everyone's lung function is compressed near the ceiling, a modifier study using lung function has almost no variance left to explain. 如果所有人的肺功能都被压缩在天花板附近，那么用肺功能做修饰基因研究就几乎没有剩余变异可解释。 Residual disease activity is, structurally, a proposal for where the remaining variance now lives. 而残余疾病活动在结构上正是一个关于"剩余变异如今存在于何处"的提议。 That makes it a phenotype-definition problem dressed as a clinical review. 这让它成为一个披着临床综述外衣的表型定义问题。

JORDAN Remind me why anyone believes modifiers exist in the first place. 提醒我一下，人们最初为什么相信修饰基因存在。

ALEX Because people who share the same CFTR genotype do not share the same disease. 因为携带相同 CFTR 基因型的人，患的并不是同一种程度的疾病。 Two people homozygous for the most common cystic fibrosis variant can differ enormously in how fast their lung function declines and in which organs are affected. 两个携带最常见囊性纤维化变异纯合型的人，其肺功能下降的速度和受累的器官可能相差极大。 Classical family studies — comparing identical twins, who share everything genetic, with non-identical siblings, who share about half — established that a meaningful share of that remaining variation is heritable and lies outside CFTR. 经典的家系研究——把遗传上完全相同的同卵双胞胎与大约共享一半遗传物质的异卵同胞进行比较——确立了剩余变异中相当一部分是可遗传的，并且位于 CFTR 之外。 That is the founding observation of the whole modifier field. 这就是整个修饰基因领域的奠基性观察。 What has changed is that the phenotype those studies measured is now the one being compressed by treatment. 而改变了的是：那些研究所测量的表型，如今正被治疗压缩。 So the modifier question did not get answered, it got relocated — and this review is, functionally, a map of where it relocated to. 所以修饰基因的问题并没有被回答，它只是被搬走了——而这篇综述在功能上，就是一张关于它搬到哪里去了的地图。

JORDAN And the buildable thing? 那可以动手做的东西呢？
