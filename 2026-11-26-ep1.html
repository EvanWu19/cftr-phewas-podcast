ClinVar: improvements to accessing data (Landrum et al., 2020)

Track A — CF/CFTR variant interpretation & harmonization

Melissa Landrum and colleagues at NCBI describe a 2020 round of upgrades to ClinVar, the public archive of human genetic variants and their submitted disease interpretations: a redesigned, disease-grouped Variation page, the production release of the variant-centric VCV accession system (distinct from the older, per-submission RCV accessions), and search-engine tuning. It's a short descriptive report rather than an evaluation study — there's no benchmark quantifying the retrieval improvements — but the infrastructure it documents (VCV accessions, the zero-to-four-star review-status model, and explicit haplotype/genotype representation) is exactly the stable, queryable backbone the Karen collaboration needs for CFTR variant-notation harmonization. Buildable artifact: a CFTR ClinVar Harmonization Snapshot tool that takes a list of CFTR variants (HGVS or rsID), pulls each one's current VCV record via NCBI's E-utilities, and outputs a table of aggregate classification, star rating, submitter conflicts, and citations — a small (S), afternoon-to-a-couple-days build that becomes the ground-truth layer any predictive tool (AlphaMissense included) gets checked against.

Plain-English glossary

  • ClinVar — NCBI's free, public archive of human genetic variants and submitted interpretations of their relationship to disease. No translation; proper name.（中文：NCBI维护的一个免费公开数据库，收录人类基因变异及其与疾病关系的提交解读。专有名词，不翻译。）
  • RCV (Reference ClinVar accession) — a per-submission, per-condition accession: tied to one submitter's claim that one variant causes one specific condition.（中文：RCV（ClinVar参考编号）——按"每次提交—每种疾病"划分的编号，对应某一提交者关于某变异导致某特定疾病的单条主张。）
  • VCV (Variation ClinVar accession) — the variant-centric accession introduced in production in April 2019: one record per variant, aggregating across submitters and conditions; this is the stable identifier to cite or query against.（中文：VCV（变异ClinVar编号）——2019年4月正式上线的、以变异为中心的编号，每个变异对应一条记录，汇总所有提交者和疾病信息；是用于引用或查询的稳定标识符。）
  • Review status / star rating — ClinVar's zero-to-four-star scale reflecting how much independent submitter agreement backs a classification (0 = no criteria reported, 1 = single submitter with criteria, 2 = multiple submitters, no conflicts, 3 = expert panel, 4 = practice guideline) — a proxy for evidentiary weight, not biological confidence.（中文：审查状态/星级评分——ClinVar从零星到四星的评级体系，反映有多少独立提交者的一致意见支持某项分类（0=未提供判定标准，1=单一提交者有判定标准，2=多提交者无冲突，3=专家小组评审，4=纳入临床指南），衡量的是证据分量，而非生物学置信度本身。）
  • Haplotype / genotype representation — the case where a variant was never classified standalone, only ever reported as part of a combination with other variants; a gap the 2020 redesign explicitly aimed to surface rather than hide.（中文：单倍型/基因型呈现——指某个变异从未被单独分类过，只作为与其他变异的组合被报告的情况；2020年的改版明确希望把这类情况清楚呈现出来，而不是让它被忽略。）
  • E-utilities — NCBI's programmatic REST query interface; the tool you'd actually script against to pull ClinVar (or other NCBI database) records in bulk rather than clicking through the website.（中文：E-utilities——NCBI的程序化REST查询接口；想批量拉取ClinVar或其他NCBI数据库记录时，真正会写脚本调用的工具，而不是在网站上手动点击。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a classification tier meaning the available evidence doesn't yet support calling a variant benign or pathogenic.（中文：VUS（意义未明变异）——一种分类等级，表示现有证据尚不足以判定该变异为良性还是致病性。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • VCV accessions — one stable, queryable identifier per variant, aggregating all submitted interpretations.
  • E-utilities — NCBI's REST API for programmatic, scriptable bulk retrieval of ClinVar records.
  • Star-rating review status — a ready-made, five-tier confidence signal (0–4 stars) for filtering or weighting automated variant-classification pipelines.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen collaboration — CFTR variant interpretation / notation harmonization. Buildable artifact: a CFTR ClinVar Harmonization Snapshot tool — input a list of CFTR variants (HGVS or rsID), pull each one's current VCV record via E-utilities, output a table of aggregate classification, star rating, submitter conflicts, and citations, refreshable on demand. Difficulty: S (afternoon-to-a-couple-days; the main design decision is how to represent the haplotype/genotype edge case).

What to verify first

  • Confirm the star-rating thresholds and accession conventions described in this 2020 paper still match current ClinVar documentation exactly.
  • Check how CFTR's own variants are actually distributed across review-status tiers before assuming mostly high-confidence (3–4 star) records.
  • Verify how VCV handles updates over time — whether older accessions are silently superseded or versioned in a trackable way — since a harmonization snapshot is only as good as its freshness guarantees.
