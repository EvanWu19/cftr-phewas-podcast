Ep3 (Track C 1/2 — registry QC): Defining and improving data quality in medical registries

Paper: Arts DG, De Keizer NF, Scheffer GJ. "Defining and improving data quality in medical registries: a literature review, case study, and generic framework." Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association (JAMIA), 2002.

Blurb

A literature review plus a single (unnamed) case study produced a generic two-by-three framework for registry data-quality procedures: one axis is central (coordinating center) versus local (data-collection site), the other is prevention, detection, or correction. The framework is qualitative — a taxonomy for sorting any given QC procedure into one of six cells, usable either to design quality control for a new registry or to audit an existing one's current procedures. It predates the modern EHR era by roughly two decades, so its central/local and prevention/detection/correction distinctions, drawn from paper-era registries, need checking against how a modern EHR-integrated registry's data actually flows — that's left open rather than assumed. It doesn't touch the outlier-detection pipeline directly; its contribution is vocabulary and a framework worth borrowing (pipeline-stage category (e)). Buildable artifact: a QC Procedure Classifier that tags every method already in this project's own QC catalog against the two-by-three grid and surfaces gaps.

Plain-English glossary

  • Central procedure — a data-quality activity run by a registry's coordinating center, which aggregates data from all sites and can run analyses across the whole population.（中文:由注册库的协调中心执行的数据质量活动,协调中心负责汇总所有站点的数据,并对整个人群做分析。）
  • Correction — the remediation actions taken once a data problem has been detected, such as amending a record or flagging a value as unreliable.（中文:在发现数据问题之后采取的补救行动,例如修订记录或将某个数值标记为不可靠。）
  • Detection — finding imperfect data and tracing its cause after it has already entered the system.（中文:在数据已经进入系统之后,发现有缺陷的数据并追溯其原因。）
  • Local procedure — a data-quality activity run at the site where patient data is actually collected (a hospital, clinic, or provider).（中文:在实际采集患者数据的站点——如医院、诊所或医护人员处——执行的数据质量活动。）
  • OMOP (Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership common data model) — a standardized schema that maps clinical data from different source systems into one consistent structure, enabling multi-site analysis.（中文:一套标准化的数据结构,把来自不同源系统的临床数据映射到统一的结构中,使跨站点分析成为可能。）
  • Prevention — stopping insufficient data quality before it happens, e.g., range checks at data entry or staff training.（中文:在问题发生之前阻止数据质量不足,例如数据录入时的范围检查或人员培训。）
