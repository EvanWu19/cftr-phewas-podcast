Blurb

The first adult CF prevalence estimate ever produced in Bangladesh: 130 adults with CT-confirmed bronchiectasis at the national tertiary referral hospital in Dhaka, all given a pilocarpine sweat chloride test. Twelve (9.2%) tested at or above 60 mmol/L — median age 43, mean sweat chloride 66.9 — and a further 25 (19.3%) landed in the intermediate 30–59 band and were never followed up, "due to resource constraints." All 12 CF patients had abnormal spirometry (75% obstructive, 25% mixed, zero pure restrictive) versus 19.5% normal among non-CF (p=0.002), and mild obstruction was far commoner in CF (50% vs 8.6%, p=0.002) — consistent with residual function, since an adult who reaches 43 undiagnosed must have had some CFTR working. The paper's headline gradient — FEV1/FVC falling 76.3 → 67.7 → 61.1 across the three sweat-chloride bins (p=0.008) — is the number to be careful with: neither component moved (FEV1 p=0.29, FVC p=0.45), and the non-CF comparison group is heavily restrictive (31.4%), which mechanically raises their ratio. The gradient may be describing the comparator. Where it lands: Liz / CFFPR phenotype & data quality (method only). Buildable artifact: sweat-chloride-continuum-analyzer — stop binning a continuous CFTR-function readout at a diagnostic threshold someone else chose.

Plain-English glossary

  • Bronchiectasis — permanent abnormal widening of the airways from chronic infection and inflammation; a self-sustaining damage loop, and a finding rather than a diagnosis (TB, childhood pneumonia, immune deficiency and CF all cause it).（中文：气道因慢性感染与炎症而永久性异常增宽；它是一个"征象"而非诊断，结核、儿童期肺炎、免疫缺陷和 CF 都可致病。）
  • CFTR modulator — a drug acting directly on the CFTR protein; lowers sweat chloride, which means a post-treatment sweat chloride measures drug response, not genotype.（中文：直接作用于 CFTR 蛋白的药物；会降低汗液氯离子，因此治疗后的数值测的是药物反应而非基因型。）
  • FEV1/FVC ratio — not a measure of how sick, but of what kind of sick. Obstruction lowers it; restriction raises it (FVC falls faster than FEV1). This is why a group-difference in the ratio can be a statement about the comparison group.（中文：它衡量的不是病得多重，而是病的种类。阻塞性使其降低，限制性反而使其升高——所以比值的组间差异可能是在描述对照组。）
  • Intermediate sweat chloride (30–59 mmol/L) — a band defined by diagnostic indecision, not biology; these patients are neither confirmed nor excluded, and in this study they were never followed up.（中文：由"诊断上无法决断"而非生物学定义的区间；这些患者既未确诊也未排除，本研究中他们从未被随访。）
  • Obstructive vs restrictive spirometry pattern — obstructive = air can't get out fast (plugged, narrowed airways: the CF pattern); restrictive = lungs can't get big (stiff or small lungs).（中文：阻塞性＝气流呼不出去（气道堵塞狭窄，即 CF 的模式）；限制性＝肺张不开（肺僵硬或体积小）。）
  • Residual function — partial surviving CFTR activity from milder variants; produces narrower, later-onset, sometimes single-organ disease — which is how adult "idiopathic" bronchiectasis can turn out to be CF.（中文：较温和变异留下的部分 CFTR 活性；导致病变范围更窄、发病更晚、有时只累及单一器官——这正是成人"特发性"支气管扩张可能其实是 CF 的原因。）
  • Sweat chloride test (pilocarpine iontophoresis) — the closest thing to a direct functional assay of CFTR in a living person. The sweat duct reclaims chloride through CFTR; broken CFTR ⇒ salty sweat. <30 normal, 30–59 intermediate, ≥60 diagnostic. It is a phenotype, and says nothing about which variants a person carries.（中文：最接近"活体内 CFTR 蛋白直接功能测定"的检查。汗腺导管通过 CFTR 回收氯离子；CFTR 失灵则汗液变咸。它是一个表型，完全不能说明携带了哪些变异。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Sweat chloride as a continuous CFTR-function readout, not a three-way bin. The diagnostic threshold (60) exists so a clinician can make a yes/no call; using it as an analysis variable throws away most of the information.
  • The intermediate band as a study population in its own right — 19.3% of this cohort, defined only by the fact that nobody knows what to do with them.
  • GLI race-neutral reference equations for percent-predicted spirometry — a modern, defensible choice worth adopting.
  • Enrichment-by-exclusion as an explicit design (excluding known post-TB bronchiectasis) — legitimate for finding cases, fatal if the resulting rate is quoted as a population prevalence.
  • Decomposing a ratio before believing it — check whether the numerator and denominator moved; if neither did, the ratio is telling you about pattern, not severity.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Liz / CFFPR phenotype & data quality — METHOD ONLY. (Nothing here describes or assumes anything about registry internals; this is an external analytic method.)

sweat-chloride-continuum-analyzer (difficulty S) — input: a table of sweat chloride values + a phenotype. Instead of a three-group comparison, fit the phenotype on sweat chloride as a continuous predictor with a spline, estimating the shape of the relationship rather than assuming it. Then ask the question the binned analysis structurally cannot: **is there a monotone gradient within the intermediate band** — do people at 55 look different from people at 35, when both are labeled "not CF"? A modeling choice, not an engineering project.

What to verify first

  • Is sweat chloride measured consistently enough to be treated as continuous? It's an analog assay with real between-lab and between-instrument variation. Look at repeat measurements on the same person first — if measurement noise swamps the biological gradient, the whole idea collapses.
  • Modulator era. CFTR modulators lower sweat chloride. Any continuous analysis must anchor on a pre-treatment value or it is measuring pharmacology, not CFTR function.
  • The FEV1/FVC gradient is confounded by restriction in the comparison group (31.4% restrictive among non-CF). Re-derive it with the restrictive pattern modeled before believing it is a CFTR dose-response.
  • rho = −0.477, p = 0.117, n = 12. That is noise; the authors say so.
  • Prevalence is enriched by design (post-TB bronchiectasis excluded, in a high-TB-burden country, at a national referral center). 9.2% is not a population prevalence and must never be quoted as one.
  • Multiplicity. Many Fisher's exact tests, no correction described. The two p=0.002 results are robust; p=0.008 is the fragile one.

Gap-map / catalog

CLOSES:

  • A first quantified estimate of CF among adults with (non-TB) bronchiectasis in Bangladesh — 9.2% by sweat chloride, median age 43, M:F 3:1 — in a population with no prior data of any kind.
  • A documented size for the intermediate sweat-chloride band in a South Asian adult bronchiectasis population (19.3%), and an explicit statement that these patients receive no further CFTR testing due to resource constraints.
  • A clean phenotype signal consistent with residual function in late-diagnosed adults (all CF abnormal on spirometry, but obstruction disproportionately mild: 50% vs 8.6%).

LEAVES OPEN:

  • The entire allele question. No genotyping was performed — not one variant sequenced. From the population where the CF allele spectrum is least known, we now have a prevalence estimate and zero alleles. This is the hole, and it is the one worth naming.
  • Whether the FEV1/FVC gradient survives adjustment for the restrictive pattern in the comparator.
  • What the 25 intermediate patients actually are (CFTR-related disorder? carriers? measurement noise? undiagnosed CF?).
  • No modifier gene — this paper is CFTR-intrinsic and, in fact, genotype-free. catalog: no new genes; CFTR entry updated with the ascertainment gap.

ALEX Track C today is Majumder and colleagues, 2026, in Health Science Reports: "Prevalence of Cystic Fibrosis and Pattern of Lung Function Impairment in Adults Presenting With Bronchiectasis at a Tertiary Care Hospital in Resource Poor Country Bangladesh." 今天的 Track C 是 Majumder 等人 2026 年发表在《Health Science Reports》上的论文：《在资源匮乏国家孟加拉国一家三级医院就诊的成人支气管扩张患者中囊性纤维化的患病率与肺功能损害模式》。 It's open access, we have the full text, and it is a small paper — 130 patients, one hospital in Dhaka. 它是开放获取的，我们拿到了全文，而且它是一篇小论文——130 位患者，达卡的一家医院。 We're reading it because it quietly documents a hole in the world's CF data, and because buried in one of its tables is a methodological idea worth stealing. 我们读它，是因为它悄悄记录了全球 CF 数据中的一个空洞，也因为在它某一张表格里埋着一个值得偷的方法学想法。

JORDAN Start with the biology, because I need two things defined. 从生物学开始，因为我需要两个定义。 What is bronchiectasis, and what is a sweat chloride test actually measuring? 什么是支气管扩张，还有汗液氯离子检测到底在测什么？

ALEX Bronchiectasis（支气管扩张症）is permanent, abnormal widening of the airways. 支气管扩张症是气道永久性的异常增宽。 Chronic infection and inflammation damage the airway wall, it loses its structure and dilates, and the dilated airway then clears mucus badly — which invites more infection, which causes more damage. 慢性感染和炎症损伤气道壁，气道失去结构而扩张，扩张后的气道排痰能力变差——这又招来更多感染，造成更多损伤。 It's a self-sustaining loop, and it is a *finding*, not a diagnosis. 这是一个自我维持的循环，而且它是一个*征象*，不是一个诊断。 Lots of things cause it: prior tuberculosis, childhood pneumonia, immune deficiency, and — the reason we're here — cystic fibrosis. 很多东西都能引起它：既往结核、儿童期肺炎、免疫缺陷，以及——我们今天讨论的原因——囊性纤维化。

JORDAN And the sweat test? 那汗液检测呢？

ALEX The sweat chloride test is the closest thing medicine has to a **direct functional assay of the CFTR protein in a living person**. 汗液氯离子检测是医学上最接近**对活人体内 CFTR 蛋白的直接功能测定**的东西。 Here's the mechanism, and it's worth getting right. 机制值得讲清楚。 In the sweat gland, the coil secretes salty fluid, and then as that fluid travels up the duct toward the skin, the body reclaims the salt — it pulls chloride back out through CFTR channels sitting in the duct wall. 在汗腺里，腺体分泌含盐的液体，当这些液体沿导管向皮肤上行时，身体会把盐回收——通过导管壁上的 CFTR 通道把氯离子拉回去。 If CFTR works, the sweat that reaches your skin is dilute. 如果 CFTR 正常工作，到达皮肤的汗液是稀的。 If CFTR doesn't work, the chloride stays in the sweat, and the sweat is salty. 如果 CFTR 不工作，氯离子留在汗里，汗就是咸的。 That's the whole test. 这就是全部原理。 You stimulate a patch of skin with pilocarpine（毛果芸香碱，一种刺激出汗的药物）driven in by a small current, collect the sweat, and measure the chloride. 你用一点电流把毛果芸香碱导入一小块皮肤以刺激出汗，收集汗液，测量其中的氯离子。 Under 30 millimoles per litre is normal, 30 to 59 is the **intermediate** range, and 60 or above is diagnostic for CF. 低于 30 毫摩尔／升是正常，30 到 59 是**中间**范围，60 及以上则可诊断 CF。

JORDAN So it's not a genetic test at all. 所以它根本不是一个遗传学检测。

ALEX It is not, and hold onto that, because it becomes the central problem with this paper. 确实不是，请记住这一点，因为它会变成这篇论文的核心问题。 The sweat chloride number is a **phenotype**. 汗液氯离子数值是一个**表型**。 It tells you how much CFTR function a person has. 它告诉你一个人有多少 CFTR 功能。 It tells you *nothing at all* about which variants they carry. 它*完全不*告诉你他们携带哪些变异。

JORDAN Okay. Why does this study exist? 好。这项研究为什么会存在？

ALEX Because of an assumption that has been quietly wrong for decades. 因为一个悄悄错了几十年的假设。 CF is filed in most clinicians' heads as a childhood disease of people of European ancestry. 在多数临床医生的脑子里，CF 被归档为"欧洲血统者的儿童期疾病"。 Both halves of that are wrong at the edges, and this paper is standing on the edge. 这句话的两半在边缘处都是错的，而这篇论文正站在那个边缘上。 On the "childhood" half: if you carry two **severe** variants — essentially no functioning CFTR protein — you get classical, multi-system, early-onset CF. 关于"儿童期"这一半：如果你携带两个**严重**变异——基本上没有任何有功能的 CFTR 蛋白——你会得到经典的、多系统的、早发型 CF。 But if you carry **milder** variants that leave partial CFTR function — what the field calls **residual function**（残余功能）— you can get a much narrower disease that may hit only one organ. 但如果你携带**较温和**的变异，留下部分 CFTR 功能——这个领域称之为**残余功能**——你可能得到一种范围窄得多的疾病，甚至只累及一个器官。 And that single organ is often the lung, presenting in adulthood as "idiopathic bronchiectasis." 而那个器官往往就是肺，在成年期以"特发性支气管扩张"的形式出现。

JORDAN Meaning some fraction of adults labeled "idiopathic" are actually CF that nobody looked for. 也就是说，一部分被标为"特发性"的成年人，其实是没人去找的 CF。

ALEX That's the hypothesis, and the paper cites the prior evidence honestly. 这就是假设，论文也诚实地引用了先前证据。 A Barcelona study found CFTR mutations in 36% of 55 adults with idiopathic bronchiectasis. 巴塞罗那的一项研究在 55 位特发性支气管扩张成年患者中发现 36% 携带 CFTR 突变。 A ten-year retrospective in Paris found CF in 46 of 601 adults with diffuse bronchiectasis — 7.6%. 巴黎一项十年回顾性研究在 601 位弥漫性支气管扩张成年患者中发现 46 例 CF——7.6%。 On the "European ancestry" half: the CF registries that define our sense of the disease are overwhelmingly European and North American. 关于"欧洲血统"这一半：定义我们对这个疾病认知的那些 CF 登记库，绝大多数来自欧洲和北美。 There has never been an adult CF prevalence estimate in Bangladesh. 孟加拉国从未有过成人 CF 患病率的估计。 Not a low one. 不是一个低的估计。 *None*. 是*没有*。

JORDAN So what did they do? 那他们做了什么？

ALEX Cross-sectional, one center — Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujib Medical University in Dhaka, the national tertiary referral hospital — from June 2023 to November 2024. 横断面研究，单中心——达卡的 Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujib 医科大学，国家级三级转诊医院——时间从 2023 年 6 月到 2024 年 11 月。 130 adults with bronchiectasis confirmed on high-resolution CT. 130 位经高分辨率 CT 确认的成人支气管扩张患者。 Everyone got a pilocarpine iontophoresis sweat chloride test and spirometry. 每个人都做了毛果芸香碱离子导入汗液氯离子检测和肺功能测定。 Spirometry classified into normal, obstructive, restrictive and mixed patterns using the GLI race-neutral equation for percent-predicted values, which is a modern and defensible choice worth noting. 肺功能测定按正常、阻塞性、限制性和混合型分类，预计值百分比采用 GLI 的种族中立方程，这是一个现代且站得住脚的选择，值得一提。 And now the exclusion criterion that shapes everything: **known post-tuberculosis bronchiectasis was excluded**. 现在是塑造了一切的那条排除标准：**已知的结核后支气管扩张被排除了**。

JORDAN In Bangladesh. 在孟加拉国。

ALEX In Bangladesh, where TB is one of the leading causes of bronchiectasis. 在孟加拉国，而结核是那里支气管扩张的主要病因之一。 So the denominator is not "adults with bronchiectasis." 所以分母不是"患支气管扩张的成年人"。 The denominator is "adults with bronchiectasis whose obvious cause had already been ruled out." 分母是"患支气管扩张、且明显病因已被排除的成年人"。 That is a perfectly reasonable design for enriching the sample — but it means the prevalence number they produce is **not** a population prevalence, and it must never be quoted as one. 作为富集样本的设计，这完全合理——但这意味着他们得出的患病率**不是**人群患病率，绝不能被当作人群患病率来引用。 This is my main design pushback, and I'd have put it in the abstract. 这是我主要的设计反驳，我会把它写进摘要里。

JORDAN Fine. What did they find? 好。他们发现了什么？

ALEX Of the 130, **12 had a sweat chloride at or above 60 — 9.2%**. 130 人中，**12 人的汗液氯离子达到或超过 60——占 9.2%**。 Mean sweat chloride in that group was 66.9, range 60 to 87. 该组平均汗液氯离子为 66.9，范围 60 到 87。 Median age of the CF patients was **43**, interquartile range 29 to 55. CF 患者的年龄中位数是 **43 岁**，四分位间距 29 到 55。 Male-to-female ratio three to one. 男女比 3 比 1。 And a further **25 patients — 19.3% — landed in the intermediate band**, 30 to 59. 另有 **25 位患者——19.3%——落在中间范围**，30 到 59。

JORDAN Forty-three years old, and being told for the first time they might have cystic fibrosis. 四十三岁，第一次被告知自己可能患有囊性纤维化。

ALEX Yes. 是的。 And the 19.3% is the part that keeps me up. 而那 19.3% 才是让我难以释怀的部分。 The authors state plainly that those 25 patients **did not undergo further CFTR physiological testing, due to resource constraints**. 作者直白地写道，那 25 位患者**因资源限制未接受进一步的 CFTR 生理学检测**。 So a fifth of this cohort is sitting in a diagnostic no-man's-land, and nobody will ever know. 于是这个队列的五分之一停留在一片诊断的无人区，而没有人会知道答案。

JORDAN What about the lung function? 肺功能呢？

ALEX Three results, in descending order of how much I believe them. 三个结果，按我相信的程度递减排列。 The one I believe: **every single CF patient had abnormal spirometry** — 75% pure obstructive, 25% mixed, zero pure restrictive — while 19.5% of non-CF patients were normal, p equals 0.002. 我相信的那个：**所有 CF 患者的肺功能测定都异常**——75% 是单纯阻塞性，25% 是混合型，零例单纯限制性——而 19.5% 的非 CF 患者是正常的，p 等于 0.002。 The obstructive pattern is exactly what CFTR biology predicts: dehydrated airway surface, thick mucus, small-airway plugging. 阻塞性模式正是 CFTR 生物学所预测的：气道表面脱水、黏液黏稠、小气道堵塞。 The second: mild obstruction was far more common in CF than non-CF — 50% versus 8.6%, p equals 0.002 — which fits the residual-function story, since adults who survived to 43 undiagnosed must have had *some* CFTR working. 第二个：轻度阻塞在 CF 组比非 CF 组常见得多——50% 对 8.6%，p 等于 0.002——这符合残余功能的说法，因为能未确诊活到 43 岁的成年人，必定还有*一些* CFTR 在工作。

JORDAN And the third? 第三个呢？

ALEX The third is the one they lead with, and it's the one I want to take apart. 第三个是他们主打的那个，也是我想拆开来看的那个。 The FEV1-over-FVC ratio fell monotonically across the three sweat chloride bins: median 76.3 in the normal-sweat group, 67.7 in the intermediate group, 61.1 in the CF group, p equals 0.008. FEV1／FVC 比值在三个汗液氯离子分箱中单调下降：正常汗液组中位数 76.3，中间组 67.7，CF 组 61.1，p 等于 0.008。 A dose-response across a CFTR-function gradient. 一条沿着 CFTR 功能梯度的剂量—反应关系。 Beautiful. 很漂亮。 And I do not trust it as stated. 但我不相信它被陈述的那个样子。

JORDAN Why not? 为什么？

ALEX Because look at what *didn't* move. 因为看看那些*没有*动的东西。 FEV1 percent-predicted across the same three bins: 60.0, 51.0, 67.5 — not monotone, p equals 0.29, no signal. 同样三个分箱里的 FEV1 预计值百分比：60.0、51.0、67.5——不单调，p 等于 0.29，没有信号。 FVC: 76.0, 76.0, 81.6 — p equals 0.45, no signal. FVC：76.0、76.0、81.6——p 等于 0.45，没有信号。 The *ratio* moved and neither of its *components* did. *比值*动了，而它的两个*分量*都没动。 That happens when the numerator and denominator shift together — and here's the mechanism: FEV1-over-FVC is not a measure of how sick you are, it's a measure of *what kind* of sick you are. 当分子和分母一起变化时就会出现这种情况——机制在这里：FEV1／FVC 不是衡量你病得多重，而是衡量你病的*种类*。 A **restrictive** defect — stiff, small lungs — actually *raises* the ratio, because FVC drops faster than FEV1. **限制性**缺陷——僵硬、体积小的肺——实际上会*抬高*这个比值，因为 FVC 下降得比 FEV1 更快。 And the non-CF group in this cohort is **the restrictive group**: restrictive was their single most common pattern at 31.4%. 而这个队列里的非 CF 组正是**限制性的那一组**：限制性是他们最常见的模式，占 31.4%。

JORDAN So the gradient might be describing the comparison group, not the CF group. 所以这个梯度可能是在描述对照组，而不是 CF 组。
