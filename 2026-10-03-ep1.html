Blurb

Song J and colleagues (2026, Alzheimer's & Dementia: Translational Research & Clinical Interventions) ran 37 variant-effect predictors — AlphaMissense, ESM-1b, CADD, EVE and 33 others — against a consolidated set of 235 unique PSEN1/PSEN2/APP missense variants, correlating each predictor's score against real in-vitro biochemical output (Aβ37/38/40/42/43 levels, the Aβ42/Aβ40 pathogenicity ratio) and clinical age-at-onset data from early-onset Alzheimer's disease patients. 32 of 37 predictors (86.5%) correlated significantly with age at onset; 29 of 37 (78.4%) correlated with PSEN1's Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio. The detail worth flagging for CFTR work: variants of uncertain significance consistently clustered with benign variants rather than pathogenic ones on both predictor score and biochemical ratio — predictor consensus didn't resolve the VUS bucket. Structural modeling (AlphaFold3 vs. cryo-EM, low RMSD) supported a buried-hydrophobic-residue-to-loss-of-function, exposed-charged-residue-to-dominant-negative pattern, though the authors frame this as a hallmark rather than proof. The buildable artifact: port the paper's predictor-vs-functional-assay correlation template to CFTR, using existing CFTR2/theratyping functional data (forskolin-induced swelling, iodide efflux) against existing predictor scores, to see whether CFTR's own VUS bucket shows the same benign-like clustering — a direct lead on which VUS deserve a second look.

Plain-English glossary

  • Age at onset (AAO) — the age at which a patient's disease symptoms first appear; used here as a clinical severity proxy.（中文：患者疾病症状首次出现的年龄，本文用作临床严重程度的替代指标。）
  • Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio — the ratio of two amyloid-beta peptide lengths produced by gamma-secretase cleavage of APP; a higher ratio is the field's standard biochemical marker of pathogenicity in early-onset Alzheimer's disease.（中文：γ-分泌酶切割APP产生的两种不同长度β-淀粉样蛋白肽段的比值；比值越高是该领域公认的早发性阿尔茨海默病致病性生化标志物。）
  • Dominant-negative (DN) effect — a mutant protein that stays structurally folded but interferes with or "poisons" the function of the wild-type complex it's part of.（中文：突变蛋白仍保持结构折叠，但会干扰或"毒害"其所在野生型复合体的功能。）
  • Gamma-secretase — the enzyme complex (catalytic subunit encoded by PSEN1/PSEN2) that cleaves APP to produce amyloid-beta peptides.（中文：由PSEN1/PSEN2编码其催化亚基的酶复合体，负责切割APP生成β-淀粉样蛋白肽段。）
  • Loss-of-function (LoF) effect — a mutation that destabilizes a protein's structure, typically via a buried hydrophobic residue, causing misfolding and degradation.（中文：使蛋白质结构不稳定的突变，通常发生在埋藏的疏水残基上，导致蛋白错误折叠并被降解。）
  • Relative solvent accessibility (RSA) — a measure of how exposed a given amino acid residue is on a protein's surface versus buried in its hydrophobic core.（中文：衡量某个氨基酸残基暴露在蛋白质表面还是埋藏于疏水核心内部的指标。）
  • Spearman's rank correlation — a non-parametric correlation statistic used throughout this paper to relate predictor scores to biochemical/clinical outcomes.（中文：本文用于关联预测器评分与生化/临床结局的一种非参数相关性统计量。）
  • Variant-effect predictor (VEP) — a computational tool (e.g. AlphaMissense, CADD, REVEL, EVE) that scores a missense variant's likely pathogenicity from sequence, evolutionary, and/or structural features.（中文：根据序列、进化保守性和/或结构特征，对错义变异致病性可能性进行打分的计算工具。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • AlphaMissense — deep-learning missense pathogenicity predictor, used as the exemplar VEP throughout.
  • ESM-1b / protein language models — sequence-embedding-based variant scorers, among the 37 tested.
  • CADD, EVE — among the 37 benchmarked predictors.
  • AlphaFold3 — structural modeling, superimposed on experimental cryo-EM structures to compute per-residue RSA.
  • Spearman's correlation (scipy) — the core statistical test linking predictor scores to biochemical/clinical outcomes.
  • Kruskal–Wallis + Dunn's post-hoc — used for unbalanced-sample-size pathogenic/benign/VUS group comparisons.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation. Buildable: a CFTR predictor-vs-functional-assay correlation audit — merge existing predictor scores (AlphaMissense/REVEL/CADD) with existing CFTR2/theratyping functional assay values (forskolin-induced swelling, iodide efflux) for every CFTR variant that has both, run the same Spearman correlation framework, and check whether CFTR's VUS bucket clusters with benign the way PSEN1's did here. Difficulty: S–M (no retraining, a merge-and-correlate script against public data).

What to verify first

  • The actual sample size of CFTR variants with both a predictor score and a public functional assay value — a small n (comparable to this paper's APP n=25) would make any null result underpowered rather than informative.
  • Which CFTR functional assay is the right analogue to the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio — forskolin-induced swelling and iodide efflux measure different things, and the choice could shift which variants look discordant.
  • That any dominant-negative-vs-loss-of-function claim for a specific CFTR variant gets its own structural check, rather than borrowing the RSA pattern from this unrelated protein family.
