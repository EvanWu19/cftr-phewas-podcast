Track C — CFTR genotype-phenotype associations in pediatric CF (Arpini et al., Pharmacogenet Genomics 2026)

ABSTRACT-ONLY. Not open access; this episode is built from the structured abstract. Exact statistical tests, genotype-combination handling, and covariate adjustment are not visible — effect directions are as reported, not validated.

Blurb. A cross-sectional, retrospective study of 110 children/adolescents (<18) with confirmed CF followed 2007–2024 at a state reference center in Espírito Santo, Brazil. CFTR variants were classified by functional consequence and grouped by severity, then tested against pancreatic insufficiency, FEV1%, Shwachman-Kulczycki score, nutrition, and chronic Pseudomonas aeruginosa colonization. Findings: F508del predominated (72.7% of alleles) amid high heterogeneity; two class I–III variants → more pancreatic insufficiency (P=0.026); F508del → worse FEV1 (P=0.031) and bone demineralization (P=0.026); Shwachman-Kulczycki correlated negatively with age/age-at-diagnosis and positively with BMI z-score and FEV1; no significant genotype association with chronic Pseudomonas, liver disease, or overall severity. It reproduces the classic CF severity gradient in an under-represented population. Where it lands: Liz-sponsored CFFPR phenotype/stratification (method only). Buildable artifact: a CFTR genotype-to-severity-group labeler (ordinal minimal-function allele count 0/1/2).

Plain-English glossary

  • CFTR functional classes (I–VI) — a grouping of CFTR variants by how they disrupt the protein; classes I–III ("minimal function": no protein / no trafficking / no gating) are generally most severe, IV–VI milder.（中文：CFTR 功能类别（I–VI）按变异破坏蛋白质的方式分组；I–III 类（"最小功能"：不产蛋白/不转运/不开门）通常最严重，IV–VI 类较轻。）
  • F508del (p.Phe508del) — the most common CFTR variant worldwide, a class II folding/trafficking defect.（中文：F508del 是全球最常见的 CFTR 变异，属 II 类折叠/转运缺陷。）
  • FEV1 — forced expiratory volume in 1 second; the standard spirometry measure of lung function.（中文：FEV1 是一秒用力呼气容积，衡量肺功能的标准肺量计指标。）
  • genotype–phenotype association — the statistical link between which variants a person carries and their clinical outcomes.（中文：基因型-表型关联是"携带哪些变异"与临床结局之间的统计联系。）
  • pancreatic insufficiency — the pancreas fails to deliver enough digestive enzymes to the gut, impairing fat/nutrient absorption; tightly linked to severe CFTR classes.（中文：胰腺功能不全是胰腺无法向肠道输送足够消化酶，损害脂肪/营养吸收，与严重 CFTR 类别密切相关。）
  • Pseudomonas aeruginosa colonization — chronic airway infection by this bacterium, a key CF prognostic marker.（中文：铜绿假单胞菌定植是该细菌引起的慢性气道感染，是 CF 的关键预后标志。）
  • Shwachman-Kulczycki score — an older composite CF clinical severity score (activity, exam, nutrition, X-ray); higher = better.（中文：Shwachman-Kulczycki 评分是较早的 CF 综合临床严重程度评分（活动、体检、营养、X 线），分数越高越好。）

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到读paper时间。 Today's Track C paper is "CFTR genotype-phenotype associations and clinical outcomes in pediatric cystic fibrosis," by Arpini and colleagues, in Pharmacogenetics and Genomics, 2026. 今天 Track C 的论文是 Arpini 等人 2026 年发表在《Pharmacogenetics and Genomics》上的《儿童囊性纤维化中 CFTR 基因型-表型关联与临床结局》。 One honest caveat up front: this paper is not open access, so we're working from the structured abstract only, and I'll flag where that limits us. 先说一个诚实的提醒：这篇论文不是开放获取，所以我们只能依据结构化摘要来讲，我会在受限的地方标出来。 We read it because it's a clean regional genotype-severity example for the phenotype-stratification lens. 我们读它，是因为它是一个干净的、区域性的"基因型-严重程度"例子，正对表型分层的视角。

JORDAN Let me anchor the biology first. 先让我把生物学基础搞清楚。 What does "genotype-phenotype" mean in CF, and what are these "classes" of variants? 在 CF 中"基因型-表型"是什么意思，这些变异的"类别"又是什么？

ALEX Genotype is which two CFTR variants a person carries — CF is autosomal recessive, so everyone has two. 基因型是指一个人携带的是哪两个 CFTR 变异——CF 是常染色体隐性遗传，所以每个人都有两个。 Phenotype is the clinical picture that results: lung function, pancreatic status, nutrition, and so on. 表型是由此产生的临床表现：肺功能、胰腺状态、营养状况等等。 CFTR variants are grouped into functional classes, I through VI, by how they break the protein. CFTR 变异按其破坏蛋白质的方式分为功能类别，I 到 VI 类。 Classes I to III are the "minimal function" end — no protein made, no protein trafficked to the cell surface, or protein present but the channel gate won't open — and they're generally the most severe. I 到 III 类是"最小功能"那一端——不产生蛋白质、蛋白质无法转运到细胞表面、或蛋白质存在但通道门无法打开——它们通常最严重。 Classes IV to VI leave some residual function and tend to be milder. IV 到 VI 类保留一些残余功能，往往较轻。 F508del, the most common variant worldwide, is a class II folding-and-trafficking defect. F508del 是全球最常见的变异，属于 II 类折叠与转运缺陷。

JORDAN And a couple of the clinical outcomes they measure? 还有他们测量的几个临床结局？

ALEX Two worth defining. 有两个值得定义。 Pancreatic insufficiency means the pancreas can't deliver enough digestive enzymes to the gut, so fat and nutrients aren't absorbed — it's tightly linked to the severe variant classes. 胰腺功能不全 (pancreatic insufficiency) 是指胰腺无法向肠道输送足够的消化酶，导致脂肪和营养吸收不良——它与严重变异类别密切相关。 It's one of the most reliable genotype-driven traits in CF, which is why it's often used as a proxy for severe genotype. 它是 CF 中最可靠的、由基因型驱动的性状之一，因此常被用作严重基因型的替代指标。 And the Shwachman-Kulczycki score is an older composite clinical severity score for CF, rating general activity, physical exam, nutrition, and X-ray findings, where higher is better. Shwachman-Kulczycki 评分是一个较早的 CF 综合临床严重程度评分，评估一般活动、体格检查、营养和 X 线表现，分数越高越好。 FEV1, forced expiratory volume in one second, is the standard spirometry measure of lung function. FEV1（一秒用力呼气容积）是衡量肺功能的标准肺量计指标。 In pediatrics it's usually expressed as percent predicted, adjusting for age, sex, and height so that a growing child can be compared against reference norms. 在儿科它通常以"预计值百分比"表示，会对年龄、性别和身高作校正，这样一个正在生长的孩子才能与参考标准比较。 And bone demineralization — the last outcome that shows up in their results — refers to reduced bone mineral density, a recognized CF comorbidity driven by malabsorption, inflammation, and low vitamin D. 还有骨密度降低——他们结果中出现的最后一个结局——指的是骨矿物质密度下降，这是 CF 一个公认的共病，由吸收不良、炎症和低维生素 D 驱动。

JORDAN Why does a variant's "class" predict severity so reliably in the first place? 为什么一个变异的"类别"一开始就能如此可靠地预测严重程度？

ALEX Because CFTR is a chloride and bicarbonate channel at the surface of epithelial cells, and the class tells you which step of the channel's life cycle is broken. 因为 CFTR 是上皮细胞表面的一个氯离子和碳酸氢盐通道，而类别告诉你通道生命周期中的哪一步坏了。 Class I means no protein is made at all; class II means the protein misfolds and never reaches the surface; class III means it reaches the surface but the gate won't open. I 类意味着根本不产生蛋白质；II 类意味着蛋白质错误折叠、永远到不了表面；III 类意味着它到了表面但门打不开。 In all three, essentially no functional channel sits at the membrane, so chloride transport collapses — that's why I to III cluster together as "minimal function." 这三种情况下，膜上基本没有有功能的通道，所以氯离子转运崩溃——这就是为什么 I 到 III 类聚在一起被称为"最小功能"。 Classes IV to VI leave a partially working channel — reduced conductance, less protein, or faster turnover — so residual function buffers the phenotype. IV 到 VI 类留下一个部分工作的通道——电导降低、蛋白质变少、或周转更快——所以残余功能缓冲了表型。 That mechanistic ladder is why grouping by class, rather than by individual variant, is a defensible way to reduce dozens of genotypes down to a few severity strata. 正是这个机制阶梯，使得"按类别而非按单个变异"分组，成为把几十种基因型压缩为少数几个严重程度层的一种站得住脚的做法。

JORDAN So what's the motivation for a study like this? 那这样一个研究的动机是什么？

ALEX Most large CF genotype-phenotype evidence comes from North American and European registries. 大多数大型 CF 基因型-表型证据来自北美和欧洲的登记系统。 Populations with different ancestry and different variant spectra are underrepresented, and the practical question is whether the severity gradients established in those registries reproduce elsewhere. 具有不同血统和不同变异谱的人群代表性不足，而实际问题是：那些登记系统中确立的严重程度梯度在其他地方能否重现。 This study looks at a state public-health CF cohort in Espírito Santo, Brazil — a setting with more genotypic heterogeneity and more rare variants than a typical registry snapshot. 这项研究考察了巴西圣埃斯皮里图州一个州立公共卫生系统的 CF 队列——那是一个基因型异质性更高、罕见变异比典型登记系统快照更多的环境。 The value for us isn't a new mechanism; it's a worked example of stratifying a real cohort by variant severity and testing associations. 对我们的价值不在于一个新机制，而在于一个把真实队列按变异严重程度分层并检验关联的完整示例。

JORDAN Why does representation matter so much scientifically, not just for equity? 为什么代表性在科学上如此重要，而不只是出于公平？

ALEX Because a genotype-phenotype rule learned in one population can silently break in another for reasons that have nothing to do with CFTR. 因为一条在某个人群中学到的基因型-表型规则，可能会因为与 CFTR 毫无关系的原因，在另一个人群中悄悄失效。 Modifier genes — genes elsewhere in the genome that tune CF severity — differ in frequency across ancestries, so the same CFTR genotype can land at a different average severity in a different population. 修饰基因 (modifier gene)——基因组其他位置调节 CF 严重程度的基因——在不同血统间频率不同，所以同一个 CFTR 基因型在不同人群中可能落在不同的平均严重程度上。 Environmental factors, access to care, and the age structure of the cohort shift things further. 环境因素、就医可及性、以及队列的年龄结构会进一步改变结果。 So a study like this is partly a check on transportability — does the severity ordering survive the move to a new population — and partly a hunt for where it doesn't, which is where modifier signal hides. 所以这样一项研究，部分是对"可迁移性 (transportability)"的检验——严重程度排序在迁到新人群后能否存活——部分是寻找它不能存活的地方，而那里正是修饰信号藏身之处。

JORDAN And "cross-sectional retrospective" — spell out what that buys and costs. 还有"横断面回顾性"——说清楚它带来什么、又付出什么代价。

ALEX Retrospective means they pulled existing chart data rather than enrolling patients and following them forward. 回顾性意味着他们调取已有的病历数据，而不是招募患者并向前随访。 It's cheap and fast and uses real-world care data, but it inherits whatever gaps and inconsistencies the charts already had. 它便宜、快、且使用真实世界的诊疗数据，但它继承了病历本已存在的任何空缺和不一致。 Cross-sectional means one snapshot per person, so you can associate genotype with a current outcome, but you can't watch FEV1 decline over time or establish that genotype preceded the outcome in any causal sense. 横断面意味着每人一个快照，所以你能把基因型与一个当前结局关联起来，但你无法观察 FEV1 随时间下降，也无法在任何因果意义上确立基因型先于结局。 For our purposes the design is fine — we're lifting the stratification method, not the causal claims. 就我们的目的而言，这个设计没问题——我们要借用的是分层方法，而不是因果论断。

ALEX There's a second reason this cohort is worth a look: the Brazilian CF variant spectrum is not a copy of the North American one. 还有第二个理由让这个队列值得一看：巴西的 CF 变异谱并不是北美的翻版。 Admixed populations carry F508del at lower frequency than Northern European populations and carry a longer tail of regionally-specific and rare variants. 混合血统人群中 F508del 的频率低于北欧人群，且携带更长尾的、区域特异的和罕见的变异。 That matters because a severity-stratification scheme built and tuned on registries dominated by F508del homozygotes may behave differently when a large share of the cohort sits in the "rare / unclassified" zone. 这一点很重要，因为一个在以 F508del 纯合子为主的登记系统上构建和调校的严重程度分层方案，当队列中很大一部分落在"罕见 / 未分类"区间时，可能表现不同。 So this is also a stress test of how robust the class-based grouping is when heterogeneity is high. 所以这也是对"基于类别的分组在异质性高时有多稳健"的一次压力测试。

ALEX The design, from the abstract: a cross-sectional retrospective study of 110 individuals under 18 with confirmed CF, followed at a state reference center between 2007 and 2024. 从摘要看设计：一项横断面回顾性研究，纳入 110 名 18 岁以下、确诊 CF 的个体，2007 至 2024 年间在一个州级参考中心随访。 Cross-sectional means outcomes are measured at essentially one time point per person rather than tracked longitudinally, which limits any claim about progression or causation. 横断面意味着每个人的结局基本上是在一个时间点测量的，而不是纵向追踪，这限制了任何关于进展或因果的论断。 CFTR variants were classified by functional consequence and grouped by severity, then tested against pancreatic insufficiency, FEV1 percent predicted, the Shwachman-Kulczycki score, nutritional status, and chronic Pseudomonas aeruginosa airway colonization. CFTR 变异按功能后果分类并按严重程度分组，然后与胰腺功能不全、FEV1 预计值百分比、Shwachman-Kulczycki 评分、营养状况以及慢性铜绿假单胞菌 (Pseudomonas aeruginosa) 气道定植进行检验。 Because we only have the abstract, I can't tell you the exact statistical tests, how they handled the two-variant genotype combinations, or how they controlled for age — and those details matter, so treat effect directions as reported, not as validated. 由于我们只有摘要，我无法告诉你确切的统计检验、他们如何处理双变异的基因型组合、或如何校正年龄——而这些细节很重要，所以请把效应方向当作"文中所报告的"，而不是"已被验证的"。

JORDAN What did they find? 他们发现了什么？

ALEX Four results from the abstract. 摘要里有四个结果。 First, the cohort was genotypically heterogeneous: F508del predominated at 72.7% of alleles, but a substantial fraction were non-F508del and rare pathogenic variants. 第一，队列基因型异质性高：F508del 占等位基因的 72.7% 为主，但相当一部分是非 F508del 和罕见致病变异。 Second, individuals carrying two class I-III variants had a significantly higher frequency of pancreatic insufficiency, with a p-value of 0.026. 第二，携带两个 I-III 类变异的个体，胰腺功能不全的频率显著更高，p 值为 0.026。 Third, presence of p.Phe508del — that's F508del — was associated with worse lung function, p equals 0.031, and with bone demineralization, p equals 0.026. 第三，p.Phe508del（即 F508del）的存在与更差的肺功能相关，p 等于 0.031，并与骨密度降低相关，p 等于 0.026。 Fourth, the Shwachman-Kulczycki score correlated negatively with age and age at diagnosis, and positively with BMI z-score and FEV1. 第四，Shwachman-Kulczycki 评分与年龄和诊断时年龄呈负相关，与 BMI z 分数和 FEV1 呈正相关。 Notably, they found no significant association between genotype and chronic Pseudomonas colonization, liver disease, or overall disease severity. 值得注意的是，他们未发现基因型与慢性铜绿假单胞菌定植、肝病或总体疾病严重程度之间有显著关联。

JORDAN How do we read those numbers responsibly? 我们该如何负责任地解读这些数字？

ALEX Carefully, because n is 110 and these are unadjusted association p-values near the 0.05 line. 要谨慎，因为 n 只有 110，而这些是接近 0.05 界线的未校正关联 p 值。 The severity direction — two minimal-function variants track with pancreatic insufficiency, F508del tracks with worse lung function — is exactly what the large registries show, so this is a consistency check in a Brazilian pediatric cohort, not a new discovery. 严重程度的方向——两个最小功能变异与胰腺功能不全相伴，F508del 与更差肺功能相伴——正是大型登记系统所显示的，所以这是在巴西儿童队列中的一致性验证，而不是新发现。 The bone-demineralization link is the more interesting thread, but with a p of 0.026 and no visible adjustment for age, disease duration, or nutritional status from the abstract, I'd hold it loosely. 骨密度降低这条线更有意思，但在 p 为 0.026、且摘要中看不到对年龄、病程或营养状况的校正的情况下，我会松散地看待它。 Bone density falls naturally with worse nutrition and longer disease, both of which travel with F508del homozygosity, so the association could be partly a stand-in for overall severity rather than a direct genotype effect on bone. 骨密度会随更差的营养和更长的病程自然下降，而这两者都与 F508del 纯合相伴，所以这个关联可能部分是"总体严重程度"的替身，而非基因型对骨骼的直接效应。

JORDAN And the null results — no link to Pseudomonas or liver disease — do those tell us anything? 那些阴性结果——与铜绿假单胞菌或肝病无关联——它们告诉我们什么吗？

ALEX Mostly they tell us the study was underpowered for those outcomes, rather than that no relationship exists. 主要是它们告诉我们这项研究对那些结局的检验效能不足，而不是说不存在关系。 Chronic Pseudomonas colonization and CF liver disease are both strongly shaped by non-CFTR factors — environment, treatment adherence, and modifier genes outside CFTR — and they accumulate with age, so a pediatric cross-section catches them at low prevalence. 慢性铜绿假单胞菌定植和 CF 肝病都强烈受非 CFTR 因素塑造——环境、治疗依从性、以及 CFTR 之外的修饰基因——而且它们随年龄累积，所以一个儿童横断面在低患病率时就把它们捕捉住了。 With 110 children you simply may not have enough events to detect a genotype effect even if one is there. 用 110 个孩子，即便存在基因型效应，你也可能没有足够的事件数来检测到它。 That's a useful reminder for our own stratified analyses: a null at small n is not evidence of absence. 这对我们自己的分层分析是一个有用的提醒：小样本下的阴性并不是"不存在"的证据。

JORDAN Before we get to the build — does this paper add anything to the modifier-gene catalog we've been keeping? 在讲到构建之前——这篇论文给我们一直维护的修饰基因目录添了什么吗？
