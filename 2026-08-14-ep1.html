How did this episode land for you?

Blurb

A ten-patient retrospective study out of three Australian tertiary referral centres re-curated germline MEN1 variants of uncertain significance (VUS) using a structured ACMG/AMP pipeline that folds together phenotype specificity, family segregation data, population frequency, and three computational predictors — REVEL, AlphaMissense, and SpliceAI — plus AlphaFold/PyMOL protein structural modelling. Seven of ten VUS (70%) were reclassified as likely pathogenic, with computational scores (median REVEL 0.913, median AlphaMissense 0.999) statistically indistinguishable from known ClinVar pathogenic variants and significantly higher than benign ones (p<0.001). Reclassified variants clustered structurally within menin's JunD- and MLL-binding domains. MEN1 is not CFTR, and this paper is abstract-only in our index (no PMCID, not open access), but the underlying workflow — evidence-integrated VUS re-curation combining clinical, computational, and structural layers — is a gene-agnostic template. It maps directly onto the Karen Raraigh collaboration opening around CFTR variant interpretation and harmonization, and is portable as a worked example of what an ACMG/AMP-style reclassification pipeline looks like end to end.

Plain-English Glossary

  • ACMG/AMP — The joint criteria from the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology for classifying genetic variants (benign to pathogenic) using weighted evidence codes.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会与分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类标准，通过加权证据代码对变异进行良性到致病性的分级）
  • AlphaFold — A deep-learning protein structure predictor that generates a 3D model of a protein from its amino acid sequence, used here to locate variants relative to menin's functional domains.（中文：一种深度学习蛋白质结构预测工具，可根据氨基酸序列生成蛋白质三维模型，本文用它定位变异相对menin功能结构域的位置）
  • AlphaMissense — An in-silico predictor that scores missense variants for their likelihood of disrupting protein function.（中文：一种计算机预测工具，为错义变异打分，评估其破坏蛋白质功能的可能性）
  • Cascade testing — Genetic testing of at-risk relatives once a pathogenic variant is confirmed in one family member.（中文：在某一家族成员确认携带致病变异后，对其余有风险的亲属进行的基因检测）
  • ClinVar — A public database of clinically observed genetic variants and their classifications, used here as the reference distribution for known pathogenic and benign variants.（中文：一个公开的临床变异数据库，本文用它作为已知致病和良性变异的参照分布）
  • Frameshift variant — A variant (usually an insertion or deletion) that shifts the reading frame of a gene, typically producing a truncated or nonfunctional protein.（中文：通常由插入或缺失引起、使基因阅读框发生偏移的变异，通常导致蛋白质被截短或失去功能）
  • In-frame deletion — A deletion that removes a multiple of three nucleotides, preserving the reading frame but removing one or more amino acids.（中文：缺失的核苷酸数为三的倍数，阅读框不变，但会丢失一个或多个氨基酸）
  • In-silico predictor — A computational (as opposed to lab-based) tool that estimates a variant's functional impact from sequence or structural features.（中文：通过序列或结构特征估算变异功能影响的计算工具，区别于实验室实测方法）
  • JunD-binding domain — A functional region of the menin protein where it interacts with the transcription factor JunD; part of the structural evidence used for reclassification in this paper.（中文：menin蛋白上与转录因子JunD相互作用的功能区域；本文用作重新分类的结构证据之一）
  • Likely pathogenic — A specific ACMG/AMP classification tier indicating strong (but not maximal) evidence that a variant causes disease.（中文：ACMG/AMP分类体系中的一个具体等级，表示有较强但非最高确定性的证据表明该变异致病）
  • MEN1 / menin — MEN1 is the gene (and menin its protein product) responsible for multiple endocrine neoplasia type 1; the subject gene of this paper, not CFTR-related.（中文：MEN1是导致1型多发性内分泌腺瘤病的基因，menin是其编码的蛋白质；是本文的研究对象基因，与CFTR无关）
  • MLL-binding domain — A second functional region of menin, interacting with the MLL histone-methyltransferase complex; also used as structural evidence here.（中文：menin的另一个功能区域，与MLL组蛋白甲基转移酶复合物相互作用；本文同样用作结构证据）
  • Missense variant — A single-nucleotide change that alters one amino acid in the resulting protein.（中文：改变蛋白质中一个氨基酸的单核苷酸变异）
  • PyMOL — A molecular visualization program used to inspect and assess AlphaFold-generated protein structures.（中文：一款分子可视化软件，用于查看和评估AlphaFold生成的蛋白质结构）
  • REVEL — An in-silico predictor that combines multiple sub-scores into a single ensemble estimate of a missense variant's pathogenicity.（中文：一种计算机预测工具，整合多个子分数得出错义变异致病性的综合评分）
  • Segregation — Evidence from whether a variant co-occurs with disease across multiple affected/unaffected relatives in a family.（中文：根据变异是否在家族中多名患病/未患病亲属间共同出现来判断的一种证据）
  • SpliceAI — An in-silico predictor that estimates whether a variant disrupts normal splice-site recognition.（中文：一种计算机预测工具，估算变异是否破坏正常的剪接位点识别）
  • Splice-affecting variant — A variant predicted or shown to alter normal mRNA splicing.（中文：被预测或证实会改变正常mRNA剪接过程的变异）
  • VUS (Variant of Uncertain Significance) — A variant classification meaning current evidence is insufficient to call it either benign or pathogenic.（中文：意义未明变异，指目前证据不足以将该变异判定为良性或致病）

Methods/Tools to Lift

  • REVEL — ensemble in-silico missense pathogenicity score.
  • AlphaMissense — deep-learning missense pathogenicity score.
  • SpliceAI — deep-learning splice-disruption predictor.
  • AlphaFold — protein structure prediction for locating variants in 3D.
  • PyMOL — structural visualization/assessment of variant placement relative to functional domains.
  • ACMG/AMP re-curation framework — standardized evidence-code system for VUS reclassification.
  • Kruskal-Wallis comparison against ClinVar LP/P vs. LB/B distributions — a lightweight sanity check that a cohort's computational scores resemble known-pathogenic vs. known-benign reference groups.

Where It Lands + Buildable Artifact

Maps to the Karen Raraigh collaboration priority — CFTR variant interpretation / VUS pathogenicity prediction / variant-notation harmonization. This paper is a directly portable worked template (gene-agnostic ACMG/AMP re-curation pipeline: phenotype + segregation + REVEL/AlphaMissense/SpliceAI + AlphaFold/PyMOL structural context) even though its subject gene (MEN1) is unrelated to CFTR.

Buildable artifact: cftr-vus-recuration-worklist (difficulty S/M) — takes a CFTR VUS list (from ClinVar/CFTR2), pulls REVEL/AlphaMissense/SpliceAI scores and an AlphaFold structural model per variant, flags whether each variant falls within a known CFTR functional domain (NBD1, NBD2, transmembrane domains, R-domain) analogous to this paper's JunD-/MLL-binding domain clustering, and outputs a triage worklist mirroring the paper's ACMG/AMP evidence-table structure.

What to Verify First

  • n=10 is very small — the 70% reclassification rate is a proportion of ten cases, not a validated rate at CFTR-VUS scale.
  • Single-country (all-Australian), likely single re-curation team — unclear whether ACMG/AMP judgment calls (e.g., segregation "strength") generalize or were independently double-curated.
  • Abstract-only: no access to the per-variant evidence table, individual REVEL/AlphaMissense/SpliceAI scores, or the actual re-curation worksheet — treat as a structural template, not a line-for-line validated pipeline.
  • Structural transferability is unconfirmed: menin is a small nuclear-interacting protein with two binding domains, while CFTR is a large multi-domain ABC transporter (TMDs, NBD1/NBD2, R-domain) — whether "domain clustering equals pathogenicity evidence" carries over across such different folds is an open structural-biology question, not something to assume.
