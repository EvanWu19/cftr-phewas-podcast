Ep4 (Track C) — Fanconi anemia growth charts: a low-N, sex-asymmetric disease-specific reference

2026-09-04 · Barbus C, Rayannavar A, Miller BS et al. (2024) · American Journal of Medical Genetics Part A · Abstract-only

Same LMS-based recipe as ep3, same University of Minnesota endocrinology group, applied to a genuinely rare disease: Fanconi anemia (FA), an inherited DNA-repair disorder causing progressive bone marrow failure, short stature, and congenital anomalies, often compounded by hematopoietic cell transplant conditioning. From a single-center retrospective cohort (sample size not given in the abstract), the paper finds a sex-asymmetric divergence pattern: FA males' 50th percentile for both height- and weight-for-age overlaps the general population's 3rd percentile throughout childhood, while FA females diverge on weight only, and only in a narrow 6-24-month window, before stabilizing near the population 50th percentile. BMI followed weight's shape with different adiposity-rebound timing and broader variability in females. Beyond ep3's general "population norms over-flag a disease cohort" lesson, this paper adds a distinct wrinkle: the divergence itself is sex- and time-window-specific, meaning a single sex-blind, childhood-wide threshold would misfire asymmetrically. Buildable artifact: a low-N LMS feasibility notebook using entirely public data.

Plain-English glossary

  • Fanconi anemia (FA) — an inherited DNA-repair disorder causing progressive bone marrow failure, elevated cancer risk, and short stature/congenital anomalies as part of the disease itself.（中文：一种遗传性DNA修复障碍疾病，会导致进行性骨髓衰竭、癌症风险升高，以及作为疾病本身一部分出现的身材矮小和先天异常。）
  • Hematopoietic cell transplant (HCT) — bone marrow/stem cell transplant, often needed in FA for marrow failure; conditioning chemotherapy/radiation has its own well-documented growth-suppressive effect, a potential confound for any FA growth chart that doesn't stratify by transplant status.（中文：造血干细胞移植，FA患者因骨髓衰竭常需接受，其预处理化疗/放疗本身就有已充分记录的抑制生长效应，是任何未按移植状态分层的FA生长曲线图的潜在混杂因素。）
  • Survivorship pattern (general epidemiology concept, headword kept in English) — in a small, long-follow-up rare-disease cohort, which patients survive to later ages and remain observable can itself shape the apparent trajectory.（中文：在小规模、长随访的罕见病队列中，哪些患者能存活到更晚年龄并持续被观察到，本身就可能塑造观察到的生长轨迹表现。）
  • LMS method (see ep3 glossary) — the same semiparametric growth-curve-fitting approach used here at a much smaller sample size than ep3's CAH cohort.（中文：与第三集相同的半参数生长曲线拟合方法，本文应用于比CAH队列小得多的样本量。）

Full citation

Barbus C, Rayannavar A, Miller BS, Jenkins MJ, Addo OY, Rayes A, Ahrweiler N, Olson A, Pohlkamp Z, Wagner JE, MacMillan ML. Development of specific growth charts for children with Fanconi anemia. American Journal of Medical Genetics Part A. 2024;194(7):e63554. PMID 38317562 · DOI

Methods / tools to lift

  • LMS/GAMLSS chart construction at low N — same method as ep3, a real-world test case for how far down in sample size the recipe still produces a usable chart.
  • Sex-stratified divergence reporting — reporting height/weight/BMI divergence separately by sex rather than pooled, surfacing a pattern a pooled analysis would have hidden.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation, primary — sex- and time-window-specific divergence means a uniform threshold would misfire asymmetrically; secondary angle on feasibility at low sample size. MVP: low-N LMS feasibility notebook — subsample a public pediatric reference dataset to roughly FA-cohort size, refit LMS curves, characterize how much percentile-curve uncertainty grows as N shrinks, as a gate before considering any small-subgroup-specific chart. Difficulty: S-M, entirely public data.

What to verify first

  • Sample size, visit count, and confidence intervals around the percentile curves — none given in the abstract.
  • Whether/how hematopoietic cell transplant status was accounted for or stratified — a real confound if pooled with non-transplanted patients.
  • Whether the girls'-weight-recovery pattern reflects biology or a survivorship/cohort-composition artifact — not resolvable from the abstract.
  • No claims here about CFFPR's current thresholds — external methodological lesson only.

Error types covered / not covered

Covered: reference-chart mismatch bias, sex-asymmetric divergence pattern, low-N chart-fitting feasibility. Not covered: individual-record data-entry errors (unit conversion, digit transposition, decimal shift, carry-forward) — outside this paper's scope entirely.
