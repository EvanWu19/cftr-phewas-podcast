Systematic data quality assessment of electronic health record data to evaluate study-specific fitness: Report from the PRESERVE research study

PRESERVE is a 15-site, PCORnet-based observational study of blood-pressure control in pediatric chronic kidney disease (178,928 patients total, per-site N from 2,657 to 24,863). The PEDSnet Data Coordinating Center built and ran a study-specific data quality assessment (SSDQA) process in two rounds — DQ1 (distributed aggregate query, 79 checks, 115 issues) and DQ2 (centralized row-level query, 65 checks across 13 domains, 157 issues) — tagging every check against five categories (concordance, completeness, plausibility, consistency, conformance, extending Kahn's original three) and every found issue against four priority tiers (urgent, high, medium, low) assigned by coordinating-center reviewers. The standout finding for this track: an urgent-priority "height values low" plausibility flag, cross-referenced to a cohort-attrition issue — implausible height readings were materially affecting study eligibility, not just sitting as a footnote — alongside medium-priority flags for site-level anomalies in height and weight record counts. 123 of the identified gaps were resolved and 2 institutions were saved from full exclusion. Paired deliberately with yesterday's METRIC-framework episode: METRIC supplies the vocabulary, PRESERVE shows what operationalizing that vocabulary into a running, prioritized triage system looks like in practice.

Plain-English glossary

Common Data Model (CDM) — a standardized relational schema (here, PCORnet's own CDM, conceptually parallel to OMOP) that lets the same analytic query run identically across every participating site.（中文:一套标准化的关系型模式[此处为PCORnet自有的CDM,概念上与OMOP相似],使同一条分析查询能在所有参与站点上以完全一致的方式运行。）

Concordance — agreement of data elements across multiple sources (e.g. diagnoses and medications), one of PRESERVE's five DQ categories, added on top of Kahn's original three.（中文:多个数据来源之间数据要素的相符程度[如诊断和用药记录],是PRESERVE五个数据质量类别之一,是在Kahn原有三个类别基础上新增的。）

eGFR (estimated glomerular filtration rate) — the standard clinical measure of kidney function in pediatric CKD, calculated from height plus a serum marker (creatinine or cystatin C) — meaning a bad height value corrupts a downstream clinical calculation, not just a standalone growth metric.（中文:儿科CKD中评估肾功能的标准临床指标,由身高结合血清标志物[肌酐或胱抑素C]计算得出——意味着错误的身高数值会污染下游临床计算,而不仅仅是一个独立的生长指标。）

ETL (extract, transform, load) — the process by which a site's raw source data is converted into the network's Common Data Model format.（中文:将站点原始数据转换为网络通用数据模型格式的过程。）

PCORnet — a large, multi-institutional US EHR research network that standardizes participating sites' data into a shared CDM.（中文:美国一个大型多机构EHR研究网络,将参与站点的数据标准化为共享的CDM格式。）

PEDSnet — the pediatric-focused arm of PCORnet; its Data Coordinating Center developed and ran PRESERVE's SSDQA process.（中文:PCORnet中专注于儿科的分支机构;其数据协调中心开发并运行了PRESERVE的SSDQA流程。）

Priority tiering (urgent/high/medium/low) — PRESERVE's four-level scheme for triaging detected data quality issues by how much they threaten analysis, assigned by coordinating-center reviewer judgment rather than an automated rule.（中文:PRESERVE用于对已发现的数据质量问题按照对分析的威胁程度进行分诊的四级方案,由协调中心评审员判断分配,而非自动化规则。）

SSDQA (study-specific data quality assessment) — a data-quality process tailored to a specific study's analysis plan and variables, distinct from standing, context-agnostic network-level QC checks.（中文:针对某项具体研究的分析计划和变量量身定制的数据质量流程,不同于与具体场景无关的常规网络级QC检查。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Kahn-plus-two category scheme — concordance, completeness, plausibility, consistency, conformance — a directly reusable extension of the standard Kahn taxonomy.
  • Two-round distributed-then-centralized DQ design — aggregate-only first pass, row-level second pass, to catch issues before exposing more granular data.
  • Four-tier priority triage (urgent/high/medium/low) — a reusable scheme for routing scarce human review time toward the issues that actually threaten analysis.
  • Issue catalog with cross-referencing — linking a data-element-level issue (e.g. height) to its downstream analytic consequence (e.g. cohort attrition).

What to verify first

  • Reproducibility of the priority tiering across different reviewers is untested — it was two specific coordinating-center reviewers' collaborative judgment, not an algorithmic rule.
  • Generalizability of the check catalog and priority logic beyond a pediatric CKD population on PCORnet's specific infrastructure to a different disease area or registry data model is open.

Pipeline stage: (c) human review budget, primary; (a) candidate generation, secondary.

Error types covered / not covered: Covers implausible-value detection (height/weight, "low" and anomalous-count directions) and cross-references flags to downstream eligibility impact; does not itself distinguish unit conversion, digit transposition, decimal shift, or carry-forward as distinct mechanisms — issues are categorized by data quality dimension (e.g. plausibility) rather than by underlying error mechanism.
