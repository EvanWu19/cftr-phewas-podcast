Blurb

The ClinGen Sequence Variant Interpretation working group's practical recipe for three ACMG/AMP evidence criteria that had been left underspecified since 2015: PP1 (co-segregation supporting pathogenicity), BS4 (non-segregation supporting benign), and PP4 (phenotype specificity). Working from real diseases — cystinosis, Cockayne syndrome, Marfan syndrome, tuberous sclerosis — the group derives two lookup tables (diagnostic yield → PP4 points; segregating meioses → PP1 points) inside the Bayesian points framework, and reaches one load-bearing conclusion: PP4 and PP1 are not independent — they are two views of the same linkage fact, so in a locus-homogeneous, high-yield gene the phenotype already carries the evidence and adding co-segregation double-counts. All locus-level evidence is capped at +5.0 points because of linkage disequilibrium — and the worked example of that danger is a CFTR variant: p.Ile148Thr, presumed pathogenic for years until it was found benign but in LD with the true culprit c.3067_3072del (p.Ile1023_Val1024del). Where it lands: Karen / variant interpretation. Buildable artifact: cftr-cosegregation-points-helper — a small script that scores PP1/PP4 by the paper's tables, enforces the +5.0 cap, and flags when PP1 is being mis-applied to a locus-homogeneous CFTR presentation.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP framework — the 2015 consensus system of evidence codes (PVS1, PS3, PP1, PP3, PP4, BS4, BP4, …) used worldwide to classify a sequence variant as pathogenic or benign.（中文：2015 年的共识证据代码系统，全球用来判定序列变异是致病还是良性。）
  • BS4 — the benign counterpart of PP1: a variant that fails to co-segregate (an affected person lacks it, or an unaffected person carries it) is strong evidence against pathogenicity.（中文：PP1 的良性对应项——变异不共分离时，是反对致病性的强证据。）
  • Co-segregation — a variant travelling together with the disease through a family; affected relatives carry it, unaffected relatives don't.（中文：变异在家族中与疾病一起传递——患者携带、非患者不携带。）
  • Diagnostic yield — the fraction of patients with a given clinical diagnosis in whom sequencing actually finds a causative variant.（中文：临床诊断为某病的患者中，测序真正找到致病变异的比例。）
  • Linkage / LOD score — linkage is the fact that loci near each other on a chromosome are inherited together; the LOD score is the log-odds statistic quantifying that co-inheritance evidence.（中文：连锁指染色体上相邻位点一起遗传；LOD score 是量化这种共遗传证据的对数优势比统计量。）
  • Linkage disequilibrium (LD) — two variants sitting so close they are almost always inherited as a unit; co-segregation cannot tell a causal variant from its innocent LD neighbor. The CFTR I148T / I1023_V1024del pair is the classic cautionary case.（中文：两个变异靠得太近、几乎总一起遗传；共分离无法把致病变异和它无辜的 LD 邻居区分开。CFTR 的 I148T / I1023_V1024del 就是经典警示。）
  • Locus homogeneity vs heterogeneity — homogeneity: one and only one gene causes the disease (cystinosis, and closely, classic CF); heterogeneity: several genes can (Bardet-Biedl >27 loci; recessive hearing loss ≥66 genes).（中文：同质性指有且只有一个基因致病（胱氨酸症，及接近的典型 CF）；异质性指多个基因都能致病。）
  • PP1 — the ACMG/AMP code for co-segregation evidence supporting pathogenicity.（中文：支持致病性的共分离证据代码。）
  • PP4 (phenotype specificity) — a clinical presentation so specific to one gene that observing it is itself evidence for pathogenicity; its strength is tied to diagnostic yield.（中文：表型对某单一基因如此特异，以致观察到它本身就是致病证据；其强度与诊断检出率挂钩。）
  • Penetrance / phenocopy rate — penetrance: probability a genotype carrier actually shows the disease; phenocopy rate: people showing the phenotype for non-genetic reasons. Both must be favorable for a phenotype-implicates-gene argument to hold.（中文：外显率＝携带者真正发病的概率；拟表型率＝因非遗传原因表现该表型的人。两者都需有利，"表型指向基因"的论证才成立。）
  • Phase — which variant sits on which of the two parental alleles; a single informative parent-to-child meiosis can establish it in dominant disease.（中文：哪个变异位于两条亲本等位基因中的哪一条；显性病中一次有信息量的亲子减数分裂即可确定。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Bayesian points framework — prior probability of pathogenicity 0.102; evidence expressed as points on a log-odds scale (+6 ≈ likely pathogenic / 90%; +10 ≈ pathogenic / 99%).
  • Two lookup tables — diagnostic yield → PP4 points, and count of segregating meioses → PP1 points; the operational core of the paper.
  • +5.0 locus-evidence cap — any evidence that implicates only a locus (PP4, PP1) is capped, because an observed variant may merely be in LD with the true cause.
  • Fungibility of evidence — points distributed across candidate variants on one allele (e.g. 48%/48% → +2.5 each) can be redistributed when variant-specific evidence (PVS1, REVEL/BP4, BS2) arrives; and benign evidence at one locus becomes pathogenic evidence at another under locus heterogeneity.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP: cftr-cosegregation-points-helper (S) — input a family pedigree table + a phenotype-specific CFTR diagnostic-yield estimate; output PP4 and PP1 points from the paper's tables, the enforced +5.0 cap, and a warning flag when PP1 is being applied to a locus-homogeneous, high-yield classic-CF presentation where PP4 already carries the evidence (double-counting).

What to verify first

  • Which phenotype is being classified — classic CF (high phenotype specificity) vs a CFTR-related disorder / CBAVD (low). PP4 points depend entirely on how sharply the phenotype points to CFTR.
  • Diagnostic yield must be phenotype- and ancestry-specific, not one global number; yield varies with how the phenotype is defined and with cohort ancestry.
  • CFTR2 functional class of the variant — variants of varying clinical consequence (e.g. R117H + poly-T) violate the locus-homogeneity assumption; the paper explicitly warns against use on low-penetrance / susceptibility variants.
  • No claim here touches any registry's internal data — the tool operates purely on external ACMG/AMP classification rules.

ALEX Track A today is Biesecker and colleagues, 2024, in the American Journal of Human Genetics: "ClinGen guidance for use of the PP1/BS4 co-segregation and PP4 phenotype specificity criteria for sequence variant pathogenicity classification." 今天的 Track A 是 Biesecker 等人 2024 年发表在《美国人类遗传学杂志》上的论文：《ClinGen 关于在序列变异致病性分类中使用 PP1/BS4 共分离标准与 PP4 表型特异性标准的指南》。 It's open access, we have the full text, and it comes from the ClinGen Sequence Variant Interpretation working group — the people who maintain the rules everyone else follows. 它是开放获取的，我们拿到了全文，而且它来自 ClinGen 序列变异解读工作组——也就是维护那套所有人都遵循的规则的那群人。 We're reading it because it is the recipe for turning "this variant runs in the family" into a number, and because its central worked example of what can go wrong is a CFTR variant. 我们读它，是因为它是把"这个变异在家族里遗传"变成一个数字的配方，也因为它讲"哪里会出错"用的核心例子恰好是一个 CFTR 变异。

JORDAN Before we touch the rules, define the vocabulary, because this whole paper is written in an alphabet soup. 在碰规则之前，先把词汇定义清楚，因为整篇论文都写在一锅字母汤里。 What is co-segregation, and what are PP1, BS4, and PP4? 什么是共分离，PP1、BS4、PP4 又是什么？

ALEX Start with the framework. 先从框架说起。 In 2015 the ACMG and AMP — the two big American labs-and-pathology bodies — published a system for deciding whether a genetic variant causes disease. 2015 年，ACMG（美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会）和 AMP（分子病理学会）这两个美国的大型实验室与病理学机构，发布了一套用于判断某个遗传变异是否致病的系统。 It's a set of evidence codes, each of which pushes toward "pathogenic" or "benign." 它是一组证据代码，每一个都把判断推向"致病"或"良性"。 Co-segregation（共分离）means a variant travels together with the disease through a family — affected relatives carry it, unaffected relatives don't. 共分离（co-segregation）指一个变异在家族中与疾病一起传递——患病的亲属携带它，未患病的亲属不携带。 The code for co-segregation supporting pathogenicity is PP1（一个支持"致病"的共分离证据代码）. 支持致病性的共分离证据代码是 PP1。 The mirror image — the variant fails to travel with the disease, an affected person lacks it or an unaffected person has it — is BS4（一个支持"良性"的非共分离证据代码），strong evidence against pathogenicity. 它的镜像——变异没有和疾病一起传递，患者不携带、或未患者携带——就是 BS4，一个反对致病性的强证据。 And PP4（表型特异性证据代码）is the phenotype specificity criterion: the patient's clinical presentation is so specific to this one gene that seeing it is itself evidence. 而 PP4 是表型特异性标准：患者的临床表现对这一个基因如此特异，以至于看到它本身就是一种证据。

JORDAN And I keep seeing "LOD score" and "linkage." 我还老看到 "LOD score" 和 "linkage"。 Are those the same idea? 那是同一个概念吗？

ALEX Closely related. 密切相关。 Linkage（连锁）is the classical genetics fact that two things near each other on a chromosome are inherited together. 连锁（linkage）是经典遗传学的一个事实：染色体上彼此靠近的两个东西会一起遗传。 A LOD score（连锁的对数优势比统计量）is the statistic that quantifies how strong that co-inheritance evidence is — literally the log of the odds. LOD score 是量化这种共遗传证据强度的统计量——字面意思就是优势比的对数。 Co-segregation analysis for a single variant, the authors point out, is formally a special case of linkage analysis. 作者指出，针对单个变异的共分离分析，在形式上是连锁分析的一个特例。 Two more terms you need. 还有两个你需要的术语。 Locus homogeneity（基因座同质性）means one and only one gene causes the disease; locus heterogeneity（基因座异质性）means several different genes can. 基因座同质性（locus homogeneity）指有且只有一个基因导致该病；基因座异质性（locus heterogeneity）指有好几个不同的基因都能导致它。 And diagnostic yield（诊断检出率）is the fraction of patients with the clinical diagnosis in whom sequencing actually finds a causative variant. 诊断检出率（diagnostic yield）是在临床诊断为该病的患者中，测序真正找到致病变异的比例。 Hold those two, because the whole paper turns on them. 记住这两个，因为整篇论文都围着它们转。

JORDAN You keep saying "high yield" and "the phenotype guarantees the gene." 你老说"高检出率"和"表型锁定基因"。 What else has to be true for that to hold? 还得满足什么条件这才成立？

ALEX Two more properties. 还有两个性质。 Penetrance（外显率）is the probability that someone carrying the causative genotype actually shows the disease — high penetrance means carriers reliably get sick. 外显率（penetrance）是携带致病基因型的人真正表现出疾病的概率——高外显率意味着携带者可靠地发病。 Phenocopy rate（拟表型率）is the reverse leak: people who have the phenotype for reasons other than the gene in question. 拟表型率（phenocopy rate）是反向的渗漏：那些因为该基因以外的原因而表现出这个表型的人。 The clean argument — "having this phenotype implicates this gene" — only works when penetrance is high and the phenocopy rate is near zero. 那个干净的论证——"有这个表型就指向这个基因"——只有在外显率高、拟表型率接近零时才成立。 The moment a phenotype has many non-genetic causes, PP4 weakens, because seeing the phenotype no longer points cleanly at one locus. 一旦某个表型有很多非遗传的原因，PP4 就会减弱，因为看到该表型不再干净地指向单一基因座。 For cystic fibrosis that matters, because "bronchiectasis in an adult" is riddled with phenocopies while "classic CF in an infant with high sweat chloride" is not. 对囊性纤维化来说这很关键，因为"成人的支气管扩张"充满了拟表型，而"婴儿典型 CF 伴高汗氯"则不然。

JORDAN So what was actually broken before this paper? 那在这篇论文之前，到底是什么坏了？

ALEX The 2015 guidelines said PP1 "may be used as stronger evidence with increasing segregation data" — and then never said how. 2015 年的指南说 PP1"可以随共分离数据增加而作为更强的证据"——然后从没说该怎么做。 So labs had a criterion they were told to scale, with no scale. 于是实验室手里有一条被要求分级的标准，却没有分级尺。 Worse, earlier attempts blurred two genuinely different questions: is this the right disease gene at all, versus is this specific variant the culprit. 更糟的是，早先的尝试把两个真正不同的问题搅在了一起：这到底是不是正确的疾病基因，与这个具体的变异是不是元凶。 And nobody had worked out how co-segregation behaves under recessive or X-linked inheritance, only dominant. 而且没人搞清楚共分离在隐性或 X 连锁遗传下如何表现，只处理过显性。 That matters enormously for cystic fibrosis, which is autosomal recessive. 这对囊性纤维化极其重要，因为它是常染色体隐性遗传。 The group's goal was a practical, points-based recipe consistent with the Bayesian version of the 2015 rules. 这个工作组的目标，是给出一个实用的、基于点数的配方，并与 2015 年规则的贝叶斯版本保持一致。

JORDAN Bayesian points — walk me through what they actually built. 贝叶斯点数——带我过一遍他们到底造了什么。

ALEX The Bayesian framework assigns every variant a prior probability of being pathogenic — they use 0.102 — and then each piece of evidence multiplies the odds up or down. 这个贝叶斯框架给每个变异一个先验致病概率——他们用 0.102——然后每一条证据把优势比向上或向下相乘。 Evidence is expressed in points on a log scale, where about +6 points reaches "likely pathogenic" at 90% posterior, and +10 reaches "pathogenic" at 99%. 证据用对数尺度上的点数表示，大约 +6 点达到 90% 后验概率的"可能致病"，+10 点达到 99% 的"致病"。 The working group convened as a ClinGen subcommittee, met monthly starting in 2018, and reasoned through real diseases rather than abstractions. 这个工作组作为 ClinGen 的一个小组委员会成立，从 2018 年起每月开会，用真实疾病而不是抽象概念来推理。 Cystinosis for locus homogeneity, Cockayne syndrome for two-gene heterogeneity, Marfan syndrome for dominant inheritance, tuberous sclerosis, Bardet-Biedl, hearing loss. 用胱氨酸症讲基因座同质性，用 Cockayne 综合征讲双基因异质性，用马凡综合征讲显性遗传，还有结节性硬化、Bardet-Biedl 综合征、听力损失。 The deliverable is a pair of lookup tables: one converts a gene's diagnostic yield into PP4 points, the other converts a count of segregating meioses into PP1 points. 交付物是两张查找表：一张把某基因的诊断检出率换算成 PP4 点数，另一张把共分离减数分裂的次数换算成 PP1 点数。 And here is the design choice I'd push on: they impose a hard cap of +5.0 points on any evidence that only implicates a locus rather than a specific variant. 而这是我想质疑的设计选择：他们对任何只指向基因座、而非指向具体变异的证据，强加了 +5.0 点的硬上限。

JORDAN Why cap it? 为什么要设上限？ If the phenotype is a slam dunk, why not let the points run? 如果表型板上钉钉，为什么不让点数一路涨上去？

ALEX Because of linkage disequilibrium, and their example is a CFTR variant. 因为连锁不平衡，而且他们的例子就是一个 CFTR 变异。 Linkage disequilibrium, LD（连锁不平衡，指两个变异因位置相近而在人群中倾向于一起被遗传）, means two variants sit so close together that they are almost always inherited as a unit. 连锁不平衡 LD 指两个变异靠得太近，几乎总是作为一个整体被遗传。 The variant p.Ile148Thr in CFTR was for years presumed pathogenic — and then found to be benign, merely in LD with the actual culprit, c.3067_3072del, p.Ile1023_Val1024del. CFTR 中的 p.Ile148Thr 变异曾多年被推定为致病——后来发现它其实是良性的，只是与真正的元凶 c.3067_3072del（p.Ile1023_Val1024del）处于连锁不平衡。 Co-segregation cannot tell those two apart, because they travel together. 共分离无法区分这两者，因为它们一起遗传。 So no matter how perfectly a variant tracks with disease, that alone can never prove it is the causal one rather than its innocent neighbor. 所以无论一个变异与疾病共同传递得多么完美，仅凭这一点也永远无法证明它才是致病的那个，而不是它无辜的邻居。 The +5.0 cap encodes that humility. +5.0 的上限，把这份谦逊编码了进去。

JORDAN Okay, give me the results — the counterintuitive ones. 好，把结果给我——尤其是反直觉的那些。

ALEX The headline is that PP4 and PP1 are not independent — they are two views of the same linkage fact, which breaks a rule of the naive Bayesian classifier that says evidence should be independent. 头条结论是：PP4 和 PP1 并不独立——它们是同一个连锁事实的两种视角，而这违反了朴素贝叶斯分类器"证据应相互独立"的规则。 In a gene like cystinosis with locus homogeneity and a 95.8% diagnostic yield, the phenotype alone essentially guarantees the gene, so the phenotype criterion PP4 already carries near-maximal evidence — and you must not then also add co-segregation PP1, because that's double-counting the same fact. 在像胱氨酸症这样基因座同质、诊断检出率 95.8% 的基因里，表型本身几乎就能锁定基因，所以表型标准 PP4 已经携带了接近最大的证据——你绝不能再叠加共分离 PP1，因为那是对同一事实的重复计数。 Second result: when an allele carries two candidate variants, you distribute the evidence — split the posterior probability, so two variants each get about 48%, which converts to +2.5 points apiece. 第二个结果：当一条等位基因上带有两个候选变异时，你要分摊证据——把后验概率对半分，两个变异各约 48%，换算过来各得 +2.5 点。 And that split is fungible: if other evidence later favors one variant, the points can be redistributed to it. 而且这种分摊是可流转的：如果之后有别的证据偏向其中一个变异，点数可以重新分配给它。 Third, and elegant: benign evidence at one locus becomes pathogenic evidence at another. 第三点，也很优雅：一个基因座上的良性证据会变成另一个基因座上的致病证据。 In Cockayne syndrome, where CSA explains about 70% and CSB about 30%, finding that variants at one gene fail to segregate essentially excludes it — which pushes the probability of the other gene toward 100%. 在 Cockayne 综合征里，CSA 解释约 70%、CSB 约 30%，若发现某个基因上的变异不共分离，就基本排除了它——这把另一个基因的概率推向 100%。 For genes with extreme heterogeneity — Bardet-Biedl has more than 27 loci, recessive hearing loss at least 66 — they set a floor: don't push PP4 below +1.0 points, equivalent to a 20% yield, and lean on co-segregation instead. 对于极度异质的基因——Bardet-Biedl 有超过 27 个基因座，隐性听力损失至少 66 个——他们设了一个下限：不要把 PP4 压到 +1.0 点以下（相当于 20% 的检出率），转而依靠共分离。

JORDAN Does recessive versus dominant change the recipe? 隐性和显性会改变这个配方吗？

ALEX Yes, and the paper works both out. 会，而且论文都推导了。 In autosomal-dominant Marfan syndrome, where 91 to 93% of typical cases carry an FBN1 variant, a single parent-to-child transmission with both affected is enough to establish phase — to distinguish which of two variants co-segregates — and after that first informative meiosis, further co-segregations add nothing, because intragenic crossovers are too rare to matter. 在常染色体显性的马凡综合征里，91% 到 93% 的典型病例携带 FBN1 变异，只需一次父母到子女、双方都患病的传递，就足以确定相位（phase）——分辨两个变异中哪个共分离——而在这第一次有信息量的减数分裂之后，再多的共分离也毫无增益，因为基因内交叉太罕见，可以忽略。 But — and this is the nuance — that phenotype specificity only holds for the full Marfan presentation with a high Ghent score, not for an isolated thoracic aortic aneurysm, which is locus-heterogeneous and earns few or no PP4 points. 但——这是微妙之处——那种表型特异性只适用于 Ghent 评分高的完整马凡表现，而不适用于孤立的胸主动脉瘤，后者基因座异质，只能得到很少或零 PP4 点数。 The same logic warns against over-crediting an incompletely phenotyped patient or a young child, who hasn't yet manifested the specific features. 同样的逻辑警告我们不要给表型未完全刻画的患者、或还没表现出特异特征的幼儿过多的记分。 Where two dominant genes exist — tuberous sclerosis, roughly 26% TSC1 and 69% TSC2 — co-segregation becomes genuinely useful again, because now the phenotype does not point to a single locus. 而当存在两个显性基因时——结节性硬化，约 26% 是 TSC1、69% 是 TSC2——共分离才重新变得真正有用，因为此时表型并不指向单一基因座。 The rule of thumb that falls out: the more the phenotype alone fingers one gene, the less co-segregation buys you, and vice versa. 由此得出的经验法则是：表型本身越能锁定单一基因，共分离能给你的增益就越少，反之亦然。

JORDAN So where does this land for us, and what could Runyu actually build? 那这对我们落在哪里，Runyu 又能真正造点什么？
