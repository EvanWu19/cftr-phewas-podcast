Ep1 — 2026-07-12 — Track A (CFTR variant interpretation & harmonization)

Blurb

SpTransformer is the first splicing predictor that answers where in the body. It is a dual-encoder Sinkhorn-Transformer trained on GTEx v8 plus a four-species mammalian organ transcriptome, and for every nucleotide of a ~9 kb pre-mRNA window it emits donor/acceptor probabilities for "all tissues" and for fifteen individual human tissues simultaneously. On held-out splice-site prediction it reports ~85% top-k accuracy and ~91% PR-AUC, ahead of SpliceAI, Pangolin, MMSplice, HAL and MaxEntScan. Scored across 1,273,053 ClinVar SNVs, it predicts that 57–64% of intronic and 56–58% of synonymous pathogenic variants are splice-altering — i.e. most of the pathogenicity of the "silent" classes is splicing in disguise. The payoff experiment: in 95 diabetic-nephropathy patients it predicted kidney-specific splicing from blood WES and validated at 83% accuracy against RNA-seq from the matched kidney biopsies — predict in the accessible tissue, verify in the inaccessible one. For us this lands on the Karen / variant-interpretation priority, and the buildable artifact is cftr-tissue-splice-panel: a per-variant matrix of SpliceAI + SPiP + fifteen SpTransformer tissue ΔSplice z-scores over every public CFTR variant, flagging VUS whose tissue profile disagrees with their clinical classification.

Plain-English glossary

Alternative splicing — the same gene being cut and rejoined in different ways, producing different transcripts in different tissues; >90% of human genes do it.（中文：可变剪接——同一基因在不同组织中以不同方式剪接，产生不同转录本。）

cis-regulatory element (CRE) — a short sequence motif near a splice site that strengthens or weakens its use; read by RNA-binding proteins.（中文：顺式调控元件——剪接位点附近的短序列基序，可增强或削弱该位点的使用。）

Cryptic splice site — a splice site created by a mutation where none existed in the reference sequence.（中文：隐蔽剪接位点——由突变新造出来、参考序列中原本不存在的剪接位点。）

ΔSplice — this paper's variant score: the change in predicted splice-site strength between reference and alternate sequence.（中文：ΔSplice——本文的变异评分，即参考与替代序列之间预测剪接强度的差值。）

Exon / intron — the parts of a gene retained in the mature message vs the parts cut out.（中文：外显子／内含子——基因中保留在成熟 mRNA 中的部分，与被剪掉的部分。）

GTEx — the Genotype-Tissue Expression atlas; bulk RNA-seq across dozens of human tissues (v8: 838 donors, 17,382 samples, 53 tissues). Source of the tissue-usage labels here.（中文：GTEx——人类多组织基因表达图谱，本文组织使用率标签的来源。）

In-silico mutagenesis — mutating each input nucleotide computationally to see how much the model's output moves; a sequence-model attribution method.（中文：计算机模拟诱变——逐个改变输入碱基以观察模型输出变化，属序列模型的归因方法。）

Missense / nonsense / synonymous variant — a substitution that changes an amino acid / creates a premature stop / leaves the protein sequence unchanged.（中文：错义／无义／同义变异——改变氨基酸／产生提前终止密码子／不改变蛋白序列的替换。）

Pre-mRNA — the full-length transcript, still containing introns, before splicing.（中文：前体信使 RNA——剪接之前、仍含内含子的全长转录本。）

RNA-binding protein (RBP) — a protein that binds splicing regulatory motifs; different tissues express different RBPs, which is why splicing is tissue-specific.（中文：RNA 结合蛋白——结合剪接调控基序的蛋白；不同组织表达不同 RBP，这正是剪接具有组织特异性的原因。）

Splice donor / acceptor site — the motifs at the start (5′) and end (3′) of an intron that the spliceosome cuts at.（中文：供体／受体位点——内含子起点与末端的剪接基序。）

Spliceosome — the RNA–protein machine that removes introns and joins exons.（中文：剪接体——切除内含子、连接外显子的 RNA-蛋白复合机器。）

Splice-altering (spliceogenic) variant — a variant that changes which splice sites are used.（中文：致剪接改变的变异——会改变剪接位点使用方式的变异。）

Tissue usage — how often a given splice site is actually used in a given tissue, measured from RNA-seq.（中文：组织使用率——某剪接位点在某组织中实际被使用的频率。）

VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant with insufficient evidence to call pathogenic or benign.（中文：VUS——证据不足、无法判定致病或良性的变异。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • SpTransformer — dual convolutional encoders → Sinkhorn Transformer (sparse attention for long inputs); input = 1,000 nt target + 4,000 nt context each side; output = per-nucleotide donor/acceptor probability for all-tissue + 15 tissues.
  • The two-encoder trick — train one encoder on human GTEx and a second on a cross-species (4 mammals) organ transcriptome with a different label schema, then fuse. Ablation: all four species > partial > none; primates contribute most. Transferable pattern for any setting where you have one rich-but-small labeled set and one broad-but-coarsely-labeled set.
  • ΔSplice — reference-vs-alternate delta of predicted splice strength; the standard variant-scoring wrapper around a per-position sequence model.
  • Tissue z-score against a background reference set — the fairness control. Raw per-tissue deltas reward variants that shift splicing everywhere; z-scoring against a background distribution of common GTEx splice sites is what makes "brain-specific" mean brain-specific. This is the single most stealable idea in the paper.
  • In-silico mutagenesis + MEME motif discovery + ATtRACT — attribution → de novo motifs → matched RBP motifs (e.g. PTBP1 in APBB2); how you show a sequence model learned biology rather than memorized position.
  • Predict-in-accessible / validate-in-inaccessible — blood WES → predicted kidney-specific splicing → validated against RNA-seq of the matched kidney biopsy (83%). A study-design template, not just a result.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority served: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation.

cftr-tissue-splice-panel (difficulty: M) — Input: a VCF of every CFTR variant obtainable from public sources (ClinVar + CFTR2 + gnomAD), HGVS-normalized. Does: scores each variant with SpliceAI, SPiP (ep1, 2026-07-11) and SpTransformer, and emits one row per variant with the two scalar scores plus the fifteen tissue ΔSplice z-scores side by side, and one derived flag — does this variant's tissue profile disagree with its clinical classification? Output: the panel table + a shortlist of VUS with a strong single-tissue splice signal (a hypothesis with an address). Public data only; no registry internals.

What to verify first

  • Which fifteen tissues. Read the supplement. If airway epithelium / pancreas / intestine / sweat gland are absent, the tissue axis is decorative for CFTR — say so and stop. This check gates the whole artifact.
  • Bulk ≠ epithelium. GTEx labels are bulk-tissue averages over every cell type in the biopsy; the authors concede tissue splicing is really the sum over single cells. CF is an epithelial disease — how far can a bulk lung/pancreas label proxy the cell type that matters?
  • Representability of CFTR's signature lesions. The model consumes SNVs. The intron-9 poly-T/TG tract is a length polymorphism, not a substitution — check whether it can be encoded at all. (Same blind spot flagged for SPiP.)
  • Threshold transfer. The ΔSplice > 0.27 cutoff was tuned on a kidney cohort. Any CFTR cutoff must be recalibrated against variants with functional evidence, not inherited.
  • Training-label ceiling. GTEx common splicing events only; no individual-level splicing variability; authors state performance degrades as the number of tissue tasks grows.
  • Deep-intronic reach. Context is ±4,000 nt around the target window — check how a lesion like 3849+10kbC>T is windowed and scored before assuming coverage.

Track A · Variant interpretation

SpliceTransformer predicts tissue-specific splicing linked to human diseases

You N · Nature Communications · 2024

PMC full text PDF DOI PubMed
Read full transcript (29 turns · ~2233 words)

ALEX Today's paper is SpliceTransformer, which the authors shorten to SpTransformer, by Ningyuan You, Chang Liu and colleagues at Zhejiang University, published in Nature Communications in October 2024. 今天这篇论文是 SpliceTransformer，作者简称它为 SpTransformer，由浙江大学的 Ningyuan You、Chang Liu 及其同事完成，2024 年 10 月发表于《Nature Communications》。 It is a deep-learning model that predicts RNA splicing directly from genomic sequence, and the genuinely new part is that it predicts it separately for fifteen different human tissues. 这是一个直接从基因组序列预测 RNA 剪接 (RNA splicing) 的深度学习模型，而真正新颖之处在于，它针对十五种不同的人体组织分别做出预测。 This is Track A, variant interpretation, and we are reading it because it adds an axis our CFTR splice stack does not have yet — not just "does this variant break splicing," but "where in the body does it break splicing." 这是 Track A，即变异解读轨道；我们读它，是因为它给我们的 CFTR 剪接工具栈补上了一个还不存在的维度——不只是"这个变异会不会破坏剪接"，而是"它会在身体的哪个部位破坏剪接"。 The full text is open access, so every number today comes from the paper itself. 全文是开放获取的，所以今天引用的每一个数字都来自论文本身。

JORDAN Give me the biology first, because I want to be precise about what "splicing" even is here. 先给我讲生物学，因为我想弄清楚这里说的"剪接"到底是什么。

ALEX A human gene is not a continuous coding sequence — it is broken into exons, the pieces that end up in the final message, and introns, the pieces that get cut out. 人类基因并不是一段连续的编码序列——它被分成外显子 (exon)，即最终会保留在成熟信使 RNA 中的片段，以及内含子 (intron)，即会被剪掉的片段。 The gene is first transcribed into a full-length copy called pre-mRNA that still contains both, and then a large RNA-protein machine called the spliceosome cuts out the introns and glues the exons together. 基因首先被转录成一份仍然同时包含两者的全长拷贝，称为前体信使 RNA (pre-mRNA)，然后一台被称为剪接体 (spliceosome) 的大型 RNA-蛋白复合机器把内含子切掉，再把外显子拼接起来。 The spliceosome finds the cut points using short sequence motifs: the splice donor site at the start of the intron, the splice acceptor site at its end, plus weaker helper signals scattered nearby that are collectively called cis-regulatory elements. 剪接体依靠一些短的序列基序来找到切割点：内含子起点处的供体位点 (splice donor site)、内含子末端的受体位点 (splice acceptor site)，以及散布在附近、统称为顺式调控元件 (cis-regulatory element) 的较弱辅助信号。 A mutation that damages one of those motifs, or that accidentally creates a brand-new one, is called a splice-altering variant, and a new site created where none existed before is called a cryptic splice site. 一个破坏了这些基序、或者意外制造出一个全新基序的突变，被称为致剪接改变的变异；而在原本不存在的地方新造出来的位点，被称为隐蔽剪接位点 (cryptic splice site)。

JORDAN And "alternative splicing" is when the same gene gets cut different ways in different places? 那"可变剪接"就是同一个基因在不同地方被以不同方式剪切？

ALEX Exactly, and that is the whole hinge of this paper. 正是如此，而这恰恰是这篇论文的全部支点。 More than ninety percent of human genes are alternatively spliced, and which version gets made depends on the tissue — the same exon may be included in brain and skipped in liver. 超过百分之九十的人类基因存在可变剪接 (alternative splicing)，而具体产生哪个版本取决于组织——同一个外显子可能在脑中被保留，在肝中被跳过。 The reason is that the helper motifs are read by RNA-binding proteins, and different tissues express different RNA-binding proteins, so the same DNA letter is interpreted differently depending on which cell is reading it. 原因在于，那些辅助基序是由 RNA 结合蛋白 (RNA-binding protein) 读取的，而不同组织表达不同的 RNA 结合蛋白，因此同一个 DNA 字母会因为读取它的细胞不同而被解读成不同的含义。 One more term you will need: tissue usage, which is just how often a given splice site is actually used in a given tissue, measured from RNA sequencing. 还有一个术语你会用到：组织使用率 (tissue usage)，它指的就是某个剪接位点在某个组织中实际被使用的频率，通过 RNA 测序来测量。

JORDAN So what was actually broken before this paper? 那在这篇论文之前，究竟是什么东西没解决？

ALEX The field had good splicing predictors — MaxEntScan from the motif era, then MMSplice, then SpliceAI, which is the one everybody actually uses, and Pangolin, which extended SpliceAI to four tissues. 这个领域已经有不错的剪接预测器——基序时代的 MaxEntScan，之后是 MMSplice，再之后是 SpliceAI（这是大家实际都在用的那个），以及把 SpliceAI 扩展到四种组织的 Pangolin。 But almost all of them answer a tissue-blind question: they give you one number per variant, as if the human body were a single organ. 但它们几乎全都在回答一个"组织盲"的问题：每个变异只给你一个数字，仿佛人体只是一个器官。 That is a strange thing to accept, because the clinical question is never tissue-blind. 这其实是个很奇怪的前提，因为临床问题从来都不是组织无关的。 If a variant creates a cryptic splice site that is only used in brain, the patient gets a neurological disease, not a liver disease — and if you only look at the patient's blood, which is the tissue you can actually get, you will see nothing at all. 如果某个变异制造出一个只在脑中被使用的隐蔽剪接位点，病人得的就是神经系统疾病，而不是肝病——而如果你只看病人的血液，也就是你唯一真正能取到的组织，你将什么也看不到。

JORDAN That last point is the one that hurts. 最后这一点才是真正扎心的。 The tissue you can biopsy is almost never the tissue that is sick. 你能活检的组织，几乎从来都不是生病的那个组织。

ALEX Right, and that inaccessibility is exactly the hole the authors are trying to fill computationally. 没错，而这种"取不到"正是作者试图用计算手段去填补的空洞。 The ideal experiment is to sequence the RNA of the diseased tissue and just look at what got spliced wrong. 理想实验是对病变组织做 RNA 测序，直接看剪接出了什么错。 You cannot biopsy a living brain for a diagnostic workup, so the ambition is to predict from DNA sequence alone what the RNA in that inaccessible tissue would have looked like. 你不可能为了做诊断而活检一个活人的大脑，所以这里的野心是：仅凭 DNA 序列，预测那个取不到的组织里的 RNA 本来会是什么样子。 The second motivation is variant interpretation volume — as sequencing moves from panels to whole genomes, most of what you find is noncoding, and you have no idea what any of it does. 第二个动机是变异解读的体量——随着测序从基因套餐走向全基因组，你发现的大部分变异都是非编码的，而你完全不知道它们在做什么。

JORDAN So what did they build? 那他们究竟造了什么？

ALEX The model takes a raw pre-mRNA sequence, one-hot encoded, and for every single nucleotide position it outputs the probability that this position is a splice donor, a splice acceptor, or neither — and it does this simultaneously for "all tissues" and for each of fifteen human tissues. 这个模型接收原始的 pre-mRNA 序列，做独热编码 (one-hot encoding)，然后对每一个核苷酸位置输出它是供体位点、受体位点、或都不是的概率——而且它同时为"总体组织"和十五种人体组织中的每一种做出这一预测。 The input is a target window of one thousand nucleotides padded on each side by four thousand nucleotides of context, so roughly nine kilobases of sequence per forward pass. 输入是一个一千个核苷酸的目标窗口，两侧各补上四千个核苷酸的上下文，因此每次前向传播大约看九千个碱基的序列。 Architecturally it is two convolutional encoders feeding a Sinkhorn Transformer — a sparse-attention variant chosen because full attention over nine kilobases is computationally unacceptable. 在架构上，它是两个卷积编码器再接一个 Sinkhorn Transformer——之所以选择这种稀疏注意力变体，是因为在九千个碱基上做完整注意力计算是不可接受的。

JORDAN Why two encoders rather than one? 为什么是两个编码器而不是一个？

ALEX Because they had two datasets that do not have the same shape. 因为他们手上有两个形状并不一致的数据集。 One encoder is trained on GTEx version 8, which is the human tissue RNA atlas — eight hundred thirty-eight donors, seventeen thousand three hundred eighty-two samples across fifty-three tissues. 一个编码器在 GTEx 第 8 版上训练，那是人类组织 RNA 图谱——八百三十八位供体，覆盖五十三种组织的一万七千三百八十二个样本。 The other is trained on a cross-species mammalian organ transcriptome covering four species, which only carries labels for four organs — brain, heart, liver, kidney. 另一个则在一个跨物种的哺乳动物器官转录组上训练，覆盖四个物种，但只带有四个器官的标签——脑、心、肝、肾。 The bet is that splicing grammar is conserved across mammals, so the evolutionary data teaches the model general sequence rules that the human data alone would not supply, and the ablation supports it: models trained with all four extra species beat the partial and the no-extra-data versions, with the primate dataset contributing the most. 他们押注的是剪接的"语法"在哺乳动物之间是保守的，因此进化数据能教会模型一些单靠人类数据学不到的通用序列规则；消融实验支持了这一点：用全部四个额外物种训练的模型胜过只用部分或完全不用额外数据的版本，其中灵长类数据集贡献最大。

JORDAN Push back on something for me. 帮我挑一个刺。

ALEX Here is my design objection, and it is about the labels, not the architecture. 我的设计异议在标签上，而不在架构上。 The tissue-usage labels come from GTEx bulk RNA sequencing, which means the "tissue" is a blended average over every cell type in that biopsy. 组织使用率的标签来自 GTEx 的批量 RNA 测序 (bulk RNA-seq)，这意味着所谓"组织"是那份活检样本中所有细胞类型的一个混合平均值。 The authors concede this in the discussion — real tissue-specific splicing is the sum of the splicing events of all the single cells in that tissue, and they say so plainly. 作者在讨论部分承认了这一点——真正的组织特异性剪接是该组织中所有单细胞剪接事件的总和，他们坦率地说明了这一点。 They also concede that GTEx gives them only common splicing events, so individual-level splicing variability is invisible to the training labels, and that performance degrades as the number of tissue tasks grows. 他们还承认，GTEx 只提供常见的剪接事件，因此个体层面的剪接变异性对训练标签是不可见的，并且随着组织任务数量增加，性能会下降。 Those three concessions all point the same direction: the model is confident about the average behavior of a mixed tissue, which is not the same object as the behavior of one epithelial cell type. 这三点让步都指向同一个方向：模型对一个混合组织的平均行为很有把握，而这与某一种上皮细胞类型的行为并不是同一个对象。

JORDAN Noted. 记住了。 Does it actually beat SpliceAI? 那它真的胜过 SpliceAI 吗？

ALEX On the held-out splice-site prediction task it reports roughly eighty-five percent top-k accuracy and roughly ninety-one percent area under the precision-recall curve, ahead of SpliceAI, Pangolin, MMSplice, HAL and MaxEntScan. 在留出集的剪接位点预测任务上，它报告了大约百分之八十五的 top-k 准确率和大约百分之九十一的精确率-召回率曲线下面积，领先于 SpliceAI、Pangolin、MMSplice、HAL 和 MaxEntScan。 On tissue usage — distinguishing sites with low versus high usage in a given tissue — it has a clear margin over everything else, which is unsurprising since almost nothing else is trying. 在组织使用率上——区分某个组织中低使用率与高使用率的位点——它对其他所有方法都有明显优势，这并不令人意外，因为几乎没有别的工具在做这件事。 Then they define a variant score, ΔSplice, as the change in predicted splice strength between reference and alternate sequence, and run it over one million two hundred seventy-three thousand and fifty-three single-nucleotide variants in ClinVar. 接着他们定义了一个变异评分 ΔSplice，即参考序列与替代序列之间预测剪接强度的变化，并把它跑遍了 ClinVar（公共变异-临床意义数据库）中的一百二十七万三千零五十三个单核苷酸变异。

JORDAN And what falls out of a million variants? 那一百多万个变异跑下来，掉出了什么？

ALEX The headline: between fifty-seven and sixty-four percent of intronic pathogenic and likely-pathogenic variants, and fifty-six to fifty-eight percent of pathogenic synonymous variants, are predicted to be splice-altering. 头条结论是：内含子区致病与可能致病变异中，有百分之五十七到六十四；同义变异中致病的有百分之五十六到五十八——被预测为会改变剪接。 That is the sixty-percent number in the abstract, and it is a claim that most of the pathogenicity of the "silent" and "just intronic" classes is actually splicing in disguise. 这就是摘要里那个百分之六十的数字，它主张的是："沉默"变异和"只是内含子里"这两类变异的致病性，大部分其实是伪装起来的剪接效应。 Missense and nonsense variants, by contrast, show minimal ΔSplice, which is a nice internal sanity check — the model is not just relabeling everything pathogenic as splice-altering. 相比之下，错义变异 (missense variant) 与无义变异 (nonsense variant) 的 ΔSplice 极小，这是一个很漂亮的内部合理性检查——模型并没有把所有致病变异都重新贴上"致剪接"的标签。 Two validated examples: a variant in FGB where the model finds a cryptic site twenty-four nucleotides upstream of the mutation, and a TP53 variant where the cryptic site sits two hundred nucleotides upstream — both previously confirmed by RT-PCR. 两个已验证的例子：FGB 基因中的一个变异，模型在突变上游二十四个核苷酸处找到一个隐蔽位点；以及 TP53 中的一个变异，隐蔽位点位于上游两百个核苷酸处——两者此前都已通过 RT-PCR 实验确认。

JORDAN Two hundred nucleotides away from the mutation. 距离突变两百个核苷酸。 That is not something a motif scanner would ever catch. 这是任何基序扫描器都绝无可能捕捉到的。

ALEX That is the argument for the long context window, and the in-silico mutagenesis backs it up — in the GLA gene the model puts real weight on positions three hundred, four hundred and nine hundred nucleotides downstream of an exon. 这正是长上下文窗口的论据，而计算机模拟诱变 (in-silico mutagenesis) 也支持这一点——在 GLA 基因中，模型对某个外显子下游三百、四百和九百个核苷酸处的位置赋予了实质性的权重。 Then the tissue results: for each of the fifteen tissues they compute a z-score against a background distribution built from common GTEx splice sites, so a variant that shifts splicing everywhere does not outrank a variant that shifts it in exactly one place. 接着是组织层面的结果：对十五种组织中的每一种，他们相对于由 GTEx 常见剪接位点构建的背景分布计算一个 z 分数，这样一来，在所有组织中都改变剪接的变异，就不会盖过只在某一个组织中改变剪接的变异。 TTN, the giant sarcomere gene, picks up over three hundred tissue-specific splicing alterations in heart and muscle and almost none elsewhere. TTN 这个巨大的肌节基因，在心脏和肌肉中获得了三百多个组织特异性剪接改变，而在其他组织中几乎没有。 DMD variants around exon fifty-one come out brain-specific, matching two known brain-specific dystrophin isoforms. DMD 基因中围绕第五十一号外显子的变异被判定为脑特异性，这与两个已知的脑特异性 dystrophin 蛋白亚型相吻合。 That is the model recovering tissue biology it was never explicitly told. 这是模型自行还原出了没有人明确教给它的组织生物学。

JORDAN And the clinical test at the end? 那最后那个临床验证呢？
