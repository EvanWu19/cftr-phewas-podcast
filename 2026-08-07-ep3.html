Track C — Is the SPLUNC1-Orai1 axis a critical determinant of lung health?

Sourcing: full text retrieved successfully via the PubMed MCP full-text tool (PMC12312389).

Blurb

Rachel Tarran's 2025 review in Biochemical Society Transactions synthesizes her lab's work on a mechanistic partnership behind an already-known CF and asthma gene modifier: SPLUNC1 (official gene name BPIFA1), a small secreted airway protein whose C-terminal alpha-6 helix binds and inhibits the calcium channel Orai1, the central executor of store-operated calcium entry (SOCE). Low sputum SPLUNC1 correlates with more frequent CF exacerbations and worse lung function across several diseases (asthma, non-CF bronchiectasis, IPF, pulmonary arterial hypertension), and STIM1-Orai1 co-localization — a proxy for Orai1 activation — is elevated in CF and asthma lung tissue. Tarran's group engineered a protease-resistant SPLUNC1-derived peptidomimetic, ELD607, which reduced neutrophilia, inflammatory cytokines, and improved survival in a mouse bacterial-pneumonia/ARDS model, acting through macrophage biology rather than direct antimicrobial killing. Everything here, though, is protein- and expression-level evidence — not genetic-variant evidence — which is exactly the gap the episode's buildable artifact is built to check. Buildable artifact: bpifa1-orai1-locus-scanner.

Plain-English glossary

  • Gene modifier — a gene separate from CFTR that doesn't cause CF by itself but shifts how severe the disease turns out to be once present.（中文:一个独立于CFTR的基因,本身不致病,但会影响已患病个体的疾病严重程度。）
  • SPLUNC1 / BPIFA1 — Short Palate Lung and Nasal epithelial Clone 1, also called Bacterial Permeability Increasing protein Family member A1; a small secreted airway protein and established CF/asthma gene modifier.（中文:一种小型分泌性气道蛋白,是已确立的CF/哮喘基因修饰因子,有两个曾用名。）
  • Orai1 / SOCE (store-operated calcium entry) — Orai1 is a plasma-membrane calcium channel; SOCE is the signaling pathway where ER calcium depletion (sensed by STIM1) activates Orai1 to trigger a second, larger calcium influx that turns on inflammatory transcription factors.（中文:Orai1是细胞膜上的钙离子通道;SOCE是内质网钙耗竭(由STIM1感知)激活Orai1、触发第二波更大钙内流并激活炎症转录因子的信号通路。）
  • NEDD4.2 — a ubiquitin ligase that tags Orai1 for internalization and degradation after SPLUNC1 binding induces a conformational change.（中文:一种泛素连接酶,在SPLUNC1结合诱导构象变化后,给Orai1打上标签使其被内化降解。）
  • eQTL (expression quantitative trait locus) — a genetic variant associated with how much a nearby gene is expressed; used to link a GWAS signal to a specific gene.（中文:一种与附近基因表达量相关的遗传变异位点,用于把GWAS信号与具体基因联系起来。）
  • ARDS (acute respiratory distress syndrome) — a severe, life-threatening lung inflammation syndrome, the model system used to test ELD607 in mice.（中文:一种严重的、危及生命的肺部炎症综合征,是小鼠实验中用于测试ELD607的模型系统。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Cross-disease evidence synthesis — sputum protein measurement + single-cell RNA-seq atlas mining + super-resolution microscopy (STIM1-Orai1 co-localization) across five lung diseases.
  • Protease-resistant peptidomimetic engineering — ELD607, redesigned from SPLUNC1's alpha-6 region to resist neutrophil-elastase degradation, 14x more potent and ~30% smaller (better aerosol delivery) than the native peptide.

  • ELD607 therapeutic data is mouse-only; inhaled-peptide delivery/stability has its own translational hurdles not fully addressed in the review.
  • STIM1-Orai1 co-localization is a protein-activity readout, not a genetic-variant signal — don't assume BPIFA1/ORAI1 will show up in a CF GWAS without checking.

Gap-map / catalog

  • CLOSES: identifies a specific, previously uncatalogued mechanistic partner (Orai1/ORAI1) for the established modifier SPLUNC1/BPIFA1; synthesizes cross-disease (asthma/CF/COPD/IPF/PAH) evidence and an in-development therapeutic (ELD607).
  • LEAVES OPEN: whether BPIFA1 or ORAI1 carry independent genetic-association evidence at the summary-statistics level; what down-regulates SPLUNC1 in diseases beyond the IL-13/asthma axis; independent replication of the core binding/degradation mechanism outside Tarran's own lab.

JORDAN First, some vocabulary, because this protein has had an identity crisis. 先讲点词汇,因为这个蛋白质的身份一直挺混乱。

ALEX It really has. 确实如此。SPLUNC1 stands for "short palate lung and nasal epithelial clone 1" — it was first named for where it's expressed, then renamed BPIFA1, for "bacterial permeability increasing protein family member 1," based on a structural resemblance to an antimicrobial protein family. SPLUNC1的全称是"short palate lung and nasal epithelial clone 1"(短腭肺鼻上皮克隆1号)——最初是根据它的表达部位命名的,后来又因为在结构上与一类抗菌蛋白家族相似,而被改名为BPIFA1,即"bacterial permeability increasing protein family member 1"(细菌通透性增加蛋白家族成员1)。The review sticks with SPLUNC1, and so will we. 这篇综述沿用了SPLUNC1这个名字,我们也照做。It's a small, 27-kilodalton secreted protein with a disordered region that binds a sodium channel, and a folded region built from six alpha helices and two beta sheets — one of those helices, called alpha-6, is the one that matters most for today's story, because it binds and inhibits our other main character, Orai1. 它是一种小型的、27千道尔顿的分泌蛋白,有一段无序区域能结合一种钠离子通道,还有一段由六个α螺旋和两个β折叠构成的折叠区域——其中一个叫α6的螺旋对今天的故事最重要,因为它能结合并抑制我们今天的另一位主角——Orai1。

JORDAN And Orai1? Orai1又是什么?

ALEX Orai1 is a calcium channel sitting in the plasma membrane, and it's the main executor of a signaling pathway called store-operated calcium entry, or SOCE. Orai1是位于细胞膜上的一种钙离子通道,是一条名为"库存操纵性钙内流"(store-operated calcium entry,SOCE,库存操纵性钙内流的英文缩写)信号通路的主要执行者。Here's the short version: a receptor on the cell surface gets activated, that triggers calcium to be released from an internal storage compartment called the endoplasmic reticulum, a sensor protein called STIM1 detects that drop and relocates to the cell membrane, and STIM1 then activates Orai1 to let a second, larger wave of calcium flood in from outside the cell. 简单版本是这样的:细胞表面的一个受体被激活,触发钙离子从一个叫内质网(endoplasmic reticulum)的内部储存腔室中释放出来,一种叫STIM1的传感蛋白探测到这个下降,并转移到细胞膜上,然后STIM1激活Orai1,让第二波、更大规模的钙离子从细胞外涌入。That calcium wave then switches on inflammatory transcription factors and drives things like mucus secretion and cytokine release. 这波钙离子内流随后会激活炎症相关的转录因子,并驱动诸如黏液分泌和细胞因子释放等过程。

ALEX So here's the setup. 背景是这样的。SPLUNC1 has been known for a while as a gene modifier in both asthma and cystic fibrosis — its sputum protein levels inversely correlate with how often CF patients have exacerbations, and it's similarly reduced in non-CF bronchiectasis and in asthma with high IL-13 signaling. SPLUNC1早已被认为是哮喘和囊性纤维化(CF)共同的一个基因修饰因子(gene modifier)——它在痰液中的蛋白水平与CF患者急性加重的频率呈负相关,在非CF支气管扩张症以及IL-13信号活跃的哮喘患者中也同样有所降低。

JORDAN Quick refresher on what "gene modifier" means in this context, for anyone catching up. 快速回顾一下"基因修饰因子"在这里是什么意思,方便跟上进度的听众。

ALEX In CF, the primary disease gene is always CFTR — every case starts with two disease-causing CFTR alleles. 在CF中,主要致病基因永远是CFTR——每一个病例都始于两个致病的CFTR等位基因。A gene modifier is a separate gene, elsewhere in the genome, that doesn't cause CF by itself but shifts how severe the disease turns out to be once you already have it — worse or better lung function, more or fewer exacerbations, faster or slower disease progression. 基因修饰因子则是基因组中另一个独立的基因,它本身不会导致CF,但会在你已经患病的前提下,影响疾病表现出来的严重程度——肺功能更差或更好,急性加重更多或更少,病情进展更快或更慢。SPLUNC1 has been in that modifier category for a while; what's new here is the mechanistic partner. SPLUNC1在这个修饰因子类别里已经有一段时间了;这里的新内容是它的机制伙伴。 But knowing SPLUNC1 correlates with disease severity doesn't tell you the mechanism, and Tarran's own lab had previously discovered that SPLUNC1 binds and inhibits Orai1, so the open question motivating this review is whether that specific interaction, the SPLUNC1-Orai1 axis, is what actually links low SPLUNC1 to worse lung inflammation across multiple diseases, not just CF. 但知道SPLUNC1与疾病严重程度相关,并不能告诉你机制——而Tarran自己的实验室此前已经发现SPLUNC1能结合并抑制Orai1,所以这篇综述想要回答的核心问题是:这个具体的相互作用,也就是SPLUNC1-Orai1轴,是否正是把低SPLUNC1水平和多种疾病(不只是CF)中更严重的肺部炎症联系起来的真正原因。

JORDAN Why does the mechanism matter if the correlation's already known? 如果相关性已经知道了,为什么机制还这么重要?

ALEX Because mechanism is what tells you whether you should activate the pathway or shut it down, and this review shows the field genuinely disagrees on that point. 因为机制决定了你到底应该去激活这条通路,还是去关闭它——而这篇综述表明,这个领域在这一点上确实存在分歧。One camp wants to activate SOCE in CF, because it can turn on an alternative chloride channel, TMEM16A, that doesn't depend on CFTR at all — appealing since it could hydrate CF airway mucus through a completely CFTR-independent route. 一派希望在CF中激活SOCE,因为它能开启一个完全不依赖CFTR的替代性氯离子通道TMEM16A——这很有吸引力,因为它可以通过一条完全绕开CFTR的途径,给CF气道黏液补水。The other camp wants to inhibit Orai1, because excess SOCE also drives mucin secretion, cytokine release, and the transcription factors that entrench chronic inflammation. 另一派则希望抑制Orai1,因为过量的SOCE同样会驱动黏蛋白分泌、细胞因子释放,以及那些会使慢性炎症根深蒂固的转录因子。You can't resolve that tension without understanding exactly how SPLUNC1 sits in this pathway. 不搞清楚SPLUNC1在这条通路里到底处于什么位置,就无法化解这种张力。

JORDAN This is a review, so what's actually "built" here? 这是一篇综述,那这里到底"造"了什么东西?

ALEX Two things worth flagging. 有两件事值得一提。First, the review synthesizes evidence across five different lung diseases — asthma, CF, COPD, idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, and pulmonary arterial hypertension — pulling together sputum protein measurements, single-cell RNA sequencing atlases, and Orai1 expression and STIM1-Orai1 co-localization data from human lung tissue and mouse models, into one working model: SPLUNC1's alpha-6 helix binds Orai1, triggers a conformational change that lets the ubiquitin ligase NEDD4.2 tag Orai1 for internalization and degradation, and that's the brake that keeps SOCE in check. 第一,这篇综述整合了来自五种不同肺部疾病——哮喘、CF、慢性阻塞性肺病(COPD)、特发性肺纤维化(IPF)和肺动脉高压(PAH)——的证据,把痰液蛋白测量、单细胞RNA测序(scRNAseq,单细胞RNA测序的英文缩写)图谱,以及来自人体肺组织和小鼠模型的Orai1表达和STIM1-Orai1共定位数据,汇总成了一个统一的工作模型:SPLUNC1的α6螺旋结合Orai1,触发一次构象变化,让泛素连接酶NEDD4.2给Orai1打上标签,使其被内化并降解——这就是抑制SOCE的"刹车"。Second, and this is the closer-to-tool-development piece, Tarran's group engineered a protease-resistant peptidomimetic of that alpha-6 region, called ELD607, deliberately redesigned because native SPLUNC1 and the native alpha-6 peptide both get chewed up by neutrophil elastase, the same protease that's abundant in CF sputum. 第二点更接近"工具开发"的部分是,Tarran团队设计了一种对蛋白酶有抗性的α6区域拟肽(peptidomimetic),叫ELD607——之所以刻意重新设计,是因为天然的SPLUNC1和天然的α6肽段都会被中性粒细胞弹性蛋白酶(neutrophil elastase)降解,而这种蛋白酶在CF痰液中含量丰富。

ALEX Here's my pushback, though. 不过这是我的质疑。This review draws heavily — not exclusively, but heavily — on Tarran's own lab's prior papers for the central mechanistic claims: the SPLUNC1-Orai1 binding, the NEDD4.2 degradation pathway, the ELD607 mouse data. 这篇综述在核心机制论断上——不是完全,但很大程度上——依赖于Tarran自己实验室此前发表的论文:SPLUNC1-Orai1的结合、NEDD4.2降解通路、ELD607的小鼠数据。That's not a flaw in the biology, but it does mean we're reading one lab's coherent model of a pathway, not yet a field-wide consensus independently replicated by other groups — worth keeping in mind before treating any of this as settled. 这并不是生物学本身的缺陷,但确实意味着我们读到的是一个实验室对某条通路自洽的模型,而不是已经被其他团队独立重复验证过的、整个领域的共识——在把这些内容当作定论之前,这一点值得留意。

JORDAN What are the actual numbers behind this model? 支撑这个模型的具体数据是什么?

ALEX On the human-tissue side, using super-resolution microscopy at around 35-nanometer resolution on paraffin-embedded lungs, the group found that STIM1-Orai1 co-localization — their proxy for how much Orai1 is actually being activated — was significantly increased in both asthma and CF lungs compared with normal controls, concentrated in immune cells and submucosal glands rather than the airway epithelium itself, and unchanged in the alveolar regions, consistent with neither disease being primarily an alveolar process. 在人体组织方面,研究团队用大约35纳米分辨率的超分辨率显微镜观察石蜡包埋的肺组织,发现STIM1-Orai1共定位——他们用来衡量Orai1实际被激活程度的替代指标——在哮喘和CF肺组织中都显著高于正常对照,主要集中在免疫细胞和黏膜下腺体,而不是气道上皮本身,肺泡区域则没有变化,这与这两种疾病都不是以肺泡为主的病变过程相符。On the therapeutic side, in a mouse bacterial pneumonia and ARDS model, inhaled ELD607 wasn't itself antimicrobial — it wasn't killing bacteria directly — but it reduced neutrophil counts and neutrophil elastase levels in the lung, reduced markers of lung tissue damage, cut down a panel of inflammatory cytokines including IL-6, TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, and IL-17, and improved both blood oxygen levels and survival. 在治疗方面,在一个细菌性肺炎和急性呼吸窘迫综合征(ARDS,急性呼吸窘迫综合征的英文缩写)的小鼠模型中,吸入型ELD607本身没有抗菌作用——它不直接杀死细菌——但它降低了肺部中性粒细胞计数和中性粒细胞弹性蛋白酶水平,减少了肺组织损伤标志物,削减了包括IL-6、TNF-α、IL-1β和IL-17在内的一系列炎症细胞因子,并改善了血氧水平和生存率。Depleting alveolar macrophages, but not depleting neutrophils, abolished ELD607's protective effect, meaning its benefit runs through macrophage biology, not through directly blocking neutrophils. 清除肺泡巨噬细胞——而非清除中性粒细胞——会消除ELD607的保护作用,这说明它的益处是通过巨噬细胞生物学发挥的,而不是直接阻断中性粒细胞。

JORDAN And beyond CF and asthma, what did the review find in the other diseases? 除了CF和哮喘之外,这篇综述在其他疾病里发现了什么?

ALEX A mixed and honestly reported picture, which I appreciated. 一幅复杂但坦诚呈现的图景,这一点我很欣赏。In idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, data-mining of a public IPF cell atlas suggested SPLUNC1 message was reduced while Orai1 was increased in fibroblasts and myofibroblasts, the cells driving the scarring — but the review is explicit that SPLUNC1's role in IPF specifically has not been directly studied yet, this is an inference from atlas data, not a dedicated experiment. 在特发性肺纤维化中,对一个公开IPF细胞图谱的数据挖掘提示,在驱动瘢痕形成的成纤维细胞和肌成纤维细胞中,SPLUNC1的信使RNA水平降低,而Orai1水平升高——但综述明确指出,SPLUNC1在IPF中的具体作用尚未被直接研究过,这只是基于图谱数据的推断,不是专门设计的实验。In pulmonary arterial hypertension, a severe disease with up to 55% mortality at three years, SPLUNC1 was significantly down in multiple patient omics datasets and confirmed by direct measurement in a rat model, alongside known Orai1 upregulation in the diseased vasculature. 在肺动脉高压中——这是一种三年死亡率高达55%的重症——SPLUNC1在多个患者组学数据集中显著降低,并在大鼠模型中通过直接测量得到证实,同时已知Orai1在病变血管中表达上调。And in COPD, the picture actually conflicts across two cited studies — one found no change in SPLUNC1, another found it up-regulated at the message level — and the review flags smoking status as a likely confounder the field hasn't adequately controlled for, since combustible cigarette smoke is separately known to bind and inactivate SPLUNC1 protein directly. 而在COPD中,两项被引用的研究实际上相互矛盾——一项发现SPLUNC1没有变化,另一项发现它在信使水平上调——综述指出吸烟状态可能是这个领域尚未充分控制的一个混杂因素,因为已知可燃卷烟烟雾会直接结合并使SPLUNC1蛋白失活。

SPLUNC1(其官方基因名为BPIFA1)和Orai1(基因名ORAI1)都是值得拿去和真实CF遗传关联数据核对的候选基因——因为这篇综述里的一切证据都停留在蛋白质水平和表达水平,而不是遗传变异层面的证据。
