How did this episode land for you?

The METRIC-framework for assessing data quality for trustworthy AI in medicine: a systematic review

A PRISMA-guided systematic review (5,408 candidate studies across Web of Science, PubMed, and ACM Digital Library, narrowed to 120 eligible) that synthesizes three previously separate threads of data-quality literature — general data quality frameworks, big-data frameworks, and ML-specific empirical studies — into a single specialized vocabulary for medical AI training data: the METRIC-framework. From 461 distinct extracted terms (991 mentions), the authors hierarchically cluster down to 38 dimensions and subdimensions, presented as 15 headline "awareness dimensions" under 5 top-level clusters (Measurement process, Timeliness, Representativeness, Informativeness, Consistency). The framework is explicitly a taxonomy, not a measurement tool — the authors state directly that it doesn't yet include a guideline for assessing or measuring any dimension, and it's meant to be applied against a specific, known ML use case rather than judged in a vacuum. For the registry-QC track, this is a pipeline-stage (e) paper: vocabulary and framework, not a change to candidate generation, adjudication, review budget, or evaluation directly. The buildable artifact is a one-page audit scoring an existing anthropometric flagging method against all 15 dimensions, to make explicit which kinds of data quality problems it does and doesn't address.

Plain-English glossary

ALCOA(+) — a set of data-integrity principles (attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original, accurate, plus later additions) from good-clinical/laboratory/manufacturing-practice guidelines, distinct from "data quality."（中文:一套数据完整性准则[可追溯、清晰易读、同步记录、原始、准确,及后续新增项],源自良好临床/实验室/生产规范指南,不同于"数据质量"概念。）

Data integrity — maintaining the accuracy and consistency of a dataset across its entire lifecycle (was it recorded correctly, has it stayed unaltered).（中文:在数据集整个生命周期中保持准确性和一致性[记录是否正确、是否保持未被篡改]。）

Data quality (fitness for use) — whether data, even if fully intact, is actually suited to a specific intended purpose; the framing this paper's whole taxonomy is built around.（中文:数据即便完全未被篡改,是否真正适合某个具体预期目的;本文整个分类体系都是围绕这一框定构建的。）

Informative missingness — whether the fact that a value is missing itself carries predictive signal, distinct from missingness as a simple completeness problem.（中文:某个值缺失这一事实本身是否携带预测信号,不同于将缺失单纯视为完整性问题。）

Kahn framework — an earlier, widely cited EHR data-quality reference framework (completeness, plausibility, conformance) that METRIC builds on top of rather than replaces.（中文:一个较早、被广泛引用的EHR数据质量参考框架[完整性、合理性、一致性],METRIC框架是在其基础上继续构建,而非取而代之。）

PRISMA — the standard reporting checklist governing how a systematic review searches, screens, and reports its literature corpus.（中文:系统综述在检索、筛选和报告文献语料库时所遵循的标准报告清单。）

Target class balance — whether the sizes of outcome classes in a dataset are adequate for machine-learning use, one of the 15 METRIC dimensions.（中文:数据集中结局类别的样本量是否足以支撑机器学习使用,是METRIC框架15个维度之一。）
