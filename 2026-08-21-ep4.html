SITAR — a useful instrument for growth curve analysis

Cole TJ, Donaldson MD, Ben-Shlomo Y. International Journal of Epidemiology, 2010.

The foundational SITAR (SuperImposition by Translation And Rotation) paper: a shape-invariant mixed model that summarizes an individual's growth curve with three random-effect parameters — size (vertical shift), tempo (horizontal/timing shift), and velocity (age-scale stretch/shrink) — layered on a single shared natural-cubic-spline mean curve, fit via nlme in R. Tested on Christ's Hospital School boys (N=3245, n=129,508 heights, ages 9–19) and girls with Turner syndrome in a randomized oxandrolone trial (N=105, n=1321). The three-parameter adjustment reduced residual standard deviation from 6.6 cm to 0.8 cm in the schoolboy cohort (98.9% variance reduction), matching individually-fit Preece-Baines curve performance; a subsequent fixed-residual trim (>2.4 cm absolute) removed 952 points (0.74% of the total) as recording-error outliers. Growth-outcome findings: pubertal tempo correlated with IGF-1 measured 50 years later (P=0.01, N=1009); oxandrolone increased growth velocity by 23% versus placebo (P=10⁻⁸). It lands as a candidate-generation reference — the model-fit-then-fixed-residual-threshold design structurally resembles a spline mixed-effects flagging approach — and, more durably, as reusable vocabulary: size/tempo/velocity is the direct foundation for later trajectory-matching methods already in this queue.

Plain-English glossary

  • age at peak height velocity (APHV) — the age at which a child's height-gain rate (velocity) peaks during the pubertal growth spurt.（中文：儿童在青春期生长突增期间，身高增长速率（速度）达到峰值时的年龄。）
  • Preece-Baines curve — a five-parameter parametric curve fit individually to each child's pubertal height data, used here as the individually-fit gold-standard comparator to SITAR's shared-curve approach.（中文：一种五参数的参数曲线，对每个儿童的青春期身高数据分别单独拟合，本文将其作为逐人拟合的金标准，用来与 SITAR 的共享曲线方法作比较。）
  • residual standard deviation (RSD) — the spread of the differences between observed and model-predicted height values; used throughout as the primary fit-quality/error metric.（中文：观测身高值与模型预测身高值之间差异的离散程度；本文全篇用作主要的拟合质量/误差指标。）
  • shape-invariant model — a model assuming every individual's growth curve has the same underlying shape, differing only in how that shared shape is shifted and scaled to fit them — as opposed to allowing genuinely different curve shapes per individual.（中文：一种假设每个人的生长曲线具有相同底层形状的模型，个体间的差异仅在于该共享形状如何被平移和缩放以适配自己——区别于允许每个人拥有真正不同曲线形状的模型。）
  • SITAR (SuperImposition by Translation And Rotation) — the shape-invariant mixed model this paper introduces: one shared mean curve plus three per-subject random effects (size, tempo, velocity).（中文：本文提出的形状不变混合模型：一条共享的平均曲线，加上三个个体特异的随机效应（大小、节奏、速度）。）
  • Turner syndrome — a chromosomal disorder (a missing or malformed second X chromosome) causing short stature and primary ovarian failure; one of this paper's two test cohorts.（中文：一种染色体疾病（第二条 X 染色体缺失或畸变），导致身材矮小和原发性卵巢功能衰竭；本文的两个检验队列之一。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • SITAR model (R sitar package / nlme) — shape-invariant mixed model summarizing individual growth curves via size/tempo/velocity random effects on a shared spline mean curve.
  • Fixed-residual-threshold outlier trim — a post-model-fit rule flagging any point with absolute residual beyond a calibrated cutoff as a likely recording error.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Liz / CFFPR data quality (method only).

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation — the fixed-residual-threshold-after-model-fit design structurally resembles a spline mixed-effects flagging approach; also (e) vocabulary/framework — size/tempo/velocity is a reusable three-parameter growth-deviation description, foundational to later trajectory-matching methods already in this queue.

Buildable: sitar-residual-trim-prototype (S/M) — fit a SITAR model (R's sitar package) to synthetic pediatric height data with injected outlier/error points, apply a residual threshold recalibrated to the synthetic data's own residual standard deviation (not this paper's 2.4 cm figure), and compare flagged points against a conditional-studentized-residual approach from another paper already in this queue.

What to verify first

  • That the 2.4 cm threshold was calibrated for this specific school-measurement dataset's own measurement precision, and would need recalibration for any different height/weight dataset.
  • Whether the shape-invariance assumption holds for a population whose growth may genuinely change shape (not just timing) — e.g. a modulator-era CF cohort where treatment initiation could shift a weight trajectory's shape.
  • Whether a fixed-threshold residual trim would systematically miss error classes different from this paper's known error type (isolated recording spikes), such as unit-conversion errors or carried-forward duplicate values.

Pipeline stage

(a) Candidate generation, via the fixed-residual-threshold-after-model-fit design. Also (e) vocabulary/framework, arguably the more durable contribution.

Error types covered / not covered

  • Covered: isolated height-recording-error spikes (large absolute deviations from the fitted, subject-adjusted curve).
  • Not covered: unit conversion errors, decimal-point shifts, carry-forward/duplicate values, and real-clinical-event confusion are not separately addressed — the paper's own known error type was measurement-recording spikes, and generalization to other error classes is untested here.

ALEX Today's paper is the foundational one: "SITAR — a useful instrument for growth curve analysis," by Cole, Donaldson, and Ben-Shlomo, published in the International Journal of Epidemiology in 2010. 今天这篇论文是奠基性的一篇：Cole、Donaldson 和 Ben-Shlomo 撰写的《SITAR——一种用于生长曲线分析的实用工具》，发表于 2010 年的《国际流行病学杂志》（International Journal of Epidemiology）。 It introduces SITAR, SuperImposition by Translation And Rotation, a shape-invariant mixed model that summarizes an individual's entire growth curve with just three numbers, tested on height data from two very different cohorts: 3,245 English schoolboys and 105 girls with Turner syndrome in a randomized drug trial. 论文提出了 SITAR，全称"通过平移与旋转实现叠加"（SuperImposition by Translation And Rotation），这是一种形状不变的混合模型，仅用三个数字就能概括一个人完整的生长曲线，并在两个截然不同的队列的身高数据上进行了检验：3245 名英国男学生，以及 105 名参与随机药物试验的特纳综合征（Turner syndrome）女孩。 We're on Track C, registry data quality, and this paper is already a natural precursor to other entries in our queue — the People-Like-Me trajectory-matching paper we covered a few episodes back builds directly on SITAR, so today we're going back to the source. 我们仍在 C 赛道——登记数据质量——这篇论文其实已经是队列中其他条目的天然前身——我们几集之前讨论过的"类似患者匹配"（People-Like-Me）轨迹匹配方法，正是直接建立在 SITAR 之上的，所以今天我们回到源头。

JORDAN Set up the vocabulary first. 先讲清楚相关术语。 What's a growth spurt in the technical sense they're using, and what's a shape-invariant model? 他们所用的技术意义上的"生长突增"（growth spurt）是什么？"形状不变模型"又是什么？

ALEX In pediatric growth terms, the pubertal growth spurt is the period where height velocity — the rate of height gain per unit time — rises sharply, peaks, then falls back down as puberty completes; the age at which that velocity peaks is called age at peak height velocity, APHV for short, and the peak value itself is peak height velocity, PHV. 用儿科生长的术语来说，青春期生长突增是指身高速度——即单位时间内身高增长的速率——急剧上升、达到峰值，然后随着青春期结束而回落的这段时期；速度达到峰值时的年龄，称为身高峰值速度年龄（age at peak height velocity），简称 APHV，而峰值本身则称为身高峰值速度（peak height velocity），简称 PHV。 Both the timing and the intensity of that spurt vary a lot between individuals — some children hit their spurt earlier and more steeply, others later and more gradually — and that variation is exactly what a growth-curve summary method needs to capture. 这次生长突增的时机和强度在个体之间差异很大——有些孩子的突增来得更早、更陡峭，有些则更晚、更平缓——这种差异正是一种生长曲线概括方法需要捕捉的内容。 A shape-invariant model is a specific, elegant simplification: instead of assuming every child's growth curve has a genuinely different shape that needs its own set of curve-fitting parameters, it assumes everyone's curve has fundamentally the same shape — one shared mean curve — and that individuals differ only in how that shared shape gets shifted and stretched to fit them. 形状不变模型是一种特定而精巧的简化方式：它不假设每个孩子的生长曲线形状真的各不相同、都需要一套自己的曲线拟合参数，而是假设每个人的曲线本质上都是同一个形状——一条共享的平均曲线——个体之间的差异，仅仅在于这个共享形状被如何平移和拉伸来适配自己。 The alternative approach the paper contrasts SITAR against is fitting the Preece-Baines curve individually to each child — a parametric curve with five separate parameters per child that fits pubertal height extremely well, with a residual standard deviation of about 6 to 7 millimeters, but at the cost of fitting hundreds or thousands of separate curves and ending up with five numbers per child that aren't especially intuitive to relate to exposures or outcomes. 论文用来对比的替代方法，是给每个孩子单独拟合 Preece-Baines 曲线——一种参数曲线，每个孩子有五个独立的参数，对青春期身高的拟合效果非常好，残差标准差约为 6 到 7 毫米，但代价是要拟合成百上千条独立曲线，最终每个孩子得到五个数字，而这些数字与暴露因素或结局之间的关联并不那么直观。

JORDAN So what problem was SITAR actually solving — what wasn't working before? 那 SITAR 究竟解决了什么问题——之前是什么地方行不通？

ALEX The motivating problem is dimensionality reduction for a fundamentally repeated-measures object. 促使他们开发这个方法的核心问题，是对一个本质上是重复测量的对象进行降维。 A child's growth curve is a series of highly correlated measurements over years, and if you want to relate that curve to something that happened earlier, like a prenatal exposure, or something that happens later, like an adult health outcome, you need to boil the whole curve down to a small number of meaningful summary values rather than working with dozens of raw, correlated timepoints directly. 一个孩子的生长曲线，是横跨数年、高度相关的一系列测量值，如果你想把这条曲线与更早发生的事情（比如产前暴露）或更晚发生的事情（比如成年后的健康结局）联系起来，你就需要把整条曲线压缩成少数几个有意义的概括数值，而不是直接处理几十个原始的、彼此相关的时间点。 The paper credits an earlier author, Beath, with the core idea — a shape invariant model, first applied to infant weight in the first two years of life — and this paper's contribution is to take that same idea, apply it specifically to height during puberty, a period with a much more distinctive curve shape than infancy, and rigorously test whether it actually captures individual variability as well as the gold-standard individually-fit Preece-Baines approach does. 论文将这一核心思想归功于更早的一位作者 Beath——他首次将形状不变模型应用于婴儿出生后头两年的体重——本文的贡献在于，把这个同样的思路，具体应用到青春期身高上，这是一个曲线形状比婴儿期更为鲜明的阶段，并严格检验它是否真的能像金标准的逐人拟合 Preece-Baines 方法一样，很好地捕捉个体差异。 The second aim, equally important for our purposes, is showing that the resulting per-subject summary parameters are actually useful for relating growth to real exposures and outcomes — not just a mathematically elegant dimensionality reduction, but one that produces biologically interpretable, analysis-ready numbers. 第二个目标，对我们而言同样重要，是证明由此得到的、按个体划分的概括参数，确实能用于把生长与真实的暴露和结局联系起来——不仅仅是一种数学上精巧的降维，而是能产出具有生物学可解释性、可直接用于分析的数字。

JORDAN Walk me through the exact model and the exact decision rule for flagging bad data. 具体讲讲这个模型本身，以及标记不良数据的具体决策规则。

ALEX The model itself fits one shared mean curve, h of t, a natural cubic spline of height against age with six degrees of freedom, plus three subject-specific random effects layered on top: size, tempo, and velocity. 模型本身拟合一条共享的平均曲线，h(t)，是身高对年龄的自然三次样条曲线，具有六个自由度，再叠加三个个体特异的随机效应：大小（size）、节奏（tempo）和速度（velocity）。 Size is a vertical shift — a taller-than-average child gets a positive size parameter, shifting the whole mean curve up to match their own height level. 大小是一种垂直平移——身高高于平均水平的孩子，其大小参数为正值，会把整条平均曲线向上平移以匹配其自身的身高水平。 Tempo is a horizontal shift on the age axis, capturing how early or late that child hits puberty relative to the average — negative tempo means earlier puberty, positive means later, following Tanner's original terminology for pubertal timing. 节奏是年龄轴上的水平平移，捕捉该儿童相对于平均水平进入青春期的早晚——节奏为负表示青春期更早，为正表示更晚，这沿用了 Tanner 关于青春期时机的原始术语。 Velocity is the cleverest of the three: it's a scaling of the age axis itself, stretching or shrinking time so that a child with a short, steep growth spurt gets one value and a child with a long, gradual one gets the opposite value — geometrically it looks like tilting or rotating the curve, which is where the "rotation" in the SITAR acronym comes from. 速度是三者中最巧妙的一个：它是对年龄轴本身的缩放，通过拉伸或压缩时间，使得生长突增短而陡峭的孩子得到一个数值，生长突增长而平缓的孩子得到相反的数值——从几何上看，这就像是把曲线倾斜或旋转，这正是 SITAR 缩写中"旋转"（rotation）一词的由来。 The model is fit using the nlme function in R, with age centered and, critically, tested on both a raw-age scale and a log-age scale to see which fits better for a given dataset. 该模型使用 R 中的 nlme 函数进行拟合，年龄经过中心化处理，且关键的是，分别在原始年龄尺度和对数年龄尺度上进行了检验，以确定哪种尺度对给定数据集拟合得更好。 Now, the exact outlier rule, which is the part most directly relevant to us: after fitting all three random effects, the authors computed each point's residual — how far the actual measurement sits from that child's fully-adjusted, back-transformed curve — and any residual exceeding 2.4 centimeters in absolute value was classified as an outlier and trimmed from the main analysis, explicitly described as removing visible "spikes" — height recording errors — while retaining the main body of legitimate data. 现在讲这个与我们最直接相关的部分——具体的离群值规则：在拟合完全部三个随机效应之后，作者计算了每个数据点的残差——即实测值与该儿童经过完全调整、反变换后的曲线之间的偏离程度——任何绝对值超过 2.4 厘米的残差都被判定为离群值，并从主要分析中剔除，论文明确将此描述为去除可见的"尖峰"——即身高记录错误——同时保留主体的合法数据。

JORDAN Where would you push back on the model itself? 你会在模型本身上提出哪些质疑？

ALEX Two places. 两个地方。 First, shape invariance is a strong assumption — it says every individual's curve is the same shape, just shifted and scaled, which works well for a fairly homogeneous, healthy pubertal-height process, but would presumably strain or break for children whose growth curve genuinely changes shape rather than just timing or scale, like a child with catch-up growth after an intervention, or growth faltering followed by recovery — SITAR has no mechanism to represent an actual shape change, only shift and stretch. 第一，形状不变是一个很强的假设——它假定每个人的曲线形状相同，只是被平移和缩放，这对于一个相当同质、健康的青春期身高过程来说效果很好，但对于生长曲线真正发生形状改变、而不仅仅是时机或尺度变化的儿童——比如干预后出现追赶性生长的儿童，或先出现生长迟滞后又恢复的儿童——这个假设大概率会承受压力甚至失效——SITAR 没有任何机制来表示真正的形状改变，只能表示平移和拉伸。 Second, the choice between raw age and log age was made empirically, per dataset, by comparing model deviance — for the schoolboy cohort, log age fit meaningfully better; for the Turner syndrome cohort, raw age fit slightly better — which is a reasonable thing to check, but it does mean the model's exact form isn't fixed in advance, it's tuned per population, and that flexibility is worth remembering before assuming a specific transformation will carry over to a new dataset untested. 第二，原始年龄和对数年龄之间的选择，是按数据集通过比较模型偏差经验性做出的——对于男学生队列，对数年龄拟合效果明显更好；对于特纳综合征队列，原始年龄拟合效果略好——这是一个合理的检验步骤，但也意味着模型的具体形式并非预先固定，而是按人群调优的，在假设某种特定变换可以未经检验就直接套用到新数据集之前，这种灵活性值得牢记。

JORDAN So how well did it actually perform, and what's the flag rate on that outlier rule? 那它的实际表现如何？那条离群值规则的标记率是多少？

ALEX On the schoolboy cohort — 3,245 boys, 129,508 individual height measurements — the raw residual standard deviation around the fitted spline, before any subject-specific adjustment, was 6.6 centimeters. 在男学生队列中——3245 名男孩，129508 次个体身高测量——在进行任何个体特异性调整之前，围绕拟合样条曲线的原始残差标准差为 6.6 厘米。 Adjusting for size alone brought that down to 2.3 centimeters; adding tempo brought it to 1.2 centimeters; adding velocity, the third and final random effect, brought it to 0.8 centimeters — a 98.9% reduction in variance using just three numbers per child. 仅调整大小一项，就将其降至 2.3 厘米；加入节奏后降至 1.2 厘米；加入速度——第三个也是最后一个随机效应——后降至 0.8 厘米——仅用每个孩子三个数字，就实现了 98.9% 的方差降低。 After that three-parameter adjustment, the paper reports the data still showed visible spikes — obvious recording-error outliers — and trimming residuals beyond 2.4 centimeters removed 952 points, exactly 0.74% of the 129,508 total, bringing the residual standard deviation down further to 0.7 centimeters, matching the performance of the individually-fit Preece-Baines curves this method was designed to replace. 完成这三个参数的调整之后，论文报告数据中仍然存在明显的尖峰——即明显的记录错误离群值——剔除超过 2.4 厘米的残差，去除了 952 个数据点，恰好占 129508 个总数据点的 0.74%，将残差标准差进一步降至 0.7 厘米，达到了这个方法本要替代的逐人拟合 Preece-Baines 曲线的性能水平。 The Turner syndrome cohort showed the same pattern at smaller scale: raw residual standard deviation 5.9 centimeters, dropping to 0.6 centimeters after adjusting for all three parameters, also about 99% of variance explained. 特纳综合征队列在更小的规模上展现出相同的模式：原始残差标准差为 5.9 厘米，调整全部三个参数后降至 0.6 厘米，同样解释了约 99% 的方差。 On the substantive growth-outcome side: in the schoolboy cohort, tempo correlated with insulin-like growth factor 1, IGF-1, measured 50 years later, at P equals 0.01 after adjusting for a secular trend confound, across 1,009 men — earlier puberty tracked with higher IGF-1 decades afterward. 在实质性的生长-结局关联方面：在男学生队列中，在校正长期趋势混杂因素后，节奏与 50 年后测得的胰岛素样生长因子 1（IGF-1）之间存在相关性，P 值为 0.01，涉及 1009 名男性——青春期更早，与几十年后更高的 IGF-1 水平相关。 In the Turner syndrome trial, girls randomized to oxandrolone showed a dramatically increased velocity parameter compared to placebo, plus 23%, 95% confidence interval 16 to 30%, at P equals 10 to the minus 8 — a striking result given the trial only involved 105 girls — with much smaller effects on size and tempo, meaning the drug's effect operated specifically through growth velocity rather than overall height or pubertal timing. 在特纳综合征试验中，被随机分配接受羟甲烯龙（oxandrolone）治疗的女孩，其速度参数相比安慰剂组显著增高，增幅达 23%，95% 置信区间为 16% 到 30%，P 值为 10 的负 8 次方——考虑到这项试验仅涉及 105 名女孩，这是一个相当惊人的结果——而对大小和节奏的影响则小得多，这意味着该药物的效果特异地通过生长速度发挥作用，而非整体身高或青春期时机。

JORDAN Does this change the CFFPR outlier pipeline? 这会改变 CFFPR 的离群值处理流程吗？

ALEX Framed as an external method only: this lands squarely as (a), a candidate generator — and specifically, the exact fixed-residual-threshold-after-model-fit design that Liz's own spline 生长数据所用的样条混合效应方法相似，因此本文是一个有用的参考点，展示了这种设计的某个经过良好验证的版本是如何被校准和评估的，同时不对 CFFPR 当前实际的阈值或标记率做任何断言。 It also lands on (e), vocabulary and framework, arguably its most durable contribution: size, tempo, and data — it's already the direct foundation for the People-Like-Me trajectory-matching method we covered earlier in this queue. 它也落在 (e) 词汇与框架这个环节，或许是它最持久的贡献：大小、节奏、速度，是一种紧凑、具有生物学可解释性的三参数描述，用来刻画任何个体的生长如何偏离群体曲线，即便脱离标记不良数据这个具体场景也同样有用——它已经是我们此前在这个队列中讨论过的"类似患者匹配"轨迹匹配方法的直接基础。 The buildable artifact: call it sitar-residual-trim-prototype —
