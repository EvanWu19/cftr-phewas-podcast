Show notes — Track B — Evaluating MedDRA-to-ICD terminology mappings (Zhang X et al. 2024)

Blurb. A quantitative + qualitative audit of the crosswalk between two clinical vocabularies — MedDRA (adverse-event/drug-safety terms) and ICD (diagnosis/billing codes) — as mediated by UMLS and OMOP CDM. The authors extract mapped pairs strictly by shared UMLS CUI, collapse to the MedDRA preferred-term (PT) level, and measure two things separately: coverage (only 27.23% of MedDRA PT terms map to any ICD term) and quality (of 1,804 human-reviewed pairs, Cohen's kappa 0.803, only 51.44% are exact matches; 42.13% are PT-broader-than-ICD). Coverage is wildly uneven by system organ class (Pregnancy/Ear/Congenital >50%; Investigations 4.82%), and a candidate-ranking pass shows the crosswalk is expandable (exact matches recoverable for 56 of 2,400 sampled unmapped terms; plausible pairs for 72%). Where it lands: AI-tooling/career lane. Buildable artifact: a terminology-crosswalk auditor that reports coverage %, exact-vs-broader-vs-narrower distribution, per-category coverage, and ranked candidate additions — reusable for harmonizing CF variant/phenotype terminologies (HGVS/CFTR2 → ClinVar/registry phenotype codes).

Plain-English glossary

  • CUI (concept unique identifier) — UMLS's linking unit; two terms sharing a CUI are asserted to mean the same concept.（中文：CUI —— UMLS 的连接单元；共享同一 CUI 的两个术语被断言表示同一概念。）
  • HGVS — the standardized notation rules for writing genetic variants.（中文：HGVS —— 书写遗传变异的标准化命名规范。）
  • ICD (International Classification of Diseases) — the diagnosis vocabulary used for billing/records; ICD-9-CM/ICD-10-CM are US clinical-modification versions.（中文：ICD —— 用于计费与病历的诊断词表；ICD-9-CM/ICD-10-CM 为美国临床修订版。）
  • LLT / PT / SOC — MedDRA hierarchy: lowest-level term (leaf) → preferred term (parent) → system organ class (top bucket).（中文：LLT/PT/SOC —— MedDRA 的层级：最底层术语→首选术语→系统器官分类。）
  • MedDRA — the Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities; the adverse-event/drug-safety reporting vocabulary.（中文：MedDRA —— 用于监管活动的医学词典，即不良事件/药物安全报告词表。）
  • OMOP CDM — the OHDSI community's standardized observational-data schema, shipping its own vocabulary mappings.（中文：OMOP CDM —— OHDSI 社区的标准化观察性数据模型，自带词表映射。）
  • Pharmacovigilance — the science of monitoring drug safety after approval.（中文：药物警戒 —— 上市后监测药物安全的学科。）
  • UMLS (Unified Medical Language System) — a meta-thesaurus ingesting many vocabularies and linking synonymous concepts.（中文：UMLS —— 吸收多套词表并连接同义概念的元词库。）

Full citation

Zhang X, et al. Evaluating MedDRA-to-ICD terminology mappings. BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making 2024;24(Suppl):40. PMID 38326827 · PMC PMC10851449 · DOI 10.1186/s12911-023-02375-1

Methods / tools to lift

  • Strict CUI-based extraction — count a mapped pair only on shared UMLS CUI (MRCONSO); looser "possibly synonymous" (RQ) links shown to be noisy.
  • Two-axis measurement — report coverage and match-granularity (exact / broader / narrower / partial) as separate metrics, not one score.
  • Human-graded sample with kappa — clinically trained annotators, two teams, Cohen's kappa 0.803, adjudicate to consensus.
  • Candidate-ranking recovery pass — a self-developed ranker suggests best ICD candidates for unmapped terms (built because UMLS API ranking missed correct matches outside its top 1,000).
  • Version pinning + trend tracking — UMLS 2020AB, OMOP v5; coverage shown to drift (declining since ~2016 as terms outpace curation).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves AI-tooling / career (secondary: Liz / CFFPR phenotype & data quality — method only, no internals). Paste-ready MVP: terminology-crosswalk auditor — input two term lists + an existing mapping table → output coverage %, exact-vs-broader-vs-narrower distribution, per-category coverage, and a ranked list of candidate additions for unmapped terms. Difficulty: M. CF use: audit HGVS/CFTR2 → ClinVar/registry-phenotype crosswalks and locate the thin spots before relying on them.

What to verify first

  • Match rubric transfer — exact/broader/narrower is defined for disease terms; variant notation needs a genetics-specific equivalence rubric before reusing the grader.
  • Version drift — pin vocabulary versions; re-run when they change (the paper shows year-to-year drift).
  • Candidate ranker unvalidated on genetics text — treat suggestions as leads for expert review, not automatic additions.
  • No registry internals — external harmonization method on public vocabularies only.

ALEX Welcome back. 欢迎回来。 Today's Track B paper is "Evaluating MedDRA-to-ICD terminology mappings," by Xinyu Zhang and colleagues, in BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making, 2024. 今天 Track B 这一篇是张欣宇等人 2024 年发表在《BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making》上的《评估 MedDRA 到 ICD 的术语映射》。 We are reading it because it is a rare thing — a paper that actually measures how good a crosswalk between two clinical vocabularies really is, instead of assuming it works. 我们读它，是因为它很难得——一篇真正去度量两套临床词表之间"对照映射"到底有多好的论文，而不是想当然地假设它能用。 That measurement discipline is the transferable object, and it maps straight onto variant and phenotype terminology cleanup. 那种度量的纪律性正是可迁移的东西，它能直接映射到变异与表型术语的清理上。

JORDAN Let me get the vocabulary straight first, because this one is dense with acronyms. 我先把词汇理清楚，因为这一篇缩写特别密集。 What are MedDRA and ICD? MedDRA 和 ICD 是什么？

ALEX Both are controlled medical vocabularies — standardized lists of terms so that different systems mean the same thing by the same word. 两者都是受控医学词表——标准化的术语清单，让不同系统用同一个词表达同一个意思。 MedDRA, the Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities, is the vocabulary of adverse-event and drug-safety reporting; when a regulator logs a side effect, it is coded in MedDRA. MedDRA（用于监管活动的医学词典，即药物不良事件与安全性报告的术语表）是不良事件与药物安全报告的词表；监管机构记录一个副作用时，就用 MedDRA 编码。 ICD, the International Classification of Diseases, is the diagnosis vocabulary used for billing and health records — ICD-9-CM and ICD-10-CM are the clinical-modification versions in US charts. ICD（国际疾病分类）是用于计费与病历的诊断词表——ICD-9-CM 和 ICD-10-CM 是美国病历里用的"临床修订"版本。 A crosswalk between them lets you connect a drug-safety signal to a diagnosis code, which matters for pharmacovigilance, the science of monitoring drug safety after approval. 两者之间的对照映射让你能把一个药物安全信号连到一个诊断编码，这对药物警戒 (pharmacovigilance)——即上市后监测药物安全的学科——很重要。

JORDAN And the machinery in the middle — UMLS, OMOP, CUI, PT, LLT, SOC — walk me through those. 中间那套机器——UMLS、OMOP、CUI、PT、LLT、SOC——给我讲讲。

ALEX UMLS, the Unified Medical Language System, is a giant meta-thesaurus that ingests dozens of vocabularies and tries to link synonymous concepts. UMLS（统一医学语言系统）是一个庞大的元词库，它吸收几十套词表，试图把同义概念连起来。 Its linking unit is the CUI, the concept unique identifier — if two terms from two vocabularies share a CUI, UMLS is asserting they mean the same concept. 它的连接单元是 CUI（concept unique identifier，概念唯一标识符）——如果来自两套词表的两个术语共享一个 CUI，UMLS 就是在断言它们表示同一个概念。 OMOP CDM, the Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership Common Data Model, is the OHDSI community's standardized data schema, and it ships its own vocabulary mappings. OMOP CDM（观察性医疗结局伙伴关系通用数据模型）是 OHDSI 社区的标准化数据结构，它自带一套词表映射。 Inside MedDRA the hierarchy matters: an LLT, lowest-level term, is the granular leaf; a PT, preferred term, is its parent; and every PT belongs to a SOC, a system organ class, the top-level bucket like "cardiac disorders." 在 MedDRA 内部，层级很重要：LLT（lowest-level term，最底层术语）是细粒度的叶子；PT（preferred term，首选术语）是它的父节点；每个 PT 都属于一个 SOC（system organ class，系统器官分类），即像"心脏疾病"这样的顶层归类。 The authors do all their coverage math at the PT level, because that is where adverse-event systems like the FDA's FAERS actually record events. 作者的所有覆盖率计算都在 PT 层做，因为像 FDA 的 FAERS 这样的不良事件系统正是在这一层记录事件的。

JORDAN Why does a genetics person care about a drug-safety-to-billing-code crosswalk? 一个搞遗传学的人，为什么要关心药物安全到计费编码的对照映射？ ALEX: Because the shape of the problem is identical to ours, even though the nouns differ. 因为问题的形态和我们的完全一样，尽管名词不同。 Any time you connect two independently built vocabularies — safety terms to diagnoses, or variant notations to phenotype codes — you inherit the same three failure modes: terms with no partner, partners that are only approximately equivalent, and a linking authority you cannot see inside. 每当你连接两套独立构建的词表——安全术语连诊断、或变异记法连表型编码——你就继承了同样的三种失效模式：没有对应方的术语、只是大致等价的对应方、以及一个你看不进去内部的连接权威。 This paper is a worked example of measuring all three, and the measurement is what transfers. 这篇论文是一个把这三样都度量出来的实例，而度量本身正是可迁移的部分。

JORDAN So what was unknown or contested that made this worth doing? 那有什么未知或有争议的东西，让这件事值得做？

ALEX The unspoken assumption in a lot of data-harmonization work is that if UMLS says two terms are linked, the mapping is good. 很多数据协调工作里有个没说出口的假设：只要 UMLS 说两个术语相连，映射就是好的。 The authors wanted to actually quantify that — both coverage, meaning how many MedDRA terms have any ICD mapping at all, and quality, meaning whether the linked pairs are genuinely equivalent or just approximately related. 作者想真正把这量化——既量覆盖率，即有多少 MedDRA 术语有任何 ICD 映射，也量质量，即相连的配对到底是真等价，还是只是大致相关。 That distinction is easy to skate over and expensive to get wrong. 这个区分很容易被糊弄过去，而搞错了代价高昂。 If you build a pharmacovigilance study on a crosswalk that is only two-thirds equivalent, your signal is contaminated in ways you never see. 如果你在一个只有三分之二等价的对照映射上做药物警戒研究，你的信号会以你永远看不见的方式被污染。 The contested part is subtle — a mapping can be "correct" in UMLS's own sense yet still be broader or narrower than the term you meant, and that directional mismatch is exactly what a naive pipeline swallows silently. 有争议的部分很微妙——一个映射在 UMLS 自己的意义上可以是"正确"的，却仍然比你想要的术语更宽或更窄，而这种方向性的错配正是一个天真流水线会悄无声息吞下去的东西。

JORDAN How did they actually measure it? 他们究竟怎么度量的？

ALEX The extraction rule is deliberately strict — they take a mapped pair only when the MedDRA term and the ICD term share the same CUI in UMLS's MRCONSO file, and they justify that by showing that the looser relationship links, the ones tagged "related and possibly synonymous," are noisy. 提取规则是有意从严的——只有当 MedDRA 术语与 ICD 术语在 UMLS 的 MRCONSO 文件里共享同一个 CUI 时，他们才算作一个映射配对，并且他们用数据证明更松的关系连接（那些被标为"相关且可能同义"的）很嘈杂，来为此辩护。 They pull mappings from both UMLS 2020AB and OMOP CDM v5, collapse everything to the PT level, and de-duplicate. 他们从 UMLS 2020AB 和 OMOP CDM v5 两边拉映射，全部塌缩到 PT 层，再去重。 Then comes the human-graded quality check: four clinically trained annotators, split into two teams, hand-review a 10% sample of mapped pairs and a sample of unmapped ones, labeling each as exact match, PT-broader-than-ICD, PT-narrower, or partial overlap. 然后是人工评级的质量检验：四名受过临床训练的标注员分成两队，人工复核 10% 的已映射配对样本以及一份未映射样本，把每个标注为"精确匹配""PT 比 ICD 宽""PT 更窄"或"部分重叠"。 For the unmapped terms they run a self-developed ranking function to suggest the best candidate ICD term, because they found UMLS's own API ranking so weak that the correct match sometimes did not appear in the top thousand results. 对未映射术语，他们跑一个自研的排序函数来推荐最佳候选 ICD 术语，因为他们发现 UMLS 自己的 API 排序太弱，正确匹配有时连前一千个结果里都进不去。

JORDAN What is the trend over time they mention? 他们提到的随时间的趋势是什么？ ALEX: They tracked UMLS mapping coverage from 2009 to 2020 and found it slowly *decreasing* since around 2016. 他们追踪了 UMLS 映射覆盖率从 2009 到 2020 年的变化，发现自 2016 年左右起在缓慢*下降*。 New terms are added to the meta-thesaurus faster than mappings are curated, so coverage erodes even as the vocabulary grows. 新术语加进元词库的速度快于映射被人工整理的速度，所以即使词表在增长，覆盖率反而被侵蚀。 That is a governance lesson as much as a technical one — a crosswalk is a living object that decays if nobody maintains it, and an audit is only valid for the versions it was run on. 这既是技术教训也是治理教训——对照映射是一个活的对象，无人维护就会衰减，而一次审计只对它所依据的版本有效。

ALEX If I push back on one design choice, it is the annotation sampling. 如果要对某个设计选择提出质疑，我会挑标注抽样。 They sampled only about 100 unmapped PT terms per SOC because expert annotation is slow, and they concede that may not represent the full distribution. 他们每个 SOC 只抽了约 100 个未映射 PT 术语，因为专家标注很慢，而且他们承认这可能不能代表完整分布。 The inter-rater subjectivity also clusters — for strings like "methanol, ethanol, isopropanol" listed under "alcohol," one judgment call about narrower-versus-broader ripples across many neighboring pairs, inflating that category. 评审者之间的主观性也会成簇出现——对于"甲醇、乙醇、异丙醇"这类列在"酒精"下的字符串，一个关于"更窄还是更宽"的判断会波及许多相邻配对，把那个类别撑大。 It is honest of them to name it, but it means the category percentages carry more uncertainty than a single number suggests. 他们点明这一点是诚实的，但这意味着那些类别百分比携带的不确定性比单个数字看起来要大。

JORDAN Give me the numbers. 给我数字。

ALEX Coverage first: after de-duplication, 6,413 unique MedDRA PT terms have a mapping to at least one ICD term through either UMLS or OMOP — that is just 27.23% of all MedDRA PT terms. 先说覆盖率：去重后，有 6,413 个唯一的 MedDRA PT 术语通过 UMLS 或 OMOP 至少映射到一个 ICD 术语——这仅占全部 MedDRA PT 术语的 27.23%。 So nearly three-quarters of the adverse-event vocabulary has no diagnosis-code counterpart at all. 也就是说，不良事件词表里近四分之三根本没有对应的诊断编码。 Coverage is wildly uneven across SOCs: "Pregnancy," "Ear and labyrinth," and "Congenital, familial and genetic disorders" clear 50%, while "General disorders" and "Investigations" — the lab-test and measurement bucket — sit below 10%. 覆盖率在各 SOC 之间极不均衡："妊娠""耳与迷路"以及"先天性、家族性和遗传性疾病"超过 50%，而"全身性疾病"和"检查"——即实验室检验与测量那一类——低于 10%。 The "Investigations" class has the most PT terms of any SOC yet only 4.82% map, largely because lab results fall outside ICD's scope. "检查"类的 PT 术语数量在所有 SOC 中最多，却只有 4.82% 能映射，主要因为实验室结果落在 ICD 的范围之外。

ALEX Now quality: of 1,804 mapped pairs reviewed, two annotators reached a Cohen's kappa of 0.803, and only 51.44% were rated an exact match. 再说质量：在复核的 1,804 个映射配对中，两名标注员达到 Cohen's kappa 0.803，而只有 51.44% 被评为精确匹配。 Another 42.13% were "PT broader than ICD," meaning the safety term is more general than the diagnosis code it was linked to. 另有 42.13% 是"PT 比 ICD 宽"，意思是安全性术语比它被连到的诊断编码更笼统。 On the unmapped side, of 2,400 sampled terms, 56 had an exact ICD match that UMLS had simply missed, and for 72% of unmapped terms their candidate search surfaced at least a plausible pair — evidence the crosswalk is expandable, not saturated. 在未映射一侧，2,400 个抽样术语中，有 56 个存在被 UMLS 直接漏掉的精确 ICD 匹配，而对 72% 的未映射术语，他们的候选检索至少浮现出一个看似合理的配对——这证明这个对照映射是可扩展的，而非已经饱和。 The headline reframes the whole thing: the standard vocabulary crosswalk everyone trusts is one-quarter complete and only half exact where it does exist. 这个重点重新定义了整件事：人人信任的标准词表对照映射只完成了四分之一，而在它存在之处也只有一半是精确的。

JORDAN Was the exact-match number the same for mapped and unmapped groups? 已映射组和未映射组的精确匹配数字一样吗？ ALEX: The interesting symmetry is that the same non-exact relationship types — broader, narrower, partial overlap — showed up in both groups. 有意思的对称性在于，同样的非精确关系类型——更宽、更窄、部分重叠——在两组里都出现了。 That tells you the gaps are not random noise; they reflect a genuine structural mismatch in how the two vocabularies carve up the same clinical reality. 这告诉你这些缺口不是随机噪声；它们反映的是两套词表在切分同一临床现实时真实存在的结构性错配。 For a builder, that is the useful signal — the errors are systematic, which means a rule-based or ranking-based repair pass can actually recover a predictable chunk of them rather than chasing one-offs. 对造工具的人来说，这才是有用的信号——错误是系统性的，意味着一个基于规则或基于排序的修复流程真的能找回其中可预测的一大块，而不是去追个案。

JORDAN So where does this land for us, and what would Runyu build? 那这对我们落在哪里，Runyu 会造什么？

ALEX This lands on the AI-tooling and career lane. 这落在 AI 工具与职业发展这条通道上。 The transferable method is the two-axis audit — measure a crosswalk on coverage and on match-granularity separately, grade a human sample with a kappa, and run a candidate-ranking pass to recover the misses. 可迁移的方法是双轴审计——分开度量一个对照映射的覆盖率和匹配粒度，用 kappa 评一份人工样本的等级，再跑一遍候选排序来找回被漏掉的。 The buildable artifact is a **terminology-crosswalk auditor**: give it two vocabularies and an existing mapping table, and it returns coverage percentage, the distribution of exact-versus-broader-versus-narrower matches, per-category coverage, and a ranked list of candidate additions for the unmapped terms. 可造的产物是一个**术语对照映射审计器**：给它两套词表和一张现有的映射表，它返回覆盖率百分比、精确/更宽/更窄匹配的分布、分类别覆盖率，以及为未映射术语给出的候选补充排序清单。 The direct CF use is harmonizing CF variant and phenotype terminologies — mapping HGVS variant notation and CFTR2 labels onto ClinVar and registry-style phenotype vocabularies, and knowing precisely where the crosswalk is thin before you rely on it. 直接的 CF 用途是协调 CF 的变异与表型术语——把 HGVS（变异命名的标准化书写规范）的变异记法和 CFTR2 标签映射到 ClinVar 和登记库风格的表型词表上，并在你依赖它之前，精确知道对照映射在哪里薄弱。 The MVP is a script that ingests two term lists plus a crosswalk and emits a coverage-and-granularity report with candidate suggestions; input is any two vocabularies with a mapping; difficulty is medium. MVP 是一个脚本：读入两份术语清单加一张对照映射，输出一份覆盖率与粒度报告并附候选建议；输入是任意两套带映射的词表；难度是中 (M)。
