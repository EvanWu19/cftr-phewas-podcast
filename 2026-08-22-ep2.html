Blurb

The TARGET Statement (Cashin, Hansford, Hernán, et al., JAMA, September 2025) is a consensus-based EQUATOR-network reporting guideline for observational studies that explicitly emulate a target trial. Developed through a four-stage process — a systematic review of current reporting practice, a two-round survey of 18 international experts, a three-day in-person consensus meeting, and a pilot with 108 stakeholders — the guideline lands as a 21-item checklist across six sections (abstract, introduction, methods, results, discussion, other information). Key recommendations: identify the study explicitly as a target-trial emulation, state the causal question and reason for emulation, specify the target trial protocol (estimand, assumptions, analysis plan) and how it maps to the observational data, then report the estimate, precision, and sensitivity analyses. This paper is closed access — we're working from the abstract only. It lands in the AI-tooling/career track not because it ships code (it doesn't — that's the pushback), but because a machine-checkable compliance linter built on its 21 items is a genuinely shippable artifact that signals rigorous, reproducible RWE pipeline-building, directly relevant to Runyu's career positioning.

Plain-English glossary

  • Causal estimand — the precise causal quantity a study is trying to estimate (e.g. the effect of treatment A vs. B on an outcome, under a specified population and time frame).（中文：研究试图估计的精确因果量，例如在特定人群和时间范围下，治疗A与治疗B相比对结局的影响。）
  • EQUATOR network — "Enhancing the Quality and Transparency of Health Research," the umbrella organization behind most reporting guidelines (CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA, etc.).（中文：即"提升健康研究质量与透明度"网络，是CONSORT、STROBE、PRISMA等大多数报告规范背后的伞形组织。）
  • RECORD / RECORD-PE — existing reporting guidelines for studies using routinely-collected health data, with a pharmacoepidemiology-specific extension; broader in data-type scope but not specific to target-trial-emulation design.（中文：针对常规收集医疗数据研究的现有报告规范，及其药物流行病学专用扩展版本；覆盖的数据类型更广，但不专门针对目标试验模拟这一设计范式。）
  • Reporting guideline / checklist — a discipline for what must be stated explicitly in a paper so a reader can evaluate the work, not a statistical method.（中文：规定论文中必须明确说明哪些内容的一套规范，便于读者评估研究质量，而非一种统计方法本身。）
  • Target trial emulation — designing an observational analysis to mimic the protocol of a hypothetical randomized trial that can't actually be run.（中文：设计一项观察性分析，去模拟一个实际上无法开展的假设性随机对照试验的方案。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • 21-item TARGET checklist — structured reporting items across 6 sections for target-trial-emulation studies.
  • EQUATOR-network guideline-development process — systematic review → expert survey rounds → consensus meeting → stakeholder pilot, a reusable template for building any consensus checklist.
  • Machine-readable schema encoding — the buildable gap: none of TARGET, RECORD, or RECORD-PE currently ship a structured/machine-checkable format.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the AI-tooling / career positioning priority. Tool sketch: target-checklist-linter — encode the 21 TARGET items as a structured YAML/JSON schema with required fields (causal question, estimand, eligibility criteria, treatment strategies, follow-up, outcome, causal contrast, analysis plan, sensitivity analyses) and an explicit "not applicable + justification" escape hatch; write a small validator script that checks a filled-in protocol against the schema and flags missing sections. Difficulty: S/M (mostly schema design + validation script, a few days). Designed to eventually absorb more than one checklist (RECORD-PE alongside TARGET) rather than being hard-coded to one guideline.
