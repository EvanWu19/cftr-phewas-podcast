Ep4 (Track C, pick 2) — 2026-09-07

The Danish National Patient Registry: a review of content, data quality, and research potential

Schmidt M, et al. Clin Epidemiol, 2015.

Blurb

A systematic review of validation studies against the Danish National Patient Registry (DNPR), one of the world's oldest nationwide hospital registries (1977–2012: >8 million people, >7.2M inpatient / ~6M outpatient / >5M ED contacts). The paper's real contribution is methodological: it defines validity as positive predictive value (PPV) against medical-record review as a "reference standard" (not "gold standard"), and completeness as sensitivity, then systematically catalogs 114 papers validating 253 total diagnosis codes/algorithms, recalculating proportions via Wilson's score method for comparability. Headline finding: PPV ranged from under 15% to 100% across codes — heterogeneity, not a single accuracy number, is the honest summary. Three national validation efforts (1980, 1990, 2003) trace real improvement over decades but also show validity is not a fixed property — it shifts with coding practice, payment-system incentives (the 2002 DRG system), and diagnostic-criteria changes (a 2000 troponin-criteria change caused an apparent, non-real jump in recorded MI incidence 2000–2004). This gives Track C its clearest citable precedent for reporting a flagging method's validation results without a true gold standard: separate validity/completeness, expect and report categorical heterogeneity, and validate against a randomly sampled stratum rather than only algorithm-flagged cases.

Plain-English glossary

  • Positive predictive value (PPV) — the proportion of records flagged/coded as having a condition that truly have it, confirmed against an independent reference.（中文:阳性预测值,被标记/编码为患有某病症的记录中,真正患病的比例,需对照独立参考标准确认。）
  • Reference standard (vs. gold standard) — an independent check (here, medical record review) used to estimate the truth; called a "reference" rather than "gold" standard because the check itself can contain errors.（中文:参考标准(区别于金标准)——用于估计真实情况的独立核查手段(此处为病历审查);之所以称为"参考"而非"金"标准,是因为核查本身也可能含有误差。）
  • Completeness (registry) — the proportion of true cases in the population that the registry actually captured, driven mainly by sensitivity and missing data.（中文:登记处的完整性——人群中真实病例被登记处实际捕捉到的比例,主要取决于敏感度和数据缺失情况。）
  • Wilson's score method — a statistical method for calculating confidence intervals around a proportion, used here to standardize CIs across studies that reported them inconsistently.（中文:Wilson得分法,一种计算比例置信区间的统计方法,本文用它统一了各研究报告方式不一致的置信区间。)
  • Diagnostic drift — a change in measurement/diagnostic protocol over time that shifts recorded rates in a way that can look like a true change in the underlying population.（中文:诊断漂移——测量或诊断方案随时间发生的变化,可能使记录到的比率发生变化,看起来像是潜在人群本身真的发生了变化。）
  • DRG (Diagnosis-Related Group) — a hospital payment classification system; its introduction can shift coding incentives and thus completeness/validity, in either direction depending on the diagnosis.（中文:诊断相关分组,一种医院付费分类系统;其引入可能改变编码激励,从而影响完整性/有效性,具体方向因诊断而异。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • PPV-against-reference-standard as the validity metric; sensitivity as the completeness metric — reported separately, never conflated.
  • Systematic bibliography design: search, screen, extract (contact type, diagnosis type, codes, validity/completeness measures, reference standard, results) across all located validation studies.
  • Wilson's score method for standardizing confidence intervals across studies with inconsistent CI reporting.
  • Validation via randomly sampled discharges/records, not only registry-flagged-as-suspect cases.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Track C pipeline-stage answer: (d) evaluation, primary — the clearest citable precedent for reporting flag-validation results without a true gold standard (separate validity/completeness, expect categorical heterogeneity). Also touches (c) the human review budget via its randomly-sampled-stratum validation design. Buildable: a PPV/completeness reporting template — a small spec or script taking flagged + randomly-sampled-unflagged records with adjudicated outcomes and outputting PPV, sensitivity, and Wilson-score CIs by category, ready for a future CFFPR outlier-work validation writeup. Difficulty: S.

What to verify first

  • This evidence base is entirely about categorical diagnosis codes, not continuous anthropometric measurements — whether the PPV-against-reference-standard framing needs adaptation for a specific height/weight value is an open translation question.
  • Diagnostic drift (a protocol/criteria change masquerading as a population change) is a general risk for any long-running measurement series, not just diagnosis codes.
  • This paper says nothing about how any specific registry's height/weight fields are currently coded, validated, or cleaned.

Pipeline stage

(d) Evaluation, primary; (c) review budget, secondary (randomly-sampled-stratum validation design).

Error types covered / not covered

Not a value-level flagging method — covers diagnosis-code validity/completeness broadly, including a worked example of the sensitivity/specificity tradeoff in unspecified-vs-specific coding (atrial fibrillation vs. flutter). Does not address unit conversion, digit transposition, decimal shift, carry-forward, or real-clinical-event confusion, the anthropometric-specific error types tracked elsewhere in this catalog.

JORDAN Let's start with the two terms doing the most work here — validity and completeness. 我们先从这里最核心的两个术语说起——有效性(validity)和完整性(completeness)。

ALEX Validity here is measured as positive predictive value, or PPV: of everyone the registry recorded as having a given disease or code, what proportion truly has it, confirmed by an independent check, usually a medical record review. 这里的有效性是用阳性预测值(PPV)来衡量的:在所有被登记处记录为患有某种疾病或某个编码的人群中,有多大比例是真正患病的,由一个独立核查手段来确认,通常是病历审查。 The paper is careful to call medical record review a "reference standard," not a "gold standard" — because a chart can itself contain errors, so it's a better approximation of the truth than the registry record, not a perfect one. 这篇论文特意把病历审查称为"参考标准"(reference standard),而不是"金标准"(gold standard)——因为病历本身也可能存在错误,所以它只是比登记处记录更接近真相的一个近似值,而不是完美的真相本身。 Completeness is the other axis entirely: of everyone who truly has the disease, what proportion did the registry actually capture — which is driven mainly by the registry's sensitivity and by how much data is simply missing. 完整性则是完全不同的另一个维度:在所有真正患病的人群中,登记处实际捕捉到了多大比例——这主要取决于登记处的敏感度,以及数据缺失的程度。 A registry can be highly valid — everything it records is basically correct — while still being incomplete, because it never captured a lot of true cases in the first place; those are two separate failure modes, and mixing them up leads to overconfident or wrongly-targeted fixes. 一个登记处可以具有很高的有效性——它记录的内容基本都是准确的——但同时又不完整,因为它一开始就没有捕捉到大量真正的病例;这是两种完全不同的失效模式,把它们混为一谈,会导致过度自信或者用力错方向的修复措施。

JORDAN And why does it matter that the DNPR has been around since 1977 specifically? 为什么丹麦国家患者登记处(DNPR)自1977年起就存在这件事特别重要?

ALEX Because the paper's whole systematic review exists to solve a problem created by that longevity: over roughly four decades, more than a hundred separate research groups each independently validated a handful of specific diagnosis codes for their own study's purposes, and those results were scattered across the literature with no single overview pulling them together. 因为这篇论文整个系统性综述的存在,正是为了解决由这种"历史悠久"所带来的一个问题:在大约四十年的时间里,一百多个独立的研究团队,各自为了自己研究的需要,分别验证了少数几个特定的诊断编码,而这些结果散落在各处文献中,从未有一份综述把它们汇总起来。 The registry itself covers a huge population — during 1977 through 2012 it registered just over 8 million people, accounting for more than 7.2 million inpatient contacts, nearly 6 million outpatient contacts, and just over 5 million emergency department contacts — nationwide, universal-healthcare coverage that makes it genuinely useful for studying rare diseases and long-term trends, but only if researchers know which specific codes have actually been checked for accuracy and which haven't. 登记处本身覆盖的人群规模巨大——在1977年至2012年间,共登记了略超过800万人,涉及超过720万次住院接触、近600万次门诊接触,以及略超过500万次急诊接触——这种全国范围、全民医保覆盖的规模,使其在研究罕见病和长期趋势方面具有真正的实用价值,但前提是研究者要知道哪些具体编码真正经过了准确性核查,哪些还没有。

ALEX On the methods: they searched MEDLINE for validation studies of DNPR data, screened titles and abstracts, checked reference lists of retrieved papers for anything missed, and added studies known to the authors beforehand — a fairly standard systematic-review search strategy. 说到方法:他们检索了MEDLINE中关于DNPR数据的验证研究,筛选标题和摘要,检查已检索论文的参考文献列表以补充遗漏的文献,并加入了作者本身已知的相关研究——这是一套相当标准的系统性综述检索策略。 They identified 114 papers, validating between 1 and 40 codes or algorithms each, for 253 validated codes/algorithms in total, and for each one extracted the patient-contact type, diagnosis type, the exact codes or algorithm used, the validity measure (PPV or negative predictive value), the completeness measure (sensitivity or specificity), the reference standard used, and the actual numeric results. 他们共识别出114篇论文,每篇验证1到40个编码或算法不等,合计验证了253个编码/算法,并针对每一项都提取了患者接触类型、诊断类型、所使用的具体编码或算法、有效性指标(PPV或阴性预测值)、完整性指标(敏感度或特异度)、所用的参考标准,以及实际的数值结果。 To make the results comparable across studies that reported confidence intervals inconsistently — different numbers of decimal places, some missing CIs entirely — they recalculated every proportion from the raw absolute numbers using Wilson's score method, succeeding for 89% of the 114 studies. 为了让不同研究之间的结果具有可比性——这些研究报告置信区间的方式并不一致,有的小数位数不同,有的干脆缺失置信区间——他们用Wilson得分法,根据原始绝对数字重新计算了每一个比例,在114项研究中有89%成功完成了这一重新计算。

ALEX I'd flag one real limitation of this design, and it's one the authors are upfront about themselves: because validation is usually a secondary aim buried inside a study whose main focus was something else, even a careful systematic search can't guarantee it found every relevant paper — validation results simply aren't reliably indexed by title, abstract, or keywords. 我想指出这个设计中的一个实际局限,作者自己也坦诚承认了这一点:由于验证通常只是某项研究中隐藏的次要目标,而该研究的主要焦点是别的问题,即使是仔细的系统性检索,也无法保证找到了所有相关论文——验证结果并不能可靠地通过标题、摘要或关键词被检索到。 So the 114-paper bibliography should be read as a substantial, useful, but almost certainly incomplete inventory of what's actually been validated, not an exhaustive one. 所以这份包含114篇论文的文献目录,应该被理解为一份内容丰富、有实用价值,但几乎可以肯定并不完整的"已验证内容清单",而不是一份详尽无遗的清单。

JORDAN So what did the actual numbers look like? 那实际的数字结果是什么样的?

ALEX This is the headline finding, and it's not a single number — it's the range. 这是这篇论文最核心的发现,而且它不是一个单一的数字——而是一个范围。 Across the 253 validated codes and algorithms, PPVs ranged from below 15% to 100%, and that spread held up even within a single validation study: in the second of three national-level Danish Health and Medicines Authority validation efforts — 1,094 discharges sampled nationwide in 1990 — five clinical specialties were checked at the five-digit diagnosis-code level, and correct categorization ranged from a low of 66% PPV for medical diagnoses up to a high of 83% for diagnoses tied to orthopedic surgery, with correctness rising further when secondary diagnoses or coarser three-digit codes were allowed. 在253个经过验证的编码和算法中,PPV的范围从低于15%到100%不等,而且这种差异在单项验证研究内部也同样存在:在丹麦卫生与药品管理局三次全国性验证工作中的第二次——1990年全国抽样1,094份出院记录——针对五个临床专科在五位诊断编码层级进行了核查,分类正确率从内科诊断最低的66% PPV,到骨科手术相关诊断最高的83% PPV不等,而如果允许纳入次要诊断或使用更粗略的三位编码层级,正确率还会进一步提高。 The earliest of the three national validations, a 1980 pilot of 1,000 discharges from a single hospital, actually concluded that primary-diagnosis validity at the time was not sufficient for research use at all — a useful reminder that "the registry has been validated" is not a fixed, permanent property; it changes over time as coding practices, incentives, and classification systems change. 三次全国性验证中最早的一次,是1980年针对单一医院1,000份出院记录的试点研究,当时的结论实际上是:主要诊断的有效性根本不足以用于研究——这是一个有用的提醒:"该登记处已经过验证"并不是一个固定不变、一劳永逸的属性;它会随着编码实践、激励机制和分类系统的变化而变化。 The most recent of the three, a 2003 sample of 420 discharges, found admission-type and referral-type errors down to 3% and 8% respectively, with dates, hospital codes, and patient identifiers all accurate — suggesting real improvement over the decades, but again, only for the specific fields that particular study happened to check. 三次验证中最近的一次,基于2003年420份出院记录的抽样,发现入院类型和转诊类型的错误率分别降至3%和8%,而日期、医院编码和患者标识符全部准确——这表明数十年来确实有实质性的改善,但同样,这只是针对该项研究恰好检查过的那些具体字段而言。

JORDAN Is there a concrete example of how a coding choice trades one kind of accuracy for another? 有没有一个具体的例子,能说明某种编码选择如何在不同类型的准确性之间做取舍?

ALEX Yes, and it's a clean one. 有,而且是一个很清晰的例子。 Atrial fibrillation and atrial flutter have separate diagnosis codes at the most granular level, but a large share of records get coded with the vaguer "not otherwise specified" version instead — and since roughly 95% of everything captured under that unspecified code is actually atrial fibrillation and only about 5% is flutter, using the unspecified code raises the sensitivity of an atrial-fibrillation case definition — you catch more true cases — while lowering its specificity, since you're also sweeping in some flutter cases that don't belong. 心房颤动和心房扑动在最细粒度层级上有各自独立的诊断编码,但相当一部分记录却被编码为更模糊的"未特指"版本——而由于归入这个"未特指"编码下的病例中,大约95%实际上是心房颤动,只有约5%是心房扑动,因此使用这个"未特指"编码,会提高心房颤动病例定义的敏感度——能捕捉到更多真实病例——但同时会降低其特异度,因为其中也混入了一些本不属于该定义的心房扑动病例。 That's exactly the sensitivity-versus-specificity tradeoff every flagging rule in this catalog has to navigate, just expressed through a coding-practice quirk instead of a statistical threshold — the lesson generalizes even though the specific mechanism, ICD-code granularity, doesn't map directly onto a continuous anthropometric measurement. 这正是本目录中每一条标记规则都必须权衡的敏感度与特异度之间的取舍,只不过这里是通过一种编码实践中的特殊现象表现出来的,而不是一个统计学阈值——尽管其具体机制(ICD编码粒度)并不能直接套用到连续型人体测量数据上,但这个教训本身是可以推广的。 Two other structural factors worth naming: the 2002 introduction of a Diagnosis-Related Group payment system may have changed hospitals' financial incentive to code completely, in ways that could cut either direction depending on the diagnosis; and private hospitals and clinics remain a persistent source of under-reporting despite reporting having been mandatory since 2003, particularly for services paid for entirely out-of-pocket. 还有两个结构性因素值得一提:2002年引入的按疾病诊断相关分组(DRG)付费系统,可能改变了医院完整编码的经济激励,其影响方向因具体诊断而异,可能是正面也可能是负面;此外,尽管自2003年起报告已成为强制性要求,私立医院和诊所仍然是漏报的一个持续来源,尤其是那些完全自费支付的服务。
