Ep1 — Track A: SPiP, the splicing predictor that names the mechanism

2026-07-11 · Track A — CF/CFTR variant interpretation & harmonization

Blurb

Leman and a French diagnostic consortium built SPiP, a random-forest pipeline that scores a variant against every splicing motif at once — donor and acceptor consensus sites, branch point, polypyrimidine tract, and exonic splicing regulatory elements — rather than specializing in one. They trained it on 4,616 variants across 227 genes that had actually been taken into the lab and had their RNA read out, then deliberately spiked in 95,000 common "control" variants to force the class balance back to a realistic ~2% positive rate. SPiP reaches AUC 0.986 versus 0.965 for SpliceAI and 0.766 for SQUIRLS, and holds up on 426 newly published variants (0.931 vs 0.913 vs 0.892). But SpliceAI still wins in deep intronic territory, which is exactly where CF's 3849+10kbC>T lives. Where it lands: Karen / variant interpretation. The buildable artifact is not "run SPiP" — it's a router that uses SPiP's motif attribution plus distance-to-exon to decide which tool's score to trust for each CFTR VUS, and outputs a predicted mechanism the wet lab can act on.

Plain-English glossary

  • Acceptor site (3' splice site) — the motif marking where an intron ends and the next exon begins.（中文：受体位点，标记内含子结束、下一个外显子开始的序列基序。）
  • Branch point — a motif inside the intron, usually 20–50 bases upstream of the acceptor, that acts as the chemical pivot of the splicing reaction.（中文：分支点，位于内含子内部、受体位点上游约 20–50 个碱基处，是剪接化学反应的支点。）
  • Cryptic / de novo splice site — a splice site that a variant either newly creates, or strengthens from a latent site the cell was previously ignoring.（中文：隐蔽/新生剪接位点，指变异新造出的、或把细胞原本忽略的潜在位点强化后启用的剪接位点。）
  • Deep intronic — defined in this paper empirically as more than 150 nucleotides from the nearest exon; beyond that distance the dominant failure mode changes from splice-site shift to pseudo-exon creation.（中文：深部内含子，本文经验性地定义为距最近外显子超过 150 个核苷酸的区域。）
  • Donor site (5' splice site) — the motif marking where an exon ends and an intron begins.（中文：供体位点，标记外显子结束、内含子开始的序列基序。）
  • Exon / intron — exons are the gene segments retained in the final message; introns are cut out.（中文：外显子是保留在成熟信使 RNA 中的片段，内含子是被剪除的片段。）
  • Exon skipping — the splicing failure in which an entire exon is left out of the message; the most common class, 72.97% of spliceogenic variants here.（中文：外显子跳跃，整个外显子被略过，本文中占致剪接变异的 72.97%。）
  • HGVS — the standardized written form for naming variants, e.g. NM_000492.4:c.1521_1523del.（中文：HGVS 是变异命名的标准化书写规范。）
  • Intron retention — the splicing failure in which the intron simply stays in the message.（中文：内含子保留，指内含子未被剪除、直接留在成熟信息中。）
  • Minigene assay — a lab construct in which an exon plus its flanking introns is dropped into a plasmid so you can watch how a cell splices it.（中文：微基因实验，把外显子及其两侧内含子放入质粒，观察细胞如何剪接。）
  • Polypyrimidine tract — a run of C's and T's between the branch point and the acceptor site that helps recruit the spliceosome.（中文：多聚嘧啶序列，位于分支点与受体位点之间的一段 C/T 富集序列，帮助招募剪接体。）
  • Pre-mRNA — the raw transcript, before introns are removed.（中文：前体 mRNA，指内含子尚未被剪除的原始转录本。）
  • Pseudo-exon — a chunk of intron promoted into the final message because a deep intronic variant created a new splice site; this is the mechanism of CF's 3849+10kbC>T.（中文：假外显子，深部内含子变异造出新剪接位点后，一段内含子被"提拔"进成熟信息 —— 正是 CF 中 3849+10kbC>T 的机制。）
  • Spliceosome — the molecular machine that removes introns and joins exons.（中文：剪接体，负责切除内含子、连接外显子的分子机器。）
  • Spliceogenic variant — the paper's term for any variant that alters splicing, whatever the mechanism.（中文：致剪接变异，本文用来统称任何会改变剪接的变异。）
  • Splicing regulatory element (ESR/ESE/ESS) — short, weakly conserved sequences inside exons that encourage or discourage the spliceosome from including that exon; why a synonymous variant can delete an exon.（中文：剪接调控元件，位于外显子内部、保守性较弱的短序列，可促进或抑制该外显子被包含 —— 这解释了为何同义变异也能删掉整个外显子。）
  • Splice site shift — the machinery uses a nearby site instead of the natural one, producing a partial exon deletion or partial intron retention.（中文：剪接位点偏移，剪接机器改用附近位点，造成外显子部分缺失或内含子部分保留。）
  • Synonymous variant — a DNA change that doesn't change the amino acid — and therefore looks harmless until you check its effect on splicing.（中文：同义变异，不改变氨基酸的 DNA 变化 —— 因此在检查剪接效应之前看起来无害。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • SPiP — random forest over motif-specific sub-scores; outputs a 0–1 spliceogenicity score plus the motif it thinks was hit plus a confidence interval. The motif attribution is the part worth stealing — it converts a score into a testable mechanism.
  • The engineered class prior — a Bayesian estimate put the real spliceogenic rate at 2.08% (CI 1.71–2.53%), so they added 95,000 common variants (MAF >5%) as presumed negatives to make precision numbers mean something. Lift the idea, distrust the assumption (see below).
  • The de novo/cryptic metascore — logistic regression over MaxEntScan + an SSF-like PWM + ESRseq values, trained on ~570K true Ensembl splice sites vs ~202M random AG/GT dinucleotides (1:381 imbalance). AUC 0.971 alone.
  • The 150-nt routing rule — within 150 nt of an exon, 97% of spliceogenic intronic variants act by creating/shifting a splice site; beyond 150 nt, 93% act by creating a pseudo-exon. This is a routing rule, not a footnote.
  • Component tools — MaxEntScan (maximum-entropy consensus splice-site scorer); SSF-like PWM (position weight matrix for splice sites); BPP (branch-point predictor); QUEPASA (ESE/ESS ratio scorer); SpliceAI (32-layer deep net trained on wild-type sequence); SQUIRLS (random forest trained on ClinVar-confirmed variants).
  • Runtime — SPiP 0.304 s/variant/CPU vs SpliceAI 7.63 s/variant/CPU. Matters at exome/genome scale.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands on: Karen Raraigh collaboration / CFTR variant interpretation.

cftr-splice-motif-triage-panel (difficulty: M) — MVP one-liner: Take every CFTR VUS from ClinVar + CFTR2 in HGVS c. notation on the canonical transcript, score each with both SPiP and SpliceAI, then route the trust — SPiP's score when its motif attribution says consensus site / branch point / polypyrimidine tract / ESR, SpliceAI's score when the variant sits >150 nt into an intron — and emit a ranked shortlist annotated with predicted mechanism and the assay that would test it (minigene construct vs patient-RNA sequencing).

What to verify first

  • Is CFTR one of the 227 training genes? Open Supplementary Table S2. If yes, any CFTR benchmark we compute is optimistic; if no, we don't know how it transfers. This is the first thing to check and it's a 10-minute job.
  • The control-variant assumption is known-broken in our gene. The 5T allele in CFTR intron 9 sits at ~5–10% frequency in many populations and is unambiguously splice-modulating — under SPiP's "MAF >5% = presumed benign" rule it would be labeled a control.
  • Repeat-length variants may not be representable at all. The poly-T (5T/7T/9T) and TG tract are variable-length repeats, not substitutions. Check whether either tool accepts them in any meaningful form; suspect the honest answer is no.
  • Threshold recalibration. The 0.452 cutoff was optimized across 227 genes. A CF-specific threshold would need recalibration — with the caveat that our best-characterized splice variants would then be positive controls, not an evaluation set.
  • SPiP's deep-intronic weakness is CF-shaped. Median SPiP score for pseudo-exon/intron-retention events: 0.026. Even SpliceAI, the winner there, has a 75% FDR in deep introns.
  • Public data only. ClinVar, CFTR2, gnomAD. Nothing here needs or should touch registry internals.

ALEX Today we're reading SPiP — the Splicing Prediction Pipeline — by Raphaël Leman and a large French consortium, published in Human Mutation in 2022. 今天我们要读的是 SPiP —— 剪接预测流水线（Splicing Prediction Pipeline）—— 作者是 Raphaël Leman 和一个规模很大的法国联合团队，2022 年发表在《Human Mutation》上。 It's Track A, variant interpretation, and it's fully open access, so everything we say today comes from the full text, not just the abstract. 这属于 Track A，也就是变异解读方向，而且是完全开放获取的，所以今天我们讲的每一句都来自全文，而不只是摘要。 We're reading it because it is the tool that refuses to pick a lane. 我们读它，是因为它是一个"拒绝只走一条路"的工具。 Most splicing predictors ask one question about one motif. 大多数剪接预测工具只针对一种基序（motif）问一个问题。 SPiP asks all of them at once, and then tells you which one it thinks broke. SPiP 则一次性把所有问题都问一遍，然后告诉你它认为是哪一个出了问题。 And for CFTR, that "which one broke" part is the whole game. 而对 CFTR 来说，"是哪一个坏了"这一点恰恰是全部关键。

JORDAN Before we go further, I want the vocabulary, because I think the biology here is doing more work than the machine learning. 在往下走之前我想先把词汇搞清楚，因为我觉得这里真正吃重的是生物学，而不是机器学习。 Start with splicing itself. 先从剪接（splicing）本身说起。

ALEX A human gene is not a continuous instruction. 人类的基因并不是一段连续的指令。 It's written as exons — the parts that end up in the final message — separated by introns, which are cut out. 它由外显子（exon，最终会保留在成熟信使 RNA 里的片段）和内含子（intron，会被剪掉的片段）交替组成。 The cell transcribes the whole thing into pre-mRNA, then a machine called the spliceosome cuts out the introns and stitches the exons together. 细胞先把整段转录成前体 mRNA（pre-mRNA），然后由一个叫剪接体（spliceosome）的复合体把内含子切掉、把外显子拼接起来。 The spliceosome finds the cut points using short sequence motifs. 剪接体靠一些短的序列基序来找到切割位点。 There are four you need to know. 有四个你需要知道。 The donor site, or 5' splice site, marks where an exon ends. 供体位点（donor site，又称 5' 剪接位点）标记外显子的结束处。 The acceptor site, or 3' splice site, marks where the next exon begins. 受体位点（acceptor site，又称 3' 剪接位点）标记下一个外显子的起始处。 The branch point sits inside the intron, usually twenty to fifty bases upstream of the acceptor, and it's the chemical pivot of the reaction. 分支点（branch point）位于内含子内部，通常在受体位点上游二十到五十个碱基处，是这个化学反应的支点。 And between the branch point and the acceptor sits the polypyrimidine tract — a run of C's and T's that helps recruit the machinery. 分支点和受体位点之间是多聚嘧啶序列（polypyrimidine tract），一串 C 和 T 的重复，作用是帮助招募剪接机器。

JORDAN So four motifs, and a variant can break any of them. 所以是四种基序，而一个变异可以破坏其中任何一种。

ALEX Five, actually, and the fifth is the troublesome one. 其实是五种，而第五种最麻烦。 Sitting inside the exons themselves are splicing regulatory elements — short sequences that either encourage the spliceosome to include that exon, or discourage it. 外显子内部还藏着剪接调控元件（splicing regulatory element），是一些短序列，作用要么是鼓励剪接体包含这个外显子，要么是劝阻它。 They're weakly conserved, they're everywhere, and they're the reason a "silent" synonymous variant in the middle of an exon can silently delete the whole exon. 它们保守性很弱、到处都是，也正因如此，一个位于外显子中间、看似"沉默"的同义变异（synonymous variant）可以悄无声息地把整个外显子删掉。

JORDAN And when splicing goes wrong, what does the damage actually look like? 那么剪接一旦出错，损伤具体长什么样？

ALEX Four flavors, and the paper names them precisely. 有四种形态，论文把它们精确地命名了。 Exon skipping — the exon gets left out entirely. 外显子跳跃（exon skipping）—— 整个外显子被完全略过。 Splice site shift — the machinery uses a nearby site instead, so you get a partial exon deletion or a partial intron retention. 剪接位点偏移（splice site shift）—— 剪接机器改用附近的另一个位点，结果是外显子被部分删除，或者内含子被部分保留。 Pseudo-exon creation — a variant deep inside an intron creates a brand-new splice site out of nothing, and a chunk of intron gets promoted into the message. 假外显子生成（pseudo-exon creation）—— 内含子深处的一个变异凭空造出一个新的剪接位点，于是一段内含子被"提拔"进了成熟信息里。 And full intron retention — the intron simply stays. 以及完整内含子保留（intron retention）—— 内含子干脆就留下不走了。 The paper calls any variant that produces any of these a spliceogenic variant. 论文把任何产生上述后果的变异统称为致剪接变异（spliceogenic variant）。 One more term: deep intronic. 还有一个词：深部内含子（deep intronic）。 The authors define it empirically as more than 150 nucleotides from the nearest exon, and we'll see why that number matters. 作者用经验方式把它定义为距离最近外显子超过 150 个核苷酸的区域，我们待会儿会看到这个数字为什么重要。

JORDAN Why is this the paper for a CF person, specifically? 那么具体到做囊性纤维化的人，为什么是这篇论文？

ALEX Because CFTR is a splicing gene wearing a protein gene's costume. 因为 CFTR 本质上是一个"剪接基因"，只是穿着"蛋白基因"的外衣。 Three of the most consequential non-F508del lesions in cystic fibrosis are splicing lesions, and each one is a different motif. 囊性纤维化中除 F508del 之外最有影响的三个病变里，全都是剪接病变，而且每一个对应不同的基序。 3849+10kbC>T is the textbook deep intronic variant — it creates a cryptic splice site ten kilobases into an intron and generates a pseudo-exon. 3849+10kbC>T 是教科书级的深部内含子变异 —— 它在内含子里一万个碱基的深处造出一个隐蔽剪接位点（cryptic splice site），并生成一个假外显子。 2789+5G>A weakens a donor site without destroying it, which is why it leaves residual function. 2789+5G>A 削弱了一个供体位点但没有摧毁它，这正是它保留部分残余功能的原因。 And the poly-T and TG tracts in intron 9 are, functionally, a natural polypyrimidine tract of variable strength — the 5T allele produces more skipping of the next exon than 7T or 9T, and the TG repeat length tunes it further. 而第 9 内含子里的多聚 T 序列和 TG 序列，在功能上就是一段强度可变的天然多聚嘧啶序列 —— 5T 等位基因导致下一个外显子的跳跃比 7T 或 9T 更多，而 TG 重复的长度还会进一步调节这一点。 So CF already has, in its clinical folklore, one example of every motif class this paper models. 所以囊性纤维化在它的临床"民间知识"里，早就为这篇论文建模的每一类基序各准备了一个实例。

JORDAN What was the state of the art before SPiP, and what were they unhappy about? 那么在 SPiP 之前的最好方法是什么，作者对它不满意在哪里？

ALEX SpliceAI, mostly, and the complaint is subtle and worth sitting with. 主要是 SpliceAI，而他们的抱怨很微妙，值得停下来体会。 SpliceAI is a thirty-two-layer deep network, and it is very good. SpliceAI 是一个三十二层的深度网络，而且确实很强。 But it was trained on wild-type sequence — it learned to answer the question "given this sequence, where are the splice sites?" 但它是在野生型序列上训练的 —— 它学会回答的问题是"给定这段序列，剪接位点在哪里？" It was not trained on the splicing impact of variants. 它并不是在"变异对剪接的影响"上训练的。 You then infer variant effect by scoring reference and alternate sequences and taking the difference. 你只能通过给参考序列和替代序列各打一次分、取差值，来推断变异效应。 That's a clever hack, but it's an inference, not the trained objective. 这是个聪明的技巧，但它是一次推断，而不是被训练的目标。 SPiP inverts that: it trains directly on a curated corpus of variants that were taken into the lab and had their RNA looked at. SPiP 把这一点反过来：它直接在一个经过人工审编的变异语料上训练，这些变异都被送进实验室、真的看过它们的 RNA。

JORDAN How big is that corpus, and where did it come from? 这个语料有多大，来自哪里？

ALEX 4,616 variants across 227 genes, of which 1,924 are proven spliceogenic — proven by RT-PCR on patient RNA, or by minigene splicing assays, which are the constructs where you drop the exon and its flanking introns into a plasmid and watch what the cell does with it. 4,616 个变异，横跨 227 个基因，其中 1,924 个被证实是致剪接的 —— 证据来自对患者 RNA 的 RT-PCR，或者来自微基因剪接实验（minigene assay），也就是把外显子和它两侧的内含子放进一个质粒、观察细胞如何处理它的构建体。 4,114 published, 502 unpublished from French diagnostic labs. 其中 4,114 个来自已发表文献，502 个来自法国诊断实验室的未发表数据。 227 genes, 161 clinical syndromes, aggregated up to 2020. 227 个基因、161 种临床综合征，数据截止到 2020 年。 That is one of the largest curated splicing-truth sets in existence. 这是目前存在的最大的、经过人工审编的剪接真值集合之一。

JORDAN But that set is 42% positive. 但这个集合里有 42% 是阳性的。 Real sequencing data is nothing like that. 真实测序数据完全不是这个样子。

ALEX And that is the single smartest move in the paper. 而这正是这篇论文里最聪明的一手。 They ran a Bayesian estimate and concluded that within those 227 genes, a randomly chosen variant has about a 2.08% probability of being spliceogenic, with a confidence interval of 1.71 to 2.53%. 他们做了一个贝叶斯估计，得出结论：在这 227 个基因内，随机抽一个变异，它是致剪接变异的概率约为 2.08%，置信区间是 1.71% 到 2.53%。 So they deliberately spiked in 95,000 "control variants" — common variants with minor allele frequency above 5% in those same genes — to drag the class balance back to reality. 于是他们刻意掺入了 95,000 个"对照变异"—— 也就是这些基因里次等位基因频率高于 5% 的常见变异 —— 把类别比例硬拉回现实。 Final corpus: 99,616 variants, roughly 98% negative. 最终语料是 99,616 个变异，其中约 98% 是阴性的。 That's an engineered prior, and it's the reason their precision numbers mean anything. 这是一个人为设定的先验，也正因为如此，他们的精确率数字才有意义。

JORDAN Push on that. 就这一点你得较真一下。 "Common therefore harmless" is an assumption, not a measurement. "常见所以无害"是一个假设，不是一次测量。

ALEX It is, and I'd push on it hard, especially in CF. 确实如此，而且我会在这一点上使劲追问，尤其是在囊性纤维化里。 Their defense is that 82% of the 227 genes are firmly tied to a clinical syndrome, so a variant at above 5% frequency probably isn't wrecking gene function. 他们的辩护是：227 个基因中有 82% 与某个临床综合征牢固相关，所以一个频率高于 5% 的变异大概率不会摧毁基因功能。 That's reasonable on average and wrong in the specific case that matters most to us. 这在平均意义上是合理的，但恰恰在我们最关心的那个具体情形里是错的。 The 5T allele in CFTR intron 9 sits around 5 to 10% in many populations. CFTR 第 9 内含子的 5T 等位基因在许多人群中的频率大约在 5% 到 10%。 It is common. 它是常见的。 It is also, unambiguously, a splice-modulating allele — that's its entire mechanism. 它同时也毫无疑问是一个调节剪接的等位基因 —— 这就是它的全部机制。 Under this training scheme it would be labeled a control. 在这套训练方案下，它会被标注为对照。 So the frequency-equals-benign shortcut has a known, named counterexample sitting in our gene. 所以"频率高就等于良性"这条捷径，在我们这个基因里有一个已知的、有名有姓的反例。

JORDAN Okay. 好。 What's actually inside the model? 那模型内部到底是什么？
