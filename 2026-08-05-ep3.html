Show notes — Track C — 2026-08-05

Impact of Gene Modifiers on Cystic Fibrosis Phenotypic Profiles: A Systematic Review — Ward A, Mauleon R, Ooi CY, Rosic N. Human Mutation, 2024.

Blurb

A PRISMA-registered systematic review synthesizing 70 full-text studies (2010–2023, plus a pre-2010 replication check) covering 81 distinct CF modifier genes across 160 polymorphic sites — the closest thing to a master map of non-CFTR modifier evidence across every CF-affected organ system: pulmonary (60 genes/97 polymorphisms), gastroenterological (25 genes), reproductive/musculoskeletal, and modulator-therapy response (only 2 genes ever evaluated). TNF is the single most-studied modifier gene (9 studies, 3 organ systems); SLC26A9 shows a threefold CFRD risk increase for one variant; SERPINA1's landmark liver-disease modifier finding (Bartlett et al.) has seen little follow-up. The single most consequential structural finding: of 70 studies, only one ever tested gene-gene interaction directly. For us this lands on summary-stats colocalization at known modifier loci — the review functions as an evidence-weighted candidate list, and the natural next move is a prioritization tool that ranks these 81 genes by replication depth, pre-2010 replication, organ-system breadth, and available public summary-stats infrastructure, to sequence which loci are actually worth a colocalization or Mendelian-randomization follow-up.

Plain-English glossary

  • Gene modifier (GM) — a genetic variant outside CFTR that doesn't cause CF alone but shifts disease severity in someone who already has two CFTR disease-causing variants; evidenced by CFTR-identical twins with discordant severity.（中文：基因修饰因子——CFTR 之外、本身不致病，但会改变已携带两个致病性 CFTR 变异者疾病严重程度的遗传变异；同卵双胞胎 CFTR 基因型相同却严重程度不同即为证据。）
  • Theratyping — grouping CFTR variants by functional impact and predicted response to modulator drugs (e.g. Trikafta).（中文：药物分型——按功能影响和对调节剂药物（如 Trikafta）的预测反应，对 CFTR 变异进行分组。）
  • PRISMA — Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses, the standard reporting framework for systematic reviews.（中文：系统综述与荟萃分析优先报告条目——系统综述的标准报告框架。）
  • STROBE — a 22-item checklist for assessing reporting quality/risk of bias in observational studies.（中文：一份用于评估观察性研究报告质量/偏倚风险的 22 条目清单。）
  • Candidate-gene study — a study design that tests one pre-selected gene's variants against an outcome, as opposed to scanning the whole genome.（中文：候选基因研究——检验一个预先选定基因的变异与某结局关系的研究设计，区别于全基因组扫描。）
  • CFRD (CF-related diabetes) — a distinct diabetes subtype common in people with CF, associated with worse pulmonary outcomes.（中文：CF 相关糖尿病——CF 患者中常见的一种独特糖尿病亚型，与更差的肺部结局相关。）
  • Meconium ileus (MI) — a bowel obstruction in newborns, often the first clinical sign of CF.（中文：胎粪性肠梗阻——新生儿的肠道梗阻，常是 CF 的首个临床征象。）
  • Mendelian randomization (MR) — a method using genetic variants as instrumental variables to test for causal relationships between an exposure and an outcome.（中文：孟德尔随机化——利用遗传变异作为工具变量，检验暴露因素与结局之间因果关系的方法。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • PRISMA + PROSPERO pre-registration — the standard rigor framework for a systematic review, worth mirroring for any internal modifier-gene evidence synthesis.
  • STROBE risk-of-bias scoring — applied to every included study without exclusion, preserving the full quality spectrum rather than only the cleanest studies.
  • Structured Excel data-extraction template — genotype, modifier gene, study method, population, outcome — a reusable schema for literature-synthesis work.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: summary-stats colocalization at known modifier loci. MVP (paste-ready): Take this review's gene-and-polymorphism table as input; for each locus, query public GWAS Catalog / Open Targets resources for available summary statistics; rank candidates by a composite score of replication-study count, pre-2010 replication status, number of organ systems implicated, and modulator-response data availability; output a ranked shortlist to sequence colocalization/MR follow-up work. Input: review's gene table (or equivalent curated list). Difficulty: S–M.

What to verify first

  • Gene-gene interaction is almost entirely untested — only 1 of 70 studies tested it directly; any joint/colocalization modeling on this list explores essentially untested territory.
  • Replication count ≠ clean evidence weight — TNF's 9 studies partly reflect citation-density (it's one of the most-studied genes in human biology), the same concern flagged for TNF in the earlier in-silico CF pain-modifier paper.
  • Outcome-measure heterogeneity — differing FEV1 reference equations and severity definitions across studies complicate pooling effect sizes; flag comparable-outcome subsets rather than silently averaging.

Gap-map / catalog: closes / leaves open

  • Closes: a comprehensive, evidence-graded current-day landscape of 81 candidate CF modifier genes across all major organ systems, explicit replication status (pre-2010 vs. novel), and the pharmacogenetic/modulator-response layer (thin as it is).
  • Leaves open: gene-gene interaction virtually untested (1/70 studies); no modulator-era (post-Trikafta) systematic GM-response data; outcome-measure standardization needed before cross-study effect-size synthesis; which genes' evidence trail is deep enough to support summary-stats colocalization vs. single-study-only candidates.

ALEX CFTR mutations are classified into six functional classes, one through six, based on how they disrupt the CFTR protein — class one means no protein gets made at all, class two means the protein is made but misfolded and degraded before reaching the cell surface, and so on down to class six, which affects protein stability once it's already at the membrane. CFTR 突变被分为六个功能分类，一到六类，依据是它们如何破坏 CFTR 蛋白——一类意味着完全不产生蛋白质，二类意味着蛋白质虽然被合成出来，但折叠错误，还没到达细胞表面就被降解掉了，以此类推，直到六类，影响的是蛋白质抵达细胞膜之后的稳定性。 A gene modifier, or GM, is something different: it's a genetic variant sitting in a completely separate gene, outside CFTR entirely, that doesn't cause CF by itself but shifts how severe or mild the disease looks in someone who already has two disease-causing CFTR variants. 而基因修饰因子 (gene modifier, GM) 是另一回事：它是位于一个完全不同基因中的遗传变异，完全在 CFTR 之外，本身并不致病，但会改变一个已经携带两个致病性 CFTR 变异的人身上，疾病表现出来的严重或轻微程度。 The clearest evidence that GMs exist at all comes from monozygotic twin studies: identical twins with the exact same CFTR genotype, including the common p.Phe508del variant homozygous in both alleles, can still show meaningfully different disease severity, and since their CFTR sequence is identical, something else in the rest of the genome has to be doing the differentiating. GM 确实存在这一点，最清晰的证据来自同卵双胞胎研究：拥有完全相同 CFTR 基因型的同卵双胞胎——包括双等位基因都携带常见的 p.Phe508del 变异——依然可能表现出有意义的疾病严重程度差异，而由于他们的 CFTR 序列完全相同，基因组其余部分的某些因素必然在起着区分作用。 A related term you'll hear is theratyping: grouping CFTR variants by their functional impact on the protein and their predicted response to modulator drugs like the CFTR corrector-potentiator combination Trikafta, which matters because this review also looks at how GMs affect modulator response, not just baseline disease severity. 你还会听到一个相关术语叫“药物分型 (theratyping)”：按照 CFTR 变异对蛋白质的功能影响、以及对调节剂类药物（比如 CFTR 矫正剂-增效剂组合 Trikafta）的预测反应来对变异进行分组，这一点很重要，因为这篇综述还考察了 GM 如何影响调节剂反应，而不仅仅是基线疾病严重程度。

ALEX They had, but in a fragmented way. 确实有，但方式是碎片化的。 The authors point out that despite decades of individual GM studies, no prior systematic review had connected GMs to the entire span of CF phenotypes across multiple organ systems in one place, and none had also documented how GM variation affects CFTR modulator drug efficacy or absorption — the pharmacogenetic angle. 作者指出，尽管已有数十年针对单个 GM 的研究，但此前没有任何一篇系统综述，把 GM 与跨多个器官系统的 CF 表型全谱联系在一起呈现，也没有同时记录 GM 变异如何影响 CFTR 调节剂药物的疗效或吸收——也就是药物基因组学的角度。 CF itself, despite being a single-gene, autosomal recessive disorder affecting an estimated 70,000 to 90,000 people worldwide, shows extreme clinical heterogeneity even within the same CFTR mutation class, and CFTR protein loss disrupts chloride and bicarbonate ion transport across epithelial tissue broadly enough that it touches the lungs, pancreas, liver, intestine, and reproductive system all at once — so a modifier acting anywhere in that network could plausibly shift multiple organ outcomes simultaneously. CF 本身，尽管是单基因、常染色体隐性遗传疾病，全球估计影响 7 万至 9 万人，但即便在同一个 CFTR 突变分类内部，也表现出极端的临床异质性，而 CFTR 蛋白功能丧失，破坏的是跨上皮组织的氯离子和碳酸氢根离子转运，其影响范围广泛到同时波及肺、胰腺、肝脏、肠道和生殖系统——所以任何一个在这张网络中任意位置起作用的修饰因子，都有可能同时改变多个器官的结局。 The review sets three specific objectives: synthesize current evidence on GM genetic variation relative to CF disease progression, pharmacokinetics, and phenotype; document current research connecting CFTR variation, GMs, and modulator therapies together; and identify potential GMs worth targeting for future drug development. 这篇综述设定了三个具体目标：整合目前关于 GM 遗传变异与 CF 疾病进展、药代动力学和表型之间关系的证据；记录把 CFTR 变异、GM 和调节剂疗法三者联系在一起的现有研究；并识别出未来药物开发中值得瞄准的潜在 GM。
