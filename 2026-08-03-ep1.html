Mapping the Non-Canonical Splicing Variants: Decrypting the Hidden Genetic Architecture of Idiopathic Male Infertility — Li K, Chen Y, Tang D, Sheng Y, Han X, Geng H, Zhang N, Duan Z, Wang G, Gao Y, Guo R, Hua R, Ding Z, Xu C, Shen Q, Yu Z, Song B, Lv M, Xu Y, Wu H, Wu J, Cao Y, He X. Advanced Science, 2026.

Blurb

This paper systematically hunts for splicing damage hiding outside the ACMG's canonical ±2 splice-site window. The authors curated 2,404 reported male-infertility variants, scored them with an 18-method ensemble (SPCards) plus SpliceAI, and functionally validated candidates by minigene assay — finding that non-canonical splicing variants (NCSVs) now account for 28.99% of all reported splicing variants in male infertility, a 53% expansion over prior estimates. Reanalyzing whole-exome data from 718 genetically-unsolved patients turned up NCSVs in 12 (1.67%), including the first-ever pathogenic TMF1 variant, confirmed in a knock-in mouse that reproduces the human sperm phenotype. Notably, their own curated table already flags two CFTR missense variants (c.743G>C, c.1115A>G) as likely splice-disrupting in CBAVD patients — a direct bridge to CFTR variant interpretation. For us this lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation: the ensemble-score-then-minigene-confirm pipeline is a template for rescreening CFTR VUS and "negative" diagnostic cases for hidden splicing damage.

Plain-English glossary

  • Canonical splice site (±2) — the two bases immediately flanking each exon-intron junction; the ACMG's default window for calling a variant a likely loss-of-function splice change.（中文：经典剪接位点——紧邻每个外显子-内含子交界的两个碱基；ACMG 默认用来判定“很可能功能丧失”剪接变异的窗口。）
  • Non-canonical splicing variant (NCSV) — a variant that disrupts splicing while sitting outside the ±2 window, often missed by standard pipelines.（中文：非经典剪接变异——位于 ±2 窗口之外却仍破坏剪接的变异，常被标准流程漏检。）
  • Minigene assay — a functional test that clones a target exon plus flanking intron (wild-type vs. mutant) into a plasmid to directly observe splicing outcome by RT-PCR.（中文：微基因构建实验——将目标外显子及两侧内含子（野生型与突变型）克隆入质粒，通过 RT-PCR 直接观察剪接结果的功能验证方法。）
  • RT-PCR — reverse-transcription PCR; converts RNA to cDNA and amplifies it to read out splicing/expression.（中文：逆转录 PCR——将 RNA 转为 cDNA 并扩增，用于读出剪接或表达情况。）
  • Exon skipping / intron retention — two common abnormal-splicing outcomes: a whole exon is dropped, or an intron is kept in the mature mRNA.（中文：外显子跳跃／内含子保留——两种常见的异常剪接结果：整个外显子被丢弃，或内含子被保留在成熟 mRNA 中。）
  • Acrosome — the cap-like structure over a sperm's head needed to penetrate the egg during fertilization.（中文：顶体——精子头部的帽状结构，受精时穿透卵子所必需。）
  • Asthenoteratozoospermia — a semen phenotype combining poor sperm motility (astheno-) with abnormal sperm shape (terato-).（中文：弱畸形精子症——精子活力低下与形态异常并存的精液表型。）
  • CBAVD — congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens; a CFTR-related disorder causing obstructive azoospermia with normal spermatogenesis.（中文：先天性双侧输精管缺如——一种 CFTR 相关疾病，导致梗阻性无精症但精子发生正常。）
  • HGVS — the standardized nomenclature for describing DNA/protein sequence variants.（中文：变异命名的标准化书写规范。）
  • VUS — variant of uncertain significance; not confidently classified pathogenic or benign.（中文：意义未明的变异——尚未被有把握地判定为致病或良性。）
  • ClinGen SVI / PVS1 — the ClinGen Sequence Variant Interpretation working group's rule for scoring a loss-of-function variant as strong pathogenic evidence.（中文：ClinGen 序列变异解读工作组制定的规则，用于将功能丧失变异评为强致病性证据。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • SPCards — a splicing-prediction platform pooling 18 integrated prediction methods into one composite variant-splicing score.
  • SpliceAI — deep-learning splice-site probability predictor, used here as a second independent scoring arm alongside SPCards.
  • Minigene splicing assay — clone target exon + ~500bp flanking intron (WT vs. mutant) into pcDNA3.1 or pSPL3 exon-trap vector, transfect, RT-PCR readout.
  • Ensemble-agreement thresholding — define a candidate splicing variant as one flagged by ≥9/18 SPCards methods OR SpliceAI ≥0.5, rather than trusting any single tool.
  • Biallelic/X-linked WES reanalysis — rescreen genetically-unsolved exome cases for a candidate NCSV paired in trans with another pathogenic-looking variant.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation. MVP (paste-ready): Take every CFTR variant currently classified as VUS or benign missense/synonymous that sits within ~50bp of a splice junction but outside ±2, score with an open ensemble (SpliceAI + Pangolin + others) using a majority-agreement threshold analogous to SPCards≥9. Input: CFTR variants in HGVS. Output: ranked table of minigene-follow-up candidates, prioritized by ensemble agreement and predicted % of protein disrupted. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • Tool substitution — SPCards itself isn't a drop-in tool; re-derive an analogous ensemble-agreement threshold with open predictors rather than assuming SPCards cutoffs transfer directly.
  • Minigene limits — short constructs (1–3 exons, ~500bp flanking intron) can misbehave even on wild-type sequence and miss large-scale mis-splicing events (full intron retention, multi-exon skipping); pair with patient RNA where possible.
  • Base-rate transfer — the 29% NCSV share found in a male-infertility cohort is a plausibility argument for looking in CFTR, not an expected hit rate.

ALEX Genes are transcribed into pre-messenger-RNA, and then splicing cuts out the introns and stitches the exons together into mature mRNA. 基因被转录成前体信使 RNA (pre-mRNA)，然后剪接过程会切除内含子 (intron)，把外显子 (exon) 拼接成成熟的 mRNA。 The cell recognizes where to cut using short sequence signals right at the exon-intron boundary — conventionally defined as the two bases immediately flanking each junction, written as the "plus-or-minus-2" positions. 细胞依靠紧贴外显子—内含子边界的短序列信号来识别切割位置——按惯例被定义为紧邻每个交界处的两个碱基，写作“正负 2 (±2)”位置。 ACMG, the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics, treats a variant at those exact ±2 positions as a likely loss-of-function, or LoF, variant — meaning the gene product is probably broken.
