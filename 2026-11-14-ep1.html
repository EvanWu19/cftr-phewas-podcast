Ep1 — Track A: Toward Advanced Quantitative Prediction of Missense Variant Effects

Paper: Ye S, Özkan S, Díaz-Vásquez A, Padilla N, de la Cruz X. "Toward Advanced Quantitative Prediction of Missense Variant Effects." International Journal of Molecular Sciences. 2026;27(19):8777. Open access (CC BY 3.0). Full text retrieved from the publisher (MDPI) after both documented Europe PMC routes — fullTextXML via the MED route, and the BioC pmcoa mirror — failed for this very recent (30 Sep 2026) article; not abstract-only.

A two-stage, interpretable missense variant-effect model called QAFIsplit, built as an auditable alternative to opaque predictors like AlphaMissense. Stage one predicts generic positional mutational tolerance from sequence-conservation and AlphaFold-derived structural descriptors; stage two predicts the effect of the specific substitution using XGBoost on 28 features, including the stage-one tolerance score. Trained and validated against 60 deep mutational scanning (DMS) datasets rather than clinical labels, specifically to avoid the circularity risk of training and benchmarking on the same kind of ClinVar-derived data. On a 402-domain DMS benchmark it beats AlphaMissense in 86% of domains and roughly ties EVE; on 12,787 ClinVar/Humsavar variants its own clinical AUC (0.90) trails supervised classifiers like BayesDel (0.96), but folding its unsupervised score into a meta-predictor lifts that ensemble's AUC from 0.94 to 0.95 — the paper's real claim is non-redundant signal, not outright superiority. For the Karen Raraigh collaboration, the transferable idea is the two-number, inspectable decomposition itself (position tolerance + structural deviation) rather than any specific accuracy number. The buildable artifact: a small pipeline computing QAFIsplit-style tolerance and structural-deviation features for CFTR missense residues from the CFTR AlphaFold model and a CFTR multiple sequence alignment, to sit alongside existing AlphaMissense/REVEL scores in VCEP review material as an auditable supplement, not a replacement.

Plain-English glossary

  • AlphaFold structural descriptor — A feature (burial depth, local packing density, secondary structure context) computed from an AlphaFold-predicted 3D protein structure rather than from sequence alone.（中文：从AlphaFold预测的三维蛋白质结构中计算出的特征,而非仅从序列中获得,例如埋藏深度、局部堆积密度和二级结构背景。）
  • AUC (area under the ROC curve) — A classifier-discrimination metric; not re-explained here since it's assumed knowledge, but flagged because several specific AUC values anchor this episode's results.（中文：一种分类器区分能力的评价指标;本节不再重复讲解,因为假设听众已经熟悉,但在此特别标注,因为本期多个具体数值都基于它。）
  • CAGI (Critical Assessment of Genome Interpretation) — A community blind-prediction challenge where predictors are scored on variants whose true clinical outcome is withheld until after submission.（中文：一项社区组织的盲测预测挑战赛,预测工具在提交结果前无法得知变异的真实临床结局。）
  • ClinVar / Humsavar — Public databases of clinically classified genetic variants (pathogenic, benign, uncertain) used as ground truth or benchmark sets for variant-effect predictors.（中文：公开的临床变异分类数据库(致病、良性、意义不明),常被用作变异效应预测工具的真值或基准测试集。）
  • Deep mutational scanning (DMS) — An experimental method that creates and functionally measures thousands of variants of one protein in parallel, giving a continuous, experimental readout of how damaging each substitution is.（中文：一种实验方法,平行构建并测量一个蛋白质的成千上万个变异体的功能表现,给出每个替换变异破坏程度的连续实验读数。）
  • Endophenotype — A measurable molecular-level intermediate (e.g., protein stability or function) that sits between a genetic change and its clinical outcome, used here as the model's actual prediction target instead of a clinical pathogenic/benign label.（中文：介于基因变化和临床结局之间、可测量的分子层面中间表型,例如蛋白质稳定性或功能,本文中作为模型的实际预测目标,而非临床致病/良性标签。）
  • Missense variant — A single-nucleotide change that results in one amino acid being substituted for another in the encoded protein.（中文：一种单核苷酸变化,导致编码蛋白质中的一个氨基酸被替换为另一个。）
  • Position-tolerance score (mtPSP) — QAFIsplit's stage-one output: how much mutational disruption a given residue position generically tolerates, independent of which amino acid is substituted in.（中文：QAFIsplit第一阶段的输出:某个残基位置本身能够耐受多大程度的突变破坏,与具体替换成哪种氨基酸无关。）
  • ProteinGym — A standardized, public collection of DMS benchmark datasets used to compare variant-effect predictors on a common footing.（中文：一个标准化的公共DMS基准数据集合集,用于在统一标准下比较不同的变异效应预测工具。）
  • VCEP (Variant Curation Expert Panel) — A ClinGen expert panel that reviews and classifies variants in a specific gene, producing the kind of auditable classification record this episode's buildable artifact is meant to support.（中文：ClinGen下设的专家小组,负责审核和分类某一特定基因的变异,本期节目所设计的可构建工具正是为了支持这类需要留痕审核的分类工作。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • QAFIsplit two-stage decomposition — split a variant-effect score into a position-level tolerance term and a substitution-specific structural term, each independently inspectable.
  • mtPSP positional-tolerance regression — a simple multiple linear regression on 10 descriptors (3 MSA-derived, 7 AlphaFold-derived) predicting generic positional mutation tolerance.
  • vePSP XGBoost model — gradient-boosted trees over 28 features (including the tolerance score) predicting the specific substitution's effect.
  • Correlation-weighted ensemble fusion (v3.0) — combining N=30 protein-specific models by their mutual correlation rather than a flat median.
  • Meta-predictor fusion with clinically-trained scores (QAFIMeta) — folding an unsupervised molecular score in alongside supervised clinical classifiers to capture non-redundant signal.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh collaboration (CFTR variant interpretation), method only. MVP: Compute QAFIsplit-style position-tolerance and structural-deviation features for every CFTR missense residue from the CFTR AlphaFold model and a CFTR-specific multiple sequence alignment, and output a two-number, inspectable breakdown per variant to display alongside (not replace) existing AlphaMissense/REVEL scores in VCEP review material. Input: a CFTR missense variant list + CFTR AlphaFold model + MSA. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR variants were included at all in the paper's 42,173-variant clinical comparison set — not stated in the paper.
  • How much of CFTR's structure falls into AlphaFold's lower-confidence regions, given CFTR is a multi-pass transmembrane ABC transporter and AlphaFold confidence is known to degrade in membrane-embedded regions.
  • Whether QAFIsplit's code and the 30 protein-specific ensemble models are actually published and reproducible in practice.
  • Whether the interpretability gain is worth pursuing as a companion signal given QAFIsplit's own clinical AUC (0.90) trails BayesDel (0.96) by six points — a judgment call the paper does not resolve.
