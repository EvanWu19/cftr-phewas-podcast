Blurb

Today's episode pairs a CorEvitas Psoriasis Registry poster on risk-continuation windows for serious infection with Suissa and Dell'Aniello's 2020 taxonomy of time-related biases in pharmacoepidemiology — retrieved abstract-only, so the episode confines itself to what the abstract states plus the well-established, uncontested description of these bias types. The CorEvitas team (Chubb, Moore-Clingenpeel, and colleagues, including a co-author from Eli Lilly) compared IL-17A inhibitors against non-IL-17A biologics for serious infection across 30-, 60-, and 90-day risk-continuation windows, finding that shorter windows produce more discrete exposure episodes, the 90-day window gives the highest incidence rate, and hazard ratios are stable across windows. The episode uses Suissa's framework to place that finding inside the larger family of time-related biases — immortal time, time-window bias, depletion of susceptibles, and latency/lag time — and argues that the risk-continuation window is fundamentally a pharmacological judgment about how long a drug's biological effect persists, not a data-cleaning parameter. What this arms Runyu with: a precise, mechanics-level account of what a risk-continuation window is, why its length should come from pharmacology rather than convenience, and why a stable comparative hazard ratio across window choices does not mean the absolute incidence rate is equally robust — a distinction that matters directly for PASS reporting and label language.

Plain-English glossary

Depletion of susceptibles — The phenomenon where people most likely to have an early adverse reaction to a drug do so and leave the observed cohort, so the remaining followed population is a survivorship-filtered group and the apparent hazard drifts down over time for reasons unrelated to the drug becoming safer.（中文：易感人群耗竭，指对药物敏感、易早期发生不良事件的人较早退出队列，导致后续观察到的风险人为下降。）

Exposure episode — A continuous stretch of drug use, from initiation to a defined end point, which may bridge small gaps between doses depending on how the risk-continuation window is set.（中文：暴露阶段，指从用药开始到设定终点的一段连续用药时间，是否跨越给药间隙取决于风险延续窗口的设定。）

Immortal time bias — Bias arising when a cohort definition creates a stretch of calendar time during which, by construction, a person cannot experience the outcome and still be classified as exposed — a built-in period of guaranteed survival that is not biologically real.（中文：免疫时间偏倚，指队列定义中人为造成一段"不可能发生结局"的时间段，却仍被计为暴露期，造成偏倚。）

IL-17A inhibitor — A biologic drug class for psoriasis and psoriatic arthritis (e.g., secukinumab, ixekizumab) that blocks interleukin-17A signaling.（中文：IL-17A抑制剂，一类用于治疗银屑病及银屑病关节炎的生物制剂，通过阻断白介素17A信号通路起作用。）

Once-exposed-always-at-risk — The boundary-case policy of never closing the exposure window — every event after first drug use counts as exposed indefinitely. Not used in practice but useful as a reference extreme against which real risk-continuation windows are compromises.（中文：一旦暴露即永远处于风险中，是一种从不关闭暴露窗口的极端假设,仅作为参照,不用于实际分析。）

Risk-continuation window — The length of time, after the last dose or the end of a dispensing/administration interval, during which a person continues to be counted as exposed and at risk for an outcome attributable to the drug.（中文：风险延续窗口，指末次给药或给药间期结束后，继续将该人计为暴露并处于风险中的时长。）

Serious infection — A standard pharmacovigilance outcome for immune-modulating drugs: infections severe enough to require hospitalization or IV antibiotics, often the basis for label warnings.（中文：严重感染，是免疫调节类药物药物警戒中的标准结局指标，指需要住院或静脉抗生素治疗的感染。）

Time-window bias — Choosing the wrong length or rule for the risk-continuation window, so events outside the period the drug's biology could plausibly explain get credited or blamed to the drug.（中文：时间窗偏倚，指风险延续窗口的长度或规则设定不当，导致药理上不合理的事件被错误归因于该药物。）
