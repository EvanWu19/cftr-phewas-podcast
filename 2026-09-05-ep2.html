Ep2 (Track B) — A Large Language Model for Complex Cardiology Care

O'Sullivan JW, Palepu A, Saab K, et al. 2026. Nature Medicine. Full text via PMC12920087.

Blurb

This is a randomized controlled trial — still a rarity for medical LLMs — testing AMIE (Articulate Medical Intelligence Explorer, built on Gemini 2.0 Flash with no fine-tuning) as a decision-support tool for hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and other inherited cardiac disease. 107 real, consecutive patients from Stanford's inherited cardiovascular center were assessed by general cardiologists randomized to work with or without AMIE, using both text reports and raw multimodal diagnostic data (ECG, echocardiogram, CMR, cardiopulmonary exercise testing). Blinded subspecialists preferred AMIE-assisted assessments overall (46.7% vs. 32.7%, p=0.02) and found significantly fewer clinically significant errors (13.1% vs. 24.3%) and missing content (17.8% vs. 37.4%). AMIE itself was adapted to this subspecialty using only nine development cases. The transferable piece for CFFPR isn't the cardiology content — it's the blinded RCT evaluation design, directly applicable to testing an LLM-assisted adjudication copilot for flagged registry records.

Plain-English glossary

  • CMR (cardiac magnetic resonance imaging) — an MRI scan of the heart's structure and function.（中文:心脏磁共振成像,一种检查心脏结构和功能的磁共振扫描。）
  • CPX (cardiopulmonary exercise testing) — a test measuring heart and lung performance during exercise.（中文:心肺运动试验,一种在运动状态下测量心肺功能的检查。）
  • HCM (hypertrophic cardiomyopathy) — a genetic heart-muscle disease causing abnormal thickening of the heart wall; a leading cause of sudden cardiac death in young adults.（中文:肥厚型心肌病,一种导致心壁异常增厚的遗传性心肌疾病,是年轻成年人猝死的主要原因之一。）
  • TTE (transthoracic echocardiogram) — an ultrasound scan of the heart taken through the chest wall.（中文:经胸超声心动图,一种经胸壁进行的心脏超声检查。）
  • Ambulatory Holter monitor — a portable device that records the heart's electrical rhythm continuously over an extended period (hours to days).（中文:动态心电图监测仪,一种可连续佩戴数小时至数天、记录心脏电活动节律的便携设备。）
  • Automation bias — the tendency for a person to over-rely on an AI system's output without sufficient independent scrutiny.（中文:自动化偏见,指人们过度依赖AI系统输出结果、而缺乏充分独立审查的倾向。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Blinded A/B preference comparison plus a separate individual-assessment rubric (errors / extra content / missing content / reasoning / bias) — a two-layer evaluation design.
  • Multistep LLM inference (web search + self-critique) as an alternative to fine-tuning for adapting a general-purpose model to a narrow subspecialty, using a handful of development cases.
  • Public, de-identified evaluation dataset + ten-domain rubric released for replication.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Liz / CFFPR data quality (method only). Buildable artifact: registry-adjudication-copilot (M) — for a small set of already-flagged height/weight records, have an LLM draft a flag/no-flag recommendation with rationale from the flagged value plus surrounding longitudinal context; compare adjudicator agreement and time-to-decision with and without the draft, scored against a small chart-reviewed sample using a blinded design mirroring this paper's RCT.

What to verify first

  • Whether AMIE's nine-case, low-data adaptation efficiency transfers to a narrower registry-adjudication task with its own conventions.
  • Generalization beyond one center, one language (English), and one patient population.
  • Automation-bias risk — whether showing the model's draft before vs. after independent human judgment changes adjudicator behavior.
  • Baseline inter-rater agreement among human adjudicators absent any AI involvement, as a prerequisite for a meaningful ground truth.
  • That the outcome measure (preference) is not the same as verified downstream accuracy against adjudicated truth.
