Ep1 — Track A — Decoding splicing variants in high-throughput sequencing (Hersent 2026)

Nine intronic variants of uncertain splicing significance, drawn from patients with neurodegenerative disease, get run through multiple complementary splicing-prediction tools and then checked against blood RNA transcript analysis (and, for one case, a minigene assay). Splicing abnormalities turned up in 8 of 9 cases, but every single algorithm tested — deep learning and motif-based alike — got at least one variant wrong, in different and complementary ways. The standout case is a variant with no canonical splice-site proximity at all: it creates an SRSF2-dependent deep-intronic splicing enhancer, driving pseudoexon inclusion, a mechanism a splice-site-focused predictor would never flag. This isn't a CFTR paper, but it's a direct template for the tool-discordance problem CFTR deep-intronic VUS create — and it lands squarely on the Karen Raraigh variant-interpretation collaboration. The buildable artifact is a concordance tracker: run CFTR candidate variants through two or three splice predictors, flag disagreement, and cross-reference existing RNA-level evidence.

Plain-English glossary

Cryptic splice site — A splice site created by a variant, not normally used by the spliceosome, that gets activated instead of (or in addition to) the real one.（中文:由变异新产生、正常情况下剪接体不会使用的剪接位点,一旦被激活就会取代或干扰正常剪接位点。）

Deep-intronic variant — A variant located far from any exon boundary, deep inside an intron, historically assumed to be functionally silent.（中文:位于内含子深处、远离外显子边界的变异,过去常被默认为没有功能后果。）

Minigene assay — A functional test where a variant plus surrounding genomic sequence is cloned into a small expression construct and transfected into cells to directly observe how it's spliced.（中文:将变异及其周围基因组序列克隆进小型表达载体并转染细胞,以直接观察其剪接结果的功能验证实验。）

Pseudoexon — A stretch of intronic sequence that gets mistakenly spliced into the mature transcript as if it were a real exon, usually disrupting the reading frame.（中文:被错误地当作真正外显子拼接进成熟转录本的一段内含子序列,通常会破坏阅读框。）

Splice donor site / splice acceptor site — The exon-end and next-exon-start boundaries where the spliceosome cuts to remove an intron.（中文:剪接体切割去除内含子时所依赖的外显子末端边界(供体位点)与下一外显子起始边界(受体位点)。）

Splicing enhancer (exonic/intronic, ESE/ISE) — A short sequence motif that recruits a splicing factor protein to help the spliceosome recognize a nearby exon.（中文:能招募剪接因子蛋白、帮助剪接体识别附近外显子的一段短序列基序。）

SRSF2 — A splicing factor protein that normally helps the spliceosome recognize real exons; in this paper, the protein recruited by the variant-created enhancer.（中文:一种正常情况下帮助剪接体识别真正外显子的剪接因子蛋白;本文中即为变异新造增强子所招募的蛋白。）

VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant whose effect on disease risk or protein function has not been established.（中文:对疾病风险或蛋白功能的影响尚未明确的变异。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Parallel use of multiple complementary splicing-prediction tools rather than a single score, explicitly to catch each tool's blind spots.
  • Blood RNA transcript analysis as the ground-truth check on predicted splicing consequences.
  • Minigene assay as the functional-validation step for variants not explained by canonical splice-site disruption.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh variant-interpretation collaboration. Buildable: cftr-splice-concordance-tracker (S/M) — take a candidate list of CFTR deep-intronic / borderline splice-region variants, pull scores from 2–3 complementary tools (e.g. SpliceAI, CADD-Splice), flag meaningful disagreement, and cross-reference ClinVar/literature for existing RNA-level evidence. Output: a ranked "needs functional confirmation" table.

What to verify first

  • Whether findings from neurodegenerative-disease genes and blood-accessible transcripts generalize to CFTR, given tissue-specific splicing and CFTR's low blood expression.
  • The real false-negative rate of any given tool — 9 variants is a proof of concept, not a calibration set.
  • How well a minigene construct (stripped of full genomic/chromatin context) predicts in vivo splicing behavior.
