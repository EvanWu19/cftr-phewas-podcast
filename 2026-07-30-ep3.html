Show notes — Track C — 2026-07-30

Cystic Fibrosis and Male Infertility: From Genetics to Future Perspectives in Assisted Reproductive Technologies — Kaltsas A, et al., Genes (Basel), 2025.

Blurb

Up to 97–98% of men with CF are infertile, almost always from congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens (CBAVD) causing obstructive azoospermia — while spermatogenesis stays intact (the testis works; the plumbing is missing). This review is a Track C pick because it contains one of CF's cleanest worked examples of a cis genetic modifier. About 78% of CBAVD men carry a detectable CFTR mutation, and the single most common genotype is F508del/5T (~17%). The 5T allele is a Class V splicing variant in intron 8 whose penetrance is set by the length of the adjacent (TG)m dinucleotide repeat: 5T next to a long TG tract (TG12/TG13) causes far more exon-9 skipping and much higher CBAVD risk than the same 5T next to a shorter TG — so the same variant is benign or disease-causing depending purely on its haplotype. The paper also flags ADGRG2 (GPR64) as an X-linked phenocopy gene for CBAVD in CFTR-negative men, and honestly frames an unresolved question: whether CFTR contributes to sperm function (bicarbonate/pH, motility) beyond pure obstruction. Where it lands: the rare-and-cis corner of the modifier catalog. Buildable artifact — cftr-poly-t-tg-annotator — a lookup that context-adjusts a 5T call by its TG-repeat length into a stratified CBAVD/CFTR-RD risk flag.

Plain-English glossary

  • ADGRG2 / GPR64 — a G-protein-coupled-receptor gene whose mutations cause X-linked vasal aplasia that phenocopies CBAVD in CFTR-negative men (different gene, different inheritance).（中文：ADGRG2/GPR64，其突变导致 X 连锁输精管发育不全，在 CFTR 阴性男性中拟态 CBAVD。）
  • CBAVD — congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens: both sperm-carrying tubes missing → obstructive azoospermia; the archetypal CF male-reproductive defect.（中文：先天性双侧输精管缺如，两侧输精管缺失导致梗阻性无精症。）
  • cis modifier — a variant whose consequence is set by its genetic neighbor on the same chromosome strand, not by a gene elsewhere in the genome (trans).（中文：顺式修饰因子，其后果由同一条染色体链上的邻近序列决定，而非基因组他处的反式因素。）
  • compound heterozygote — carrying a different mutation on each of the two gene copies.（中文：复合杂合子，两个基因拷贝上各带一个不同的突变。）
  • haplotype — the specific combination of variants (here TG-length + T-length) on one chromosome copy.（中文：单倍型，同一条染色体拷贝上各变异的具体组合。）
  • intron / exon skipping — an intron is the non-coding stretch between coding exons; a splicing variant can cause the neighboring exon to be left out of the final mRNA.（中文：内含子是编码外显子间的非编码片段；剪接变异可致相邻外显子被排除出最终 mRNA。）
  • obstructive azoospermia — no sperm in the ejaculate due to a blockage/absence of ducts, with normal sperm production (vs non-obstructive = testicular failure).（中文：梗阻性无精症，因管道阻塞/缺失导致精液无精子，但精子生产正常。）
  • poly-T tract / 5T allele — a run of thymines in CFTR intron 8; the short 5T form causes exon-9 skipping and reduced functional CFTR (a Class V variant).（中文：CFTR 第 8 号内含子的多聚 T 序列；短的 5T 型导致第 9 号外显子跳过、正常 CFTR 减少。）
  • spermatogenesis — sperm production in the testis; preserved in CF, which is why sperm retrieval + ICSI works.（中文：睾丸内的精子发生；在 CF 中保留，故取精加 ICSI 有效。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • (TG)m(T)n haplotype interpretation — read the poly-T length together with the adjacent TG-repeat length; TG12/TG13 + 5T = high exon-9-skipping / high CBAVD risk, shorter TG + 5T = lower risk.
  • Phenocopy-gene panel logic — in CFTR-negative CBAVD, sequence ADGRG2 (X-linked) before concluding; inheritance mode changes counseling.
  • Obstructive-vs-non-obstructive semen fingerprint — low volume + acidic pH + absent fructose + azoospermia = bilateral obstruction (seminal-vesicle involvement), distinguishing CBAVD from testicular failure.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: CF modifier / rare-and-cis-modifier catalog (also feeds Karen / variant interpretation).

Paste-ready MVP — cftr-poly-t-tg-annotator (difficulty S–M): input = a CFTR genotype including the intron-8 (TG)m(T)n haplotype; output = a context-adjusted CBAVD/CFTR-RD penetrance/severity flag (5T+TG12/TG13 → high-risk, 5T+shorter-TG → lower-risk) with supporting literature attached. A discrete, reusable piece of the variant-interpretation layer that keeps the haplotype explicit rather than reading the 5T variant in isolation.

What to verify first

  • Phasing — the cis story requires knowing 5T and TG length are on the same chromosome copy (and their relation to the second allele); check whether the haplotype is truly resolved or assumed.
  • Incomplete penetrance — a high-risk haplotype raises CBAVD probability, doesn't determine it; output must be a risk flag with honest uncertainty, not a verdict.
  • Scope to reproductive phenotype — the functional-vs-obstructive question is unsettled; keep the annotator scoped to CBAVD/CFTR-RD and don't overclaim about sperm function.
  • No registry internals — external-literature method on public variant biology; makes no claim about any registry's genotype/data internals and decides no research design.

Gap-map / catalog: closes / leaves open

  • Closes: documents the (TG)m(T)n cis-modifier of the CFTR 5T allele with penetrance direction (TG12/13 → higher CBAVD risk); adds ADGRG2/GPR64 as an X-linked CBAVD phenocopy gene; anchors CBAVD CFTR-mutation carrier rates (~78% of CBAVD; F508del/5T ~17%).
  • Leaves open: whether CFTR contributes to sperm function (bicarbonate/pH/motility, oxidative DNA fragmentation, altered sperm small-RNA) independently of obstruction — proposed, unproven; no quantified penetrance model for (TG)m(T)n across the full genotype spectrum.

ALEX Today's Track C paper is "Cystic Fibrosis and Male Infertility: From Genetics to Future Perspectives in Assisted Reproductive Technologies," by Kaltsas and colleagues, published in Genes in 2025. 今天的 C 轨论文是 Kaltsas 等人 2025 年发表在《Genes》上的《囊性纤维化与男性不育：从遗传学到辅助生殖技术的未来展望》。 It's open access and we read the full text. 它是开放获取的，我们读了全文。 It's a review, and while the clinical arc is about fertility, the reason it's a Track C pick — the modifier-gene catalog — is that it's one of the cleanest worked examples in CF of a *cis* genetic modifier: a variant whose consequence depends entirely on the genetic context sitting right next to it. 这是一篇综述，虽然临床主线讲的是生育，但它成为 C 轨选题——修饰基因目录——的原因，是它是 CF 中一个*顺式*遗传修饰因子最干净的实例之一：一个变异，其后果完全取决于紧挨着它的遗传背景。

JORDAN Let me get grounded first. 我先把基础打好。 What's the connection between CFTR and male infertility at all? CFTR 和男性不育之间到底有什么联系？

ALEX It's one of the most striking extrapulmonary facts in CF: up to 97 to 98% of men with cystic fibrosis are infertile. 这是 CF 中最引人注目的肺外事实之一：高达 97% 到 98% 的囊性纤维化男性是不育的。 And the cause is almost always the same — congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens, abbreviated CBAVD. 而原因几乎总是相同的——先天性双侧输精管缺如，缩写 CBAVD (congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens)。 The vas deferens is the tube that carries sperm out of the testis; if it's absent on both sides, sperm can't get into the ejaculate, which is called obstructive azoospermia. 输精管是把精子从睾丸输送出去的管道；如果两侧都缺失，精子就无法进入精液，这叫做梗阻性无精症 (obstructive azoospermia，指因通道阻塞导致精液中无精子)。 The crucial point is that spermatogenesis — sperm production in the testis — is usually completely intact. 关键在于，精子发生 (spermatogenesis，即睾丸内的精子生产) 通常是完全正常的。 The testis works fine; the plumbing is missing. 睾丸功能正常；缺的是"管路"。

JORDAN Why would a chloride channel gene cause a tube to be missing? 一个氯离子通道基因，为什么会导致一根管道缺失？

ALEX This is the developmental-biology part, and it's a nice mechanism. 这是发育生物学的部分，机制很漂亮。 CFTR is expressed in the epithelium lining the developing male ducts, where it moves chloride and bicarbonate to keep secretions watery. CFTR 表达在发育中的男性管道上皮里，它在那里转运氯离子和碳酸氢盐，好让分泌物保持稀薄流动。 The favored model isn't that the vas fails to form — it's that it forms and then degenerates in utero. 目前更被接受的模型不是输精管没能形成，而是它先形成、然后在子宫内退化。 Defective CFTR means dehydrated, thick secretions in the fetal duct; those obstruct the lumen, trigger inflammation and fibrosis, and the vas is resorbed, leaving a fibrous remnant or nothing by birth. CFTR 缺陷导致胎儿管道里的分泌物脱水、变稠；这些分泌物阻塞管腔、引发炎症和纤维化，输精管随之被吸收，到出生时只剩一段纤维化残迹或什么都不剩。 The evidence that it's degeneration rather than true agenesis: the kidneys, which branch off earlier in development, are usually normal — unilateral renal agenesis shows up in only about 11% of CBAVD cases — so the insult lands late, after the kidney and duct lineages have separated. 支持"退化"而非"真正未发生"的证据是：肾脏——它在发育中更早分支出去——通常是正常的，单侧肾缺如只出现在约 11% 的 CBAVD 病例中——所以损伤发生得晚，是在肾脏和管道谱系分开之后。

JORDAN And the semen itself has a signature? 精液本身有一个特征？

ALEX A very specific one, worth knowing because it's diagnostic. 有一个非常特异的特征，值得记住，因为它有诊断价值。 The seminal vesicles, which also come from the same Wolffian duct, are usually absent or hypoplastic too, so the semen is low-volume, acidic, and has no fructose. 精囊——它同样来自那条 Wolffian 管——通常也缺失或发育不全，所以精液量少、呈酸性、且不含果糖。 That triad — low volume, acidic pH, absent fructose, on top of azoospermia — is the fingerprint of a bilateral obstructive process, and it's how you tell it apart from non-obstructive azoospermia, where the testis itself is failing and the semen volume and chemistry are normal. 这个三联征——量少、酸性、无果糖，再加上无精症——是双侧梗阻过程的指纹，也是把它与非梗阻性无精症区分开的方法；后者是睾丸本身在衰竭，精液的量和化学成分都是正常的。

JORDAN OK, so now the genetics. 好，那现在讲遗传学。 You said this is a clean example of a cis modifier — walk me through that. 你说这是顺式修饰因子的干净实例——带我过一遍。

ALEX Start with the numbers, because they frame it. 先从数字开始，因为它们提供了框架。 About 78% of men with CBAVD carry a detectable CFTR mutation; of those, roughly 46% are compound heterozygotes — a different mutation on each of the two CFTR copies — and about 28% carry only a single detectable mutation. 大约 78% 的 CBAVD 男性携带一个可检出的 CFTR 突变；其中约 46% 是复合杂合子——两个 CFTR 拷贝上各带一个不同的突变——约 28% 只携带一个可检出的突变。 The single most common genotype in CBAVD is F508del paired with something called the 5T allele, and it accounts for about 17% of cases. CBAVD 中最常见的单一基因型，是 F508del 与一个叫做 5T 等位基因的东西配对，占约 17% 的病例。 That F508del/5T combination typically gives you vasal agenesis while sparing the lungs and pancreas — reproductive disease as the *only* manifestation. F508del/5T 这个组合通常导致输精管缺如，却不累及肺和胰腺——把生殖系统疾病作为*唯一*的表现。

JORDAN What is the 5T allele, exactly? 5T 等位基因究竟是什么？

ALEX This is where the cis-modifier story lives. 顺式修饰因子的故事就在这里。 In intron 8 of CFTR — an intron is the non-coding stretch between two coding exons — there's a run of thymine bases, a poly-T tract, that comes in three common lengths: 5T, 7T, or 9T. 在 CFTR 的第 8 号内含子里——内含子是两个编码外显子之间的非编码片段——有一段胸腺嘧啶碱基的连续重复，即一个多聚 T 序列 (poly-T tract)，它有三种常见长度：5T、7T 或 9T。 The length of that tract controls how efficiently the neighboring exon 9 gets spliced into the final messenger RNA. 这段序列的长度，控制着相邻的第 9 号外显子被剪接进最终信使 RNA 的效率。 The 5T version — the shortest — causes a large fraction of transcripts to skip exon 9, producing a nonfunctional CFTR protein. 5T 版本——最短的那个——会导致很大一部分转录本跳过第 9 号外显子，产生无功能的 CFTR 蛋白。 So 5T isn't a mutation in the coding sequence at all; it's a splicing modifier, and it's classified as a Class V variant — one that reduces the *quantity* of normal CFTR rather than abolishing it outright. 所以 5T 根本不是编码序列里的突变；它是一个剪接修饰因子，被归为 V 类变异——一种减少正常 CFTR *数量*、而非彻底废除它的变异。

JORDAN And you said its effect isn't even fixed — it depends on something next to it? 而且你说它的效应甚至不是固定的——它取决于旁边的某个东西？

ALEX That's the beautiful part, and it's the modifier-of-a-modifier layer. 这就是精妙之处，是"修饰因子的修饰因子"这一层。 Immediately upstream of the poly-T tract is a TG dinucleotide repeat, the (TG)m tract, which itself varies in length. 紧挨着多聚 T 序列的上游，是一段 TG 双核苷酸重复，即 (TG)m 序列，它本身长度也可变。 The penetrance of the 5T allele — how likely it is to actually cause exon-9 skipping and CBAVD — is modulated by how long that adjacent TG repeat is. 5T 等位基因的外显率——它实际导致第 9 号外显子跳过和 CBAVD 的可能性——被那段相邻 TG 重复的长度所调节。 A 5T allele sitting next to a long TG tract, TG12 or TG13, causes much more exon-9 skipping and confers a substantially higher CBAVD risk than the same 5T next to a shorter TG repeat. 一个 5T 等位基因，若旁边是较长的 TG 序列（TG12 或 TG13），会导致多得多的第 9 号外显子跳过，其 CBAVD 风险也远高于同样的 5T 旁边接一段较短 TG 重复的情形。 So the *same* 5T variant is more or less pathogenic depending purely on the haplotype it lives on — the specific combination of TG length and T length on that one chromosome copy. 于是*同一个* 5T 变异，其致病性的高低，完全取决于它所处的单倍型 (haplotype，即同一条染色体拷贝上各变异的具体组合)——也就是那条染色体上 TG 长度和 T 长度的具体搭配。 That is exactly what a cis modifier is: a variant whose consequence is set by its genetic neighbor on the same strand, not by anything trans across the genome. 这正是顺式修饰因子的定义：一个变异，其后果由同一条链上的遗传"邻居"决定，而不是由基因组中其他位置的反式因素决定。

JORDAN Is CFTR the only gene that can cause this picture? CFTR 是唯一能造成这种情形的基因吗？

ALEX No, and this is the other catalog-worthy gene in the paper. 不是，这是论文里另一个值得进目录的基因。 In CFTR-negative men with CBAVD, you should look at ADGRG2, also known as GPR64. 在 CFTR 阴性的 CBAVD 男性中，你应该看 ADGRG2，也称 GPR64。 ADGRG2 encodes a G-protein-coupled receptor expressed in the efferent ducts, and mutations in it cause an X-linked form of vasal aplasia that phenocopies CBAVD — bilateral absent vas, obstructive azoospermia — but with completely normal lungs and pancreas and normal CFTR. ADGRG2 编码一个 G 蛋白偶联受体，表达在输出小管里，它的突变导致一种 X 连锁的输精管发育不全，在表型上与 CBAVD 相同——双侧输精管缺失、梗阻性无精症——但肺和胰腺完全正常、CFTR 也正常。 The reason it matters beyond diagnosis is the inheritance: X-linked, not autosomal recessive, so the genetic-counseling implications for the family are entirely different. 它超出诊断意义之外的重要性在于遗传方式：X 连锁，而非常染色体隐性，所以对家庭的遗传咨询意义完全不同。 It's a clean phenocopy gene — same phenotype, different gene, different inheritance — which is exactly the kind of entry the catalog should carry. 它是一个干净的表型拟态基因——相同表型、不同基因、不同遗传方式——正是目录应该收录的那类条目。

JORDAN Let me push on the "purely obstructive" story. 我想追问一下"纯梗阻"这个说法。 Is it really just missing plumbing, or does CFTR do something to the sperm too? 真的只是缺了管路吗，还是 CFTR 对精子本身也有作用？

ALEX The paper is careful and honest here, and it's the most interesting open edge. 论文在这里很谨慎、很诚实，这也是最有意思的开放边界。 The dominant mechanism is unquestionably obstructive, and CFTR mutations do *not* cause non-obstructive azoospermia — the frequency of CFTR variants in men with true testicular failure is the same as in the general population. 主导机制毫无疑问是梗阻性的，而且 CFTR 突变*不*导致非梗阻性无精症——真正睾丸衰竭的男性中 CFTR 变异的频率，与普通人群相同。 But there are hints of a subtler role. 但有一些更微妙作用的迹象。 CFTR is expressed in sperm and epididymal epithelium, where it helps regulate luminal pH and bicarbonate — and bicarbonate is part of what activates sperm motility. CFTR 表达在精子和附睾上皮里，帮助调节管腔的 pH 和碳酸氢盐——而碳酸氢盐是激活精子运动能力的一部分。 One cohort of azoospermic men carrying R117H or F508del found that about half had a palpable, present vas deferens yet still had impaired sperm retrieval — suggesting CFTR might affect sperm function somewhat independently of the obstruction. 一个携带 R117H 或 F508del 的无精症男性队列发现，约一半人有可触及的、存在的输精管，却仍然取精困难——提示 CFTR 可能在某种程度上、独立于梗阻地影响精子功能。 The authors flag oxidative-stress-linked DNA fragmentation and altered sperm small-RNA profiles as proposed but unproven mechanisms. 作者把与氧化应激相关的 DNA 断裂、以及精子小 RNA 谱的改变，标注为已提出但尚未证实的机制。 So the honest read is: obstruction is the story, but a functional CFTR contribution to subfertility in select cases is a real, unresolved question. 所以诚实的解读是：梗阻是主线，但在部分病例中 CFTR 对生育力下降的功能性贡献，是一个真实的、尚未解决的问题。

JORDAN Before we get to us — there's a counseling angle here that seems to make the genotype interpretation matter clinically, not just academically. 在讲到我们之前——这里有一个咨询层面的角度，似乎让基因型解读在临床上、而不只是学术上变得重要。

ALEX It matters a lot, and it's why getting the haplotype right isn't a curiosity. 它非常重要，而这正是为什么把单倍型搞对不是一件无关紧要的事。 Because spermatogenesis is preserved, sperm-retrieval-plus-ICSI works remarkably well in these men — surgical retrieval succeeds above 95% of the time, versus 50% or less in true testicular failure. 由于精子发生得以保留，取精加 ICSI (卵胞浆内单精子注射) 在这些男性身上效果出奇地好——手术取精成功率超过 95%，而真正睾丸衰竭时只有 50% 或更低。 So a man with CBAVD can very often father a biological child. 所以一个 CBAVD 男性往往能生育有血缘的孩子。 But the moment he can, the partner's carrier status becomes the critical variable: if he carries CFTR mutations and she is also a CFTR carrier, the child can inherit classic CF, not just CBAVD. 但一旦他能生育，伴侣的携带者状态就成了关键变量：如果他携带 CFTR 突变、而她也是 CFTR 携带者，孩子就可能遗传到典型的 CF，而不只是 CBAVD。 So classifying the man's alleles precisely — including whether a 5T is on a high-risk or low-risk TG background — feeds directly into the reproductive risk the couple is counseled on. 所以精确地对这位男性的等位基因分类——包括一个 5T 是位于高风险还是低风险的 TG 背景上——直接关系到这对伴侣所接受咨询的生殖风险。

JORDAN And that's a genotype-to-phenotype prediction problem, which is squarely our territory. 而这是一个基因型到表型的预测问题，正好是我们的地盘。

ALEX Exactly — it's the same variant-interpretation task we keep circling, just pointed at a reproductive phenotype instead of lung disease. 正是如此——这和我们反复围绕的变异解读任务是同一个，只不过它指向的是一个生殖表型，而不是肺病。 The distinctive thing CBAVD adds is that the phenotype is unusually binary and unusually penetrant given the right genotype, which makes it a good test case for whether a haplotype-aware interpretation actually outperforms a variant-only one. CBAVD 添加的独特之处在于，在恰当的基因型下，这个表型异常地二元、也异常地高外显，这使它成为一个很好的检验样本，用来验证一个"识别单倍型的"解读是否真的胜过一个"只看单个变异的"解读。 If reading the (TG)m(T)n haplotype changes the predicted risk category for a meaningful fraction of 5T carriers, that's direct evidence the haplotype layer earns its keep. 如果读取 (TG)m(T)n 单倍型，能改变相当一部分 5T 携带者的预测风险类别，那就是直接的证据，说明这个单倍型层次值得保留。

JORDAN So where does this land for us? 那这对我们落在哪里？
