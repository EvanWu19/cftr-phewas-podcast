AI-Assisted WES Variant Interpretation in Neurodevelopmental Disorders: A Narrative Review of Existing Tools

Treccarichi S, Vinci M, Musumeci A, Galati Rando R, Fallea A, Brancato D, Bruno F, Federico C, Saccone S, Elia M, Cali F. Genes 2026.

This narrative review maps the full AI toolkit clinicians now draw on for whole-exome sequencing (WES) variant interpretation in neurodevelopmental disorders (NDDs) — pathogenicity predictors (CADD, REVEL, PrimateAI, EVE, AlphaMissense), splicing predictors (MaxEntScan, MMSplice, SQUIRLS, SpliceAI, Pangolin), phenotype-driven gene prioritization (Exomiser, LIRICAL, Phen2Gene, AMELIE), sequence-to-function models (Enformer, AlphaGenome), and protein structure prediction (AlphaFold2/3, RoseTTAFold, ESMFold). Its central, sharper-than-expected argument: concordance between predictors that share training data, annotations, or evolutionary features is not independent confirmation of pathogenicity, and the paper organizes tools by which distinct evidentiary dimension — molecular consequence, evolutionary tolerance, splicing, phenotype match, structural context — they actually interrogate, rather than by novelty. It lands on the Karen/CFTR variant-interpretation priority: the transferable method is a discipline for telling redundant computational evidence apart from genuinely complementary evidence, which this episode turns into a buildable CFTR Predictor-Redundancy Flagger (medium difficulty) — a tool that flags concordant predictor scores sharing an underlying feature or data source as non-independent, and routes a curator toward the next genuinely distinct evidence type instead.

Plain-English glossary

ACMG/AMP — The joint classification framework from the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology used to sort variants into pathogenic, likely pathogenic, VUS, likely benign, or benign categories.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学院与分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类指南体系。）

BP4 / PP3 — Within ACMG/AMP, BP4 is the evidence code for a computational prediction supporting a benign effect; PP3 is the code for one supporting a pathogenic effect.（中文：ACMG/AMP框架内的证据项——BP4表示支持良性的计算证据，PP3表示支持致病性的计算证据。）

ClinGen Variant Curation Expert Panel (VCEP) — A gene- or disease-specific expert group that issues calibrated, ACMG/AMP-compliant classification rules for variants in that gene.（中文：针对特定基因或疾病设立的专家小组，制定符合ACMG/AMP框架、经过校准的变异分类规则。）

HGVS notation — The standardized system for writing exactly where a variant is located and what it changes, e.g. c.643G>A for a coding-sequence change or p.(Ala215Thr) for the resulting amino-acid substitution.（中文：人类基因组变异协会制定的标准化变异书写规范，用于精确记录变异的位置及其改变内容。）

MAVE (multiplexed assay of variant effect) — A functional-genomics technique that experimentally tests the effect of large numbers of variants in parallel, providing calibrated functional evidence for variant classification.（中文：变异效应多重化检测技术，可并行对大量变异进行功能测定，为变异分类提供经过校准的功能证据。）

NMD (nonsense-mediated decay) — The cell's RNA quality-control pathway that degrades transcripts carrying a premature stop codon; whether a truncating variant escapes or triggers NMD changes its disease mechanism.（中文：细胞对携带提前终止密码子的转录本进行降解的质量控制机制，截短变异是否触发NMD会改变其致病机制。）

pLDDT / PAE — Confidence metrics from AI structure-prediction models: pLDDT estimates per-residue local structural confidence, while PAE (predicted aligned error) estimates confidence in the relative position of residue pairs.（中文：pLDDT为逐残基的局部结构预测置信度指标，PAE（预测对齐误差）用于衡量残基间相对位置的置信度。）

RMSD (root-mean-square deviation) — A metric quantifying the average positional difference between corresponding atoms of two aligned protein structures; lower values indicate closer structural agreement.（中文：均方根偏差，衡量两个对齐蛋白结构中对应原子位置差异的指标，数值越低表示结构越接近。）

Splice variant — A genetic variant that alters pre-mRNA splicing rather than the protein sequence directly, which can disrupt the final protein even from a position outside any exon.（中文：改变RNA前体剪接方式而非直接改变蛋白序列的变异，即使位于外显子之外也可能破坏最终蛋白质。）

VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A genetic variant for which current evidence is insufficient to classify as either pathogenic or benign.（中文：现有证据既不足以判定致病、也不足以判定良性的基因变异。）

Full citation: Treccarichi S, et al. "AI-Assisted WES Variant Interpretation in Neurodevelopmental Disorders: A Narrative Review of Existing Tools." Genes 2026. — PMID: 42938392 — PMCID: PMC13704639 — DOI: 10.3390/genes17101276

Methods / tools to lift

  • CADD, REVEL, PrimateAI/PrimateAI-3D, EVE, AlphaMissense — missense pathogenicity predictors spanning ensemble supervised learning, deep learning, and unsupervised evolutionary modeling.
  • MaxEntScan, MMSplice, SQUIRLS, SpliceAI, Pangolin — splicing-effect predictors from classic motif models to deep sequence models.
  • Exomiser, Genomiser, LIRICAL, Phen2Gene, AMELIE, Xrare, ORVAL/VarCoPP — HPO-based and literature-based phenotype-to-gene/variant prioritization tools.
  • Enformer, AlphaGenome, Corgi, EVEE/Evo2 — sequence-to-function models predicting regulatory and molecular consequences of variants.
  • AlphaFold2, AlphaFold3, RoseTTAFold, ESMFold — AI protein/biomolecular structure prediction, with pLDDT/PAE/pTM/ipTM confidence metrics.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Karen / CFTR variant interpretation (method only). Buildable artifact: CFTR Predictor-Redundancy Flagger (M) — takes a CFTR VUS list with pulled computational scores (REVEL, CADD, AlphaMissense, SpliceAI, EVE) plus a once-built lookup table of each predictor's training data/features; flags concordant-but-non-independent score pairs, tags mechanism class (missense/splice/truncating), and routes toward the next genuinely distinct evidence type.

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR already has its own ClinGen VCEP with calibrated PP3/BP4 thresholds, the way SCN2A does.
  • Whether CFTR has MAVE/functional-assay coverage comparable to BRCA1 or TP53, rather than assuming parity from this paper alone.
  • Region-by-region pLDDT/PAE confidence before treating any CFTR AlphaFold structural comparison as informative.
