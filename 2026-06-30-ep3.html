Intestinal current measurement and nasal potential difference to make a diagnosis of cases with inconclusive CFTR genetics and sweat test. Minso R, Schulz A, Stanke F, Welte T, Dittrich AM, Tümmler B, et al. BMJ Open Respiratory Research, 2020. (Open access — full text via BioC PMC-OA.)

Blurb

A single expert center (Hannover) applied standardized NPD and ICM electrophysiology — direct functional readouts of the CFTR chloride channel in airway and gut — to 237 hard cases with inconclusive sweat test and CFTR genetics over 2009–2020. One-third (81/237) were diagnosed with CF or CFTR-related disorder, including 76% (22/29) of those with a genotype of unknown/variable significance and 27% (51/190) of those carrying only one or no identified CFTR mutation. Most diagnosed patients carry wild-type CFTR coding sequence on at least one allele, implying the causal lesion lies in non-coding regulatory regions or elsewhere in the genome and acts in a tissue-dependent way — directly evidenced by 11/26 patients showing organ-discordant tracings (CF-range in one organ, normal in the other). The transferable idea is treating genotype–phenotype (and organ-specific) discordance as a phenotype in its own right. Buildable artifact: a genotype–phenotype discordance flagger that scores and ranks cases where genotype severity and observed organ involvement diverge. Serves the Liz / CFFPR phenotype & data-quality priority (external method only).

Plain-English glossary

  • CFTR-related disorder (CFTR-RD) — a milder condition from partial CFTR dysfunction that doesn't meet full CF criteria.（中文：CFTR 相关疾病（CFTR-RD），由部分 CFTR 功能障碍引起、未达完整 CF 诊断标准的较轻病症。）
  • ENaC (epithelial sodium channel) — the sodium channel whose activity NPD also measures alongside CFTR chloride flow.（中文：ENaC（上皮钠通道），NPD 在测量 CFTR 氯流的同时也测量其活动的钠通道。）
  • Ex vivo — performed on living tissue removed from the body (e.g. a rectal biopsy in a chamber).（中文：离体（ex vivo），在取出体外的活组织上进行（如小室中的直肠活检）。）
  • Exon — the protein-coding stretch of a gene that standard sequencing targets.（中文：外显子（exon），基因中编码蛋白、标准测序所针对的片段。）
  • Intestinal current measurement (ICM) — ex vivo test of CFTR chloride secretory current in a rectal biopsy mounted in an Ussing chamber.（中文：肠道电流测量（ICM），在 Ussing 小室中测量直肠活检 CFTR 氯分泌电流的离体检查。）
  • Nasal potential difference (NPD) — in vivo voltage across nasal epithelium reflecting CFTR/ENaC ion transport.（中文：鼻电位差（NPD），在体测量鼻上皮两侧电压、反映 CFTR/ENaC 离子转运。）
  • Sweat test — classic CF diagnostic measuring chloride concentration in sweat.（中文：汗液试验，测量汗中氯离子浓度的经典 CF 诊断方法。）
  • Ussing chamber — lab rig that clamps an epithelial sheet and measures ion currents across it.（中文：Ussing 小室，夹住一片上皮组织并测量穿过它的离子电流的实验装置。）
  • Variant of uncertain significance (VUS) — a DNA change whose disease effect is not yet known.（中文：临床意义未明的变异（VUS），对疾病影响尚不清楚的 DNA 改变。）
  • Wild-type — the normal, non-mutated reference sequence.（中文：野生型（wild-type），正常、未突变的参考序列。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Functional discordance as phenotype — organ-specific CFTR readouts (NPD vs. ICM) treated as separate axes, not collapsed to one CF/not-CF label.
  • Re-derived control reference values — in-house thresholds from 68 non-CF controls (ICM cutoff shifted 34→39 µA/cm²) to anchor interpretation.
  • CFTR2-consistency check — when two variants are identified, electrophysiology cross-validated against CFTR2 classification.

What to verify first

  • Phenotype completeness — check for differential missingness across organ systems before trusting any discordance flag.
  • Genotype severity scale — needs a defensible CFTR-genotype → expected-severity mapping (CFTR2 + functional class is one external source).
  • Age / modulator confounding — adjust raw discordance for treatment and age effects on organ phenotype.
  • Hypothesis-generating, not diagnostic — flags point to cases for closer look, never a non-coding-causation conclusion on their own; no research design is presumed settled.
