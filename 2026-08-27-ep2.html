How did this episode land for you?

Track B — AI tooling. Shashikumar, Wardi, Nemati and colleagues at UC San Diego ship COMPOSER-LLM, an open-source system that pairs a validated structured sepsis-risk model (COMPOSER) with a retrieval-augmented LLM (Mixtral 8x7B) that reads unstructured clinical notes — but only for the narrow band of cases where COMPOSER's own score is uncertain (0.5-0.75). The LLM extracts 51 discrete clinical signs/symptoms via RAG, feeds them to a Bayesian differential-diagnosis calculator across sepsis and 18 mimics, and only fires an alert if severe sepsis ranks top-5 and bacterial infection is separately confirmed. Retrospectively and prospectively, this more than doubled positive predictive value and cut false alarms per patient-hour more than fourfold versus the structured model alone, while holding sensitivity flat. For registry/bioinformatics tooling work, the transferable idea isn't sepsis-specific — it's the pattern of spending LLM compute selectively, only on a base model's own uncertainty band, using RAG over free text to resolve ambiguity that structured fields can't capture.

Plain-English glossary

Sepsis-3 / SOFA score — the consensus definition requiring infection plus organ dysfunction, where dysfunction is quantified by the Sequential Organ Failure Assessment (SOFA), a six-organ-system point scale.（中文:脓毒症的共识定义,要求同时具备感染证据和器官功能障碍,后者用SOFA评分——一个覆盖六个器官系统的量表——来量化。）

Best Practice Advisory (BPA) — the standard EHR mechanism for firing a clinical alert to a nurse or clinician.（中文:电子健康记录系统中,向护士或临床医生触发临床警报的标准机制。）

Retrieval augmented generation (RAG) — a technique where relevant text chunks are retrieved from a document store and appended to an LLM's prompt, rather than relying on the LLM's internal knowledge alone.（中文:一种技术,从文档库中检索相关文本片段并附加到LLM的提示词中,而不是仅依赖LLM内部的知识。）

Conformal prediction — a statistical technique COMPOSER uses to reject out-of-distribution inputs (e.g., from data entry errors) rather than issuing an overconfident score.（中文:COMPOSER用来拒绝分布外输入(例如由数据录入错误导致)的一种统计技术,避免给出过度自信的分数。）

Positive predictive value (PPV) / false alarms per patient hour (FAPH) — standard alerting-system metrics: PPV is the share of alerts that are true positives; FAPH normalizes alert burden by patient-hours monitored.（中文:警报系统的标准指标:PPV是警报中真阳性的比例;FAPH按监测的患者小时数对警报负担做归一化。）

Full citation

Shashikumar SP, Wardi G, Malhotra A, Nemati S, et al. (2025). Development and prospective implementation of a large language model based system for early sepsis prediction. npj Digital Medicine. PMID: 40379845 | PMC12084535 | DOI: 10.1038/s41746-025-01689-w

Methods / tools to lift

  • COMPOSER — validated structured deep-learning sepsis risk model with a conformal-prediction OOD rejection module.
  • Mixtral 8x7B + RAG — instruction-tuned open-weight LLM extracting discrete clinical signs/symptoms from notes via Chroma vector store + HuggingFace Instructor embeddings.
  • Bayesian differential-diagnosis calculator — combines extracted signs/symptoms into ranked likelihoods across sepsis and 18 mimics.
  • LangChain — Python framework used to implement the full extraction pipeline, keeping the stack vendor-swappable.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the AI-tooling / career-development priority. MVP: an open-source "second-look" module that wraps any existing structured risk/flagging model — activates only in that model's own uncertain band, runs RAG over associated free-text notes, and outputs a re-scored confidence plus an auditable list of retrieved evidence. Prototype first on a public de-identified note dataset (e.g., MIMIC), not registry data. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • Whether dense, decision-justifying ED notes are representative of the note density/style relevant to other clinical documentation — RAG extraction quality may not transfer to sparser, more templated notes.
  • The extraction pipeline's actual hallucination/disagreement rate across the three-run majority vote — not reported in the paper.
  • The Bayesian likelihood priors were hand-built for sepsis-mimics specifically by two clinician-authors; porting to a new clinical question means rebuilding that layer, not just swapping prompts.
  • Nothing here implies anything about what data quality signals CFFPR's own registry notes might support.

ALEX Today's Track B pick is "Development and prospective implementation of a large language model based system for early sepsis prediction" by Shashikumar, Wardi, Nemati and colleagues at UC San Diego, published this year in npj Digital Medicine. 今天B轨道要读的是Shashikumar、Wardi、Nemati等人在加州大学圣地亚哥分校发表在《npj数字医学》上的论文《用于脓毒症早期预测的大语言模型系统的开发与前瞻性实施》。It ships an open-source system called COMPOSER-LLM, and we're reading it because it's a validated, deployed-in-production pattern for pairing a structured risk model with an LLM that reads unstructured notes — exactly the kind of hybrid architecture a registry tool needs. 它开源了一个叫COMPOSER-LLM的系统,我们读它是因为它是一个已经在生产环境中部署、经过验证的模式——把一个结构化风险模型和一个能读取非结构化病历笔记的LLM配对起来,而这正是一个登记库工具所需要的那种混合架构。

JORDAN Before we get into the model, what's the clinical backdrop here? 在讲模型之前,先说说这里的临床背景是什么?

ALEX Sepsis is the body's dysregulated response to infection, and it's a leading cause of in-hospital death — about one in three US hospital deaths is attributable to it. 脓毒症是人体对感染产生的失调反应,是院内死亡的主要原因之一——在美国,大约每三例院内死亡中就有一例与脓毒症有关。The field uses the Sepsis-3 consensus definition, which requires evidence of infection plus organ dysfunction, and organ dysfunction is quantified with the SOFA score — the Sequential Organ Failure Assessment, a point scale across six organ systems where an increase of two or more points signals dysfunction. 该领域使用Sepsis-3共识定义,要求同时具备感染证据和器官功能障碍,而器官功能障碍是用SOFA评分来量化的——即序贯器官衰竭评估,这是一个覆盖六个器官系统的评分量表,评分增加两分或以上就意味着出现了功能障碍。

ALEX One more piece of vocabulary before we go further: a Best Practice Advisory, or BPA, is the standard EHR mechanism for firing a clinical alert — a pop-up or nurse-facing notification triggered by a rule or model score inside the electronic health record. 再补充一个术语:最佳实践提醒(Best Practice Advisory,简称BPA)是电子健康记录(EHR)系统中触发临床警报的标准机制——一个由规则或模型分数触发的弹窗或面向护士的通知。Every false alarm from a sepsis model becomes a BPA that a nurse has to interrupt their workflow to evaluate, which is the whole reason false-alarm rate matters as much as sensitivity here. 脓毒症模型产生的每一次误报都会变成一次BPA,护士必须中断手头的工作去评估它,这正是为什么在这里误报率和敏感度同等重要的原因。

ALEX The background is straightforward: COMPOSER, the structured model this paper builds on, was already deployed at two UCSD emergency departments and was associated with a 17% relative reduction in in-hospital sepsis mortality in a prior quality improvement study. COMPOSER,也就是这篇论文所基于的结构化模型,此前已经在两家UCSD急诊科部署,并在一项质量改进研究中显示与院内脓毒症死亡率相对下降17%相关。It's a deep neural network that ingests vital signs, labs, demographics, comorbidities and medications hourly, and it includes a conformal prediction module specifically to reject out-of-distribution inputs that might arise from data entry errors. 它是一个深度神经网络,每小时摄入生命体征、化验结果、人口学信息、合并症和用药情况,并且包含一个conformal prediction模块,专门用来拒绝那些可能由数据录入错误导致的分布外(out-of-distribution)输入。

ALEX The problem the authors set out to fix is that COMPOSER, like most structured-data sepsis models, ignores clinical notes entirely — and notes often carry exactly the context that disambiguates a real septic patient from a sepsis-mimic. 作者试图解决的问题是:COMPOSER和大多数纯结构化数据的脓毒症模型一样,完全忽略了临床笔记——而笔记里往往恰好包含能把真正的脓毒症患者和"脓毒症模拟症"区分开的关键背景信息。Prior approaches to using notes — document-level embeddings from something like ClinicalBERT, topic modeling, or tf-idf — tend to lose local, specific context, offer poor interpretability, and are prone to picking up hospital-specific copy-paste artifacts rather than genuine clinical signal. 此前使用笔记的方法——比如用ClinicalBERT这类模型生成文档级嵌入、主题建模,或tf-idf——往往会丢失局部、具体的语境,可解释性差,而且容易学到某家医院特有的"复制粘贴"痕迹,而不是真正的临床信号。

JORDAN Why not just make the structured model bigger or add more features, instead of bolting on a whole separate LLM pipeline? 为什么不干脆把结构化模型做得更大、加更多特征,而不是再挂上一整套独立的LLM流程?

ALEX Because the information that disambiguates sepsis from a mimic often just isn't present as a structured field at all — a nurse's free-text description of "cold, clammy skin with a history of GI bleed" is the kind of detail that distinguishes hypovolemic shock from septic shock, and no amount of scaling a model trained only on vitals and labs recovers information that was never coded as a discrete variable in the first place. 因为能把脓毒症和"脓毒症模拟症"区分开的信息,往往根本就没有以结构化字段的形式存在——护士用自由文本写下的"皮肤湿冷、有消化道出血史"这类细节,正是区分低血容量性休克和脓毒性休克的关键,而无论把一个只用生命体征和化验数据训练的模型做得多大,都无法找回那些从一开始就没有被编码成离散变量的信息。That's the structural argument for why you need a second data modality, not just a bigger model on the first one. 这就是为什么需要引入第二种数据模态、而不只是把第一种模态上的模型做大的结构性理由。

JORDAN So how does the LLM actually get used — does it replace COMPOSER? 那这个LLM到底是怎么用的——它取代了COMPOSER吗?

ALEX No, it's a selective second opinion, only invoked when COMPOSER is uncertain. 不,它是一个有选择性的"二次意见",只有在COMPOSER拿不准的时候才会被调用。COMPOSER produces an hourly risk score; above a high threshold of 0.75 it fires an alert outright, and below 0.5 it stays quiet. COMPOSER每小时输出一个风险分数;如果高于0.75这个高阈值,就直接触发警报;如果低于0.5,就保持静默。For the uncertain middle band, 0.5 to 0.75 — which is where most false alarms cluster — the LLM steps in. 而对于0.5到0.75这个不确定的中间区间——大多数误报都聚集在这里——LLM就会介入。

ALEX In that band, an instruction fine-tuned Mixtral 8x7B model uses retrieval augmented generation, or RAG, to pull the most relevant chunks from the patient's clinical notes — the ED provider note, history and physical, progress notes, and imaging reports — and extract whether each of 51 predefined clinical signs and symptoms is present, using a prompt structured around a specific sign or symptom at a time. 在这个区间里,一个经过指令微调的Mixtral 8x7B模型会用检索增强生成(retrieval augmented generation,简称RAG)技术,从患者的临床笔记——急诊医生记录、病史与体格检查、病程记录、影像报告——中抽取最相关的片段,并针对51个预先定义好的临床体征和症状,逐一判断每一个是否存在,每次的提示词都围绕一个具体体征或症状展开。The notes get chunked into 1,000-token pieces with 300-token overlap, embedded with a HuggingFace Instructor model, stored in a Chroma vector database, and the top five most relevant chunks are retrieved for each query. 笔记被切分成每段1000个token、重叠300个token的片段,用HuggingFace的Instructor模型做嵌入,存入Chroma向量数据库,每次查询检索出最相关的前五个片段。To limit hallucination, the temperature is set low at 0.3, and each extraction is run three times with majority vote deciding the final answer. 为了限制幻觉(hallucination),温度参数被设得很低,为0.3,而且每次抽取都会运行三次,以多数结果作为最终答案。

ALEX Those extracted signs and symptoms feed a Bayesian likelihood calculator that ranks 19 possible diagnoses — severe sepsis plus 18 sepsis-mimics like cardiogenic shock, pulmonary embolism and diabetic ketoacidosis — and an alert only fires if severe sepsis lands in the top five differentials and the LLM separately confirms a suspicion of bacterial infection. 这些抽取出的体征和症状会输入一个贝叶斯似然计算器,对19种可能的诊断进行排序——重度脓毒症加上18种"脓毒症模拟症",比如心源性休克、肺栓塞和糖尿病酮症酸中毒——只有当重度脓毒症进入前五名鉴别诊断列表、并且LLM另外确认存在细菌感染怀疑时,警报才会触发。

ALEX Here's my pushback: the disease-given-symptom likelihoods that drive that Bayesian calculator were derived by two of the study's own authors and then tuned with Bayesian optimization on the same development cohort. 我的质疑在于:驱动这个贝叶斯计算器的"给定症状后疾病的似然值"是由研究的两位作者自行拟定的,然后又在同一个开发队列上用贝叶斯优化做了调参。That's a reasonable way to bootstrap a system, but it also means the differential diagnosis logic is only as good as two clinicians' priors plus whatever the development cohort happened to contain — it's a hand-built expert system wrapped around the LLM's extraction, not something learned end-to-end, and that combination of manual priors and single-site tuning is exactly the kind of thing that can silently fail to transfer to a different patient population. 这是引导系统起步的合理方式,但也意味着鉴别诊断逻辑的好坏,完全取决于这两位临床医生的先验判断,加上开发队列恰好包含了什么样的数据——这本质上是围绕LLM抽取结果搭建的一个手工专家系统,而不是端到端学习出来的东西,而这种"人工先验加单中心调参"的组合,恰恰是那种可能在悄无声息中无法迁移到另一个患者人群的问题。

ALEX One design choice worth naming before results: the authors also built and tested a simpler baseline where the LLM is just asked directly, in one shot, whether the notes describe severe sepsis — no extraction of individual signs and symptoms, no Bayesian differential diagnosis layer. 在讲结果之前,还有一个值得点名的设计选择:作者还构建并测试了一个更简单的基线方法,直接一次性问LLM笔记里是否描述了重度脓毒症——不抽取单独的体征和症状,也不经过贝叶斯鉴别诊断这一层。That baseline, called COMPOSER-LLM-baseline, badly underperformed the full pipeline, with sensitivity of just 34.4% on the same retrospective cohort. 这个被称为COMPOSER-LLM-baseline的简化版本,表现明显不如完整流程,在同一个回顾性队列上敏感度只有34.4%。That comparison is the clearest evidence in the paper that structuring the LLM's task — extract discrete signs and symptoms, then reason over them with an explicit probabilistic model — matters more than just handing a raw clinical question to a capable LLM and trusting the answer. 这个对比是论文中最清楚的证据,说明把LLM的任务结构化——先抽取离散的体征和症状,再用一个显式的概率模型对它们进行推理——比直接把一个原始的临床问题丢给一个能力不错的LLM、然后相信它给出的答案要重要得多。

JORDAN Did the numbers back up the approach? 数据支持这个方法吗?

ALEX Clearly, yes. 明确地说,是的。On the retrospective validation cohort of 1,746 ED encounters, standalone COMPOSER had a sensitivity of 72.9%, a positive predictive value of just 22.6%, and 0.037 false alarms per patient hour. 在包含1746例急诊就诊记录的回顾性验证队列上,单独的COMPOSER模型敏感度为72.9%,阳性预测值仅为22.6%,每患者小时误报率为0.037。Adding the LLM pipeline held sensitivity essentially flat at 72.1% while more than doubling PPV to 52.9% and cutting false alarms per patient hour more than fourfold, to 0.0087. 加入LLM流程后,敏感度基本持平在72.1%,而阳性预测值提高到了52.9%以上,提升超过一倍,同时每患者小时误报率下降了四倍以上,降到了0.0087。In concrete terms, that's the difference between roughly 1.5 false alarms every two hours on a 20-bed unit and about a third of one. 换算成具体感受,这大约相当于一个20张床位的病区里,从每两小时约1.5次误报,降到大约三分之一次。

ALEX Prospective deployment across two EDs from May to June 2024 held up almost identically — 70.8% sensitivity, 58.2% PPV, 0.0086 false alarms per patient hour — so this wasn't a retrospective-only result that fell apart in production. 从2024年5月到6月在两家急诊科的前瞻性部署结果几乎一致——敏感度70.8%,阳性预测值58.2%,每患者小时误报率0.0086——所以这不是一个只在回顾性数据上好看、一到生产环境就崩溃的结果。And a manual chart review of 50 false positives found that 62% of them had a genuine clinical suspicion of bacterial infection at the time of the alert, meaning most "false alarms" were still clinically reasonable flags a clinician would want to see, not nonsense triggers. 而对50例假阳性病例的人工病历审查发现,其中62%在警报触发时确实存在真实的细菌感染临床怀疑,也就是说,大多数"假阳性"其实仍然是临床医生会希望看到的合理提示,而不是毫无意义的误触发。

ALEX There's also a sub-analysis restricted to patients where a clinician had already ordered blood cultures and antibiotics — a proxy for genuine clinical suspicion of infection — and there COMPOSER-LLM pushed PPV up to 80.1% and F1 to 77.2%, versus 55.4% and 63.7% for standalone COMPOSER on the same subset. 论文还有一项限定于临床医生已经下达血培养和抗生素医嘱这一亚组的分析——作为真实临床感染怀疑的代理指标——在这个亚组中,COMPOSER-LLM把阳性预测值推高到了80.1%,F1分数推高到77.2%,而单独的COMPOSER模型在同一亚组上分别只有55.4%和63.7%。That's the strongest signal in the paper that the LLM layer is adding genuine clinical context, not just noise reduction. 这是论文中最有力的信号,表明LLM层确实增加了真实的临床背景信息,而不只是在做噪声抑制。

ALEX One more result worth flagging: for the patients COMPOSER-LLM still flagged as false positives, the differential diagnosis tool's top-five list contained the patient's actual eventual diagnosis 83.1% of the time in the retrospective cohort and 73.2% prospectively. 还有一个值得一提的结果:在那些COMPOSER-LLM仍然标记为假阳性的患者中,鉴别诊断工具给出的前五名列表里包含了患者最终真实诊断的比例,在回顾性队列中为83.1%,在前瞻性队列中为73.2%。So even when the alert was technically wrong about sepsis, the system was usually still pointing the clinician toward the right diagnosis — which reframes a chunk of the "false alarm" cost as a diagnostic aid rather than pure noise. 也就是说,即使警报在"是否为脓毒症"这个判断上技术性地出错了,系统通常仍然把临床医生指向了正确的诊断方向——这就把一部分所谓的"误报"成本,重新定义为一种诊断辅助,而不是纯粹的噪声。
