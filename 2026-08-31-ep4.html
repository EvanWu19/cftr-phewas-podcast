Ep4 — Track C (2/2): SITAR-d: extending the SITAR growth curve model to allow for variability in post-pubertal velocity

Abstract-only this episode — no PMCID exists for this record and Europe PMC marks it closed access. Cole, Sandhu, and Elhakeem (Cole is also lead author of the original SITAR paper already in our catalog, 2026-08-21) extend SITAR with a fourth random effect, "post-growth," letting post-pubertal weight velocity vary between individuals — addressing SITAR's known weakness for weight, which (unlike height) keeps changing well after puberty. Fit to height and log-weight for 3,172 boys (128,157 measurements) from Christ's Hospital School, England, birth cohorts 1936–1969, SITAR-d improved residual SD for both height (0.74→0.67 cm) and weight (2.52%→2.38%), and the cohort showed a secular trend toward earlier puberty timing (but not adult mean size) across the study period. This is directly relevant to a well-documented, general clinical fact worth naming: CFTR modulator therapy is known to cause substantial weight gain in patients well past puberty, meaning any growth model assuming stable post-pubertal weight change risks misreading a genuine drug-driven trajectory shift as an anomaly. But the model itself was built and validated on a historical, all-male, single-school cohort decades removed from any modern chronic-disease or modulator-treated population, so it lands as a candidate alternative expected-value model to test, not a ready-to-use one.

Plain-English glossary

  • SITAR (SuperImposition by Translation and Rotation) — a growth-curve model summarizing an individual's curve relative to a shared population curve using per-person size (vertical shift), tempo (pubertal timing shift), and velocity (age-axis stretch) parameters.（中文：SITAR（平移旋转叠加法），一种生长曲线模型，用逐人的size（垂直位移）、tempo（青春期时间点位移）、velocity（年龄轴拉伸）三个参数，将个体曲线相对于群体共享曲线进行概括。）
  • Post-growth random effect — SITAR-d's new fourth parameter, letting post-pubertal weight velocity vary between individuals, addressing weight's tendency to keep changing after height plateaus.（中文：post-growth（生长后）随机效应，SITAR-d新增的第四个参数，使青春期后的体重速度可以因人而异，用以解决体重在身高趋于平稳后仍持续变化的问题。）
  • Log-weight modeling — fitting weight on the log scale so residuals are proportional (a percentage of the person's own weight) rather than absolute, keeping error comparable across very different body sizes.（中文：对数体重建模，在对数尺度上拟合体重，使残差表现为比例（个人自身体重的百分比）而非绝对值，从而在体型差异很大的个体间保持误差的可比性。）
  • Secular trend (growth) — a systematic shift in growth timing or size across successive birth cohorts, distinct from any individual-level or disease-driven change.（中文：（生长）长期趋势，指连续出生队列间生长时间点或体型的系统性变化，区别于任何个体层面或疾病驱动的变化。）
  • CFTR modulator-driven weight gain — a well-documented clinical effect of CFTR modulator therapy (e.g. the 2019 US-approved triple-combination drug), producing substantial weight increase in patients including those well past puberty, for pharmacological rather than measurement-error reasons.（中文：CFTR调制剂驱动的体重增加，CFTR调制剂疗法（如2019年在美获批的三联复方药物）已有充分记录的临床效应，会使患者（包括已过青春期者）体重显著增加，原因是药理作用而非测量误差。）
