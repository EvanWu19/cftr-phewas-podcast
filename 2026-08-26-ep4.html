Ep4 — Equity-focused individual-level filtering for EHR big-data cleaning (abstract-only)

Buchanan, Hopkins, Boyer, and Fohner (International Journal of Epidemiology, 2025) argue that standard EHR data-filtering rules are tuned to what they call "socially constructed dominant populations," risking systematic data loss in marginalized/Indigenous communities whose biologically normal values fall outside majority-tuned cutoffs. Tested on Yukon-Kuskokwim Health Corporation EHR data (1,262,035 records, 12,402 individuals, 2002-2012, largely Yup'ik/Alaska Native), their "phenomenological" (individual-level) approach has three components: (1) exclude absolute biologically-impossible values, (2) exclude values >3 SD from an individual's own longitudinal mean rather than a population reference, (3) impute missing quantitative/qualitative values. Reported qualitatively: more observations/participants/outcome-range retained than a common population-derived filter, with a sensitivity analysis finding no compromise to downstream result integrity — but no quantitative retention percentages or effect sizes are given in the abstract; this episode is genuinely abstract-only (Europe PMC fullTextXML and BioC routes both empty despite a PMCID existing) and flagged as such throughout. Structurally close to growthcleanr's within-child EWMA logic, generalized to adult/general EHR data and reframed around an explicit equity argument.

Plain-English glossary

  • Phenomenological (as used here) — Loosely, "individual-centered": filtering rules built around each person's own measurement history rather than a fixed population-derived cutoff.（中文：在此处宽泛地指"以个体为中心"：围绕每个人自身的测量历史构建过滤规则，而非使用固定的、基于人群推导的截断阈值。）
  • Population-derived cutoff — A threshold (e.g., WHO/CDC biologically-implausible-value bounds) computed from where a large reference population's values fall.（中文：一个从大型参考人群数值分布中计算得出的阈值（例如WHO/CDC的生物学不可信数值边界）。）
  • Community-based participatory research — A research approach developed in partnership with, not just applied to, the community being studied.（中文：一种与被研究社区合作开发、而非仅仅应用于该社区的研究方法。）
  • Data filtering (in EHR-cleaning context) — The step of excluding implausible or invalid values before an EHR dataset is used for epidemiologic analysis.（中文：在EHR数据集用于流行病学分析之前，剔除不可信或无效数值的步骤。）
