Blurb

Weiskopf's 2017 paper in EGEMS introduces 3x3 DQA, a guideline for assessing electronic health record (EHR) data quality that is built around task-dependence — the idea that "good enough" data quality always depends on what you're trying to do with the data. The framework crosses three core constructs (complete, correct, current) with three data dimensions (patients, variables, time), giving nine operationalized cells, each paired with a recommended category of assessment method, but deliberately no numeric cutoffs — those are left to the user's own clinical and analytic judgment. An expert review (6 of 10 invited experts responded) rated the concrete recommendations favorably (67–100% agreement on several cells) while the more abstract framework and construct definitions scored more mixed, with real, specific critiques the authors report candidly. This is a pure vocabulary-and-framework paper — it doesn't give a new flagging algorithm — so today's buildable artifact is a 3x3 DQA coverage scorecard: a one-page mapping of existing height/weight flagging work onto the nine cells, useful for precisely describing scope to a registry lead without any specific detection method changing.

Plain-English glossary

  • Task-dependence / fitness-for-use — the principle that whether a dataset's quality is "good enough" depends entirely on the intended use, not a universal quality score.（中文：任务依赖性／适用性，指数据集质量是否"足够好"完全取决于其预期用途，而非某个放之四海而皆准的质量分数。）
  • 3x3 DQA framework — three core data quality constructs (complete, correct, current) crossed with three data dimensions (patients, variables, time), giving nine operationalized assessment cells.（中文：3x3数据质量评估框架，将三个核心数据质量构念（完整、正确、时新）与三个数据维度（患者、变量、时间）交叉，形成九个可操作化的评估格子。）
  • Concordance — agreement between two or more data elements that are expected to be consistent with each other (e.g. a diabetes diagnosis with a matching abnormal lab).（中文：一致性，指两个或多个本应彼此一致的数据元素之间的吻合程度（例如糖尿病诊断与对应的异常化验值相符）。）
  • Plausibility — whether a value "makes sense" given external clinical knowledge (e.g. a biologically implausible lab result).（中文：合理性，指某个数值在外部临床知识下是否"说得通"（例如一个在生物学上不可能出现的化验结果）。）
  • Currency — whether data were recorded at a clinically relevant time, or with sufficient frequency and regularity to be useful.（中文：时新性，指数据是否在具有临床意义的时间点被记录，或是否以足够的频率和规律性被记录，从而具有实际价值。）
