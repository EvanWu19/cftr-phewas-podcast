How did this episode land for you?

Ep2 (Track B — AI tooling): Implementation of an opioid use disorder machine-learning phenotype in real-time for the ADAPT clinical trial

Li and colleagues (2026, PLOS Digital Health) ship a complete, deployed pipeline rather than a model-in-a-notebook: a random-forest phenotype that scores emergency-department patients for opioid use disorder (OUD) from data available at or before triage, running nightly OMOP-harmonization-then-daily-inference inside a live Epic EHR (Clarity/Caboodle) across three EDs, with under-24-hour latency between data and alert. Trained on 11 years of visit data (2014-2025, 3.1M+ unique patients) via PySpark, the random forest used count-based features (diagnosis, medication, and lab-positive counts, not binary flags) against a deliberately extreme "high-specificity computable training label" (>250 cumulative OUD ICD codes). Retrospective discrimination against that label was striking — AUROC 0.99, AUPRC 0.92 — but a 217-patient gold-standard validation sample, built by stratified sampling of model-positive and model-negative encounters and adjudicated against DSM-5 criteria by physicians (inter-reviewer kappa 0.86), tells a more honest story once properly re-weighted: because the real-world flag rate was only 3.26% (28,284 of 866,569 eligible 2025 encounters), the raw within-stratum agreement rates (89.3%/95.7%) don't translate directly into population-level performance. Design-weighting by that flag rate yields population sensitivity ~0.40 (95% CI 0.24-0.64), specificity 0.996, and estimated OUD prevalence ~7.1% — a high-specificity, moderate-sensitivity screening tool, a genuinely different performance story than the headline AUROC suggests alone. The episode lands the design-weighted stratified-sampling evaluation strategy — not any claim about CFFPR's own flagging model — on Liz's assignment, and ends on a buildable Design-Weighted Flag-Evaluator script.

Plain-English glossary

Computable phenotype — An algorithmic, automatable definition of "does this patient have condition X" built from structured EHR fields (diagnoses, meds, labs) rather than a clinician's in-the-moment judgment.（中文：基于结构化EHR字段（诊断、用药、化验）构建的、可自动批量运行的"该病人是否患有X"的算法化定义，而非临床医生当场的主观判断。）

Design-weighted estimate — A population-level estimate (sensitivity, specificity, prevalence, etc.) computed by re-weighting a deliberately stratified sample's within-stratum results by the stratum's real frequency in the source population, rather than reporting raw sample agreement as if it applied to everyone.（中文：通过把分层抽样样本各层内的结果，按该层在源人群中真实出现的频率重新加权，得到的人群层面估计值（敏感性、特异性、患病率等），而不是直接把样本内的原始一致率当作适用于全人群的结果。）

DSM-5 criteria — The Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, 5th edition's diagnostic criteria, used here as the clinical gold-standard definition of opioid use disorder for physician chart-review adjudication.（中文：《精神障碍诊断与统计手册》第五版的诊断标准，在本文中作为医生病历审查判定阿片类药物使用障碍的临床金标准。）

ETL (extract-transform-load) — The pipeline step that pulls raw data out of a source system's native tables, reshapes it, and loads it into a target schema — here, into the OMOP common data model.（中文：将原始数据从源系统的原生表中抽取出来、转换格式，再加载进目标数据结构的流程步骤——本文中目标结构是OMOP通用数据模型。）

Gold standard (clinical) — The best available ground truth for validating a model, here physician chart review against DSM-5 criteria, distinct from the cheaper computable label used for training.（中文：用于验证模型的、目前能拿到的最可靠真相，本文中是医生依据DSM-5标准进行的病历审查，区别于训练时用的更廉价的可计算标签。）

OMOP common data model — A standardized schema (maintained by the OHDSI research network) that different health systems map their native EHR data into, so code and models become portable across sites that also use OMOP.（中文：由OHDSI研究网络维护的标准化数据结构，不同医疗系统将各自原生的EHR数据映射进这一结构，使代码和模型可以在同样使用OMOP的机构之间迁移复用。）

Stratified sampling (in this evaluation context) — Deliberately oversampling both model-positive and model-negative encounters when building a chart-review validation sample, so reviewers have enough of each group to adjudicate — which then requires re-weighting results back to the real population distribution.（中文：在构建病历审查验证样本时，刻意对模型判为阳性和阴性的记录都做过采样，让审阅人员两边都有足够样本可审定——这样做之后，结果必须重新加权回真实人群分布才能使用。）

Full citation

Li, et al. Implementation of an opioid use disorder (OUD) machine-learning phenotype in real-time for the ADAPT clinical trial. PLOS Digital Health. 2026. PMID: 42691440. PMCID: PMC13541308. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pdig.0001140.

Methods / tools to lift

  • OMOP/OHDSI harmonization pipeline — nightly ETL from native EHR tables into a common data model before any modeling runs.
  • PySpark feature engineering — count-based (not binary) longitudinal features at true EHR scale.
  • High-specificity computable training label — an extreme, documentation-noise-resistant threshold rule used to generate a cheap training label when gold-standard labels are too expensive to produce at scale.
  • Design-weighted stratified-sample evaluation — the reusable statistical technique for validating a rare-outcome flagging model without reviewing the full population.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Liz's CFFPR data-quality assignment — method only (the evaluation strategy, not any claim about the CFFPR flagging model itself). Buildable artifact: Design-Weighted Flag-Evaluator — a small script that takes total/flagged encounter counts plus a stratified, adjudicated chart-review sample and outputs design-weighted prevalence, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, and NPV with confidence intervals (delta method or stratified bootstrap). Rough difficulty: S-M — a contained statistics problem with a clear worked example to mirror, no model training required.

What to verify first

  • How much of the 0.99 AUROC against the training label reflects documentation/utilization density rather than OUD itself — the authors name this confound but don't quantify it with a dedicated stratified sensitivity analysis.
  • Whether global-mode imputation introduces measurable bias in specific features or subgroups — not tested directly in the paper.
  • Whether a single-health-system, single-EHR-vendor pipeline generalizes to a different vendor's data structure or population — an open question per the authors, not a settled one.
  • Whether the adjudication-sample size needed for a reasonably tight design-weighted confidence interval (still wide here at n=217) is achievable within a given registry team's actual review budget — not answered by this paper.

JORDAN One more — what's a "gold standard" doing in a sentence about machine learning, instead of in a sentence about CF genetics? 再问一个——"金标准"（gold standard）这个词怎么跑到机器学习这边来了，不是CF遗传学里才用的吗？

ALEX Same meaning, different domain — here it's physician chart review against the DSM-5 diagnostic criteria for opioid use disorder, done by trained reviewers, used as the best available truth to check the model against, as opposed to the cheaper computable label used for training. 意思是一样的，只是换了个领域——这里指的是训练过的审阅人员，依据DSM-5（《精神障碍诊断与统计手册》第五版）对阿片类药物使用障碍的诊断标准所做的人工病历审查，被当作目前能拿到的最可靠真相，用来检验模型，而不是训练时用的那种更廉价的可计算标签。

JORDAN What problem is this actually trying to solve? 这篇论文到底想解决什么问题？

ALEX Start with the scale of the thing. 先说规模。The authors cite more than 109,000 US overdose deaths in 2022 alone, with nearly 70 percent involving opioids, and emergency departments are frequently a person's only point of contact with the healthcare system before or after an overdose. 作者引用的数据是，仅2022年美国就有超过109000人死于药物过量，其中近70%涉及阿片类药物，而急诊科往往是这些人在过量用药前后唯一接触医疗系统的地方。Buprenorphine — a medication for opioid use disorder — reduces both illicit opioid use and opioid-related mortality, and can be started right in the ED. 丁丙诺啡（Buprenorphine）是一种用于治疗阿片类药物使用障碍的药物，既能减少非法阿片使用，也能降低阿片相关死亡率，而且可以直接在急诊科启动使用。Despite that, only about 6 to 8 percent of eligible patients actually receive it there. 尽管如此，符合条件的病人中实际在急诊科用上这个药的，只有大约6%到8%。

JORDAN Why such a gap if the medication works and can be started immediately? 既然这个药有效、又能立即启用，为什么会有这么大的差距？

ALEX A mix of things — prescriber inexperience with the medication, workflow friction, concerns about diversion, and persistent stigma. 原因是多方面的——开药医生对这种药物不熟悉、工作流程上的阻力、担心药物被转卖滥用，以及持续存在的病耻感。The authors' own group had already tried a clinical decision support tool for this in an earlier trial called EMBED, and it didn't move population-level buprenorphine initiation rates. 作者所在团队之前在一项叫EMBED的试验里已经试过一种临床决策支持（clinical decision support）工具，但它并没有带动整体人群层面的丁丙诺啡启用率。The new ADAPT trial is explicitly trying to improve on that, using what's called a Multiphase Optimization Strategy design, and this paper is the piece that comes before the trial itself — building and validating the patient-identification engine the trial will depend on. 新的ADAPT试验明确是想在此基础上做改进，用的是所谓的多阶段优化策略（Multiphase Optimization Strategy）设计，而这篇论文是试验正式开始之前的准备工作——构建并验证试验将依赖的病人识别引擎。

JORDAN Why does identification need a machine-learning model instead of just, say, a billing-code rule? 为什么识别病人需要机器学习模型，而不是直接用账单编码规则？

ALEX Because diagnosis codes for OUD are typically applied at the end of the encounter or afterward, which is too late to trigger an in-the-moment alert, and rule-based approaches anchored only to diagnosis codes systematically under-detect OUD that hasn't been coded yet. 因为OUD的诊断编码通常是在就诊结束时或之后才录入的，这对触发"当场"警报来说已经太晚了，而且只依赖诊断编码的规则方法，系统性地漏检那些还没被编码的OUD。The authors wanted something that scores risk from data available at or before triage — before anyone has written down a diagnosis — so the alert can actually influence the visit it fires during, not just end up as a retrospective note in the chart. 作者想要的是能用分诊之时或之前就有的数据来打风险分数的东西——在任何人写下诊断之前——这样警报才能真正影响到它触发的这次就诊，而不是最终只变成病历里一条回顾性的记录。

JORDAN And this had to work across a huge amount of historical data to even get built. 而且这个模型得先在海量历史数据上训练才能建出来。

ALEX Right — the retrospective development phase pulled eleven years of visit data, 2014 through 2025, across three emergency departments in one US health system, covering over 3.1 million unique patients before any class-balancing was applied for training. 对——回顾性开发阶段用了十一年的就诊数据，从2014年到2025年，覆盖美国同一个医疗系统下的三个急诊科，在为训练做任何类别平衡处理之前，覆盖了超过310万名独立病人。

JORDAN So walk me through what they actually built. 具体讲讲他们到底搭建了什么。

ALEX All data came out of the health system's Epic EHR — specifically its Clarity and Caboodle data stores — through an ETL pipeline into that OMOP-harmonized Computational Health Platform I mentioned. 所有数据都来自该医疗系统的Epic电子病历系统——具体是它的Clarity和Caboodle数据存储——经过ETL流程进入我刚提到的那个经OMOP标准化的计算医疗平台。Feature engineering and model development ran in PySpark, meaning this was built to scale across a true big-data volume rather than a single analyst's laptop-sized sample. 特征工程和模型开发都是在PySpark上跑的，也就是说这套系统是按真正的大数据体量来设计的，而不是分析师笔记本电脑上能处理的那种小样本。Features were counts, not simple yes/no flags — number of relevant diagnoses, number of medication orders, number of positive lab results — specifically chosen to preserve longitudinal burden and utilization signal rather than collapsing it into a binary indicator. 特征用的是计数，而不是简单的有/无标记——相关诊断的数量、用药处方的数量、化验阳性结果的数量——这样选择是为了保留纵向的疾病负担和医疗使用信号，而不是把它压缩成一个二元指标。

JORDAN And the label they trained against wasn't a clinician's judgment? 他们训练用的标签不是临床医生的判断？

ALEX Right, because manual physician adjudication at scale is expensive and OUD prevalence in the ED population is low. 对，因为大规模人工医生审定的成本很高，而且急诊人群里OUD的患病率本身就低。So they built what they call a high-specificity computable training label: a patient counts as a training-positive only if they have more than 250 cumulative OUD-related ICD codes in their record. 所以他们构建了一个他们称之为"高特异性可计算训练标签"的东西：只有当一个病人病历里累积的OUD相关ICD编码超过250次，才被算作训练集里的阳性样本。That's a deliberately extreme threshold, chosen specifically to avoid false positives from copy-and-paste documentation habits and codes appearing redundantly across the problem list, past medical history, and encounter diagnoses. 这是一个刻意设得很极端的阈值，专门为了避免因为复制粘贴的记录习惯，以及同一个编码在问题列表、既往病史和本次就诊诊断里重复出现而产生的假阳性。

JORDAN Then the model itself? 那模型本身呢？

ALEX A random forest classifier, trained on a balanced dataset — majority-class undersampling followed by minority-class oversampling — then evaluated on a held-out test set. 是一个随机森林分类器，在一个经过平衡处理的数据集上训练——先对多数类欠采样，再对少数类过采样——然后在一个留出的测试集上评估。Deployment itself runs nightly OMOP harmonization followed by daily inference, closing the loop between data and alert with latency under 24 hours. 部署流程本身是每晚做一次OMOP标准化处理，然后每天做一次推理，让数据到警报之间的闭环延迟控制在24小时以内。

JORDAN Where would you push back on the design? 你会对这个设计提出什么质疑？

ALEX Two spots. 两个地方。First, the training label and the count-based features both encode the same underlying thing — how much contact a patient has had with the health system — so some of that spectacular retrospective discrimination may reflect documentation density rather than OUD itself; a patient with true OUD but thin prior contact is exactly the person most likely to get both a negative label and a negative prediction, which is a blind spot baked into the method, not just a limitation mentioned in passing. 第一，训练标签和基于计数的特征，底层编码的其实是同一件事——病人和医疗系统接触的密集程度——所以那个惊人的回顾性判别能力，有一部分可能反映的是记录密度，而不是OUD本身；一个真正患有OUD、但之前跟系统接触很少的病人，恰恰最容易同时拿到阴性标签和阴性预测，这是方法本身内置的盲点，不只是随口提一句的局限。Second, missing values were imputed using the simple global mode — the single most common value across everyone — which systematically overrepresents common values and can obscure real between-patient and between-site variability; it's a defensible first pass, but a plain default, not a carefully chosen missingness model. 第二，缺失值用的是简单的全局众数填补——也就是所有人里最常见的那个值——这会系统性地放大常见值的比例，可能掩盖病人之间、机构之间真实存在的差异；作为第一版处理方式可以理解，但这只是一个普通的默认做法，不是精心设计的缺失值模型。

JORDAN Did the authors acknowledge that themselves? 作者自己承认了这些问题吗？

ALEX To their credit, yes, both points are explicitly in their limitations section — this isn't a case where I'm the one surfacing a hidden flaw, it's a case of taking their own stated caveats seriously enough to put weight on them. 值得肯定的是，是的，这两点都明确写在他们的局限性部分里——这不是我在挖出什么隐藏的缺陷，而是认真对待他们自己已经说明的警示，并给它们应有的权重。

JORDAN Did it actually work? 这个模型实际效果如何？

ALEX On raw discrimination, strikingly well, at least against the training label it was built on: an area under the ROC curve of 0.99, and an area under the precision-recall curve of 0.92, which matters more than AUROC alone given how imbalanced OUD is in a general ED population. 从原始判别能力来看，表现非常亮眼，至少对它训练所依据的那个标签来说：ROC曲线下面积（AUROC）达到0.99，精确率-召回率曲线下面积（AUPRC）为0.92——考虑到一般急诊人群里OUD的类别极度不平衡，后面这个指标比单看AUROC更重要。But that's discrimination against the computable label, not against a clinical truth standard, so it's not directly comparable to prior chart-review-validated OUD models reporting AUROCs in the 0.80 to 0.95 range. 但这是针对可计算标签的判别能力，不是针对临床真实标准的，所以不能直接拿来跟之前那些用病历审查验证过的OUD模型相比，那些模型报告的AUROC在0.80到0.95之间。

JORDAN What happened when they checked it against actual physician adjudication? 那拿去跟真正的医生审定对比时，结果怎么样？
