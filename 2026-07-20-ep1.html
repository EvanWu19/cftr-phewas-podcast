Ep1 — Track A: fourteen "deleterious" CFTR nsSNPs, and how many independent votes a predictor panel really has

Blurb. Abbas and colleagues (Scientific Reports, 2026) pulled all 1,843 non-synonymous SNPs of CFTR from Ensembl (transcript ENST00000003084.11), triaged them to 14 with a stack of in-silico deleteriousness predictors, characterized those 14 with structure and stability tools plus AlphaFold and AlphaMissense, and then genotyped 2 of them by tetra-primer ARMS-PCR in 100 hepatocellular carcinoma patients and 100 controls. The top hit, rs397508297 (E588V), sits in the ATPase domain, scores 0.977 on MetaRNN and 0.97 on REVEL, and is called destabilizing by all three ΔΔG tools. The episode reads the paper as a teaching case rather than a result: the six-predictor "consensus" is built largely from meta-predictors that consume each other's outputs, the ΔΔG tools sign-flip on four variants, the PyMOL superimposition RMSD of 0.00 is a procedural artefact rather than a finding, and the reported case-control contrast (70% heterozygotes in patients vs 19% in controls) sits alongside a control group that itself deviates from Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium — the classic genotyping-error signature. Where it lands: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation. Buildable artifact: a predictor-correlation auditor that reports how many effectively independent predictor votes a VUS evidence table actually contains.

Plain-English glossary

  • ABC transporter — the protein family CFTR belongs to (ATP-binding cassette): two membrane-spanning domains forming the channel pore, two nucleotide-binding domains that bind and hydrolyse ATP to gate it, and a regulatory domain controlled by phosphorylation.（中文：ABC 转运蛋白家族——CFTR 所属的蛋白家族，由跨膜结构域、结合并水解 ATP 的核苷酸结合结构域和受磷酸化调控的调节结构域组成。）
  • Allosteric site — a location where binding changes the protein's activity at a distant site; for an ATP-gated channel, losing one is mechanistically meaningful.（中文：变构位点——在某处发生结合会改变蛋白质远端活性的位点。）
  • AlphaMissense — a deep-learning predictor that assigns a pathogenicity score to every possible missense substitution in a protein, viewable as a heatmap over the AlphaFold structure.（中文：AlphaMissense——为蛋白质中每一种可能的错义替换打致病性分数的深度学习预测工具。）
  • ARMS-PCR (tetra-primer) — allele-specific PCR: primers designed to extend on only one allele, so the presence or absence of a band calls the genotype.（中文：ARMS-PCR——等位基因特异性 PCR，靠条带有无判读基因型。）
  • ΔΔG (delta-delta-G) — predicted change in protein folding free energy caused by a variant; negative values mean the variant destabilizes the folded protein.（中文：ΔΔG——变异引起的蛋白质折叠自由能变化，负值表示去稳定。）
  • HGVS — the standardized nomenclature for writing variants, always anchored to an explicit reference transcript (e.g. p.Glu588Val rather than legacy E588V).（中文：HGVS——变异命名的标准化书写规范，必须绑定明确的参考转录本。）
  • Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium (HWE) — the expected relationship between allele and genotype frequencies in a large randomly-mating population; deviation in controls usually signals genotyping error or population structure, not biology.（中文：哈迪-温伯格平衡——群体中等位基因频率与基因型频率的预期关系；对照组偏离通常提示分型错误或群体结构问题。）
  • Meta-predictor — a variant-effect tool that takes the outputs of other predictors as input features (CADD, REVEL, MetaLR, PredictSNP2), which is why their agreement is correlated by construction.（中文：元预测器——把其他预测工具的输出当作输入特征的变异效应工具，因此它们之间的一致性天然相关。）
  • Missense / non-synonymous SNP (nsSNP) — a single-base change that alters the encoded amino acid; the hard class, because the effect ranges from catastrophic to completely inert.（中文：错义变异／非同义 SNP——改变所编码氨基酸的单碱基变异，效应从致命到完全无害都有，是最难判读的一类。）
  • Nonsense variant — a change introducing a premature stop codon, truncating the protein; almost always damaging.（中文：无义变异——引入提前终止密码子、使蛋白质截短的变异，几乎总是有害的。）
  • pLDDT — AlphaFold's per-residue confidence score (0–100); above 90 means high confidence in that residue's predicted position.（中文：pLDDT——AlphaFold 对每个残基位置预测的置信度评分，0–100，高于 90 表示置信度高。）
  • RMSD — root-mean-square deviation of atomic positions between two superimposed structures; 0.00 means no detectable structural difference at all.（中文：RMSD——两个叠合结构之间原子位置的均方根偏差，0.00 表示完全无可检测差异。）
  • rsID — a dbSNP accession pointing at a genomic position and its alleles; it is not a protein-consequence statement.（中文：rsID——dbSNP 数据库的变异编号，指向基因组位置及其等位基因，并不表示蛋白质层面的后果。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Ensembl variant table export, anchored to an explicit transcript (ENST00000003084.11) — the right instinct; everything downstream should inherit that anchor.
  • Two-stage triage: coarse filter (PredictSNP2, CADD, FunSeq2, GWAVA) then fine scoring (SIFT, PolyPhen-2, CADD, REVEL, MetaLR, Mutation Assessor) — a reusable funnel shape, but see the correlation caveat.
  • ΔΔG panel: SDM, MUpro, DynaMut — worth running as a disagreement detector, not as a consensus generator.
  • MutPred2 — predicts specific functional consequences (e.g. loss of allosteric site at F587, p = 0.03) rather than a single score; more actionable for mechanism-writing.
  • Project HOPE — physicochemical interpretation of a substitution (size, charge, hydrophobicity, domain, conservation) in plain language.
  • AlphaFold + AlphaMissense heatmap — structure with per-residue confidence (pLDDT) plus a per-substitution pathogenicity surface.
  • dbNSFP (not used by the paper, needed for the artifact) — precompiled predictor scores for every possible human missense variant; the practical way to assemble a multi-predictor table.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority served: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation.

predictor-correlation-auditor (S–M) — input a list of CFTR variants normalized to HGVS; pull SIFT, PolyPhen-2, CADD, REVEL, MetaLR, MutationAssessor and AlphaMissense from dbNSFP; output (a) a pairwise rank-correlation matrix across predictors on that variant set, (b) a curated provenance map of which tools consume which others as input features, and (c) a headline effective-independent-predictor count so a VUS evidence table cannot double-count one signal wearing six hats.

What to verify first

  • Run HWE on the control group before reading any association. Controls here deviate badly (observed A-A/T-T/A-T = 63/17/19 vs expected 52.56/7.56/39.88) — heterozygote deficit in controls is the textbook signature of allele dropout or population structure.
  • Confirm ARMS-PCR calls by Sanger on a subset. A rare variant appearing heterozygous in 70% of a 100-person series is not a plausible population-genetic observation; assay specificity is the leading hypothesis.
  • Account for the selection funnel in multiple testing — 14 variants chosen by predictors, 2 genotyped, significance reported at an uncorrected p < 0.05.
  • Do not let "predicted to damage CFTR channel function" slide into "cancer risk allele." Different claims, different evidence requirements.
  • Normalize legacy protein notation (E588V) to HGVS on the current reference transcript before joining to CFTR2, ClinVar or gnomAD.
  • Treat the RMSD = 0.00 superimposition as a procedural artefact — in-silico mutation in PyMOL swaps a side chain without re-folding the backbone.
  • Check ancestry composition of cases and controls — gnomAD allele frequency is the implicit denominator for every predictor in the stack.

JORDAN Before the methods, give me the vocabulary. 在讲方法之前，先给我把词汇过一遍。 What is an nsSNP? nsSNP 是什么？

ALEX A SNP is a single-nucleotide polymorphism — one base position in the genome that differs between people. SNP 是单核苷酸多态性 (single nucleotide polymorphism)，就是基因组上某一个碱基位置在人与人之间存在差异。 A non-synonymous SNP, nsSNP, is one that changes the amino acid the codon encodes. 非同义 SNP (non-synonymous SNP，简称 nsSNP) 指的是那个改变会让密码子编码的氨基酸发生变化。 In clinical language that is a missense variant. 用临床的说法，这就是错义变异 (missense variant)。 Contrast it with a nonsense variant, which introduces a stop codon and truncates the protein. 与之相对的是无义变异 (nonsense variant)，它会引入一个终止密码子，把蛋白质截短。 The reason missense is the hard class is that a nonsense variant is almost always bad, whereas a missense variant might be catastrophic or completely inert, and the sequence alone does not tell you which. 错义变异之所以是最难的一类，是因为无义变异几乎总是有害的，而错义变异可能是灾难性的，也可能完全无害，而单看序列本身并不能告诉你是哪一种。 That gap is the entire reason variant-effect predictors exist. 这个空白正是变异效应预测工具存在的全部理由。

JORDAN And the naming — the paper uses rs numbers and things like E588V. 还有命名方式的问题——论文里用了 rs 编号，也用了 E588V 这样的写法。

ALEX Three naming systems are in play and you have to keep them apart. 这里同时出现了三套命名系统，你必须把它们分开。 An rsID, like rs397508297, is a dbSNP accession — it points at a genomic position and its alleles, not at a protein consequence. rsID，比如 rs397508297，是 dbSNP（一个变异位点编号数据库）的登记号，它指向的是一个基因组位置和它的等位基因，而不是蛋白质层面的后果。 E588V is legacy one-letter protein notation: glutamate at residue five eighty-eight becomes valine. E588V 是传统的单字母蛋白质写法：第 588 位的谷氨酸变成了缬氨酸。 HGVS is the standardized nomenclature, and the same change would be written p.Glu588Val, always tied to an explicit reference transcript. HGVS（变异命名的标准化书写规范）是规范写法，同一个改变会写成 p.Glu588Val，并且永远绑定一个明确的参考转录本。 This paper does anchor its transcript — ENST00000003084.11 — which is more than many papers do. 这篇论文确实标明了它用的转录本 ENST00000003084.11，这一点比很多论文做得好。 But everything downstream is reported in the legacy protein style, so joining these fourteen variants to CFTR2 or ClinVar means a normalization step first. 但它下游所有结果都用传统蛋白质写法呈现，所以要把这十四个变异连到 CFTR2 或 ClinVar 上，第一步必须做标准化。

JORDAN What about the protein architecture they keep referring to? 那他们反复提到的蛋白质结构又是什么？

ALEX CFTR is an ABC transporter — ATP-binding cassette. CFTR 属于 ABC 转运蛋白家族，ABC 是 ATP 结合盒 (ATP-binding cassette) 的缩写。 It has two membrane-spanning domains that form the chloride pore, two nucleotide-binding domains that bind and hydrolyse ATP to gate the channel, and a regulatory domain in between that is controlled by phosphorylation. 它有两个跨膜结构域构成氯离子通道孔道，两个核苷酸结合结构域负责结合并水解 ATP 来控制通道开关，中间还有一个受磷酸化调控的调节结构域。 So "this variant sits in the ATPase domain" is a mechanistic claim: it says the variant may disturb the gating cycle rather than the pore itself. 所以"这个变异位于 ATP 酶结构域"是一个机制层面的说法：它意味着该变异可能扰乱的是门控循环，而不是孔道本身。 Two more terms. 还有两个词。 Delta-delta-G is the predicted change in folding free energy — negative means the variant destabilizes the folded protein. ΔΔG 是预测的折叠自由能变化——负值表示该变异让折叠后的蛋白质变得不稳定。 And pLDDT is AlphaFold's per-residue confidence score, zero to one hundred; above ninety means AlphaFold is confident about where that residue sits. pLDDT 是 AlphaFold 给每个残基的置信度打分，范围 0 到 100；高于 90 表示 AlphaFold 对该残基的位置很有把握。

JORDAN Okay. So what problem were they trying to solve? 好。那他们想解决的问题是什么？

ALEX The framing is that CFTR is not only a cystic fibrosis gene. 他们的切入点是：CFTR 不只是一个囊性纤维化基因。 CFTR expression is reported as decreased in intestinal, lung, breast, prostate and pancreatic cancers, and increased in gastric, ovarian and cervical cancers. 文献报道 CFTR 表达在肠癌、肺癌、乳腺癌、前列腺癌和胰腺癌中下降，而在胃癌、卵巢癌和宫颈癌中上升。 So there is a plausible, and genuinely under-studied, question about whether coding variation in CFTR contributes to cancer risk. 因此，CFTR 的编码区变异是否参与癌症风险，这是一个合理且确实研究不足的问题。 The operational problem is scale. 而实际操作上的问题是规模。 They pulled one thousand eight hundred forty-three nsSNPs for CFTR out of Ensembl. 他们从 Ensembl 里调出了 CFTR 的 1,843 个 nsSNP。 You cannot functionally assay eighteen hundred variants. 你不可能对一千八百个变异逐个做功能实验。 So the paper is really a triage paper: can a stack of computational tools narrow eighteen hundred candidates down to a handful worth taking to the bench? 所以这篇论文本质上是一篇分诊论文：一堆计算工具能不能把一千八百个候选缩小到值得拿去做实验的少数几个？ That question is not going away, and it is exactly the question a CFTR variant-of-uncertain-significance workflow has to answer. 这个问题不会消失，而且它恰恰就是 CFTR 意义未明变异 (VUS) 工作流必须回答的问题。

JORDAN How did they do the narrowing? 他们是怎么做这个筛选的？

ALEX Two stages. 分两个阶段。 First, a coarse filter using PredictSNP2, CADD, FunSeq2 and GWAVA — CADD being a score that integrates many annotations into one deleteriousness ranking. 第一步是粗筛，用了 PredictSNP2、CADD、FunSeq2 和 GWAVA，其中 CADD（把多种注释整合成单一有害性排名的评分）是最常见的一个。 That took eighteen forty-three down to fourteen. 这一步把 1,843 个压到了 14 个。 Second, they scored those fourteen with SIFT, PolyPhen-2, CADD again, REVEL, MetaLR and Mutation Assessor. 第二步，他们又用 SIFT、PolyPhen-2、再次用 CADD、REVEL、MetaLR 和 Mutation Assessor 给这 14 个打分。 Then structure and stability: SDM, MUpro and DynaMut for delta-delta-G, MutPred2 for predicted functional consequences, Project HOPE for physicochemical interpretation, PSIPRED for secondary structure, AlphaFold for the three-dimensional model, and the AlphaMissense pathogenicity heatmap layered on top. 然后是结构与稳定性：用 SDM、MUpro、DynaMut 算 ΔΔG，用 MutPred2 预测功能后果，用 Project HOPE 做理化性质解读，用 PSIPRED 预测二级结构，用 AlphaFold 建三维模型，再叠加 AlphaMissense 的致病性热图。 Then normal mode analysis in IMOD and short molecular dynamics in myPresto to look at flexibility. 之后用 IMOD 做简正模式分析、用 myPresto 跑短时分子动力学来观察柔性变化。 Finally, a wet-lab arm: one hundred hepatocellular carcinoma patients and one hundred controls from a hospital in Islamabad, genotyped for two of the fourteen by tetra-primer ARMS-PCR. 最后是湿实验部分：来自伊斯兰堡一家医院的 100 名肝细胞癌患者和 100 名对照，用四引物 ARMS-PCR 对 14 个中的 2 个做基因分型。 ARMS-PCR is an allele-specific PCR: you design primers that only extend on one allele, so the presence or absence of a band calls the genotype. ARMS-PCR 是等位基因特异性 PCR：你设计只能在某一个等位基因上延伸的引物，靠条带的有无来判读基因型。

ALEX Here is my design objection, and it is the one that matters most. 下面是我对设计的意见，也是最重要的一条。 The paper treats agreement across six predictors as six pieces of evidence. 这篇论文把六个预测工具的一致性当成了六份证据。 It is not. 但它不是。 CADD, REVEL, MetaLR and PredictSNP2 are meta-predictors — they consume the outputs of primary tools like SIFT and PolyPhen-2 as input features, and several were trained on overlapping pathogenic variant sets. CADD、REVEL、MetaLR 和 PredictSNP2 都是元预测器——它们把 SIFT、PolyPhen-2 这类初级工具的输出当作输入特征，而且其中几个的训练集在致病变异上是重叠的。 So consensus among them is heavily correlated by construction. 因此它们之间的一致性在设计上就是高度相关的。 Stacking correlated predictors does not add independent evidence; it multiplies one signal and makes it look like many. 把相关的预测器叠在一起并不会增加独立证据；它只是把同一个信号复制了很多份，让它看起来像很多份证据。

JORDAN Does the paper's own data show that? 论文自己的数据能看出这一点吗？

ALEX It shows the opposite problem, which is almost as interesting. 它显示的是相反的问题，而这几乎同样有意思。 The stability tools genuinely disagree, and the paper does not dwell on it. 稳定性工具之间存在真实的分歧，而论文没有深入讨论。 MUpro calls all fourteen destabilizing. MUpro 判定这十四个全都是去稳定的。 But DynaMut and SDM call D984N, D985N, R1162L and D1341G stabilizing — SDM gives D1341G plus two point three three zero kilocalories per mole and R1162L plus one point nine two zero. 但 DynaMut 和 SDM 认为 D984N、D985N、R1162L 和 D1341G 是稳定化的——SDM 给 D1341G 的值是 +2.330 千卡每摩尔，给 R1162L 是 +1.920。 That is not a rounding difference, that is a sign flip. 这不是舍入误差，这是正负号翻转。 When your predictors are correlated you get false confidence, and when they disagree you get an unresolved question — and the paper reports both situations in the same voice. 当预测器相关时你得到的是虚假的确信，当它们分歧时你得到的是悬而未决的问题——而这篇论文用同一种语气报告了这两种情况。

JORDAN What came out at the top? 最终排在最前面的是什么？

MutPred2 预测它会导致 F587 位的一个变构位点丢失，p 值为 0.03。 An allosteric site is a place where binding at one location changes protein activity at a distant location — for a channel that gates on ATP, that is a mechanistically sensible thing to lose. 变构位点 (allosteric site) 是指在某一位置发生结合会改变蛋白质在另一远端位置活性的地方——对于一个靠 ATP 门控的通道来说，丢掉这样一个位点在机制上是说得通的。 All three stability tools agreed E588V is destabilizing: minus zero point one seven, minus one point four three zero, minus zero point four five six. 三个稳定性工具在 E588V 上一致认为是去稳定的：−0.17、−1.430、−0.456。 AlphaFold's model was high confidence, most residues above pLDDT ninety. AlphaFold 的模型置信度高，大多数残基的 pLDDT 超过 90。

ALEX And then there is a result that stops me. 然后有一个结果让我停了下来。 They superimposed each mutant structure on wild type in PyMOL and report RMSD values of zero point zero zero. 他们在 PyMOL 里把每个突变体结构与野生型叠合，报告的 RMSD 值是 0.00。 RMSD is root-mean-square deviation of atomic positions — zero means no detectable structural difference at all. RMSD 是原子位置的均方根偏差——0 意味着完全检测不到结构差异。 That directly contradicts the destabilization and flexibility narrative built over the preceding ten figures. 这与前面十张图建立起来的"去稳定、柔性改变"的叙事直接矛盾。 The honest reading is that in-silico point mutation in PyMOL swaps a side chain without re-folding the backbone, so of course the backbones superimpose perfectly. 诚实的解读是：在 PyMOL 里做电脑突变只是替换了侧链，并没有重新折叠主链，所以主链当然完美叠合。 It is an artefact of the procedure, not evidence of anything, and it should not have been reported as a finding. 这是操作流程的假象，不是任何证据，本不该作为一项发现来报告。

JORDAN And the genotyping — did the case-control part work? 那基因分型呢——病例对照那部分成立吗？
