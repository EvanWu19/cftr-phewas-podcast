How did this episode land for you?

Track A — Calibrating ML predictors for missense VUS under ClinGen (Zhuo et al. 2026)

Blurb. Zhuo, Bi, Aggarwal, and Liao ask a deployment-grade question: before a clinician uses an AI score as PP3/BP4 evidence, how should that score be calibrated? They run AlphaMissense, PrimateAI-3D, and REVEL through ClinGen's quantitative calibration framework across three datasets (ClinVar_2023, ClinGen, and the Columbia CDEX diagnostic-exome cohort), stratified by inheritance mode and gene mechanism. REVEL wins overall — especially for autosomal recessive and loss-of-function-tolerant dominant variants — while AlphaMissense wins specifically for dominant variants in haploinsufficiency genes. Applying the recalibrated PP3/BP4 criteria upgrades >10% of ClinGen VUS to likely pathogenic and downgrades <3% to likely benign, with REVEL driving upgrades and AlphaMissense driving downgrades. The tools are complementary, not interchangeable. Because CFTR is autosomal recessive, the paper implicitly nominates REVEL's recessive calibration as first-line for CFTR. Buildable artifact: a CFTR missense calibration notebook that maps each tool's raw scores to CFTR-specific PP3/BP4 cutoffs. Note: closed-access — covered abstract-only on-air.

Plain-English glossary.

  • ACMG/ClinGen framework — the rulebook clinical labs use to classify variants; ClinGen refines ACMG's rules with quantitative, evidence-strength calibration.（中文：临床实验室用来分类变异的规则手册；ClinGen在ACMG基础上加入了定量的证据强度校准。）
  • AlphaMissense — DeepMind's 2023 structure-aware protein language model that predicts missense pathogenicity.（中文：DeepMind于2023年发布、基于蛋白结构感知语言模型的错义变异致病性预测工具。）
  • Calibration (ClinGen sense) — empirically mapping a raw predictor score to a defined evidence strength (supporting/moderate/strong) via the posterior probability of pathogenicity it corresponds to.（中文：把预测工具的原始分数，按其对应的致病后验概率，映射到明确的证据强度等级。）
  • CFTR2 — the CF-specific expert-curated variant database used as the gold-standard truth set for CFTR variants.（中文：针对囊性纤维化的专家审编变异数据库，是CFTR变异的金标准真值集。）
  • ClinVar — NIH's public archive of variant–clinical-significance assertions.（中文：NIH的公开数据库，收录变异与其临床意义的判定。）
  • Haploinsufficiency — one functional gene copy is not enough; losing half the dose causes disease.（中文：单倍剂量不足——基因只剩一个正常拷贝仍不够，剂量减半即致病。）
  • In silico predictor — a computational tool that scores a variant's likely damage.（中文：用计算机预测变异破坏性的工具。）
  • Loss-of-function tolerant — a gene where losing one copy's function does not by itself cause disease.（中文：功能缺失耐受——丢失一个拷贝的功能本身不致病的基因。）
  • Meta-predictor — a model that fuses many upstream predictors into one score; REVEL is the classic example.（中文：把多个上游预测器融合成单一分数的模型，REVEL为典型。）
  • Missense variant — a single-base change swapping one amino acid for another.（中文：单碱基改变导致一个氨基酸被替换。）
  • PP3 / BP4 — ClinGen evidence codes: computational support for pathogenic (PP3) or benign (BP4).（中文：ClinGen证据代码——计算证据支持致病(PP3)或良性(BP4)。）
  • PrimateAI-3D — a predictor learning from primate cross-species variation plus 3D protein structure.（中文：结合灵长类跨物种变异与三维蛋白结构的预测工具。）
  • REVEL — a widely used missense meta-predictor.（中文：广泛使用的错义变异元预测器。）
  • VUS — variant of uncertain significance; neither pathogenic nor benign under current evidence.（中文：意义未明变异，现有证据下既不能判致病也不能判良性。）

Full citation. Zhuo X, Bi X, Aggarwal V, Liao J. Calibration and evaluation of machine-learning algorithms for missense variant classification under ACMG/ClinGen recommendations. Genetics in Medicine. 2026. PMID 42159056; DOI 10.1016/j.gim.2026.102606. (Closed access; no PMC ID.)

Methods / tools to lift.

  • ClinGen quantitative calibration — maps predictor scores to evidence strengths via local likelihood ratios / posterior probability, rather than raw AUC.
  • Stratification by inheritance mode and gene mechanism (AR / AD-haploinsufficiency / AD-LOF-tolerant) — calibrate separately per mechanism, don't pool.
  • Non-circular evaluation via a real diagnostic-exome cohort (CDEX) to avoid ClinVar training-label contamination.
  • Tool-complementarity view: REVEL for recessive + LOF-tolerant, AlphaMissense for haploinsufficiency dominants.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Serves Karen / variant interpretation. MVP: cftr_calibration.ipynb — input a CSV of CFTR missense variants with CFTR2/ClinVar labels → fetch AlphaMissense + PrimateAI-3D + REVEL scores → compute CFTR-local score→evidence-strength thresholds via ClinGen likelihood-ratio calibration → output a per-tool PP3/BP4 cutoff table. Difficulty: M (hard part is a clean, non-circular truth set).

What to verify first.

  • Non-circularity: confirm calibration variants weren't in each tool's training data.
  • Sample size per evidence bin for CFTR — if too sparse, borrow mechanism-matched (recessive) calibration and declare it.
  • Full-text confirmation of the exact calibration estimator and the assumed prior probability of pathogenicity (the prior shifts every threshold).
  • CFTR is AR → prioritize REVEL's recessive calibration over AlphaMissense per this paper's stratified result.

ACMG是美国医学遗传学学会，ClinGen则是由NIH资助、负责细化这套规则的联盟。 Inside that rulebook, each piece of evidence has a code; the two that matter today are PP3 and BP4. 在这套手册里，每一条证据都有一个代码；今天关键的两条是PP3和BP4。 PP3 means computational evidence supporting pathogenic, and BP4 means computational evidence supporting benign. JORDAN Why does the choice of dataset matter so much here — aren't ClinVar and a diagnostic exome cohort basically the same kind of data? 为什么数据集的选择在这里如此重要——ClinVar和一个诊断性外显子队列难道不是基本同类的数据吗？

ALEX They're very different in one way that decides the result. 它们在一个决定结果的方面非常不同。 ClinVar is an aggregate of assertions submitted by many labs, and those assertions were often made using the very predictors we're now testing, so the labels are contaminated by the tools' own influence. ClinVar是许多实验室提交的判定的汇总，而这些判定往往正是用我们现在要测试的那些预测器做出的，所以标签被工具自身的影响污染了。 That's the circularity trap in its purest form: you grade the tool against answers the tool helped write. 这就是循环论证陷阱最纯粹的形式：你用工具帮忙写出的答案去给工具打分。 A diagnostic exome cohort like CDEX is closer to ground truth because classifications there are anchored to actual patient phenotypes and family segregation, not just prior in-silico scores. 像CDEX这样的诊断性外显子队列更接近真值，因为那里的分类锚定于真实的患者表型和家系共分离，而不仅仅是先前的计算机预测分数。 Segregation means tracking whether the variant travels with disease through a family — a strong, independent line of evidence. 共分离指的是追踪一个变异是否在家族中与疾病一同传递——这是一条强有力的、独立的证据线。 So when a tool's ranking survives on CDEX and not just on ClinVar, you should trust that ranking a great deal more. 因此，当一个工具的排名在CDEX上、而不只是在ClinVar上依然成立时，你就该对那个排名信任得多。 The practical lesson for us is that any calibration we do for CFTR must be judged on the least circular truth set we can assemble, or the whole exercise flatters itself. 对我们的实际教训是：我们为CFTR做的任何校准，都必须在我们能拼出的循环性最弱的真值集上评判，否则整个过程就是在自我吹捧。

JORDAN Fair. So does it work — what did the numbers actually say? 有道理。那它到底管不管用——数字究竟说了什么？

ALEX It surprises people because the field has a bias toward newer, bigger, structure-aware architectures. 之所以让人意外，是因为整个领域偏爱更新、更大、结构感知的架构。 The intuition is that a model that sees the protein's 3D fold and learns from evolutionary language should dominate an older ensemble. 直觉是，一个能看到蛋白三维折叠、又从进化语言中学习的模型，应当碾压一个较老的集成方法。 But two things cut against that intuition under the ClinGen lens. 但在ClinGen的视角下，有两点与这种直觉相悖。 First, REVEL was itself trained to fuse many predictors including conservation and structural features, so it already absorbs much of what the newer tools add. 第一，REVEL本身就是被训练来融合许多预测器的，包括保守性和结构特征，所以它已经吸收了新工具带来的大部分信息。 Second, calibration rewards a well-behaved score distribution, not raw discriminative power. 第二，校准奖励的是分布行为良好的分数，而非原始的判别能力。 A model can have a superb AUC yet a lumpy score-to-truth mapping, and calibration exposes exactly that. 一个模型可以有极佳的AUC，却有一个坑坑洼洼的"分数→真值"映射，而校准恰恰会把这一点暴露出来。 So the lesson isn't "old beats new"; it's "benchmark rank and calibrated evidence strength are different axes, and clinical use lives on the second one." 所以教训不是"旧的胜过新的"，而是"基准排名和校准后的证据强度是两个不同的维度，而临床应用活在第二个维度上"。

JORDAN And the ten-percent-upgrade number — how should I feel about that operationally? 那个百分之十上调的数字——从操作层面我该如何看待它？

ALEX It cuts both ways, and that's the honest read. 它是双刃的，这才是诚实的解读。 On the encouraging side, moving more than ten percent of VUS to likely pathogenic means real patients get a definitive answer where before they had a shrug. 令人鼓舞的一面是，把超过百分之十的VUS移到"可能致病"，意味着一些真实病人从"耸耸肩不知道"变成了得到明确答案。 In a recessive disease like CF, a confirmed second pathogenic allele can flip a diagnosis or open a modulator eligibility. 在像囊性纤维化这样的隐性疾病里，确认第二个致病等位基因，可能改变一个诊断、或打开一个调节剂治疗的资格。 On the cautionary side, an automated reclassification of that magnitude is only as trustworthy as the calibration underneath it. 而需警惕的一面是，如此规模的自动重分类，其可信度完全取决于底层的校准。 If the calibration set was even slightly circular, you'd be upgrading variants on inflated confidence and pushing clinical decisions off a shaky number. 如果校准集哪怕只有一点点循环污染，你就是在虚高的信心上上调变异，把临床决策建立在一个不稳的数字之上。 That asymmetry — upgrades change management more than downgrades — is why the downgrade rate being under three percent is reassuring: the tools are conservative about calling things benign. 这种不对称性——上调对临床处理的改变大于下调——正是下调率低于百分之三令人安心的原因：这些工具在判定"良性"时是保守的。 The operational takeaway is to treat PP3 upgrades as evidence to combine with the rest of the ACMG criteria, never as a standalone verdict. 操作层面的结论是：把PP3上调当作要与其余ACMG标准合并的一条证据，而绝不当作一个独立的最终判决。
