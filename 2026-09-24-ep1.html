Blurb

This episode pairs a CorEvitas simulation abstract (Mansfield, Singleton, Moore-Clingenpeel, Harrold, Litman; ISPE 2025) comparing propensity score matching against IPTW in the CorEvitas psoriasis registry, at three levels of covariate imbalance, with Austin and Stuart's 2015 Statistics in Medicine paper on best practice for IPTW balance diagnostics — full text available for this one. The CorEvitas result is the textbook pattern: IPTW wins under minimal imbalance, matching wins as imbalance grows, and IPTW's full-sample-size appearance hides a collapsing effective sample size under high imbalance. The foundational paper supplies the mechanics that make that finding legible: which population each weighting scheme actually targets, how positivity failure shows up as extreme weights, why truncation is a bias-variance trade rather than a fix, how Kish effective sample size reveals what that trade costs, and why standardized mean difference is a convention, not a hypothesis test. What this arms Runyu with: the vocabulary to describe a weighting-versus-matching decision as a decision about estimand and population first, and about variance second.

Plain-English glossary

ATE (average treatment effect) — The effect of moving the entire population, both arms, from control to treatment; the estimand targeted by standard IPTW weights.（中文：平均治疗效应，标准IPTW所针对的估计目标，即让整个人群都接受治疗与都不接受治疗的效果之差。）

ATT (average treatment effect in the treated) — The effect in the population that actually received treatment; targeted by weights that give treated subjects weight one and reweight controls to resemble them.（中文：治疗组中的平均治疗效应，针对实际接受治疗人群的估计目标。）

ATO (average treatment effect in the overlap population, overlap weights) — The effect in the subpopulation with genuine clinical equipoise between treatments; overlap weights down-weight subjects with propensity scores near zero or one.（中文：重叠人群的平均治疗效应，重叠权重法针对两种治疗都合理可能的人群。）

ATM (average treatment effect via matching weights) — The weighting analogue of one-to-one propensity score matching; targets roughly the matched population without physically dropping subjects.（中文：通过匹配权重估计的平均治疗效应，效果类似倾向评分匹配但不实际剔除个体。）

Confounding by indication — Systematic difference in who receives a treatment based on disease severity or history, common in registries and the usual driver of covariate imbalance.（中文：适应症混杂，指患者接受某治疗的可能性与疾病严重程度或病史相关，是登记数据中协变量不平衡的常见原因。）

Covariate imbalance — A difference in the distribution of measured baseline characteristics between treatment groups before adjustment.（中文：协变量不平衡，指调整前各治疗组基线特征分布的差异。）

Effective sample size (Kish ESS) — A measure, computed from the weights, of how many equally-weighted independent observations a weighted analysis is statistically equivalent to; can be far smaller than the raw row count when weights are extreme.（中文:Kish有效样本量,根据权重计算得出,反映加权分析在统计效力上相当于多少个等权重的独立观测值,权重极端时可能远小于原始样本量。）

Extreme weights — Very large inverse probability weights arising from subjects with propensity scores near zero or one; the mechanical signature of a positivity violation or model misspecification.（中文：极端权重，指因倾向评分接近0或1而产生的过大逆概率权重，通常是正性假设违反或模型设定错误的表现。）

ISPE — The International Society for Pharmacoepidemiology, the venue where much registry and weighting methods work, including this episode's CorEvitas abstract, is first presented.（中文：国际药物流行病学学会，许多登记数据与加权方法研究首次公开发表的学术平台。）

Kish effective sample size formula — Roughly the square of the sum of weights divided by the sum of squared weights; the standard diagnostic for how much information a weighted sample retains.（中文：Kish有效样本量公式，约等于权重之和的平方除以权重平方和，是衡量加权样本保留信息量的标准诊断指标。）

Positivity — The assumption that every subject has a nonzero probability of receiving either treatment; violations manifest as extreme weights.（中文：正性假设，指每个个体接受任一治疗的概率都不为零，违反时表现为极端权重。）

Robust (sandwich-type) variance estimator — A variance estimator that accounts for the fact that IPTW weights are themselves estimated from data, not fixed and known; needed because treating weights as fixed understates uncertainty.（中文：稳健（三明治型）方差估计量，用于考虑IPTW权重本身是由数据估计而非已知固定值的事实，若忽略这一点会低估不确定性。）

Standardized mean difference (SMD) — The difference in covariate means scaled by pooled standard deviation, used to assess balance after weighting; a conventional threshold such as 0.10 is a rule of thumb, not a statistical test.（中文：标准化均数差,用于评估加权后协变量平衡性的指标,常用的0.10阈值只是经验惯例,而非统计检验。）

Weight truncation (trimming) — Capping weights above a chosen threshold, often a percentile such as the 1st/99th; trades bias for reduced variance and requires pre-specified justification rather than post-hoc selection.（中文：权重截断（修剪），将权重限制在设定阈值以内（如第1和第99百分位数），以偏倚换取方差降低，需要事先说明理由而非事后选择。）

Weighted Kaplan-Meier / weighted Cox model — Survival analysis methods adapted for IPTW, using weighted risk sets and, for the Cox model, robust standard errors to account for estimated weights.（中文：加权Kaplan-Meier曲线／加权Cox模型，将IPTW方法应用于生存分析，Cox模型需搭配稳健标准误以反映权重的估计不确定性。）

Standard IPTW, weight equal to one over the propensity score for the treated and one over one minus the propensity score for the untreated, targets the average treatment effect, the ATE, the effect if the entire population, both arms combined, had received treatment versus not. That's not the only estimand on the table. ATT weights, which the paper also derives, multiply the standard weight by the propensity score, so treated subjects get weight one and controls get reweighted to look like the treated group. That targets the average treatment effect in the treated, which is usually the clinically relevant question in a PASS, because you're asking about the safety of the drug in the population that actually takes it, not in some hypothetical population where everyone does. Austin and Stuart's case study shows this concretely, stabilized weights with mean near one and a tight standard deviation when positivity looks fine, but they flag that when the mean drifts from one or the tails get extreme, that's diagnostic of near-positivity failure or model misspecification, not something to paper over with a fix.

A cohort of two thousand can have a Kish ESS of two hundred if a handful of subjects are carrying enormous weight. The row count in your dataset says two thousand.
