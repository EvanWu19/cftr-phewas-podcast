Ep3 (Track C — registry QC, 1 of 2): Detecting rare without labeling it first

Nawaz and colleagues (2025, PLOS ONE) benchmark fourteen unsupervised anomaly-detection algorithms from the PyOD library (One-Class SVM, ECOD, HBOS, Isolation Forest, LOF, GMM, and others) for flagging low (<2,500g), very low (<1,500g), and extreme (<1,000g) birth weight in 3,509 pregnancies from the UAE's Mutaba'ah cohort, using only 22 first-trimester features and training each detector exclusively on the "normal" class to sidestep the label-scarcity and class-imbalance problems that plague supervised approaches. Best results: One-Class SVM reached AUCROC 0.57 (plain LBW), 0.6424 (very LBW), and 0.72 (extreme LBW) — detection counterintuitively improved as the category got rarer and more extreme. A novel feature-perturbation interpretability technique (replace one feature with its training-set mean, measure the resulting score shift) performed comparably to the Isolation-Forest-specific Local-DIFFI method per clinician validation, while being model-agnostic and cheaper than SHAP/LIME. A cited precedent, Röchner et al., applied a closer analog — unsupervised detection directly on EHR records in a cancer registry — flagging ~28% as implausible vs. 8% by random selection at 94% specificity. The paper is explicit that its "anomaly" is a genuinely rare clinical outcome, not a suspected data-entry error — a distinction that must not be collapsed when porting the method. Buildable artifact: an Unsupervised-Detector Bake-off for Registry Anthropometric Candidates.

Plain-English glossary

  • WHO birth-weight categories — globally standardized clinical thresholds: low birth weight (<2,500g), very low (<1,500g), extreme low (<1,000g), each tier associated with a step up in neonatal risk; the same kind of fixed-reference convention as a CDC/WHO growth-chart z-score cutoff, though birth weight is a single cross-sectional measurement rather than a longitudinal trajectory.（中文：WHO出生体重分类，全球统一的临床阈值：低出生体重（<2500克）、极低（<1500克）、超低（<1000克），每一级别对应新生儿风险的进一步升高；与CDC/WHO生长曲线图中的z-score切点属于同一类固定参照惯例，但出生体重是一次横断面测量，而非纵向轨迹。）
  • Mutaba'ah study — a longitudinal mother-and-child cohort in Al Ain, Abu Dhabi, UAE, recruiting pregnant women through hospital medical records.（中文：Mutaba'ah研究，阿联酋阿布扎比艾因市的一项纵向母婴队列研究，通过医院病历招募孕妇。）
  • Anomaly vs. error — this paper's "anomaly" is a genuinely rare true clinical outcome (a baby really born underweight), not a suspected data-entry mistake; a registry QC pipeline usually hunts for the latter, and the two should not be conflated when porting a method between them.（中文：异常与错误的区别——本文中的"异常"是一个真实存在的罕见临床结局（确实出生体重过低的婴儿），而不是一次疑似的数据录入错误；注册数据质量流程通常要找的是后者，在方法迁移时不应将两者混为一谈。）
  • Feature-perturbation interpretability — replacing one feature at a time with its training-set mean and measuring the resulting shift in anomaly score, to see how much that feature drove the original flag; model-agnostic, unlike Local-DIFFI (Isolation-Forest-specific).（中文：特征扰动可解释性，每次用训练集均值替换一个特征并测量所产生的异常分数变化，以判断该特征对原始标记的驱动程度；与模型无关，不同于仅适用于Isolation Forest的Local-DIFFI。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • One-class / unsupervised anomaly-detection bake-off (PyOD) — benchmarking many detector families (angle-, cluster-, histogram-, density-, isolation-, ensemble-based) side by side via AUCROC/AUCPR under severe label scarcity, without requiring a full supervised gold standard.
  • Feature-perturbation interpretability — a cheap, model-agnostic alternative to SHAP/LIME for explaining which input feature drove an anomaly score.
  • FindFPOF + autoencoder on registry EHRs (Röchner et al., cited precedent) — a closer structural analog: unsupervised detection directly on registry records, not a clinical-outcome proxy.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation, primary — a direct template for benchmarking unsupervised detectors under severe class imbalance and near-total label scarcity, exactly the setting any registry flag that only a tiny fraction of records will ever get adjudicated against faces; secondary touch on (d) evaluation, since AUCROC/AUCPR allow performance comparison without a full labeled gold standard. Buildable artifact: an Unsupervised-Detector Bake-off for Registry Anthropometric Candidates. MVP: run a representative PyOD subset (One-Class SVM, ECOD, HBOS, Isolation Forest) on engineered per-encounter anthropometric features (deviation from individual trend, velocity, z-score), benchmark via AUCROC/AUCPR against whatever small adjudicated subset exists, and port the feature-perturbation technique to surface which feature drove each flag. Difficulty: medium.

Error types covered / not covered

Covers: genuinely rare, extreme outcome-category membership in tabular cross-sectional clinical features, benchmarked across many unsupervised detector families, with cheap model-agnostic interpretability. Does not cover: unit conversion errors, digit transposition, decimal shifts, carry-forward values, or genuine data-entry/transcription errors specifically — the paper's "anomaly" is a real clinical outcome, not a suspected recording mistake, and that distinction is the central caveat of this episode.

What to verify first

  • Whether this paper's 22 maternal/first-trimester features transfer at all to a height/weight QC setting, where the relevant engineered features (deviation from individual trend, inter-visit velocity) are structurally different — only the benchmarking methodology carries over, not the feature set.
  • Whether the counterintuitive "rarer is easier to detect" pattern found here for a true rare outcome would hold the same way for implausible-value detection, where the flagged thing is a suspected error rather than a real extreme state.
  • Whether the clinician validation of the feature-perturbation technique, done specifically for prenatal birth-weight features, would need to be redone for an anthropometric-QC adjudication context before trusting its explanations there.

JORDAN Before the methods, what's the clinical convention here — why 2,500 grams, 1,500 grams, and 1,000 grams specifically as the cutoffs? 在讲方法之前，先说说这里的临床惯例——为什么具体是2500克、1500克和1000克这几个切点？

ALEX These are WHO-defined birth-weight categories, not statistically derived thresholds from this dataset: low birth weight is under 2,500 grams, very low birth weight is under 1,500 grams, and extreme low birth weight is under 1,000 grams, each tier associated with a step up in neonatal risk — infection, respiratory complications, feeding difficulty, and longer-term risk of cardiovascular disease and diabetes. 这些是世界卫生组织（WHO）定义的出生体重分类，而不是从这个数据集中统计推导出来的阈值：低出生体重是指低于2500克，极低出生体重是指低于1500克，超低出生体重是指低于1000克，每一级别都对应着新生儿风险的进一步升高——感染、呼吸系统并发症、喂养困难，以及远期心血管疾病和糖尿病风险。 It's worth flagging that these are globally standardized clinical category boundaries, the same kind of fixed-reference convention as a CDC or WHO growth-chart z-score cutoff — a useful parallel to keep in mind, even though birth weight itself is a single cross-sectional measurement at delivery, not a longitudinal trajectory the way a child's growth curve is. 值得指出的是，这些是全球统一的临床分类界限，和CDC或WHO生长曲线图中的z-score切点属于同一类固定参照惯例——这是一个值得记住的有用类比，尽管出生体重本身是分娩时的一次横断面测量，而不像儿童生长曲线那样是一条纵向轨迹。 The data here come from the Mutaba'ah study, a longitudinal mother-and-child cohort run out of Al Ain, Abu Dhabi, in the UAE, recruiting pregnant women through hospital medical records under Abu Dhabi Health Research and Technology Ethics Committee approval.这里的数据来自Mutaba'ah研究，这是一项在阿联酋阿布扎比艾因市开展的纵向母婴队列研究，通过医院病历招募孕妇，并获得了阿布扎比卫生研究与技术伦理委员会的批准。

JORDAN So why treat this as anomaly detection instead of just a classification problem with class weighting or oversampling? 那为什么要把这当作异常检测问题，而不是直接用类别加权或过采样来做分类问题？

ALEX Because the existing literature's standard fix — SMOTE and similar oversampling, or undersampling the majority class — has real costs: undersampling throws away information from the majority class, and oversampling synthesizes new minority points that can shift the decision boundary and encourage overfitting, especially when the minority class is this rare. 因为现有文献中常用的解决办法——SMOTE及类似的过采样技术，或者对多数类进行欠采样——都有实际代价：欠采样会丢弃多数类中的信息，而过采样会合成新的少数类样本点，这可能会偏移决策边界并助长过拟合，尤其是在少数类如此稀少的情况下更是如此。 The authors' alternative is to train each detector only on the "normal" (non-low-birth-weight) class and let it learn what normal looks like, then score every instance by how far it deviates from that learned normal — a framing that sidesteps the oversampling/undersampling trade-off entirely and, just as importantly, doesn't require you to have any confirmed low-birth-weight labels at training time at all. 作者采用的替代方案是只用"正常"（非低出生体重）这一类来训练每个检测器，让它学习"正常"长什么样，然后根据每个样本偏离这个习得的"正常"的程度来打分——这种框架完全绕开了过采样/欠采样的权衡问题，同样重要的是，它在训练阶段根本不需要任何已确认的低出生体重标签。 That's precisely the setting a registry QC candidate generator lives in too: the overwhelming majority of records are unlabeled, only a small fraction ever get human adjudication, and waiting for enough confirmed error labels to train a supervised classifier isn't realistic.这恰恰正是一个注册数据质量候选生成器所处的情境：绝大多数记录都没有标签，只有一小部分最终会经过人工裁定，而等待积累到足够的已确认错误标签来训练一个有监督分类器，并不现实。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually ran, then. 那就带我走一遍他们具体跑了什么。

ALEX Fourteen PyOD-library detectors — covering angle-based, clustering-based, histogram-based, density-based, isolation-based, and ensemble families, things like One-Class SVM, ECOD, HBOS, Isolation Forest, LOF, and GMM — each trained separately for the LBW, VLBW, and ELBW categories out of 3,509 pregnancies and 22 first-trimester features, evaluated via 2x5-fold cross-validation with AUCROC and AUCPR. 他们使用了PyOD库中的十四种检测器——涵盖基于角度、基于聚类、基于直方图、基于密度、基于隔离和集成等多个家族，比如One-Class SVM、ECOD、HBOS、Isolation Forest、LOF和GMM——针对3509例妊娠和22个孕早期特征，分别对LBW、VLBW和ELBW这三个类别单独训练，并通过2×5折交叉验证，用AUCROC和AUCPR进行评估。 On top of the detection layer, they introduce a feature-perturbation interpretability technique: replace one feature at a time with its training-set mean, recompute the anomaly score, and the size of the resulting shift tells you how much that feature was driving the original score — cheaper than SHAP or LIME, and unlike Local-DIFFI, which only works for Isolation Forest, this one is model-agnostic. 在检测层之上，他们引入了一种特征扰动可解释性技术：每次用训练集均值替换一个特征，重新计算异常分数，所产生分数变化的大小，就反映了该特征对原始分数的驱动程度——比SHAP或LIME更便宜，而且与只适用于Isolation Forest的Local-DIFFI不同，这种方法与模型无关。 I do want to flag something the authors are careful about but that's easy to blur when porting this elsewhere: their "anomaly" here is a genuinely rare clinical outcome — a baby that really was born underweight — not a suspected data-entry mistake, and that's a different thing from what a registry QC pipeline is usually hunting for. 我想特别指出一点，作者自己处理得很谨慎，但在把方法迁移到别处时很容易被模糊掉：他们这里的"异常"，是一个真实存在的罕见临床结局——一个确实出生体重过低的婴儿——而不是一次被怀疑的数据录入错误，这和注册数据质量流程通常要找的东西，是两种不同的目标。

JORDAN Before the results — did the literature review turn up anything closer to an actual registry QC use case, rather than a clinical-risk-prediction one? 在讲结果之前——文献综述部分有没有提到更接近真正的注册数据质量用例的工作，而不是临床风险预测？

ALEX One study they cite is a much closer analog than the birth-weight framing itself. 他们引用的一项研究，比出生体重这个框架本身更接近真正的类比对象。 Röchner and colleagues applied unsupervised methods — a frequent-pattern-outlier-factor technique called FindFPOF, plus an autoencoder — directly to the task of identifying questionable electronic health records in a German cancer registry of over 21,000 records, which is structurally the same problem a CFFPR-style pipeline faces, just in a different disease registry. JORDAN Does the detection actually work well, though? 那检测效果到底好不好？

ALEX Modestly, and the numbers are worth being precise about rather than rounding up. 效果属于一般，而且这些数字值得精确引用，而不是笼统地往好的方向概括。 For plain LBW, the best model — One-Class SVM — only reached an AUCROC of 0.57, barely above chance; for very LBW, One-Class SVM's AUCROC rose to 0.6424, with ECOD and HBOS both posting the best AUCPR at 0.045; and for extreme LBW, the rarest and most severe category, One-Class SVM reached its best overall AUCROC at 0.72, closely followed by HBOS at 0.712, with SOS leading AUCPR at 0.037. 对于普通的LBW，表现最好的模型——One-Class SVM——AUCROC也只达到0.57，仅略高于随机水平；对于极低出生体重，One-Class SVM的AUCROC提升到0.6424，ECOD和HBOS的AUCPR并列最高，均为0.045；而对于超低出生体重，这个最罕见、最严重的类别，One-Class SVM达到了整体最佳的AUCROC——0.72，HBOS紧随其后为0.712，而SOS在AUCPR上领先，为0.037。 There's a genuinely counterintuitive pattern buried in those numbers: detection got better, not worse, as the category got rarer and more extreme — the authors attribute this to extreme cases being more distinctive and further from the normal range, so even though there's less data to learn from, what is there stands out more clearly; an F-test on the AUCROC scores confirms models differ significantly from each other for plain LBW (p=0.0076) but not for the very-low or extreme categories, where performance across detectors converges. 这些数字里藏着一个确实违反直觉的规律：随着类别变得更罕见、更极端，检测效果反而变好而不是变差——作者将其归因于极端病例在统计上更具区分度、离正常范围更远，所以即便可供学习的数据更少，存在的那部分信号也更加清晰；对AUCROC分数的F检验证实，在普通LBW上，各模型之间存在显著差异（p=0.0076），但在极低或超低出生体重类别上并不存在显著差异，说明各检测器的表现趋于一致。 On the interpretability side, clinician experts judged the feature-perturbation method about as useful as Local-DIFFI for understanding which prenatal features drove a flag, which matters because it means you don't have to give up interpretability to get model-agnostic flexibility.在可解释性方面，临床专家评估认为，特征扰动方法在帮助理解哪些孕期特征驱动了某次标记这一点上，效果大致与Local-DIFFI相当，这一点很重要，因为这意味着你不必为了获得与模型无关的灵活性而牺牲可解释性。

JORDAN What would we need to check before trusting any of this on real anthropometric data? 在把这些内容真正用到真实的人体测量数据之前，我们需要确认什么？
