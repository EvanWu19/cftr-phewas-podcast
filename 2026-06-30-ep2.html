Blurb

SAMURAI is the first nf-core-based Nextflow pipeline purpose-built for copy-number-alteration (CNA) analysis from shallow whole-genome sequencing (sWGS). Its value to us is engineering, not biology: a three-block architecture (preprocessing → copy-number detection → postprocessing) that forks by sample type (solid biopsy vs. liquid biopsy), containerizes every tool (QDNAseq, ASCAT.sc, ichorCNA, WisecondorX) with Docker/Singularity, and stitches their outputs together with harmonization scripts into one report. Validation is strong — overlap coefficients >0.9 (gains) / >0.8 (losses) vs. ground truth, 0.99 Pearson on a real ovarian-cancer case, >0.95 tumor-fraction correlation in plasma, ~90 min for 200 samples. The transferable pattern is the reusable nf-core blueprint. Buildable artifact: a minimal nf-core-style "variant-annotation harmonizer" that fans out AlphaMissense / ESM1b / SpliceAI / CFTR2 as parallel containerized modules and reconciles them into one per-variant evidence table — the substrate the Track A PP3/BP4 calibration notebook would run on. Serves the AI-tooling / career priority.

Plain-English glossary

  • BWA-MEM / BWA-MEM2 — standard tools that align sequencing reads to a reference genome.（中文：BWA-MEM / BWA-MEM2，把测序 reads 比对到参考基因组的标准工具。）
  • Cell-free / circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) — fragmented DNA floating in blood plasma; tumor-derived when ctDNA.（中文：游离 DNA / 循环肿瘤 DNA（ctDNA），漂浮在血浆中的碎片化 DNA，来自肿瘤时称 ctDNA。）
  • Copy number alteration (CNA) — a gain or loss of a DNA segment ≥50 bp, usually somatically acquired in tumors.（中文：拷贝数改变（CNA），一段 ≥50 bp 的 DNA 的增多或缺失，通常是肿瘤中体细胞获得的。）
  • Copy number variation (CNV) — smaller, usually inherited copy-number differences (distinct from CNAs).（中文：拷贝数变异（CNV），更小、通常遗传而来的拷贝数差异，与 CNA 不同。）
  • Depth / coverage — average number of times each genome position is sequenced.（中文：深度 / 覆盖度，基因组每个位点平均被测序的次数。）
  • HGVS — standardized syntax for naming variants.（中文：HGVS，变异命名的标准化书写规范。）
  • nf-core — community standard layered on Nextflow: conventions, testing, prebuilt modules for portable pipelines.（中文：nf-core，建立在 Nextflow 之上的社区标准，提供约定、测试和预制模块，让流程可移植。）
  • Nextflow — workflow manager describing steps as processes, handling parallelization, resume, and containerization.（中文：Nextflow，工作流管理器，把步骤描述为 process，处理并行、续跑和容器化。）
  • Overlap coefficient (Szymkiewicz-Simpson) — similarity metric for how much two sets of genomic segments overlap.（中文：重叠系数（Szymkiewicz-Simpson），衡量两组基因组区段重合程度的相似性指标。）
  • Panel of normals — a reference set of normal samples used to flag sample-specific copy-number deviations.（中文：正常样本组（panel of normals），用作参照、以标记样本特异拷贝数偏离的正常样本集合。）
  • Shallow whole-genome sequencing (sWGS) — whole-genome sequencing at very low depth (1x–0.1x) to detect CNAs cheaply.（中文：浅层全基因组测序（sWGS），用很低深度（1x–0.1x）对全基因组测序，以低成本检测 CNA。）
  • Tumor fraction / purity / ploidy — fraction of tumor DNA in a sample / proportion of tumor cells / number of chromosome copies.（中文：肿瘤分数 / 纯度 / 倍性，样本中肿瘤 DNA 的比例 / 肿瘤细胞占比 / 染色体拷贝数。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Three-block modular nf-core architecture — preprocessing / detection / postprocessing, each independently swappable.
  • Fork-by-input-type design — one pipeline, two workflows selected by sample type (solid vs. liquid biopsy); maps to fork-by-variant-class.
  • Container-everything — Docker/Singularity per tool for bit-for-bit reproducibility.
  • Harmonization scripts — custom glue that normalizes heterogeneous tool outputs into one tidy report (the reusable idea, and the surface to audit).
  • Skip-able preprocessing — accept pre-aligned BAM to bypass QC/alignment.

What to verify first

  • Container availability / licensing — do AlphaMissense, ESM1b, SpliceAI ship runnable images or only bulk precomputed TSVs?
  • Genome-build consistency — every module on the same reference build, or the join silently mismatches coordinates.
  • Join key — robust normalization to one HGVS/VCF-normalized key before merging tool outputs.
  • Scale profile — SAMURAI timings are big-window CNA; per-variant exome-scale annotation needs its own benchmark.

ALEX A copy number alteration, or CNA, is a gain or loss of a chunk of DNA at least 50 base pairs long. 拷贝数改变（copy number alteration，简称 CNA）指的是一段至少 50 个碱基对长的 DNA 发生了增多或缺失。 Normally you have two copies of each region, one per chromosome; a CNA means a stretch is now present in three, four, or zero copies. 正常情况下每个区段你有两份拷贝，每条染色体一份；CNA 意味着某段现在变成了三份、四份，或者零份。 The paper distinguishes CNAs from CNVs — copy number variations — which are smaller and usually inherited, whereas the CNAs they care about are somatically acquired, meaning they arise in a tumor during life rather than being passed down. 论文把 CNA 与 CNV（copy number variation，拷贝数变异）区分开——CNV 更小、通常是遗传来的，而他们关心的 CNA 是体细胞获得性的（somatically acquired），即在一生中肿瘤里产生，而不是遗传得来的。 In cancer these gains and losses are everywhere because tumors have unstable genomes. 在癌症里，这些增多和缺失到处都是，因为肿瘤的基因组很不稳定。

JORDAN And "shallow whole-genome sequencing"? 那"浅层全基因组测序"呢？

ALEX Shallow whole-genome sequencing, sWGS, means you sequence the entire genome but at very low depth — from 1x coverage down to as low as 0.1x. 浅层全基因组测序（shallow whole-genome sequencing，sWGS）指的是你对整个基因组测序，但深度很低——从 1 倍覆盖度一直低到 0.1 倍。 Depth, or coverage, is how many times on average each position is read. 深度，或者说覆盖度，是指平均每个位点被读到多少次。 At 0.1x you can't reliably call single-base mutations, but you can still count reads in big windows and detect whether a region has gained or lost copies. 在 0.1 倍下你无法可靠地判读单碱基突变，但你仍然可以在大窗口里统计 reads 数，从而判断某个区段是增多还是缺失。 It's cheap, which is the whole point — you trade single-base resolution for affordability and the ability to scan many samples or low-input material like cell-free DNA. 它便宜，这正是关键——你用单碱基分辨率去换取可负担性，以及扫描大量样本或低投入材料（比如游离 DNA）的能力。 Cell-free DNA, or ctDNA when it's tumor-derived, is fragmented DNA floating in blood plasma. 游离 DNA，当它来自肿瘤时叫 ctDNA（circulating tumor DNA，循环肿瘤 DNA），是漂浮在血浆里的碎片化 DNA。

JORDAN Break those blocks down. 把这几个区块拆开讲。
