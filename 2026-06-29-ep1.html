How did this episode land for you?

Show notes — Track A — Anwar et al. 2024, CFTR mutation classification & modulator therapies

Blurb. A 2024 open-access review (Advances in Respiratory Medicine) that organizes CFTR variants into the six functional classes (I no protein → VI unstable protein) and maps each class to its modulator strategy (read-through agents, correctors, potentiators, and the ETI triple combination). We read it on Track A through the variant-interpretation lens. The review's most useful point for us is also its self-flagged weakness: a class label is a lossy, mechanism-based teaching scaffold, not a clinical predictor — the same variant maps to several classes (F508del → II/III/VI), and severity can hinge on cis-context like the R117H intron-9 poly-T tract rather than the headline amino-acid change. Where it lands: a CFTR-variant-class-annotation-and-discordance table that joins each HGVS variant to its mechanistic class, computational predictions (AlphaMissense/REVEL/CADD), and any modulator-response label, then ranks rows where those disagree — the high-value queue for a Karen Raraigh curation conversation.

Plain-English glossary

  • ABC transporter — a family of membrane proteins that use ATP to move substrates across membranes; CFTR is an atypical member that moves anions down their gradient.（中文：ABC 转运蛋白，一类用 ATP 供能跨膜运输的蛋白；CFTR 是其中非典型的一员，运送阴离子。）
  • Apical membrane — the top, lumen-facing surface of an epithelial cell, where CFTR sits.（中文：顶端膜，上皮细胞面向管腔的那一面，CFTR 就位于此。）
  • cis / haplotype context — variants on the same physical chromosome copy; their combination, not just one variant, sets the phenotype.（中文：顺式/单倍型背景，同一条染色体拷贝上的一组变异，共同决定表型。）
  • Conductance (channel) — how much ion current the open pore passes; class IV variants narrow it.（中文：电导，打开的通道孔能通过的离子电流大小；第四类变异使其降低。）
  • Corrector — a modulator that improves folding/trafficking of misfolded CFTR (e.g., lumacaftor, tezacaftor, elexacaftor).（中文：校正剂，改善错误折叠 CFTR 折叠与转运的调节剂。）
  • Exon / intron — exons are the coding segments kept in mature mRNA; introns are transcribed then spliced out.（中文：外显子/内含子，外显子是保留在成熟 mRNA 中的编码片段，内含子被转录后剪接掉。）
  • Frameshift — an insertion/deletion not a multiple of three that shifts the reading frame, usually truncating the protein.（中文：移码，非三倍数的插入/缺失，改变阅读框，通常使蛋白截短。）
  • Gating / open probability — how readily the channel's gate opens; class III variants (G551D) cut open probability ~100-fold.（中文：门控/开放概率，通道闸门打开的难易；第三类变异把开放概率降约百倍。）
  • HGVS — the standardized syntax for writing variants (c./p./g. notations).（中文：HGVS，变异命名的标准化书写规范。）
  • Missense / nonsense — missense swaps one amino acid; nonsense creates a premature stop codon.（中文：错义/无义，错义替换一个氨基酸，无义产生提前终止密码子。）
  • Modulator class (I–VI) — taxonomy grouping CFTR variants by which step of the protein life cycle is broken.（中文：突变分类（I–VI），按蛋白生命周期中被破坏的环节给 CFTR 变异分组。）
  • NBD / R domain — nucleotide-binding domains bind ATP to open the channel; the regulatory R domain must be PKA-phosphorylated first.（中文：NBD/R 结构域，核苷酸结合域结合 ATP 以开通道；调控性 R 结构域须先被 PKA 磷酸化。）
  • Poly-T tract (intron 9) — a 5T/7T/9T thymidine run near R117H; shorter tracts increase exon skipping and worsen phenotype.（中文：poly-T 序列（第 9 内含子），R117H 附近 5T/7T/9T 的胸腺嘧啶串，越短越易跳外显子、表型越重。）
  • Potentiator — a modulator that increases open probability of surface CFTR (ivacaftor).（中文：增效剂，提高表面 CFTR 开放概率的调节剂。）
  • Read-through agent — a drug that promotes ribosomal read-through of a premature stop codon (ataluren, aminoglycosides).（中文：通读药物，促使核糖体通读提前终止密码子的药物。）
  • Theratyping — classifying a variant by its measured response to modulators rather than by sequence alone.（中文：theratyping，按变异对调节剂的实测反应（而非仅凭序列）来分型。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • The six-class mechanistic taxonomy (I no-protein, II misfold/traffic, III gating, IV conductance, V reduced synthesis, VI instability) as an annotation schema — but treat class as curatorial, not canonical.
  • The class→modulator mapping (read-through / corrector / potentiator / ETI) as a "is there an actionable therapy?" annotation column.
  • The R117H poly-T example as the design rationale for a mandatory cis-context column in any variant table.
  • Theratyping (I1234V responded to ETI despite in-silico prediction it wouldn't) as the reason to log observed drug response separately from predicted class.

What to verify first

  • Modulator-response evidence here mixes trials, case reports, and in-vitro theratyping; the review is non-systematic — tag each response label by evidence tier before treating it as truth.

Read full transcript (20 turns · ~1976 words)

ALEX Today we're reading a 2024 review by Anwar and colleagues in Advances in Respiratory Medicine, titled "Cystic Fibrosis: Understanding CFTR Mutation Classification and Modulator Therapies." 今天我们读的是 Anwar 等人 2024 年发表在《Advances in Respiratory Medicine》上的一篇综述，题目是《理解 CFTR 突变分类与调节剂疗法》。 It's an open-access synthesis of how the field sorts CFTR variants into functional classes and matches each class to a drug strategy. 这是一篇开放获取 (open access，即可免费全文阅读) 的综述，讲的是这个领域如何把 CFTR 基因变异按功能分成几类，并为每一类匹配一种药物策略。 This is a Track A episode, so we're reading it through the variant-interpretation lens — the Karen Raraigh collaboration thread. 这是 Track A（变异解读）这条线的一集，所以我们是带着"变异解读"的视角来读它的，也就是对接 Karen Raraigh 合作的那条线。 One honest caveat up front: we read the full open-access text for this one, so the numbers are from the paper itself, not just the abstract. 先坦白一个前提：这一篇我们读到了完整的开放获取全文，所以下面的数字都来自论文正文，而不只是摘要。

JORDAN Before we go further, give me the vocabulary. 在继续之前，先给我把术语过一遍。 What is CFTR actually, and what do people mean when they say a "class" of mutation? CFTR 到底是什么？人们说某个突变属于某一"类 (class)"时，又是什么意思？

ALEX CFTR is the gene and the protein it codes for — the Cystic Fibrosis Transmembrane conductance Regulator. CFTR 既指这个基因，也指它编码的蛋白——囊性纤维化跨膜电导调节因子。 The protein sits in the apical membrane — the top surface — of epithelial cells lining the airways, pancreatic ducts, gut, sweat ducts, and vas deferens. 这个蛋白位于上皮细胞的顶端膜 (apical membrane，即面向管腔的那一面)，分布在气道、胰管、肠道、汗腺导管和输精管的上皮里。 It is a channel that moves chloride and bicarbonate ions out of the cell, and it belongs to the ABC transporter family. 它是一个把氯离子和碳酸氢根离子运出细胞的通道，属于 ABC 转运蛋白 (ABC transporter，一类用 ATP 供能的膜转运蛋白) 家族。 Structurally it has two membrane-spanning domains, two nucleotide-binding domains — NBD1 and NBD2 — and a regulatory R domain. 在结构上它有两个跨膜结构域、两个核苷酸结合域 (nucleotide-binding domain)——NBD1 和 NBD2——以及一个调控性的 R 结构域。 ATP binds the NBDs and makes them dimerize, which opens the pore; the R domain has to be phosphorylated by PKA first to let that happen. ATP 结合到 NBD 上让它们二聚化，从而打开通道孔；而在这之前，R 结构域必须先被 PKA（蛋白激酶 A）磷酸化才行。

JORDAN And the mutation classes? 那突变的分类呢？

ALEX The class system sorts variants by which step of that life cycle they break. 这个分类系统是按变异破坏了上述生命周期里的哪一步来归类的。 Class I is no protein made — nonsense, frameshift, or canonical splice variants that kill the message. 第一类是根本没造出蛋白——无义 (nonsense)、移码 (frameshift) 或经典剪接位点 (splice site) 变异，把信使 RNA 给毁了。 Class II is protein made but misfolded and destroyed before it reaches the surface — that's F508del. 第二类是蛋白造出来了，但折叠错误，在到达细胞表面之前就被降解了——这就是 F508del。 Class III is protein at the surface but the gate won't open — that's G551D. 第三类是蛋白到了表面，但闸门打不开——这就是 G551D。 Class IV is a narrowed pore with reduced conductance, class V is too little protein made, and class VI is protein that reaches the surface but is too unstable and recycled too fast. 第四类是孔道变窄、电导降低，第五类是造出的蛋白太少，第六类是蛋白能到表面但太不稳定、回收得太快。

JORDAN So what was the problem this review set out to organize? 那这篇综述想要梳理的问题是什么？

ALEX The core tension is that CFTR has roughly two thousand reported variants, but only about three hundred sixty are considered disease-causing. 核心矛盾在于：CFTR 已报告的变异大约有两千个，但只有约三百六十个被认为是致病的。 So the first job is interpretation — deciding which variants matter and how. 所以第一项工作就是解读——判断哪些变异有意义、以及如何有意义。 The class framework was the field's attempt to make that tractable, by grouping variants by shared molecular defect rather than treating each one as unique. 分类框架是这个领域为了让问题变得可处理而做的尝试，它按共同的分子缺陷把变异归组，而不是把每个变异都当作独一无二的来对待。 The payoff is therapeutic: if you know a variant's class, you have a first guess at which modulator drug should help. 它的回报在治疗层面：如果你知道一个变异属于哪一类，你就对哪种调节剂药物可能有效有了初步猜测。 A gating defect — class III — points to a potentiator that pries the gate open; a folding defect — class II — points to a corrector that helps the protein fold and traffic. 一个门控缺陷——第三类——指向一种增效剂 (potentiator)，把闸门撬开；一个折叠缺陷——第二类——指向一种校正剂 (corrector)，帮助蛋白正确折叠并转运。

ALEX But the review is candid that this clean picture leaks. 但这篇综述很坦诚地承认，这个干净的图景是会"漏水"的。 The same variant can belong to multiple classes at once. 同一个变异可以同时属于多个类别。 F508del is the headline example: it is a folding defect, a trafficking defect, and once at the membrane it also gates poorly and is unstable — so it touches classes II, III, and VI. F508del 就是最典型的例子：它既是折叠缺陷，又是转运缺陷，而且即便到了膜上，它的门控也很差、还不稳定——所以它同时牵涉第二、第三和第六类。 R117H is officially class IV, a conductance defect, but it also carries a class III gating component. R117H 官方归为第四类（电导缺陷），但它同时还带有第三类的门控成分。 And there's a deeper subtlety with R117H: its severity depends on a separate poly-T tract in intron 9. 而 R117H 还有一个更深的微妙之处：它的严重程度取决于第 9 内含子里一段独立的多聚胸腺嘧啶 (poly-T) 序列。

JORDAN Wait — explain that poly-T thing, because that sounds like exactly the kind of genotype subtlety I'd miss. 等等——解释一下这个 poly-T 的事，因为这听起来正是那种我会漏掉的基因型细节。

ALEX The intron 9 poly-T tract comes in three lengths — five, seven, or nine thymidines, written 5T, 7T, 9T. 第 9 内含子的 poly-T 序列有三种长度——五个、七个或九个胸腺嘧啶，写作 5T、7T、9T。 An intron is the part of the gene transcribed but spliced out before the protein is made. 内含子 (intron) 是基因里被转录、但在造蛋白之前会被剪接掉的那一段。 The shorter the poly-T, the more often the splicing machinery skips a nearby exon, which lowers the amount of functional CFTR. poly-T 越短，剪接机器就越容易跳过附近的一个外显子 (exon)，从而降低有功能的 CFTR 的数量。 So R117H sitting next to 5T is clinically much more severe than the same R117H next to 7T. 所以紧挨着 5T 的 R117H，在临床上比紧挨着 7T 的同一个 R117H 要严重得多。 The same amino-acid change has different meaning depending on a variant in cis — on the same chromosome copy. 同一个氨基酸改变，其含义取决于一个顺式 (cis，即在同一条染色体拷贝上) 的变异。 That single fact is why a class label alone can't predict severity, and why variant interpretation has to track haplotype context, not just the headline mutation. 这一个事实就解释了为什么单凭一个分类标签无法预测严重程度，也解释了为什么变异解读必须追踪单倍型 (haplotype，即同一条染色体上一组共同遗传的变异) 的背景，而不只是那个标志性的突变。

ALEX Let's talk about what the review actually assembles, because as a review its "method" is the taxonomy itself plus the drug mapping. 我们来谈谈这篇综述实际上整理了什么，因为作为一篇综述，它的"方法"就是这套分类法本身加上药物的对应关系。 For each class it pins down the molecular lesion, names representative variants, and lists the modulator strategy. 它为每一类都钉住了分子层面的损伤、点名了代表性变异，并列出了对应的调节剂策略。 Class I gets read-through agents like ataluren and aminoglycosides such as gentamicin, which try to push the ribosome past a premature stop codon. 第一类对应通读药物 (read-through agent)，如 ataluren 和氨基糖苷类抗生素如庆大霉素，它们试图推动核糖体越过一个提前出现的终止密码子。 Class II gets correctors — lumacaftor, tezacaftor, elexacaftor — that improve folding and trafficking. 第二类对应校正剂——lumacaftor、tezacaftor、elexacaftor——它们改善折叠和转运。 Class III gets potentiators — ivacaftor — that increase the channel's open probability. 第三类对应增效剂——ivacaftor——它提高通道的开放概率 (open probability)。 And the now-standard triple therapy ETI — elexacaftor, tezacaftor, ivacaftor — combines two correctors with a potentiator to rescue F508del. 而如今已成标准的三联疗法 ETI——elexacaftor、tezacaftor、ivacaftor——把两种校正剂和一种增效剂合在一起，来挽救 F508del。

ALEX Here's the design choice I'd push back on, and to their credit the authors flag it too. 这里有一个我想提出质疑的设计选择，而值得肯定的是，作者们自己也点出了这一点。 The class system is a teaching scaffold built on mechanism, but it was never meant to be a clinical predictor, and the review sometimes lets those two roles blur. 这套分类系统是一个建立在机制之上的教学脚手架，但它从来就不是用来做临床预测的，而综述有时让这两种角色变得模糊。 A variant's class tells you the dominant defect in a dish, not the patient's lung function or their drug response. 一个变异的类别告诉你的是培养皿里占主导的缺陷，而不是病人的肺功能或他们的药物反应。 The review even cites a case where an in-silico prediction said a variant wouldn't respond to ETI, and then seven patients with that I1234V variant did respond. 综述甚至引用了一个案例：计算机预测 (in-silico) 说某个变异对 ETI 不会有反应，结果七位带有该 I1234V 变异的病人却确实有反应。 That gap between predicted class behavior and observed response is the whole reason variant interpretation can't stop at a class label. 预测的类别行为与观察到的反应之间的这道鸿沟，正是变异解读不能止步于一个分类标签的根本原因。

JORDAN So when we get to results, what are the concrete numbers worth holding onto? 那说到结果，有哪些具体数字值得记住？

ALEX A few anchor numbers. 几个锚定数字。 F508del accounts for roughly two-thirds of CF-causing alleles worldwide, and the review cites it as present in up to about eighty-five percent of clinical CF cases when you count people carrying at least one copy.
