Ep3 (Track C, #1) — People-Like-Me Growth Prediction, Now with Mahalanobis Matching

Abstract-only. Jin et al. (2025, Computers in Biology and Medicine) extend the People-Like-Me (PLM) family of curve-matching prediction methods — a k-nearest-neighbors-style approach applied to entire longitudinal trajectories rather than static features — by introducing Mahalanobis distance as the match-selection metric, which accounts for correlation between timepoints rather than treating each timepoint independently. Co-authored by Stef van Buuren (brokenstick model, covered 2026-08-15) and Margaret Rosenfeld (CF pulmonologist), and validated on both simulated data and clinical growth data explicitly described as being from children with cystic fibrosis (cohort not specified in the abstract). Mahalanobis-based PLM reportedly outperformed both standard PLM matching and linear mixed models (LMM) consistently across tested scenarios — no effect sizes or specific metrics were available from the abstract alone. The buildable artifact: a PLM matcher on brokenstick-preprocessed synthetic trajectories, compared against a spline mixed-effects baseline to see whether matched-neighbor expected trajectories flag different points than a population-level model.

Plain-English glossary

  • Brokenstick model — a linear mixed model with time recoded as a spline at shared knots, used to put irregular-visit trajectories onto a common age grid so they become comparable. (中文：brokenstick模型，一种把时间重新编码为共享结点处样条的线性混合模型，用于把不规则就诊轨迹放到一个共同的年龄网格上，使其可以互相比较。)
  • Curve matching — finding individuals in a reference database whose trajectory-so-far most closely resembles a target patient's, then using their subsequent trajectory to predict the target's future. (中文：曲线匹配，指在参考数据库中寻找迄今轨迹与目标病人最相似的个体，再用他们后续的轨迹来预测目标病人的未来走向。)
  • LMM (linear mixed model) — the standard parametric alternative for longitudinal prediction: one model fit across the whole population with individual random effects. (中文：线性混合模型，纵向预测的标准参数化替代方法，在整个人群上拟合一个模型并加入个体随机效应。)
  • Mahalanobis distance — a multivariate distance measure that accounts for covariance structure between variables (here, between timepoints), rather than treating each independently. (中文：马氏距离，一种考虑变量间协方差结构的多变量距离度量，本文中用于考虑不同时间点之间的相关性，而不是把各时间点当作独立的。)
  • People-Like-Me (PLM) — a family of curve-matching prediction methods that estimate a target patient's future trajectory from the observed trajectories of the most similar matched individuals. (中文："和我相似的人"方法，一类曲线匹配预测方法，根据与目标病人最相似的匹配个体的已观测轨迹，来估计目标病人的未来走向。)
