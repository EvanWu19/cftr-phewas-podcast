Blurb

The ClinGen RASopathy expert panels rewrote their gene-specific ACMG/AMP variant-classification rules, and this paper is the clearest recent worked example of how that whole cycle runs. Not a CFTR paper — RASopathies are RAS/MAPK gain-of-function disorders — but the machinery is exactly what a CFTR classification effort (the Karen lane) rides on: recurate gene-disease validity, tailor each evidence code to the disease mechanism, calibrate REVEL cutoffs on your own P/B variant set, adopt point-based de novo and case-observation scoring, then pilot the new spec on already-classified variants to see what moves. The finding to carry: tightening PM2 to Supporting and capping PS3 at Moderate would, on the rulebook alone, drop 44% (14/32) of pathogenic variants a tier — but new case evidence backfilled all but three. The buildable artifact is an acmg-spec-impact-simulator: toggle a proposed specification change over a table of already-scored variants and get a before/after reclassification diff, so you see the damage before you ship a rule change.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP framework — the 2015 rule system that classifies a DNA variant from Pathogenic to Benign using evidence codes.（中文：一套 2015 年制定的规则体系，用证据代码把 DNA 变异从"致病"到"良性"分级。）
  • Evidence code (e.g., PVS1, PM2, PP3, BA1, BS3, BP4) — a labeled unit of evidence; the letters give direction (P=pathogenic, B=benign) and strength (VS=very strong, S=strong, M=moderate, P=supporting).（中文：一个带标签的证据单元；字母表示方向（P 致病、B 良性）和强度（VS 非常强、S 强、M 中等、P 支持性）。）
  • VCEP (Variant Curation Expert Panel) — a ClinGen-authorized standing group that specializes the generic ACMG/AMP framework for one gene or disease family.（中文：ClinGen 授权的常设专家组，把通用 ACMG/AMP 框架针对某一基因或疾病家族做专门化定制。）
  • GCEP (Gene Curation Expert Panel) — the companion panel that scores how solid a gene-disease relationship is (Limited → Definitive).（中文：配套专家组，评估基因-疾病关系的确凿程度（从"有限"到"明确"）。）
  • Gain-of-function vs loss-of-function — a variant that makes a protein/pathway overactive vs one that breaks it; RASopathies are gain-of-function, most disease genes are loss-of-function.（中文：功能获得型让蛋白/通路过度活跃，功能丧失型则使其失效；RASopathy 属功能获得型，多数疾病基因属功能丧失型。）
  • de novo variant — a variant present in a child but neither parent; strong evidence for dominant disease.（中文：仅在患儿身上出现、父母都没有的新发变异；是显性遗传病的有力证据。）
  • Proband — the index affected individual in a family under study.（中文：所研究家系中最初被确诊的患者。）
  • gnomAD — the Genome Aggregation Database, reference catalog of variation across ~800,000 people, used to judge how rare a variant is.（中文：基因组聚合数据库，收录约八十万人变异的参考目录，用于判断变异的罕见程度。）
  • REVEL — an ensemble missense-pathogenicity score (0–1) blending 13 predictors; ≥0.7 supports damage (PP3), ≤0.3 supports benign (BP4).（中文：REVEL 是融合 13 个预测工具的错义致病性集成评分（0–1）；≥0.7 支持有害（PP3），≤0.3 支持良性（BP4）。）
  • Missense constraint — a gnomAD-derived measure of how strongly a gene avoids missense variation in the healthy population; the panel restricts PP2 to genes above a constraint score of 3.09.（中文：基于 gnomAD 的指标，衡量一个基因在健康人群中回避错义变异的程度；专家组把 PP2 限定用于约束分数高于 3.09 的基因。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • VCEP specification cycle — the repeatable pattern: recurate gene-disease validity → specialize each ACMG/AMP code to the mechanism → calibrate computational thresholds on your own variants → adopt point-based scoring → pilot on classified variants.
  • REVEL threshold calibration on in-house variants — validate (don't assume) the ≥0.7 / ≤0.3 cutoffs against your gene's known P and B variants before applying PP3/BP4.
  • Point-based case-observation scoring — convert PS2/PM6/PS4/BS2 from all-or-nothing codes into additive points keyed to phenotype match and parentage confirmation.
  • PP2 gating by missense constraint — apply the "missense-intolerant gene" code only where a constraint score justifies it (here, >3.09).
  • Pilot-set impact assessment — re-run the new spec over already-classified variants and count tier crossings before adopting it.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP:

acmg-spec-impact-simulator (M) — input: a table of already-classified variants, each row a variant × its per-criterion ACMG codes + Bayesian points. Toggle a proposed spec change (e.g., PM2 Moderate→Supporting; cap PS3 at Moderate); the tool recomputes total points via the Bayesian combining rubric and outputs a before/after class for every variant plus a count of tier crossings and the list of movers. Encodes per-gene caps (e.g., a Moderate gene-disease relationship limits max class to LP).

What to verify first

  • Whether RAS-calibrated REVEL cutoffs transfer to CFTR — almost certainly not; anchor on CFTR-specific REVEL calibration (Bergquist et al. line of work) instead.
  • Data quality behind point-based case scores — the simulator's downgrade counts are only as good as the per-criterion code assignments fed in.
  • Gene-specific combining exceptions (e.g., Moderate-validity caps, GoF-vs-LoF handling of splice/PVS1) must be encoded per gene, not assumed uniform.
  • This concerns only the classification logic layered on top of variant data — it makes no assumption about how any particular registry stores genotype or phenotype internally.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读片室。 Today's Track A paper is "Updated ACMG/AMP specifications for variant interpretation and gene curations from the ClinGen RASopathy expert panels," first-authored by Emma Wilcox out of the Broad Institute, published in Genetics in Medicine Open in 2025. 今天的 Track A 论文是《来自 ClinGen RASopathy 专家组的变异解读与基因判定的 ACMG/AMP 标准更新》，第一作者是 Broad 研究所的 Emma Wilcox，2025 年发表于 Genetics in Medicine Open。 And I want to be upfront about something: this is not a CFTR paper. 我要先说清楚一件事：这不是一篇关于 CFTR 的论文。 It is about RASopathies — Noonan syndrome and its relatives. 它讲的是 RASopathy——努南综合征 (Noonan syndrome) 及其相关疾病。 We picked it anyway, because it is the cleanest recent blueprint for how a gene-specific expert panel actually writes the rules that classify variants, and that machinery is exactly what a CFTR classification effort rides on. 我们仍然选了它，因为它是近期最清晰的范本，展示了一个针对特定基因的专家组究竟如何撰写用于变异分类的规则，而这套机制正是 CFTR 分类工作所依赖的。

JORDAN Okay, so before we go anywhere, I need the vocabulary. 好，那我们开始之前，我需要先搞清楚术语。 What is a VCEP, and what is this ACMG/AMP thing? 什么是 VCEP，什么又是 ACMG/AMP？

ALEX Good place to start. 这是个很好的起点。 ACMG/AMP is the 2015 framework — from the American College of Medical Genetics and the Association for Molecular Pathology — for deciding whether a DNA variant is pathogenic or benign. ACMG/AMP 是 2015 年的一套框架，由美国医学遗传学会 (ACMG) 和分子病理学会 (AMP) 制定，用来判断一个 DNA 变异是致病的还是良性的。 It works by a set of evidence codes. 它的运作方式是一套证据代码。 Each code is a letter-number tag: PVS1 is "predicted null variant, very strong," PM2 is "absent from population databases, moderate," PP3 is "computational tools predict damage, supporting," and on the benign side you have BA1, BS3, BP4, and so on. 每个代码是一个字母加数字的标签：PVS1 表示"预测的无功能变异，非常强"，PM2 表示"在人群数据库中缺失，中等强度"，PP3 表示"计算工具预测有害，支持性证据"，良性一侧则有 BA1、BS3、BP4 等等。 P is pathogenic, B is benign, and the middle letters — VS, S, M, P — are the strength. P 代表致病 (pathogenic)，B 代表良性 (benign)，中间的字母 VS、S、M、P 表示证据强度（非常强、强、中等、支持性）。 A VCEP is a Variant Curation Expert Panel — a standing international group of geneticists and lab scientists that ClinGen authorizes to take that generic framework and specialize it for one gene or disease family. VCEP 是变异判定专家组 (Variant Curation Expert Panel)——一个由 ClinGen 授权的、常设的国际遗传学家和实验室科学家小组，负责把这套通用框架针对某一个基因或疾病家族进行专门化定制。

JORDAN And why does it need specializing? 那为什么需要专门化定制呢？ Isn't a rule a rule? 规则不就是规则吗？

ALEX That is the whole point of the paper, so let me sit on it. 这正是这篇论文的核心，所以我要多讲两句。 The generic ACMG/AMP framework is deliberately vague, because a rule that fits every gene fits none of them well. 通用的 ACMG/AMP 框架是刻意保持模糊的，因为一条适用于所有基因的规则，对每个基因其实都不够贴合。 Take PM2 — "the variant is absent from gnomAD." 拿 PM2 举例——"该变异在 gnomAD 中缺失"。 gnomAD is the Genome Aggregation Database, the reference catalog of variation across roughly 800,000 people. gnomAD（基因组聚合数据库，收录了约八十万人遗传变异的参考目录）。 For a common recessive disease, absence means a lot; for an ultra-rare dominant one, almost every variant is absent, so the code carries little information. 对于一种常见的隐性遗传病，"缺失"意味着很多；而对于一种极为罕见的显性遗传病，几乎每个变异都是缺失的，所以这个代码携带的信息量就很少。 RASopathies are unusual in a way that matters here: they are driven by gain-of-function variants. RASopathy 在一个关键点上很不寻常：它们是由功能获得型 (gain-of-function) 变异驱动的。 Most disease genes are loss-of-function — the variant breaks the protein. 大多数疾病基因是功能丧失型 (loss-of-function)——变异破坏了蛋白质。 In RASopathies the variant makes the RAS/MAPK signaling pathway hyperactive instead. 而在 RASopathy 中，变异反而让 RAS/MAPK 信号通路过度活跃。 That single fact bends how almost every evidence code should be applied. 就这一个事实，就改变了几乎每一条证据代码应当如何被应用。

JORDAN So the panel had already written RASopathy rules once. 所以这个专家组之前已经写过一版 RASopathy 规则了。 What forced the update? 是什么迫使他们更新？

ALEX Three things, and they will sound familiar to anyone working a large registry. 三个原因，任何在大型登记库里工作的人都会觉得眼熟。 First, the science moved: they recognized a new recessive form of Noonan syndrome tied to biallelic LZTR1 variants, which the old dominant-only rules could not handle. 第一，科学在进步：他们识别出一种新的隐性型努南综合征，与双等位基因 LZTR1 变异相关，而旧的仅针对显性遗传的规则无法处理这种情况。 Second, testing changed: variants now show up incidentally in exome and genome sequencing, in people who were never suspected of having a RASopathy, and the rules had no category for that. 第二，检测方式变了：变异现在会在外显子组和基因组测序中偶然出现，出现在那些从未被怀疑患有 RASopathy 的人身上，而规则里没有相应的类别。 Third, the central ClinGen Sequence Variant Interpretation working group issued new recommendations that every panel is expected to fold in. 第三，ClinGen 的中央序列变异解读 (Sequence Variant Interpretation, SVI) 工作组发布了新建议，每个专家组都应当纳入。 The contested question underneath all of it: if you tighten the rules, do you quietly destabilize thousands of variants that labs already reported to patients? 所有这些背后有一个有争议的问题：如果你收紧规则，是否会悄悄地动摇成千上万个实验室已经报告给患者的变异？

ALEX Let me walk the methods, because the structure is the transferable part. 让我梳理一下方法，因为它的结构才是可迁移的部分。 They did two jobs. 他们做了两件事。 The gene curation panel re-evaluated six gene-disease relationships using ClinGen's scoring rubric, awarding points for case-level and functional evidence. 基因判定专家组用 ClinGen 的评分标准重新评估了六个基因-疾病关系，为病例层面和功能层面的证据打分。 The variant panel then rewrote the criteria: eleven codes for dominant disease, three for the new recessive disease, and four specifically to align with the central SVI recommendations. 随后变异专家组重写了标准：针对显性遗传病的十一个代码、针对新发现的隐性遗传病的三个代码，以及专门用于与中央 SVI 建议保持一致的四个代码。 The changes I would flag for us: they dropped PM2 from moderate to supporting strength, to stop over-weighting mere rarity. 我想特别指出的改动是：他们把 PM2 从中等强度降为支持性强度，以避免过度看重"罕见"本身。 They moved de novo evidence and case-observation codes — PS2, PM6, PS4 — onto an explicit point-based system, where a proband contributes 0.5, 1, or more points depending on how well their phenotype matches and whether parentage is confirmed. 他们把新发变异 (de novo) 证据和病例观察代码——PS2、PM6、PS4——改成了一套明确的积分制，一个先证者根据其表型匹配程度以及亲子关系是否确认，贡献 0.5、1 或更多分。 And they calibrated REVEL. 他们还校准了 REVEL。

JORDAN REVEL being the computational predictor. REVEL 就是那个计算预测工具。

ALEX Right — REVEL is an ensemble score that blends thirteen individual missense predictors into one number from zero to one. 对——REVEL（一种把十三个独立的错义预测工具融合成一个 0 到 1 之间数值的集成评分）。 The central recommendation is: use greater-than-or-equal-to 0.7 as supporting evidence for damage, PP3, and less-than-or-equal-to 0.3 as supporting evidence for benign, BP4. 中央建议是：用大于等于 0.7 作为支持有害的证据（即 PP3），用小于等于 0.3 作为支持良性的证据（即 BP4）。 The panel did not just adopt those numbers; they tested them on their own variants. 专家组没有直接照搬这些数字，而是在他们自己的变异集上做了检验。 Here is my one pushback on the design. 这是我对其设计的一点质疑。 REVEL, like most predictors, was trained on data that leans toward loss-of-function pathogenicity, but RASopathies are gain-of-function. REVEL 和大多数预测工具一样，其训练数据偏向功能丧失型的致病性，但 RASopathy 是功能获得型的。 The authors are honest about this — they explicitly decline to strengthen PP3 or BP4 beyond supporting, precisely because the algorithm's assumptions do not match the disease mechanism. 作者对此很坦诚——他们明确拒绝把 PP3 或 BP4 提升到支持性以上的强度，正是因为算法的假设与该疾病的机制不匹配。 That is exactly the kind of caveat that would transfer to CFTR, where the mechanism is again not vanilla loss-of-function. 这恰恰是可以迁移到 CFTR 的那类注意事项，因为 CFTR 的机制同样不是简单的功能丧失型。

JORDAN Before results — you mentioned a new recessive form and this LZTR1 gene twice. 在讲结果之前——你两次提到了一种新的隐性型和这个 LZTR1 基因。 What makes it hard? 它难在哪里？

ALEX LZTR1 is a lovely, nasty example of why gene-specific rules exist. LZTR1 是一个很典型也很棘手的例子，说明了为什么需要针对特定基因的规则。 The same gene causes disease two different ways: dominant Noonan syndrome through dominant-negative missense variants, and recessive Noonan syndrome through biallelic loss-of-function. 同一个基因以两种不同方式致病：通过显性负性 (dominant-negative) 的错义变异导致显性努南综合征，通过双等位基因的功能丧失导致隐性努南综合征。 So the panel wrote a decision procedure: establish the inheritance pattern from the family data first; if a variant looks like loss-of-function with no clear pattern, score it under the recessive rules — PVS1, PM3, and a stricter PM2 threshold below 0.000025 to account for carriers; otherwise lean on functional data. 于是专家组写了一套决策流程：先从家系数据确定遗传模式；如果一个变异看起来是功能丧失型且模式不明，就按隐性规则评分——用 PVS1、PM3，以及一个更严格的、低于 0.000025 的 PM2 阈值来考虑携带者；否则依靠功能数据。 If nothing resolves, it stays a variant of uncertain significance. 如果都无法确定，它就仍是意义不明的变异 (variant of uncertain significance, VUS)。 The transfer to CFTR is direct: whenever one gene has more than one mechanism, a single flat rulebook will misclassify, and you need mechanism-aware branching. 对 CFTR 的迁移是直接的：只要一个基因有不止一种机制，一套扁平的规则手册就会误分类，你需要能感知机制的分支逻辑。

JORDAN And there was something about borrowing evidence across related genes? 还有一点是关于在相关基因之间借用证据的？

ALEX Yes — PS1, and it is genuinely clever. 是的——PS1，而且它确实很巧妙。 PS1 normally says: if this exact amino-acid change was already proven pathogenic, you can borrow that strength. PS1 通常是说：如果这个完全相同的氨基酸改变已经被证明是致病的，你就可以借用那份证据强度。 The RAS panel extended it to paralogous positions — the same residue change at the analogous spot in a sister gene within the family. RAS 专家组把它扩展到了旁系同源 (paralogous) 位点——即在同一家族的姊妹基因中处于类似位置的相同残基改变。 A paralogue is a gene born from an ancestral duplication, so RAF1 and BRAF, or the several RAS genes, share aligned residues that do the same structural job. 旁系同源基因是由一次祖先基因复制产生的，所以 RAF1 和 BRAF、或几个 RAS 基因之间，会共享处于对齐位置、承担相同结构功能的残基。 They aligned each gene family and let a proven pathogenic change in one member lend evidence to the matching change in another. 他们对每个基因家族做了序列比对，让某一成员中已被证实致病的改变，为另一成员中对应的改变提供证据。 CFTR does not have a close paralogue family in the same way, so PS1 there stays literal — but the underlying move, borrowing calibrated evidence across a principled alignment, is worth keeping in the toolbox. CFTR 没有同样意义上的近旁系同源家族，所以在 CFTR 中 PS1 保持字面含义——但其底层思路，即沿着一个有原则的比对来借用经过校准的证据，值得留在工具箱里。

JORDAN So did the numbers hold up? 那这些数字站得住脚吗？

ALEX They held up well on their own terms. 就其自身而言，站得住脚。 For the REVEL calibration, forty pathogenic variants averaged a score of 0.83, with a standard deviation of 0.12; thirty-two benign variants averaged 0.25, standard deviation 0.17. 在 REVEL 校准方面，四十个致病变异的平均分是 0.83，标准差 0.12；三十二个良性变异平均分 0.25，标准差 0.17。 When they extended to a larger set, 99% of pathogenic and likely-pathogenic variants fell below the 0.3 benign cutoff, and 98.5% of benign variants fell above the 0.7 damaging cutoff — so the generic thresholds genuinely worked for these genes. 当他们扩展到更大的变异集时，99% 的致病和可能致病变异都落在了 0.3 这个良性阈值以下，98.5% 的良性变异落在了 0.7 这个有害阈值以上——所以通用阈值对这些基因确实有效。 On gene-disease validity, five of the six relationships were upgraded to Definitive; only MRAS stayed at Moderate, for lack of clinical cases. 在基因-疾病有效性方面，六个关系中有五个被升级为"明确 (Definitive)"；只有 MRAS 因缺乏临床病例仍停留在"中等 (Moderate)"。 But the result that should make us sit up is the pilot. 但真正应该让我们警觉的结果是那次预演。
