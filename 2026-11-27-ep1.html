How did this episode land for you?

dbNSFP v4: the lookup layer under every CFTR VUS call

dbNSFP v4.1 (Liu et al., Genome Medicine, 2020) is the precomputed lookup table underneath a huge share of variant-interpretation pipelines: every possible nonsynonymous and splice-site SNV in the human exome, annotated with 46 deleteriousness and conservation scores, now rebuilt on GRCh38/GENCODE29 with transcript-specific predictions and clean HGVS c./p. notation. The episode digs into what the update actually changed, and into results showing that transcript choice can swing a variant's apparent pathogenicity, that many of the 46 scores cluster into two correlated families (likely reflecting shared ClinVar training data), and that a new three-class VUROC metric reranks which predictors actually hold up against hard negative controls. It lands squarely on the Karen Raraigh collaboration, with a buildable MVP: a CFTR-focused aggregator that surfaces the VUROC-informed top predictors and flags transcript-disagreement variants, instead of treating all 46 columns as equally trustworthy votes.

Plain-English glossary

AUROC — Area under the receiver operating characteristic curve; measures how well a score separates true positives from negatives across all classification thresholds.（中文：ROC曲线下面积，衡量评分在各个分类阈值下区分真阳性与阴性样本能力的指标。）

Ensemble score — A predictor built by combining the outputs of several other predictors, typically via machine learning.（中文：集成评分，通过机器学习整合多个其他预测工具的输出所得到的综合评分。）

HGVS — The Human Genome Variation Society's standardized notation for describing a variant at the coding-DNA level ("c.") and the resulting protein level ("p.").（中文：变异命名的标准化书写规范，"c."表示编码DNA层面的改变，"p."表示由此导致的蛋白质层面改变。）

nsSNV (nonsynonymous SNV) — A single nucleotide variant that changes the amino acid encoded by a codon; a missense variant.（中文：改变密码子所编码氨基酸的单核苷酸变异，即错义变异。）

ssSNV (splice-site SNV) — A single nucleotide variant near an exon-intron boundary that can disrupt splicing of the pre-mRNA.（中文：位于外显子-内含子交界附近、可能干扰前体mRNA剪接过程的单核苷酸变异。）

Transcript support level (TSL) — A tiered system rating how well a gene-model transcript is supported by experimental evidence, used alongside APPRIS to choose which transcript's score to trust.（中文：转录本支持等级，根据实验证据对转录本模型可靠程度进行分级的系统，常与APPRIS搭配使用，帮助选择该信任哪个转录本的评分。）

VUROC — A three-class extension of AUROC introduced in this paper, jointly ranking true-positive, common-variant, and singleton-variant groups in a single metric.（中文：本文提出的三分类ROC曲面下体积指标，可同时对真阳性组、常见变异组与单例变异组进行联合排序。）

Full citation

Liu X, Li C, Mou C, Dong Y, Tu Z. "dbNSFP v4: a comprehensive database of transcript-specific functional predictions and annotations for human nonsynonymous and splice-site SNVs." Genome Medicine (2020). PMID: 33261662 · PMC: PMC7709417 · DOI: 10.1186/s13073-020-00803-9

Methods / tools to lift

  • dbNSFP — a precomputed table of 46 deleteriousness/conservation scores for every possible nsSNV/ssSNV, queryable by HGVS notation, genomic position, rsID, or gene.
  • WGSA — the pipeline dbNSFP uses to reconcile ANNOVAR/snpEff/VEP HGVS c./p. notation into one consistent representation per variant.
  • VUROC — a 3-class AUROC generalization for benchmarking predictors against both common and rare (singleton) negative controls simultaneously, rather than one control set at a time.
  • APPRIS / TSL — transcript-quality tiering usable as a tiebreaker whenever transcript-specific scores disagree for the same variant.
  • dbscSNV / SPIDEX / SpliceAI / dbMTS — companion databases joint-queryable with dbNSFP for splice-effect and microRNA-target-site predictions.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Karen / CFTR variant interpretation and notation harmonization. Buildable MVP: cftr_vus_aggregator.py — input a list of CFTR variants (HGVS or canonical-transcript VCF coordinates), pull the matching dbNSFP CFTR-region rows, rank by the VUROC-top predictors (BayesDel_addAF, ClinPred, VEST4, REVEL) rather than a naive 46-way vote, and flag any variant with high transcript-score dispersal for manual transcript-choice review. Difficulty: S/M.

What to verify first

  • Which CFTR transcript dbNSFP's transcript-specific columns are actually keyed to, versus the clinical MANE Select / CFTR2 convention — a mismatch would invalidate the transcript-dispersal flag.
  • Whether any flagged CFTR variant that's already in ClinVar shares training lineage with the top-ranked predictors, before treating multi-score agreement as independent confirmation.
  • That newer predictors (AlphaMissense, ESM-based models, EVE) get layered in on top, since this 2020 snapshot predates all of them.
  • Missing-data handling: ClinPred, MutationAssessor, and especially LINSIGHT (noncoding-focused) have double-digit-to-near-total missing rates for some positions, so the aggregator needs a fallback ranking, not just the top predictor.
  • No claims made about CFFPR's own internal variant storage or reporting conventions anywhere in this episode — that mapping is for Runyu and the registry team to work out directly.

Read full transcript (42 turns · ~1735 words)

ALEX Today's paper is dbNSFP version four point one, published in Genome Medicine in 2020 by Xiaoming Liu and colleagues. 今天要讲的论文是 dbNSFP 4.1 版本，由刘晓明（Xiaoming Liu）等人于2020年发表在《Genome Medicine》期刊上。It's a database, not a predictor, and it sits in Track A, our variant-interpretation track. 它是一个数据库，而不是一个预测模型，属于我们的赛道A——变异解读方向。

JORDAN A database episode — why does that matter for Karen's work? 一期讲数据库的节目——这对 Karen 的工作有什么意义？

ALEX Because almost every VUS pathogenicity call starts by pulling scores out of something shaped exactly like this, and dbNSFP v4 is still one of the most widely used lookup layers underneath clinical variant pipelines. 因为几乎每一次VUS（意义不明变异）致病性判断，起点都是从像这样的数据库里取出评分，而dbNSFP v4至今仍是临床变异分析流程背后最常用的查询层之一。

JORDAN Let's start with the acronyms — what's an nsSNV versus an ssSNV? 我们先从缩写说起——nsSNV 和 ssSNV 有什么区别？

ALEX An SNV is a single nucleotide variant — one base changed in the DNA sequence. SNV（单核苷酸变异，single nucleotide variant）是指DNA序列中单个碱基发生了改变。A nonsynonymous SNV, or nsSNV, changes the amino acid a codon encodes — that's a missense variant. 错义变异（nonsynonymous SNV，简称nsSNV）会改变密码子编码的氨基酸——也就是我们常说的missense变异。A splice-site SNV, or ssSNV, sits near an exon-intron boundary and can disrupt how the spliceosome cuts and joins the pre-mRNA. 剪接位点变异（splice-site SNV，简称ssSNV）位于外显子-内含子交界处附近，可能干扰剪接体对前体mRNA的剪切和拼接过程。

JORDAN And HGVS — I keep seeing "c." and "p." notations in reports. HGVS——我总在报告里看到"c."和"p."这样的写法。

ALEX HGVS is the Human Genome Variation Society's standardized notation. HGVS（变异命名的标准化书写规范，Human Genome Variation Society）是一套标准化的变异命名体系。A "c." designation describes the change at the coding-DNA level, like c.1521_1523delCTT, and a "p." designation describes the resulting protein change, like p.Phe508del — that's the CFTR deletion you already know as F508del. "c."表示在编码DNA层面的改变，例如 c.1521_1523delCTT；"p."表示由此导致的蛋白质层面改变，例如 p.Phe508del——也就是你熟悉的CFTR缺失变异F508del。

JORDAN And transcript-specific scoring — why would the same variant get different scores depending on transcript? 转录本特异性评分——为什么同一个变异会因为转录本不同而得到不同的评分？

ALEX A gene can have multiple transcript isoforms, and a variant's codon position, or even whether it falls in a coding exon at all, can differ between them. 一个基因可以有多个转录本（isoform），同一个变异在不同转录本中的密码子位置，甚至是否落在编码外显子内，都可能不同。dbNSFP uses APPRIS and TSL — transcript support level, a tier system for how well-supported a transcript model is — to help you pick the right one. dbNSFP 使用 APPRIS 和 TSL（转录本支持等级，Transcript Support Level，一种衡量转录本模型可靠程度的分级指标）来帮助你选择合适的转录本。

ALEX The motivation goes back to the explosion of whole-exome and whole-genome sequencing in clinical and research settings. 这项工作的动机要追溯到临床和科研环境中外显子组测序（WES）和全基因组测序（WGS）的爆发式增长。Every sequencing run throws off thousands of variants, and investigators need a fast way to triage which ones could plausibly be disease-causing. 每一次测序都会产生成千上万个变异，研究者需要一种快速的方法来筛选出哪些变异可能真正致病。

JORDAN So what existed before dbNSFP? 那在dbNSFP之前已经有哪些工具？

ALEX Tools like Ensembl's VEP, ANNOVAR, and SnpEff — these do gene-model-based annotation, telling you a variant is nonsynonymous, which gene and exon it's in, things like that. 像 Ensembl 的 VEP（Variant Effect Predictor，变异效应预测器）、ANNOVAR 和 SnpEff 这些工具，做的是基于基因模型的注释——告诉你某个变异是不是错义变异、它在哪个基因和外显子里。But they mostly stop at description — they don't tell you how deleterious a variant actually is. 但它们大多止步于描述性注释——并不会告诉你这个变异究竟有多大的致病可能性。For that you need a separate predictor, like SIFT, PolyPhen2, or CADD, and historically you'd have to run each one separately and stitch the outputs together yourself. 要知道这一点，你需要单独的预测工具，比如 SIFT、PolyPhen2 或 CADD，而过去你得把它们一个个跑一遍，再自己把结果拼接起来。

JORDAN That sounds like exactly the kind of busywork a database should eliminate. 这听起来正是数据库应该消除的那种繁琐劳动。

ALEX Exactly — that's dbNSFP's whole premise, going back to version one: pre-compute a huge table of every possible nonsynonymous and splice-site SNV, annotated with dozens of predictor scores, so you just look up your variant instead of running tools from scratch. 没错——这正是dbNSFP从第一版起的核心理念：把每一个可能的错义和剪接位点SNV都预先算好，标注上几十种预测工具的评分，做成一张庞大的表格，这样你只需要查表，而不必从头跑一遍工具。This paper covers the jump from version 3 to version 4.1, and it's a substantial rebuild, not a patch. 这篇论文讲的是从第3版到4.1版的跃迁，是一次大规模重建，而不是小修小补。

JORDAN What changed structurally? 结构上发生了什么变化？

ALEX They rebuilt the whole variant list on the GRCh38 reference and GENCODE version 29 gene models — the older versions were still pinned to hg19 — and they only kept transcripts with complete protein-coding annotations. 他们在 GRCh38 参考基因组和 GENCODE 第29版基因模型基础上重建了整个变异列表——旧版本还停留在hg19——并且只保留了具有完整蛋白编码注释的转录本。They nearly doubled the predictor count, from 28 to 46 scores total, added a loss-of-function score called ALoFT, and — this is the part that matters most for harmonization work — they started reporting proper HGVS c. and p. notation for every variant, generated through a pipeline called WGSA that reconciles ANNOVAR, snpEff, and VEP outputs. 预测评分数量几乎翻倍，从28种增加到总共46种，还新增了一个名为ALoFT的功能丧失（loss-of-function）评分；而对变异标注协调工作来说最重要的一点是，他们开始为每个变异提供规范的HGVS c.和p.记法，这是通过一个叫WGSA的流程生成的，该流程会整合ANNOVAR、snpEff和VEP三者的输出结果使其保持一致。That reconciliation step is exactly the kind of notation-harmonization problem you'll run into constantly. 这种协调步骤正是你会反复遇到的那类变异命名协调问题。

JORDAN Walk me through what actually ends up in the table. 具体说说表里到底有什么内容。

ALEX For every one of the 81.8 million nonsynonymous SNVs and 2.2 million splice-site SNVs they enumerate, you get up to 36 deleteriousness prediction scores, 9 conservation scores, and that one loss-of-function score — 46 columns of prediction alone. 对于他们枚举的8180万个错义SNV和220万个剪接位点SNV中的每一个，你最多能拿到36种致病性预测评分、9种保守性评分，再加上那一个功能丧失评分——光预测类列就有46个。Thirteen of the deleteriousness scores are transcript-specific, meaning they're computed per-isoform rather than collapsed to one value per genomic position. 其中13种致病性评分是转录本特异性的，也就是说它们是按每个转录本分别计算的，而不是对每个基因组位置只给出一个合并值。They also mapped every protein ID between GENCODE/Ensembl and UniProt, and pulled in transcript-quality tiers like APPRIS and TSL so you have a principled way to pick which transcript's score to trust. 他们还把每个蛋白质ID在GENCODE/Ensembl和UniProt之间做了映射，并引入了APPRIS和TSL这样的转录本质量分级，让你有一个有依据的方法来选择该信任哪个转录本的评分。

JORDAN And delivery — how do you actually query it? 那实际怎么查询呢？

ALEX Two ways: a downloadable standalone version with a Java command-line and GUI tool for bulk, offline queries — good for scanning an entire exome — and a web service backed by SQL Server for one-off lookups of a handful of variants. 有两种方式：一种是可下载的独立版本，配有Java命令行工具和图形界面，适合批量、离线查询——适合扫描整个外显子组；另一种是由SQL Server支撑的网页服务，适合临时查几个变异。

JORDAN Anything else bundled in besides the 46 scores themselves? 除了这46种评分本身，还捆绑了别的内容吗？

ALEX Yes — the downloadable version also lets you jointly query four companion databases for splice and microRNA effects: dbscSNV and SPIDEX for splice-interrupting SNVs, the newly added support for SpliceAI, a third-party deep-learning splice predictor, and dbMTS for microRNA target-site variants. 有的——下载版本还支持联合查询四个针对剪接和microRNA效应的配套数据库：dbscSNV和SPIDEX用于预测干扰剪接的SNV，新增支持的SpliceAI是一个第三方的深度学习剪接预测工具，而dbMTS则用于microRNA靶位点变异。For an ssSNV flagged as splice-relevant, that's useful — you get a splice-specific second opinion without leaving the same query. 对于被标记为剪接相关的ssSNV来说，这很有用——你不用跳出同一次查询，就能获得一个针对剪接的"第二意见"。

JORDAN Where would you push back on this design? 你会在设计上提出什么质疑？

ALEX Here's my pushback — stacking 46 scores into one table makes consumption easy, but it doesn't resolve a deeper problem: a lot of these predictors, especially the ensemble ones like REVEL, ClinPred, and BayesDel, were trained wholly or partly on ClinVar-derived labels. 我的质疑在这里——把46种评分堆进一张表确实方便使用，但它并没有解决一个更深层的问题：很多预测工具，尤其是REVEL、ClinPred和BayesDel这类集成评分（ensemble score），其训练数据全部或部分来自ClinVar标签。So when several of them "agree" on a variant, that agreement can partly reflect shared training data rather than independent evidence. 所以当其中几个工具对某个变异"达成一致"时，这种一致性可能部分反映的是共享的训练数据，而不是真正独立的证据。dbNSFP doesn't flag which scores share lineage, so a naive majority vote across its 46 columns can quietly double-count the same underlying signal. dbNSFP 并不会标注哪些评分系出同源，因此如果天真地对这46列做多数投票，很可能会在不知不觉中对同一个底层信号重复计数。

JORDAN Did they quantify how much the transcript choice actually matters? 他们有没有量化转录本选择到底有多大影响？

ALEX Yes — for variants that hit more than one transcript, they computed a dispersal statistic across the 12 transcript-specific scores, basically how spread out the min, max, and average scores are for the same variant. 有的——对于命中多个转录本的变异，他们针对12种转录本特异性评分计算了一个离散统计量，本质上衡量的是同一变异在不同转录本上的最小值、最大值和平均值之间的差异程度。Except for FATHMM, every one of those scores showed a sizable chunk of variants with high dispersal, meaning picking the "wrong" transcript can meaningfully change whether a variant looks benign or damaging. 除FATHMM外，其余每一种评分都有相当一部分变异表现出高离散度，这意味着选错转录本可能会实实在在地改变一个变异看起来是良性还是致病的判断。

JORDAN And across all 45 scores, how correlated are they with each other? 那在全部45种评分之间，彼此的相关性如何？

ALEX About 43% of pairwise correlations were strong, above 0.5, and the scores actually split into two separated clusters by hierarchical clustering — one cluster with CADD, ClinPred, BayesDel, and REVEL, and a second with MetaLR, MetaSVM, M-CAP, and DEOGEN2. 大约43%的两两相关性属于强相关（高于0.5），通过层次聚类发现这些评分实际上分成两个独立的簇——一簇包括CADD、ClinPred、BayesDel和REVEL，另一簇包括MetaLR、MetaSVM、M-CAP和DEOGEN2。That's consistent with my circularity point — scores within a cluster likely share training signal. 这和我前面提到的循环性问题是吻合的——同一簇内的评分很可能共享了训练信号。Median binary-prediction agreement across scores was 0.65 — reasonably high, but far from consensus. 各评分间二分类预测的中位一致率为0.65——算是相当高，但远算不上共识。

JORDAN What about raw predictive performance? 那原始预测性能表现如何？

ALEX They benchmarked against ClinVar-derived true positives, against two negative control sets — common gnomAD variants and singleton gnomAD variants. 他们以ClinVar来源的真阳性变异为基准，对照两组阴性对照集——gnomAD中的常见变异和单例（singleton）变异。Against common-variant negatives, the best AUROC hit 0.999 — almost trivial. 以常见变异为阴性对照时，最佳AUROC达到0.999——几乎是平凡的结果。Against the much harder singleton negatives, the best dropped to 0.837, with ClinPred, VEST4, REVEL, MutPred, and BayesDel_addAF leading. 而以更难区分的单例变异为阴性对照时，最佳成绩降到0.837，领先的是ClinPred、VEST4、REVEL、MutPred和BayesDel_addAF。They also introduced a three-class metric, VUROC, to jointly rank true-positive, common, and singleton groups at once — BayesDel_addAF topped that at 0.744, with ClinPred close behind. 他们还引入了一个三分类指标VUROC（三分类ROC曲面下体积，对AUROC的扩展，可同时对真阳性组、常见变异组和单例变异组进行联合排序），在该指标上BayesDel_addAF以0.744排名第一，ClinPred紧随其后。

JORDAN One practical thing before we move on — how complete is the data, score to score? 在往下讲之前，再问一个实际问题——各评分的数据完整度怎么样？

ALEX Uneven, and worth knowing before you build anything on top of it. 并不均衡，而这是你在此基础上搭建任何工具之前都应该了解的。Average missing rate across the 36 deleteriousness scores is 11%, MVP has the lowest at 0.028%, but ClinPred is missing 21.7% of positions, MutationAssessor 22.2%, and LINSIGHT a striking 97.7% — though that last one is expected, since LINSIGHT was designed for noncoding variants, not coding SNVs. 在这36种致病性评分中，平均缺失率为11%，MVP的缺失率最低，只有0.028%，但ClinPred缺失21.7%的位点，MutationAssessor缺失22.2%，LINSIGHT的缺失率高达97.7%——不过最后这一点是预期之中的，因为LINSIGHT本来就是为非编码变异设计的，不是针对编码区SNV的。So any aggregator needs a fallback ranking when a top-tier predictor like ClinPred is simply absent for a given CFTR position. 所以任何聚合工具都需要有一套备用排序方案，以应对像ClinPred这样的顶级预测工具恰好在某个CFTR位点缺失数据的情况。

ALEX This maps directly onto the Karen collaboration — VUS pathogenicity prediction and notation harmonization are literally what dbNSFP is built for. 这直接对应到与Karen的合作——VUS致病性预测和变异命名协调，正是dbNSFP存在的意义所在。

JORDAN So what's the buildable thing here? 那这里有什么可以动手做的东西？

ALEX A CFTR-focused multi-predictor aggregator. 一个聚焦CFTR的多预测工具聚合器。Input: a list of CFTR variants, either as HGVS or VCF coordinates on the canonical transcript. 输入：一份CFTR变异列表，可以是HGVS格式，也可以是基于规范转录本的VCF坐标。The tool pulls the relevant rows out of dbNSFP's CFTR region, surfaces the VUROC-informed top predictors — BayesDel, ClinPred, VEST4, REVEL — rather than a naive 46-way vote, and separately flags any variant where the transcript-dispersal statistic is high, so you know when a second opinion on transcript choice is needed before trusting the score at all. 工具会从dbNSFP里提取出CFTR区域相关的行，优先展示VUROC指标推荐的顶尖预测工具——BayesDel、ClinPred、VEST4、REVEL——而不是对46种评分做一次天真的投票，同时单独标记出转录本离散度统计量较高的变异，提醒你在信任评分之前，需要先就转录本选择取得第二意见。

JORDAN How hard would that be to prototype? 原型做起来有多难？
