Show notes — Track C — 2026-08-03

Challenges of Preimplantation Genetic Counselling in the Context of Cystic Fibrosis and Other CFTR-Related Disorders: A Monocentric Experience in a Cohort of 92 Couples — Sorrentino U, Menegazzo M, Gabbiato I, Calosci D, Zambon CF, Zuccarello D. Genes, 2024.

Blurb

A retrospective clinical review from the University Hospital of Padua (2016–2022) of 92 couples referred for genetic counselling to assess eligibility for PGT-M (Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Monogenic disorders) around CF and CFTR-related disorders. Across 184 individuals the clinic found 51 distinct CFTR variants, reassessed every one against CFTR2, CFTR-France, and ClinVar simultaneously, and turned up a concrete database-discordance example: c.3485G>T (R1162L) is classified as CFTR-RD causing by CFTR-France but non-CF-causing by CFTR2 — the same DNA change, opposite verdicts depending on which database a lab checks. Fifty of 92 couples were eligible for PGT-M, 11 landed in genuine uncertainty (VUS combinations), and 16 completed at least one ART/PGT-M cycle (8 unaffected births, 8 without success). For us this lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation and harmonization: the buildable artifact is a discordance flagger that queries CFTR2, CFTR-France, and ClinVar side by side for any CFTR variant and surfaces disagreement rather than picking a winner.

Plain-English glossary

  • PGT-M — Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Monogenic disorders; tests IVF embryos before transfer so only unaffected/carrier embryos are implanted.（中文：单基因病植入前遗传检测——在体外受精胚胎移植前进行检测，只移植未受累或携带者胚胎。）
  • CFTR-related disorder (CFTR-RD) — a clinical entity involving CFTR dysfunction (e.g. CBAVD, chronic pancreatitis) that doesn't meet full diagnostic criteria for classic CF.（中文：CFTR 相关疾病——涉及 CFTR 功能障碍但不符合经典 CF 完整诊断标准的病症，如先天性双侧输精管缺如、慢性胰腺炎。）
  • CBAVD — congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens; the most common CFTR-RD, causing obstructive azoospermia.（中文：先天性双侧输精管缺如——最常见的 CFTR 相关疾病，导致梗阻性无精症。）
  • MLPA — Multiplex Ligation-dependent Probe Amplification; detects larger deletions/duplications that standard sequencing can miss.（中文：多重连接依赖式探针扩增技术——用于检测标准测序可能漏检的较大缺失或重复。）
  • Poly-T tract / TG-repeat (intron 8) — a splicing-modifying repeat element adjacent to the CFTR 5T allele; longer TG repeats (TG12/TG13/TG14) raise penetrance/severity of the same 5T variant.（中文：poly-T 区段／TG 重复（第 8 内含子）——位于 CFTR 5T 等位基因旁、影响剪接的重复序列；更长的 TG 重复会提高同一个 5T 变异的外显率／严重程度。）
  • VCC (varying clinical consequence) — a CFTR2 classification for variants that don't reliably predict a single clinical outcome.（中文：临床后果可变——CFTR2 用于那些无法可靠预测单一临床结局的变异分类。）
  • ART — assisted reproductive technology (e.g. IVF), the umbrella procedure PGT-M is performed within.（中文：辅助生殖技术——如体外受精，PGT-M 是在其框架内进行的检测。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Three-source variant reclassification — reassess every CFTR variant against CFTR2, CFTR-France, and ClinVar simultaneously (plus manual literature search) rather than trusting one database.
  • NGS whole-gene sequencing + MLPA reflex testing — second/third-level workup when a partner's second pathogenic allele can't otherwise be confirmed.
  • CFTR2/CFTR-France modulator cross-reference — check whether a predicted-offspring genotype would be eligible for CFTR modulator therapy, folded directly into counseling.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation & harmonization. MVP (paste-ready): Given a list of CFTR variants (HGVS or legacy notation), normalize each and query classification from CFTR2, CFTR-France, and ClinVar side by side. Output a table flagging any variant where the three sources disagree, showing all three labels rather than collapsing to one verdict. Difficulty: M (CFTR-France has no clean API — scraping/snapshot maintenance is the real cost).

What to verify first

  • CFTR-France access — no formal API; confirm any scraping approach is reliable and current, or use a timestamped periodic snapshot.
  • No majority-vote shortcut — three-way disagreement shouldn't auto-resolve by 2-vs-1; a single strong functional study can outweigh two databases that haven't incorporated it yet.
  • Snapshot currency — this cohort's classifications reflect a Dec-2023 database snapshot; any variant flagged today needs a fresh lookup, not a assumption that Table 1 is still accurate.
  • Gap-map — CLOSES: concrete counseling-scale numbers for CFTR variant spectrum, PGT-M eligibility outcomes, modulator-eligibility overlap, and a real cross-database classification discordance (R1162L); reinforces the TG-repeat/poly-T cis-modifier haplotype with population frequencies (TG12T5/TG13T5/TG14T5) and a borderline real case. LEAVES OPEN: which database "wins" on a discordant call requires expert re-adjudication, not automation; ART/PGT-M success is still bounded by ordinary IVF biology regardless of genetic clarity.

JORDAN Let's set up the vocabulary. 我们先把词汇讲清楚。 What's the difference between PGT-M and ordinary prenatal diagnosis? PGT-M 和普通的产前诊断有什么区别？

ALEX Prenatal diagnosis tests a fetus after a pregnancy is already established, so a positive result forces a couple to decide whether to continue an existing pregnancy. 产前诊断是在怀孕已经发生之后对胎儿进行检测，所以一个阳性结果会迫使夫妇决定是否继续这次已经存在的妊娠。 PGT-M — Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Monogenic disorders — happens earlier, on embryos created through in vitro fertilization, before any embryo is transferred to the uterus. PGT-M——单基因病植入前遗传检测 (Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Monogenic disorders)——发生得更早，是在通过体外受精 (in vitro fertilization) 创建的胚胎上进行的，在任何胚胎被移植入子宫之前。 The couple only transfers embryos found to be unaffected or, at most, a healthy carrier, so the decision moves from "what do we do about this pregnancy" to "which embryo do we transfer." 夫妇只移植那些被判定为未受累、或至多是健康携带者的胚胎，于是决策就从“我们该如何处理这次妊娠”转变为“我们该移植哪一个胚胎”。 CF was actually the very first monogenic disease ever diagnosed this way, back in 1992. 事实上，CF 正是 1992 年历史上第一个通过这种方式被诊断的单基因病。

JORDAN And CFTR-related disorders, or CFTR-RD — how is that different from classic CF? 那 CFTR 相关疾病 (CFTR-related disorders, CFTR-RD) 呢——它和经典 CF 有什么不同？

ALEX CFTR dysfunction isn't all-or-nothing; different combinations of variants leave different amounts of residual channel function, so you get a whole spectrum of presentations. CFTR 功能障碍不是全有或全无的；不同的变异组合会留下不同程度的残余通道功能，于是就出现了一整个表现谱系。 Classic CF is the severe end — lung disease, pancreatic insufficiency, and so on. 经典 CF 是严重的那一端——肺部疾病、胰腺功能不全等等。 CFTR-RD is the term for conditions that involve CFTR dysfunction but don't meet the full diagnostic criteria for CF — the most common examples being congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens, or CBAVD, in men, and chronic or recurrent pancreatitis. CFTR-RD 则是指那些涉及 CFTR 功能障碍、但不符合 CF 完整诊断标准的病症——最常见的例子是男性的先天性双侧输精管缺如 (CBAVD)，以及慢性或复发性胰腺炎。 That spectrum is exactly why counseling is hard: the same variant combination information has to predict where on that spectrum a future child would land. 正是这个谱系让咨询变得困难：同样的变异组合信息，必须预测未来孩子会落在这个谱系的哪个位置上。

JORDAN What problem was this clinic actually trying to solve by writing this paper up? 这家诊所写这篇论文，究竟是想解决什么问题？

ALEX CF has an unusually high carrier frequency — up to one in twenty-five people of Caucasian ancestry — and several forces have converged to push more couples toward PGT-M specifically. CF 的携带者频率异常高——在白种人血统人群中高达二十五分之一——而且有几股力量正合力把更多夫妇推向 PGT-M 这一具体选项。 Carrier-screening programmes have expanded, so more people learn their carrier status before ever trying to conceive. 携带者筛查项目不断扩大，于是更多人在尝试怀孕之前就已经知道了自己的携带者状态。 CFTR modulator therapies — elexacaftor, tezacaftor, ivacaftor, and lumacaftor — have meaningfully extended life expectancy and quality of life, which changes how couples weigh the prospect of an affected child. CFTR 调节剂疗法——依伐卡托、泰扎卡托、艾利卡托和鲁马卡托——已经显著延长了预期寿命并提升了生活质量，这改变了夫妇权衡“生一个受累孩子”这件事的方式。 And more precise, more affordable sequencing means variant panels keep growing, which sounds like unambiguous progress but actually multiplies the number of borderline, hard-to-classify results a genetic counselor has to interpret. 而更精准、更实惠的测序意味着变异检测面板在不断扩大，这听起来像是毫无争议的进步，实际上却成倍增加了遗传咨询师需要解读的、处于边界地带、难以分类的结果数量。 The clinic's own patients arrive having already been tested by many different external labs, each with its own variant panel, so before any counseling can happen, someone has to reconcile results generated under completely different assumptions about which variants even matter. 这家诊所自己的病人到来时，往往已经被许多不同的外部实验室检测过，每家实验室都有自己的一套变异面板，所以在任何咨询开始之前，都必须有人先把这些在完全不同假设下——关于哪些变异才算数——生成的结果协调统一起来。 So this paper is really a case study in what "variant interpretation" costs in practice, once it has to produce a single clear yes-or-no answer for a couple sitting in the room. 所以这篇论文其实是一个案例研究，展示了“变异解读”在实践中真正需要付出的代价——一旦它必须为坐在诊室里的一对夫妇给出一个清晰明确的是或否的答案。

JORDAN So what did they actually do with these 92 couples? 那么他们对这 92 对夫妇具体做了什么？

ALEX This was a retrospective review at the University Hospital of Padua, covering January 2016 through December 2022 — 92 couples, 184 individuals, referred because they were flagged as at elevated risk of having a child with CF or a CFTR-RD. 这是在帕多瓦大学医院进行的一项回顾性研究，覆盖 2016 年 1 月到 2022 年 12 月——92 对夫妇，共 184 人，因被标记为生育患有 CF 或 CFTR-RD 的孩子的风险升高而被转诊而来。 Every CFTR variant these couples carried — referenced against the NM_000492.4 transcript — was reassessed using ACMG/AMP criteria and cross-checked against three separate resources at once: the CFTR2 database, the CFTR-France database, and ClinVar, plus a manual literature search. 这些夫妇携带的每一个 CFTR 变异——以 NM_000492.4 转录本为参照——都用 ACMG/AMP 标准重新评估，并同时与三个独立资源交叉核对：CFTR2 数据库、CFTR-France 数据库和 ClinVar，外加一次人工文献检索。 Where a partner's carrier status for a second, trans-acting pathogenic variant couldn't be confirmed, the clinic offered deeper testing — whole-gene next-generation sequencing plus MLPA, a technique that detects larger deletions or duplications that ordinary sequencing can miss. 如果无法确认伴侣的第二个、反式作用致病变异的携带者状态，诊所会提供更深入的检测——全基因二代测序 (next-generation sequencing) 加上 MLPA，一种能检测出普通测序会漏检的较大缺失或重复的技术。 A couple was judged eligible for PGT-M when the combination of variants across both partners was predicted to cause CF or a CFTR-RD broader than CBAVD alone. 当一对夫妇双方的变异组合被预测会导致 CF 或不局限于 CBAVD 的更广泛 CFTR-RD 时，这对夫妇就被判定为符合 PGT-M 资格。

JORDAN Before we get to the numbers — why bother checking three different variant databases instead of picking the best one and trusting it? 在我们看数字之前——为什么要检查三个不同的变异数据库，而不是挑一个最好的、信任它就好？

ALEX Because, as this paper demonstrates in practice, the databases don't always agree, and picking just one would silently hide that disagreement from the couple sitting across the table. 因为正如这篇论文在实践中所展示的那样，这些数据库并不总是意见一致，而只挑一个来用，会悄悄把这种分歧从坐在对面的夫妇那里隐藏起来。 Using three sources side by side isn't redundancy for its own sake — it's the only way to notice that a "yes" from one database and a "no" from another both exist for the exact same variant. 同时使用三个来源并不是为了冗余而冗余——这是唯一能让人注意到“同一个变异，一个数据库说是，另一个数据库说否”这种情况确实存在的方法。 That's the harmonization problem in miniature, and this clinic is living inside it every single day. 这正是协调统一问题的一个缩影，而这家诊所每一天都生活在这个问题之中。

JORDAN Give me the headline numbers. 给我讲讲头条数字。

ALEX Across the 184 individuals they found 51 distinct CFTR variants — 37 exonic, 14 intronic including poly-T tract variants — and about two-thirds, 34 of 51, were classified as CF-causing, of varying clinical consequence, or CFTR-RD causing. 在这 184 人中，他们找到了 51 个不同的 CFTR 变异——37 个外显子变异，14 个内含子变异（含 poly-T 区段变异）——其中大约三分之二，51 个里的 34 个，被分类为致 CF、临床后果可变 (varying clinical consequence, VCC)，或致 CFTR-RD。 F508del was by far the most common, at 31.25 percent of variant alleles, followed by a handful of well-known nonsense and frameshift variants — R1162X, G542X, a frameshift often called 2184insA — each around 5 percent, then N1303K and D1152H further down the list. F508del 是迄今为止最常见的变异，占变异等位基因的 31.25%，其后是几个知名的无义和移码变异——R1162X、G542X，一个常被称为 2184insA 的移码变异——各占约 5%，再往下是 N1303K 和 D1152H。 Fifty of the 92 couples were judged eligible for PGT-M; 23 of those 50 — 46 percent — had F508del in at least one partner, and both partners carried it in eight cases. 92 对夫妇中有 50 对被判定为符合 PGT-M 资格；这 50 对中的 23 对——占 46%——至少一方携带 F508del，而有 8 对夫妇双方都携带它。 Eleven couples landed in real uncertainty — a pathogenic variant paired with a variant of uncertain significance, or two VUS together — and were counseled case by case rather than given a flat yes or no. 十一对夫妇陷入了真正的不确定境地——一个致病变异搭配一个意义未明的变异 (VUS)，或者两个 VUS 配对——他们得到的是逐案讨论的咨询，而不是简单的是或否。 The remaining 31 didn't meet the criteria for the procedure. 剩下的 31 对不符合该项检测的适用标准。 On the treatment side, cross-referencing CFTR2 and CFTR-France showed that 39 of 61 couples with high or undetermined risk — 64 percent — had a variant combination whose affected offspring could plausibly benefit from a CFTR modulator, which the clinic folds directly into the counseling conversation. 在治疗方面，交叉参考 CFTR2 和 CFTR-France 显示，61 对高风险或风险未定的夫妇中有 39 对——64%——其变异组合使得受累后代很可能从某种 CFTR 调节剂中获益，诊所会把这一点直接纳入咨询对话。 And the direct discordance example: c.3485G>T, protein change R1162L, is classified as CFTR-RD causing by CFTR-France but as non-CF-causing by CFTR2 — the exact same DNA change, two different clinical verdicts depending on which database a lab happened to check. 而直接的分歧例子是：c.3485G>T，蛋白质改变 R1162L，被 CFTR-France 分类为致 CFTR-RD，却被 CFTR2 分类为非致 CF——完全相同的 DNA 改变，取决于实验室恰好查的是哪个数据库，得到两种不同的临床判决。

JORDAN That's a striking finding to hang a whole episode on — the same variant, opposite calls. 这真是一个足以撑起整集节目的惊人发现——同一个变异，截然相反的判决。 What happened to the couples who followed through? 那些真正走完流程的夫妇后来怎么样了？

ALEX Sixteen couples completed at least one ART cycle with PGT-M. 十六对夫妇完成了至少一个带 PGT-M 的辅助生殖技术 (ART) 周期。 Eight had wildtype or healthy-carrier embryos identified and went on to unaffected births; the other eight didn't succeed — two biochemical pregnancies, three cycles with no blastocyst suitable for transfer, and three failed implantations after transfer. 八对成功鉴定出野生型或健康携带者胚胎，并诞下了未受累的孩子；另外八对没有成功——两例生化妊娠、三个周期没有适合移植的囊胚，以及移植后三例植入失败。 The paper is candid that even once the genetics are sorted out, PGT-M's success rate is still bounded by ordinary IVF biology. 这篇论文坦诚地指出，即便遗传学问题已经理清，PGT-M 的成功率仍然受制于普通试管婴儿 (IVF) 生物学本身的限制。

JORDAN Where does this land for us? 这对我们落在哪里？

ALEX This lands on Karen and CFTR variant interpretation and harmonization, and the R1162L discordance is the cleanest possible anchor. 这落在 Karen 合作项目和 CFTR 变异解读与协调统一上，而 R1162L 的分歧正是最干净利落的落脚点。 The buildable artifact is a CFTR database-discordance flagger. 可构建的产物是一个 CFTR 数据库分歧标记工具 (database-discordance flagger)。 The MVP takes a list of CFTR variants, in HGVS or legacy notation, normalizes each one, and looks up its classification in CFTR2, CFTR-France, and ClinVar side by side — the same three-source approach this clinic used by hand for every single couple. MVP 的输入是一份 CFTR 变异列表，可以是 HGVS 或传统命名法，先将每一个标准化，再并排查询它在 CFTR2、CFTR-France 和 ClinVar 中的分类——正是这家诊所对每一对夫妇手动做的这套三源核对方法。 The output is a table that flags any variant where the three sources disagree, showing all three labels together rather than collapsing them into one number, because the disagreement itself, as we just saw with R1162L, is exactly the information a counselor needs to see. 输出是一张表，标记出三个来源意见不一致的任何变异，把三个标签并列展示，而不是把它们压缩成一个数字，因为正如我们刚看到的 R1162L 一样，分歧本身正是咨询师需要看到的信息。 Difficulty medium: CFTR2 and ClinVar both have queryable structured data, but CFTR-France doesn't offer a clean API, so scraping or maintaining a periodically refreshed local snapshot is the actual engineering cost. 难度中等：CFTR2 和 ClinVar 都有可查询的结构化数据，但 CFTR-France 没有提供干净的 API，所以抓取或维护一份定期刷新的本地快照才是真正的工程成本。

JORDAN Does the tool get to decide which database is right when they disagree? 当它们意见不一致时，这个工具能不能自己决定哪个数据库是对的？
