Ep3 (Track C — registry QC, 1 of 2): The outlier that hides because every number looks fine

Arribas-Gil and Romo (2014, Biostatistics) introduce the outliergram, a visualization-plus-detection method for finding "shape outliers" in samples of curves — trajectories that stay well within the normal range at every single time point but whose overall pattern of movement relative to the rest of the sample doesn't resemble anything else in the cohort. The method plots each curve's Modified Band Depth (MBD, how centrally it sits within bands formed by other curve pairs) against its Modified Epigraph Index (MEI, its rank-based vertical position), exploiting a theoretical near-parabolic relationship between the two that holds for curve samples with limited crossing; curves falling conspicuously below that parabola are flagged as shape outliers. It builds on and complements the earlier functional boxplot, which targets magnitude outliers instead. Only the abstract is retrievable for this paper (no PMCID, OA: N), so no specific flag-rate or sensitivity figures are available; it is flagged abstract-only throughout. Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation. Buildable artifact: a Growth-Trajectory Shape-Outlier Screen.

Plain-English glossary

  • Magnitude outlier vs. shape outlier — a magnitude outlier is a curve that sits mostly outside the range of the rest of the sample at most time points (easy to spot); a shape outlier stays within the normal range everywhere but has an overall trajectory pattern unlike anything else in the sample (hard to spot).（中文：幅度异常值是在大多数时间点上整体落在样本范围之外的曲线，容易发现；形状异常值在各处数值都正常，但整体轨迹模式与样本中其他曲线都不相似，难以发现。）
  • Functional data analysis — the branch of statistics treating entire curves (not single numbers) as the unit of observation; a cohort's growth trajectories are a textbook example.（中文：功能数据分析，统计学的一个分支，把整条曲线（而非单个数值）作为观测单位；一个队列的生长轨迹就是典型例子。）
  • Modified Band Depth (MBD) — a depth measure scoring how often a curve lies within the "band" traced by pairs of other curves in the sample; a curve deep inside the pack scores high.（中文：修正带深度，衡量一条曲线落在样本中其他曲线两两形成的"带状区域"内的频率；处于曲线群中心的曲线得分较高。）
  • Modified Epigraph Index (MEI) — a rank-based depth measure of how often a curve lies entirely above other curves in the sample, capturing vertical position.（中文：修正上方图指数，一种基于排名的深度指标，衡量一条曲线整体位于其他曲线上方的频率，用以刻画纵向位置。）
  • Functional boxplot — an earlier generalization of the box-and-whisker plot to curve samples, using band depth to define a central region; targets magnitude outliers, complementary to the outliergram's shape-outlier focus.（中文：功能箱线图，是箱线图在曲线样本上的早期推广，利用带深度定义中心区域；主要针对幅度异常值，与outliergram针对形状异常值的功能互补。）

Full citation

Arribas-Gil A, Romo J. "Shape outlier detection and visualization for functional data: the outliergram." Biostatistics (Oxford, England). 2014. PMID: 24622037 | DOI: 10.1093/biostatistics/kxu006

Methods / tools to lift

  • Modified Band Depth (MBD) + Modified Epigraph Index (MEI) — two complementary depth measures for curve samples, one sensitive to central position, one to vertical rank.
  • The outliergram — a scatter-plot visualization of MBD against MEI with a theoretical reference curve, flagging curves that fall conspicuously below it as shape outliers.
  • Functional boxplot (prior art) — band-depth-based generalization of the boxplot to curve samples, for magnitude outliers; worth pairing with the outliergram rather than choosing one over the other.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation, primary — a shape-outlier screen catches trajectories invisible to a magnitude-based spline/mixed-effects model by construction, since it never strays outside the normal range at any single point. Buildable artifact: a Growth-Trajectory Shape-Outlier Screen. MVP: compute MBD and MEI across a cohort's height-for-age or weight-for-age z-score trajectories, render the outliergram, and route any curve falling conspicuously below the expected MBD-MEI relationship into the existing candidate queue as a shape-flagged case, labeled distinctly from magnitude-flagged ones so adjudication can treat them differently. Difficulty: medium.

Error types covered / not covered: Covers trajectories with an anomalous overall shape/pattern relative to peers (e.g., unusual zig-zagging or rank-crossing behavior) that stays within normal magnitude range throughout; does not cover gross single-value implausibility, unit conversion errors, digit transposition, decimal shifts, or carry-forward values — those remain the job of magnitude-based checks, which this method is explicitly meant to complement rather than replace.

What to verify first

  • How much real pediatric growth trajectories cross each other within the normal range, since the MBD-MEI parabolic relationship assumes limited crossing, and this paper's own validation (simulation + microarray data) doesn't test growth-curve-like crossing patterns.
  • How the outliergram's "conspicuously below the parabola" threshold should be calibrated for anthropometric data specifically, rather than borrowed from the paper's own simulation or microarray settings.
  • Whether shape-flagged cases, once routed to adjudication, actually separate cleanly into data-entry error versus genuine unusual-but-real growth patterns — this paper offers no evidence either way for anthropometric data.
