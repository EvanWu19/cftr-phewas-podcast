Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): A comparative survey of functional evidence use in hearing and vision loss genetics

Inan and colleagues (2026, Communications Medicine) surveyed 82 clinical and research geneticists working in hearing loss (HL) and vision loss (VL) — genetically heterogeneous, heavily gene-overlapping fields with a severe variant-of-uncertain-significance (VUS) burden (44% of HL variants, 46% of VL variants, 89% of deafness-gene variants in the Deafness Variation Database) — about how they access, trust, and apply functional evidence for variant classification. The sample, recruited almost entirely through ClinGen's own expert-panel networks, is almost certainly a ceiling rather than an average: 73% work at academic medical centers and 41% are ClinGen-affiliated. Even so, the findings are striking. Confidence in functional evidence varies sharply by assay type (highest for transcript-based and patient-derived cell assays, lowest for simple non-mammalian animal models) and geography (MaveDB trust is 62% in the US but 66% of European and 53% of Asian respondents are simply unaware it exists). A structural asymmetry between the hearing-loss and ocular expert panels — one reserving top-tier evidence almost exclusively for knock-in mouse models, the other accepting a much broader range of in vitro assays — likely explains part of the confidence gap between domains. The buildable artifact: a CFTR Functional-Evidence Confidence Ledger that audits CFTR's own VUS against the same assay-type confidence hierarchy this survey used, to find VUS whose only functional support sits in the tier curators trust least.

Plain-English glossary

ACMG/AMP guidelines — The joint framework from the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology that assigns weighted evidence codes to a variant and sums them to reach a pathogenic/benign classification.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会和分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类框架，为变异打上加权证据代码并求和得出分类结论。）

ClinGen / VCEP / GCEP — ClinGen (Clinical Genome Resource) is the consortium that refines ACMG/AMP rules for specific genes and diseases through Variant Curation Expert Panels (VCEPs) and Gene Curation Expert Panels (GCEPs), each setting its own calibration for how strong a piece of evidence must be.（中文：ClinGen（临床基因组资源联盟）通过变异审核专家组（VCEP）和基因审核专家组（GCEP），为特定基因和疾病细化ACMG/AMP规则，每个专家组自行校准证据强度标准。）

CanVIG-UK — The Cancer Variant Interpretation Group UK, which has proposed frameworks for interpreting variants showing reduced penetrance or intermediate/conflicting functional-assay phenotypes.（中文：英国癌症变异解读小组，提出了用于解读外显率降低或功能实验表型中间/矛盾变异的框架。）

Deep mutational scan — A high-throughput experiment that measures the functional effect of nearly every possible amino-acid substitution in a protein at once.（中文：一种高通量实验，一次性测量蛋白质几乎所有可能氨基酸替换的功能影响。）

MAVE (multiplexed assay of variant effects) — A family of high-throughput functional assays that test thousands of variants in a gene simultaneously in a relevant cellular model, producing a functional score for nearly every possible variant at once.（中文：一类高通量功能实验，能在一个相关细胞模型中同时检测一个基因里成千上万个变异，一次性为几乎所有可能的变异打出功能分数。）

MPRA (massively parallel reporter assay) — A high-throughput technique for testing the regulatory effect of many DNA sequence variants at once, used to capture evidence from gene-regulatory variation.（中文：一种大规模并行报告基因实验，用于同时检测大量DNA序列变异的调控效应，可捕捉基因调控变异相关的证据。）

PS3 / BS3 — ACMG/AMP evidence codes for functional data: PS3 ("pathogenic, strong") supports a damaging effect; BS3 ("benign, strong") supports a neutral effect, each requiring a well-validated assay calibrated by the relevant expert panel.（中文：ACMG/AMP中关于功能证据的代码：PS3（强致病证据）支持变异有损害性，BS3（强良性证据）支持变异为中性，均需经相关专家组校准的、充分验证的实验支持。）

VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant for which the available evidence does not clearly support either a pathogenic or benign classification.（中文：意义不明变异，指现有证据不足以明确判定为致病或良性的变异。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • REDCap-hosted structured survey design (62 items spanning demographics, current/anticipated activity, and tiered confidence ratings) as a template for internally auditing curator confidence and practice.
  • Confidence-tiering of functional-evidence types (biochemical, transcript-based, patient-derived cell, gene-edited, animal model) as a reusable rubric for scoring any gene's evidence base, not just HL/VL.
  • VUS subclassification (tiering within the 10–90% posterior-probability range) as a lower-effort alternative to waiting on new functional data.

ALEX Today's paper is "A comparative survey of functional evidence use in hearing and vision loss genetics," by Inan and colleagues, published in Communications Medicine in 2026. 今天这篇论文叫《听力与视力丧失遗传学中功能证据使用情况的比较调查》，作者是Inan及其同事，发表于《Communications Medicine》，2026年。We're in Track A today — the Karen Raraigh lane, variant interpretation and harmonization. 今天是A赛道——也就是Karen Raraigh合作方向，变异解读与标准化（harmonization）。It's not a methods paper so much as a survey of 82 genetics experts, and it's useful precisely because it tells us how confident real curators are in the functional evidence CFTR curation also leans on. 严格说这不是一篇方法学论文，而是对82位遗传学专家的问卷调查，但它的价值恰恰在于：它告诉我们，真正做变异审核（curation）的专家们，对CFTR审核同样依赖的那类功能证据，到底有多大信心。

JORDAN Before we get into results, unpack the vocabulary — what exactly is a VUS, and what's PS3/BS3? 在讲结果之前，先讲讲术语——VUS到底是什么，PS3/BS3又是什么？

ALEX VUS stands for variant of uncertain significance — a DNA change found in a disease gene where the available evidence doesn't clearly point to pathogenic or benign. VUS，意义不明变异（variant of uncertain significance），指的是在致病基因里发现的一个DNA变化，但现有证据还不足以明确判断它是致病还是良性。Classification runs on the ACMG/AMP framework, a joint guideline from the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology that assigns evidence codes to a variant and sums them into a verdict. 变异分类遵循ACMG/AMP框架（美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会与分子病理学会联合制定的指南），它为每个变异打上证据代码，再把这些代码加总得出结论。PS3 and BS3 are two of those codes, specifically for functional evidence — PS3 is "pathogenic, strong," meaning a well-validated functional assay shows the variant is damaging; BS3 is the mirror image, "benign, strong," meaning the assay shows it's neutral. PS3和BS3是其中两个代码，专门对应功能证据——PS3是"强致病证据"，意味着一个经过充分验证的功能实验显示该变异有损害性；BS3则相反，是"强良性证据"，意味着实验显示它是中性的。ClinGen, the Clinical Genome Resource, refines these ACMG/AMP rules through disease-specific Variant Curation Expert Panels and Gene Curation Expert Panels — VCEPs and GCEPs — each of which writes its own calibration for exactly how strong a given assay has to be before it counts as PS3 or BS3 for that gene. ClinGen（临床基因组资源联盟）通过疾病专属的变异审核专家组（VCEP）和基因审核专家组（GCEP）来细化ACMG/AMP规则，每个专家组都会为自己的基因制定校准标准，规定某个实验要达到多强才能算作该基因的PS3或BS3。CFTR has its own VCEP doing exactly this.CFTR基因也有自己的VCEP在做同样的事情。

JORDAN And MAVEs — that's the big new thing this survey keeps coming back to? MAVE——这篇调查反复提到的新事物——指的是什么？

ALEX MAVE is multiplexed assays of variant effects — a family of high-throughput techniques that test thousands of variants in a gene simultaneously in a relevant cellular model, producing a functional score for nearly every possible change at once instead of one variant at a time. MAVE，变异效应多重检测（multiplexed assays of variant effects），是一类高通量技术，能在一个相关的细胞模型里同时检测一个基因里成千上万个变异，一次性为几乎所有可能的变化打出功能分数，而不是一次只测一个变异。The field calls the end product an "atlas of variant effects" — the idea being you pre-generate functional evidence for an entire gene before anyone even orders the clinical test. 这个领域把最终产出称为"变异效应图谱"（atlas of variant effects）——核心思路是在任何人送检临床检测之前，就为整个基因预先生成功能证据。MaveDB is the repository where these datasets get deposited and linked to clinical evidence. MaveDB是存放这些数据集、并将其与临床证据关联起来的数据库。

JORDAN So walk me through why this survey exists — what was unresolved before it? 那说说这篇调查存在的原因——在它之前，什么问题还没解决？

ALEX The VUS burden is enormous and growing. VUS的积压规模巨大且仍在增长。As of July 2025, more than 2 million ClinVar entries — 57% of the database — are VUS or have conflicting classifications across labs. 截至2025年7月，ClinVar数据库中超过200万条记录——占数据库的57%——是VUS或在不同实验室之间存在分类冲突。Hearing loss and vision loss are especially bad cases because they're genetically heterogeneous with heavy gene overlap: Genomics England's PanelApp lists 191 hearing-loss genes and 518 vision-loss genes, 41 of them shared across both panels. 听力丧失和视力丧失是特别糟糕的例子，因为它们的遗传异质性很强，且基因高度重叠：英国基因组学项目的PanelApp列出了191个听力丧失基因和518个视力丧失基因，其中41个在两份名单中都出现。Despite heavy submission volume to ClinVar, 44% of hearing-loss variants and 46% of vision-loss variants are still VUS or conflicting, and the Deafness Variation Database classifies 89% of variants across 224 deafness genes as VUS. 尽管提交给ClinVar的数据量很大，44%的听力丧失变异和46%的视力丧失变异仍是VUS或存在分类冲突，而耳聋变异数据库（Deafness Variation Database）更是将224个耳聋基因中89%的变异归为VUS。That uncertainty shows up directly in diagnostic yield — large hearing and ocular cohorts report positive diagnoses only 39 to 51 percent of the time. 这种不确定性直接体现在诊断阳性率上——大型听力和眼科队列的阳性诊断率只有39%到51%。

JORDAN And MAVEs are supposed to fix that — so why survey people about it instead of just counting how much MAVE data exists? MAVE理论上应该能解决这个问题——那为什么要做问卷调查，而不是直接统计现有的MAVE数据量？

ALEX Because generating the data isn't the bottleneck anymore — adoption is. 因为生成数据已经不是瓶颈了——采纳（adoption）才是。Recent functional studies on CNGA3, a vision-loss gene, and KCNQ4, a hearing-loss gene, already demonstrated that medium- and high-throughput assays can drive real reclassification of VUS. 已有针对视力丧失基因CNGA3和听力丧失基因KCNQ4的近期功能研究证明了，中通量和高通量实验确实可以推动VUS的真正重新分类。But there's an earlier, more general survey — Park and colleagues — that found genetics professionals across many disease areas report unclear familiarity, preparedness, and willingness to actually use this kind of data clinically. 但有一项更早、范围更广的调查——Park及其同事所做的——发现跨多个疾病领域的遗传学专业人员，对于在临床上真正使用这类数据,其熟悉程度、准备程度和意愿都不明确。This survey narrows that question to exactly the two domains, hearing and vision, that share the most gene overlap and the most VUS burden, to find domain-specific barriers rather than generic ones. 这篇论文把那个问题缩小到了基因重叠和VUS负担都最严重的两个领域——听力和视力——目的是找出领域特有的障碍，而不是泛泛的障碍。

JORDAN Give me the actual mechanics — who answered, and how was it run? 具体说说操作细节——是谁回答的，怎么做的？

ALEX It's a REDCap-hosted survey, 62 items across demographics, current and anticipated professional activities, and — the bulk of it — confidence and barriers around functional evidence. 这是一份通过REDCap平台发布的问卷，共62题，涵盖人口学信息、当前及预期的职业活动，以及占大头的部分——围绕功能证据的信心与障碍。It ran for six months and was distributed through ClinGen's own hearing-loss VCEP and ocular GCEP mailing lists, PanelApp reviewer networks, professional societies, and direct email to authors of relevant sequencing-cohort papers pulled from PubMed. 调查持续了六个月，通过ClinGen自己的听力丧失VCEP和眼科GCEP邮件列表、PanelApp审核员网络、专业学会，以及直接给PubMed上相关测序队列论文作者发邮件来分发。198 people started it; 82 completed responses were analyzed, representing 30 countries, with the US at 16% and Germany at 13% as the largest single-country shares. 198人开始填写；最终82份完整回答被纳入分析，覆盖30个国家，美国占16%、德国占13%，是占比最高的两个国家。73% worked in academic medical centers and 41% were ClinGen-affiliated. 73%的受访者在学术医疗中心工作，41%与ClinGen有关联。

ALEX Here's where I'd push on the design a bit. 这里我想对设计方式提出一点质疑。A 41%-completion rate off 198 starters, recruited almost entirely through ClinGen's own VCEP and GCEP mailing lists, means this sample is drawn from the most engaged, most ClinGen-aware slice of the field — not a representative cross-section of everyone doing HL/VL variant classification. 198名起始受访者中最终只有41%完成问卷，而招募渠道几乎全部是ClinGen自己的VCEP和GCEP邮件列表，这意味着这个样本来自整个领域里参与度最高、对ClinGen最了解的那一群人——而不是所有从事听力/视力变异分类工作者的代表性抽样。So every confidence number in this paper is probably a ceiling, not an average — the baseline confidence and awareness across the broader diagnostic-lab community is very plausibly lower than what's reported here. 所以这篇论文里报告的每一个信心数字，很可能都是一个上限，而不是平均值——在更广泛的诊断实验室群体中，基线的信心和认知水平很可能比这里报告的更低。That matters when you're trying to use this as a benchmark for anywhere outside the ClinGen network. 如果你想把这份数据当作ClinGen网络之外任何地方的参照基准，这一点就很重要了。

JORDAN So what did the engaged slice actually say? 那么，这群参与度最高的人到底说了什么？

ALEX Groups split into 48% hearing-loss specialists, 23% vision-loss specialists, and 29% working across both. 受访者分为三组：48%是听力丧失专家，23%是视力丧失专家，29%同时从事两个领域。About half curate over 100 variants a year, and 54% report conflicting functional evidence as a common problem. 大约一半受访者每年审核超过100个变异，54%的人表示遇到功能证据相互矛盾是常见问题。Confidence varied sharply by assay type: biochemical assays sat at 47 to 63% confidence, transcript-based assays like splicing assays and patient-derived cell models were the most trusted at 52 to 75%, gene-edited in vitro models landed at 46 to 69%, and complex in vivo systems like animal models ranged widely, 37 to 67%. 不同实验类型的信心水平差异明显：生化实验的信心为47%到63%，基于转录本的实验（如剪接实验，splice assay）和患者来源细胞模型最受信任，达52%到75%，基因编辑体外模型为46%到69%，而复杂的体内系统（如动物模型）波动很大，介于37%到67%之间。Less complex animal models — zebrafish, Drosophila — scored lowest, 11 to 54%. 较简单的动物模型——斑马鱼、果蝇——得分最低，只有11%到54%。And despite all the attention MAVEs get, overall trust in MaveDB sat at just 11 to 26%, with sharp geographic splits: 62% of US respondents were confident in it, while 66% of European and 53% of Asian respondents reported simply being unaware the resource existed. 尽管MAVE备受关注，但MaveDB的整体信任度只有11%到26%，而且地区差异明显：62%的美国受访者对它有信心，而66%的欧洲受访者和53%的亚洲受访者表示根本不知道这个资源的存在。

JORDAN That's a huge awareness gap for something marketed as the future of VUS resolution. 对于一个被宣传为VUS解决方案"未来"的资源，这样的认知差距实在太大了。What were the actual barriers people named? 受访者具体提到了哪些障碍？

ALEX The single most-cited barrier for hearing-loss specialists specifically was interpreting reduced penetrance or variable expressivity — 58% flagged it. 听力丧失专家最常提到的单一障碍是解读外显率降低（reduced penetrance）或表现度差异（variable expressivity）——58%的人提到了这一点。More generally, concerns about the quality and accuracy of functional evidence itself ran 50 to 68%, and difficulty locating and interpreting functional data in the literature or variant databases ran 17 to 53%. 更普遍的是，对功能证据本身质量和准确性的担忧占50%到68%，而在文献或变异数据库中查找和解读功能数据的困难占17%到53%。One structural detail I think matters a lot here: the two expert panels have set very different bars for what counts as strong evidence. 我认为有一个结构性细节非常重要：两个专家组对什么算作强证据，设定了非常不同的门槛。The hearing-loss Clinical Domain Working Group reserves PS3_Strong almost exclusively for knock-in mouse models — for GJB2, only electrical coupling and dye-transfer assays were judged to reach even moderate-level evidence. 听力丧失临床领域工作组（CDWG）几乎只把PS3_Strong这一最高等级留给基因敲入小鼠模型——对GJB2而言，只有电耦联和染料转移实验才被判定能达到中等级别的证据。The ocular group, by contrast, approved a much broader range of in vitro assays as strong-level evidence for glaucoma, Leber congenital amaurosis, and X-linked inherited retinal disorders. 相比之下，眼科组则批准了更宽泛的一类体外实验作为青光眼、Leber先天性黑内障和X连锁遗传性视网膜疾病的强证据。That asymmetry alone probably explains a good chunk of the confidence gap between the two domains — it's not that hearing-loss assays are worse, it's that the bar is set higher.这种不对称性，很可能就解释了两个领域之间信心差距的很大一部分——并不是听力丧失的实验本身更差，而是门槛被设得更高。

JORDAN Beyond fixing the evidence-tier mismatch, what did respondents actually say would help? 除了解决证据等级的不对称问题，受访者实际说了什么会有帮助？

ALEX Workshops, additional VCEP specifications, and disease-specific guidelines were rated useful by 54 to 83% of respondents, and improved access to primary functional data plus standardized assessments through ClinVar was rated highly beneficial — 72 to 96%. 工作坊、新增的VCEP规范以及疾病专属指南，被54%到83%的受访者评为有用，而通过ClinVar获得更完善的原始功能数据访问权限和标准化评估，则被评为高度有益——72%到96%。The thing I find most telling is that 58 to 86% of respondents said they'd want the ability to directly request de novo generation of functional data for a specific variant, or request a gene-wide deep mutational scan — meaning curators don't just want better access to existing data, they want a mechanism to commission new data on demand. 我觉得最能说明问题的是，58%到86%的受访者表示，他们希望能直接请求为某个特定变异生成全新的功能数据，或者请求对整个基因做一次深度突变扫描（deep mutational scan）——也就是说，审核员想要的不只是更好地访问已有数据，而是一种能按需"下单"生成新数据的机制。On the reduced-penetrance problem specifically, groups like the Cancer Variant Interpretation Group UK, CanVIG-UK, have proposed frameworks for interpreting variants that show intermediate or conflicting phenotypes in functional assays, and the paper also points to massively parallel reporter assays, MPRAs, a high-throughput technique for testing regulatory variants, as a way to pull in evidence from gene-regulatory variation that classical PS3/BS3 frameworks weren't built to capture. 针对外显率降低（reduced penetrance）这个具体问题，像英国癌症变异解读小组（CanVIG-UK）这样的团队，已经提出了用于解读那些在功能实验中表现出中间或矛盾表型的变异的框架，论文还提到了大规模并行报告基因实验（massively parallel reporter assay，MPRA）——一种用于检测调控区变异的高通量技术——作为一种纳入基因调控变异证据的方式，而传统的PS3/BS3框架本来就不是为捕捉这类证据而设计的。

JORDAN Did anything actually move the needle on VUS resolution, concretely? 有没有什么东西确实实质性地推动了VUS的解决？
