Ep3 — Track C — Registry Data Quality & Anthropometric Outlier Detection (1 of 2)

Blurb

Mramba et al. (2024, European Journal of Clinical Nutrition) apply a simple interquartile-range (IQR) rule, computed separately at each follow-up visit rather than pooled across a person's whole trajectory, to flag potential outliers in a continuous time-varying dietary covariate (vitamin B12 intake) in the TEDDY longitudinal cohort. They then test whether the flagged points actually matter by refitting an extended time-dependent Cox model with a robust sandwich variance estimator and examining partial residual diagnostics. The abstract reports (qualitatively, without specific numbers) that extreme vitamin B12 observations belonging to children who went on to develop Islet Autoimmunity had a stronger influence on the model than extreme observations in children who didn't, and that the flagged outliers changed the direction of hazard ratios, standard errors, or the apparent strength of association if left unadjudicated. This is a candidate-generator paper for the CFFPR outlier pipeline: the transferable pattern is visit-level (not person-level) flagging followed by an explicit downstream influence check, rather than treating "extreme" as automatically synonymous with "matters." Abstract-only — the Europe PMC full text was not retrievable despite a PMCID existing.

Plain-English glossary

  • Islet Autoimmunity (IA) — the appearance of autoantibodies against pancreatic beta cells, typically detectable years before a clinical type 1 diabetes diagnosis; the outcome event in this study.（中文：针对胰腺β细胞产生的自身抗体，通常在1型糖尿病临床确诊前数年即可检测到；本研究中的结局事件。）
  • Partial residual diagnostic plot — a standard Cox-model diagnostic that isolates how much a single observation's residual is pulling on the model fit for a specific covariate, used to distinguish merely-extreme points from actually-influential ones.（中文：一种标准的Cox模型诊断方法，用于分离出单个观测值的残差对某一特定协变量拟合结果的拉动程度，用来区分"仅仅数值极端"与"真正具有影响力"的数据点。）
  • TEDDY (The Environmental Determinants of Diabetes in the Young) — a large international longitudinal cohort following children at elevated genetic risk for type 1 diabetes from birth, with repeated clinic visits collecting diet, growth, and lab data.（中文：一项大型国际纵向队列研究，从出生起追踪1型糖尿病遗传风险较高的儿童，通过反复门诊随访收集饮食、生长和化验数据。）
  • Time-dependent (time-varying) covariate — a predictor whose value is updated at each time point in a survival model, rather than fixed at a single baseline measurement.（中文：在生存模型中数值随每个时间点更新，而不是固定在单一基线测量值的预测变量。）
  • Visit-level (per-visit) outlier detection — computing an outlier threshold separately at each scheduled measurement occasion, rather than pooling all of a person's or the whole population's measurements into one threshold.（中文：在每一次预定测量场合分别计算离群值阈值，而不是把某个人或整个人群的所有测量值汇总后计算一个统一阈值。）

Full citation

Mramba LK, Liu X, Lynch KF, et al. Detecting potential outliers in longitudinal data with time-dependent covariates. European Journal of Clinical Nutrition. 2024. PMID: 38172348 | PMC11003829 | DOI: 10.1038/s41430-023-01393-6

Methods/tools to lift

  • Per-visit IQR flagging — computing outlier thresholds separately at each follow-up visit rather than pooling across a trajectory or population, to account for distribution shifts by age/study phase.
  • Extended time-dependent Cox model with robust sandwich variance estimator — the downstream model used to test whether flagged points actually change estimated hazard ratios and standard errors.
  • Partial residual diagnostics — used to separate "extreme" from "influential" flagged points.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Liz / CFFPR data quality (method only). Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generator. Buildable artifact: visit-level-iqr-vs-model-influence — input a synthetic or public longitudinal dataset with a time-varying continuous measure and a survival/regression outcome; compute per-visit IQR flags at a few multiplier thresholds, refit the downstream model with/without flagged points, and report the shift in estimated effect and standard error. Difficulty: S (a threshold loop plus two model fits).

What to verify first

  • The actual IQR multiplier and flag rate used — not stated in the abstract, needed to judge how conservative or aggressive the screen is.
  • Whether the "outliers cluster in cases" finding is specific to vitamin-B12/Islet-Autoimmunity biology or a more general statistical phenomenon that would also be expected for anthropometric outliers.
  • TEDDY's actual per-visit sample sizes, since sparse visit schedules could make per-visit IQR quartile estimates unstable.
  • This paper says nothing about how CFFPR's own height/weight visit data is currently screened or flagged — separate, unverified question.

Error types covered / not covered

  • Covered: generic statistical extremity in a continuous time-varying measurement, agnostic to cause — the method flags any visit-level value far from that visit's IQR, whether the underlying cause is a true biological extreme, a measurement error, or a data-entry error.
  • Not covered: the method does not distinguish error types — no explicit handling of unit-conversion errors, digit transposition, decimal-place shifts, carry-forward/stale-value errors, or discrimination between a data-entry error and a genuine extreme clinical event; that adjudication step is explicitly left as "further investigation," not performed by the algorithm itself.
