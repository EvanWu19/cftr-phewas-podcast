Ep4 — Track C: which BMI-change metric has a stable noise floor

Freedman DS, Goodwin Davies AJ, Phan TT, Cole FS, Pajor N, Rao S, Eneli I, Kompaniyets L, Lange SJ, Christakis DA, Forrest CB. (2022). Measuring BMI change among children and adolescents. Pediatric Obesity.

Full text retrieved (PMC11135243). Using PEDSnet (a multi-institutional pediatric EHR network standardized to an OMOP-based common data model, 7 sites, 5.3M subjects), the authors compared six BMI metrics — raw BMI, standard BMIz, extended BMIz (a CDC recalibration for severe obesity), percent-of-median (%50th), log-scale percent-of-median (log %50th), and percent-of-95th-percentile (%95th) — across 285,643 children with three BMI measurements over 10–14 months. Data were first cleaned using Daymont's growthcleanr algorithm (33.0M→28M weights, 20.3M→18M heights excluded as likely errors). BMIz's within-child variability compresses sharply at high BMI (SD 0.27→0.06 across five BMI categories, a >4-fold spread) because of how the LMS method's S parameter behaves at the distribution's tail; log %50th showed the most stable variability (only ~30% range) and the weakest correlation (r=0.13) between a child's BMI level and their own measurement noise. However, changes in log %50th, %50th, and %95th were nearly interchangeable in practice (r≥0.97). Same broader research program as ep3's WHO/CDC chart-transition paper (Freedman is a co-author there too), but this paper's own contribution is the metric-choice finding, not a new error-detection method — growthcleanr is cited as external upstream preprocessing.

Plain-English glossary

  • PEDSnet / OMOP common data model — a multi-institutional pediatric EHR research network standardizing data from different hospital systems to a shared structure (an expansion of the OMOP/OHDSI common data model), so the same field means the same thing regardless of source institution.（中文：一个多机构儿科电子健康记录研究网络，将不同医院系统的数据标准化为统一结构（OMOP/OHDSI 通用数据模型的扩展版本），使同一字段无论来自哪家机构含义都一致。）
  • Kahn-framework-style data-quality assessment — a formal, automated-check-based workflow (here: >1000 checks) assessing completeness, plausibility, and conformance of clinical data before research use.（中文：一种正式的、基于自动化检查的工作流程（此处为一千多项检查），在数据用于研究之前评估临床数据的完整性、合理性和一致性。）
  • BMIz compression — the flattening of the BMI-to-z-score relationship at high BMI values, caused by the LMS method's S (coefficient-of-variation) parameter behavior at the tail of the reference distribution, so a fixed real BMI change produces a much smaller z-score change in children with already-high BMI.（中文：体质指数与 z 分数关系在高体质指数数值处趋于平缓的现象，由 LMS 方法中 S（变异系数）参数在参考分布尾部的行为所致，导致体质指数原本较高的儿童，同样的真实体质指数变化只会产生小得多的 z 分数变化。）
  • Extended BMIz — a CDC-published recalibration of BMIz for children with severe obesity, modeling the distribution tail as a half-normal shape using more recent national survey data instead of the standard LMS curve.（中文：CDC 发布的针对重度肥胖儿童的体质指数 z 分数重新校准方法，使用更新的全国调查数据将分布尾部建模为半正态形状，而非使用标准 LMS 曲线。）
  • Percent-of-median / log percent-of-median (%50th, log %50th) — BMI expressed as a percentage of the age/sex-specific median (on an arithmetic or natural-log scale); the log scale makes equal-magnitude percentage increases and decreases symmetric.（中文：将体质指数表示为特定年龄/性别中位数的百分比（算术标度或自然对数标度）；对数标度能让相同幅度的百分比增加和减少呈现对称性。）
  • Within-child standard deviation — the variability of a single child's own repeated measurements over time, as distinct from the more commonly used between-child SD across a population.（中文：单个儿童自身多次重复测量随时间的变异程度，区别于更常用的、跨人群的组间标准差。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • log %50th (log-scale percent-of-median BMI) — a BMI-change metric with within-child variability largely independent of BMI level, proposed as more appropriate than BMIz for measuring change.
  • Within-child SD vs. mean-value correlation check — the paper's diagnostic technique for detecting whether a metric's noise scales with its signal, generalizable to evaluating any longitudinal registry metric's suitability for change-based thresholds.
  • References growthcleanr ([[growthcleanr (Daymont within-child EWMA method)]] in the QC catalog) as external upstream error-exclusion preprocessing, applied at PEDSnet EHR scale (33.0M weights / 20.3M heights → 28M / 18M after exclusion).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation, primary — a fixed BMIz-change threshold is not equally sensitive across the BMI range (over-flags noise in low-BMI children, under-flags real/implausible changes in high-BMI children); a percent-of-median metric would flag more uniformly. Buildable artifact: script recomputing both BMIz-change and log %50th-change for the same longitudinal visit pairs, applying a fixed-SD threshold under each and checking whether flag rate stays constant across baseline-BMI strata. Difficulty: Small (pure computation on public CDC growth-chart parameters and already-computed percentiles/z-scores).

What to verify first

  • Whether CFFPR already uses any BMI/weight-change threshold rule, and if so, which metric it's currently expressed in.
  • Whether the modulator-era weight-gain population (rapid, genuine weight increases after CFTR modulator initiation) sits in a BMI range where BMIz-compression or log %50th's behavior diverges meaningfully from this general pediatric population.
  • Whether CFFPR's own preprocessing already removes the extreme-value noise that Daymont's algorithm removed here, before any metric-choice difference would matter.
  • Whether the near-interchangeability of log %50th, %50th, and %95th (r≥0.97) found here holds in a much smaller, disease-specific cohort with a differently shaped BMI distribution.

Error types covered / not covered

Covered: none directly — this is a metric-choice/measurement paper, not an error-flagging method; its relevance is to how a threshold on BMI/weight change behaves across the BMI distribution once error exclusion (via growthcleanr, applied upstream) has already happened. Not covered: unit conversion errors, digit transposition, decimal-shift errors, carry-forward errors, or discriminating a genuine clinical/modulator-driven weight change from a data-entry error at the individual-record level.

David Freedman 也是今天前一篇生长曲线图论文的共同作者，而这一篇提出了一个相关但不同的问题：一旦你信任了曲线图本身，在几种表达体质指数变化的方式中，哪一种才能真正给出一个跨不同体型儿童之间稳定、可比较的度量？ We got the full text this time, via PMC. 这次我们通过 PMC 拿到了全文。

JORDAN Before the metrics — what's PEDSnet, and why does it matter that this data comes from there specifically? 在讲具体指标之前——什么是 PEDSnet，这份数据来自那里这一点为什么重要？

ALEX PEDSnet is a multi-institutional clinical research network that pools electronic health record data from several of the largest US children's hospital systems into one standardized structure — this particular study drew on seven contributing sites and 5.3 million subjects. PEDSnet 是一个多机构临床研究网络，将来自美国多家最大儿童医院系统的电子健康记录数据汇集到一个标准化结构中——这项具体研究使用了七个参与站点、530 万名受试者的数据。 The standardization matters because raw EHR data from different hospital systems uses different internal codes and table structures, so PEDSnet maps everything to a common data model — an expanded version of the Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership, or OMOP, common data model — meaning a "weight measurement" or a "diagnosis" means the same structural thing regardless of which hospital it originally came from. 标准化之所以重要，是因为来自不同医院系统的原始电子健康记录使用不同的内部编码和表结构，所以 PEDSnet 把所有数据都映射到一个通用数据模型（common data model）——即观察性医疗结果合作组织（Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership，简称 OMOP）通用数据模型的一个扩展版本——这意味着无论数据最初来自哪家医院，"体重测量"或"诊断"在结构上都代表同一件事。 That's the same OMOP/OHDSI ecosystem that comes up across a lot of registry data-quality and common-data-model tooling, so it's worth knowing by name even outside this one paper. 这正是我们在很多登记处数据质量和通用数据模型工具讨论中反复出现的同一个 OMOP/OHDSI 生态系统，所以即使脱离这一篇论文，这个名字也值得记住。 PEDSnet also runs a formal data-quality assessment workflow with over a thousand automated checks for completeness, plausibility, and conformance before researchers touch the data — a concrete example of the Kahn-framework style vocabulary for describing what "data quality" actually means in an EHR pipeline. PEDSnet 还在研究人员接触数据之前，运行一套正式的数据质量评估流程，包含一千多项针对完整性、合理性和一致性的自动化检查——这是一个具体的例子，展示了 Kahn 框架（Kahn framework）风格的词汇是如何描述电子健康记录流水线中"数据质量"实际含义的。

JORDAN And they explicitly used the growthcleanr algorithm we already know from earlier episodes, right? 而且他们明确使用了我们之前节目里已经了解过的 growthcleanr 算法，对吧？

ALEX Right — before doing any of their own metric analysis, they ran Daymont's longitudinal outlier-detection method to identify and exclude likely-erroneous weight and height measurements, which took them from 33.0 million weights and 20.3 million heights down to 28 million and 18 million respectively. 没错——在做他们自己的指标分析之前，他们先运行了 Daymont 的纵向异常值检测方法，用来识别并排除疑似有误的体重和身高测量值，这使得数据从 3300 万条体重记录和 2030 万条身高记录，分别减少到了 2800 万和 1800 万条。 So this paper isn't proposing a new error-detection method at all — it's a downstream application that leans directly on growthcleanr as a preprocessing step, then asks a completely separate question about how to quantify change once the obvious errors are already gone. 所以这篇论文根本不是在提出一种新的错误检测方法——它是一个下游应用，直接依赖 growthcleanr 作为预处理步骤，然后提出了一个完全独立的问题：在明显的错误已经被剔除之后，该如何量化变化。

JORDAN So what's the actual problem with just using BMIz change, which is what everyone already uses? 那么，直接使用体质指数 z 分数变化——也就是大家已经在用的方法——到底有什么问题？

ALEX The relationship between raw BMI and BMIz is curvilinear, not a straight line, and it flattens out at high BMI values because of how the LMS method's S parameter — the coefficient of variation — behaves at the tails of the CDC reference population. 原始体质指数（BMI）和体质指数 z 分数之间的关系是曲线性的，而不是一条直线，并且在体质指数值较高时会趋于平缓，这是因为 LMS 方法中的 S 参数——变异系数——在 CDC 参考人群分布尾部的表现所致。 The paper gives a concrete worked example: a 10% decrease in BMI for a 12-year-old boy with an initial BMI of 18 produces roughly a 0.9 SD drop in BMIz, but the exact same 10% decrease for a boy with a BMI of 35 produces only about a 0.15 SD drop. 论文给出了一个具体的实例：对于一个初始体质指数为 18 的 12 岁男孩，体质指数下降 10% 会导致体质指数 z 分数下降大约 0.9 个标准差，但对于一个体质指数为 35 的男孩，同样是下降 10%，体质指数 z 分数却只会下降大约 0.15 个标准差。 That means the same real percentage change in body size translates into wildly different z-score movement depending purely on where a child starts, which is a problem for intervention studies — a genuinely successful weight-management program in a child with severe obesity could show up as a "clinically unimportant" BMIz change even after a substantial real BMI reduction — and it's just as much a problem for any automated system that uses a single BMIz-change threshold to flag implausible jumps, since that one threshold is effectively far stricter for low-BMI children and far looser for high-BMI children. 这意味着相同的真实体型变化百分比，会因为孩子起始体质指数的不同，转化为差异巨大的 z 分数变动，这对干预性研究是个问题——一个针对重度肥胖儿童的、真正成功的体重管理项目，即使体质指数实际大幅下降，也可能表现为一个"临床上不重要"的体质指数 z 分数变化——而这对任何使用单一体质指数 z 分数变化阈值来标记不合理跳变的自动化系统而言，同样是个问题，因为这样一个阈值对低体质指数儿童实际上严格得多，而对高体质指数儿童则宽松得多。

JORDAN What alternative metrics did they compare, and what's the concrete rule they used to decide which one was "best"? 他们比较了哪些替代指标，用什么具体规则来判定哪一个是"最优"的？

ALEX Six metrics: raw BMI itself, standard CDC BMIz, extended BMIz — a CDC-published recalibration for severe obesity that models the tail of the distribution as a half-normal shape using more recent measurements at least a month apart, and when a child had more than one eligible set of three, they kept the set whose span was closest to 365 days. 他们的队列是 285,643 名 2 岁到未满 18 岁的儿童，每个人恰好有三次相隔 10 到 14 个月的体质指数测量，相邻测量之间至少间隔一个月，如果一个孩子有多组符合条件的三次测量，就保留时间跨度最接近 365 天的那一组。 The decision rule for "optimal": compute each metric's within-child standard deviation across that child's own three measurements, then check whether that within-child variability correlates with the metric's own mean or initial value — the metric they were looking for is the one where noise doesn't scale with the signal, so a single change threshold behaves consistently across the whole BMI distribution instead of being effectively stricter or looser depending on where a child starts. 判定"最优"的规则是：计算每个指标在该儿童自己三次测量中的组内标准差（within-child standard deviation），然后检验这种组内变异性是否与该指标自身的均值或初始值相关——他们要找的是这样一个指标：噪声不随信号大小而放大，从而一个单一的变化阈值能够在整个体质指数分布范围内表现一致，而不是因孩子起始值不同而实际上或严或宽。 If I'm pushing on this design choice, it's that minimizing the correlation between a metric's value and its own variability is a statistical convenience property — homoscedasticity, roughly — and that's not automatically the same thing as choosing the metric that best tracks a real underlying biological change like fat-mass change; the paper does lean on other studies' body-fat correlations to support log %50th, but its own primary analysis is optimizing for stable variance, not directly for biological validity, and those two goals happen to point the same direction here without being logically identical. 如果要对这个设计选择提出质疑，那就是：让一个指标的数值与其自身变异性之间的相关性最小化，是一种统计上的便利性质——大致相当于方差齐性（homoscedasticity）——而这并不自动等同于选出一个最能追踪真实底层生物学变化（比如脂肪量变化）的指标；论文确实援引了其他研究中关于体脂相关性的结果来支持对数中位数百分比（log JORDAN What did they actually find? 他们的实际发现是什么？

ALEX The within-child standard deviation for BMIz ranged from 0.27 down to 0.06 across five BMI categories — more than a fourfold spread, the compression problem in numbers — while log %50th's range across the same categories was only about 30%, from 3.85 down to 3.05. 体质指数 z 分数的组内标准差在五个体质指数分组中从 0.27 一路降到 0.06——超过四倍的跨度，这就是压缩问题的具体数字体现——而对数中位数百分比在同样的分组中，跨度只有大约 30%，从 3.85 到 3.05。 Correlating each metric's mean value with its own within-child variability: raw BMI, percent-of-median, and percent-of-95th-percentile all showed moderate positive correlations of 0.31 to 0.47, meaning more variable at higher values; BMIz and extended BMIz showed inverse correlations of -0.32 to -0.37, meaning less variable at higher values — the compression effect again; and log %50th showed the weakest correlation by far, just 0.13, meaning its variability was the most independent of a child's actual BMI level. 将每个指标的均值与其自身的组内变异性做相关分析：原始体质指数、中位数百分比和第 95 百分位百分比都显示出 0.31 到 0.47 %50th, and %95th between the first and third visit were highly intercorrelated, r of 0.97 or higher, meaning in practice, using any of these three metrics for an intervention study or a change-detection rule would likely lead to very similar conclusions — log %50th's edge over its two siblings is more mathematical elegance, symmetric percentage changes on a log scale, than a dramatically different empirical answer. 但这里有一个重要的限定条件：对数中位数百分比、中位数百分比和第 95 百分位百分比这三者，从第一次到第三次就诊之间的变化高度相关，相关系数达到 0.97 或更高，这意味着在实践中，无论用这三个指标中的哪一个来做干预研究或变化检测规则，很可能都会得出非常相似的结论——对数中位数百分比相对于另外两个"兄弟"指标的优势，更多是数学上的优雅（对数标度上百分比变化的对称性），而不是经验结果上的显著差异。 The findings held up across sensitivity analyses using 2 or 4 measurements instead of 3, excluding children with chronic conditions, and stratifying by age, sex, and race-ethnicity, and mean BMIz in this EHR-derived cohort (0.45 SD) was reasonably close to the nationally representative NHANES survey (0.53 SD), which supports some generalizability despite PEDSnet not being a random population sample. 这些发现在使用 2 次或 4 次测量而非 3 次、排除患有慢性病的儿童，以及按年龄、性别、种族族裔分层的敏感性分析中都保持稳健，而且这个基于电子健康记录的队列中的平均体质指数 z 分数（0.45 个标准差）与具有全国代表性的 NHANES 调查（0.53 个标准差）相当接近，这在一定程度上支持了其可推广性，尽管 PEDSnet 并不是一个随机人群样本。 On the paper's own stated aim — identifying which metric is optimal for assessing short-term BMI change — yes, it delivered a clear answer, with the caveat about practical near-equivalence among the top three metrics stated by the authors themselves. 就论文自己设定的目标——找出哪个指标最适合评估短期体质指数变化——而言，它确实给出了一个明确的答案，但也伴随着作者自己指出的、前三名指标在实践中近乎等价这一限定条件。

JORDAN Does this change anything about how a change-based flagging rule should be built? 这是否会改变一个基于变化的标记规则应该如何构建？
