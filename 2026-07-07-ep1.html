How did this episode land for you?

Show notes — Track A — SpliceAI-visual (de Sainte Agathe et al., 2023)

Blurb. SpliceAI is a deep-learning splicing predictor, but its default output is a delta score — the change in raw splice-site probability between reference and variant. This paper's tool, SpliceAI-visual, exposes the underlying raw scores per nucleotide and plots them as tracks in a genome browser (igv.js), integrated into the free MobiDetails variant-interpretation site and shipped as a Google Colab notebook. The key insight: a variant can push an already-primed cryptic site over the edge while producing a tiny delta, so the widely used 0.2 delta cutoff silently filters out real deep-intronic and canonical-site pathogenic variants — the "delta-score pitfall." Worked examples (SCN1A, MFGE8, CASK, KMT2D, TTN, SETD5, plus 13 complex delins) show the raw view rescuing missed variants and, crucially, modulating the ACMG PVS1 loss-of-function criterion when a canonical-site variant is predicted to stay in-frame. Where it lands: the Karen CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration. Buildable artifact: a CFTR splice-variant raw-score annotation notebook that flags delta-pitfall and PVS1-modulation candidates across CFTR2/ClinVar intronic variants.

Plain-English glossary

  • Acceptor site — the 3' splice signal at the intron-exon junction where the spliceosome joins the next exon.（中文：受体位点，内含子-外显子交界处、剪接机器连接下一个外显子的 3' 信号。）
  • Canonical splice site — the highly conserved +/-1 or +/-2 intronic positions immediately flanking an exon; variants here usually disrupt splicing.（中文：经典剪接位点，紧邻外显子、高度保守的内含子 +/-1、+/-2 位置。）
  • Cryptic / pseudo-exon — a normally-silent splice site that a variant activates, wrongly including a chunk of intron as a "pseudo-exon."（中文：隐蔽位点/假外显子，被变异激活的沉默剪接位点，错误地把一段内含子当作外显子纳入。）
  • Deep intronic variant — a variant hundreds-to-thousands of bases into an intron, far from exons; routinely missed by proximity filters.（中文：深内含子变异，位于内含子深处、远离外显子，常被"靠近外显子"的过滤规则漏掉。）
  • Donor site — the 5' splice signal at the exon-intron junction.（中文：供体位点，外显子-内含子交界处的 5' 剪接信号。）
  • Exon / intron — exons are the retained coding stretches; introns are spliced out.（中文：外显子是保留的编码片段；内含子被剪除。）
  • Exon skipping — a splice-disrupting variant causes a whole exon to be dropped from the mRNA.（中文：外显子跳跃，剪接被破坏导致整个外显子从 mRNA 中丢失。）
  • gnomAD — large population allele-frequency database; high frequency argues against a severe-disease claim.（中文：gnomAD，大型人群等位基因频率数据库；高频提示不太可能是严重致病变异。）
  • HGVS — the standardized grammar for writing variant names (e.g., c.172+1G>A).（中文：HGVS，变异命名的标准化书写规范。）
  • MANE transcript — the jointly agreed NCBI-EMBL reference transcript, so everyone annotates the same isoform.（中文：MANE 转录本，NCBI 与 EMBL 共同商定的参考转录本，保证大家标注同一异构体。）
  • Minigene assay — a lab test that clones a region into a plasmid to read out how it splices.（中文：小基因实验，把目标区域克隆进质粒以读出其剪接方式的实验。）
  • NMD (nonsense-mediated decay) — the cell's pathway that degrades transcripts carrying premature stop codons.（中文：NMD，细胞降解带有提前终止密码子转录本的通路。）
  • PVS1 / ACMG — ACMG is the variant-classification framework; PVS1 is its "very strong" loss-of-function pathogenic criterion, applied by default to canonical splice-site variants.（中文：PVS1/ACMG，ACMG 是变异分类框架，PVS1 是其针对功能丧失的"非常强"致病判据，默认套用于经典剪接位点变异。）
  • Raw score vs delta score — SpliceAI's raw score is the absolute splice-site probability at a position; the delta is the reference-to-variant change. The delta can hide a variant that adds onto an already-high raw score.（中文：原始分数 vs delta 分数，原始分数是某位置作为剪接位点的绝对概率，delta 是参考到变异的变化量；delta 会掩盖叠加在已很高原始分数上的变异。）
  • RT-PCR / RNA-seq — wet-lab readouts of actual transcript splicing.（中文：RT-PCR/RNA 测序，检测实际转录本剪接的湿实验手段。）
  • Splicing — removal of introns and joining of exons to form mature mRNA.（中文：剪接，去除内含子、连接外显子形成成熟 mRNA 的过程。）
  • VUS — variant of uncertain significance.（中文：VUS，意义不明变异。）

Full citation

de Sainte Agathe JM, Filser M, Isidor B, et al. SpliceAI-visual: a free online tool to improve SpliceAI splicing variant interpretation. Human Genomics. 2023. PMID 36765386 · PMC PMC9912651 · DOI 10.1186/s40246-023-00451-1. Full text via PubMed (PMC9912651).

Methods / tools to lift

  • Raw-score (RS) view — run SpliceAI independently on reference and variant, expose absolute per-nucleotide donor/acceptor probabilities instead of only the delta.
  • Delta-score-pitfall rule — flag variants where delta < 0.2 but the reference raw score at the affected site is already high (a low-delta-on-high-baseline blind spot).
  • PVS1-modulation logic — when a canonical splice-site variant is predicted to yield an in-frame retention or a rescuing site, down-weight the PVS1 "very strong" criterion (per ClinGen SVI guidance).
  • Graphical bedGraph tracks in igv.js — reference vs variant splice landscape side by side; also handles complex delins that standard SpliceAI VCFs skip.
  • Pre-compute + real-time hybrid — 57,271 transcripts (19,120 MANE) pre-scored; variant allele computed on demand (~30 s) via Flask API on SLURM, CPU-only.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

  • Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation.
  • MVP (paste-ready): cftr-splice-rawscore-notebook — input a CFTR variant list (HGVS); run SpliceAI on reference + variant sequences for each; output a table flagging (a) delta-score-pitfall cases (delta<0.2 on high reference RS) and (b) PVS1-modulation candidates (canonical-site variants predicted in-frame). Difficulty: M (SpliceAI Colab / MobiDetails API exist; work is the CFTR batch harness + two flag rules).

What to verify first

  • SpliceAI trained on general/GTEx splicing — check how raw scores hold in airway/intestinal epithelium where CFTR splicing matters.
  • Calibrate a CFTR-specific raw-score operating point; do not assume the generic 0.2 delta cutoff transfers.
  • SpliceAI sensitivity is lower for deep-intronic variants — precisely where CFTR's hard cases (3849+10kb C>T etc.) live.
  • In-silico prediction is triage, not proof: RT-PCR / RNA-seq from the right tissue remains the arbiter.
  • External-methods layer on public variant sets only — no registry-internal genotype/data claims.

JORDAN Before we go further, can you ground me in splicing itself? 在继续之前，你能先帮我把剪接本身讲清楚吗？ I know the data-science side, but this is the biology I'm still building. 我懂数据科学那一面，但这块生物学我还在补。

ALEX Sure. 当然。 A gene is transcribed into a pre-mRNA that contains exons, the coding stretches, and introns, the intervening stretches that must be cut out. 一个基因被转录成前体 mRNA (pre-mRNA)，里面既有外显子 (exon，即编码片段)，也有内含子 (intron，即必须被剪掉的间隔片段)。 Splicing is the process that removes the introns and stitches the exons together. 剪接 (splicing) 就是去掉内含子、把外显子拼接起来的过程。 The machinery recognizes short signals at the intron boundaries: a donor site at the exon-intron junction on the 5' side, and an acceptor site at the intron-exon junction on the 3' side. 剪接机器识别内含子边界上的短信号：5' 端外显子-内含子交界处的供体位点 (donor site)，以及 3' 端内含子-外显子交界处的受体位点 (acceptor site)。 The two nucleotides immediately inside the intron are almost always highly conserved, and those are the "canonical" splice positions. 内含子内侧紧邻的两个核苷酸几乎总是高度保守的，这就是所谓的"经典" (canonical) 剪接位点。 A variant that breaks a donor or acceptor can cause exon skipping, where a whole exon is dropped, or activate a cryptic site that creates a pseudo-exon, where a chunk of intron gets wrongly included. 破坏供体或受体的变异，可能导致外显子跳跃 (exon skipping，即整个外显子被丢掉)，或者激活一个隐蔽位点 (cryptic site)，形成假外显子 (pseudo-exon，即一段内含子被错误地包含进来)。

JORDAN And a "deep intronic" variant is one far from any exon? 那"深内含子" (deep intronic) 变异指的是离外显子很远的变异？

ALEX Right, sitting hundreds or thousands of bases into the intron, far from the canonical sites. 对，位于内含子里几百到几千个碱基处，远离经典位点。 Those are exactly the variants routine pipelines miss, because everyone filters on proximity to exons. 这些恰恰是常规流程会漏掉的变异，因为大家都按"靠近外显子"来过滤。 CFTR has famous ones — the classic 3849+10kb C>T creates a cryptic exon deep in an intron. CFTR 就有著名的例子——经典的 3849+10kb C>T 会在一个内含子深处产生一个隐蔽外显子。

JORDAN So where does SpliceAI come in, and what's the problem the paper is fixing? 那 SpliceAI 是从哪儿介入的，这篇论文又在修正什么问题？

ALEX SpliceAI is a deep-learning model that scores, for each position, the probability that it acts as a splice donor or acceptor. SpliceAI 是一个深度学习模型，它为每个位置打分，给出该位置作为剪接供体或受体的概率。 Its real power is that it reasons about the whole transcript, so it can predict exon skipping or cryptic activation, not just a local hit. 它真正的强项是能对整条转录本进行推理，因此能预测外显子跳跃或隐蔽位点激活，而不只是局部的一个命中。 But here's the catch the authors zero in on. 但作者盯住的问题就在这里。 SpliceAI's headline output is a delta score — DS — the difference between the raw score of the reference allele and the raw score of the variant allele. SpliceAI 对外输出的是 delta 分数 (DS，delta score)——即参考等位基因的原始分数与变异等位基因的原始分数之间的差值。 The community uses a 0.2 delta-score cutoff, which the original authors themselves called "permissive," a high-recall threshold. 学界普遍使用 0.2 的 delta 分数阈值，而 SpliceAI 原作者自己称它是"宽松的" (permissive)，一个高召回率的阈值。

JORDAN Why does this matter enough to build a whole tool around it? 这为什么重要到值得为它专门造一个工具？ ALEX: Because splicing is a huge and under-served slice of pathogenicity. 因为剪接是致病性中一大块、却长期被忽视的部分。 The authors note that up to sixty percent of pathogenic variants are thought to alter correct splicing of the transcript. 作者指出，多达六成的致病变异被认为会改变转录本的正确剪接。 Yet confirming a splice effect in the lab needs RT-PCR, RNA sequencing, or a minigene assay, which are slow, expensive, and sometimes need tissue you cannot easily get. 然而在实验室确认一个剪接效应，需要 RT-PCR、RNA 测序或小基因实验，这些既慢又贵，有时还需要难以获取的组织。 So a predictor that tells you which variants are worth that effort, and which functional test to run, is doing real triage. 所以一个能告诉你哪些变异值得花这份力气、又该做哪种功能实验的预测工具，是在做真正的分诊。 The danger is a predictor that is confidently wrong in a systematic way — and the delta-score pitfall is exactly that kind of systematic blind spot. 危险在于一个以系统性方式自信地犯错的预测工具——而 delta 分数陷阱正是这样一种系统性盲区。

JORDAN I sense a "but" coming. 我感觉后面有个"但是"。

ALEX A big one. 一个大大的"但是"。 A delta score only measures the change. delta 分数只衡量变化量。 If the reference position already has a high raw score — say a cryptic site that's halfway armed — a variant can push it over the edge while adding only a tiny delta. 如果参考位置本身原始分数就已经很高——比如一个已经半激活的隐蔽位点——那么变异可能把它推过临界点，却只带来极小的 delta。 So a truly splice-altering variant can hide under the 0.2 cutoff. 于是一个真正改变剪接的变异，可能就藏在 0.2 阈值之下。 That's what the paper calls the "delta-score pitfall." 这就是论文所说的"delta 分数陷阱" (delta-score pitfall)。 The second problem: raw delta values are just numbers, hard to interpret when long-distance effects are in play. 第二个问题：原始的 delta 数值只是一串数字，当涉及远距离效应时很难解读。 The third: most public SpliceAI implementations won't even process complex deletion-insertions, the delins variants. 第三：大多数公开的 SpliceAI 实现甚至无法处理复杂的缺失-插入 (delins) 变异。
