Track B — OHDSI Standardized Vocabularies (Reich et al., JAMIA 2024)

Blurb. Reich and colleagues describe the OHDSI Standardized Vocabularies — the mandatory, centrally-maintained reference ontology underneath the OMOP common data model that lets 331 data sources / 2.1B patient records / 34 countries speak one semantic language. As of March 2023 it holds ~8.76M valid concepts from 136 vocabularies with >28M relationships. Three ideas do the work: domains (each concept's semantic category maps to a fixed OMOP field), standard concepts (one representative per meaning; source concepts link via "Maps to"), and polyhierarchies (multi-parent hierarchical trees). Only 66.8% of source concepts have a "Maps to" edge and many mappings are many-to-one, so mapping stays a curated task. Where it lands: AI-tooling / career priority, bridging to Liz-sponsored phenotype/data-quality work (method only). Buildable artifact: a variable-to-OMOP concept mapper — feed a data dictionary, get ranked standard-concept candidates + domain + confidence for human sign-off (scriptable, LLM-assisted Usagi).

Plain-English glossary

  • ATHENA — the OHDSI web tool for browsing and downloading the vocabulary releases as CSV tables.（中文：ATHENA 是 OHDSI 的网页工具，用于浏览和下载词表版本（CSV 表格）。）
  • closed-world assumption — the modeling stance that every fact and its timing is known, so absence of a record means absence of the fact; it forbids standard concepts for negative/past facts.（中文：封闭世界假设是一种建模立场，认为每个事实及其时间都已知，所以记录缺失即代表事实不存在；它禁止为否定/过去事实设立标准概念。）
  • CONCEPT / CONCEPT_RELATIONSHIP / CONCEPT_ANCESTOR — the core vocabulary tables: concept metadata, directed relationships (incl. "Maps to"), and the precomputed transitive hierarchy.（中文：这些是核心词表：概念元数据、有向关系（含"映射到"）、以及预计算的传递层级。）
  • domain — a concept's semantic category (Condition, Drug, Procedure, Measurement, Observation, Device, etc.), each tied to a specific OMOP CDM field.（中文：域是概念的语义类别（病症、药物、操作、测量、观察、器械等），每个都绑定到某个 OMOP CDM 字段。）
  • OHDSI — Observational Health Data Sciences and Informatics, the open-science community that maintains OMOP and the vocabularies (pronounced "Odyssey").（中文：OHDSI 是"观察性医疗数据科学与信息学"开放科学社区，维护 OMOP 和词表（读作"奥德赛"）。）
  • OMOP CDM — Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership common data model; a fixed table schema any health dataset is reshaped into.（中文：OMOP CDM 是"观察性医疗结局合作组织"通用数据模型，一个固定的表结构，任何健康数据集都可被重塑成它。）
  • ontology — a structured catalog of concepts plus defined relationships between them (a graph, not just a word list).（中文：本体是结构化的概念目录加上概念间定义好的关系（一张图，而非仅仅词表）。）
  • polyhierarchy — a hierarchical tree that allows a concept to have more than one parent.（中文：多重层级是允许一个概念拥有多个父节点的层级树。）
  • standard vs source concept — the single elevated representative for a meaning (standard, usable to record facts) vs the original vocabulary codes that "Maps to" it (source).（中文：标准概念是某含义被提升的唯一代表（可用于记录事实），源概念是映射到它的原始词表编码。）
  • Usagi — the OHDSI tool for semi-automated source-to-standard concept mapping with human review.（中文：Usagi 是 OHDSI 的工具，做半自动的"源到标准"概念映射并配合人工审阅。）

Full citation

Reich C, Ostropolets A, Ryan P, et al. OHDSI Standardized Vocabularies—a large-scale centralized reference ontology for international data harmonization. J Am Med Inform Assoc. 2024;31(3):583-590. PMID: 38175665 · PMC: PMC10873827 · DOI: 10.1093/jamia/ocad247

Methods / tools to lift

  • ATHENA download — vocabulary shipped as standardized CSVs (CONCEPT, CONCEPT_RELATIONSHIP, CONCEPT_ANCESTOR, etc.); pin a release version for reproducibility.
  • Standard-concept resolution — join source codes to their standard representative via "Maps to"; use CONCEPT_ANCESTOR to expand a concept to all descendants (cohort/covariate construction).
  • Domain routing — use each concept's domain to place facts in the correct OMOP field.
  • Usagi — semi-automated source→standard mapping with human adjudication; the pattern to script + LLM-assist.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves AI-tooling / career (bridge to Liz / CFFPR phenotype & data quality, method only). Paste-ready MVP:

Variable-to-OMOP concept mapper (M): input = a data dictionary CSV (variable name, description, units, value set); query ATHENA CONCEPT + CONCEPT_RELATIONSHIP plus an embedding search over concept names; output = a review-ready sheet of ranked standard-concept-ID candidates per variable, with domain + confidence, for a human curator to sign off (scriptable, LLM-assisted Usagi).

What to verify first

  • Mapping is curated, not automatic: only 66.8% of source concepts have a "Maps to" edge; many are many-to-one — tool proposes, human disposes.
  • Measurement domain (where CF lab/lung-function variables live) has the highest share of one-to-many / many-to-many "Maps to" — extra scrutiny needed.
  • Model units and value sets, not just the concept ID — a measurement concept without correct units is a trap.
  • Confirm license and pin a vocabulary version for reproducibility.
  • External method + public vocabulary only — no assumptions about any registry's internal genotype/data structures.

ALEX Welcome back. 欢迎回来。 Today's Track B paper is "OHDSI Standardized Vocabularies — a large-scale centralized reference ontology for international data harmonization," by Christian Reich and colleagues, in JAMIA, 2024. 今天 Track B 的论文是 Christian Reich 等人 2024 年发表在 JAMIA 上的《OHDSI 标准化词表——用于国际数据协调的大规模集中式参考本体》。 This is a Track B tooling pick, and it's less about a model and more about the semantic plumbing that lets many databases speak one language. 这是一篇 Track B 的工具类选题，它讲的不是某个模型，而是让许多数据库说同一种语言的"语义管道"。 We're reading it because harmonization is the open AI lane for your registry work. 我们读它，是因为数据协调正是你在登记系统工作中开放的那条 AI 赛道。

JORDAN Let me get the vocabulary straight first, because half of this is jargon. 先让我把词汇搞清楚，因为这里一半都是术语。 What is an "ontology," and what is OMOP? 什么是"本体 (ontology)"，OMOP 又是什么？

ALEX An ontology, in this context, is a structured catalog of concepts plus the defined relationships between them — not just a word list, but a graph that says "this is a kind of that" and "this maps to that." 在这个语境下，本体是一个结构化的概念目录，加上概念之间定义好的关系——它不只是一个词表，而是一张图，能表达"这是那的一种"以及"这个映射到那个"。 OMOP is the Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership common data model, or CDM — a fixed table schema that any health dataset can be reshaped into. OMOP 是"观察性医疗结局合作组织"的通用数据模型 (common data model, CDM)——一个固定的表结构，任何健康数据集都可以被重塑成这种形式。 OHDSI, pronounced "Odyssey," is the open-science community that maintains all of this. OHDSI（读作"奥德赛"）是维护这一切的开放科学社区。 So the object of the paper is the vocabulary layer that sits underneath the OMOP tables and tells you which codes are allowed and how they relate. 所以这篇论文的对象，是坐落在 OMOP 表底下的那一层词表，它告诉你哪些编码是被允许的、以及它们之间如何关联。

JORDAN These are data-model terms, not biology — so this is the ramp for me too, but for a different reason. 这些是数据模型的术语，不是生物学——所以这对我也是一个学习点，但原因不同。 Why does a registry person care about a giant vocabulary? 一个搞登记系统的人为什么要关心一个巨大的词表？

ALEX Because the entire value of a registry in cross-cohort research depends on your variables meaning the same thing as someone else's. 因为一个登记系统在跨队列研究中的全部价值，取决于你的变量与别人的变量含义相同。 If your database records a lung-function measurement under one local code and a European CF cohort records it under another, no shared analysis is possible until both are mapped to a common concept. 如果你的数据库用一个本地编码记录肺功能测量，而一个欧洲 CF 队列用另一个编码记录，那么在两者都被映射到一个共同概念之前，任何共享分析都无法进行。 That mapping is exactly what this vocabulary provides, at industrial scale. 那个映射正是这份词表所提供的，而且是工业规模的。 And the harmonization payoff compounds: once your data is in OMOP, the entire OHDSI analytics stack — cohort builders, characterization tools, patient-level prediction, population-level estimation — runs on your data unchanged, because those tools were written against the common model, not against your local schema. 而且协调的回报是复利式的：一旦你的数据进入 OMOP，整个 OHDSI 分析栈——队列构建器、特征刻画工具、患者层面预测、群体层面估计——都能原封不动地在你的数据上运行，因为那些工具是针对通用模型写的，而不是针对你的本地表结构。 That's the argument for doing the mapping work: it's a one-time cost that unlocks a large body of shared, validated tooling. 这就是"值得去做映射工作"的论据：它是一次性成本，却能解锁一大批共享的、经过验证的工具。

ALEX The problem they set out to solve is what they call the dependence on the context of source data representation. 他们要解决的问题，是他们所说的"对源数据表示背景的依赖"。 The OHDSI network spans 331 data sources, 2.1 billion patient records, across 34 countries — including big networks like All of Us, eMERGE, EHDEN, and N3C. OHDSI 网络覆盖 331 个数据源、21 亿条患者记录、遍及 34 个国家——包括 All of Us、eMERGE、EHDEN 和 N3C 这样的大型网络。 Every one of those sites uses different source coding systems — ICD-9, ICD-10, SNOMED, Read, local dictionaries. 其中每个站点都使用不同的源编码系统——ICD-9、ICD-10、SNOMED、Read、本地字典。 Without a central reference ontology, a cohort definition written at one site is meaningless at another. 没有一个中央参考本体，在一个站点写的队列定义在另一个站点就毫无意义。 The paper's thesis is that a single, mandatory, centrally-maintained vocabulary — rather than each site mapping ad hoc — is what makes the network reproducible. 论文的论点是：一个单一的、强制的、集中维护的词表——而不是每个站点各自临时映射——才是让整个网络可复现的关键。

JORDAN Why wouldn't an existing standard like SNOMED or the UMLS already solve this? 为什么像 SNOMED 或 UMLS 这样已有的标准还不能解决这个问题？

ALEX That's the key argument of the paper, and it's a subtle one. 这是论文的核心论点，而且很微妙。 Traditional biomedical ontologies were built to describe a domain and improve search, and they do narrow the semantic space. 传统的生物医学本体是为了描述一个领域、改进检索而构建的，它们确实收窄了语义空间。 But used on their own they fall short in a distributed network for two reasons. 但单独使用时，它们在分布式网络中会力有不逮，原因有二。 First, no single source vocabulary covers every domain a patient record touches — SNOMED is strong on conditions, RxNorm on drugs, LOINC on labs — so you inevitably need many vocabularies stitched together. 第一，没有任何单一的源词表能覆盖患者记录涉及的每个域——SNOMED 擅长病症、RxNorm 擅长药物、LOINC 擅长化验——所以你不可避免地需要把多个词表缝合在一起。 Second, and more importantly, mapping alone isn't enough; you need one designated standard representative per meaning so that an analyst querying across sites asks for exactly one concept, not a shifting bag of synonyms. 第二，也更重要，光有映射还不够；你需要为每个含义指定一个标准代表，这样跨站点查询的分析师只需请求恰好一个概念，而不是一堆变动的同义词。 The OHDSI contribution is that praxis layer — the rules and the standard-concept designations — sitting on top of the imported vocabularies. OHDSI 的贡献就是那个"实践层"——规则加上标准概念的指定——它坐落在被导入的词表之上。

ALEX And the scale of the problem is why this matters. 而问题的规模正是它重要的原因。 A CF phenotype question — say, comparing pulmonary-exacerbation rates across cohorts — is only answerable if "pulmonary exacerbation," the drugs used to treat it, and the lung-function measurements all resolve to the same standard concepts everywhere. 一个 CF 表型问题——比如跨队列比较肺部急性加重率——只有当"肺部急性加重"、用于治疗它的药物、以及肺功能测量在各处都解析到相同的标准概念时，才能回答。 That's the difference between a paper you can reproduce and a one-off you can't. 这就是"一篇你能复现的论文"和"一个你无法复现的一次性结果"之间的区别。

JORDAN Okay, how is it actually built? 好，它到底是怎么构建的？

ALEX Three ideas do most of the work. 三个理念承担了大部分工作。 First, domains: every concept is assigned a semantic category — Condition, Drug, Procedure, Measurement, Observation, Device, and so on — and each domain maps to a specific field in the OMOP tables. 第一是"域 (domain)"：每个概念都被赋予一个语义类别——病症、药物、操作、测量、观察、器械等等——而每个域都映射到 OMOP 表中的一个特定字段。 A condition concept can only go in the condition field, which keeps the data self-consistent. 一个病症概念只能进入病症字段，这让数据保持自洽。 Second, standard concepts: when several vocabularies contain a concept with the same meaning, they elevate exactly one to be the "standard" representative, and only standard concepts are allowed to record a fact. 第二是"标准概念 (standard concept)"：当多个词表里有含义相同的概念时，他们把其中恰好一个提升为"标准"代表，且只有标准概念才被允许用来记录事实。 The others become source concepts, linked to the standard one by a "Maps to" relationship. 其余的成为"源概念"，通过一个"映射到 (Maps to)"关系连接到那个标准概念。 Third, polyhierarchies: concepts are organized into hierarchical trees that allow more than one parent, so a drug product can roll up to multiple classification concepts at once. 第三是"多重层级 (polyhierarchy)"：概念被组织成允许多个父节点的层级树，所以一个药品可以同时归入多个分类概念之下。

ALEX There's also a quality-assurance layer worth naming. 还有一个值得点名的质量保证层。 For each semiannual release they run a multi-stage QA process — automated and manual — that enforces referential integrity, checks domain and concept-class and relationship rules, verifies validity dates and status, and examines the polyhierarchies for consistency. 每个半年度版本，他们都运行一个多阶段的质量保证 (QA) 流程——自动加人工——强制引用完整性、检查域与概念类别与关系规则、核验有效性日期与状态、并检查多重层级的一致性。 That discipline is why they can promise that content stays consistent across releases, which is what lets two data sites on different versions still interoperate. 正是这种纪律，让他们能承诺内容在各版本间保持一致，而这也是不同版本的两个数据站点仍能互通的原因。

ALEX A design choice I'd push on is the closed-world assumption they adopt. 一个我想质疑的设计选择，是他们采用的"封闭世界假设 (closed-world assumption)"。 It means every fact and its timing is assumed known, and absence of a record means absence of the fact. 它意味着假设每个事实及其时间都是已知的，记录的缺失即代表事实的缺失。 That's clean, but it forbids standard concepts for negative or past facts — "No indigestion" or "Old myocardial infarction" can't be standard concepts, and instead get handled through workarounds like a "History of" observation concept. 这很干净，但它禁止为否定事实或过去事实设立标准概念——"无消化不良"或"陈旧性心肌梗死"不能作为标准概念，而要通过像"既往史"观察概念这样的变通办法来处理。 For a registry with a lot of derived and historical fields, that modeling constraint is something you'd feel in practice. 对于一个有大量派生字段和历史字段的登记系统，这个建模约束你在实践中是会感受到的。 And notice this touches genetics directly: the paper mentions OHDSI Working Groups issuing revised content in specific areas including genetic data, so how variants themselves get represented in OMOP is still an evolving corner — worth watching for anyone mapping a genotype-bearing registry. 而且注意这直接触及遗传学：论文提到 OHDSI 工作组在特定领域发布修订内容，其中包括遗传数据，所以变异本身如何在 OMOP 中被表示仍是一个演进中的角落——对任何映射带基因型登记系统的人都值得关注。

JORDAN Give me the scale numbers — does it deliver? 给我规模数字——它兑现了吗？

ALEX As of March 2023 the vocabulary holds about 8.76 million valid concepts — 10.6 million counting retired ones — drawn from 136 source vocabularies, 101 of them imported from external sources. 截至 2023 年 3 月，该词表包含约 876 万个有效概念——连同已停用的共 1060 万个——来自 136 个源词表，其中 101 个是从外部来源导入的。 Standard concepts are 40.5% of the total; source concepts about half. 标准概念占总数的 40.5%；源概念约占一半。 There are over 28 million relationships between concepts, always stored in both directions. 概念之间有超过 2800 万条关系，且总是双向存储。 The most common relationship is hierarchical "Is a / Subsumes" at 38.3%, followed by mapping relationships at 14.1%, which map 66.8% of source concepts to a standard one. 最常见的关系是层级式的"是一种 / 包含 (Is a / Subsumes)"，占 38.3%，其次是映射关系，占 14.1%，它把 66.8% 的源概念映射到某个标准概念。 As a concrete example: SNOMED-CT's Condition domain alone carries 112,118 concepts, covering essentially every diagnosable condition except detailed cancer, which ICD-O-3 supplies. 举个具体例子：单是 SNOMED-CT 的病症域就有 112,118 个概念，基本覆盖了除详细癌症以外的所有可诊断病症，而详细癌症由 ICD-O-3 补充。

ALEX The mapping table itself is worth dwelling on because it's where the danger lives. 映射表本身值得多停留一下，因为危险就藏在那里。 The paper breaks down "Maps to" relationships by cardinality: one-to-one, many-to-one, one-to-many, and many-to-many. 论文按基数拆解了"映射到"关系：一对一、多对一、一对多、和多对多。 The most common are many-to-one — many source codes collapsing to a single standard concept, which is safe. 最常见的是多对一——多个源编码汇聚到单个标准概念，这是安全的。 But the Measurement domain stands out: it carries the highest share of one-to-many and many-to-many mappings, meaning a single lab code can legitimately fan out to several standard concepts. 但测量域很突出：它的一对多和多对多映射占比最高，意味着单个化验编码可以合理地扇出到多个标准概念。 A naive automated join over Measurement would silently pick one and lose the rest — exactly the kind of quiet error that corrupts a phenotype analysis. 对测量域做天真的自动连接会悄悄挑一个而丢掉其余——正是那种会毁掉表型分析的隐蔽错误。 That's why the tool we'll sketch has to surface cardinality and force human review, not just emit a best guess. 这就是为什么我们将勾勒的工具必须暴露基数并强制人工审阅，而不只是给出一个最佳猜测。

ALEX On adoption — which is the real test for infrastructure — since ATHENA, the browse-and-download tool, launched in 2015, more than 8,600 users have performed over 50,000 downloads of the vocabulary releases. 关于采用度——这是基础设施真正的检验——自 2015 年浏览下载工具 ATHENA 上线以来，已有超过 8,600 名用户完成了超过 50,000 次词表版本的下载。 And they release semiannually while keeping content stable enough that the original 2009 OMOP experiment can still be reproduced today. 而且他们每半年发布一次，同时保持内容足够稳定，以至于 2009 年最初的 OMOP 实验今天仍可复现。 That backward compatibility is the quiet achievement here. 这种向后兼容性正是这里悄无声息的成就。 It started with 22 vocabularies in 2009 and has grown to 136 without breaking that reproducibility, a growth-with-stability track record that's rare in health informatics. 它从 2009 年的 22 个词表起步，成长到 136 个而没有破坏那种可复现性——这种"边成长边稳定"的记录在健康信息学中是罕见的。

JORDAN Does the vocabulary handle genetic or variant data at all, given where this project is headed? 考虑到这个项目的走向，这份词表到底处不处理遗传或变异数据？

ALEX Only partially, and that's an important limitation to name. 只是部分处理，而这是一个重要的、需要点明的局限。 OMOP was born from drug-safety surveillance, so its mature domains are Condition, Drug, Procedure, and Measurement — the clinical layer. OMOP 诞生于药物安全监测，所以它成熟的域是病症、药物、操作和测量——即临床层。 Genomic variants don't have a first-class, fully-standardized home in the core model yet; a Working Group has been extending it, but variant representation is far less settled than, say, a lab test. 基因组变异在核心模型里还没有一个头等的、完全标准化的归宿；一个工作组一直在扩展它，但变异表示远不如比如一次化验那样成熟。 The practical consequence for us is a division of labor: map the CF phenotype and clinical variables into OMOP where the vocabulary is strong, and keep the CFTR genotype layer in a purpose-built representation — like the HGVS-normalized, class-labeled scheme we keep coming back to — rather than forcing variants into a domain that isn't ready for them. 对我们的实际后果是一种分工：把 CF 表型和临床变量映射到词表强壮的 OMOP 里，而把 CFTR 基因型层保留在一个专门构建的表示中——比如我们反复提到的、经 HGVS 规范化并带类别标签的方案——而不是硬把变异塞进一个尚未准备好的域。 That's a cleaner architecture anyway. 无论如何这都是更干净的架构。
