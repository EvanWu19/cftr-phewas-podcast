Ep4 — Optimizing the efficiency and effectiveness of data quality assurance in a multicenter clinical dataset

Fu A, Shen T, Roberts SB, et al. (2025), Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association. Abstract-only due to a fetch anomaly (empty full-text field returned despite a PMCID on record and Europe PMC listing free HTML/PDF access) — not genuine closed access. Using the GEMINI multicenter hospital-medicine network (462,226 admissions, 32 hospitals, 2021-2023), the authors report that semi-automated data-quality checks caught 79 issues (14 large-impact), and that a substantial follow-on manual chart-abstraction effort — 2,676 patient encounters across 19 hospitals — found only 4 additional meaningful errors, against 365 manual-abstraction false positives that scaled roughly linearly with review volume. A simulation experiment then directly answers the review-budget question: 95% sensitivity for the transfusion-data error was reached after reviewing just 5 records, while the physician-identifier table needed 18 — a field-specific, not uniform, answer. This is the closest published treatment this podcast has found of the actual question behind Liz's assignment: how much human review is genuinely worth doing on a pool of flagged records. The buildable idea: run the same kind of simulation on a labeled subset of any CFFPR-adjacent candidate-generator's flagged pool, field by field, to find the minimum review volume for a target sensitivity rather than assuming one review-fraction number fits every field.

Plain-English glossary

  • Chart abstraction — A human reader going into the original medical record (clinical note, lab report, transfusion record) to manually pull out or verify a specific data point, rather than trusting an automated extraction pipeline's output.（中文：由人工阅读者进入原始病历，手动提取或核实某个具体数据点，而非直接采信自动化提取流程的结果。）
  • Adjudication — The step where a flagged, ambiguous case gets a final human judgment call — not just whether a value is plausible, but whether it reflects a real clinical event, a data-entry error, or something in between.（中文：对一个被标记的模糊病例做出最终人工判断的步骤——判断的不只是数值是否合理，还包括它是真实临床事件、数据录入错误，还是介于两者之间。）
  • Semi-automated data-quality check — A rule-based or statistical check that surfaces candidate issues automatically, which a data team still has to review and resolve; the candidate-generation layer, not a fully automated pipeline.（中文：能够自动发现候选问题的基于规则或统计的检查，但仍需数据团队人工查看并处理；属于候选生成层，而非全自动流程。）
  • Review-budget simulation — Sampling increasing numbers of manually reviewed records from a labeled subset and plotting detection sensitivity against review cost, to find the minimum review volume needed for a target sensitivity.（中文：从已标注子集中抽取逐渐增加的人工审查记录数，绘制检测敏感度与审查成本的关系曲线，从而找出达到目标敏感度所需的最小审查量。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Field-specific (not global) review-budget simulation — sensitivity-vs-review-volume curves computed separately per field/error type, since 5 records sufficed for one field and 18 for another.
  • Explicit false-positive cost accounting — reporting the 365 chart-abstraction false positives investigated, not just the 4 true finds, giving an honest full-cost picture of manual review.
  • Staged QA design — running manual validation only after semi-automated issues are already resolved, isolating the marginal value manual review adds on top of automated checks rather than conflating the two.
