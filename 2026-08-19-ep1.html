Ep1 (Track A) — Lilace: A Purpose-Built Statistical Model for FACS-Based Deep Mutational Scanning

Freudenberg et al. (2026, Genome Biology) fix a gap that sits directly underneath CFTR functional-assay evidence: no statistical method had been purpose-built for FACS-based deep mutational scanning (DMS) data before this paper. The standard "mean bin" scoring approach ignores replicate variance and sequencing coverage and offers no real false discovery rate (FDR) control; the alternative, Enrich2, borrows modeling assumptions from growth-based DMS experiments that are structurally misspecified for FACS data. Lilace is a Bayesian hierarchical model built around three ideas — a unimodal effect-size shift relative to synonymous negative controls, explicit measurement-noise modeling, and position-level information sharing — that achieves roughly a 90% FDR reduction over the next-best method while mostly preserving sensitivity, validated on both simulations and real OCT1/Kir2.1 screens. Because CFTR functional assays already use FACS-based protocols like VAMP-seq, any existing CFTR DMS dataset with replicate-level bin counts is a direct candidate for Lilace reanalysis to produce uncertainty-quantified, FDR-controlled variant scores for ACMG PS3/BS3 evidence.

Plain-English glossary

  • Deep mutational scanning (DMS) — a pooled experimental method that tests the functional effect of nearly every possible amino-acid substitution in a protein at once.（中文：深度突变扫描，一种汇总实验方法，可同时检测蛋白质中几乎所有可能氨基酸替换的功能效应。）
  • FACS (fluorescence-activated cell sorting) — sorts a cell population into bins by fluorescence intensity, used as a DMS readout for properties like protein abundance or activity.（中文：FACS（荧光激活细胞分选），根据荧光强度将细胞群体分选到不同分选门中，在DMS实验中用于读出蛋白质丰度或活性等特性。）
  • Synonymous variant — a DNA change that does not alter the amino acid sequence, used as the negative-control baseline in DMS experiments.（中文：同义突变，一种不改变氨基酸序列的DNA变化，在DMS实验中用作阴性对照基线。）
  • False discovery rate (FDR) — the expected proportion of "significant" calls that are actually false positives.（中文：错误发现率，被标记为"显著"的结果中实际为假阳性的预期比例。）
  • Enrich2 — the most widely used general-purpose DMS scoring tool, built on modeling assumptions from growth-based (not FACS-based) DMS experiments.（中文：Enrich2，使用最广泛的通用DMS评分工具，其建模假设来自基于生长而非基于FACS的DMS实验。）
  • Position-level shrinkage — a Bayesian technique that shares statistical strength across variants at the same protein position to improve estimates for data-limited variants.（中文：位点层面的收缩，一种贝叶斯技术，在同一蛋白质位点的变异间共享统计强度，以改善数据有限变异的估计。）
  • MCMC / variational inference — MCMC (Markov chain Monte Carlo) is Lilace's accurate but slower default fitting method; variational inference is a faster, approximate alternative.（中文：MCMC（马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛）是Lilace默认使用的、更准确但较慢的拟合方法；变分推断是一种更快但近似的替代方法。）
  • VAMP-seq — a FACS-based DMS protocol for measuring protein abundance/stability, used both in this paper's OCT1 validation dataset and in published CFTR functional assays.（中文：VAMP-seq，一种基于FACS的DMS方案，用于测量蛋白质丰度/稳定性，既用于本文的OCT1验证数据集，也用于已发表的CFTR功能实验。）
  • ACMG PS3/BS3 — American College of Medical Genetics evidence codes for variant classification based on well-established functional assay results (PS3 = pathogenic-supporting, BS3 = benign-supporting).（中文：ACMG PS3/BS3，美国医学遗传学学院基于成熟功能实验结果的变异分类证据代码（PS3为致病性支持证据，BS3为良性支持证据）。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Lilace — open-source Bayesian hierarchical model (Stan for full MCMC, Pyro for variational inference) for FACS-based DMS variant effect scoring with FDR control.
  • Position-level shrinkage — statistical technique for pooling information across same-position variants; portable to any variant-effect model with per-residue structure.
  • Synonymous-masking validation — an empirical FDR-estimation technique (randomly mask 20% of known-benign synonymous variants, check false-call rate) usable on any real dataset without a full ground truth.

What to verify first

  • Whether any published CFTR DMS dataset actually provides replicate-level bin counts (Lilace's required input), not just a precomputed summary score.
  • Whether Lilace's position-level shrinkage assumption transfers appropriately to CFTR's specific domain architecture, since it was validated on OCT1 and Kir2.1.
  • Whether Lilace's conservative bias (fewer discoveries than looser methods) is the right tradeoff for a PS3/BS3 pipeline, where missed vs. false pathogenic calls carry different consequences.
  • Runtime and documentation maturity of the open-source release before adopting it as a recurring pipeline step.

ALEX Today's Track A pick is a 2026 paper in Genome Biology by Jonas Freudenberg and colleagues, "Accurate variant effect estimation in FACS-based deep mutational scanning data with Lilace." 今天A轨道选的是2026年发表在《基因组生物学》（Genome Biology）上的一篇论文，作者是Jonas Freudenberg等人，题目是《用Lilace对基于FACS的深度突变扫描数据进行准确的变异效应估计》。 It's a statistics paper, not a CFTR paper, but it fixes a problem that sits directly underneath how CFTR functional-assay evidence gets built and trusted. 这是一篇统计学论文，不是直接讲CFTR的，但它解决的问题恰恰是CFTR功能性实验证据能否被构建和信任的底层基础。 The paper introduces Lilace, a Bayesian statistical model for scoring how disruptive a mutation is, purpose-built for an experimental design that, until now, had no dedicated statistical method at all. Lilace是一个贝叶斯统计模型，用于给突变的破坏性程度打分，专为一种此前完全没有专属统计方法的实验设计而设计。 We're reading it for Track A, the Karen collaboration track on CFTR variant interpretation. 我们把它归入A轨道，也就是与Karen合作、专注CFTR变异解读的那条线。

JORDAN Before we get into Lilace itself, walk me through what a "FACS-based deep mutational scanning" experiment actually is. 在讲Lilace本身之前，先说说"基于FACS的深度突变扫描"实验到底是什么。

ALEX Deep mutational scanning, or DMS, is a way to test the functional effect of essentially every possible amino-acid substitution in a protein, all at once, in a single pooled experiment. 深度突变扫描（deep mutational scanning，缩写DMS）是一种方法，可以在一次汇总实验中，同时检测蛋白质中几乎每一个可能的氨基酸替换对功能的影响。 You build a library of cells, each expressing one variant of the protein, then you need a way to read out which variants behave normally and which are broken. 你先构建一个细胞文库，每个细胞表达蛋白质的一个变异版本，然后需要一种方法来读出哪些变异表现正常、哪些出了问题。 FACS, fluorescence-activated cell sorting, is one common readout: you tag the protein's abundance or activity with a fluorescent signal, then sort the whole cell population into a small number of bins — typically four — based on how brightly each cell fluoresces. FACS（fluorescence-activated cell sorting，荧光激活细胞分选）是一种常见的读出方式：你用荧光信号标记蛋白质的丰度或活性，然后根据每个细胞发光的强弱，把整个细胞群体分选进少数几个分选门（通常是四个）。 Each bin is then sequenced, and the resulting read counts tell you, indirectly, how much of that variant's cell population landed in each brightness bin. 每个分选门随后被测序，得到的读数间接告诉你，该变异对应的细胞群体有多少落在每个亮度分选门里。 A variant that behaves like wild-type lands in the same bins as the synonymous, silent-mutation controls; a variant that destabilizes the protein or kills its function shifts into different bins. 表现得像野生型的变异会落在与同义突变（synonymous mutation，即不改变氨基酸序列的沉默突变）对照组相同的分选门里；而破坏蛋白稳定性或功能的变异，则会转移到不同的分选门。 This is exactly the kind of assay that's been used to generate CFTR functional data — measuring how much a variant disrupts CFTR's folding, trafficking to the cell surface, or chloride-channel activity — and functional assay results like these are exactly the class of evidence ACMG guidelines allow as PS3, pathogenic-supporting, or BS3, benign-supporting, in variant classification. 这正是产生CFTR功能数据所用的实验类型——测量某个变异对CFTR折叠、转运到细胞表面或氯离子通道活性的破坏程度——而这类功能实验结果，正是ACMG指南在变异分类中允许作为PS3（致病性支持证据）或BS3（良性支持证据）的证据类别。

JORDAN So the readout is noisy bin counts, and somebody has to turn that into a clean "this variant is bad" or "this variant is fine" call. 所以读出的是有噪声的分选门计数，得有人把它转化成清晰的"这个变异是坏的"还是"这个变异没问题"的判断。 That sounds like exactly the kind of thing that already has an established method. 这听起来应该已经有成熟的方法了。

ALEX You'd think so, but the paper's whole motivating point is that no statistical method had been purpose-built for this. 你可能会这么想，但这篇论文的核心出发点正是：此前没有任何统计方法是专门为此设计的。 The standard approach in practice is a "mean bin" score: you compute a count-weighted average of the bin labels for a variant, then flag it as significant if it falls outside some number of standard deviations of the synonymous control distribution. 实际操作中的标准做法是"平均分选门"评分：对某个变异的分选门标签计算按读数加权的平均值，如果它超出同义突变对照组分布的若干个标准差范围，就标记为显著。 That approach ignores replicate-to-replicate variability, ignores how much sequencing coverage a variant got, and typically skips an explicit hypothesis test entirely, so there's no principled way to control the false discovery rate, or FDR — the proportion of your "significant" calls that are actually false positives. 这种方法忽略了重复实验之间的变异性，忽略了每个变异获得的测序覆盖度，而且通常完全跳过了显式的假设检验，所以没有一种有原则的方法来控制错误发现率（false discovery rate，缩写FDR）——也就是你标记为"显著"的结果中，实际上是假阳性的比例。 The other existing option is to borrow statistical models built for a different kind of DMS experiment — growth-based assays, where variant fitness is read out as a change in the relative growth rate of the cell population over multiple generations, not a single fluorescence snapshot. 另一种现有选项是借用为另一类DMS实验设计的统计模型——基于生长（growth-based）的实验，这类实验中变异的适应度是通过细胞群体在多代生长中的相对生长速率变化来读出的，而不是单次的荧光快照。 The best-known one is a tool called Enrich2. 其中最知名的工具叫Enrich2。 Growth-based models assume something like a consistent slope in how a variant's read counts change across generations, and that assumption is simply wrong for FACS data, where you have one static distribution across four bins, not a trajectory over time. 基于生长的模型假设变异的读数在多代之间以某种一致的斜率变化，而这个假设对于FACS数据根本不成立——FACS数据是四个分选门上的一个静态分布，而不是随时间变化的轨迹。 So people have been using tools that are statistically misspecified for the data they're actually applying them to. 所以人们一直在用统计学上并不适配所处理数据的工具。

JORDAN What does Lilace do differently? 那Lilace有什么不同做法？

ALEX Lilace is a Bayesian hierarchical model built around three structural ideas about what FACS-DMS data actually looks like. Lilace是一个贝叶斯层级模型，围绕关于FACS-DMS数据真实结构的三个核心思想构建。 First, a variant's effect shows up as a unimodal shift in its fluorescence distribution relative to the synonymous negative-control distribution — the whole distribution moves one direction, it doesn't split into two peaks. 第一，变异的效应表现为其荧光分布相对于同义突变阴性对照分布的单峰位移——整个分布向一个方向移动，而不是分裂成两个峰。 Second, the observed bin counts are a noisy proxy for that underlying distribution, and Lilace explicitly models that measurement noise with an overdispersion term, rather than treating raw counts as ground truth. 第二，观测到的分选门计数只是对该潜在分布的一个带噪声的替代，Lilace用一个过度离散（overdispersion）项显式地对这种测量噪声建模，而不是把原始计数当作真值来处理。 Third, variants at the same amino-acid position in the protein tend to have correlated effects, so Lilace borrows statistical strength across a position — a technique called position-level shrinkage — which especially helps variants with limited data. 第三，蛋白质中同一氨基酸位点上的不同变异，其效应往往是相关的，所以Lilace会在同一位点内共享统计强度，这种技术叫做位点层面的收缩（position-level shrinkage），对数据量有限的变异尤其有帮助。 On top of that, Lilace uses the residual variance within the synonymous control group itself as a negative-control-based bias correction, to properly calibrate how confident it should be that a variant is truly different from wild-type. 除此之外，Lilace还利用同义突变对照组内部的残差方差，进行一种基于阴性对照的偏差校正，从而更准确地校准"该变异真的不同于野生型"这一判断的置信度。

ALEX Here's where I'd push back a little on the design: Lilace fits with full Markov chain Monte Carlo, or MCMC, sampling by default, which is accurate but can take hours per protein, so they also built a faster variational-inference version as a speed-for-accuracy tradeoff. 这里我想稍微质疑一下这个设计：Lilace默认使用完整的马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛（Markov chain Monte Carlo，缩写MCMC）采样来拟合，这样做准确但每个蛋白质可能要跑上几个小时，所以他们也构建了一个更快的变分推断（variational inference）版本，作为速度换准确度的折中方案。 That's a reasonable engineering call, but it does mean the "fast" version of the tool is quietly a worse version of the tool in high-variance settings, and a user has to know to make that tradeoff deliberately. 这是一个合理的工程决策，但也意味着这个工具的"快速版"在高方差场景下悄悄地是一个更差的版本，用户必须清楚地意识到这个权衡并主动做出选择。

JORDAN If the mean-bin approach and Enrich2 were both statistically shaky, why did they become the default in the first place? 如果平均分选门方法和Enrich2在统计上都站不住脚，那它们最初为什么会成为默认选择？

ALEX Mostly historical momentum. 主要是历史惯性的原因。 Enrich2 was built early and became the most widely cited general-purpose DMS scoring tool, so people reached for it even when applying it to FACS data it wasn't designed for, since having some established tool felt safer than having none. Enrich2很早就被开发出来，并成为被引用最多的通用型DMS评分工具，所以即便把它应用于并非为其设计的FACS数据，人们依然会选择使用它，因为有一个成熟的工具用，总比完全没有工具让人感觉更安心。 The mean-bin approach, meanwhile, requires no special software at all — it's just an average and a standard-deviation cutoff you can compute in a spreadsheet — so it persisted purely because it's the path of least resistance, not because anyone believed it was statistically rigorous. 而平均分选门方法完全不需要专门的软件——它只是一个平均值加标准差阈值，在电子表格里就能算出来——所以它之所以能延续下来，纯粹是因为它是阻力最小的路径，而不是因为有人真的相信它在统计上是严谨的。 Neither tool had ever been benchmarked head-to-head against a known ground truth for FACS data specifically, which is part of what makes this paper's simulation work valuable on its own, independent of Lilace itself. 这两个工具此前都从未针对FACS数据、在已知真值的条件下做过正面基准测试，这也是这篇论文的模拟实验部分本身就具有价值的原因之一，独立于Lilace这个模型本身。

JORDAN Did it actually work better than the existing approaches? 它实际效果真的比现有方法更好吗？

ALEX The headline number is about a 90% reduction in false discovery rate compared to the next-best-performing method, in the paper's default, data-estimated simulation setting, while mostly maintaining sensitivity outside of the highest-variance scenarios. 最核心的数字是，在论文默认的、基于真实数据估计参数的模拟场景中，与表现第二好的方法相比，Lilace的错误发现率降低了约90%，同时在方差最高的场景之外，基本保持了灵敏度（sensitivity）。 They validated this two ways: simulations seeded from real OCT1 and Kir2.1 datasets with a known ground truth, and an empirical proxy on six real published FACS screens where they randomly masked 20% of synonymous variants and checked how often those known-benign variants got incorrectly called significant. 他们用两种方式验证了这一点：一是用真实的OCT1和Kir2.1数据集参数生成、具有已知真值的模拟数据；二是在六个真实已发表的FACS筛选实验上做的一个经验性替代验证——随机遮蔽20%的同义突变，检查这些已知为良性的变异被错误标记为显著的频率。 In the real-data check, Lilace and one specific baseline — a Benjamini-Hochberg-corrected version of the mean-bin approach — were the only two methods that actually controlled FDR at the nominal 0.05 level. 在真实数据的检验中，Lilace和一个特定的基线方法——经过Benjamini-Hochberg校正的平均分选门方法——是仅有的两种真正能把错误发现率控制在名义0.05水平的方法。 As an independent sanity check, since there's no true positive-control set for high-effect variants, they also compared variant scores against AlphaMissense pathogenicity predictions, and Lilace's effect sizes had the highest or tied-highest correlation with AlphaMissense across every dataset tested. 作为一个独立的合理性检验——由于不存在针对高效应变异的真正阳性对照集——他们还把变异评分与AlphaMissense的致病性预测做了对比，结果在所有测试数据集中，Lilace的效应值与AlphaMissense的相关性都是最高或并列最高。 The tradeoff is that Lilace is conservative: applied to a real OCT1 screen, it didn't call about 36% of the variants that the older Enrich2 method called significant, mostly because those calls didn't hold up once replicate variance was properly accounted for. 代价是Lilace比较保守：应用于真实的OCT1筛选数据时，它没有把旧方法Enrich2标记为显著的约36%的变异也标记为显著，主要是因为一旦正确考虑了重复实验间的方差，这些判断就站不住脚了。

ALEX One more result worth flagging: they also applied Lilace across two different phenotypes measured on the same Kir2.1 protein — surface expression and overall abundance — and combined the two discovery sets to classify each variant as affecting abundance only, surface expression only, both, or neither. 还有一个值得一提的结果：他们还把Lilace应用于同一个Kir2.1蛋白测量的两种不同表型——细胞表面表达和整体丰度——并把这两组判定结果结合起来，把每个变异分类为只影响丰度、只影响表面表达、两者都影响、或两者都不影响。 Since abundance is upstream of surface expression biologically, a good method's discovery pattern should respect that hierarchy, and Lilace's classifications leaned more toward that expected biological ordering than the mean-bin-based alternatives did. 由于从生物学上讲丰度是表面表达的上游环节，一个好的方法给出的判定模式理应尊重这一层级关系，而Lilace的分类结果比基于平均分选门的替代方法，更符合这种预期的生物学顺序。 That's a useful independent check, because it doesn't rely on any external ground truth at all — it's testing internal consistency against known biology. 这是一个有用的独立检验，因为它完全不依赖任何外部真值——它检验的是模型判定结果与已知生物学之间的内部一致性。

JORDAN So where does this land for the Karen collaboration specifically? 那这对Karen合作具体意味着什么？
