Show notes — Track C — 2026-07-25

Blurb. Cigana et al. (Pathogens, 2025; San Raffaele) follow up a Collaborative Cross mouse QTL — a 31-gene interval linked to survival after respiratory Pseudomonas aeruginosa infection — by testing one candidate, S1PR1 (sphingosine-1-phosphate receptor 1, a GPCR), in human CF cell models. Using CRISPR/Cas9 they knocked out S1PR1 in CFTR-deficient IB3-1 (F508del/W1282X) and CFTR-corrected C38 bronchial epithelial cells. S1PR1 loss reduced viability, increased cytotoxicity, and enhanced IL-8 (neutrophil-recruiting) secretion under P. aeruginosa exoproducts — specifically in the CFTR-deficient background; the same knockout did much less with working CFTR. Mutant S1PR1 mislocalized perinuclearly, and even wild-type S1PR1 accumulated perinuclearly in CFTR-deficient cells, suggesting CFTR ER stress impairs S1PR1 maturation. Immunohistochemistry on 5 CF lungs showed markedly reduced S1PR1 vs non-CF. Where it lands: CF modifier catalog + summary-stats colocalization. Buildable: mouse-QTL-to-human-modifier mapper — triage the other 30 QTL genes against human GWAS/co-expression.

Plain-English glossary

  • Collaborative Cross — a large panel of genetically diverse lab mice bred to capture broad natural genetic variation.（中文：一大套基因多样化的实验小鼠品系，用以捕捉广泛的天然遗传变异。）
  • Endoplasmic reticulum (ER) stress — clogging/overload of the protein-folding machinery; F508del CFTR is retained and degraded there, and may impair maturation of other proteins.（中文：蛋白折叠机器被堵塞/超载；F508del 的 CFTR 滞留并在此被降解，可能损害其他蛋白的成熟。）
  • Exoproducts — secreted bacterial molecules (toxins, enzymes, signaling factors), as opposed to the whole live bug.（中文：细菌分泌出来的分子——毒素、酶、信号因子——而非整只活菌。）
  • GPCR (G-protein-coupled receptor) — a large family of cell-surface receptors that sense an external signal and relay it inward.（中文：一大类细胞表面受体，感知外部信号并向细胞内传递。）
  • IB3-1 / C38 — a matched bronchial epithelial cell-line pair; IB3-1 is CFTR-deficient (F508del/W1282X), C38 is IB3-1 corrected with functional CFTR.（中文：一对配对的支气管上皮细胞系；IB3-1 为 CFTR 缺陷型，C38 是用有功能 CFTR 修正的 IB3-1。）
  • IL-8 (interleukin-8) — the cytokine that recruits neutrophils, whose over-activity drives much CF airway damage.（中文：招募中性粒细胞的细胞因子，中性粒细胞过度活跃造成大量 CF 气道损伤。）
  • Modifier gene — a gene other than CFTR that shapes CF disease severity.（中文：CFTR 之外、影响 CF 病情严重程度的基因。）
  • Perinuclear localization — clustered around the nucleus (Golgi/ER); a sign a receptor never reached the cell surface.（中文：聚集在细胞核周围（高尔基体/内质网），说明受体未能到达细胞表面。）
  • Pseudomonas aeruginosa — the dominant chronic bacterial pathogen in CF airways.（中文：CF 气道中最主要的慢性细菌病原体。）
  • QTL (quantitative trait locus) — a genomic region statistically linked to a quantitative trait; here it held 31 candidate genes.（中文：在统计上与某数量性状相关联的基因组区域；此处含 31 个候选基因。）
  • S1P / S1PR1 — sphingosine-1-phosphate, a bioactive signaling lipid, and its receptor S1PR1, which maintains endothelial barrier integrity and guides immune-cell traffic.（中文：1-磷酸鞘氨醇（一种生物活性信号脂质）及其受体 S1PR1，维持内皮屏障完整并引导免疫细胞迁移。）

Full citation

Cigana C, et al. Role of S1PR1 in Modulating Airway Epithelial Responses to Pseudomonas aeruginosa in Cystic Fibrosis. Pathogens. 2025. PMID 41305383; PMC PMC12655217; DOI 10.3390/pathogens14110000.

Methods / tools to lift

  • QTL candidate → mechanistic follow-up — take a gene from a mouse survival QTL and test it in a human CF cell model.
  • CRISPR/Cas9 knockout in a matched CFTR-deficient/corrected pair — isolate the gene's effect with and without CFTR by holding background constant except for CFTR.
  • Context-dependent readout design — same edit in two backgrounds reveals gene×background interaction.
  • Immunolocalization as a trafficking readout — perinuclear vs diffuse distinguishes a surface receptor from one stuck in the ER/Golgi.
  • Multiple-testing discipline — Šidák (IL-8) and two-way ANOVA + Bonferroni (viability).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves CF modifier catalog + summary-stats colocalization.

mouse-QTL-to-human-modifier mapper (S–M): take the 31-gene Collaborative Cross QTL interval → map each gene to its human ortholog → cross-reference against public human CF lung-function/modifier GWAS summary stats + lung co-expression → emit a ranked shortlist of which QTL genes have independent human support, feeding colocalization prioritization.

What to verify first

  • Causal direction (human tissue): low S1PR1 in end-stage CF lung could be consequence, not cause; seek early-stage/non-explant tissue.
  • Knockout vs natural variant: a 550-bp deletion is a full LoF, not the graded regulatory variant the mouse QTL implies; test a subtler change.
  • Model limits: immortalized cell lines + only 5 tissue donors; needs primary cells and a larger, earlier cohort.
  • Locus resolution: S1PR1 is 1 of 31 QTL genes; not fine-mapped against neighbors in humans — the human causal gene at that locus is still open.
  • No registry internals: external mouse/cell/public-tissue work only.

Gap-map / catalog

  • CLOSES: adds S1PR1 as a candidate CF modifier in the infection/inflammation domain, with mechanism (ER-stress-linked mislocalization) and a CFTR-context-dependent functional effect (worse viability/cytotoxicity + higher IL-8 only in CFTR-deficient cells); links CF to a shared low-S1PR1/high-inflammation pattern also seen in IPF.
  • LEAVES OPEN: causal direction in human lung; whether a realistic (non-knockout) S1PR1 variant reproduces the effect; independent-cohort/primary-cell replication; human fine-mapping of the 31-gene locus; any gene–gene interaction with specific CFTR variant classes beyond "CFTR-deficient vs corrected."

Read full transcript (27 turns · ~2039 words)

ALEX Track C today is a CF modifier-gene paper: "Role of S1PR1 in Modulating Airway Epithelial Responses to Pseudomonas aeruginosa in Cystic Fibrosis," by Cigana and colleagues, in Pathogens, 2025, out of the San Raffaele group. 今天的 Track C 是一篇 CF 修饰基因论文：Cigana 等人 2025 年发表在《Pathogens》上、来自 San Raffaele 团队的《S1PR1 在囊性纤维化中调节气道上皮对铜绿假单胞菌反应的作用》。 We're reading it because it names a specific candidate modifier — S1PR1 — and, more interestingly, shows its effect only really appears in the CFTR-deficient background. 我们读它，是因为它点名了一个具体的候选修饰基因——S1PR1——而且更有意思的是，它显示这个基因的效应其实只在 CFTR 缺陷的背景下才真正显现。 That gene-in-context idea is the heart of the episode. 这个"基因依情境而定"的思想，正是本集的核心。

JORDAN Start me at the beginning. 从头给我讲起。 What is a modifier gene, and what is S1PR1? 什么是修饰基因，S1PR1 又是什么？

ALEX A modifier gene is a gene other than CFTR that shapes how severe CF disease is in a given person. 修饰基因 (modifier gene) 是指 CFTR 之外的、能影响某个人 CF 病情严重程度的基因。 Two people with the exact same CFTR mutation can have wildly different lung outcomes, and modifiers are part of why. 两个携带完全相同 CFTR 突变的人，肺部结局可能天差地别，而修饰基因是原因之一。 S1PR1 stands for sphingosine-1-phosphate receptor 1. S1PR1 代表 1-磷酸鞘氨醇受体 1 (sphingosine-1-phosphate receptor 1)。 It's a GPCR — a G-protein-coupled receptor, the large family of cell-surface receptors that sense an outside signal and relay it inward. 它是一个 GPCR（G 蛋白偶联受体，一大类细胞表面受体，负责感知外部信号并把它传递到细胞内部）。 The signal it senses is sphingosine-1-phosphate, or S1P, a bioactive lipid molecule that cells use for messaging. 它感知的信号是 1-磷酸鞘氨醇，即 S1P，一种细胞用来传递信息的生物活性脂质分子。 S1PR1 is best known for keeping the barrier between blood vessels and tissue tight, and for guiding immune-cell traffic, and it's expressed in endothelial cells, epithelial cells, and several immune cell types. S1PR1 最为人所知的是维持血管与组织之间屏障的紧密，以及引导免疫细胞的迁移，它在内皮细胞、上皮细胞和多种免疫细胞中都有表达。

JORDAN And Pseudomonas aeruginosa — that's the CF lung bug? 还有铜绿假单胞菌——那是 CF 肺里的那种细菌？

ALEX Yes — Pseudomonas aeruginosa is the dominant chronic pathogen in CF airways and a major driver of decline. 是的——铜绿假单胞菌 (Pseudomonas aeruginosa) 是 CF 气道里最主要的慢性病原体，也是病情恶化的一个主因。 One more term you'll hear: exoproducts, which are the secreted molecules — toxins, enzymes, signaling factors — that the bacterium releases, as opposed to the whole live bug. 还有一个你会听到的词：外分泌产物 (exoproducts)，指细菌释放出来的分泌性分子——毒素、酶、信号因子——而不是整只活的细菌。 The study stimulates cells with those exoproducts to mimic infection. 这项研究用这些外分泌产物去刺激细胞，以模拟感染。 And IL-8 — interleukin-8 — is the cytokine they track, because it's the main signal that recruits neutrophils, the immune cells whose over-activity does much of the collateral lung damage in CF. 而 IL-8——白介素 8 (interleukin-8)——是他们追踪的细胞因子，因为它是招募中性粒细胞的主要信号，而中性粒细胞的过度活跃在 CF 里造成了大量的附带肺损伤。

JORDAN So where did S1PR1 even come from as a candidate? 那 S1PR1 作为一个候选基因最初是从哪来的？

ALEX This is a nice piece of the story. 这是故事里很漂亮的一段。 The group previously used the Collaborative Cross, a large panel of genetically diverse lab mice bred to capture a wide spread of natural genetic variation. 该团队此前用了 Collaborative Cross（协作杂交），一大套基因多样化的实验小鼠品系，专门培育出来以捕捉广泛的天然遗传变异。 Across that panel, survival after respiratory Pseudomonas infection was heritable — it varied with genetics. 在这套品系里，呼吸道铜绿假单胞菌感染后的存活率是可遗传的——它随基因型而变化。 They mapped a quantitative trait locus — a QTL, a stretch of genome statistically linked to a trait — that contained 31 protein-coding genes, and S1PR1 sat inside it. 他们定位到一个数量性状位点 (quantitative trait locus)——即 QTL，一段在统计上与某性状相关联的基因组区域——其中包含 31 个蛋白编码基因，而 S1PR1 就落在里面。 So S1PR1 is a candidate hauled out of a mouse genetic screen, and this paper is the follow-up asking: does it actually do something in a human CF context? 所以 S1PR1 是从小鼠遗传筛选里捞出来的一个候选基因，而这篇论文是后续追问：它在人类 CF 情境下是否真的起作用？ For context, S1PR1 joins a modifier landscape that already includes genes like TGFB1, involved in inflammation and fibrosis, IL8 in cytokine signaling, EDNRA in vascular tone, and EHF, a transcription factor. 作为背景，S1PR1 加入的这个修饰基因图景里，已经有像 TGFB1（参与炎症和纤维化）、IL8（细胞因子信号）、EDNRA（血管张力）以及 EHF（一个转录因子）这样的基因。 What makes S1PR1 interesting is that it lands squarely in the host–pathogen and inflammation axis rather than the structural-remodeling side. 让 S1PR1 有意思的地方在于，它稳稳地落在宿主–病原体和炎症这条轴上，而不是结构重塑那一侧。

JORDAN And S1PR1's day job — the barrier and immune-traffic role — is that why it fits the inflammation story? 而 S1PR1 的本职工作——屏障和免疫迁移的角色——是它契合炎症叙事的原因吗？

ALEX Exactly. 正是。 S1PR1 signaling normally keeps the endothelial barrier — the lining between blood and tissue — tight, and when that signaling fails, barrier permeability rises and inflammation gets amplified. S1PR1 信号通常让内皮屏障——血液与组织之间的衬里——保持紧密，而当这个信号失效时，屏障通透性升高、炎症被放大。 The authors also note that S1PR1 is downregulated in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, another chronic lung disease, so a low-S1PR1, high-inflammation pattern isn't unique to CF. 作者还指出，S1PR1 在特发性肺纤维化 (idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis) 这另一种慢性肺病里也是下调的，所以"低 S1PR1、高炎症"这个模式并非 CF 独有。 That cross-disease echo makes the mechanism more believable, even if it doesn't prove the CF-specific direction. 这种跨疾病的呼应让机制更可信一些，尽管它并不能证明 CF 特异的方向。

JORDAN How did they test that? 他们是怎么检验的？

ALEX They built human cell models with CRISPR/Cas9 gene editing. 他们用 CRISPR/Cas9 基因编辑构建了人类细胞模型。 They took two bronchial epithelial cell lines: IB3-1, which is CFTR-deficient — it carries F508del on one allele and W1282X on the other, both severe CF mutations — and C38, which is IB3-1 corrected with a working CFTR. 他们取了两个支气管上皮细胞系：IB3-1，它是 CFTR 缺陷的——一条等位基因带 F508del、另一条带 W1282X，两个都是严重的 CF 突变——以及 C38，它是用有功能的 CFTR 修正过的 IB3-1。 So the pair differs mainly in whether CFTR works, which lets them ask what S1PR1 does with and without CFTR. 于是这一对细胞主要的区别在于 CFTR 是否有功能，这让他们能追问：在有和没有 CFTR 的情况下，S1PR1 分别做了什么。 They designed guide RNAs against the single coding exon of S1PR1, delivered them with a lentiviral vector carrying Cas9 and a puromycin-resistance marker for selection, then flow-sorted single cells into clones and sequenced them. 他们针对 S1PR1 唯一的编码外显子设计了向导 RNA，用携带 Cas9 和嘌呤霉素抗性标记（用于筛选）的慢病毒载体递送，然后用流式分选把单个细胞分成克隆并测序。 One representative C38 knockout clone had a 550-base-pair deletion right after codon 13, so it's a genuine loss-of-function edit. 一个代表性的 C38 敲除克隆在第 13 号密码子之后有一个 550 碱基对的缺失，所以这是一个货真价实的功能缺失编辑。

ALEX Here's a design point I'd push on before we get to results. 在讲结果之前，有一个设计点我想先质疑。 A 550-base-pair CRISPR deletion is a hard knockout — it destroys the gene. 一个 550 碱基对的 CRISPR 缺失是一个彻底的敲除——它摧毁了这个基因。 But the natural modifier signal from the mouse QTL is presumably a subtle regulatory variant, not a full deletion. 但来自小鼠 QTL 的天然修饰信号，大概是一个细微的调控变异，而不是一个整段缺失。 So this models what happens when you remove S1PR1 entirely, which is a clean biology experiment, but it's a different thing from the graded human variation that started the whole hunt. 所以这模拟的是"完全去掉 S1PR1 会怎样"，是个干净的生物学实验，但它和当初引发整场搜寻的那种分级的人类变异，是两回事。 Worth holding that gap in mind. 值得把这个落差记在心里。

JORDAN Fair. 有道理。 So what did removing S1PR1 do? 那去掉 S1PR1 造成了什么？

ALEX First they looked at where the protein sits. 他们先看蛋白待在哪里。 In the CFTR-corrected C38 cells, normal S1PR1 is diffuse throughout the cell, as a working surface receptor should be. 在 CFTR 修正过的 C38 细胞里，正常的 S1PR1 弥散分布在整个细胞里，就像一个有功能的表面受体应有的样子。 In the mutant, it collapses into a perinuclear location — clustered around the nucleus in compartments like the Golgi or endoplasmic reticulum — which is the signature of a protein that never made it to the surface. 在突变体里，它塌缩到核周 (perinuclear) 的位置——聚集在核附近的区室里，比如高尔基体或内质网 (endoplasmic reticulum)——这是一个"从未到达细胞表面"的蛋白的标志。 And strikingly, even wild-type S1PR1 in the CFTR-deficient IB3-1 cells already showed perinuclear accumulation. 而且引人注目的是，即便是 CFTR 缺陷的 IB3-1 细胞里的野生型 S1PR1，也已经显示出核周堆积。 That's a hint that a broken CFTR alone disturbs S1PR1's trafficking. 这暗示，单是一个坏掉的 CFTR，就已经扰乱了 S1PR1 的转运。

JORDAN Why would CFTR being broken affect a totally different protein? 为什么 CFTR 坏了会影响一个完全不同的蛋白？

ALEX The proposed mechanism is ER stress. 提出的机制是内质网应激。 F508del CFTR gets retained in the endoplasmic reticulum in an immature form and degraded rather than reaching the membrane. F508del 的 CFTR 会以一种不成熟的形式滞留在内质网里、被降解，而不是到达细胞膜。 That clogs and stresses the protein-maturation machinery, and the authors argue this collateral stress impairs the maturation of other proteins passing through — plausibly S1PR1 among them. 这会堵塞并压垮蛋白成熟的机器，作者认为这种附带的应激会损害其他经过该通路的蛋白的成熟——S1PR1 很可能就是其中之一。 So the CFTR defect and the S1PR1 defect aren't independent — the first partly creates the second. 所以 CFTR 缺陷和 S1PR1 缺陷并不是相互独立的——前者部分地造成了后者。 That's the mechanistic version of "gene in context." 这就是"基因依情境而定"的机制版本。

JORDAN And the functional readouts? 那功能读数呢？

ALEX They stimulated the cells with exoproducts from a clinical Pseudomonas strain called AA2, and measured viability and cytotoxicity by metabolic activity, plus IL-8 by an ELISA assay. 他们用一个叫 AA2 的临床铜绿假单胞菌菌株的外分泌产物刺激细胞，通过代谢活性测量存活率和细胞毒性，再用 ELISA 实验测 IL-8。 In the CFTR-deficient background, knocking out S1PR1 reduced viability and increased cytotoxicity even at baseline, and Pseudomonas exoproducts pushed that further down. 在 CFTR 缺陷的背景下，敲除 S1PR1 甚至在基线就降低了存活率、增加了细胞毒性，而铜绿假单胞菌的外分泌产物把这个进一步压低。 Crucially, the effect was clearest in the CFTR-mutant cells — the same S1PR1 knockout did much less in the CFTR-corrected background. 关键在于，效应在 CFTR 突变细胞里最为清晰——同样的 S1PR1 敲除，在 CFTR 修正过的背景下作用小得多。 And S1PR1 loss significantly enhanced IL-8 secretion in the CFTR-deficient cells, meaning a stronger neutrophil-recruiting inflammatory signal. 而 S1PR1 的缺失在 CFTR 缺陷细胞里显著增强了 IL-8 的分泌，也就是一个更强的、招募中性粒细胞的炎症信号。 So losing S1PR1 makes the CF-like cell both more fragile and more inflamed under bacterial challenge. 所以失去 S1PR1 让这个类 CF 的细胞在细菌攻击下既更脆弱、又更炎症。 That combination matters, because in CF it's precisely the excess neutrophil response that drives much of the self-inflicted airway damage — so a modifier that dials IL-8 up in the CF background is nudging exactly the wrong knob. 这个组合很重要，因为在 CF 里，恰恰是过度的中性粒细胞反应驱动了大量自我造成的气道损伤——所以一个在 CF 背景下把 IL-8 调高的修饰基因，正好拧动了那个错误的旋钮。 It's also a reminder that the readouts were done with statistical care — IL-8 comparisons used a Šidák correction and the viability comparisons a two-way analysis with a Bonferroni correction, both standard guards against reading too much into many pairwise tests. 这也提醒我们，这些读数在统计上是谨慎处理的——IL-8 的比较用了 Šidák 校正，存活率的比较用了双因素分析加 Bonferroni 校正，两者都是防止从大量两两比较里过度解读的标准护栏。

JORDAN Did they connect it to actual human lungs? 他们把它和真实的人类肺连起来了吗？

ALEX They did, modestly. 连了，但规模不大。 They ran immunohistochemistry on lung tissue from five people with CF, mostly F508del homozygous, versus non-CF lungs. 他们对五名 CF 患者（大多为 F508del 纯合）与非 CF 肺的肺组织做了免疫组化。 In non-CF lungs, S1PR1 protein was clearly present in the alveolar epithelium, the vascular endothelium, and bronchial epithelial cells. 在非 CF 肺里，S1PR1 蛋白清晰地存在于肺泡上皮、血管内皮和支气管上皮细胞中。 In the CF lungs, the signal was markedly reduced or nearly absent. 在 CF 肺里，信号明显减少甚至几乎消失。 That's consistent with the cell-model story — but I want to be careful about it, because it's exactly where the causal direction gets slippery. 这与细胞模型的说法一致——但我想在这点上格外小心，因为这正是因果方向变得滑不留手的地方。

JORDAN Say more about that. 展开说说。

ALEX The CF lungs they sampled are end-stage — these are explanted, severely diseased tissues. 他们取样的 CF 肺是终末期的——这些是移植时切除的、严重病变的组织。 So low S1PR1 in CF lung could be a cause of severe disease, or it could be a consequence of the tissue destruction and chronic inflammation that severe CF produces. CF 肺里 S1PR1 低，可能是严重病情的一个原因，也可能是严重 CF 所导致的组织破坏和慢性炎症的一个结果。 With five samples and no early-stage tissue, you can't separate those. 只有五个样本、又没有早期组织，你没法把这两者分开。 The cell model helps — there the CRISPR knockout is upstream by construction, so at least in the dish S1PR1 loss causes the worse phenotype. 细胞模型有帮助——在那里 CRISPR 敲除按构造就是上游的，所以至少在培养皿里，S1PR1 的缺失确实导致了更差的表型。 But the human-tissue correlation on its own is suggestive, not decisive. 但人类组织的相关性本身只是提示性的，而非决定性的。
