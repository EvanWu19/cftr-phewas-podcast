Ep4 (Track C — registry QC, 2 of 2): A prior-wave-anchored flagging rule, and how to check it without a gold standard

Dugravot, Sabia, Singh-Manoux and colleagues (2015, PLoS ONE) tackle outlier detection in the Whitehall II cognitive-aging cohort after staff noticed participants spilling over time limits during group-administered testing at the study's fourth wave. Their four-step procedure: (1) identify which specific tests show the non-adherence signature by comparing decline estimates from three waves vs. four, stratified by test site; (2) choose between a simple sociodemographic prediction model and a richer one with health behaviors/measures — the richer model fit better; (3) define an outlier as a studentized residual exceeding 2 SD, where the SD is deliberately estimated from the prior three waves only, never from the wave being screened, to avoid the outliers inflating their own detection threshold; (4) test the impact of exclusion on known risk-factor associations. Roughly 7% of observations were flagged across three affected tests, with no external gold standard — plausibility was established indirectly via a site-specific pattern, inconsistency with established practice-effect literature, and the fact that excluding outliers strengthened or newly revealed expected associations between age/diabetes and cognitive decline. The transferable method generalizes directly to anthropometric data. Buildable artifact: a Prior-Wave-Anchored Mixed-Model Residual Flagger.

Plain-English glossary

  • Data provenance / Kahn-framework vocabulary — the distinction between conformance (structural validity), plausibility (biological sensibility), and completeness as separate data-quality dimensions; non-adherence to protocol is a failure mode that can leave a value well-formed and individually plausible while still being wrong.（中文：数据来源/Kahn框架相关词汇——将符合性（结构有效性）、合理性（生物学上是否合乎常理）与完整性区分为不同的数据质量维度；"未遵循实验方案"是一种失效模式，它可能使某个数值格式规范、单独看也合理，却依然是错误的。）
  • Non-adherence to protocol — a deviation from the intended data-collection procedure (e.g. not respecting a time limit) that can produce individually well-formed, plausible-looking but erroneous values.（中文：未遵循实验方案，指偏离了既定的数据采集流程（例如未遵守时间限制），可能产生格式规范、看似合理却实际有误的数值。）
  • Practice/retest effect — the tendency for scores on a repeated test to improve over successive administrations due to growing familiarity, typically diminishing in magnitude at later waves.（中文：练习效应/重测效应，指在反复测试中，由于对测试越来越熟悉而导致成绩提升的趋势，这种提升通常会随着波次增加而逐渐减弱。）
  • Studentized residual — a residual (observed minus predicted value) divided by an estimate of its standard deviation, used as a scale-independent way to flag unusually large discrepancies.（中文：学生化残差，指一个残差（观察值减去预测值）除以其标准差的估计值，用作一种与量纲无关的方法，来标记异常大的偏差。）

Full citation

Dugravot A, Sabia S, Shipley MJ, Welch C, Kivimaki M, Singh-Manoux A. "Detection of Outliers Due to Participants' Non-Adherence to Protocol in a Longitudinal Study of Cognitive Decline." PLoS ONE. 2015. PMID: 26161552 | PMC4498688 | DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0132110

Methods / tools to lift

  • Prior-wave-anchored studentized residual — a flagging threshold's standard deviation drawn only from already-vetted prior data, never from the wave being screened, to avoid circularity.
  • Site/context-stratified signature detection — comparing estimates with and without a suspect wave, split by a plausible causal factor (test site here), to identify which specific measures are actually affected.
  • Known-association validity check — testing whether excluding flagged values moves known, independently-established associations in the expected direction, as a no-gold-standard sanity check.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation, primary — a prior-wave-anchored mixed-model residual rule that avoids using the screened wave's own data to set its detection threshold; (d) evaluation, secondary — the known-association check is a reusable no-gold-standard validity test for any flagging rule. Buildable artifact: a Prior-Wave-Anchored Mixed-Model Residual Flagger — fit a mixed-effects model predicting each visit's height/weight from the individual's own prior trajectory plus covariates, compute a studentized residual using an SD from prior waves only, flag beyond a threshold, then sanity-check by testing whether a known association strengthens as expected after exclusion. Difficulty: medium.

Error types covered / not covered: Covers within-individual implausible change relative to a model-based expectation, specifically tuned here to one direction (implausible improvement) matching this study's particular non-adherence mechanism; does not address unit conversion, digit transposition, decimal-shift, or carry-forward errors, and does not by itself distinguish a flagged value from a genuine real-world clinical event without the downstream association check.

What to verify first

  • Whether a one-directional flag (this paper's choice, tuned to its specific artifact) is appropriate for height/weight, where implausible drops and jumps are both plausible error mechanisms, unlike this cognitive-testing case.
  • How many prior waves are needed for a stable SD estimate, and how to handle individuals with few prior visits (a cold-start problem this cohort's up-to-three-prior-waves design didn't have to confront sharply).
  • Whether the prior-wave-anchoring scheme has been formally stress-tested with simulation for circularity risk, rather than only argued for conceptually.
  • Whether the "known-association" validity check requires pre-specifying the right association, and how that choice itself would need to be justified.

JORDAN Let's set up a couple of registry-provenance concepts before the method, since this paper is really a case study in a broader clinical-data-quality idea: non-adherence to protocol. 在讲方法之前，我们先铺垒几个与注册数据来源相关的概念，因为这篇论文本质上是"未遵循实验方案"这一更广泛的临床数据质量问题的一个案例研究。

ALEX Right — this sits in a Kahn-framework-adjacent space, if you know that vocabulary: data quality frameworks for EHR and registry data typically separate "conformance" — does the data look structurally valid — from "plausibility" — does the value make biological sense — from "completeness." 对，如果你熟悉这套词汇的话——这属于与Kahn框架相邻的概念空间：针对电子健康记录（EHR）和注册数据的质量框架，通常会把"符合性"（conformance，即数据在结构上是否有效）、"合理性"（plausibility，即数值在生物学上是否合乎常理）与"完整性"（completeness）区分开来。 Non-adherence to protocol is a distinct failure mode that doesn't fit cleanly into any one of those buckets — the data point can be perfectly well-formed, perfectly plausible on its own, and still be wrong, because the process that generated it didn't follow the intended procedure. 而"未遵循实验方案"是一种独特的失效模式，它并不能干净地归入上述任何一个类别——某个数据点可能格式完全规范，单独看也完全合理，但依然是错的，因为生成这个数据点的过程本身就没有遵循既定的程序。 That's a useful category to have in your head for registry work generally: some errors are visible in the value itself, and some are only visible if you know something about how the value was collected. 对于注册库相关的工作而言，这是一个值得牢记在心的有用分类：有些错误能在数值本身中被看出来，而有些错误只有当你了解数值的采集过程时才能被发现。

JORDAN So what actually happened in this study? 那么这项研究中究竟发生了什么？

ALEX The Whitehall II study has run a cognitive test battery across four waves — 1997-99, 2002-04, 2007-09, and 2012-13. Whitehall II研究在四个波次中进行了认知测试——分别是1997-99年、2002-04年、2007-09年和2012-13年。 Part of that battery is administered in a group setting at the clinic, with instructions played over headphones and a strict time limit per test — a ten-minute vocabulary test sandwiched between timed reasoning and verbal fluency tests. 其中部分测试是在诊所以小组形式进行的，指令通过耳机播放，每项测试都有严格的时间限制——一项十分钟的词汇测试，被安排在限时的推理测试和言语流畅性测试之间。 During the fourth wave, study staff noticed some participants weren't respecting those time boundaries — using leftover vocabulary-test time to keep working on reasoning or fluency items instead. 在第四波测试期间，研究人员注意到部分参与者并未遵守这些时间界限——他们利用词汇测试剩余的时间，继续完成推理或流畅性测试的题目。 That observed non-adherence is what triggered this entire outlier-detection exercise after the fact. 正是这种被观察到的未遵循方案行为，在事后促成了整个异常值检测工作的开展。

JORDAN And the four-step procedure they built in response? 那么他们为此构建的四步流程是什么？

ALEX Step one: figure out which specific tests actually show the signature of non-adherence, rather than assuming all of them do. 第一步：找出哪些具体的测试真正显示出了未遵循方案的特征，而不是假定所有测试都受到了影响。 They compared each test's annual rate of decline estimated from the first three waves against the rate estimated once the fourth wave was included, stratified by whether testing happened at the clinic or at home. 他们比较了每项测试基于前三波数据估算出的年度衰退速率，与纳入第四波数据后重新估算出的速率，并按测试地点是诊所还是家庭进行了分层。 Reasoning, phonemic fluency, and semantic fluency showed significantly slower apparent decline once wave four was added — but only among participants tested at the clinic in a group setting; memory and vocabulary, and anyone tested at home one-on-one, showed no such pattern. 推理、言语流畅性和语义流畅性在纳入第四波数据后，显示出的表观衰退速率明显变慢——但这种现象只出现在诊所以小组形式接受测试的参与者中；记忆力和词汇测试，以及任何在家中一对一接受测试的参与者，都没有表现出这种模式。 That site-specific pattern is itself strong indirect evidence pointing at the group-testing procedure as the cause, rather than, say, a real cohort-wide slowdown in cognitive decline. 这种与测试地点相关的特定模式本身就是一个有力的间接证据，指向了群体测试流程本身是问题的原因，而不是说，比如，整个队列的认知衰退速度真的出现了普遍性的减缓。

JORDAN Step two was choosing a prediction model? 第二步是选择一个预测模型？

ALEX Right — build a model to predict each person's expected wave-four score from everything known before that wave. 对——构建一个模型，根据该波次之前已知的所有信息，来预测每个人在第四波测试中理应得到的分数。 They compared a simple linear mixed model using only sociodemographic covariates — age, sex, education, and so on, with random intercept and slope terms to account for repeated measures on the same person — against a more complex model that added health behaviors, health measures, test site, and retest count. 他们比较了一个简单的线性混合模型——仅使用社会人口学协变量，比如年龄、性别、教育水平等，并加入随机截距和随机斜率项，以考虑同一人身上重复测量数据之间的相关性——与一个更复杂的模型，后者额外加入了健康行为、健康测量指标、测试地点以及重测次数。 The complex model produced consistently smaller mean residuals across all three affected tests, meaning it fit the real data better, so that's the one they committed to for defining outliers. 这个更复杂的模型在所有三项受影响的测试中，始终产生更小的平均残差，这意味着它对真实数据的拟合更好，因此他们最终选用这个模型来定义异常值。

JORDAN And step three is where the actual flagging rule lives. 第三步才是真正的标记规则所在。

ALEX This is the part worth being precise about. An outlier is defined as a studentized residual — the gap between a person's observed and predicted wave-four score, divided by a standard deviation — exceeding a threshold of 2. 这一部分值得精确地讲清楚。异常值被定义为一个学生化残差（studentized residual）——即一个人在第四波测试中观察值与预测值之间的差距，除以一个标准差——超过了2这个阈值。 Here's the deliberate design choice: that standard deviation isn't computed from the wave-four data being tested — it's computed from the residuals of the exact same prediction model applied retrospectively to the first three waves. 这里有一个刻意的设计选择：这个标准差并不是从正在被检验的第四波数据中计算出来的——而是把完全相同的预测模型，回溯性地应用于前三波数据，从这些数据的残差中计算出来的。 That matters because if you computed the SD from the wave containing the outliers themselves, the outliers would inflate the very standard deviation used to detect them, making the threshold too lenient. 这一点很重要，因为如果你是从包含异常值本身的那一波数据中计算标准差，异常值就会使这个用于检测它们自身的标准差被抬高，从而使阈值变得过于宽松。 Anchoring the SD on prior, presumably clean waves avoids that circularity. 把标准差锚定在先前、推定为干净的波次数据上，就能避免这种循环论证。 And one more deliberate choice: they only flagged large positive residuals — unusual improvement — not large negative ones, because in this specific context a big decline is plausible on its own (neurological disease exists) while a big improvement, after accounting for everything the model knows, is the implausible direction given the non-adherence mechanism they suspected. 还有一个刻意的选择：他们只标记大的正向残差——即异常的成绩提升——而不标记大的负向残差，因为在这个具体情境下，大幅度的成绩下降本身是合理的（确实存在神经系统疾病这种可能性），而在模型已经考虑了所有已知因素之后，出现大幅度的成绩提升，才是基于他们所怀疑的未遵循方案机制而显得不合理的那个方向。

JORDAN I want to push on that last point. ALEX That's a fair and important caveat, and it generalizes directly to anything we'd build from this: the choice of which direction, or both directions, to flag has to be justified by the specific error mechanism you're hunting for, not applied as a default. 这是一个公平且重要的提醒，它也能直接推广到我们可能基于此构建的任何东西上：应该标记哪个方向、还是两个方向都标记，必须根据你所要排查的具体错误机制来论证，而不能默认一概而论。 There's a second thing worth being skeptical of too — using a model-based residual to both detect and effectively define what counts as a "real" versus "erroneous" observation carries some circularity risk in principle, since the model itself was estimated from data that includes some of the same people. 还有第二点也值得保持怀疑——用一个基于模型的残差，同时承担"检测"异常值和实际上"定义"何为"真实"值与"错误"值这两项任务，原则上存在一定的循环论证风险，因为模型本身也是从包含同一批人的数据中估计出来的。 Anchoring the SD on prior waves mitigates that, but doesn't eliminate it entirely — it's the kind of thing you'd want to stress-test with simulation before trusting blindly on a new variable. 把标准差锚定在先前波次的数据上可以缓解这个问题，但并不能完全消除它——这是那种在盲目信任它应用于新变量之前，你会想要用模拟方法去压力测试一番的事情。

JORDAN Step four, then — what did they actually find? 那么第四步——他们究竟发现了什么？

ALEX Using the complex model, this flagged roughly 7% of observations as outliers across the three affected tests — 434 of 5,516 for reasoning, 377 of 5,513 for phonemic fluency, 370 of 5,516 for semantic fluency. 使用这个复杂模型，在三项受影响的测试中，大约7%的观测值被标记为异常值——推理测试中5516人中有434人，言语流畅性测试中5513人中有377人，语义流畅性测试中5516人中有370人。 Critically, there was no gold standard here — the researchers never directly observed who did or didn't violate the time limit for any individual. 关键的是，这里并没有任何金标准——研究人员从未直接观察到究竟是哪个具体的人违反了时间限制。 Their evidence for "this flagging rule is catching real errors, not real extreme values" is entirely indirect, and it's a genuinely elegant evaluation-without-gold-standard design: outliers weren't distinguishable from others by age, sex, or education in any systematic way, which argues against a demographic confound; outliers showed a cognitive trajectory that reversed — declining over the first three waves, then improving at wave four — which contradicts the established practice-effect literature predicting that retest improvement should shrink over successive waves, not suddenly appear larger at the fourth sitting; and, most convincingly, when they excluded the flagged outliers and re-estimated the known, literature-established associations of age and diabetes with cognitive decline, those associations got stronger and, for diabetes and phonemic fluency, newly significant — exactly what you'd expect if the outliers were artificially masking real risk-factor effects, and not what you'd expect if they'd just thrown away genuine extreme values. 他们证明"这条标记规则确实捕捉到了真实的错误、而不是真实的极端值"的证据，全部是间接性的，而这恰恰是一种相当精妙的、在没有金标准情况下进行评估的设计：异常值在年龄、性别或教育水平上，并没有表现出任何系统性的、能与其他人区分开来的特征，这就排除了人口学混杂因素这一解释；异常值表现出的认知轨迹发生了逆转——在前三波中持续下降，却在第四波中出现提升——这与已有的"练习效应"（practice effect）文献相矛盾，后者预测重测带来的成绩提升应该随着波次增加而逐渐减小，而不是在第四次测试时突然变大；而最具说服力的是，当他们剔除被标记的异常值、重新估计年龄和糖尿病与认知衰退之间这两个已被文献确立的关联时，这些关联变得更强，而且就糖尿病与言语流畅性而言，关联从原本的无统计学意义变成了新出现的显著关联——如果异常值确实在人为地掩盖真实的风险因素效应，这正是你会预期看到的结果；而如果剔除的只是真实的极端值，你本不该看到这样的结果。

JORDAN That third piece — testing against known risk-factor associations — feels like it's doing double duty as a method, not just a result. 第三点——用已知的风险因素关联来做检验——感觉它本身就是一种方法，而不仅仅是一个结果。

ALEX Exactly right, and that's the piece I'd pull out as a distinct, reusable technique on its own: when you don't have ground truth for whether a flagging rule is catching genuine errors, test whether removing the flagged values pushes known, independently-established associations in the expected direction. 完全正确，这正是我想把它单独挑出来、作为一项独立且可重复使用的技术的原因：当你没有金标准来判断一条标记规则究竟是不是真的捕捉到了真实的错误时，可以检验剔除被标记的数值之后，是否让已知的、独立确立的关联朝着预期的方向发生变化。 That's a general-purpose sanity check for any flagging rule, anthropometric or otherwise. 这是一种适用于任何标记规则的通用合理性检验方法，无论是针对人体测量数据还是其他数据。

JORDAN So, does this change the CFFPR outlier pipeline? 那么，这会改变CFFPR的异常值处理流程吗？
