Plain-English glossary

Covariate balance / standardized difference of means (SDM) — A measure comparing baseline characteristics between two treatment groups, scaled to their pooled standard deviation; a maximum SDM above roughly 0.10 is conventionally read as a signal of possible confounding.（中文："协变量平衡/标准化均数差",衡量两治疗组基线特征差异并按合并标准差标度;最大标准化差超过约0.10通常被视为潜在混杂的信号。）

Empirical calibration — Using the observed distribution of negative control estimates to adjust (widen or shift) an effect estimate's confidence interval and p-value so they reflect the study's actual, empirically observed error rate rather than a nominal one.（中文："经验校准",利用阴性对照效应估计的观测分布,调整效应估计的置信区间与P值,使其反映研究实际观测到的误差率,而非名义误差率。）

Empirical equipoise / preference score — A preference score is a propensity score rescaled to the prevalence of each treatment in the population; a patient is in empirical equipoise when that score falls roughly between 0.3 and 0.7, meaning genuine uncertainty about which treatment they would receive based on observed characteristics.（中文："经验均势/偏好评分",偏好评分是按各治疗在人群中患病率重新标度的倾向评分;当该评分大致落在0.3至0.7之间时,患者处于"经验均势"状态,表示根据可观测特征,其接受何种治疗存在真实的不确定性。）

Expected absolute systematic error (EASE) — A single summary metric of a study design's systematic error, computed by integrating the absolute value of the empirical null distribution across a large panel of negative control outcomes.（中文："期望绝对系统误差（EASE）",对研究设计系统误差的单一汇总指标,通过对大量阴性对照结局的经验零分布取绝对值积分计算得出。）

Generalizability (max SDM) — A standardized-difference-of-means comparison between the analytic (final, adjusted) population and the target population a study intends to generalize to; large differences suggest the analytic sample may not represent the population of interest.（中文："外推性（最大标准化差）",比较分析（最终调整后）人群与研究拟外推目标人群之间的标准化均数差;差异较大提示分析样本可能无法代表目标人群。）

Minimum detectable relative risk (MDRR) — The smallest relative risk a study's actual sample size and event count could reliably detect at 80% power; because observational sample size is fixed after the fact, this replaces a prospective power calculation.（中文："最小可检测相对风险（MDRR）",在80%把握度下,研究实际样本量与事件数能可靠检测到的最小相对风险;由于观察性研究的样本量事后已固定,该指标替代了前瞻性把握度计算。）

Negative control outcome — An exposure-outcome pair where the true causal effect is known or strongly believed to be null; applying a study's exact design to a negative control outcome reveals bias in the design itself rather than in the outcome.（中文："阴性对照结局",一种暴露-结局配对,其真实因果效应已知或被高度确信为无效应;将研究的完全相同设计应用于阴性对照结局,可揭示设计本身存在的偏倚,而非结局本身的问题。）

Pre-specification / unblinding (of diagnostics) — Declaring diagnostic thresholds before seeing study results, then either interpreting diagnostics before unblinding effect estimates, or only unblinding results from analyses that pass those pre-set thresholds — intended to prevent post hoc rationalization of a failed assumption.（中文："预先设定/揭盲（诊断指标）",在看到研究结果之前预先设定诊断阈值,随后要么在揭盲效应估计前先解读诊断结果,要么只揭盲通过预设阈值的分析结果——目的是防止对未通过的假设进行事后合理化解释。）
