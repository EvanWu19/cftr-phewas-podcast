Show notes — Track A — 2026-08-06

When Standard Newborn Screening Isn't Enough: Diagnostic Challenges in the Age of Globalization — Ortuzar Menendez M, et al. (Hospital Universitario de Navarra, Spain). Clinical Case Reports, 2026.

Blurb

A short clinical case report from Hospital Universitario de Navarra in Spain: a full-term baby boy, born to parents from a small Andean village in Ecuador, tests positive on newborn CF screening, but the standard targeted CFTR mutation panel — built around variants common in Caucasian European populations — comes back completely negative. The clinical team keeps following him anyway, pushed by borderline sweat chloride results and a suspicion of consanguinity, through a third and fourth sweat test before finally escalating to full CFTR gene sequencing. That sequencing finds a homozygous c.1826A>G (p.His609Arg) missense variant, previously reported only in patients of Ecuadorian-Andean ancestry — a variant the Caucasian-tuned panel was never built to catch. The baby is diagnosed with atypical, pancreatic-sufficient CF. For us this lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation: the buildable move is a panel-coverage gap-finder that cross-checks any targeted screening panel against ancestry-stratified CF variant frequency data before the next case forces the question.

Plain-English glossary

  • IRT (immunoreactive trypsinogen) — a pancreatic enzyme precursor measured in newborn blood; it rises early in CF before other symptoms appear, and is the first-tier newborn CF screening test.（中文：免疫反应性胰蛋白酶原——一种在新生儿血液中检测的胰腺酶前体，在 CF 患儿出现其他症状之前就会升高，是新生儿 CF 筛查的第一层检测。）
  • Sweat chloride test — measures chloride ion concentration in sweat; elevated because CFTR fails to reabsorb chloride in the sweat duct. Above ~60 mmol/L is diagnostic, under 30 is normal, and the range between is indeterminate.（中文：汗氯测试——测量汗液中氯离子浓度；CF 患者因 CFTR 无法在汗腺导管中重新吸收氯离子而升高。约 60 mmol/L 以上具有诊断意义，低于 30 为正常，中间为不确定区间。）
  • Targeted mutation panel — a fixed list of the most common disease-causing CFTR variants in the population the panel was designed for, used for genetic confirmation instead of sequencing the whole gene.（中文：靶向突变检测组合——一份固定的清单，收录该检测组合设计所针对人群中最常见的致病性 CFTR 变异，用于基因确诊而非对整个基因测序。）
  • Full-gene sequencing — reads every base of the CFTR gene, catching rare or population-specific variants a targeted panel was never designed to include.（中文：全基因测序——读取 CFTR 基因的每一个碱基，能捕捉靶向检测组合从未纳入的罕见或特定人群变异。）
  • HGVS notation — the standardized way of writing a variant, with a DNA-level form (e.g. c.1826A>G) and a protein-level form (e.g. p.His609Arg).（中文：HGVS——变异命名的标准化书写规范，包含 DNA 层面写法（如 c.1826A>G）和蛋白质层面写法（如 p.His609Arg）。）
  • Missense variant — a DNA change that swaps one amino acid for another without truncating the protein.（中文：错义变异——一种使蛋白质中一个氨基酸被替换为另一个、但不截短蛋白质的 DNA 改变。）
  • Homozygous / consanguinity — homozygous means the same variant is inherited from both parents; this is more likely when parents share recent ancestry (consanguinity), as in this case's isolated Andean village.（中文：纯合／近亲关系——纯合指同一变异从父母双方各遗传一份；当父母有较近的共同血统（近亲关系）时更易发生，如本病例中偏远的安第斯村庄。）
  • Pancreatic sufficiency / atypical CF — pancreatic sufficiency (confirmed by fecal elastase >200 μg/g) means the pancreas still functions adequately despite a CF-causing genotype; combined with a variant usually linked to typical (pancreatic-insufficient) CF, this combination is classified as atypical CF.（中文：胰腺功能正常／不典型 CF——胰腺功能正常（粪便弹性蛋白酶 >200 μg/g 确认）指尽管携带致病基因型，胰腺仍能正常发挥功能；当这种表现与通常关联典型（胰腺功能不全型）CF 的变异同时出现时，被归类为不典型 CF。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Escalation pathway to full CFTR gene sequencing — triggered by borderline sweat chloride results plus ancestry-based suspicion of consanguinity, after a negative targeted panel.
  • Fecal elastase testing — used to confirm pancreatic sufficiency and classify the atypical-CF phenotype.
  • Dual HGVS notation reporting (c./p.) — reporting both the DNA-level and protein-level variant description for clinical clarity.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation & harmonization. MVP (paste-ready): Take any targeted CFTR mutation panel's variant list (e.g. a national newborn-screening panel) plus an ancestry-stratified CF-causing-variant frequency table (gnomAD v4, biobank catalogs); for a target ancestry group, output every variant with meaningful allele frequency in that ancestry that the panel doesn't cover at all, ranked and cited. Input: panel variant list + target ancestry. Difficulty: S–M.

What to verify first

  • Single-case classification confidence — p.His609Arg's pathogenicity rests on a small number of prior case reports; check its current CFTR2/ClinVar classification directly rather than assuming from this paper.
  • Phenotype variability is a one-data-point observation — the pancreatic-sufficient, atypical presentation here versus prior "typical CF" reports for the same variant can't be resolved by a single case (real variability vs. modifier effect vs. short follow-up).
  • Ancestry-catalog resolution — verify how current/complete ancestry-stratified frequency catalogs are for Indigenous Andean populations specifically; coarse "Latino/Admixed" biobank buckets may still miss a variant this locally concentrated.
