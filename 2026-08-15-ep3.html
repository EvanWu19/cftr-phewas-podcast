How did this episode land for you?

Blurb

Lotspeich et al. (2026, JAMIA Open) test whether large language models can safely expand a clinician-written "roadmap" of auxiliary ICD-10 diagnoses used to recover missing electronic health record values for the allostatic load index (ALI), a 10-biomarker computable phenotype. Building on prior work where expert chart review recovered missing ALI components at real but expensive cost (100 hours for 100 patients), the team used the R ellmer package with Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash to propose expanded search-term roadmaps under two prompt variants (no-context "baseline" and context-seeded), then had two clinicians adjudicate the LLM's additions before deployment. Across 100 chart-reviewed patients with 413 missing data points, expert review recovered 49 (12%); the adjudicated LLM roadmap recovered 73 (18%) — more than experts, less than the unadjudicated LLM variants (83-89), a deliberate balance of coverage against clinical relevance. Scaled to the full 1000-patient sample, the algorithm recovered 789 of 4009 missing components (20%), raising the per-patient median of non-missing components from 6 to 7 — reaching all 1000 patients rather than the 100 who underwent chart review. Lands primarily on (c) the human review budget: the design pattern of LLM-proposed candidates gated by a bounded, one-time clinician-adjudication step (rather than per-patient review) is directly transferable to expanding a candidate outlier-flagging rule set.

Plain-English Glossary (alphabetical)

  • ALI (allostatic load index) — a computable phenotype capturing cumulative physiological "wear and tear" from chronic stress across cardiovascular, metabolic, and inflammatory systems, computed from 10 biomarkers each discretized against a clinical threshold. ALI（过载负荷指数）——一种计算表型，捕捉慢性压力在心血管、代谢、炎症系统上累积造成的生理"耗损"，由10项生物标志物按临床阈值离散化计算得出。
  • Chart review (source document verification) — manually comparing an analytical dataset (e.g., extracted EHR data) against clinical source documents (e.g., electronic charts) to identify differences; accurate but resource-intensive and doesn't scale. 病历审查（源文件核实）——手动比对分析数据集（如提取的EHR数据）与临床源文件（如电子病历），以发现差异；准确但耗费资源，难以规模化。
  • ellmer (R package) — an R package enabling programmatic "tool-calling" interaction with LLM APIs, used here to loop prompts against Gemini 2.5 Flash. ellmer（R包）——一个支持以程序化"工具调用"方式与LLM API交互的R包，本文用它循环调用Gemini 2.5 Flash的提示词。
  • ICD-10 — International Classification of Diseases, 10th revision; the structured diagnosis-code vocabulary this paper's roadmap logic matches against. ICD-10——国际疾病分类第10版；本文路线图逻辑用于匹配的结构化诊断编码词汇表。
  • Roadmap — a clinician-curated list of auxiliary diagnoses that can substitute for a missing biomarker (e.g., a sepsis diagnosis implying "unhealthy" inflammatory status when C-reactive protein is missing); the methodological backbone this paper expands with LLMs. 路线图——由临床专家整理的辅助诊断清单，可在生物标志物缺失时作为替代（例如脓毒症诊断暗示"不健康"的炎症状态，用于替代缺失的C反应蛋白）；本文用LLM扩展的方法论支柱。
  • Weiskopf and Weng data quality dimensions — a standard EHR data-quality vocabulary (completeness, correctness, concordance, plausibility, currency) this paper's "validation" and "recovery" goals map onto (concordance and completeness respectively). Weiskopf与Weng数据质量维度——一套标准EHR数据质量词汇（完整性、正确性、一致性、合理性、时效性），本文的"验证"与"恢复"两个目标分别对应其中的一致性与完整性。

Full Citation

JAMIA Open. 2026;9(1):ooag080. doi: 10.1093/jamiaopen/ooag080. PMID: 42237983. PMCID: PMC13228140. Code/data: github.com/lucymcgowan/ehr-llm-validation.

Methods/Tools to Lift

  • Clinician-written "roadmap" of auxiliary diagnosis search terms as the starting candidate-generation rule set.
  • LLM prompt-loop with self-consistency sampling (20 independent runs per variant) taking the superset of proposed terms; matching count plateaus after ~10 iterations.
  • Two prompt variants (no-context "baseline" vs. context-seeded with clinician examples) as a controlled ablation of prompt design.
  • Bounded, one-time clinician-adjudication step gating LLM-proposed additions before deployment — a per-roadmap cost, not a per-patient cost.
  • Multiwave adaptive sampling strategy for selecting the chart-review validation subsample (balanced case-control + largest-residual selection).
  • Downstream-model reproduction check: re-running a prior association analysis on algorithm-recovered vs. chart-review-recovered data to confirm the substantive conclusion survives.

Where It Lands + Buildable Artifact

Pipeline stage: primarily (c) the human review budget — the LLM-proposed/clinician-adjudicated roadmap pattern is a template for shrinking the human-hours cost of expanding a rule set without eliminating expert oversight, and the cost does not scale with the number of patients reviewed. Secondarily touches (a) candidate generation, since the LLM's role is proposing candidates a human then gates rather than either fully manual rule-writing or ungated LLM output. No claims made about the registry's actual current missing-data patterns or roadmap logic.

Buildable artifact: roadmap-expansion-harness — given any clinician-written starting rule set (search terms, thresholds, flag criteria), prompt an LLM for candidate expansions using a no-context and a context-seeded variant, log the plateau curve across repeated self-consistency runs, and package the superset into a human-adjudication queue with provenance (new vs. original) flagged. MVP: prompt-loop script + review-tracking spreadsheet. Difficulty: S-M.

What to Verify First

  • Single EHR, single health system, single LLM provider's free tier (Gemini 2.5 Flash) — behavior may not transfer to other models; the authors themselves could not fully disentangle prompting differences from model improvement over time in earlier pilot runs.
  • This method addresses missingness, not correctness or plausibility — extending to other data-quality dimensions is explicitly named as future work, not tested here.
  • Both algorithmic recovery and chart review pulled diagnosis codes from anywhere in the study period (no lookback window) — the authors themselves flag this as an open direction that could change the numbers.
  • The clinician-adjudication step, while bounded and non-per-patient, is still a real human-hours cost — not literally free scaling.
  • Nothing here should be read as a claim about how any registry's own missing-data patterns or roadmap logic currently work.

Pipeline Stage

(c) Human review budget — primary. (a) Candidate generation — secondary.

Error Types Covered / Not Covered

Covers: missingness (absence of a value) recoverable via a clinically-plausible auxiliary-diagnosis inference chain.

Does not cover: incorrect but non-missing values (this paper reports these as rare and out of scope); implausible-value/outlier detection; unit-conversion, carry-forward, duplicate, or transcription errors; any data-quality dimension beyond completeness (and, secondarily, concordance via the earlier chart-review validation step).
