Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): Calibrating JAG1 functional data into ACMG/AMP evidence weights

Hayeck et al. (2026, Am J Hum Genet) take an existing multiplexed assay of variant effects (MAVE) dataset for JAG1 — the gene behind Alagille syndrome — and run it through maveLLR, a method that converts a raw functional score into a per-variant log-likelihood ratio of pathogenicity (LLRp), calibrated against the ACMG/AMP Bayesian evidence-weight scale. The payoff is concrete: definitive evidence coverage of the reference variant set rises from 62% to 81%, the number of variants in the full library carrying pathogenic-direction evidence rises from 486 to 610, and applying the new weights to a retrospective cohort of 29 JAG1 VUS cases reclassifies six (21%) to likely pathogenic or pathogenic. The gene isn't CFTR, but the calibration recipe — reference-set KDE densities, per-variant LLRp, gene-specific threshold mapping — is directly portable to any CFTR functional assay with a reference set of CFTR2-classified variants. Buildable artifact: a CFTR Functional-Score LLRp Calibrator script.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP — the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology framework for classifying DNA variants into benign, likely benign, VUS, likely pathogenic, or pathogenic using tiered evidence strength.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会/分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类指南，将变异按证据强度分级归入良性、可能良性、意义不明、可能致病或致病五类。）
  • Alagille syndrome (ALGS) — an autosomal-dominant multisystem disease caused primarily by JAG1 variants, affecting the liver, heart, eyes, skeleton, and face.（中文：一种主要由JAG1变异引起的常染色体显性遗传多系统疾病，累及肝脏、心脏、眼睛、骨骼和面部。）
  • EGF-like domain — a small protein structural module stabilized by disulfide bonds between conserved cysteine residues; loss of those cysteines is a recurrent disease mechanism.（中文：一种由保守半胱氨酸残基间二硫键稳定的小型蛋白结构模块；这些半胱氨酸的缺失是一种反复出现的致病机制。）
  • Haploinsufficiency — a disease mechanism where having only one functional copy of a gene (instead of two) is not enough for normal function.（中文：一种致病机制，指仅有一个功能正常的基因拷贝（而非两个）不足以维持正常功能。）
  • LLRp (log-likelihood ratio of pathogenicity) — a per-variant score computed from the ratio of how likely its functional-assay readout is under a "pathogenic" reference distribution versus a "benign" one, used to assign ACMG/AMP evidence weight.（中文：根据某变异的功能检测读数在"致病"参考分布与"良性"参考分布下出现概率的比值计算出的变异级评分，用于分配ACMG/AMP证据权重。）
  • MAVE (multiplexed assay of variant effects) — a high-throughput experiment that measures a functional readout (e.g., localization, stability) for thousands of variants of one gene simultaneously.（中文：一种高通量实验，可同时测量某一基因成千上万个变异的功能读数，如定位或稳定性。）
  • OddsPath — an earlier, coarser method for converting functional-assay data into pathogenicity evidence using a single global threshold rather than a per-variant likelihood ratio.（中文：一种较早、较粗糙的方法，使用单一的全局阈值而非逐变异的似然比，将功能检测数据转化为致病性证据。）
  • RMSD (root-mean-square deviation) — a structural biology metric quantifying how much a mutant protein structure deviates from the wild-type backbone at the atomic level.（中文：结构生物学中的指标，用于量化突变蛋白结构在原子水平上与野生型骨架的偏离程度。）
  • ΔΔG (change in folding free energy) — a computational estimate of how much a mutation destabilizes a protein's folded structure.（中文：一种计算得出的估计值，衡量某个突变对蛋白折叠结构稳定性的破坏程度。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • maveLLR — statistical method converting MAVE functional scores into log-likelihood ratios of pathogenicity via class-conditional kernel density estimation.
  • Bayesian ACMG/AMP evidence-weight scale (Tavtigian-style) — numeric weights (2.54/1.27/0.63/0.31/−0.31/−1.27) that LLRp bins are calibrated against.
  • AlphaFold2 + FoldX + Rosetta — structural modeling and stability-prediction stack used to corroborate functional-assay calls with RMSD and ΔΔG.
  • SpliceAI / Pangolin — splice-effect predictors used to flag a false-negative case the membrane-localization assay structurally cannot detect.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen / CFTR variant interpretation priority. Buildable artifact: CFTR Functional-Score LLRp Calibrator — a script taking variant ID + functional score + reference pathogenic/benign label (where available), fitting per-class KDE densities, computing per-variant LLRp, and binning into ACMG/AMP evidence tiers with configurable gene-specific thresholds. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • Whether evaluating any CFTR calibration against the same reference set used to fit its thresholds introduces circularity — a held-out validation set would be needed.
  • What failure modes a chosen CFTR functional assay structurally cannot detect (e.g., splicing, a binding interaction outside the assay's readout) — same blind-spot problem the JAG1 assay showed on 5 of 9 already-classified pathogenic/likely-pathogenic variants.
  • Whether thresholds are gene-specific (as in this paper) and would need independent CFTR-specific calibration rather than reuse of JAG1's thresholds.
  • Whether combining LLRp with structural-prediction scores (AlphaMissense, ESM, ΔΔG) risks double-counting correlated evidence rather than genuinely increasing confidence.

ALEX Here's the motivating problem. 这里是这项研究的出发点问题。 Over 900 JAG1 missense variants are reported in ClinVar, and more than 85% of them are either VUS or carry conflicting classifications — that's an enormous unresolved burden for a single gene. 作者此前已经针对JAG1的前七个外显子（错义变异富集的区域，也是结合NOTCH2的关键结构域）生成了一套MAVE数据，并基于膜定位分数的阈值加置信区间，将486个变异归类为功能异常。 But that original calibration used a coarser method — OddsPath (OddsPath) — essentially drawing one fixed JORDAN Walk me through the actual mechanics of maveLLR. 具体说说maveLLR的运作机制吧。

你应该已经很熟悉核密度估计了，所以新的地方只在于这一步映射：对任意一个变异的分数，取这两条密度曲线的比值，取对数，这个对数似然比再被映射到一套基因特异性的阈值网格上，而这套网格已经预先校准到了ACMG/AMP SVI框架下的贝叶斯证据权重量表——比如极强致病权重是2.54，一路到支持级的0.31。 So every variant gets a continuous score JORDAN You mentioned a retrospective cohort of VUS cases — did they also check the calibration against variants that were already confidently classified, as a kind of sanity check in the other direction? 你提到了那个VUS回顾性队列——他们有没有反过来，拿已经明确分类好的变异来检验一下这套校准方法，作为另一方向的合理性检查？

ALEX They did, and it's the most useful part of the paper for thinking about failure modes. 他们确实做了，而这也是整篇论文里对思考失效模式最有价值的部分。 Of nine individuals already classified as likely pathogenic or pathogenic before this analysis, only four (44%) actually showed abnormal membrane localization — meaning the MAVE missed the other five entirely, for reasons that are individually instructive. 在九个在本次分析之前就已被判定为可能致病或致病的个体中，只有四个（44%）确实表现出异常的膜定位——也就是说，这套MAVE完全漏掉了另外五个，而每一个漏掉的原因都很有启发性。 One variant sits at the last nucleotide of an exon and is predicted by SpliceAI and Pangolin to disrupt splicing — invisible to a cDNA-overexpression assay by construction, since splicing never happens in that system. 其中一个变异位于某个外显子的最后一个核苷酸上，被SpliceAI和Pangolin预测会破坏剪接——由于该检测系统本身采用cDNA过表达方式，剪接根本不会在其中发生，因此这类变异对该检测而言天然不可见。 Two others sit near or within the NOTCH2-binding region rather than affecting membrane trafficking, and one, p.Cys234Tyr, is a cysteine-to-tyrosine change that AlphaFold2 modeling shows causes minimal structural disruption — RMSD under 1.2 Å, versus up to 5 Å for other cysteine losses in EGF-like domains — so the assay correctly reports it as structurally unremarkable even though it's clinically pathogenic through some other mechanism. 另外两个则位于NOTCH2结合区域内或其附近，影响的并非膜转运过程；还有一个变异p.Cys234Tyr是一种半胱氨酸变成酪氨酸的改变，AlphaFold2建模显示它造成的结构破坏极小——均方根偏差（RMSD）不到1.2埃，而其他EGF样结构域内的半胱氨酸缺失可达5埃——所以这套检测准确地报告该变异在结构上并无异常，尽管它通过某种其他机制仍具有临床致病性。 That's the honest shape of a single-readout functional assay: good at catching one failure mode, structurally blind to others, and a calibration framework like LLRp can only be as complete as the assay feeding it. 这正是单一读数功能检测的真实面貌：它能很好地捕捉到一种失效模式，但对其他机制在结构上是盲区，而像LLRp这样的校准框架，其完整性终究受限于喂给它的那套检测本身。

JORDAN So where does this actually plug into what Runyu's doing? 那这篇论文到底能落到Runyu正在做的哪件事上呢？

ALEX Squarely onto the Karen collaboration — CFTR variant interpretation. 正好落在与Karen合作的CFTR变异解读项目上。 CFTR has its own functional assay literature — things like forskolin-induced swelling assays, patch-clamp conductance measurements, and some published deep-mutational-scanning-style datasets on specific domains — and the core transferable idea here isn't the biology, it's the calibration recipe: take any existing functional score distribution for CFTR, anchor it against a reference set of CFTR2-classified pathogenic and benign variants, compute a per-variant LLRp via kernel density estimation, and output an ACMG/AMP evidence weight instead of a lab-specific arbitrary cutoff.
