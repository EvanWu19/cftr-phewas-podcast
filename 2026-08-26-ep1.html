Ep1 — Autoencoder pretraining + deep ensemble for gene-specific missense pathogenicity prediction

Lee, Kang, and Hwang (BioData Mining, 2026) tackle the data-scarcity problem that plagues gene-specific pathogenicity prediction: across 30 hereditary cancer predisposition genes, they curated 19,227 ClinVar-labeled missense variants, of which 18,198 (95%) are VUS and only 1,029 have confident pathogenic/benign labels. Instead of discarding the unlabeled VUS, they pretrain a denoising autoencoder on the full unlabeled pool, then fine-tune a 50-network deep ensemble on the small labeled slice, per gene (BRCA1, BRCA2, MLH1, MSH2) or per disease group. The combined method reaches a mean AUPRC of 0.940 (SD 0.070) across 120 test sets, beating genome-wide weakly-supervised models (AlphaMissense, ESM1b, PrimateAI, popEVE, MisFit) by more than 5 points and edging out ClinVar-trained baselines (ClinPred, XGBoost, RF) by 2+ points, with the ensemble specifically improving robustness against any single poorly-performing network. No CFTR content — the transferable idea is the pretrain-on-unlabeled-VUS-then-fine-tune pattern applied to a single deeply-characterized gene, which maps directly onto CFTR2's own VUS-heavy shape.

Plain-English glossary

  • Hereditary cancer predisposition gene — A gene where an inherited germline pathogenic variant substantially raises lifetime cancer risk, as opposed to somatic mutations accumulating only within a tumor.（中文：携带一个遗传性致病种系变异就会显著提高终生患癌风险的基因，区别于只在肿瘤内部累积的体细胞突变。）
  • Germline variant — A genetic variant present in every cell of the body from birth, inherited from a parent.（中文：从出生起就存在于全身每个细胞中、由父母遗传而来的遗传变异。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant lacking enough evidence to be confidently classified as benign or pathogenic under the ACMG/AMP framework.（中文：在ACMG/AMP框架下缺乏足够证据被自信判定为良性或致病性的变异。）
  • ClinVar review status — A star-rated indicator of how much submitter/expert-panel agreement backs a variant's clinical classification.（中文：反映有多少提交者/专家小组意见支持某变异临床分类的星级指标。）
  • Denoising autoencoder — A neural network pretrained to reconstruct clean input from a noised version, here used to learn feature representations from unlabeled VUS before any labeled fine-tuning.（中文：一种通过从带噪声输入重建干净输出来预训练的神经网络，这里用于在任何有标签微调之前，从未标注VUS中学习特征表示。）
  • Deep ensemble — A collection of independently trained neural networks (here, 50) whose combined predictions are more robust than any single network's.（中文：一组独立训练的神经网络（此处为50个），其组合预测比任何单个网络都更鲁棒。）
  • AUPRC (area under the precision-recall curve) — A classifier-performance metric particularly informative under class imbalance; not re-explained on air, Runyu knows this cold.（中文：一种在类别不平衡情况下尤其有信息量的分类器性能指标；因听众已熟悉，节目中未重新讲解。）
  • MANE Select transcript — The single representative transcript per gene, agreed upon by RefSeq and Ensembl, used for consistent variant annotation.（中文：由RefSeq和Ensembl共同确认的每个基因的代表性转录本，用于统一的变异注释。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • Denoising-autoencoder pretraining on unlabeled VUS — learns a feature representation from the abundant unlabeled pool before any labeled fine-tuning.
  • 50-network deep ensemble — improves robustness against any single poorly-performing model (AUPRC 0.909 vs. 0.875 for worst individual network, P=0.002).
  • Disease-specific vs. gene-specific training-setup comparison — a reusable design for deciding which pooling strategy wins depending on how much gene-specific labeled data exists.
  • Per-variant importance profile + 2D feature-projection interpretability layer — shows which features drove a call and how pretraining changes learned representations.

What to verify first

  • Whether the gnomAD-based benign-labeling caveat (carrier variants in a population database) applies to CFTR2's curation process.

ALEX The field has basically three tiers of approach. JORDAN Where would you push back on that setup? 你会在这个设置里对哪里提出质疑？

ALEX The gnomAD allele-frequency cutoff at 0.5% is generous for a recessive disease gene but questionable for genes like BRCA1 and BRCA2, where even a rare heterozygous carrier variant should probably be flagged regardless of exact frequency threshold — and more importantly, they don't fully spell out how the disease-specific training pool's pathogenic-to-benign base rate compares to the gene-specific one, which matters a lot for how you'd calibrate a probability output versus just a rank score. 0.5%这个gnomAD等位基因频率阈值，对于一个隐性遗传病基因来说算是比较宽松的，但对BRCA1和BRCA2这类基因就有待商榷了——即使是罕见的杂合携带者变异，也应该被标记出来，不太应该只看具体的频率阈值；更重要的是，他们并没有完全说清楚疾病特异性训练池的致病/良性基础比例与基因特异性训练池相比如何，而这一点对于如何校准概率输出而不仅仅是排序分数，影响很大。

JORDAN Okay, results — did the pretraining-plus-ensemble combination actually pay off? 好，来看结果——预训练加集成的组合到底有没有带来实际收益？
