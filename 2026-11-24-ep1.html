Al-Eitan LN, Abusalem MT, Alghamdi MA, Di Silvestre D. Biology 2026. This paper has nothing to do with CFTR, but it is a clean, fully worked template for the exact pipeline Karen's variant-interpretation work needs: a funnel from raw missense variants down to a short, structurally-justified shortlist. Starting from 454 missense SNPs in the tumor-suppressor gene RASSF2, the authors ran five independent pathogenicity predictors (SIFT, PolyPhen2, PhD-SNPg, E-SNPs&GO, AlphaMissense) to a five-way consensus of 75 variants, then three structural-stability predictors (DDMut, DynaMut2, ThermoMPNN) against an AlphaFold structure — explicitly excluding any variant whose predicted stability change fell in a known unreliable "dead zone" — down to 19 final candidates. Those 19 then got a full workup: evolutionary conservation, domain mapping, cancer-driver scoring validated against real patient tumor samples, and predicted-interaction-disruption scoring against AlphaFold3-modeled protein complexes. Four variants stood out as consistently damaging across nearly every analysis. Plain-English glossary

  • ΔΔG (change in Gibbs free energy) — Measures how much more or less stable a folded protein is after a single amino acid change; negative values indicate predicted destabilization.（中文：衡量一次氨基酸替换之后，折叠蛋白质变得更稳定还是更不稳定；负值表示预测为失稳。）
  • ipTM (interface predicted template modelling) — An AlphaFold-family confidence score specifically for a predicted protein-protein complex's interface; values above 0.8 are confident, below 0.6 suggest a likely failed prediction.（中文：AlphaFold系列模型中专门针对预测蛋白质复合物界面的置信度评分；高于0.8为高置信，低于0.6则可能是预测失败。）
  • PAE (predicted aligned error) — A full matrix from AlphaFold showing how confident the model is about the relative position of every residue pair to every other one.（中文：AlphaFold给出的一个完整矩阵，显示模型对任意两个残基之间相对位置的信心程度。）
  • pLDDT (predicted Local Distance Difference Test) — AlphaFold's per-residue confidence score, 0–100, for local structural accuracy.（中文：AlphaFold对每个残基局部结构准确性的置信度评分，范围0到100。）
  • pTM (predicted template modelling) — AlphaFold's confidence score for a structure's overall fold, distinct from the per-residue pLDDT.（中文：AlphaFold对整体折叠结构的置信度评分，与逐残基的pLDDT不同。）
  • RA domain (Ras-associating domain) — The folded region of RASSF2 that physically binds active, GTP-loaded KRAS.（中文：RASSF2中负责物理结合活化、结合GTP状态的KRAS的折叠区域。）
  • SARAH domain (Sav/Rassf/Hpo domain) — The folded region of RASSF2 that binds the kinases MST1/MST2 to trigger apoptosis via the Hippo pathway.（中文：RASSF2中负责结合MST1/MST2激酶、通过Hippo信号通路触发细胞凋亡的折叠区域。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Five-tool pathogenicity consensus — SIFT, PolyPhen2, PhD-SNPg, E-SNPs&GO, and AlphaMissense run together; only variants flagged by all five advance.
  • Dead-zone-excluded stability consensus — DDMut, DynaMut2, and ThermoMPNN, with variants in the ±0.5 kcal/mol ΔΔG range explicitly dropped as unreliable.
  • AlphaFold3 complex modeling — building protein-protein complexes for variant-impact scoring, with explicit pTM/ipTM/PAE confidence reporting rather than treating the complex as ground truth.
  • mCSM-PPI2 / MutPred-PPI — two independent tools scoring a missense variant's predicted impact on a specific protein-protein interface.
  • CScape + cBioPortal/AACR GENIE cross-check — pairing a computational cancer-driver classifier with real patient-sample validation.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation (method only — no claim made about CFFPR's own structural or functional data holdings). Buildable artifact: CFTR Missense VUS Consensus-and-Stability Screener — feed a CFTR missense VUS list (HGVS or ClinVar IDs) through the same five pathogenicity predictors for a consensus-pathogenic subset, then run the same three stability predictors against CFTR's own AlphaFold structure (UniProt P13569) with the same ±0.5 kcal/mol dead-zone rule, outputting a ranked, ConSurf-annotated shortlist for curator review. Difficulty: M. Pairs naturally with the already-logged Predictor-Redundancy Flagger (2026-11-20) to add a confidence-in-consensus layer.

What to verify first

  • All three stability predictors here were validated on RASSF2's soluble, cytoplasmic RA and SARAH domains — whether they generalize to CFTR's membrane-embedded, multi-pass ABC-transporter fold is untested and needs checking before trusting the numbers the same way.
  • The ±0.5 kcal/mol stability "dead zone" was borrowed from general tool-reliability literature, not derived from CFTR data, and would need its own recalibration.
  • Every protein-protein-interaction disruption call in this paper rests on AlphaFold3 complexes with only low-to-moderate confidence (pTM 0.52–0.68, ipTM range similar) — any CFTR-interactor complex analysis needs its own confidence check before an interaction-disruption claim is trusted.
  • The five-predictor "consensus" may partly reflect correlated training signal (PhD-SNPg trained on ClinVar labels, AlphaMissense on population-frequency data overlapping PolyPhen2's heuristics) rather than fully independent confirmation — worth checking predictor feature overlap before reading agreement as strong evidence.

JORDAN No CFTR anywhere in the title — why's this the Track A pick? 标题里完全没提到CFTR——为什么这篇会被选进A线？

ALEX Because it isn't really about RASSF2's biology at all — it's a clean, fully worked example of exactly the multi-stage pipeline, five pathogenicity predictors into a consensus, then structure, then stability, then conservation, that you'd want to run on a stack of CFTR missense VUS before anything reaches Karen's desk. 因为这篇论文其实根本不是在讲RASSF2本身的生物学——它是一个干净、完整跑通的工作流程示范：五个致病性预测工具先出一个共识结果，再接结构分析，再接稳定性分析，再接保守性分析，而这正是你会想要在CFTR错义VUS批量进入Karen那里之前，拿来跑一遍的流程。

JORDAN Before the pipeline, give me the vocabulary — what's a protein domain, and what are these RA and SARAH domains that keep coming up? 在讲流程之前，先把词汇讲清楚——什么是蛋白质结构域？论文里反复出现的RA结构域和SARAH结构域又是什么？

ALEX A domain is a self-contained, folded chunk of a protein that does one job on its own — RASSF2 has an RA domain（Ras-associating结构域，RA domain), which is the piece that physically grabs onto KRAS, and a SARAH domain（Sav/Rassf/Hpo结构域，SARAH domain), which is the piece that binds the kinases MST1 and MST2 to trigger apoptosis. 结构域就是蛋白质里一段能独立折叠、自己完成一项功能的部分——RASSF2有一个RA结构域（Ras-associating结构域，英文RA domain），负责物理上抓住KRAS；还有一个SARAH结构域（Sav/Rassf/Hpo结构域，英文SARAH domain），负责结合MST1和MST2这两个激酶来触发细胞凋亡（apoptosis，程序性细胞死亡）。

JORDAN And the stability math — what's this ΔΔG number the stability tools keep spitting out? 还有稳定性的那套数学——这些稳定性工具反复给出的ΔΔG数字是什么？

ALEX ΔΔG（突变前后蛋白质吉布斯自由能的变化，ΔΔG, change in Gibbs free energy) measures how much more or less stable a folded protein is after a single amino acid swap; a negative value means the mutant is less stable than the wild type, which is the direction you worry about. ΔΔG衡量的是一个氨基酸替换之后，折叠好的蛋白质变得更稳定还是更不稳定——负值意味着突变体比野生型更不稳定，也就是需要担心的那个方向。

JORDAN And AlphaFold's confidence scores — pLDDT, pTM, ipTM, PAE — walk me through those once. 还有AlphaFold的置信度指标——pLDDT、pTM、ipTM、PAE——帮我过一遍。

ALEX pLDDT（预测的局部距离差异检验，predicted Local Distance Difference Test）scores each residue's local structure from 0 to 100, pTM（预测的模板建模评分，predicted template modelling）scores the overall fold, ipTM（界面预测模板建模评分，interface predicted template modelling）does the same job specifically for a predicted protein-protein complex's interface, and PAE（预测对齐误差，predicted aligned error）is a full matrix showing how confident the model is about the relative position of every residue pair to every other one — together they tell you whether to trust a structure, or a complex, at all. pLDDT（预测的局部距离差异检验）给每个残基的局部结构打一个0到100的分；pTM（预测的模板建模评分）给整体折叠打分；ipTM（界面预测模板建模评分）专门给预测出来的蛋白质-蛋白质复合物界面打分；PAE（预测对齐误差）则是一整个矩阵，显示模型对任意两个残基之间相对位置的把握有多大信心——这几个指标合在一起，才能判断一个结构、或者一个复合物，到底值不值得信。

JORDAN So why RASSF2 specifically — what's the biology this paper is trying to nail down? RASSF2具体为什么被选中——这篇论文到底想搞清楚什么生物学问题？

ALEX RASSF2 is a tumor suppressor gene（抑癌基因，tumor suppressor gene) on chromosome 20 that acts as a Ras effector — it physically binds active, GTP-loaded KRAS through its RA domain, and that binding is what lets it travel to the nucleus and drive apoptosis. RASSF2是20号染色体上的一个抑癌基因，作为一个Ras效应蛋白发挥作用——它通过RA结构域，物理结合处于活跃状态、结合了GTP的KRAS，而正是这种结合让它能进入细胞核，推动细胞凋亡。

ALEX Through its SARAH domain, it also binds the kinases MST1 and MST2, keeping MST2 in a phosphorylated state that protects it from degradation, so loss of RASSF2 function doesn't just remove one brake on cancer, it removes two. 通过SARAH结构域，它还会结合MST1和MST2这两个激酶，让MST2保持磷酸化状态，从而不被降解——所以RASSF2功能的丧失，拿掉的不是一道刹车，而是两道。

ALEX Almost all prior work on RASSF2 in cancer has focused on one inactivation route, promoter hypermethylation（启动子高甲基化，promoter hypermethylation) silencing the gene entirely in colorectal, lung, gastric, breast, and thyroid tumors — nobody had systematically asked what happens when RASSF2 is still expressed, but a point mutation damages the protein itself. 几乎所有关于RASSF2与癌症的既往研究，都只盯着一条失活路径——启动子高甲基化，在结直肠癌、肺癌、胃癌、乳腺癌和甲状腺癌里把这个基因整体沉默掉；但从来没有人系统性地问过，如果RASSF2本身还在表达，只是蛋白质结构被一个点突变破坏了，会发生什么。

JORDAN And that gap is what this paper tries to fill. 而这正是这篇论文想要填补的空白。

ALEX Exactly — of the 19,303 known SNPs in RASSF2, 454 change an amino acid, and until this paper nobody had run those 454 missense variants through a structure-and-stability lens to ask which ones would plausibly break the protein, independent of methylation status. 正是——在RASSF2已知的19,303个SNP里，有454个会改变氨基酸；在这篇论文之前，从来没有人把这454个错义变异放进结构与稳定性的分析镜头下，去问哪些变异真的可能破坏蛋白质本身，而这与甲基化状态无关。

ALEX Investigating that experimentally, one variant at a time with site-directed mutagenesis and stability assays, is slow and expensive, which is exactly why a computational triage pipeline — cheap, fast, and able to screen all 454 at once — is the sensible first move before anyone picks up a pipette. 如果用定点突变加稳定性实验一个一个地做实验验证，既慢又昂贵——这正是为什么一个计算层面的初筛流程——便宜、快速、能一次性筛完全部454个变异——是在任何人动手做湿实验之前，更合理的第一步。

JORDAN Walk me through the pipeline itself. 带我过一遍这套流程本身。

ALEX Start to finish: all 454 missense variants, pulled from dbSNP relative to RASSF2's MANE transcript, went through five independent pathogenicity predictors — SIFT, PolyPhen2, PhD-SNPg, E-SNPs&GO, and AlphaMissense — and only the 75 variants every single one of the five flagged as pathogenic moved forward. 从头到尾：全部454个错义变异，相对RASSF2的MANE转录本从dbSNP中提取出来，先经过五个独立的致病性预测工具——SIFT、PolyPhen2、PhD-SNPg、E-SNPs&GO和AlphaMissense——只有这五个工具全部一致判定为致病的75个变异，才会进入下一步。

ALEX Those 75 then went through three structural-stability predictors — DDMut, DynaMut2, and ThermoMPNN — run against the AlphaFold structure of RASSF2, and the authors deliberately excluded any variant whose ΔΔG fell within plus-or-minus 0.5 kcal/mol of zero, citing evidence that stability predictors are unreliable in exactly that dead zone; that left 19 variants. 这75个变异接着被放进三个结构稳定性预测工具——DDMut、DynaMut2和ThermoMPNN——对照RASSF2的AlphaFold结构来跑；作者特意排除了ΔΔG落在正负0.5 kcal/mol这个死区范围内的变异，理由是有证据表明稳定性预测工具在这个区间内是不可靠的；筛完剩下19个变异。

ALEX Those final 19 then got the full workup — gnomAD and ClinVar for population frequency and existing classification, ConSurf for conservation, InterPro for domain mapping, NetSurfP-3.0 for secondary structure and surface accessibility, CScape plus cBioPortal and AACR GENIE for somatic cancer-driver evidence, NetPhos and GPS for phosphorylation sites, MutPred2 and Project HOPE for predicted molecular mechanism, and finally mCSM-PPI2 and MutPred-PPI run against AlphaFold3-modeled RASSF2-KRAS and RASSF2-MST1 complexes, plus an existing AlphaFold-database RASSF2-MST2 complex, to score impact on the two key interactions. 最后这19个变异得到了完整的分析套餐——用gnomAD和ClinVar查人群频率和已有分类，用ConSurf查保守性，用InterPro做结构域定位，用NetSurfP-3.0分析二级结构和表面可及性，用CScape加cBioPortal和AACR GENIE查体细胞癌症驱动突变证据，用NetPhos和GPS预测磷酸化位点，用MutPred2和Project HOPE预测分子机制，最后用mCSM-PPI2和MutPred-PPI，针对AlphaFold3建模出的RASSF2-KRAS和RASSF2-MST1复合物，以及已有的AlphaFold数据库里的RASSF2-MST2复合物，给这两个关键相互作用打分。

JORDAN That's a lot of tools agreeing with each other before anything counts. 这么多工具要互相一致，才算数，工作量不小。

ALEX Which is exactly where I'd push back — the five-tool "consensus" at the top of the funnel is treated as five independent lines of evidence, but PhD-SNPg is literally trained on roughly 104,000 ClinVar-labeled deleterious and neutral SNPs, and AlphaMissense is fine-tuned on population-frequency data that overlaps conceptually with what PolyPhen2's physicochemical heuristics are already trying to capture, so agreement across all five could partly reflect shared blind spots in overlapping training signal, not five truly independent confirmations. 这正是我想提出异议的地方——这五个工具在筛选漏斗最前端的"共识"，被当成五条独立的证据线，但PhD-SNPg实际上是用大约104,000个ClinVar标注的致病/良性SNP训练出来的，而AlphaMissense又是在和PolyPhen2的理化性质启发式方法在概念上有重叠的群体频率数据上微调出来的——所以五个工具的一致，有一部分可能只是反映了训练信号里重叠带来的共同盲区，而不是五条真正独立的确认。

ALEX That's the same predictor-redundancy caution we logged back on the twentieth — it doesn't invalidate the pipeline, it just means "all five agree" should be read as stronger than any one tool alone, not as five-times independent. 这和我们十一月二十号那期记在案的预测工具冗余问题是同一类担忧——这并不会让这套流程失效，只是说"五个工具都同意"应该被理解成比单个工具更强的证据，而不是五倍独立的证据。

JORDAN Give me the headline numbers. 给我讲讲最核心的数字。

ALEX Of the 454 missense variants, 75 cleared the five-tool pathogenicity consensus, and of those, 19 cleared the three-tool stability consensus — 14 sitting in the RA domain and 2 in the SARAH domain, with all 19 landing at ConSurf conservation scores of 7 to 9 on a 9-point scale, 13 of them at the maximum score of 9. 在454个错义变异里，75个通过了五工具致病性共识，其中19个又通过了三工具稳定性共识——14个落在RA结构域，2个落在SARAH结构域，全部19个在ConSurf九分制保守性评分上都在7到9之间，其中13个拿到了满分9分。

ALEX Four variants stood out across almost every downstream analysis: V193D, L235P, L247P, and Y275C were all highly conserved with severe predicted structural damage, and Y275C sat right at the predicted RASSF2-MST2 interface, where mCSM-PPI2 and MutPred-PPI both flagged it as disruptive to that complex. 有四个变异在几乎每一步下游分析里都格外突出：V193D、L235P、L247P和Y275C都是高度保守、且被预测结构损伤严重的变异，而Y275C恰好位于预测出的RASSF2-MST2界面上，mCSM-PPI2和MutPred-PPI两个工具都判定它会破坏这个复合物的相互作用。

ALEX But here's the honesty check the authors built in themselves — the AlphaFold3 RASSF2-KRAS complex only reached pTM 0.63 and ipTM 0.52, which is low-to-moderate confidence at best, while RASSF2-MST1 and the database RASSF2-MST2 model landed in the 0.6-to-0.8 moderate-confidence band, so every PPI-disruption call in this paper rides on top of a structural guess, not a solved structure. 但作者自己也内置了一个诚实检验——AlphaFold3预测的RASSF2-KRAS复合物只达到pTM 0.63、ipTM 0.52，充其量算低到中等置信度，而RASSF2-MST1和数据库里的RASSF2-MST2模型落在0.6到0.8这个中等置信度区间；也就是说，这篇论文里每一个"会破坏相互作用"的判断，底层依据都是一个结构猜测，而不是一个已解析的真实结构。

ALEX CScape independently flagged all 19 variants as potentially oncogenic, nine with high confidence, and cBioPortal confirmed one of them, R245Q, had actually turned up in a real colon adenocarcinoma patient sample, with nearby codon positions hit by different substitutions in lung and uterine tumors — a nice piece of external validation that these aren't purely theoretical hotspots. CScape独立地把全部19个变异都标记为潜在致癌性变异，其中9个是高置信度；cBioPortal也证实其中一个，R245Q，确实在一份真实的结肠腺癌患者样本中出现过，附近的密码子位置在肺癌和子宫内膜样癌中也被不同的替换命中——这算是一个不错的外部验证，说明这些并不是纯理论上的热点。

JORDAN Anything beyond the variant-level predictions themselves? 除了变异本身的预测结果之外，还有别的吗？

ALEX Yes — the authors also ran a correlation analysis called muTarget, comparing gene expression in tumor samples carrying a RASSF2 coding mutation against samples without one, across colon and lung adenocarcinoma cohorts of several thousand patients each. 有——作者还跑了一个叫muTarget的相关性分析，在结肠和肺腺癌各有几千个患者的队列里，比较携带RASSF2编码突变的肿瘤样本和不携带突变的样本之间的基因表达差异。

ALEX In colon cancer, RASSF2 mutation correlated with lower expression of KRT23 among other genes; in lung cancer, it correlated with lower expression of BCL2L14, which itself has pro-apoptotic activity, so the authors read that as a plausible double-hit on apoptosis — RASSF2's own loss of function plus a knock-on effect on a second pro-apoptotic gene. 在结肠癌中，RASSF2突变和包括KRT23在内的若干基因表达下降相关；在肺癌中，它和BCL2L14表达下降相关，而BCL2L14本身具有促凋亡活性，所以作者把这解读为对细胞凋亡的一个可能的"双重打击"——既有RASSF2自身功能的丧失，又连带影响了另一个促凋亡基因。

ALEX This is purely correlational, drawn from bulk tumor cohorts rather than an engineered isogenic cell line, so it's suggestive of a downstream network effect rather than proof of a direct transcriptional mechanism — the authors say as much themselves. 这纯粹是相关性分析，数据来自批量肿瘤队列，而不是经过基因工程改造的同源细胞系，所以它更像是提示了一种下游网络效应，而不是直接转录机制的证明——作者自己也是这么说的。

JORDAN So where does a RASSF2 cancer-biology paper actually land for CFTR work? 那一篇讲RASSF2癌症生物学的论文，到底怎么落到CFTR的工作上？
