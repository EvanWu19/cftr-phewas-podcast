Show notes — Track C — CF genetic heterogeneity in Torbat Jam (Iran): from common variants to large deletions

Blurb. Ghaeninejhad et al. (Respiratory Medicine, 2026) genotyped 7 unrelated clinically-diagnosed CF patients (sweat chloride >60 mmol/L) from the under-studied, highly consanguineous Torbat Jam region of Iran, using an escalating assay ladder: Sanger sequencing of all 27 CFTR exons → MLPA for copy-number variants → whole exome sequencing for those still negative. Mutations were found in 4/7 (57.1%): F508del (c.1521_1523delCTT) homozygous in one and heterozygous in another; the c.1210-11T>G (5T;TG12) poly-T/TG splice allele in two compound heterozygotes; a missense c.547C>A (p.Leu183Ile); and — the methodological star — a homozygous deletion of the entire exon 2 that only MLPA could see, in a patient with no sequence variant at all. 3/7 (42.9%) stayed unresolved even after WES, which the authors read as an argument for deep intronic variants or extra-genic modifiers and for whole genome sequencing. Read as ABSTRACT-ONLY (not OA; no PMCID; Europe PMC isOpenAccess=N). It serves the modifier/WGS long game and motivates a cf-diagnostic-yield-ladder that quantifies detection yield across stacked assays and flags one-allele-only patients as the highest-value candidates for the next rung.

Plain-English glossary

  • Consanguinity — marriage between blood relatives, which raises the chance of inheriting the same rare allele from both parents (more homozygous cases).（中文：近亲婚配，有血缘关系者通婚，提高从父母双方遗传到同一罕见等位基因的概率。）
  • Autosomal recessive — a disease that requires two broken gene copies to appear.（中文：常染色体隐性遗传，需要两个损坏的基因拷贝才发病。）
  • Homozygous / compound heterozygous — two identical disease variants / two different disease variants, one on each copy.（中文：纯合／复合杂合，两个相同的致病变异／两个不同的致病变异，各在一个拷贝上。）
  • Sanger sequencing — the classic method that reads a DNA segment letter by letter; blind to whole missing blocks.（中文：Sanger 测序，逐字读取一段 DNA 的经典方法；对整块缺失是盲的。）
  • Exon — a protein-coding piece of a gene (CFTR has 27).（中文：外显子，基因中编码蛋白的片段（CFTR 有 27 个）。）
  • MLPA (multiplex ligation-dependent probe amplification) — counts gene-copy dosage; detects deletions/duplications that sequencing misses.（中文：多重连接依赖式探针扩增，清点基因拷贝剂量，检出测序漏掉的缺失/重复。）
  • CNV (copy number variation) — a deleted or duplicated block of the genome.（中文：拷贝数变异，基因组中被删除或重复的一整段。）
  • WES / WGS (whole exome / whole genome sequencing) — reading all protein-coding regions / the entire genome.（中文：全外显子组／全基因组测序，读取所有蛋白编码区／整个基因组。）
  • Missing heritability — a clearly genetic disease whose causal variant cannot be found with the assays used.（中文：缺失的遗传度，明明是遗传病，却用现有检测找不到致病变异。）
  • Deep intronic variant — a variant in non-coding depths that exon-focused sequencing does not reach.（中文：深部内含子变异，位于测序够不到的非编码深处。）
  • Extra-genic modifier — a gene outside CFTR that influences disease presentation.（中文：基因外修饰因子，位于 CFTR 之外、影响疾病表现的基因。）
  • Sweat chloride — a functional readout of CFTR; elevated (>60 mmol/L) supports a CF diagnosis.（中文：汗液氯离子，CFTR 功能的读数，升高（>60）支持 CF 诊断。）
  • Poly-T/TG tract (5T;TG12; c.1210-11T>G) — a length polymorphism in intron 9 that lowers exon-9 splicing efficiency; often milder/single-organ presentations.（中文：内含子 9 的长度多态性，降低外显子 9 剪接效率，常见较轻/单器官表现。）
  • Founder allele — a variant common in a population because it descends from a shared ancestor.（中文：奠基者等位基因，因源自共同祖先而在某群体中常见的变异。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Escalating assay ladder — Sanger (SNVs/small indels) → MLPA (copy-number / large deletions) → WES (beyond CFTR), each rung chosen to catch what the prior is blind to.
  • MLPA as the CNV safety net — the exon-2 homozygous deletion was a sequencing-invisible false negative rescued only by copy-counting.
  • Unresolved-fraction-as-signal — a cohort's leftover unexplained cases are a structured diagnostic gap whose shape argues for the next assay (WGS), not random noise.
  • Ancestry-aware allele enumeration — writing the real variant spectrum of an under-studied population, against a Europe-tuned baseline.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the modifier / whole-genome long game (Track C); secondary Liz / CFFPR data-quality (method only).

cf-diagnostic-yield-ladder (S/M) — input a per-patient table of CF-causing alleles found (0/1/2) + which assays were run; output (1) a yield curve as assays stack (Sanger → +MLPA → +WES → +WGS) showing each rung's marginal return, and (2) a ranked worklist flagging one-allele-only and zero-allele patients as highest-value next-rung candidates. Dual-serves the Karen harmonization lane, since stacking assays first requires normalizing variant names (legacy CF names, HGVS, MLPA copy-number calls) into one notation. Method only, demoable on published cohort tables; no claim about any registry's contents.

What to verify first

  • Abstract-only — per-patient table, phenotypes, and variant classifications are unretrieved; the ladder needs that granularity.
  • n=7 — 57.1% and 42.9% are 4 and 3 people; treat as directions, never quote "43% unresolved" as a population missing-heritability rate.
  • "Unresolved after WES" ≠ modifiers — equally consistent with exome coverage gaps, an uncalled CFTR CNV, or a technical miss; modifiers remain a hypothesis.
  • Poly-T/TG trap — a length polymorphism, not a substitution; defeats naive HGVS handling, hand-check.
  • Consanguinity — homozygosity is a founder-allele footprint, not a severity axis; don't let the ladder become a homozygosity ranking.

Gap-map / catalog

Closes — a first documented CFTR allele profile for the Torbat Jam region (Khorasan, Iran): F508del present, the 5T;TG12 splice allele in two compound hets, a p.Leu183Ile missense, and a homozygous whole-exon-2 deletion caught only by MLPA; and a concrete demonstration that a sequencing-only pipeline yields false negatives in this population.

Leaves open — the causal genotype for 42.9% of the cohort remains unknown even after WES; whether the unresolved fraction reflects deep intronic variants, uncalled structural variants, or extra-genic modifiers is untested; no modifier gene is named (the paper argues for the existence of extra-genic modifiers, not for any specific locus); and the classification status of p.Leu183Ile (CF-causing vs VUS) is not stated — a direct Karen/variant-interpretation loose end.
