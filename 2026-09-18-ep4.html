Ep4 — Software Application Profile: TIDAL — Tool to Implement Developmental Analysis of Longitudinal data

Kwong A, Edmondson-Stait A, Xu E, Thompson E, Parker R, Elhakeem A, Romaniuk L, Pearson R (2025), International Journal of Epidemiology. TIDAL is a free R package / Docker image / RShiny app wrapping lme4-based multilevel growth-curve modeling (polynomial fixed effects through quartic, random intercept/linear/quadratic) for non-statistician users — clinicians, public health analysts, educators — who understand their trait deeply but shouldn't need to become mixed-model experts to plot it correctly. The workflow stages a descriptive plot before model fitting, surfaces the raw lme4 syntax after fitting, and layers on plain-language interpretation (co-produced with users and people with lived experience of the traits studied), age-specific predicted-outcome lookups with 95% CIs, and an AUC-as-cumulative-exposure summary — not to be confused with ROC-AUC. Demonstrated on two synthetic-data examples (Millennium Cohort Study emotional symptoms; ALSPAC sex-stratified height, where the boy-girl height gap widens from -1.57cm at age 13 to -15.52cm at age 18) and informally validated across seven cohorts including UK Biobank. Paired with ep3 (Hernandez/psme P-splines): today supplies the reviewer-facing interpretation layer for the same class of growth curve ep3's model produces. TIDAL's own limitation to polynomial time terms (no splines/SITAR/GAMLSS) means its interface pattern, not its modeling backend, is what's worth transplanting for anthropometric flagging.

Plain-English glossary

  • TIDAL — Tool to Implement Developmental Analysis of Longitudinal data; a free R package/Docker/RShiny app for multilevel growth-curve modeling aimed at non-statistician users.（中文：一款面向非统计背景用户的免费多层生长曲线建模工具，提供R包、Docker镜像和RShiny应用三种形式。）
  • AUC (area-under-the-trajectory-curve, cumulative exposure) — In this growth-curve context, AUC means the area under a fitted outcome curve over an age window, representing total cumulative "dose" of an outcome — NOT the ROC-AUC classifier-discrimination metric.（中文：在生长曲线语境下，AUC指拟合结局曲线在某年龄区间下方的面积，代表该结局的累积"剂量"——并非分类器区分能力的ROC-AUC指标。）
  • Polynomial vs. spline time terms — TIDAL models time/age with polynomial terms (linear through quartic) rather than splines/SITAR/GAMLSS; polynomials can misbehave near sharp nonlinear turning points like the adiposity rebound discussed in ep3.（中文：TIDAL用多项式（一次到四次）而非样条/SITAR/GAMLSS来建模时间/年龄；多项式在急剧非线性转折点附近（如ep3讨论的脂肪重聚）可能表现不佳。）
  • Co-produced interpretation text — Plain-language result summaries developed jointly with end users and people with lived experience of the studied trait, not written by developers alone.（中文：与终端用户及对所研究特征有亲身经历者共同开发的通俗语言结果摘要，而非仅由开发者独自撰写。）
