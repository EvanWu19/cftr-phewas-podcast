Ep1 — Track A — Variant Interpretation & Harmonization

Blurb

McDonald et al. (2024, PLOS One) stress-tested AlphaMissense — a deep-learning pathogenicity predictor that combines a learned protein-structure model (descended from AlphaFold) with evolutionary conservation across species — against the richest CFTR-specific dataset available: CFTR2's curated clinical outcome data (sweat chloride, pancreatic insufficiency rate, Pseudomonas infection rate, FEV1) plus a stack of published in vitro functional assays covering CFTR folding, trafficking, and channel conductance, and a theratyping dataset measuring corrector-drug response. AlphaMissense classified CF-causing and non-CF-causing variants well (95%/78% accuracy), but showed a real false-positive problem in the ambiguous/VVCC zone (ROC AUC 0.66 vs. 0.91 for benign and 0.80 for pathogenic calls), correlated only modestly with clinical severity metrics (Pearson 0.31–0.46), correlated best with direct channel-conductance data (Pearson −0.70) rather than trafficking alone, and showed essentially no signal for predicting which corrector drug a variant would respond to. This lands in the Karen Raraigh / CFTR2-harmonization lane as a reusable calibration recipe — not a tool to adopt outright, but a template for how any general-purpose predictor should be benchmarked against CFTR2 before being trusted, seeding a buildable calibration-report generator.

Plain-English glossary

  • AlphaMissense — a deep-learning model that predicts missense-variant pathogenicity by combining a learned protein-structure model (descended from AlphaFold) with evolutionary conservation across related species; outputs a continuous score roughly 0–1.（中文：一种深度学习模型，结合习得的蛋白质结构模型（脱胎于AlphaFold）与跨物种进化保守性信息，预测错义变异的致病性，输出一个大约0到1的连续评分。）
  • CFTR2 — a curated, expert-reviewed registry pairing specific CFTR variants with pooled clinical outcome data (sweat chloride, pancreatic insufficiency, infection rates) across tens of thousands of people with CF.（中文：一个经过专家审核整理的数据库，将具体CFTR变异与来自数万名CF患者的汇总临床结局数据关联起来。）
  • CRMS/CFSPID — CFTR-related metabolic syndrome / CF Screen Positive Inconclusive Diagnosis: infants who screen positive on newborn screening but don't clearly meet CF diagnostic criteria, often carrying one CF-causing variant and one VUS.（中文：CFTR相关代谢综合征／CF筛查阳性但诊断不明，指新生儿筛查阳性但未明确符合CF诊断标准的婴儿，常携带一个致病变异和一个VUS。）
  • Missense variant — a single DNA change that swaps one amino acid for another in the resulting protein.（中文：DNA上的一个单点变化，导致蛋白质中一个氨基酸被替换为另一个。）
  • Theratype — how a specific CFTR variant responds to modulator drugs (correctors/potentiators); a variant can be clearly disease-causing yet respond very differently to different drugs.（中文：某个具体CFTR变异对调节剂类药物（矫正剂/增效剂）的反应方式；一个变异可以明确致病，却对不同药物的反应差异很大。）
  • VVCC — variants of varying clinical consequence: a CFTR2-specific classification for variants that cause CF in some people and not others depending on the paired variant.（中文：临床结果多变的变异，CFTR2特有的分类，指同一变异在不同个体身上是否致病取决于配对的另一变异。）

Full citation

McDonald EF, Oliver KE, Schlebach JP, Meiler J, Plate L. Benchmarking AlphaMissense pathogenicity predictions against cystic fibrosis variants. PLOS One. 2024. PMID: 38271453 | PMC10810519 | DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0297560

Methods/tools to lift

  • AlphaMissense — dual structure + evolutionary-conservation deep learning pathogenicity predictor, scored here against CFTR2 ground truth.
  • ESM (Evolutionary Scale Modeling) — protein language model trained via masked-token prediction, used as a comparator predictor.
  • EVE (Evolutionary model of Variant Effect) — unsupervised generative model trained on multiple-sequence alignments, used as a second comparator predictor.
  • Class-specific ROC/AUC decomposition — splitting a 3-way pathogenicity score into three separate one-vs-rest ROC curves to expose which class (here, ambiguous/VVCC) a predictor is weakest on.
  • Multi-modal correlation benchmarking — correlating a single predictor score against several orthogonal readouts (clinical metrics, deep mutational scanning, western blot trafficking, electrophysiology conductance, theratyping) to localize what the model has and hasn't actually learned.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh / CFTR2-harmonization priority. Buildable artifact: cftr-predictor-calibration-report — input a CFTR2 variant export plus scores from one or more pathogenicity predictors (AlphaMissense, REVEL, CADD, ESM, EVE), reproduce this paper's three-class ROC-AUC breakdown and clinical-metric correlation plots, and flag variants where predictors disagree with each other or with CFTR2's own classification. Difficulty: S-M (standard ROC/correlation code; the CFTR2 data pull is the fiddly part).

What to verify first

  • Whether isolating same-genotype/homozygous CFTR2 patients changes the reported clinical correlations, given the compound-heterozygosity confound baked into every allele-level correlation in this paper.
  • Whether the reported calibration gaps (especially the 0.66 ambiguous-class AUC) still hold for current AlphaMissense versions and newer predictors released since 2024.
  • That CFTR2 classifications move over time — any calibration report needs to be re-run against current data, not trusted as a one-time result.
  • This paper says nothing about how CFTR variants are currently curated, flagged, or scored inside the CFF registry itself — separate, unverified question.

JORDAN Before we get into the numbers — what exactly is a missense variant, and what makes AlphaMissense different from earlier tools? 在讲具体数字之前——什么是错义变异，AlphaMissense跟以前的工具相比又有什么不同？

ALEX A missense variant is a single DNA change that swaps one amino acid for another in the resulting protein — as opposed to a nonsense variant, which creates a premature stop, or a frameshift, which garbles everything downstream. 错义变异（missense variant）是指DNA上的一个单点变化，导致所编码蛋白质中一个氨基酸被替换成另一个氨基酸——这不同于无义变异（会提前产生终止密码子）或移码变异（会打乱其后所有的编码序列）。 Missense variants are the hardest to call because sometimes a swapped amino acid does almost nothing, and sometimes it wrecks the protein's fold or function entirely. 错义变异是最难判断的一类，因为有时氨基酸的替换几乎没有影响，有时却会彻底破坏蛋白质的折叠或功能。 AlphaMissense predicts which is which using two ingredients: a learned protein structure model — descended from AlphaFold, DeepMind's structure-prediction system — and evolutionary conservation, meaning how tolerant that specific amino-acid position has been to change across millions of years of related species. AlphaMissense通过两种信息来区分这两种情况：一是习得的蛋白质结构模型——脱胎于DeepMind的结构预测系统AlphaFold；二是进化保守性（evolutionary conservation），即在数百万年的物种演化中，某个特定氨基酸位点对变化的耐受程度。 The idea is that positions evolution has kept nearly frozen are usually structurally or functionally load-bearing, so a variant there is more likely to be damaging. 其基本思路是：进化过程中几乎被"冻结"不变的位点，通常在结构或功能上承担着关键作用，因此这些位点上发生的变异更可能是有害的。 AlphaMissense outputs a single continuous score from roughly 0 to 1, and for CFTR specifically this paper treats scores 0.56 to 1.00 as a pathogenic call, 0.34 to 0.56 as ambiguous, and 0.04 to 0.34 as benign. AlphaMissense输出一个大约从0到1的连续评分，而针对CFTR，本文将0.56到1.00的评分判定为致病，0.34到0.56判定为意义不明，0.04到0.34判定为良性。

JORDAN And CFTR2 — that's the database they're benchmarking against? 而CFTR2——就是他们用来做基准比对的数据库？

ALEX Right — CFTR2 is a curated, expert-reviewed registry that pairs specific CFTR variants with clinical outcome data pooled across tens of thousands of people with CF: sweat chloride levels, from the sweat test that remains the diagnostic gold standard for CF; pancreatic insufficiency rate, meaning the fraction of carriers who need enzyme replacement to digest food; and Pseudomonas aeruginosa infection rate, a marker of lung disease severity. 没错——CFTR2是一个经过专家审核整理的数据库，把具体的CFTR变异与来自数万名CF患者的临床结局数据关联起来：汗液氯离子（sweat chloride）水平，来自汗液检测，这项检测至今仍是CF诊断的金标准；胰腺功能不全（pancreatic insufficiency）比例，即需要依赖酶替代疗法才能消化食物的患者比例；以及铜绿假单胞菌（Pseudomonas aeruginosa）感染率，这是肺部疾病严重程度的一个标志。 CFTR2 sorts variants into CF-causing, non-CF-causing, VUS — variant of uncertain significance — and a fourth CFTR2-specific bucket called VVCC, variants of varying clinical consequence, meaning the same variant produces CF in some people and not others depending on what's on the other chromosome copy. CFTR2把变异分为致病（CF-causing）、非致病（non-CF-causing）、VUS（意义未明变异）以及CFTR2特有的第四类——VVCC，即临床结果多变的变异（variants of varying clinical consequence），指同一个变异在有些人身上会导致CF，在另一些人身上则不会，具体取决于另一条染色体拷贝上携带的是什么变异。 One more term for later: theratype, meaning how a specific CFTR variant responds to modulator drugs like the corrector-potentiator combination Trikafta — a variant can be clearly disease-causing and still differ enormously in whether a drug actually helps it. 再补充一个后面会用到的术语：theratype（治疗类型），指某个具体的CFTR变异对调节剂类药物——比如矫正剂-增效剂联合用药Trikafta——的反应情况。一个变异可以明确致病，但在药物是否真正对它有效这一点上，可能存在巨大差异。

ALEX So here's what motivated this study. 这项研究的出发点是这样的。 CFTR has more than 2,000 catalogued variants, and only a fraction — CFTR2 lists 804 out of 2,111 at last count in the paper — have enough clinical or lab data to be confidently classified. CFTR基因已编目的变异超过2000个，但其中只有一小部分——论文写作时CFTR2记录的2111个变异中有804个——拥有足够的临床或实验数据可以被明确分类。 The rest sit as VUS, and next-generation sequencing keeps finding more of them faster than anyone can characterize them one at a time in a lab. 其余的都停留在VUS状态，而下一代测序技术（NGS）发现新变异的速度，远远快于实验室逐个进行功能表征的速度。 That gap matters most for a specific patient group: people with CRMS, CFTR-related metabolic syndrome, also called CFSPID, CF Screen Positive Inconclusive Diagnosis — infants who screen positive on newborn screening but don't clearly meet CF diagnostic criteria, often because they carry one CF-causing variant and one VUS. 这一差距对某一特定患者群体影响最大：CRMS（CFTR相关代谢综合征）患者，也称CFSPID（CF筛查阳性但诊断不明），指那些新生儿筛查呈阳性、但并不明确符合CF诊断标准的婴儿——往往是因为他们携带一个致病变异和一个VUS。 Somewhere between 11 and 48 percent of these CRMS/CFSPID infants eventually convert to a full CF diagnosis, and right now there's no good way to predict which ones will, partly because nobody can say with confidence whether their VUS is actually pathogenic. 在这些CRMS/CFSPID婴儿中，大约有11%到48%最终会转为明确的CF诊断，而目前尚没有很好的方法可以预测哪些婴儿会转化，部分原因就在于没有人能够有把握地判断他们所携带的VUS究竟是否致病。 When AlphaMissense was published, it made pathogenicity predictions for essentially every possible amino-acid substitution across the human proteome, calibrated to roughly 90 percent accuracy against ClinVar broadly. AlphaMissense发表时，为人类整个蛋白质组中几乎所有可能的氨基酸替换都给出了致病性预测，并针对ClinVar数据库整体校准至约90%的准确率。 That's a huge advance over prior tools that only covered a handful of characterized CFTR positions. 相比之前只覆盖少数已表征CFTR位点的工具，这是一个巨大的进步。 But ClinVar-wide accuracy doesn't tell you how the model performs on one specific gene with one specific, unusual biology — CFTR is a large multi-domain membrane channel, and its variants don't just need a pathogenic/benign call, they eventually need a mechanism and a drug-response answer too. 但是在整个ClinVar数据库上的准确率，并不能告诉你这个模型在某一个具有特殊生物学特性的基因上表现如何——CFTR是一个大型的多结构域膜通道蛋白，它的变异不仅需要一个"致病/良性"的判断，最终还需要回答机制和药物反应的问题。 So the authors set out to stress-test AlphaMissense against the richest CFTR-specific resource available: CFTR2's clinical outcome data, plus a stack of published in vitro functional datasets covering how CFTR variants fold, traffic to the cell surface, and conduct chloride. 因此，作者们着手用目前最丰富的CFTR专项资源来对AlphaMissense进行压力测试：CFTR2的临床结局数据，再加上一系列已发表的体外功能数据集，涵盖CFTR变异的折叠、向细胞表面转运，以及氯离子传导能力。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually did. 具体讲讲他们到底做了什么。

ALEX They pulled 176 missense variants from CFTR2's Variant List History — 169 with a clinical classification, 110 CF-causing, 41 VVCC, 18 non-CF-causing, plus the 7 VUS CFTR2 currently lists — and For prediction accuracy, they built ROC curves treating each of the three AlphaMissense output classes — pathogenic, ambiguous, benign — in turn as the positive class, so effectively three separate scanning）研究，测量了100个变异的细胞表面免疫染色强度；一个更大规模的免疫印迹数据集，测量了538个变异的成熟CFTR蛋白与未成熟蛋白的比例——即C带与B带比值（C-to-B band ratio），这是转运效率的标准读数；以及546个变异的forskolin诱导通道电流，这是氯离子传导功能的直接电生理测量指标。 Finally, they pulled a published theratyping dataset and colored variants by AlphaMissense score to see whether the score tracked which corrector compound, VX-661 or VX-445, a variant responded to. 最后，他们调用了一份已发表的theratyping（治疗类型分型）数据集，按AlphaMissense评分给变异上色，以观察该评分是否与变异对矫正剂VX-661或VX-445的反应类型相关。 They also benchmarked AlphaMissense against two competing predictors — ESM, a protein language model trained by masked-token prediction across large protein-sequence databases, and EVE, an unsupervised generative model trained on multiple-sequence alignments of evolutionarily related proteins. 他们还将AlphaMissense与两种竞争性预测工具进行了对比——ESM，一种通过在大规模蛋白质序列数据库上做掩码词元预测（masked-token prediction）训练出的蛋白质语言模型；以及EVE，一种基于进化相关蛋白质的多序列比对训练出的无监督生成模型。

JORDAN What would you push back on? 你会对哪个环节提出质疑？

ALEX Here's where I'd push back: the clinical correlations are computed at the allele level, but plenty of these patients are compound heterozygotes carrying two different variants — one of unknown severity paired with something else entirely — so a patient's sweat chloride or lung function is really the combined effect of two alleles, not a clean readout of the one variant AlphaMissense is being scored against. 我想在这里提出质疑：这些临床相关性分析是在等位基因层面计算的，但很多患者是复合杂合，携带两个不同的变异——一个严重程度未知的变异，搭配另一个完全不同的变异——因此患者的汗液氯离子或肺功能实际上是两个等位基因共同作用的结果，而不是AlphaMissense所评分的那一个变异的干净读数。 The paper acknowledges this as a factor but doesn't attempt to isolate homozygous-genotype patients or otherwise control for it, which likely dilutes every one of those correlation numbers in an unknown direction. 论文承认了这一因素的存在，但并未尝试单独分析纯合基因型患者，也没有采取其他方式加以控制，这很可能在未知方向上稀释了所有这些相关系数。

JORDAN So did it actually work? 那它到底管不管用？

ALEX Mixed, and specifically mixed in an informative way. 结果好坏参半，而且这种参半的方式本身很有信息量。 On the clean classification task, AlphaMissense got 95 percent of the 110 CF-causing variants right — 104 out of 110 — and 78 percent of the 18 non-CF-causing variants right, 14 out of 18. 在纯粹的分类任务上，AlphaMissense对110个致病变异的判断准确率达到95%（104/110），对18个非致病变异的判断准确率为78%（14/18）。 The ROC AUCs told a more nuanced story: 0.91 for benign calls, 0.80 for pathogenic calls, but only 0.66 for ambiguous — meaning AlphaMissense has a real false-positive problem specifically in that middle VVCC zone, over-predicting pathogenicity.
