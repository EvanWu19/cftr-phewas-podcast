How did this episode land for you?

Eight rare CFTR variants in organoids + cell lines — a domain-structured theratyping study (Track C)

Blurb. Railean et al. (Molecular Diagnosis & Therapy, 2026, open access) from Amaral's Lisbon group characterize eight rare CFTR variants (p.Pro5Leu, p.Pro205Ser, p.Leu206Trp, p.Arg347Pro, p.Ile507del, p.Ser945Leu, p.Met1137Arg, p.Asp1152His) using patient-derived intestinal organoids (forskolin-induced swelling, FIS) plus CFBE cell lines expressing each variant in isolation. Seven of ten organoid genotypes were rescued by available CFTR modulators; two were not (p.Arg347Pro/F508del at the predictive low forskolin dose, and p.Ile507del/p.Gln890Ter). The headline pattern: transmembrane-domain variants were more amenable to rescue than the NBD1 variant. Six patients started therapy with FEV1 gains and sweat-chloride drops; FEV1-vs-sweat-chloride correlated at r²≈0.92 and organoid swelling-vs-FEV1 at r²≈0.99 (n=6, fragile). Where it lands: the CF modifier / rare-variant catalog — the buildable artifact is a cftr-variant-domain-theratyping-map giving modulator-ineligible variants a domain-based rescue prior.

Plain-English glossary.

  • Apical membrane — the epithelial cell surface facing the lumen (airway/gut cavity), where CFTR sits.（中文：顶膜是上皮细胞面向管腔（气道／肠腔）的一面，CFTR位于此处。）
  • Band C (western blot) — the mature, fully glycosylated/processed form of CFTR that reached the surface; its recovery signals successful correction.（中文：C带是成熟、完全糖基化并到达细胞表面的CFTR形式，其恢复标志校正成功。）
  • CFTR modulator (CFTRm) — small molecule fixing the defective CFTR protein; a potentiator (ivacaftor) holds the channel open, a corrector (tezacaftor/elexacaftor) aids folding/trafficking.（中文：CFTR调节剂是修复缺陷CFTR蛋白的小分子；增效剂ivacaftor维持通道开放，校正剂tezacaftor/elexacaftor帮助折叠与运输。）
  • CFTR variant classes I–VII — group mutations by defect: I no protein, II misfolding/trafficking (F508del), III gating, IV conductance, V reduced amount, VI reduced stability, VII no mRNA.（中文：CFTR变异按缺陷分I–VII类：I无蛋白，II折叠/运输（F508del），III门控，IV电导，V数量减少，VI稳定性下降，VII无mRNA。）
  • FIS assay (forskolin-induced swelling) — forskolin raises cAMP → activates CFTR → chloride+water enter the organoid → it swells; swelling proxies restored CFTR function.（中文：FIS实验中福斯高林升高cAMP激活CFTR，氯和水进入类器官使其肿胀，肿胀代表CFTR功能恢复。）
  • NBD1 / TMD — nucleotide-binding domain 1 (cytoplasmic, binds ATP for gating) and transmembrane domains (form the membrane pore).（中文：NBD1是核苷酸结合结构域1（胞质、结合ATP驱动门控），TMD是形成膜孔的跨膜结构域。）
  • PDIO — patient-derived intestinal organoid, grown from a rectal biopsy, retaining the person's full genetic background.（中文：PDIO是患者来源肠道类器官，由直肠活检培养，保留个体完整遗传背景。）
  • Sweat chloride — standard functional biomarker of CFTR activity; lower = more channel function.（中文：汗液氯化物是CFTR活性的标准功能标志物，越低表示通道功能越强。）
  • Theratyping vs theranostics — theratyping uses engineered cell lines (one variant in isolation); theranostics uses patient-derived material carrying the whole genetic background.（中文：药型分型用工程细胞系（单一变异），治疗诊断学用携带完整遗传背景的患者来源材料。）
  • Ussing chamber — apparatus measuring transepithelial ion current, giving residual CFTR chloride current as % of wild type.（中文：尤斯灌流室测量跨上皮离子电流，给出残余CFTR氯电流占野生型的百分比。）

Methods / tools to lift.

  • FIS assay in PDIOs — quantitative, patient-specific readout of restored CFTR function; low forskolin dose (0.128 µM) is the clinically predictive reference.
  • Isolated cell-line theratyping (CFBE) — express one variant at a time to separate variant-specific effects from the compound-het background.
  • Band-C western blot + Ussing chamber — mechanism readouts (processing rescue % and residual chloride current % of wild type).
  • Domain-structured interpretation — mapping variants to N-term/TMD1/NBD1/TMD2 to explain and predict rescue amenability (TMD > NBD1).
  • Organoid-to-clinic correlation — swelling-vs-FEV1 and FEV1-vs-sweat-chloride as validation that the assay predicts individual response.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Serves the CF modifier / rare-variant catalog (Track C). Paste-ready MVP: cftr-variant-domain-theratyping-map (S–M) — build a table of rare CFTR variants annotated by protein domain (N-term/TMD1/NBD1/TMD2/NBD2), pull each variant's published theratyping/organoid-rescue result + class + band-C % + any clinical response from public sources (CFTR2 + primary literature), compute a per-domain rescue-amenability prior, and flag modulator-ineligible variants as expanded-access candidates. Living document; the rescue-resistant subgroup (e.g. NBD1) defines where modifier biology has most leverage.

What to verify first.

  • Domain is a coarse predictor — quantify how much variance the domain prior explains before relying on it (within-domain heterogeneity shown in the paper).
  • Compound-het confound — most organoids carry F508del; variant-specific claims should lean on isolated CFBE data, not organoid alone.
  • Clinical correlations rest on n=6 — treat effect sizes (r²≈0.99) as provisional; seek larger cohorts before quoting as settled.
  • External method only — built from public literature/variant databases; no claim about any registry's internal genotype or clinical data; decides no research design.

Gap-map / catalog.

  • Closes: adds variant-level, domain-resolved rescue evidence for eight rare CFTR alleles (p.Pro5Leu, p.Pro205Ser, p.Leu206Trp, p.Arg347Pro, p.Ile507del, p.Ser945Leu, p.Met1137Arg, p.Asp1152His); reinforces the TMD-more-rescuable-than-NBD1 rule; supplies CFTR2 carrier counts for several.
  • Leaves open: no modifier-gene findings — this is CFTR-intragenic variant characterization, not trans modifier biology; the domain prior's predictive value across the full rare-variant tail is untested; the rescue-resistant subgroup (NBD1, class I second alleles) still lacks a mapped modifier catalog.

ALEX Welcome back for our Track C paper. 欢迎回到我们的Track C这一集。 Today it is "Personalized Medicine in Cystic Fibrosis: Characterization of Eight Rare CFTR Variants in Intestinal Organoids and Cellular Models," by Railean, Rodrigues, Pankonien, and colleagues, from Margarida Amaral's group in Lisbon, published in Molecular Diagnosis & Therapy in 2026. 今天这篇是《囊性纤维化中的个体化医疗：在肠道类器官和细胞模型中对八个罕见CFTR变异的表征》，作者是Railean、Rodrigues、Pankonien等人，来自里斯本Margarida Amaral课题组，2026年发表于《Molecular Diagnosis & Therapy》。 It is open access, so we have the full text. 它是开放获取的，所以我们拿到了全文。 We are reading it because it is a clean, variant-level theratyping study that feeds the modifier and rare-variant catalog directly. 我们读它，是因为它是一项干净的、变异层面的"药型分型 (theratyping)"研究，能直接充实修饰因子与罕见变异目录。

JORDAN Lots of new terms here — start with the biology. 这里有不少新术语——先从生物学讲起。 What is a CFTR modulator, and what are these "classes" of variant? CFTR调节剂是什么，这些变异的"类别"又是什么？

ALEX CFTR is a chloride and bicarbonate channel at the apical membrane of epithelial cells — the surface facing the lumen, the airway or the gut cavity. CFTR是上皮细胞顶膜 (apical membrane，即面向管腔、气道或肠腔的那一面) 上的一个氯离子和碳酸氢根通道。 When it is broken, salt and water balance across the tissue fails, and in the lung that means thick mucus and recurrent infection. 当它损坏时，组织两侧的盐和水平衡失效，在肺里就表现为黏稠的黏液和反复感染。 CFTR modulators — the field abbreviates them CFTRm — are small molecules that fix the defective protein rather than the gene. CFTR调节剂——领域内缩写为CFTRm——是修复缺陷蛋白（而非基因）的小分子药物。 There are two mechanistic types. 它们有两种作用机制。 A potentiator, like ivacaftor, holds the channel open longer once it reaches the surface — it raises the open probability. 增效剂 (potentiator)，比如ivacaftor，让已经到达细胞表面的通道保持更久的开放——它提高通道的开放概率。 A corrector, like tezacaftor or elexacaftor, helps the protein fold and traffic to the surface in the first place. 校正剂 (corrector)，比如tezacaftor或elexacaftor，帮助蛋白先正确折叠并运输到细胞表面。 The variant classes group mutations by what goes wrong: class I is no protein made, class II is misfolding and failed trafficking — that is F508del, the common one — class III is a gating defect, class IV a conductance defect, class V reduced amount, and so on. 变异类别按"哪里出错"给突变分组：I类是完全不产生蛋白，II类是错误折叠加运输失败——那就是最常见的F508del——III类是门控缺陷，IV类是电导缺陷，V类是数量减少，等等。 Roughly, correctors rescue class II, potentiators rescue class III/IV. 大致而言，校正剂拯救II类，增效剂拯救III/IV类。

JORDAN And the two model systems — organoids and cell lines. Why both? 那两套模型系统——类器官和细胞系。为什么两个都要？

ALEX This is the theratyping-versus-theranostics distinction, and it is the heart of the method. 这就是"药型分型 (theratyping)"与"治疗诊断学 (theranostics)"的区别，也是这个方法的核心。 Theratyping uses engineered cell lines that each express one CFTR variant in isolation — clean, controlled, tells you the biology of that single variant. 药型分型用工程化的细胞系，每一株只单独表达一个CFTR变异——干净、可控，告诉你那一个变异本身的生物学。 Here they used CFBE cells, a bronchial epithelial line, transduced to express each variant one at a time. 这里他们用的是CFBE细胞，一种支气管上皮细胞系，通过转导让它一次只表达一个变异。 Theranostics uses patient-derived material that carries the person's whole genetic background — here, patient-derived intestinal organoids, abbreviated PDIOs, grown from rectal biopsies. 治疗诊断学则使用来自患者的材料，携带此人完整的遗传背景——这里是患者来源的肠道类器官 (patient-derived intestinal organoids，缩写PDIOs)，由直肠活检培养而来。 The readout for organoids is the forskolin-induced swelling assay — the FIS assay. 类器官的读出指标是"福斯高林诱导的肿胀实验 (forskolin-induced swelling assay)"，即FIS实验。 Forskolin raises cyclic AMP, which activates CFTR; if CFTR works, chloride and water flow into the organoid lumen and it visibly swells. 福斯高林 (forskolin) 升高环磷酸腺苷 (cyclic AMP)，从而激活CFTR；如果CFTR有功能，氯离子和水就流入类器官的腔内，它便肉眼可见地肿胀。 So swelling is a direct, quantitative proxy for restored CFTR function, and prior work established that the response at a low forskolin dose correlates with real clinical benefit. 所以肿胀是CFTR功能恢复的一个直接、定量的代理指标，而既往研究确立了低剂量福斯高林下的反应与真实临床获益相关。

JORDAN Why does the forskolin dose matter — why not just push it high and get the biggest swelling? 为什么福斯高林的剂量重要——为什么不直接加到很高、拿到最大的肿胀？

ALEX Because a high dose can force swelling that does not translate to the clinic, so it would overpredict benefit. 因为高剂量能强行制造出无法转化到临床的肿胀，从而高估获益。 The paper uses 0.128 micromolar as the reference precisely because prior studies found that concentration best correlates with real clinical outcomes — it is calibrated against patients, not chosen for maximum signal. 论文之所以用0.128微摩尔作为参照，正是因为既往研究发现这个浓度与真实临床结局的相关性最好——它是对着患者校准出来的，而不是为了信号最大而选的。 You can see the distinction inside their own data: one genotype, p.Arg347Pro, showed no response at the predictive low dose but did swell at a higher dose — and they correctly read that as probably no clinical benefit, not a rescue. 你可以在他们自己的数据里看到这种区别：有一个基因型p.Arg347Pro，在有预测力的低剂量下没有反应，却在更高剂量下确实肿胀了——他们正确地把它读作"大概没有临床获益"，而不是一次拯救。 That is a nice reminder that the readout is only as good as the dose you validated it at. 这很好地提醒我们：读出指标的好坏，取决于你在哪个剂量上验证过它。

JORDAN What is the gap this fills? 这填补了什么空白？

ALEX Modulators are approved for specific genotypes, but there is a long tail of rare variants that are non-eligible on paper yet might actually respond. 调节剂是针对特定基因型获批的，但有一长串罕见变异在纸面上不符合适用条件，实际上却可能有反应。 If you could show, in a patient's own organoids, that their rare variant responds to an approved drug, you could expand access to people currently locked out. 如果你能在患者自己的类器官里证明其罕见变异对某个已获批药物有反应，你就能为目前被拒之门外的人扩大用药机会。 That is the personalized-medicine promise: use the assay as a theranostic tool to predict who benefits, one person at a time. 这就是个体化医疗的承诺：把这个实验当作治疗诊断工具，一次一个人地预测谁会获益。 The eight variants they characterize span the protein: p.Pro5Leu near the N-terminus, p.Pro205Ser, p.Leu206Trp, and p.Arg347Pro in the first transmembrane domain, p.Ile507del in nucleotide-binding domain 1, and p.Ser945Leu, p.Met1137Arg, p.Asp1152His in the second transmembrane domain. 他们表征的八个变异横跨整个蛋白：靠近N末端的p.Pro5Leu，第一跨膜结构域 (transmembrane domain, TMD1) 里的p.Pro205Ser、p.Leu206Trp和p.Arg347Pro，核苷酸结合结构域1 (nucleotide-binding domain 1, NBD1) 里的p.Ile507del，以及第二跨膜结构域 (TMD2) 里的p.Ser945Leu、p.Met1137Arg、p.Asp1152His。 The transmembrane domains are the parts of the channel that sit in the membrane and form the pore; NBD1 is a cytoplasmic domain that binds ATP to drive gating. 跨膜结构域是通道嵌在膜内、形成孔道的部分；NBD1是一个胞质结构域，通过结合ATP来驱动门控。

JORDAN Why would the domain a variant sits in predict whether a drug can rescue it? 为什么变异所在的结构域能预测药物是否可以拯救它？

ALEX Because the modulators act on specific failure modes, and different domains tend to fail in different ways. 因为调节剂作用于特定的失效模式，而不同结构域往往以不同的方式失效。 Correctors help a misfolded protein reach the surface, and many transmembrane-domain variants cause a folding-and-trafficking problem that a corrector can partly fix. 校正剂帮助错误折叠的蛋白到达细胞表面，而许多跨膜结构域的变异造成的正是折叠与运输的问题，校正剂能部分修复。 NBD1 is where the most stubborn defects live — F508del itself sits right next to the Ile507 position studied here — and disruptions there can destabilize the protein so deeply that no current modulator restores meaningful surface protein. NBD1是最顽固缺陷所在之处——F508del本身就紧挨着这里研究的Ile507位点——而这里的破坏可能把蛋白破坏得如此之深，以至于现有的任何调节剂都无法恢复有意义的表面蛋白。 So domain is a proxy for mechanism, and mechanism is what the drug either can or cannot address. 所以结构域是"机制"的一个代理，而机制正是药物能否处理的东西。 That is why the group's recurring finding — transmembrane variants more rescuable than NBD1 — is not a coincidence but a structural expectation. 这就是为什么该课题组反复出现的发现——跨膜变异比NBD1更易被拯救——不是巧合，而是一个来自结构的预期。 It is also why domain can never be the whole story: two variants in the same domain can still fail differently, which is exactly the heterogeneity we will flag later. 这也是为什么结构域永远不可能是故事的全部：同一结构域里的两个变异仍可能以不同方式失效，而这正是我们稍后要标注的异质性。

JORDAN Give me the results — did the rare variants respond? 给我讲结果——这些罕见变异有反应吗？

ALEX Seven of the ten organoid genotypes were rescued by currently available modulators, and two were not. 十个类器官基因型中有七个被现有调节剂拯救，两个没有。 Rescued: the two p.Asp1152His genotypes, p.Pro5Leu/F508del, p.Leu206Trp/F508del, p.Ser945Leu/F508del, p.Pro205Ser/p.Tyr1092Ter, and p.Met1137Arg/c.2657+5G>A. 被拯救的有：两个p.Asp1152His基因型、p.Pro5Leu/F508del、p.Leu206Trp/F508del、p.Ser945Leu/F508del、p.Pro205Ser/p.Tyr1092Ter，以及p.Met1137Arg/c.2657+5G>A。 Not rescued at the clinically predictive low forskolin dose: p.Arg347Pro/F508del and p.Ile507del/p.Gln890Ter. 在有临床预测力的低剂量福斯高林下未被拯救的是：p.Arg347Pro/F508del和p.Ile507del/p.Gln890Ter。 p.Asp1152His is instructive — it responded to ivacaftor alone, which fingerprints it as a gating or conductance defect with residual function, and indeed prior work classes it as class IV. p.Asp1152His很有启发——它单用ivacaftor就有反应，这就把它指认为一个带残余功能的门控或电导缺陷，而既往研究确实把它归为IV类。 The cell-line side gave the mechanism: western blots showed which variants regain the mature, fully-processed "band C" form of CFTR after corrector treatment, and Ussing-chamber measurements gave residual chloride current as a percentage of wild type — for example p.Ser945Leu recovered mature CFTR to roughly 17% of wild type. 细胞系那一侧给出了机制：蛋白质印迹 (western blot) 显示哪些变异在校正剂处理后重新获得成熟、完全加工的CFTR"C带 (band C)"形式，而尤斯灌流室 (Ussing chamber) 测量给出残余氯电流占野生型的百分比——例如p.Ser945Leu把成熟CFTR恢复到约野生型的17%。 The domain pattern is the headline finding: variants in the transmembrane domains were more amenable to modulator rescue than the one in NBD1, which is consistent with the group's earlier work. 结构域模式是最重磅的发现：跨膜结构域里的变异比NBD1里的那个更容易被调节剂拯救，这与该课题组早先的工作一致。

JORDAN Hold on — most of these organoids also carry F508del. How do they know the swelling came from the rare variant and not the common one? 等一下——这些类器官大多也带着F508del。他们怎么知道肿胀是来自那个罕见变异，而不是那个常见变异？

ALEX That is exactly the confound they had to defend against, and it is why the two model systems earn their keep. 这正是他们必须防范的混杂因素，也正是那两套模型系统各自不可或缺的原因。 F508del on its own has essentially no residual function, so when an organoid shows baseline swelling before any corrector, that residual activity most likely comes from the rare variant, not the F508del allele.
