Ep2 (Track B — AI tooling): Prediction of Antimicrobial Resistance in People Living With Cystic Fibrosis Using Machine Learning

Jiang and colleagues (2026, MedComm) built an EHR-only machine-learning pipeline to predict, ahead of the 48-to-96-hour culture turnaround, which of five commonly used antibiotics a chronic CF lung infection will resist. Using a decade of data (2012–2022) from a single large UK adult CF center — 209 patients, 12,618 analyzable sputum cultures after excluding cultures with no prior AST result to forward-fill — they trained five classifiers on multi-window (6-month, 24-month, all-time) cumulative antibiotic-exposure features, with patient-level train/test splitting and cross-validation throughout. XGBoost was the most consistently strong model, reaching AUCs of 0.75–0.80 across the five antibiotics, with SHAP analysis showing a telling split: in P. aeruginosa-positive cultures, longer-term resistance history mattered most, while in non-Pseudomonal cultures, recent resistance and recent antibiotic usage mattered more. The paper ships no public code repository — a miss relative to Track B's usual bar — but the pipeline architecture itself (multi-window exposure engineering, patient-safe cross-validation, a forward-fill-with-exclusion step for missing longitudinal labels, SHAP reporting) is a clean, reusable template for any longitudinal registry outcome-prediction problem, independent of the AMR topic. Single-center data, a decade-long window susceptible to AST-breakpoint drift, and IV-only antibiotic capture (no oral/inhaled exposure) are the paper's own stated limitations.

Plain-English glossary

AmpC derepression — A resistance mechanism in which a normally-suppressed bacterial beta-lactamase enzyme becomes active, breaking down beta-lactam antibiotics like ceftazidime and meropenem.（中文：一种耐药机制，指细菌体内通常被抑制的β-内酰胺酶被重新激活，从而分解头孢他啶、美罗培南等β-内酰胺类抗生素。）

AST (antibiotic susceptibility testing) — The lab test, typically taking 48–96 hours, that determines whether a cultured pathogen is resistant or susceptible to specific antibiotics.（中文：抗菌药物敏感性检测，通常需要48到96小时，用于判断培养出的病原体对特定抗生素是耐药还是敏感。）

Collateral resistance / collateral sensitivity — When resistance to one antibiotic increases (collateral resistance) or decreases (collateral sensitivity) the likelihood of resistance to a different antibiotic, because a shared underlying mechanism affects both.（中文：对一种抗生素的耐药性，因共享的底层机制而使细菌对另一种抗生素的耐药性增加（交叉耐药）或降低（交叉敏感）。）

Liverpool Epidemic Strain (LES) — A highly transmissible, typically more persistent and resistant strain of P. aeruginosa first identified in Liverpool that can spread between people with CF.（中文：利物浦流行株，一种高度可传播、通常更顽固、更耐药的铜绿假单胞菌株，最初在利物浦发现，可在CF患者之间传播。）

OprD loss — Loss of an outer-membrane porin protein that carbapenem antibiotics like meropenem need to enter the bacterial cell, conferring resistance.（中文：细菌外膜孔蛋白OprD的缺失，而美罗培南等碳青霉烯类抗生素需要通过该孔蛋白进入细菌细胞，缺失后产生耐药性。）

Pathoadaptation — The process by which a pathogen population evolves within a single chronically-infected patient over repeated treatment cycles, rather than simply being exposed to a drug once.（中文：病原适应性进化，指病原体种群在单个慢性感染患者体内、经过反复治疗周期不断演化的过程，而不是只接触一次药物。）

TRIPOD+AI — A reporting checklist (Transparent Reporting of a multivariable prediction model for Individual Prognosis Or Diagnosis, extended for AI) for transparently documenting how a clinical prediction model involving AI methods was built and validated.（中文：一套用于透明报告涉及AI方法的临床预测模型构建与验证过程的清单规范。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • Multi-window cumulative-exposure feature engineering (6-month / 24-month / all-time) for any longitudinal exposure variable.
  • Patient-level stratified train/test splitting and patient-level k-fold cross-validation to prevent leakage in longitudinal clinical data.
  • Within-patient forward-filling of missing longitudinal labels, paired with exclusion of cultures with no prior value and exclusion of forward-filled labels from the test set.
  • SHAP-based feature-importance reporting split by clinically meaningful subgroup (here, pathogen status).
  • TRIPOD+AI as a reporting-transparency checklist for any AI-involved clinical prediction model.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the AI-tooling / career-development priority. Buildable: Registry Longitudinal Multi-Window Exposure-Outcome Predictor Template — a parameterized pipeline implementing patient-level-safe splitting/CV, multi-window exposure features, forward-fill-with-exclusion-flag handling of missing labels, and SHAP reporting, aimed at any longitudinal registry outcome-prediction task. Input: any longitudinal patient-event table. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • Typical forward-fill gap length in any new dataset — long gaps relative to how fast the outcome changes call for a tighter exclusion threshold than "exclude if no prior value."
  • Whether the new outcome is subject to the same kind of temporal drift AST breakpoints showed here, which may call for per-era recalibration rather than one fixed model.
  • Single-center-to-multi-center transportability, given this paper's own finding that performance differed by subgroup for some antibiotics but not others.
  • Whether the 6-month/24-month/all-time windows are appropriate for a new outcome, or need re-deriving from that outcome's own dynamics.

JORDAN Before results — what's actually going on biologically with antimicrobial resistance in chronic CF infection? What makes it different from an acute infection? 在讲结果之前——慢性CF感染中的抗菌药物耐药性，生物学上到底发生了什么？它和急性感染有什么不同？

ALEX In CF, Pseudomonas aeruginosa — the dominant chronic lung pathogen — doesn't just get exposed to antibiotics once and resist or not; it lives in the airway for years and undergoes pathoadaptation, meaning the bacterial population itself evolves within a single patient over repeated treatment cycles. 在CF患者体内，铜绿假单胞菌（Pseudomonas aeruginosa）——占主导地位的慢性肺部病原体——不是只接触一次抗生素然后产生或不产生耐药；它会在气道内存活多年，并发生"病原适应性进化"（pathoadaptation），也就是说，细菌种群本身会在同一个患者体内、经过反复的治疗周期不断演化。Resistance to beta-lactams like ceftazidime and meropenem can arise through several distinct mechanisms stacking up over time: AmpC derepression, where a normally-suppressed beta-lactamase enzyme gets switched on; loss of OprD, an outer-membrane porin the drug needs to get into the cell; and efflux-pump upregulation, where the bacterium just pumps the drug back out faster than it can act. 对头孢他啶（ceftazidime）和美罗培南（meropenem）这类β-内酰胺类药物的耐药性，可能通过几种不同机制随时间叠加产生：AmpC去抑制（AmpC derepression，一种通常被抑制的β-内酰胺酶被重新激活）；OprD（药物进入细菌细胞所需的一种外膜孔蛋白）的缺失；以及外排泵（efflux pump）表达上调，让细菌能更快地把药物泵出体外。These mechanisms can produce collateral resistance or collateral sensitivity — meaning resistance to one drug can make the bug more or less likely to resist a completely different drug, because the underlying mechanism affects both. 这些机制可能产生"交叉耐药"（collateral resistance）或"交叉敏感"（collateral sensitivity）——也就是说，对一种药物的耐药性,可能让细菌对另一种完全不同的药物更容易或更不容易产生耐药，因为底层机制同时影响两者。

JORDAN And the Liverpool Epidemic Strain that gets mentioned — what's that? 论文里提到的"利物浦流行株"（Liverpool Epidemic Strain）是什么？

ALEX LES is a specific, highly transmissible strain of P. aeruginosa first identified in Liverpool that can spread between CF patients and tends to be more persistent and resistant than typical sporadic infections — it's tracked as its own variable in UK CF cohorts because it behaves differently from strain to strain. LES（利物浦流行株）是一种特定的、高度可传播的铜绿假单胞菌株，最初在利物浦被发现，可以在CF患者之间传播，而且通常比普通的散发性感染更顽固、更耐药——在英国的CF队列研究中，它会被作为一个独立变量单独追踪，因为不同菌株之间的行为表现并不相同。In this cohort, 34.9% of patients carried LES. 在这个队列中，34.9%的患者携带LES。

JORDAN And CFTR modulators — how do they fit into the resistance picture? CFTR调节剂（modulator）又如何与耐药性问题产生关联？

ALEX Modulator therapies like Trikafta partially restore CFTR protein function and meaningfully improve overall well-being, but even after successful modulator treatment, up to 60% of previously-infected patients remain chronically infected with P. aeruginosa. 像Trikafta这样的CFTR调节剂疗法，能部分恢复CFTR蛋白的功能，显著改善患者的整体健康状况，但即便调节剂治疗成功，之前被感染的患者中仍有高达60%会持续感染铜绿假单胞菌。So the authors' point is that cumulative lifetime antibiotic exposure isn't going away just because modulators exist — AMR becomes a life-course issue that this kind of prediction tool still needs to address even in the modulator era. 所以作者的观点是，即便有了调节剂，累积终生的抗生素暴露问题也不会消失——抗菌药物耐药性（AMR）会成为一个贯穿整个生命历程的问题，而这类预测工具即便在调节剂时代，仍然需要去应对它。

JORDAN So what was actually unknown before this paper — why hadn't anyone done this already? 那么，在这篇论文之前，到底有什么是未知的——为什么之前没人做过这个？

ALEX Routine antibiotic susceptibility testing, AST, takes 48 to 96 hours to come back, so it structurally cannot inform the antibiotic choice at the moment treatment starts, especially during an acute exacerbation when a clinician needs to act immediately. 常规的抗菌药物敏感性检测（antibiotic susceptibility testing，AST）需要48到96小时才能出结果，所以从结构上说，它根本无法在治疗开始的那一刻、为抗生素选择提供参考，尤其是在急性加重期（acute exacerbation），临床医生需要立刻采取行动的时候。ML-based prediction of resistance has already been applied in acute infection and critical-care settings, and even with pathogen whole-genome sequencing in acute bloodstream infections. 基于机器学习的耐药性预测，此前已经被应用于急性感染和重症监护场景，甚至在急性血流感染中结合病原体全基因组测序使用过。But the authors state plainly that, to their knowledge, nobody had applied this to the chronic lung-infection setting specifically — predicting resistance within the same patient over a decade of repeated, evolving infection is a fundamentally different and harder problem than predicting it for a single acute episode. 但作者明确指出，就他们所知，此前没有人把这套方法专门应用到慢性肺部感染场景——在同一名患者体内、针对长达十年、反复演化的感染预测耐药性，这与预测单次急性发作的耐药性，是一个本质上不同、也更困难的问题。

JORDAN Walk me through the cohort and the feature setup. 讲讲队列和特征设置。

ALEX Single center — the Adult CF Centre at Liverpool Heart and Chest Hospital — with a decade of EHR data, 2012 to 2022: 290 people with CF, 16,800 positive sputum cultures, and 72,964 intravenous antibiotic-days. 单中心研究——利物浦心胸医院成人CF中心——拥有十年的EHR数据，从2012年到2022年：290名CF患者，16,800次阳性痰培养，以及72,964个静脉抗生素治疗日。They built exposure features at the level of the sputum sample: cumulative antibiotic days and usage frequency, computed over three separate windows — the preceding 6 months, the preceding 24 months, and the entire prior study period. 他们在每个痰样本层级构建暴露特征：累积抗生素使用天数和使用频率，分别在三个不同的时间窗口内计算——前6个月、前24个月，以及整个此前的研究周期。For missing AST results — since not every antibiotic gets tested on every culture — they used within-patient forward filling, carrying forward the most recent prior result for that antibiotic in that patient, and excluded any culture with no prior result to carry forward at all, which dropped 746 cultures and left a final analytic set of 12,618 sputum cultures across 209 patients. 对于缺失的AST结果——因为不是每次培养都会检测每种抗生素——他们采用了患者内部的前向填充（forward filling），即用同一患者该抗生素最近一次的检测结果来填补缺失值，并剔除了完全没有任何历史结果可供填补的培养，这一步排除了746次培养，最终分析集为209名患者的12,618次痰培养。Train/test splitting and five-fold cross-validation were both done at the patient level, so no single patient's cultures could appear in both the training and test sets. 训练/测试集的划分和五折交叉验证都是在患者层级完成的，确保同一患者的培养数据不会同时出现在训练集和测试集中。

JORDAN Any baseline numbers worth flagging before we get to the model? 在讲模型之前，有没有什么基线数字值得提一下？

ALEX Resistance rates varied a lot by drug: 56.6% of samples were resistant to ciprofloxacin, 60.6% to ceftazidime, 46.0% to meropenem, 33.4% to piperacillin-tazobactam, and only 11.9% to tobramycin, which was the rarest resistance of the five. 不同药物的耐药率差异很大：56.6%的样本对环丙沙星耐药，60.6%对头孢他啶耐药，46.0%对美罗培南耐药，33.4%对哌拉西林/他唑巴坦耐药，而对妥布霉素的耐药率只有11.9%，是五种药物中最低的。Median patient age was 33, 42.1% were male, 55.5% had CF-related diabetes, and over a third carried the Liverpool Epidemic Strain. 患者的中位年龄为33岁，42.1%为男性，55.5%患有CF相关糖尿病，超过三分之一携带利物浦流行株。More than a third of samples showed multi-drug resistance overall, which the authors call out as illustrative of just how much of a burden this already is in a single cohort. 总体上超过三分之一的样本表现出多重耐药，作者特别指出，这说明在单个队列中，这一负担已经有多大。The study followed the TRIPOD+AI reporting standard, a checklist for transparently reporting how a multivariable prediction model for individual prognosis or diagnosis was built and validated when AI methods are involved — it's the kind of reporting discipline that makes a model's limitations auditable rather than buried in supplementary material. 这项研究遵循了TRIPOD+AI报告规范——一套用于在涉及AI方法时，透明报告个体预后或诊断多变量预测模型构建与验证过程的清单——这种报告规范能让模型的局限性变得可审查，而不是被埋在补充材料里。

ALEX Here's where I'd push back a little on the design. 这里我想对设计方式提出一点质疑。Forward-filling a missing AST result with the most recent prior value assumes resistance status is stable across the gap — but we just established that resistance in CF evolves within a patient over time through pathoadaptation, so a long gap between tests could easily mean the forward-filled label is stale by the time it's used to train or evaluate the model. 用最近一次的历史值去前向填充缺失的AST结果，等于假设耐药状态在这段间隔期内是稳定的——但我们刚才已经说过，CF患者体内的耐药性会随着病原适应性进化而发生变化，所以如果两次检测之间间隔很长，那么用来训练或评估模型的那个前向填充标签，很可能早已过时。The paper does exclude forward-filled labels from the test set specifically to avoid this contaminating evaluation, which is the right instinct, but it doesn't report how long the typical forward-fill gap actually is, so we can't tell how stale the training labels themselves might be. 论文确实特别把前向填充的标签排除在测试集之外，以避免污染评估结果，这个思路是对的，但论文并没有报告典型的前向填充间隔到底有多长，所以我们无法判断训练标签本身可能有多"过时"。

JORDAN What did the models actually achieve? 模型实际达到了什么效果？

ALEX Across five models — naive Bayes, logistic regression, support vector classifier, random forest, and XGB — XGB was the most consistently strong performer, with AUCs of 0.75 for ciprofloxacin, 0.80 for ceftazidime, 0.76 for meropenem, 0.79 for piperacillin-tazobactam, and 0.78 for tobramycin. 在五种模型中——朴素贝叶斯、逻辑回归、支持向量分类器、随机森林和XGB——XGB的表现最稳定，对环丙沙星（ciprofloxacin）的AUC为0.75，对头孢他啶（ceftazidime）为0.80，对美罗培南（meropenem）为0.76，对哌拉西林/他唑巴坦（piperacillin-tazobactam）为0.79，对妥布霉素（tobramycin）为0.78。Stratifying by P. aeruginosa status versus non-Pseudomonal organisms, performance was broadly similar between the two groups except for two antibiotics: the model performed significantly better on ceftazidime in the P. aeruginosa group, and significantly better on meropenem in the non-Pseudomonal group. 按铜绿假单胞菌（P. aeruginosa）阳性与非铜绿假单胞菌分组后，两组的表现总体相近，只有两种药物例外：模型对头孢他啶的预测在P. aeruginosa阳性组中表现明显更好，而对美罗培南的预测在非P. aeruginosa组中表现明显更好。

JORDAN And SHAP told them what, exactly? SHAP分析具体告诉了他们什么？

ALEX The clearest pattern: in the P. aeruginosa group, longer-term resistance history — the full prior period or the preceding 24 months — was consistently more predictive than recent 6-month resistance; in the non-Pseudomonal group, the opposite held, with recent resistance and recent antibiotic usage mattering more. 最清晰的模式是：在P. aeruginosa组中，更长期的耐药史——整个此前周期或前24个月——始终比近期6个月的耐药情况更具预测力；而在非铜绿假单胞菌组中，情况正好相反，近期耐药和近期抗生素使用反而更重要。That tracks with P. aeruginosa's chronic persistence and cumulative resistance buildup versus the more episodic nature of non-Pseudomonal infections. 这和P. aeruginosa的慢性持续性、累积耐药性积累的特点相符，而非铜绿假单胞菌感染则更具偶发性。They also found specific collateral-resistance signals: total prior colistimethate use predicted ciprofloxacin resistance, but in opposite directions in the two subgroups, and in the P. aeruginosa group, piperacillin-tazobactam usage was an important predictor of tobramycin resistance and vice versa. 他们还发现了具体的交叉耐药信号：既往多黏菌素（colistimethate）累积使用量能预测环丙沙星耐药性，但在两个亚组中方向相反；而在P. aeruginosa组中，哌拉西林/他唑巴坦的使用量是妥布霉素耐药性的重要预测因子，反过来也是如此。

JORDAN Anything surprising in why the Pa and non-Pa groups didn't differ more? 关于Pa组和非Pa组为什么没有表现出更大差异，有什么出乎意料的地方吗？

ALEX The authors actually found this a bit surprising themselves, given how notoriously adaptable P. aeruginosa is at generating resistance within a single chronic infection. 作者自己其实也觉得有点意外，因为铜绿假单胞菌在单次慢性感染中生成耐药性的能力是出了名的强、适应性极高。One explanation they raise is co-infection — people with CF frequently carry more than one pathogen in the airway at once, and that's not accounted for in traditional resistance testing, which usually profiles just one isolate per culture even though cross-talk between different members of the lung microbiota has been shown to influence resistance patterns and pathogenicity more broadly. 他们提出的一种解释是合并感染——CF患者的气道里经常同时携带不止一种病原体，而传统的耐药性检测通常只针对每次培养中的一个分离株进行分析，并没有考虑到这一点，尽管已有研究表明，肺部微生物群中不同成员之间的相互作用，会更广泛地影响耐药模式和致病性。They also point out that routine clinical AST typically tests only one isolate per sample, which can't capture the population diversity pathoadaptation produces within a single patient — future work incorporating whole-genome sequencing of multiple P. aeruginosa isolates per patient, the way it's already been used successfully in acute bloodstream infections, could resolve more of that hidden diversity. 他们还指出，常规临床AST通常只检测每个样本中的一个分离株，无法捕捉病原适应性进化在单个患者体内产生的群体多样性——未来如果能对同一患者的多个铜绿假单胞菌分离株进行全基因组测序，就像在急性血流感染中已经成功应用过的那样，就有可能解析出更多这种隐藏的多样性。

JORDAN Does the paper claim this is ready for clinical use? 论文有没有声称这已经可以用于临床？
