Show notes — miRNA network in CF sibling pairs (Track C, 2026-08-01)

Blurb. Mustafaoğlu and colleagues (Turkish Journal of Medical Sciences, 2025, open access) run the first miRNA expression study in nasal epithelial cells of CF sibling pairs who share a CFTR genotype but have discordant severity — a design that controls for genotype and much of the genetic/environmental background, isolating what varies above the DNA. Four patients from two families (F508del/G85E and F508del homozygous, all Class II), profiled on miRNA arrays. Six miRNAs were differentially expressed between severe and mild siblings (miR-449c-5p, miR-92b-3p, miR-34c-3p/-5p, miR-6732-5p, miR-4793-3p); target prediction + Enrichr enrichment nominated an inflammation module (CXCL1, CXCL2, DUSP1, GCLC, ICAM1, KIT, PRKAA2, PTGS2). The strongest, internally-consistent signal is a miR-449c-5p / CXCL1 axis: miR-449c-5p targets CXCL1, and CXCL1 was measured down ~5.28-fold in severe siblings (p=0.01). The honest caveat is n=4 with mostly predicted targets — hypothesis-generating, not validated. Where it lands: CF modifier / variant catalog as a non-genetic, expression-level modifier layer — a buildable cf-mirna-modifier-candidate-tracker that curates published CF miRNA hits and cross-references their targets against the known modifier-gene catalog to find convergence.

Plain-English glossary

  • Ciliogenesis — the formation of cilia; the miR-34/449 families regulate it, and cilia drive airway mucus clearance central to CF.（中文：纤毛发生，纤毛的形成过程；miR-34/449 家族调控它，而纤毛驱动的气道黏液清除是 CF 的核心。）
  • Discordant phenotype — same genotype, different disease severity between individuals (here, siblings).（中文：表型不一致，相同基因型但个体间（此处为同胞）疾病严重程度不同。）
  • Differentially expressed — a gene or miRNA present at significantly different levels between two groups (here, severe vs mild).（中文：差异表达，某基因或 miRNA 在两组间（此处为重症 vs 轻症）水平显著不同。）
  • Enrichment analysis — testing whether a gene list is over-represented in curated pathways (KEGG/Reactome/GO), scored by p/q-value.（中文：富集分析，检验一份基因列表是否在精选通路（KEGG/Reactome/GO）中被过度代表，用 p/q 值打分。）
  • microRNA (miRNA) — a ~20–24 nt non-coding RNA that down-regulates target genes by binding their 3′ UTR.（中文：微小 RNA，约 20–24 个核苷酸的非编码 RNA，通过结合靶基因的 3′ UTR 来下调它们。）
  • Nasal epithelial cells — accessible CFTR-expressing airway cells used as a surrogate for the lower airway.（中文：鼻上皮细胞，可获取的、表达 CFTR 的气道细胞，用作下气道的替代样本。）
  • Three-prime UTR (3′ UTR) — the untranslated tail of an mRNA where miRNAs bind to regulate it.（中文：3′ 非翻译区，mRNA 尾端不翻译的区段，miRNA 在此结合以调控它。）
  • Class II mutation — a CFTR trafficking-defect class (defined by F508del): protein is made but misfolds and fails to reach the cell surface.（中文：II 类突变，一种 CFTR 转运缺陷类（由 F508del 定义）：蛋白能合成但错误折叠、无法到达细胞表面。）

Full citation

Mustafaoğlu A, Noyan S, Akkaya Ulum YZ, Gür D, et al. miRNA-target gene network analysis in siblings with cystic fibrosis and phenotypic variability. Turk J Med Sci. 2025. PMID 40933967; PMC PMC12419052; DOI 10.55730/1300-0144.6054.

Methods / tools to lift

  • Sibling-pair discordant design — same CFTR genotype + shared background isolates modifiers above the DNA; strips confounding vs unrelated-patient comparisons.
  • Nasal epithelial miRNA arrays — accessible CFTR-expressing airway surrogate for expression profiling.
  • Target prediction + Enrichr enrichment — map differentially-expressed miRNAs to target genes, then test target lists against KEGG/Reactome/GO libraries (p/q/combined score).
  • miRNA–mRNA interaction network — find interacting target clusters (here the miR-449c-5p → CXCL1/CXCL2/PTGS2/ICAM1 inflammation cluster).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: CF modifier / variant catalog (non-genetic, expression-level modifier layer).

Paste-ready MVP: cf-mirna-modifier-candidate-tracker (S–M) — living table collecting, from published CF miRNA studies, every differentially-expressed miRNA + predicted/measured targets, annotated with effect direction, tissue, and phenotype comparison; cross-reference targets against the known CF-modifier gene catalog to flag convergence across independent cohorts.

What to verify first

  • Power — n=4 across two families is hypothesis-generating only; every candidate needs replication in an independent, larger CF cohort.
  • Predicted vs measured targets — most targets are database predictions; a luciferase/direct-binding assay is needed to confirm an miRNA represses a given 3′ UTR. Only CXCL1 has a measured fold-change.
  • Tissue + direction — CXCL1 measured in nasal (upper-airway surrogate); check whether the shift holds in the lower airway where severe disease manifests.
  • Confounding by disease state — severe patients have more infection/inflammation; a chemokine/miRNA difference could be a consequence, not a cause. Needs longitudinal/interventional data.
  • Curation-and-lookup method over published expression studies; no registry-internal data.

Gap-map / catalog

  • Closes: documents a non-genetic (miRNA expression) modifier layer in CF; nominates a specific candidate axis (miR-449c-5p ↓CXCL1, ~5.28-fold, p=0.01) plus an inflammation target module (CXCL1/CXCL2/PTGS2/ICAM1) and secondary targets (DUSP1 via miR-92b-3p, KIT via miR-4793-3p); reinforces the sibling-discordant design as a genotype-controlled modifier probe.
  • Leaves open: no genetic (DNA) modifier locus; targets mostly predicted, not functionally validated; n=4 unpowered; nasal-vs-lower-airway and cause-vs-consequence unresolved; no convergence tested against the existing modifier-gene catalog (the tracker's job).

ALEX Welcome back. 欢迎回来。 Today's Track C paper is "miRNA-target gene network analysis in siblings with cystic fibrosis and phenotypic variability," by Ayşe Mustafaoğlu and colleagues. 今天 Track C 的论文是 Ayşe Mustafaoğlu 及其同事发表的《囊性纤维化同胞中的 miRNA-靶基因网络分析与表型变异》。 It's in the Turkish Journal of Medical Sciences, 2025, open access. 它发表在 2025 年的《Turkish Journal of Medical Sciences》上，开放获取。 We're reading it because it goes after the oldest puzzle in CF genetics — why two people with the same CFTR mutation get sick to very different degrees — using a layer we don't often cover: microRNA. 我们读它，是因为它去追问 CF 遗传学里最古老的谜题——为什么两个带有相同 CFTR 突变的人，患病的严重程度却差别很大——用的是我们不常涉及的一层：微小 RNA（microRNA）。

JORDAN Let me get the vocabulary first. 先让我把术语理清楚。 What's a microRNA, and why would it explain differences between siblings with the same mutation? 什么是微小 RNA，它又为什么能解释携带相同突变的同胞之间的差异？

ALEX A microRNA, or miRNA, is a tiny non-coding RNA, only about twenty to twenty-four nucleotides long. 微小 RNA（miRNA）是一种很小的非编码 RNA，只有大约 20 到 24 个核苷酸长。 It doesn't code for a protein; instead it regulates other genes after they've been transcribed. 它不编码蛋白，而是在其他基因被转录之后去调控它们。 It binds to the three-prime untranslated region — the tail end of a messenger RNA — and either blocks translation or triggers the mRNA's degradation, so it turns target genes down. 它结合到信使 RNA（mRNA）的 3′ 非翻译区（3′ untranslated region，即 mRNA 尾端的一段），要么阻止翻译、要么触发 mRNA 降解，从而把靶基因调低。 One miRNA can regulate many targets, so a shift in a single miRNA can ripple across a whole network of genes. 一个 miRNA 可以调控许多靶点，所以单个 miRNA 的变动，会在整个基因网络里产生连锁反应。 Here's why that's relevant to identical genotypes. 这就是它与相同基因型相关的原因。 The CFTR mutation is fixed and shared between siblings, but miRNA expression is not fixed — it varies with environment, cell state, and the rest of the genome. CFTR 突变是固定的、同胞之间共享的，但 miRNA 的表达不是固定的——它随环境、细胞状态以及基因组的其余部分而变化。 So if severe and mild siblings differ in which miRNAs are switched on, that's a candidate mechanism for modifying disease severity downstream of the CFTR defect itself. 所以，如果重症和轻症的同胞在"哪些 miRNA 被开启"上不同，那这就是一个候选机制，能在 CFTR 缺陷本身的下游去修饰疾病的严重程度。

JORDAN And they picked siblings on purpose. 而且他们特意挑了同胞。

ALEX That's the whole design logic, and it's clever for a small study. 这正是整个设计逻辑，对一个小型研究来说很巧妙。 Siblings share not just the CFTR genotype but roughly half their genetic background and much of their early environment, which strips away a lot of the noise that confounds unrelated-patient comparisons. 同胞不仅共享 CFTR 基因型，还共享大约一半的遗传背景和许多早期环境，这剥离掉了很多在无关患者比较中会造成混杂的噪声。 They studied four CF patients from two families — two siblings each — with discordant phenotypes, meaning one sibling clearly more severe than the other. 他们研究了来自两个家庭的四位 CF 患者——每家两个同胞——表型不一致（discordant），也就是一个同胞明显比另一个更严重。 In family one, both siblings carried F508del paired with G85E; the twelve-year-old brother had mild hepatic involvement and a predicted FEV-one of eighty-eight, while his sixteen-year-old sister had cirrhosis, CF-related diabetes, and higher sweat chloride. 在第一个家庭，两个同胞都携带 F508del 与 G85E 的组合；十二岁的弟弟只有轻度肝脏受累、预测 FEV1（一秒用力呼气量）为 88，而十六岁的姐姐则有肝硬化、CF 相关糖尿病，汗液氯离子也更高。 In family two, both were homozygous for F508del; the younger sibling had recurrent lung infection and the other progressive severe disease. 在第二个家庭，两人都是 F508del 纯合；较小的同胞有反复肺部感染，另一个则是进行性重症。 Every patient carried Class II mutations, the trafficking-defect class that F508del defines. 每位患者都携带 II 类突变，也就是 F508del 所定义的转运缺陷类。 They collected nasal epithelial cells — an accessible, CFTR-expressing airway surrogate — and profiled miRNA expression on arrays. 他们采集了鼻上皮细胞——一种可获取的、表达 CFTR 的气道替代样本——并用芯片检测了 miRNA 的表达。

JORDAN What did the miRNA arrays turn up? miRNA 芯片查出了什么？

ALEX Six miRNAs came up differentially expressed between severe and mild: miR-449c-5p, miR-92b-3p, miR-34c-3p, miR-34c-5p, miR-6732-5p, and miR-4793-3p. 有六个 miRNA 在重症与轻症之间差异表达：miR-449c-5p、miR-92b-3p、miR-34c-3p、miR-34c-5p、miR-6732-5p 和 miR-4793-3p。 Then they asked what those miRNAs regulate, using bioinformatic target-prediction databases and enrichment tools like Enrichr, which tests target gene lists against libraries such as KEGG, Reactome, and Gene Ontology. 然后他们用生物信息学的靶点预测数据库、以及像 Enrichr 这样的富集分析工具，去问这些 miRNA 调控什么——Enrichr 会把靶基因列表拿去和 KEGG、Reactome、基因本体论（Gene Ontology）等库做比对。 The candidate target genes were CXCL1, CXCL2, DUSP1, GCLC, ICAM1, KIT, PRKAA2, and PTGS2. 候选靶基因是 CXCL1、CXCL2、DUSP1、GCLC、ICAM1、KIT、PRKAA2 和 PTGS2。 If you know CF airway biology, that list jumps out: CXCL1 and CXCL2 are neutrophil-recruiting chemokines, ICAM1 is an adhesion molecule in inflammation, and PTGS2 is COX-2, the prostaglandin enzyme — this is an inflammation module. 如果你了解 CF 气道生物学，这份清单会立刻跳出来：CXCL1 和 CXCL2 是招募中性粒细胞的趋化因子，ICAM1 是炎症中的黏附分子，PTGS2 就是 COX-2、即前列腺素合成酶——这是一个炎症模块。 The network analysis showed a strong interaction cluster among the miR-449c-5p targets specifically — CXCL1, CXCL2, PTGS2, and ICAM1. 网络分析显示，恰恰是 miR-449c-5p 的靶点之间——CXCL1、CXCL2、PTGS2、ICAM1——形成了一个强互作簇。

JORDAN The other miRNAs — do their targets tell a different story from the CXCL1 cluster? 其他那些 miRNA——它们的靶点讲的是和 CXCL1 簇不同的故事吗？

ALEX They spread the map out a bit, which is part of why the authors focus on the miR-449c cluster. 它们把这张图铺得更开了一些，这也是作者聚焦 miR-449c 簇的部分原因。 Their target-gene validation step narrowed the candidate miRNAs to three that they carried forward — miR-34c-3p, miR-92b-3p, and miR-449c-5p. 他们的靶基因验证步骤把候选 miRNA 缩窄到往下带的三个——miR-34c-3p、miR-92b-3p 和 miR-449c-5p。 Of those, CXCL1, CXCL2, ICAM1, and PTGS2 came back as targets of miR-449c-5p, while DUSP1 was a target of miR-92b-3p and KIT a target of miR-4793-3p. 其中，CXCL1、CXCL2、ICAM1、PTGS2 是 miR-449c-5p 的靶点，而 DUSP1 是 miR-92b-3p 的靶点，KIT 是 miR-4793-3p 的靶点。 That matters biologically because DUSP1 is a phosphatase that dampens stress-and-inflammation signaling, and KIT is a growth-factor receptor tied to cell differentiation — so the wider target set touches inflammation resolution and epithelial cell fate, not just the chemokine burst. 这在生物学上很重要，因为 DUSP1 是一个抑制应激与炎症信号的磷酸酶，KIT 是一个与细胞分化相关的生长因子受体——所以更广的靶点集触及炎症消退和上皮细胞命运，而不只是趋化因子的爆发。 The miR-34c family is also worth a note: the miR-34 family is a well-studied set of miRNAs in epithelial biology and ciliogenesis, and cilia function is central to airway clearance in CF, so its appearance here isn't random. miR-34c 家族也值得一提：miR-34 家族是上皮生物学和纤毛发生（ciliogenesis）中被充分研究的一组 miRNA，而纤毛功能是 CF 气道清除的核心，所以它出现在这里并非偶然。 The honest read is that the strongest, most internally-consistent signal is the miR-449c-5p / inflammatory-chemokine axis, and the rest is a broader candidate net. 诚实的解读是：最强、内部最自洽的信号是 miR-449c-5p / 炎症趋化因子轴，其余的是一张更宽的候选网。

JORDAN How solid is the enrichment step — the leap from a miRNA list to "this is about inflammation"? 富集这一步有多牢靠——从一份 miRNA 列表跳到"这跟炎症有关"？

ALEX That leap is exactly where you should apply pressure, because it's the softest joint in the pipeline. 这个跳跃恰恰是你该施压的地方，因为它是整条流水线里最软的关节。 Enrichr and similar tools take a gene list and ask which curated pathway libraries — KEGG, Reactome, Gene Ontology biological process — are over-represented, scoring with a p-value, an adjusted q-value, and a combined rank score. Enrichr 之类的工具拿一份基因列表，去问哪些精选的通路库——KEGG、Reactome、基因本体论的生物过程——被过度代表，并用 p 值、校正后的 q 值和一个组合排名分数来打分。 The catch is that the input gene list here is itself mostly predicted, so you're doing enrichment on top of prediction — two inferential layers stacked, each with its own false-positive rate. 关键在于，这里的输入基因列表本身大多是预测出来的，所以你是在预测之上做富集——两层推断叠在一起，每层都有自己的假阳性率。 The one hard measurement — CXCL1 down five-point-two-eight-fold — is what keeps the inflammation story from being purely computational, but it's a single gene in four patients. 唯一的硬测量——CXCL1 下降 5.28 倍——是让这个炎症故事不至于纯属计算推演的东西，但它只是四位患者里的单个基因。 So I'd describe the enrichment as a plausible direction, well-aligned with CF airway biology, that earns a targeted follow-up rather than a conclusion. 所以我会把这个富集描述为一个合理的方向，与 CF 气道生物学吻合得很好，它值得一个有针对性的后续，而不是一个结论。

JORDAN And they had one measured expression change, not just prediction. 而且他们有一个实测的表达变化，不只是预测。

ALEX Right, and that's the anchor number. 对，那就是关键的锚点数字。 CXCL1 expression was decreased about five-point-two-eight-fold in the severe siblings compared with the mild, with a p-value of zero-point-zero-one. 在重症同胞中，CXCL1 的表达比轻症降低了约 5.28 倍，p 值为 0.01。 That direction is worth pausing on: miR-449c-5p targets CXCL1, so higher miR-449c-5p would push CXCL1 down, and CXCL1 is lower in the severe patients — the pieces are internally consistent, pointing at a miR-449c-5p / CXCL1 axis tied to inflammation. 这个方向值得停一下：miR-449c-5p 靶向 CXCL1，所以更高的 miR-449c-5p 会把 CXCL1 压低，而 CXCL1 在重症患者里确实更低——各部分内部自洽，指向一个与炎症相关的 miR-449c-5p / CXCL1 轴。 The authors' conclusion is appropriately narrow: they highlight miR-449c-5p's interaction with CXCL1 and the other inflammatory targets, and call for functional analysis of miR-449c-5p as the next step. 作者的结论恰如其分地克制：他们强调 miR-449c-5p 与 CXCL1 及其他炎症靶点的互作，并呼吁把对 miR-449c-5p 的功能分析作为下一步。 The design push I'd make is obvious and they say it themselves — this is four patients. 我要提的设计质疑很明显，而且他们自己也说了——这只有四位患者。 The target genes are predicted by databases, and only CXCL1 has a measured fold-change, so most of this is a hypothesis-generating network, not a validated mechanism. 靶基因是数据库预测的，只有 CXCL1 有实测的倍数变化，所以这大部分是一个生成假设的网络，而不是一个被验证的机制。

JORDAN Why nasal cells at all — why not just draw blood, which is easier? 为什么偏偏用鼻细胞——为什么不直接抽血，那不是更容易吗？

ALEX Because for a CFTR-driven disease, the tissue has to actually express CFTR and sit in the affected organ system, and blood does neither. 因为对于一个由 CFTR 驱动的疾病，样本组织必须真正表达 CFTR、并且处于受累的器官系统里，而血液两者都不满足。 Nasal epithelial cells are a long-standing surrogate in CF: they're airway epithelium, they express CFTR, and you can brush them from the nose without a bronchoscopy. 鼻上皮细胞在 CF 里是一个由来已久的替代样本：它们是气道上皮、表达 CFTR，而且可以从鼻腔刷取，不需要做支气管镜。 The whole logic of measuring miRNA in nasal cells rather than blood is that you want the regulatory state of the cells where the disease plays out, and a blood miRNA profile would mostly reflect circulating immune cells, not airway epithelium. 在鼻细胞而非血液里测 miRNA 的整个逻辑是：你想要的是疾病真正上演的那些细胞的调控状态，而血液 miRNA 谱大多反映的是循环免疫细胞，而不是气道上皮。 The trade-off is that the nose is an upper-airway surrogate for the lower airway where severe lung disease actually happens, so it's the accessible stand-in, not the target tissue itself. 代价是鼻腔是下气道的上气道替代样本，而重症肺病其实发生在下气道，所以它是可获取的替身，而不是靶组织本身。 That distinction is exactly the kind of thing we'd want to flag when we carry a candidate forward. 这个区别，正是我们往下带一个候选者时想标注清楚的那类东西。

JORDAN So where does this land for us? 那这对我们意味着什么落点？

ALEX This lands on the CF modifier and variant catalog — specifically a non-genetic, expression-level modifier layer. 这个落点在 CF 修饰因子与变异目录上——具体说，是一个非遗传的、表达层面的修饰因子层。 Most of our Track C catalog is DNA — modifier genes and their variants. 我们 Track C 目录里的大多数条目是 DNA——修饰基因及其变异。 This paper is a reminder that phenotype can also be modified above the DNA, through regulatory RNA that varies even when the genotype doesn't, and it nominates specific candidates — miR-449c-5p and an inflammatory target module — worth carrying forward. 这篇论文提醒我们：表型也可以在 DNA 之上被修饰，通过即使基因型不变也会变化的调控 RNA，而它提名了具体的候选者——miR-449c-5p 和一个炎症靶点模块——值得往下带。 So the buildable artifact is a cf-mirna-modifier-candidate-tracker. 所以可构建的东西是一个 cf-mirna-modifier-candidate-tracker（CF miRNA 修饰因子候选追踪器）。 The MVP: a living table that collects, from published CF miRNA studies, every differentially-expressed miRNA and its predicted or measured target genes, annotated with direction of effect, tissue, and phenotype comparison, then cross-references the targets against the known CF-modifier gene catalog to flag convergence — for instance, does an miRNA target sit near or upstream of a gene we already track as a modifier?
