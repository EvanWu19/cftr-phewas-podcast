Blurb

Maharlou, Fierek, Maier, and Oeltze-Jafra (2026, Studies in Health Technology and Informatics) — abstract-only (two-page conference proceedings, no PMCID, closed access) — describe an Isolation Forest-based anomaly detector run across the P4D (Personalized, Predictive, Precise, and Preventive Medicine for Major Depression) cohort's multidimensional clinical variables, with flagged outliers visually sanity-checked via t-SNE projection. The paper's actual contribution is layering current-generation LLMs (GPT-5, GPT-4, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Llama 3) on top of the fixed detection step to generate concise, patient-specific natural-language explanations of each flagged anomaly for clinical stakeholders, evaluated via an unspecified "reasoning-based framework" against clinical assessments. GPT-5 was reported most clinically helpful and format-consistent; Llama 3 showed limitations in "contextual precision" — no quantitative numbers are given in the abstract for any of this. The result is integrated into the existing P4D Dashboard. Explicitly paired with ep3 in today's closing exchange: a model-free distance ensemble generates auditable flags; this paper's pipeline adds a natural-language explanation layer on top so a reviewer can judge, in seconds, whether a flag is a data error or a genuine clinical event. Buildable artifact: a flag-explainer harness that prompts an LLM (both an API-based model and a comparable open-weight local model, given data-residency constraints) to generate a structured plausibility explanation for any existing candidate-generation flag, evaluated against a small human-adjudicated case set.

  • Clinical data dashboard — a monitoring interface a study team uses to watch incoming data quality in near real time during an active clinical study.（中文：研究团队在临床研究进行期间用来近乎实时监控数据质量的监测界面。）
  • "4 Ps" precision-medicine framing — an organizing philosophy (Personalized, Predictive, Precise, Preventive) for structuring a clinical research program around four complementary goals.（中文：一种组织理念（个性化、预测性、精准化、预防性），围绕四个互补目标来构建临床研究项目。）
  • t-SNE (t-distributed stochastic neighbor embedding) — a dimensionality-reduction technique used here as a visual plausibility check, not a formal validation metric, to see whether flagged points look visually separated from the rest of the data cloud.（中文：一种降维技术，本文中用作一种视觉合理性核查而非正式验证指标，用于查看被标记的点是否在视觉上与其他数据点云分离。）
