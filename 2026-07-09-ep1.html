How did this episode land for you?

Track A — The landscape of tolerated genetic variation in humans and primates (Gao et al., Science 2023)

Blurb. Gao and colleagues sequenced 809 individuals across 233 primate species and cataloged 4.3 million common missense variants that map one-to-one onto human protein positions. Because humans and other primates share near-identical protein sequences, variants that are common (and thus tolerated by selection) in another primate are almost always benign in humans: high-confidence ClinVar variants seen in at least one nonhuman primate were benign/likely-benign ~99% of the time, versus 63% for ClinVar overall and 80% for gnomAD-observed variants. They reclassified >4 million previously-uncertain human missense variants as likely benign (>50× ClinVar) and trained PrimateAI-3D, a 3D-convolutional network on voxelized AlphaFold structures, which beat 15 other predictors across six clinical benchmarks. Critically for this project, CFTR is the #1 of 39 genes showing human-specific relaxed selective constraint. Where it lands: the Karen Raraigh / variant-interpretation priority. Buildable artifact: a CFTR missense benign-evidence annotator that joins primate presence + PrimateAI-3D scores against current CFTR2/ClinVar labels and surfaces discordances.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP framework (BS/BP codes) — the standard rulebook for classifying a variant, which assigns weighted evidence codes; BS = benign-strong, BP = benign-supporting.（中文：ACMG/AMP 是变异分类的标准规则体系，会给出加权的证据代码；BS 是"良性-强"，BP 是"良性-支持"。）
  • ångström (Å) — a length unit equal to 0.1 nanometer, roughly the size of a single atom; the voxel grid used 2 Å cubes.（中文：埃是长度单位，等于 0.1 纳米，大约是单个原子的尺寸；体素网格用的是 2 埃的立方体。）
  • AlphaFold DB — a database of protein 3D structures predicted from sequence by the AlphaFold deep-learning system; used here as the structural input.（中文：AlphaFold DB 是用 AlphaFold 深度学习系统从序列预测出的蛋白质三维结构数据库；这里作为结构输入。）
  • deep mutational scanning (DMS) — a lab assay that experimentally measures the functional effect of many amino-acid substitutions in a protein at once.（中文：深度突变扫描是一种实验方法，一次性测量蛋白质中大量氨基酸替换的功能效应。）
  • HGVS — the standardized grammar for writing sequence variants unambiguously (e.g., c.1521_1523delCTT).（中文：HGVS 是无歧义书写序列变异的标准化命名规范，例如 c.1521_1523delCTT。）
  • homology modeling — building an approximate 3D structure for a protein by using the known structure of a related protein as a template.（中文：同源建模是借用一个亲缘蛋白质的已知结构作模板，为目标蛋白质构建近似三维结构。）
  • missense variant — a single-nucleotide change that substitutes one amino acid for another in the protein.（中文：错义变异是一个单核苷酸改变，使蛋白质中一个氨基酸被另一个替换。）
  • ortholog — the corresponding gene/residue in another species descended from a common ancestor.（中文：直系同源是指另一物种中源自共同祖先的对应基因或残基。）
  • protein language model — a model trained (like a text language model) to predict masked amino acids from sequence context, yielding a variant-effect signal.（中文：蛋白质语言模型像文本语言模型一样，训练去根据序列上下文预测被遮蔽的氨基酸，从而给出变异效应信号。）
  • selective constraint — how strongly natural selection removes damaging variants from a gene; "relaxed" constraint means fewer are removed.（中文：选择约束指自然选择清除某基因中有害变异的强度；"放松"的约束意味着被清除的更少。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant with insufficient evidence to call benign or pathogenic.（中文：VUS 是意义未明变异，证据不足以判为良性或致病。）
  • voxelization — chopping the 3D space around a residue into a grid of small cubes (voxels) so a 3D convolutional network can process protein structure.（中文：体素化是把某残基周围的三维空间切成小立方体（体素）的网格，让三维卷积网络能处理蛋白质结构。）
  • whole-genome sequencing (WGS) — reading an organism's complete DNA sequence.（中文：全基因组测序是读取一个生物完整的 DNA 序列。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • Primate common-variant resource — 4.3M likely-benign missense variants at primad.basespace.illumina.com (noncommercial license on request); a ClinVar-independent benign catalog.
  • PrimateAI-3D — semi-supervised 3D-CNN on voxelized AlphaFold/HHpred structures; code at github.com/Illumina/PrimateAI-3D; scores available on request. Trained on 4.5M benign missense variants with a 3-part loss (benign-vs-unknown, masked-residue fill-in, protein-LM rank).
  • Ortholog mapping — 1:1 residue mapping to GRCh38; drop positions lacking unambiguous 1:1 mapping or with nonconcordant translation.
  • Constraint modeling — observed vs expected variant counts via a trinucleotide mutation-rate model, per species; intersection of two orthogonal methods to call human-specific constraint shifts (39 genes; CFTR ranked #1).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP:

CFTR missense benign-evidence annotator (S–M): input = list of CFTR missense variants in HGVS; for each, look up the orthologous residue substitution in the primate common-variant resource + PrimateAI-3D score, join against current CFTR2/ClinVar classification, and output a table flagging "primate-supported benign" candidates that are currently VUS/pathogenic (i.e., discordances to discuss with a CF geneticist).

What to verify first

  • Licensing/access terms for the primate variant scores and PrimateAI-3D predictions (noncommercial, on request).
  • Per-variant, whether the benign signal is a directly observed primate variant vs an imputed PrimateAI-3D score (only ~6.3% of possible human missense variants are directly covered; 98% of primate variants are <0.01% AF).
  • Treat primate tolerance as one weighted ACMG line of evidence, not a standalone verdict: the authors found 36 ClinVar "pathogenic" variants tolerated in primates, of which 8 → VUS and 9 hypomorphic/mild on review.
  • Local AlphaFold structure confidence (pLDDT) at specific CFTR residues before trusting structure-based scores; 13.5% of proteins were homology-modeled.
  • Method transfer only — no claim about how any registry stores or curates genotype data.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读paper时间。 Today's Track A paper is "The landscape of tolerated genetic variation in humans and primates," by Hong Gao and colleagues, published in Science in 2023. 今天 Track A 的论文是 Hong Gao 等人 2023 年发表在《Science》上的《人类与灵长类中可耐受遗传变异的全景图》。 It is the paper behind PrimateAI-3D, and it is the one where CFTR shows up by name as a gene under unusually relaxed selection in humans. 这就是 PrimateAI-3D 背后的那篇论文，而且 CFTR 在文中被点名为一个在人类中受到异常放松的选择压力的基因。 We're reading it because it hands you a benign-evidence resource you can point straight at CFTR missense variants. 我们读它，是因为它给了你一个可以直接用在 CFTR 错义变异上的"良性证据"资源。

JORDAN Before we go further, define a couple of terms for me. 在往下讲之前，先给我定义几个词。 What is a "missense variant," and what does "tolerated" mean here? 什么是"错义变异 (missense variant)"，这里说的"可耐受 (tolerated)"又是什么意思？

ALEX A missense variant is a single DNA change that swaps one amino acid in the protein for a different one. 错义变异是指一个单碱基 DNA 改变，使蛋白质中的一个氨基酸被换成另一个氨基酸。 It does not truncate the protein; it just substitutes a residue, and the effect can range from harmless to disease-causing. 它不会截断蛋白质，只是替换一个残基，其后果可以从完全无害到致病不等。 "Tolerated" means natural selection has let the variant persist at appreciable frequency in a population, which is indirect evidence that it doesn't badly break the protein. "可耐受"意味着自然选择让这个变异在某个群体中以可观的频率保留下来，这间接说明它没有严重破坏蛋白质功能。 The core logic of the paper is: humans and other primates share almost identical protein sequences, so a variant that is common and tolerated in, say, a macaque is probably benign in humans too. 论文的核心逻辑是：人类和其他灵长类的蛋白质序列几乎完全相同，所以一个在比如猕猴中常见且被耐受的变异，在人类中很可能也是良性的。 Chimpanzees and humans share 99.4% protein sequence identity, which is what makes this transfer credible. 黑猩猩和人类的蛋白质序列一致性达到 99.4%，正是这一点让这种跨物种推断可信。

JORDAN And how does that connect to the clinical labels Runyu actually cares about? 那这跟 Runyu 真正关心的临床标签有什么关系？

ALEX In clinical variant interpretation we use the ACMG/AMP framework, which assigns evidence codes. 在临床变异解读中，我们使用 ACMG/AMP 框架，它会给出证据代码。 Two of those codes, BS and BP, are benign-strong and benign-supporting — reasons to call a variant harmless. 其中两个代码 BS 和 BP，分别是"良性-强"和"良性-支持"，是判定一个变异无害的理由。 A large, trustworthy catalog of benign missense variants is exactly the kind of thing that feeds a BS or BP call. 一个庞大且可信的良性错义变异目录，正是可以支撑 BS 或 BP 判定的那种东西。 That's the through-line for today: this paper builds that catalog at a scale nothing else matches. 这就是今天的主线：这篇论文以其他资源无法企及的规模构建了这样一个目录。

JORDAN So what was actually unsolved before this? 那在这篇论文之前，究竟有什么没解决？

ALEX The bottleneck in genomic medicine is that most variants are variants of uncertain significance, or VUS. 基因组医学的瓶颈在于，绝大多数变异都是"意义未明变异"，也就是 VUS（临床意义不确定、既不能判为致病也不能判为良性的变异）。 Out of more than 70 million possible protein-altering variants in the human genome, only about 0.1% are annotated in clinical databases like ClinVar. 在人类基因组中超过 7000 万个可能的蛋白质改变变异里，只有大约 0.1% 在 ClinVar 这样的临床数据库中有注释。 The rest are unlabeled, and the reason is simple: most human variants are individually very rare, so you never see enough patients to establish their effect. 其余的都没有标签，原因很简单：大多数人类变异各自都极其罕见，你永远看不到足够多的患者来确立它们的效应。 The clever move here is to stop relying only on human data. 这里聪明的做法，是不再只依赖人类数据。 Instead of waiting for rare human variants to accumulate, you borrow common variants from 233 other primate species that have been running natural selection experiments for millions of years. 与其等待罕见的人类变异慢慢积累，不如从另外 233 个灵长类物种借用常见变异——这些物种已经进行了数百万年的自然选择"实验"。 Earlier work had tried this with a handful of species; the open question was whether it scaled, and how far out the primate tree you could trust the signal. 早期工作用少数几个物种尝试过这个思路；悬而未决的问题是它能否规模化，以及在灵长类进化树上你能信任这个信号到多远的分支。

ALEX There was also a training-data problem for machine learning. 这里还有一个机器学习的训练数据问题。 Variant effect predictors need labeled benign and pathogenic examples, but the labeled pool is tiny and biased. 变异效应预测器需要有标签的良性和致病样本，但有标签的样本池又小又有偏。 Many existing predictors were trained on ClinVar itself, which creates circularity when you then benchmark them on ClinVar. 许多现有的预测器就是在 ClinVar 上训练的，当你再用 ClinVar 去评测它们时，就产生了循环论证。 The primate strategy sidesteps that: the benign labels come from population frequency in other species, not from human clinical annotation. 灵长类策略绕开了这个问题：良性标签来自其他物种中的群体频率，而不是来自人类临床注释。

JORDAN Why can't we just use common human variants as the benign labels and skip the monkeys? 为什么我们不能干脆用常见的人类变异作为良性标签，而跳过猴子呢？

ALEX Because human benign discovery plateaus. 因为人类良性变异的发现会触顶。 The authors point out that the number of common human missense variants — those above 0.1% allele frequency — saturates at only about 100,000 no matter how many more people you sequence. 作者指出，常见人类错义变异——那些等位基因频率高于 0.1% 的——无论你再多测多少人，都会在大约 10 万个左右饱和。 There just aren't that many variants that are both common and benign in our species. 在我们这个物种里，既常见又良性的变异就是没有那么多。 The 233 primate species, by contrast, are independent evolutionary experiments, so each one contributes a fresh set of tolerated variants that humans never sampled — that's how they reached 4.3 million. 相比之下，233 个灵长类物种是相互独立的进化实验，所以每一个都贡献了一批人类从未采样到的、被耐受的变异——他们正是这样达到 430 万个的。 Humans and primates end up playing complementary roles: humans anchor the clinical labels, primates supply the benign training mass. 人类和灵长类最终扮演互补的角色：人类锚定临床标签，灵长类提供良性训练的体量。

JORDAN Okay, walk me through what they actually did. 好，带我过一遍他们具体做了什么。

ALEX They obtained whole-genome sequencing — reading the full DNA sequence — for 809 individuals across 233 primate species, 703 of them newly sequenced. 他们对 233 个灵长类物种的 809 个个体做了全基因组测序（即读取完整的 DNA 序列），其中 703 个是新测序的。 From that they cataloged 4.3 million unique common missense variants that map cleanly one-to-one onto human protein positions. 由此他们编录了 430 万个能与人类蛋白质位置干净地一一对应的、独特的常见错义变异。 For calibration: compared with gnomAD, a human reference database of 141,456 individuals, the primate cohort actually contained about 20% more exome variants despite sequencing one one-hundred-and-seventy-fifth as many individuals. 作为一个标度：与包含 141,456 个人的人类参考数据库 gnomAD 相比，灵长类队列实际上多包含约 20% 的外显子变异，尽管测序的个体数只有其 1/175。 That's how much diversity is sitting in those species. 那些物种中蕴藏的遗传多样性就是如此之高。

ALEX Then they built PrimateAI-3D to impute the variants they didn't directly observe. 然后他们构建了 PrimateAI-3D，用来推断那些没有直接观测到的变异。 It's a semi-supervised 3D convolutional neural network — and here's the part worth pausing on for the biology — it operates on the voxelized three-dimensional structure of the protein. 它是一个半监督的三维卷积神经网络——这里值得为生物学部分停一下——它作用于蛋白质的"体素化"三维结构。 Voxelizing means they take the folded protein and chop the space around the target amino acid into a 3D grid of little cubes, seven by seven by seven, each two ångströms on a side. 体素化 (voxelization) 的意思是，他们把折叠好的蛋白质，围绕目标氨基酸的空间切成一个三维网格的小立方体，7×7×7 个，每个边长 2 埃 (ångström，即 0.1 纳米)。 An ångström is a tenth of a nanometer, roughly the scale of a single atom. 一个埃是十分之一纳米，大致是单个原子的尺度。 For structure they used AlphaFold DB predicted structures — AlphaFold being the deep-learning system that predicts a protein's 3D fold from its sequence. 结构方面他们用了 AlphaFold DB 中预测的结构——AlphaFold 是那个能从蛋白质序列预测其三维折叠的深度学习系统。 The model trains on three objectives at once: separating common primate variants from matched controls, filling in a masked amino acid from its structural context, and matching per-gene rankings from a protein language model. 模型同时优化三个目标：把常见灵长类变异与匹配的对照区分开、根据结构上下文填补一个被遮蔽的氨基酸、以及匹配来自蛋白质语言模型 (protein language model) 的每个基因内的变异排序。

ALEX One design choice I'd push on: PrimateAI-3D leans heavily on AlphaFold predicted structures, and for 13.5% of proteins the sequence didn't match exactly, so they had to homology-model those. 一个我想质疑的设计选择：PrimateAI-3D 高度依赖 AlphaFold 预测的结构，而对于 13.5% 的蛋白质，序列并不完全匹配，所以他们不得不对这些做同源建模 (homology modeling)。 Structure quality is uneven across the proteome, and for a specific gene like CFTR you'd want to know how good the local structure confidence actually is before trusting a score. 蛋白质组中各处的结构质量参差不齐，对于像 CFTR 这样的特定基因，在信任某个分数之前，你会想知道其局部结构置信度到底有多好。

JORDAN Did the benign-transfer idea actually hold up in the numbers? 那"良性可迁移"这个想法在数字上真的站得住脚吗？

ALEX Strikingly well. 出奇地好。 Among high-confidence ClinVar variants — two-star review or above — human missense variants also seen in at least one nonhuman primate were benign or likely benign about 99% of the time. 在高置信度的 ClinVar 变异中（两星评审及以上），那些同时在至少一个非人灵长类中出现的人类错义变异，约 99% 的情况下是良性或可能良性的。 Compare that to 63% for ClinVar missense variants in general, and 80% for variants merely seen in gnomAD. 相比之下，一般的 ClinVar 错义变异只有 63%，仅在 gnomAD 中出现的变异也只有 80%。 And the signal was specific to primates: when they tried the same trick with mouse, rat, cow, dog, chicken, or zebrafish, benign rates fell to 71 to 87%, because at greater evolutionary distance the surrounding sequence context has drifted enough to change what a substitution does. 而且这个信号是灵长类特有的：当他们用小鼠、大鼠、牛、狗、鸡或斑马鱼试同样的把戏时，良性率降到 71%–87%，因为进化距离更远时，周围的序列背景已经漂移到足以改变一个替换的后果。 In total they reclassified more than 4 million human missense variants of previously unknown consequence as likely benign — a more than 50-fold increase over ClinVar. 他们总共把超过 400 万个此前后果未知的人类错义变异重新归类为可能良性——比 ClinVar 增加了 50 倍以上。

ALEX On the prediction side, they benchmarked PrimateAI-3D against 15 other published methods across six clinical tasks. 在预测方面，他们把 PrimateAI-3D 与另外 15 种已发表的方法在六项临床任务上做了对标。 It came first on every one — including a UK Biobank task of 200,643 individuals, de novo mutation separation in developmental-disorder and autism cohorts, ClinVar classification, and correlation with deep mutational scanning assays. 它在每一项上都排第一——包括一项 200,643 人的 UK Biobank 任务、发育障碍与自闭症队列中的新发突变 (de novo mutation) 区分、ClinVar 分类，以及与深度突变扫描 (deep mutational scanning) 实验的相关性。 After PrimateAI-3D there was no consistent runner-up; second place was occupied by six different classifiers across the six benchmarks. 排在 PrimateAI-3D 之后没有稳定的第二名；六个基准中的第二名由六个不同的分类器分别占据。

JORDAN What does "outperformed" mean concretely — how much better, and why should we believe it isn't just overfitting? "胜出"具体是什么意思——好多少，我们又凭什么相信它不只是过拟合？

ALEX Two things make it credible. 有两件事让它可信。 First, the win was broad, not narrow: PrimateAI-3D placed first across six independent benchmarks that stress different failure modes — patient cohorts, de novo mutation separation, ClinVar classification, and correlation with lab-measured deep mutational scans. 第一，这个胜出是宽泛的、而非狭窄的：PrimateAI-3D 在六个独立的、考验不同失败模式的基准上都排第一——患者队列、新发突变区分、ClinVar 分类、以及与实验室测量的深度突变扫描的相关性。 A model that overfits one benchmark rarely tops all six. 一个过拟合某个基准的模型很少能在全部六个上都居首。 Second, and this is the deeper point, its benign labels come from primate population frequency rather than from ClinVar, so it wasn't trained on the same annotations it's being tested against. 第二，也是更深的一点，它的良性标签来自灵长类群体频率而非 ClinVar，所以它不是在用来测试它的那批注释上训练的。 The authors note that most competing classifiers were trained on ClinVar or overlapping databases, which can inflate their ClinVar scores through circularity — so PrimateAI-3D topping ClinVar despite not being trained on it is actually the most telling result. 作者指出，大多数竞争分类器是在 ClinVar 或有重叠的数据库上训练的，这会通过循环论证抬高它们的 ClinVar 分数——所以 PrimateAI-3D 在没有用 ClinVar 训练的情况下反而在 ClinVar 上居首，才是最有说服力的结果。

JORDAN And the CFTR angle you keep hinting at? 还有你一直在暗示的 CFTR 那部分呢？

ALEX This is the payoff for our project. 这是与我们项目直接相关的收获。 They looked for genes whose selective constraint differs between humans and the rest of the primate lineage, and found 39 such genes at a false discovery rate below 0.05. 他们寻找那些在人类与其余灵长类谱系之间选择约束不同的基因，在错误发现率 (FDR) 低于 0.05 的标准下找到了 39 个这样的基因。 The top three genes where human constraint dropped the most relative to primates were CFTR, GJB2, and CD36 — cystic fibrosis, hereditary deafness, and platelet glycoprotein deficiency. 人类约束相对灵长类下降最多的前三个基因是 CFTR、GJB2 和 CD36——分别对应囊性纤维化、遗传性耳聋和血小板糖蛋白缺乏症。 All three are autosomal recessive disease genes where deleterious mutations are unusually common in specific human populations, consistent with relaxed selection. 这三个都是常染色体隐性遗传病基因，其有害突变在特定人群中异常常见，与放松的选择相一致。 So CFTR isn't a footnote here — it's a headline example of a gene where the human population carries more tolerated-looking-but-actually-deleterious variation than you'd naively expect. 所以 CFTR 在这里不是一个脚注，而是一个头条级的例子：这个基因在人群中携带的"看似可耐受、实则有害"的变异，比你天真预期的要多。
