Ep2 (Track B — AI tooling): Ten principles for analyzing data you'll never pool

Chen and colleagues (2026, JAMIA) distill a decade of deploying Privacy-Preserving Distributed Algorithms (PDA) — an R-package family of federated statistical methods downloaded over 13,300 times and used in more than 25 national and 5 international studies — into ten guiding principles spanning study preparation, analysis, and reporting. The framework lets multiple sites fit a shared statistical model by exchanging only prespecified summary statistics rather than pooling patient-level records, with some newer algorithms achieving "lossless," single-round ("one-shot") equivalence to a pooled analysis for certain model families. A real-world use case applying newer one-shot methods to COVID-19 mortality across three large US/Japan EHR and claims datasets reproduced expected risk factors (age, sex, diabetes, hypertension) consistent with existing literature. The transfer here is to Runyu's AI-tooling/career priority: federated multi-site analysis is exactly the kind of infrastructure skill senior RWE-and-genomics roles expect. Buildable artifact: a Federated GLM Demo.

Plain-English glossary

  • Aggregation unit — the level of analysis (an individual hospital, clinical site, or whole network) that gets its own index variable inside the statistical model, distinguishing one unit's subjects from another's.（中文：分析所处的层级（单个医院、临床站点或整个网络），它在统计模型内部拥有自己的索引变量，用以区分不同单元的研究对象。）
  • Cohort diagnostics — profiling a study cohort at each site before any modeling begins (incidence rates, characteristic distributions, overlaps) to catch misclassification before it contaminates downstream results.（中文：在任何建模开始之前，对每个站点的研究队列进行特征剖析（发病率、特征分布、重叠情况），以在污染下游结果之前捕捉分类错误。）
  • Distributed Research Network (DRN) — a collaborative infrastructure where multiple institutions pursue a shared research question while each keeps its own patient-level data under local governance (e.g., OHDSI, PCORnet, Sentinel).（中文：一种协作性基础设施，多个机构围绕共同研究问题合作，但各自在本地治理下保留患者个体层面数据（例如OHDSI、PCORnet、Sentinel倡议）。）
  • Distribution unit — the operational group of data partners that actually exchanges summary statistics with each other; may or may not coincide with the aggregation unit.（中文：实际相互交换汇总统计量的数据合作方分组，这是一个操作层面的概念，可能与聚合单元重合，也可能不重合。）
  • Lossless / one-shot (distributed algorithm) — "lossless" means a distributed analysis produces the same estimates as a hypothetical pooled analysis; "one-shot" means it needs only a single round of inter-site communication. Both properties hold only for specific model families under specific conditions, not universally.（中文："无损"指分布式分析能得出与假设性汇总分析相同的估计值；"一次性"指只需单轮站点间通信即可完成。两者都只在特定模型族、特定条件下成立，并非普遍适用。）
  • OMOP Common Data Model (OMOP CDM) — the standardized table structure and vocabulary (built on SNOMED CT, ICD-10, etc.) that lets independent sites express the same clinical concepts the same way, a precondition for cross-site comparison.（中文：标准化的数据表结构与词汇体系（建立在SNOMED CT、ICD-10等术语标准之上），使独立站点能以统一方式表达相同临床概念，是跨站点比较的前提条件。）

Full citation

Chen, et al. "PDA (Privacy-Preserving Distributed Algorithms) in action: ten principles for high-quality multi-site clinical evidence generation." Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association. 2026. PMID: 42440280 | PMC13580733 | DOI: 10.1093/jamia/ocag119

Methods / tools to lift

  • PDA R package / PDA-OTA — the open-source R package implementing the algorithm family, plus a web-based platform for tracking summary-statistic submission and synthesis across sites in real time.
  • DLMM / COLA-GLM / COLA-GLM-H / COLA-GLMM — one-shot, lossless distributed algorithms for generalized linear and mixed models.
  • ODACH / ODACoRH — distributed algorithms specifically designed to model between-site heterogeneity rather than assume it away.
  • Distributed Hospital Comparer / dGEM-disparity — downstream GLMM-based modules for hospital profiling and quantifying care disparities attributable to site-of-care differences, built on top of the core framework.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves AI-tooling / career: federated, privacy-preserving multi-site analysis is an infrastructure competency, not a disease-specific tool. Tool sketch — Federated GLM Demo: simulate patient-level data split across several fake "sites," compute and exchange only prespecified summary statistics in the spirit of a one-shot method like DLMM, combine them into a single coefficient estimate, and benchmark against a pooled analysis on the same simulated data.

What to verify first

  • Whether the specific "lossless" and "one-shot" guarantees actually hold for whatever model is being fit — these properties are model-family-specific per the paper, not universal.
  • How much upfront OMOP CDM harmonization the real use cases required before federated analysis could start — often the most labor-intensive part of a multi-site study, and treated as a precondition rather than something PDA itself solves.
  • That distributed methods don't eliminate confounding, selection bias, or measurement error inherent to observational data — those live upstream of pooled-versus-distributed.
  • That evidence for observational methodology generally (e.g., OHDSI LEGEND-T2DM reproducing trial findings) is kept separate from evidence specific to PDA's distributed-design choice when weighing confidence in any one federated result.

ALEX The privacy mechanics are worth one more beat, because "privacy-preserving" covers two genuinely different things here.
