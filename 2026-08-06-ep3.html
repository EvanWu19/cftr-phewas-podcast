A Robust Association Test Leveraging Unknown Genetic Interactions: Application to Cystic Fibrosis Lung Disease — Kim S, et al. Genetic Epidemiology, 2025.

Blurb

Kim and colleagues propose qJLS, a quantile-regression-based joint location-scale test built to catch genetic associations that hide behind unmeasured gene-gene or gene-environment interactions — signals that show up as variance heterogeneity rather than a simple mean shift. Unlike existing joint tests (DGLM, JLS, JLS-Score), which assume normally distributed errors and need a rank-based transformation whose side effects were previously unverified, qJLS controls type I error regardless of the true error distribution while matching or beating the other tests' power, especially for tail-specific interactions the others miss almost entirely. Applied to 1,997 unrelated CF patients in the Canadian CF Gene Modifier Study across 5.5 million variants, qJLS was the only one of four tests to find a genome-wide-significant lung-disease locus: rs9513900 on chromosome 13, an intergenic variant that's a significant GTEx lung eQTL for NALCN, a sodium leak channel gene. For us this lands on summary-stats colocalization: the paper's eQTL check was a simple significance test, not formal colocalization, leaving NALCN vs. the neighboring gene ITGBL1 as the causal driver still genuinely open.

Plain-English glossary

  • GWAS (genome-wide association study) — a scan of millions of genetic variants across the genome for statistical association with a trait.（中文：全基因组关联研究——对基因组中数以百万计的遗传变异进行扫描，寻找与某性状的统计学关联。）
  • Genetic interaction (G×G / G×E) — when a variant's effect on a trait depends on another gene (G×G) or an environmental exposure (G×E) that wasn't measured.（中文：基因交互作用（G×G／G×E）——某变异对性状的影响取决于另一个未被测量的基因（基因×基因）或环境暴露（基因×环境）。）
  • Variance heterogeneity / heteroscedasticity — unequal spread of trait values across genotype groups; the statistical fingerprint an unmeasured genetic interaction leaves behind.（中文：方差异质性／异方差性——性状取值在不同基因型分组间的离散程度不相等；是未被测量的基因交互作用留下的统计学"指纹"。）
  • Quantile regression — a regression framework that models a variant's effect separately across percentiles of the trait's distribution, not just at the mean.（中文：分位数回归——一种在性状分布的各个百分位点上分别建模变异效应、而非只关注均值的回归方法。）
  • Rank-based inverse normal transformation — a data transformation that forces a variable into a normal shape before testing, commonly used to satisfy normality assumptions in older joint tests.（中文：基于秩次的逆正态变换——一种在检验前把变量强行转换成正态分布形状的数据变换，常用于满足旧式联合检验的正态性假设。）
  • eQTL (expression quantitative trait locus) — a genetic variant statistically associated with how much a nearby gene is expressed; one way to connect an anonymous statistical signal to a specific candidate gene.（中文：eQTL（表达数量性状位点）——一种与附近某基因表达量在统计学上相关联的遗传变异位点；是把匿名统计信号与具体候选基因联系起来的方法之一。）
  • Linkage disequilibrium (LD) — the tendency of nearby genetic variants to be inherited together, which can make two separate causal variants appear to produce one shared signal.（中文：连锁不平衡——邻近遗传变异倾向于被一起遗传的现象，可能使两个各自独立的致病变异看起来像是产生了同一个信号。）
  • Genome-wide significance — the strict, multiple-testing-corrected statistical threshold a signal must clear to count as a real discovery across a genome-wide scan.（中文：全基因组显著性——在全基因组扫描中，一个信号必须跨过的、经过多重检验校正的严格统计学阈值，才能被视为真正的发现。）
  • Colocalization analysis — a formal statistical method estimating the posterior probability that a GWAS signal and an eQTL signal share one single causal variant, rather than just both clearing a significance threshold independently.（中文：共定位分析——一种正式的统计方法，用于估算 GWAS 信号与 eQTL 信号共享同一个致病变异的后验概率，而不仅仅是两者各自独立地跨过显著性阈值。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • qJLS (quantile regression-based joint location-scale test) — a rankscore-based test combining location and scale statistics into one test statistic that's distribution-free under the null hypothesis.
  • Simulation-first validation protocol — extensive type I error / power simulation across multiple error distributions, sample sizes, and MAFs before ever touching real data.
  • GTEx eQTL lookup — used to assign a candidate causal gene to an intergenic significant locus (a simple significance check, not formal colocalization).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: summary-stats colocalization at known modifier loci. MVP (paste-ready): Take the chromosome 13 region around rs9513900 (or any other catalogued CF modifier locus with a candidate nearby gene); pull the full regional GWAS summary statistics plus the full regional GTEx eQTL summary statistics for candidate genes; run a formal Bayesian colocalization test (coloc) to compute the posterior probability the GWAS and eQTL signals share a single causal variant, rather than relying on a simple lead-SNP eQTL significance check. Input: locus regional GWAS sumstats + regional GTEx eQTL sumstats. Difficulty: S–M.

What to verify first

  • Causal gene unresolved — NALCN has eQTL support, but formal colocalization (not yet done) is what would actually adjudicate NALCN vs. the neighboring gene ITGBL1.
  • Single-cohort discovery — only partial internal replication across Canadian sub-cohorts; independent external CF cohort replication is the standard next step before treating this as an established modifier.
  • Independence requirement — qJLS can't handle related individuals or repeated longitudinal measurements as-is; any registry application needs either a one-person-per-family reduction (checked case by case) or a not-yet-built extension.

Gap-map / catalog: closes / leaves open

  • Closes: demonstrates that a more powerful, distribution-robust association test recovers a real CF lung-disease modifier signal (rs9513900 / NALCN region) in a well-characterized Canadian cohort that three standard joint location-scale tests all failed to reach genome-wide significance on.
  • Leaves open: formal causal-gene resolution between NALCN and ITGBL1 via colocalization; independent external replication; extension of qJLS to related-individual or longitudinal registry data structures.
