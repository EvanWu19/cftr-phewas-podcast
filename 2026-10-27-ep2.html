Ep2 (Track B — AI tooling): HPO's 2024 update — what's in the repo, not just the ontology

Gargano and colleagues, on behalf of the HPO Consortium (2024, Nucleic Acids Research), report the Human Phenotype Ontology's progress since 2020: 2,239 new terms, 612 new/updated definitions, 1,377 new synonyms, 671 renamed classes, roughly 49,235 new phenotype-disease annotations (now citing 9,573 PMIDs), and a sustained open-source community of 177 contributors who opened 3,715 GitHub issues since the last report. The headline infrastructure pieces are internationalization (ten released language translations via a versioned Babelon/LinkML data model with explicit translation-status tracking), the new Medical Action Ontology (MAxO, 1,757 terms for treatments and interventions, tightly coupled to HPO and Mondo), and fenominal, a concept-recognition tool whose BLAST-inspired, typo-tolerant matching improved recall by 10% on scientific text and 20% on real EHR records. The paper's value for this podcast is architectural: HPO/MAxO/fenominal are reusable, versioned, benchmarked infrastructure — the kind of foundation layer worth building on rather than reinventing. Buildable artifact: a registry free-text phenotyping pipeline.

Plain-English glossary

  • HPO (Human Phenotype Ontology) — a logically structured vocabulary of human phenotypic abnormalities, with stable identifiers and parent-child relationships, enabling computational reasoning over patient features.（中文：人类表型本体，一套具有逻辑结构的人类表型异常词汇表，带有稳定标识符和上下位关系，可支持对患者特征进行计算推理。）
  • Deep phenotyping / semantic similarity — capturing patient features at a granular, structured level rather than a loose label, which then allows computing how phenotypically alike two patients or a patient and a candidate disease are.（中文：深度表型分析与语义相似度——在精细、结构化层面捕捉患者特征而非笼统标签，从而可以计算两名患者、或患者与候选疾病之间的表型相似程度。）
  • GA4GH Phenopacket Schema — a standardized file format for exchanging a patient's genomic and phenotypic data between systems using shared vocabularies like HPO.（中文：一种标准化文件格式，借助HPO等共享词汇表，在不同系统之间交换患者的基因组与表型数据。）
  • MAxO (Medical Action Ontology) — a structured vocabulary of medical procedures, therapies, and interventions, tightly coupled to HPO and the Mondo Disease Ontology.（中文：医疗行为本体，一套结构化的医疗操作、疗法与干预措施词汇表，与HPO及Mondo疾病本体紧密耦合。）
  • Concept recognition (CR) — the task of automatically scanning free text to identify mentions of ontology terms, as opposed to manual encoding.（中文：概念识别，指自动扫描自由文本以识别本体术语提及的任务，区别于人工编码。）
  • fenominal — the Monarch team's Java library for HPO concept recognition, notable for a BLAST-inspired method that tolerates typographical errors in clinical text.（中文：Monarch团队开发的HPO概念识别Java程序库，其亮点是一种受BLAST启发、可容忍临床文本中拼写错误的方法。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • fenominal — Java library for HPO concept recognition with typo-tolerant, BLAST-inspired k-mer matching, trained against 2.9M clinical notes.
  • Babelon + LinkML — a versioned, tabular translation data model with explicit translation-status tracking (candidate/under review/official) for any multilingual terminology system.
  • MAxO — a reusable structured vocabulary for medical actions/treatments, couplable to any phenotype or disease ontology.
  • GA4GH Phenopacket Schema — a standardized interchange format for genomic + phenotypic patient data.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves AI-tooling / career. HPO, MAxO, and fenominal are exactly the reusable foundation/representation infrastructure a senior RWE-genomics data scientist should be comfortable building on. Buildable artifact: a registry free-text phenotyping pipeline. MVP: take fenominal, or a from-scratch reimplementation of its k-mer/BLAST-inspired typo-tolerant matching approach, run it over a sample of free-text clinical fields to extract structured HPO terms with confidence scores, package the output as a GA4GH Phenopacket, and benchmark recall with and without the typo-handling step, the way fenominal's own paper does. Difficulty: medium.

What to verify first

  • Whether fenominal's typo model, trained on general clinical notes, transfers cleanly to CF-specific vocabulary and abbreviations, or needs domain-specific retraining.
  • Whether HPO's current term coverage adequately represents CF-specific phenotypes in the first place — a question for domain review, not something to assume from this paper.
  • Whether any CFFPR free-text fields exist in a form this kind of pipeline could run against at all — a registry-structure question outside what this paper can answer.
  • Whether a lighter-weight dictionary lookup, without fenominal's full typo-tolerant machinery, would already capture most of the value for a narrower CF-specific vocabulary, given the 10%/20% recall gains were measured on general clinical text, not a constrained domain vocabulary.

ALEX Today's paper is in Nucleic Acids Research, 2024 — Gargano and colleagues, on behalf of the whole HPO Consortium, "The Human Phenotype Ontology in 2024: phenotypes around the world." 今天的论文发表在2024年的《Nucleic Acids Research》上——作者是Gargano及其同事，代表整个HPO联盟，题目是《2024年的人类表型本体：走向世界的表型》。 It's a Track B pick, and it's a good one for the career-tooling lens specifically because HPO isn't a one-off study — it's reusable infrastructure, versioned, open-source, and already sitting underneath nearly every clinical-genetics diagnostic tool in use today. 这是Track B的选题，而且从工具开发与职业发展的角度来看，它特别合适，因为HPO不是一次性的研究——它是可复用的基础设施，有版本管理、开源，并且已经支撑着当今几乎所有在用的临床遗传学诊断工具。 This episode is less "what did they discover" and more "what did they ship, and what's in the repo."这一集的重点不在于"他们发现了什么"，而在于"他们发布了什么，仓库里又有什么"。

JORDAN So what is HPO, exactly, and why does a phenotype need an "ontology" in the first place? 那HPO到底是什么？一个表型为什么一开始就需要一个"本体"？

ALEX HPO, the Human Phenotype Ontology, is a logically structured vocabulary of human phenotypic abnormalities — each term has a stable identifier, a definition, and explicit parent-child relationships to other terms, so a computer can reason over it the way it can't reason over free-text clinical notes. HPO，即人类表型本体，是一套具有逻辑结构的人类表型异常词汇表——每个术语都有一个稳定的标识符、一个定义，以及与其他术语之间明确的上下位关系，这样计算机就可以对它进行推理，而无法对自由文本的临床记录做到这一点。 "Deep phenotyping" means capturing a patient's features at this granular, structured level rather than a loose diagnosis label, and once you have that, you can compute semantic similarity between two patients' term sets — essentially asking "how phenotypically alike are these two people," mathematically, using the ontology's tree structure — which is the computational backbone behind rare-disease gene discovery and diagnostic matching. "深度表型分析"指的是在这种精细、结构化的层面上捕捉患者的特征，而不是用一个笼统的诊断标签，一旦你拥有了这种结构化数据，就可以计算两个患者术语集合之间的"语义相似度"——本质上是借助本体的树状结构，以数学方式回答"这两个人在表型上有多相似"——这正是罕见病基因发现和诊断匹配背后的计算基础。 HPO is also the shared vocabulary behind the GA4GH Phenopacket Schema, a standardized file format the paper discusses for exchanging a patient's genomic and phenotypic data between systems — think of it as the common currency that lets one clinic's structured patient record mean the same thing in another clinic's software.HPO同时也是GA4GH Phenopacket Schema背后的共享词汇表，这是论文中提到的一种标准化文件格式，用于在不同系统之间交换患者的基因组和表型数据——可以把它理解为一种"通用货币"，让一家诊所的结构化患者记录，在另一家诊所的软件里也能表达相同的含义。

JORDAN And the Medical Action Ontology, MAxO — is that a separate thing, or part of HPO itself? 而医疗行为本体MAxO——这是一个独立的东西，还是HPO的一部分？

ALEX Separate but tightly coupled. HPO describes what's wrong with a patient; MAxO, new since the group's last major update, describes what was done about it — medications, surgical procedures, ablations, biologics, behavioral interventions, deep brain stimulation, anything that counts as a "medical action." 两者是独立的，但紧密耦合。HPO描述的是患者身上出了什么问题；而MAxO是自该团队上一次重大更新以来新增的本体，描述的是"针对这个问题做了什么"——药物治疗、外科手术、消融治疗、生物制剂、行为干预、脑深部电刺激，任何算得上"医疗行为"的东西。 MAxO's development is deliberately coupled to both HPO and the Mondo Disease Ontology, so a system can eventually link phenotype, diagnosis, and treatment into one computable chain instead of three disconnected vocabularies.MAxO的开发被有意地与HPO和Mondo疾病本体都紧密关联起来，这样一个系统最终就可以把表型、诊断和治疗串联成一条可计算的链条，而不是三套互不相通的独立词汇表。

ALEX Now the background — why does this update exist at all? HPO has been under active development since 2008, and it's a flagship piece of the Monarch Initiative, the broader effort to make biomedical data interoperable across organisms and data sources. 现在来说说背景——这次更新究竟为什么存在？HPO自2008年起就持续活跃开发，是Monarch倡议的旗舰项目之一，而Monarch倡议是一项更广泛的努力，目标是让跨物种、跨数据源的生物医学数据能够互通互用。 Since their last NAR report in 2020, two problems had become hard to ignore: first, the ontology itself had gaps — entire clinical areas like behavioral/psychiatric phenotypes, prenatal phenotypes, and inborn errors of immunity were under-represented, which clinicians in those specialties flagged directly; second, HPO was functionally an English-only tool, which is a real barrier when the Chinese HPO consortium, Spanish-speaking genetics departments, and national rare-disease registries in France and the Netherlands all need to encode patient data in their own languages. 自2020年他们上一次在NAR发表报告以来，两个难以忽视的问题逐渐显现：第一，本体本身存在空白——像行为/精神类表型、产前表型、原发性免疫缺陷这样的整块临床领域都代表性不足，这些专科的临床医生直接指出了这一点；第二，HPO在功能上基本是一个只支持英语的工具，而当中国HPO（CHPO）联盟、西班牙语国家的遗传学科室，以及法国和荷兰的国家罕见病登记系统都需要用自己的语言来编码患者数据时，这就成了一个实实在在的障碍。 So this update is really two parallel pushes: fill the clinical gaps through targeted workshops, and build real internationalization infrastructure rather than one-off translated term lists.所以这次更新实际上是两条并行的推进：一是通过专项研讨会填补临床空白，二是建立真正的国际化基础设施，而不是零散的、一次性的术语翻译清单。

JORDAN Let's get concrete — what did they actually build for the translation piece? 我们来具体说说——他们在翻译这部分到底做了什么？

ALEX They built Babelon, a tabular data model maintained with LinkML, that stores each term's translation alongside metadata like "translation status" — candidate, meaning an algorithm or outside contributor proposed it; under review, meaning the core language team proposed it but hasn't ratified it; or official, meaning it's been formally accepted. 他们构建了Babelon，一种用LinkML维护的表格化数据模型，用于存储每个术语的翻译及其元数据，比如"翻译状态"——候选（candidate），表示由算法或外部贡献者提出；审核中（under review），表示核心语言团队提出了但尚未正式批准；或正式（official），表示已经正式被采纳。 Crucially, when an English label changes, the system doesn't silently keep a stale translation — it automatically downgrades that term's status from official back to candidate and generates a report so the language working group knows exactly what needs re-curating, which is a clean solution to the "translation drift" problem any multilingual system eventually faces. 关键的是，当英文标签发生变化时，系统不会悄无声息地保留一个已经过时的翻译——它会自动把该术语的状态从"正式"降级回"候选"，并生成一份报告，让语言工作组清楚知道哪些内容需要重新审定，这是对任何多语言系统最终都会遇到的"翻译漂移"问题的一个干净解决方案。 Ten languages have translations released in the HPO International Edition, including a mix of fully professional clinical-geneticist translation, AI-assisted drafts with mandatory human ratification for languages like Czech, and three partial Indigenous-language translations — Dusun, Nyangumarta, and Tiwi — developed in direct consultation with Community Elders.目前HPO国际版已发布十种语言的翻译，涵盖了多种方式的组合：有些语言（比如捷克语）采用了完全由临床遗传学专家翻译、或AI辅助起草后必须经人工审定的方式；还有三种原住民语言的部分翻译——杜顺语、Nyangumarta语和Tiwi语——是在与社区长者直接协商下完成的。

ALEX I want to flag one design choice here, though. 不过这里我想提出一个值得关注的设计选择。 Letting "candidate" translations — including raw AI-translated drafts for languages like Czech and Japanese — sit live in the released ontology before a human ratifies them is a defensible speed-versus-safety tradeoff, but it does mean a clinician somewhere could, in principle, be encoding a patient's phenotype using a translation nobody with clinical authority has signed off on yet. 不过让"候选"翻译——包括像捷克语和日语这样原始的AI翻译草稿——在人工审定之前就已经存在于已发布的本体中，这是一种可以理解的、在速度与安全性之间的权衡，但这确实意味着，原则上某个地方的临床医生可能正在用一份还没有任何具备临床权威性的人签字确认过的翻译，去编码患者的表型。 The paper doesn't quantify how often that actually bites — how many patient records, in practice, get encoded against a not-yet-ratified candidate term — which feels like exactly the kind of gap you'd want closed before leaning on translation status as a trust signal in a production pipeline.论文并没有量化这种情况实际发生的频率有多高——实际上有多少患者记录是用一个尚未经过审定的候选术语编码的——这正是在生产环境的流程中，把"翻译状态"当作一种可信度信号之前，你会希望先弄清楚的那种空白。

JORDAN And fenominal — that's the concept-recognition tool? Walk me through what it does. 还有fenominal——那是那个概念识别工具吗？带我了解一下它具体做什么。

ALEX Right. Concept recognition, or CR, is the task of scanning free-text clinical notes and automatically pulling out mentions of HPO terms, and it's traditionally slow and error-prone when done by hand. 没错。概念识别（CR）指的是扫描自由文本的临床记录，自动提取出其中提到的HPO术语，这项任务如果靠人工完成，传统上既慢又容易出错。 Fenominal is the Monarch team's Java library for doing this automatically, and its specific contribution here is handling typographical errors — a challenge most CR tools either ignore or assume is solved. fenominal是Monarch团队用来自动完成这项工作的Java程序库，而它在这里的特殊贡献，是处理文本中的拼写错误——这是大多数概念识别工具会忽略、或假定已经解决的一个难题。 They built a method inspired by BLAST, the classic biosequence-alignment algorithm, that screens text for candidate matches using k-mer counts and scores them against patterns of typical spelling-error behavior derived from 2.9 million real clinical notes, then validated it against a purpose-built gold-standard corpus of typographical errors. 他们构建了一种受BLAST（经典的生物序列比对算法）启发的方法，利用k-mer计数在文本中筛选候选匹配，再依据从290万份真实临床记录中归纳出的典型拼写错误模式对其打分，并用一个专门构建的拼写错误金标准语料库对其进行验证。 The result: a 10% recall improvement on scientific-publication text, and a 20% recall improvement on actual EHR records, which tells you something important — the messier and more typo-prone the source text, the more this specific capability matters, exactly the profile you'd expect from registry free-text fields rather than polished manuscripts.结果是：在科学文献文本上提升了10%的召回率，而在真实电子健康记录（EHR）上提升了20%的召回率——这说明了一个重要的事实：源文本越混乱、越容易出现拼写错误，这项能力就越重要，而这恰恰正是登记系统中的自由文本字段、而非精心打磨过的论文手稿所呈现的特征。

JORDAN You mentioned clinical tools already lean on HPO — which ones, and how? 你提到已经有临床工具在依赖HPO——具体是哪些，又是怎么用的？

ALEX A handful of production diagnostic products now encode patient features as HPO terms directly: SimulConsult combines symptoms, family history, and lab results into a differential-diagnosis list; PhenoTips and Face2Gene both let clinicians enter deep phenotype profiles, with Face2Gene adding facial-analysis-derived phenotype suggestions; and on the computational-genomics side, Exomiser and LIRICAL both rank candidate causal variants by combining a patient's HPO profile with their genomic variant list, using semantic similarity to the known phenotype profile of each candidate disease. 目前已经有若干进入生产环境的诊断产品，直接把患者特征编码为HPO术语：SimulConsult把症状、家族史和实验室检测结果综合成一份鉴别诊断清单；PhenoTips和Face2Gene都允许临床医生录入深度表型档案，Face2Gene还会加入基于面部分析得出的表型建议；而在计算基因组学一侧，Exomiser和LIRICAL都会把患者的HPO表型档案与其基因组变异列表结合起来，利用与每个候选疾病已知表型档案之间的语义相似度，对候选致病变异进行排序。 That's the practical payoff of a well-maintained ontology — it's not an academic artifact sitting in a paper, it's load-bearing infrastructure inside tools clinicians actually run during a real diagnostic workup.这正是一个维护良好的本体所带来的实际回报——它不是躺在论文里的学术产物，而是临床医生在真实诊断流程中实际运行的工具内部，承重的基础设施。

JORDAN And the closing section mentions integrating HPO into electronic health records directly — is that already happening, or still aspirational? 结尾部分提到要把HPO直接整合进电子健康记录——这已经在发生了，还是仍停留在愿景阶段？

ALEX Framed carefully as ongoing, not finished. 论文的措辞很谨慎，说这是正在进行中，而不是已经完成。 The HPO consortium is contributing to an effort to integrate HPO and the GA4GH Phenopacket Schema directly into EHR systems, aiming for standardized, computable rare-disease data capture at the point of care rather than after-the-fact manual encoding by a curator. HPO联盟正在参与一项把HPO和GA4GH Phenopacket Schema直接整合进电子健康记录系统的工作，目标是在诊疗现场就实现标准化、可计算的罕见病数据采集，而不是事后由审定者进行人工编码。 That matters for exactly the kind of registry Runyu works with: if structured phenotype capture ever moves upstream into the point of care, the free-text-to-HPO pipeline we just sketched becomes a stopgap rather than a permanent fixture — worth knowing which future you're actually building for before investing heavily in either one.这对于像Runyu所工作的这类登记系统来说意义重大：如果结构化的表型采集真的有一天前移到诊疗现场本身，那么我们刚才构想的"自由文本转HPO"流程就会变成一个权宜之计，而不是一个永久性的方案——在大力投入其中任何一个方向之前，先弄清楚自己究竟是在为哪种未来做准备，是值得的。

JORDAN So does the whole update actually move the needle — what are the headline numbers? 那么整次更新到底有没有实质进展——有哪些关键数字？

ALEX Since the 2020 report: 2,239 new terms, 612 new or updated definitions, 1,377 new synonyms, and 671 renamed classes, all measured using their own OAK diff tooling rather than manual counting. 自2020年报告以来：新增了2239个术语，新增或更新了612条定义，新增了1377个同义词，并重命名了671个类别——这些数字都是用他们自己的OAK差异比较工具测算的，而不是人工统计。 On the annotation side — the links tying specific phenotypes to specific diseases — they added roughly 49,235 new annotations, now citing 9,573 distinct PubMed IDs as evidence. 在注释方面——也就是把具体表型与具体疾病关联起来的链接——他们新增了大约49235条注释，目前引用了9573个不同的PubMed文献ID作为证据。 And the community-scale signal is real: 177 individual contributors opened 3,715 issues and left 5,159 comments on their GitHub repository since September 2020, which for an open-source ontology project is a genuinely large, sustained volunteer engineering effort, not a closed academic deliverable.社区规模的信号也确实可观：自2020年9月以来，共有177位独立贡献者在他们的GitHub仓库中提交了3715个issue，并留下了5159条评论——对于一个开源本体项目来说，这是一种真正大规模、持续的志愿工程参与，而不是一个封闭的学术成果。

JORDAN Where does this land for Runyu, then — which priority does it actually serve? 那这对Runyu来说落在哪里——它实际上服务于哪个优先事项？
