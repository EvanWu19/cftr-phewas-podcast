Blurb

Groenwold RHH's 2020 Diagnostic and Prognostic Research paper names and illustrates a feedback-loop problem with missing-indicator clinical prediction models: if a missing measurement is itself informative about a patient's outcome (because a clinician's decision not to order a test already reflects their judgment of risk), a model can legitimately exploit that signal — but the moment the model is deployed and clinicians learn, explicitly or implicitly, that absence-of-measurement is predictive, their own ordering behaviour can change, breaking the very mechanism the model relied on. A controlled synthetic illustration (20,000 subjects, two predictors, four missing-data-handling methods, four deployment scenarios) shows zero/mean imputation with a missingness indicator actually beating a no-missing-data reference model when the missingness mechanism matches between development and deployment, but degrading sharply when it shifts, while multiple imputation is more robust once missingness becomes genuinely random. This pairs with ep3's centre-clustering correction as an upstream/downstream combination: ep3 corrects the model a residual gets compared against; ep4 is a caution about what happens after a value is flagged and deleted rather than corrected — that decision itself creates a new, potentially informative missingness pattern that any later downstream model inherits. Buildable: a missingness-mechanism transportability stress test, generalizing the paper's own four-scenario design into a reusable pre-flight check.

Plain-English glossary

  • Informative missingness — when whether a value is missing is itself related to the underlying outcome being studied (e.g. a clinician's decision not to order a test already reflects their risk assessment), rather than missing at random.（中文：具有信息量的缺失，指一个值是否缺失本身就与所研究的潜在结局相关（例如临床医生不开具某项检查的决定，本身就反映了其风险判断），而非随机缺失。）
  • Missing-indicator approach — a modeling technique that adds an extra binary variable flagging whether a given predictor was measured at all, alongside its value when observed, allowing a model to learn from the missingness pattern itself.（中文：缺失指示符方法，一种建模技术，加入一个额外的二元变量来标记某预测变量是否被测量过，与其被观测到时的数值一并使用，使模型能够从缺失模式本身中学习。）
  • Transportability (of a prediction model) — whether a model's performance, developed in one setting or time period, holds up when applied in a different setting, patient mix, or time period.（中文：（预测模型的）可迁移性，指一个模型在某一环境或时间段内开发出的性能，在应用于不同环境、患者构成或时间段时是否依然成立。）
  • Calibration-in-the-large — a measure of whether a model's average predicted probability matches the actual observed outcome rate overall, as distinct from the c-statistic's measure of discrimination between individuals.（中文：整体校准（calibration-in-the-large），衡量模型预测概率的总体均值是否与实际观察到的结局发生率相符的指标，区别于c统计量所衡量的个体间区分能力。）
  • c-statistic / Brier score — standard clinical-prediction-model performance metrics: the c-statistic measures how well a model ranks higher-risk above lower-risk subjects; the Brier score is a mean-squared-error-style measure of overall predicted-probability accuracy.（中文：c统计量／Brier分数，临床预测模型的标准性能指标：c统计量衡量模型能否将高风险个体排在低风险个体之前；Brier分数则是一种均方误差式的、衡量预测概率总体准确性的指标。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Four-scenario deployment stress test — developing a model under one missingness mechanism, then re-scoring it under multiple shifted mechanisms (fully observed, random missingness, fully missing) to quantify transportability risk, rather than assuming development-time performance will hold.
  • mice (multiple imputation by chained equations) — the R package used for the multiple-imputation comparison arm, a standard, robust approach once missingness becomes closer to random.
  • c-statistic / Brier score / calibration-in-the-large, reported together — using all three jointly rather than any single metric, since different missing-data methods failed on different metrics in different scenarios.

JORDAN "The curse of knowing" is a great title, but what exactly is the curse? "知晓的诅咒"这个标题起得很好，但这个"诅咒"具体指的是什么?

ALEX Start with the setup: in EHR data, a missing measurement is often not random — a doctor decides whether to order a test based on their own judgment of the patient's risk, so the fact that cholesterol was never measured can itself carry information about prognosis, often in a favorable direction, because the doctor saw no reason to check. 先从问题的设定讲起：在电子健康记录（EHR）数据中，一项测量值的缺失往往并非随机——医生是根据自己对患者风险的判断来决定是否开具某项检查的，所以"胆固醇从未被测量"这件事本身，就可能携带着关于预后的信息，而且往往是偏向有利的方向，因为医生当时判断没有必要去检查。 A common way to use that signal in a clinical prediction model is a missing-indicator approach — you add an extra variable that simply flags whether a given predictor was measured at all, alongside whatever value it has when it is measured, and the model can then learn that "measurement absent" itself predicts the outcome. 在临床预测模型中利用这一信号的一种常见做法是"缺失指示符"（missing-indicator）方法——你额外加入一个变量，单纯标记某个预测变量是否被测量过，再加上它被测量到时的实际数值，模型便可以学习到"未测量"这件事本身就能预测结局。 The "curse" is a feedback loop: the moment a model like this gets deployed and a clinician learns, explicitly or implicitly, that the absence of a measurement is itself predictive, that knowledge can change their future ordering behaviour — and the instant their behaviour changes, the very missingness pattern the model learned to exploit stops meaning what it used to mean. "诅咒"指的是一个反馈循环：一旦这样的模型被部署投入使用，而临床医生明确或隐含地了解到"缺失本身"就具有预测性，这种认知就可能改变他们今后开具检查的行为——而一旦他们的行为发生改变，模型原本学会利用的那种缺失模式，就不再具有原来的含义了。 There's a related, slightly unsettling finding the paper cites in passing: in one study of commonly used lab measurements, the timing of when a test was requested was actually a better predictor of three-year mortality risk than the test's own result — which is a vivid illustration of just how much signal can live in care-process metadata rather than the measured values themselves. 论文中顺带提到了一个相关的、略令人不安的发现：在一项针对常用实验室检验的研究中，检验被申请的时间点，竟然比检验结果本身更能预测三年死亡风险——这生动地说明了，有多少信号其实存在于诊疗过程的元数据中，而非测量值本身。

JORDAN That's the concept, framed in terms of a doctor's ordering behaviour. 这就是这个概念，以医生开具检查的行为来框定的。 Walk me through how they actually tested it. 讲讲他们实际上是怎么测试这一点的。

ALEX They built a controlled, synthetic illustration specifically so the mechanism could be isolated cleanly, rather than trying to untangle it from messy real EHR data. 他们专门构建了一个受控的合成示例，目的正是为了把这个机制干净地分离出来，而不是试图从杂乱的真实EHR数据中把它理清楚。 Twenty thousand synthetic subjects were generated with two predictors, P and U, both uniformly distributed between zero and one; P was treated as potentially observable, while U was deliberately never observed by any method, representing the kind of unmeasured confounding that's always lurking in real data. 他们生成了两万名合成受试者，带有两个预测变量P和U，均服从0到1之间的均匀分布；P被设定为"有可能被观测到"的变量，而U则被设定为无论用哪种方法都永远不会被观测到的变量，用以代表真实数据中始终潜藏的那种未测量混淆。 A binary outcome Y was simulated from a logistic function of both P and U combined, present in roughly 34% of subjects, and missingness of P itself — call it R — was also made to depend on both P and U through its own logistic function, so that whether P was observed in about half of subjects was itself entangled with the very same two predictors driving the outcome. 一个二元结局Y则通过对P和U二者的逻辑函数联合模拟得到，大约34%的受试者结局为阳性；而P本身的缺失情况——记为R——也同样通过其自身的逻辑函数依赖于P和U两者，于是P在大约一半受试者中是否被观测到，本身就与驱动结局的这同两个预测变量纠缠在了一起。 Four approaches to handling the missing values of P were compared: zero imputation plus a missingness indicator, mean imputation plus a missingness indicator, complete-case analysis using only subjects with an observed P, and multiple imputation by chained equations using the mice package in R. 研究比较了处理P缺失值的四种方法：零值填补加缺失指示符、均值填补加缺失指示符、仅使用观测到P的受试者的完全病例分析（complete-case analysis），以及使用R语言mice包的链式方程多重填补（multiple imputation）。 A second, independently generated dataset of another 20,000 subjects was then used to test each developed model under four separate deployment scenarios: the same informative missingness mechanism as during development; ALEX The headline result is genuinely counterintuitive at first glance: in the matched scenario, where the deployment missingness mechanism was identical to the one used in development, zero and mean imputation with a missingness indicator actually outperformed a reference model that was developed and tested on data with no missing values at all. 最核心的结果乍一看确实违反直觉：在匹配场景下，也就是部署时的缺失机制与开发阶段完全一致的情况下，带缺失指示符的零值填补和均值填补，表现竟然优于一个在完全没有缺失值的数据上开发和测试的参照模型。 The reason is almost poetic: because missingness of P itself depended on both P and U, the pattern of missingness carried more information about the outcome than the single observed value of P alone did in the reference model — so exploiting that informative missingness was, in this one matched scenario, genuinely the better strategy. 原因几乎可以说有些诗意：正因为P本身的缺失依赖于P和U二者，缺失模式所携带的、关于结局的信息，实际上比参照模型中单独一个被观测到的P值所携带的信息还要多——所以在这一个匹配场景下，利用这种具有信息量的缺失，确实是更优的策略。 But the moment the scenario shifted, that advantage reversed or evaporated depending on exactly how it shifted: in the scenario with no missing values at all, these same indicator-based models showed poor calibration-in-the-large;
