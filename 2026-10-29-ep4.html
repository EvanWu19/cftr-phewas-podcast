Ep4 (Track C 2/2 — registry QC): A taxonomy for the pile of checks nobody indexed

Rogers and colleagues (2019, EGEMS) propose a minimal entity-attribute-value-inspired conceptual model — every data-quality (DQ) check reduced to exactly one "data element" (its primary focus) and one "function" (the qualitative/quantitative measure applied to it) — and test whether a repurposed clinical-trial-eligibility NLP system can extract these at scale across two networks' narrative check libraries: OHDSI (239 checks, 172 used in the main NLP analysis) and Kaiser Permanente's CESR (3,434 checks). NLP accuracy on held-out testing checks reached 76%/98% (data element/function) for OHDSI and 97%/89% for CESR — the OHDSI data-element drop from 100% on training is a textbook overfitting signature the authors attribute to unrepresented adjective modifiers. Domain analysis found OHDSI's checks concentrated in Person (55/172, 32%), while CESR's concentrated in Medication (764/3,434, 22%); only 8 of the resulting domains overlapped between networks, and a "Vital" domain (weight/blood pressure/pulse, 86 CESR checks) appears nowhere in OHDSI's 12-domain list — a narrow, sample-specific finding, not a claim that OHDSI's broader community has no anthropometric checks. Because "Vital" is an explicitly named domain in this taxonomy, it's directly useful for tagging this podcast's own already-catalogued anthropometric methods for the first time in a comparable way. Buildable artifact: a DQ-Check Element-by-Function Tagger extending yesterday's landscape one-pager.

Plain-English glossary

  • Entity-attribute-value (EAV) model — a general pattern for flexibly representing heterogeneous data as an object of interest (entity), a descriptor of it (attribute), and the descriptor's content (value); here, a DQ check is the entity.（中文：实体-属性-值（EAV）模型，一种灵活表示异构数据的通用模式，由关注对象（实体）、对其的描述（属性）及描述内容（值）构成；此处每条数据质量检查本身就是"实体"。）
  • Domain (data-quality/common-data-model sense) — a category grouping related data elements (e.g. Person, Medication, Visit) — not to be confused with a protein structural domain from yesterday's InterPro episode.（中文：domain（数据质量/通用数据模型意义上），一个用于归类相关数据元素的类别（如Person、Medication、Visit）——不要与昨天InterPro那一集里蛋白质结构域的含义混淆。）
  • QDM-CQL (Quality Data Model / Clinical Quality Language) — an alternative, machine-executable framework for representing and running clinical quality checks, more expressive but with real mapping-coverage gaps.（中文：QDM-CQL（质量数据模型/临床质量语言），另一种可供机器执行的临床质量检查表示与运行框架，表达力更强，但映射覆盖率也存在真实缺口。）
  • Vital (domain) — the data-element domain covering weight, blood pressure, and pulse measurements in this taxonomy.（中文：Vital（生命体征领域），该分类体系中涵盖体重、血压和脉搏测量的数据元素领域。）

JORDAN Let's get the vocabulary straight. 我们先把词汇弄清楚。 What's an "entity-attribute-value" model, and what's this "domain" word doing here — is it the same "domain" from yesterday's protein-structure episode? "实体-属性-值（entity-attribute-value）"模型是什么？这里的"domain"这个词又是什么意思——和昨天蛋白质结构那一集里的"domain"是同一个意思吗？

ALEX Good catch, and no — that's exactly the kind of term overload worth flagging. 问得好，而且答案是不一样的——这正是一种值得特别指出的术语重叠现象。 In protein bioinformatics, a "domain" is a structurally folded region of a protein; here, in data-quality and common-data-model vocabulary, a "domain" is a category used to group related data elements together — things like Person, Condition, Medication, or Visit — the same kind of grouping OHDSI already uses to organize its own OMOP schema. 在蛋白质生物信息学里，"domain"指的是蛋白质中一个结构上折叠形成的区域；而在这里，在数据质量和通用数据模型的语境下，"domain"指的是一个用来把相关数据元素归类在一起的类别——比如Person（人员）、Condition（诊断）、Medication（用药）或Visit（就诊）——这和OHDSI自己用来组织OMOP模式的分组方式是同一类概念。 Entity-attribute-value, or EAV, is a general modeling pattern for representing heterogeneous data flexibly: an entity is the object of interest, an attribute describes it, and the value is the attribute's actual content — borrowed here because a DQ check itself becomes the entity, with "data element" and "function" as its two attributes. 实体-属性-值（entity-attribute-value，EAV）是一种用来灵活表示异构数据的通用建模模式：实体是关注的对象，属性是对该对象的一种描述，值是该属性的实际内容——这里借用这个模式，是因为一条数据质量检查本身就成了"实体"，而"数据元素"和"功能"则是它的两个"属性"。 Concretely, a check like "number of persons with at least one drug occurrence" has the data element "persons" — the primary focus — and the function "number of" — the quantitative measure applied to it. 举个具体的例子，像"至少有一次用药记录的人数"这样一条检查，它的数据元素是"persons"（人员）——也就是主要关注对象——而功能是"number of"（计数）——也就是施加在这个对象上的量化测量方式。

JORDAN Why did this need a new model at all — what was actually broken? 为什么需要一个新模型——到底哪里出了问题？

ALEX The underlying problem is sheer volume and format heterogeneity: large clinical data research networks had each accumulated hundreds to thousands of data-quality checks, stored as plain narrative text in spreadsheets, in whatever format each network's own team happened to use, with no shared way to say what any given check is actually checking. 根本问题在于数量庞大、格式又五花八门：大型临床数据研究网络各自积累了成百上千条数据质量检查，以纯文本叙述形式存放在电子表格里，用的是各个网络团队自己习惯的格式，彼此之间没有共同的方式来说明某条检查到底在检查什么。 This paper works with two such networks: OHDSI, contributing 239 total checks — 172 "pre-computational" checks from a prior study plus 67 additional community-derived ones — and Kaiser Permanente's Center for Effectiveness and Safety Research, CESR, a clinical research network built across Kaiser's eight regional research centers, contributing 3,434 checks. 这篇论文研究了两个这样的网络：OHDSI贡献了共计239条检查——172条来自此前一项研究的"前计算（pre-computational）"检查，再加上67条社区补充的检查——以及凯撒医疗（Kaiser Permanente）效果与安全研究中心（Center for Effectiveness and Safety Research，CESR），一个跨越凯撒八个区域研究中心建立的临床研究网络，贡献了3434条检查。 Without a shared conceptual model, you simply can't answer basic questions like "how many of our checks are actually about medications versus visits," "do we have any checks at all on a particular data element," or "how does our check coverage compare to a different network's" — and manually re-tagging thousands of narrative checks by hand isn't realistic, which is exactly why the paper's second goal is testing whether natural language processing can do that tagging at scale instead. 没有共享的概念模型，你根本答不出一些最基本的问题，比如"我们的检查里到底有多少是关于用药的、多少是关于就诊的""我们对某个特定数据元素到底有没有任何检查"，或者"我们的检查覆盖面和另一个网络相比怎么样"——而手动给成千上万条叙述式检查重新打标签并不现实，这正是为什么这篇论文的第二个目标，是测试自然语言处理能不能在规模化场景下替代人工完成这个打标签工作。

JORDAN Walk me through how they actually built and tested this. 具体讲讲他们是怎么搭建和测试这个模型的。

ALEX They started by manually reviewing a random 10% sample from each network to define the model, landing on exactly two constructs per check — one data element, one function — then repurposed an existing open-source NLP system, originally built for parsing clinical-trial eligibility criteria, to extract those two terms automatically, training it on a small annotated subset — 18 OHDSI checks, 344 CESR checks — before applying it to the much larger remaining testing set. 他们首先从每个网络中随机抽取10%的样本进行人工审阅以确定模型，最终确定每条检查对应恰好两个构件——一个数据元素，一个功能——然后沿用了一个现有的开源NLP系统，这个系统原本是为解析临床试验入组标准设计的，用它来自动提取这两个术语，先在一个小型标注子集上训练——18条OHDSI检查，344条CESR检查——再应用到规模大得多的剩余测试集上。 They set a 90% accuracy threshold per construct per network, and if the model didn't clear it, they annotated more checks and retrained iteratively until it did — a sensible, if slightly circular, quality gate since the threshold is applied to data the model was itself trained toward. 他们为每个网络的每个构件设定了90%的准确率门槛，如果模型没达到这个标准，就再标注更多检查、反复重新训练直到达标——这是一个合理但略带循环性质的质量门槛，因为这个门槛本身就是用模型被训练去逼近的那批数据来检验的。 Here's the design choice worth pushing back on: the model enforces exactly one data element and one function per check, on the stated assumption that each check has a single intended focus — but their own worked example shows the cost of that constraint directly, since the OHDSI check "number of persons with at least one drug occurrence, by drug_concept_id" gets reduced to just "persons" and "number of," silently dropping the fact that the check is actually stratified by drug concept and requires each person to have at least one drug occurrence. 这里有一个值得质疑的设计选择：这个模型强制每条检查只对应一个数据元素和一个功能，其假设前提是每条检查都有单一的关注焦点——但他们自己给出的例子就直接展示了这种约束的代价，因为OHDSI那条"至少有一次用药记录的人数，按drug_concept_id分层"的检查，被简化成了仅仅"persons"和"number of"两个词，悄悄丢掉了这条检查实际上是按药物概念分层的、而且要求每个人至少有一次用药记录这个事实。 That's a real loss of information for any check whose value lies precisely in its qualifying clause, not just its headline focus. 对于任何价值恰恰在于其限定条件、而不只是其主旨焦点的检查来说，这是一种实实在在的信息损失。
