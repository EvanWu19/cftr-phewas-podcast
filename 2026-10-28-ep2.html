Ep2 (Track B — AI tooling): Harvest and harmonize, don't rebuild

Chandak, Huang, and Zitnik (2023, Scientific Data) introduce PrimeKG, a multimodal precision-medicine knowledge graph harmonizing 20 public biomedical resources (DrugBank, DisGeNET, MONDO, HPO, Orphanet, Reactome, SIDER, UMLS, and more) into one versioned, downloadable, reproducible graph: 129,375 nodes across 10 types and 4,050,249 undirected relationships, covering 17,080 clinically-grouped diseases (90.8% of Orphanet's rare-disease catalog) with richer drug-disease edge types (indication, contraindication, off-label use) than prior efforts like SPOKE, HSDN, or GARD. The disease-entity-resolution problem — the same disease fragmenting into dozens of inconsistently-granular ontology concepts — is solved with a ClinicalBERT-embedding similarity cutoff plus manual approval, collapsing 22,236 raw MONDO concepts into the 17,080 grouped diseases. A held-out drug-repurposing validation against 40 FDA approvals made after the graph's mid-2021 cutoff found significant network-proximity signal in 8 of 10 testable cases. The paper's core lesson for this podcast is a tool-development pattern — harvest and harmonize a large public resource rather than rebuilding bespoke infrastructure — more than any specific fact inside the graph. Buildable artifact: a CF-Focused Subgraph Extractor from PrimeKG.

Plain-English glossary

  • Knowledge graph (nodes and edges) — a network where nodes are entities (a disease, a gene, a drug) and edges are typed relationships between them (e.g., "this drug treats this disease").（中文：知识图谱，节点代表实体（疾病、基因、药物），边代表它们之间带类型的关系（例如"这种药物治疗这种疾病"）。）
  • Ontology (MONDO, UMLS, HPO, Orphanet) — a controlled, hierarchical vocabulary assigning one stable identifier per concept; MONDO unifies disease vocabularies, UMLS is a broad biomedical concept metathesaurus, HPO (Human Phenotype Ontology) codes clinical signs/symptoms, and Orphanet focuses on rare diseases.（中文：本体，一种受控的层级化词汇表，为每个概念分配唯一稳定标识符；MONDO统一疾病词汇，UMLS是更广泛的生物医学概念元词库，HPO（人类表型本体）编码临床体征/症状，Orphanet专注于罕见病。）
  • Disease entity resolution — the problem of the same biological disease being split into differently-worded, inconsistently-granular concepts across different ontologies (e.g., autism: 37 MONDO concepts vs. 192 in UMLS vs. 6 in Orphanet).（中文：疾病实体解析，指同一种生物学疾病在不同本体中被拆分成措辞不同、颗粒度不一致的概念这一问题（例如自闭症：MONDO中37个概念，UMLS中192个，Orphanet中6个）。）
  • ClinicalBERT embedding similarity — using a BERT-family language model pretrained on biomedical/clinical text to represent concept names as vectors, then grouping names whose vectors are similar enough (here, cosine similarity ≥0.98) as candidates for merging, with a human approving each final match.（中文：ClinicalBERT嵌入相似度，使用在生物医学/临床文本上预训练的BERT系语言模型，将概念名称表示为向量，再将向量足够相似（此处余弦相似度≥0.98）的名称作为合并候选，并由人工最终审核确认每一项匹配。）
  • Network proximity (drug repurposing) — measuring the shortest graph-path distance between a drug and a disease as a proxy for how likely that drug is to be therapeutically relevant to that disease.（中文：网络接近度（药物重定位），通过计算药物与疾病在图谱中的最短路径距离，作为该药物对该疾病具有治疗相关性可能性的代理指标。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • ClinicalBERT-embedding disease grouping — collapsing redundant ontology concepts into clinically meaningful nodes via language-model embedding similarity plus human approval, reusable for harmonizing any fragmented controlled vocabulary.
  • Largest-connected-component extraction (iGraph) — a simple sanity check confirming a merged multi-source graph is genuinely well-connected (99.998% of edges retained here) rather than scattered islands.
  • Held-out temporal validation via network proximity — testing a knowledge graph's usefulness against real-world events (post-cutoff FDA approvals) rather than only internal consistency metrics.
  • Rich drug-disease edge typing (indication/contraindication/off-label) — encoding negative and near-positive relationships, not just positive "treats" edges, for richer downstream model training signal.

ALEX Today's Track B pick is in Scientific Data, originally 2023 — Chandak, Huang, and Zitnik, "Building a knowledge graph to enable precision medicine," the paper behind PrimeKG. 今天Track B的选题发表在《Scientific Data》上，最初发表于2023年——作者是Chandak、Huang和Zitnik，题目是《构建一个支持精准医学的知识图谱》，也就是PrimeKG背后的论文。 We're reading it because it's a textbook tool-development example: instead of building bespoke infrastructure every time you need to connect a gene to a disease to a drug, the authors harvested twenty separate public resources, harmonized them, and shipped the whole thing as one versioned, downloadable, reproducible graph with the build code published alongside it. 我们读这篇论文的原因是，它是一个典型的工具开发范例：作者没有每次需要把基因、疾病和药物连接起来时都重新搭建定制化的基础设施，而是收集了二十个独立的公共资源，对它们进行协调统一，并把整个成果作为一个带版本、可下载、可复现的图谱发布出来，同时公开了构建代码。 That "harvest and harmonize, don't rebuild" pattern is the thing worth stealing here, more than any specific disease or drug fact inside the graph.这种"收集与协调，而非重新构建"的模式，比图谱内部任何具体的疾病或药物事实都更值得我们借用。

JORDAN Let's set up the vocabulary first — what actually is a "knowledge graph" in this biomedical sense, and what are MONDO, UMLS, HPO, and Orphanet? 我们先把词汇准备好——在这个生物医学语境下，"知识图谱"到底是什么？MONDO、UMLS、HPO和Orphanet又是什么？

ALEX A knowledge graph here is just nodes and edges — a node is an entity like a disease, a gene, a drug, or an anatomical structure, and an edge is a typed relationship between two nodes, like "this drug treats this disease" or "this gene is expressed in this anatomy." 在这里，知识图谱其实就是节点和边——一个节点代表一个实体，比如疾病、基因、药物或解剖结构，而一条边代表两个节点之间的某种带类型的关系，比如"这种药物治疗这种疾病"或"这个基因在这个解剖部位表达"。 The hard part in biomedicine is that every resource names the same disease differently, so you need ontologies — controlled, hierarchical vocabularies that assign one stable identifier per concept. 生物医学领域的难点在于，每个资源对同一种疾病的命名方式都不一样，所以你需要本体——一种受控的、层级化的词汇表，为每个概念分配一个稳定的唯一标识符。 MONDO is a disease ontology built specifically to unify other disease vocabularies under one system; UMLS is a much broader biomedical concept metathesaurus covering far more than just diseases; HPO, the Human Phenotype Ontology, codes clinical signs and symptoms rather than diagnoses themselves; and Orphanet is a database focused specifically on rare diseases, with curated prevalence and clinical-management information most other resources don't bother collecting.MONDO是一个专门为统一其他疾病词汇表而构建的疾病本体；UMLS是一个范围更广的生物医学概念元词库，覆盖的内容远不止疾病；HPO，即人类表型本体，编码的是临床体征和症状，而不是诊断本身；Orphanet则是一个专门聚焦罕见病的数据库，收录了大多数其他资源都不会去整理的患病率和临床管理信息。

JORDAN So why did we need yet another knowledge graph — what was missing from what already existed? 那为什么我们还需要另一个知识图谱——现有的那些到底缺了什么？

ALEX A few things were already out there, but each had a real limitation. 确实已经存在一些图谱，但每一个都有实际的局限。 SPOKE was an early, influential effort linking heterogeneous biomedical databases around diseases, but it only covered 137 diseases and had no textual clinical-guideline layer; the Human Disease Network and Human Symptom-Disease Network were important early demonstrations of disease-symptom connectivity but were narrow in scope; GARD focused exclusively on rare diseases and so had nothing to say about common ones; and CORD-19, built during the pandemic, was COVID-specific by design.市面上已经有一些工作，但各有局限。SPOKE是一项早期且颇具影响力的工作，围绕疾病连接了多个异质生物医学数据库，但它只覆盖137种疾病，也没有文本化的临床指南层；人类疾病网络和人类症状-疾病网络是展示疾病-症状关联性的重要早期尝试，但范围较窄；GARD专门聚焦罕见病，所以对常见病完全没有涉及；而CORD-19是在疫情期间构建的，设计之初就专门针对新冠。 The deeper problem behind all of them is what the authors call disease entity resolution: the same biological disease gets split into dozens of differently-worded, inconsistently-granular concepts across ontologies — their own worked example is autism, where MONDO lists 37 separate concepts, UMLS lists 192, and Orphanet lists just 6, with no obvious one-to-one mapping between any of them. 所有这些工作背后更深层的问题，就是作者所说的"疾病实体解析"：同一种生物学意义上的疾病，在不同本体中会被拆分成几十个措辞不同、颗粒度也不一致的概念——他们自己给出的例子就是自闭症，MONDO列出了37个独立概念，UMLS列出了192个，而Orphanet只列出了6个，这些概念之间没有明显的一一对应关系。 Without solving that, you can't build one coherent, dense graph around diseases — you'd end up with dozens of disconnected disease nodes representing what's really a single clinical entity.如果不解决这个问题，就无法围绕疾病构建出一个连贯、致密的图谱——最终会得到几十个彼此不相连的疾病节点，而它们实际上代表的是同一个临床实体。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built, then — how did twenty separate resources turn into one graph? 那就带我走一遍他们具体构建了什么——二十个独立资源是怎么变成一个图谱的？

ALEX They settled on ten node types — disease, gene/protein, drug, phenotype/effect, anatomy, biological process, molecular function, cellular component, pathway, and exposure — and for each type they picked one primary ontology as the canonical identifier space, like MONDO for disease and Entrez Gene IDs for gene/protein, then mapped every other resource's records onto those identifiers. 他们确定了十种节点类型——疾病、基因/蛋白质、药物、表型/效应、解剖结构、生物过程、分子功能、细胞组分、通路和暴露因素——并为每种类型选定了一个主要本体作为标准标识符空间，比如疾病用MONDO，基因/蛋白质用Entrez基因ID，然后把其他每一个资源的记录都映射到这些标识符上。 To solve the disease entity resolution problem, they used a BERT-family language model called ClinicalBERT — pretrained on biomedical text and clinical notes — to embed disease-group name candidates into a shared vector space, computed cosine similarity between those embeddings, and applied an empirically chosen similarity cutoff of 0.98, after which a human still manually approved each suggested grouping before it was finalized; this collapsed 22,236 raw disease concepts in MONDO down to 17,080 clinically meaningful grouped diseases. 为了解决疾病实体解析问题，他们使用了一种名为ClinicalBERT的BERT系语言模型——该模型在生物医学文本和临床记录上进行了预训练——把候选的疾病分组名称嵌入到一个共享的向量空间中，计算这些嵌入之间的余弦相似度，并采用了一个经验选定的相似度阈值0.98，在此基础上，仍由人工对每一个建议的分组逐一进行最终审核确认；这一过程把MONDO中原始的22,236个疾病概念，合并归并为17,080个具有临床意义的分组疾病。 After harmonizing and merging all twenty resources into one raw graph, they extracted its largest connected component using the iGraph library — a step that drops any node with no path connecting it to the rest of the graph — and this retained 99.998% of the original edges, confirming the graph was already well-connected rather than a scattering of disconnected islands; they then layered in textual features for drug and disease nodes from DrugBank, Drug Central, Mayo Clinic, Orphanet, and UMLS, so each node carries not just graph structure but human-readable clinical description.在协调并合并全部二十个资源成为一个原始图谱之后，他们使用iGraph库提取出其最大连通分量——这一步会剔除任何与图谱其余部分没有路径相连的节点——结果保留了原始边数的99.998%，这证实了该图谱本身已经高度连通，而不是一堆零散的孤岛；随后，他们从DrugBank、Drug Central、Mayo Clinic、Orphanet和UMLS中为药物和疾病节点补充了文本特征，所以每个节点携带的不只是图结构，还有人类可读的临床描述。

ALEX Here's where I'd push back a little. 不过这里有个地方我想提出一点质疑。 The disease-grouping step leans on a manually-approved similarity cutoff chosen empirically rather than validated against an independent gold standard, and the paper is explicit that a human approved every suggested match — which is fine for a one-time 2021 build, but it's in real tension with the paper's own framing of PrimeKG as "easy to update" as new data becomes available, since every future ontology refresh that touches disease groupings would need that same manual-approval bottleneck repeated. 疾病分组这一步依赖的是一个经验选定、且经人工逐一审核确认的相似度阈值，而不是针对独立的金标准进行验证——对于2021年那次性构建来说这没问题，但这与论文自身所宣称的"随着新数据出现，PrimeKG易于更新"的说法存在实际张力，因为未来任何涉及疾病分组的本体更新，都需要重复这同一道人工审核的瓶颈。 In practice, that means PrimeKG as released is a frozen snapshot from mid-2021 — useful, but not a live, continuously-refreshed resource, and anyone building on it today needs to be clear-eyed about exactly how stale the underlying ontology versions already are.实际上，这意味着公开发布的PrimeKG是2021年年中的一个冻结快照——有用，但不是一个持续更新的实时资源，任何今天想在它基础上构建应用的人，都需要清楚地认识到其底层本体版本到底已经过时到什么程度。

JORDAN Does it actually work, though — did they validate it on anything beyond just building it? 不过它到底有没有真正起作用——除了构建出来之外，他们有没有在别的方面验证过它？

ALEX Two validations, both genuinely useful. 两项验证，都确实有用。 First, the scale numbers themselves: PrimeKG has 129,375 nodes and 4,050,249 undirected relationships — the paper's own edge-count table lists 8,100,498 directed edges because each undirected relationship is stored in both directions — spanning 17,080 grouped diseases, and it captures 90.8% of all 9,348 rare diseases catalogued in Orphanet, which the authors note is one to two orders of magnitude better disease coverage than SPOKE, HSDN, or GARD individually. 首先是规模本身的数字：PrimeKG有129,375个节点和4,050,249条无向关系——论文自己的边数统计表列出的是8,100,498条有向边，因为每一条无向关系都被双向各存储了一次——覆盖17,080个经过分组的疾病，并且捕捉到了Orphanet收录的全部9,348种罕见病中90.8%的病种，作者指出，这比SPOKE、HSDN或GARD任何单独一个的疾病覆盖范围都高出一到两个数量级。 Second, a genuine held-out validation: they took 40 drugs the FDA approved after PrimeKG's June 2021 data cutoff, found 11 were repurposed existing drugs rather than brand-new compounds, and ran a network-proximity analysis — the shortest graph-path distance between each repurposed drug and its newly-approved disease indication, compared against 1,000 randomly sampled non-indicated diseases. 其次是一项真正意义上的留出集验证：他们选取了FDA在PrimeKG数据截止日期（2021年6月）之后批准的40种药物，发现其中11种是老药新用，而非全新化合物，然后进行了一项网络接近度分析——计算每种重新利用的药物与其新获批适应症之间在图谱中的最短路径距离，并与1000个随机抽取的非适应症疾病进行对比。 Only one of the eleven pairs already had a direct indication edge in PrimeKG, confirming there was no temporal data leakage from the future into the 2021 snapshot, and for eight of the ten statistically testable pairs, the real drug-disease distance was significantly shorter than the randomized baseline after Bonferroni correction — for familial hypercholesterolemia specifically, you'd only need to screen about 3.4% of all drugs in the graph to land on the one that actually got approved.在这十一对药物-疾病组合中，只有一对在PrimeKG中已经存在直接的适应症边，这证实了未来数据没有泄漏进2021年这个快照里，而在十对可进行统计检验的组合中，有八对在经过Bonferroni校正后，真实的药物-疾病距离显著短于随机基线——以家族性高胆固醇血症为例，你只需要筛查图谱中大约3.4%的药物，就能找到那一个真正获批的药物。

JORDAN You mentioned the autism example earlier as the disease-entity-resolution problem — did they actually confirm their grouping strategy resolved it sensibly, or just assert it? 你之前提到自闭症的例子，用来说明疾病实体解析问题——他们有没有真正确认自己的分组策略合理地解决了这个问题，还是只是断言而已？

ALEX They ran a dedicated case study on it, and it's worth walking through because it's honest about a real limitation. 他们专门对此做了一项案例研究，值得讲一讲，因为它坦率地承认了一个真实的局限。 Autism has been clinically described as falling into roughly three subgroups — one marked mainly by seizures, one by multisystem and gastrointestinal involvement, and one by psychiatric comorbidity — but none of MONDO's 37 raw autism concepts, UMLS's 192, or Orphanet's 6 map cleanly onto those three clinical subgroups; many of MONDO's concepts are things like "autism, susceptibility to, 1" and "autism, susceptibility to, 2," which read as database bookkeeping artifacts rather than clinically meaningful categories. 自闭症在临床上被描述为大致分为三个亚组——一组主要表现为癫痫发作，一组主要涉及多系统和胃肠道症状，一组则以精神共病为主——但无论是MONDO的37个原始自闭症概念、UMLS的192个，还是Orphanet的6个，都没有干净地对应到这三个临床亚组上；MONDO里许多概念类似"自闭症易感性1型""自闭症易感性2型"，读起来更像是数据库记账产物，而不是具有临床意义的分类。 Their BERT-based grouping strategy collapses the raw ontology entries into fewer, denser nodes, but the paper is careful to say this makes the graph more clinically meaningful in general rather than claiming it specifically recovers those three known autism subgroups — an honest gap between what the grouping buys you and what it would take to actually validate disease-subtype resolution.他们基于BERT的分组策略把原始本体条目合并成了数量更少、连接更密集的节点，但论文很谨慎地表示，这只是让图谱整体上更具临床意义，而没有声称它能够具体还原出那三个已知的自闭症亚组——这是分组所带来的好处，与真正验证疾病亚型解析之间一个诚实存在的差距。

JORDAN And the richer drug-disease edges you mentioned earlier — indication, contraindication, off-label — why does that matter beyond just having more data? 你之前提到更丰富的药物-疾病边类型——适应症、禁忌症、说明书外用途——这除了数据更多之外，为什么重要？
