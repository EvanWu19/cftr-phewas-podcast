Blurb

This episode covers "From text to translation: using language models to prioritize variants for clinical review" (Li W, Li X, Lavallee E, Saparov A, Zitnik M, Cassa C; Genome Medicine, 2026). The authors mined over 2.3 million ClinVar free-text variant summaries, filtered them down to ~1.2 million evidence-bearing sentences, and fine-tuned a BioBERT-base model ("ClinVar-BERT") to classify whether a lab's own written justification shows evidence of pathogenic/likely-pathogenic, benign/likely-benign, or uncertain status — rather than just trusting the lab's final label. ClinVar-BERT's VUS-level predictions correlated with independent deep mutational scanning and AlphaMissense data in BRCA1, TP53, and PTEN, and the model flagged thousands of ClinGen-panel variants for expert re-review. For this track, it lands as a transferable pipeline — not a CFTR result — pointing toward a buildable prototype: a CFTR evidence-lean scorer built on the same architecture, applied to CFTR's own ClinVar submission text.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP — The points-based evidence framework (from the American College of Medical Genetics and the Association for Molecular Pathology) that labs use to combine case, computational, and functional evidence into a five-tier variant classification.（中文：美国医学遗传学会与分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类证据评分框架，综合病例、计算和功能证据，得出五级分类结果。）
  • AlphaMissense — A structure-aware deep learning model from DeepMind that predicts a missense variant's pathogenicity.（中文：DeepMind开发的、基于蛋白结构信息的深度学习模型，用于预测错义变异的致病性。）
  • ClinVar — The public NIH database where clinical labs submit variant classifications along with free-text summaries justifying them.（中文：NIH维护的公共数据库，临床实验室在此提交变异分类结果及相应的自由文本说明。）
  • DMS (Deep Mutational Scanning) — A lab method that systematically tests thousands of variants of a gene for functional effect within a single experiment.（中文：一种实验方法，在单次实验中系统性检测某基因数千个变异的功能影响。）
  • HGVS — The standardized notation system for writing out a genetic variant (e.g., c.1521_1523delCTT).（中文：书写基因变异的标准化命名规范，例如c.1521_1523delCTT。）
  • REVEL — An ensemble computational score that combines multiple existing pathogenicity predictors into one number.（中文：一种整合多种现有致病性预测工具的综合计算评分。）
  • VCEP / ClinGen — A Variant Curation Expert Panel, a disease- or gene-specific group under the ClinGen umbrella tasked with periodically re-reviewing variants, especially VUS.（中文：变异审核专家小组，隶属于ClinGen框架下，针对特定疾病或基因，负责定期重新审核变异，尤其是VUS。）
  • VUS — Variant of Uncertain Significance: a variant with insufficient evidence to be called clearly pathogenic or clearly benign.（中文：意义不明的变异，指证据不足以明确判定为致病或良性的变异。）

Full citation

Li W, Li X, Lavallee E, Saparov A, Zitnik M, Cassa C. From text to translation: using language models to prioritize variants for clinical review. Genome Medicine. 2026. PMID: 42157253 | PMCID: PMC13352863 | DOI: 10.1186/s13073-026-01661-7

Methods/tools to lift

  • Sentence-level evidence classifier — keeps only sentences containing actual evidence, discarding description/conclusion sentences.
  • MinHash-based near-duplicate removal — hashing technique applied at the submission level to strip boilerplate/near-identical text.
  • BioBERT-base fine-tuning — domain-pretrained BERT (PubMed abstracts + PMC full text) fine-tuned on a stratified, class/lab/gene-balanced training set.
  • LR+ (positive likelihood ratio) calibration — converts model probabilities into calibrated evidence-strength estimates using already-classified ClinVar variants.
  • Lab-submission-to-variant aggregation — rolls up multiple submission-level predictions per variant into a multi-submitter consensus triage signal.
  • Domain-vs-general-domain LM benchmarking — compares BioBERT/ClinicalBERT/ScholarBERT against general BERT/RoBERTa to justify the domain-fine-tuning approach.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority: Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation & harmonization.

Buildable artifact — CFTR Evidence-Lean Scorer: Input CFTR ClinVar free-text submission summaries; run the sentence-evidence filtering step; apply a fine-tuned (or the published) ClinVar-BERT-style model to score each submission's pathogenic-vs-benign evidence lean; aggregate to flag CFTR VUS with discordant or strongly-leaning multi-submitter evidence for expert review. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • Whether genome-wide ClinGen panel norms generalize to CFTR-specific curation (CFTR2 has its own conventions distinct from generic ClinVar/ClinGen practice).
  • Whether CFTR-specific free-text submission volume is sufficient to fine-tune on, versus applying general ClinVar-BERT weights as-is.
  • How to calibrate LR+ thresholds for a single-gene, single-disease context rather than a genome-wide one.
  • The circularity risk that persists at single-gene scale: the model still learns from lab-written evidence text to predict lab-reached conclusions.

JORDAN Before we get into the paper, let's set up vocabulary, because this whole thing lives inside the variant classification world. 在进入论文之前，我们先梳理一下词汇，因为这篇论文整个建立在变异分类体系之上。 First, HGVS — that's just the standardized notation for writing out a variant, like c.1521_1523delCTT for the CFTR F508del change. ALEX Right, and the framework labs use to decide pathogenic versus benign versus VUS is ACMG/AMP — a points-based rubric from the American College of Medical Genetics and the Association for Molecular Pathology that weighs case data, computational predictions, and functional evidence. 对，实验室用来判断致病、良性还是VUS的框架叫ACMG/AMP（美国医学遗传学会与分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类证据框架），是一套打分规则，综合病例数据、计算预测和功能实验证据。 ClinVar is the public NIH database where labs submit their variant classifications and the free-text summaries justifying them — that free text is the raw material for this whole paper. ClinVar是NIH维护的公共数据库，实验室会把变异分类结果和说明理由的自由文本提交到这里，这些文本正是这篇论文的原始素材。

JORDAN One more thing worth nailing down — ACMG/AMP isn't itself a model, it's a checklist curators fill in by hand, right? 还有一点值得确认——ACMG/AMP本身不是一个模型，而是审核专家手动填写的一份清单，对吗？

ALEX Exactly — each piece of evidence gets assigned a strength code, something like 'moderate' or 'strong,' and those codes combine through fixed rules to land on one of five tiers, from benign to pathogenic, with VUS sitting in the unresolved middle. 没错——每一条证据都会被赋予一个强度等级，比如"中等"或"强"，这些等级通过固定规则组合，最终落在从良性到致病的五个层级中的某一级，而VUS正好停留在悬而未决的中间位置。

JORDAN And VCEP — Variant Curation Expert Panel — under the ClinGen umbrella, is a disease- or gene-specific group of experts who periodically re-review variants, especially the ones stuck as VUS. VCEP（变异审核专家小组）是ClinGen框架下针对特定疾病或基因设立的专家小组，负责定期重新审核变异，尤其是那些卡在VUS状态的变异。 Two more: deep mutational scanning, or DMS, is a lab method that systematically tests thousands of variants of a gene for functional effect in one experiment, and REVEL and AlphaMissense are computational pathogenicity scores — REVEL an ensemble of existing predictors, AlphaMissense a newer structure-aware deep learning model from DeepMind. 还有两个：DMS（深度突变扫描）是一种实验方法，一次性系统测试某个基因数千个变异的功能效应；REVEL和AlphaMissense是计算致病性评分工具——REVEL整合了多种现有预测方法，AlphaMissense则是DeepMind推出的、基于蛋白结构信息的新一代深度学习模型。 Those two give us something orthogonal to check the model's predictions against. 这两个工具为我们提供了可以用来检验模型预测结果的独立参照。

ALEX So here's the problem this paper is chasing. 这篇论文要解决的问题是这样的。 Even with sequencing getting cheap and fast, most rare coding variants are still not well enough characterized to use clinically — they just sit there as VUS. 即便测序已经变得又便宜又快，大多数罕见的编码区变异仍然没有被充分表征到可以用于临床的程度，它们就一直停留在VUS状态。 That's a real bottleneck for personalized medicine, because a VUS tells a clinician almost nothing actionable. 这对个性化医疗来说是一个真正的瓶颈，因为VUS基本无法给临床医生提供可操作的信息。

JORDAN And the twist is, it's not that there's no evidence out there — a lot of variants actually do have clinical evidence curated in ClinVar, submitted by labs doing diagnostic testing. 有意思的是，问题并不是完全没有证据——ClinVar里实际上已经积累了大量由诊断检测实验室提交的变异相关临床证据。 It's that the evidence is scattered across free-text summaries that nobody is systematically mining, and it still isn't enough, on a case-by-case basis, to flip most VUS into a definitive call. 问题在于这些证据散落在自由文本摘要中，没有人系统性地挖掘它们，而且逐个案例来看，这些证据往往还不足以把大多数VUS翻转为明确的分类结果。

ALEX VCEPs are the people whose job is to go back and re-review that evidence and reach a classification, but they're working gene by gene, panel by panel, and the backlog of VUS is enormous relative to expert bandwidth. VCEP的工作就是回头重新审核这些证据并作出分类判断，但他们是按基因、按专家小组逐一进行的，相对于专家的工作量，VUS的积压规模非常庞大。 There was no systematic way to say "look at this subset of VUS first because the text evidence already leans one way" — curators were essentially triaging blind, or relying on manual review of each submission. 此前并没有一种系统化的方法来说"先看这批VUS，因为文本证据已经有明显倾向"——审核专家基本上是在盲审，或者依赖对每条提交记录的人工复核。

JORDAN Do we have a sense of just how big that VUS backlog is, relative to how many variants get cleanly resolved? 我们对这个VUS积压到底有多大、相对于能被干净解决的变异数量来说有多严重，有大致的概念吗？

ALEX The paper doesn't give us a single backlog number to quote, but the framing is blunt — most rare coding variants never clear the bar for a definitive call, and that's exactly why they built a tool aimed at re-triage rather than first-pass classification. 论文里没有给出一个可以直接引用的积压总数，但表述得很直接——大多数罕见编码区变异从未达到可以明确判定的门槛，这正是他们开发这个工具的原因，目标是重新分诊，而不是做首次分类。

JORDAN So the open question going in is: can you take all that unstructured free text — the sentences labs write justifying their calls — and train a model to read the evidence the way a curator would, separate from just parroting back the label the lab already assigned? 所以这里悬而未决的问题是：能不能利用所有这些非结构化的自由文本——也就是实验室为自己的判断撰写的说明句子——训练一个模型，像审核专家那样去读懂证据本身，而不是单纯复述实验室已经给出的标签？ And can that model's read of the evidence be validated against something that didn't come from the labs at all, like functional data or computational scores? 而这个模型对证据的解读，能否用完全不来自实验室的东西——比如功能实验数据或计算评分——加以验证？ That's the gap they're filling. 这正是这篇论文要填补的空白。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually did. 跟我讲讲他们具体是怎么做的。

ALEX They started from over 2.3 million free-text variant summaries pulled from ClinVar. 他们从ClinVar中提取了超过230万条变异相关的自由文本摘要作为起点。 First pass: a sentence-level classifier to keep only sentences that actually contain evidence, as opposed to sentences that are just describing the variant or stating a conclusion. 第一步：用一个句子级分类器，只保留真正包含证据内容的句子，而不是单纯描述变异或陈述结论的句子。 Second pass: MinHash-based near-duplicate removal — a hashing technique that flags and strips out near-identical submissions — applied at the submission level, since labs often reuse boilerplate language across many variants. 第二步：基于MinHash（一种可以识别并剔除近似重复文本的哈希技术）在提交记录层面去重，因为实验室经常在很多变异记录中重复使用模板化的语言。 That left about 1.2 million evidence-focused summaries. 这样筛选下来还剩约120万条以证据为核心的摘要。

JORDAN And the model itself? 模型本身是什么？

ALEX They fine-tuned BioBERT-base — a BERT model pre-trained on PubMed abstracts and PMC full text — on a stratified training set of about 114,000 summaries, balanced roughly 1 to 2 to 2 across benign/likely-benign, pathogenic/likely-pathogenic, and VUS, stratified by class, lab, and gene, with an 80/20 train/test split. 他们在约11.4万条摘要的分层训练集上微调了BioBERT-base（一个在PubMed摘要和PMC全文上预训练的BERT模型），良性/可能良性、致病/可能致病、VUS三类按大约1比2比2的比例配平，并按分类结果、实验室和基因进行分层，训练集与测试集按80/20划分。 That's "ClinVar-BERT." 这个模型就是"ClinVar-BERT"。 It outputs a probability the text shows evidence of P/LP, B/LB, or uncertain status, and then they calibrate how strong that evidence is using positive likelihood ratios, or LR+, derived from variants ClinVar already classified as pathogenic or benign. 它会输出文本显示P/LP、B/LB或不确定状态证据的概率，然后用LR+（阳性似然比，一种衡量证据强度的指标）来校准证据强度，这个LR+是从ClinVar中已经判定为致病或良性的变异中推算出来的。 Finally they roll lab-submission-level predictions up to the variant level, so if multiple labs submitted on the same variant, you get a consensus triage signal. 最后他们把实验室提交层面的预测汇总到变异层面，这样如果同一个变异有多个实验室提交记录，就能得到一个共识层面的分诊信号。

JORDAN Worth flagging — they also admit they hit overfitting early on, where the model was learning template patterns instead of actual evidence content, so they had to redo the preprocessing to strip conclusion statements while keeping evidence sentences, and they stress-tested alternative text-processing approaches to check for leakage. 值得一提的是，他们也承认早期遇到了过拟合问题，模型学到的是模板套话而不是真正的证据内容，所以他们重新设计了预处理流程，去掉结论句、保留证据句，并测试了多种替代文本处理方法来检查是否存在信息泄漏。 They also benchmarked general-domain models like BERT and RoBERTa against biomedical ones like BioBERT, ClinicalBERT, and ScholarBERT — general domain did much worse. 他们还把通用领域模型（如BERT和RoBERTa）与生物医学领域模型（如BioBERT、ClinicalBERT、ScholarBERT）做了对比——通用领域模型表现差很多。

ALEX Here's where I push back a little, though. 不过这里我要提出一点质疑。 The ground truth for fine-tuning is still free text written by the same labs whose final classifications you're ultimately trying to improve on or re-triage — so there's a circularity risk baked in from the start. 用于微调的真实标签依然是由同一批实验室撰写的自由文本，而你最终想要改进或重新分诊的，正是这些实验室自己给出的分类结论——所以从一开始就存在循环论证的风险。 And the MinHash dedup plus sentence-level evidence filtering is doing a lot of work to clean the data, but any aggressive filtering step risks throwing away nuance — a borderline qualifying phrase that a human curator would weight heavily might just get classified as "not evidence" and dropped. 另外，MinHash去重加上句子级的证据过滤，在数据清洗上做了大量工作，但任何激进的过滤步骤都有可能丢掉细微差别——一句人类审核专家会非常看重的、带有保留意味的表述，可能被模型直接判定为"非证据"而被剔除。

JORDAN Okay, so did it actually work? 那么，这个方法到底有没有用？

ALEX On the base classification task, yes, dramatically. 在基础分类任务上，确实有用，而且效果非常显著。 ClinVar-BERT hit 97.2% test accuracy, versus 37.6% for the identical BioBERT architecture without any ClinVar fine-tuning. ClinVar-BERT的测试集准确率达到97.2%，而同样的BioBERT架构如果不经过ClinVar微调，准确率只有37.6%。 That's the fine-tuning doing essentially all the work. 这说明微调几乎贡献了全部的效果提升。

JORDAN But accuracy on held-out ClinVar records is still testing against the same kind of label it was trained on. 但在ClinVar的保留测试记录上测准确率，本质上还是在用它训练时所依赖的同一种标签来检验自己。 What about independent validation? 那独立验证的结果呢？

ALEX That's the more interesting part. 这才是更有意思的部分。 For VUS specifically — remember, these have no ground-truth label by definition — they checked whether ClinVar-BERT's pathogenic-vs-benign evidence call correlated with orthogonal data. 对于VUS——记住，这类变异本来就没有确定的标签——他们检验了ClinVar-BERT判断的致病或良性证据倾向，是否与独立的正交数据相关。 Variants the model flagged as having pathogenic-leaning evidence showed significantly different deep mutational scanning functional-impact estimates in BRCA1, TP53, and PTEN compared to benign-leaning ones — the exact p-values are redacted in the text we have access to, so we'll say honestly: significant, but we can't give you the number. 被模型标记为偏向致病证据的变异，在BRCA1、TP53和PTEN这三个基因中，与偏向良性证据的变异相比，其深度突变扫描功能影响评估结果存在显著差异——我们拿到的文本中具体p值被隐去了，所以老实说：差异是显著的，但我们没法给出具体数字。 For classifying variants as damaging versus function-retaining using that DMS data, the model's evidence calls reached an AUROC of 0.927. 在用DMS数据区分变异是"有害"还是"保留功能"这个任务上，模型证据判断的AUROC达到了0.927。 The same VUS predictions also correlated with significantly different AlphaMissense scores across those same three genes. 同样这些VUS预测结果，在这三个基因上也与AlphaMissense评分的显著差异相符。

JORDAN And at the triage level? 那在分诊层面呢？

ALEX Across genes on secondary-findings screening lists and ClinGen expert panels, they prioritized 7,644 variants where at least two clinical summaries for the same VUS were predicted to show pathogenic evidence, and 7,042 variants where at least two summaries were predicted to show benign evidence. 在次要发现筛查基因列表和ClinGen专家小组覆盖的基因范围内，他们筛选出7,644个变异，其中每个变异至少有两条临床摘要被模型预测显示出致病证据；另有7,042个变异，至少有两条摘要被预测显示出良性证据。 Averaged out, that's 143 variants prioritized per VCEP, ranging from 8 to 907 depending on the panel. 平均下来，每个VCEP大约有143个变异被优先标记，不同专家小组之间从8个到907个不等。 So does it deliver on its stated aim — discerning pathogenic versus benign evidence in free text to help prioritize re-review? 那么它是否实现了自己宣称的目标——从自由文本中辨别致病与良性证据，从而帮助优先安排复审？ Within the limits of what they tested, yes. 在他们测试过的范围内，答案是肯定的。

JORDAN So where does this actually land for us? 那这对我们来说到底意味着什么？
