Ep1 (Track A) — SSEP: Substrate-Specific Effect Predictor for Transporter Variants

Park et al. (2026, Clinical and Translational Science) tackle a problem that looks a lot like CFTR variant scoring from a different angle: drug transporter variants (OCT1, MATE, CNT, OATP families) often have effects that flip direction depending on which drug is being transported — the same OCT1 variant can crush uptake of one drug while boosting uptake of another. Existing variant-effect predictors (SIFT, PolyPhen-2, CADD, AlphaMissense) are substrate-agnostic and give one score per variant, which structurally cannot represent this. The authors built SSEP, a feed-forward deep neural network trained on 172 curated variant-substrate uptake pairs plus deep mutational scanning (DMS) fine-tuning data, that combines protein language model embeddings (ESM-2) with structural docking features to output a different score per variant-drug pair. On held-out data it beat generic predictors (Spearman's ρ = 0.64, AUC 0.912 vs. AlphaMissense's 0.889), and applying it to UK Biobank prescription data uncovered a real OCT1-metformin dosing association that a substrate-agnostic score had erased entirely. The buildable artifact: a CFTR modulator-specific rescue-score prototype, using the same substrate-specific → modulator-specific reframing, starting from a simple stratification of existing VEP scores by CFTR domain and modulator-binding pocket before attempting anything SSEP-scale.

Plain-English glossary

  • AUC / AUCROC — area under the receiver-operating-characteristic curve, a standard classifier performance metric. (中文：接收者操作特征曲线下面积，一种常用的分类器性能指标。)
  • DMS (deep mutational scanning) — an experimental method that generates thousands of variants of a protein and measures each one's function in a single pooled assay. (中文：深度突变扫描，一种系统性构建蛋白质大量变异体并在一次实验中测量其功能的方法。)
  • ESM-2 — a protein language model, a neural network trained on large protein-sequence databases that represents each amino acid position as a vector encoding evolutionary/structural context. (中文：一种蛋白质语言模型，在大规模蛋白序列数据上训练，能把每个氨基酸位点表示为编码进化与结构信息的向量。)
  • Molecular docking — a computational method that predicts how a small molecule (e.g. a drug) fits into a binding pocket on a protein structure. (中文：分子对接，一种预测小分子如何嵌入蛋白质结合口袋的计算方法。)
  • SKAT-O — a rare-variant association test designed to detect signal when a gene carries many different rare variants that a single-variant test would miss. (中文：一种罕见变异关联检验方法，用于检测某基因携带多个不同罕见变异时单变异检验会遗漏的关联信号。)
  • SLC transporter — solute carrier transporter, a membrane protein family that actively moves molecules (drugs, metabolites, ions) across cell membranes. (中文：溶质载体转运体，一类主动搬运分子穿过细胞膜的膜蛋白家族。)
  • Substrate — the molecule (drug/compound) that a transporter moves. (中文：底物，转运体所运输的分子，如药物或化合物。)
  • Substrate-specific effect — the phenomenon where the same transporter variant has different, sometimes opposite, functional effects depending on which substrate is involved. (中文：底物特异性效应，指同一个转运体变异对不同底物产生不同甚至相反功能效应的现象。)

Full citation

Park Y, et al. (2026). Feed-Forward Deep Neural Networks Predict Substrate-Specific Effects of Transporter Variants to Explain Drug Response Variability. Clinical and Translational Science. PMID: 42104208 | PMC13156069 | DOI: 10.1111/cts.70592

Methods / tools to lift

  • SSEP-SNV / SSEP-UV — two-module feed-forward DNN for substrate-specific transporter variant scoring (missense-complete vs. indel/partial-annotation fallback).
  • ESM-2 embeddings — pretrained protein language model features usable for any variant-effect scoring task.
  • AutoDock Vina — open-source docking tool used to place ligands into wild-type/mutant binding pockets.
  • SKAT-O + burden testing — rare-variant aggregation tests applied against UK Biobank prescription-dose phenotypes.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen collaboration (CFTR variant interpretation / functional-effect scoring). MVP: compile published CFTR variant-by-modulator rescue data (correction/potentiation % from CFTR2 and modulator trial literature), stratify existing generic VEP scores by CFTR structural domain and modulator-binding pocket as a first pass (difficulty S–M), before attempting a full docking + DNN pipeline analogous to SSEP (difficulty L).

What to verify first

  • SSEP's own per-gene performance varied widely (AUC 0.72 for OATP1B1 vs. 0.91–1.00 for others) — check whether CFTR's four structural modules behave more like the well- or poorly-performing genes before assuming clean transfer.
  • Substrate transport and channel-modulator binding are mechanistically different (through-protein transport vs. allosteric external-pocket binding) — the docking/feature pipeline needs real adaptation, not a straight copy.
  • SSEP scores showed drug-dependent systematic offsets per the paper's own discussion — check for an analogous modulator-dependent bias before trusting cross-modulator comparisons.
  • UK Biobank validation used real-world prescription records, not controlled pharmacokinetics — any registry-based validation of a CFTR version needs the same framing caveat.
  • Whether a UK-Biobank-style large-scale burden test is the right validation strategy for a registry-scale dataset is a separate open design question.

JORDAN Why two separate models instead of one? 为什么要用两个独立的模型，而不是一个？

JORDAN Did it actually work, though? 那它到底有没有真的起作用？

SKAT-O test — SKAT-O being a statistical test designed specifically to detect association when a gene carries many different rare variants that a single-variant test would miss — SSEP produced the strongest signal of any predictor tested, p equals 0.014 for burden and 0.026 for SKAT-O. 在一个罕见变异负荷检验和SKAT-O检验中——SKAT-O是一种专门设计用来检测某个基因携带许多不同罕见变异时的关联信号的统计检验，而这种信号是单变异检验会漏掉的——SSEP在所有被测试的预测工具中产生了最强的信号，负荷检验p值为0.014，SKAT-O检验p值为0.026。 So the substrate-specific framing wasn't just conceptually cleaner, it uncovered a real association that a generic score had erased. 所以底物特异性的框架不仅仅是概念上更清晰，它还发现了一个原本被通用分数抹平的真实关联。 They also dug into which features actually drove substrate-specific outcomes, and the top hits were all properties of the drug molecule itself, not the protein — ligand molecular weight was the strongest discriminator, with larger ligands more often associated with increased rather than decreased activity, followed by shape complexity, ring count, and molecular symmetry. 他们还深入分析了到底是哪些特征真正驱动了底物特异性的结果，排名最靠前的都是药物分子本身的属性，而不是蛋白质的属性——配体分子量是最强的区分因素，分子量更大的配体往往和活性升高而不是降低相关，其次是形状复杂度、环的数量和分子对称性。 That's a useful reminder that a substrate-specific model has to represent the chemistry of what's being moved, not just the protein doing the moving. 这提醒我们，一个底物特异性模型必须表示被搬运物质本身的化学性质，而不只是负责搬运的蛋白质本身。
