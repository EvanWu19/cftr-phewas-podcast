Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): Fine-mapping causal splice variants with sQTL mapping plus deep-learning splice-effect prediction

Wang and colleagues (Genome Biology, 2026) integrate single-cell splicing-QTL (sQTL) mapping with deep-learning-based splice-effect annotation and statistical fine-mapping to pick out the single, likely-causal variant from within a linked candidate set — then validate one with CRISPR. Using scHi-HOST across 96 lymphoblastoid cell lines with and without influenza A virus (IAV) infection, they find ~43,000 sQTL SNP-junction pairs across 217 genes during infection, prioritize 57 likely causal splice-affecting variants, and experimentally confirm one, rs2297616 in PARP2, which disrupts a 5' splice donor site to produce a 13-amino-acid-longer protein isoform associated with higher viral load. This episode is abstract-only (a very recent 2026 paper with no full text yet available via Europe PMC's fetch routes), flagged on-air throughout. It lands on the Karen/CFTR variant-interpretation priority, with a buildable artifact adapting the sQTL-plus-splice-prediction-plus-fine-mapping pipeline to CFTR splice-region VUS.

Plain-English glossary

  • molQTL (molecular quantitative trait locus) — A genetic variant statistically linked to variation in an intermediate molecular readout (e.g. gene expression or splicing) rather than a whole-body disease phenotype directly.（中文：一种在统计上与某种中间分子层面读数（如基因表达或剪接）相关联的遗传变异，而非直接与整体疾病表型相关联。）
  • sQTL (splicing quantitative trait locus) — A molQTL that links a genetic variant to how a gene's transcript gets spliced, rather than to how much mRNA gets made.（中文：一种molQTL，将遗传变异与某基因转录本的剪接方式联系起来，而非与mRNA产量联系起来。）
  • Splice donor / acceptor site — The boundary sequences at the start (5' donor) and end (3' acceptor) of an intron where the spliceosome cuts and rejoins exons; variants disrupting either can shift, skip, or create splice junctions.（中文：内含子起始端（5'供体）和末端（3'受体）的边界序列，是剪接体切割并重新连接外显子的位置；破坏其中任一位点的变异都可能导致剪接位点移位、外显子跳过或产生新的剪接连接。）
  • Linkage disequilibrium (LD) — Statistical correlation between nearby SNPs that are co-inherited, meaning an association signal usually implicates a whole cluster of candidate variants rather than one.（中文：相邻SNP之间由于被共同遗传而产生的统计相关性，意味着一个关联信号通常会牵连一整簇候选变异，而不是单一一个。）
  • Statistical fine-mapping — The step that narrows a set of LD-linked candidate variants down to the smallest credible set plausibly containing the true causal variant, using association-strength and LD-structure patterns.（中文：利用关联强度和LD结构模式，把一组LD连锁的候选变异缩小到一个最小、可信的集合，使其中大概率包含真正致病变异的那一步分析。）
  • Lymphoblastoid cell line (LCL) — Epstein-Barr-virus-immortalized B cells derived from genotyped individuals; a standard, renewable source of genetically diverse human cells for molecular-QTL studies.（中文：由EB病毒将已测序个体的B细胞永生化得到的细胞系，是分子QTL研究中标准、可持续使用的遗传多样性人类细胞来源。）
  • CRISPR-mediated editing (as functional validation) — Directly manipulating a candidate causal site in the genome and observing whether the predicted downstream effect (here, isoform ratio and viral load) follows, moving a statistical association to a demonstrated causal mechanism.（中文：直接在基因组中操作一个候选致病位点，并观察预测的下游效应（此处为蛋白异构体比例和病毒载量）是否随之改变，从而把一个统计学关联推进为经证实的因果机制。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • scHi-HOST — a single-cell GWAS-style method for mapping genetic effects on cellular phenotypes (here, splicing) at single-cell resolution rather than averaged across a bulk sample.
  • Deep-learning splice-effect annotation — a predictor used to score each LD-linked candidate SNP for its plausible disruption of a 5' donor or 3' acceptor site, providing a functional prior layered onto statistical fine-mapping.
  • Statistical fine-mapping combined with a functional prior — ranking LD-linked candidates using both association strength/LD structure and an orthogonal functional score, rather than either alone.
  • CRISPR-mediated causal validation — editing the candidate splice site directly to confirm that it, and not a linked neighbor, drives the downstream molecular and phenotypic effect.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation (method only), specifically for splice-region and deep-intronic VUS where SpliceAI-type scores alone leave ambiguity among several linked candidates. Buildable artifact — CFTR Splice-Candidate Fine-Mapping Pipeline: take a CFTR splice-region VUS plus its LD-linked neighbors, score each with a splice-effect predictor to generate a functional prior, and combine that with whatever genotype-phenotype or junction-usage data is available (even a small internal panel) via basic statistical fine-mapping to produce a ranked candidate list rather than a single point estimate. Difficulty: M-L (medium to large) — the fine-mapping statistics and splice-prediction scoring are off-the-shelf; assembling a CFTR-relevant junction-usage or allele-specific-expression dataset to fine-map against is the real work.

What to verify first

  • Whether any CFTR-relevant RNA-seq or splice-junction dataset — in airway epithelial or other CF-relevant tissue, including public resources like GTEx — has enough genotyped individuals in the right tissue to run even a stripped-down fine-mapping step; this paper's cohort doesn't transfer, only its method.
  • Whether a deep-learning splice-effect annotator trained broadly performs comparably well on CFTR's specific intron-exon architecture, or needs CFTR-specific recalibration.
  • Whether the paper's "prioritized as likely causal" claim for its 57 variants (a probabilistic ranking, not a validated set beyond the one CRISPR-tested case) and its reported overlap with 100+ GWAS traits (reported as catalog overlap, not independently re-validated trait by trait) should be read as a stronger claim than they actually are.
