Blurb

Diagnosis via sweat chloride → CFTR sequencing + MLPA (to catch large deletions/duplications). Allele breakdown: 84.1% pathogenic, 6.9% likely pathogenic, 5.5% structural, 3.5% VUS/benign; by protein effect 289 in-frame, 279 missense, 162 nonsense, splice-site in 237 alleles. Catalog headline: 9 novel variants across 12 alleles in 9 patients (ACMG: 1 pathogenic, 7 likely pathogenic, 1 VUS) plus complex alleles in 32 patients. ~70% were modulator-eligible by genotype but only 23.7% received one (no reimbursement in Turkey). Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG classification — the American College of Medical Genetics framework for calling variants pathogenic / likely pathogenic / VUS / benign.（中文：美国医学遗传学学会用于把变异判定为致病/可能致病/意义未明/良性的框架。）
  • Allele — one copy of a gene; you carry two, one per parent.（中文：等位基因——基因的一份拷贝，每人两份，来自父母各一。）
  • CFTR2 — the CF-specific clinical database classifying CFTR variants by disease-causing status.（中文：CF 专属的临床数据库，按是否致病对 CFTR 变异分类。）
  • cis / trans (phase) — cis = two variants on the same physical copy; trans = on opposite copies.（中文：顺式=两变异在同一条拷贝上；反式=在两条不同拷贝上。）
  • Complex allele — a single gene copy carrying two or more variants together in cis.（中文：复杂等位基因——同一份基因拷贝上顺式携带两个及以上变异。）
  • CFTR modulator — drugs (e.g. elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor) that partially restore defective CFTR protein for specific genotypes.（中文：CFTR 调节剂——如 elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor，针对特定基因型部分修复缺陷蛋白的药物。）
  • FEV1 — forced expiratory volume in 1 second; standard lung-function measure.（中文：一秒用力呼气容积，标准肺功能指标。）
  • Frameshift — an indel not divisible by three that scrambles all downstream codons.（中文：移码——非 3 倍数的插入/缺失，打乱下游所有密码子。）
  • MLPA — multiplex ligation-dependent probe amplification; detects large deletions/duplications sequencing misses.（中文：多重连接依赖式探针扩增，检测测序漏掉的大片段缺失/重复。）
  • Novel variant — one not previously described in the literature or reference databases.（中文：新变异——此前未在文献或参考数据库中描述过的变异。）
  • Sweat test — measures sweat chloride; the classic CF diagnostic since CFTR is a chloride channel.（中文：汗液检测——测汗液氯离子，CF 经典诊断，因 CFTR 是氯离子通道。）

ALEX Welcome back for our Track C episode. 欢迎回到我们的 Track C 单集。 Today's paper is "Exploring Turkey's mosaic of novel variants and complex alleles in cystic fibrosis genetics," first author Ceren Ayça Yıldız, with the Marmara University group led by Bülent Karadağ, in Pediatric Pulmonology, 2024. 今天的论文是《探索土耳其囊性纤维化遗传学中新变异与复杂等位基因的镶嵌图景》，第一作者 Ceren Ayça Yıldız，来自 Bülent Karadağ 领导的马尔马拉大学团队，2024 年发表于《儿科肺脏病学》(Pediatric Pulmonology)。 It's a genotype-phenotype description of the largest CF center in Turkey, and it feeds the modifier and variant catalog. 它是对土耳其最大 CF 中心的一次基因型—表型描述，为修饰基因与变异目录供料。

JORDAN Let me get the genetics vocabulary set before the numbers. 在看数字之前，我先把遗传学词汇备好。 What's a "complex allele," and how is it different from just having two variants? 什么是“复杂等位基因”，它和单纯拥有两个变异有什么不同？

ALEX Great question, because it's a CF-specific subtlety. 好问题，因为这是 CF 特有的微妙之处。 An allele is one copy of the gene — you have two, one from each parent. 一个等位基因 (allele) 是基因的一份拷贝——你有两份，分别来自父母。 A complex allele is when a single copy carries two or more variants together, in cis — on the same physical strand. 复杂等位基因是指单独一份拷贝同时携带两个或更多变异，且处于顺式 (cis)——在同一条物理链上。 复杂等位基因（同一条染色体拷贝上同时携带两个及以上变异，即顺式排列）。 That matters because the combination can behave differently from either variant alone — sometimes one variant modifies whether the other is pathogenic, and sometimes it changes whether a CFTR modulator drug will work. 这很重要，因为组合的行为可能与任一单个变异都不同——有时一个变异会改变另一个是否致病，有时它会改变某种 CFTR 调节剂药物是否有效。 So "which variants, and are they on the same allele" is a real clinical question, not bookkeeping. 所以“是哪些变异，以及它们是否在同一条等位基因上”是一个真实的临床问题，而非记账细节。

JORDAN And "novel variant" just means not previously reported? “新变异”就是指以前没被报道过的？

ALEX Right — one not previously described in the literature or the reference databases. 对——一个此前未在文献或参考数据库中被描述过的变异。 The two databases that matter here are CFTR2, the CF-specific clinical database that classifies variants by whether they cause CF, and ACMG, the American College of Medical Genetics framework for calling a variant pathogenic, likely pathogenic, VUS, and so on. 这里重要的两个数据库是 CFTR2（CF 专属的临床数据库，按变异是否致 CF 来分类）和 ACMG（美国医学遗传学学会用于判定变异为致病、可能致病、意义未明等的框架）。 ACMG（美国医学遗传学学会的变异致病性判定框架）。 A VUS, again, is a variant of uncertain significance — one you can't yet confidently call. VUS 再说一次，就是意义未明的变异——一个你还无法有把握判定的变异。

JORDAN Why does a Turkey cohort matter to a CF genetics project rooted in US and European data? 一个扎根于美欧数据的 CF 遗传学项目，为什么要在意土耳其队列？

ALEX Because the allele spectrum is population-specific, and that's the whole point. 因为等位基因谱是因群体而异的，而这正是关键所在。 Most of what we "know" about CFTR variant frequencies comes from Northern-European-descent populations. 我们对 CFTR 变异频率所“知道”的大部分，都来自北欧血统的群体。 Turkey sits at the crossroads of Europe and Asia with a deep history of migration and consanguineous marriage, so its variant landscape diverges. 土耳其地处欧亚十字路口，有悠久的迁徙史和近亲通婚，所以它的变异图景是有分歧的。 For a variant-interpretation and modifier catalog, under-represented populations are where the novel alleles and the un-classified variants live. 对一个变异解读与修饰基因目录来说，代表性不足的群体正是新等位基因和未分类变异栖身之处。 If you only ever look where the light is good, you systematically miss those. 如果你只在光线好的地方找，你会系统性地漏掉它们。

JORDAN Fair. So what was the cohort and how did they genotype it? 有道理。那么队列是什么，他们怎么做的基因分型？

ALEX The cohort is 481 people with CF registered in the European CF Society Patient Registry and followed at the Marmara center since 2015. 队列是 481 名登记在欧洲囊性纤维化学会患者登记册、自 2015 年起在马尔马拉中心随访的 CF 患者。 Diagnosis followed the standard path: a positive or borderline sweat chloride test, then CFTR sequencing plus MLPA. 诊断遵循标准路径：汗液氯离子检测阳性或临界，然后做 CFTR 测序加 MLPA。 The sweat test measures chloride in sweat — elevated chloride is the classic CF signature, since CFTR is a chloride channel. 汗液检测测量汗液中的氯离子——氯离子升高是 CF 的经典标志，因为 CFTR 是一个氯离子通道。 汗液检测（测汗液氯离子，CF 的经典诊断，因为 CFTR 是氯离子通道）。 MLPA — multiplex ligation-dependent probe amplification — is a lab method that catches large deletions or duplications that ordinary sequencing reads right past. MLPA（多重连接依赖式探针扩增，一种能捕捉普通测序会漏掉的大片段缺失或重复的实验方法）。 That two-method combination is deliberate: sequence for the small stuff, MLPA for the big structural stuff. 这两种方法的组合是刻意的：测序抓小变异，MLPA 抓大的结构变异。

JORDAN Why does catching the big structural variants matter so much here? 为什么在这里捕捉大的结构变异这么重要？

ALEX Because standard sequencing has a blind spot for them. 因为标准测序对它们有个盲区。 Sequencing reads base-by-base, so it's excellent at single-letter changes but can miss a deletion that removes a whole exon — if the entire region is gone, there's simply nothing to read as "different." 测序是逐个碱基读取的，所以它对单字母改变很在行，却可能漏掉一个删除了整个外显子的缺失——如果整个区域都没了，就根本没有可读作“不同”的东西。 MLPA fills that gap by measuring copy number — how many copies of each exon are present. MLPA 通过测量拷贝数——每个外显子存在几份——来填补这个空缺。 In this cohort 5.5% of alleles were structural variants, which is a meaningful slice you'd lose with sequencing alone. 在这个队列里，5.5% 的等位基因是结构变异，这是单靠测序会丢掉的一块可观比例。 For catalog work the lesson is that a variant list built without a copy-number method is quietly incomplete. 对目录工作而言，教训是：一份不含拷贝数方法构建的变异列表，是悄悄地不完整的。

JORDAN One design point I'd push on: 481 patients from one center is a lot for Turkey, but it's still a single referral site. 我想质疑一个设计点：481 名患者对土耳其来说很多，但仍然只是单一转诊点。 Isn't there selection bias baked in? 这不是天生带着选择偏倚吗？

ALEX Yes, and they're upfront that it's descriptive and retrospective from one tertiary center. 是的，他们坦白这是来自单一三级中心的描述性、回顾性研究。 A referral center concentrates the more severe and more diagnostically puzzling cases, so the novel-variant rate here may run higher than in the general Turkish CF population. 转诊中心会富集更严重、诊断上更棘手的病例，所以这里的新变异率可能高于土耳其 CF 总体人群。 That's a caveat to carry, not a disqualifier — for cataloging novel alleles, a referral center is actually where you want to be looking. 这是要带着的注意事项，而非否定理由——对于编目新等位基因，转诊中心恰恰是你想去找的地方。

JORDAN So what did they find? 那他们发现了什么？

ALEX A broad spectrum: 136 different CFTR variants across the cohort. 一个宽广的谱系：整个队列中有 136 种不同的 CFTR 变异。 F508del, the most common CF variant worldwide, was less prevalent here than the US and European averages — that's the population signal. F508del，全球最常见的 CF 变异，在这里的比例低于美欧平均——这就是那个群体信号。 Of all alleles, 84.1% were pathogenic, 6.9% likely pathogenic, 5.5% structural variants, and the remaining 3.5% were VUS, likely benign, or benign. 在所有等位基因中，84.1% 是致病的，6.9% 可能致病，5.5% 是结构变异，剩下 3.5% 是 VUS、可能良性或良性。 By protein effect they counted 289 in-frame, 279 missense, and 162 nonsense variants, with splice-site variants in 237 alleles. 按蛋白质效应，他们数出 289 个框内变异、279 个错义、162 个无义，还有 237 个等位基因带剪接位点变异。 And the headline for the catalog: nine novel variants across 12 alleles in nine patients, of which ACMG called one pathogenic, seven likely pathogenic, and one a VUS. 而目录的重头戏是：九个新变异，分布在九名患者的 12 个等位基因中，其中 ACMG 判定一个致病、七个可能致病、一个为 VUS。 They also flagged complex alleles in 32 patients. 他们还标记了 32 名患者存在复杂等位基因。

JORDAN Those protein-effect counts — in-frame, missense, nonsense — what do they tell us? 那些蛋白质效应计数——框内、错义、无义——告诉我们什么？

ALEX They sketch the mechanistic mix of how CFTR fails in this population. 它们勾勒出在这个群体中 CFTR 失效方式的机制构成。 A nonsense variant introduces a premature stop codon, usually giving little or no protein — historically the hardest class to treat with modulators, though read-through strategies are being explored. 一个无义变异引入提前终止密码子，通常几乎不产生蛋白质——历来是最难用调节剂治疗的一类，尽管通读策略正在探索中。 A missense variant makes a full-length protein with one wrong amino acid, which may fold or traffic poorly but is often exactly the class modulators can rescue. 一个错义变异会产生一个全长蛋白质但有一个错误氨基酸，它可能折叠或转运不良，却往往正是调节剂能挽救的那一类。 In-frame changes keep the reading frame intact, so their severity varies widely. 框内改变保持阅读框完整，所以其严重程度差异很大。 The reason the breakdown matters for a catalog is that variant class is a first-pass proxy for both severity and druggability — it tells you which buckets to prioritize. 这个分类对目录之所以重要，是因为变异类别是严重程度和可成药性的初步代理指标——它告诉你该优先关注哪些桶。

JORDAN Can you make one of those novel variants concrete? 你能把其中一个新变异说具体吗？

ALEX Sure — take c.865_869del, protein R289Nfs*17, a five-nucleotide deletion at the splice site of exon 7 that shifts the reading frame. 当然——比如 c.865_869del，蛋白质记为 R289Nfs*17，是外显子 7 剪接位点上一个五核苷酸缺失，导致阅读框移位。 A frameshift like this scrambles every downstream codon and usually truncates the protein, and the patient carrying it homozygously had severe disease — she died at 17 with an FEV1 of 24% and a BMI Z-score of −6.26. 这样的移码会打乱下游每一个密码子，通常使蛋白质截短，携带其纯合的患者病情严重——她 17 岁去世，FEV1 为 24%，BMI Z 分数为 −6.26。 FEV1, forced expiratory volume in one second, is the standard lung-function measure; 24% is very advanced disease. FEV1（一秒用力呼气容积，标准的肺功能指标）；24% 属于非常晚期的疾病。 Contrast that with c.3847A>G, R1283G, a missense change paired with F508del in a boy who had only mild respiratory symptoms and, importantly, was eligible for the elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor modulator combo. 与之对比的是 c.3847A>G，即 R1283G，一个与 F508del 配对的错义改变，患儿只有轻微呼吸道症状，而且重要的是，他符合 elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor 调节剂组合的用药资格。 Same gene, two novel variants, opposite ends of severity — that's the catalog value. 同一个基因，两个新变异，处在严重程度的两极——这就是目录的价值。

JORDAN What about the complex alleles in 32 patients — why single those out? 那 32 名患者里的复杂等位基因呢——为什么要单独拎出来？

ALEX Because complex alleles are where genotype-phenotype prediction quietly breaks. 因为复杂等位基因正是基因型—表型预测悄悄失灵的地方。 If you see two variants and assume they're on opposite alleles, you might read a patient as a compound heterozygote when actually both sit in cis on one allele and the other allele is something else entirely. 如果你看到两个变异就假设它们在两条不同的等位基因上，你可能会把一名患者读作复合杂合子，而实际上两者都顺式地在一条等位基因上，另一条等位基因完全是别的东西。 That mis-phasing can flip a modulator-eligibility call, because eligibility rules care about what's on which allele. 这种相位判断错误可能翻转调节剂用药资格的判定，因为资格规则在意的是什么在哪条等位基因上。 A well-known example is a second variant in cis that changes how a primary variant responds to a drug — so cataloging complex alleles isn't trivia, it's decision-relevant. 一个著名的例子是顺式的第二个变异改变了主变异对药物的反应——所以为复杂等位基因编目不是琐碎小事，而是与决策相关的。

JORDAN You mentioned modulator eligibility. 你提到了调节剂用药资格。 Was there a treatment story? 有治疗层面的故事吗？

ALEX There was, and it's a sobering one. 有，而且发人深省。 About 70% of patients had a genotype eligible for at least one modulator, but only 23.7% were actually receiving one, largely because CFTR modulators lacked reimbursement coverage in Turkey. 大约 70% 的患者其基因型至少符合一种调节剂的用药资格，但实际只有 23.7% 在用，主要因为 CFTR 调节剂在土耳其缺乏医保报销。 CFTR modulators are the drugs — like elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor — that partially fix the defective protein for specific genotypes. CFTR 调节剂就是那类药物——如 elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor——能针对特定基因型部分修复缺陷蛋白。 So genotyping the population is a precondition for allocating those drugs — you can't target a therapy you haven't found the variant for. 所以给人群做基因分型是分配这些药物的前提——你无法针对一个尚未找到变异的目标去用药。

JORDAN Where does this land for us, and what's the buildable thing? 这对我们落在哪里，可构建的东西是什么？

ALEX This lands on the Karen collaboration and the modifier/variant catalog — variant interpretation and harmonization. 这落在 Karen 合作项目和修饰/变异目录上——变异解读与协调。 The transferable observation is that population-specific reports like this one are scattered novel-variant sources that need to be harmonized into one notation before they're usable. 可迁移的观察是：像这样的群体专属报告是分散的新变异来源，在可用之前需要被协调成统一的记法。 The buildable artifact — MVP: a small parser that ingests a published CF cohort's variant table, normalizes every entry to HGVS with the legacy name preserved, cross-checks each against CFTR2 and ClinVar, and outputs a tidy table flagging which variants are novel or reclassified relative to those databases. 可构建的产物——MVP：一个小解析器，读入某篇已发表 CF 队列的变异表，把每一条目规范化为 HGVS 并保留旧称，逐一与 CFTR2 和 ClinVar 交叉核对，输出一张整洁的表，标记哪些变异相对这些数据库是新的或被重新分类的。 Input: a published variant table like this paper's Table 3; output: a harmonized, database-cross-referenced variant list. 输入：像本文表 3 那样的已发表变异表；输出：一份协调过、与数据库交叉引用的变异列表。 Difficulty: small to medium — the HGVS normalization is the tricky part. 难度：小到中等——HGVS 规范化是最棘手的部分。

JORDAN Why is HGVS normalization the hard part rather than the database lookups? 为什么难点是 HGVS 规范化，而不是数据库查询？

ALEX Because the same variant can be written many legitimate ways, and only one is canonical. 因为同一个变异可以有多种合法写法，而只有一种是规范的。 A deletion's coordinates can shift depending on which repeated base you assign it to, so HGVS has a strict "align to the 3-prime-most position" rule that naive string matching ignores. 一个缺失的坐标会因你把它归到哪个重复碱基而移动，所以 HGVS 有一条严格的“对齐到最靠 3′ 端位置”的规则，而朴素的字符串匹配会忽略它。 On top of that, legacy CF names like F508del and 1677delTA don't map to c-dot notation by any simple pattern — you need a reference-sequence-aware library to translate them. 更麻烦的是，像 F508del、1677delTA 这样的 CF 旧称无法通过任何简单模式映射到 c. 点式记法——你需要一个感知参考序列的库来翻译它们。 If you skip proper normalization, your database cross-check silently fails to match variants that are actually the same one, and you'll mislabel known variants as novel. 如果你跳过正确的规范化，你的数据库交叉核对会悄悄地匹配不上其实是同一个的变异，你会把已知变异错标成新变异。 That's the trap the parser has to close. 这就是解析器必须堵上的陷阱。

JORDAN That's the harmonization piece Karen raised directly. 那正是 Karen 直接提出的协调工作。
