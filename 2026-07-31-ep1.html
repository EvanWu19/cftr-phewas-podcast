How did this episode land for you?

Track A — In silico predictor performance is gene-specific (cancer-gene panel)

Blurb. Nelson et al. (Human Mutation, 2026) apply the ClinGen SVI / Pejaver-2022 recommended in-silico predictor thresholds (PP3/BP4, at supporting/moderate/strong likelihood-ratio targets of 2.1/4.3/18.7) to five cancer-predisposition genes — TP53, ATM, BRCA1, BRCA2, TERT — using expert-curated truth sets from the ClinGen Evidence Repository (plus a TP53 transactivation-assay set from Fortuno 2018). Across REVEL, MutPred2, BayesDel, VEST4, CADD and AlphaMissense, no single tool was best for all five genes; the general pattern was high specificity bought at the cost of sensitivity. TERT was the systematic failure: every tool fell below 65% sensitivity for pathogenic variants, and AlphaMissense (best-in-class for TP53) did not rescue it. Using the structure-only tool MISCAST, they show TERT's pathogenic variants are enriched at low structural-impact scores — a gene unusually tolerant of physicochemical change with many surface-exposed, interaction-disrupting pathogenic residues underrepresented in training data. Gene-specific cutoff recalibration (e.g. VEST4 down to 0.5 for TERT; AlphaMissense >0.40 pathogenic / <0.09 benign) recovered performance. The transferable lesson for CFTR: validate PP3/BP4 cutoffs on CFTR's own truth set before trusting them, and stratify by buried-vs-surface residue — buildable as cftr-predictor-gene-calibration.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP criteria — the standard menu of evidence codes clinical labs combine to classify a variant as pathogenic/benign.（中文：临床实验室用来把变异分类为致病/良性的标准证据条目清单。）
  • BP4 / PP3 — the ACMG codes for "in-silico evidence supports benign" (BP4) and "supports pathogenic" (PP3).（中文：ACMG 中表示“计算机证据支持良性”(BP4) 和“支持致病”(PP3) 的条目。）
  • Constraint (gene constraint) — how strongly natural selection has depleted variation in a gene; a highly constrained gene tolerates few changes.（中文：自然选择在一个基因中清除变异的程度；高度受约束的基因几乎不容许改变。）
  • Missense variant — a single-base change that swaps one amino acid for another; protein is still made but one residue differs.（中文：单碱基改变，把一个氨基酸换成另一个；蛋白仍能合成但一个残基不同。）
  • MAVE — multiplexed assay of variant effect; a high-throughput experiment measuring function of thousands of variants at once.（中文：多重变异效应实验，一次性测量成千上万个变异功能的高通量实验。）
  • OddsPath / positive likelihood ratio — the statistical strength attached to an ACMG evidence code; the ladder is ~2.1 (supporting), 4.3 (moderate), 18.7 (strong).（中文：附加在 ACMG 证据条目上的统计强度，阶梯约为 2.1 支持、4.3 中等、18.7 强。）
  • Relative solvent accessibility (RSA) — whether an amino-acid residue is buried in the protein core or exposed on its surface.（中文：某氨基酸残基是埋在蛋白核心里还是暴露在表面。）
  • Splice site — the intron/exon boundary that determines how RNA is cut and joined; variants there can alter the protein via mis-splicing.（中文：内含子与外显子的交界，决定 RNA 如何剪切拼接；此处的变异可通过错误剪接改变蛋白。）
  • Truth set — a set of variants already confidently classified pathogenic/benign, used to benchmark a tool.（中文：一批已被自信判定为致病/良性的变异，用来对工具做基准测试。）
  • VUS — variant of uncertain significance; seen in a patient but not yet callable as pathogenic or benign.（中文：意义未明变异，在患者身上见到但尚不能判定致病或良性。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Per-gene truth-set benchmarking with a power floor — before grading a predictor on a gene, require ≥30 pathogenic + ≥31 benign classified variants (power for a 97/97 tool at moderate strength).
  • Likelihood-ratio → evidence-strength mapping (Pejaver 2022): score interval must clear PLR 2.1 / 4.3 / 18.7 for supporting / moderate / strong; report sensitivity + specificity alongside PLR, not PLR alone.
  • Structure-only audit (MISCAST P3DFi) — run a transparent structure-only scorer next to opaque ensembles to isolate whether a gene obeys the usual structure→damage relationship.
  • RSA stratification — split variants buried vs surface (RSA >20% = exposed); predictors underperform on surface/interaction residues.
  • Gene-specific cutoff optimization — pick the highest-AUC tool, revise its threshold from ROC to recover sensitivity while holding specificity.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / CFTR variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP:

cftr-predictor-gene-calibration (M) — Assemble a CFTR-specific truth set (CFTR2 CF-causing + benign missense, cross-checked against ClinGen) → score each variant with REVEL / AlphaMissense / VEST4 / BayesDel → compute per-tool sensitivity, specificity, and positive likelihood ratio at the ClinGen PP3/BP4 thresholds → read off CFTR-optimized cutoffs where the generic thresholds underperform. Stratify variants by relative solvent accessibility from published CFTR cryo-EM structures (buried NBD core vs surface residues). Output: a per-tool, per-stratum calibration table + recommended CFTR cutoffs. Pairs with cftr-calibration-notebook (2026-07-28).

What to verify first

  • Power per stratum — after splitting CFTR missense by RSA, each bucket must independently clear ~30 pathogenic / 31 benign or the PLRs are noise.
  • Circularity — many CFTR2 classifications may already use predictor evidence; build a stricter subset classified without PP3/BP4 (mirroring the authors' second truth set).
  • In-silico is never final — a strong predictor call is one ACMG code among many, not a verdict; keep human-in-loop.
  • Structure availability — confirm RSA can be computed reliably from CFTR cryo-EM for the residues of interest.
  • No registry internals — built entirely on public variant databases (CFTR2, ClinGen, ClinVar) and published structures.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读论文时间。 Today's Track A paper is "The Performance of In Silico Prediction Tools for Variant Curation in a Panel of Cancer Genes," by Niles Nelson and colleagues out of the University of Tasmania and the Children's Medical Research Institute in Sydney. 今天 Track A 的论文是 Niles Nelson 及其同事发表的《计算机预测工具在一组癌症基因中用于变异判读的表现》，作者来自塔斯马尼亚大学和悉尼儿童医学研究所。 It came out in Human Mutation in 2026, and it's fully open access. 这篇文章 2026 年发表在《Human Mutation》上，完全开放获取。 We're reading it because it asks a deceptively simple question: when a variant-effect predictor gives you a score, and a guideline tells you the cutoff, does that cutoff actually hold up gene by gene? 我们读它，是因为它问了一个看似简单的问题：当一个变异效应预测工具给你一个分数、指南又告诉你一个阈值时，这个阈值真的能在每一个基因上成立吗？ And the answer, it turns out, is often no. 而答案是——常常不能。

JORDAN Before we get into it, set up the vocabulary for me. 在深入之前，先帮我把术语铺一下。 What exactly is a missense variant, and what's a VUS? 到底什么是错义变异，什么又是 VUS？

ALEX A missense variant is a single DNA base change that swaps one amino acid in the protein for a different one. 错义变异 (missense variant) 是指单个 DNA 碱基的改变，把蛋白质里的一个氨基酸换成了另一个。 The protein still gets made, but one building block is different, and that swap may or may not break function. 蛋白质照样能合成，但其中一个组成单元变了，而这个替换有可能破坏功能，也可能没有影响。 A VUS is a "variant of uncertain significance" — we've seen it in a patient but we don't yet have enough evidence to call it disease-causing or harmless. VUS（意义未明变异，即我们在患者身上看到了它，但还没有足够证据判断它是致病还是无害）。 The whole game of variant curation is turning VUSs into confident "pathogenic" or "benign" calls. 整个变异判读的核心，就是把 VUS 转化成有把握的“致病”或“良性”判定。

JORDAN And where do the prediction tools fit into that? 那预测工具在这里面扮演什么角色？

ALEX The clinical framework everyone uses is the ACMG/AMP criteria, a menu of evidence codes you combine to reach a classification. 大家都在用的临床框架是 ACMG/AMP 标准（一套证据条目的清单，你把它们组合起来得出分类结论）。 Two of those codes come straight from computational predictors: PP3 says "the in-silico evidence supports pathogenic," and BP4 says "the in-silico evidence supports benign." 其中两个条目直接来自计算预测工具：PP3 表示“计算机证据支持致病”，BP4 表示“计算机证据支持良性”。 Here's the part that's specific to genomics and worth slowing down on. 下面这一点是基因组学特有的，值得放慢讲。 Each evidence code carries a strength — supporting, moderate, strong, very strong — and that strength is defined statistically as a likelihood ratio, what the field calls an OddsPath. 每个证据条目都带有一个强度——支持、中等、强、非常强——而这个强度在统计上被定义为一个似然比，这个领域称之为 OddsPath。 To reach supporting, moderate, or strong, a tool's score interval has to hit a positive likelihood ratio of about 2.1, 4.3, or 18.7 respectively. 要达到支持、中等或强，一个工具的分数区间必须分别达到大约 2.1、4.3 或 18.7 的阳性似然比。 Those numbers come from Pejaver and colleagues in 2022, who sorted a big pooled set of known variants and read off the score thresholds that deliver each strength. 这些数字来自 Pejaver 等人 2022 年的工作，他们把一大批已知变异排序，读出了能达到每个强度的分数阈值。

JORDAN So the thresholds are calibrated on a pile of genes all mixed together. 所以这些阈值是在一大堆基因混在一起的数据上校准的。 This paper's suspicion is that a one-size-fits-all cutoff might not fit any one gene well? 这篇文章的怀疑是：一个通用阈值可能对任何单个基因都不太合身？

ALEX Exactly, and they test it head-on. 正是如此，而他们直接做了检验。 They took five genes — TP53, ATM, BRCA1, BRCA2, and TERT — all cancer-predisposition genes where a wrong call has real clinical weight. 他们选了五个基因——TP53、ATM、BRCA1、BRCA2 和 TERT——都是癌症易感基因，判错了会有实实在在的临床后果。 The problem they're wrestling with is that for rare missense variants, you usually can't apply the full ACMG menu at all. 他们面对的难题是：对于罕见的错义变异，你通常根本没法用上完整的 ACMG 条目清单。 Pathogenic variants can be private to a single family, phenotypes are late-onset and incompletely penetrant, and functional assays are slow and expensive. 致病变异可能只存在于某一个家系，表型又是晚发且外显不全，而功能实验既慢又贵。 So the predictor score often carries a lot of the weight, which is exactly why you need to trust it. 所以预测分数往往承担了很大一部分判断，而这恰恰是你必须能信任它的原因。

JORDAN How do you even check whether a predictor is trustworthy for one specific gene? 那你到底怎么去检验一个预测工具在某个特定基因上值不值得信任？

ALEX You build what's called a truth set: a collection of variants in that gene already confidently classified as pathogenic or benign by independent evidence. 你要构建一个所谓的“真值集”(truth set)：一批在该基因中、已经由独立证据自信地判定为致病或良性的变异。 They pulled these from the ClinGen Evidence Repository, which is expert-panel-curated, plus for TP53 a set from Fortuno 2018 grounded in transactivation assays. 他们从 ClinGen 证据库（由专家组人工审校）中提取，另外对 TP53 还用了 Fortuno 2018 基于转录激活实验的一组变异。 There's a nice power calculation here: for a tool that's 97% sensitive and 97% specific, you need at least 30 pathogenic and 31 benign variants to be adequately powered to test even a moderate-strength target. 这里有个漂亮的效力计算：对于一个灵敏度 97%、特异度 97% 的工具，你至少需要 30 个致病和 31 个良性变异，才有足够效力去检验哪怕是中等强度的目标。 That's why only five genes made the cut — they needed genes with 61-plus classified missense variants each. 这就是为什么只有五个基因入选——他们需要每个基因都有 61 个以上已分类的错义变异。 The tools tested were REVEL, MutPred2, BayesDel without allele frequency, VEST4, and CADD, plus AlphaMissense as a newer comparator. 测试的工具包括 REVEL、MutPred2、不含等位基因频率的 BayesDel、VEST4 和 CADD，再加上较新的 AlphaMissense 作为对照。

JORDAN Quick check — those are all just different flavors of the same idea, a model that scores how damaging an amino-acid swap is? 快速确认一下——这些都只是同一个想法的不同版本，即一个给氨基酸替换打“有多大破坏性”分数的模型？

ALEX Right, but the flavors matter. 对，但版本之间的差异很重要。 REVEL is a random-forest meta-predictor that blends a dozen older tools plus conservation and allele frequency. REVEL 是一个随机森林元预测器，融合了十几个更早的工具，再加上保守性和等位基因频率。 MutPred2 is a neural network trained on curated variants plus protein structural and functional data. MutPred2 是一个神经网络，用人工审校的变异加上蛋白结构和功能数据训练。 AlphaMissense is the newest, built on the AlphaFold protein-structure lineage. AlphaMissense 是最新的，建立在 AlphaFold 蛋白结构谱系之上。 CADD is the odd one out — it was not trained on curated disease variants at all, and it folds in some splice-site prediction. CADD 是个例外——它根本没有用人工审校的疾病变异训练，而且它还纳入了一些剪接位点 (splice site，即内含子与外显子交界处、决定 RNA 如何被剪切拼接的位置) 的预测。 They also brought in one structure-only tool, MISCAST, which scores purely from the variant's predicted impact on the folded protein — I'll come back to why that's clever. 他们还引入了一个纯结构工具 MISCAST，它只根据变异对折叠蛋白的预测影响来打分——稍后我会讲为什么这一招很聪明。

ALEX One design choice I'd push on. 有一个设计选择我想质疑一下。 Some of their truth-set variants may themselves have been classified using predictor evidence in the first place. 他们真值集里的一些变异，最初可能本身就是用预测工具的证据来分类的。 That's circular — if PP3 helped call a variant pathogenic, then grading PP3 against that variant flatters the tool. 这是循环论证——如果 PP3 帮忙把一个变异判成了致病，那再拿 PP3 去这个变异上评分，就会高估这个工具。 To their credit, they built a stricter second truth set where variants had to reach their classification without using PP3 or BP4 at all. 值得肯定的是，他们又构建了一个更严格的第二真值集，其中的变异必须在完全不用 PP3 或 BP4 的情况下就能达到分类结论。 That's the honest way to do it, and the fact that they bothered tells you they took the circularity seriously. 这才是诚实的做法，而他们肯下这个功夫，说明他们认真对待了这个循环问题。

JORDAN Okay, so what did the numbers say? 好，那结果的数字说了什么？

ALEX The headline is that no single tool was best across all five genes. 核心结论是：没有任何单一工具在五个基因上都表现最好。 At the recommended supporting-level PP3 cutoffs, the tools had a median sensitivity of about 84% and a median specificity of about 91% — but that average hides a lot. 在推荐的支持级 PP3 阈值下，这些工具的中位灵敏度约 84%、中位特异度约 91%——但这个平均值掩盖了很多东西。 Across the board the pattern was high specificity bought at the cost of sensitivity, meaning the tools rarely cry pathogenic wrongly, but they miss a lot of true pathogenic variants. 总体的模式是：高特异度是以牺牲灵敏度换来的，也就是说工具很少错误地把良性喊成致病，但它们漏掉了很多真正的致病变异。 At the strong-evidence cutoff, sensitivity collapsed below 40% for most tools, even though specificity stayed excellent — out of 2,113 variants assessed, false positives at the strong level numbered in the single digits. 在强证据阈值下，大多数工具的灵敏度跌到 40% 以下，尽管特异度仍然极佳——在 2,113 个被评估的变异中，强级别的假阳性只有个位数。

JORDAN So for clinical use, you'd rather miss some than falsely condemn a variant. 所以在临床使用上，你宁可漏掉一些，也不愿错误地给一个变异定罪。 That trade seems right. 这个取舍看起来是对的。

ALEX It is the right trade for diagnostics, and the authors say so. 对诊断而言这确实是对的取舍，作者也是这么说的。 But now the interesting failure. 但现在说说那个有趣的失败案例。 One gene, TERT, broke the tools badly. 有一个基因，TERT，把这些工具彻底难住了。 Every tool showed sensitivity below 65% for pathogenic TERT variants, and AlphaMissense — which was actually the best tool for TP53, hitting a likelihood ratio that reached the strong level — did not rescue TERT. 每个工具对致病 TERT 变异的灵敏度都低于 65%，而 AlphaMissense——它其实是 TP53 上表现最好的工具，似然比达到了强级别——也没能救回 TERT。 So the same predictor is genuinely strong on one gene and genuinely weak on another. 所以同一个预测工具，在一个基因上是真的强，在另一个基因上是真的弱。

JORDAN Why would TERT specifically be so hard? 为什么偏偏是 TERT 这么难？

ALEX This is where the structure-only tool earns its keep. 这正是那个纯结构工具发挥价值的地方。 They used MISCAST's structural score to show that TERT's pathogenic variants don't behave like the average disease gene the tools were trained on. 他们用 MISCAST 的结构分数表明，TERT 的致病变异并不像工具训练时依据的“平均疾病基因”那样表现。 TERT is highly constrained and unusually tolerant of physicochemical amino-acid changes, so the usual "big chemical change equals damage" signal is muted. TERT 高度受约束，而且对氨基酸的理化改变异常耐受，所以通常那种“化学变化大就等于有害”的信号被削弱了。 And crucially, many pathogenic TERT variants sit on the protein surface, disrupting DNA, RNA, or protein interactions rather than collapsing the fold. 更关键的是，许多致病的 TERT 变异位于蛋白质表面，破坏的是与 DNA、RNA 或蛋白的相互作用，而不是让折叠塌陷。 That connects to a concept called relative solvent accessibility, or RSA — basically whether a residue is buried in the protein's core or exposed on its surface. 这就牵出一个概念叫相对溶剂可及性 (relative solvent accessibility，即 RSA，本质上是判断某个氨基酸残基是埋在蛋白核心里、还是暴露在表面上)。 Predictors are trained mostly on buried variants, where damage is easy to see, so they systematically underperform on surface-exposed pathogenic variants. 预测工具主要在“埋藏型”变异上训练，那里损害容易看出来，所以它们在“表面暴露型”致病变异上会系统性地表现不佳。 One striking number from a related analysis: adding a structural-instability threshold lifted AlphaMissense specificity on buried variants from 34% to 95%. 一个来自相关分析的醒目数字：加上一个结构不稳定性阈值后，AlphaMissense 在埋藏型变异上的特异度从 34% 升到了 95%。

JORDAN So the fix isn't a better single number, it's knowing the gene and the residue's location. 所以解法不是一个更好的单一数字，而是了解这个基因、以及这个残基的位置。 Did they show you can recover performance? 他们证明了能把表现挽回来吗？

ALEX Yes, and simply. 证明了，而且很简单。 For TERT, they took the tool with the highest area under the curve — VEST4 — and revised its cutoff down from the generic threshold to 0.5, and sensitivity climbed while specificity stayed near 0.9 to 1.0. 对 TERT，他们取了曲线下面积最高的工具——VEST4——把它的阈值从通用值下调到 0.5，灵敏度上升了，而特异度保持在 0.9 到 1.0 附近。 They did the same trick for AlphaMissense on TERT: a pathogenic call above 0.40 and a benign call below 0.09 pushed benign-variant specificity to 100%. 他们对 TERT 上的 AlphaMissense 也用了同样的招：致病判定阈值设为 0.40 以上、良性判定设为 0.09 以下，把良性变异的特异度推到了 100%。 The lesson is that gene-specific recalibration works when you have enough classified variants to do it. 教训是：当你有足够多已分类的变异时，针对某个基因重新校准是奏效的。

JORDAN You've mostly talked about the pathogenic side. 你主要讲的是致病那一侧。 What about calling variants benign — is that symmetric? 那把变异判成良性呢——这是对称的吗？

ALEX It's not symmetric, and that's an underappreciated point. 它不是对称的，而这是一个被低估的要点。 BP4, the benign code, was even more skewed toward high specificity and poor sensitivity than PP3. BP4——那个良性条目——比 PP3 更偏向高特异度、低灵敏度。 In other words, when a tool does call a variant benign it's usually right, but it stays silent on many genuinely benign variants. 换句话说，当一个工具真的把某个变异判成良性时，它通常是对的，但它对许多确实良性的变异保持沉默。 The best performer on the benign side was VEST4, which met the supporting and moderate likelihood-ratio targets for almost every gene. 在良性这一侧表现最好的是 VEST4，它在几乎每个基因上都达到了支持级和中等级的似然比目标。 That asymmetry matters clinically, because a confident benign call is what lets you take a variant off the table and stop worrying a family. 这种不对称在临床上很重要，因为一个有把握的良性判定，才是让你把一个变异排除、不再让一个家庭担忧的依据。

JORDAN And you promised to say why the structure-only tool was clever, beyond just explaining TERT. 你还答应过要讲那个纯结构工具为什么聪明，而不只是用来解释 TERT。
