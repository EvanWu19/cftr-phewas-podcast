VUStruct: A compute pipeline for high throughput and personalized structural biology — Moth CW, et al. (Vanderbilt University / Washington University in St. Louis, with the Undiagnosed Diseases Network). PLoS Computational Biology, 2026.

Blurb

VUStruct automates the manual, webserver-hopping workflow that structural biologists have used in a handful of celebrated single-gene case studies, and turns it into a holistic, case-wide pipeline: upload a set of candidate variants, and VUStruct selects the best available 3D structures (PDB, SwissModel, ModBase, AlphaFold), then launches five parallel calculations — Rosetta ΔΔG for folding stability, PathProx for spatial clustering against known ClinVar pathogenic and gnomAD benign variants, ScanNet for protein-protein interaction disruption, MusiteDeep for post-translational modification site disruption, and DiGePred/DIEP for digenic disease interaction — and assembles everything into a drillable report with interactive 3D structure viewers. It does not predict pathogenicity; it supplies mechanistic context alongside whatever DNA-based score a clinician already has. Applied to 175+ Undiagnosed Diseases Network patient cases, it contributed structural evidence to solving a PASNA-syndrome case (CACNA1D F767L, destabilization comparable to a known-pathogenic control) and a digenic Diamond-Blackfan-anemia case (RPS19/RPL27). For us this lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation: CFTR already has multiple solved cryo-EM structures in both open and closed states, making it an unusually favorable target for a CFTR-specific structural-context companion report to whatever sequence-based VUS score is already in use.

Plain-English glossary

  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a genetic variant that current evidence cannot confidently classify as disease-causing or benign.（中文：意义不明的变异——现有证据尚不足以自信地判定为致病或良性的遗传变异。）
  • ΔΔG (change in free energy of folding) — a physics-based estimate of how much a mutation destabilizes (positive) or stabilizes (negative) a protein's folded structure.（中文：折叠自由能变化——基于物理学原理，估算一个突变使蛋白质折叠结构失稳（正值）或更稳定（负值）的程度。）
  • Digenic disease — a disease that only manifests when a person carries variants in two different genes together, neither of which is sufficient alone.（中文：双基因病——只有当一个人同时携带两个不同基因中的变异时才会致病，单独任一变异都不足以致病。）
  • PathProx — a spatial-clustering method that checks whether a variant sits, in 3D structure, closer to known-pathogenic (ClinVar) or known-benign (gnomAD) variant clusters.（中文：一种空间聚类方法，用于检查某变异在三维结构中，位置更靠近已知致病（ClinVar）还是已知良性（gnomAD）变异簇。）
  • Post-translational modification (PTM) site — a position on a protein that gets chemically modified after translation (e.g. phosphorylation), often critical for regulating protein activity.（中文：翻译后修饰位点——蛋白质翻译完成后被化学修饰（如磷酸化）的位置，常对调控蛋白质活性至关重要。）
  • SIFTS database — a resource cross-referencing protein structural database (PDB) entries to UniProt sequence positions, used to align structures to transcripts.（中文：一个将蛋白质结构数据库（PDB）条目与 UniProt 序列位置交叉引用的资源，用于把结构与转录本对齐。）
  • Singularity container — a containerization technology (like Docker, but designed for shared HPC clusters) used here to run each calculation in an isolated, reproducible environment.（中文：一种容器化技术（类似 Docker，但专为共享的高性能计算集群设计），本文用它让每项计算在隔离、可复现的环境中运行。）
  • NGL Viewer — a browser-based interactive 3D molecular structure viewer used to inspect a variant's spatial environment.（中文：一款基于浏览器的交互式三维分子结构查看器，用于查看某变异所处的空间环境。）
  • Cryo-EM (cryo-electron microscopy) — an experimental technique for determining protein 3D structure by flash-freezing samples and imaging with electrons; CFTR has solved cryo-EM structures in both open and closed conformational states.（中文：冷冻电镜——一种通过速冻样品并用电子成像来解析蛋白质三维结构的实验技术；CFTR 已有开放态和关闭态两种构象的冷冻电镜结构。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Rosetta ΔΔG (Cartesian/Monomer) — physics-based folding-stability estimate; requires a free academic Rosetta Commons license.
  • PathProx — spatial-clustering pathogenicity-context method against ClinVar/gnomAD, mapped onto 3D structure.
  • ScanNet — ML model estimating protein-protein interaction surface disruption.
  • MusiteDeep — deep-learning post-translational-modification-site disruption predictor.
  • DiGePred / DIEP — ML classifiers for predicting digenic disease gene-pair interactions.
  • SIFTS-based structure selection — principled algorithm for picking non-redundant, high-coverage, high-confidence structures (PDB/SwissModel/ModBase/AlphaFold) per transcript.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation. MVP (paste-ready): Take a curated CFTR VUS list plus CFTR's open- and closed-state cryo-EM structures; run Rosetta ΔΔG (or FoldX if the Rosetta academic license is a blocker) for each variant; run a PathProx-style spatial-clustering check against CFTR2-classified pathogenic and gnomAD benign variants mapped onto the same structures; output a per-variant table with ΔΔG, nearest-neighbor spatial classification, and an agreement/disagreement flag against the existing sequence-based call, for human review — not automated reclassification. Input: CFTR VUS list + two structure files. Difficulty: L.

What to verify first

  • Licensing — Rosetta ΔΔG needs a Rosetta Commons academic license (free but not instant); any substitute (e.g. FoldX) needs its own validation against known CFTR pathogenic/benign variants.
  • Absence of signal is not benignity — even in already-solved UDN cases, 26% of VUStruct runs found no significant structural signal at all; a quiet CFTR VUS result means "structural context didn't clarify this one," not "likely benign."
  • Domain-architecture transfer — PathProx was validated across UDN genes of varying size/architecture; CFTR's two nucleotide-binding domains plus a uniquely disordered R-domain is its own case to check against CFTR2's classified variants before trusting a novel clustering call.

JORDAN Let's ground the vocabulary first. 我们先把词汇打好基础。 What exactly is a VUS, and why isn't a structure-based tool just another pathogenicity score? 什么是 VUS？为什么一个基于结构的工具不只是又一个致病性评分工具？

ALEX A VUS, variant of uncertain significance, is a genetic change found in a patient's DNA that clinicians can't yet confidently call disease-causing or benign — the evidence sits in a gray zone. VUS（意义不明的变异，variant of uncertain significance）是指在患者 DNA 中发现的一种遗传改变，临床医生目前还无法自信地判定它是致病的还是良性的——证据处在灰色地带。 Most VUS triage today runs entirely on gene-phenotype association databases and DNA-based scoring tools — things like GERP for evolutionary constraint, SIFT for sequence homology, or newer machine-learning scores like AlphaMissense — all of which compress a variant's biology down into a single number describing how likely it is to be damaging. 目前大多数 VUS 分诊完全依赖基因-表型关联数据库和基于 DNA 的打分工具——比如衡量进化保守性的 GERP、基于序列同源性的 SIFT，或者像 AlphaMissense 这样较新的机器学习评分——它们都是把一个变异的生物学复杂性压缩成一个数字，描述它有多大可能是有害的。 VUStruct does something categorically different: instead of producing yet another single pathogenicity number, it maps the variant onto the protein's actual 3D structure and asks a mechanistic question — does this amino acid change destabilize the fold, break a binding interface, or sit near a site that's already known to be pathogenic in 3D space? VUStruct 做的是一件本质上不同的事：它不是再产出一个致病性数字，而是把变异映射到蛋白质的真实三维结构上，问一个机制层面的问题——这个氨基酸变化会不会破坏折叠稳定性、会不会破坏一个结合界面、又或者它在三维空间中是否恰好靠近一个已知的致病位点？ Two more terms you'll need: ΔΔG, the change in free energy of protein folding a mutation causes — a positive value generally means the mutation destabilizes the protein — and digenic disease, a disease that only manifests when a person carries variants in two different genes together, neither of which would cause disease alone. 你还会用到两个术语：ΔΔG，即一个突变引起的蛋白质折叠自由能变化——正值通常意味着该突变使蛋白质失稳；以及双基因病 (digenic disease)，指的是一种疾病，只有当一个人同时携带两个不同基因中的变异时才会显现，单独携带任何一个都不致病。 The final piece is PathProx: it checks whether a VUS sits spatially closer, in the folded 3D structure, to a cluster of known-pathogenic ClinVar variants or to a cluster of benign gnomAD population variants. 最后一个概念是 PathProx：它检查一个 VUS 在折叠后的三维结构中，空间位置上更靠近一簇已知致病的 ClinVar 变异，还是更靠近一簇良性的、来自 gnomAD 人群数据库的变异。

JORDAN So what was actually missing before VUStruct — why did clinics need a new pipeline layered on top of the scores they already had? 那么在 VUStruct 之前究竟缺了什么——为什么诊所还需要在已有评分工具之上再叠加一条新流程？

ALEX The core problem the authors point to is specificity. 作者指出的核心问题是特异性 (specificity)。 Variant-effect predictors do reasonably well distinguishing known pathogenic from known benign variants across huge population-scale variant sets, but that population-level accuracy doesn't transfer cleanly to the small, pre-filtered handful of candidate variants a clinician is actually staring at in a single rare-disease case — even AlphaMissense's scores are described as unreliable for specific variants and prone to high false-positive rates in that regime. 变异效应预测工具在大规模人群变异数据集上，区分已知致病和已知良性变异的表现相当不错，但这种群体层面的准确性，并不能干净地迁移到临床医生在单个罕见病病例中实际面对的、经过预筛选的少数几个候选变异上——就连 AlphaMissense 的评分，在这种情境下也被形容为不可靠，且容易出现高假阳性率。 More fundamentally, a pathogenicity score answers a binary question — pathogenic or benign — but gives no mechanistic hypothesis about how the mutation causes disease, and biology unfolds in 3D space and time, not in a compressed scalar. 更根本的是，一个致病性评分回答的是一个二元问题——致病还是良性——但完全不给出关于这个突变如何致病的机制性假设，而生命的生物学过程本就发生在三维空间和时间之中，而非某个被压缩过的标量数字里。 Prior single-gene case studies had already shown the power of this approach: structural modeling of a de novo variant in KCNC2 revealed it could physically block the ion channel pore, providing a rational mechanistic hypothesis for a patient's epilepsy syndrome, and structure-based calculations on a variant in MSH2 showed it destabilized the protein and led to its cellular degradation, causing Lynch syndrome — in both cases the structural analysis outperformed traditional genetic predictors. 此前已有针对单个基因的病例研究证明了这种方法的力量：对 KCNC2 基因一个新生 (de novo) 变异的结构建模显示，它可能从物理上堵塞离子通道的孔道，为患者的癫痫综合征提供了一个合理的机制性假设；而针对 MSH2 基因一个变异的结构计算显示，它使蛋白质失稳并导致其在细胞内被降解，从而引发林奇综合征 (Lynch syndrome)——这两个案例中，结构分析的表现都超过了传统的遗传学预测工具。 Those single-gene successes led to a generalized structure-based clinical workflow, but that workflow still required heavy manual labor: a human has to select the right 3D structure, manually forward it to a scattering of different external webservers that each perform one specific calculation, and then manually integrate all those separate results into a final report. 那些单基因层面的成功，催生了一套通用化的、基于结构的临床工作流程，但这套流程依然需要大量人工投入：需要有人手动挑选合适的三维结构，手动把它转发给分散在各处、各自只做一种特定计算的外部网页服务，再手动把这些零散的结果整合成一份最终报告。 VUStruct's goal is not to replace the pathogenicity score, but to automate that entire manual structural-context workflow into one holistic, case-wide pipeline, so it scales from single celebrated case studies to routine clinical use across many patients at once. VUStruct 的目标不是取代致病性评分，而是把这整套人工的结构背景分析工作流程，自动化成一条统一的、覆盖整个病例的流程，让它从个别值得庆祝的单一病例研究，扩展到能同时服务多名患者的常规临床应用。

ALEX VUStruct runs in five phases after a user uploads a set of variants through a web form. VUStruct 在用户通过网页表单上传一组变异之后，分五个阶段运行。 Phase one takes genomic coordinates, runs them through Ensembl's Variant Effect Predictor to get missense calls, then restricts the analysis to transcripts that cross-reference to Swiss-Prot-curated Uniprot protein records — because the authors found that reference-genome annotations are surprisingly unstable, with thousands of curated Uniprot sequences having no clean Ensembl cross-reference and hundreds more differing in actual amino acid sequence despite matching lengths. 第一阶段接收基因组坐标，通过 Ensembl 的变异效应预测工具 (Variant Effect Predictor, VEP) 得出错义变异判定，然后把分析范围限制在能与 Swiss-Prot 精编的 Uniprot 蛋白质记录建立交叉引用的转录本上——因为作者发现，参考基因组的注释信息出人意料地不稳定，数千条经过精编的 Uniprot 序列根本找不到干净的 Ensembl 交叉引用，还有数百条即便长度相同，实际氨基酸序列却存在差异。 Phase two mines the Protein Data Bank, SwissModel, ModBase, and AlphaFold for every available structure covering each transcript, aligning them via the SIFTS cross-reference database and selecting a final set that minimizes redundancy while maximizing variant coverage, model confidence, and experimental quality — multimeric protein complexes get prioritized when available. 第二阶段从蛋白质数据库 (PDB)、SwissModel、ModBase 和 AlphaFold 中挖掘每个转录本对应的所有可用结构，通过 SIFTS 交叉引用数据库进行比对，并挑选出一个能在减少冗余的同时、最大化变异覆盖度、模型置信度和实验质量的最终结构集合——如果存在多聚体蛋白复合物结构，会被优先选用。 Phase three launches the actual calculations as job arrays on a SLURM high-performance-computing cluster, each running inside a Singularity container: Rosetta ΔΔG estimates folding-stability impact, PathProx does the ClinVar-versus-gnomAD spatial-clustering analysis I just described, ScanNet is a machine-learning model that estimates whether a variant disrupts a protein-protein interaction surface, MusiteDeep is a deep-learning model predicting whether the variant disrupts a post-translational modification site, and DiGePred plus DIEP are machine-learning classifiers trained specifically to flag digenic disease interactions between pairs of variants. 第三阶段把实际计算作为作业数组，提交到 SLURM 高性能计算集群上运行，每个作业都在一个 Singularity 容器内执行：Rosetta ΔΔG 估算折叠稳定性所受的影响，PathProx 执行我刚才描述的 ClinVar 对比 gnomAD 空间聚类分析，ScanNet 是一个机器学习模型，用于估计某个变异是否破坏了蛋白-蛋白相互作用界面，MusiteDeep 是一个深度学习模型，用于预测该变异是否破坏了一个翻译后修饰位点，而 DiGePred 和 DIEP 则是两个专门训练用来标记成对变异之间双基因病相互作用的机器学习分类器。 Phase four is live progress monitoring through a web dashboard that talks to the HPC scheduler, and phase five assembles everything into a tiered report — a case-wide landing page summarizing every variant, drilling down into per-transcript reports with PFAM domain diagrams, and finally into interactive NGL Viewer sessions for each individual 3D structure, where you can visually inspect a variant's spatial neighborhood and color the model by PathProx score or AlphaFold confidence. pushback: the paper is explicit that VUStruct's goal "is not to predict pathogenic variants per se" — it deliberately declines to give a final verdict, and instead hands the clinician a stack of five JORDAN Fair pushback. 这个质疑有道理。 So does it actually help solve real cases — what happened when they ran it? 那么它到底有没有真正帮上解决实际病例——运行结果是什么？

ALEX VUStruct has been applied to genetic variant sets from over 150 Vanderbilt UDN patients and 25 Washington University patients, more than 175 cases total, and the authors walk through two concrete solved cases in detail. VUStruct 已经被应用于超过 150 名范德堡 UDN 患者和 25 名圣路易斯华盛顿大学患者的遗传变异集合，总计超过 175 个病例，作者详细介绍了其中两个已被解决的具体案例。 The first is a patient with PASNA syndrome — a condition involving the L-type voltage-gated calcium channel gene CACNA1D — where VUStruct flagged a candidate variant, F767L, as structurally destabilizing via Rosetta ΔΔG. 第一个是一名患有 PASNA 综合征的患者——该病涉及 L 型电压门控钙通道基因 CACNA1D——VUStruct 通过 Rosetta ΔΔG 计算，把候选变异 F767L 标记为在结构上会导致失稳。 The team followed up with targeted Rosetta simulations comparing F767L against a known pathogenic variant at the same position, F767S, using the channel's closed-state cryo-EM structure as a positive control;
