Show notes — Track B — 2026-07-22

Evaluation and mitigation of the limitations of large language models in clinical decision-making — Hager P, Jungmann F, Rueckert D et al., Nature Medicine (2024).

Blurb

LLMs ace medical licensing exams but exams don't test the sequential, messy job of real clinical decision-making. This paper ships MIMIC-CDM, a curated benchmark of 2,400 real MIMIC-IV cases across four abdominal pathologies (appendicitis, cholecystitis, diverticulitis, pancreatitis), plus an evaluation framework that forces a model to autonomously gather history, order exams and labs, interpret them, and follow diagnostic and treatment guidelines — scoring every step, not just the final answer. Across open-weight 2023 models (Llama 2, OASST, WizardLM) and medically tuned ones (Clinical Camel, Meditron), LLMs diagnosed significantly worse than four hospital physicians (P<0.001; 16–25 point gap), degraded further when made to gather information themselves (Llama 2: 58.8%→45.5%), skipped guideline-required exams, failed treatment guidelines on severe cases, couldn't reliably classify a lab value against its own reference range, and swung with instruction wording and information order. The durable contribution is the framework and failure taxonomy (code on GitHub), not the now-historical model scores. Where it lands: AI-tooling / career, via an agentic-process eval harness that scores registry-task LLMs on process, rule adherence, and robustness — not just final accuracy.

Plain-English glossary

  • MIMIC-IV — a large, de-identified database of real ICU electronic health records (labs, notes, orders).（中文：大型去标识化的重症监护电子健康记录数据库。）
  • Clinical decision-making — the multi-step process of gathering history, ordering/interpreting tests, following guidelines, and committing to a diagnosis and treatment.（中文：采集病史、开具与判读检查、遵循指南、最终诊断与治疗的多步骤过程。）
  • Appendicitis / cholecystitis / diverticulitis / pancreatitis — inflammation of the appendix, gallbladder, colonic pouches, and pancreas; all present as abdominal pain but need different work-ups.（中文：阑尾、胆囊、结肠憩室、胰腺的炎症，都表现为腹痛但需不同诊查。）
  • Second reader — an AI that reviews a case a doctor has already worked up; the least demanding deployment mode.（中文：复核医生已处理病例的AI，最不苛刻的部署方式。）
  • Diagnostic vs treatment guidelines — the rulebooks for which tests to order and which therapies to give per condition.（中文：规定按病症开哪些检查、给哪些治疗的规则手册。）
  • Physical examination — the hands-on bedside assessment guidelines call for, preferably first.（中文：指南要求、最好排在最前的床旁体格检查。）
  • Instruction robustness — whether a model's output stays stable when the prompt is reworded or information reordered.（中文：提示被重新措辞或信息重新排序时模型输出是否稳定。）
  • Instruction tuning — post-training that teaches a model to follow multi-step task formats; the medical models lacked it.（中文：教模型遵循多步骤任务格式的后训练；医学模型缺少这一步。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • MIMIC-CDM / MIMIC-CDM-FI — a curated 2,400-case clinical-decision benchmark and its full-information variant (separates reasoning from information-gathering). Dataset recreation code: github.com/paulhager/MIMIC-Clinical-Decision-Making.
  • Evaluation framework — autonomous multi-step simulation scoring diagnosis, exam/lab ordering, guideline adherence, lab interpretation, and instruction robustness: github.com/paulhager/MIMIC-Clinical-Decision-Making-Framework.
  • Analysis framework — reproduces all plots and statistics: github.com/paulhager/MIMIC-Clinical-Decision-Making-Analysis. (Python 3.10, pytorch 2.1.1, transformers 4.35.2, langchain 0.0.339.)
  • Process-over-accuracy scoring — grade information-gathering completeness, rulebook adherence, and robustness deltas, not just final-answer accuracy.
  • Grounding the yardstick — score against both published guidelines and the treatments patients actually received.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: AI-tooling / career (secondary: Liz / CFFPR phenotyping, method only).

Paste-ready MVP — agentic-process eval harness for registry tasks (M): take an LLM doing a multi-step task with a known correct information-gathering path (e.g. extracting a structured phenotype from free-text notes); log four axes — (1) which fields it requested, (2) whether it followed the defined rule set, (3) final accuracy, (4) the performance delta when the same prompt is reordered/reworded. Output is a four-axis scorecard whose robustness delta is the number nobody reports. Input: an LLM + a task with a known reference process. Difficulty medium, concentrated in defining the reference process and rule checks. Re-point at each new model release to re-measure in an afternoon.

What to verify first

  • Stale absolute numbers: models are 2023 open weights; never quote the scores as current — re-run the framework on the model you actually intend to use.
  • Data access cost: MIMIC-IV requires credentialed data-use training to reproduce the exact dataset.
  • Task-transfer question: failures were measured on free-text ICU decision-making; check whether they transfer to structured, more constrained registry curation before assuming they do.
  • Robustness-test design: rewordings must be plausible clinician phrasings, not adversarial ones, or the robustness delta measures the wrong thing; the reorder/split should be principled.
  • Autonomy vs supervision: the harsh scores are for fully-autonomous operation — keep the supervised-second-reader conclusion separate.

ALEX Today's Track B paper is "Evaluation and mitigation of the limitations of large language models in clinical decision-making," by Paul Hager and colleagues, published in Nature Medicine in 2024. 今天的Track B论文是"大型语言模型在临床决策中局限性的评估与缓解"，作者是Paul Hager等人，2024年发表于《自然医学》。 It matters for us because it ships a real, reusable evaluation framework — code on GitHub, built on 2,400 actual patient cases — not just a leaderboard number. 它对我们重要，是因为它交付了一个真实、可复用的评估框架——代码在GitHub上，建立在2,400个真实患者病例之上——而不只是一个排行榜数字。 The whole paper is a lesson in how exam-style accuracy hides deployment failure. 整篇论文都在讲一个道理：考试式的准确率如何掩盖了部署时的失败。

JORDAN Let me get the clinical terms first, because this isn't genomics. 我先搞清楚临床术语，因为这不是基因组学。 What is MIMIC, and what are these four diseases? MIMIC是什么，这四种疾病又是什么？

ALEX MIMIC-IV is a large, de-identified database of real electronic health records from intensive-care admissions — labs, notes, orders, the whole record. MIMIC-IV是一个大型的、去标识化的真实电子健康记录 (EHR) 数据库，来自重症监护住院——化验、病历、医嘱，整份记录都有。 The authors carved out 2,400 patients who came to the emergency department with acute abdominal pain. 作者从中划出了2,400个因急性腹痛来急诊的患者。 The four pathologies are appendicitis, cholecystitis, diverticulitis, and pancreatitis — inflammation of the appendix, the gallbladder, pouches in the colon, and the pancreas respectively. 这四种病症是阑尾炎、胆囊炎、憩室炎和胰腺炎——分别是阑尾、胆囊、结肠憩室和胰腺的炎症。 They're a good test set because they present similarly — abdominal pain — but need different tests and different treatments to tell apart. 它们是很好的测试集，因为表现相似——都是腹痛——但需要不同的检查和不同的治疗才能区分开。

JORDAN And what's the difference between passing a medical exam and "clinical decision-making"? 通过一场医学考试和"临床决策"有什么区别？

ALEX A medical licensing exam gives you a tidy vignette and asks for the answer. 医师执照考试给你一段整洁的病例摘要，然后问你答案。 Real decision-making is a multi-step process: you gather history, order the right physical exam and labs, interpret them, follow diagnostic and treatment guidelines, and only then commit. 真实的决策是一个多步骤过程：你采集病史、开出正确的体格检查和化验、解读它们、遵循诊断指南和治疗指南，然后才做决定。 LLMs had already aced the exams, so the open question was whether they could do the messy, sequential job. LLM（大型语言模型）早已在考试里拿了高分，所以悬而未决的问题是：它们能不能做那个杂乱的、有先后顺序的活儿。 A "second reader," by the way, is an AI that reviews a case a doctor has already worked up — the easiest possible deployment mode. 顺便说，"第二阅片者" (second reader) 是指复核一位医生已经处理过的病例的AI——这是最容易的部署方式。

ALEX Here's the background tension the paper is built on. 这就是论文所建立的背景张力。 If you only score final-answer accuracy on a fully-worked-up case, an LLM can look competent. 如果你只对一个已经完整处理好的病例给最终答案打分，LLM看起来可以很能干。 But that score never tests whether the model would have gathered the right information on its own, or followed the rulebook, or stayed stable when you reworded the prompt. 但那个分数从不检验：模型是否会自己去采集正确的信息、是否遵循规则手册、是否在你重新措辞提示时保持稳定。 The authors' thesis is that these process skills are exactly what deployment requires and exactly what benchmarks omit. 作者的论点是：这些过程性技能正是部署所需要的，也正是基准测试所忽略的。 There's a subtle trap they're pointing at: a model can reach the right diagnosis on a fully-worked-up case for the wrong reasons, and you'd never know from the accuracy score alone. 他们指出了一个微妙的陷阱：模型可以在一个已完整处理的病例上出于错误的理由得到正确的诊断，而你光看准确率分数永远不会知道。 An exam rewards the destination; a clinic depends on the route. 考试奖励的是终点；而临床依赖的是路线。 The moment you make the model walk the route itself, the accuracy that looked reassuring on the exam falls apart. 一旦你让模型自己走这条路线，那个在考试上看起来令人安心的准确率就瓦解了。 So they built a framework that forces the model to run the whole process autonomously and scores every step. 于是他们构建了一个框架，迫使模型自主地跑完整个流程，并对每一步打分。

JORDAN So concretely, what does the framework make the model do? 那具体来说，这个框架让模型做什么？

ALEX They give the model only the patient's history of present illness — the opening complaint — and nothing else. 他们只给模型患者的现病史——最初的主诉——别的什么都不给。 The model then has to specify which physical exams and lab tests it wants, receive those results, and decide when it has enough to diagnose and propose treatment. 模型接着必须指明它想要哪些体格检查和化验、接收那些结果、并判断何时信息足够到可以诊断并提出治疗。 They call this dataset MIMIC-CDM, for Clinical Decision Making. 他们把这个数据集叫做MIMIC-CDM，CDM即临床决策。 They also built an easier variant, MIMIC-CDM-FI, where all information is provided up front, to separate reasoning from information-gathering. 他们还建了一个更容易的变体MIMIC-CDM-FI，即一开始就提供全部信息，用来把推理能力和信息采集能力分开。 They tested open-weight models available in 2023 — Llama 2, Open Assistant, WizardLM — plus two medically tuned ones, Clinical Camel and Meditron. 他们测试了2023年可获得的开放权重模型——Llama 2、Open Assistant（OASST）、WizardLM——外加两个医学微调过的模型，Clinical Camel和Meditron。 They deliberately used open models so the evaluation is reproducible and the model can't have memorized a public answer key. 他们刻意使用开放模型，这样评估可复现，模型也不可能记住一个公开的答案库。 And they ran a reader study: four hospital physicians from two countries diagnosed an 80-patient subset for a human baseline. 他们还做了一项阅片者研究：来自两个国家的四位住院医师诊断了一个80人的子集，作为人类基线。 They scored the models against both the diagnostic guidelines and the actual treatments patients received, so the yardstick is grounded in real care, not an idealized textbook. 他们把模型同时对照诊断指南和患者实际接受的治疗来评分，所以这把尺子扎根于真实的医疗，而不是一本理想化的教科书。

JORDAN Where would you push on that design? 你会在这个设计上质疑哪里？

ALEX Two places. 两处。 First, every model is an open-weight model from 2023, so the absolute scores are already historical — this is not a claim about the frontier model you'd reach for today. 第一，每个模型都是2023年的开放权重模型，所以这些绝对分数已经是历史了——这不是关于你今天会选用的前沿模型的论断。 The durable contribution is the framework and the failure taxonomy, not the number next to Llama 2. 持久的贡献是那个框架和失败的分类，而不是Llama 2旁边的那个数字。 Second, the framework fixes "fully autonomous agent" as the task, but that's a design choice, not the only deployment mode. 第二，这个框架把"完全自主的智能体"固定为任务，但那是一个设计选择，不是唯一的部署方式。 A model that fails autonomously might still be safe and useful as a supervised second reader — the paper's own less-punishing condition — and we should keep those two conclusions separate. 一个在自主状态下失败的模型，作为一个有人监督的第二阅片者可能仍然安全又有用——那正是论文里那个惩罚较轻的条件——我们应该把这两个结论分开。

JORDAN Fair. 有道理。 So what did the numbers say? 那些数字说了什么？

ALEX Even with all information provided, the LLMs diagnosed significantly worse than the clinicians, with P below 0.001 and a gap of roughly 16 to 25 percentage points. 即便提供了全部信息，LLM的诊断也显著差于临床医师，P值小于0.001，差距大约16到25个百分点。 They matched the doctors only on appendicitis, the simplest call. 它们只在阑尾炎——最简单的判断——上追平了医生。 When the models had to gather information themselves, performance dropped further: Llama 2's mean fell from about 58.8% to 45.5%. 当模型必须自己去采集信息时，表现进一步下滑：Llama 2的均值从约58.8%跌到45.5%。 The medically tuned models did not do better — being trained on medical text didn't buy accuracy, and one of them kept diagnosing "gallstones" without recognizing the inflammation. 医学微调过的模型也没有更好——用医学文本训练并没有换来准确率，其中一个还一直诊断"胆结石"而认不出炎症。 The reason the medical models lagged is instructive: they weren't instruction-tuned, so they couldn't reliably follow the multi-step format the task demanded. 医学模型落后的原因很有启发：它们没有经过指令微调，所以无法可靠地遵循任务要求的多步骤格式。 That's a quiet but important point — domain knowledge and the ability to operate inside a workflow are different capabilities, and training for the first can cost you the second. 这是一个不起眼但重要的点——领域知识和在一个工作流程内运作的能力是两种不同的能力，为前者训练可能会让你失去后者。

ALEX The guideline-adherence numbers make the process failure concrete. 指南遵循的数字把过程性失败讲得很具体。 On physical examinations, only Llama 2 Chat asked consistently — around 98% of cases — while WizardLM asked in as few as roughly 64%. 在体格检查上，只有Llama 2 Chat始终会问——约98%的病例——而WizardLM最低时只有约64%的病例会问。 The other models omitted an examination that every guideline calls essential and preferably first. 其余模型省略了一项每一部指南都称为必需、且最好排在最前的检查。 And on treatment, the models were consistent only for the easy reflexes — appendectomy for appendicitis, antibiotics — but fell apart on the severe, patient-specific plans, which is exactly where a wrong call does the most harm. 在治疗上，模型只在简单的条件反射上保持一致——阑尾炎做阑尾切除、用抗生素——但在严重的、针对个体的方案上崩溃了，而那恰恰是错误决定伤害最大的地方。

JORDAN And beyond raw accuracy, what were the process failures? 除了原始准确率之外，那些过程性失败是什么？

ALEX This is the part that transfers. 这部分才是可迁移的。 The models frequently failed to order the exams that guidelines require — only one model consistently asked for a physical examination at all. 模型经常没能开出指南要求的检查——只有一个模型始终会要求做体格检查。 They failed to follow treatment guidelines, especially for the more severe cases. 它们没能遵循治疗指南，尤其是对更严重的病例。 They could not reliably interpret a lab result even when handed the value and its reference range — a task any numerically literate person does perfectly. 即便把化验值和它的参考范围都递给它们，它们也无法可靠地判读一个化验结果——而这是任何有数字素养的人都能做到完美的任务。 And they were not robust: changing the wording of the instructions, or the order in which information was presented, swung performance substantially. 而且它们不稳健：改变指令的措辞，或改变信息呈现的顺序，都会让表现大幅摆动。 So the model's answer depended partly on who was typing and how — which is disqualifying for a clinical tool. 所以模型的答案部分取决于是谁在打字、怎么打——这对一个临床工具来说是不合格的。 They also found the models were sensitive to the sheer quantity of information provided, and to the order in which it arrived — the same facts in a different sequence produced different diagnoses. 他们还发现模型对所提供信息的数量本身、以及信息到达的顺序都很敏感——同样的事实换一个顺序就产生了不同的诊断。 A clinician's reasoning shouldn't move because the labs came before the history instead of after; a model's did. 一位临床医生的推理不该因为化验排在病史之前而不是之后就发生变化；模型的推理却变了。 That's the deepest of the failures, because it means the system has no stable internal model of the case — only a reaction to the current arrangement of text. 这是所有失败里最深的一个，因为它意味着这个系统对病例没有一个稳定的内部模型——只有对当前文本排列方式的一个反应。

JORDAN The lab-interpretation failure surprised me most — can you unpack it? 化验判读的失败最让我意外——能展开讲讲吗？

ALEX They gave the model a lab value together with its reference range and asked one thing: is this below, within, or above the range. 他们把一个化验值连同它的参考范围一起给模型，只问一件事：这个值是低于、在范围内、还是高于范围。 That's a pure comparison — no medicine required, just reading two numbers. 这是一个纯粹的比较——不需要任何医学知识，只是读两个数字。 The models could not do it reliably, and they were worst precisely on the abnormal results, which are the ones that carry the clinical signal. 模型却无法可靠地完成它，而且恰恰在异常结果上表现最差，而异常结果正是携带临床信号的那些。 That failure is diagnostic in itself: a system that can't consistently say a value exceeds its own stated range isn't reasoning over the labs at all — it's pattern-matching around them. 那个失败本身就有诊断意义：一个连"某个值超出了它自己给出的范围"都说不稳定的系统，根本不是在对化验做推理——它是在化验周围做模式匹配。

JORDAN And imaging — did they lean on scans instead? 那影像呢——它们是不是转而依赖扫描？

ALEX Sometimes they matched the imaging the real doctors ordered, but often they committed to a diagnosis without requesting an abdominal scan at all. 有时它们与真实医生所开的影像相符，但常常在根本没要求做腹部扫描的情况下就锁定了一个诊断。 That's the same pattern as the missing physical exam and the skipped labs — a tendency to decide before the evidence is in. 这与缺失的体格检查、被跳过的化验是同一种模式——一种在证据到齐之前就下决定的倾向。 The authors frame this bluntly: hasty decision-making plus poor accuracy is a patient-safety risk, not a rough draft you can polish. 作者直白地把这点框定为：仓促决策加上糟糕的准确率，是一个患者安全风险，而不是一份你能打磨的草稿。

JORDAN Before the takeaway — why would we ever prefer building an eval harness over just prompting a newer, better model? 在总结之前——我们为什么会宁愿去搭一个评估工具，而不是干脆去提示一个更新更好的模型？

ALEX Because the harness is what tells you whether the newer model is actually better on your task, rather than better on a benchmark. 因为正是这个工具，能告诉你那个更新的模型在你的任务上是否真的更好，而不是在某个基准上更好。 A model can gain ten points on a public leaderboard and still skip the field your protocol requires, or still flip its answer when a colleague phrases the request differently. 一个模型可以在公开排行榜上涨十分，却依然跳过你协议要求的那个字段，或者依然在同事换个说法提问时改变答案。 The harness outlives any single model — you re-point it at each new release and it re-measures process, robustness, and rule adherence in an afternoon. 这个工具比任何单个模型都活得久——你把它重新指向每一个新版本，它在一个下午里就能重新测量过程、稳健性和规则遵循。 That reusable measuring stick, not any one model, is the portfolio-legible thing to build. 那个可复用的量尺，而不是任何一个模型，才是值得构建、也能在作品集里被读懂的东西。

JORDAN So where does this land for us? 那这对我们落在哪里？
