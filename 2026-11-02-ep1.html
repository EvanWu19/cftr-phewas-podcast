Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): Rapid minigene workflow for functional reclassification of splicing variants in hereditary cancer diagnostics

Calandra and colleagues (2026, Journal of Medical Genetics) describe a diagnostic-lab pipeline for resolving splice-region variants of uncertain significance (VUS) fast enough to sit inside routine clinical reporting. Across a 77-gene hereditary-cancer panel run on 2,142 individuals, the group found 384 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants across 54 genes (an ~18% diagnostic yield), while 17% of cases carried a VUS, many suspected to affect pre-mRNA splicing — the category computational predictors can flag as "probably disrupted" but rarely resolve on their own. Their fix streamlines the minigene functional assay itself: synthetic DNA replaces patient-sample PCR amplification of the variant region, and recombination-based cloning replaces slower classical restriction-enzyme cloning, cutting out two of the traditionally slowest steps. AlphaGenome — a sequence-to-regulatory/splicing-effect model, not to be confused with the structure-prediction model AlphaFold — is used purely as a triage layer, ranking candidate splice-disrupting VUS and flagging cases where computational evidence is internally discordant for prioritized wet-lab testing. The authors report that the workflow reclassified previously unresolved VUS and clarified discordant-evidence cases, though the abstract (the only text available — Europe PMC's fullTextXML 404'd and the BioC mirror returned no result for this very recent paper) gives no per-variant counts or turnaround-time figures. The episode maps the "synthetic-DNA minigene + splicing-model triage, model never making the final call" architecture onto CFTR's own deep-intronic/splice-region VUS burden (the classic pseudoexon-activating c.3849+10kbC>T is the worked example) and lands on a buildable triage list for the Karen Raraigh variant-interpretation collaboration.

Plain-English glossary

ACMG/AMP framework — The rule system (American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology) clinical labs use to classify a variant as pathogenic, likely pathogenic, uncertain, likely benign, or benign by combining weighted evidence codes.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会/分子病理学会制定的变异分类标准体系，通过加权证据代码判断变异的致病性。）

AlphaGenome — A sequence-to-function model that predicts regulatory and splicing consequences of a DNA change directly from sequence, without modeling protein structure; a different model lineage from the structure-predicting AlphaFold.（中文：一种直接从DNA序列预测调控和剪接后果的序列到功能模型，不涉及蛋白质结构，与预测结构的AlphaFold是不同谱系的模型。）

Cryptic splice site / pseudoexon — A deep-intronic variant can create a new splice site strong enough to be used instead of the canonical one, causing a stretch of intron to be mis-spliced into the transcript as if it were a real exon (a "pseudoexon"), as in CFTR's c.3849+10kbC>T.（中文：深部内含子变异可能产生一个强度足以被误用的新剪接位点，使一段内含子被错误地当作"假外显子"拼接进转录本，CFTR的c.3849+10kbC>T就是典型例子。）

Diagnostic yield — The proportion of tested individuals for whom a panel finds a variant confidently classified as disease-causing (18% here).（中文：接受检测的个体中，panel找到可被确信判定为致病变异的比例，本文中为18%。）

Exon skipping / intron retention — Two of the possible aberrant-splicing outcomes when a splice variant disrupts normal recognition: a whole exon gets left out of the mature transcript, or an intron that should be removed stays in.（中文：剪接变异破坏正常识别后可能出现的两种异常剪接结果：整段外显子被遗漏在成熟转录本之外，或本应被切除的内含子被保留下来。）

Minigene assay — A functional test that clones a variant's genomic exon-intron context into a plasmid, transfects it into a standard cell line, and reads out the resulting splicing pattern directly by RT-PCR or sequencing — used when the patient's accessible tissue (e.g., blood) doesn't naturally express or splice the relevant isoform.（中文：将变异的基因组外显子-内含子上下文克隆进质粒并转染标准细胞系，通过RT-PCR或测序直接检测剪接结果的功能实验，用于患者可取材组织不表达或不以相同方式剪接相关转录本的情况。）

PVS1 — The ACMG/AMP evidence code for "predicted loss of function, very strong," the strongest evidence tier; for splice variants outside the canonical splice dinucleotides, applying it confidently generally requires experimental confirmation.（中文：ACMG/AMP框架中"预测功能丧失，极强证据"代码，是最高等级的证据；对于不落在经典剪接二核苷酸上的剪接变异，通常需要实验确认才能放心使用。）

Recombination-based cloning — A cloning method that inserts a DNA fragment into a vector via sequence-specific recombination rather than restriction-enzyme cut-and-paste, generally faster and more reliable.（中文：通过序列特异性重组而非限制性内切酶剪切拼接的方式，将DNA片段插入载体的克隆方法，通常更快、更可靠。）

VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant that is neither confidently pathogenic nor confidently benign given current evidence; the diagnostic "limbo" category this workflow targets.（中文：根据现有证据既不能确信致病也不能确信良性的变异，是这套流程想要处理的诊断"灰色地带"类别。）

Full citation

Calandra N, et al. Rapid minigene workflow for functional reclassification of splicing variants in hereditary cancer diagnostics. Journal of Medical Genetics. 2026. PMID: 42642213. PMCID: PMC13638327. DOI: 10.1136/jmg-2026-111675.

Methods / tools to lift

  • Synthetic-DNA minigene construction — order the exact variant-containing sequence rather than amplifying it from patient genomic DNA, removing a patient-sample-dependent step.
  • Recombination-based cloning — faster, more reliable vector assembly than classical restriction-enzyme cloning.
  • AlphaGenome triage scoring — rank candidate splice-disrupting VUS and flag inter-tool/inter-evidence discordance to decide what's worth a wet-lab assay.
  • RT-PCR / sequencing readout — direct observation of the resulting splicing pattern post-transfection.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh collaboration (CFTR variant interpretation and splice-variant evidence triage). Buildable artifact: CFTR Splice-VUS Triage List — pull every CFTR variant currently classified as a VUS in ClinVar/CFTR2 that's annotated intronic/splice-region, score with SpliceAI, Pangolin, and AlphaGenome (where accessible), and rank by predicted disruption strength plus inter-predictor disagreement to produce a short, defensible shortlist of variants worth a minigene functional follow-up. Rough difficulty: M (scripted scoring and database pulls; the real judgment call is setting a sensible discordance threshold; no wet-lab work required to produce the shortlist itself).

What to verify first

  • Whether AlphaGenome's splicing-prediction performance has been independently head-to-head benchmarked against SpliceAI and Pangolin on a shared variant set, rather than relying on the developers' or this paper's own framing.
  • How well a minigene's artificial exon-intron context — removed from the gene's normal genomic neighborhood — actually recapitulates in vivo splicing for a given gene; a known general limitation, not something this paper resolves.
  • The actual resolved-VUS count and turnaround-time figures, which the abstract does not report; would need the full paper or a direct author request.
  • Whether the discordance-triggered testing strategy itself was validated against a held-out set of variants with already-known functional status, rather than only applied prospectively.

ALEX Today's paper is "Rapid minigene workflow for functional reclassification of splicing variants in hereditary cancer diagnostics," Calandra and colleagues, Journal of Medical Genetics, 2026. 今天这篇论文是《遗传性癌症诊断中剪接变异（splicing variant）功能性重新分类的快速微基因（minigene）工作流程》，作者是Calandra及其同事，发表于《Journal of Medical Genetics》，2026年。

JORDAN Why this one, and which track are we in? 为什么选这篇，今天是哪条赛道？

ALEX Track A, the Karen Raraigh lane — variant interpretation and harmonization. A赛道，也就是Karen Raraigh合作的方向——变异解读与标准化。This is a diagnostic lab turning a wet-lab functional assay into something fast enough to sit inside routine clinical reporting, which is exactly the bottleneck CFTR splice-region variants keep running into. 这是一家诊断实验室把一个湿实验功能性检测，改造成快到能嵌入日常临床报告流程的工具——而这恰恰是CFTR剪接区变异一直卡住的瓶颈。

JORDAN Before we go further, walk me through the vocabulary. 在往下讲之前，先给我讲讲术语。What's a minigene assay, concretely? 微基因实验（minigene assay）具体是什么？

ALEX You take the genomic DNA around a candidate variant — the exon plus flanking intron sequence — and clone it into a plasmid backbone that already has its own promoter and a couple of generic flanking exons. 你取候选变异周围的基因组DNA——也就是这个外显子（exon）加上两侧的内含子（intron）序列——把它克隆进一个质粒骨架，这个质粒本身已经带有启动子和两段通用的侧翼外显子。You transfect that plasmid into a cell line, let it get transcribed and spliced, then read out the resulting mRNA by RT-PCR or sequencing to see whether the variant changed which exons got stitched together. 你把这个质粒转染进细胞系，让它被转录和剪接，再通过RT-PCR（逆转录聚合酶链反应，一种检测剪接后mRNA序列的方法）或测序读出产生的mRNA，看这个变异是否改变了外显子的拼接方式。

JORDAN And why do you need a trick like that instead of just sequencing the patient's own RNA? 为什么需要这种办法，而不是直接测序病人自己的RNA？

ALEX Because the gene you care about might not be expressed, or not spliced the same way, in whatever tissue is easy to biopsy — usually blood. 因为你关心的那个基因，在容易取样的组织里——通常是血液——可能根本不表达，或者剪接方式不一样。CFTR splicing in airway epithelium doesn't always look like CFTR splicing in a lymphoblast line. CFTR在气道上皮细胞里的剪接情况，并不总是和它在淋巴母细胞系里的剪接情况一样。A minigene sidesteps that by forcing expression in a standard cell line, independent of the patient's own accessible tissue. 微基因实验绕开了这个问题，强制在一个标准细胞系里表达，完全不依赖病人本身能取到的组织。

JORDAN And a VUS is a variant of uncertain significance — we've used that term before. VUS也就是意义未明变异（variant of uncertain significance），这个词我们之前用过。What's new here is AlphaGenome? 这次新出现的是AlphaGenome？

ALEX Right, and don't confuse it with AlphaFold. 对，别把它和AlphaFold搞混了。AlphaFold predicts a protein's 3D structure from its amino acid sequence. AlphaFold是根据氨基酸序列预测蛋白质的三维结构。AlphaGenome is a different model from the same lineage — it predicts regulatory and splicing consequences of a DNA sequence change directly, without ever touching protein structure. AlphaGenome是同一血统下的另一个模型——它直接预测DNA序列变化对调控和剪接的后果，完全不涉及蛋白质结构。Here it's used purely as a triage step: scoring candidate splice-disrupting variants so the wet lab knows which ones are worth the minigene effort. 在这篇论文里它纯粹被用作分诊步骤：给候选的剪接破坏性变异打分，让湿实验室知道哪些变异值得花精力做微基因检测。

JORDAN So what was broken that this paper is trying to fix? 那这篇论文想解决的问题到底出在哪？

ALEX Start with the diagnostic landscape. 先说诊断层面的大背景。Multi-gene panel sequencing for hereditary cancer risk is now routine — you sequence dozens of genes at once rather than one at a time, because a patient's family history rarely points unambiguously at a single gene. 针对遗传性癌症风险的多基因panel（gene panel）检测现在已经是常规操作——你一次测几十个基因，而不是一个一个测，因为病人的家族史很少能明确指向单一基因。The authors ran a 77-gene panel across 2,142 individuals referred for hereditary cancer risk. 作者对2142名因遗传性癌症风险被转诊的个体做了一个77基因panel检测。They found 384 pathogenic or likely pathogenic variants spread across 54 genes, which works out to a diagnostic yield of roughly 18 percent — meaning for about one in five people tested, the panel found a variant clinicians felt confident calling disease-causing. 他们在54个基因里找到384个致病或可能致病（pathogenic/likely pathogenic）变异，诊断阳性率（diagnostic yield）大约是18%——也就是说每测五个人，大约就有一个人被panel找到了临床医生有信心判定为致病的变异。

JORDAN That leaves four out of five with nothing, or something murkier? 那剩下五分之四的人，是什么都没发现，还是结果更模糊？

ALEX Murkier, for a meaningful slice of them. 对相当一部分人来说，是更模糊。Seventeen percent of all cases in this cohort carried a variant of uncertain significance — not nothing, not confidently pathogenic, just unresolved. 这个队列里17%的病例携带意义未明变异（VUS）——不是什么都没有，也不是确定致病，就是悬而未决。And the authors flag that a large fraction of those VUS were suspected splice-region variants, which is its own special category of diagnostic limbo. 作者还指出，这些VUS里有很大一部分被怀疑是剪接区变异，这本身就是一类特殊的、卡在诊断灰色地带的变异。

JORDAN Why are splice variants harder than, say, a straightforward missense change? 为什么剪接变异比普通的错义变异（missense variant）更难判断？

ALEX Because a missense variant has one fairly contained question — does this amino acid substitution break the protein's function? 因为错义变异的问题相对单一——这个氨基酸替换会不会破坏蛋白质功能？A splice variant has a combinatorial question — does the spliceosome still recognize this as a splice site, and if not, which of several possible alternative outcomes happens: exon skipping, intron retention, use of a nearby cryptic site, or activation of a pseudoexon buried in what used to be normal intron? 剪接变异的问题是组合性的——剪接体（spliceosome）还能不能认出这是一个剪接位点，如果不能，接下来会发生哪种后果：外显子跳读（exon skipping）、内含子滞留（intron retention）、启用附近的隐蔽剪接位点（cryptic splice site），还是激活原本是正常内含子区域里的假外显子（pseudoexon）？Computational predictors are decent at flagging that something is probably disrupted, but they're much shakier at telling you exactly what the downstream transcript looks like, and ACMG/AMP guidelines are explicit that you generally can't apply the strongest loss-of-function evidence code, PVS1, confidently without some kind of experimental confirmation for anything outside the canonical splice dinucleotides. 计算预测工具在标记"大概出问题了"这件事上还算靠谱，但要说清楚下游转录本具体变成什么样，它们就没那么可靠了；而ACMG/AMP（美国医学遗传学与基因组学学院和分子病理学协会联合制定的变异分类指南）明确规定，对于不落在经典剪接二核苷酸上的变异，通常不能放心使用最强的功能丧失证据代码PVS1（表示"预测导致功能丧失"的强证据代码），除非有某种实验确认。

JORDAN So in silico tools get you to "suspicious," not to "resolved." 所以计算工具只能让你到"可疑"这一步，到不了"确定"。

ALEX Exactly — and that gap, suspicious-but-unresolved, is precisely the clinical backlog this workflow is built to drain. 没错——而"可疑但未解决"这个缺口，正是这套工作流程想要清理掉的临床积压。The whole motivation is that a lab doing thousands of panels a year accumulates VUS faster than any slow, bespoke functional-assay pipeline can clear them, and patients are left in diagnostic limbo — no actionable result, but also no closure. 整个研究动机在于，一个每年做几千个panel的实验室积累VUS的速度，比任何慢速、定制化的功能检测流程清理它们的速度都快，病人就被晾在诊断的灰色地带——没有可用的结果，也没有定论。

JORDAN So what did they actually build? 那他们到底做出了什么？

ALEX A streamlined minigene pipeline, designed from the ground up to be fast enough for routine clinical turnaround rather than a one-off research exercise. 一套精简的微基因流程，从一开始就是为了快到能满足常规临床报告节奏而设计的，不是一次性的科研练习。Two design choices do most of the work. 两个设计选择起到了大部分作用。First, they use synthetic DNA rather than amplifying the variant region out of patient genomic DNA — you just order the exact sequence you want, variant included, instead of running PCR and cloning off a patient sample. 第一，他们使用合成DNA，而不是从病人基因组DNA里把变异区域扩增出来——你直接订购想要的确切序列，变异包含在内，而不用对病人样本做PCR和克隆。Second, they use recombination-based cloning to drop that synthetic fragment into the minigene vector, which is faster and more reliable than classical restriction-enzyme cloning. 第二，他们用基于重组的克隆（recombination-based cloning）把合成片段放进微基因载体，这比传统的限制性内切酶克隆更快、更可靠。Together, that cuts out two of the slowest, most failure-prone steps in a traditional minigene build. 这两点加在一起，砍掉了传统微基因构建里最慢、最容易失败的两个步骤。

JORDAN And AlphaGenome's job is just to decide which variants get that treatment? AlphaGenome的作用就是决定哪些变异值得走这套流程？

ALEX Right — it's a prioritization filter sitting in front of the wet lab. 对——它是摆在湿实验室前面的优先级过滤器。Given the volume of candidate splice-affecting VUS coming out of a 77-gene panel, you can't minigene-test everything; AlphaGenome's predicted splicing disruption scores are used to rank candidates and to flag cases specifically where computational evidence is internally discordant — where different predictors, or different lines of in silico evidence, disagree with each other. 考虑到一个77基因panel会产生大量候选的剪接影响VUS，不可能把所有变异都拿去做微基因检测；AlphaGenome预测的剪接破坏分数被用来给候选变异排序，并专门标记出计算证据内部不一致的情况——也就是不同的预测工具，或不同类型的计算证据彼此意见不一致。Those discordant cases get prioritized for the assay precisely because the computation alone can't settle them. 这些存在分歧的病例会被优先安排做实验，正是因为单靠计算无法解决它们。
