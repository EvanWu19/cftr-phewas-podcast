How did this episode land for you?

Empowering bioinformatics communities with Nextflow and nf-core

Langer, Amaral, Baudement, and the nf-core core team's 2025 Genome Biology paper is the community's own progress report: a bibliometric survey of workflow-management-system adoption (Nextflow now holds ~43% of citation share among the major systems, with 94% user satisfaction in a 608-person survey), a tour of recent nf-core/Nextflow architecture (DSL2's modules and subworkflows, now over 1400 modules and ~80 subworkflows; plugins like nf-test, nf-validation, nf-co2footprint, and nf-prov), and a case study of the EuroFAANG farmed-animal-genomics consortium's progressive transition from a 78-analysis, four-language patchwork to a largely Nextflow/nf-core-standardized pipeline set. The paper's own numbers make the strongest case for deployability: 83% of nf-core's released pipelines deploy off-the-shelf without crashing, nearly four times the rate measured for the competing Snakemake Workflow Catalog. For the AI-tooling/career priority, the buildable artifact is a direct port of one of Runyu's own ad hoc CFTR-WGS or registry scripts into a minimal nf-core-style Nextflow pipeline — demonstrated by this paper's own example of BovReg's Bash eQTL workflow, ported with minimal refactoring.

Plain-English glossary

  • WfMS (workflow management system) — Software that chains computational steps into a reproducible, parallelizable, portable pipeline (examples: Nextflow, Snakemake, Galaxy).（中文：工作流管理系统，把一连串计算步骤串成一个可复现、可并行、可移植的流程的软件，例子包括Nextflow、Snakemake、Galaxy。）
  • Nextflow — A programmer-facing, language-agnostic workflow engine that can wrap existing Bash, Perl, or other scripts directly rather than requiring a full rewrite, and supports execution across HPC clusters and cloud.（中文：一个面向程序员、跨语言的工作流引擎，可以直接包装已有的Bash、Perl等脚本而不需要彻底重写，并支持在高性能计算集群和云上执行。）
  • nf-core — A community-curated registry of ready-to-run Nextflow pipelines built on a shared template, with linting, continuous-integration (CI) testing, and enforced best practices.（中文：一个由社区整理维护的、建立在共享模板之上的Nextflow流程注册库，带有代码规范检查、持续集成测试和强制执行的最佳实践。）
  • DSL2 (Domain-Specific Language 2) — Nextflow's second-generation syntax, which lets a pipeline be split into reusable modules (single tasks) and subworkflows (chains of modules).（中文：Nextflow的第二代领域专用语言语法，让一个流程可以拆分成可复用的模块（单个任务）和子工作流（若干模块的串联）。）
  • Module / subworkflow — A module wraps one computational task with its own declared software dependencies (via containers/Conda); a subworkflow chains several modules together; both are reusable across any pipeline.（中文：模块封装单个计算任务及其软件依赖（通过容器/Conda声明）；子工作流把若干模块串联起来；两者都可以在任意流程之间复用。）
  • Idempotent task execution — Each task gets a unique identity from a hash of its inputs and script, so re-running a pipeline skips work that hasn't changed.（中文：幂等任务执行，每个任务根据其输入和脚本内容算出一个唯一哈希标识，所以重新运行流程时会跳过没有变化的部分，不会重复计算。）
  • eQTL (expression quantitative trait locus) — A genetic variant associated with variation in gene expression level, used here as the BovReg workflow's analysis target.（中文：eQTL，即影响基因表达水平的遗传变异位点，本文中是BovReg工作流的分析对象。）
  • FAIR principles — Findability, Accessibility, Interoperability, and Reusability — the data/software stewardship standard nf-core pipelines are designed to meet.（中文：FAIR原则——可被找到、可获取、可互操作、可重复使用——是nf-core流程设计所遵循的数据与软件管理标准。）

Full citation

Langer BE, Amaral A, Baudement MO, et al., The nf-core Community. Empowering bioinformatics communities with Nextflow and nf-core. Genome Biology. 2025;26(1):228. PMID: 40731283 | PMC: PMC12309086 | DOI: 10.1186/s13059-025-03673-9

Methods / tools to lift

  • nf-core/tools CLI — scaffolds a new pipeline from the shared template, launches pipelines via command line or a browser-based GUI, manages module/subworkflow versions.
  • nf-test — a testing framework for Nextflow pipelines and components.
  • nf-validation — JSON-schema validation of pipeline parameters and sample sheets.
  • nf-prov — generates a provenance report of exactly what ran, with which parameters, for a given pipeline run.
  • nf-co2footprint — tracks energy consumption and carbon emissions per pipeline run.
  • Bioconda / Biocontainers — community-curated container/package collections each nf-core module declares its dependencies against.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: AI-tooling / career.

Port one existing ad hoc CFTR-WGS or registry-analysis script into a minimal nf-core-style Nextflow pipeline: scaffold with nf-core/tools create, wrap the existing script logic as one or two modules with minimal refactoring (mirroring BovReg's Bash-to-Nextflow eQTL port), add an nf-validation parameter schema, and add one or two nf-test cases. Difficulty: S–M.

What to verify first

  • Whether Nextflow's HPC/cloud task-parallelism model actually buys anything for single-machine, pandas-scale registry/EHR tabular work, versus being a sequencing-era tool applied where it isn't needed.
  • Whether nf-core's heavily sequencing-centric module ecosystem (ATAC-seq, ChIP-seq, RNA-seq) has anything reusable for tabular registry data-quality work, or whether every module would need to be written from scratch anyway.
  • The paper's own caveat that container-based dependency declaration "may stop short of solving incompatibilities caused by versioning" — worth testing directly on a small pilot with two or three real dependencies rather than taking as a footnote.
  • Three of nf-core's 7 steering-committee seats and 7 of its 14 core-team seats are held by Seqera employees (the company that commercializes Nextflow); useful context when weighing any claim about the community's independence or trajectory.

ALEX Today's paper is "Empowering bioinformatics communities with Nextflow and nf-core," by Langer, Amaral, Baudement, and a long list of co-authors representing the nf-core core team, published in Genome Biology in 2025. 今天这篇论文题为《用Nextflow和nf-core赋能生物信息学社区》（Empowering bioinformatics communities with Nextflow and nf-core），作者是Langer、Amaral、Baudement，以及代表nf-core核心团队的一长串共同作者，2025年发表于《Genome Biology》期刊。 It's Track B, AI tooling and career. 这是今天的Track B节目，主题是AI工具与职业发展。 This isn't a new tool — it's the nf-core community's own progress report on itself, plus a case study of a large research consortium adopting it from scratch. 这不是一个新工具——而是nf-core社区自己写的一份自我进展报告，外加一个大型研究联盟从零开始采用它的案例研究。

JORDAN Let's get oriented. 我们先弄清楚基本概念。 What's a workflow management system, in the sense this paper means it? 这篇论文所说的工作流管理系统具体是指什么?

ALEX A WfMS（工作流管理系统，workflow management system的缩写）ties together a chain of computational steps — alignment, variant calling, quantification, whatever — into something that's reproducible, parallelizable across HPC clusters or cloud, and portable between a laptop and a supercomputer without rewriting the logic each time. WfMS把一连串计算步骤——比对、变异检测、定量，等等——串成一个整体，让它可复现、可以在高性能计算集群或云上并行，并且能在笔记本电脑和超级计算机之间搬运而不需要每次重写逻辑。 Nextflow is one of the two dominant programmer-facing ones, alongside Snakemake; Galaxy is the third major option, but aimed at a graphical-interface audience instead. Nextflow是面向程序员的两大主流工具之一，另一个是Snakemake；Galaxy是第三个主要选项，但面向的是图形界面用户。

JORDAN And nf-core specifically? nf-core具体是什么?

ALEX nf-core is a community-curated registry of ready-to-run Nextflow pipelines, built on a shared template with linting（代码规范自动检查）, continuous-integration testing — CI testing（持续集成测试）, and enforced best practices, so a pipeline you download from nf-core is far more likely to actually run on the first try than one you find on a random lab's GitHub. nf-core是一个由社区整理维护的、可直接运行的Nextflow流程注册库，建立在一套共享模板之上，带有代码规范自动检查、持续集成测试，以及强制执行的最佳实践，所以你从nf-core下载的流程，比随便哪个实验室GitHub仓库里的流程，第一次跑起来就能成功的概率要高得多。 The technical backbone making this modular is DSL2（Nextflow的第二代领域专用语言，Domain-Specific Language，支持把流程拆分成可复用的模块和子工作流）, Nextflow's second-generation Domain-Specific Language, which lets a pipeline be split into reusable modules and subworkflows instead of one giant monolithic script. 让这一切模块化的技术核心是DSL2——也就是Nextflow的第二代领域专用语言——它让一个流程可以被拆分成可复用的模块和子工作流，而不是一整块巨大的脚本。

ALEX Context on why this paper exists now. 说说这篇论文为什么会在这个时间点出现。 Using Google Scholar citation counts as a rough proxy, Nextflow and Snakemake usage has grown sharply since 2018, while Galaxy has stayed roughly flat after peaking in 2021 — total citations across these systems hit 4706 in 2024, about two and a half times the 2018 figure. 用谷歌学术的引用数作为一个粗略代理指标，Nextflow和Snakemake的使用量自2018年以来大幅增长，而Galaxy在2021年达到峰值后基本保持平稳——这几类系统的总引用数在2024年达到4706次，大约是2018年的2.5倍。 Nextflow alone now accounts for roughly 43 percent of that citation share, and it's the highest-ranked of these tools on GitHub by star count, around position 3210 out of an estimated 420 million public repositories. 单独看Nextflow，目前大约占了43%的引用份额，在GitHub按star数排名也是这几个工具里最高的，大约排在4.2亿个公开仓库中的第3210位。

JORDAN Why does a registry on top of the workflow engine matter so much? 在工作流引擎之上再搭一个注册库，为什么这么重要?

ALEX Because the engine alone doesn't guarantee a pipeline actually runs when someone else downloads it. 因为光有引擎本身，并不能保证别人下载这个流程之后真的能跑起来。 An independent study measuring "automated reproduction" — whether a pipeline deploys off-the-shelf without crashing — found that 83 percent of nf-core's released pipelines deployed as expected, nearly four times the rate measured for the equivalent Snakemake registry, the Snakemake Workflow Catalog. 一项独立研究专门测量了自动化复现能力——也就是一个流程能否原样部署、不崩溃——结果发现nf-core发布的流程里有83%能按预期部署，几乎是对应的Snakemake注册库（Snakemake Workflow Catalog）测得比例的四倍。 That gap is the entire argument for why a curated, template-enforced registry is worth the overhead of joining it. 这个差距正是加入一个有模板约束、社区整理的注册库值得承受额外开销这一整套论证的核心。

JORDAN And the community scale backing this up? 支撑这一切的社区规模有多大?

ALEX As of February 2025: 124 pipelines, over 2600 GitHub contributors including more than 1200 organization members, and over 10,000 users on the project's Slack. 截至2025年2月：124个流程，超过2600名GitHub贡献者，其中包括超过1200名机构成员，以及超过1万名使用项目Slack的用户。 Governance runs through a 7-person steering committee and a 14-person core team, with new pipelines and changes discussed on Slack first, then formalized as GitHub pull requests needing at least one or two reviewer approvals depending on whether it's a release. 治理结构由一个7人指导委员会和一个14人核心团队负责运作，新流程和改动先在Slack上讨论，再正式化为GitHub拉取请求，根据是否是发布版本，需要至少一到两名审核者批准。 Worth flagging plainly: Seqera, the company that commercializes Nextflow, holds 3 of the 7 steering-committee seats and 7 of the 14 core-team seats, and the paper's own competing-interests section discloses that some authors hold Seqera stock. 有一点值得明确指出：商业化运营Nextflow的公司Seqera，在7人指导委员会中占了3席，在14人核心团队中占了7席，论文自己的利益冲突声明里也披露了部分作者持有Seqera的股票。 That doesn't invalidate the technical content, but it's useful context for reading any claim about the community's health or trajectory. 这并不会让技术内容失效，但在阅读任何关于社区健康状况或发展轨迹的说法时，这是个有用的背景信息。

JORDAN How does a community that size actually onboard new people? 这么大规模的社区，到底是怎么把新人带进来的?

ALEX Deliberately, and with real resourcing behind it. 很有章法，而且背后投入了真实的资源。 The core team runs free Nextflow and nf-core training videos every six months — over 35,000 views on YouTube between 2022 and 2023 alone — plus more than 100 recorded "nf-core/bytesize" webinars on specific techniques and pipelines, 16 hackathons since the project's founding, and a mentorship program that deliberately pairs experienced developers with newcomers from underrepresented groups. 核心团队每六个月都会发布免费的Nextflow和nf-core培训视频——仅2022到2023年间YouTube观看量就超过3.5万次——另外还有超过100场录制的"nf-core/bytesize"专题网络讲座，项目成立以来累计办了16次hackathon，还有一个刻意把经验丰富的开发者和来自欠代表群体的新人配对起来的导师计划。 Registered Slack users grew more than tenfold, from 782 to over 10,800, in the five years since the DSL2 syntax shipped in 2020 — a growth curve that tracks the tooling transition, not just general interest in bioinformatics. Slack注册用户数在2020年DSL2语法发布后的五年里增长了十倍以上，从782人涨到超过1万800人——这条增长曲线对应的是工具本身的迁移，而不只是对生物信息学的一般兴趣上升。

ALEX On the architecture side, the headline development is DSL2 itself — splitting a pipeline into modules, which wrap a single computational task, and subworkflows, which chain several modules together, both reusable across any pipeline, not just the one they were written for. 从架构角度看，最核心的进展就是DSL2本身——把一个流程拆分成模块（module，封装单一计算任务）和子工作流（subworkflow，把若干模块串联起来），两者都可以在任意流程之间复用，而不只局限于最初编写它们的那条流程。 The nf-core/modules repository has accumulated over 1400 modules and roughly 80 subworkflows in under five years, and — this is the part that matters for reuse — each module declares its own software dependencies via container images and Conda packages, so you get the right environment for free rather than fighting dependency conflicts yourself. nf-core/modules这个仓库在不到五年时间里积累了超过1400个模块和大约80个子工作流，而真正值得关注的是——每个模块通过容器镜像和Conda包声明自己的软件依赖，所以你能免费获得正确的运行环境，而不需要自己去跟依赖冲突搏斗。

JORDAN What else is in the toolbox beyond the pipelines themselves? 除了流程本身，工具箱里还有什么?

ALEX Several plugins worth knowing by name: nf-test for testing pipeline components, nf-validation for JSON-schema validation of parameters and sample sheets, nf-co2footprint for tracking energy use and carbon emissions per run, and nf-prov for generating a provenance report of exactly what ran with which parameters. 几个值得记住名字的插件：nf-test用于测试流程组件，nf-validation用于对参数和样本表做JSON schema校验，nf-co2footprint用于追踪每次运行的能耗和碳排放，nf-prov用于生成一份到底跑了什么、用了什么参数的溯源报告。 The nf-core/tools CLI rounds this out — it can scaffold a brand-new pipeline from the shared template, launch an existing one through either command line or a browser-based GUI, and manage module versions. nf-core/tools这个命令行工具把这些串了起来——它可以从共享模板生成一个全新的流程骨架，通过命令行或基于浏览器的图形界面启动已有流程，还能管理模块版本。

JORDAN Where would you push back? 你会在哪里提出质疑?

ALEX The paper frames container-based dependency declaration as basically solving the reproducibility problem, but it says itself, almost in passing, that this "may stop short of solving incompatibilities caused by versioning" between two components that still can't coexist. 论文把基于容器的依赖声明基本上当作已经解决了可复现性问题，但它自己几乎是一句带过地承认，这可能无法完全解决两个组件之间因版本问题而产生的、彼此仍然无法共存的不兼容问题。 That's a real gap dressed up as a minor caveat — containerizing each module separately helps a lot, but it doesn't guarantee the pipeline as a whole is internally consistent, and for anyone building a long-lived production pipeline rather than a one-off analysis, that distinction matters more than the paper's framing suggests. 这其实是一个被包装成小小注意事项的真实缺口——把每个模块分别容器化确实很有帮助，但并不能保证整个流程内部是一致的，对于要搭建长期生产流程而不是一次性分析的人来说，这个区别比论文的措辞所暗示的更重要。

JORDAN Does the main case study actually back up the pitch? 主案例研究是否真的支撑了这篇论文的论点?

ALEX The EuroFAANG case study is the strongest piece of evidence in the paper. EuroFAANG这个案例研究是整篇论文里最有说服力的证据。 Six Horizon 2020 research consortia — AQUA-FAANG, BovReg, GENE-SWitCH, RUMIGEN, GEroNIMO, and HoloRuminant — spanning fish, cattle, pig, and chicken genomics, surveyed their 15 partner labs and found 78 separate analyses running across four different languages: 70 in Nextflow, 3 in Snakemake, 4 in Bash, 1 in Perl. 六个欧盟Horizon 2020项目联盟——AQUA-FAANG、BovReg、GENE-SWitCH、RUMIGEN、GEroNIMO和HoloRuminant——横跨鱼类、牛、猪和鸡的基因组学研究，对其15个合作实验室做了调研，发现一共有78项独立分析分别用四种不同的语言在跑：70个用Nextflow，3个用Snakemake，4个用Bash，1个用Perl。 Nextflow already dominating made it the obvious consolidation target. Nextflow本来就占了绝大多数，这让它自然成为整合的目标。 The concrete transition example: BovReg's eQTL（表达数量性状位点，expression quantitative trait locus，指影响基因表达水平的遗传变异位点）analysis workflow was originally written in plain Bash, and got ported into Nextflow with minimal refactoring and minimal revalidation, because Nextflow is explicitly language-agnostic — it can wrap an existing Bash or Perl script directly rather than requiring a full rewrite. 一个具体的迁移例子：BovReg的eQTL分析流程最初是用纯Bash写的，后来以最小的重构和最小的重新验证成本迁移进了Nextflow，因为Nextflow明确支持跨语言——它可以直接包装一段现有的Bash或Perl脚本，而不需要彻底重写。

JORDAN Did they actually move the needle on standardization, or just talk about it? 他们是真的推动了标准化，还是只是说说而已?

ALEX Concretely: partners contributed back to existing nf-core pipelines like nf-core/atacseq, nf-core/chipseq, and nf-core/smrnaseq; the Roslin Institute ported an internal Iso-Seq pipeline into the nf-core collection as nf-core/isoseq to close a PacBio-annotation gap; and GEroNIMO and BovReg jointly built nf-pegASE, a new pipeline for allele-specific expression analysis, on the nf-core template, with plans to formally contribute it back. 具体来说：合作伙伴向nf-core/atacseq、nf-core/chipseq和nf-core/smrnaseq这些已有流程提交了贡献；Roslin研究所把一个内部的Iso-Seq流程以nf-core/isoseq的形式迁移进了nf-core，弥补了PacBio注释方面的空白；GEroNIMO和BovReg联合基于nf-core模板构建了一个新流程nf-pegASE，用于等位基因特异性表达分析，并计划正式贡献回nf-core。 Of 17 pipelines used across the six consortia, 4 were shared by at least three of them — real convergence, not just aspiration. 在六个联盟使用的17个流程里，有4个被至少三个联盟共同使用——这是真实的趋同，不只是一种愿景。 The paper is honest that quantifying the full benefit bibliometrically will take another two to four years given publication lag, but a separate 608-person Nextflow user survey reported 94 percent satisfaction, up 2 points from the year before. 论文也坦诚地指出，考虑到发表的滞后性，要用文献计量学的方式完全量化这种收益还需要再等两到四年，但另一项针对608名Nextflow用户的调研显示满意度达到94%，比前一年提高了2个百分点。

JORDAN One more thing before we move on — how did they actually choose Nextflow over the alternatives? 在往下讲之前还有一点——他们当初到底是怎么在几个选项里选中Nextflow的?

ALEX Through direct consultation across the 15 partner labs, not a top-down mandate. 通过对15个合作实验室的直接调研，而不是自上而下的强制规定。 Nextflow being the overwhelming incumbent made the decision close to automatic, but the paper also credits language agnosticism and idempotent task execution — each task gets a unique identity based on a hash of its inputs and its script, so re-running a pipeline doesn't redo work that hasn't changed — as the two properties that made the transition low-friction rather than a rewrite project. Nextflow本来就是压倒性的主流选择，这让决策几乎是自动的，但论文也把跨语言支持和幂等任务执行（idempotent task execution，每个任务根据其输入和脚本内容算出一个哈希值作为唯一标识，所以重新运行流程不会重复执行没有变化的部分）这两个特性，归功为让这次迁移变得低摩擦，而不是一场重写项目的关键原因。 Adoption also wasn't uniform top-down: AQUA-FAANG had two institutions — the University of Edinburgh and the Norwegian University of Life Sciences — formally take responsibility for deployment and training, while GENE-SWitCH simply recommended Nextflow in its original proposal and left implementation details to each partner. 采用过程也不是统一的自上而下推动：AQUA-FAANG是由两家机构——爱丁堡大学和挪威生命科学大学——正式负责部署和培训，而GENE-SWitCH只是在最初的立项提案里推荐使用Nextflow，具体怎么落地则留给每个合作方自行决定。 Both routes worked, which is itself useful data for anyone trying to introduce a new standard into an existing team without full authority to mandate it. 两种路径都奏效了，这本身对任何想在一个现有团队里推行新标准、却又没有完全强制权力的人来说，都是一条有用的信息。

ALEX Where this lands for you specifically is the AI-tooling and career priority — building reproducible-pipeline chops as a visible, portfolio-grade skill for the senior RWE-times-genomics roles you're targeting. 这对你来说具体落在哪里：AI工具与职业发展这个优先级——把搭建可复现流程打造成一项看得见的、能写进作品集的技能，服务于你瞄准的高级RWE结合基因组学岗位。 The EuroFAANG case study is basically a template for exactly this kind of move: take an existing ad hoc script, port it into Nextflow with minimal refactoring first, and only add nf-core-template rigor where it actually earns its keep.
