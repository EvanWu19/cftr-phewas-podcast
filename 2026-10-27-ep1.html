Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): Watching another gene's VCEP show its work

Ritter and colleagues (2026, Genetics in Medicine), on behalf of the ClinGen Von Hippel-Lindau (VHL) Variant Curation Expert Panel (VCEP), document the full process of tailoring the general ACMG/AMP variant-classification guideline to VHL disease: substantially rewriting eight evidence codes, benchmarking REVEL/SIFT/PolyPhen against 112 ClinVar-classified VHL variants (REVEL ≥0.6 reached 92.6% accuracy, but the panel adopted ClinGen's more conservative ≥0.664 threshold for cross-gene consistency), calibrating SpliceAI/VarSeak splice-variant cutoffs, running a domain-level pathogenic:benign odds calculation to justify hotspot calls, and deliberately capping a large saturation-genome-editing (SGE) functional dataset (2,268 variants) at "Supporting" strength rather than "Strong" because the assay only reliably captures some VHL disease subtypes. A 31-variant pilot run resolved cleanly with no dramatic reversals, and variants lacking prior CIViC/Hypothes.is biocuration were far more likely to stay stuck as VUS. The paper's value for this podcast is the process itself — a live example of the gene-specific calibration exercise CFTR's own VCEP already runs. Buildable artifact: a CFTR Evidence-Code Calibration Auditor.

Plain-English glossary

  • ClinGen / VCEP (Variant Curation Expert Panel) — ClinGen is the NIH-funded effort formalizing variant classification across the field; a VCEP is a gene- or disease-specific panel it charters to tailor the general ACMG/AMP guideline to one gene's actual biology.（中文：ClinGen是由美国国立卫生研究院资助、致力于规范整个领域变异分类流程的机构；VCEP是其为某个基因或疾病专门成立的委员会，负责把通用的ACMG/AMP指南细化到该基因的具体生物学特性。）
  • ACMG/AMP evidence codes — short labels (PVS1, PS3, PS4, PM1, BS2, BS3, BS4, BP5, etc.) that combine with assigned weights into a final pathogenic-to-benign classification.（中文：一系列带权重的简短标签，组合后得出从致病到良性的最终变异分类判定。）
  • REVEL — an ensemble in-silico missense-pathogenicity predictor combining several individual computational tools into one score.（中文：一种集成式的错义变异致病性计算预测工具，将多个单独工具的结果综合成一个分数。）
  • SpliceAI — a deep-learning model estimating how likely a variant is to disrupt normal RNA splicing.（中文：一种基于深度学习的模型，用于估计某个变异破坏正常RNA剪接的可能性。）
  • Saturation genome editing (SGE) — a CRISPR-based functional-assay technique generating nearly every possible variant at a locus in parallel and reading out each one's functional consequence at scale.（中文：饱和基因组编辑，一种基于CRISPR的功能检测技术，并行产生某位点几乎所有可能的变异，并大规模读出每个变异的功能后果。）
  • PS4 (case-evidence code) — the ACMG/AMP code scoring whether a variant appears significantly more often in affected people than controls, scalable from Supporting to Very Strong across accumulated unrelated cases.（中文：ACMG/AMP中的病例证据代码，评估某变异在患病者中出现的频率是否显著高于对照组，随无关病例累积可从"支持"级提升到"极强"级。）
  • CIViC / Hypothes.is — two biocuration platforms: CIViC stores discrete, queryable structured evidence items; Hypothes.is lets curators annotate passages directly on source documents for richer context.（中文：两个文献审定平台——CIViC存储离散、可查询的结构化证据条目；Hypothes.is则让审定者直接在原始文献上标注段落以捕捉更丰富的语境。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • REVEL / SIFT / PolyPhen confusion-matrix benchmarking — compare in-silico predictors against a gene's own ClinVar-classified variants before trusting a published threshold.
  • SpliceAI + VarSeak dual-tool splice calibration — pairing a deep-learning splice predictor with a second, independently-built tool before assigning pathogenic or benign splice codes.
  • Domain-level pathogenic:benign odds calculation — a simple, reusable way to justify a hotspot/critical-domain evidence code from existing ClinVar submissions.
  • CIViC + Hypothes.is dual-platform biocuration triage — using a structured database and a web-annotation layer together to prioritize which variants are worth curator time.

ALEX ClinGen, the Clinical Genome Resource, is the NIH-funded effort that formalizes how variants get classified across the field, and a VCEP — Variant Curation Expert Panel — is a gene- or disease-specific committee ClinGen charters to take the general ACMG/AMP guideline and tighten it for one gene's actual biology. ACMG/AMP本身的工作原理，是把一系列带权重的"证据代码"组合起来，得出最终从致病到良性的判定——比如PVS1代表功能缺失型的无效变异，PS3代表功能实验证据，PM1代表突变热点，BS2代表在健康未患病成年人身上观察到该变异。 CFTR already has its own VCEP running this exact calibration exercise, so every decision VHL's panel makes here is a worked example you can hold up against CFTR's.CFTR自己的VCEP也在进行完全相同的校准工作，所以这个VHL专家小组在这里做出的每一个决定，都是一个可以拿来和CFTR的决定相互对照的实例。

ALEX Okay — background. VHL disease is an autosomal-dominant hereditary cancer syndrome caused by germline pathogenic variants in the VHL tumor-suppressor gene, with a prevalence somewhere between 1 in 39,000 and 1 in 91,000, driving tumors across the kidney, retina, central nervous system, adrenal gland, and pancreas. 好，先说背景。VHL病是一种常染色体显性遗传的癌症综合征，由VHL肿瘤抑制基因的生殖细胞致病变异引起，患病率大约在三万九千分之一到九万一千分之一之间，会在肾脏、视网膜、中枢神经系统、肾上腺和胰腺等多个部位引发肿瘤。 VHL protein normally forms a complex — the paper calls it VCB-CR — with elongin B/C and cullin 2 that, under normal oxygen conditions, tags HIF1-alpha for degradation; lose that function and HIF1-alpha accumulates as if the cell were starved of oxygen, driving tumor formation. VHL蛋白正常情况下会与elongin B/C和cullin 2形成一个复合体——论文中称为VCB-CR——在正常氧气条件下，这个复合体会标记HIF1-α（缺氧诱导因子1α）以降解。一旦这个功能丧失，HIF1-α就会像细胞处于缺氧状态一样大量积累，从而驱动肿瘤的形成。

JORDAN So why can't a generic ACMG/AMP guideline just handle VHL the way it handles everything else? 那为什么一套通用的ACMG/AMP指南不能像处理其他基因一样直接处理VHL呢？

ALEX Because the generic codes don't know VHL's biology. 因为那些通用代码并不了解VHL的具体生物学特性。 PM1, the hotspot code, means nothing until someone has actually mapped VHL's mutational hotspots; BS2, the healthy-adult code, is calibrated for conditions with early, full penetrance, but VHL disease isn't fully penetrant until around age 65, so a generic BS2 threshold would wrongly call pathogenic variants benign in healthy 40-year-olds who simply haven't developed tumors yet. PM1，也就是热点代码，在没有人真正绘制出VHL的突变热点图之前毫无意义；BS2，也就是健康成年人代码，是针对那些发病早、且外显率完全的疾病情况校准的，但VHL病要到大约65岁外显率才完全，所以直接套用通用的BS2阈值，会错误地把那些仅仅是还没发展出肿瘤的健康40岁携带者身上的致病变异判定为良性。 That's precisely why ClinGen charters a VCEP per gene rather than trying to write one rule that fits every gene on earth — and it's why the VHL VCEP, formed in 2018 with three sub-teams split across computational/functional/splicing, segregation/allelic/de novo, and population genetics, spent years substantially rewriting eight of the standard evidence codes before ever touching a real variant.这正是ClinGen为什么要为每个基因单独组建一个VCEP，而不是试图写出一套放之四海而皆准的规则——这也正是为什么VHL的VCEP在2018年成立、分成计算/功能/剪接、家系分离/等位/新发突变、以及人群遗传学这三个子团队后，在真正处理任何一个变异之前，先花了数年时间对八项标准证据代码进行了实质性的重写。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built, then — how do REVEL and SpliceAI get turned into a VHL-specific rule? 那就带我走一遍他们具体做了什么——REVEL和SpliceAI是怎么被转化为VHL专属规则的？

ALEX For missense variants, they pulled 112 clinically classified VHL variants from ClinVar and ran REVEL, SIFT, and PolyPhen against them, building confusion matrices at different thresholds. 对于错义变异，他们从ClinVar中抽取了112个已有临床分类的VHL变异，分别用REVEL、SIFT和PolyPhen进行打分，并在不同阈值下构建混淆矩阵。 REVEL at ≥0.6 hit 92.6% accuracy — 91 of 92 pathogenic/likely-pathogenic and 22 of 30 benign/likely-benign correctly sorted — while PolyPhen topped out around 79% and SIFT around 55%; rather than keep their own locally-optimized 0.6 cutoff, they adopted the more conservative ≥0.664 threshold that ClinGen's own Sequence Variant Interpretation group had already derived across many genes, trading a little VHL-specific accuracy for consistency with the broader framework. REVEL在阈值≥0.6时达到了92.6%的准确率——92个致病/可能致病变异中有91个被正确分类，30个良性/可能良性变异中有22个被正确分类——而PolyPhen的准确率上限约为79%，SIFT约为55%；但他们并没有沿用自己本地优化出来的0.6这个阈值，而是采用了ClinGen的序列变异解读（SVI）工作组此前已经跨多个基因得出的、更为保守的≥0.664阈值，以换取与更广泛框架的一致性，牺牲了一点VHL特有的准确性。 For splicing, SpliceAI scores ≥0.5 combined with a commercial tool called VarSeak scoring class 4 or 5 earns the pathogenic splice code, while the mirror-image combination — SpliceAI ≤0.1 plus VarSeak class 1 or 2 — earns the benign one; for the hotspot code PM1, they ran an "odds" calculation, counting pathogenic versus benign ClinVar variants inside each structural domain, and found the first beta domain carrying a roughly 10-to-1 pathogenic odds, enough to justify a moderate-strength PM1 call for any missense variant that lands there.对于剪接变异，SpliceAI评分≥0.5并结合一种名为VarSeak的商业工具给出4级或5级评分，就可以获得致病性剪接证据代码；反过来，SpliceAI评分≤0.1且VarSeak评为1级或2级的组合，则可以获得良性证据代码；而对于热点代码PM1，他们进行了一种"比值"计算，统计每个结构域内致病与良性ClinVar变异的数量，发现第一个β结构域内致病变异与良性变异的比值约为10比1，足以支持任何落在该结构域内的错义变异获得中等强度的PM1判定。

JORDAN One thing I noticed in the methods — they say they used PS4 "instead of" PP4. What's the actual difference, and why does it matter? 我注意到方法部分里，他们说用PS4"取代"了PP4。这两者到底有什么区别，为什么这很重要？

ALEX PP4 is the standard code for "the patient's phenotype is specific for the disease caused by this gene," scored once per case at a fixed weak strength; PS4 is the code for "this variant is seen significantly more often in affected people than in controls," and ACMG/AMP allows it to scale from Supporting all the way to Very Strong as case counts accumulate across unrelated probands. PP4是标准证据代码，意思是"患者的表型对该基因所致疾病具有特异性"，每个病例只计一次，权重固定且较弱；PS4则是"该变异在患病者中出现的频率显著高于对照组"，而ACMG/AMP允许PS4随着无关患者病例数的累积，从"支持"级一路提升到"极强"级。 VHL disease has no single genetic test that's independently diagnostic, so the VCEP folded phenotype-specificity into PS4 itself — a proband meeting strict Danish clinical criteria scores a full point, one with only renal cell carcinoma and pheochromocytoma but no multi-gene panel scores a partial 0.25, and the points sum across unrelated probands until they cross a strength threshold. VHL病没有任何单一的基因检测可以独立确诊，所以这个VCEP把表型特异性直接并入了PS4本身——一个完全符合丹麦临床标准的患者记1整分，一个仅有肾细胞癌和嗜铬细胞瘤但没有做多基因检测的患者记0.25分，这些分数在无关患者之间累加，直到跨过某个强度阈值。 It's a clever workaround for a disease where "phenotype specificity" and "case evidence" aren't cleanly separable — and it's worth checking whether CFTR, where the phenotype is comparatively much more specific, actually needs the same kind of merge.这是一个针对"表型特异性"与"病例证据"无法清晰分开的疾病所设计的巧妙变通方法——值得检查的是，对于表型特异性要高得多的CFTR而言，是否真的需要同样的合并处理。

JORDAN And the two biocuration platforms, CIViC and Hypothes.is — are those redundant, or genuinely different tools? 还有那两个文献审定平台，CIViC和Hypothes.is——它们是重复的，还是真正不同的工具？
