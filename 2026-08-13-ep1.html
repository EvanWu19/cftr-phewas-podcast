Ep1 — Track A — BoltzOmics: Predicting genetic variant effects on drug binding with Boltz-2 (Ngo 2026)

BoltzOmics wraps Boltz-2, a deep-learning "co-folding" model that jointly predicts a protein-drug complex structure and a binding-affinity score in a single pass, in an orchestration layer built for screening many mutations against many drugs at once. Validated across four deliberately different drug targets — the hERG and NaV1.5 cardiac ion channels, the CYP3A4 metabolic enzyme, and the HER2 kinase domain — it achieved Pearson correlations with experimental potency data up to 0.86 for wild-type proteins and 0.72 for mutants, with pairwise ranking accuracy consistently in the 60–80% range against a 50% random baseline: the model is considerably better at ranking which variant binds tighter than at nailing an exact potency number. Every mutation tested sat inside or next to the known drug-binding site — distal or allosteric mutations were out of scope. This lands on the Karen Raraigh variant-interpretation track: generic pathogenicity predictors say whether a CFTR VUS looks structurally damaged, but say nothing about whether a modulator would still bind it. The buildable artifact is cftr-eti-boltz-screen — an M-difficulty pipeline that generates wild-type and mutant CFTR structures for VUS near the TMD/NBD1 modulator-binding interfaces, docks elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor against each, and ranks variants by predicted binding-affinity shift as a functional-assay triage list.

Plain-English glossary

Missense variant — A single DNA change that swaps one amino acid for another in the resulting protein.（中文:DNA中的一个单碱基改变,导致翻译出的蛋白质中某一个氨基酸被替换成另一个。）

CFTR modulator / ETI — A small-molecule drug that binds directly to the CFTR protein to correct its folding or function; elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor (ETI) is the current three-drug standard-of-care combination.（中文:直接结合CFTR蛋白以纠正其折叠或功能的小分子药物;elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor(ETI)是目前由三种药物组成的标准治疗方案。）

Binding pocket — A specific 3D cavity on a protein's surface shaped to fit a particular drug molecule.（中文:蛋白质表面一个专门为容纳某种药物分子而成型的三维空腔。）

TMD / NBD1 / NBD2 — CFTR's transmembrane domains (TMDs), which form the chloride channel, and nucleotide-binding domains (NBD1, NBD2), which power the channel's gating; most modulator binding sites sit at the interfaces between these domains.（中文:CFTR的跨膜结构域(TMD)构成氯离子通道,核苷酸结合结构域(NBD1、NBD2)为通道开闭提供动力;大多数调节剂结合位点位于这些结构域的交界处。）

Boltz-2 (co-folding model) — A deep-learning model that, given a protein sequence and a drug's chemical structure, jointly predicts the 3D protein-drug complex and a binding-affinity score in one pass, without a separate docking step.（中文:一种深度学习模型,输入蛋白质序列和药物化学结构后,一次推理即可同时预测三维蛋白-药物复合物结构与结合亲和力评分,无需单独的对接步骤。）

pIC50 — The negative log of IC50 (the drug concentration needed to cut a target's activity in half); a standard potency scale where a bigger number means tighter binding.（中文:IC50(将靶点活性抑制一半所需药物浓度)的负对数值,是常用的效价量表,数值越大代表结合越紧密。）

SMILES string — A compact text notation for representing a molecule's chemical structure, used here as the drug-input format for Boltz-2.（中文:一种紧凑的分子化学结构文本表示法,在本文中作为Boltz-2的药物输入格式。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Boltz-2 — open-source structure-and-affinity co-folding deep learning model, the computational core of the pipeline.
  • SIFT / PolyPhen-2 / AlphaMissense — established variant-effect predictors used to flag likely-damaging variants for prioritization before screening.
  • PLIP (Protein-Ligand Interaction Profiler) — used for downstream structural interpretation of predicted binding interfaces.
  • OpenMM-based refinement — optional post-prediction step to resolve local steric clashes in predicted complexes.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation priority. Buildable: cftr-eti-boltz-screen (M) — generate WT/mutant CFTR structures via Boltz-2 for missense VUS near TMD/NBD1 modulator-binding interfaces, dock elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor against each, rank by predicted binding-affinity shift as a functional-assay triage list.

What to verify first

  • How the method performs — or whether it's even appropriate — for a distal/allosteric CFTR VUS, since every validated mutation in this paper sat inside or next to the binding pocket.
  • Whether training-data overlap (the affinity module trained on public databases dominated by wild-type measurements) inflates apparent performance more or less for a well-studied protein like CFTR than for the four benchmark targets.
  • Whether a static wild-type-vs-mutant structural comparison misses gating-state-dependent pharmacology, given the paper's own caveat that ion-channel drug binding is state-dependent and CFTR has its own gating cycle.

JORDAN And the tool in this paper, Boltz-2 — what is it actually doing? Boltz-2是这篇论文中使用的工具——它到底在做什么?

ALEX Boltz-2 is a deep-learning "co-folding" model — give it a protein sequence and a drug's chemical structure, and in one pass it jointly predicts the 3D protein-drug complex and estimates a binding-affinity score, without a separate docking step. Boltz-2是一种深度学习"共折叠"(co-folding)模型——输入蛋白质序列和药物的化学结构,它在一次推理中就能同时预测三维蛋白-药物复合物结构,并给出结合亲和力评分,不需要单独的对接(docking)步骤。 That score is usually reported as pIC50 — the negative log of the IC50, the drug concentration needed to cut the target's activity in half — so a bigger pIC50 means tighter binding. 这个评分通常以pIC50表示——即IC50(将靶点活性抑制一半所需药物浓度)的负对数值——因此pIC50越大代表结合越紧密。

ALEX Mechanically, BoltzOmics wraps Boltz-2 in an orchestration layer built for variant screening rather than one-off predictions. 从机制上讲,BoltzOmics把Boltz-2包装进了一个为变异筛选而不是单次预测而设计的编排层(orchestration layer)。 You give it a wild-type sequence, and it can query databases for known variants and flag likely-damaging ones using established variant-effect predictors — SIFT, PolyPhen-2, and AlphaMissense, tools that score how disruptive an amino-acid substitution looks based on evolutionary conservation and structural context — or you can just hand it your own variant list directly, which is the path a CFTR use case would take since CFTR's VUS are already catalogued elsewhere. 你输入一个野生型序列,它就可以查询数据库获取已知变异,并用已有的变异效应预测工具——SIFT、PolyPhen-2和AlphaMissense,这些工具根据进化保守性和结构背景来评估某个氨基酸替换的破坏性——标记出可能有害的变异,或者你也可以直接提供自己的变异列表,这正是CFTR应用场景会采用的路径,因为CFTR的VUS已经在别处编目过了。 You also supply drug compounds as SMILES strings, a compact text notation for molecular structure. 你还需要以SMILES字符串的形式提供候选药物化合物,这是一种紧凑的分子结构文本表示法。 Boltz-2 then predicts wild-type and mutant protein structures, docks each drug into each variant, and estimates a pIC50 for every protein-drug pair — and critically, the tool caches shared intermediate computations like sequence alignments across related runs so a batch screen of many variants against many drugs doesn't redo the expensive parts from scratch. 然后Boltz-2会预测野生型和突变体的蛋白质结构,把每种药物对接到每个变异体上,并为每对蛋白-药物组合估算一个pIC50值——关键的是,该工具会在相关运行之间缓存共享的中间计算结果(比如序列比对),这样对多个变异和多种药物进行批量筛选时,就不必每次都重新计算这些昂贵的步骤。
