Show notes — Track A — Complete CFTR gene sequencing in 5,058 individuals with cystic fibrosis (Raraigh et al., 2022)

Blurb. Raraigh, Cutting, and colleagues applied whole genome sequencing to 5,058 people with CF and focused on the complete CFTR gene — every exon, intron, regulatory edge, and large rearrangement — interpreting variants in three phases (known/structural → novel loss-of-function → remaining) under CFTR2 or ACMG rules. Complete sequencing pushed the fraction of individuals with both causative variants identified from 95.8% (registry/chart-reported) to 98.1%, delineated 18 structural variants (2 novel), surfaced 16 previously unreported variants among 371 interpreted, and found additional uncertain-effect variants riding on solved alleles (incl. 76 F508del homozygotes). It lands on the Karen Raraigh variant-interpretation/harmonization collaboration; the buildable artifact is a CFTR genotype-completeness auditor that ranks incompletely-genotyped individuals by the most probable missing-variant class. NOTE: read abstract-only (not open access) — paper specifics beyond the structured abstract were not verified on-air.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP criteria — standardized American College of Medical Genetics & Genomics framework that classifies a variant from pathogenic to benign using evidence codes.（中文：ACMG 是把变异从致病到良性分级的通用标准化框架。）
  • CFTR2 — CF-specific variant database that assigns disease liability (CF-causing / varying consequence / non-CF-causing) by combining clinical and functional evidence.（中文：CFTR2 是结合临床与功能证据给出 CF 致病性判断的 CF 专属变异库。）
  • Complex allele — two or more sequence changes carried on the same chromosome copy (in cis), interpreted jointly.（中文：复杂等位基因 — 位于同一条染色体拷贝上、需联合解读的两个及以上变异。）
  • Exon / Intron — exons are the protein-coding segments of a gene; introns are the intervening non-coding segments removed by splicing.（中文：外显子是编码蛋白的片段，内含子是会被剪接掉的非编码片段。）
  • F508del homozygote — a person carrying the most common CF-causing variant (F508del) on both CFTR copies.（中文：两条 CFTR 都带最常见 F508del 变异的个体。）
  • gnomAD — large reference database of population allele frequencies used to gauge how common a variant is.（中文：gnomAD — 用于判断变异在人群中常见程度的大规模等位基因频率库。）
  • HGVS — standardized rules for writing a variant relative to a named reference sequence.（中文：HGVS — 相对于指定参考序列书写变异的标准化命名规范。）
  • Loss-of-function variant — a change expected to abolish protein function (premature stop, frameshift, canonical splice-site disruption).（中文：预期彻底破坏蛋白功能的变异，如提前终止、移码、经典剪接位点破坏。）
  • Missense variant — a single-base change that swaps one amino acid for another.（中文：错义变异 — 使一个氨基酸被替换的单碱基改变。）
  • Splice site — the precise intron boundary where splicing removes an intron; disruption can mis-process the transcript.（中文：剪接位点 — 内含子被切除的精确边界，破坏会导致转录本错误加工。）
  • Structural variant — a large insertion or deletion (e.g. whole exon[s]) rather than a single-letter change.（中文：结构变异 — 大片段的插入或缺失，而非单字母改变。）
  • Whole genome sequencing (WGS) — sequencing the entire genome, enabling detection of novel and structural variants a coding panel cannot reach.（中文：全基因组测序 — 测整个基因组，可发现编码 panel 触及不到的新变异和结构变异。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Three-phase variant interpretation — catalog-match known + structural variants → discover novel loss-of-function → investigate the remaining residue; an efficient triage pattern for any large-cohort variant-resolution effort.
  • CFTR2 / ACMG dual interpretation rules — apply a disease-specific database call where available and fall back to a general evidence framework otherwise.
  • Full-gene focus on WGS — restrict an expensive genome-wide assay to one gene's complete sequence (incl. introns, regulatory edges, structural events) to recover variants panels miss.
  • Solve-rate as the headline metric — fraction of individuals with both causative alleles resolved, benchmarked against the pre-existing registry/chart genotype.

Input: public per-individual variant table + CFTR2 + gnomAD. Difficulty: S–M (bottleneck = HGVS normalization; complex & large-deletion alleles fail silently). What to verify first

  • Structural-variant sensitivity of short-read WGS — anything it misses never enters the 98.1% solve rate; measure SV recall before trusting completeness.
  • Transcript/assembly reference for every HGVS string — fix one reference and measure the HGVS round-trip rate before trusting the normalized table.
  • "Previously unreported" adjudication — re-check the 16 novel calls against current ClinVar/gnomAD snapshots ("unreported in 2021" is a moving target).
  • No registry-internals claim — this uses externally published methods and public variant resources only.

ALEX Good morning. 早上好。Today we are reading Raraigh and colleagues, "Complete CFTR gene sequencing in 5,058 individuals with cystic fibrosis informs variant-specific treatment," published in the Journal of Cystic Fibrosis in 2022. 今天我们读的是 Raraigh 等人的论文《对 5,058 名囊性纤维化患者进行完整的 CFTR 基因测序，为变异特异性治疗提供依据》，2022 年发表在《Journal of Cystic Fibrosis》。The senior author is Garry Cutting, and the first author is Karen Raraigh — yes, that Karen Raraigh. 通讯作者是 Garry Cutting，第一作者是 Karen Raraigh——没错，就是那位 Karen Raraigh。We are reading it in Track A, variant interpretation and harmonization, because it is, in effect, the field's reference description of what a complete CFTR genotype looks like at population scale. 我们把它放在 A 轨道——变异解读与标准化——因为它实际上就是这个领域对"群体规模下完整的 CFTR 基因型长什么样"的参考性描述。One honest flag up front: this paper is not open access, so we are working from the structured abstract only, and I will say "the abstract reports" rather than pretend we read the figures. 先老实说明一点：这篇论文不是开放获取的，所以我们只能依据结构化摘要来讲，我会说"摘要报告了"，而不会假装我们读过里面的图表。

JORDAN Let me ground myself first. 我先把基础搞清楚。When you say "complete CFTR gene sequencing," what is the contrast — complete versus what? 你说的"完整的 CFTR 基因测序"，是相对于什么而言的"完整"？

ALEX Great question, and it is the whole point. 好问题，而这正是关键所在。Most clinical CF genetic testing historically looked only at the coding regions of CFTR — the exons that become protein — plus a short list of well-known intronic variants. 历史上大多数临床 CF 基因检测只看 CFTR 的编码区——也就是会翻译成蛋白质的外显子（exon，基因中编码蛋白的片段）——再加上一小串已知的内含子变异。Here they used whole genome sequencing and then focused on the full CFTR gene — every exon, every intron, the regulatory edges, and large rearrangements. 这里他们用的是全基因组测序（whole genome sequencing），然后聚焦于完整的 CFTR 基因——每一个外显子、每一个内含子（intron，基因中不编码蛋白、会被剪接掉的片段）、调控边缘区，以及大片段重排。The key contrast is that panel testing can only find variants it is designed to look for, while complete sequencing can also surface novel and structural variants. 核心对比在于：panel（靶向检测套餐）只能找到它被设计去找的变异，而完整测序还能发现新的变异和结构变异。

JORDAN And a "structural variant" — that is bigger than a single-letter change? 那"结构变异"——是比单个碱基改变更大的那种？

ALEX Right. 对。A structural variant here means a large insertion or deletion — losing or gaining a whole chunk of the gene, sometimes a whole exon or several. 这里的结构变异（structural variant）指的是大片段的插入或缺失——丢失或获得基因的一整块，有时是一整个外显子，甚至好几个。Those are exactly the variants a short coding panel tends to miss, because the missing piece does not show up as a misspelled letter — it shows up as an absence. 这些恰恰是短的编码 panel 最容易漏掉的变异，因为缺失的那块不会表现为一个拼错的字母——而是表现为"缺了一块"。

JORDAN And a "loss-of-function variant"? 那"功能缺失型变异"呢？

ALEX A loss-of-function variant is one that we expect to abolish the protein's function — a premature stop, a frameshift, a canonical splice-site disruption. 功能缺失型变异（loss-of-function variant）是指我们预期会彻底破坏蛋白功能的变异——比如提前终止、移码、或经典剪接位点（splice site，内含子被切除的精确位置）被破坏。In a recessive disease like CF, a person needs two such damaging variants — one on each copy of the gene — to be affected. 在像 CF 这样的隐性遗传病中，一个人需要有两个这样的致病变异——基因的两条拷贝各一个——才会发病。

JORDAN One more primer question — why does the same variant need "harmonizing" at all? 再问一个基础问题——同一个变异为什么还需要"标准化"？

ALEX Because a variant has a physical identity but several legal ways to be written. 因为一个变异有一个物理上的身份，却有好几种合法的书写方式。The same deletion can be named relative to different CFTR transcripts or different genome assemblies, and labs and registries do not always pick the same one. 同一个缺失，可以相对于不同的 CFTR 转录本或不同的基因组版本来命名，而各实验室和登记库并不总是选同一个。So before you can even ask "do two sources agree about this variant's significance," you first have to prove they are talking about the same variant. 所以在你能问"两个来源对这个变异的意义是否一致"之前，你得先证明它们说的是同一个变异。That is the unglamorous core of harmonization, and it is exactly the layer this paper's complete-genotype catalog feeds. 这就是标准化里不起眼的核心，而这恰恰是这篇论文的完整基因型目录所喂养的那一层。

JORDAN So what problem motivated doing this on five thousand people? 那么，是什么问题促使他们在五千人身上做这件事？

ALEX The clinical driver is variant-specific therapy. 临床上的驱动力是变异特异性治疗。CF treatment has shifted from managing symptoms to small-molecule modulators that target the defective CFTR protein, and crucially, eligibility depends on knowing the exact variant a person carries. CF 的治疗已经从对症管理转向了针对缺陷 CFTR 蛋白的小分子调节剂（modulator），而关键在于，是否有资格用药取决于知道一个人到底携带哪个变异。If a clinically affected person does not have two identified causal variants, the abstract notes they typically get no further interrogation of CFTR beyond the coding regions. 如果一个临床上已确诊的患者没有被找到两个致病变异，摘要指出，他们通常不会对 CFTR 做编码区以外的进一步排查。That is the gap: a real patient, an unambiguous diagnosis, but an incomplete genotype on paper — and therefore an uncertain path to a targeted drug. 这就是缺口：一个真实的患者、一个明确的诊断，但纸面上的基因型却不完整——因此通往靶向药物的路径也不确定。

JORDAN So "we know they have CF, we just cannot fully explain it genetically yet." 也就是"我们知道他们患有 CF，只是还无法在基因层面完全解释"。

ALEX Exactly, and that unexplained fraction is where the value of complete sequencing concentrates. 正是如此，而那一部分无法解释的病例，恰恰是完整测序价值最集中的地方。Before this kind of study, the field did not have a clean population-scale estimate of how much of that gap deep CFTR sequencing could actually close. 在这类研究之前，这个领域并没有一个干净的、群体规模的估计，来回答"深度 CFTR 测序到底能填补多少这个缺口"。And it is contested in a quiet way: how much of the residual is rare missense, how much is structural, how much is deep-intronic — nobody had laid it out at this scale with consistent interpretation rules. 而且这里有一种安静的争议：剩余未解释的部分里，有多少是罕见错义变异，多少是结构变异，多少是深内含子变异——没人在这个规模上用一致的解读规则把它摊开过。

JORDAN Walk me through how they actually did it. 带我过一遍他们具体是怎么做的。

ALEX Whole genome sequences were analyzed on 5,058 individuals with CF, and they then concentrated on the full CFTR gene sequence. 他们对 5,058 名 CF 患者做了全基因组测序，然后把分析集中在完整的 CFTR 基因序列上。The interpretation ran in three phases, and I think the phasing is the smart design move. 解读分三个阶段进行，我认为这种分阶段设计是个聪明的做法。Phase one screened for known variants and structural variants — the things you can match against an existing catalog. 第一阶段筛查已知变异和结构变异——也就是那些可以和现有目录比对上的东西。Phase two was discovery of novel loss-of-function variants — new premature stops, frameshifts, canonical splice disruptions not seen before. 第二阶段是发现新的功能缺失型变异——以前没见过的提前终止、移码、经典剪接破坏。Phase three investigated the remaining variants, the harder residue that does not fall into either clean bucket. 第三阶段排查剩下的变异，也就是那些不能干净归入前两类的、更难处理的残余。Every variant from the first two phases, plus coding-region variants from the third, was interpreted according to CFTR2 or ACMG criteria. 前两个阶段的所有变异，加上第三阶段的编码区变异，都按照 CFTR2 或 ACMG 标准来解读。

JORDAN Define those two for me — CFTR2 and ACMG. 帮我定义这两个——CFTR2 和 ACMG。

ALEX CFTR2 is the CF-specific variant database that classifies a variant by combining clinical and functional evidence into a disease-liability call — CF-causing, of varying consequence, or non-CF-causing. CFTR2 是 CF 专属的变异数据库，它把临床证据和功能证据结合起来，给出一个疾病致病性判断——致 CF、后果可变、或不致 CF。ACMG, here, is the general American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics framework for classifying any variant from pathogenic to benign using standardized evidence codes. 这里的 ACMG 指的是美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会（American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics）的通用框架，用标准化的证据代码把任何变异从致病到良性分级。The pushback I would raise on the design is the phased, catalog-first approach: it is efficient, but it can bias what counts as "novel," because anything the early phases match gets resolved before the hard third phase even runs. 我会对这个设计提出的质疑是它"先比目录"的分阶段思路：它很高效，但可能会让"什么算新变异"产生偏倚，因为凡是早期阶段能匹配上的，在最难的第三阶段开始之前就已经被处理掉了。And short-read whole genome sequencing has real limits calling large structural variants and anything in repetitive sequence, so the structural-variant count is a floor, not a ceiling. 而且短读长全基因组测序在判定大的结构变异、以及任何重复序列区域的变异时有实打实的局限，所以结构变异的计数是个下限，而不是上限。

JORDAN Okay — did it deliver? 好——那它有成果吗？

ALEX The headline number is solve rate. 最核心的数字是"解决率"。Both causative variants were identified in 98.1 percent of subjects — 4,960 of 5,058 — compared with 95.8 percent, 4,847 people, who had a registry- or chart-reported disease-causing genotype going in. 在 98.1% 的受试者中找到了两个致病变异——5,058 人中的 4,960 人——而一开始有登记或病历报告的致病基因型的人是 95.8%，即 4,847 人。That is a 2.3 percentage-point gain, which sounds modest until you remember it is 113 additional people moved from "incompletely explained" to "fully genotyped." 这是 2.3 个百分点的提升，听起来不大，但别忘了那意味着有 113 个人从"未完全解释"变成了"完整基因型确定"。Across the whole effort they interpreted 371 variants, of which 16 — 4.3 percent — were previously unreported. 在整个工作中，他们解读了 371 个变异，其中 16 个——4.3%——是以前从未报告过的。

JORDAN And the structural variants you flagged earlier? 还有你前面提到的结构变异呢？

ALEX Full-gene sequencing delineated 18 structural variants — large insertions or deletions — of which two were novel. 完整基因测序界定了 18 个结构变异——大片段插入或缺失——其中两个是新发现的。Those are precisely the variants a coding panel would have reported as a blank, so finding eighteen of them is the clearest illustration of why "complete" matters. 这些恰恰是编码 panel 会报告为空白的变异，所以找到十八个，正是"完整"为何重要的最清楚的例证。They also found additional CFTR variants of uncertain effect in 76 F508del homozygotes and in 21 individuals with other combinations of CF-causing variants. 他们还在 76 名 F508del 纯合子（F508del homozygote，两条 CFTR 都带最常见的 F508del 变异）和 21 名携带其他致病变异组合的个体中，发现了额外的、效应不确定的 CFTR 变异。And of the 98 individuals still unsolved, the abstract reports that 78 carried one identified variant — meaning the remaining gap is mostly "one allele found, one still hiding," not "nothing found." 在仍未解决的 98 人中，摘要报告有 78 人携带一个已识别的变异——也就是说，剩下的缺口大多是"找到一个等位基因，另一个还藏着"，而不是"什么都没找到"。

JORDAN I want to slow down on that finding of uncertain-effect variants in F508del homozygotes — why is that interesting if they are already explained? 我想在"F508del 纯合子里发现效应不确定的变异"这个结果上慢一点——如果他们的基因型已经被解释清楚了，这为什么还值得关注？

ALEX Because "explained" and "fully characterized" are not the same thing. 因为"已解释"和"已完全刻画"不是一回事。An F508del homozygote has two copies of the most common CF-causing variant, so the diagnosis is settled — but the abstract reports they still found additional CFTR variants of uncertain effect riding along on those alleles. 一个 F508del 纯合子带有两份最常见的致 CF 变异，所以诊断是确定的——但摘要报告说，他们仍在这些等位基因上发现了一同存在的、效应不确定的额外 CFTR 变异。Those extra changes can sit in cis — on the same chromosome copy as F508del — and form what geneticists call a complex allele. 这些额外的改变可能位于顺式（in cis，指在同一条染色体拷贝上）——和 F508del 在同一条染色体上——构成遗传学家所说的复杂等位基因（complex allele）。And complex alleles matter clinically because a second change on the same allele can modify how the protein behaves and, potentially, how it responds to a modulator. 而复杂等位基因在临床上很重要，因为同一等位基因上的第二个改变可能改变蛋白的行为，并可能改变它对调节剂的反应。

JORDAN So even a "solved" genotype can hide information relevant to drug response? 所以即便是一个"已解决"的基因型，也可能藏着与药物反应相关的信息？

ALEX Exactly, and that is the deeper reason complete sequencing is not just about the unsolved 2 percent. 正是如此，而这正是完整测序不只是为了那未解决的 2% 的更深层原因。It also re-reads the supposedly-solved majority and occasionally finds something that re-frames the allele. 它还会重新读取那些被认为已解决的大多数，偶尔会发现一些重新定义这个等位基因的东西。The honest caveat is that "uncertain effect" means exactly that — these are not yet actionable, they are flags for follow-up functional or clinical work. 老实的告诫是"效应不确定"就是字面意思——这些目前还不可据以行动，它们只是需要后续功能或临床研究的标记。But for a harmonization project, those flags are gold, because they are a curated list of where the public picture of an allele is incomplete. 但对一个标准化项目来说，这些标记是金矿，因为它们是一份"某个等位基因的公开图景在哪里不完整"的精选清单。

JORDAN Where does this land for what you are actually working on? 那这对你正在做的事情落到哪里？
