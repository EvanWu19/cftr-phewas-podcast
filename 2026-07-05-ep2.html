Show notes — Track B — LLM-driven sepsis knowledge graph (2026-07-05)

Blurb

Yang et al. (JMIR 2025) build a sepsis knowledge graph by pointing GPT-4 at multicenter real-world data — 10,544 deidentified sepsis patients from three Western China hospitals, plus PubMed guidelines and a biomarker database. Instead of a hand-labeled supervised NER pipeline, they use zero/few-shot prompt engineering to extract subject–relation–object triples across nine entity types and eight relation types, fuse entities to UMLS via scispaCy (0.85 similarity threshold + manual review), and load everything into a Neo4j graph queryable through py2neo. GPT-4 few-shot scored F 76.76 on their in-house sepsis set (vs Qwen2 43.77, Llama3 48.39) and F 65.42 on public CMeEE (edging BERT-CRF 62.11, Med-BERT 60.66). A GraphRAG layer on top gives multi-hop clinical decision support, scored with BLEU/ROUGE. Honest about hallucinations (e.g. "sepsis caused by exercise"). Where it lands: the AI-tooling / career priority. Buildable artifact: a CF-literature knowledge-graph builder that turns modifier-gene abstracts into a queryable gene–phenotype graph — a machine-readable backend for the Track C modifier catalog.

Plain-English glossary

  • Entity linking / knowledge fusion — mapping surface forms ("HF", "Heart Failure", synonyms) to one canonical concept with a stable ID.（中文：实体链接／知识融合，把不同写法映射到同一个带稳定 ID 的规范概念。）
  • GraphRAG — retrieval-augmented generation whose retrieval walks a knowledge graph (multi-hop paths) instead of a flat document store.（中文：GraphRAG，检索增强生成，其检索在知识图谱上做多跳游走，而非在扁平文档库中检索。）
  • HGNC — the authority that assigns official human gene symbols (e.g. SLC26A9).（中文：HGNC，负责分配官方人类基因符号的机构，例如 SLC26A9。）
  • Knowledge graph — facts stored as typed nodes (entities) and edges (relationships) you can traverse.（中文：知识图谱，把事实存成带类型的节点（实体）和边（关系），可沿关系遍历。）
  • NER (named entity recognition) — tagging text spans as diseases, drugs, labs, etc.（中文：NER，命名实体识别，把文本片段标注为疾病、药物、化验等。）
  • Relation extraction — deciding what relationship connects two entities, yielding a subject–relation–object triple.（中文：关系抽取，判断两个实体间的关系，产出"主语—关系—宾语"三元组。）
  • UMLS — the Unified Medical Language System, a master vocabulary giving each concept a stable ID (e.g. hypertension = C0020538).（中文：UMLS，统一医学语言系统，为每个概念赋予稳定 ID，如高血压 = C0020538。）
  • Zero-shot / few-shot — prompting with no examples vs a handful of worked examples pasted into the prompt.（中文：零样本／少样本，提示时不给示例，或在提示里贴入少量做好的示例。）

Full citation

Yang H, Li J, Zhang C, Sierra AP, Shen B. Large Language Model-Driven Knowledge Graph Construction in Sepsis Care Using Multicenter Clinical Databases: Development and Usability Study. J Med Internet Res. 2025;27:e65537. PMID: 40146985 · PMC: PMC11986385 · DOI: 10.2196/65537

Methods / tools to lift

  • Few-shot triple-extraction prompt — give GPT-4 the head-type list, tail-type list, and relation list; ask for (subject, relation, object) triples; paste 2 worked examples.
  • Dual schema design — top-down (experts predefine entity/relation types) + bottom-up (mine entities from data, fold back).
  • scispaCy entity-linker → UMLS (en_core_sci_sm) — standardize casing/punctuation, then word-embedding + Jaccard similarity, threshold 0.85, manual review below.
  • Neo4j + py2neo — graph storage populated via the OpenAI API.
  • GraphRAG decision-support layer — retrieval walks the graph for multi-hop answers; evaluate with BLEU + ROUGE-1/2/L vs clinician gold.
  • Biopython PubMed pull — automate guideline/literature ingestion.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: AI-tooling / career.

Paste-ready MVP: A script that ingests a folder of CF genetic-modifier abstracts (+ PMIDs), runs a few-shot LLM prompt to extract gene–phenotype–direction triples, links gene symbols to HGNC and phenotypes to UMLS, and loads them into a small Neo4j graph queryable for "which genes modify CF lung disease, and in which direction" — with a provenance edge back to source sentence + PMID for every fact. Difficulty: M (extraction is easy; entity-linking + gold-standard eval are the real work).

What to verify first

  • Evaluation floor — a single-annotator, 61-record gold standard is weak; require ≥2 annotators + inter-rater agreement before trusting F-scores.
  • Fact-level hallucination control — every edge needs provenance (source sentence + PMID); false edges (their "sepsis caused by exercise") must be catchable in review.
  • Entity-linking precision — 0.85 fuzzy similarity mis-merges/splits concepts; for gene symbols use authoritative HGNC mapping, not string similarity.
  • Scope discipline — literature-only; never registry genotype internals. Any move toward registry data is a separate, governed decision.

ALEX Welcome back. 欢迎回来。 Today's Track B paper is "Large Language Model-Driven Knowledge Graph Construction in Sepsis Care Using Multicenter Clinical Databases," by Hao Yang, Jiaxi Li, Chi Zhang, Alejandro Pazos Sierra, and Bairong Shen, published in the Journal of Medical Internet Research in 2025. 今天 Track B（AI 工具专题）的论文是《用多中心临床数据库以大语言模型驱动构建脓毒症知识图谱》，作者是 Hao Yang、Jiaxi Li、Chi Zhang、Alejandro Pazos Sierra 和 Bairong Shen，2025 年发表在《Journal of Medical Internet Research》上。 We read it because it ships a concrete, reproducible pipeline: take messy multicenter clinical text, run GPT-4 with prompt engineering to pull out entities and relations, and load the result into a Neo4j graph you can actually query. 我们读它是因为它交付了一条具体、可复现的流水线：拿到杂乱的多中心临床文本，用带提示工程的 GPT-4 抽取实体和关系，再把结果装载进一个你真的能查询的 Neo4j 图数据库。 That recipe is the AI-tooling / career priority, mapped onto registry data. 这套配方就是"AI 工具 / 职业发展"优先事项，映射到注册登记数据上。

JORDAN Let me get the vocabulary first, because this is a genomics-and-clinical audience. 先把词汇讲清楚，因为我们的听众来自基因组学和临床。 What is a knowledge graph, in the medical sense? 在医学语境里，什么是知识图谱（knowledge graph）？

ALEX A knowledge graph stores facts as a network of nodes and edges rather than as rows in a table. 知识图谱把事实存成一个由节点和边构成的网络，而不是表格里的行。 Each node is an entity — a disease, a symptom, a biomarker, a drug — and each edge is a typed relationship, like "has symptom" or "recommended medication." 每个节点是一个实体——疾病、症状、生物标志物、药物——每条边是一种带类型的关系，比如"具有症状"或"推荐用药"。 The payoff is that you can traverse relationships: from a disease, hop to its complications, then to the drugs that treat those. 好处是你可以沿关系遍历：从一个疾病，跳到它的并发症，再跳到治疗这些并发症的药物。 The second term is entity recognition, often called NER, named entity recognition. 第二个术语是实体识别，常称 NER（named entity recognition，命名实体识别）。 That's the step that reads free text and tags the spans that are diseases, drugs, or labs. 那是读取自由文本、标注出哪些片段是疾病、药物或化验的步骤。 The third is relation extraction: given two entities in a sentence, decide what relationship, if any, connects them, producing a subject–relation–object triple. 第三个是关系抽取：给定一句话里的两个实体，判断它们之间是什么关系（如果有的话），产出一个"主语—关系—宾语"的三元组。 The fourth is knowledge fusion or entity linking: mapping "Heart Failure" and "HF" and any Chinese synonym to one canonical concept. 第四个是知识融合，或叫实体链接：把 "Heart Failure"、"HF" 以及任何中文同义词映射到同一个规范概念上。 Here they link everything to UMLS, the Unified Medical Language System, a master vocabulary that gives each concept a stable ID like C0020538 for hypertension. 这里他们把一切都链接到 UMLS（Unified Medical Language System，统一医学语言系统），一个主词表，为每个概念赋予一个稳定的 ID，比如高血压是 C0020538。 That linking step is what turns three hospitals' idiosyncratic wording into one coherent graph. 正是这个链接步骤，把三家医院各行其是的措辞变成了一张连贯的图。

JORDAN Why do they even need an LLM for this? 他们为什么非得用大语言模型来做这个？ Haven't people been building medical NER for years? 医学 NER 不是已经做了很多年了吗？

ALEX They have, and that's exactly the pain point. 是的，而这恰恰是痛点所在。 The classical route is a supervised pipeline: you hand-label thousands of clinical sentences, design a neural network, and train it, which is slow, expensive, and brittle when the vocabulary shifts. 经典路线是有监督流水线：你手工标注数千条临床句子，设计一个神经网络，再训练它——这既慢又贵，而且一旦词汇发生变化就很脆弱。 Sepsis makes it worse because it's heterogeneous and buried in unstructured notes: 49 million cases a year worldwide, mortality between 15 and 25 percent, and survivors with long-tail complications. 脓毒症让情况更糟，因为它异质性强、又埋在非结构化病历里：全球每年 4900 万例，病死率在 15% 到 25% 之间，幸存者还有长尾并发症。 The bet is that a general LLM, prompted well, can do entity and relation extraction with zero or few labeled examples, skipping the annotation bottleneck. 他们押注的是：一个通用大语言模型，只要提示得当，就能在零个或极少标注样例下完成实体和关系抽取，绕过标注瓶颈。 Zero-shot means you describe the task in the prompt and give no examples; few-shot means you paste in a handful of worked examples inside the prompt. 零样本（zero-shot）意味着你只在提示里描述任务、不给样例；少样本（few-shot）意味着你在提示里贴入少量做好的示例。 The scientific question is whether that actually beats a purpose-trained BERT model on real clinical text, or whether it just looks convenient. 科学问题在于：这到底能不能在真实临床文本上打败一个专门训练的 BERT 模型，还是只是看起来方便。

JORDAN One thing I want to flag before the build: this is patient data from real hospitals. 在讲搭建之前我想提一点：这是来自真实医院的患者数据。 How do they handle sending that to a commercial model like GPT-4? 他们是怎么处理把这些数据发给像 GPT-4 这样的商业模型的？

ALEX They deidentify every record before it touches GPT-4, stripping names, addresses, and patient IDs following standard protocols. 他们在每条记录接触 GPT-4 之前都做去标识化，按标准流程去掉姓名、地址和患者 ID。 They also got ethics-committee approval and relied on the original informed consent for secondary analysis. 他们也获得了伦理委员会批准，并依据原始知情同意进行二次分析。 But they're honest that using a commercial API still carries data-leakage risk, and their stated future plan is to deploy local open models like Llama 3.1 and Qwen 2 inside a controlled environment. 但他们坦承，使用商业 API 仍带有数据泄露风险，他们明确的未来计划是在受控环境内部署 Llama 3.1 和 Qwen 2 等本地开源模型。 That's a design point worth carrying: for any registry-adjacent work, the extraction model living inside your own walls matters as much as its accuracy. 这是一个值得铭记的设计点：对于任何与注册登记相邻的工作，抽取模型是否运行在你自己的围墙之内，与它的准确性同等重要。 It's also why I'd point a CF version at published abstracts first — literature is public, so the privacy surface is essentially zero. 这也是为什么我会先把 CF 版本指向已发表的摘要——文献是公开的，所以隐私暴露面基本为零。

JORDAN That makes sense — prove the pipeline on public text where nothing can leak. 有道理——先在没有任何东西会泄露的公开文本上验证流水线。 So walk me through what they actually built and ran. 那带我过一遍他们实际搭建并运行了什么。

ALEX The data is real-world: 10,544 sepsis patients from three hospitals in Western China between 2020 and 2023, 8,497 from West China Hospital plus two smaller sites. 数据是真实世界的：2020 到 2023 年间来自中国西部三家医院的 10,544 名脓毒症患者，其中 8,497 名来自华西医院，另加两个较小的院区。 They enrich it with sepsis guidelines pulled from PubMed via Biopython, plus a sepsis-biomarker database. 他们还用通过 Biopython 从 PubMed 抓取的脓毒症指南，以及一个脓毒症生物标志物数据库来丰富数据。 The schema is defined two ways at once: top-down, where experts predefine the entity concepts and relationship types, and bottom-up, where entities are mined from the data and folded back in. 模式（schema）是同时用两种方式定义的：自上而下，由专家预先定义实体概念和关系类型；自下而上，从数据中挖掘实体再折返纳入。 The core prompt tells GPT-4: here is a list of head-entity types, tail-entity types, and relations; extract triples from this sentence. 核心提示告诉 GPT-4：这是头实体类型、尾实体类型和关系的清单；从这句话里抽取三元组。 The few-shot version pastes in two fully worked examples with their triples. 少样本版本贴入了两个完整做好的示例及其三元组。 For fusion they use scispaCy's entity-linker against UMLS, standardize casing and punctuation, then match with word-embedding and Jaccard similarity above a 0.85 threshold, with manual review below it. 在融合环节，他们用 scispaCy 的实体链接器对接 UMLS，统一大小写和标点，再用词嵌入和 Jaccard 相似度以 0.85 为阈值匹配，低于阈值的做人工复核。 Storage is Neo4j, the graph database, populated through py2neo plus the OpenAI API. 存储用的是图数据库 Neo4j，通过 py2neo 加 OpenAI API 填充。 One design choice I'd push on hard: their gold standard is thin. 有一个设计选择我要重点追问：他们的金标准很薄。 The sepsis evaluation set was annotated by a single medical expert on just 61 patients' records, so the headline F-scores rest on a small, single-annotator reference. 脓毒症评估集只由一名医学专家在区区 61 名患者的病历上标注，所以那些抢眼的 F 分数建立在一个小规模、单标注者的参照之上。

JORDAN Before the numbers, help me picture the nine entity types and eight relations. 在讲数字之前，帮我想象那九类实体和八类关系。 Is that schema arbitrary, or does it come from somewhere? 那个模式是随意定的，还是有出处？

ALEX It's a deliberate clinical ontology, not arbitrary. 它是一个刻意设计的临床本体，而非随意的。 The nine entities are diseases, symptoms, biomarkers, imaging exams, lab tests, subtypes, pathogenic mechanism, pharmacotherapy, and surgery — the natural nouns of a clinical narrative. 九类实体是疾病、症状、生物标志物、影像检查、化验、亚型、致病机制、药物治疗和手术——临床叙事中自然的名词。 The eight relations are the verbs that connect them: has-symptom, complications, recommended medication, recommended imaging, recommended lab tests, related biomarkers, caused-by, and treat. 八类关系是连接它们的动词：具有症状、并发症、推荐用药、推荐影像、推荐化验、相关生物标志物、由……引起、以及治疗。 Each entity also carries attributes, so a biomarker node stores its cutoff value, sensitivity, and specificity, not just a name. 每类实体还带属性，所以一个生物标志物节点存储的是它的临界值、敏感性和特异性，而不只是一个名字。 For a CF-modifier version, you'd swap this ontology wholesale: the entities become gene, variant, phenotype, and cohort, and the relations become modifies, increases-risk-of, decreases-risk-of, and measured-in. 对一个 CF 修饰基因版本，你会整体替换这个本体：实体变成基因、变异、表型和队列，关系变成修饰、增加……风险、降低……风险、以及测量于。 That schema-swap is basically the whole customization; the extraction machinery underneath is identical. 这个模式替换基本上就是全部的定制工作；底层的抽取机器是完全相同的。

JORDAN Give me the numbers. 把数字给我。 Did GPT-4 actually beat the trained models? GPT-4 真的打败了训练好的模型吗？

ALEX On their sepsis-specific dataset, GPT-4 with few-shot prompting reached an F-score of 76.76. 在他们脓毒症专用数据集上，用少样本提示的 GPT-4 达到了 76.76 的 F 分数。 That beat the other LLMs cleanly: Qwen2 at 43.77, Llama3 at 48.39, and GPT-3.5 at 55.53. 这干净利落地压过了其他大语言模型：Qwen2 是 43.77，Llama3 是 48.39，GPT-3.5 是 55.53。 On the public CMeEE Chinese medical NER benchmark, GPT-4 few-shot hit 65.42, edging out BERT-CRF at 62.11 and Med-BERT at 60.66. 在公开的 CMeEE 中文医学 NER 基准上，GPT-4 少样本达到 65.42，险胜 BERT-CRF 的 62.11 和 Med-BERT 的 60.66。 Notice the gap: GPT-4 wins comfortably on the in-house sepsis set but only narrowly on the standardized public benchmark. 注意这个差距：GPT-4 在自建脓毒症集上赢得从容，但在标准化公开基准上只是险胜。 The final graph has 1,894 nodes and 2,021 relationships. 最终的图谱有 1,894 个节点和 2,021 条关系。 They then demonstrate queries: top complications came out as pneumonia at 32.1 percent, hypoproteinemia at 28.5, septic shock at 25.9. 随后他们演示查询：排名靠前的并发症为肺炎 32.1%、低蛋白血症 28.5%、脓毒性休克 25.9%。 Honest about limits, they flag hallucinations like GPT-4 recommending a "blood cholesterol test" for sepsis or asserting "sepsis caused by exercise," and weakness on traditional-Chinese-medicine terms. 他们诚实地面对局限，指出了幻觉，比如 GPT-4 为脓毒症推荐"血胆固醇检测"，或断言"运动导致的脓毒症"，以及在中医术语上的薄弱。

JORDAN Let's slow down on the GraphRAG part, because that's the piece that turns a static graph into something clinicians actually touch. 我们在 GraphRAG 这部分放慢一点，因为正是它把一张静态图谱变成临床医生真正会用的东西。 What's the difference between plain RAG and GraphRAG here? 这里的普通 RAG 和 GraphRAG 有什么区别？

ALEX Plain retrieval-augmented generation retrieves flat chunks of text by similarity and stuffs them into the prompt. 普通的检索增强生成按相似度检索扁平的文本块，塞进提示里。 That works for a single fact but breaks on questions that need chaining. 这对单个事实有效，但在需要串联的问题上就崩了。 GraphRAG instead retrieves a subgraph and walks typed edges, so a question like "for a septic patient with this complication, what medication is recommended and what does it interact with" becomes a multi-hop traversal rather than a keyword match. GraphRAG 则检索一个子图并沿带类型的边游走，于是像"对于有这种并发症的脓毒症患者，推荐什么药物、它与什么相互作用"这样的问题，就变成了多跳遍历，而不是关键词匹配。 The authors argue this is exactly what complex diseases need, because clinical reasoning is inherently relational. 作者认为这正是复杂疾病所需要的，因为临床推理本质上是关系性的。 They score the improvement with BLEU and ROUGE against clinician gold answers. 他们用 BLEU 和 ROUGE 对照临床医生的金标准答案给改进打分。 I'll push back on the metric, though: BLEU and ROUGE measure text overlap, not clinical correctness. 不过我要对这个指标提出异议：BLEU 和 ROUGE 衡量的是文本重叠，而非临床正确性。 A confidently wrong answer that reuses the reference's vocabulary can score well, so these numbers tell you fluency, not safety. 一个自信错误、却复用了参照答案词汇的回答也能得高分，所以这些数字告诉你的是流畅度，而非安全性。

JORDAN So the graph is the trustworthy layer and the LLM on top is the risky layer. 所以图谱是可信的那一层，而它上面的大语言模型是有风险的那一层。 That's a useful way to think about it. 这是个有用的思考角度。

ALEX Exactly, and that separation is the design lesson worth stealing. 正是如此，而这种分离正是值得借鉴的设计教训。 The knowledge graph is auditable: every node and edge can carry a provenance link and be checked by a human. 知识图谱是可审计的：每个节点和每条边都能带一个溯源链接，供人工核查。 The generative layer is where hallucination lives, so you want the graph to constrain the model, not the other way around. 生成层才是幻觉栖身之处，所以你希望是图谱去约束模型，而不是反过来。 If you build the CF version, the same principle holds: the extracted gene-phenotype edges are the ground truth you curate, and any LLM answer must cite back to them. 如果你构建 CF 版本，同一原则成立：抽取出的"基因—表型"边是你审编的地面真相，而任何大语言模型的回答都必须回引到它们。 That's what keeps a literature graph from becoming a confident fabrication machine. 这正是让文献图谱不至于沦为一台自信的编造机器的关键。
