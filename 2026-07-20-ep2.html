Ep2 — Track B: NYUTron, and the two numbers that decide whether a model gets deployed

Blurb. Jiang, Liang and colleagues (Nature, 2023) pretrained a 109M-parameter BERT-style encoder on NYU Notes — 7,247,694 clinical notes from 387,144 patients across four hospitals, 4.11 billion words, 2011–2020 — then fine-tuned it across five clinical and operational tasks (30-day readmission, in-hospital mortality, Charlson comorbidity index imputation, length-of-stay bin, insurance claim denial), compressed it, and ran it live through an inference engine (NYUTriton) that reads discharge notes as physicians sign them. Retrospective AUC 78.7–94.9%, +5.36 to +14.7 points over structured baselines. The argument that matters is architectural, not accuracy-driven: reading the note collapses an entire structured-feature extraction pipeline into a single input, which is their answer to the last-mile problem. The two numbers we read the paper for are the random-vs-temporal split gap (84.13% vs 80.2%) and the prospective precision of 20.58% at 82.30% recall on a 11.17% base rate. Where it lands: AI tooling / career. Buildable artifact: a deployment-readiness evaluation harness reporting temporal-split degradation and precision at a fixed clinician alert budget.

Plain-English glossary

  • 30-day all-cause readmission — a discharged patient returning to hospital within 30 days for any reason; simultaneously a clinical event and a billing event, which is why it dominates this literature.（中文：30 天全因再入院——患者出院后 30 天内因任何原因再次入院，既是临床事件也是账单事件。）
  • APACHE2 / SAPS2 — intensive-care severity scores built from physiological variables such as heart rate and age; used here as the structured baseline for mortality prediction.（中文：APACHE2／SAPS2——基于心率、年龄等生理变量的重症监护严重程度评分。）
  • Charlson comorbidity index (CCI) — a weighted count of chronic conditions used as a crude patient-severity summary; 22% of the dataset lacked it, which is why the paper frames it as an imputation task.（中文：Charlson 合并症指数——慢性病的加权计数，用作粗略的疾病严重程度概括。）
  • Clostridioides difficile enterocolitis — a healthcare-associated bacterial gut infection; appeared in 3 of the 27 physician-judged preventable readmissions.（中文：艰难梭菌肠炎——一种医院获得性肠道细菌感染。）
  • CMS penalty — under Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services rules, hospitals face financial penalties for excess readmissions; 81.9% of unplanned captured readmissions were penalizable.（中文：CMS 处罚——美国医疗保险和医疗补助服务中心对再入院率过高的医院施加的财务处罚。）
  • LACE — a four-item bedside readmission risk score: Length of stay, Acuity of admission, Charlson comorbidity index, Emergency department visits in the past 6 months.（中文：LACE——由住院时长、入院急性程度、Charlson 合并症指数、近半年急诊次数构成的四项床旁再入院风险评分。）
  • Last-mile problem — the observation that the overwhelming majority of medical predictive algorithms are trained, tested and published but never deployed into real care.（中文：最后一公里问题——绝大多数医疗预测算法被训练、测试、发表，却从未部署到真实诊疗中。）
  • MLM (masked language modelling) — the self-supervised pretraining objective used here: mask words in a note and train the model to fill them back in.（中文：MLM——掩码语言建模，本文使用的自监督预训练目标，遮挡病历中的词并训练模型还原。）
  • Temporal split — holding out test data drawn from the future of the training data, rather than randomly; the split that resembles deployment.（中文：时间划分——用训练数据之后的时间段作为测试集，而非随机划分，更接近真实部署情形。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Note-as-single-input architecture — replaces a multi-feature structured extraction pipeline with one text field; the deployment-friction argument, and the paper's real contribution.
  • BERT-style encoder (109M params) + MLM pretraining — a small model on highly tailored data, explicitly counter to the >1B-parameter generative trend.
  • NYUTriton inference engine — compressed model wired into the live record, triggered on note signature; the deployment pattern worth copying regardless of model class.
  • Temporal-split model selection — random test 84.13% vs temporal test 80.2%; they select on the temporal number. Steal this.
  • Data-efficiency curve — NYUTron reached ~75% AUC at 10,000 fine-tuning examples where random-init needed 100,000; a 10× gain in exactly the small-labelled-cohort regime rare-disease work lives in.
  • Cross-site protocol — pretrain on all sites, fine-tune locally; local fine-tuning always beat external fine-tuning at the test site.
  • Physician panel adjudication of true positives — converts a performance metric into a graded count of avoidable harm (61% unplanned, 81.9% penalizable, 27/100 preventable).
  • Code: the paper states preprocessing code is available on GitHub; notes and weights carry PHI and do not travel — the recipe is reusable, the model is not.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority served: AI tooling / career.

deployment-readiness-harness (S–M) — wrap any longitudinal prediction model plus a dated dataset; return four numbers: (1) random-split performance, (2) temporal-split performance, (3) the gap between them as a drift proxy, and (4) precision at a fixed clinician alert budget (top-k patients per week someone can realistically act on). Nobody deploys a threshold — they deploy a workload. Model-agnostic, language-model-agnostic, and every output is a number a clinical stakeholder understands on sight.

What to verify first

  • Characterize the 512-token truncation against your own note format — the model reads roughly the first page; the paper does not report what falls off the end.
  • Interrogate the label. All-cause readmission includes planned returns, and planned readmissions received much higher predicted probabilities (82.1% ± 27.3%) than unplanned ones (31.9% ± 31.1%) — some apparent skill is recognizing scheduled events.
  • Recompute precision at your own base rate. 20.58% precision at an 11.17% event rate is close to arithmetically forced; AUC will not warn you about alert fatigue.
  • Treat the physician head-to-head as a demonstration, not a comparison — 20 discharge summaries, 6 raters.
  • Transportability: one health system, its own note conventions; the authors themselves report sensitivity to department, to demographics, and month-to-month fluctuation.
  • Reproducibility: preprocessing code on GitHub, but PHI-bound notes and weights mean you can reuse the recipe, not the artifact.
  • Over-reliance risk — the authors flag that seamless workflow integration is precisely what makes over-trust likely; continuous monitoring of human-machine interaction and drift is part of the build, not an afterthought.

ALEX Today's Track B paper is Jiang, Liang and colleagues, twenty twenty-three, in Nature: "Health system-scale language models are all-purpose prediction engines." 今天的 Track B 论文是 Jiang、Liang 及其同事 2023 年发表在《Nature》上的《医疗系统规模的语言模型是通用预测引擎》。 It is open access and we have the full text. 论文开放获取，我们拿到了全文。 The system is called NYUTron, built and deployed inside NYU Langone Health. 这个系统叫 NYUTron，是在 NYU Langone Health 医疗系统内部构建并部署的。 We're reading it for one reason above all others: it is a paper that made it past the last mile. 我们读它最主要的理由只有一个：它是一篇真正走完了"最后一公里"的论文。 Most clinical prediction models are trained, published, and never touched again. 大多数临床预测模型被训练出来、发表出来，然后就再也没人碰过。 This one got deployed, ran prospectively for four months, and reported what happened. 而这一个被部署了，前瞻性地运行了四个月，并且报告了实际发生了什么。

JORDAN Give me the clinical vocabulary first — I know the machine learning side, but not the hospital-operations side. 先给我讲临床方面的词汇——机器学习那一侧我懂，医院运营这一侧不懂。

ALEX Four things. 四个概念。 Thirty-day all-cause readmission means a patient is discharged and comes back to the hospital within thirty days for any reason. 30 天全因再入院 (30-day all-cause readmission) 指的是患者出院后 30 天内因任何原因再次入院。 The reason it dominates this literature is financial and regulatory: under Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services rules, hospitals are penalized for excess readmissions, so a readmission is simultaneously a clinical event and a billing event. 它之所以主导这一领域的文献，原因是财务与监管上的：根据美国医疗保险和医疗补助服务中心 (CMS) 的规定，再入院过多的医院会被处罚，所以再入院同时是一个临床事件和一个账单事件。 Second, LACE — it is a four-item bedside risk score: length of stay, acuity of admission, Charlson comorbidity index, and emergency department visits in the past six months. 第二，LACE，这是一个四项床旁风险评分：住院时长、入院急性程度、Charlson 合并症指数、以及过去六个月的急诊就诊次数。 Third, the Charlson comorbidity index itself is a weighted count of chronic conditions used as a crude severity summary. 第三，Charlson 合并症指数本身是对慢性病的加权计数，用作一个粗略的疾病严重程度概括。 Fourth, SAPS2 and APACHE2 are intensive-care severity scores built from physiological variables like heart rate and age. 第四，SAPS2 和 APACHE2 是基于心率、年龄等生理变量构建的重症监护严重程度评分。 All four are the traditional structured-feature baselines this paper is trying to beat. 这四者都是这篇论文试图超越的传统结构化特征基线。

JORDAN And the problem they say they're solving? 那他们说自己要解决的问题是什么？

ALEX They call it the last-mile problem. 他们称之为"最后一公里问题"。 The overwhelming majority of medical predictive algorithms are trained, tested and published, but never deployed to affect real care. 绝大多数医疗预测算法被训练、测试、发表，却从未被部署到真实诊疗中产生影响。 Their diagnosis of why is specific and, I think, correct. 他们对原因的诊断很具体，我认为也是对的。 Structured-input models require a pipeline that assembles named features — length of stay, comorbidity index, lab values — from a live electronic health record, and every one of those features is a separate extraction, mapping and validation problem that breaks when the record changes. 基于结构化输入的模型需要一条流水线，从实时电子健康记录 (EHR) 中拼装出一个个命名特征——住院时长、合并症指数、化验值——而每一个特征都是一个独立的抽取、映射和验证问题，一旦记录结构变化就会失效。 Their bet is that all of that information already exists, integrated, in a place with a much simpler interface: the note the physician wrote. 他们的赌注是，所有这些信息其实已经以整合好的形式存在于一个接口简单得多的地方：医生写下的病历文本。 If a model can read the note, the feature pipeline collapses into one input. 如果模型能读懂病历，整条特征流水线就坍缩成了一个输入。 That is an architectural argument about deployment friction, not an accuracy argument, and it is the most interesting claim in the paper. 这是一个关于部署摩擦的架构论证，而不是关于准确率的论证，也是这篇论文里最有意思的主张。

JORDAN Why an encoder model rather than a generative one? 为什么用编码器模型而不是生成式模型？

ALEX Because the job is classification, not conversation. 因为这个任务是分类，不是对话。 An encoder reads the whole note bidirectionally and emits one number; a generative model produces text that then has to be parsed, and its output depends on how you prompted it. 编码器双向读取整份病历并输出一个数字；生成式模型产出的是文本，之后还得解析，而且输出结果依赖于你怎么写提示词。 The authors are explicit that large generative models are highly dependent on user inputs and prompting and are therefore not easily adapted for automating basic clinical and operational tasks. 作者明确指出，大型生成式模型高度依赖用户输入与提示词，因此不容易被用来自动化基础的临床与运营任务。 They also frame their choice as a deliberate departure from the field's direction — a model under one billion parameters trained on highly tailored data, against a research trend toward models above one billion parameters trained on large non-specific corpora. 他们也把自己的选择表述为对领域主流方向的有意背离——一个参数量低于十亿、在高度定制数据上训练的模型，对抗的是参数量超过十亿、在大规模非特定语料上训练的研究趋势。 For a prediction task with a fixed output space, I think they are right, and the deployment story is what earns them that argument. 对于输出空间固定的预测任务，我认为他们是对的，而部署的实际结果为这一论点提供了支撑。

JORDAN So what did they actually build? 那他们实际造了什么？

ALEX Four steps. 四步。 First, the corpus: a dataset they call NYU Notes — seven million two hundred forty-seven thousand six hundred ninety-four clinical notes from three hundred eighty-seven thousand one hundred forty-four patients across four hospitals, January twenty-eleven to May twenty-twenty, totalling four point one one billion words. 第一步是语料库：他们称之为 NYU Notes 的数据集——来自四家医院、387,144 名患者的 7,247,694 份临床病历，时间跨度为 2011 年 1 月至 2020 年 5 月，共计 41.1 亿词。 They included notes signed by physicians, residents, physician assistants, nurse practitioners and fellows, and excluded billing-derived, invalid or empty notes. 他们纳入了由医师、住院医师、医师助理、执业护士和专科培训医师签署的病历，排除了源自账单、无效或空白的病历。 Second, pretraining: a one hundred nine million parameter BERT-style encoder with a masked language modelling objective, run until validation loss plateaued. 第二步是预训练：一个 1.09 亿参数的 BERT 式编码器，采用掩码语言建模 (masked language modelling, MLM) 目标，训练到验证损失趋于平稳。 Third, fine-tuning on five labelled tasks — thirty-day readmission, in-hospital mortality, Charlson comorbidity index imputation, length-of-stay bin, and insurance claim denial. 第三步是在五个有标注任务上微调——30 天再入院、住院死亡、Charlson 合并症指数填补、住院时长分箱、以及保险理赔拒付。 Fourth, deployment: the model was compressed into an accelerated format and loaded into an inference engine they call NYUTriton, which interfaces with the live record and reads discharge notes at the moment the treating physician signs them. 第四步是部署：模型被压缩为加速格式，载入他们称为 NYUTriton 的推理引擎，该引擎与实时记录对接，在主治医师签署出院小结的那一刻读取它。

ALEX My design objection is the input window. 我对设计的意见在输入窗口上。 They truncate notes at five hundred twelve tokens. 他们把病历截断在 512 个 token。 A discharge summary at a tertiary hospital is routinely far longer than that. 三甲医院的一份出院小结通常远长于这个长度。 So the model is reading roughly the first page and discarding the rest, and nothing in the paper characterizes what falls off the end or whether the informative content is front-loaded. 所以模型实际上读的大约是第一页，其余部分被丢弃，而论文中没有任何内容说明被截掉的是什么，也没有说明有信息量的内容是否集中在前面。 My second objection is the baseline. 我的第二点意见是基线。 Beating LACE plus gradient-boosted trees is beating a four-feature score. 战胜 LACE 加梯度提升树，等于战胜了一个四特征的评分。 That is the standard clinical comparator, so it is a fair thing to report, but a four-feature baseline losing to a hundred-million-parameter model trained on four billion words is not by itself a surprising result. 那确实是临床上的标准对照，所以报告它是合理的，但一个四特征基线输给一个在 40 亿词上训练的一亿参数模型，本身并不令人意外。

JORDAN What are the numbers? 那具体数字是什么？

ALEX Across the five tasks, area under the curve ranged from seventy-eight point seven to ninety-four point nine percent, with improvements of five point three six to fourteen point seven percentage points over the structured baselines. 五个任务的 AUC 范围是 78.7% 到 94.9%，相比结构化基线提升了 5.36 到 14.7 个百分点。 In-hospital mortality was the strongest at ninety-four point nine. 住院死亡预测最强，为 94.9%。 Readmission was seventy-nine point eight seven, comorbidity index imputation eighty-nine point four, length of stay seventy-eight point seven, insurance denial eighty-seven point two. 再入院为 79.87%，合并症指数填补 89.4%，住院时长 78.7%，保险拒付 87.2%。 But the numbers I actually care about are the ones about experimental design. 但我真正在意的数字是关于实验设计的那些。 They report a random split and a temporal split separately. 他们分别报告了随机划分和时间划分。 Random test area under the curve was eighty-four point one three; temporal test was eighty point two. 随机划分测试集的 AUC 是 84.13%；时间划分测试集是 80.2%。 That four-point gap is the cost of pretending your test data does not come from the future, and they use the temporal number for model selection. 这 4 个百分点的差距，就是"假装测试数据不是来自未来"所付出的代价，而他们用时间划分的数字来做模型选择。

JORDAN And the pretraining — did it earn its cost? 那预训练呢——它的成本值回来了吗？

ALEX Partly, and the honest answer is more interesting than a yes. 部分值回来了，而诚实的答案比一个"是"更有意思。 Against a randomly initialized model, NYUTron reached about seventy-five percent area under the curve with ten thousand fine-tuning examples, where random-init needed one hundred thousand. 与随机初始化的模型相比，NYUTron 用一万个微调样本就达到约 75% 的 AUC，而随机初始化需要十万个。 That is a tenfold data-efficiency gain, which is the regime that matters when your labelled cohort is small. 这是十倍的数据效率提升，而当你的有标注队列很小时，这正是最要紧的情形。 But a model pretrained elsewhere on clinical text, from the University of Florida, closed most of the gap once enough local fine-tuning data was available. 但一个在别处、用佛罗里达大学临床文本预训练的模型，在有了足够的本地微调数据之后，把差距基本追平了。 The authors draw the practical conclusion themselves: high-quality fine-tuning data is more valuable than pretraining, and if compute is limited, locally fine-tune an externally pretrained clinical model. 作者自己得出了实用结论：高质量的微调数据比预训练更有价值，如果算力有限，就在外部预训练的临床模型上做本地微调。 Their own compute was twenty-four A100 GPUs for three weeks of pretraining. 他们自己的预训练用了 24 张 A100 GPU、跑了三周。

JORDAN What about moving between hospitals? 那在不同医院之间迁移呢？

ALEX They ran a clean factorial on that, using two geographically separated hospitals in the same system — Tisch in Manhattan and NYU Langone Hospital–Brooklyn. 他们在这一点上做了一个干净的析因实验，使用了同一系统内两家地理上分离的医院——曼哈顿的 Tisch 和布鲁克林的 NYU Langone Hospital。 They pretrained three models — one on Manhattan, one on Brooklyn, one on all four sites — then fine-tuned each on either Manhattan or Brooklyn data, then tested at either site. 他们预训练了三个模型——一个用曼哈顿数据、一个用布鲁克林数据、一个用全部四个院区数据——然后各自在曼哈顿或布鲁克林数据上微调，再在两地分别测试。 Two results fall out cleanly. 有两个结论很清晰。 Pretraining on all sites was best at both test sites. 用全部院区数据预训练在两个测试地点都最好。 And for every model, fine-tuning locally beat fine-tuning at the other site, regardless of where pretraining happened. 而且对每一个模型来说，本地微调都优于在另一地点微调，无论预训练是在哪里做的。 So the operational rule is: pretrain broadly, fine-tune locally. 因此可操作的规则是：广泛预训练，本地微调。 That is a genuinely portable finding, and it is the kind of result that only shows up if you build the experiment as a grid rather than reporting one number. 这是一个真正可迁移的发现，也是那种只有把实验设计成网格、而不是只报一个数字时才会浮现出来的结果。

JORDAN And the deployment result — the part nobody usually gets to report. 还有部署结果——那通常没人有机会报告的部分。

ALEX January to April twenty twenty-two, prospective, single-arm, non-interventional. 2022 年 1 月到 4 月，前瞻性、单臂、非干预性研究。 Twenty-nine thousand two hundred eighty-six discharged encounters. 29,286 次出院就诊。 Three thousand two hundred seventy-one patients returned within thirty days — a base rate of eleven point one seven percent. 3,271 名患者在 30 天内返院，基础发生率为 11.17%。 Area under the curve seventy-eight point seven zero, recall eighty-two point three zero percent, precision twenty point five eight percent. AUC 为 78.70%，召回率 82.30%，精确率 20.58%。 Sit with that precision number, because it is the whole lesson. 请在这个精确率上停一停，因为它就是全部的教训。 Four out of every five flagged patients did not come back. 每五个被标记的患者中有四个并没有返院。 An eighty percent false-alarm rate is not a failure of the model — at an eleven percent base rate it is close to arithmetically forced — but it is the number that decides whether clinicians keep looking at the alert. 80% 的误报率并不是模型的失败——在 11% 的基础发生率下这几乎是算术上必然的——但它决定了临床医生会不会继续看这个提醒。 Area under the curve does not tell you that. AUC 是看不出这一点的。

JORDAN Did they check whether the true positives were worth anything? 他们有没有检查那些真阳性是否有实际价值？
