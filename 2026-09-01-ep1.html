Ep1 — Track A: Memo-Patho, an alignment-free pathogenicity predictor for transmembrane proteins

Bao Y, Liu Z, Zhao F, Li W, et al. (2026). Bridging local–global transmembrane protein contexts with contrastive pretraining for alignment-free pathogenicity prediction. Briefings in Bioinformatics.

Memo-Patho is a deep learning framework built to predict whether a mutation in a transmembrane protein (TMP) is pathogenic or benign without relying on evolutionary alignment or an experimental structure — the two crutches most variant-effect predictors lean on, and the two things transmembrane proteins tend to be short on. It fuses two protein language models (ESM2 and ProtT5) through a dual local/global feature stream and a transformer fusion block, pretrained with a within-protein supervised contrastive objective, then fine-tuned for binary classification. Across TMP benchmarks under protein-level holdout splits it reaches up to 0.93 accuracy, and it transfers zero-shot to an independent 55-variant KCNQ1 ion-channel cohort. CFTR is architecturally the same class of protein — a multi-pass transmembrane domain architecture (TMD1/TMD2) — which makes this a strong candidate template for the Karen Raraigh CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration.

Plain-English glossary

  • Alignment-free — a prediction method that doesn't rely on comparing a protein's sequence across species (an evolutionary alignment) to judge how conserved, and therefore how likely to be functionally important, a given position is.（中文：一种不依赖跨物种序列比对来判断某个位点保守程度、进而推断其功能重要性的预测方法。）
  • CFTR2 — a curated, expert-reviewed database that classifies CFTR variants by clinical evidence, used as a primary source of labeled benign/pathogenic CFTR variants.（中文：一个经专家审核、依据临床证据对 CFTR 变异进行分类的整理数据库，是获取已标注良性/致病 CFTR 变异的主要来源。）
  • Contrastive pretraining — a training strategy where the model learns from triplets (an anchor, a similar "positive" example, and a dissimilar "negative" example), pulling representations of similar things together and pushing dissimilar things apart before any final classification task.（中文：一种训练策略，模型从三元组（锚点、相似的"正样本"、不相似的"负样本"）中学习，在做最终分类任务之前，先把相似样本的表示拉近、不相似样本的表示推远。）
  • Group split / protein-level Ind split — an evaluation setup where entire proteins (not just individual variants) are held out of training, so test performance reflects generalization to genuinely unseen proteins rather than memorized examples.（中文：一种评估设置，整个蛋白质（而非仅个别变异）被排除在训练集之外，因此测试表现反映的是对真正未见过蛋白质的泛化能力，而不是对已记忆样本的复现。）
  • Protein language model (PLM) — a transformer model trained on large databases of raw protein sequences to learn general sequence patterns, used here (ESM2, ProtT5) to generate embeddings without any explicit evolutionary comparison.（中文：一种基于 Transformer、在海量原始蛋白质序列数据库上训练的模型，用来学习序列的一般规律；本文中的 ESM2 和 ProtT5 都是 PLM，用于生成嵌入而无需显式的跨物种比较。）
  • Transmembrane domain (TMD) — a segment of a protein that spans the cell membrane; CFTR has two, TMD1 and TMD2, each built from multiple membrane-spanning helices.（中文：蛋白质中穿过细胞膜的片段；CFTR 有两个跨膜结构域，TMD1 和 TMD2，各由多段跨膜螺旋组成。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a clinically observed variant without enough evidence yet to classify as pathogenic or benign.（中文：临床上已观察到、但证据尚不足以判定为致病或良性的变异。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • ESM2 / ProtT5 — pretrained protein language models used to generate alignment-free sequence embeddings; both freely available.
  • Within-protein contrastive triplet training — a labeled-data-efficient pretraining scheme (anchor / benign positive / pathogenic negative within the same protein) that could be replicated directly on CFTR2-labeled CFTR variants.
  • Protein-level group split — an evaluation discipline worth adopting for any CFTR-specific model to check true generalization, not just variant-level cross-validation.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh collaboration (CFTR variant interpretation / VUS pathogenicity prediction). MVP: pull CFTR missense variants and CFTR2/ClinVar labels, embed with ESM2-650M, build within-protein contrastive triplets, train a compact classifier as a complementary alignment-free score alongside AlphaMissense/REVEL. Difficulty: Medium.

What to verify first

  • Whether any ABC-transporter-family proteins (CFTR's family) appeared in Memo-Patho's own training data, which could undercut the protein-level holdout's guarantee for a CFTR replication.
  • Known training-data biases (family imbalance, alpha-helical skew, over-studied proteins) flagged by the authors themselves — not fully resolved by the protein-level split.
  • Whether the authors released working code/weights, or only an architecture description — changes the build from adaptation to reimplementation.
  • The 0.93 accuracy figure is a ceiling across "diverse TMP benchmarks," not a single number — confirm which benchmark composition is most CFTR-relevant before quoting it.

ALEX Today we're reading Memo-Patho, a 2026 paper from Bao, Liu, Zhao, Li and colleagues in Briefings in Bioinformatics. 今天我们要讲的是 Memo-Patho，这是包（Bao）、刘（Liu）、赵（Zhao）、李（Li）等人 2026 年发表在《生物信息学简报》（Briefings in Bioinformatics）上的一篇论文。 It's a deep learning framework for predicting whether a mutation in a transmembrane protein is pathogenic or benign, and it's built specifically to work without evolutionary alignment data. 这是一个深度学习框架，用来预测跨膜蛋白（transmembrane protein）中的某个突变是致病的还是良性的，而且它的设计目标就是在没有进化比对数据的情况下也能工作。 We're covering it in Track A because CFTR itself is a multi-pass transmembrane protein, and this method is explicitly aimed at the domain architecture that generic predictors struggle with. 我们把它放进 Track A 是因为 CFTR 本身就是一个多次跨膜的蛋白质，而这个方法正是针对通用预测工具处理得不好的那类结构域架构设计的。

JORDAN Before we get into the model, let's set up the vocabulary, because a few of these terms are doing a lot of work. 在进入模型细节之前，我们先把几个术语讲清楚，因为它们承载了很多含义。 What's a transmembrane protein, and why is it a special case for pathogenicity prediction? 什么是跨膜蛋白，它为什么在致病性预测里是个特殊情况？

ALEX A transmembrane protein spans the cell membrane one or more times, with segments sitting inside the lipid bilayer and other segments exposed on either side. 跨膜蛋白是一次或多次穿过细胞膜的蛋白质，它的一部分肽段嵌在脂双层（lipid bilayer）内部，另一部分则暴露在膜的两侧。 CFTR is a classic example — it has two transmembrane domains, TMD1 and TMD2, each built from multiple membrane-spanning helices, plus two nucleotide-binding domains and a regulatory domain sitting outside the membrane. CFTR 就是一个经典例子——它有两个跨膜结构域，TMD1 和 TMD2（TMD1/TMD2，两个跨膜结构域），每个都由多段跨膜螺旋组成，此外还有两个核苷酸结合结构域和一个位于膜外的调节结构域。 The problem for pathogenicity prediction is that most variant-effect predictors — VEPs — lean heavily on evolutionary conservation, meaning they look at how well a position is preserved across a multiple sequence alignment, or MSA, spanning many species. 对致病性预测来说，问题在于大多数变异效应预测工具（variant-effect predictor，VEP）严重依赖进化保守性，也就是看某个位点在跨越许多物种的多序列比对（multiple sequence alignment，MSA）中保守程度如何。 Transmembrane proteins tend to have fewer usable homologs and more sequence divergence in their membrane-spanning segments, so MSA-based signal gets noisy exactly where you need it most. 跨膜蛋白往往可用的同源序列较少，跨膜片段的序列分化程度也更高，所以恰恰在最需要保守性信号的地方，基于 MSA 的信号反而变得嘈杂。 Structure-based methods like AlphaMissense have the same weak spot when a full experimental or high-confidence predicted structure for a multi-pass membrane protein isn't available. 像 AlphaMissense 这样基于结构的方法，在多次跨膜蛋白缺乏完整的实验结构或高置信度预测结构时，也存在同样的弱点。

JORDAN So "alignment-free" here means the model doesn't lean on that cross-species comparison at all? "无比对"（alignment-free）在这里是不是意味着模型完全不依赖跨物种比较？

ALEX Right — instead it leans on protein language models, or PLMs, which are transformer models trained on huge databases of raw protein sequences to learn general patterns of what sequences look like without ever being told about evolution explicitly. 没错——它转而依赖蛋白质语言模型（protein language model，PLM），这是一类基于 Transformer 的模型，在海量原始蛋白质序列数据库上训练，学习序列的一般规律，而完全不需要显式告知它进化关系。 Memo-Patho uses two of them, ESM2 and ProtT5, to generate sequence embeddings, and pairs those with structural proxies — features that approximate local 3D context — derived purely from sequence rather than from an experimental or AlphaFold structure. Memo-Patho 使用了两个这样的模型，ESM2 和 ProtT5，来生成序列嵌入（sequence embedding），并将它们与结构代理特征（structural proxy，即近似局部三维环境但完全从序列推导而来、不依赖实验结构或 AlphaFold 结构的特征）配对使用。 One more term worth flagging before we move on: a variant of uncertain significance, or VUS, is a variant that's been observed clinically but doesn't yet have enough evidence to call pathogenic or benign — that's the category this kind of tool is trying to shrink. 在继续之前还有一个术语值得提一下：意义未明变异（variant of uncertain significance，VUS）指的是临床上已经观察到、但证据还不足以判定为致病或良性的变异——这类工具的目标正是缩小这一类别的规模。

JORDAN Okay, so what problem were the authors actually trying to solve, and why now? 好，那作者到底想解决什么问题，为什么是现在？

ALEX Pathogenicity prediction for transmembrane proteins sits at an awkward intersection. 跨膜蛋白的致病性预测正好处在一个尴尬的交叉点上。 They're clinically enormous — ion channels, transporters, and receptors are a huge share of drug targets and disease genes — but the standard toolkit for variant-effect prediction was built and validated mostly on soluble, well-alignable proteins. 它们在临床上极其重要——离子通道、转运蛋白和受体占据了药物靶点和致病基因的很大一部分——但变异效应预测的标准工具箱大多是针对可溶、易于比对的蛋白质构建和验证的。 The paper cites prior TMP-specific attempts — MutTMPredictor, mCSM-membrane, Pred-MutHTP — as evidence that people have tried to specialize existing frameworks for membrane proteins, but the authors argue none of them explicitly model the biophysical constraint that a mutation's effect depends on both its immediate local environment inside the membrane and its role in the protein's larger global architecture at the same time. 论文引用了此前一些针对跨膜蛋白的专门尝试——MutTMPredictor、mCSM-membrane、Pred-MutHTP——作为已有人尝试为膜蛋白定制现有框架的证据，但作者认为，这些方法都没有明确建模这样一个生物物理约束：一个突变的效应同时取决于它在膜内的局部环境，也取决于它在蛋白质整体架构中所扮演的角色。 So the specific gap they're targeting is a representation-learning gap — how do you build a feature representation that captures both scales at once, without needing alignment or a solved structure? 所以他们瞄准的是一个表征学习（representation learning）上的空白——在不依赖比对或已解析结构的前提下，如何构建一种同时捕捉这两个尺度信息的特征表示？ Before this work, the choice was largely alignment-dependent methods that struggle on TMPs, or generic PLM-based scorers that don't specifically encode membrane biophysics — nothing occupied the middle ground of TMP-specific, alignment-free, and structure-optional at once. 在这项工作之前，可选的路径大体上要么是依赖比对、但在跨膜蛋白上表现欠佳的方法，要么是通用的基于 PLM 的评分工具、但没有专门编码膜蛋白的生物物理特性——同时具备"跨膜蛋白专用、无需比对、结构可选"这三个特点的中间地带一直是空的。

JORDAN What did they actually build to fill that gap? 他们具体构建了什么来填补这个空白？

ALEX The architecture has two parallel streams. 这个架构有两条并行的流。 A global feature stream pools PLM embeddings across the whole sequence, combined with a contextual structural feature drawn from the neighborhood around the mutation, to represent the protein's broader context. 全局特征流（global feature stream）把整条序列的 PLM 嵌入做池化处理，再结合从突变位点邻域提取的上下文结构特征，用来表示蛋白质的整体背景。 A local feature stream focuses tightly on the mutation site itself, extracting sequence and structural features at that exact locus. 局部特征流（local feature stream）则紧紧聚焦在突变位点本身，提取该确切位置的序列和结构特征。 Those two streams are fused through a transformer block using multi-head self-attention, so the model can learn how the local mutation site interacts with the global protein context, and the fused representation feeds a final classifier that outputs a benign-or-pathogenic call. 这两条流通过一个使用多头自注意力（multi-head self-attention）机制的 Transformer 模块进行融合，让模型能够学习局部突变位点如何与全局蛋白质背景相互作用，融合后的表示最终输入一个分类器，给出良性或致病的判断。 The distinguishing piece, though, is what happens before fine-tuning: a supervised contrastive pretraining phase. 不过真正与众不同的部分，是在微调之前发生的事情：一个有监督对比预训练（supervised contrastive pretraining）阶段。 The model is trained on triplets — an anchor, which is the original unmutated sequence context; a positive, a known benign variant in that same protein; and a negative, a known pathogenic variant in that same protein — and the contrastive loss pulls anchor-positive representations together while pushing anchor-negative representations apart. 模型在三元组（triplet）上进行训练——锚点（anchor）是原始的未突变序列上下文；正样本（positive）是同一蛋白质中一个已知的良性变异；负样本（negative）是同一蛋白质中一个已知的致病变异——对比损失（contrastive loss）会把锚点和正样本的表示拉近，同时把锚点和负样本的表示推远。 That within-protein framing is deliberate: instead of asking "does this variant look pathogenic in general," it asks "does this variant look more like the benign or the pathogenic examples we've already seen in this exact protein," which is a much more targeted signal. 这种"蛋白质内部"（within-protein）的设计是刻意为之的：它问的不是"这个变异整体上像不像致病的"，而是"在这个具体蛋白质中，这个变异更像我们已经见过的良性例子，还是致病例子"，这是一个针对性强得多的信号。 If I'm pushing back on one design choice, it's this: that framing only works as well as the within-protein labeled examples you already have, so for a protein with very few characterized benign or pathogenic variants to begin with, the contrastive signal has thin material to work with. 如果要对一个设计选择提出质疑，那就是这一点：这种框架的效果完全取决于你已经拥有的蛋白质内部标注样本的数量，所以对于一个本身已知的良性或致病变异都很少的蛋白质，对比信号能利用的素材就会很单薄。

JORDAN How did they actually test whether it works? 他们究竟是怎么测试这个方法是否有效的？

ALEX Two evaluation regimes. 两种评估方案。 A "Mix" dataset and an "Ind," or independent, dataset, with the independent split done at the protein level — meaning entire proteins, not just individual variants, are held out of training and only seen at test time. 一个叫 "Mix" 数据集，另一个叫 "Ind"，也就是独立（independent）数据集，其中独立划分是在蛋白质层面完成的——意味着整个蛋白质，而不仅仅是单个变异，会被完全排除在训练集之外，只在测试阶段出现。 That protein-level holdout matters a lot, because it's the only way to check whether the model has learned something general about transmembrane biophysics rather than just memorizing which specific proteins tend to have pathogenic mutations. 这种蛋白质层面的留出（holdout）非常重要，因为这是检验模型是否真的学到了跨膜蛋白生物物理学的一般规律、而不是仅仅记住了哪些特定蛋白质更容易出现致病突变的唯一方法。 Hyperparameters were tuned only on training folds via 10-fold cross-validation and then frozen before touching the held-out test sets, which is the right order of operations to avoid leakage. 超参数只在训练折（training fold）上通过十折交叉验证（10-fold cross-validation）调优，然后在接触留出测试集之前就固定下来，这是避免数据泄漏（leakage）的正确操作顺序。 They also ran ablations: the full model with both ESM2 and ProtT5 consistently beat either PLM alone, showing the two embeddings carry complementary rather than redundant information, and a smaller ESM2-650M backbone performed comparably to the full-size version, which matters for anyone thinking about running this without a large compute budget. 他们还做了消融实验（ablation）：同时使用 ESM2 和 ProtT5 的完整模型始终优于单独使用任一个 PLM，说明这两种嵌入携带的是互补而非冗余的信息；而更小的 ESM2-650M 主干模型表现与完整版本相当，这对任何想在没有大量算力预算的情况下运行该方法的人来说都很重要。

JORDAN Did it actually deliver on the pathogenicity-prediction claim? 它在致病性预测这个核心目标上真的做到了吗？

ALEX Across the TMP benchmarks and under those protein-level group splits, Memo-Patho consistently outperformed the comparison predictors — general ones like MutPred2, PLM-integrated ones like E-SNPs&GO, structure-informed ones like AlphaMissense, and the TMP-specific baselines — reaching up to 0.93 accuracy. 在这些跨膜蛋白基准测试和蛋白质层面的分组划分下，Memo-Patho 持续优于对比方法——包括通用方法 MutPred2、整合 PLM 的方法 E-SNPs&GO、基于结构信息的方法 AlphaMissense，以及那些跨膜蛋白专用的基线方法——准确率最高达到 0.93。 The more interesting result for our purposes is the zero-shot transfer: they took the trained model, without any retraining, and applied it to an independent cohort of 55 variants in KCNQ1, a voltage-gated potassium ion channel, and it generalized well. 对我们来说更有意思的结果是零样本迁移（zero-shot transfer）：他们把训练好的模型，不做任何再训练，直接应用到 KCNQ1（一种电压门控钾离子通道）55 个变异组成的独立队列上，结果泛化效果良好。 They used an AlphaFold2-predicted structure of full-length KCNQ1 for visualizing where those 55 variants sit spatially, but explicitly noted that structure was for visualization only and was never fed into Memo-Patho as an input — the prediction itself stayed alignment-free and structure-free throughout. 他们使用 AlphaFold2 预测的 KCNQ1 全长结构来可视化这 55 个变异在空间上的分布，但明确说明这个结构只用于可视化，从未作为输入喂给 Memo-Patho——预测过程本身自始至终都是无比对、无结构依赖的。 That KCNQ1 transfer is the closest thing in the paper to a CFTR-relevant proof point, since both are multi-pass ion-channel-family transmembrane proteins the model never saw labeled examples from during training. 这次 KCNQ1 迁移测试是这篇论文中与 CFTR 最相关的证据，因为两者都是多次跨膜的离子通道家族蛋白，而模型在训练阶段从未见过来自它们的标注样本。

JORDAN Where does this land for the work Runyu actually has in front of him? 这项工作和 Runyu 手头实际的工作能对上吗？

ALEX This maps to exactly one current priority — the Karen Raraigh collaboration on CFTR variant interpretation and VUS pathogenicity prediction. 这正好对应上一个当前的优先事项——与 Karen Raraigh 合作开展的 CFTR 变异解读和 VUS 致病性预测。 CFTR is architecturally very close to what this method was built and validated for: a multi-pass transmembrane protein where MSA-based and structure-based predictors both face real headwinds, and where a large pool of CFTR2-curated benign and pathogenic missense variants already exists to seed exactly the kind of within-protein contrastive triplets the method depends on. 从结构上说，CFTR 与这个方法所针对和验证的目标非常接近：一个多次跨膜的蛋白质，基于 MSA 和基于结构的预测方法都面临实际的阻力，而且已经存在大量 CFTR2 精心整理的良性和致病错义变异，正好可以用来构建该方法所依赖的那种蛋白质内部对比三元组。 The buildable artifact is a CFTR-specific replication: pull CFTR missense variants and their CFTR2/ClinVar classifications, embed them with ESM2 (the smaller 650M backbone, given the ablation result), build within-protein contrastive triplets from the already-labeled variants, and train a compact classifier to score CFTR VUS as a complementary, alignment-free signal alongside AlphaMissense and REVEL for Karen's harmonization effort. 可搭建的原型是一个 CFTR 专属的复现：抓取 CFTR 的错义变异及其 CFTR2/ClinVar 分类结果，用 ESM2（根据消融实验结果，选用较小的 650M 主干模型）生成嵌入，从已标注的变异中构建蛋白质内部对比三元组，训练一个轻量分类器，为 CFTR 的 VUS 打分，作为 AlphaMissense 和 REVEL 之外的一个互补的、无比对依赖的信号，服务于 Karen 的协调整理工作。 Rough difficulty is medium — the architecture is described in enough detail to reimplement, PLM embeddings are freely available, and CFTR has enough labeled variants to build triplets from, but reproducing their exact ablation-tested pipeline and tuning it well would take real iteration, not just a weekend script. 大致难度是中等——架构描述得足够详细，可以复现；PLM 嵌入是免费可用的；CFTR 也有足够多已标注的变异可以用来构建三元组；但要复现他们经过消融验证的完整流程并调好参数，需要真正的反复迭代，而不只是一个周末就能写完的脚本。

JORDAN What would we want to check before trusting this for CFTR specifically? 在把它真正用于 CFTR 之前，我们需要检查些什么？

ALEX A few things. 有几点。 First, whether any ABC-transporter-family proteins, which CFTR belongs to, appeared anywhere in Memo-Patho's training data — if so, the protein-level "Ind" split might not fully prevent family-level leakage, and any CFTR-specific replication would need its own clean holdout. 首先，是否有任何属于 ABC 转运蛋白家族（CFTR 正是其中一员）的蛋白质出现在 Memo-Patho 的训练数据中——如果有，那么蛋白质层面的 "Ind" 划分可能无法完全防止家族层面的数据泄漏，任何针对 CFTR 的专属复现都需要自己建立干净的留出集。 Second, the paper itself flags that training data drawn from ClinVar and UniProt carries known biases — uneven representation across TMP families, a
