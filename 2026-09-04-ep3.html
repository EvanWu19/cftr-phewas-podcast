Ep3 (Track C) — CAH-specific growth charts: why general-population norms over-flag a disease cohort

2026-09-04 · Sarafoglou K et al. (2026) · Journal of Clinical Endocrinology and Metabolism · Full text (open access)

Children with classic congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH) grow differently from the general population — androgen excess accelerates bone maturation, and chronic supraphysiological glucocorticoid therapy suppresses growth-plate signaling — so this paper builds LMS-fitted, disease-specific height/weight/BMI reference charts from 515 patients and 8,692 visits across two pooled cohorts, precleaned with growthcleanr. The headline finding: CDC charts classify CAH children as overweight/obese at several-fold higher rates than CAH-specific charts do (9x for girls at age 4), an artifact of tall stature and increased lean mass rather than true adiposity, and adiposity rebound occurs earlier than the general-population norm (3.3-3.9 years vs. 5-6). The structural lesson transfers directly to any CF outlier pipeline: anchoring a disease-shifted cohort's implausible-value thresholds to a general-population reference doesn't just add noise, it manufactures systematic, directional over-flagging — pipeline stage (a), candidate generation. Buildable artifact: a disease-cohort-shift simulator using entirely public data.

Plain-English glossary

  • 21-hydroxylase deficiency — the enzyme defect underlying classic CAH; shunts cortisol precursors into the androgen synthesis pathway.（中文：经典型CAH的酶缺陷病因，把皮质醇合成前体分流进雄激素合成通路。）
  • LMS method / GAMLSS — the semiparametric growth-curve-fitting approach (lambda-mu-sigma) that CDC and WHO themselves use to build population reference charts, smoothing skewness/median/spread as continuous functions of age.（中文：CDC和WHO自身用于构建人群参考曲线图的半参数生长曲线拟合方法，将偏度、中位数和离散度拟合为随年龄连续变化的函数。）
  • Adiposity rebound — the point in early childhood (~5-6y in the general population) where BMI stops falling from its post-infancy dip and begins rising again; earlier rebound predicts higher later obesity risk.（中文：幼儿期BMI结束下降、重新上升的转折点，一般人群约在5-6岁；提前出现预示日后肥胖风险更高。）
  • Nodal-point analysis — comparing a disease-specific chart against a population reference at fixed ages using quantile tests.（中文：在固定年龄点用分位数检验比较疾病专属曲线图与人群参考标准的方法。）
  • growthcleanr (kept in English; prior-episode term) — the within-child EWMA-based pediatric growth-data cleaning tool, used here as unremarked upstream preprocessing before LMS modeling.（中文：一种基于个体内加权移动平均的儿科生长数据清洗工具，本文中作为LMS建模前不加特别说明的预处理步骤使用。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • LMS/GAMLSS chart construction — the same reference-chart-fitting method CDC/WHO use, applicable to any disease cohort with enough longitudinal visits.
  • growthcleanr as standard upstream preprocessing — clerical-error cleaning (same-day duplicates, carry-forward) run before any statistical modeling.
  • Nodal-point quantile testing — a template for quantifying divergence from a population reference at fixed ages.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation, primary. MVP: disease-cohort-shift simulator — take public pediatric reference data, synthetically shift the distribution in a disease-plausible direction, growthcleanr-clean it, LMS-fit it, and quantify how much a fixed-threshold outlier rule's flag rate changes purely from that shift. No CFFPR data involved — a feasibility/sensitivity check on the general method. Difficulty: S-M.

What to verify first

  • Multi-center treatment heterogeneity (GH, GnRH agonists, aromatase inhibitors) — the paper's own sensitivity analysis found no growth-pattern differences by treatment, but only at a coarse level.
  • Measurement precision across clinics using different stadiometers — a limitation the authors themselves flag.
  • No claims here about what CFFPR's current height/weight thresholds assume or how they perform on any CF subgroup — external methodological warning only.

Error types covered / not covered

Covered: reference-chart mismatch bias (population norm vs. disease-shifted cohort), stature/lean-mass-driven BMI misclassification. Not covered: individual-record data-entry errors, unit conversion, digit transposition, decimal shift, carry-forward (that layer is handled upstream by growthcleanr, not by this paper's own contribution).
