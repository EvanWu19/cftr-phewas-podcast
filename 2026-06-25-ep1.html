How did this episode land for you?

Blurb. Sosnay et al. (2013, Nature Genetics) is the empirical backbone of CFTR2 — the project that took 159 CFTR variants seen at allele frequency ≥0.01% across 39,696 CF patients and classified them using two independent lines of evidence: a clinical criterion (sweat chloride) and a functional criterion (CFTR maturation by Western blot plus chloride-current measurement, anchored on a ~10% function threshold). 127 of 159 variants met both criteria and were labeled CF-causing; a penetrance study in 2,188 fathers flagged 10 nonpenetrant variants and confirmed 147 had no nonpenetrance evidence. This is the labeled gold standard against which AlphaMissense, REVEL, CADD, and SpliceAI are still benchmarked. Where it lands: the Karen / variant-interpretation priority. The buildable artifact is a CFTR variant-label harmonizer that normalizes any variant notation to canonical HGVS and joins it against the current CFTR2 classification plus a predictor score.

Plain-English glossary

  • Allele frequency — how common a given variant is among all the gene copies in a population.（中文：某个变异在人群所有基因拷贝中出现的频率。）
  • Allelic heterogeneity — one disease being caused by many different variants in the same gene.（中文：同一种病由同一基因里许多不同变异引起。）
  • Complex allele — two or more variants sitting on the same gene copy, so their individual effects can't be separated.（中文：同一条基因拷贝上有两个及以上变异，单个效应无法拆分。）
  • Frameshift — an insertion/deletion that shifts the reading frame, garbling the protein downstream.（中文：插入或缺失打乱阅读框，使下游蛋白质错乱。）
  • Genotype / phenotype — the DNA sequence a person carries / the observable clinical traits that result.（中文：基因型是携带的DNA序列；表型是由此产生的可观察临床特征。）
  • HGVS — the standardized grammar for writing variants, e.g. p.Phe508del.（中文：HGVS 是书写变异的标准化规范，如 p.Phe508del。）
  • In-frame deletion — loss of a whole codon (one amino acid) without shifting the frame.（中文：整密码子缺失，丢一个氨基酸但不移码。）
  • Meconium ileus — not in this paper's glossary (see Track C).
  • Missense variant — a single-base change that swaps one amino acid for another.（中文：单碱基改变把一个氨基酸换成另一个，即错义变异。）
  • Nonsense variant — a change creating a premature stop codon.（中文：产生提前终止密码子的变异，即无义变异。）
  • Penetrance — the probability that carrying a variant actually produces the disease.（中文：携带某变异真正导致疾病的概率，即外显率。）
  • Premature termination codon (PTC) — an early stop signal that usually triggers destruction of the mRNA, so little protein is made.（中文：提前的终止信号，通常触发mRNA降解，几乎不产蛋白。）
  • Splice site (GT-AG) — the canonical nucleotides marking intron boundaries; variants here disrupt RNA splicing.（中文：标记内含子边界的标准核苷酸；此处变异破坏剪接。）
  • Sweat chloride — a standardized in-vivo measure of CFTR function; high values indicate CF.（中文：标准化的体内CFTR功能测量，数值高提示CF。）
  • Variant of uncertain significance (VUS) — a variant whose disease impact is unknown.（中文：致病影响未知的变异，即意义不明变异。）

Full citation

Sosnay PR, et al. Defining the disease liability of variants in the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator gene. Nat Genet. 2013;45(10):1160–1167. PMID: 23974870 · PMC: PMC3874936 · DOI: 10.1038/ng.2745

Methods / tools to lift

  • Two-evidence classification — combine an in-vivo clinical readout (sweat chloride) with a wet-lab functional readout (CFTR maturation + chloride current) to label variants; a useful template for any "is this variant real?" decision.
  • Penetrance-via-unaffected-carriers — using fathers of patients (who must have functional CFTR) as a natural control to detect nonpenetrant variants — an elegant design that transfers to other recessive conditions.
  • CFTR2 labeled set — the downstream resource: CF-causing / non-CF-causing / varying labels for benchmarking predictors.
  • MALDI-ToF multiplex genotyping (Sequenom iPLEX) — the assay used to screen all 159 variants.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP: CFTR variant-label harmonizer — input a list of CFTR variant strings in any notation (legacy F508del, HGVS p.Phe508del, genomic coords); normalize each to one canonical HGVS ID; output a table joining canonical ID × current CFTR2 label × AlphaMissense score. Difficulty: S–M (normalization edge cases are the work).

What to verify first

  • Join against the current CFTR2 release, not the 2013 snapshot — labels have been updated.
  • Cohort was 95% Caucasian — allele frequencies and the variant set skew European-ancestry; check how that distorts anything layered on top.
  • Legacy→HGVS mapping is trap-laden (same protein change, different nucleotide origin) — validate the harmonizer against a hand-checked set before trusting it.
  • The ~10% function threshold is a soft boundary, not a wall — variants near the edge (residual-function / modulator-response) need case-by-case treatment.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的"读论文"小屋。 Today is a Track A episode, the variant-interpretation track. 今天是 Track A，也就是"变异解读"这条线。 We're going all the way back to a 2013 paper that quietly became the rulebook everyone else cites. 我们要一路回到 2013 年的一篇论文，它悄悄地变成了后来所有人都引用的"规则手册"。 The paper is "Defining the disease liability of variants in the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator gene," first author Patrick Sosnay, published in Nature Genetics. 这篇论文叫《界定 CFTR 基因变异的致病责任》，第一作者是 Patrick Sosnay，发表在《自然·遗传学》(Nature Genetics) 上。 We're reading it because it's the empirical backbone of CFTR2 — the gold-standard variant classification that every CFTR predictor is still benchmarked against. 我们读它，是因为它是 CFTR2 的实证基础——而 CFTR2 是一套金标准的变异分类，至今所有 CFTR 预测模型都拿它来做基准 (benchmark)。

JORDAN Before we go further, I want the vocabulary straight. 在我们继续之前，我想先把术语弄清楚。 When you say "variant," what exactly are we talking about? 你说的"变异 (variant)"到底指什么？ ALEX: A variant is just a place where one person's DNA sequence differs from the reference. 变异就是某个人的 DNA 序列在某个位置和参考序列不一样的地方。 In CFTR, almost 2,000 such variants had been reported by 2013, but only a few dozen had real evidence of actually causing cystic fibrosis. 在 CFTR 基因里，到 2013 年已经报告了将近 2000 个这样的变异，但只有几十个有真正的证据能证明它们确实会导致囊性纤维化 (cystic fibrosis)。 JORDAN: And the rest? 那剩下的呢？ ALEX: The rest are what clinicians call VUS — variants of uncertain significance. 剩下的就是临床上说的 VUS（意义不明的变异，variants of uncertain significance）。 You see the spelling change in a patient's gene, but you genuinely don't know if it's harmless or disease-causing. 你能看到病人基因里的拼写变化，但你真的不知道它是无害的还是致病的。 JORDAN: And HGVS — I keep seeing that. 还有 HGVS——我老是看到这个词。 ALEX: HGVS（变异命名的标准化书写规范）is the agreed grammar for writing variants down. HGVS 是大家约定好的、写下变异的"标准语法"。 So "p.Phe508del" means: at protein position 508, the phenylalanine amino acid is deleted. 比如 "p.Phe508del" 的意思是：在蛋白质第 508 位，苯丙氨酸 (phenylalanine) 这个氨基酸被删掉了。 The old clinical world called that same thing "F508del" or "ΔF508." 旧的临床世界把同一个东西叫 "F508del" 或 "ΔF508"。 Same variant, three spellings — and that mismatch is going to matter a lot later. 同一个变异，三种写法——这个不一致后面会非常关键。

JORDAN So what was actually broken before this paper? 那在这篇论文之前，到底有什么是"坏掉"的？ ALEX: The core problem is allelic heterogeneity. 核心问题是等位基因异质性 (allelic heterogeneity)。 That means one disease — cystic fibrosis — can be caused by a huge number of different variants in the same gene. 意思是同一种病——囊性纤维化——可以由同一个基因里大量不同的变异引起。 When a diagnostic lab sequenced CFTR and found a rare spelling change, they often couldn't tell the family whether it mattered. 当诊断实验室给 CFTR 测序、发现一个罕见的拼写变化时，他们常常无法告诉这个家庭：这到底要不要紧。 JORDAN: And there was already a database, right? 而且当时已经有一个数据库了，对吧？ ALEX: Yes — the Cystic Fibrosis Mutation Database, started in 1990, right after CFTR was discovered. 对——囊性纤维化突变数据库 (CFMD)，1990 年就建立了，就在 CFTR 被发现之后不久。 But it was a catalog of nucleotide variation with almost no phenotype attached. 但它只是一个核苷酸变异的目录，几乎没有挂上任何表型 (phenotype) 信息。 It told you a variant existed; it didn't tell you what it did to a patient. 它告诉你某个变异存在；但没告诉你它对病人做了什么。 JORDAN: So the gap is: lots of spellings, almost no proof of meaning. 所以这个缺口就是：一堆拼写，几乎没有关于含义的证据。 ALEX: Exactly, and next-generation sequencing was about to make that gap explode. 正是如此，而下一代测序 (next-generation sequencing) 马上就要让这个缺口爆炸式扩大。 Every new patient sequenced means more rare variants of unknown meaning. 每多测一个病人，就多一批意义不明的罕见变异。 The CFTR2 project was the answer: assemble clinical data plus variants from national registries and big clinics, and decide, variant by variant, whether each one causes disease. CFTR2 项目就是答案：把来自各国登记处和大型诊所的临床数据加上变异汇集起来，一个变异一个变异地判定它是否致病。

JORDAN Tell me about the data they pulled together. 跟我说说他们汇集的数据。 ALEX: This is the part that still impresses me. 这部分到现在还让我印象深刻。 They collected genotype and phenotype data on 39,696 cystic fibrosis patients from registries and clinics across North America and Europe. 他们收集了来自北美和欧洲登记处与诊所的 39,696 名囊性纤维化病人的基因型 (genotype) 和表型数据。 That was about 57% of the roughly 70,000 CF patients estimated to exist at the time. 那大约是当时估计存在的约 7 万名 CF 病人的 57%。 JORDAN: That's an enormous fraction of an entire disease population. 那是整个疾病人群中非常大的一部分。 ALEX: It is, and it's why the result carries weight. 确实，这也是为什么这个结果有分量。 In that population they saw 1,044 distinct CFTR variants. 在这个人群里他们看到了 1,044 个不同的 CFTR 变异。 The single variant p.Phe508del accounted for 70% of all the disease alleles. 单单 p.Phe508del 这一个变异，就占了所有致病等位基因的 70%。 JORDAN: So one variant dominates, and then a very long tail. 所以一个变异占主导，然后是一条很长的尾巴。 ALEX: A classic long tail. 经典的长尾。 They focused on the 159 variants that each had an allele frequency of at least 0.01% in the cohort. 他们聚焦在那 159 个变异——每一个在队列里的等位基因频率 (allele frequency) 至少有 0.01%。 Those 159 are the ones common enough to actually study and to matter clinically. 这 159 个就是足够常见、可以真正研究、并且临床上有意义的那些。 JORDAN: Why draw the line at frequency rather than just doing all 1,044? 为什么按频率划线，而不是干脆把 1,044 个全做了？ ALEX: Two reasons, one statistical and one practical. 两个原因，一个统计上的，一个实际上的。 Statistically, a variant seen in only one or two patients gives you almost no clinical signal. 统计上，一个只在一两个病人身上见到的变异几乎给不了你临床信号。 Practically, each missense variant needed its own wet-lab experiment, and you cannot run two thousand of those. 实际上，每个错义变异都需要它自己的湿实验，而你没法跑两千个这样的实验。 So frequency is the triage filter that makes the whole project tractable. 所以频率就是那个让整个项目变得可处理的分诊过滤器。 JORDAN: And the tradeoff is the rare tail stays unresolved. 而代价是那条罕见的尾巴仍然悬而未决。 ALEX: Exactly — and that unresolved tail is still where most VUS work lives today. 正是如此——而那条没解决的尾巴，至今仍是大多数 VUS 工作所在的地方。

JORDAN So how do you decide whether a variant "causes" CF? 那你怎么判定一个变异"导致"了 CF 呢？ ALEX: They used two independent kinds of evidence: clinical and functional. 他们用了两类相互独立的证据：临床的和功能的。 The clinical criterion was sweat chloride concentration. 临床标准是汗液氯离子浓度 (sweat chloride concentration)。 Sweat chloride is a standardized in-vivo readout of how well CFTR is working — high chloride means the channel is failing. 汗液氯离子是一个标准化的体内读数，反映 CFTR 工作得好不好——氯离子高就意味着这个通道在失灵。 So patients carrying a given variant could be placed on the CF severity spectrum by their sweat chloride. 所以携带某个变异的病人，可以根据汗液氯离子被放到 CF 严重程度谱系上。 JORDAN: And the functional side? 那功能这一边呢？ ALEX: That's lab work on the protein itself. 那是对蛋白质本身做的实验室工作。 First they split the variants by mechanism. 他们先按机制把变异分开。 They set aside two common polythymidine-tract variants in intron 9 — the 5T and 7T variants — which had been studied to death already. 他们把内含子 9 里两个常见的多聚胸腺嘧啶段变异——5T 和 7T 变异——单独拎出来，因为这俩早就被研究透了。 Of the remaining 157, eighty were predicted to introduce a premature termination codon. 剩下的 157 个里，有 80 个被预测会引入一个提前终止密码子 (premature termination codon, PTC)。 JORDAN: Meaning the protein gets cut short. 意思是蛋白质被提前截断了。 ALEX: Right — 35 nonsense variants, 15 in the canonical splice GT-AG sites, and 30 frameshift insertions or deletions. 对——35 个无义变异 (nonsense)、15 个落在标准剪接位点 GT-AG 上的、还有 30 个移码 (frameshift) 插入或缺失。 A premature termination codon usually triggers the cell to destroy the messenger RNA, so little or no protein is made. 提前终止密码子通常会让细胞把信使 RNA (mRNA) 销毁掉，所以几乎不产生蛋白质。 Those you can mostly call disease-causing on mechanism alone. 这些你基本上单凭机制就能叫它致病。 JORDAN: And the trickier ones? 那更棘手的呢？ ALEX: The other 67 were missense or in-frame: 65 missense changes that swap one amino acid for another, and 2 in-frame deletions that drop a single amino acid. 另外 67 个是错义 (missense) 或框内的：65 个错义变化把一个氨基酸换成另一个，还有 2 个框内缺失去掉一个氨基酸。 These still make a full-length protein, so you have to actually test each one. 这些仍然会造出全长的蛋白质，所以你必须真的去一个个测它们。 They expressed each variant CFTR in HeLa cells and in Fischer Rat Thyroid cells, and used Western blotting to see whether the protein matured properly. 他们把每个变异型 CFTR 在 HeLa 细胞和费舍尔大鼠甲状腺 (FRT) 细胞里表达，再用蛋白质印迹 (Western blot) 看蛋白质有没有正常成熟。 Then they measured chloride current in FRT cells for 63 of them to see if the channel actually conducts. 然后他们对其中 63 个测了 FRT 细胞里的氯离子电流，看这个通道到底导不导电。

ALEX Here's a design choice I'd push on. 这里有一个设计选择我想质疑一下。 They anchored "disease-causing" on a roughly 10% CFTR-function threshold. 他们把"致病"锚定在大约 10% 的 CFTR 功能阈值上。 That threshold came from primary airway cells of patients carrying nine established genotypes. 那个阈值来自携带九种既定基因型的病人的原代气道细胞。 JORDAN: Why push on that? 为什么要质疑这个？ ALEX: Because a single fixed threshold turns a continuous biological quantity into a binary label. 因为一个固定的单一阈值，把一个连续的生物学量变成了一个非黑即白的标签。 A variant sitting just above or just below 10% gets sorted into opposite bins, even though biologically they're almost identical. 一个刚好在 10% 上面或下面的变异会被分到相反的两类，尽管它们在生物学上几乎一模一样。 That edge is exactly where the residual-function and modulator-response questions live today. 而那条边界，恰恰就是今天"残余功能"和"调节剂响应"问题所在的地方。 It's a reasonable line — I'd just want it treated as a soft boundary, not a wall. 这是一条合理的线——我只是希望它被当作一条软边界，而不是一堵墙。 JORDAN: Why does the missense category need so much more work than the truncating ones? 为什么错义这一类比截断那一类需要多那么多工作？ ALEX: Because truncating variants are mechanistically obvious — no protein, no function, disease. 因为截断变异在机制上是显而易见的——没蛋白质、没功能、得病。 A missense change is subtle: the protein still gets made, and it might fold fine and work, or fold fine and not conduct, or fail to mature at all. 一个错义变化很微妙：蛋白质照样被造出来，它可能折叠得好又能工作，或者折叠得好却不导电，或者根本无法成熟。 So you have to measure two separate things — does it mature, and does it conduct chloride. 所以你必须分别测两件事——它成不成熟，以及它导不导氯离子。 The Western blot catches the first failure and the chloride current catches the second. 蛋白质印迹抓住第一种失败，氯离子电流抓住第二种。 Together they give you a two-dimensional fingerprint of what's broken. 两者合起来，给你一个二维的指纹，告诉你哪里坏了。

JORDAN So what did the verdicts come out to? 那最后的判定结果是怎样的？ ALEX: Of the 159 variants, 127 met both the clinical and functional criteria and were classified as cystic-fibrosis-causing. 在 159 个变异里，有 127 个同时满足临床和功能两个标准，被归类为致囊性纤维化的。 JORDAN: And the leftover variants that didn't cleanly pass? 那些没有干净通过的剩余变异呢？ ALEX: For those, they ran a clever penetrance study using fathers of CF patients. 对那些，他们用 CF 病人的父亲做了一个巧妙的外显率 (penetrance) 研究。 The logic: a father of a CF child must himself have a working CFTR gene, because reduced CFTR function causes male infertility. 逻辑是：一个 CF 患儿的父亲，自己必须有一个正常工作的 CFTR 基因，因为 CFTR 功能下降会导致男性不育。 So a variant that keeps showing up on the healthy, non-transmitted gene of fathers is probably not fully penetrant. 所以一个总是出现在父亲那条健康的、未遗传出去的基因上的变异，很可能不是完全外显的。 JORDAN: How many fathers? 多少个父亲？ ALEX: They genotyped 2,188 fathers; about 100 carried one of the 159 variants in trans with an accepted CF-causing variant. 他们给 2,188 个父亲做了基因分型；大约 100 个父亲携带这 159 个变异之一，并与一个公认的致病变异处于反式 (in trans)。 Ten variants showed up as nonpenetrant — each landing on the healthy gene of at least two fathers. 有 10 个变异表现为非外显——每一个都至少落在两个父亲的健康基因上。 In the end, 147 variants had no evidence of nonpenetrance. 最后，有 147 个变异没有任何非外显的证据。 JORDAN: And the famously messy ones? 那些出了名"难搞"的呢？ ALEX: They flagged variants like p.Asp1152His as variable-penetrance, and complex alleles like the p.Arg74Trp pairing where you can't separate one variant's effect from its partner's. 他们把像 p.Asp1152His 这样的变异标为可变外显，把像 p.Arg74Trp 这样的复杂等位基因 (complex allele) 也标出来——在这种情况下你没法把一个变异的效应和它搭档的分开。 That honesty about uncertainty is, frankly, the most useful part of the paper. 老实说，这种对不确定性的诚实，是这篇论文里最有用的部分。

ALEX Let me say where this lands for us, and I'll name one priority: Karen and variant interpretation. 让我说说这对我们意味着什么，并且我点名一个优先事项：Karen 和变异解读。 CFTR2 gave the field a labeled gold-standard set — variants tagged CF-causing, non-CF-causing, or varying.
