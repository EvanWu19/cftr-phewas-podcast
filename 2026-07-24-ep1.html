How did this episode land for you?

Track A — Compressing the collective knowledge of ESM into a single protein language model

Blurb. Dinh, Jang, Zaitlen and Ntranos (Nature Methods, 2026) challenge the assumption that sequence-only protein language models are intrinsically weaker than hybrid predictors that require multiple sequence alignments or 3D structure. They introduce a co-distillation framework: run the whole ESM family, aggregate each variant by taking the most confident (minimum-LLR) vote across models (ESMIN), then retrain every model to match that target so each absorbs the evolutionary signals its siblings caught. Iterating converges the family into a single 3-billion-parameter model, VESM-3B, that matches or beats 24 competing methods on ClinVar and ProteinGym DMS using sequence alone. Crucially, VESM's accuracy does not decay for rare variants and does not borrow allele-frequency evidence, so it beats AlphaMissense on the clinically hard rare-benign-vs-rare-pathogenic task. Where it lands: the Karen / variant-interpretation priority — a CFTR VUS re-scoring notebook that runs VESM-3B over CFTR missense variants of uncertain significance and ranks them by disagreement with their current CFTR2 classification.

Plain-English glossary

  • Allele frequency — how common a variant is in the population; also used as clinical evidence, which creates circularity when a model trains on it.（中文：等位基因频率——一个变异在人群中有多常见；它同时也被当作临床证据，模型若拿它训练就会产生数据循环。）
  • DMS (deep mutational scanning) — a lab assay measuring the functional effect of thousands of mutations in one protein at once.（中文：DMS——一次性测量一个蛋白质里成千上万个突变功能效应的实验室实验。）
  • HGVS — the standardized notation for writing a variant, e.g. p.Gly551Asp for a protein change.（中文：HGVS——书写变异的标准化命名规范，例如蛋白质改变写作 p.Gly551Asp。）
  • LLR (log-likelihood ratio) — the model's log-scale preference for the wild-type amino acid over the mutant; very negative means "damaging."（中文：LLR——模型在对数尺度上对野生型氨基酸相对突变氨基酸的偏好；非常负表示"有害"。）
  • Missense variant — a single-letter DNA change that swaps one amino acid for another in the protein.（中文：错义变异——一个 DNA 字母的改变，把蛋白质里的一个氨基酸换成另一个。）
  • MSA (multiple sequence alignment) — the same protein from many species lined up to reveal evolutionarily conserved positions.（中文：MSA——把来自多个物种的同一蛋白质对齐，用来显示进化上保守的位置。）
  • Pathogenicity — whether a variant causes disease (pathogenic) or not (benign).（中文：致病性——一个变异是否致病。）
  • PLM (protein language model) — a neural network trained on raw protein sequences that learns which amino acids are expected at each position.（中文：PLM——在原始蛋白质序列上训练、学习每个位置该出现哪些氨基酸的神经网络。）
  • Protein domain — a conserved structural/functional unit within a protein (e.g. KRAB, BRICHOS).（中文：蛋白质结构域——蛋白质内保守的结构/功能单元，例如 KRAB、BRICHOS。）
  • Splice variant — a change that alters how RNA is cut and joined rather than swapping an amino acid.（中文：剪接变异——改变 RNA 剪切拼接方式的变异，而非替换氨基酸。）
  • VEP (variant effect prediction) — scoring how damaging a given amino acid change is likely to be.（中文：VEP——给一个氨基酸改变可能有多大危害打分。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant that cannot currently be confidently called pathogenic or benign.（中文：VUS——目前无法有把握判为致病或良性的变异。）

Full citation

Dinh T, Jang SK, Zaitlen N, Ntranos V. Compressing the collective knowledge of ESM into a single protein language model. Nature Methods. 2026. PMID: 41912799 · PMC13076216 · doi:10.1038/s41592-026-03050-9 · Code: github.com/ntranoslab/vesm

Methods / tools to lift

  • ESMIN aggregation — per-variant minimum-LLR across an ensemble; amplifies a signal only one model caught, instead of diluting it via averaging.
  • Co-distillation — retrain every ensemble member to match the ensemble's maximum-confidence target; each model learns its siblings' blind spots.
  • Parameter-efficient fine-tuning — freeze the backbone, train only the last hidden layer + LM head (~3% of params); a 35M model finishes in ~35 min on one H100.
  • VESM-3B / smaller VESM (650M/150M/35M) — released, sequence-only variant-effect models; run on a single GPU.
  • Regression-against-effect-size — correlate per-variant scores with Genebass summary-statistic β coefficients to test whether scores track continuous phenotype severity from summary stats alone.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / variant interpretation. MVP (paste-ready): "CFTR VUS re-scoring notebook — input: CFTR missense VUS list (HGVS protein) from CFTR2/ClinVar; run VESM-3B (github.com/ntranoslab/vesm) over the CFTR protein sequence; output: ranked table of each VUS with its VESM score vs current CFTR2 class, sorted by disagreement. Difficulty S–M (single-GPU inference, one sequence)."

What to verify first

  • CFTR-specific calibration — benchmark VESM on CFTR variants that already have gold-standard functional data (CFTR2) before trusting it on unknowns.
  • Scope — VESM scores missense only; splicing/frameshift/nonsense CF variants are out of scope, so this covers one lane of the genotype.
  • Continuous-phenotype transfer — the effect-size regression result is UK Biobank blood biomarkers; unverified whether it transfers to CF traits.
  • No registry internals — all validation is on public CFTR data; nothing here assumes anything about registry genotype internals.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读书间。 Today's Track A paper is "Compressing the collective knowledge of ESM into a single protein language model," by Tung Dinh, Se-Kyeong Jang, Noah Zaitlen and Vasilis Ntranos, published in Nature Methods in 2026. 今天的 Track A 论文是《把 ESM 家族的集体知识压缩进一个蛋白质语言模型》，作者是 Tung Dinh、Se-Kyeong Jang、Noah Zaitlen 和 Vasilis Ntranos，2026 年发表在《自然·方法》上。 We're reading it because it directly attacks the problem at the center of the Karen collaboration: predicting whether a protein-changing variant is harmful, using only the protein sequence. 我们读它，是因为它正面攻击了 Karen 合作的核心问题：仅凭蛋白质序列，去预测一个改变蛋白质的变异是否有害。 And the code is fully open. 而且代码是完全开源的。

JORDAN Before we get into what they built, give me the vocabulary. 在进入他们做了什么之前，先给我把词汇讲清楚。 What exactly is "variant effect prediction"? 到底什么是"变异效应预测"（variant effect prediction）？

ALEX Variant effect prediction, or VEP, is the task of taking a single amino acid change in a protein and scoring how damaging it is likely to be. 变异效应预测，简称 VEP，就是拿到蛋白质里的一个氨基酸改变，然后给它打分，评估它可能有多大危害。 The specific changes we care about here are missense variants — a single DNA letter change that swaps one amino acid for another in the protein. 我们这里关心的具体改变是错义变异 (missense variant)——一个 DNA 字母的改变，把蛋白质里的一个氨基酸换成了另一个。 A protein language model, or PLM, is a neural network trained on hundreds of millions of raw protein sequences, the way a text language model is trained on sentences. 蛋白质语言模型 (protein language model)，简称 PLM，是一种在数亿条原始蛋白质序列上训练出来的神经网络，就像文本语言模型是在句子上训练出来的一样。 It learns which amino acids are "expected" at each position, so when a variant swaps in an unexpected one, the model flags it. 它学会了在每个位置上"应该"出现哪些氨基酸，所以当一个变异换进了一个意料之外的氨基酸时，模型就会把它标记出来。 The score they use is the LLR — the log-likelihood ratio — which here just means the log of how much the model prefers the original amino acid over the mutant. 他们用的分数是 LLR（对数似然比，衡量模型有多偏好原始氨基酸而不是突变氨基酸的一个对数量）。 A very negative LLR means "the model is confident the original belongs there, so this mutation is suspicious." 一个非常负的 LLR 意味着"模型确信原来的氨基酸就该在那儿，所以这个突变很可疑"。

JORDAN And I keep hearing "sequence-only" versus "hybrid." 我一直听到"仅序列"和"混合"这两种说法。 What's the distinction? 它们的区别是什么？

ALEX A sequence-only model sees nothing but the string of amino acids. 一个仅序列模型只看得到那一串氨基酸字母。 A hybrid model additionally feeds in a multiple sequence alignment — MSA, which is a stack of the same protein from many species lined up to reveal which positions evolution has kept fixed — and often a 3D protein structure. 一个混合模型还会额外喂进一个多序列比对 (multiple sequence alignment，简称 MSA，把来自许多物种的同一个蛋白质对齐叠放，用来显示进化保留了哪些固定位置)，通常还会喂进一个三维蛋白质结构。 The prevailing wisdom before this paper was that hybrids win because that extra evolutionary and structural context is worth a lot. 在这篇论文之前，主流看法是混合模型更强，因为那些额外的进化和结构背景很值钱。 The two benchmarks you'll hear are ClinVar, a public database of variants that clinicians have labeled pathogenic or benign, and ProteinGym DMS. 你会听到的两个基准是 ClinVar（一个由临床医生标注为致病或良性的变异公共数据库）和 ProteinGym DMS。 DMS stands for deep mutational scanning — a lab experiment that measures the functional effect of thousands of mutations in one protein at once. DMS 指的是深度突变扫描 (deep mutational scanning)——一个实验室实验，一次性测量一个蛋白质里成千上万个突变的功能效应。

JORDAN Okay, so what was actually bugging them about the state of the field? 好，那到底是这个领域的什么现状让他们不满？

ALEX The top-scoring variant predictors — SaProt, TranceptEVE, AlphaMissense, PrimateAI-3D — are all hybrids. 得分最高的变异预测器——SaProt、TranceptEVE、AlphaMissense、PrimateAI-3D——全都是混合模型。 They lean on structure, alignments, or population genetics data to get their accuracy. 它们靠结构、比对或群体遗传学数据来换取准确率。 That reliance creates three real problems. 这种依赖带来了三个实实在在的问题。 First, availability: experimental 3D structures exist for only a small fraction of proteins, and good alignments are hard to build for many others. 第一，可得性：只有一小部分蛋白质有实验测定的三维结构，而许多其他蛋白质很难构建出好的比对。 Second, brittleness: when you strip the structure and MSA away from models like SaProt and TranceptEVE, their performance collapses, which tells you the sequence backbone alone was weak. 第二，脆弱性：当你把结构和 MSA 从 SaProt、TranceptEVE 这类模型上剥掉时，它们的表现会崩塌，这说明它们光靠序列骨干是很弱的。 Third — and this one matters most for clinical genetics — data circularity. 第三——而这一点对临床遗传学最要紧——数据循环性。 AlphaMissense is trained partly on human and primate allele-frequency data. AlphaMissense 的训练部分用到了人类和灵长类的等位基因频率数据。 An allele frequency is just how common a variant is in the population. 等位基因频率 (allele frequency) 说白了就是一个变异在人群中有多常见。 But allele frequency is also one of the pieces of evidence clinicians use to call a variant benign in the first place. 但等位基因频率同时也是临床医生用来把一个变异判为良性的证据之一。 So a model that learned from frequency, then gets tested on frequency-influenced labels, is partly grading its own homework. 所以一个从频率里学习、然后又在受频率影响的标签上被测试的模型，在某种程度上是在给自己批改作业。

ALEX The authors' bet was that sequence-only ESM models are not fundamentally weaker — they just aren't reading their own knowledge out efficiently. 作者的赌注是：仅序列的 ESM 模型并非天生更弱——它们只是没有高效地把自己已有的知识读出来。 ESM stands for evolutionary scale modeling, a well-known family of open protein language models. ESM 指的是进化尺度建模 (evolutionary scale modeling)，一个著名的开源蛋白质语言模型家族。 The insight is that different ESM models have different blind spots. 关键洞察是：不同的 ESM 模型各有各的盲区。 They show one model correctly flags a conserved KRAB protein domain as sensitive while another misses it entirely, and for a different domain the roles flip. 他们展示了一个模型能正确地把保守的 KRAB 蛋白质结构域标记为敏感，而另一个模型完全漏掉它；换一个结构域，两者的角色又反过来。

JORDAN So the knowledge is scattered across the family, and no single model has all of it. 所以知识是散落在整个家族里的，没有哪一个模型拥有全部。

ALEX Exactly, and the whole paper is about gathering it into one model. 正是如此，整篇论文讲的就是把这些知识汇聚进一个模型。 The first move is aggregation. 第一步是聚合。 For every possible mutation they run all eleven ESM models and, instead of averaging the scores, they take the minimum LLR — the single most confident "this is damaging" vote across the family. 对每一个可能的突变，他们运行全部十一个 ESM 模型，然后不是取平均分，而是取最小的 LLR——也就是整个家族里最有把握的那一票"这是有害的"。 They call that ESMIN. 他们把这叫做 ESMIN。 The intuition is that averaging dilutes a subtle signal that only one or two models caught, whereas taking the most confident vote amplifies it. 直觉是：取平均会把只有一两个模型捕捉到的微弱信号稀释掉，而取最有把握的那一票则会把它放大。 They back this with a small theoretical model showing minimum-aggregation wins specifically when pathogenic scores vary more across models than benign scores do — which is what ESM models actually do. 他们用一个小的理论模型来支撑这一点，证明当致病分数在模型间的波动大于良性分数时，取最小值的聚合就会占优——而这恰好是 ESM 模型的真实特性。

ALEX The second and central move is co-distillation. 第二步、也是核心的一步，是协同蒸馏 (co-distillation)。 Distillation normally means training a small "student" model to imitate a big "teacher." 蒸馏通常是指训练一个小的"学生"模型去模仿一个大的"老师"模型。 Here every model is both student and teacher: each one is retrained to match the ESMIN maximum-confidence target, so each model absorbs the signals its siblings caught but it had missed. 这里每个模型既是学生又是老师：每个模型都被重新训练去匹配 ESMIN 这个最大置信度目标，于是每个模型都吸收了它的兄弟模型捕捉到、而它自己漏掉的信号。 Critically, they freeze most of the network and fine-tune only the last hidden layer and the output head — roughly three percent of the parameters — so training is cheap. 关键在于，他们冻结了网络的大部分，只微调最后一个隐藏层和输出头——大约百分之三的参数——所以训练很便宜。 One design choice I'd push on: they precompute the ESMIN targets once and hold them fixed, rather than recomputing as models improve. 有一个设计选择我想质疑：他们把 ESMIN 目标预先算好一次然后固定住，而不是随着模型改进而重新计算。 That's efficient, but it means later training rounds are chasing a slightly stale target. 这很高效，但也意味着后面的训练轮次是在追一个略微过时的目标。 They handle that by doing additional rounds of averaging co-distillation, which converges everything into one 3-billion-parameter model they call VESM-3B. 他们的应对办法是再做几轮取平均的协同蒸馏，最终把一切收敛进一个 30 亿参数的模型，他们称之为 VESM-3B。

JORDAN Why does taking the single most confident vote beat averaging — averaging usually feels safer? 为什么取最有把握的那一票会胜过取平均——取平均通常感觉更稳妥？

ALEX That intuition is right in most ensembles, and this is a nice exception worth understanding. 在大多数集成里那个直觉是对的，而这是一个值得理解的漂亮的例外。 Their argument is about asymmetry between the two classes. 他们的论证关乎两个类别之间的不对称。 For truly pathogenic variants, the eleven models disagree a lot — one model detected the conserved domain, others missed it — so the damaging votes are spread out. 对于真正致病的变异，十一个模型之间分歧很大——某个模型探测到了那个保守结构域，其他的漏掉了——所以"有害"的票是散开的。 For benign variants, the models mostly agree it's fine, so those votes are tightly clustered. 对于良性变异，模型大多一致认为没问题，所以那些票紧紧聚在一起。 When the pathogenic side is much more dispersed than the benign side, reaching for the single most extreme vote pulls the true pathogenics away from the benigns and increases class separation more than averaging would. 当致病一侧比良性一侧散得多时，去够那单一的、最极端的一票，会把真正的致病变异从良性变异那里拉开，其增加的类别间距比取平均更大。 Averaging, by contrast, would blur that one sharp signal back into the crowd. 相比之下，取平均会把那一个尖锐的信号重新模糊进人群里。 Interestingly, after co-distillation the asymmetry shrinks, and at that point averaging becomes the better choice — which is why their later rounds switch to it. 有意思的是，协同蒸馏之后这种不对称性缩小了，到那时取平均反而成了更好的选择——这就是为什么他们后面的轮次切换到了取平均。

JORDAN And does gathering all that scattered knowledge actually pay off on the benchmarks? 那把这些散落的知识汇聚起来，在基准上真的有回报吗？

ALEX The numbers are strong. 数字很有说服力。 The smallest model, ESM2 with 8 million parameters, jumped on ClinVar from an AUC of 65% to nearly 88% after co-distillation. 最小的模型，800 万参数的 ESM2，在 ClinVar 上经过协同蒸馏后，AUC 从 65% 跳到了将近 88%。 The converged VESM-3B matched or beat 24 competing methods, including the hybrid heavyweights, using sequence alone. 收敛后的 VESM-3B 仅凭序列就追平或击败了 24 个竞争方法，包括那些混合模型的重量级选手。 At the 90%-accuracy operating point, VESM-3B could confidently annotate 90% of ClinVar variants, versus 79% for the best previous sequence-only model. 在 90% 准确率的工作点上，VESM-3B 能有把握地标注 90% 的 ClinVar 变异，而之前最好的仅序列模型只能标注 79%。 The head-to-head with AlphaMissense is the part relevant to us. 与 AlphaMissense 的正面对决是与我们最相关的部分。 AlphaMissense's advantage shrank as they filtered to rarer variants, exposing its allele-frequency dependence, while VESM-3B held steady across the whole frequency spectrum. 随着筛选到更罕见的变异，AlphaMissense 的优势缩小了，暴露出它对等位基因频率的依赖，而 VESM-3B 在整个频率区间都保持稳定。 On rare-benign versus rare-pathogenic discrimination — the clinically hard case — the sequence-only models won. 在罕见良性 vs 罕见致病的区分上——这个临床上最难的情形——仅序列的模型胜出了。

ALEX And the last result is the one that reaches toward registry work. 最后一个结果则伸向了登记库的工作。 They took UK Biobank summary statistics from Genebass and asked whether VESM scores track the actual size of a variant's effect on continuous clinical traits, like blood biomarkers. 他们拿了来自 Genebass 的英国生物样本库 (UK Biobank) 汇总统计数据，去问 VESM 分数是否能追踪一个变异对连续临床性状（比如血液生物标志物）的实际效应大小。 They regressed the predicted scores against measured effect sizes and found VESM captured the correct direction of effect in 167 of 169 gene-phenotype pairs. 他们把预测分数对测得的效应量做回归，发现 VESM 在 169 个基因-表型对里有 167 个捕捉到了正确的效应方向。 About a third of those associations were ones a standard missense burden test had missed. 其中大约三分之一的关联是标准的错义负荷检验漏掉的。

JORDAN So where does this land for us right now? 那这对我们当下落在哪里？

ALEX This lands squarely on the Karen / variant-interpretation priority. 这正好落在 Karen / 变异解读这个优先事项上。 The reason is the allele-frequency point. 原因就是等位基因频率那一点。 CF variant interpretation lives and dies on rare and ultra-rare missense variants — many CFTR variants of uncertain significance are exactly the rare cases where frequency-based evidence is thin. CF 的变异解读成败就在于罕见和超罕见的错义变异——许多 CFTR 的意义未明变异 (variant of uncertain significance) 恰恰是频率证据稀薄的那些罕见情形。 A model whose accuracy does not decay for rare variants, and does not borrow circular frequency evidence, is exactly what you want in that setting. 一个准确率不会因变异罕见而下降、也不借用循环的频率证据的模型，正是在这种场景下你想要的。 So here's the buildable artifact. 所以这就是可动手做的产物。 A CFTR VUS re-scoring notebook: input is the list of CFTR missense variants of uncertain significance from CFTR2 or ClinVar in HGVS protein notation, the notebook runs VESM-3B from the public GitHub repo over the CFTR protein sequence to produce a per-variant score, and outputs a ranked table with each VUS scored, flagged against its current CFTR2 classification, and sorted by disagreement. 一个 CFTR VUS 重新打分的笔记本：输入是来自 CFTR2 或 ClinVar 的、以 HGVS 蛋白质记法书写的 CFTR 错义意义未明变异清单，笔记本从公开的 GitHub 仓库运行 VESM-3B，跑过 CFTR 蛋白质序列，为每个变异产生一个分数，输出一张排好序的表，每个 VUS 都被打分、与它当前的 CFTR2 分类做对比标记、并按分歧程度排序。 HGVS, by the way, is the standardized way of writing a variant — HGVS（顺便说一下，是书写变异的标准化规范）—— for a protein change it looks like p.Gly551Asp. 对一个蛋白质改变来说，它长得像 p.Gly551Asp。 Difficulty is small-to-medium: the model runs on a single GPU, and one CFTR sequence is a trivial input. 难度是小到中等：模型在单块 GPU 上就能跑，而一条 CFTR 序列是个微不足道的输入。 The output is a genetics-first conversation starter — a concrete, ranked disagreement list you could hand to Karen. 产物是一个以遗传学为先的对话开场——一张具体的、排好序的分歧清单，你可以直接递给 Karen。
