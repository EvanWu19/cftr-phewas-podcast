Blurb

OtoVCE (Ye, Wang, Chen; Frontiers in Genetics, 2026) puts a large language model inside a calibrated ACMG/AMP rule engine as a structured evidence extractor — not an end-to-end classifier — to pull case-level and functional evidence out of the published literature for hearing-loss variants of uncertain significance (VUS). A four-stage pipeline combines rule-encodable evidence (in silico predictors, gnomAD frequencies, UniProt domains, a dual-head protein language model) with literature-derived PS3/BS3/PS4 strength calls, each backed by a PubMed-verified citation, into a posterior ACMG/AMP classification. It lands as a directly portable blueprint for the Karen Raraigh CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration: a literature-evidence-extraction assistant that retrieves and verifies citations and drafts PS3/BS3/PS4 strength suggestions for human review, complementing — not replacing — the in silico predictors CFTR curation already uses.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP — The American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology rule framework that sorts a variant into one of five classes (pathogenic, likely pathogenic, uncertain significance, likely benign, benign) by combining independently weighted pieces of evidence.（中文:美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会与分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类规则体系,通过整合多条独立加权的证据,把变异归入五个类别之一。）
  • Brnich functional evidence rubric (Brnich rubric) — A published framework for grading the quality of a functional study before it's allowed to count as PS3 (pathogenic) or BS3 (benign) evidence.（中文:一套已发表的框架,用于在功能研究证据被计入PS3或BS3之前,先评估其质量等级。）
  • BS3 — The ACMG/AMP evidence code for strong functional-study evidence supporting a benign effect.（中文:ACMG/AMP证据代码,代表支持良性效应的强功能研究证据。）
  • ClinGen VCEP (Variant Curation Expert Panel) — A ClinGen-convened expert panel that takes the generic ACMG/AMP rules and calibrates gene- or disease-specific thresholds; CFTR has its own VCEP.（中文:ClinGen召集的专家小组,负责把通用ACMG/AMP规则针对特定基因或疾病领域进行阈值校准;CFTR有自己的VCEP。）
  • gnomAD — The reference population database most commonly used to look up a variant's allele frequency across large human cohorts.（中文:最常用的人群参考数据库,用于查询变异在大规模人群队列中的等位基因频率。）
  • Protein language model — A neural network trained on large numbers of protein sequences to learn which changes at a given position are typically tolerated versus disruptive.（中文:在大量蛋白质序列上训练的神经网络,用于学习某个位点上哪些变化通常是可耐受的、哪些是破坏性的。）
  • PS3 — The ACMG/AMP evidence code for strong functional-study evidence supporting a pathogenic effect.（中文:ACMG/AMP证据代码,代表支持致病效应的强功能研究证据。）
  • PS4 — The ACMG/AMP evidence code for case-control evidence, i.e., the variant's prevalence is significantly increased in affected individuals versus controls.（中文:ACMG/AMP证据代码,代表病例对照证据,即变异在患者中的比例显著高于对照组。）
  • UniProt domain annotation — The catalog, maintained in the UniProt database, of which regions of a protein correspond to known functional domains.（中文:UniProt数据库中维护的目录,标注蛋白质的哪些区域对应已知的功能结构域。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant for which the accumulated evidence isn't strong enough, in either direction, to classify it as pathogenic/likely pathogenic or benign/likely benign.（中文:意义不明确变异,指积累的证据不足以将其明确归为致病/可能致病,或良性/可能良性。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • ClinGen Hearing Loss VCEP rule set — the calibrated ACMG/AMP rule engine used to aggregate rule-encodable evidence.
  • Brnich functional evidence rubric — used to grade literature-derived functional evidence quality before it counts toward PS3/BS3.
  • Dual-head protein language model (pathogenicity + mechanism heads) — one head scores pathogenicity, the other predicts likely disease mechanism, which is then used as context for literature prompting.
  • Five-source literature retrieval + LLM-as-structured-evidence-extractor — retrieves candidate papers and extracts PS3/BS3/PS4 strength calls rather than an end-to-end verdict.
  • PubMed citation verification step — checks that every cited identifier actually exists before it's surfaced.
  • Bayesian combination of rule-based and LLM-derived evidence strengths — produces a posterior probability mapped to the five ACMG/AMP classes.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority: Karen Raraigh's CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration.

Buildable artifact: A literature-evidence-extraction assistant for CFTR VUS. MVP — input a CFTR variant, retrieve candidate papers from PubMed and a small set of other sources, use an LLM to extract case-level and functional-study evidence against a Brnich-style rubric, verify every citation exists and matches its claimed content, and output a draft PS3/BS3/PS4 strength suggestion with linked sources for human curator review. Difficulty: Medium (retrieval/citation-verification plumbing is straightforward; the hard part is building and validating a CFTR-specific extraction rubric).

What to verify first

  • Whether a CFTR-tuned mechanism head would need to be trained from scratch, or whether most of the literature-mining value survives without it.
  • Whether κ = 0.57 agreement with expert curation is good enough to run with light supervision, or whether it implies full human re-review is still required regardless.
  • Whether literature-retrieval coverage and precision tuned on a large hearing-loss literature base would hold up for a much smaller disease area like CF.
  • Whether a citation verified as real (existing in PubMed) could still misrepresent what the cited paper actually found — 100% ID verification is not the same as 100% claim fidelity.
  • The practical compute and curator-review time cost per variant, to know whether this is a net time saver or just a different shape of workload.
  • Note: none of the above is a claim about how the CFTR VCEP or the CFF Patient Registry actually operate today — those remain open questions, not something this episode asserts.
