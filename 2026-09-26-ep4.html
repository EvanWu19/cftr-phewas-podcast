Blurb

Horchani (2026, Front Artif Intell) is a critical review, not a new algorithm, organizing the fragmented active-learning (AL) literature along four axes: acquisition logic (which family of strategy selects — uncertainty, disagreement, diversity, etc.), supervision granularity (the actual unit of labor a query asks for — record, field, patch, pixel), operational regime (pool-based vs. streaming deployment), and evaluation realism (how faithfully reported gains reflect real annotation costs and constraints). It synthesizes — qualitatively, not via new experiments — trade-offs across major acquisition families and distills a compact expression for query value: informativeness plus representativeness plus diversity, minus cost, plus a shift/robustness term; the authors themselves note this is a descriptive synthesis, not a fitted or tested model. Key domain lesson (drawn mainly from medical imaging, computer vision, and NLP evidence): supervision granularity is inseparable from clinical/annotation effort — treating a volumetric mask and a pixel correction as equivalent "one query" hides true cost, and pure uncertainty sampling can over-sample redundant, spatially clustered regions. Lands primarily as pipeline stage (c), the human review budget — a framework for spending a fixed adjudication budget on the flagged records whose labels most improve the flagger — with a secondary touch on (d) evaluation via its evaluation-realism warning. Framed around a proposed active-learning acquisition-strategy comparison harness for a flagged-record review queue. Full text obtained via NCBI BioC PMC-OA.

Plain-English glossary

  • Acquisition logic — Which family of active-learning strategy is doing the sample selection (e.g. uncertainty-based, disagreement-based, diversity-based).（中文：获取逻辑，指究竟是哪一类主动学习策略在做样本选择（如基于不确定性、基于分歧、基于多样性）。）
  • Evaluation realism — How faithfully a benchmark's reported label-efficiency gains reflect real annotation costs, budgets, and workflow constraints, rather than an idealized setup.（中文：评估真实性，衡量一个基准测试所报告的标注效率收益，在多大程度上忠实反映真实标注成本、预算和工作流约束，而非理想化的设定。）
  • Operational regime — The deployment setting active learning operates under: a static one-shot pool of unlabeled data versus a live stream of continuously arriving candidates.（中文：操作场景，指主动学习运行所处的部署环境：静止不动的一次性未标注数据池，还是持续有新候选到达的实时数据流。）
  • Supervision granularity — The actual unit of human labor a single query requests (a whole record, one field, a bounding box, a pixel-level mask), independent of it being counted as "one query."（中文：监督粒度，指一次查询实际要求的人工劳动单位（一整条记录、单个字段、一个边界框、像素级标注掩膜），与它被算作"一次查询"这一计数方式无关。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • The four-axis taxonomy itself (acquisition logic, supervision granularity, operational regime, evaluation realism) as a checklist for describing any candidate active-learning setup precisely, before comparing strategies.
  • Explicitly separating "informativeness" from "representativeness/diversity" from "cost" as distinct, combinable components of query value, rather than a single acquisition score.
  • Treating supervision granularity as inseparable from real annotator/reviewer effort — a caution against label-count-only evaluation.
  • The evaluation-realism critique itself: checking whether a reported acquisition-strategy advantage was measured under seeds, budgets, and redundancy conditions resembling actual deployment.

What to verify first

  • The review's featured domain evidence is heavily imaging/vision-centric — do its specific lessons about supervision granularity and redundancy actually transfer to flat, tabular, longitudinal registry records rather than pixels or voxels?
  • The paper itself warns reported AL gains are sensitive to random seeds, budget schedules, and batch composition — any pilot comparison needs multiple seeds and honest variance reporting before trusting a single ranking.
  • What feature space should define "diversity" for a registry-encounter review queue (encounter-level summary statistics, raw time-series shape, or something else) — an open design choice this paper doesn't answer.
  • (Explicitly out of scope tonight) Whether and how any existing CFFPR review-budget process currently allocates human adjudication effort — a question for the people who own that pipeline.

Pipeline stage / error types

  • Pipeline stage: (c) the human review budget, primary; (d) evaluation, secondary.
  • Error types covered / not covered: Not an error-type detector itself — it says nothing about unit conversion, digit transposition, decimal shift, carry-forward, or real-clinical-event confusion directly. Its contribution is entirely about sequencing which already-flagged records get human attention first, so it can improve how quickly any of those error types get caught within a fixed review budget, without itself distinguishing between them.

JORDAN So what's the actual takeaway from the synthesis? 那么这份综合分析的实际结论是什么？
