Ep2 — Track B: REWARD — an open-source framework for identifying the unknown benefits of existing medications

Paper: Kern DM, Bohn J, Gilbert JP, Knoll C, Ryan PB. "REWARD — an open-source framework for identifying the unknown benefits of existing medications to inform drug discovery, development, and repurposing." Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association (JAMIA). 2026. Abstract-only — full text was not retrievable through Europe PMC's open-access routes despite the record being marked open access (fullTextXML 404; PMC-OA BioC mirror returned no result for PMC13687751).

REWARD is an open-source framework from the OHDSI lineage (co-author Patrick Ryan is one of OHDSI's architects) that flips pharmacovigilance on its head: instead of narrowly checking whether an approved drug causes harm in its approved population, it scans all medication-outcome pairs at once — thousands of drugs across millions of patients — on the OMOP common data model, using a self-controlled cohort design and an active-comparator new-user design, with negative control calibration to correct systematic bias. The abstract is explicit that results are "hypothesis-generating signals," not causal conclusions, and that the active-comparator design is reserved for giving "higher causal rigor" to the shortlist of prioritized pairs. No quantitative precision/recall or replication numbers are available from the abstract. Serves the AI-tooling/career priority: it's open-source, OMOP-based, reusable analytic software rather than a one-off analysis. Buildable artifact: a single-pair signal-scanning script applying the same active-comparator new-user design plus negative control calibration to one exposure/outcome codeset pair on OMOP-shaped data.

Plain-English glossary

  • Active comparator new-user design — Compares new starters of one drug against new starters of a similar, already-used drug for the same indication, avoiding the bias of comparing treated to untreated patients who may differ systematically.（中文:活性对照新用药者设计,比较刚开始使用某种药物的新患者和刚开始使用同一适应症下另一种已广泛使用的相似药物的新患者,以避免把治疗组和可能本身存在系统性差异的未治疗组直接比较所带来的偏倚。）
  • Empirical calibration — A technique that runs the same analytic pipeline against many known-null (negative control) pairs, measures how widely those estimates scatter, and uses that scatter to widen or recenter the confidence interval for the pair of real interest.（中文:实证校准,一种用同一套分析流程检验大量已知零效应〔负对照〕配对、测量这些估计值散布范围、再据此拓宽或重新校正真正关心配对的置信区间的技术。）
  • Negative control calibration — Testing a method against exposure-outcome pairs with a known null (no causal) relationship to empirically measure and correct residual bias.（中文:负对照校准,用已知不存在因果关系的暴露-结局配对来检验一个方法,以实证方式测量并纠正残余偏倚。）
  • OMOP common data model (CDM) — A standardized table structure and shared vocabulary, maintained by OHDSI, for representing observational health data (claims, EHR, registries) uniformly regardless of source system.（中文:OMOP通用数据模型,由OHDSI维护的一套标准化表结构和共享词汇表,用于统一表示观察性健康数据〔索赔、电子健康记录、注册库〕,不论其原始来源系统为何。）
  • Pharmacovigilance — The traditional practice of monitoring an already-approved drug for adverse effects within its approved population.（中文:药物警戒,传统做法是在药物已获批使用的人群中监测该药物的不良反应。）
  • Self-controlled cohort design — Compares a patient's outcome rate during drug exposure against their own outcome rate during a different time window, controlling for anything stable about that person.（中文:自身对照队列设计,比较患者在用药期间与同一人在另一时间窗口内的结局发生率,从而控制该患者身上稳定不变的因素。）

JORDAN Before the mechanics — what's OMOP, since it keeps coming up on this track? 在讲机制之前——OMOP到底是什么,这个赛道上它老是冒出来?

ALEX OMOP stands for Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership, and the OMOP common data model, or CDM, is a standardized schema that OHDSI maintains for representing observational health data — claims, EHR extracts, registries — in one consistent table structure with one shared vocabulary of standardized concept codes, regardless of which source system the data originally came from. OMOP全称是Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership(观察性医疗结局合作项目),而OMOP通用数据模型(common data model, CDM)是OHDSI维护的一套标准化架构,用一套一致的表结构和一套共享的标准化概念编码词汇表,来表示观察性健康数据——无论这些数据原本来自索赔记录、电子健康记录提取,还是注册库,都用同一种方式呈现,不管源系统本身是什么样子。 The payoff is that analytic code written against the OMOP CDM can, in principle, run unmodified against any database that's been mapped into it. 这样做的好处是,针对OMOP CDM编写的分析代码,原则上可以不加修改地运行在任何一个已经映射进这个模型的数据库上。 REWARD leans on two specific pharmacoepidemiology study designs you'll see named a lot in this space: a self-controlled cohort design, which compares a patient's outcome rate during drug exposure against their own outcome rate during a different time window, controlling for anything stable about that person; and an active comparator new-user design, which instead compares new starters of one drug against new starters of a similar, already-used drug for the same indication, which helps avoid the bias of comparing treated patients to untreated ones who may differ systematically. REWARD依赖两种在这个领域会反复看到的具体药物流行病学研究设计:一种是自身对照队列设计(self-controlled cohort design),它把患者在用药期间的结局发生率,与同一个人在另一个时间窗口内的结局发生率做比较,从而控制了这个人身上所有稳定不变的因素;另一种是活性对照新用药者设计(active comparator new-user design),它比较的是刚开始使用某种药物的新患者,与刚开始使用另一种针对同一适应症、已被广泛使用的相似药物的新患者,这有助于避免把接受治疗的患者和未接受治疗、可能本身系统性存在差异的患者放在一起比较所带来的偏倚。 On top of both, they run negative control calibration — testing the method against outcome pairs known to have no causal relationship, to empirically measure and correct for whatever residual bias the study design leaves behind, rather than just trusting the point estimate at face value. 在这两种设计之上,他们还运行负对照校准(negative control calibration)——用已知不存在因果关系的暴露-结局配对来检验这个方法,从而实证地测量并纠正研究设计残留的偏倚,而不是直接相信点估计值本身。

JORDAN One more term before we move on — "empirical calibration," is that the same thing as negative control calibration, or something narrower? 再问一个术语——"实证校准"(empirical calibration)和负对照校准是一回事,还是范围更窄的东西?

ALEX Negative control calibration is the general strategy — testing a method against pairs with a known true answer. 负对照校准是一个总体策略——用已知真实答案的配对来检验一个方法。 Empirical calibration is the specific statistical technique this author group and their OHDSI collaborators are known for: instead of trusting a single p-value or confidence interval at face value, you run the exact same analytic pipeline against dozens of negative controls — exposure-outcome pairs with no plausible causal link — see how widely the estimates for those known-null pairs actually scatter, and use that observed scatter to widen or recenter the confidence interval for your real pair of interest. 实证校准是这个作者群体及其OHDSI合作者以之闻名的一种具体统计技术:不是直接相信单个p值或置信区间的表面数值,而是用完全相同的分析流程去跑几十个负对照——没有合理因果关系的暴露-结局配对——看看这些已知为零效应的配对,估计值实际散布的范围有多大,再用这个观察到的散布幅度去拓宽或重新校正你真正关心的那个配对的置信区间。 It's a direct response to a well-documented finding in this literature: naive observational-study confidence intervals are systematically too narrow, because they only capture random sampling error and miss the residual confounding and measurement bias that a real database analysis always carries. 这是对这个领域一个有充分记录的发现的直接回应:朴素的观察性研究置信区间系统性地过窄,因为它们只捕捉了随机抽样误差,却遗漏了真实数据库分析里始终存在的残余混杂和测量偏倚。

ALEX The motivating gap is stated right in the objectives: big data's potential in health research "remains largely untapped," particularly for real-world data sources like administrative claims and EHRs. 这篇论文在"目的"部分直接点出了驱动性的问题缺口:大数据在健康研究中的潜力"在很大程度上仍未被充分利用",尤其是像行政索赔数据和电子健康记录这样的真实世界数据(real-world data)来源。 Claims data has historically been used in a narrow, defensive mode — pharmacovigilance, basically, checking whether a drug that's already approved for indication X causes some adverse effect in the population that's taking it for X. 索赔数据历史上一直被用于一种狭窄、防御性的模式——基本上就是药物警戒(pharmacovigilance),核查一种已经被批准用于适应症X的药物,是否会在正在因X而使用它的人群中引发某种不良反应。 What almost nobody was doing systematically was flipping that around: scanning every medication against every recorded outcome to ask, does this drug that's approved for X also seem to be doing something — good or bad — for Y, where Y was never part of the original indication? 几乎没有人系统性地做的,是反过来扫描:把每一种药物对照每一个被记录下来的结局逐一检验,问一句——这种被批准用于X的药物,是否似乎还对Y产生了某种影响(好的或坏的),而Y从来不是它最初被批准的适应症?
