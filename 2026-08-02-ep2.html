Show notes — Track B — 2026-08-02

Multi-domain rule-based phenotyping algorithms enable improved GWAS signal — Newbury A, Elhussein A, Gürsoy G. npj Digital Medicine, 2025.

Blurb

A tooling paper about the decision you make before any genetics: how you define a case. Using ~405–502k UK Biobank participants in OMOP CDM format, the authors build case/control cohorts four ways — "2+ condition", Phecode, ADO (codes + self-report), and OHDSI's multi-domain phenotype library — for seven diseases, then run GWAS on each. High-complexity, multi-domain definitions generally win: more power, more hits, and crucially more hits in coding/functional regions and colocalizing with eQTLs (a quality signal, not just bigger N). They're careful — phenotyping doesn't solve missing heritability and effects vary by disease. For us this lands on Liz / CFFPR phenotype & data-quality work (method only): a phenotype definition is a tunable, measurable knob. Buildable tool: a phenotyping-comparison harness that applies multiple case definitions to OMOP-style tables and reports case counts, overlap, and inclusion/exclusion differences — making the phenotyping choice auditable. Ships two repos.

Plain-English glossary

  • Colocalization — a test of whether the same variant drives both a disease association and a gene-expression signal, supporting causality over linkage.（中文：共定位——检验同一变异是否同时驱动疾病关联与基因表达信号，用来支持因果性而非仅位置邻近。）
  • eQTL — expression quantitative trait locus; a variant whose genotype tracks a gene's expression level.（中文：表达数量性状位点——其基因型与某基因表达水平相关的变异。）
  • GWAS — genome-wide association study; scans millions of variants for association with a phenotype.（中文：全基因组关联研究——逐个检验数百万变异是否与表型相关。）
  • OMOP CDM — Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership Common Data Model; a standardized EHR schema of fixed tables keyed by standard concept IDs.（中文：一种把杂乱 EHR 数据标准化成固定表格、以标准概念 ID 为键的通用数据模型。）
  • Phecode — a scheme grouping related billing codes into research-usable phenotypes.（中文：把相关计费编码归组为可用于研究的表型的方案。）
  • Rule-based phenotyping — defining cases/controls via explicit logic over EHR domains rather than a single code.（中文：规则化表型定义——用跨 EHR 数据域的明确逻辑而非单一编码来界定病例/对照。）
  • SNP heritability (LDSC) — proportion of trait variance attributable to common variants, estimated by LD-score regression.（中文：常见变异可解释的性状方差比例，用 LD-score 回归估计。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • OMOP CDM phenotyping — apply case-definition rules over standardized condition/drug/procedure/measurement/observation tables.
  • Four case definitions of increasing complexity — 2+ condition → Phecode → ADO (adds self-report) → OHDSI multi-domain library; the study's independent variable.
  • Multi-axis GWAS evaluation — power, hit count, LDSC SNP heritability, replication, coding/functional enrichment, eQTL colocalization, PRS accuracy; the harness for judging whether a definition helps.
  • Two open repos — UKBB OMOP phenotyping + GWAS/eval pipelines; a clean template to re-skin.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Liz / CFFPR phenotype & data quality (method only). Tool sketch (paste-ready): A Python phenotyping-comparison harness that takes OMOP-style clinical tables, applies 2–3 case definitions of increasing complexity for a chosen phenotype, and emits a comparison report — case counts, pairwise overlap, and a Venn of who each rule includes/excludes. Input: OMOP-format tables + phenotype spec. Output: definition-sensitivity report. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • Transportability — finding is from UK Biobank (volunteer, healthy-participant bias); check whether "more complex is better" holds in a disease-specific registry with a different sampling frame.
  • Complexity cost — multi-domain rules are harder to audit and can encode selection bias; check subgroup calibration/fairness, not just aggregate power.
  • eQTL reference — colocalization gains depend on the expression-reference tissue; check the tissue is disease-relevant.

ALEX Welcome back. 欢迎回来。 Today's Track B paper is "Multi-domain rule-based phenotyping algorithms enable improved GWAS signal," by Ashley Newbury, Ahmed Elhussein, and Gamze Gürsoy, published in npj Digital Medicine in 2025. 今天的 Track B 论文是《多域规则化表型定义算法能提升 GWAS 信号》，作者是 Ashley Newbury、Ahmed Elhussein 和 Gamze Gürsoy，2025 年发表于《npj 数字医学》。 We're in the tooling track, and the reason to read this one is that it ships code and it's about a decision you make before any genetics happens: how you define who counts as a case. 我们在工具这条线上，读它的理由是它附带了代码，而且讲的是在任何遗传分析发生之前你就要做的一个决定：如何定义谁算作病例。

JORDAN Let me lock down the genomics vocabulary first. 我先把基因组学的词汇钉牢。 What's a GWAS, in the sense they mean it? 他们说的 GWAS 到底是什么意思？

ALEX A genome-wide association study scans millions of common genetic variants across many people and asks, for each variant, whether one allele is more frequent in people with a disease than without. 全基因组关联研究 (GWAS) 会在许多人身上扫描数百万个常见遗传变异，并对每个变异发问：某个等位基因在患病者中是否比在非患病者中更常见。 GWAS（全基因组关联研究，逐个检验遗传变异是否与疾病相关）。 The output is, per variant, an effect size and a p-value, and hits are usually the ones passing five-times-ten-to-the-minus-eight. 输出是每个变异的效应量和一个 p 值，而“命中”通常指通过 5×10⁻⁸ 阈值的那些。 You know the statistics cold; the part worth dwelling on is what sits on the left-hand side of that association — the phenotype, the case/control label. 你对统计学了如指掌；值得停留的部分是那个关联式左边的东西——表型，也就是病例/对照的标签。

JORDAN And that label comes out of an electronic health record. 而这个标签来自电子健康记录。 What's messy about it? 它乱在哪里？

ALEX The core problem is that condition codes in an EHR are recorded for billing, not for research. 核心问题是：电子健康记录 (EHR) 里的疾病编码是为了计费而记录的，不是为了研究。 A billing code says a claim was filed, not that a rigorous diagnosis was confirmed. 一个计费编码说明有人提交了报销申请，并不代表一个严谨的诊断得到了确认。 So if you build your case group purely from condition codes — the traditional way — you get people mislabeled as cases and, worse, true cases hiding in your control group. 所以如果你纯粹用疾病编码来构建病例组——传统做法——你会得到被错标为病例的人，更糟的是，真正的病例藏在你的对照组里。 That label noise dilutes the genetic signal. 这种标签噪声会稀释遗传信号。

JORDAN Give me a concrete example of how a billing code lies. 给我一个计费编码如何“撒谎”的具体例子。

ALEX Take type 2 diabetes. 拿 2 型糖尿病来说。 A patient might get a diabetes code entered once during a visit where the clinician was ruling it out, not confirming it. 一名患者可能在一次就诊中被录入过一次糖尿病编码，而那次医生其实是在排除它、而非确诊它。 Or a real diabetic never gets the code because their care happened at a different health system that isn't in this record. 又或者一个真正的糖尿病患者从未被录入编码，因为他的诊疗发生在另一个不在这份记录里的医疗系统。 The first case pollutes your cases; the second buries a true case in your controls. 前者污染你的病例组；后者把一个真病例埋进你的对照组。 A richer definition that also requires, say, a diabetes drug or an elevated lab value filters out the first and can recover the second. 一个更丰富的定义——比如同时要求有糖尿病用药或一次异常化验值——能过滤掉前者，并可能找回后者。 That's the whole intuition of the paper in one disease. 这就是整篇论文在单一疾病上的核心直觉。

JORDAN So this is a data-quality paper wearing a genetics hat. 所以这是一篇戴着遗传学帽子的数据质量论文。 What did they actually do? 他们究竟做了什么？

ALEX They took UK Biobank data — about 405,000 to 502,000 participants — already mapped into the OMOP Common Data Model, and built case/control cohorts four different ways for each of seven diseases. 他们拿了英国生物样本库 (UK Biobank) 的数据——约 40.5 万到 50.2 万名参与者——已经映射进 OMOP 通用数据模型，并为七种疾病各用四种不同方式构建病例/对照队列。 OMOP is a standardized schema that reshapes messy EHR data into fixed tables — condition occurrence, drug exposure, procedure, measurement, observation — each row tagged with a standard concept ID. OMOP（一种把杂乱 EHR 数据重塑成固定表格的标准化数据模型——疾病发生、用药、操作、检测、观察——每行都带一个标准概念 ID）。 That standardization is what makes the whole experiment portable, and it's the same lens you'd use for any registry harmonization work. 正是这种标准化让整个实验可移植，也正是你在任何登记册协调工作中会用到的同一个视角。

JORDAN Why build this on OMOP specifically rather than just querying raw EHR tables? 为什么偏偏在 OMOP 上搭建，而不是直接查询原始 EHR 表？

ALEX Because OMOP buys you portability, and portability is the whole game for a tool you want to reuse. 因为 OMOP 为你换来可移植性，而可移植性正是一个你想复用的工具的全部意义。 When every condition, drug, and lab is mapped to a standard concept ID inside a fixed table schema, a phenotype rule written once runs unchanged against any dataset that speaks OMOP — a different hospital, a different registry, a different country. 当每一个疾病、药物和化验都被映射到固定表格模式里的一个标准概念 ID 时，一条写好一次的表型规则就能不加改动地跑在任何“说 OMOP 语言”的数据集上——不同的医院、不同的登记册、不同的国家。 Without that layer, every phenotype definition is hard-wired to one institution's quirky column names and local codes, and nothing transfers. 没有这一层，每个表型定义都被硬接线到某个机构古怪的列名和本地编码上，什么都迁移不了。 That's why OHDSI built a whole phenotype library on top of OMOP — the standardization is what makes a library possible at all. 这就是为什么 OHDSI 在 OMOP 之上建了整个表型库——正是标准化才让“库”这件事成为可能。

JORDAN What are the four ways of defining a case? 那四种定义病例的方式是什么？

ALEX They range from simple to complex. 它们从简单到复杂排列。 The simplest is "2+ condition" — you need the disease code at least twice. 最简单的是“2+ 疾病编码”——你需要至少两次出现该疾病编码。 Then Phecode, a widely used scheme that groups related billing codes into research phenotypes. 然后是 Phecode，一种被广泛使用的方案，把相关计费编码归并成研究表型。 Phecode（把计费编码归组为研究表型的常用方案）。 Then ADO, which adds self-reported conditions on top of codes. 再是 ADO，在编码之上加入自我报告的疾病。 And the most complex is OHDSI's phenotype library, where each disease gets a bespoke rule pulling from multiple domains — conditions, drugs, procedures, labs, observations. 最复杂的是 OHDSI 的表型库，每种疾病都有一套量身定制的规则，从多个数据域抽取——疾病、药物、操作、化验、观察。 For Alzheimer's, OHDSI uses five domains; for lupus, just two. 对阿尔茨海默病，OHDSI 用五个域；对红斑狼疮，只用两个。 The whole spectrum, from one domain to five, is the independent variable of the study. 从一个域到五个域的整条谱系，就是这项研究的自变量。

JORDAN One thing I'd challenge: adding more domains adds more people, and bigger cohorts trivially give more GWAS power. 我想质疑一点：增加更多数据域会纳入更多人，而更大的队列本来就会显然地带来更高的 GWAS 功效。 How do they know they're improving accuracy rather than just inflating N? 他们怎么知道自己是在提升准确度，而不只是把样本量吹大？

ALEX That's exactly the right pushback, and it's why they didn't stop at power. 这正是恰当的质疑，也正是他们没有止步于功效的原因。 They evaluated on multiple axes: raw power and hit count, yes, but also SNP heritability from LD-score regression, replication of known associations, whether new hits fall in coding and functional regions, colocalization with eQTLs, and downstream polygenic-risk-score accuracy. 他们在多个维度上评估：原始功效和命中数，是的，但还包括来自 LD-score 回归的 SNP 遗传力、对已知关联的重复验证、新命中是否落在编码区和功能区、与 eQTL 的共定位，以及下游多基因风险评分 (PRS) 的准确度。 The tell that it's quality and not just quantity is functional enrichment. 判断这是质量而非仅仅数量的关键信号，是功能富集。 If bigger cohorts only added noise, the extra hits would scatter randomly; instead the high-complexity algorithms landed more hits inside coding and regulatory DNA and more variants colocalizing with eQTLs. 如果更大的队列只是增加噪声，那多出来的命中会随机散落；但事实上高复杂度算法的命中更多落在编码和调控 DNA 内，更多变异与 eQTL 共定位。

JORDAN And what is functional enrichment, concretely, so I know why it's the tell? 那具体来说功能富集是什么，好让我明白它为什么是关键信号？

ALEX The genome is mostly non-coding, so a random variant usually lands in DNA that doesn't obviously do anything. 基因组大部分是非编码的，所以一个随机变异通常落在没有明显功能的 DNA 上。 Functional enrichment means your hits fall inside coding exons or known regulatory elements more often than chance would predict. 功能富集意味着你的命中落在编码外显子或已知调控元件里的频率高于随机预期。 If a better case definition produces hits that are enriched for functional DNA, those hits are more likely to be biologically causal rather than statistical flukes. 如果一个更好的病例定义产生的命中在功能性 DNA 上富集，那这些命中更可能是生物学上的因果，而非统计上的偶然。 That's why enrichment, not raw count, is the honest signal of quality. 这就是为什么富集、而非原始计数，才是质量的诚实信号。

JORDAN Define eQTL and colocalization for me — that's the genomics I don't carry around. 给我定义一下 eQTL 和共定位——这是我不太熟的基因组学部分。

ALEX An eQTL is a genetic variant whose genotype is associated with the expression level of a gene — a variant that turns a gene's volume knob up or down. eQTL（表达数量性状位点，即其基因型与某基因表达水平相关的遗传变异，相当于调节基因音量的旋钮）。 Colocalization is the test of whether the same variant is driving both your disease association and that expression signal, which is evidence the variant is causal and not just riding along on nearby DNA. 共定位是检验同一个变异是否同时驱动你的疾病关联与那个表达信号，这是该变异具有因果作用、而非只是搭了附近 DNA 便车的证据。 共定位（检验同一变异是否同时驱动疾病关联与基因表达信号，用来支持因果性）。 So more eQTL colocalization means the extra hits are more likely to be biologically real, not statistical debris. 所以更多的 eQTL 共定位意味着那些多出来的命中更可能是生物学上真实的，而不是统计残渣。

JORDAN Good — so what are the actual results? 好——那实际结果是什么？

ALEX High-complexity, multi-domain phenotyping generally won across the seven diseases. 高复杂度、多域的表型定义在七种疾病上普遍胜出。 It increased power, produced more hits, and — the important part — more of those hits sat in coding and functional regions and colocalized with eQTLs. 它提升了功效，产生更多命中，而且——重要的部分是——更多命中落在编码区和功能区，并与 eQTL 共定位。 They're careful not to over-claim: phenotyping choice does not solve missing heritability, and the effect size varies by disease. 他们很谨慎，不过度声称：表型定义的选择并不能解决“缺失的遗传力”问题，而且效应大小因病而异。 But the direction is consistent — investing in a richer case definition pays off downstream in signal quality, not just quantity. 但方向是一致的——在更丰富的病例定义上投入，会在下游以信号质量而非仅仅数量的形式得到回报。

JORDAN You mentioned replication and polygenic risk scores as other axes. 你提到了重复验证和多基因风险评分作为其他维度。 Did the complex definitions win there too? 复杂定义在那些维度上也赢了吗？

ALEX Largely yes, and those two axes are the ones I trust most. 大体上是的，而且这两个维度是我最信任的。 Replication asks whether the hits you find reappear in an independent, well-established set of known associations — if a fancier definition surfaces more real, previously-reported signals, that's hard to fake with noise. 重复验证问的是：你找到的命中是否在一组独立、公认的已知关联中再次出现——如果一个更讲究的定义浮现出更多真实的、此前已被报道的信号，那用噪声是很难伪造的。 A polygenic risk score sums up many small variant effects into one predictor of disease risk; if a cleaner case definition trains a PRS that predicts better in held-out people, the phenotype was genuinely less noisy. 多基因风险评分把许多微小的变异效应加总成一个疾病风险预测器；如果一个更干净的病例定义训练出的 PRS 在留出人群中预测得更好，那说明这个表型确实噪声更小。 So the story isn't just "more hits" — it's "hits that replicate and predict," which is the version that actually matters. 所以故事不只是“更多命中”——而是“能重复、能预测的命中”，这才是真正重要的版本。

JORDAN Was there any disease where complexity didn't help? 有没有哪种疾病上复杂性没帮上忙？

ALEX Yes, and that's the useful nuance. 有，而这正是有用的细微之处。 For some diseases the simpler Phecode or 2+ condition rule performed on par with the elaborate OHDSI definition — the power curves overlapped. 对某些疾病，较简单的 Phecode 或“2+ 编码”规则与精细的 OHDSI 定义表现相当——功效曲线重叠了。 That tells you the payoff from complexity is disease-dependent: for a condition that's cleanly coded and rarely ambiguous, extra domains add little, while for messy, under-coded conditions they add a lot. 这告诉你复杂性的回报是因病而异的：对一个编码干净、很少含糊的疾病，额外的数据域帮助不大，而对杂乱、编码不足的疾病，它们帮助很大。 That's a design lesson, not a universal rule — you tune the definition to the disease, you don't max it out blindly. 这是一条设计教训，而非普适定律——你要针对疾病去调定义，而不是盲目拉满。

JORDAN Where does this land for us? 这对我们落在哪里？

ALEX This lands on the Liz-sponsored CFFPR phenotype and data-quality work — method only, and I'll say nothing about registry internals. 这落在 Liz 支持的 CFFPR 表型与数据质量工作上——只谈方法，我对登记册的内部结构一字不提。 The transferable idea is that a phenotype definition is a knob you can tune and measure, and that multi-domain rules beat single-domain code-counting. 可迁移的思想是：表型定义是一个你可以调节并度量的旋钮，而多域规则胜过单域数编码。 The buildable artifact — and since this is Track B, it's the tool sketch — is a phenotyping-comparison harness. 可构建的产物——因为这是 Track B，它就是工具草图——是一个表型定义对比框架。 MVP: a Python tool that takes a clinical dataset in OMOP-style tables, applies two or three case definitions of increasing complexity for a chosen phenotype, and emits a comparison report — case counts, overlap, and a Venn of who each rule includes or excludes. MVP：一个 Python 工具，接收 OMOP 风格表格的临床数据集，对某个选定表型应用两到三种复杂度递增的病例定义，并输出一份对比报告——病例数、重叠情况，以及每条规则纳入或排除了谁的韦恩图。 Input: OMOP-format tables plus a phenotype spec; output: a definition-sensitivity report. 输入：OMOP 格式表格加一份表型规格说明；输出：一份定义敏感性报告。 Difficulty: medium. 难度：中等。

JORDAN What would make that harness genuinely useful rather than a toy? 什么能让那个框架真正有用、而不只是个玩具？

ALEX The payoff is turning an invisible modeling assumption into a report a domain expert can argue with. 关键回报是把一个看不见的建模假设，变成一份领域专家可以据以争论的报告。 Right now, when someone runs an analysis on a clinical dataset, the case definition is often buried in one line of code that nobody revisits. 眼下，当有人在临床数据集上跑分析时，病例定义往往埋在一行没人再回头看的代码里。 If instead you emit a report showing "definition A gives 4,000 cases, definition B gives 6,500, and here are the 2,500 people they disagree on," a clinician can look at that disagreement set and tell you which rule is closer to reality. 如果你转而输出一份报告，写明“定义 A 给出 4000 例，定义 B 给出 6500 例，这里是它们分歧的 2500 人”，临床医生就能看这个分歧集，告诉你哪条规则更贴近现实。 That review loop — human eyes on the boundary cases — is where the tool earns its keep. 这个复核回路——人眼盯着边界病例——正是这个工具挣得其价值的地方。 It converts a silent judgment call into an auditable, discussable artifact. 它把一个无声的判断，转化为一个可审计、可讨论的产物。

JORDAN So it's a tool that makes the phenotyping choice visible instead of hidden. 所以它是一个让表型定义的选择变得可见、而非隐藏的工具。
