How did this episode land for you?

Show notes — SpliceSelectNet (Track A, 2026-08-01)

Blurb. Miyachi and colleagues (Nucleic Acids Research, 2026, open access) present SpliceSelectNet (SSNet), a hierarchical Transformer that predicts splice sites from DNA windows up to 100 kb at single-nucleotide resolution. By borrowing a Swin-Transformer-style local-to-global design — dense attention inside small blocks, then progressive merging for long-range attention — it captures distal regulatory signals without the quadratic cost that limits plain transformers, and reaches roughly 20× farther than SpliceAI's practical ~5 kb window. It matches SpliceAI's recall while sharply improving precision (F1 up, false alarms down), beats SpliceAI and Pangolin on AUROC/AUPRC for aberrant-splicing detection, and — in a DMD long-range decoy experiment plus ClinVar deep-intronic validation — detects effects that older tools structurally cannot see. Attention scores, validated by in-silico mutagenesis, double as an interpretability layer. Where it lands: the Karen / CFTR variant-interpretation move — a buildable cftr-deep-intronic-splice-screener that runs SSNet across the full CFTR gene to triage deep-intronic VUS (e.g. 3849+10kbC>T, 2789+5G>A) for wet-lab minigene confirmation.

Plain-English glossary

  • Acceptor site — the splice site at the 3′ end of an intron, where the intron is cut and the next exon begins.（中文：受体位点，内含子 3′ 端的剪接切点，内含子在此被切除、下一个外显子开始。）
  • Cryptic splice site — a sequence that is not normally used as a splice site but can be activated by a variant, causing abnormal splicing.（中文：隐蔽剪接位点，正常不被使用、但可被变异激活从而导致异常剪接的序列。）
  • Deep-intronic variant — a variant located far inside an intron, away from exon boundaries, often missed by exon-focused panels.（中文：深内含子变异，位于内含子深处、远离外显子边界的变异，常被以外显子为中心的检测漏掉。）
  • Donor site — the splice site at the 5′ start of an intron, where the preceding exon ends and the intron is cut.（中文：供体位点，内含子 5′ 起始处的剪接切点，前一个外显子在此结束、内含子被切除。）
  • Exon / intron — exons are the coding segments kept in mature mRNA; introns are the segments removed during splicing.（中文：外显子是保留在成熟 mRNA 中的编码片段；内含子是剪接时被去除的片段。）
  • Receptive field — the span of input sequence a model can actually "see" when scoring a position.（中文：感受野，模型给某个位置打分时实际能"看到"的输入序列范围。）
  • Splicing enhancer / silencer — regulatory sequence elements (exonic or intronic) that increase or decrease use of a splice site, sometimes from a distance.（中文：剪接增强子/沉默子，增强或抑制某剪接位点使用的调控序列元件，有时作用于较远距离。）
  • Pseudo-exon — a stretch of intron that gets abnormally spliced into the mRNA as if it were a real exon, usually triggered by a deep-intronic variant.（中文：假外显子，一段内含子被异常地当作真外显子拼接进 mRNA，通常由深内含子变异触发。）
  • Splicing — the process of removing introns and joining exons to produce mature mRNA.（中文：剪接，去除内含子、连接外显子以生成成熟 mRNA 的过程。）

Full citation

Miyachi Y, et al. SpliceSelectNet: a hierarchical Transformer-based deep learning model for splice site prediction. Nucleic Acids Research. 2026. PMID 42328788; PMC PMC13284721; DOI 10.1093/nar/gkag625.

Methods / tools to lift

  • Hierarchical (Swin-style) local-to-global attention — dense attention inside small blocks, then progressive merging → long-range (100 kb) attention without full-resolution quadratic cost.
  • In-silico mutagenesis as interpretability audit — mutate each position, confirm the model attends to positions whose mutation moves the prediction.
  • Long-range decoy experiment (DMD gene) — plant decoy splice signals at increasing distances (to 10 kb+) to measure true long-range detection, independent of annotation.
  • Experimentally-confirmed validation sets — SpliceVarDB (>50k variants, confirmed splice-altering/not/low-freq) and SSCVDB (~30k splice-site-generating mutations via juncmut over SRA transcriptomes).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation.

Paste-ready MVP: cftr-deep-intronic-splice-screener (M) — input a CFTR intronic candidate variant + reference sequence; run SSNet across the surrounding 100 kb; output predicted create/destroy-splice-site call + attention heatmap flagging the responsible region; rank reported CFTR intronic VUS for wet-lab minigene follow-up by predicted impact and distance from nearest canonical site.

What to verify first

  • CFTR-specific calibration — benchmark numbers are cross-gene (Gencode/GTEx/ClinVar); check SSNet on CFTR's own known splice + deep-intronic variants before trusting scores.
  • Training-set leakage — if CFTR pathogenic splice variants were in training/Pangolin sets, accuracy on those exact variants is circular; use a held-out CFTR evaluation.
  • Prediction ≠ verdict — a predicted splice change is a hypothesis; minigene/RNA assay still confirms the actual transcript. The screener orders wet-lab work, it does not replace it.
  • Attention = correlate, not mechanism — treat the heatmap as a lead for where to look, not proof.
  • Runs entirely on public reference sequence + published variants; no registry-internal data.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读论文时间。 Today's Track A paper is "SpliceSelectNet: a hierarchical Transformer-based deep learning model for splice site prediction," by Yuki Miyachi and colleagues. 今天 Track A 的论文是宫地佑树（Yuki Miyachi）及其同事发表的《SpliceSelectNet：一个基于层级式 Transformer 的剪接位点预测深度学习模型》。 It came out in Nucleic Acids Research in 2026 and it's fully open access. 这篇文章 2026 年发表在《Nucleic Acids Research》上，完全开放获取。 We're reading it because it pushes the context window for splice prediction out to a hundred kilobases, and that long reach is exactly what CFTR deep-intronic variants demand. 我们读它，是因为它把剪接预测的上下文窗口拉长到了十万个碱基（100 kb），而这种长距离视野，正是 CFTR 深内含子变异所需要的。

JORDAN Before we get into it, build up the vocabulary for me. 在深入之前，先帮我把术语铺一下。 What actually happens at a splice site, and why would deep learning need to look so far away from it? 剪接位点上到底发生了什么，深度学习又为什么要看到离它那么远的地方？

ALEX Splicing is the step where the cell cuts the non-coding introns out of a freshly copied RNA and stitches the coding exons together. 剪接（splicing）是细胞把刚转录出的 RNA 中不编码蛋白的内含子（intron）剪掉、再把编码的外显子（exon）拼接起来的过程。 The two cut points are the donor site at the start of an intron and the acceptor site at its end. 两个剪切点分别是内含子起始处的供体位点（donor site）和末端的受体位点（acceptor site）。 The cell recognizes those points partly from short motifs right at the boundary, but also from regulatory sequences that can sit thousands of bases away. 细胞识别这些位点，一部分靠边界上的短序列基序（motif），但也依赖那些可能位于数千碱基之外的调控序列。 Those distal elements are called splicing enhancers and silencers — exonic or intronic — and they tune whether a given site actually gets used. 这些远端元件被称为剪接增强子（enhancer）和沉默子（silencer），有外显子型也有内含子型，它们调节某个位点到底会不会被用上。 Here is the genomics reason the long reach matters. 下面就是长距离视野之所以重要的基因组学原因。 A variant deep inside an intron, far from any normal splice site, can create a brand-new cryptic splice site or activate a pseudo-exon — a chunk of intron that suddenly gets spliced in as if it were a real exon. 一个位于内含子深处、远离任何正常剪接位点的变异，可以制造出一个全新的隐蔽剪接位点（cryptic splice site），或者激活一个假外显子（pseudo-exon，即一段内含子突然被当作真外显子拼接进来）。 If your model can only see a few hundred bases around the site, it is structurally blind to those events. 如果你的模型只能看到位点周围几百个碱基，那它在结构上就对这类事件视而不见。

JORDAN And that blindness has a real cost in CF specifically. 而这种视而不见，在 CF 上有实实在在的代价。

ALEX It does, and this is the motivation worth sitting with. 确实如此，这也是值得停下来体会的动机。 The best-known deep-learning tool for splicing is SpliceAI, a convolutional network that reads sequence and scores each position as a donor, acceptor, or neither. 剪接领域最著名的深度学习工具是 SpliceAI，它是一个卷积网络（CNN），读取序列并给每个位置打分，判断它是供体、受体还是都不是。 SpliceAI set the benchmark, but it has two limitations the authors zero in on. SpliceAI 树立了标杆，但作者盯住了它的两个局限。 First, its practical receptive field reaches only about five kilobases on either side of a target, so ultra-deep-intronic effects fall outside it. 第一，它的实际感受野（receptive field，即模型能"看到"的序列范围）在目标两侧各约五千碱基，所以超深内含子的效应落在了它的视野之外。 Second, it is a stack of convolutions, so it is hard to interpret without bolt-on methods like in-silico mutagenesis or gradient attribution. 第二，它是一堆卷积层，如果不加上诸如计算机模拟诱变（in-silico mutagenesis）或梯度归因这样的额外分析，就很难解释它为什么这么判。 Newer transformer models — SpliceBERT, Spliceformer, SpliceTransformer — try to widen the window, but they either stay short, at around nine hundred nucleotides for SpliceBERT, or they pay a punishing computational cost because plain attention scales quadratically with sequence length. 更新的 transformer 模型——SpliceBERT、Spliceformer、SpliceTransformer——试图拓宽窗口，但它们要么依然很短（SpliceBERT 只有约 900 个核苷酸），要么付出高昂的计算代价，因为普通注意力机制（attention）的开销随序列长度呈平方增长。 So the field had a genuine gap: nobody could look across a hundred kilobases at single-base resolution without the compute blowing up. 所以这个领域有一个真实的空白：没人能在保持单碱基分辨率的同时，横跨十万碱基去看，而又不让计算量爆炸。

JORDAN So how do they get a hundred kilobases without that quadratic explosion? 那他们是怎么在不引发平方级爆炸的情况下拿到十万碱基的？

ALEX This is the clever architectural move, and it borrows an idea from computer vision. 这是一个巧妙的架构设计，思路借自计算机视觉。 They build a hierarchical transformer inspired by the Swin Transformer. 他们构建了一个受 Swin Transformer 启发的层级式（hierarchical）transformer。 The trick is local-to-global: they chop the hundred-kilobase input into small local patches, run dense attention inside each patch, then progressively merge patches into coarser representations and run attention again at that higher level. 关键在于从局部到全局：他们把十万碱基的输入切成小的局部片段（patch），在每个片段内部运行密集注意力，然后逐步把片段合并成更粗粒度的表示，再在更高层次上运行注意力。 A local attention layer sharpens prediction right around each candidate site; a global attention layer then carries information across the full hundred-kilobase span. 局部注意力层负责在每个候选位点周围做精细预测；全局注意力层则把信息在整个十万碱基的跨度上传递。 Because you never run full-resolution attention over the entire sequence at once, you dodge the initial quadratic cost. 因为你从不一次性对整条序列运行全分辨率的注意力，你就绕开了一开始那个平方级的开销。 They train on three datasets: Gencode annotations, GTEx-derived splice sites, and the Pangolin training set, downloaded against the hg19 assembly. 他们在三个数据集上训练：Gencode 注释、来自 GTEx 的剪接位点，以及 Pangolin 的训练集，都基于 hg19 参考基因组下载。 GTEx matters here because it captures tissue-variable and alternative splice sites, which pushes the model to recognize novel site generation, not just canonical ones. GTEx（一个跨组织的基因表达与剪接图谱）在这里很重要，因为它涵盖了组织差异性的、可变的剪接位点，从而促使模型去识别新位点的产生，而不只是标准位点。 One design choice I'd push on: three overlapping training sources helps coverage, but it also makes it harder to attribute exactly which signal the model learned from where. 有一个设计选择我想质疑一下：三个相互重叠的训练来源有助于覆盖面，但也让人更难判断模型究竟从哪个来源学到了哪种信号。

JORDAN Walk me through the block structure a bit more concretely. 帮我把这个片段结构讲得更具体一点。 How big are these patches, and how does the merging work? 这些片段有多大，合并又是怎么运作的？

ALEX At the base configuration, the local attention operates over blocks, and for the hundred-kilobase model they reshape the input into blocks — for example fifty blocks of length twenty and fifty blocks of length two hundred at different stages — so attention stays dense but bounded inside each block. 在基础配置下，局部注意力在块（block）上运作，对于十万碱基的模型，他们把输入重塑成若干块——比如在不同阶段用五十个长度为二十的块、以及五十个长度为二百的块——这样注意力在每个块内部保持密集但有界。 The local layer refines the signal near candidate sites, and then the global layer stitches the block-level summaries together so a variant in one block can influence a prediction in a distant block. 局部层在候选位点附近细化信号，然后全局层把块级摘要缝合起来，这样一个块里的变异就能影响到遥远另一个块里的预测。 They train with a batch size of sixteen, warm the learning rate up linearly to five-times-ten-to-the-minus-five over the first eighty thousand steps and then decay it, and use mixed precision — sixteen-bit and thirty-two-bit floats together — to save memory and time, except in the softmax step where they keep full precision for numerical stability. 他们用 16 的批大小训练，在前八万步把学习率线性预热到 5×10⁻⁵ 然后衰减，并使用混合精度（mixed precision，即 16 位和 32 位浮点数混用）来节省内存和时间，只有在 softmax 步骤保留全精度以保证数值稳定。 The point of all this bookkeeping is that the hierarchy is what makes a hundred-kilobase context tractable at all — without it, plain attention over that length simply does not fit on the hardware. 这么多记账工作的意义在于：正是这个层级结构，才让十万碱基的上下文成为可能——没有它，那么长的普通注意力根本装不进硬件。

JORDAN And what did they validate against besides Gencode? 除了 Gencode，他们还用什么来验证？

ALEX Two purpose-built variant databases, which is where the aberrant-splicing claims come from. 两个专门构建的变异数据库，异常剪接的结论就来自这里。 The first is SpliceVarDB, an online collection of over fifty thousand variants gathered from more than five hundred publicly available sources, each experimentally confirmed as splice-altering, not splice-altering, or low-frequency splice-altering. 第一个是 SpliceVarDB，一个在线合集，收录了从五百多个公开来源汇集的五万多个变异，每个都经过实验确认为"改变剪接"、"不改变剪接"或"低频改变剪接"。 The second is SSCVDB, a catalog of about thirty thousand splice-site-generating mutations identified from over three hundred thousand transcriptomes in the Sequence Read Archive using an information-analysis method called juncmut. 第二个是 SSCVDB，一个约三万个"产生剪接位点"突变的目录，是用一种名为 juncmut 的信息分析方法，从 Sequence Read Archive（序列读取归档库）里三十多万份转录组中鉴定出来的。 Validating against experimentally confirmed splice effects, rather than just annotation, is what lets them claim the model detects real aberrant splicing and not just canonical site geometry. 用实验确认的剪接效应来验证，而不只是靠注释，这才让他们能宣称模型检测的是真正的异常剪接，而不只是标准位点的几何形状。

JORDAN Fair. 有道理。 So does the long reach actually pay off in the numbers? 那这种长距离视野，在数字上真的兑现了吗？

ALEX It does, and let's be specific. 兑现了，我们来看具体数字。 On the Gencode test set, SSNet matches SpliceAI on recall but sharply improves precision — SpliceAI scores precision around 0.857 while SSNet's base model reaches about 0.934, lifting the F1 score without sacrificing sensitivity. 在 Gencode 测试集上，SSNet 在召回率上与 SpliceAI 持平，但显著提高了精确率——SpliceAI 的精确率约为 0.857，而 SSNet 的基础模型达到约 0.934，在不牺牲敏感性的前提下提升了 F1 分数。 In plain terms, it finds the same true splice sites but raises far fewer false alarms. 说白了，它找到同样多的真剪接位点，但误报少得多。 On a harder expert-labeled aberrant-splicing set of about seven thousand variants, the GTEx-trained SSNet outperforms both SpliceAI and Pangolin by a wide margin on the metrics that don't depend on an arbitrary threshold — AUROC and AUPRC. 在一个更难的、由专家标注的约七千个异常剪接变异的数据集上，用 GTEx 训练的 SSNet 在那些不依赖任意阈值的指标（AUROC 和 AUPRC，即受试者工作曲线下面积和精确率-召回率曲线下面积）上，大幅超过了 SpliceAI 和 Pangolin。 For detecting whether a variant generates a novel splice site, SSNet trained last on GTEx hit AUC values around 0.818 for the full sequence, versus 0.722 for SpliceAI trained on the same data. 在判断一个变异是否会产生新剪接位点这件事上，最后在 GTEx 上训练的 SSNet 达到约 0.818 的 AUC，而在同样数据上训练的 SpliceAI 只有 0.722。 Then the headline experiment: they took the DMD gene, which has enormous introns, and planted decoy splice signals at ultra-long distances — out to ten kilobases and beyond — to see who could still detect them. 然后是最引人注目的实验：他们拿了 DMD 基因（它有巨大的内含子），在超长距离处——直到一万碱基乃至更远——植入诱饵剪接信号，看谁还能检测到。 SSNet caught distal effects that fall completely outside SpliceAI's and Pangolin's reach, and they validated the same long-range sensitivity against real pathogenic deep-intronic variants pulled from ClinVar. SSNet 捕捉到了完全落在 SpliceAI 和 Pangolin 视野之外的远端效应，并用从 ClinVar 中取出的真实致病深内含子变异，验证了同样的长距离敏感性。

JORDAN And they claim it's interpretable, not just accurate. 而且他们声称它是可解释的，不只是准确。 What does interpretable mean here concretely? 这里的可解释具体是什么意思？

ALEX The attention scores themselves become the explanation. 注意力分数本身就成了解释。 They ran systematic in-silico mutagenesis — mutate each position, watch the prediction move — and showed that the positions the model attends to are the same positions whose mutation actually changes the prediction. 他们做了系统的计算机模拟诱变——逐个位置突变，观察预测的变化——并证明模型所关注的位置，正是那些一旦突变就真会改变预测的位置。 So the attention heatmap isn't decorative; it points at functionally influential sequence, though the authors are careful to say it's a correlate, not a literal mechanistic explanation. 所以注意力热图不是装饰性的，它指向了功能上有影响的序列，尽管作者谨慎地说明这是一种相关性，而非字面意义上的机制解释。 On speed, they benchmarked inference on an NVIDIA H100 and showed SSNet stays efficient at ten-kilobase inputs where SpliceTransformer's runtime blows up and SpliceBERT lags. 在速度上，他们在 NVIDIA H100 上基准测试了推理，显示 SSNet 在一万碱基的输入上仍保持高效，而 SpliceTransformer 的运行时间会爆炸、SpliceBERT 也拖后腿。 The honest caveat is that all of this is benchmark performance on curated datasets, not prospective clinical use. 一个诚实的保留意见是：这一切都是在精选数据集上的基准表现，而不是前瞻性的临床应用。

JORDAN So where does this land for us? 那这对我们意味着什么落点？

ALEX This lands squarely on the Karen collaboration — CFTR variant interpretation. 这个落点正好在 Karen 的合作上——CFTR 变异判读。 Here's the specific hook. 具体的抓手是这样的。 CFTR has famous pathogenic variants that sit deep in introns, far from any exon boundary, and they cause disease precisely by creating cryptic splice sites and pseudo-exons. CFTR 有一些著名的致病变异，深藏在内含子里，远离任何外显子边界，它们致病的机制正是制造隐蔽剪接位点和假外显子。 The classic example is 3849+10kbC>T, which sits ten thousand bases into an intron and activates a pseudo-exon; another is 2789+5G>A. 经典例子是 3849+10kbC>T，它位于内含子内一万碱基处，会激活一个假外显子；另一个是 2789+5G>A。 A tool with a five-kilobase reach can miss 3849+10kbC>T structurally; a hundred-kilobase model can see it. 一个只有五千碱基视野的工具，可能在结构上就漏掉 3849+10kbC>T；而一个十万碱基的模型能看到它。 So the buildable artifact is a cftr-deep-intronic-splice-screener. 所以可构建的东西是一个 cftr-deep-intronic-splice-screener（CFTR 深内含子剪接筛查器）。 The MVP: feed it the full CFTR gene sequence with a candidate intronic variant applied, run SSNet across the surrounding hundred-kilobase window, and output whether the variant is predicted to create or destroy a splice site, with the attention heatmap flagging the responsible region.
