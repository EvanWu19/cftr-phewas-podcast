How did this episode land for you?

Track A — Spectrum and Classification of CFTR and ADGRG2 Variants in Chinese Patients With Isolated CAVD (Yuan et al., 2026)

Blurb. A study of 199 Chinese men with isolated congenital absence of the vas deferens (iCAVD — the mildest end of the CFTR disease spectrum, causing obstructive infertility) plus 148 female partners, with every exon of CFTR and ADGRG2 Sanger-sequenced. The scientific value is not the fertility framing but the reclassification workflow: the authors resolve variants that ClinVar classifies inconsistently by triangulating three independent evidence lines — population allele frequency in the correct ancestry (East-Asian gnomAD), family-based cis/trans phase, and a minigene splicing assay. That machinery reclassified p.Ile556Val to likely benign (it was no more common than in controls and sat in cis with a real pathogenic variant — riding along, not causing), confirmed p.Gln1352His as pathogenic, and overturned the group's own earlier SpliceAI-based splice predictions when four "predicted-damaging" splice variants showed no aberrant splicing in vitro. It lands on the Karen variant-interpretation lane, and the buildable artifact is a poly-T-aware CFTR variant reclassifier that combines East-Asian allele frequency, a phase-aware in-cis caution flag, and canonical HGVS normalization of the messy 5T/poly-T notation.

Plain-English glossary.

  • ACMG guidelines — the American College of Medical Genetics rulebook for classifying a variant as pathogenic, benign, or uncertain by combining weighted evidence lines.（中文：ACMG 指南，通过组合加权证据线来判定变异致病/良性/未明的规则手册。）
  • ADGRG2 — an X-linked gene; a second cause of CAVD, hemizygous in males so one loss-of-function variant suffices.（中文：ADGRG2，一个 X 连锁基因，是 CAVD 的第二个病因，男性中为半合子，单个功能丧失变异即可致病。）
  • CAVD / CBAVD / CUAVD — congenital absence of the vas deferens; bilateral (both sides) or unilateral (one side).（中文：先天性输精管缺如；双侧型 / 单侧型。）
  • CFTR-related disorder (CFTR-RD) — a CFTR-caused condition milder than classic CF, e.g. iCAVD, typically one severe plus one mild variant with residual CFTR function.（中文：CFTR 相关疾病，比经典 CF 轻的 CFTR 病症，通常一严重加一轻微变异，保留残余功能。）
  • cis / trans (phase) — two variants on the same chromosome copy (cis) vs opposite copies (trans); trans in an affected person is pathogenic evidence, cis with a known pathogenic allele is a benign clue.（中文：顺式/反式（相位），同一条染色体拷贝上 vs 相反拷贝上；患者中反式是致病证据，与已知致病等位基因顺式则是良性线索。）
  • cosegregation — tracking whether a variant travels with disease through a family, used here to establish phase from parents.（中文：共分离，追踪变异是否随疾病在家系中传递，这里用来从父母确定相位。）
  • gnomAD — the Genome Aggregation Database, a large reference catalog of variation in presumably healthy people, used as an allele-frequency control.（中文：gnomAD，来自推定健康人群的大型变异参考目录，用作等位基因频率对照。）
  • HGVS — the standardized nomenclature for writing variants; poly-T tracts are a notorious place it gets written inconsistently.（中文：HGVS，变异命名的标准化书写规范；多聚 T 片段是它最容易被写得不一致的地方。）
  • hemizygous — having only one copy of a gene (as males do for X-linked ADGRG2), so a single variant is fully expressed.（中文：半合子，只有一个基因拷贝（如男性对 X 连锁的 ADGRG2），单个变异即完全表达。）
  • minigene assay — cloning an exon plus flanking intron into a plasmid, expressing it in cells, and reading out whether a variant causes mis-splicing — a direct functional splice test.（中文：小基因实验，把外显子连同两侧内含子克隆进质粒并在细胞中表达，读出变异是否造成错误剪接——一种直接的剪接功能检测。）
  • obstructive azoospermia — no sperm in the ejaculate because of a blockage (here, missing vas deferens), not a production failure.（中文：梗阻性无精子症，因梗阻（此处为输精管缺如）导致精液中无精子，而非生成障碍。）
  • poly-T tract / 5T allele — a variable run of T bases before CFTR exon 10; the short 5T version promotes exon-10 skipping and reduced CFTR, a splice-affecting risk allele.（中文：多聚 T 片段 / 5T 等位基因，CFTR 第 10 外显子前一段长度可变的 T；较短的 5T 促进外显子跳读、降低 CFTR，是影响剪接的风险等位基因。）
  • SpliceAI — a deep-learning predictor of splice-altering effects; the paper reaffirms a score ≥0.2 as the trustworthy threshold for non-canonical sites.（中文：SpliceAI，基于深度学习的剪接改变效应预测器；论文重申非经典位点分数 ≥0.2 才是可信门槛。）
  • variant of uncertain significance (VUS) — a variant with insufficient evidence to call pathogenic or benign.（中文：意义未明变异，证据不足以判定致病或良性。）

Methods / tools to lift.

  • Triangulated reclassification — never trust one database call; stack ancestry-matched population frequency (gnomAD East-Asian, as BS1/PM2 evidence), family cis/trans phase, and a functional assay.
  • Minigene splicing assay — direct in-vitro splice readout to confirm or refute computational splice predictions (overturned four SpliceAI calls here).
  • SpliceAI calibration — ≥0.2 as the actionable threshold for non-canonical splice-site variants.
  • In-cis-with-pathogenic as a benign clue — a variant riding along with the true causal allele is a reason to doubt its own pathogenicity, not to double-count it.
  • Ancestry-tailored panels — Chinese iCAVD hotspots (5T, p.Gln1352His, p.Ile556Val, p.Gly970Asp) differ from Caucasian (F508del, 5T, p.Arg117His); F508del is rare in Chinese CF/CFTR-RD.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Serves Karen / variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP: cftr-polyT-aware-reclassifier (M) — input a CFTR variant list with per-person genotypes; output, per variant, the East-Asian gnomAD allele frequency as a BS1/PM2 evidence line, a phase-aware flag when the variant is in cis with a known pathogenic allele, and a canonical HGVS normalization collapsing poly-T/TG legacy notations (c.1210-12T[5]) and strict forms (c.1210-7_1210-6del) to one identifier.

What to verify first.

  • Minigene is a proxy in HEK293T cells, not native CF-relevant epithelium — confirm a "no aberrant splicing" call in a relevant cell type before retiring a variant.
  • Frequency-based benign evidence requires ancestry-matched controls — an East-Asian frequency says nothing reliable about a European patient.
  • Phase from a single family is a small sample; scale confidence with the number of concordant families.
  • The 5T allele's severity depends on neighboring TG-repeat length and tissue context — treating it as a single fixed-effect variant is a simplification.
  • All of the above is method on public, published data — no claims about any registry's genotype storage or internals.

JORDAN So iCAVD is basically the mildest thing a broken CFTR can do — infertility instead of full-blown CF. 所以 iCAVD 基本上是 CFTR 出问题时能造成的最轻后果——是不育，而不是完整的囊性纤维化。 There's a name for that, right? 这有个专门的说法，对吧？

JORDAN The 5T allele keeps coming up. What is it exactly? 5T 等位基因一直在出现，它到底是什么？
