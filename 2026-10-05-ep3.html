Blurb

Cole TJ's 2019 commentary in Statistics in Medicine rounds off a collection of papers on growth trajectory modeling and centile construction: Anderson et al.'s HBGDki comparison of six longitudinal growth models (Laird-Ware, SITAR, brokenstick, FACE, and relatives) across 21 studies and ~800,000 measurements, and Ohuma & Altman's INTERGROWTH-21st work on GAMLSS-family centile construction for birthweight and fetal head circumference. Cole's central critique is that the models recommended as "best" (brokenstick/FACE; a fractional-polynomial ST3 model; a three-level hierarchical fetal head-circumference model) were chosen by cross-validated fit or visual centile appearance, without checking either whether that fit timescale predicts later health outcomes, or whether the chosen model's own tails are calibrated — and in the fetal head-circumference case, the "optimal" model turned out to have 5% of observations beyond each nominal 3rd/97th centile instead of 3%, a 67% excess. This directly touches the CFFPR outlier pipeline's candidate-generation stage: a substrate model's timescale silently decides what counts as an anomaly, and its tail calibration needs to be checked, not assumed. Buildable: a substrate-model timescale-and-calibration audit script, run before adopting any growth-trajectory or GAMLSS/LMS reference curve as a flagging substrate.

Plain-English glossary

  • HAZ (height-for-age Z-score) — a child's height transformed to a normal-equivalent deviate against a reference population, with mean 0 and SD 1 regardless of age or sex.（中文：HAZ（年龄身高Z分数），将儿童身高相对于参照人群转换为正态等效离差，无论年龄或性别，均值为0、标准差为1。）
  • LMS method / GAMLSS — LMS fits smooth age-varying curves for a distribution's mean, SD, and skewness to construct centiles; GAMLSS (generalized additive models for location, scale and shape) generalizes this to more distribution families and smoothing techniques.（中文：LMS方法／GAMLSS，LMS通过拟合随年龄变化的均值、标准差和偏度的平滑曲线来构建百分位曲线；GAMLSS（位置-尺度-形状的广义加性模型）将其推广到更多分布族和平滑技术。）
  • SITAR / brokenstick / FACE / Laird-Ware — longitudinal growth trajectory models that represent each child's curve as a population mean plus individual deviation, differing in how smooth the mean and deviations are: SITAR shifts/scales a common curve per subject; brokenstick is a linear spline; FACE (functional PCA) fits cubic splines; Laird-Ware is a polynomial mixed model.（中文：SITAR／brokenstick／FACE／Laird-Ware，均为纵向生长轨迹模型，将每个孩子的曲线表示为群体均值加个体偏差，区别在于均值和偏差各自的平滑程度：SITAR对一条公共曲线做个体化的平移/缩放；brokenstick是线性样条；FACE（函数主成分分析）拟合三次样条；Laird-Ware是多项式混合模型。）
  • Prescriptive vs. descriptive growth reference — a prescriptive reference (e.g. WHO standard, INTERGROWTH-21st) defines what growth should look like in a healthy population; a descriptive reference (e.g. a national birthweight chart) simply describes the actual local population distribution.（中文：规范性／描述性生长参照系，规范性参照系（如WHO标准、INTERGROWTH-21st）定义健康人群"应有"的生长状态；描述性参照系（如某国出生体重图表）只是描述当地人群的实际分布。）
  • Worm plot — a diagnostic plot (a rotated Q-Q plot) used to check whether a fitted distribution's residuals are normal across the covariate range; a near-linear worm indicates good fit.（中文：蠕虫图（worm plot），一种诊断图（旋转后的Q-Q图），用于检验拟合分布的残差在协变量范围内是否呈正态；接近直线的蠕虫图表示拟合良好。）
  • Tail calibration — whether the actual proportion of observations falling beyond a reference curve's nominal extreme centiles (e.g. 3rd/97th) matches that nominal proportion.（中文：尾部校准，指落在参照曲线名义极端百分位（如第3/第97百分位）之外的观测值实际比例，是否与该名义比例相符。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Cross-validated MSE on raw vs. Z-score scale — the model-comparison criterion Anderson et al. used to recommend brokenstick/FACE, worth reusing as a first-pass check on any candidate substrate model.
  • Effective timescale from d.f./knot spacing — reading a trajectory model's implicit detection timescale directly off its degrees of freedom or spline knot spacing (brokenstick's ~140-day/4.6-month segments here).
  • Direct tail-calibration check — measuring the actual percentage of held-out observations beyond a reference curve's nominal 3rd/97th centile, the diagnostic that caught the fetal head-circumference model's 5%-vs-3% miscalibration.

What to verify first

  • Whether a trajectory model's superior cross-validated fit on short-term growth actually predicts later health outcomes — none of the papers in this collection tested that, and Cole flags it as the open question.
  • Whether any candidate height/weight flagging substrate has had its own tail calibration directly measured, rather than inferred from a reasonable-looking centile plot or AIC/BIC ranking alone.
  • That illustrative single-child trajectory figures (as in the Anderson papers) are checked against aggregate cross-validated numbers rather than trusted at face value, since Cole flags the example child's trajectory as suspiciously atypical.

Error types covered / not covered

Not a specific flagging algorithm — covers model-choice and calibration issues that determine sensitivity to any error type via the chosen timescale (shorter-timescale models like brokenstick are better matched to catching single-point errors such as digit transposition or decimal shift; longer-timescale models like SITAR/quadratic Laird-Ware may smooth over them). Does not address unit conversion or carry-forward errors directly, nor distinguish data-entry error from a real clinical event.

JORDAN Before any of the models — what's a growth centile actually encoding, and what's HAZ? 在讲任何模型之前——生长百分位曲线到底编码的是什么，HAZ又是什么？

ALEX A growth reference answers "what's normal at this age," and a centile chart turns a child's raw height or weight into a percentile rank against that reference — the 50th centile is the reference median, the 3rd and 97th mark the conventional outer bounds. 一个生长参照系（growth reference）回答的是"在这个年龄，什么是正常的"，而百分位曲线图（centile chart）把一个孩子的原始身高或体重转换成相对于该参照系的百分位排名——第50百分位是参照系的中位数，第3和第97百分位则是常规的外侧边界。 HAZ, height-for-age Z-score, is the same idea on a standardized scale: it's the measurement transformed to a normal-equivalent deviate against a reference population, so it has mean 0 and SD 1 regardless of age or sex, which is what the LMS method and its relatives compute. HAZ，即年龄身高Z分数（height-for-age Z-score），是同一个想法在标准化尺度上的表达：它把测量值相对于参照人群转换成一个正态等效离差（normal-equivalent deviate），因此无论年龄或性别，均值为0、标准差为1——这正是LMS方法及其同类方法所计算的内容。 The LMS method, and its extensions under GAMLSS — generalized additive models for location, scale, and shape — fit smooth curves for the distribution moments (mean, SD, and for the extensions, skewness and kurtosis) as functions of age or gestation, and the centiles are then read straight off those fitted moment curves. LMS方法，以及它在GAMLSS（位置-尺度-形状的广义加性模型，generalized additive models for location, scale and shape）框架下的扩展版本，为分布的各个矩（均值、标准差，以及在扩展版本中的偏度和峰度）拟合随年龄或孕周变化的平滑曲线，而百分位曲线就直接从这些拟合出的矩曲线中读出。 The other model family in play here is longitudinal trajectory models — quadratic Laird-Ware, SITAR, brokenstick, and FACE — which instead of just describing the population distribution at each age, model each individual child's trajectory as a population mean curve plus a subject-specific deviation, and they differ mainly in how smooth the mean curve and the deviations are allowed to be. 这里涉及的另一类模型是纵向轨迹模型——二次Laird-Ware模型、SITAR、brokenstick和FACE——它们不只是描述每个年龄点上的群体分布，而是把每个孩子的个体轨迹建模为一条群体均值曲线加上一个个体特异的偏差，它们的主要区别在于均值曲线和个体偏差各自被允许多"光滑"或多"灵活"。

JORDAN So what was this whole collection of papers trying to settle? 那这一整批论文到底想解决什么问题？

ALEX Cole is rounding off a collection with two distinct strands. The first, from Anderson and colleagues under the Gates-funded HBGDki initiative — Healthy Birth, Growth and Development knowledge integration — compares six trajectory models (linear and quadratic Laird-Ware, SITAR, brokenstick, multilevel spline, and FACE) across 21 studies covering over 120,000 children and nearly 800,000 measurement occasions, aiming to recommend which model best captures individual growth trajectories for height and weight. 第一条线来自Anderson及其同事，属于盖茨基金会资助的HBGDki倡议（Healthy Birth, Growth and Development knowledge integration，健康出生、生长与发育知识整合）——他们在21项研究、超过12万名儿童、近80万次测量记录上，比较了六种轨迹模型（线性和二次Laird-Ware、SITAR、brokenstick、多层样条，以及FACE），目的是推荐哪种模型最能捕捉身高和体重的个体生长轨迹。 The second strand, from Ohuma and Altman under the similarly Gates-funded INTERGROWTH-21st project, reviews design and statistical methodology for international fetal and neonatal growth references, working through the GAMLSS family for two worked examples — birthweight centiles and fetal head-circumference centiles. 第二条线来自Ohuma和Altman，来自同样由盖茨基金会资助的INTERGROWTH-21st项目，审视了国际胎儿和新生儿生长参照系的设计与统计方法，并用GAMLSS这一模型家族，针对出生体重百分位曲线和胎儿头围百分位曲线给出了两个实例分析。 The open question threading through both strands, which Cole makes his central point, is this: a model that best detects short-term growth faltering on cross-validated fit isn't automatically the model that best predicts later health outcomes, because those two things operate on different timescales, and nobody in this collection actually tested the second half of that claim. 贯穿这两条线的核心未决问题，也是Cole在评论中反复强调的要点是：一个在交叉验证拟合度上最善于捕捉短期生长迟滞的模型，并不自动等于最善于预测后续健康结局的模型，因为这两件事运作在不同的时间尺度上，而这批论文中没有一篇真正检验过后半句话。

JORDAN Walk me through the actual decision rule each group used to pick a "winning" model. 跟我讲讲每一组到底用了什么具体的决策规则来挑出"获胜"模型。

ALEX For the trajectory models, Anderson et al. used cross-validated mean squared error, computed separately on the raw height scale and on the HAZ Z-score scale, across all 21 studies, and on that basis recommended brokenstick and FACE for future work, since both fit noticeably better than the two Laird-Ware models and SITAR on the Z-score scale. 对于轨迹模型，Anderson等人使用了交叉验证均方误差（cross-validated MSE），分别在原始身高尺度和HAZ Z分数尺度上、在全部21项研究中计算，并在此基础上推荐brokenstick和FACE用于未来工作，因为这两者在Z分数尺度上明显优于两种Laird-Ware模型和SITAR。 For the GAMLSS birthweight centiles, Ohuma and Altman compared ten distribution-and-smoothing combinations by AIC and BIC, but then selected their final "best buy" model — a fractional polynomial with the skew-t ST3 distribution — based on centile appearance and the linearity of worm plots rather than on the AIC/BIC ranking itself, even though cubic-spline LMS-family models actually scored better on both information criteria. 对于GAMLSS出生体重百分位曲线，Ohuma和Altman用AIC和BIC比较了十种分布与平滑方法的组合，但他们最终选定的"最佳选择"模型——一个采用偏斜t分布（ST3）的分数多项式模型——却是基于百分位曲线的外观和蠕虫图（worm plot）的线性程度，而不是AIC/BIC的排名本身，尽管三次样条LMS系列模型在这两个信息准则上实际得分更好。 I'd push back on that choice directly: picking a model by eyeballing centile shape after the formal fit statistics already disagreed with you is exactly the kind of soft, visually-driven decision rule that's easy to defend in a paper and hard to defend as a reproducible protocol — and Cole catches a second instance of the same pattern in the fetal head-circumference example, where a three-level hierarchical model gets chosen as "optimal" despite other evidence against it. 我想直接对这个选择提出质疑：在正式的拟合统计量已经与你的判断不一致之后，仅凭肉眼观察百分位曲线的形状来挑选模型，正是那种在论文里容易自我辩护、但作为可复现流程却很难站得住脚的软性决策规则——而Cole在胎儿头围的例子里抓住了同一种模式的第二个例证，那里一个三层层级模型被选为"最优"，尽管有其他证据与此相悖。

JORDAN What was that other evidence — give me the actual numbers. 那个"其他证据"是什么——给我具体数字。

ALEX On the trajectory-model side, FACE failed to fit entirely for 8 of the 21 studies — a real robustness problem for a model being recommended as a default — and the brokenstick model's winning fit comes from five spline segments spread across roughly 700 days, meaning it's tuned to detect growth deviations on a timescale of about 140 days, or 4.6 months, not the full 2-year-plus span that quadratic Laird-Ware or SITAR summarize. 在轨迹模型这一侧，FACE在21项研究中有8项完全无法拟合——对于一个被推荐为默认选项的模型来说，这是一个真实的稳健性问题——而brokenstick模型的胜出拟合来自分布在约700天内的五段样条，这意味着它被调校为在约140天、即4.6个月的时间尺度上检测生长偏差，而不是二次Laird-Ware或SITAR所概括的整整两年以上的跨度。 On the GAMLSS side, the INTERGROWTH-21st birthweight reference used 20,302 neonates overall, but only 113 at 34 weeks gestation and just 51 at 33 weeks — thin enough at the preterm tail that Cole contrasts it directly with a UK national birthweight-centile study by Norris et al. using 1.2 million births, including 4,954 born before 32 weeks, for a precision comparison that isn't close. 在GAMLSS这一侧，INTERGROWTH-21st出生体重参照系总共使用了20,302名新生儿，但在孕34周时只有113例，孕33周时仅有51例——在早产这一端数据之稀薄，以至于Cole直接将它与Norris等人使用120万例出生数据（其中4,954例为32周前出生）的英国全国出生体重百分位研究做对比，二者在精度上根本不在同一量级。 And for the fetal head-circumference example, the three-level hierarchical model chosen as optimal turned out to be measurably biased in the tails: 5% of observations fell below the nominal 3rd centile and 5% above the nominal 97th, a 67% excess over the 3% that should appear on each side by definition — meaning the "best" model by one criterion was quantifiably miscalibrated by another. 而在胎儿头围的例子中，被选为最优的三层层级模型实际上在尾部存在可量化的偏差：5%的观测值落在名义第3百分位之下，5%落在名义第97百分位之上，比按定义本应出现在每一侧的3%超出了67%——也就是说，按一个标准选出的"最优"模型，按另一个标准衡量却是可量化地失准的。

JORDAN Does this change anything about the CFFPR outlier pipeline, or is it purely background reading? 这篇文章会改变CFFPR异常值检测流程中的什么吗，还是纯粹的背景阅读？

ALEX This one lands squarely on candidate generation, with a secondary hit on evaluation — it doesn't hand over a flagging algorithm, but it's the clearest treatment yet of why a substrate model's chosen timescale directly determines what gets flagged versus smoothed away. 这篇文章正好落在候选生成（candidate generation）这一环节，同时在评估（evaluation）环节也有次要影响——它没有直接给出一个标记算法，但它是迄今为止对"基底模型所选时间尺度如何直接决定什么被标记、什么被平滑掉"这一问题最清晰的阐述。 If a height/weight flagging substrate were brokenstick-like, its implicit "normal" envelope is tuned to roughly five-month fluctuations — plausibly well-matched to catching a true one-off data-entry error, since a decimal-shift or digit-transposition error is a single-point discontinuity shorter than that window — whereas a SITAR- or quadratic-Laird-Ware-like substrate gives a more parsimonious, longer-timescale trajectory that could just as easily smooth over a genuine short-term anomaly rather than flagging it, depending on which error type is actually the target. 如果一个身高/体重标记的基底模型是brokenstick式的，它隐含的"正常"范围被调校为大约五个月的波动——这或许很适合捕捉一次性的真实数据录入错误，因为小数点错位或数字颠倒这类错误是短于这个窗口的单点不连续——而SITAR式或二次Laird-Ware式的基底模型则给出了更简约、时间尺度更长的轨迹，这反而可能会把一个真实的短期异常平滑掉而不是标记出来，具体取决于真正要针对的错误类型。 The GAMLSS tail-miscalibration finding is the second hit, on evaluation: if any candidate flagging approach normalizes height/weight to a Z-score using an LMS/GAMLSS-fitted reference curve before applying a threshold, that reference curve's own tail calibration — the actual percentage beyond the nominal 3rd/97th centile — needs to be checked directly, the way Cole did here, rather than assumed from a reasonable-looking centile plot or a favorable AIC. GAMLSS尾部失准这一发现是第二个影响点，落在评估环节：如果任何候选标记方法在应用阈值之前，先用LMS/GAMLSS拟合的参照曲线把身高/体重转换为Z分数，那么这条参照曲线自身的尾部校准——即超出名义第3/第97百分位的实际比例——就需要像Cole在这里所做的那样直接去检验，而不能从一张看起来合理的百分位图或一个不错的AIC值就想当然地推断出来。 The buildable artifact is a substrate-model timescale-and-calibration audit: a small diagnostic script that, for any candidate substrate model, reports its effective detection timescale from its degrees of freedom or knot spacing, its cross-validated MSE on both raw and Z-score scales, and a direct tail-calibration check — the actual percentage of held-out points beyond the nominal 3rd and 97th centile — flagging any candidate whose tails are measurably miscalibrated before it's trusted as a flagging substrate. 可搭建的工具是一个基底模型时间尺度与校准审查工具：一个小型诊断脚本，对任意候选基底模型，报告其由自由度或样条结点间距所决定的有效检测时间尺度、在原始尺度和Z分数尺度上的交叉验证均方误差，以及一个直接的尾部校准检验——即保留样本中实际落在名义第3和第97百分位之外的比例——在任何候选模型的尾部被证明可量化失准时，在信任它作为标记基底之前先发出警示。 Difficulty is roughly S to M — the diagnostics themselves are standard mixed-model and spline machinery Runyu already has, the work is mainly in wiring up the three checks together as a standard pre-adoption gate rather than inventing anything new. 难度大致为S到M——这些诊断本身是Runyu已经熟悉的标准混合模型和样条工具，工作量主要在于把这三项检查整合成一个标准化的"采用前检查关卡"，而不是发明什么新东西。

JORDAN What would we need to check before trusting any of this enough to act on it? 在真正信任这些结论、据此行动之前，我们需要核实什么？
