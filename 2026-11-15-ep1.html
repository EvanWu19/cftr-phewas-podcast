Ep1 (Track A — variant interp): Predicting genome-wide functional constraints with GPN-Star

Chengzhong Ye, Gonzalo Benegas, and colleagues in Yun Song's lab at Berkeley introduce GPN-Star, a phylogeny-aware genomic language model that scores functional constraint across the whole genome — coding and non-coding — directly from whole-genome alignments and species trees. Unlike single-sequence genomic language models (Nucleotide Transformer, Evo 2), which learn evolutionary signal implicitly and at enormous compute cost, GPN-Star explicitly encodes the species tree via a phylogeny-informed cross-attention mechanism, reaching strong performance with 200-million-parameter models trained in days on eight GPUs. It doesn't dethrone allele-frequency-supervised missense scorers like AlphaMissense on ClinVar, but it clearly wins on non-coding pathogenic variants, promoter variants, GWAS fine-mapped variants, and heritability enrichment — territory existing CFTR tools largely can't reach because they're missense- or coding-only. The buildable artifact: a CFTR constraint cross-reference screen built entirely from the paper's public, precomputed genome-wide score files, flagging deep-intronic and regulatory VUS that current tools simply can't score.

Plain-English glossary

  • Clade-level embedding — a pooled representation of a whole branch of the species tree, built by grouping related source species and weighting their sequences by evolutionary distance.（中文：把进化树上相近的一组物种序列按进化距离加权汇总成的一个表示，用来代表整个分支而不是单个物种。）
  • Deep mutational scanning (DMS) — a functional assay that measures the fitness effect of every possible amino-acid substitution in a given protein; ProteinGym is a benchmark built from many such assays.（中文：一种功能实验，测量某个蛋白质所有可能的氨基酸替换对其功能/适应度的影响；ProteinGym是由许多这类实验汇总而成的基准测试集。）
  • Fine-mapping / PIP — the process of narrowing a GWAS association signal down to the specific causal variant within a region, reported as a posterior inclusion probability (PIP).（中文：从GWAS信号区域中缩小范围，找出真正驱动信号的具体变异的过程，结果用后验纳入概率(PIP)表示。）
  • Genomic language model (gLM) — a model trained to predict masked DNA bases from surrounding sequence context, used to estimate evolutionary constraint without labeled data.（中文：通过预测被遮盖的DNA碱基来学习序列上下文信息的模型，用于在没有标注数据的情况下估计进化约束。）
  • GWAS (genome-wide association study) — a study design that scans the genome for statistical associations between genetic variants and a trait or disease.（中文：在全基因组范围内扫描遗传变异与某一性状或疾病之间统计学关联的研究设计。）
  • Heritability enrichment / S-LDSC — a method (stratified LD score regression) for testing whether a genomic annotation captures a disproportionate share of a trait's genetic variance.（中文：一种名为S-LDSC(分层LD score回归)的方法，用于检验某个基因组注释是否捕获了某性状遗传变异中不成比例的一部分。）
  • PhyloP / PhastCons — classical statistical conservation scores computed from whole-genome alignments, long the standard tool for variant interpretation (used inside CADD).（中文：基于全基因组比对计算出的经典保守性统计评分，长期以来是变异解读的标准工具，CADD内部也使用它们。）
  • Rare variant association testing (RVAT) / DeepRVAT — gene-level statistical testing that aggregates rare variants within a gene to boost statistical power; DeepRVAT is a deep-learning framework for this that uses variant annotations like AlphaMissense as input.（中文：在基因层面聚合稀有变异以提高统计效力的检验方法；DeepRVAT是一个使用AlphaMissense等变异注释作为输入的深度学习框架。）
  • Whole-genome alignment (WGA) — lining up the same stretch of DNA across many species so conserved versus freely-drifting positions can be identified.（中文：把许多物种同一段DNA对齐排列，从而识别出哪些位点保守、哪些自由漂变。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • GPN-Star — phylogeny-aware genomic language model with public pretrained weights and precomputed genome-wide SNV scores (human + 5 model organisms) on Hugging Face; code at github.com/songlab-cal/gpn.
  • Mutation-rate calibration — normalizing a raw gLM score against a background mutation-rate estimate from neutral sites, to isolate selective constraint from mutability.
  • DeepRVAT — a deep-set-network framework for rare variant association testing that accepts external variant-effect annotations as input features.
  • S-LDSC — stratified LD score regression for testing whether a genomic annotation is enriched for trait heritability.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / CFTR variant interpretation (method only — the paper does not discuss CFTR). Buildable: CFTR Constraint Cross-Reference Screen — pull GPN-Star's public precomputed vertebrate/mammal/primate scores across the CFTR locus and flanking regulatory/deep-intronic regions, join against the current ClinVar/CFTR2 VUS list, and flag VUS in the top constrained percentile that missense- or coding-only tools (REVEL, CADD, AlphaMissense) cannot score at all. No model training required — scores are already public. Difficulty: S-M.

What to verify first

  • Whether the CFTR locus has good-quality, unfragmented alignment coverage across the species used (synteny can fragment near structurally rearranged reference regions — a general weakness the authors themselves flag).
  • Whether a top-percentile constraint score for a deep-intronic or regulatory VUS actually tracks pathogenicity the way it does in the paper's benchmarks, which are built from cross-gene OMIM/HGMD variants, not CFTR-specific FRT/CFBE functional assay data.
  • Which of the three evolutionary timescales (vertebrate/mammal/primate) to trust for a given CFTR candidate variant — the paper gives no decision rule, so agreement across all three may be a safer bar than any single timescale alone.
  • Whether GPN-Star's alignment-dependent, fixed-column architecture can meaningfully score indel variants at all, given that F508del — the single most common CFTR pathogenic allele — is itself a 3-bp deletion.

ALEX The other two terms you'll hear a lot: fine-mapping and heritability enrichment. 接下来会频繁出现的另外两个术语：精细定位 (fine-mapping) 和遗传度富集 (heritability enrichment)。 Fine-mapping takes a GWAS hit — a genomic region statistically associated with a trait — and narrows down which specific variant inside that region is actually driving the signal, reported as a posterior inclusion probability, or PIP. 精细定位 (fine-mapping) 是指从一个GWAS (genome-wide association study，全基因组关联研究) 信号区域出发，缩小范围找出真正驱动这个信号的具体变异，结果用后验纳入概率 (posterior inclusion probability, PIP) 来表示。 Heritability enrichment, measured here with a method called S-LDSC (stratified LD score regression, a way of partitioning a trait's heritability across different genomic annotations), asks whether a given annotation — say, "top 0.1% most constrained by this model" — captures a disproportionate share of a trait's genetic variance. 遗传度富集则是用一种叫S-LDSC (stratified LD score regression，按不同基因组注释对某个性状的遗传度进行拆分的方法) 的方法来衡量：某个注释类别（比如"被这个模型判定为最受限制的前0.1%"）是否捕获了该性状遗传变异中不成比例的一部分。 Neither is specific to this paper, but they're the two main yardsticks the authors use to prove GPN-Star's scores aren't just academically interesting. 这两个概念本身都不是这篇论文独有的，但作者正是用这两把尺子来证明GPN-Star的评分不只是学术上有趣而已。

JORDAN So walk me through why anyone needed a new gLM — what was broken with the existing ones? 那说说看，为什么还需要一个新的gLM——现有的方法出了什么问题？

ALEX Single-sequence gLMs like Nucleotide Transformer and Evo 2 learn evolutionary constraint implicitly, by masking nucleotides in one species's genome and predicting them back from sequence context alone — no alignment, no explicit cross-species information. 像Nucleotide Transformer和Evo 2这样的单序列gLM是隐式地学习进化约束的——它们在单一物种的基因组上遮盖一些核苷酸，然后仅凭序列上下文把它们预测回来，不使用比对信息，也不显式利用跨物种信息。 That buys flexibility — these models can score indels and structural variants, and don't need an alignment at inference time — but the paper's own benchmarks show they still lag behind the decades-old PhyloP and PhastCons on several human variant-interpretation tasks, especially in distal regulatory elements, even at massive scale: Evo 2 was trained on over 2,000 H100 GPUs for months. 这换来了灵活性——这些模型可以给插入缺失和结构变异打分，推理时也不需要比对——但论文自己的基准测试显示，在多项人类变异解读任务上，它们依然落后于已有几十年历史的PhyloP和PhastCons，尤其是在远端调控元件上，即便规模已经很庞大：Evo 2用了两千多块H100 GPU训练了数月之久。 The authors' prior model, GPN-MSA, tried folding alignment information back in, trained on a 90-vertebrate-species alignment. 作者之前的模型GPN-MSA尝试把比对信息重新纳入进来，基于90个脊椎动物物种的比对训练而成。 GPN-Star is the architectural upgrade: instead of just feeding in raw aligned sequences, it explicitly encodes the species tree itself. GPN-Star是架构上的升级版：它不再只是简单地输入原始的比对序列，而是显式地编码物种树本身。

JORDAN And the payoff of encoding the tree explicitly is what, exactly? 显式编码物种树带来的好处具体是什么？

ALEX Two things. 两点。 First, efficiency — GPN-Star's largest models are 200 million parameters, trained in days on 8 A100s, yet they beat models that are orders of magnitude more expensive to train, because explicit homology information from the alignment does work that a single-sequence model has to learn the hard way. 第一，效率——GPN-Star最大的模型只有2亿参数，用8块A100训练几天就能完成，却能打败训练成本高出几个数量级的模型，因为比对带来的显式同源信息，替单序列模型省下了它本要费力学习的那部分工作。 Second, flexibility within the alignment paradigm: because the model groups related source species into clade-level embeddings — pooled representations of whole branches of the tree, weighted by evolutionary distance — the same framework trains cleanly on vertebrate, mammal, or primate alignments, and even on five non-human species, with only minor tuning. 第二，在"比对"这一范式内部的灵活性：因为模型把相关的源物种归并成"进化支级" (clade-level) 的嵌入表示——也就是按进化距离加权、把整个进化树分支汇总成的表示——同一套框架只需稍作调整，就能干净地训练在脊椎动物、哺乳动物或灵长类比对上，甚至训练在五个非人类物种上。

ALEX Architecture-wise, it's an encoder-only transformer trained with a masked-language-modelling objective — same idea as BERT, but the "sentence" is a window of a whole-genome alignment and its species tree, split into a handful of target sequences and many source sequences. 从架构上看，这是一个用掩码语言模型 (masked language modelling) 目标训练的仅编码器 (encoder-only) transformer——思路跟BERT一样，只不过这里的"句子"是一段全基因组比对窗口及其对应的物种树，被拆分成少量目标序列和大量源序列。 Each encoder block has ordinary self-attention over the target sequence, a phylogeny-informed cross-attention module that pulls in the clade-level source embeddings weighted by tree distance, and a feed-forward layer; the output head predicts nucleotide probabilities at masked positions in the targets. 每个编码器模块都包含对目标序列的常规自注意力 (self-attention)、一个按树距离加权、从进化支级源嵌入中提取信息的"系统发生树感知交叉注意力" (phylogeny-informed cross-attention) 模块，以及一个前馈层；输出层则预测目标序列中被遮盖位置的核苷酸概率。 They trained three human models — vertebrate-, mammal-, and primate-scale — plus five model-organism versions for mouse, chicken, fruit fly, roundworm, and Arabidopsis, each on 18 to 135 aligned species depending on what's available. 他们训练了三个人类模型——分别对应脊椎动物、哺乳动物和灵长类三个尺度——外加五个模式生物版本，涵盖小鼠、鸡、果蝇、线虫和拟南芥，每个版本根据数据可得性用18到135个物种的比对训练而成。
