Ep1 — Clinically interpretable deep learning for breast cancer missense variant pathogenicity prediction

Ahmad RM, AlDhaheri N, Mohamad MS, Ali BR (2026), Frontiers in Bioinformatics. This paper runs a fair, head-to-head benchmark of seven deep learning architectures (MLP, DNN, CNN, RNN, LSTM, GRU, Transformer) for classifying breast cancer missense variants as pathogenic or benign, trained on 11,542 pathogenic and 12,498 benign variants pooled across a curated 105-gene breast-cancer panel. The headline result is GRU, which wins on every internal metric (AUC 0.9956) and — more interestingly for this podcast — also posts the lowest calibration error (ECE 0.0095) and the tightest cross-seed variance of any architecture, showing that discrimination and calibration can converge on the same winner rather than trading off. The episode isn't about a new CFTR tool (the paper never studies CFTR, and its 105-gene cancer panel is a different biological shape of problem than a single recessive gene); it's about the reporting standard the paper models: calibration and case-level interpretability (LIME, permutation importance) reported as first-class criteria alongside AUC, not an afterthought. The buildable idea it leaves behind is a calibration-plus-interpretability report card that could be generated for any CFTR-relevant pathogenicity score.

Plain-English glossary

  • Missense variant — A single-nucleotide DNA change that substitutes one amino acid for another in the resulting protein, which may or may not disrupt protein function.（中文：单个核苷酸改变导致蛋白质中一个氨基酸被替换，可能破坏也可能不影响蛋白质功能。）
  • ACMG/AMP evidence codes — The standard rulebook codes for classifying variant pathogenicity; PVS1 flags predicted loss-of-function, PM2 flags population rarity, PP3 is supportive computational evidence, BS1/BS2 are benign counter-evidence (population frequency, healthy carriers).（中文：用于变异致病性分类的标准规则代码；PVS1标记预测功能丧失，PM2标记人群稀有性，PP3是支持性计算证据，BS1/BS2是良性反证。）
  • ClinVar — NIH's public variant classification database; each submission carries a star rating (0-4) reflecting the strength of expert review behind it.（中文：NIH维护的公开变异分类数据库；每条提交记录都有0到4星的评级，反映其专家评审力度。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant lacking enough evidence to call pathogenic or benign.（中文：证据不足以判定为致病或良性的变异。）
  • Conservation scores (phyloP, GERP++) — Measures of how evolutionarily conserved a DNA position is across species; a highly conserved position that's mutated is more likely to be functionally important.（中文：衡量某DNA位点在物种间进化保守程度的指标；高度保守的位点若发生突变，更可能具有功能重要性。）
  • Meta-predictors (FATHMM-MKL, DEOGEN2, BayesDel) — Machine-learning models that combine multiple underlying functional-impact scores into a single deleteriousness estimate.（中文：整合多个功能影响评分、输出单一有害性估计的机器学习模型。）
  • AlphaMissense — An AlphaFold-derived pathogenicity score trained on protein structure and population/evolutionary data.（中文：基于AlphaFold蛋白质结构训练出的致病性评分工具。）
  • VEP (Variant Effect Predictor) — Ensembl's standard tool for annotating a raw genetic variant with functional consequence and downstream scores.（中文：Ensembl开发的标准工具，用于给原始遗传变异标注功能后果及下游评分。）
  • LIME — Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations; a technique that explains one individual prediction by approximating the model locally with a simpler, interpretable one.（中文：一种可解释性技术，通过在局部用简单模型近似复杂模型，来解释单个预测结果。）
  • Circularity (in variant classifiers) — When a predictive feature is itself a model trained on the same labels used as ground truth, inflating apparent performance without adding independent signal.（中文：指预测特征本身是基于与真值标签相同来源训练出的模型，从而虚高表面性能，而未带来独立信号。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Calibration as a first-class selection criterion — ECE and Brier score reported and optimized alongside AUC, not as an afterthought; isotonic recalibration applied and reported for every model.
  • Five-seed mean±SD as the primary performance estimator — avoids optimistic bias from best-seed cherry-picking; best-seed run reserved only for case-level interpretability where a fixed prediction set is needed.
  • DeLong's test for pairwise architecture comparison — the correct statistic for comparing correlated ROC curves, used to confirm the top-two-architecture gap is statistically real.
  • Explicit circularity handling — hard-excluding unambiguously circular features (ClinPred) pre-feature-selection, and flagging/ablating partially circular ones (am_pathogenicity) rather than ignoring the issue.
  • Dual interpretability (global + local) — permutation importance for global feature ranking, LIME on representative TP/TN/FP/FN cases for case-level explanation.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation priority — method only, not a specific classifier (the paper never studies CFTR). MVP: a calibration-plus-interpretability report card — compute ECE, Brier score, and a reliability diagram for any existing or future CFTR pathogenicity score, plus LIME or permutation-importance panels for a handful of representative CFTR calls (confident TP/TN plus a couple of borderline/discordant cases) — generated automatically as a one-pager to read alongside any raw score. Difficulty: S-M.

What to verify first

  • Whether germline/somatic pooling changes calibration specifically (not just AUC) — an aggregate ECE could hide subgroup-level overconfidence.
  • How much of the reported performance reflects circularity versus independent signal, beyond the one gene-held-out ablation (AUC 0.815 without am_pathogenicity).
  • Whether the external ClinGen validation (LSTM AUC 0.9457) would hold on a truly independent annotation pipeline, given it shares VEP with the training data.
  • What baseline calibration existing CFTR-relevant scores (AlphaMissense, REVEL) already have, before assuming a calibration report card adds large marginal value.

ALEX Missense variants make up a huge share of clinically reported breast cancer findings, and most of them land as VUS rather than a clean pathogenic or benign call — genetic counselors are stuck holding a result they can't act on. 错义变异在乳腺癌的临床报告结果中占了很大比例，而其中大多数最终被归类为VUS，而不是明确的致病或良性——遗传咨询师因此常常拿着一个无法据以行动的结果。Traditional functional assays that could resolve a VUS directly are slow and expensive, which is why the field has leaned so heavily on computational predictors like SIFT and PolyPhen for decades. 能够直接解决VUS问题的传统功能实验既缓慢又昂贵，这也是为什么该领域几十年来一直高度依赖SIFT、PolyPhen这类计算预测工具。But most of those tools, and even newer entrants like AlphaMissense and REVEL, share two weaknesses the authors call out directly: they don't generalize well to variants in genes or populations underrepresented in their training data, and they return an opaque score with no traceable link back to the kind of evidence a geneticist actually reasons with. 但作者直接指出，大多数这类工具，甚至包括AlphaMissense和REVEL这样较新的工具，都存在两个共同弱点：它们对训练数据中代表性不足的基因或人群变异泛化能力差，而且只会返回一个不透明的分数，无法追溯到遗传学家实际推理时所依赖的那类证据。There's also a quieter methodological problem the paper takes seriously: deep learning benchmarks in genomics rarely report calibration alongside discrimination, so a model can look excellent on AUC while its predicted probabilities are meaningless as probabilities. 论文还认真对待了一个不太显眼的方法学问题：基因组学中的深度学习基准测试很少在报告判别能力（AUC）的同时报告校准度，因此一个模型可能AUC表现优异，但它给出的预测概率作为"概率"本身却毫无意义。So the stated goal here is threefold: benchmark seven DL architectures under identical, fair conditions; treat calibration as a first-class selection criterion alongside AUC, not an afterthought; and pair every prediction with case-level interpretability so a clinician can see why, not just what. 因此本文设定的目标有三个：在完全相同、公平的条件下对七种深度学习架构进行基准测试；把校准度作为与AUC同等重要的首要模型选择标准，而不是事后补充；并为每个预测都配上病例层面的可解释性，让临床医生不仅能看到"是什么"，还能看到"为什么"。

JORDAN What would you push back on here? 你会对哪一点提出质疑？

ALEX The germline/somatic mixing is the one that jumps out at me. 最让我在意的是胚系和体细胞变异被混在一起训练这件事。A germline BRCA1 variant carried in every cell and driving hereditary cancer risk is not the same clinical object as a somatic mutation acquired by a tumor, and the paper trains one pooled classifier across both without stratifying performance by origin — they say so themselves in the limitations, but it means today's headline AUC numbers are an average across two different biological questions, not a clean answer to either one. 一个在每个细胞中都携带、驱动遗传性癌症风险的胚系BRCA1变异，和肿瘤后天获得的体细胞突变，并不是同一类临床对象，而这篇论文训练了一个汇总的分类器，却没有按变异来源对性能分层报告——作者自己在局限性部分也承认了这一点，但这意味着今天看到的头条AUC数字其实是两个不同生物学问题的平均值，而不是对其中任何一个问题的干净答案。The other thing worth flagging is circularity: several of the ten selected features, including am_pathogenicity, are themselves models trained partly on ClinVar, the same source supplying the ground-truth labels here — the authors do handle this more honestly than most papers, excluding the worst offender (ClinPred) outright and running a gene-held-out ablation without am_pathogenicity, but it's still worth remembering that "AUC 0.99" partly reflects how well the model reproduces other tools' prior labeling, not purely independent biological signal. 另一个值得指出的问题是循环论证：被选中的十个特征中有几个，包括am_pathogenicity，本身就是部分基于ClinVar训练出来的模型，而ClinVar恰恰也是本文真值标签的来源——作者对这个问题的处理确实比大多数论文更诚实，直接剔除了问题最严重的ClinPred，还做了一个不含am_pathogenicity的基因留出消融实验，但仍然值得记住的是，"AUC 0.99"这个数字里有一部分反映的是模型对其他工具既有标签的复现能力，而不是纯粹独立的生物学信号。
