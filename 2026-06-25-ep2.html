How did this episode land for you?

Blurb. Singhal et al. (2023, Nature) — the Med-PaLM paper — matters here not for the model but for the evaluation framework it shipped. The authors built MultiMedQA (six existing medical QA datasets + the new HealthSearchQA, 3,173 consumer questions) and, crucially, a human-evaluation rubric that grades free-text answers along multiple axes: scientific-consensus alignment, incorrect content, omission, possible harm, and bias. Flan-PaLM 540B set multiple-choice SOTA (67.6% on MedQA/USMLE, +17% over prior best), but the rubric exposed what accuracy hid: only 61.9% of Flan-PaLM's long-form answers aligned with scientific consensus and 29.7% were potentially harmful — versus 92.6% and 5.9% for Med-PaLM after instruction prompt tuning on just 65 clinician-written exemplars, near clinician parity (92.9% / 5.7%). Where it lands: the AI-tooling / career priority. Buildable artifact: a CF clinical-QA evaluation harness that scores any candidate LLM along the Med-PaLM axes against a curated CF question bank.

Plain-English glossary

  • Chain-of-thought prompting — prompting a model to show intermediate reasoning steps before its answer.（中文：让模型先写出中间推理步骤再给答案的提示方式。）
  • HealthSearchQA — a dataset of 3,173 commonly searched consumer health questions introduced in this paper.（中文：本文新建的 3,173 个常被搜索的消费者健康问题数据集。）
  • Instruction prompt tuning — a parameter-efficient alignment method that learns a small soft-prompt prefix from a few exemplars without changing the base model weights.（中文：一种参数高效的对齐法，只从少量范例学一段"软提示"前缀，不改主体权重。）
  • Instruction tuning (Flan) — fine-tuning a model to follow natural-language instructions across many tasks.（中文：让模型学会跨多任务遵循自然语言指令的微调。）
  • MultiMedQA — the assembled benchmark spanning exam, research, and consumer medical questions.（中文：涵盖考试、研究、消费者三类医学问题的组合基准。）
  • PaLM 540B / Flan-PaLM — a 540-billion-parameter LLM and its instruction-tuned variant.（中文：5400 亿参数的大语言模型及其指令微调版本。）
  • Scientific-consensus alignment — whether an answer agrees with prevailing clinical guidelines.（中文：回答是否与主流临床指南一致。）
  • USMLE / MedQA — US Medical Licensing Examination–style multiple-choice questions.（中文：美国执业医师资格考试风格的选择题。）

Full citation

Singhal K, et al. Large language models encode clinical knowledge. Nature. 2023;620(7972):172–180. PMID: 37438534 · PMC: PMC10396962 · DOI: 10.1038/s41586-023-06291-2

Methods / tools to lift

  • Multi-axis human-eval rubric — grade free-text answers on consensus alignment, incorrect content, omission, harm extent/likelihood, and bias; the reusable core of the paper.
  • Curated domain question bank — pairing exam/research/consumer questions with clinician-written reference answers (HealthSearchQA pattern).
  • Instruction prompt tuning — parameter-efficient alignment from ~65 exemplars; cheap to retarget to a new clinical domain.
  • Contamination check — the paper's own caution that large models may memorize public benchmarks; build a held-out, private question set.
  • Released data — MultiMedQA's open-source components + HealthSearchQA shipped as supplementary.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves AI-tooling / career. Paste-ready MVP: CF clinical-QA eval harness — input a CF question bank (variant interpretation, modulator eligibility, CFRD screening, nutrition) with reference answers + a model endpoint; output a per-axis scorecard (consensus alignment, incorrect content, omission, harm, bias) across candidate LLMs. Difficulty: M — writing defensible reference answers + a clear rubric (clinician in the loop) is the real labor.

What to verify first

  • Rubric is subjective — clinician harm estimates vary even among experts; use ≥2 raters + an inter-rater agreement check before any score is meaningful.
  • Consensus is time-varying — date-stamp reference answers and plan refreshes; acute in CF given fast-moving modulator guidance.
  • Benchmark contamination — a large model may have memorized public sets; keep the CF question bank private/held-out so scores aren't inflated.
  • 65-exemplar fragility — the safety gain rides on a tiny hand-curated set; exemplar quality dominates if the recipe is reused on CF questions.

ALEX Welcome back. 欢迎回来。 This is a Track B episode — AI for registry and bioinformatics work, with a tool-builder's eye. 这是一期 Track B——面向登记数据和生物信息学工作的 AI，带着工具开发者的眼光。 Today's paper is "Large language models encode clinical knowledge," first author Karan Singhal, published in Nature in 2023. 今天这篇论文是《大语言模型编码了临床知识》，第一作者 Karan Singhal，2023 年发表在《自然》(Nature) 上。 This is the Med-PaLM paper, and we're reading it not for the model but for the evaluation framework it shipped. 这就是那篇 Med-PaLM 论文，而我们读它不是为了那个模型，而是为了它交付出来的那套评估框架 (evaluation framework)。 The eval rubric is the reusable asset here, and it maps cleanly onto scoring an LLM against a registry clinical question-and-answer corpus. 那套评估标准才是这里可复用的资产，它能干净地映射到"用一套登记数据的临床问答语料去给某个大语言模型打分"。

JORDAN I know LLMs, but let me make sure I have the clinical-benchmark vocabulary. 我懂大语言模型，但让我确认一下临床基准的术语。 What's MultiMedQA? MultiMedQA 是什么？ ALEX: MultiMedQA is the benchmark they assembled — six existing medical question-answering datasets plus one new one. MultiMedQA 是他们拼起来的基准——六个已有的医学问答数据集，再加一个新的。 It spans three flavors of question: professional medical-exam questions, biomedical-research questions, and consumer health questions. 它涵盖三种风味的问题：专业医学考试题、生物医学研究题、和消费者健康题。 JORDAN: Give me the named pieces. 把里面有名字的部件给我点一下。 ALEX: MedQA is USMLE-style — US Medical Licensing Examination questions, four or five options. MedQA 是 USMLE 风格的——美国执业医师资格考试题，四到五个选项。 MedMCQA is Indian medical-entrance-exam questions. MedMCQA 是印度医学入学考试题。 PubMedQA asks yes/no/maybe questions over biomedical abstracts. PubMedQA 是在生物医学摘要上问"是／否／也许"的题。 And the new one they introduced, HealthSearchQA, is 3,173 commonly searched consumer health questions. 而他们新引入的那个，HealthSearchQA，是 3,173 个被频繁搜索的消费者健康问题。 JORDAN: So it's not one test, it's a battery covering very different audiences. 所以它不是一个测验，而是一套覆盖非常不同人群的测验组合。 ALEX: Exactly — and that breadth is the point, because a model can ace board exams and still give a dangerous answer to a worried parent. 正是如此——这种广度就是关键，因为一个模型可以在执照考试上拿满分，却仍然给一个焦虑的家长一个危险的答案。 JORDAN: Why mix consumer questions in with exam questions at all? 为什么要把消费者问题和考试问题混在一起？ ALEX: Because the failure modes are completely different. 因为失败的方式完全不同。 An exam question has one defensible answer and a clean key. 考试题有一个站得住脚的答案和一份干净的标准答案。 A consumer question — "my child has a fever and a rash, what do I do" — is open-ended, and the harm of a bad answer is immediate and physical. 一个消费者问题——"我孩子发烧又起疹子，我该怎么办"——是开放式的，而一个糟糕答案的伤害是即时的、肉体上的。 HealthSearchQA exists precisely to stress-test that open-ended, real-world setting. HealthSearchQA 存在的目的，恰恰就是去压力测试那种开放式的、真实世界的场景。

JORDAN One more piece of vocabulary — hard prompt versus soft prompt? 再问一个词——硬提示和软提示有什么区别？ ALEX: A hard prompt is ordinary text you write, like "answer this medical question carefully." 硬提示就是你写的普通文字，比如"请仔细回答这个医学问题"。 A soft prompt is a small set of learned numerical vectors prepended to the input — not human-readable, but tuned by gradient descent to steer the model. 软提示则是一小组学习出来的数值向量，加在输入前面——人读不懂，但通过梯度下降调出来引导模型。 Instruction prompt tuning learns that soft prefix while leaving the giant base model frozen. 指令提示微调学的就是那段软前缀，而把庞大的基座模型冻结不动。 JORDAN: So you steer a 540-billion-parameter model without retraining it. 所以你不用重训就能引导一个 5400 亿参数的模型。 ALEX: Right, and that's why it's called parameter-efficient — you're tuning a sliver, not the whole thing. 对，这就是为什么它叫参数高效——你调的是极小的一片，而不是整个模型。

JORDAN So what was the gap they were trying to close? 那他们想填补的缺口是什么？ ALEX: Before this, people assessed medical LLMs almost entirely with automated accuracy on narrow multiple-choice benchmarks. 在此之前，人们评估医学大语言模型几乎全靠在狭窄的选择题基准上的自动化准确率。 JORDAN: And why is that not enough? 那为什么这不够？ ALEX: Because in a safety-critical domain, a single accuracy number hides everything that matters. 因为在一个安全攸关的领域里，单一的准确率数字把所有真正重要的东西都藏起来了。 A model can pick the right multiple-choice letter and still, in free-form text, hallucinate convincing misinformation or bake in bias that worsens health disparities. 一个模型可以选对那个选择题字母，却仍然在自由文本里编造出看似可信的错误信息，或者植入会加剧健康不平等的偏见。 JORDAN: So the missing thing is a way to grade long-form answers like a clinician would. 所以缺的是一种像临床医生那样去给长篇回答打分的方法。 ALEX: That's the heart of it. 这就是核心。 They built a human-evaluation framework that rates free-text answers along multiple axes: alignment with scientific consensus, presence of incorrect content, omission of important content, possible extent and likelihood of harm, and possible bias. 他们搭了一个人工评估框架，沿着多个维度给自由文本回答打分：与科学共识的一致性、是否含错误内容、是否遗漏重要内容、可能造成伤害的程度和可能性、以及可能的偏见。 They also added comprehension, knowledge recall, and reasoning. 他们还加了理解、知识回忆和推理。 That rubric — not the model weights — is what I want Runyu to walk away with. 那套评分标准——而不是模型权重——才是我希望 Runyu 带走的东西。 JORDAN: Why is the model itself the wrong thing to fixate on? 为什么模型本身是不该执着的东西？ ALEX: Because the specific model is already obsolete — this is a 2023 paper and the field moves in months. 因为这个具体的模型已经过时了——这是 2023 年的论文，而这个领域以月为单位推进。 But the question "how do you tell whether a medical model is safe to put in front of a patient" does not go obsolete. 但"你怎么判断一个医学模型能不能放到病人面前才安全"这个问题不会过时。 The evaluation methodology outlives any single model, and that's the durable, transferable thing. 评估方法学比任何单个模型活得更久，而那才是经久、可迁移的东西。 JORDAN: So we read it as a methods paper wearing a model-results costume. 所以我们把它当成一篇方法学论文，只是穿着模型结果的外衣。 ALEX: Couldn't have said it better. 说得不能更好了。

JORDAN Now walk me through what they actually built. 现在带我看看他们究竟做了什么。 ALEX: The base model is PaLM, a 540-billion-parameter LLM. 基座模型是 PaLM，一个 5400 亿参数的大语言模型。 They also used Flan-PaLM, the instruction-tuned variant. 他们还用了 Flan-PaLM，也就是经过指令微调 (instruction-tuned) 的变体。 JORDAN: And Med-PaLM itself? Med-PaLM 本身呢？ ALEX: Med-PaLM is Flan-PaLM after one more step they call instruction prompt tuning. Med-PaLM 是 Flan-PaLM 再经过一个他们叫"指令提示微调 (instruction prompt tuning)"的步骤之后得到的。 It learns a small soft-prompt prefix from just a handful of exemplars. 它只从极少量的范例里学习一段软提示前缀。 JORDAN: How few exemplars? 多少范例算少？ ALEX: They had a panel of five clinicians write exemplar answers and ended up using 65 examples. 他们让五位临床医生写范例答案，最后用了 65 个例子。 Sixty-five examples to steer a 540-billion-parameter model toward safer, better-grounded medical answers. 用 65 个例子去把一个 5400 亿参数的模型引导向更安全、更有依据的医学回答。 JORDAN: That's a striking ratio. 这个比例很惊人。 ALEX: It is, and here's the design choice I'd push back on. 确实，这里有一个设计选择我想反驳一下。 Sixty-five clinician-written exemplars is a tiny, hand-curated set, and the whole safety improvement rides on it. 65 个临床医生手写的范例是一个非常小、手工挑选的集合，而整个安全性提升都押在它上面。 That makes the result feel less like robust alignment and more like a very good demonstration. 这让结果感觉不太像稳健的对齐，而更像一个非常出色的演示。 If you reused this recipe on CF-specific questions, the quality of those few exemplars would dominate everything — which is a warning and an opportunity at the same time. 如果你把这套配方用在 CF 专门的问题上，那几个范例的质量会主导一切——这既是警告，也是机会。

JORDAN How did they coax the multiple-choice accuracy up in the first place? 他们一开始是怎么把选择题准确率哄上去的？ ALEX: Two tricks beyond just scale. 除了单纯堆规模之外还有两个技巧。 First, chain-of-thought prompting — asking the model to reason step by step before committing to an answer. 第一，思维链 (chain-of-thought) 提示——让模型在给出答案之前一步步推理。 Second, self-consistency — sampling many reasoning paths and taking a majority vote. 第二，自一致性 (self-consistency)——采样很多条推理路径，再取多数投票。 They ran 41 decodes with chain-of-thought and voted across them. 他们用思维链跑了 41 次解码，然后在它们之间投票。 JORDAN: And scale itself mattered a lot? 而规模本身也很重要？ ALEX: Enormously. 极其重要。 Going from the 8-billion to the 540-billion model roughly doubled accuracy, and for PaLM the jump was over 30%. 从 80 亿到 5400 亿的模型，准确率大约翻了一倍，而对 PaLM 来说提升超过了 30%。 The 540B Flan-PaLM beat the 62B variant by more than 14 points and the 8B by more than 24. 540B 的 Flan-PaLM 比 62B 的高出 14 个百分点以上，比 8B 的高出 24 个以上。 That scaling curve is part of why they argue medical question-answering looks like an emergent ability. 那条规模曲线，是他们论证"医学问答看起来像一种涌现能力 (emergent ability)"的部分理由。

JORDAN Okay, did it actually work? 好，那它到底有没有用？ ALEX: On the multiple-choice side, yes, decisively. 在选择题这边，有用，而且是决定性的。 Flan-PaLM 540B hit 67.6% accuracy on MedQA — the USMLE-style set — beating the previous state of the art by more than 17%, and beating the prior best model, DRAGON, by about 20 points. Flan-PaLM 540B 在 MedQA——也就是 USMLE 风格的那套——上达到 67.6% 的准确率，比之前的最好成绩高了超过 17%，比之前最好的模型 DRAGON 高了大约 20 个百分点。 It reached 57.6% on MedMCQA and 79.0% on PubMedQA. 它在 MedMCQA 上达到 57.6%，在 PubMedQA 上达到 79.0%。 On the harder five-option MedQA it still got 62.0%. 在更难的五选项 MedQA 上它仍拿到 62.0%。 JORDAN: Were there subjects where it was especially strong? 有没有哪些科目它特别强？ ALEX: On the MMLU clinical subsets it hit 83.8% on professional medicine and 80.4% on clinical knowledge, beating other large models like Gopher, Chinchilla, and Galactica. 在 MMLU 的临床子集上，它在专业医学上拿到 83.8%、在临床知识上拿到 80.4%，打败了 Gopher、Chinchilla、Galactica 等其他大模型。 Though, to be fair, they themselves raise the contamination worry: a model trained on much of the web may have seen some of these public test items during pre-training. 不过，公平地说，他们自己也提出了污染的担忧：一个在大半个互联网上训练的模型，可能在预训练时就见过这些公开测试题的一部分。 So a high score on a public benchmark is never quite as clean as it looks. 所以一个公开基准上的高分，永远不会像看上去那么干净。 JORDAN: And the part you actually care about — the long-form human eval? 那你真正在意的部分——长篇人工评估呢？ ALEX: This is where it gets interesting. 这正是有意思的地方。 A panel of clinicians judged only 61.9% of Flan-PaLM's long-form answers to be aligned with scientific consensus. 一组临床医生判定，Flan-PaLM 的长篇回答里只有 61.9% 与科学共识一致。 After instruction prompt tuning, Med-PaLM hit 92.6% — essentially level with the clinician-written answers at 92.9%. 经过指令提示微调后，Med-PaLM 达到了 92.6%——基本和临床医生手写答案的 92.9% 持平。 JORDAN: That's a huge jump from the same base model. 从同一个基座模型来看，这是个巨大的飞跃。 ALEX: And it's worth being precise about what changed. 而且值得精确地说清楚到底变了什么。 The base model's raw knowledge didn't move — the multiple-choice scores were already strong. 基座模型的原始知识没有变——选择题分数本来就很强。 What instruction prompt tuning fixed was the packaging: grounding answers in consensus, not omitting key caveats, and not drifting into harmful advice. 指令提示微调修好的是"包装"：把答案锚定在共识上、不遗漏关键提醒、不漂移到有害建议上。 JORDAN: So it taught manners, not facts. 所以它教的是举止，不是事实。 ALEX: Roughly, yes. 大致是的。 And the harm axis is even more telling. 而伤害这个维度更说明问题。 29.7% of Flan-PaLM's answers were rated as potentially leading to harm; for Med-PaLM that dropped to 5.9%, close to the clinicians' 5.7%. Flan-PaLM 的回答里有 29.7% 被评为可能导致伤害；到了 Med-PaLM 这个数字降到 5.9%，接近临床医生的 5.7%。 Helpfulness rose from 60.6% to 80.3%. 有用性从 60.6% 升到了 80.3%。 So the multiple-choice score barely tells you which model is safe to deploy — the rubric does. 所以选择题分数几乎没法告诉你哪个模型部署起来是安全的——是那套评分标准告诉你的。 JORDAN: Did they also check what ordinary people thought, not just clinicians? 他们有没有也看看普通人怎么想，而不只是临床医生？ ALEX: They did — a separate lay-user panel rated helpfulness, and the same story held: Flan-PaLM helpful in about 60% of cases, Med-PaLM in about 80%, but still short of human clinicians. 看了——一个单独的非专业用户小组评了有用性，结论一样：Flan-PaLM 大约 60% 的情况有用，Med-PaLM 大约 80%，但仍然不如人类临床医生。 They were also candid that their bias evaluation was preliminary — a few probing questions, not a full fairness audit. 他们也坦白自己的偏见评估只是初步的——几个探针问题，而不是一次完整的公平性审计。 That humility is part of why the framework is worth copying — it tells you what it does not yet measure. 那种谦逊正是这个框架值得照搬的部分原因——它告诉你它还没测到什么。
