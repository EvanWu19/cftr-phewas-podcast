Ep3 (Track C — registry QC, 1 of 2): A population-level health check for ten years of a national anthropometric registry

Silva, Mello e Silva, Carrilho and colleagues (2023, Revista de Saúde Pública) apply the WHO-UNICEF TEAM multi-indicator data-quality framework — completeness, sex ratio, age distribution, digit preference, implausible z-scores, dispersion, and normality — to 73.7 million child height/weight records from Brazil's Food and Nutrition Surveillance System (SISVAN), 2008–2017, the first study to apply this full battery to routinely-collected registry data rather than a one-off survey. Coverage rose from 17.7% to 45.4% of the target population, and completeness stayed near 100%, but height-digit preference stayed essentially flat around 88–90% dissimilarity for the entire decade, implausible z-scores exceeded WHO's 1% quality threshold for every index in every year even after a post-2014 improvement, and both implausibility and dispersion problems concentrated in children under two and in the most socioeconomically vulnerable regions — exactly where reliable data matters most. The authors themselves caution that excess dispersion could reflect genuine population heterogeneity rather than pure data error. This isn't a per-record flagging algorithm; it's a population-level scorecard. Buildable artifact: a Registry Anthropometric Data-Quality Scorecard.

Plain-English glossary

  • Digit preference — a systematic tendency to round measurements to preferred terminal digits (commonly 0 or 5), revealing measurement or recording shortcuts rather than true values.（中文：尾数偏好，指测量值被系统性地四舍五入到偏好的尾数（通常是0或5），反映出测量或记录过程中的偷工减料，而非真实数值。）
  • Implausible z-score — a z-score that falls outside a fixed, biologically impossible range, used as a direct plausibility check on anthropometric data.（中文：不合理z-score，指落在一个固定的、生物学上不可能出现的范围之外的z-score，用作对人体测量数据的直接合理性检验。）
  • LMS method — a statistical method that summarizes a reference population's measurement distribution at each age using three parameters (L: skewness correction, M: median, S: coefficient of variation) to compute z-scores.（中文：LMS方法，一种统计方法，用三个参数（L：偏度校正，M：中位数，S：变异系数）概括参考人群在每个年龄段的测量值分布，用以计算z-score。）
  • Myers blended index of dissimilarity — a formula quantifying how far an observed distribution (of ages in months, or terminal digits) deviates from the uniform distribution expected under accurate recording.（中文：Myers混合不相似性指数，一个用于量化观察到的分布（月龄或尾数分布）与"准确记录条件下理应呈现的均匀分布"之间偏离程度的公式。）
  • WHO vs. CDC growth references — WHO's child growth standards are prescriptive, built from healthy children raised under favorable conditions to show how children should grow; CDC's growth charts are descriptive, built from an actual cross-sectional population to show how children in that reference group did grow.（中文：WHO与CDC生长参考标准的区别——WHO儿童生长标准是规范性的，基于在有利条件下成长的健康儿童建立，用以展示儿童"应该"如何生长；CDC生长曲线图是描述性的，基于真实横断面人群建立，用以展示该参考人群中儿童"实际"的生长情况。）

Full citation

Silva NJ, Silva JFM, Carrilho TRB, et al. "Quality of child anthropometric data from SISVAN, Brazil, 2008-2017." Revista de Saúde Pública. 2023. PMID: 37878848 | PMC10519688 | DOI: 10.11606/s1518-8787.2023057004655

Methods / tools to lift

  • WHO-UNICEF TEAM multi-indicator framework — a joint assessment of completeness, sex ratio, age distribution, digit preference, implausible z-scores, dispersion, and normality, rather than any single indicator in isolation.
  • Myers blended dissimilarity index — reusable for quantifying digit preference or age/date heaping in any registry field.
  • WHO STATA igrowup macro — computes LMS-based z-scores against WHO child growth reference curves and flags values outside fixed implausibility cutoffs.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (e) nothing directly, but supplies vocabulary/framework, primary — a reusable, structured population-level data-quality battery, not an individual-record flagging rule; (d) evaluation, secondary — trend-over-time tracking of these indicators as a weak, no-gold-standard signal of whether collection practice is improving. Buildable artifact: a Registry Anthropometric Data-Quality Scorecard — compute completeness, digit-preference (Myers index), implausible-z-score percentage, dispersion, and skewness/kurtosis per year and per demographic stratum as a standing population-level dashboard, complementing rather than replacing individual-record flagging. Difficulty: small.

Error types covered / not covered: Covers systematic rounding/digit-heaping, gross biological implausibility against fixed cutoffs, and excess population-level dispersion/non-normality as aggregate signals; does not identify, flag, or adjudicate any individual erroneous record, and does not distinguish data-entry error from genuine population heterogeneity at the population-indicator level — the authors explicitly flag this as an open limitation.

What to verify first

  • Whether an apparent improving or worsening trend in these indicators reflects real data-collection improvement versus a shifting population mix over time.
  • What stratification granularity (site, region, other) would make a scorecard like this actionable rather than purely descriptive.
  • How a population-level scorecard should sit alongside whatever individual-record flagging approach eventually gets used — an open design question, not something this paper settles.
  • Whether the specific WHO implausibility cutoffs and 1% quality threshold used here are the right reference points, or whether a different population would need different benchmarks.

ALEX First of today's two Track C papers — "Quality of child anthropometric data from SISVAN, Brazil, 2008-2017," Silva, Mello e Silva, Carrilho and colleagues, Revista de Saúde Pública, 2023. 今天Track C两篇论文中的第一篇——《巴西食物与营养监测系统（SISVAN）儿童人体测量数据质量研究，2008-2017》，作者是Silva、Mello e Silva、Carrilho等人，发表于2023年的《Revista de Saúde Pública》（圣保罗公共卫生杂志）。 Full text came through on this one. 这篇论文的全文成功获取到了。 SISVAN is Brazil's Food and Nutrition Surveillance System — a routinely-collected, nationwide registry of child height and weight measurements, which makes this about as close an analog as we're going to find to a national patient registry's own anthropometric data quality, assessed at real operational scale: 73.7 million records, nearly 30 million children. SISVAN是巴西的食物与营养监测系统——一个全国范围内、常规收集的儿童身高体重测量注册数据库，这使得它几乎是我们能找到的、最接近一个全国性患者注册库自身人体测量数据质量问题的类比，而且是在真实的运营规模上进行评估的：7370万条记录，近3000万名儿童。

JORDAN Before we go further, let's ground a couple of growth-chart conventions, since they come up constantly in this kind of work. 在深入之前，我们先把几个生长曲线图的惯例讲清楚，因为它们在这类研究中会反复出现。 First — WHO versus CDC growth references. 第一——WHO（世界卫生组织）生长参考标准与CDC（美国疾病控制与预防中心）生长参考标准之间的区别。

ALEX Right, this trips people up because they sound interchangeable and aren't. WHO's child growth standards, used in this paper, are built from a prospective, multi-country sample of healthy, breastfed children raised under conditions believed to support optimal growth — it's meant to describe how children *should* grow, a prescriptive standard. 对，这一点很容易让人搞混，因为听起来它们可以互换，但实际上并不是这样。WHO的儿童生长标准——本文所采用的标准——建立在一项前瞻性的、跨多个国家的样本之上，样本对象是健康的、接受母乳喂养的儿童，且生长环境被认为有利于实现最佳生长——它的目的是描述儿童"应该"如何生长，是一种规范性标准。 CDC's growth charts, by contrast, are descriptive — built from a large cross-sectional sample of actual US children regardless of feeding method or health status, describing how children in that reference population *actually* grew. 相比之下，CDC的生长曲线图是描述性的——建立在一个庞大的横断面样本之上，样本对象是美国的真实儿童，不区分喂养方式或健康状况，描述的是该参考人群中儿童"实际"的生长情况。 WHO references are generally preferred for children under two, CDC references are more common for older US children and for weight-for-age tracking in some clinical contexts. 对于两岁以下儿童，通常更倾向于使用WHO参考标准；而对于美国的年龄较大儿童，以及某些临床场景下的年龄体重追踪，则更常使用CDC参考标准。 Mixing the two references without noticing is a quiet, common error. 在没有察觉的情况下混用这两套参考标准，是一种悄无声息却相当常见的错误。

JORDAN And the z-score itself — that's just a standardized distance from the reference median, right? 而z-score本身——就是与参考中位数之间的标准化距离，对吧？

ALEX Right, computed via something called the LMS method — the reference population's distribution at each age is summarized by three parameters: L, a Box-Cox power that corrects for skewness, M, the median, and S, the coefficient of variation. 对，是通过一种叫做LMS方法计算得出的——参考人群在每个年龄段的分布，由三个参数来概括：L，一个用于校正偏度的Box-Cox幂变换参数；M，中位数；以及S，变异系数。 Those three numbers let you convert any raw measurement into a z-score that accounts for the fact that the distribution of, say, height isn't perfectly symmetric or constant in spread across ages. 这三个数字能让你把任何一个原始测量值转换成一个z-score，并同时考虑到——比如说——身高的分布在不同年龄段并不是完全对称的、其离散程度也不是恒定的这一事实。 This paper uses the WHO macro for exactly that conversion, then applies WHO's own implausibility cutoffs on top. 这篇论文正是使用WHO的宏（macro）工具来完成这种转换，然后再在此基础上应用WHO自己设定的"不合理值"判定阈值。

JORDAN So what actually motivated this study? 那么这项研究究竟是由什么驱动的？

ALEX Brazil has had SISVAN for a long time, but historically only coverage — what fraction of the target population even has a record — was used to judge data quality. 巴西的SISVAN系统已经存在了很长时间，但历史上只有覆盖率——也就是目标人群中究竟有多大比例拥有记录——被用来评判数据质量。 The authors wanted to apply the fuller WHO-UNICEF TEAM framework — that's the Technical Expert Advisory group on nutrition Monitoring — which recommends assessing completeness, sex ratio, age distribution, digit preference, implausible z-scores, dispersion, and normality jointly, not just coverage in isolation. 作者希望应用更全面的WHO-UNICEF TEAM框架——即营养监测技术专家咨询小组（Technical Expert Advisory group on nutrition Monitoring）——该框架建议综合评估完整性、性别比例、年龄分布、尾数偏好、不合理z-score、离散程度和正态性，而不是孤立地只看覆盖率。 This is the first study to apply that full battery to Brazil's individual-level, routinely-collected health-system registry data, rather than to a one-off population survey. 这是首次将这一完整的评估体系应用于巴西个体层面、常规收集的卫生系统注册数据，而不是应用于一次性的人群调查数据。 That distinction matters: survey data is collected once, by trained field staff, under a protocol; registry data like SISVAN — like a patient registry — accumulates continuously from ordinary clinical encounters, with whatever variation in training, equipment, and diligence that implies. 这个区别很重要：调查数据是由经过培训的现场工作人员，依照统一方案，一次性采集完成的；而像SISVAN这样的注册数据——就像患者注册库一样——是从日常临床诊疗过程中持续累积而来的，这也就意味着在人员培训水平、设备条件和工作细致程度上都存在各种各样的差异。

JORDAN Walk me through the actual indicator battery, then — what's each one actually catching? 那就带我走一遍这套具体的指标体系——每一项究竟是在捕捉什么问题？

ALEX Seven categories. Completeness covers coverage of the target population, plus how often birth date, weight, and height are even filled in. 七大类。完整性涵盖了目标人群的覆盖率，以及出生日期、体重和身高这些字段究竟有多大比例被真正填写。 Sex ratio is a simple sanity check — boys divided by girls should land near the population's known ratio; a big deviation suggests systematic recording or selection problems rather than biology. 性别比例是一个简单的合理性检验——男孩数除以女孩数应该接近人群已知的比例；如果出现大幅偏离，说明存在系统性的记录或选择性问题，而不是生物学上的原因。 Age distribution uses histograms plus something called the Myers "blended" index of dissimilarity — a formula that quantifies how far the observed distribution of ages in months deviates from the uniform distribution you'd expect if ages were recorded accurately. 年龄分布则使用直方图，以及一种叫做Myers"混合"不相似性指数（Myers blended index of dissimilarity）的东西——这是一个公式，用于量化观察到的以月为单位的年龄分布，与"假如年龄被准确记录"时理应呈现的均匀分布之间，究竟偏离了多少。 Digit preference applies that same Myers index to the terminal digits of height and weight measurements — if people are rounding to the nearest half-centimeter or whole kilogram instead of recording precisely, you'll see a pileup on zero and five. 尾数偏好则是把同样的Myers指数应用到身高和体重测量值的最后一位数字上——如果人们是把测量值四舍五入到最近的半厘米或整公斤、而不是精确记录，你就会看到数字堆积在"0"和"5"上。 Implausible z-scores are the direct biological-plausibility check, using WHO's own cutoffs — for example, height-for-age z-scores outside minus six to plus six are flagged as impossible. 不合理z-score则是直接的生物学合理性检验，使用WHO自己设定的阈值——例如，年龄身高比z-score超出负6到正6的范围，就会被判定为不可能的数值而被标记出来。 And dispersion and normality — standard deviation, skewness, kurtosis of the remaining plausible z-scores — catch subtler problems: even values that individually pass the implausibility check can collectively look wrong if the whole distribution is too spread out or oddly shaped compared to the WHO reference population. 而离散度和正态性——即剩余合理z-score的标准差、偏度和峰度——则用来捕捉更细微的问题：即便每一个数值单独来看都通过了不合理性检验，但如果整体分布相对于WHO参考人群来说过于分散，或者形状异常，那么集合起来看依然可能显示出问题。

JORDAN I want to push on the implausibility and dispersion indicators specifically. If this registry's population is genuinely more socioeconomically vulnerable, with higher rates of real stunting and malnutrition, couldn't some of that "excess dispersion" just be real biological variation, not data-quality noise? 我想特别就不合理性和离散度这两个指标提出一点质疑。如果这个注册库所覆盖的人群本身在社会经济上确实更为脆弱，存在更高的真实生长迟缓和营养不良发生率，那么这种所谓的"离散度超标"，是不是有可能本来就是真实的生物学变异，而不是数据质量方面的噪音？

ALEX That's exactly the tension the authors themselves raise, almost word for word — they explicitly state there's no consistent evidence that dispersion and deviation from a Gaussian shape are due to data quality alone, and that the WHO reference population was restricted to healthy children growing under favorable conditions, so a more heterogeneous, socioeconomically vulnerable population could legitimately look different even with perfect measurement. 这正是作者自己几乎逐字提出的那种张力——他们明确指出，目前并没有一致的证据表明离散度和偏离高斯分布的形态完全是由数据质量问题造成的，而且WHO参考人群本身就局限于在有利条件下健康成长的儿童，因此一个更加异质、社会经济上更脆弱的人群，即便在测量完全准确的情况下，也完全可能呈现出不同的分布特征。 That's a genuinely good piece of epistemic honesty for a QC paper to include, and it's the single biggest thing to carry forward: these indicators are strong at flagging that something might be off, weak at telling you, on their own, whether the something is a data error or real population heterogeneity. 对于一篇数据质量论文来说，能把这一点写出来，是一种真正良好的认知诚实，这也是我们最应该带走的一点：这些指标在"发现某些地方可能有问题"这件事上很强，但在"仅凭自身判断这个问题究竟是数据错误、还是真实的人群异质性"这件事上是比较弱的。

JORDAN So what did they actually find, numbers-wise? 那么，从具体数字来看，他们究竟发现了什么？

ALEX Coverage climbed steadily — from 17.7% to 45.4% of the SUS-using target population between 2008 and 2017 — real progress, but still leaving well over half the intended population unmeasured in the final year, and concentrated unevenly: only 11 of 27 federative units topped 50% coverage by 2017. 覆盖率稳步攀升——从2008年到2017年，在使用统一卫生系统（SUS）的目标人群中，覆盖率从17.7%上升到45.4%——这是实实在在的进步，但在最后一年，仍然有超过一半的目标人群未被纳入测量，而且分布也很不均衡：到2017年，27个联邦行政区中只有11个覆盖率超过了50%。 Completeness of birth date and measurements was consistently near 100% — the mandatory fields get filled in. 出生日期和测量值的完整性则始终接近100%——这些强制性字段基本都被填写了。 Digit preference for height was the most striking finding to me: the dissimilarity index sat around 88 to 90% across the entire ten-year period, essentially unchanged — meaning height measurements are almost uniformly being rounded to preferred terminal digits, year after year, with no improvement trend at all. 对我来说，身高尾数偏好是最引人注目的发现：在整整十年的时间跨度里，不相似性指数一直维持在88%到90%左右，几乎没有任何变化——这意味着身高测量值几乎普遍地在被四舍五入到人们偏好的尾数上，年年如此，完全没有任何改善的趋势。 Weight digit preference, by contrast, fell from 50.2% to 40.4% — real improvement, likely because digital scales display easy-to-read decimals, while height still depends on reading a ruler mark. 相比之下，体重的尾数偏好则从50.2%下降到了40.4%——这是真正的改善，很可能是因为电子体重计显示的是便于读取的小数，而身高测量仍然依赖于人工读取标尺刻度。 Implausible z-scores stayed above the WHO's 1% quality threshold for every single index in every single year, even after a post-2014 improvement — height-for-age at 1.9% and BMI-for-age at 2.9% by 2017. 不合理z-score在每一个指标、每一个年份都始终高于WHO设定的1%质量阈值，即便在2014年之后出现了改善的趋势——到2017年，年龄身高比仍为1.9%，年龄体重指数比仍为2.9%。 And the implausibility and dispersion problems weren't evenly distributed — they concentrated specifically in children under two years old and in the North, Northeast, and Midwest regions, which are also the regions carrying the heaviest burden of real malnutrition. 而且，不合理性和离散度方面的问题分布并不均匀——它们集中出现在两岁以下的儿童，以及北部、东北部和中西部地区，而这些恰恰也是真实营养不良负担最重的地区。

JORDAN That last point feels important — the worst data quality lands exactly where the surveillance system matters most. 最后这一点感觉特别重要——数据质量最差的地方，恰恰是这个监测系统最需要发挥作用的地方。

ALEX Exactly, and the authors say so directly — those groups are the ones most vulnerable to nutritional problems, which is precisely the population that needs the most reliable estimates to drive policy. 没错，作者也直接这样说——这些群体恰恰是最容易受到营养问题影响的人群，而这正是最需要可靠估计数据来指导政策的那部分人群。

JORDAN So — does this change the CFFPR outlier pipeline? 那么——这篇论文会改变CFFPR的异常值处理流程吗？

ALEX Mostly it's option (e) — nothing directly, but it supplies vocabulary and a framework worth borrowing, with a secondary touch of (d), evaluation. 主要是选项(e)——没有直接的改变，但它提供了一套值得借用的术语体系和框架，并带有一点选项(d)，即评估方面的补充价值。 This paper isn't a per-record flagging algorithm at all — it's a population-level health-check battery, run annually, that monitors whether the registry as a whole is getting better or worse over time and where. 这篇论文根本不是一个逐条记录的标记算法——它是一套人群层面的健康检查指标体系，每年运行一次，用来监测整个注册库随时间推移是在变好还是变差，以及在哪些地方变化。 The genuinely reusable piece is the structured vocabulary itself: completeness, digit preference quantified via the Myers index, implausibility percentage against a fixed cutoff, dispersion, and shape — as a standing scorecard rather than an ad hoc one-off check, sliced by year, region, and age group to surface where and when quality is degrading. 真正值得重复使用的部分是这套结构化的术语体系本身：完整性、通过Myers指数量化的尾数偏好、相对于固定阈值的不合理值百分比、离散度，以及分布形状——作为一个按年份、地区和年龄组切分的常设记分卡，而不是一次性的临时检查，用以揭示数据质量在何时、何地出现退化。 The secondary evaluation angle is that tracking digit preference and dispersion trends over time functions as a weak, no-individual-gold-standard signal of whether data collection practice is actually improving — useful context alongside, never instead of, individual-record flagging. 次要的评估角度在于，追踪尾数偏好和离散度随时间的变化趋势，可以作为一个较弱、不依赖个体层面金标准的信号，用来判断数据采集实践是否真的在改善——这是可以与逐条记录的标记方法并行参考的有用背景信息，而绝不能取而代之。

JORDAN What's the buildable artifact, then? 那么，可构建的工具是什么？

ALEX A Registry Anthropometric Data-Quality Scorecard. 一个"注册库人体测量数据质量记分卡"（Registry Anthropometric Data-Quality Scorecard）。 MVP: for a longitudinal anthropometric dataset, compute the completeness, digit-preference Myers index, implausible-z-score percentage, dispersion, and skewness/kurtosis battery per year and per demographic stratum, and trend it over time as a standing population-level dashboard — a complement to, not a replacement for, individual-record flagging. 最小可行版本：针对一个纵向人体测量数据集，按年份和人口学分层计算完整性、尾数偏好Myers指数、不合理z-score百分比、离散度以及偏度峰度这一整套指标，并将其随时间变化绘制为一个常设的人群层面仪表盘——作为逐条记录标记方法的补充，而不是替代。 Difficulty: small — it's mostly descriptive statistics computed over existing fields, no new model required. 难度：小——主要是在现有字段上计算描述性统计，不需要任何新的建模工作。
