Blurb

A 2026 Genetics in Medicine paper from Pei-Yun Lin and Nadav Brandes introduces P-KNN (Pathogenicity K-Nearest Neighbors), a method for jointly calibrating multiple computational pathogenicity predictors — AlphaMissense, REVEL, EVE, and others — into one well-calibrated probability of pathogenicity, instead of the current ACMG/AMP practice of precommitting to a single tool. P-KNN places each variant as a point in a multidimensional space defined by the chosen tools' scores, then estimates pathogenicity probability as the fraction of pathogenic ClinVar-labeled variants among a query variant's K nearest neighbors in that space. Benchmarked against standard single-tool calibration and meta-predictors at four historical time points, P-KNN reportedly wins on both overall evidence strength and calibration alignment, keeps improving as new tools are added (unlike single-tool calibration), and corrects for double-counting of correlated computational and experimental evidence. This is an abstract-only read — specific benchmark numbers were not retrievable today. It lands on Karen's variant-interpretation priority: the buildable artifact is a CFTR Multi-Tool P-KNN Calibrator that jointly calibrates CFTR VUS evidence across whichever predictors are trusted.

Plain-English Glossary

  • ACMG/AMP framework — The standard rubric for classifying a variant as pathogenic, benign, or uncertain, combining weighted evidence codes from multiple categories.（中文:将变异分类为致病、良性或意义不明的标准评分体系,综合多类别的加权证据条目。）
  • BP4 / PP3 — ACMG/AMP evidence codes: BP4 is benign-supporting computational evidence, PP3 is pathogenic-supporting computational evidence.（中文:ACMG/AMP框架中的证据代码,BP4代表良性支持性计算证据,PP3代表致病支持性计算证据。）
  • ClinVar — A public database of clinically-submitted variant classifications with supporting evidence, used here as the source of ground-truth pathogenic/benign labels for the K-NN neighbor set.（中文:一个公开的临床变异分类数据库,记录支持证据,本文用它作为K最近邻邻居集的真实致病/良性标签来源。）
  • Curse of dimensionality — The general problem that as you add more dimensions (tool-score axes) to a nearest-neighbor space, any given neighborhood becomes sparser and less reliable.（中文:维度灾难,指在最近邻空间中不断增加维度(工具分数轴)时,任何给定邻域都会变得更稀疏、更不可靠的普遍性问题。）
  • HGVS nomenclature — The Human Genome Variation Society's standardized notation for describing a specific DNA, RNA, or protein variant (e.g., c.1521_1523delCTT for F508del).（中文:人类基因组变异学会制定的标准化书写规范,用于描述特定的DNA、RNA或蛋白质变异,例如F508del对应的c.1521_1523delCTT。）
  • K-nearest neighbors (K-NN) in score space — Here, finding the K other variants whose scores across all chosen predictors land closest to a query variant's scores, then using their known labels to estimate the query's pathogenicity probability.（中文:K最近邻(K-NN)方法在本文的具体用法:在所有选定预测工具的分数空间里,找出与查询变异分数最接近的K个其他变异,再用它们已知的标签来估计查询变异的致病概率。）
  • Likelihood ratio — Given a piece of evidence, how many times more likely a variant is to be pathogenic versus benign compared to a randomly chosen variant; the quantity ACMG/AMP evidence codes are actually built on.（中文:似然比,指在给定某项证据的情况下,一个变异相对于随机挑选的变异,是致病而非良性的可能性要高出多少倍;ACMG/AMP证据条目实际建立在这个量之上。）
  • Meta-predictor — A pathogenicity prediction tool (e.g., REVEL, BayesDel) that is itself trained as an ensemble over several lower-level features or sub-scores.（中文:元预测器,例如REVEL或BayesDel,是在多个底层特征或子分数上训练出的集成模型类致病性预测工具。）
  • Missense variant — A DNA change that alters a single amino acid in the resulting protein.（中文:错义变异,指一种导致所编码蛋白质中单个氨基酸发生改变的DNA变化。）
  • NBD1 (nucleotide-binding domain 1) — One of CFTR's structural domains, where F508del and many other clinically significant variants cluster.（中文:核苷酸结合域1,CFTR蛋白的一个结构域,F508del及许多其他具有临床意义的变异都聚集在此处。）
  • Variant of uncertain significance (VUS) — A variant for which there is not yet enough evidence to classify as pathogenic or benign.（中文:意义不明变异,指目前尚无足够证据判定为致病或良性的变异。）

Methods / Tools to Lift

  • Multidimensional tool-score embedding — representing each variant as one point across however many predictor-score axes you choose, rather than calibrating each axis in isolation.
  • K-NN local empirical calibration — estimating pathogenicity probability as the ClinVar-labeled pathogenic fraction among a query variant's nearest neighbors in that embedding, which scales to new tools by just adding an axis.
  • Implicit correlated-evidence discounting — a local, empirical neighbor estimate that naturally avoids the double-counting risk when two evidence sources (e.g., a computational tool and an experimental assay) share underlying signal.
