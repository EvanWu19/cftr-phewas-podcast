Show notes — Track A — 2026-07-25

Blurb. Chen et al. (Advanced Science, 2026) build CaVepP53, a TP53-specific variant-effect predictor made by adding a classification head to the ESMC protein language model and fine-tuning it on perturbation-based deep-mutational-scanning data plus ClinVar labels. The bet is that a gene-specific model trained on measured function beats big general-purpose predictors — and it does: CaVepP53 outperformed AlphaMissense and PrimateAI-3D on accuracy/precision/F1/MCC, hit ROC-AUC 0.918 on driver variants, correctly re-called 6 of 9 variants AlphaMissense got wrong, was wet-lab validated on 22 variants via prime editing + Nutlin-3a growth selection in HCT-116 cells, and transferred to five more genes (avg ROC-AUC 0.883 vs 0.841). Where it lands: the Karen / CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration. Buildable artifact: cftr-vep-finetune — a CFTR-specific fine-tuned scorer built on the same recipe, flagging the CFTR variants where a generalist and the gene-specific model disagree.

Plain-English glossary

  • AlphaMissense (AM) — a leading general-purpose missense pathogenicity predictor from DeepMind, trained across all genes.（中文：一个跨所有基因训练的通用错义致病性预测器。）
  • ClinVar — public database of clinically reported variants labeled pathogenic/benign; labels are correlation-based, not functional.（中文：收录临床报告变异并标注致病/良性的公共数据库，标签基于相关性而非功能测量。）
  • DBD (DNA-binding domain) — the region of p53 (residues 102–292) that physically grips DNA; most pathogenic TP53 missense variants cluster here.（中文：p53 蛋白中抓住 DNA 的区域，第 102–292 位残基；多数致病错义变异聚集于此。）
  • DMS (deep mutational scanning) — an experiment that makes thousands of protein variants at once and measures each one's function.（中文：一次性造出蛋白成千上万变异体并测量每个功能的实验。）
  • Driver variant — a mutation that actively drives cancer, as opposed to a passenger that rides along.（中文：主动驱动癌症的突变，区别于顺带出现的"乘客"突变。）
  • ESM / ESMC — a family of protein language models; ESMC is the newest version used as the backbone here.（中文：蛋白质语言模型家族；ESMC 是本文用作骨干的最新版本。）
  • Missense variant — a single DNA change that swaps one amino acid for another in the protein.（中文：把蛋白里一个氨基酸换成另一个的单碱基改变。）
  • Nutlin-3a / MDM2 — MDM2 normally degrades p53; Nutlin-3a blocks MDM2, stabilizing functional p53 so normal cells arrest/die and loss-of-function mutants keep growing.（中文：MDM2 通常降解 p53；Nutlin-3a 阻断 MDM2 从而稳定有功能的 p53，功能缺失突变体则能继续生长——由此可直接读出功能。）
  • Prime editing — a precise CRISPR-based technique that writes a specific edit into the genome.（中文：一种精准的基于 CRISPR 的技术，把特定编辑写入基因组。）
  • Protein language model (PLM) — a neural net trained on natural protein sequences that learns which amino-acid patterns are evolutionarily plausible.（中文：在天然蛋白序列上训练、学会哪些氨基酸组合在进化上讲得通的神经网络。）
  • Saturation mutagenesis — making every possible single-amino-acid change, position by position, across a protein.（中文：逐位点造出蛋白所有可能的单氨基酸改变。）
  • TP53 / p53 — the most-studied tumor-suppressor gene; mutated in ~half of human cancers.（中文：研究最透的抑癌基因，约一半人类癌症中发生突变。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant with insufficient evidence to call pathogenic or benign.（中文：证据不足、无法判定致病或良性的变异。）

Full citation

Chen H, et al. Causal Prediction of TP53 Variant Pathogenicity Using a Perturbation-Informed Protein Language Model. Advanced Science. 2026. PMID 41955512; PMC PMC13285122; DOI 10.1002/advs.202516332.

Methods / tools to lift

  • Classification-head fine-tune on a PLM — freeze ESMC backbone, add one classification head, output logit → sigmoid → pathogenicity probability (0.5 cutoff).
  • Perturbation-informed training labels — anchor on DMS-measured function, not just ClinVar correlation labels ("causal" signal).
  • Hyperparameter sweep — window size (sequence context), number of unfrozen layers, PLM size; bigger isn't automatically better.
  • Class-balancing step — lifts performance on label-imbalanced genes (ATM, VHL).
  • Wet-lab validation loop — prime-edit predicted variants into HCT-116, select under Nutlin-3a, read growth as loss-of-function.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / CFTR variant interpretation.

cftr-vep-finetune (M–L): assemble a labeled CFTR missense table (CFTR2 functional classes + public functional assays) → add a classification head to ESMC and fine-tune → output per-variant pathogenicity score benchmarked on a held-out CFTR2 set → rank by disagreement with a generalist (AlphaMissense) to surface discordant VUS for curation.

What to verify first

  • Circularity: ensure CFTR training labels aren't themselves predictor-derived; keep functional-assay labels as the separate gold standard.
  • Scope: missense-only — silent on splice/frameshift/nonsense, a large part of CFTR.
  • Data scale: confirm a gene-specific fine-tune actually helps at CFTR's (likely smaller) functional-data scale vs TP53's very large one.
  • No registry internals: end-to-end on public CFTR data; the method supplies external scoring only.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读论文时间。 Today's Track A paper is "Causal Prediction of TP53 Variant Pathogenicity Using a Perturbation-Informed Protein Language Model," by Chen and colleagues, published in Advanced Science in 2026. 今天 Track A 的论文是 Chen 等人 2026 年发表在《Advanced Science》上的《利用扰动信息蛋白质语言模型对 TP53 变异致病性的因果预测》。 They build a model they call CaVepP53. 他们构建了一个叫 CaVepP53 的模型。 We're reading it because it's the cleanest recent demonstration that a gene-specific variant predictor can beat the big general-purpose ones — and that idea maps straight onto CFTR variant interpretation for the Karen collaboration. 我们读它，是因为它是近期最干净的一个例证：一个针对单个基因定制的变异预测器可以打败那些通用的大模型——而这个思路可以直接映射到 CFTR 变异解读上，正好服务于 Karen 的合作方向。

JORDAN Before we get into what they built, give me the vocabulary. 在进入他们做了什么之前，先给我理一下词汇。 What is TP53 and why do we keep hearing about it? TP53 是什么，为什么我们总是听到它？ And what's a "protein language model"? 还有，什么叫"蛋白质语言模型"？

ALEX TP53 is a tumor suppressor gene — the protein it makes, p53, patrols the cell for DNA damage and can halt the cell cycle or trigger apoptosis, which is programmed cell death. TP53 是一个抑癌基因 (tumor suppressor gene)——它编码的蛋白 p53 会在细胞里巡查 DNA 损伤，并能让细胞周期停下来，或者触发凋亡 (apoptosis)，也就是程序性细胞死亡。 It's mutated in about half of all human cancers, so it's the most-studied gene of its kind. 它在大约一半的人类癌症里都发生突变，所以是同类基因里被研究得最透的一个。 A missense variant is a single DNA change that swaps one amino acid for another in the protein. 错义变异 (missense variant) 是指一个 DNA 位点的改变，把蛋白里的一个氨基酸换成了另一个。 The hard question is always: does that swap actually break the protein, or is it harmless? 难的问题永远是：这个替换到底破坏了蛋白功能，还是无害的？ A protein language model, or PLM, is a neural network trained on millions of natural protein sequences. 蛋白质语言模型，即 PLM（一种在数百万条天然蛋白序列上训练出来的神经网络）。 It learns which amino acid patterns are "grammatical" — evolutionarily plausible — the same way a text model learns which word sequences sound natural. 它学会了哪些氨基酸组合是"合乎语法的"、在进化上讲得通的，就像文本模型学会哪些词序听起来自然一样。 The family of models here is called ESM, and the specific newest one they use is ESMC. 这里用到的模型家族叫 ESM，他们用的最新的那个具体型号叫 ESMC。

JORDAN And a couple more — they keep mentioning DMS and saturation mutagenesis. 还有几个词——他们反复提到 DMS 和饱和突变。 What are those? 那是什么？

ALEX DMS stands for deep mutational scanning (an experiment that makes thousands of variants of a protein at once and measures each one's function in a dish). DMS 指的是深度突变扫描（一种实验，一次性造出一个蛋白的成千上万个变异体，并在培养体系里测量每一个的功能）。 Saturation mutagenesis means you make every possible single amino-acid change, position by position, until the whole protein is "saturated" with tested variants. 饱和突变 (saturation mutagenesis) 的意思是，你逐个位点造出所有可能的单氨基酸改变，直到整个蛋白被测试过的变异体"填满"。 The reason those matter is that they give you experimental functional labels, not just clinical guesses. 它们之所以重要，是因为它们给你的是实验测出的功能标签，而不只是临床上的猜测。 And that distinction is the whole spine of this paper. 而这个区别，正是这篇论文的核心脊梁。

JORDAN So what was the problem they were trying to fix? 那他们想解决的问题到底是什么？

ALEX The dominant way we've labeled variants is ClinVar, a public database of clinically reported variants tagged pathogenic or benign. 我们给变异贴标签的主流来源是 ClinVar（一个公共数据库，收录临床报告过的变异，标注为致病或良性）。 The trouble is that ClinVar labels come from correlation — a variant was seen in patients with disease — not from a direct functional measurement. 问题在于，ClinVar 的标签来自相关性——某个变异在患病者身上被观察到过——而不是来自直接的功能测量。 And there just aren't that many high-confidence labels for any single gene. 而且对任何单个基因来说，高置信度的标签数量都很少。 So the field went the other direction: build one giant general-purpose predictor across all genes at once. 于是这个领域走向了另一条路：一次性跨所有基因构建一个巨大的通用预测器。 AlphaMissense, which we'll call AM, is the flagship of that approach. AlphaMissense（我们简称 AM）就是这条路线的旗舰。 The authors' bet is the opposite: that each gene has its own idiosyncratic rules, and a model tuned to just TP53, on real functional data, will read those rules better than any generalist. 作者押的是相反的赌注：每个基因都有自己独特的规则，一个只针对 TP53、在真实功能数据上微调过的模型，能比任何通才更好地读懂这些规则。 The word "causal" in the title is doing work here. 标题里"因果"这个词是有分量的。 They mean the training signal comes from perturbing the protein and watching function change, rather than from an association observed after the fact. 他们的意思是，训练信号来自主动扰动蛋白、观察功能变化，而不是来自事后观察到的一个关联。

ALEX And it's worth sitting on why the correlation-versus-perturbation distinction bites so hard. 而且值得停下来想想，为什么"相关 vs 扰动"这个区别咬得这么狠。 A generalist trained mostly on ClinVar inherits ClinVar's blind spots — variants that are rare, or that come from under-studied populations, or that were themselves annotated with help from an earlier predictor. 一个主要在 ClinVar 上训练的通才，会继承 ClinVar 的盲区——那些罕见的、来自研究不足人群的、或者本身就是借助早先某个预测器标注出来的变异。 When you instead train on a perturbation assay, every label is a measured phenotype in a dish, so the model is learning the biology of the protein rather than the sociology of who got sequenced. 而当你改在扰动实验上训练，每一个标签都是培养体系里实测的一个表型，所以模型学的是这个蛋白的生物学，而不是"谁被测序了"的社会学。 That's the sense in which they call it causal — the label follows from changing the protein, not from an association. 这就是他们称之为"因果"的含义——标签是改变蛋白之后得到的结果，而不是一个关联。

JORDAN Okay, so how did they actually build CaVepP53? 好，那他们究竟是怎么构建 CaVepP53 的？

ALEX They start from the pretrained ESMC model and add a small classification head on top — one extra layer that outputs a single number. 他们从预训练好的 ESMC 模型出发，在上面加了一个小的分类头 (classification head)——一个额外的层，输出一个单一的数值。 That number is a logit, which they push through a sigmoid to get a pathogenicity probability between zero and one, with 0.5 as the cutoff. 这个数值是一个 logit（模型内部的原始打分），他们用一个 sigmoid 函数把它压成一个 0 到 1 之间的致病概率，以 0.5 作为分界线。 They fine-tune on a curated set of TP53 variants that combines ClinVar labels with the DMS perturbation data, so the model is anchored to measured function. 他们在一组精心整理的 TP53 变异上做微调，这组数据把 ClinVar 标签和 DMS 扰动数据结合起来，让模型锚定在实测功能上。 They also benchmarked eight different PLMs first to pick the backbone. 他们还先对八个不同的 PLM 做了基准测试来挑选骨干模型。 Interestingly, AM actually scored best on the raw benchmark — but its source code isn't fully available and retraining it is enormously expensive, so they went with the ESM family instead. 有意思的是，AM 在原始基准上其实得分最高——但它的源代码没有完全公开，重新训练又极其昂贵，所以他们改用了 ESM 家族。 That's an honest, practical design choice worth flagging: they picked the best model they could actually open up and modify. 这是一个诚实而务实的设计选择，值得点出来：他们选了自己真正能打开、能改动的最好的模型。

ALEX One design choice I'd push on is their second output — an interpretability layer. 我想质疑的一个设计是他们的第二个输出——一个可解释性层。 They compute the Euclidean distance between the model's internal embedding of the wild-type sequence and the mutant sequence, meaning how far apart the two representations sit in the model's high-dimensional space. 他们计算模型对野生型 (wild-type) 序列和突变序列的内部表示 (embedding) 之间的欧氏距离，也就是这两个表示在模型高维空间里相隔多远。 The idea is a bigger shift means a bigger functional impact. 想法是：位移越大，功能影响越大。 But they admit this distance only correlated modestly with the experimental functional scores. 但他们承认，这个距离与实验功能分数只有中等程度的相关。 So it's a nice narrative handle, but I wouldn't lean on it as a real second signal yet. 所以它是个好听的叙事抓手，但我还不会把它当作一个真正独立的第二信号来依赖。

JORDAN And there were a few knobs they had to tune, right? 而且他们有几个旋钮要调，对吧？ What actually mattered? 到底哪些起了作用？

ALEX Three things they swept: the window size — how much sequence context around the mutated residue the model sees; the number of unfrozen layers — how deep into the backbone they let fine-tuning reach; and the size of the pretrained PLM itself. 他们扫了三样东西：窗口大小 (window size)——模型看到突变残基周围多少序列上下文；解冻层数 (unfreezing layers)——他们让微调深入骨干网络多深；以及预训练 PLM 本身的规模。 The headline is that bigger isn't automatically better — there's a sweet spot where you unfreeze enough to specialize on TP53 without washing out what the model already knows about proteins in general. 结论是：更大并不自动更好——存在一个甜蜜点，你解冻得足够多以便专门适配 TP53，同时又不至于冲掉模型原本对蛋白的通用知识。 That's a genuinely transferable lesson, because it's the same trade-off you'd face fine-tuning on any single gene, including CFTR. 这是一条真正可迁移的经验，因为你在任何单个基因上微调时都会面对同样的权衡，CFTR 也不例外。

JORDAN Fair. 有道理。 So does the gene-specific bet pay off in the numbers? 那这个"针对单基因"的赌注，在数字上兑现了吗？

ALEX It does, fairly consistently. 兑现了，而且相当一致。 Against AM and PrimateAI-3D — another leading structure-aware predictor — CaVepP53 came out ahead on accuracy, precision, F1, and MCC. 对比 AM 和 PrimateAI-3D（另一个领先的、感知蛋白结构的预测器），CaVepP53 在准确率、精确率、F1 和 MCC 上都胜出。 On identifying driver variants specifically it hit a ROC-AUC of 0.918 with a Cohen's d of 2.03 — a very large separation between the pathogenic and benign score distributions. 在专门识别驱动变异 (driver variant) 上，它达到了 0.918 的 ROC-AUC，Cohen's d 为 2.03——致病组和良性组的打分分布分得非常开。 On a fully independent external test set of 3,945 missense variants, drawn from a separate dataset of 7,467 TP53 mutations with any overlap rigorously removed, it reached 68.2% accuracy, an 18.2-point jump over the un-tuned ESM baseline. 在一个完全独立的外部测试集上——3,945 个错义变异，取自一个另外的、包含 7,467 个 TP53 突变的数据集，并严格剔除了任何重叠——它达到了 68.2% 的准确率，比未微调的 ESM 基线高出 18.2 个百分点。

JORDAN That 68% sounds low next to the 0.9-something AUC. 这个 68% 跟零点九几的 AUC 放一起显得有点低。 What's going on there? 那是怎么回事？

ALEX Different test, harder cases — that external set is a continuous functional-activity dataset, not a clean pathogenic-versus-benign split, so it's a tougher bar. 不同的测试，更难的样本——那个外部集是一个连续的功能活性数据集，不是干净的致病对良性的二分，所以门槛更高。 The most convincing result to me is the head-to-head on hard cases. 对我来说最有说服力的结果是在难例上的正面对决。 They took nine variants that AM misclassified, and CaVepP53 correctly called six of them, 66.7%. 他们拿了九个被 AM 判错的变异，CaVepP53 正确判对了其中六个，66.7%。 That's exactly the regime you care about — the variants a generalist gets wrong. 这正是你真正在意的场景——通才判错的那些变异。 Then they ran full saturation mutagenesis over the protein: of 7,467 possible variants, 2,737 predicted pathogenic and clustered, as you'd hope, in the DNA-binding domain, the DBD, residues 102 to 292 where p53 grips DNA. 然后他们对整个蛋白做了全饱和突变预测：在 7,467 个可能的变异里，2,737 个被预测为致病，并且如你所愿地聚集在 DNA 结合结构域 (DNA-binding domain)，即 DBD，第 102 到 292 位残基——p53 抓住 DNA 的地方。

JORDAN And they didn't just trust the model — there was a wet-lab check? 而且他们没有只信模型——还有一个湿实验的验证？

ALEX Right, and this is the part I liked most. 对，这是我最喜欢的部分。 They used prime editing — a precise CRISPR-based technique that writes a specific edit into the genome — to install individual predicted variants into HCT-116 cells. 他们用了先导编辑 (prime editing)——一种精准的、基于 CRISPR 的技术，把一个特定的编辑写进基因组——把单个预测出来的变异逐一装进 HCT-116 细胞。 Then they applied Nutlin-3a, a drug that blocks MDM2 and so stabilizes functional p53, forcing normal cells to arrest or die. 然后他们施加 Nutlin-3a，一种阻断 MDM2、从而稳定有功能 p53 的药物，逼迫正常细胞停滞或死亡。 A loss-of-function p53 mutant escapes that and keeps growing, so growth under the drug is a direct functional readout. 一个功能缺失 (loss-of-function) 的 p53 突变体能逃过这一劫、继续生长，所以在药物下的生长本身就是一个直接的功能读数。 Twenty-two predicted mutations were validated this way. 用这个方法验证了 22 个预测的突变。 And the framework transferred: applied to VHL, ATM, BRCA1, RAD51C, and BAP1, it hit the highest ROC-AUC on four of five genes, averaging 0.883 versus 0.841 for the next best. 而且这个框架可以迁移：应用到 VHL、ATM、BRCA1、RAD51C 和 BAP1 上，它在五个基因里的四个上取得最高 ROC-AUC，平均 0.883，而次优的是 0.841。 One detail worth stealing there: several of those genes had badly imbalanced label sets, far more benign than pathogenic or vice versa, so they added a class-balancing step during training. 那里有一个值得偷的细节：其中几个基因的标签集严重不平衡，良性远多于致病或者反过来，所以他们在训练时加了一个类别平衡 (class-balancing) 步骤。 That lifted balanced accuracy and the other metrics specifically on the lopsided genes like ATM and VHL. 这在 ATM 和 VHL 这类偏斜严重的基因上，专门抬高了平衡准确率和其他指标。 It's a small thing, but CFTR's functional labels are almost certainly imbalanced too, so it's the kind of guard you'd want in from the start. 这是件小事，但 CFTR 的功能标签几乎肯定也是不平衡的，所以这是那种你会想从一开始就装上的护栏。

JORDAN So where does this land for us? 那这对我们来说落在哪里？
