Show notes — Deep EHR survey (Track B, AI tooling)

Blurb. Shickel et al. (IEEE J Biomed Health Inform, 2018) survey deep learning applied to electronic health records as of an August-2017 literature cutoff, organizing the field into five application buckets — information extraction, representation learning, outcome prediction, computational phenotyping, and de-identification — and the architectures behind them (MLP, CNN, RNN/LSTM/GRU, autoencoder, RBM). Its central thesis is that EHR's defining challenge, unlike images (uniform pixels) or text (uniform characters), is heterogeneity: discrete coded events, real-valued time series, and free text in one patient. It documents the field's divide-and-conquer pattern — word2vec/skip-gram code embeddings at the bottom, patient-level representations (Doctor AI's GRU, DeepPatient's autoencoder, Deepr's CNN) above — and names four still-unsolved limitations: interpretability, heterogeneity/unified representation, lack of universal benchmarks, and privacy-driven data siloing. Not a code-shipping paper; read as the field map for the embedding and phenotyping tools already in the backlog. Where it lands: AI-tooling / career. Buildable artifact: a registry concept-embedding baseline (skip-gram code embeddings + simple patient representation) as the honest floor under any heavier contextual model.

Plain-English glossary

  • Computational phenotyping — deriving a data-driven definition of who has a condition from records, rather than a single billing code or manual rule.（中文：计算表型，从记录中数据驱动地推导"谁患某病"，而非依赖单一计费代码或人工规则。）
  • Controlled vocabulary (ICD / CPT / LOINC / NDC) — standardized medical code systems for diagnoses, procedures, lab observations, and drugs respectively.（中文：受控词表，标准化医学编码体系；ICD 记诊断、CPT 记操作、LOINC 记化验、NDC 记药物。）
  • De-identification — automatically removing protected identifiers (names, dates, locations) from clinical text so it can be shared.（中文：去标识化，自动清除临床文本中的受保护标识信息以便共享。）
  • Information extraction — pulling structured facts (a diagnosis, drug, or date) out of unstructured free-text notes.（中文：信息抽取，从非结构化自由文本中拉出结构化事实。）
  • Representation learning — letting a model learn its own input features from raw data instead of hand-crafting them.（中文：表示学习，让模型从原始数据自动学习特征，而非手工设计。）
  • Data heterogeneity (in EHR) — the coexistence of codes, time series, and free text for one patient, which blocks a single uniform input format.（中文：数据异质性，同一病人身上代码、时间序列与自由文本并存，导致无法用单一统一的输入格式。）

Full citation. Shickel B, Tighe PJ, Bihorac A, Rashidi P. Deep EHR: A Survey of Recent Advances in Deep Learning Techniques for Electronic Health Record (EHR) Analysis. IEEE J Biomed Health Inform. 2018;22(5):1589–1604. PMID 29989977 · PMC PMC6043423 · DOI 10.1109/jbhi.2017.2767063. According to PubMed; full text via BioC PMC-OA.

Methods / tools to lift

  • Skip-gram / word2vec on clinical codes: turn each ICD/CPT/LOINC/NDC code into a dense embedding by context co-occurrence.
  • Patient-level representation by summing/sequencing code embeddings (Doctor AI = GRU over event-time pairs; DeepPatient = 3-layer autoencoder + LR; Deepr = embedding layer + CNN).
  • Five-bucket application taxonomy as a checklist for scoping any registry-ML project.
  • Four named limitations (interpretability, heterogeneity, benchmarks, privacy siloing) as a pre-mortem for new tooling.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. AI-tooling / career (method also serves Liz). MVP (paste-ready): take a per-person sequence of coded clinical events → train skip-gram embeddings over the code vocabulary → build a summed-encounter or autoencoder patient vector → evaluate via nearest-neighbor sanity checks + one small-label downstream task on a fixed held-out split. Difficulty M. Build this before the Med-BERT module already in the backlog so the transformer is measured against a documented classical baseline.

What to verify first

  • Temporal irregularity: summed/order-only embeddings discard inter-visit timing; confirm whether timing carries the signal before dropping it.
  • Interpretability: plan a post-hoc attribution step up front — a dense patient vector alone won't explain a flag to a clinician/reviewer.
  • Benchmarking discipline: use an own held-out evaluation with a fixed, documented split; in-sample quality is near-meaningless per the survey's own benchmark critique.
  • Currency: 2017 cutoff — treat as a method-lineage map, not a current SOTA ranking.

JORDAN One more on the data side — why is the heterogeneity such a deep problem rather than just an engineering annoyance? 在数据这边再问一个——为什么异质性是个如此深的问题，而不只是工程上的麻烦？

ALEX Because each data type wants a different inductive bias, and you can't satisfy all of them with one architecture. 因为每种数据类型想要的归纳偏置不同，而你没法用一个架构同时满足全部。A CNN's local-patch assumption is great for an image and reasonable for a vital-sign waveform, but it's meaningless across a bag of diagnosis codes that have no spatial neighborhood. CNN 的局部块假设对图像很好，对生命体征波形也合理，但对一袋没有空间邻域的诊断代码毫无意义。A sequence model's ordering assumption fits a clinical note but mismatches the codes inside a single visit, which have no real order. 序列模型的顺序假设适合一份病历记录，却与单次就诊内的代码不匹配，因为那些代码并没有真实顺序。So when the survey says heterogeneity is the defining difficulty, it's really saying there's no single representation whose assumptions are correct for codes, time series, and text at once — and that's why the unified-patient-representation goal is hard rather than just unimplemented. 所以当综述说异质性是决定性难点时，它真正想说的是：不存在一种单一表示，其假设能同时对代码、时间序列和文本都正确——这正是为什么"统一病人表示"这个目标是困难的，而不只是尚未实现。

JORDAN So what's the actual problem the survey says the field is circling around? 那综述说这个领域真正绕着转的问题是什么？

ALEX The framing is a direct analogy to image processing and natural-language processing. 它的框架是与图像处理和自然语言处理的直接类比。Before deep learning, EHR analysis ran on hand-crafted features fed to logistic regression, SVMs, random forests — and the authors are blunt that hand-engineering features was the bottleneck, the "black art" of the field. 在深度学习之前，EHR 分析靠的是手工特征喂给逻辑回归、支持向量机、随机森林——作者直言不讳地说，手工设计特征是瓶颈，是这个领域的"黑魔法"。The deep-learning promise they survey is the same one that reshaped vision and NLP: learn the representation automatically from raw data instead of hand-specifying it. 他们综述的深度学习承诺，与重塑视觉和 NLP 的那个承诺相同：从原始数据中自动学习表示，而不是手工指定它。And the central claim is that the single hardest, most unique thing about EHR — the reason you can't just copy the image playbook — is that heterogeneity. 核心论断是，EHR 唯一最难、最独特的地方——你没法直接照搬图像那套打法的原因——就是那种异质性。Images are uniformly pixels; text is uniformly characters; an EHR is codes and time series and free text all at once. 图像统一是像素，文本统一是字符，而 EHR 同时是代码、时间序列和自由文本。

JORDAN Meaning they tackle one data type at a time? 意思是他们一次处理一种数据类型？

ALEX Exactly, and there's a clear hierarchy. 正是，而且有清晰的层级。At the bottom, represent individual clinical codes as vectors — borrowing word2vec and skip-gram straight from NLP to turn an ICD code into a dense embedding, so that codes appearing in similar contexts land near each other. 在最底层，把单个临床代码表示为向量——直接从 NLP 借来 word2vec 和 skip-gram，把一个 ICD 代码变成稠密嵌入，使出现在相似语境中的代码彼此靠近。One level up, represent a whole patient — by summing or sequencing those code embeddings; this is where the named systems live. 上一层，表示整个病人——通过对那些代码嵌入求和或排序；这就是那些有名字的系统所在的层。Doctor AI feeds sequences of event-time pairs into a GRU to predict the next visit's diagnoses and medications. Doctor AI 把"事件-时间"对的序列喂进一个 GRU，预测下一次就诊的诊断和用药。DeepPatient builds patient vectors with a three-layer autoencoder, then runs plain logistic regression on top to predict a wide range of future diagnoses. JORDAN You listed five application buckets but we've mostly talked about representation. 你列了五个应用类别，但我们大多在谈表示。What do information extraction and de-identification actually look like in deep form? 信息抽取和去标识化在深度学习形式下实际长什么样？

ALEX Agreed — and notice it's not a modeling problem, it's a data-governance and incentives problem, which is why a better architecture never fixed it. 同意——而且注意它不是建模问题，而是数据治理和激励问题，这就是为什么更好的架构从未修复它。That's a useful thing to internalize before you reach for the newest model. 在你去够最新的模型之前，把这一点内化是有用的。
