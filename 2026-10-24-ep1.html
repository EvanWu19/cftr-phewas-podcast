Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): Curating the VUS before it ever shows up in clinic

Axakova and colleagues (2026, American Journal of Human Genetics) build a proactive, systematic missense variant effect map for AIRE — the gene behind Autoimmune Polyendocrine Syndrome Type 1 — scoring 9,790 of 10,900 possible amino acid substitutions with an insulin-promoter-driven GFP reporter in engineered HEK293 cells, rather than waiting for variants to appear clinically before testing them one at a time. The map performs on par with AlphaMissense, REVEL, VARITY, and ESM1b on a clinical reference set, resolves 70% of current VUS with non-undetermined functional evidence, formally reclassifies 133 of them, and tracks disease severity in a 98-person international cohort and hypoparathyroidism risk in UK Biobank — while being explicit and transparent about a real blind spot in the PHD1 domain caused by the reporter's open-chromatin genomic context. The transferable piece for Karen's CFTR work isn't the wet-lab assay itself, but the LLRp (log-likelihood-ratio-of-pathogenicity) calibration pipeline that turns raw functional scores into graded, ACMG/AMP-ready evidence. Buildable artifact: a CFTR Functional-Score LLRp Calibrator.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP — the joint framework from the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology for combining evidence types (population frequency, computational prediction, functional data) into a final variant classification.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会与分子病理学会联合制定的框架，用于将人群频率、计算预测、功能数据等不同类型的证据整合为最终的变异分类。）
  • LLRp (log-likelihood ratio of pathogenicity) — a calculation that converts a raw functional score into a number reflecting how much more likely that score is to come from a known-pathogenic variant than a known-benign one, which can then be mapped onto ACMG/AMP evidence-strength levels.（中文：一种将原始功能评分转换为数值的计算方法，该数值反映给定评分来自已知致病变异的可能性比来自已知良性变异的可能性高出多少，随后可映射到ACMG/AMP证据强度等级。）
  • MAVE (multiplexed assay of variant effects) — a single experiment that measures the functional consequence of thousands of variants of one gene simultaneously, rather than testing them one at a time.（中文：通过单次实验同时测量某个基因数千个变异的功能后果的方法，而非逐一检测。）
  • Proactive vs. reactive functional assay — reactive assays are built after a specific variant is already seen clinically; proactive maps score essentially every possible substitution ahead of time, including variants nobody has observed yet.（中文：被动式检测是在某个特定变异已在临床中被发现后才构建的；前瞻式图谱则提前对几乎所有可能的替换变异进行评分，包括尚未被观察到的变异。）
  • Variant of uncertain significance (VUS) — the clinical label for a variant that current evidence can't confidently call either benign or pathogenic.（中文：对于现有证据既不能确信判断为良性、也不能确信判断为致病性的变异所使用的临床标签。）

Full citation

Axakova, et al. "Systematic and proactive evaluation of AIRE missense variant effects." American Journal of Human Genetics. 2026. PMID: 42561935 | PMC13635998 | DOI: 10.1016/j.ajhg.2026.07.008

Methods / tools to lift

  • LLRp calibration pipeline — kernel-density estimation of known benign and known pathogenic score distributions, converted to a log-likelihood ratio and then to a categorical ACMG/AMP evidence-strength label, with an explicit "caution" flag when the confidence interval straddles the midpoint.
  • DT-POPCode (Dual-Tag Precision Oligo-Pool based Code Alteration) — the mutagenesis method used to generate near-complete single-amino-acid substitution libraries at scale.
  • Combined-allele map (CAM) score — summing single-allele functional scores across both alleles to derive a diploid genotype-level severity proxy for genotype-phenotype correlation.
  • Balanced precision-recall benchmarking — adjusting nominal precision to reflect what would be observed with an artificially balanced 50/50 benign/pathogenic reference set, for fair cross-tool comparison.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / CFTR variant interpretation, specifically by reusing the LLRp calibration pipeline on CFTR's own existing functional-assay score distributions rather than building a new CFTR MAVE from scratch. MVP: take any existing CFTR functional-assay score set plus a curated reference list of high-confidence ClinVar benign/likely-benign and pathogenic/likely-pathogenic CFTR variants, fit the same kernel-density LLRp pipeline, and output calibrated per-variant evidence-strength labels with caution flags.

What to verify first

  • How large and clean CFTR's own high-confidence reference set would be — this paper's calibration pool was tiny (16 benign, 30 pathogenic), and a thin reference set makes the kernel density estimate noisier near the tails.
  • Whether CFTR's existing functional assays carry an AIRE-PHD1-style blind spot of their own that would need its own explicit carve-out rather than being silently averaged over.
  • Whether a small-cohort genotype-phenotype correlation like the one used here for APS-1 severity would be feasible for CFTR, given how much CFTR phenotype is already shaped by modifier genes and modulator-era treatment.
  • That any single calibrated LLRp score is read in the context of its confidence interval and caution flag, not as a standalone categorical verdict.

ALEX Today's paper is from the American Journal of Human Genetics, 2026 — Axakova and colleagues, "Systematic and proactive evaluation of AIRE missense variant effects." 今天要聊的论文发表在2026年的《American Journal of Human Genetics》上——作者是Axakova等人，题目是《AIRE错义变异效应的系统性、前瞻性评估》。 This is a Track A pick, landing squarely in variant interpretation — Karen's lane — even though the gene itself, AIRE, has nothing to do with CF. 这是Track A的选题，直接落在变异解读这条线上——也就是Karen的方向——尽管这篇论文研究的基因AIRE本身和囊性纤维化毫无关系。 The reason we're reading it anyway is the strategy, not the gene: instead of waiting for a rare variant to show up in a patient and then running a one-off functional assay months or years later, the authors proactively measured the function of nearly every possible AIRE missense variant up front, before most of them have ever been seen clinically. 我们之所以仍然要读这篇论文，原因在于其策略而非基因本身：作者没有等待某个罕见变异出现在患者身上、再几个月甚至几年后才补做一次性功能检测，而是提前、主动地测量了几乎所有可能的AIRE错义变异的功能，其中大多数变异甚至从未在临床上被观察到。 That "curate before the VUS arrives" posture is exactly the kind of thing worth stealing for CFTR missense positions that haven't shown up in CFTR2 yet. 这种"在意义不明变异（VUS）出现之前就先行整理好证据"的姿态，正是值得为那些尚未出现在CFTR2数据库中的CFTR错义位点所借鉴的做法。

JORDAN Let's plant some vocabulary before the biology. 在讲生物学之前，先把一些术语讲清楚。 A variant effect map, sometimes called a MAVE — multiplexed assay of variant effects (多重变异效应检测，缩写MAVE) — is a single experiment that measures the functional consequence of thousands of variants of one gene simultaneously, rather than testing them one at a time. 变异效应图谱，有时也称为MAVE——多重变异效应检测（multiplexed assay of variant effects，缩写MAVE）——是指通过单次实验同时测量某个基因数千个变异的功能后果，而不是逐一检测。 The contrast the authors draw is "reactive" versus "proactive": a reactive assay is built after a specific variant is already seen in a patient, while a proactive map, like this one, scores essentially every possible substitution ahead of time, including variants nobody has observed yet. 作者所做的对比是"被动式（reactive）"与"前瞻式（proactive）"：被动式检测是在某个特定变异已经在患者身上被发现之后才构建的，而像本文这样的前瞻式图谱，则提前对几乎所有可能的替换变异进行评分，包括那些尚未被观察到的变异。 And variant of uncertain significance, or VUS (意义不明的变异), is the clinical label for a variant that current evidence can't confidently call either benign or pathogenic — the entire value proposition of a proactive map is resolving VUS before the clinic even asks the question. 意义不明的变异（variant of uncertain significance，缩写VUS）是临床上对那些现有证据既不能确信判断为良性、也不能确信判断为致病性的变异所使用的标签——前瞻式图谱的全部价值主张，就是在临床尚未提出问题之前，就先解决VUS的判读问题。

ALEX Two more terms worth having ready. 还有两个术语值得先准备好。 ACMG/AMP (美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会/分子病理学会) refers to the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology, whose joint framework is the dominant standard for combining different kinds of evidence — population frequency, computational prediction, functional data — into a final clinical classification of benign, likely benign, VUS, likely pathogenic, or pathogenic. ACMG/AMP指的是美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会（American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics）与分子病理学会（Association for Molecular Pathology）联合制定的框架，是目前将人群频率、计算预测、功能数据等不同类型证据整合为最终临床分类——良性、可能良性、VUS、可能致病、致病——的主流标准。 And log-likelihood ratio of pathogenicity, or LLRp (致病性对数似然比), is a way of converting a raw functional score into a single number that reflects how much more likely a given score is to come from a known-pathogenic variant than a known-benign one; LLRp values can then be mapped onto the categorical evidence-strength levels — supporting, moderate, strong — that the ACMG/AMP framework actually uses. 致病性对数似然比（log-likelihood ratio of pathogenicity，缩写LLRp）是一种将原始功能评分转换为单一数值的方法，该数值反映的是：给定的评分来自已知致病变异的可能性比来自已知良性变异的可能性高出多少；LLRp数值随后可以被映射到ACMG/AMP框架实际使用的分类证据强度等级——支持级、中等级、强级。 Today's AIRE map is built, scored, calibrated, and clinically benchmarked almost entirely through that LLRp lens, so it's worth having the concept locked in now. 今天要讲的AIRE图谱几乎完全是通过LLRp这一视角来构建、评分、校准并进行临床基准测试的，所以现在就把这个概念牢牢记住是值得的。

JORDAN So what's the actual disease, briefly? 那么，这个疾病本身简单来说是什么? AIRE causes Autoimmune Polyendocrine Syndrome Type 1, or APS-1 — a rare, mostly recessive disease where loss of AIRE function stops the body from properly deleting self-reactive T cells during development, leading to autoimmune attacks on the parathyroid, adrenal gland, and other tissues. AIRE基因导致1型自身免疫性多内分泌腺综合征（Autoimmune Polyendocrine Syndrome Type 1，缩写APS-1）——这是一种罕见的、主要为常染色体隐性遗传的疾病，AIRE功能缺失会导致人体在发育过程中无法正常清除自身反应性T细胞，进而引发针对甲状旁腺、肾上腺及其他组织的自身免疫攻击。 Two-thirds of AIRE variants reported to ClinVar are missense, and 81% of those missense variants — 345 of 426 — are currently VUS, which is the exact backlog this map is trying to clear. 在ClinVar数据库中报告的AIRE变异里，三分之二是错义变异，而这些错义变异中有81%——426个中的345个——目前被归类为VUS，而这正是本图谱试图清理的积压问题。 Prior to this study, fewer than 7% of clinically-reported AIRE missense variants had any experimental functional data at all, and where it existed, it typically arrived years after the patient first presented. 在这项研究之前，不到7%的临床报告AIRE错义变异拥有任何实验性功能数据，而即便存在，这些数据通常也是在患者首次就诊多年之后才姗姗来迟。

ALEX The building block they used to measure function was an insulin-promoter-driven GFP reporter in a special HEK293 cell line with two engineered "landing pads" — a Bxb1 recombination site for stably integrating one AIRE variant per cell at genome scale, and a Flp-In site carrying the reporter itself. 他们用来测量功能的核心工具，是在一株特殊的HEK293细胞系中构建的胰岛素启动子驱动的GFP报告系统，该细胞系带有两个经过工程改造的"着陆位点"——一个是Bxb1重组位点，用于在基因组规模上为每个细胞稳定整合一个AIRE变异；另一个是Flp-In位点，承载报告基因本身。 AIRE normally upregulates insulin expression as one of thousands of tissue-specific genes it controls, so a functioning AIRE variant should drive GFP up, while a broken one leaves GFP low. AIRE通常会使胰岛素表达上调，这是它所调控的数千个组织特异性基因之一，因此功能正常的AIRE变异应会使GFP表达上升，而功能受损的变异则会使GFP表达维持在低水平。 To generate nearly all possible single-amino-acid substitutions at scale, they developed a new mutagenesis method called DT-POPCode — Dual-Tag Precision Oligo-Pool based Code Alteration (双标签精确寡核苷酸池编码改造法) — which removes a technical bottleneck of the earlier POPCode approach, and used it to build three regional variant libraries spanning AIRE's full 545-residue length. 为了大规模生成几乎所有可能的单氨基酸替换，他们开发了一种名为DT-POPCode的新突变方法——双标签精确寡核苷酸池编码改造法（Dual-Tag Precision Oligo-Pool based Code Alteration，缩写DT-POPCode）——该方法消除了早期POPCode方法的一个技术瓶颈，并用它构建了覆盖AIRE全长545个氨基酸的三个区域性变异文库。 Cells carrying each variant were sorted by GFP level, and a TileSeq sequencing pipeline counted how often each variant appeared in high-GFP "select" versus unselected "non-select" pools, turning that enrichment ratio into a calibrated functional score where nonsense variants are pinned to 0 and synonymous variants to 1. 携带每个变异的细胞根据GFP水平被分离筛选，随后通过TileSeq测序流程统计每个变异在高GFP"筛选组"与未经筛选的"非筛选组"中出现的频率，将这一富集比率转化为经过校准的功能评分，其中无义变异的评分被固定为0，同义变异的评分被固定为1。

JORDAN I want to push on one design choice before we get to numbers. 在进入结果之前，我想先追问一个设计上的选择。 Why build the reporter in an "open chromatin" genomic safe-harbor site rather than somewhere that mimics AIRE's natural, more tightly packed chromatin environment? 为什么要把报告系统构建在一个"开放染色质"的基因组安全港位点上，而不是选择一个更能模拟AIRE自然状态下、染色质更紧密包装的环境? ALEX, you flagged this as a real limitation — walk me through it. ALEX，你指出这是一个真实存在的局限——跟我说说具体是怎么回事。

ALEX Good catch, and the authors own it directly. 问得好，作者也直接承认了这一点。 AIRE's PHD1 domain — residues 296 to 343, which binds a specific histone mark called H3K4me0 — is known to harbor most of the dominant-negative pathogenic variants seen clinically, and PHD1 function is thought to matter most when AIRE is trying to activate genes sitting in closed, repressed chromatin. AIRE的PHD1结构域——位于296至343号残基，能结合一种叫做H3K4me0的特定组蛋白标记——已知携带了临床上大多数显性负效应（dominant-negative）的致病变异，而PHD1的功能被认为在AIRE试图激活位于封闭、受抑制染色质中的基因时最为关键。 Because this reporter sits in open chromatin, the insulin promoter can get activated without needing PHD1 to do its normal closed-chromatin-opening job, so known pathogenic PHD1 variants scored as essentially tolerated — a median positional score of 0.98, nearly indistinguishable from wild type. 由于这个报告系统位于开放染色质环境中，胰岛素启动子可以在不需要PHD1发挥其正常的"打开封闭染色质"功能的情况下被激活，因此已知的PHD1致病变异评分几乎显示为"可耐受"——其中位数位置评分为0.98，几乎与野生型没有区别。 That's a real, acknowledged blind spot, not a minor footnote — the authors deliberately declined to assign benign-direction evidence anywhere in PHD1 as a safeguard, while still allowing pathogenic-direction evidence through for a couple of zinc-binding cysteines that happened to show real signal. 这是一个真实且被明确承认的盲区，并非无关紧要的脚注——作者为了安全起见，特意决定在整个PHD1结构域内都不给出"偏向良性"方向的证据，但仍允许对其中两个确实显示出真实信号的锌结合半胱氨酸位点给出"偏向致病"方向的证据。 It's the right kind of trade-off to make transparently, but it means any map built this way needs its blind spots catalogued explicitly, not assumed away. 这是一种值得坦诚做出的权衡取舍，但这也意味着，任何以这种方式构建的图谱都需要明确列出其盲区，而不能想当然地认为不存在盲区。

JORDAN Okay, so does the map actually hold up where it isn't blind? 好，那么在它没有盲区的地方，这个图谱到底表现如何? Quite well. 相当不错。 They generated scores for 9,790 of the 10,900 possible AIRE amino acid substitutions — 90% overall, and 98% of the 3,189 substitutions reachable by a single-nucleotide change, which are the ones most likely to actually show up in a patient. 他们为10900个可能的AIRE氨基酸替换中的9790个生成了评分——总体覆盖率90%，而对于那3189个可通过单核苷酸变化即可到达、也最可能在患者身上实际出现的替换，覆盖率达到98%。 Known biochemistry came through clearly: buried residues scored far more intolerant than surface residues — median 0.43 versus 0.89, p=3×10⁻¹⁴ — and known functional domains like CARD, SAND, and PHD2 were broadly recapitulated as damage-sensitive, with PHD1 as the clear, explained exception. 已知的生物化学规律在数据中清晰可见：掩埋残基的评分远比表面残基更不耐受变异——中位数分别为0.43和0.89，p=3×10⁻¹⁴——而CARD、SAND和PHD2等已知功能结构域也大体上被证实对变异敏感，PHD1则是唯一且有明确解释的例外。

ALEX Against a clinical reference set of 16 benign/likely-benign and 30 pathogenic/likely-pathogenic ClinVar variants, the map achieved a recall of 87% at 90% balanced precision — right in line with REVEL and VARITY at 100%, AlphaMissense at 93%, and ESM1b at 90%, meaning this functional assay performs on par with the leading computational predictors Karen's team already uses, rather than lagging behind them. 在一个包含16个良性/可能良性和30个致病/可能致病ClinVar变异的临床参照集上，该图谱在90%平衡精确率下的召回率达到87%——与REVEL和VARITY的100%、AlphaMissense的93%、以及ESM1b的90%处于同一水平，这意味着这个功能检测在表现上与Karen团队目前已经在使用的领先计算预测工具旗鼓相当，而不是落后于它们。 After converting scores to LLRp and then to ACMG/AMP evidence strengths, the map produced non-undetermined functional evidence for 70% of the 345 current VUS — 242 variants — and that evidence led to a formal reclassification of 133 of them, 109 with high confidence, representing confident resolution of 32% of all current AIRE VUS. 在将评分转换为LLRp，再进一步转换为ACMG/AMP证据强度等级之后，该图谱为345个现有VUS中的70%——即242个变异——提供了非"无法确定"的功能证据，这些证据促成了133个变异的正式重新分类，其中109个属于高置信度，相当于自信地解决了全部现有AIRE VUS中的32%。 Concordance with existing classifications, excluding PHD1, was 89%, and of the handful of genuine discordances, one flagged variant — p.Arg471Cys, currently annotated benign — is actually a known contributor to polygenic autoimmune risk, which the authors read as the map correctly catching something ClinVar's current single-variant framework wasn't built to express. 除PHD1之外，该图谱与现有分类的一致性为89%，而在少数真正不一致的情形中，有一个被标记出来的变异——p.Arg471Cys，目前被标注为良性——实际上是已知的多基因自身免疫风险贡献因素之一，作者将此解读为该图谱正确捕捉到了ClinVar当前这种单变异判读框架本身并未设计用来表达的信息。

JORDAN And the clinical cohort piece — does the score actually track with how sick people are? 还有临床队列那部分——这个评分是否真的能反映患者的病情严重程度?
