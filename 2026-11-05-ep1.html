Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): IDUA ClinGen VCEP ACMG/AMP specification

Blurb. Lacaria et al. (2026, Molecular Genetics and Metabolism) report on the ClinGen Lysosomal Diseases Variant Curation Expert Panel's (LD VCEP) gene-specific ACMG/AMP classification criteria for IDUA, the gene underlying mucopolysaccharidosis type I (MPS I). The work was prioritized after MPS I's 2016 addition to the US Recommended Uniform Screening Panel (RUSP) made confident, fast variant classification a direct input to newborn-screening treatment decisions. The panel has submitted classifications and supporting evidence for 131 IDUA variants to ClinVar and the public ClinGen Evidence Repository. Closed access — only the abstract was retrievable, so specific threshold values (population-frequency cutoffs, functional-assay evidence weighting) are not available from this source. The episode uses this as a comparator for whether CFTR's own VCEP's evidence-code thresholds (PM1/PM2/PP3/PS3/BS3) are well-calibrated against a sibling panel's choices, and proposes a CFTR VCEP Evidence-Code Calibration Comparator as the buildable artifact: a side-by-side table of several ClinGen VCEPs' posted specifications, pulled from the ClinGen Evidence Repository's own API.

Plain-English glossary

ACMG/AMP framework — The 2015 American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology guideline for classifying a genetic variant as pathogenic, likely pathogenic, VUS (uncertain significance), likely benign, or benign, using weighted evidence codes.（中文：2015年由美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会和分子病理学会联合发布的变异分类指南，通过加权的证据代码把变异分为致病、可能致病、意义不明、可能良性或良性。）

ClinGen — The NIH-funded Clinical Genome Resource, a national effort to standardize how genetic variants are curated and classified across the field.（中文：美国NIH资助的临床基因组资源项目，旨在全领域范围内标准化基因变异的审核与分类流程。）

ClinGen Evidence Repository — A public, structured database hosting the itemized evidence trail (which codes, what strength, why) behind a VCEP's variant classifications, distinct from ClinVar's classification-only records.（中文：一个公开的结构化数据库，存放VCEP对变异分类所用的逐项证据记录（用了哪些代码、强度多少、理由为何），区别于ClinVar里仅记录最终分类结果的条目。）

ClinVar — NCBI's public archive of genetic variant classifications submitted by labs and expert panels worldwide.（中文：NCBI维护的公开数据库，收录全球实验室和专家组提交的基因变异分类结果。）

Evidence codes (PS/PM/PP/BS/BP) — The ACMG/AMP framework's building blocks: PS (pathogenic strong), PM (pathogenic moderate), PP (pathogenic supporting), and their benign mirrors BS and BP, each tied to a specific evidence type (population frequency, functional data, phenotype match, etc.).（中文：ACMG/AMP框架的基本构件：PS（致病强证据）、PM（致病中等证据）、PP（致病支持性证据），以及对应的良性证据BS和BP，每一类都对应一种特定证据类型，如人群频率、功能数据、表型匹配等。）

IDUA / alpha-L-iduronidase — The gene (and the lysosomal enzyme it encodes) whose deficiency causes mucopolysaccharidosis type I.（中文：编码α-L-艾杜糖苷酸酶的基因，该酶在溶酶体内发挥作用，其缺乏会导致I型黏多糖贮积症。）

Mucopolysaccharidosis type I (MPS I) — A progressive, multi-system lysosomal storage disease caused by IDUA deficiency, spanning a severe infantile form (historically "Hurler syndrome") to milder, later-onset forms ("Hurler-Scheie," "Scheie").（中文：由IDUA酶缺乏导致的进行性多系统溶酶体贮积病，疾病谱从婴儿期起病的重型（历史上称Hurler综合征）到较轻、起病较晚的类型（Hurler-Scheie综合征、Scheie综合征）。）

RUSP (Recommended Uniform Screening Panel) — The US list of conditions every newborn is screened for; MPS I was added in 2016.（中文：美国新生儿统一筛查推荐目录，列出所有新生儿都应被筛查的疾病；MPS I于2016年被纳入该目录。）

VCEP (Variant Curation Expert Panel) — A ClinGen standing committee that adapts the generic ACMG/AMP framework to one gene's or disease's specific biology.（中文：ClinGen下设的变异审核专家组，负责把通用的ACMG/AMP框架，改造为适合某一个基因或疾病特定生物学特征的版本。）

VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — The classification bucket for a variant whose pathogenicity can't yet be confidently determined.（中文：指一个变异目前尚无法被有把握地判定为致病或良性的分类结果。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • ClinGen Evidence Repository API — queryable, structured source of itemized VCEP evidence-code specifications across genes, the direct input for a cross-panel comparator.
  • ACMG/AMP evidence-code framework — the shared scaffolding (PS/PM/PP/BS/BP) that makes a cross-gene specification comparison meaningful in the first place.
  • ClinVar submission pipeline — the public record of final classifications, useful as a cross-check once the evidence-code comparison flags a candidate discrepancy.

MVP: pull the posted ACMG/AMP specifications for CFTR's own VCEP plus several sibling VCEPs (IDUA/LD VCEP, a BRCA1/2 panel, a hearing-loss panel) from the ClinGen Evidence Repository API, and lay their PM1/PM2/PP3/PS3/BS3 thresholds side by side in one comparison table to flag where CFTR's own calibration looks like an outlier relative to peer panels.

What to verify first

  • Confirm the actual published IDUA thresholds from the full paper or, more reliably, the ClinGen Evidence Repository's own posted specification document — this abstract gives none of the specific values.
  • Confirm CFTR's own VCEP specification's current version and revision date before any cross-panel comparison, since specifications are updated over time.
  • Open question, not yet resolved: whether a cross-gene comparison of evidence-code thresholds is a meaningful diagnostic at all, given that different genes have genuinely different mutational mechanisms, population frequencies of pathogenic variants, and depths of case ascertainment — a threshold difference could reflect real biology rather than miscalibration.
