Show Notes — Track A (Variant Interpretation & Harmonization)

Blurb

Mazza et al. (Nucleic Acids Research, 2026) introduce HapScoreDB, a genome-wide resource that reconstructs over 130,000 common human haplotype-resolved protein sequences (proteoforms) from phased 1000 Genomes genotypes and pre-scores every one of them with three protein language models — ESM-2 (zero-shot), PoET (alignment-based), and ProSST (structure-based) — for a total of 359,697 entries across 18,745 genes. The core move is to score the whole proteoform instead of each variant in isolation, so the model can feel intramolecular epistasis — the way co-inherited coding variants interact inside one folded protein — which additive single-variant tools (SIFT, PolyPhen-2, CADD, and summed AlphaMissense) structurally cannot. A cancer-GWAS case study shows haplotypes carrying cancer risk variants sit in significantly more functionally impactful contexts, even when each variant is individually mild. Where it lands: the Karen / CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration, since CFTR is full of complex cis alleles (R117H + poly-T, R74W;D1270N) whose meaning lives in the combination. Buildable artifact: a CFTR haplotype scorer that ranks phased complex alleles by how far the whole-haplotype PLM score diverges from the sum of its single-variant AlphaMissense scores — surfacing candidate epistatic alleles for a geneticist to review.

Plain-English glossary

  • Complex allele (cis allele) — two or more variants inherited together on the same gene copy, acting within one protein or one transcript.（中文：复合等位基因——两个或更多变异在同一条基因拷贝上一起遗传，在同一个蛋白质或转录本内起作用。）
  • Haplotype — the specific set of variants sitting together on one physical chromosome copy.（中文：单倍型——坐落在同一条物理染色体拷贝上的那一组变异。）
  • HGVS — the standardized notation for writing a genetic variant.（中文：HGVS——书写遗传变异的标准化命名规范。）
  • Intramolecular epistasis — when one variant changes the functional effect of another variant within the same protein molecule.（中文：分子内上位效应——同一蛋白质分子内，一个变异改变另一个变异的功能效应。）
  • Missense variant — a single-nucleotide change that swaps one amino acid for another.（中文：错义变异——一个单核苷酸改变，把一个氨基酸换成另一个。）
  • Phasing — determining which variants are in cis (same copy) versus in trans (opposite copies).（中文：定相——判定哪些变异在顺式（同一拷贝）、哪些在反式（相对拷贝）。）
  • Protein language model (PLM) — a neural network trained on natural protein sequences that scores how "surprising" a variant sequence looks as a proxy for functional damage.（中文：蛋白质语言模型——在天然蛋白质序列上训练的神经网络，用序列"有多出人意料"来近似功能损害。）
  • Proteoform — the actual protein sequence a specific haplotype produces.（中文：蛋白型——某条特定单倍型实际生产出的蛋白质序列。）
  • Pseudo-log-likelihood ratio — the PLM score comparing a variant proteoform to wild-type; more negative = more likely damaging.（中文：伪对数似然比——PLM 将变异蛋白型与野生型比较的评分；越负越可能有害。）
  • Splicing — the RNA-level process of removing introns and joining exons; a variant can act on splicing without changing the protein sequence (so a PLM won't see it).（中文：剪接——在 RNA 层面去除内含子、拼接外显子的过程；变异可以作用于剪接而不改变蛋白质序列，因此 PLM 看不到它。）
  • TSL (transcript support level) — an annotation flag for how well-supported a transcript model is by evidence.（中文：TSL——转录本支持级别，标记一个转录本模型被证据支持的程度。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Whole-proteoform PLM scoring — score the reconstructed haplotype protein sequence, not each variant, so intramolecular epistasis is captured.
  • Three-model ensemble by philosophy — ESM-2 (zero-shot sequence), PoET (MSA/alignment-based, MSAs built with MMseqs2 vs UniRef100), ProSST (structure-based, structures from AlphaFoldDB or Boltz-predicted).
  • Additive AlphaMissense baseline — reverse AlphaMissense's logistic regression to logit scale, then sum/average per-variant logits to build a fair "additive" comparator that by construction cannot see epistasis.
  • Delta / within-transcript variability metric — flags genes whose effect depends most on how variants combine.
  • Reusable structural encoding — encode wild-type structure once into a 4,096-token structural sequence, reuse across that gene's haplotypes.
  • Query surface — Shiny web portal (search by gene symbol / rsID / CHR:POS.REF>ALT), bulk gzipped TSV download, and a REST API for pipelines.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen / CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration.

cftr-haplotype-scorer (M) — Input: phased CFTR complex alleles curated from CFTR2 + gnomAD. For each haplotype, reconstruct the full CFTR proteoform, score with a PLM (ESM-2), and rank haplotypes by the divergence between the whole-haplotype score and the sum of single-variant AlphaMissense scores; high-divergence rows = candidate epistatic complex alleles for human review.

What to verify first

  • Scope blindness: a PLM reads protein sequence only — splice-acting variants (e.g., the R117H poly-T tract) are invisible; keep them on a separate track.
  • Coverage: HapScoreDB is built on common 1000G haplotypes; most clinically important CFTR variants are rare, so expect to score CFTR proteoforms yourself rather than look them up.
  • Calibration: a low pseudo-log-likelihood ≠ a clinical pathogenicity class; anchor against known-benign and known-pathogenic CFTR complex alleles before trusting any ranking.
  • Phasing correctness: cis vs trans assignment must be right for the whole-proteoform premise to hold.
  • AlphaMissense baseline caveat: the additive comparator was reconstructed by reversing AM's logistic regression (weights not public), so treat it as a reasonable yardstick, not ground truth.
  • External, public-data method only — no registry-internal genotype claims.

JORDAN Before the details, give me the vocabulary. 在讲细节之前，先给我把术语理一理。What is a haplotype, and why does "resolved" matter? 什么是单倍型 (haplotype)，"解析" (resolved) 又为什么重要？

ALEX A haplotype is the specific set of variants that sit together on one physical copy of a chromosome — the ones you inherited on the same strand from the same parent. 单倍型指的是坐落在同一条物理染色体拷贝上的那一组变异——也就是你从同一个亲代、在同一条链上一起遗传下来的那些变异。You have two copies of most genes, so two haplotypes per gene. 你的大多数基因都有两份拷贝，所以每个基因有两条单倍型。"Haplotype-resolved" means the data knows which variants are in cis — on the same copy — versus in trans — split across the two copies. "按单倍型解析"的意思是，数据知道哪些变异在同一拷贝上（顺式，in cis），哪些分在两条不同拷贝上（反式，in trans）。That knowledge is called phasing. 这种"谁和谁在一起"的信息叫做定相 (phasing)。It matters because two variants in cis land in the same protein molecule, while the same two in trans land in two different molecules — a completely different biological situation. 它之所以重要，是因为两个顺式变异会落在同一个蛋白质分子里，而同样这两个变异如果是反式的，就分别落在两个不同的分子里——这是两种完全不同的生物学情形。

JORDAN And what is a protein language model doing here? 那蛋白质语言模型在这里是干什么的？

ALEX A protein language model, or PLM, is a neural network trained on millions of natural protein sequences, so it learns which amino acid patterns are "normal." 蛋白质语言模型（PLM，protein language model，指在数百万条天然蛋白质序列上训练、从而学会"什么样的氨基酸排列是正常的"的神经网络）。When you feed it a variant sequence, it scores how surprising that sequence looks — a very surprising, low-likelihood sequence is a good bet for being functionally damaged. 当你把一个变异序列喂给它，它会给"这条序列有多出人意料"打分——越出人意料、似然越低的序列，越可能功能受损。The key point for today: a PLM reads the entire protein at once, so it can feel the combined effect of several variants sitting together. 今天的关键点是：PLM 一次读入整条蛋白质，所以它能感受到几个变异坐在一起时的合并效应。That is exactly the thing single-variant tools throw away. 而这正是单变异工具丢掉的东西。

JORDAN So what was broken before this paper? 那在这篇文章之前，哪里出了问题？

ALEX The workhorse variant predictors — SIFT, PolyPhen-2, CADD — score one amino acid change at a time, in isolation. 那些主力变异预测器——SIFT、PolyPhen-2、CADD——都是一次只给一个氨基酸改变打分，而且是孤立地打。But coding variants frequently co-occur on the same haplotype, and inside a single folded protein they can interact. 但编码变异经常共同出现在同一条单倍型上，而在一个折叠好的蛋白质内部，它们是会相互作用的。That interaction has a name: intramolecular epistasis — where one variant changes what another variant does to the same molecule. 这种相互作用有个名字：分子内上位效应 (intramolecular epistasis)——指同一个分子里，一个变异会改变另一个变异所产生的效果。Epistasis can soften a damaging variant, or it can amplify it, or it can flip its meaning entirely. 上位效应可以缓和一个有害变异，也可以放大它，甚至彻底改变它的含义。If you score each variant alone and then just add the scores, you are assuming the variants never talk to each other. 如果你把每个变异单独打分再简单相加，你其实是在假设这些变异之间从不交流。For folding, stability, and binding, that assumption is often wrong. 而对于折叠、稳定性和结合来说，这个假设常常是错的。Here's the intuition I keep in my head: a protein isn't a bag of independent letters, it's a folded structure where residues that are far apart in sequence can be neighbors in space. 我脑子里一直记着这样一个直觉：蛋白质不是一袋互相独立的字母，而是一个折叠好的结构，序列上相距很远的残基在空间里可能是紧挨着的邻居。So a first variant that slightly weakens a contact and a second variant that leans on that same contact can together break something neither would break alone. 于是，一个稍微削弱某个接触的变异，和一个正好压在同一个接触上的第二变异，可以合力破坏一个各自单独都破坏不了的东西。That is compensatory or aggravating epistasis, and it's precisely what a whole-sequence model has a chance to feel and an additive score never will. 这就是补偿性或加重性的上位效应，而这恰恰是一个"读整条序列"的模型有机会感受到、可加评分永远感受不到的东西。

ALEX The other half of the gap is data infrastructure. 缺口的另一半是数据基础设施。Even if you wanted haplotype-level scores, there was no ready resource that had reconstructed real human haplotype protein sequences and pre-scored them. 就算你想要单倍型层面的评分，之前也没有一个现成的资源，把真实的人类单倍型蛋白质序列重建出来并预先打好分。So every group re-did phasing, sequence reconstruction, and model inference from scratch. 于是每个课题组都得从头再做一遍定相、序列重建和模型推理。HapScoreDB is the authors' attempt to build that resource once, publicly, for the whole genome. HapScoreDB 就是作者们的尝试：把这个资源公开地、面向全基因组地、一次性建好。The promise is that variant interpretation should account for genetic context, not just the single letter that changed. 它的主张是：变异解读应该考虑遗传背景，而不只是那个改变了的单个字母。

JORDAN Okay, how did they actually build it? 好，那他们到底是怎么建的？

ALEX They started from phased genotypes in the 1000 Genomes Project and transcript annotations from GENCODE and Ensembl. 他们从千人基因组计划 (1000 Genomes Project) 里的已定相基因型出发，再加上来自 GENCODE 和 Ensembl 的转录本注释。For each transcript they reconstructed the actual protein sequence each haplotype would produce — a proteoform — giving over 130,000 non-redundant common haplotype protein sequences. 对每个转录本，他们重建出每条单倍型真正会生产出的蛋白质序列——也就是一个蛋白型 (proteoform)——总共得到十三万多条非冗余的常见单倍型蛋白质序列。The full database is 359,697 entries across 78,064 transcripts from 18,745 genes, annotated with about 94,000 dbSNP rsIDs on the GRCh38 build. 整个数据库有 359,697 条记录，覆盖 18,745 个基因的 78,064 个转录本，用大约 94,000 个 dbSNP rsID 注释，基于 GRCh38 参考基因组。Each entry also carries HGVS-style protein-change descriptions, which is the standard notation for writing a variant, and links to ClinVar. 每条记录还带有 HGVS 风格的蛋白质改变描述（HGVS，变异命名的标准化书写规范），以及到 ClinVar 的链接。

ALEX Then they scored every proteoform with three protein language models chosen to represent three different philosophies. 然后他们用三个蛋白质语言模型给每一个蛋白型打分，这三个模型分别代表三种不同的建模思路。ESM-2 is zero-shot, learning only from raw sequences. ESM-2 是零样本 (zero-shot) 的，只从原始序列里学习。PoET is alignment-based, so it needs the evolutionary neighborhood of related sequences, which they built with MMseqs2 against UniRef100. PoET 是基于比对的，所以它需要相关序列的进化邻域，作者用 MMseqs2 在 UniRef100 数据库上构建了这些多序列比对 (MSA)。ProSST is structure-based, so it needs a 3D structure, pulled from AlphaFoldDB or, when missing, predicted with Boltz. ProSST 是基于结构的，所以它需要一个三维结构，从 AlphaFoldDB 里取，取不到时就用 Boltz 预测。All three were picked because they top the ProteinGym benchmark in their category. 选这三个，是因为它们在各自类别里都是 ProteinGym 基准测试的第一梯队。A couple of engineering details are worth stealing. 有几个工程细节值得偷师。For the structure-based model they encode each wild-type structure once into a compact 4,096-token structural sequence, then reuse that encoding across all of that gene's haplotypes — a nice shortcut that assumes the backbone fold doesn't move much across common variants. 对于基于结构的模型，他们把每个野生型结构一次性编码成一个紧凑的 4,096 词元的结构序列，然后在该基因的所有单倍型间复用这个编码——一个漂亮的捷径，前提假设是主链折叠在常见变异之间不会怎么移动。When AlphaFold had no structure, they predicted one with Boltz using three recycling steps and a single diffusion sample, and they excluded proteoforms longer than 2,000 residues or with the weakest transcript support level. 当 AlphaFold 没有现成结构时，他们用 Boltz 预测，跑三轮循环 (recycling) 和单个扩散样本，并排除了长于 2,000 个残基、或转录本支持级别 (TSL) 最弱的蛋白型。Those are the kinds of pragmatic cutoffs that keep a genome-scale scoring job from never finishing. 这些正是那种务实的截断，能让一个全基因组规模的打分任务不至于永远跑不完。

ALEX Here's the design choice I'd push on. 这里有个我想追问的设计选择。To get a comparison yardstick, they added AlphaMissense scores — but AlphaMissense only scores single missense changes, so for a multi-variant haplotype they had to reverse its logistic regression back to a logit scale and then sum or average the per-variant logits. 为了有个对照标尺，他们加入了 AlphaMissense 的评分——但 AlphaMissense 只能给单个错义变异 (missense variant) 打分，所以对于一个多变异单倍型，他们不得不把它的逻辑回归反推回 logit 尺度，再把每个变异的 logit 相加或求平均。That summed-AlphaMissense number is, by construction, an additive baseline — it literally cannot see epistasis. 这个"求和后的 AlphaMissense"数值，从构造上讲就是一个可加的基线——它根本看不到上位效应。So it's a fair yardstick for "does haplotype scoring beat adding up singles," but we should remember it's a yardstick they partly built, since AlphaMissense's own weights aren't public. 所以它是"单倍型评分是否胜过把单变异相加"的公平标尺，但要记得这标尺有一部分是他们自己搭的，因为 AlphaMissense 的权重本身并不公开。

JORDAN Did the whole thing actually work? 那整套东西真的管用吗？

ALEX The score distributions behave the way you'd hope. 评分的分布表现得正如你所期望。Most haplotypes score near zero — functionally neutral — with a long negative tail of predicted-damaging ones, which is the shape a good functional score should have. 大多数单倍型评分接近零——功能中性——同时拖着一条预测有害的长负尾，这正是一个好的功能评分应有的形状。Their headline metric is a pseudo-log-likelihood ratio comparing the variant proteoform to the wild-type, and a separate delta metric that captures how much internal variability a transcript carries. 他们的核心指标是一个伪对数似然比 (pseudo-log-likelihood ratio)，把变异蛋白型和野生型作比较，还有一个单独的 delta 指标，用来刻画一个转录本内部携带了多少变异性。They also checked whether the three models agree with each other, which matters because concordance is your cheapest sanity check when you have no ground truth. 他们还检查了三个模型彼此是否一致，这很重要，因为在你没有金标准的时候，一致性是你最便宜的合理性检验。The delta metric they define captures within-transcript variability, and it shows a long positive tail — some transcripts carry a wide internal spread of haplotype effects, which is a signal that those genes are where haplotype context matters most. 他们定义的 delta 指标刻画的是转录本内部的变异性，它呈现出一条长的正尾——有些转录本内部携带着很宽的单倍型效应分布，这提示这些基因正是单倍型背景最重要的地方。That's a genuinely useful way to prioritize: not "which variant is bad," but "which gene is most sensitive to how its variants are combined." 这是一个真正有用的排序思路：不是"哪个变异坏"，而是"哪个基因对它的变异如何组合最敏感"。Then comes the case study that sells the idea. 接着是那个真正卖点的案例研究。

ALEX They pulled cancer-associated coding variants from the GWAS Catalog and asked whether haplotypes carrying a cancer GWAS variant look functionally different from those that don't. 他们从 GWAS 目录里取出与癌症相关的编码变异，问：携带癌症 GWAS 变异的单倍型，是否在功能上和不携带的不一样。Using a bootstrap of the median, cancer-carrying haplotypes had significantly lower median scores across models — meaning they sit in more functionally impactful haplotype contexts, even though each common risk variant is individually mild. 用中位数的自助法 (bootstrap)，携带癌症变异的单倍型在各个模型下都有显著更低的中位评分——意思是它们处在功能影响更大的单倍型背景里，尽管每个常见风险变异单独看都很温和。That's the payoff line: individually modest alleles can combine into an amplified functional impact that single-variant annotation misses entirely. 这就是那句点题的话：单独看不起眼的等位基因，可以组合成被放大的功能影响，而单变异注释完全看不到。They highlight FUT2, where common and rare variants combine into a wide spectrum of predicted consequences. 他们特别举了 FUT2 这个例子，其中常见变异和罕见变异组合出很宽的预测后果谱。I want to be honest about what this case study does and doesn't show, though. 不过我想诚实地说清楚这个案例研究证明了什么、没证明什么。It's an enrichment signal at the population level — cancer-risk haplotypes trend toward more impactful contexts — not a claim that any single haplotype is causal. 它是一个群体层面的富集信号——癌症风险单倍型倾向于处在影响更大的背景里——而不是宣称任何单条单倍型是致病的。So it validates that the haplotype view carries information the additive view doesn't, which is the point they need to make, but it stops short of proving the scores are calibrated to real functional magnitude. 所以它验证了单倍型视角携带着可加视角没有的信息，这正是他们需要证明的点，但它没有走到"证明这些评分校准到了真实功能量级"那一步。For a resource paper, that's the honest scope — build the atlas, show it's informative, leave calibration to downstream users like us. 对一篇资源型论文来说，这是诚实的边界——把图谱建好，证明它有信息量，把校准留给像我们这样的下游用户。And it's all queryable — a Shiny web portal where you search by gene symbol, rsID, or a CHR:POS.REF>ALT coordinate, plus bulk TSV download and a REST API for pipelines. 而且这一切都可查询——一个 Shiny 网页门户，你可以用基因符号、rsID 或 CHR:POS.REF>ALT 坐标来搜索，还有批量 TSV 下载和给流程用的 REST API。

JORDAN So where does this land for us? 那这对我们落在哪儿？

ALEX This lands squarely on the Karen collaboration — CFTR variant interpretation. 这直接落在与 Karen 的合作上——CFTR 变异解读。CFTR is a textbook case for haplotype thinking, because it has famous complex alleles where the meaning lives in the combination.
