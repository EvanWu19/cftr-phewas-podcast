How did this episode land for you?

Track C — DNA methylation predicts lung function and pulmonary exacerbation in CF sputum

Blurb. Tost, Caimmi, De Sario and colleagues (Clinical Epigenetics, 2025) ran the first longitudinal epigenome-wide association study (EWAS) in cystic fibrosis sputum — the airway material dominated by infiltrating neutrophils and monocytes, i.e. the immune cells at the site of lung disease. In the MethylBiomark cohort (50 pwCF, aged 12–30, two severe CFTR variants), they profiled ~850k CpG sites over ~18 months and linked methylation to four traits: current lung function (FEV1pp), lung-function trajectory (FEV1pp slope), pulmonary exacerbation (PEX), and PEX count. Validated hits sit on inflammation genes — SOCS3 and SBNO2 (lung function, pyrosequencing-validated), BRSK2 (decline; a pancreatic β-cell kinase, hinting at the CF-related-diabetes link), ANKRD53 and GRIK2 (PEX), MCTP2 (both PEX and PEX count). A 23-CpG signature predicted PEX at 84% accuracy. Where it lands: the CF-modifier catalog — logged as an epigenetically-implicated modifier layer — with a buildable cross-tissue methylation-modifier tracker keyed to GEO GSE289379.

Plain-English glossary

  • Beta value — methylation level at a CpG, 0 (unmethylated) to 1 (fully methylated).（中文：β 值——某 CpG 的甲基化水平，0 为未甲基化、1 为完全甲基化。）
  • CFTR variant class (I / II) — class I makes no CFTR protein; class II (e.g. p.Phe508del) makes protein that misfolds and is destroyed before reaching the cell surface.（中文：CFTR 变异分类（I/II）——I 类不产生 CFTR 蛋白；II 类（如 p.Phe508del）产生的蛋白错误折叠、在到达细胞表面前被降解。）
  • CpG site — a spot where a C sits next to a G in DNA; the location that gets methylated.（中文：CpG 位点——DNA 中 C 紧挨 G 的位置，即被甲基化的地点。）
  • DNA methylation — a chemical methyl tag on DNA that doesn't change the letters but tends to turn genes down.（中文：DNA 甲基化——加在 DNA 上的化学甲基标签，不改变字母但往往调低基因活性。）
  • EWAS (epigenome-wide association study) — the methylation analogue of a GWAS; scans hundreds of thousands of CpG sites for trait association.（中文：EWAS——甲基化版的 GWAS，扫描数十万个 CpG 位点找与性状的关联。）
  • FEV1pp — forced expiratory volume in 1 second, percent predicted; the standard CF lung-function measure, lower = worse.（中文：FEV1pp——一秒用力呼气容积占预计值百分比；CF 标准肺功能测量，越低越差。）
  • meQTL (methylation QTL) — a DNA variant whose genotype predicts methylation level at a nearby CpG.（中文：meQTL——基因型能预测附近某 CpG 甲基化水平的 DNA 变异。）
  • PEX (pulmonary exacerbation) — an acute worsening of CF lung disease (cough, fever, infection) needing antibiotics, causing a sharp FEV1pp drop.（中文：PEX——CF 肺病的急性加重（咳嗽、发热、感染），需抗生素，导致 FEV1pp 骤降。）
  • Pyrosequencing — an independent, targeted method to measure methylation at a specific CpG, used here to validate array hits.（中文：焦磷酸测序——独立、靶向测量特定 CpG 甲基化的方法，此处用于验证芯片结果。）
  • Sputum — expectorated airway material; in CF, dominated by infiltrating immune cells rather than epithelial cells.（中文：痰液——咳出的气道物质；CF 中以浸润的免疫细胞为主，而非上皮细胞。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Longitudinal sputum EWAS — Illumina EPIC arrays (~850k CpGs) across up to 4 visits/patient over 18 months.
  • Cell-composition correction — statistically adjust for the neutrophil/monocyte mix so hits reflect methylation, not cell-proportion shifts (ChAMP refbase; blood-cell L-DMR IDOL reference).
  • Panel-based classification — LDA + twofold cross-validation (100 iterations) to turn top CpGs into severity/PEX classifiers.
  • Independent validation — pyrosequencing of top CpGs in separate later-visit samples.
  • In silico annotation — TFmotifView (TF binding sites), GREAT (region-based GO), eFORGE (DHS/cell-type enrichment).

What to verify first

  • Sample size & single site — 50 patients, one center; slope and PEX-count signatures never reassessed in an independent cohort (authors flag this).
  • Cause vs consequence — EWAS cannot say whether marks drive decline or are a footprint of prior inflammation.
  • meQTL confounding — check whether a local sequence variant, not the CpG itself, is driving the association before treating a CpG as an independent modifier.
  • External data only — external cohort + public GEO; no registry-internals assumptions.

Gap-map / catalog

  • Closes: adds an epigenetic modifier layer for CF lung-disease severity (SOCS3, SBNO2, BRSK2, ANKRD53, GRIK2, MCTP2) with trait-specific accuracies and validation status; reinforces the CF↔COPD shared-inflammation axis (SOCS3/SBNO2 also lung-function-linked in COPD).
  • Leaves open: no independent-cohort replication for longitudinal traits; causality undetermined; meQTL vs pure-epigenetic contribution unresolved; BRSK2→CFRD→lung-decline mechanism only hypothesized.

Read full transcript (20 turns · ~2070 words)

ALEX Today's Track C paper is "DNA methylation predicts lung function and pulmonary exacerbation in sputum samples from patients with cystic fibrosis," by Jörg Tost, Davide Caimmi and colleagues, with Albertina De Sario as senior author, in Clinical Epigenetics, 2025. 今天的 Track C 论文是《DNA 甲基化可预测囊性纤维化患者痰液样本中的肺功能和肺部急性加重》，作者是 Jörg Tost、Davide Caimmi 等人，Albertina De Sario 为通讯作者，2025 年发表在《临床表观遗传学》上。 It's a CF-modifier paper, but the modifier here isn't a gene variant — it's an epigenetic mark layered on top of the genome. 这是一篇 CF 修饰因子的论文，但这里的修饰因子不是一个基因变异——而是叠加在基因组之上的一个表观遗传标记。

JORDAN That's a new flavor for this track, so let me get the biology straight. 这对本 Track 来说是个新口味，所以让我把生物学先弄清楚。 What is DNA methylation, and what's an EWAS? 什么是 DNA 甲基化，什么又是 EWAS？

ALEX DNA methylation is a chemical tag — a methyl group — added onto the DNA, almost always at a spot where a C sits next to a G, which geneticists write as a CpG site. DNA 甲基化是一个化学标签——一个甲基基团——被加到 DNA 上，几乎总是在一个 C 紧挨着 G 的位置，遗传学家把它写作 CpG 位点 (CpG site)。 The DNA letters don't change; what changes is whether that spot is chemically marked, which tends to turn genes down. DNA 字母本身不变；改变的是那个位置是否被打上化学标记，而这种标记往往会调低基因的活性。 They measure it as a beta value between 0 and 1, where 0 is unmethylated and 1 is fully methylated. 他们用一个介于 0 和 1 之间的 β 值来测量，0 表示未甲基化，1 表示完全甲基化。 An EWAS — epigenome-wide association study — is the methylation analogue of a GWAS: instead of scanning millions of DNA variants for association with a trait, you scan hundreds of thousands of CpG sites. EWAS（表观基因组关联研究）是甲基化版本的 GWAS：不是扫描数百万个 DNA 变异去找与某个性状的关联，而是扫描数十万个 CpG 位点。 The key clinical variable throughout is FEV1pp — forced expiratory volume in one second, percent predicted — which is the standard lung-function measure in CF, and lower means worse disease. 全篇的关键临床变量是 FEV1pp（一秒用力呼气容积占预计值百分比），这是 CF 中标准的肺功能测量，数值越低表示病情越重。 And a pulmonary exacerbation, or PEX, is an acute worsening — more cough, fever, infection — that usually needs antibiotics and causes a sharp drop in FEV1pp. 而肺部急性加重（pulmonary exacerbation，简称 PEX）是一次急性恶化——咳嗽加剧、发热、感染——通常需要抗生素治疗，并导致 FEV1pp 骤降。

JORDAN And crucially, this is measured in sputum, not blood or a cheek swab. 而且关键在于，这是在痰液里测的，不是血液或口腔拭子。 Why does the sample type matter? 样本类型为什么重要？

ALEX It matters a lot, and it's the paper's distinctive angle. 它非常重要，而且是这篇论文独特的切入点。 Sputum is expectorated airway material, and in CF it's dominated by infiltrating immune cells — mostly neutrophils and monocytes. 痰液是咳出的气道物质，在 CF 中它以浸润的免疫细胞为主——主要是中性粒细胞和单核细胞。 So a methylation signal in sputum is largely reading the epigenetic state of the inflammatory cells sitting right at the site of lung disease, not a distant proxy tissue. 所以痰液里的甲基化信号在很大程度上读取的是坐落在肺病现场的炎症细胞的表观遗传状态，而不是一个远处的替代组织。 That local read is why the genes they find keep pointing at inflammation. 正是这种在地读取，解释了为什么他们找到的基因反复指向炎症。

JORDAN So what did they set out to explain? 那他们想解释什么？

ALEX The long-standing puzzle in CF is that lung-disease severity varies enormously between patients, and the CFTR genotype alone doesn't explain it — nor do the known modifier genes. CF 里长期存在的谜题是：肺病严重程度在病人之间差异极大，而单靠 CFTR 基因型解释不了它——已知的修饰基因也解释不了。 Twin studies say environment matters too, and epigenetics is a natural place where genetics and environment could meet and get written down. 双胞胎研究说环境也有影响，而表观遗传学正是遗传与环境可能相遇、并被记录下来的一个自然场所。 This group had already shown, in nasal epithelial cells, that methylation signatures could separate mild from severe CF lung disease and explain up to 70% of the variance in lung function. 这个团队之前已经在鼻上皮细胞里证明，甲基化特征可以把轻度和重度 CF 肺病区分开，并解释多达 70% 的肺功能方差。 The open question was whether sputum — easier to collect longitudinally, and dominated by the immune cells actually driving inflammation — could predict not just current lung function but the trajectory and the acute events. 尚未回答的问题是：痰液——更容易纵向采集、且以真正驱动炎症的免疫细胞为主——能否不仅预测当前肺功能，还能预测病程轨迹和急性事件。

JORDAN Why is longitudinal sampling such a big deal here — isn't one snapshot per patient usually enough for an association study? 为什么纵向采样在这里是件大事——一个关联研究里每位病人一张快照通常不就够了吗？

ALEX For a static trait, one snapshot is fine, but two of their four outcomes are inherently about change over time. 对一个静态性状来说，一张快照没问题，但他们四个结局里有两个本质上关乎随时间的变化。 The FEV1pp slope is the rate at which a patient's lung function is declining, and exacerbation count is how many acute events accumulate — neither exists in a single visit. FEV1pp 斜率是一位病人肺功能下降的速率，而急性加重次数是有多少次急性事件累积起来——两者都无法在单次访视里存在。 Methylation is also dynamic; it shifts with inflammation, infection and treatment, so a mark measured once might reflect a transient state rather than a stable predictor. 甲基化也是动态的；它随炎症、感染和治疗而变动，所以只测一次的标记可能反映的是一个短暂状态，而不是一个稳定的预测因子。 By sampling up to four times over 18 months, they can ask whether a methylation level at an early visit forecasts what happens later, which is a genuinely predictive question rather than a merely correlational one. 通过在 18 个月里采样多达四次，他们可以去问：早期访视时的一个甲基化水平能否预示后来发生的事，这是一个真正预测性的问题，而不只是一个相关性的问题。 That's the design strength — and, as we'll see, also where the small sample bites hardest. 这正是这个设计的强项——而且，我们将会看到，也是小样本咬得最狠的地方。

ALEX The design is a genuine longitudinal one, which is rare here. 这个研究设计是真正纵向的，这在此类研究中很少见。 The MethylBiomark cohort is 50 people with CF, aged 12 to 30, each carrying two severe class I or class II CFTR variants. MethylBiomark 队列是 50 位 CF 患者，年龄 12 到 30 岁，每人携带两个严重的 I 类或 II 类 CFTR 变异。 A class I variant makes no CFTR protein at all; a class II variant, like the common p.Phe508del, makes protein that misfolds and gets destroyed before reaching the cell surface. I 类变异完全不产生 CFTR 蛋白；II 类变异，比如常见的 p.Phe508del，产生的蛋白会错误折叠，在到达细胞表面之前就被降解。 They collected up to four sputum samples per patient over about 18 months and profiled roughly 850,000 CpG sites on Illumina EPIC arrays. 他们对每位病人在约 18 个月内采集多达四份痰液样本，并用 Illumina EPIC 芯片检测了大约 85 万个 CpG 位点。 A methodological detail worth respecting: because sputum is a messy mix of cell types, they statistically corrected for cell-composition heterogeneity, so a hit reflects methylation differences rather than just a shift in which cells were in the sample. 一个值得尊重的方法学细节：因为痰液是各种细胞类型的杂乱混合物，他们对细胞组成的异质性做了统计校正，这样一个阳性结果反映的是甲基化差异，而不只是样本里细胞比例的变动。 And they validated their top hits by pyrosequencing — an independent, targeted method to measure methylation at a specific CpG — in separate samples from later visits. 他们还用焦磷酸测序 (pyrosequencing，一种独立的、靶向测量特定 CpG 甲基化的方法) 在后续访视的独立样本中验证了他们最重要的阳性结果。

ALEX Let me push on one design choice before we get to results, because it shapes how much to believe. 在进入结果之前，我想质疑一个设计选择，因为它影响我们该相信多少。 They ran the exacerbation analysis in a slightly unusual way: they analyzed visit-1 samples and visit-2 samples separately, took the CpG sites significant in both, and only then pooled all 64 samples to finalize a 23-site list. 他们用一种略微不寻常的方式做了急性加重分析：分别分析了访视 1 和访视 2 的样本，取在两者中都显著的 CpG 位点，然后才把全部 64 份样本汇总，最终确定一张 23 位点的清单。 That intersection step is a reasonable way to guard against chance hits with only 50 patients, and I respect that they didn't just throw everything into one big regression. 这个取交集的步骤，在只有 50 位病人的情况下，是防范偶然阳性结果的一个合理办法，我尊重他们没有把所有东西一股脑塞进一个大回归里。 But it also means the final panel was tuned on the same samples it was then reported on, so the accuracy numbers are in-sample, not out-of-sample in the strict machine-learning sense. 但这也意味着最终的面板是在它随后被报告的那批样本上调出来的，所以那些准确率数字是样本内的，而不是严格机器学习意义上的样本外。 The pyrosequencing on later visits is the real out-of-sample check, and there only a subset of the top sites held up — which is exactly the honesty we'd want. 后续访视的焦磷酸测序才是真正的样本外检验，而在那里只有一部分头部位点站住了脚——这恰恰是我们想要的那种诚实。

JORDAN So what did the methylation actually predict, and how well? 那甲基化到底预测了什么，预测得有多好？

ALEX Four traits, with real numbers. 四个性状，都有实打实的数字。 For current lung function, they found two CpG sites that survived and validated: cg11047325 in SOCS3 and cg18608055 in SBNO2, both inflammation-regulating genes, and both had already been tied to lung function in COPD blood samples. 对于当前肺功能，他们找到了两个既显著又通过验证的 CpG 位点：SOCS3 基因里的 cg11047325 和 SBNO2 基因里的 cg18608055，两者都是调节炎症的基因，而且都已经在慢阻肺 (COPD) 的血液样本里与肺功能挂过钩。 A top-100 CpG panel separated severe from mild patients with 83% accuracy. 一个前 100 位的 CpG 面板以 83% 的准确率把重度和轻度病人分开。 For lung-function trajectory — the FEV1pp slope over time — no single site survived multiple-testing correction, but a top-3 panel still split declining patients from stable-or-improving ones at 80% accuracy. 对于肺功能轨迹——随时间变化的 FEV1pp 斜率——没有单个位点能通过多重检验校正，但一个前 3 位的面板仍以 80% 的准确率把下降的病人和稳定或好转的病人分开。 One of those three, cg09600088, maps to BRSK2, a kinase involved in insulin secretion and pancreatic beta-cell function — which is intriguing because CF-related diabetes is a major comorbidity tied to lung decline. 那三个里的 cg09600088 定位到 BRSK2，一个参与胰岛素分泌和胰腺 β 细胞功能的激酶——这很耐人寻味，因为 CF 相关糖尿病是一个与肺功能下降相关的重要合并症。 For acute exacerbations, a 23-CpG signature predicted which patients had a PEX at 84% accuracy, with the top validated site sitting in ANKRD53. 对于急性加重，一个 23-CpG 的特征以 84% 的准确率预测了哪些病人发生了 PEX，其中最重要的、通过验证的位点位于 ANKRD53。 And for exacerbation burden — the number of PEXs over 24 months — nine sites correlated, with MCTP2 notably showing up for both PEX occurrence and PEX count at different CpG sites. 而对于加重负担——24 个月内 PEX 的次数——有九个位点相关，其中 MCTP2 值得注意地在 PEX 发生和 PEX 次数上都出现了，只是在不同的 CpG 位点。

JORDAN Where does this land for our work? 这对我们的工作落在哪里？

ALEX One biological thread worth pulling first, because it's the paper's most suggestive finding. 有一条生物学线索值得先拉一下，因为它是这篇论文最有暗示性的发现。 The BRSK2 signal ties the lung-decline trajectory to pancreatic beta-cell biology, and that's not a random gene — CF-related diabetes affects roughly half of adult patients and is itself a known predictor of faster lung decline. BRSK2 信号把肺功能下降的轨迹与胰腺 β 细胞生物学联系了起来，而那不是一个随机的基因——CF 相关糖尿病影响着大约一半的成年病人，而它本身就是肺功能更快下降的一个已知预测因子。 So a methylation mark near a beta-cell kinase showing up in a lung-function trajectory analysis hints at a shared molecular axis between the endocrine and pulmonary comorbidities, rather than two independent problems. 所以一个位于 β 细胞激酶附近的甲基化标记出现在肺功能轨迹分析里，暗示了内分泌合并症与肺部合并症之间存在一条共享的分子轴，而不是两个各自独立的问题。 The other recurring theme is the CF-to-COPD bridge: SOCS3 and SBNO2 were tied to lung function in COPD blood before, and here they resurface in CF sputum, pointing at a chronic-airway-inflammation program that isn't CF-specific. 另一个反复出现的主题是 CF 到 COPD 的桥梁：SOCS3 和 SBNO2 之前在 COPD 血液里就与肺功能挂钩，而在这里它们又在 CF 痰液中重新浮现，指向一个并非 CF 专属的慢性气道炎症程序。 For a modifier catalog, that cross-disease recurrence is a useful prioritization signal — genes that show up in two chronic airway diseases are better bets for being genuinely mechanistic. 对一个修饰因子目录来说，这种跨疾病的复现是一个有用的优先级信号——在两种慢性气道疾病里都出现的基因，更有可能是真正机制性的，值得押注。
