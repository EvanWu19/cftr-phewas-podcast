RNA splicing evidence enables robust classification of BRCA1 exon 18 variants: Results from the ENIGMA consortium

Domènech-Vivó J, Tubeuf H, Mesman RLS, et al. (ENIGMA consortium). American Journal of Human Genetics, 2026.

Blurb

The ENIGMA consortium — the ClinGen Variant Curation Expert Panel for BRCA1/2 — set out to systematically resolve splicing-variant classification for one exon, BRCA1 exon 18, by testing 166 candidate variants in a minigene splicing assay, cross-checking 32 of them against patient blood-derived RNA, and linking splicing outcome to actual protein function in 18 variants via a mouse embryonic stem cell homology-directed-repair (HDR) assay. The calibrated RNA thresholds that came out (≥59% full-length transcript, or <34% exon-skipped transcript, to count as benign) were stricter than the generic ACMG/AMP guidance, and reclassified 34% of the variant set — cutting the uncertain-significance bucket roughly in half. This is not a CFTR paper; it's read as a template for CFTR splice-VUS resolution, where SpliceAI-style predictions routinely substitute for wet-lab confirmation. The buildable artifact is a CFTR-specific splice-threshold calibrator that checks whether the same generic RNA-evidence thresholds actually hold for CFTR, using whatever paired splicing-and-function data already exists in the literature or CFTR2.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP framework — the five-tier (pathogenic / likely pathogenic / uncertain / likely benign / benign) variant classification system jointly maintained by the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology, built from combinable named evidence codes.（中文:美国医学遗传学与基因组学学院和分子病理学协会联合制定的五级变异分类体系,由可组合的命名证据代码构成。）
  • BP7 / BP7_strong(RNA) — an ACMG/AMP "benign, supporting" evidence code for a variant predicted not to affect splicing; the "_strong(RNA)" variant is a VCEP-specified stronger version used when real RNA data, not just a prediction score, shows near-normal splicing.（中文:BP7是ACMG/AMP中"良性、支持性"证据代码,用于预测不影响剪接的变异;"_strong(RNA)"是专家小组指定的加强版本,用于有真实RNA数据(而非仅预测分数)显示剪接接近正常的情况。）
  • ClinGen VCEP — a Clinical Genome Resource Variant Curation Expert Panel, a gene- or gene-family-specific body that sets curation rules; ENIGMA is the VCEP for BRCA1/BRCA2.（中文:临床基因组资源变异校正专家小组,针对特定基因或基因家族制定分类规则的机构;ENIGMA是负责BRCA1/BRCA2的专家小组。）
  • Minigene assay — a plasmid-based splicing reporter: the variant exon plus flanking intron is cloned into a synthetic construct, transfected into cells, and the resulting spliced transcript is sequenced.（中文:一种基于质粒的剪接报告系统,将携带变异的外显子及其侧翼内含子克隆入人工构建体、转染入细胞后测序观察剪接结果。）
  • mESC-HDR assay — a functional assay using mouse embryonic stem cells lacking functional Brca1, complemented with a variant construct, testing whether homology-directed repair (HDR) of DNA double-strand breaks is restored.（中文:利用缺乏功能性Brca1的小鼠胚胎干细胞、以变异构建体互补后检测同源定向修复(HDR)功能是否恢复的功能检测法。）
  • Exon skipping — a splice defect in which a variant causes an exon to be excluded entirely from the mature mRNA.（中文:一种剪接缺陷,变异导致某个外显子在成熟mRNA中被完全跳过。）

ALEX Today we're reading "RNA splicing evidence enables robust classification of BRCA1 exon 18 variants: Results from the ENIGMA consortium," by Domènech-Vivó and colleagues, published in the American Journal of Human Genetics in 2026. 今天我们读的是多梅内克-维沃(Domènech-Vivó)等人发表在《美国人类遗传学杂志》上的论文,题目是《RNA剪接证据实现BRCA1第18外显子变异的可靠分类:ENIGMA联盟的研究结果》,发表于2026年。 This one's in Track A, our variant-interpretation lane, and it's not about CFTR at all — it's about BRCA1. 这篇论文属于Track A,也就是我们的变异解读赛道,内容完全不涉及CFTR,而是关于BRCA1基因的。 We're reading it because it's a clean, worked example of exactly the evidentiary architecture CFTR splice-variant classification is missing: a systematic RNA-based calibration of a specific ACMG/AMP evidence code. 我们选读它,是因为它清楚地展示了CFTR剪接变异分类目前所缺少的证据构建方法:一种基于RNA数据、针对特定ACMG/AMP证据代码的系统性校准。 Heads up before we start: this one is abstract-only for us today — no open PMC full text, so some of what follows is inference from a dense abstract rather than confirmed methods detail. 开始之前先说明一下:今天这篇论文我们只能读到摘要,没有开放的PMC全文,所以下面的一些内容是基于摘要的合理推断,而非已核实的方法细节。

JORDAN Before we get into it — BP7_strong(RNA). What is that? 在深入之前,先问一下——BP7_strong(RNA)到底是什么?

ALEX It's an ACMG/AMP evidence code. 这是一个ACMG/AMP证据代码。 The American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology jointly maintain a five-tier variant classification framework — pathogenic, likely pathogenic, uncertain significance, likely benign, benign — and each tier is reached by combining named "evidence codes," modular arguments like PS3 for functional evidence of damage or BP7 for a variant predicted not to affect splicing. 美国医学遗传学与基因组学学院和分子病理学协会共同维护一套五级变异分类框架——致病、可能致病、意义未明、可能良性、良性——每一级都是通过组合被称为"证据代码"(evidence codes)的模块化论据来达成的,比如PS3代表有功能证据显示变异有害,BP7则代表预测不影响剪接的变异。 "BP7_strong(RNA)" is a beefed-up version of that benign code specifically for cases where you have actual RNA data, not just a splice-prediction score, showing the transcript is essentially normal. 而"BP7_strong(RNA)"则是这个良性证据代码的加强版,专门用于已经有真实RNA数据——而不仅仅是剪接预测分数——证明转录本基本正常的情况。 ClinGen's Variant Curation Expert Panels, or VCEPs, are the bodies that get to specify exactly how strong that RNA evidence needs to be for a given gene, and ENIGMA is the VCEP for BRCA1 and BRCA2. ClinGen的变异校正专家小组(VCEP)负责为某个基因具体规定这类RNA证据需要达到多强的标准,而ENIGMA正是负责BRCA1和BRCA2的专家小组。

JORDAN And the two lab methods — minigene assay and this mESC-HDR thing? 那两种实验方法呢——微基因(minigene)检测法和这个mESC-HDR检测法?

ALEX A minigene assay clones the exon you care about, plus a chunk of its flanking intron sequence carrying the variant, into a small reporter plasmid, transfects it into cells, and reads out by sequencing what the spliceosome actually does with it. 微基因检测法是把你关心的外显子,连同携带变异的一段侧翼内含子序列,克隆进一个小型报告质粒里,转染进细胞,然后通过测序读出剪接体实际是如何处理这段序列的。 It's a controlled proxy for splicing when you don't have patient tissue to test directly. 当你没有患者组织可以直接检测时,它是剪接效应的一个受控替代指标。 The mESC-HDR assay is a functional readout, not a splicing readout: it uses mouse embryonic stem cells engineered to lack functional Brca1, complements them with a variant BRCA1 construct, and tests whether double-strand-break repair by homologous recombination — HDR, homology-directed repair — is restored. mESC-HDR检测法则不是剪接读数,而是功能读数:它使用经过改造、缺乏功能性Brca1的小鼠胚胎干细胞,用携带变异的BRCA1构建体进行互补,然后检测同源重组介导的双链断裂修复——也就是HDR,同源定向修复——功能是否被恢复。 That's the orthogonal ground truth this paper uses to figure out how much splicing disruption actually matters for protein function. 这正是这篇论文用来判断"剪接干扰到底要多严重才算数"的正交金标准。

JORDAN One more term before we move on — how does an evidence code like BP7_strong actually combine with everything else a lab already knows about a variant? 再问一个问题——像BP7_strong这样的证据代码,到底是怎么和实验室已知的其他信息组合起来的?

ALEX The ACMG/AMP framework works like a points system with named rules rather than raw points, but the effect is additive: a "strong" benign code like BP7_strong can, on its own or combined with a couple of "supporting" codes, push a variant from uncertain all the way to likely benign, without needing every other line of evidence — population frequency, computational prediction, segregation data — to agree. 严格来说,ACMG/AMP框架更像是一套用命名规则而非原始积分构成的体系,但效果本质上是累加的:像BP7_strong这样的"强"良性证据代码,单独使用,或者再配上一两条"支持性"证据代码,就足以把一个变异从"意义未明"直接推到"可能良性",而不需要其他每一条证据线——人群频率、计算预测、家系共分离数据——都同时支持这个结论。 That is exactly why getting the RNA threshold right matters so much here: overstate the strength of a splicing result and you can single-handedly move a variant's classification, with downstream effects on genetic counseling and risk-reducing surgery decisions. 这正是为什么把RNA阈值定准如此重要:如果高估了一个剪接结果的证据强度,就可能单凭这一条证据改变变异的分类,进而影响到遗传咨询和降低风险手术等下游临床决策。

ALEX Quick context on why this study exists at all. 简单说一下这项研究的背景。 BRCA1 exon 18 variants have historically been hard to classify consistently — different labs applying different, informal thresholds to whatever splicing data they happened to have, with no systematic RNA assessment across the exon. BRCA1第18外显子的变异历来很难被一致地分类——不同实验室对手头零散的剪接数据采用不同的、非正式的判断标准,缺乏针对整个外显子的系统性RNA评估。 That inconsistency has real consequences: a variant called "likely benign" at one lab and "uncertain" at another changes screening and management recommendations for entire families. 这种不一致会带来实际后果:同一个变异在一家实验室被判定为"可能良性",在另一家却是"意义未明",这会直接改变整个家庭的筛查和管理建议。 ENIGMA set out to fix that for exon 18 specifically, systematically assaying splicing across a large variant set and then asking a harder question: does the generic RNA-evidence threshold the field already uses — greater than 30% full-length transcript for benign, less than 70% non-functional transcript for pathogenic-supporting — actually hold up against real functional data, or is it too loose? ENIGMA联盟着手专门解决第18外显子的这个问题,系统性地检测了大量变异的剪接情况,并进一步追问一个更难的问题:该领域目前普遍使用的通用RNA证据阈值——全长转录本超过30%判定为良性、无功能转录本超过70%支持致病——在真实功能数据面前是否真的站得住脚,还是说这个标准定得太宽松了? This maps directly onto a gap in CFTR variant curation, where SpliceAI-type prediction scores routinely stand in for wet-lab splicing confirmation, especially for deep-intronic and near-splice-site variants nobody has gotten around to testing experimentally. 这与CFTR变异分类中的一个缺口直接对应——在CFTR领域,SpliceAI一类的预测分数经常被用来代替湿实验室的剪接验证,尤其是对那些深内含子区和近剪接位点变异,目前还没有人系统地做过实验验证。

ALEX On the methods: they ran the minigene assay on 166 candidate variants — that's the primary, high-throughput layer. 说到方法:他们对166个候选变异做了微基因检测——这是主要的、高通量的第一层。 A subset of 32 of those got a second check in patient blood-derived RNA from 51 individuals, RT-PCR on the actual endogenous transcript, to see whether the plasmid-based minigene result matches what really happens in a person's cells. 其中32个变异又在51名个体的血液来源RNA中做了第二轮验证,通过RT-PCR检测真实的内源转录本,看质粒微基因的结果是否和一个人细胞内实际发生的情况相符。 And a further subset of 18 went into the mESC-HDR functional assay to link splicing outcome to actual protein rescue activity. 还有一部分18个变异被进一步送入mESC-HDR功能检测,以将剪接结果与实际的蛋白质功能挽救活性联系起来。 I'd push back on the design here a little: the calibration that produces the final threshold rests on just 18 variants with functional data, while the headline 166-variant classification relies on the minigene system alone — a heterologous, plasmid-based context that doesn't always reproduce endogenous splicing efficiency because of vector-specific exon-definition effects. 我对这里的设计有一点保留意见:最终用来推导阈值的校准数据只基于18个有功能数据的变异,而头条的166个变异分类结果则单纯依赖微基因系统——这是一个异源的、基于质粒的环境,由于载体特异的外显子确定效应,并不总能重现内源剪接效率。 So most of the classification weight sits on a proxy assay that was validated against ground truth in only a small fraction of cases. 所以大部分分类的权重其实都压在了一个仅在一小部分病例中经过金标准验证的替代检测上。

JORDAN Why trust blood RNA over the minigene at all — isn't the minigene the more controlled experiment? 为什么血液RNA的可信度要高于微基因检测——微基因检测不是应该是更受控的实验吗?

ALEX Controlled isn't the same as representative. 受控不等于有代表性。 A minigene strips the variant exon out of its real genomic neighborhood and drops it into a synthetic construct with its own promoter, its own flanking exons, and its own splice-site strengths — any of which can shift how efficiently the spliceosome recognizes that exon, independent of what the variant itself does. 微基因检测把变异所在的外显子从其真实的基因组环境中剥离出来,放进一个拥有自己启动子、自己侧翼外显子和自己剪接位点强度的人工构建体中——这其中任何一个因素都可能改变剪接体识别该外显子的效率,而这种改变与变异本身无关。 Blood RNA, by contrast, captures the transcript exactly as it's made in a real cell with its native chromatin context, its native neighboring exons, and its native transcription rate — it is the thing we actually care about, just harder to obtain for every variant since it requires a consenting carrier and a blood draw rather than a plasmid order. 相比之下,血液RNA捕捉的是转录本在真实细胞中、在其原生染色质环境、原生邻近外显子和原生转录速率下的真实产物——这才是我们真正关心的东西,只是获取难度更高,因为它需要一位同意参与的携带者和一次抽血,而不是订购一份质粒那么简单。 That's exactly why this paper treats blood RNA as the calibration check on the minigene, not the other way around. 这正是为什么这篇论文把血液RNA当作校验微基因结果的基准,而不是反过来。

JORDAN Did the numbers actually work out, though? 那结果到底站得住脚吗?

ALEX Where they had overlap, yes — mRNA from blood and the minigene result showed a significant positive correlation, and mESC splicing data lined up closely with the functional HDR readout. 在有重叠数据的地方,确实站得住——血液来源的mRNA结果与微基因结果显示出显著正相关,mESC剪接数据也与功能性HDR读数高度吻合。 The calibrated thresholds that came out — at least 59% full-length transcript, or less than 34% of the exon-18-skipped transcript, for a variant to count as benign by function — are noticeably stricter than the generic ACMG/AMP guidance of over 30% full-length or under 70% non-functional. 校准后得出的阈值——全长转录本至少59%,或者外显子18跳跃转录本低于34%,才能在功能上被判定为良性——明显比ACMG/AMP通用指南中"全长转录本超过30%"或"无功能转录本低于70%"更为严格。 In plain terms: the generic threshold was too lenient for this exon, and using it uncritically would have called some variants benign that the functional data says are not. 说白了:通用阈值对这个外显子来说太宽松了,如果不加批判地直接套用,会把一些功能数据显示并非良性的变异误判为良性。 Reclassifying with the RNA evidence moved the numbers a lot: pathogenic classifications went from 28.6% to 31.7%, benign from 3.7% to 24.4%, while uncertain-significance calls dropped from 18.9% down to 8.5% — resolving roughly ten percent of the variant set out of limbo entirely. 用RNA证据重新分类后,数字变动很大:致病分类从28.6%上升到31.7%,良性分类从3.7%上升到24.4%,而意义未明的判定则从18.9%降到8.5%——相当于把大约10%的变异彻底从"悬而未决"的状态中解救出来。 Overall, mRNA data changed the classification of 34% of the 166 variants tested. 总体而言,mRNA数据改变了所测166个变异中34%的分类结果。 One more finding worth flagging: exon skipping itself was "less tolerated" than other splice outcomes — meaning which aberrant transcript you produce, not just how much normal transcript survives, matters for function. 还有一个值得注意的发现:外显子跳跃本身比其他剪接结果"耐受性更差"——也就是说,产生哪一种异常转录本,而不仅仅是正常转录本残留了多少,对功能同样重要。

JORDAN So where does this land for us? 那这对我们来说意味着什么?

ALEX One priority, clearly: Karen's CFTR variant-interpretation work. 很明确,对应一个优先事项:凯伦(Karen)的CFTR变异解读项目。 The transferable idea isn't the specific BRCA1 thresholds — it's the method: don't apply a generic, gene-agnostic RNA-evidence threshold to CFTR splice variants without checking whether it's actually calibrated for CFTR, the way ENIGMA checked it for exon 18 specifically. 可迁移的不是BRCA1那套具体阈值本身,而是这套方法:在把通用的、与基因无关的RNA证据阈值套用到CFTR剪接变异之前,先检查它是否真的针对CFTR做过校准,就像ENIGMA专门针对第18外显子所做的那样。 A concrete buildable artifact: a "CFTR splice-threshold calibrator" — pull together whatever published CFTR minigene, blood-RNA, or RT-PCR splicing-percentage data already exists, alongside any orthogonal CFTR functional readout such as forskolin-induced organoid swelling or patch-clamp chloride-conductance data, and fit the same kind of regression ENIGMA used to ask whether the generic 30%/70% thresholds hold for CFTR or need their own recalibration. 一个具体的可动手项目是"CFTR剪接阈值校准器"——把已发表的CFTR微基因、血液RNA或RT-PCR剪接百分比数据汇总起来,再配合任何正交的CFTR功能读数,比如福斯高林诱导的类器官膨胀实验或膜片钳氯电导数据,拟合出类似ENIGMA所用的回归模型,以判断通用的30%/70%阈值是否适用于CFTR,还是需要单独校准。 Input would be a literature-curated table of variant, percent full-length transcript, and functional readout; output is a calibrated threshold plus a flag for any variant whose classification would change under it. 输入是一张文献整理的表格,包含变异、全长转录本百分比和功能读数;输出则是校准后的阈值,以及标记出在新阈值下分类会发生变化的变异。 Call it a medium-difficulty build — the statistics are a straightforward regression, the real bottleneck is curating the input table from scattered literature. 难度中等——统计部分就是一个直接的回归模型,真正的瓶颈在于从零散的文献中整理出输入表格。

JORDAN What would we need to check before trusting any of this for CFTR? 在把这套方法用到CFTR之前,我们需要先核实哪些问题?

ALEX First, whether enough CFTR-specific splicing-plus-function data actually exists in the literature to support a calibration exercise like this at all — this paper had 166 variants to work with; CFTR might not have anywhere near that density of paired splicing-and-function data. 首先,文献中是否真的存在足够多的CFTR特异性剪接加功能数据,支撑起这样一次校准工作——这篇论文有166个变异可用,而CFTR目前配对的剪接加功能数据可能远远达不到这个密度。 Second, whether CFTR's own curation bodies have already published or are working on a CFTR-specific RNA-evidence threshold — that's a question for Karen and the CFTR2/ClinGen curation community, not something to assume either way. 其次,CFTR自己的分类机构是否已经发布过、或正在制定CFTR特异性的RNA证据阈值——这个问题应该去问凯伦以及CFTR2/ClinGen分类社区,而不能想当然地假设答案。 Third — and this is important — the specific functional assay does not transfer. 第三点也很重要——具体的功能检测方法本身是不能直接迁移的。 HDR is a BRCA1-specific biology; CFTR's function is chloride-channel gating, so the orthogonal ground-truth assay would have to be something CFTR-relevant, like electrophysiology or organoid swelling, not homologous-recombination repair.
