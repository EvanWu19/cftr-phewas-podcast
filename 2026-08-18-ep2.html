Ep2 (Track B) — yQTL Pipeline: A Reusable Pattern for QTL Discovery at Scale

Li et al. (2024, PLoS ONE) ship a Nextflow-based pipeline plus a companion R Shiny visualization app (github.com/montilab/yQTLpipeline) that automates the full QTL-discovery workflow — genotype format conversion, PC/GRM computation, association testing (GENESIS for related samples, MatrixeQTL for unrelated), and result merging/plotting — across many phenotypes and genotype files at once. The paper's real contribution isn't new statistics; it's orchestration and parallelization: a metabolomics use case (194 participants, 1,052 metabolites, 9.1M SNPs) that couldn't fit in memory on a single 32GB machine ran in 26 minutes via automatic phenotype-chunking, versus an estimated 90+ minutes sequential — a ~3.5-fold speedup that was also the difference between feasible and infeasible. The most transferable piece is the visualization layer: the Shiny app's input contract (trait name, QTL name, chromosome, position, p-value) is generic, so it doesn't require analysis to have come from this specific pipeline. The buildable artifact: port the four Shiny views — Manhattan, Miami, trait-QTL network, genotype-phenotype box plot — as a standalone tool that ingests any QTL/GWAS results table, decoupling visualization from whatever association method produces a future modifier-QTL analysis.

Plain-English glossary

  • GRM (genetic relationship matrix) — quantifies actual genomic relatedness between every pair of study participants, beyond pedigree records. （中文：遗传关系矩阵，在基因组层面量化研究中每对参与者之间的实际亲缘关系，超越家系记录本身。）
  • Genetic PCs — principal components computed from genome-wide genotype data, capturing population structure/ancestry as a confounder distinct from family relatedness. （中文：遗传主成分，从全基因组基因型数据计算得出，捕捉群体结构/祖源这一不同于家族相关性的混杂因素。）
  • Manhattan plot — a plot of one phenotype's association p-values across genomic position, points rising with significance. （中文：Manhattan图，展示单个表型的关联p值在基因组位置上的分布，点的高度随显著性上升。）
  • Miami plot — the two-phenotype version of a Manhattan plot: one trait plotted right-side-up, a second plotted upside-down below it, sharing the same position axis. （中文：Miami图，Manhattan图的双表型版本：一个性状正向绘制，另一个倒置绘制在下方，共享同一位置轴。）
  • QTL (quantitative trait locus) — a genomic region where genetic variation is statistically associated with variation in a measurable trait. （中文：数量性状位点，遗传变异与某可测量性状的变异存在统计关联的基因组区域。）

Full citation

Li M, et al. (2024). yQTL Pipeline: A structured computational workflow for large scale quantitative trait loci discovery and downstream visualization. PLoS ONE. PMID: 38833463 | PMC11149833 | DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0298501 — code: github.com/montilab/yQTLpipeline

Methods / tools to lift

  • Three-stage Nextflow split (Prepare.nf / Analysis.nf / Report.nf) — reduces storage/execution overhead by passing file paths as values between stages rather than copying full inputs.
  • Automatic phenotype-chunking — splits a large phenotype set into memory-feasible chunks for parallel execution, the mechanism that made the demonstrated use case runnable at all.
  • Generic Shiny visualization layer — Manhattan/Miami/trait-QTL-network/genotype-phenotype-boxplot views driven by a generic trait-QTL-chromosome-position-p-value input schema, decoupled from the upstream analysis engine.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves AI-tooling / career (registry-and-bioinformatics tool-development lens). MVP: port the Shiny app's four visualization views as a standalone tool accepting any QTL/GWAS results table in the generic schema, independent of which pipeline or association method produced it — a reusable portfolio piece rather than a one-off script. Difficulty: S–M.

What to verify first

  • The demonstrated use case (194 participants, 9.1M SNPs) is small by registry standards — chunking/parallelization behavior needs separate validation at larger scale before assuming the same memory-vs-runtime trade-off holds.
  • The paper doesn't clearly address how the genome-wide significance threshold should adjust as the number of tested phenotypes grows into the hundreds — a real multiple-testing gap.
  • Nextflow's default input-copying-to-work-directory behavior, even after this pipeline's storage-saving split, should be checked against whatever compute environment is actually available.

ALEX Track B today is a 2024 paper in PLoS ONE by Li and colleagues, "yQTL Pipeline: A structured computational workflow for large scale quantitative trait loci discovery and downstream visualization." 今天B轨道的论文来自2024年发表在《PLoS ONE》上的研究，作者是Li等人，题目是《yQTL Pipeline：面向大规模数量性状位点发现与下游可视化的结构化计算工作流》。 It ships real, public code — a Nextflow-based pipeline plus a companion R Shiny visualization app, both on GitHub under montilab slash yQTLpipeline — which is exactly the tool-development-with-a-repo profile this track is built around. 它附带了真实、公开的代码——一个基于Nextflow的流水线，加上一个配套的R Shiny可视化应用，两者都托管在GitHub上的montilab/yQTLpipeline仓库中——这正是这个轨道所聚焦的、带代码库的工具开发范式。 The one-sentence reason we're reading it: it's a concrete, reusable pattern for automating a multi-phenotype, multi-genotype-file genetic-association workflow at scale, with a visualization layer cleanly decoupled from the analysis engine underneath it. 我们读这篇论文的一句话理由是：它提供了一个具体、可复用的模式，用于自动化在多表型、多基因型文件条件下大规模运行遗传关联分析，并且下层的可视化模块与分析引擎干净地解耦。

JORDAN Before the pipeline details — GRM, genetic PCs, familial relatedness — walk me through why those specifically matter for this kind of analysis. 在讲流水线细节之前——GRM、遗传主成分、家族相关性——能不能讲讲为什么这些具体的东西在这类分析中很重要？

ALEX A genetic relationship matrix, or GRM, quantifies how related every pair of individuals in a study actually is at the genomic level, beyond whatever the pedigree records say. 遗传关系矩阵（GRM）在基因组层面量化研究中每一对个体之间的实际亲缘关系，而不仅仅依赖家系记录所声称的关系。 If you run a standard association test assuming everyone is independent when some participants are secretly siblings or cousins, you inflate your false-positive rate, because related people share both genotypes and unmeasured environmental or phenotypic similarities that look like signal but aren't. 如果你在运行标准关联检验时假设所有人都是独立的，而实际上有些参与者其实是兄弟姐妹或表亲关系，你就会拉高假阳性率，因为有亲缘关系的人不仅共享基因型，还共享未被测量的环境或表型相似性，这些看起来像信号，实际上并不是。 Genetic PCs, principal components computed from genome-wide genotype data, serve a related but distinct purpose: they capture population structure and ancestry, which is a different confounder from within-study family relatedness, and both typically get included as covariates or model terms. 遗传主成分（genetic PCs），是从全基因组基因型数据中计算出的主成分，起着相关但不同的作用：它们捕捉的是群体结构和祖源，这是与研究内家族相关性不同的另一种混杂因素，两者通常都作为协变量或模型项被纳入分析。 The tool this paper builds on for both, GENESIS, is well-established in GWAS work, but its own interface only takes one genotype file and one phenotype at a time, which becomes painfully inconvenient the moment you're testing hundreds of phenotypes against multiple chromosome-split genotype files. 本文所依赖的处理这两个问题的工具GENESIS，在GWAS研究中已经很成熟，但它自身的接口一次只能处理一个基因型文件和一个表型，一旦你需要针对数百个表型、对多个按染色体拆分的基因型文件进行检验，这就会变得非常不方便。

JORDAN So the motivation is really an orchestration problem, not a new statistical method. 所以说，动机其实是一个编排（orchestration）问题，而不是一种新的统计方法。

ALEX Exactly, and that's worth being precise about, because it shapes what this paper is and isn't contributing. 完全正确，这一点值得说清楚，因为它决定了这篇论文的贡献是什么、不是什么。 A full QTL discovery workflow isn't just the association test — it includes converting genotype files to the right format, extracting SNP missingness and allele-frequency information, computing genetic PCs and the GRM, running the test itself, and then merging and visualizing results across every phenotype and genotype file involved. 一个完整的QTL发现工作流并不仅仅是关联检验本身——它还包括将基因型文件转换为正确的格式、提取SNP缺失率和等位基因频率信息、计算遗传主成分和GRM、运行检验本身，以及在所涉及的每个表型和基因型文件之间合并与可视化结果。 Each of those steps traditionally runs as a separate command in a separate tool, which is slow, error-prone, and hard to reproduce exactly. 传统上，每一个步骤都作为不同工具中的独立命令运行，这既慢又容易出错，也很难精确复现。 The authors had already built a Nextflow-based single-phenotype pipeline for this before, called nf-gwas-pipeline, but that version could only handle one phenotype trait at a time — so the actual novel contribution of yQTL Pipeline is generalizing that earlier single-phenotype tool to genuinely large-scale, multi-phenotype, multi-genotype-file QTL discovery, with heavy parallelization built in from the start. 作者们之前已经为此构建过一个基于Nextflow的单表型流水线，叫做nf-gwas-pipeline，但那个版本一次只能处理一个表型性状——所以yQTL Pipeline真正的新贡献，是将那个更早的单表型工具推广到真正大规模、多表型、多基因型文件的QTL发现场景，并从一开始就内置了大量并行化处理。

JORDAN What's a Miami plot, since you mentioned it alongside Manhattan plots — is it just a fancier name for the same thing? 你提到了Miami图和Manhattan图放在一起——它只是同一种东西的一个更花哨的名字吗？

ALEX Not quite — a Manhattan plot shows one phenotype's association p-values across the genome, points scattered upward by significance at each genomic position. 不完全是——Manhattan图展示的是单个表型在整个基因组上的关联p值，各个基因组位置上的点根据显著性向上散布。 A Miami plot is the two-phenotype version: one phenotype's results plotted right-side-up on top, a second phenotype's results plotted upside-down on the bottom, sharing the same horizontal genomic-position axis, so you can visually compare where two different traits do or don't share significant loci without needing two separate figures. Miami图是双表型版本：一个表型的结果正向绘制在上方，另一个表型的结果倒置绘制在下方，两者共享同一条横向的基因组位置轴，这样你就可以直观比较两个不同性状在哪些位置共享或不共享显著位点，而不需要两张独立的图。 It's a small visualization trick, but a genuinely useful one whenever you're comparing a pair of related traits — which, for a modifier-QTL context, could mean comparing a lung-function phenotype against a growth or nutritional phenotype on the same plot. 这是一个不大的可视化技巧，但在比较一对相关性状时确实很有用——在修饰基因QTL的场景下，这可能意味着在同一张图上比较肺功能表型和生长或营养状况表型。

JORDAN Walk me through the actual pipeline structure. 讲讲流水线的具体结构。

ALEX It's split into three Nextflow components: Prepare.nf, Analysis.nf, and Report.nf, plus a stand-alone QC script called PreQC.nf. 它被拆分为三个Nextflow组件：Prepare.nf、Analysis.nf和Report.nf，另外还有一个独立的质控脚本PreQC.nf。 Prepare.nf handles preprocessing — converting VCF genotype files to GDS format, and computing genetic PCs and the GRM using PC-AiR and PC-Relate. Prepare.nf负责预处理——把VCF基因型文件转换为GDS格式，并使用PC-AiR和PC-Relate计算遗传主成分和GRM。 Analysis.nf runs the actual association test, in one of two modes depending on whether familial relatedness is present: workflow one uses GENESIS with a two-step procedure — first estimating a null model for the fixed effects of covariates, then testing each SNP with a linear mixed-effects model — and workflow two uses MatrixeQTL instead, for unrelated samples, offering linear, category, or interaction model options and running substantially faster since it doesn't need the mixed-model machinery. Analysis.nf执行实际的关联检验，根据是否存在家族相关性，分为两种模式之一：工作流一使用GENESIS的两步流程——先估计协变量固定效应的零模型，再用线性混合效应模型检验每个SNP；工作流二则改用MatrixeQTL，适用于无亲缘关系的样本，提供线性、分类或交互模型选项，并且由于不需要混合模型的计算开销，运行速度明显更快。 Report.nf merges everything and generates the plots. Report.nf合并所有结果并生成图表。 The clever part of the modular split is storage-driven: each Nextflow process by default copies all its input files into a work directory for reproducibility, which is expensive at genotype-data scale, so by splitting into three separate scripts, input files can be passed between them as file-path values rather than full copies, cutting the storage and execution footprint substantially. 这个模块化拆分中比较巧妙的一点，是出于存储考量：每个Nextflow进程默认会为了可复现性把所有输入文件复制进一个work目录，这在基因型数据规模下代价很高，所以通过拆分成三个独立的脚本，输入文件可以以文件路径值的形式在它们之间传递，而不是完整复制，从而大幅降低了存储和执行开销。

ALEX I'd push back a little on one design choice, though. 不过，我想对其中一个设计选择提出一点异议。 The paper doesn't spend much time on multiple-testing correction at the scale this pipeline is built for — running hundreds or thousands of phenotypes against millions of SNPs each is a genuinely enormous multiple-comparisons burden, and beyond citing the standard 5e-8 genome-wide significance threshold per individual test, there isn't a clear discussion of how that threshold should adjust as the number of phenotypes tested grows into the hundreds. 这篇论文在这个流水线所面向的规模下，对多重检验校正着墨不多——针对数百甚至数千个表型、每个都对数百万个SNP进行检验，这本身就是一个巨大的多重比较负担，除了引用标准的每次单独检验5e-8全基因组显著性阈值之外，论文并没有清楚讨论当检验的表型数量增长到数百个时，这个阈值应当如何相应调整。 That's a real gap to be aware of before trusting the pipeline's default significance calls at genuinely large phenotype counts. 在信任这个流水线在真正大量表型数目下给出的默认显著性判断之前，这是一个需要留意的真实缺口。

ALEX One more structural detail worth naming: there's also a stand-alone QC script, PreQC.nf, that runs independently of the main analysis workflows, and the whole thing is configured through a single configuration file listing input files and parameters, invoked with just three command lines total. 还有一个结构细节值得一提：有一个独立的质控脚本PreQC.nf，可以脱离主分析工作流单独运行，而整个流水线通过一个列出输入文件和参数的单一配置文件来配置，总共只需要三条命令行即可调用。 Outputs are organized into numbered folders — data and phenotype inputs, SNP information including allele frequency and missingness, individual per-phenotype-per-genotype-file results, a combined version of those, and a final results-summary folder with the merged QTL table plus every plot — which keeps a run's intermediate and final artifacts cleanly separated and easy to navigate after the fact. 输出被组织成带编号的文件夹——数据和表型输入、包含等位基因频率和缺失率的SNP信息、每个表型对每个基因型文件的单独结果、这些结果的合并版本，以及一个包含合并QTL表和所有图表的最终结果汇总文件夹——这使得一次运行的中间产物和最终产物能够清晰分离，事后也便于查阅。 The whole pipeline is released under GPL-3.0, runs on Linux and OS X, and the GitHub repo includes full configuration documentation — which matters for the AI-tooling lens specifically, since a tool that's genuinely easy for someone else to pick up and run is a different kind of contribution than a method that only the original authors can reproduce. 整个流水线以GPL-3.0协议发布，可在Linux和OS X上运行，GitHub仓库中包含完整的配置文档——这一点对AI工具化这个视角来说尤为重要，因为一个真正容易被别人拿来直接运行的工具，和一个只有原作者才能复现的方法，是两种不同性质的贡献。

JORDAN What does the pipeline actually give you for visualization, beyond the raw QTL result tables? 除了原始的QTL结果表格之外，这个流水线在可视化方面实际能提供什么？

ALEX This is arguably the most transferable part. 这大概是最具可迁移性的部分。 The R Shiny App lets a user upload the pipeline's RDS output — or any similarly-shaped data frame with phenotype trait names, QTL names, chromosomal coordinates, and p-values — and interactively generate Manhattan plots and Miami plots per phenotype, a trait-QTL network showing which traits share the same top loci across chromosomes, and genotype-phenotype box plots for any SNP that passed the significance threshold. R Shiny应用允许用户上传流水线的RDS输出——或者任何形状类似、包含表型名称、QTL名称、染色体坐标和p值的数据框——并交互式地生成每个表型的Manhattan图和Miami图、展示哪些表型在不同染色体上共享相同顶级位点的性状-QTL网络，以及针对任何通过显著性阈值的SNP绘制的基因型-表型箱线图。 The network view specifically solves a real visualization problem: a Manhattan plot can only show results for one phenotype at a time, so comparing QTL sharing across dozens or hundreds of phenotypes is otherwise unwieldy. 网络视图具体解决了一个真实的可视化问题：Manhattan图一次只能展示一个表型的结果，因此在几十甚至上百个表型之间比较QTL共享情况，若不借助这种视图会非常笨拙。 Critically, the Shiny app's input contract is generic — trait name, QTL name, chromosome, position, p-value — so it doesn't actually require the analysis to have come from this specific pipeline at all. 关键的是，这个Shiny应用的输入契约是通用的——性状名、QTL名、染色体、位置、p值——所以它其实并不要求分析必须来自这个具体的流水线。

JORDAN Does the metabolomics use case in the paper actually demonstrate the parallelization payoff, or is it mostly a correctness demo? 论文里的代谢组学用例，是真的展示了并行化的收益，还是主要只是一个正确性演示？

ALEX Both, and the numbers are specific. 两者都是，而且数字很具体。 They applied it to paired metabolomics and genotype data from the New England Centenarians Study, 194 participants, using age, gender, and years of education as covariates, 1,052 metabolites with under 20% missingness that were log-transformed, and 9.1 million SNPs. 他们把这个流水线应用到新英格兰百岁老人研究（New England Centenarians Study）的配对代谢组学和基因型数据上，共194名参与者，用年龄、性别和受教育年限作为协变量，1,052种缺失率低于20%并经过对数转换的代谢物，以及910万个SNP。 Since participants weren't related, this ran workflow two, MatrixeQTL's linear model. 由于参与者之间没有亲缘关系，这次运行使用的是工作流二，即MatrixeQTL的线性模型。 At a relaxed p-value threshold of 1e-3, every one of the 1,052 metabolites had at least one mQTL; at genome-wide significance, p less than 5e-8, that narrowed to 312 metabolites, yielding 14,983 mQTLs across 11,931 unique SNPs, with 3,052 of those SNPs shared as mQTLs by at least two different metabolites. 在1e-3的宽松p值阈值下，1,052种代谢物中的每一种都至少有一个mQTL；而在全基因组显著性阈值（p小于5e-8）下，这个数字缩小到312种代谢物，产生了14,983个mQTL，涉及11,931个独特的SNP，其中3,052个SNP作为mQTL被至少两种不同的代谢物共享。 On the parallelization payoff specifically: the full dataset couldn't even run at once on a 32GB-memory machine, so the pipeline's automatic phenotype-chunking wasn't just a speed optimization, it was what made the analysis feasible at all. 具体到并行化的收益：完整的数据集甚至无法在一台32GB内存的机器上一次性运行，所以流水线的自动表型分块功能不仅仅是一种速度优化，而是让这项分析在根本上得以进行的前提。 Running everything sequentially would have taken over 90 minutes; with parallelization across compute nodes ranging from 4 to 32GB, tailored per step, total runtime dropped to 26 minutes — a roughly 3.5-fold speedup — and the authors note the gain would be substantially larger still on bigger datasets or when modeling familial relatedness. 若按顺序运行，总耗时将超过90分钟；而在4到32GB不等、按步骤定制资源的计算节点上并行运行后，总运行时间降到了26分钟——大约3.5倍的加速——作者指出，在更大的数据集上，或者在需要建模家族相关性的情况下，这个收益还会显著更大。

JORDAN So does this land anywhere specific for us, or is it mostly a nice engineering pattern to admire? 那这对我们来说落在哪个具体的地方，还是说这主要只是一个值得欣赏的不错的工程模式？
