Ep2 (Track B) — CUP-AI-Dx: cross-platform-validated RNA classifier

Zhao Y, Pan Z, Namburi S, Pattison A, Posner A, Balachander S, Paisie CA, Reddi HV, Rueter J, Gill AJ, Fox S, Raghav KPS, Flynn WF, Tothill RW, Li S, Karuturi RKM, George J (2020). CUP-AI-Dx: A tool for inferring cancer tissue of origin and molecular subtype using RNA gene-expression data and artificial intelligence. EBioMedicine.

A deliberate left-field Track B pick: an open-source 1D-Inception convolutional network that infers cancer tissue of origin from RNA-seq gene expression, trained on 18,217 primary tumors across 32 types pooled from TCGA and ICGC, with genes fed in ordered by chromosomal coordinate. Cross-validated accuracy reaches 98.54%, but the paper's real value for Runyu is what happens next: external validation on two independent, differently-processed clinical FFPE datasets from two institutions, where accuracy drops to 86.96% and 72.46% — a concrete, quantified demonstration of the gap between same-source cross-validation and true cross-platform external validation. That gap-reporting pattern, not the cancer biology, is the transferable idea: train on one source, validate hard on a genuinely different one, and report the drop as the headline number. The buildable artifact is a reusable "train-here, validate-there" harness applying the same discipline to any registry-style classification task.

Plain-English glossary

  • Cancer of unknown primary (CUP) — Metastatic cancer where the original tumor site can't be identified despite a full workup; about 3–5% of cancer diagnoses.（中文:原发灶不明癌,即发生转移但找不到原发肿瘤部位的癌症。）
  • RNA-seq / transcriptional profiling — Sequencing that measures how actively each gene is being expressed, rather than reading the DNA sequence itself.（中文:RNA测序/转录组分析,衡量基因表达活跃程度而非DNA序列本身。）
  • TPM (transcripts per million) — A normalized gene-expression unit enabling comparison across samples run on different machines or sequencing depths.（中文:每百万转录本数,一种标准化的基因表达量单位。）
  • FFPE (formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded) — The standard tissue-preservation method for archived biopsies; convenient for long-term storage but chemically degrades RNA relative to fresh-frozen tissue.（中文:福尔马林固定石蜡包埋组织,常规病理保存方式,但会使RNA降解。）
  • DNA methylation — Chemical marks on DNA that don't change the sequence but affect which genes are switched on; carries a tissue-specific signature used by comparator tool EPICUP.（中文:DNA甲基化,不改变序列但影响基因开关状态的化学标记。）
  • Molecular subtype — Finer-grained categories within a cancer type (e.g. breast cancer's basal/luminal-A/luminal-B) that guide treatment intensity.（中文:分子亚型,某一癌症类型内部更细的分类,用于指导治疗强度。）
  • cBioPortal — A public cancer-genomics portal providing curated clinical and molecular annotations layered on top of raw TCGA data.（中文:一个公开的癌症基因组学门户网站,提供整理过的临床与分子注释。）

Full citation

Zhao Y, Pan Z, Namburi S, et al. (2020). CUP-AI-Dx: A tool for inferring cancer tissue of origin and molecular subtype using RNA gene-expression data and artificial intelligence. EBioMedicine. PMID: 33039710 · PMC: PMC7553237 · DOI: 10.1016/j.ebiom.2020.103030

Methods / tools to lift

  • 1D-Inception CNN — parallel multi-kernel-size 1D convolutions over a gene-expression vector, benchmarked against a plain 1D-CNN and a 1D-ResNet.
  • Chromosomal-coordinate feature ordering — arranging selected genes along the input vector by genomic position so a 1D convolution can pick up locally co-regulated signal.
  • Differential-expression feature selection — top-40-per-class genes by median expression separation, merged into a shared cross-class feature set.
  • UMAP embedding — used purely for exploratory visualization of whether clusters track known biology, as a sanity check before trusting classifier output.
  • Cross-platform external validation design — train on RNA-seq from two consortia, validate on independent FFPE data from two separate institutions rather than a random hold-out split.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority served: AI-tooling / career. The transferable move is the cross-platform validation discipline itself, not the cancer classification task.

Buildable MVP (tool sketch): A reusable "train-here, validate-there" harness — train a small classifier (gradient boosting or simple 1D-CNN) on one public transcriptomic or tabular registry-style dataset with meaningfully ordered features, then validate on a second, differently-sourced dataset, reporting the accuracy drop between same-source cross-validation and cross-source external validation as the headline metric. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • How much of the 98.54% → 86.96%/72.46% accuracy gap is genuine biological platform difference versus batch effects or gene-panel coverage mismatch between training features and what the clinical labs measured.
  • Whether performance on actual CUP patients (unknown ground truth) would match validation on known-origin proxy samples — the paper never tests real CUP cases.
  • Whether the differential-expression feature-selection step, run on the same labels later cross-validated, introduces information leakage beyond what the FFPE external test already exposes.

ALEX: CUP, cancer of unknown primary, is metastatic cancer where doctors can't identify the original tumor site despite a full diagnostic workup — it's about 3 to 5 percent of all cancer diagnoses, and because so much of cancer treatment is organized by tissue of origin, not knowing it leaves patients on a generic, less effective regimen. CUP,即原发灶不明癌(cancer of unknown primary),指的是已经发生转移、但即便做了完整的诊断检查也无法确定原发肿瘤部位的癌症——大约占所有癌症诊断的3%到5%,而由于癌症治疗方案很大程度上是按照组织来源来制定的,找不到原发灶就意味着患者只能接受通用、效果较差的治疗方案。 The paper's method infers that tissue of origin from RNA-seq — transcriptional profiling, meaning it measures how actively every gene is being expressed, not the DNA sequence itself. 论文所用的方法是从RNA测序(RNA-seq)数据推断组织来源——即转录组分析(transcriptional profiling),衡量的是每个基因的表达活跃程度,而不是DNA序列本身。 Expression is reported in TPM, transcripts per million, a normalized unit that lets you compare gene activity across samples run on different machines or with different sequencing depth. 表达量以TPM(transcripts per million,每百万转录本数)为单位报告,这是一种标准化单位,便于在不同机器、不同测序深度下运行的样本之间比较基因活性。 And a chunk of their validation data comes from FFPE samples — formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded tissue, the standard way pathology labs preserve biopsies for years, which is convenient for archives but chemically degrades RNA compared to fresh-frozen tissue. 他们的一部分验证数据来自FFPE样本——福尔马林固定石蜡包埋组织(formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded,FFPE),这是病理实验室长期保存活检样本的标准方式,便于长期存档,但相比新鲜冷冻组织,其中的RNA会发生化学降解。 That degradation is exactly the kind of platform mismatch we want to watch for. 而这种降解正是我们需要重点关注的一种平台差异问题。

ALEX The clinical case for this is straightforward: knowing tissue of origin is fundamental to treatment, and CUP patients without that information have historically poor survival outcomes. 这项研究的临床出发点很直接:知道组织来源对治疗方案至关重要,而缺乏这一信息的CUP患者历来生存结局较差。 Before RNA-based approaches, the leading comparator method was EPICUP, a DNA methylation-based classifier — methylation being a layer of chemical marks on DNA that doesn't change the sequence but does change which genes get switched on, and which turns out to carry a strong tissue-specific signature. 在基于RNA的方法出现之前,主流的对比方法是EPICUP,一种基于DNA甲基化(DNA methylation)的分类器——甲基化是DNA上的一层化学标记,不改变序列本身,但会影响基因的开关状态,而这种标记恰好带有很强的组织特异性信号。 The authors picked RNA-seq instead for a practical reason: it's a common, accessible genomics platform that many more labs already run, compared to specialized methylation arrays. 作者选择RNA测序而非甲基化的原因很实际:相比专门的甲基化芯片,RNA测序是一个更常见、更易获取的基因组学平台,已经有更多实验室在使用。 They trained on 18,217 primary tumor samples spanning 32 cancer types, pooled from two large pan-cancer consortia — The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) and the International Cancer Genome Consortium (ICGC) — and, notably, the training set contained zero metastatic samples: the model only ever saw primary tumors during training. 他们的训练数据来自两大泛癌症联盟——癌症基因组图谱(The Cancer Genome Atlas,TCGA)和国际癌症基因组联盟(International Cancer Genome Consortium,ICGC)——共汇集了18217个原发肿瘤样本,覆盖32种癌症类型,而且值得注意的是,训练集中不含任何转移瘤样本:模型在训练阶段只见过原发肿瘤。 That's a deliberate design choice: if the model works, it means whatever transcriptional identity marks a tissue of origin survives the trip from primary tumor to distant metastasis. 这是一个刻意的设计选择:如果模型有效,就说明标记组织来源的转录组"身份特征",在肿瘤从原发部位转移到远处之后依然能够保留下来。

JORDAN That's a strong assumption. 这是一个相当大胆的假设。 ALEX: It is, and it's actually the central premise behind every tissue-of-origin diagnostic, whether it's immunohistochemistry staining under a microscope or a molecular classifier like this one — that a metastasis retains enough of its parent tissue's identity to be recognized. 确实如此,而这也正是所有组织来源诊断方法背后的核心假设——无论是显微镜下的免疫组化染色,还是像这样的分子分类器——都假定转移灶依然保留了足够多来自其原发组织的"身份特征",从而可以被识别出来。 The paper is upfront that this doesn't always hold; some tumors are known to drift into unusual transcriptional states that don't cluster with their origin at all. 论文也坦率承认这一假设并非总是成立;已知有些肿瘤会发生转录组状态的漂移,完全不与其原发组织聚在一起。

JORDAN How's the model actually built? 模型具体是怎么搭建的? ALEX: They compare three CNN architectures head to head on the TCGA-only data: a 1D convolutional network, a Residual Net adapted to 1D, and their proposed 1D-Inception model, which runs multiple convolutional kernels of different sizes in parallel over the gene-expression vector rather than picking one kernel size upfront. 他们在仅使用TCGA数据的情况下,对三种CNN架构做了直接对比:一维卷积神经网络(1D-CNN)、改造为一维版本的残差网络(ResNet),以及他们提出的1D-Inception模型——后者在基因表达向量上并行运行多个不同尺寸的卷积核,而不是预先固定一种卷积核尺寸。 Instead of feeding in all roughly 20,000 measured genes, they do supervised feature selection first: the 40 most differentially expressed genes per cancer type, ranked by how far each gene's median expression sits from its expression in every other class, then merged across all 32 classes into a shared feature set — 791 genes for the Inception and plain 1D-CNN models. 他们没有直接输入全部约2万个测得的基因,而是先做有监督的特征选择:针对每种癌症类型,挑出中位表达量与其它类别差异最大的40个基因,再把32个类别的结果合并成一个共享特征集——用于Inception和普通1D-CNN模型的最终基因数是791个。 Critically, those genes are then ordered along the input vector by their chromosomal coordinate, not randomly or alphabetically, letting a 1D convolution pick up on locally co-regulated genes the way it would pick up on a spatial pattern in an image. 关键的一点是,这些基因随后是按照染色体坐标(chromosomal coordinate)顺序排列进输入向量的,而不是随机或按字母顺序排列,这样一维卷积就能像在图像中识别空间模式一样,捕捉到局部共调控的基因关系。

ALEX If I'm pushing back on anything here, it's the feature-selection step. 如果要挑一个可以质疑的地方,那就是这个特征选择步骤。 Differentially-expressed-gene selection is done on the same TCGA/ICGC training data the model is then cross-validated on, which is standard practice but still means the "which 791 genes matter" decision was made with knowledge of the very labels being predicted — worth keeping in mind before assuming the gene list would transfer unchanged to a new cancer type or population. 差异表达基因的筛选是在同一批用于交叉验证的TCGA/ICGC训练数据上完成的,这是标准做法,但也意味着"这791个基因重要"这个决定,本身就是在已知待预测标签的情况下做出的——在假设这份基因列表能原封不动地迁移到新的癌症类型或人群之前,这一点值得留意。

ALEX: They embedded every training sample into a 2D map using UMAP — a dimensionality-reduction technique — purely to visualize structure, and the clusters that emerged lined up with real biology in a way that's a nice sanity check on the whole approach. 他们用UMAP(一种降维技术)把每个训练样本投影到二维平面上,纯粹是为了可视化数据结构,而由此形成的聚类与真实的生物学高度吻合,这对整套方法来说算是一个不错的合理性检验。 Some cancers formed sharp, isolated clusters by organ — brain, liver, kidney — while others grouped by histology instead of organ, like melanomas clustering with squamous cell cancers from completely different body sites, because they share cell-type features that apparently matter more to the transcriptome than anatomical location does. 有些癌症按器官形成了清晰独立的簇——脑、肝、肾——而另一些则不是按器官,而是按组织学类型聚类,比如黑色素瘤会与来自完全不同身体部位的鳞状细胞癌聚在一起,因为它们共享某些细胞类型特征,而这些特征对转录组的影响似乎比解剖位置更大。 That's exactly the kind of pattern that explains, in advance, which misclassifications you should expect the model to make. 而这正是那种能提前解释——模型接下来会在哪些地方犯错——的规律。
