Show notes — Deciphering gain-of-function from loss-of-function variants with AlphaMissense (PIEZO1 case study)

Blurb. Pillai et al. (2026, Biochemistry and Biophysics Reports) use PIEZO1 — a mechanosensitive ion channel where loss-of-function variants cause lymphatic dysplasia and gain-of-function variants cause hereditary xerocytosis — as a natural experiment to ask whether today's pathogenicity predictors can tell the two mechanisms apart. Across 75 clinically annotated missense variants, AlphaMissense, CADD, and EVE all flag loss-of-function variants cleanly but leave gain-of-function variants stuck in the "ambiguous" band (AM mean 0.52). The protein language model ESM-1b is the exception: it scores both classes as pathogenic, apparently because it detects evolutionarily unusual residues even when the fold isn't destabilized. The authors sketch a weighted ensemble (code on GitHub) that beats any single model on 118 ClinVar-labeled PIEZO1 variants. Where it lands: the Karen / CFTR variant-interpretation move. Buildable artifact: a CFTR variant discordance scanner that flags variants where predictors disagree on direction or where AM parks a variant in its uncertain 0.34–0.56 band, cross-referenced to CFTR2 functional class.

Plain-English glossary

  • Gain-of-function (GoF) — a variant that makes the protein overactive or gives it a new behavior; in an ion channel, often slowed closing so ions keep flowing.（中文：功能获得型变异——让蛋白过度活跃或产生新行为的变异；在离子通道中常表现为关闭变慢、离子持续流入。）
  • Gating kinetics — how fast an ion channel opens and closes; GoF channel variants often alter this without breaking the protein's shape.（中文：门控动力学——离子通道开关的快慢；GoF 通道变异常改变它，却不破坏蛋白形状。）
  • Homotrimer — a protein assembled from three identical subunits; PIEZO1 forms a three-bladed propeller.（中文：同源三聚体——由三个相同亚基组装成的蛋白；PIEZO1 形成三叶螺旋桨。）
  • Hereditary xerocytosis (HX) — a red-blood-cell disorder caused by GoF PIEZO1 variants; cells leak cations and dehydrate.（中文：遗传性干瘪红细胞增多症——由 GoF PIEZO1 变异引起的红细胞疾病，细胞渗漏阳离子并脱水。）
  • Loss-of-function (LoF) — a variant that reduces or abolishes the protein's normal job; the classic signature current predictors detect well.（中文：功能缺失型变异——削弱或消除蛋白正常功能的变异；当前预测器擅长检测的经典特征。）
  • Lymphatic dysplasia (LD) — a developmental lymphatic-system disorder caused by LoF PIEZO1 variants.（中文：淋巴发育不良——由 LoF PIEZO1 变异引起的淋巴系统发育障碍。）
  • Mechanosensitive ion channel — a channel that opens in response to physical force (stretch, shear) on the membrane.（中文：机械敏感型离子通道——对细胞膜上的物理力（拉伸、剪切）作出反应而打开的通道。）
  • Missense variant — a DNA change that swaps one amino acid for another in the protein.（中文：错义变异——把蛋白中一个氨基酸替换成另一个的 DNA 变异。）
  • AlphaMissense (AM) — DeepMind's supervised, AlphaFold-structure-based pathogenicity predictor; outputs 0–1.（中文：DeepMind 基于 AlphaFold 结构的监督式致病性预测器，输出 0–1 评分。）
  • CADD — combines many genomic annotations into one deleteriousness score.（中文：把多种基因组注释整合成单一有害性评分的方法。）
  • EVE — a deep generative model of evolutionary sequences that scores variant effects unsupervised.（中文：变异效应的进化模型，一种从进化序列无监督评分变异效应的深度生成模型。）
  • ESM-1b — a protein language model trained on ~250M sequences; scores variants zero-shot via a log-likelihood ratio.（中文：在约 2.5 亿条序列上训练的蛋白质语言模型，通过对数似然比零样本评分变异。）
  • PremPS — a machine-learning predictor of folding free-energy change upon mutation (protein stability).（中文：预测突变导致折叠自由能变化（蛋白稳定性）的机器学习工具。）
  • cryo-EM — cryo-electron microscopy; an experimental method for solving protein 3D structures.（中文：冷冻电镜——一种解析蛋白三维结构的实验方法。）

Full citation. Pillai J, Sridhar A, Sung K, Shi L, Wu C. Methods / tools to lift

  • Multi-predictor scoring panel: AlphaMissense (0.34/0.56 cutoffs), ESM-1b (−LLR 7/8 cutoffs), CADD (15 cutoff), EVE — score the same variant set with all four and compare.
  • AlphaFold2 structure as a stand-in when no complete experimental structure exists; validate against partial cryo-EM structures by RMSD first.
  • PremPS for ΔΔG (stability change) to test whether pathogenicity tracks destabilization.
  • Weighted ensemble over predictors, validated on ClinVar labels, compared by ROC-AUC.

What to verify first

  • Sample imbalance: only 6 LoF vs 56 GoF variants — the LD/HX contrast rests on a tiny denominator.
  • Circularity risk: ensemble validated on ClinVar, which is noisy and partly derived from the same predictors.
  • Generalization: the "GoF → ambiguous" story is specific to a channel where GoF = altered gating; confirm CFTR gating variants (e.g., G551D-type) behave the same under these scores before generalizing.
  • ΔΔG probe compares one model's output to another's, not ground truth.

ALEX Today we're reading a 2026 paper from Joshua Pillai and colleagues at UC San Diego, published in Biochemistry and Biophysics Reports. 今天我们要读的是 Joshua Pillai 及其加州大学圣地亚哥分校同事 2026 年发表在《Biochemistry and Biophysics Reports》上的一篇论文。 The title is "Deciphering gain-of-function from loss-of-function variants with AlphaMissense: A case study with the mechanosensitive PIEZO1 ion channel protein." 标题是《用 AlphaMissense 区分功能获得型与功能缺失型变异：以机械敏感型 PIEZO1 离子通道蛋白为例的案例研究》。 This is a Track A episode about variant interpretation, and it lands squarely on the question Karen cares about: can a computational tool tell us not just whether a variant is bad, but in which direction it breaks the protein. 这是一期属于 Track A 的节目，主题是变异解读，它正好落在 Karen 关心的问题上：一个计算工具能否告诉我们，一个变异不仅是"坏的"，而且它以哪种方向破坏了蛋白质。 We're reading it because that direction question is exactly where today's best predictors quietly fail. 我们读它，是因为"方向"这个问题恰恰是当今最好的预测器悄悄失手的地方。

JORDAN Before we go further, I need the vocabulary. 在我们继续之前，我需要先弄清词汇。 What do "gain-of-function" and "loss-of-function" actually mean at the protein level? "功能获得型"（gain-of-function）和"功能缺失型"（loss-of-function）在蛋白质层面到底是什么意思？ ALEX: Good place to start. 这是个很好的起点。 A loss-of-function variant, LoF, breaks the protein so it does less of its job or none of it. 功能缺失型变异（LoF）会破坏蛋白质，使它的功能变弱或完全丧失。 For an ion channel like PIEZO1, that might mean the channel no longer opens properly, so ions that should flow don't. 对于像 PIEZO1 这样的离子通道，这可能意味着通道不再正常打开，本该流动的离子无法通过。 A gain-of-function variant, GoF, does the opposite: it makes the protein do too much, or do something new. 功能获得型变异（GoF）则相反：它让蛋白质做得过头，或做出新的动作。 In PIEZO1, a classic GoF effect is that the channel stays open too long — it's slow to shut off, so cations keep leaking in. 在 PIEZO1 中，一个典型的 GoF 效应是通道保持开放太久——它关闭得很慢，因此阳离子持续渗入。 JORDAN: And why is PIEZO1 a smart test case? 那为什么 PIEZO1 是一个聪明的测试对象？ ALEX: Because the same gene produces both flavors in humans, tied to two different diseases. 因为同一个基因在人体内产生这两种类型，各自对应两种不同的疾病。 Loss-of-function PIEZO1 causes lymphatic dysplasia, LD — a developmental problem with the lymphatic system. 功能缺失型 PIEZO1 会导致淋巴发育不良（lymphatic dysplasia，LD）——一种淋巴系统的发育问题。 Gain-of-function PIEZO1 causes hereditary xerocytosis, HX — a red-blood-cell disorder where the cells leak and dehydrate. 功能获得型 PIEZO1 会导致遗传性干瘪红细胞增多症（hereditary xerocytosis，HX）——一种红细胞渗漏并脱水的疾病。 So you have one protein, two opposite mechanisms, both well documented. 于是你就有了一个蛋白、两种相反的机制，而且都有充分的文献记录。 That's a rare natural experiment for testing predictors. 这对测试预测器来说是一个罕见的天然实验。

JORDAN And PIEZO1 itself — it's a mechanical sensor? PIEZO1 本身——它是一个机械传感器？ ALEX: Right. 没错。 PIEZO1 is a mechanosensitive ion channel — it opens in response to physical force on the membrane, like stretch or shear. PIEZO1 是一种机械敏感型离子通道——它对细胞膜上的物理力（如拉伸或剪切）作出反应而打开。 It's huge, over 2,500 amino acids, and it assembles as a three-bladed propeller, a homotrimer. 它非常庞大，超过 2500 个氨基酸，组装成一个三叶螺旋桨的形状，即同源三聚体（homotrimer）。 It regulates blood pressure, vascular development, and red-cell volume, among other things. 它调控血压、血管发育和红细胞体积等多种功能。 The size is important for our story: it's so big and flexible that experimental structures never captured all of it. 它的体积对我们的故事很重要：它太大、太柔性，以至于实验结构从未能完整捕捉它。 That gap is where AlphaFold comes in. 而这个空缺正是 AlphaFold 登场的地方。

JORDAN So set up the problem the authors are attacking. 那么请说明作者要攻克的问题。 ALEX: For a decade, the field poured effort into predicting whether a missense variant is pathogenic. 十年来，这个领域投入大量精力去预测一个错义变异（missense variant，即改变单个氨基酸的变异）是否致病。 A missense variant swaps one amino acid for another. 错义变异会把一个氨基酸替换成另一个。 The dominant tools — AlphaMissense, CADD, EVE, ESM — are all very good at one thing: flagging variants that destabilize or disrupt a protein, which is the signature of loss-of-function. 主流工具——AlphaMissense、CADD、EVE、ESM——都非常擅长一件事：标记出会破坏蛋白质稳定性或结构的变异，而这正是功能缺失的特征。 JORDAN: Let me get those names straight. 让我把这些名字弄清楚。 ALEX: AlphaMissense, or AM, is DeepMind's supervised model built on AlphaFold structure — it outputs a pathogenicity score from zero to one. AlphaMissense（简称 AM）是 DeepMind 基于 AlphaFold 结构构建的监督式模型——它输出一个从 0 到 1 的致病性评分。 CADD (综合注释依赖性缺失评分，一种整合多种基因组注释来给变异有害性打分的方法) combines many genomic annotations into a single deleteriousness score. CADD 把许多基因组注释整合成一个单一的有害性评分。 EVE (变异效应的进化模型，一种从进化序列中无监督学习变异效应的深度生成模型) is a deep generative model trained on evolutionary sequences. EVE 是一个在进化序列上训练的深度生成模型。 And ESM-1b (进化尺度建模模型，一种在数亿蛋白序列上训练的蛋白质语言模型) is a protein language model trained on roughly 250 million protein sequences, with no supervision on disease labels at all. 而 ESM-1b 是一个在约 2.5 亿条蛋白序列上训练的蛋白质语言模型，完全没有用疾病标签进行监督。 JORDAN: So what's the gap? 那么空缺在哪里？ ALEX: The gap is that almost nobody has asked whether these tools can tell GoF from LoF. 空缺在于，几乎没有人问过这些工具能否区分 GoF 和 LoF。 They were built and benchmarked on the pathogenic-versus-benign question. 它们是围绕"致病还是良性"这个问题构建和评测的。 But "this variant is bad" and "this variant makes the channel hyperactive" are different claims. 但"这个变异是坏的"和"这个变异让通道过度活跃"是两种不同的论断。 The clinical world increasingly needs the second one — the mechanism, not just the verdict. 临床世界越来越需要第二种——机制，而不仅仅是判决。

ALEX Here's what they built. 下面是他们所做的。 They took 75 unique missense variants in PIEZO1 with reported clinical phenotypes, drawn from a prior compilation. 他们选取了 75 个已报告临床表型的 PIEZO1 错义变异，来自之前的一份汇编。 Fifty-six of those are gain-of-function HX variants and six are loss-of-function LD variants, with a handful in other rarer phenotypes. 其中 56 个是功能获得型的 HX 变异，6 个是功能缺失型的 LD 变异，还有少数属于其他更罕见的表型。 JORDAN: That's a very lopsided set — 56 versus 6. 这是个非常不平衡的集合——56 对 6。 ALEX: It is, and that imbalance is the single biggest caveat in the paper; hold that thought. 是的，这个不平衡是全文最大的注意点；先记住这一点。 They then scored every variant with AM, ESM-1b, CADD, and EVE, using each tool's standard cutoffs. 然后他们用 AM、ESM-1b、CADD 和 EVE 给每个变异打分，采用每个工具的标准阈值。 For AM, above 0.56 is pathogenic, below 0.34 is benign, and the zone between is "ambiguous." 对 AM 来说，高于 0.56 为致病，低于 0.34 为良性，中间区间为"不确定"。 For the structure work, they used the AlphaFold2 predicted structure of PIEZO1 because no complete experimental structure exists. 在结构分析上，他们使用了 PIEZO1 的 AlphaFold2 预测结构，因为不存在完整的实验结构。 They first checked that AF2 model against eight partial cryo-EM structures. 他们先把这个 AF2 模型与八个部分解析的冷冻电镜（cryo-EM）结构做了比对。 The mean structural deviation was about 4.35 Ångström — reasonable agreement for a protein this large and flexible. 平均结构偏差约为 4.35 埃（Ångström）——对这么大、这么柔性的蛋白来说是合理的吻合度。 JORDAN: Where would you push back on the design? 你会在设计上对哪一点提出质疑？ ALEX: On the stability angle. 在稳定性这一点上。 They computed the change in folding free energy for each mutation using PremPS, a machine-learning stability predictor, to ask whether pathogenicity tracks destabilization. 他们用 PremPS（一个机器学习稳定性预测器）计算每个突变的折叠自由能变化，来考察致病性是否与去稳定化相关。 That's a sensible probe, but stacking one predictor's output against another predictor's output means you're partly measuring how two models agree, not ground truth. 这是一个合理的探针，但把一个预测器的输出叠加到另一个预测器的输出上，意味着你部分地在衡量两个模型是否一致，而不是真实基准。 They're upfront about it, but it's worth flagging. 他们对此很坦诚，但值得指出。

ALEX Now the results, and this is the payoff. 现在说结果，这是全文的核心收获。 On the loss-of-function LD variants, every tool did well — high pathogenicity scores, correct calls. 对功能缺失型的 LD 变异，每个工具都表现良好——高致病性评分，判断正确。 AM gave LD variants a mean score of 0.77, comfortably in the pathogenic range. AM 给 LD 变异的平均评分为 0.77，稳稳落在致病区间。 But on the gain-of-function HX variants, AM's mean score was 0.52 — sitting right inside the ambiguous zone. 但对功能获得型的 HX 变异，AM 的平均评分为 0.52——正好落在"不确定"区间内。 JORDAN: So AlphaMissense basically shrugs at the gain-of-function variants. 也就是说，AlphaMissense 对功能获得型变异基本上是"耸耸肩"。 ALEX: Essentially yes. 基本如此。 Seven HX variants landed as outright uncertain, and many more clustered near the threshold. 有七个 HX 变异被判为完全不确定，还有更多聚集在阈值附近。 The exception was ESM-1b, the protein language model. 例外是 ESM-1b，那个蛋白质语言模型。 It scored both classes above its pathogenic threshold: LD variants at a mean of 12.3 and HX at 9.65 on its log-likelihood-ratio scale, both statistically above the cutoff of eight. 它把两类都评在其致病阈值之上：LD 变异平均 12.3，HX 平均 9.65（在其对数似然比标度上），两者都在统计上高于阈值 8。 JORDAN: Why would the language model catch the gain-of-function ones when the structure model doesn't? 为什么语言模型能捕捉到功能获得型的变异，而结构模型却不能？ ALEX: This is the key insight. 这是关键洞见。 AM leans heavily on structural disruption — does the mutation break the fold? AM 严重依赖结构破坏——这个突变会不会破坏折叠？ Gain-of-function variants often don't break the fold at all; they subtly change gating kinetics, how fast the channel opens and closes. 而功能获得型变异往往根本不破坏折叠；它们微妙地改变门控动力学（gating kinetics），即通道开关的快慢。 So a structure-first model sees "nothing broken" and stays quiet. 于是一个结构优先的模型看到"没什么坏掉"，就保持沉默。 ESM-1b instead learned statistical regularities across hundreds of millions of sequences, and it flags residues that look evolutionarily unusual — even if they don't destabilize anything. ESM-1b 则从数亿条序列中学到了统计规律，它会标记出在进化上看起来异常的残基——即使它们并不破坏稳定性。 A gain-of-function mutation at a critical gating motif reads as "evolutionarily weird," and the language model notices. 一个位于关键门控基序的功能获得型突变读起来就是"进化上的怪异"，而语言模型注意到了它。 JORDAN: But there's a trade-off, right? 但这里有权衡，对吧？ ALEX: There is. 有的。 ESM-1b was actually less sensitive than AM on some of the loss-of-function variants. 在某些功能缺失型变异上，ESM-1b 的敏感度其实低于 AM。 So neither tool dominates — they see different things. 所以没有哪个工具占绝对优势——它们看到的是不同的东西。 That's why the authors sketch a weighted ensemble that combines predictors, validated on 118 ClinVar-classified PIEZO1 variants, and report it outperforming any single model on ROC-AUC. 这就是为什么作者勾勒出一个加权集成（ensemble）模型，把多个预测器组合起来，在 118 个 ClinVar 已分类的 PIEZO1 变异上验证，并报告其 ROC-AUC 优于任何单一模型。 The ensemble code is on their GitHub. 集成代码在他们的 GitHub 上。
