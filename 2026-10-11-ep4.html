Composite anthropometric data quality index for children under the age of 5 on the Brazilian National Food and Nutrition Surveillance System, 2019-2021

Oliveira-Santos and colleagues (Population Health Metrics, 2025) build a municipality-level composite data-quality index for Brazil's SISVAN nutrition surveillance system, covering 29,367,435 records and 8,930,881 children across 5,210 municipalities. Six indicators — coverage, age dissimilarity, digit preference for height and for weight, percentage of biologically implausible z-scores (BIV), and the standard deviation of plausible z-scores — are fed into PCA run separately for height-for-age (HAZ) and weight-for-height (WHZ), with the first principal component (62%/66% of variance) used as each municipality's composite score. Digit preference for height (median 59.4%) was far worse than for weight (31.8%); SD of plausible z-scores sat around 1.5 against a reference expectation near 1.0; and the worst composite scores clustered in Brazil's poorest regions, with HAZ and WHZ quality correlating at 0.74 — evidence these are site-level, not metric-specific, problems. There's no gold standard here: this is a descriptive ranking tool, not a validated classifier. For Runyu, the natural fit is the human-review-budget side of the CFFPR pipeline — a per-site scorecard to prioritize training or audit outreach — rather than individual-record flagging.

Plain-English glossary

  • Biologically implausible value (BIV) — a measurement outside a fixed, biologically grounded bound set by WHO (e.g. HAZ beyond ±6), not a threshold derived from the sample's own distribution.（中文:超出WHO设定的、具有生物学依据的固定界限的测量值(如HAZ超出±6),而非从样本自身分布推导出的阈值。）
  • Digit preference / Myers' dissimilarity index — a classic demographic method (originally for age-heaping) that measures deviation from a uniform distribution of last digits; applied here to recorded height and weight values.（中文:一种经典的人口学方法(最初用于检测年龄堆积),衡量末位数字偏离均匀分布的程度;本文将其应用于记录的身高和体重数值。）
  • HAZ / WHZ (height-for-age / weight-for-height z-score) — a child's measurement expressed as standard-deviation distance from the expected value for a reference population of the same age (and, for WHZ, height).（中文:将儿童的测量值表达为相对于同年龄(WHZ还需同身高)参考人群预期值的标准差距离。）
  • LMS method (Lambda-Mu-Sigma) — the statistical method behind WHO/CDC growth-chart z-scores: fits a skewness parameter, median, and coefficient of variation at each age, then applies a power transform so z-scores account for non-symmetric growth distributions.（中文:WHO/CDC生长曲线z分数背后的统计方法:在每个年龄点拟合偏度参数、中位数和变异系数,再应用幂变换,使z分数能够反映生长分布的非对称性。）
  • WHO vs. CDC growth references — WHO's standards are prescriptive (how children should grow under optimal conditions, from an international breastfed-reference cohort); CDC's references are descriptive (how US children actually grew historically); using the wrong one shifts a child's z-score and flag status.（中文:WHO标准是规范性的(描述儿童在最优条件下应如何生长,基于国际母乳喂养参照队列);CDC参照是描述性的(反映美国儿童历史上实际的生长情况);用错参考标准会改变儿童的z分数和标记结果。）
