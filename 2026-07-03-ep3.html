Show Notes — Track C — 2026-07-03

Blurb

Paranjapye, Ruffin, Harris & Corvol (J Cyst Fibros 2020) review why cystic fibrosis severity varies so much despite being a single-gene disorder, mapping the genetic modifiers of CF both inside and outside the CFTR locus. Inside the locus: cis-regulatory elements within CFTR's topologically associated domain (TAD), tissue-specific enhancers (intron-1/intron-11 intestinal via FOXA1/A2, HNF1, CDX2; −44 kb/−35 kb airway-selective, the latter immune-responsive via IRF1/NF-Y), and repressor-creating promoter variants (e.g. a site for ZNF300). Outside the locus, from the International CF Gene Modifier Consortium GWAS: lung disease → EHF/APIP (11p13), MUC4/MUC20 (3q29), SLC9A3 (5p15.3), HLA class II (6p21.3), AGTR2/SLC6A14 (Xq22-23); meconium ileus → SLC6A14, SLC26A9 (1q32.1), ATP12A, PRSS1; CF-related diabetes → TCF7L2, SLC26A9; liver disease (candidate-gene only) → ABCA4, GSTP1, MBL, SERPINA1. SLC26A9 is strikingly pleiotropic (meconium ileus, diabetes, and ivacaftor lung response — rs7512462 CC worse by 7.7 FEV1 points in G551D carriers). The stated hard problem: bridging intergenic GWAS SNPs to a nearby gene's expression/function, partly addressed with nasal-epithelial transcriptomics and GTEx. Where it lands: summary-stats colocalization — a CF-modifier-locus coloc notebook that runs entirely on public GWAS + eQTL summary statistics.

Plain-English glossary

  • Cis-regulatory element (CRE) — non-coding DNA near a gene (promoter, enhancer, insulator) that controls when and how strongly it is transcribed.（中文：顺式调控元件——基因附近的非编码DNA（启动子、增强子、绝缘子），控制基因何时、多强地转录。）
  • Colocalization — a test asking whether a GWAS signal and an eQTL signal at a locus share the same causal variant rather than merely overlapping.（中文：共定位——检验某位点的GWAS信号和eQTL信号是否共享同一个因果变异，而不只是位置重叠。）
  • eQTL — a variant whose genotype is statistically associated with a gene's expression level.（中文：eQTL——其基因型与某基因表达水平在统计上相关的变异。）
  • GWAS — genome-wide association study; scans hundreds of thousands to millions of SNPs across the genome with no prior model.（中文：GWAS（全基因组关联研究）——不带先验模型地扫描全基因组几十万到数百万个SNP。）
  • Imputation — statistically filling in un-genotyped variants from LD structure to extend a GWAS to millions of SNPs.（中文：填补——依据LD结构统计推断未分型的变异，把GWAS扩展到数百万个SNP。）
  • Linkage disequilibrium (LD) — the non-random co-inheritance of nearby variants, which lets a tag SNP stand in for its neighbors.（中文：连锁不平衡（LD）——邻近变异的非随机共同遗传，使一个标签SNP能代表其邻居。）
  • Meconium ileus — a newborn bowel obstruction affecting ~15% of CF babies (pancreatic-insufficient only).（中文：胎粪性肠梗阻——影响约15%的CF新生儿（仅胰腺功能不全者）的新生儿肠梗阻。）
  • Pancreatic insufficiency (PI) / sufficiency (PS) — whether the pancreas can deliver digestive enzymes; the one strong CFTR genotype-phenotype rule.（中文：胰腺功能不全（PI）/充足（PS）——胰腺能否输送消化酶；唯一强的CFTR基因型-表型规则。）
  • Pleiotropy — one gene affecting multiple distinct phenotypes (here SLC26A9 across meconium ileus, diabetes, drug response).（中文：多效性——一个基因影响多个不同表型（此处SLC26A9横跨胎粪性肠梗阻、糖尿病、药物响应）。）
  • Pulmonary ionocyte — a rare, high-CFTR-expressing airway cell type identified by single-cell RNA-seq.（中文：肺离子细胞——单细胞RNA测序发现的稀有、高表达CFTR的气道细胞类型。）
  • Topologically associated domain (TAD) — a self-interacting DNA neighborhood held together by CTCF/cohesin, within which enhancers loop to promoters.（中文：拓扑关联结构域（TAD）——由CTCF/cohesin维系的自我互作DNA邻域，其内增强子环化到启动子。）

Full citation

Paranjapye A, Ruffin M, Harris A, Corvol H. Genetic variation in CFTR and modifier loci may modulate cystic fibrosis disease severity. J Cyst Fibros. 2020;19 Suppl 1:S10-S14. PMID: 31734115 · PMC: PMC7036019 · DOI: 10.1016/j.jcf.2019.11.001

Methods / tools to lift

  • Age/sex-adjusted quantitative lung phenotype — one continuous trait per patient so nobody is stratified out, preserving GWAS power.
  • Consortium meta-analysis — pool cohorts (North America + France) to survive multiple-testing penalties.
  • Intergenic-SNP-to-gene bridging — pair GWAS hits with tissue expression (nasal-epithelial transcriptomics, GTEx pancreas/lung) to nominate the causal gene.
  • coloc — colocalization method testing shared causal variant between a GWAS and an eQTL signal.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves summary-stats colocalization. Paste-ready MVP: CF-modifier-locus colocalization notebook — input published CF modifier GWAS summary statistics at named loci (EHF/APIP, SLC9A3, SLC6A14, SLC26A9, MUC20) + public GTEx eQTL summary statistics for CF-relevant tissues (lung, pancreas, whole blood); run coloc per locus; output a posterior probability that each locus acts through a specific gene in a specific tissue. Difficulty: M. Runs entirely on public summary statistics — no controlled genotype data.

What to verify first

  • Ancestry/LD matching between GWAS and eQTL reference (LD mismatch fakes colocalization).
  • Tissue relevance: bulk GTEx tissue averages over rare CFTR-high cell types (ionocytes) — a real signal can be washed out, so a coloc null is not a gene null.
  • coloc's single-causal-variant assumption is fragile at intergenic, gene-dense, multi-signal loci.
  • Need full regional summary statistics, not just the lead SNP.
  • Public-summary-statistics exercise only; decides no research design and touches no registry's internal genotype data.

Gap-map / catalog

  • Closes: consolidates the CF modifier locus set across four phenotypes (lung, meconium ileus, CFRD, liver) with chromosomal locations; documents SLC26A9 pleiotropy across three phenotypes + ivacaftor response; adds the cis-regulatory/TAD architecture of the CFTR locus and its tissue-specific transcription factors.
  • Leaves open: causal gene/variant behind each intergenic hit (the review's own stated challenge); cell-type-resolved eQTL (ionocyte) rather than bulk; mechanism linking each modifier to the phenotype; whether SLC26A9's effects share one mechanism or several; genome-wide (vs candidate-gene) mapping of liver disease.

ALEX Morning — Track C today is a 2020 review in the Journal of Cystic Fibrosis. 早上好，今天Track C是一篇2020年发表在《囊性纤维化杂志》(Journal of Cystic Fibrosis)上的综述。The title is "Genetic variation in CFTR and modifier loci may modulate cystic fibrosis disease severity," by Alekh Paranjapye, Manon Ruffin, Ann Harris and Harriet Corvol. 标题是《CFTR及修饰位点的遗传变异可能调节囊性纤维化疾病严重程度》，作者是Alekh Paranjapye、Manon Ruffin、Ann Harris和Harriet Corvol。It's open access, so we have the full text. 它是开放获取(OA)的，所以我们拿到了全文。This one is a map, not a single experiment — it's the field's consolidated picture of what modifies CF severity, both inside and outside the CFTR gene. 这篇是一张地图，而不是单个实验——它是这个领域对"什么在修饰CF严重程度"的整合图景，既包括CFTR基因内部，也包括外部。For our catalog, it's a chance to fill in a whole shelf of modifier loci at once. 对我们的目录来说，这是一次同时把一整架修饰位点填满的机会。

JORDAN Start me with the core puzzle. 先给我讲核心谜题。CF is caused by one gene, CFTR — so why is there a "severity" to modify at all? CF是由一个基因CFTR引起的——那为什么还有"严重程度"需要去修饰？

ALEX Right, that's the paradox the whole review addresses. 对，这正是整篇综述要处理的悖论。CF is a single-gene recessive disorder, but two patients with the identical CFTR genotype can have very different disease. CF是一种单基因隐性遗传病，但两个CFTR基因型完全相同的患者，疾病表现可以差别很大。The one strong genotype-phenotype rule is pancreatic status: patients with two "severe" CFTR variants are usually pancreatic insufficient — PI, meaning the pancreas can't deliver digestive enzymes — while milder genotypes are pancreatic sufficient. 唯一强的基因型-表型规则是胰腺状态：带两个"严重"CFTR变异的患者通常是胰腺功能不全（PI，pancreatic insufficient，即胰腺无法输送消化酶），而较轻的基因型则是胰腺功能充足（PS）。But for lung disease, which is what kills most patients, the CFTR genotype predicts surprisingly little. 但对肺病——也就是杀死大多数患者的病——CFTR基因型的预测力却出人意料地弱。The variability comes from three sources: environment, variants within the CFTR locus that aren't the classic coding mutations, and modifier genes elsewhere in the genome. 变异性来自三个来源：环境、CFTR位点内部那些并非经典编码突变的变异、以及基因组中别处的修饰基因。

JORDAN Define "variants within the locus that aren't coding mutations" — what does that mean? 定义一下"位点内部但非编码突变的变异"——那是什么意思？

ALEX This is the cis-regulatory story, and it's worth a careful primer. 这是顺式调控(cis-regulatory)的故事，值得仔细铺垫。A gene's coding sequence is only part of what determines how much protein you make. 一个基因的编码序列，只是决定你造多少蛋白质的一部分。Cis-regulatory elements, or CREs, are stretches of DNA near the gene — promoters, enhancers, insulators — that control when and how strongly it's transcribed. 顺式调控元件(cis-regulatory elements，CREs)是基因附近的DNA片段——启动子、增强子、绝缘子——它们控制基因何时转录、转录得多强。The review makes the point that CFTR sits inside a single topologically associated domain, a TAD — a self-interacting neighborhood of DNA held together by the architectural proteins CTCF and cohesin. 综述指出CFTR位于单个拓扑关联结构域(topologically associated domain，TAD)之内——一个由建筑蛋白CTCF和cohesin维系在一起、自我互作的DNA邻域。Within that TAD, different regulatory elements loop to the CFTR promoter depending on cell type. 在这个TAD内部，不同的调控元件会依细胞类型环化(loop)到CFTR启动子上。So a variant in a deep-intronic enhancer, or one that creates a binding site for a repressor — the review gives the example of a promoter variant that creates a site for the repressor ZNF300 — can change how much CFTR you make without touching a single coding letter. 所以一个位于深内含子增强子中的变异，或一个造出抑制因子结合位点的变异——综述举了一个启动子变异造出抑制因子ZNF300结合位点的例子——都能在不碰任何一个编码字母的情况下改变你造多少CFTR。And these elements are tissue-specific: intron-1 and intron-11 elements drive CFTR in the intestine via factors like FOXA1/A2, HNF1 and CDX2, while sites 44 kb and 35 kb upstream are airway-selective. 而这些元件是组织特异的：内含子1和内含子11的元件通过FOXA1/A2、HNF1、CDX2等因子在肠道驱动CFTR，而上游44 kb和35 kb处的位点则是气道选择性的。

JORDAN That upstream airway detail — anything CF-mechanistic there? 上游那个气道细节——那里有什么CF机制上的东西吗？

ALEX Yes, and it's a nice hint at gene-environment coupling. 有，而且它很好地暗示了基因-环境的耦合。The 35 kb upstream site recruits interferon regulatory factor 1, IRF1, and NF-Y — it looks like an immune-responsive element. 上游35 kb的位点招募干扰素调节因子1(IRF1)和NF-Y——它看起来像一个免疫响应元件。The 44 kb site may respond to oxidative stress in airway cells. 44 kb的位点可能在气道细胞中响应氧化应激。And repressors like EHF and KLF5 tune CFTR down in the airway. 而像EHF和KLF5这样的抑制因子会在气道里把CFTR调低。Hold onto EHF — it comes back as a modifier gene, which is a theme: the same factors that regulate CFTR locally can also be the modifier loci that surface in genome-wide scans. 记住EHF——它作为修饰基因还会回来，这是一个主题：那些在局部调控CFTR的因子，也可能是全基因组扫描中浮现的修饰位点。

JORDAN So how did the field find the modifier genes outside the locus? 那这个领域是怎么找到位点之外的修饰基因的？

ALEX They moved from candidate genes to genome-wide association studies. 他们从候选基因转向了全基因组关联研究(GWAS)。The early candidate-gene era — pick a gene you suspect, test it — was underpowered and gave mixed results, and by design it can only find genes you already suspect. 早期的候选基因时代——挑一个你怀疑的基因去测——检验力不足、结果参差，而且从设计上只能找到你已经怀疑的基因。GWAS scans hundreds of thousands of SNPs — single-nucleotide polymorphisms — chosen to tag linkage-disequilibrium blocks across the whole genome, with no prior model, and imputation extends that to millions of variants. GWAS扫描几十万个SNP（单核苷酸多态性），这些SNP被选来标记(tag)全基因组的连锁不平衡(linkage disequilibrium，LD)区块，不带任何先验模型，而填补(imputation)又把它扩展到数百万个变异。The cost is multiple testing — a huge statistical penalty — so the field pooled patients into an International CF Gene Modifier Consortium across North America and France to get the power. 代价是多重检验——一个巨大的统计惩罚——所以这个领域把患者汇集成一个跨北美和法国的国际CF基因修饰联盟来获取检验力。A key trick worth noting: they compute a quantitative lung phenotype that adjusts for age and sex, so every patient contributes without stratifying the sample to death. 一个值得注意的关键技巧：他们计算一个针对年龄和性别校正过的定量肺表型，这样每个患者都能贡献数据，而不必把样本分层到检验力枯竭。

JORDAN Give me the actual loci — which genes came out, for which phenotype? 给我实际的位点——哪些基因出来了，对应哪个表型？

ALEX Let me go phenotype by phenotype, because that structure is the catalog. 我按表型逐个来讲，因为这个结构就是目录。For lung disease: the first genome-wide hit was the intergenic region between EHF and APIP at chromosome 11p13, in over 3,000 pancreatic-insufficient patients. 肺病方面：第一个全基因组命中是11p13染色体上EHF和APIP之间的基因间区，样本是3,000多名胰腺功能不全患者。The meta-analysis of 6,365 patients confirmed EHF/APIP and added four regions with strong CF biology: MUC4 and MUC20 at 3q29 (mucins), SLC9A3 at 5p15.3 (a sodium-hydrogen exchanger), HLA class II at 6p21.3 (immune), and AGTR2/SLC6A14 at Xq22-q23. 6,365名患者的荟萃分析确认了EHF/APIP，并新增了四个有很强CF生物学的区域：3q29的MUC4和MUC20（黏蛋白），5p15.3的SLC9A3（一个钠氢交换体），6p21.3的HLA II类（免疫），以及Xq22-q23的AGTR2/SLC6A14。For meconium ileus — the newborn bowel obstruction that affects about 15% of CF babies — GWAS found SLC6A14 at Xq23-24 and SLC26A9 at 1q32.1, later adding ATP12A and PRSS1. 对胎粪性肠梗阻(meconium ileus)——影响约15%的CF新生儿的肠梗阻——GWAS找到了Xq23-24的SLC6A14和1q32.1的SLC26A9，后来又加上ATP12A和PRSS1。For CF-related diabetes: TCF7L2, already a type-2-diabetes gene in the general population, plus SLC26A9 again. 对CF相关糖尿病：TCF7L2——它在普通人群里已是2型糖尿病基因——加上再次出现的SLC26A9。For liver disease, still candidate-gene only: ABCA4, GSTP1, an MBL haplotype, and SERPINA1 — the alpha-1-antitrypsin Z allele carried up to 47% of Z carriers to liver disease by age 25 versus 30% in non-carriers. 对肝病，仍只有候选基因：ABCA4、GSTP1、一个MBL单倍型，以及SERPINA1——α1-抗胰蛋白酶Z等位基因使多达47%的Z携带者在25岁前患上肝病，而非携带者为30%。The French CF Modifier Gene Study also reported that CF liver disease risk rises with age to about 32% by age 25 and clusters with male sex, F508del homozygosity, and a history of meconium ileus — a reminder that a modifier signal has to be read against these clinical risk factors, not in isolation. 法国CF修饰基因研究还报告，CF肝病风险随年龄上升，到25岁约为32%，并与男性、F508del纯合、以及胎粪性肠梗阻史聚集在一起——这提醒我们，一个修饰信号必须放在这些临床风险因素的背景下解读，而不是孤立地看。

JORDAN SLC26A9 keeps reappearing — lung, meconium ileus, diabetes, and drug response. SLC26A9反复出现——肺、胎粪性肠梗阻、糖尿病，还有药物反应。Is that one gene really doing all of that? 真的是一个基因在干这一切吗？

ALEX That's the pleiotropy story, and it's the review's most interesting thread. 那是多效性(pleiotropy)的故事，也是综述里最有意思的一条线。SLC26A9 is an epithelial ion channel, and it shows up as a modifier of meconium ileus, of CF-related diabetes, and of the lung response to the CFTR modulator ivacaftor. SLC26A9是一个上皮离子通道，它作为胎粪性肠梗阻、CF相关糖尿病、以及肺对CFTR调节剂ivacaftor响应的修饰因子出现。On the drug side, one SLC26A9 variant explained about 28% of the variability in lung response to ivacaftor in Canadians, and the review reports a specific finding: for the variant rs7512462 in patients carrying at least one G551D CFTR mutation, the CC genotype was associated with a worse response — a 7.7-percentage-point-larger drop in FEV1 — than carriers of the ancestral allele. 在药物方面，一个SLC26A9变异解释了加拿大人对ivacaftor肺响应变异性的约28%，综述还报告了一个具体发现：对携带至少一个G551D CFTR突变的患者，变异rs7512462的CC基因型与更差的响应相关——FEV1的下降比祖先等位基因携带者大7.7个百分点。So the same gene may help predict who benefits from a modulator. 所以同一个基因可能帮助预测谁会从调节剂中获益。The review is careful about mechanism, though — which brings up the hard part. 不过综述对机制很谨慎——这就引出了难点。

JORDAN What's the hard part? 难点是什么？

ALEX The review states it plainly: connecting a high-P-value SNP in an intergenic region to the expression or function of a nearby gene has proven a major challenge. 综述说得很直白：把一个位于基因间区、P值很高的SNP，连接到附近某个基因的表达或功能上，已被证明是一个重大挑战。Most of these hits sit between genes, not inside them. 这些命中大多落在基因之间，而不在基因内部。The way they start to bridge it is expression data: transcriptomics of nasal epithelial cells and pancreatic expression from GTEx showed that lung severity tracks SLC6A14 expression in nasal epithelium, and meconium ileus tracks SLC6A14, SLC26A9 and ATP12A expression in pancreas. 他们开始搭这座桥的方式是表达数据：鼻上皮细胞的转录组学和来自GTEx的胰腺表达显示，肺严重程度与鼻上皮中SLC6A14的表达同步，而胎粪性肠梗阻与胰腺中SLC6A14、SLC26A9、ATP12A的表达同步。That's the move from "there's a signal here" to "here's the gene it probably acts through." 这就是从"这里有个信号"到"这大概是它作用所经的基因"的转变。

JORDAN You mentioned rare cell types earlier — how do those fit the modifier picture? 你前面提到稀有细胞类型——它们如何契合这个修饰图景？

ALEX They complicate the interpretation in a way worth internalizing. 它们让解读复杂化，其复杂之处值得内化。The review notes that CFTR is not expressed uniformly across a tissue: in the airway, single-cell RNA-seq found a rare, high-CFTR-expressing cell type called the pulmonary ionocyte, mirroring a rare high-CFTR cell in the intestine. 综述指出CFTR在一个组织里并非均匀表达：在气道，单细胞RNA测序发现了一种稀有的、高表达CFTR的细胞类型，叫肺离子细胞(pulmonary ionocyte)，它与肠道中一种稀有的高CFTR细胞相呼应。So the cells that matter most for CFTR function can be a tiny fraction of the tissue. 所以对CFTR功能最重要的细胞，可能只占组织的极小一部分。That matters for modifier work because a bulk-tissue expression measurement averages over all cell types, and a signal concentrated in ionocytes could be washed out entirely. 这对修饰研究很重要，因为混合组织的表达测量是对所有细胞类型取平均，而一个集中在离子细胞里的信号可能被完全冲淡。It is exactly the reason a modifier locus can be real yet fail to colocalize with a bulk eQTL. 这正是为什么一个修饰位点可以是真实的，却无法与混合组织eQTL共定位。

ALEX Let me land it on one priority: summary-stats colocalization. 让我把它落到一个优先事项上：汇总统计的共定位(colocalization)。The transferable method is exactly that intergenic-SNP-to-gene bridge, done rigorously. 可迁移的方法正是那座"基因间SNP到基因"的桥，只不过要做得严谨。The buildable artifact is a CF-modifier-locus colocalization notebook. 可做的产物是一个CF修饰位点共定位笔记本。The MVP: take published CF modifier GWAS summary statistics at these named loci — EHF/APIP, SLC9A3, SLC6A14, SLC26A9, MUC20 — pair them with public eQTL summary statistics from GTEx in CF-relevant tissues (lung, pancreas, whole blood as proxy for immune), and run a colocalization test (a method like coloc that asks whether the GWAS signal and the eQTL signal share the same causal variant rather than merely overlapping). JORDAN What would you need to check before believing a coloc result? 在相信一个共定位结果之前你需要核对什么？
