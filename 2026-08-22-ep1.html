Ep1 — Track A — Variant Interpretation & Harmonization

Blurb

El Makhzen et al. (2026, Frontiers in Genetics) sequenced the complete ~250-kb CFTR gene in nine Moroccan individuals — six probands with suspected cystic fibrosis and three parents — using Oxford Nanopore long-read sequencing, an approach chosen specifically because standard short-read diagnostic panels built around European-frequency variants (F508del and its usual companions) miss a large fraction of CF-causing alleles in African and Mediterranean populations. The pipeline combined long-read variant calling (Clair3), two-step phasing (Eagle2 reference-based + WhatsHap read-based), structural-variant calling (Sniffles2, cuteSV), and CFTR2/ACMG classification, with all calls confirmed by Sanger sequencing. Critically, the authors went beyond in-silico classification for two previously uncharacterised variants — p.Ser364Pro and p.Phe1078Ser — by cloning them into HEK293 cells and directly measuring surface expression, substantiating pathogenicity for both and showing p.Phe1078Ser responds to Trikafta (elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor) in vitro. This lands in the Karen Raraigh / CFTR2-harmonization lane: the paper's variant-triage funnel (call → filter by population frequency → cross-reference CFTR2/ClinVar → route VUS toward orthogonal evidence) is exactly the workflow a curation collaboration would formalize, and it's the seed for a small buildable triage script.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG criteria — the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics framework for classifying variants as pathogenic, likely pathogenic, uncertain, likely benign, or benign.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会制定的变异分类标准框架，用于判定变异的致病性等级。）
  • Compound heterozygous — carrying two different disease-causing variants, one on each copy of a gene.（中文：在一个基因的两条拷贝上分别携带两个不同的致病变异。）
  • HGVS notation — the standardized system for writing variants, e.g. c.680T>G at the DNA level or p.Leu227Arg at the protein level.（中文：变异书写的标准化命名规范，例如DNA层面的c.680T>G或蛋白质层面的p.Leu227Arg。）
  • Long-read sequencing (ONT) — sequencing technology (e.g. Oxford Nanopore) that reads DNA molecules thousands to tens of thousands of base pairs long in one continuous pass, unlike short-read NGS.（中文：如牛津纳米孔等技术，能一次连续读取长达数千至数万碱基对的DNA分子，不同于短读长测序。）
  • Phasing — determining which variants sit on the same physical copy (chromosome) of a gene versus the other copy (cis vs. trans).（中文：判断哪些变异位于基因的同一条染色体拷贝上，哪些位于另一条拷贝上，即顺式与反式的区分。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant that has been observed but whose disease-causing status hasn't yet been resolved.（中文：已被观测到但致病性尚未确定的变异。）

Full citation

Methods/tools to lift

  • Clair3 — deep-learning variant caller built specifically for long-read data (SNVs and small indels).
  • Eagle2 — reference-based haplotype phasing against a population reference panel.
  • WhatsHap — read-based phasing to extend a phasing backbone to rare variants.
  • Sniffles2 / cuteSV — structural-variant callers for long-read data.
  • Ensembl VEP — variant annotation against transcript models and population databases.
  • CFTR2 / ACMG classification — the disease-specific and general variant-classification frameworks applied here.
  • Cell-surface biotinylation + immunoblot — functional assay measuring how much protein reaches the cell surface, used to reclassify VUS.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh / CFTR2-harmonization priority. Buildable artifact: cftr-vus-triage — input a small VCF/CSV of candidate CFTR variants, cross-reference CFTR2, ClinVar, and gnomAD population frequency in one pass, output a table flagging VUS with no orthogonal evidence vs. VUS already backed by an in-silico score (AlphaMissense, REVEL) from earlier Track A episodes, with a second sub-flag for treatability/theratype evidence separate from pathogenicity. Difficulty: S (mostly API calls + a join, 1-2 days).

What to verify first

  • How well the 1000 Genomes reference-based phasing backbone actually performs on North African / Amazigh-specific haplotype structure (not reported, stratified by ancestry, in this paper).
  • The cost-effectiveness claim (~5,000-euro MinION device = "affordable") is not backed by an actual cost analysis — authors flag this themselves as a limitation.
  • Whether this paper's CFTR2/ACMG classification conventions map cleanly onto whatever curation conventions Karen's group actually uses.
  • This paper says nothing about how CFTR variants are currently curated or flagged inside the CFF registry itself — separate, unverified question.
