Track C — Cystic fibrosis in a septuagenarian (Johnson & Ahmad, 2026) — ABSTRACT-ONLY

Blurb. A BMJ Case Reports case of cystic fibrosis diagnosed in a man in his 70s, after imaging done for an incidental pulmonary nodule revealed bronchiectasis, a multidisciplinary team raised CF, and diagnosis was confirmed by elevated sweat chloride plus CFTR genotyping showing compound heterozygosity (the two specific variants are redacted in the abstract). This paper is not open access and has no PMC full-text ID — read as abstract-only, flagged on-air. As evidence it is a single anecdote, but as a data point it sits at the extreme mild edge of the CF phenotype range and poses the modifier question in its purest form: given two CFTR variants, what lets one person present as a sick infant and another as an incidentally-diagnosed septuagenarian? It lands on the modifier/WGS long game (Track C), secondary Liz-style phenotyping (method only). Buildable artifact: a genotype-phenotype-discordance surfacer that models expected severity from CFTR functional class, computes the observed-minus-expected residual, and ranks the extreme-mild-survivor tail as a WGS-worthy modifier-discovery worklist.

Plain-English glossary.

  • bronchiectasis — permanent airway widening and scarring from repeated infection; a common downstream feature of CF but with many other causes, so an easily-missed CF clue in adults.（中文：支气管扩张，气道因反复感染永久性扩张并瘢痕化；囊性纤维化常见的下游表现，但病因众多，故在成人中易被漏掉的线索。）
  • compound heterozygosity — two different variants, one on each gene copy (from each parent), rather than the same variant twice.（中文：复合杂合，两个不同变异，每个基因拷贝各一个（分别来自父母），而非同一变异两次。）
  • CFTR2 — the curated database that assigns each CFTR variant a functional class; you'd place a genotype on it to judge expected severity (impossible here — variants redacted).（中文：CFTR2，给每个 CFTR 变异指定功能类别的整理型数据库；用来判断预期严重程度（此处不可行——变异被删）。）
  • genotype-phenotype discordance — observed severity far milder (or more severe) than the CFTR genotype predicts; the abstract notes correlations "do not always align with expected clinical patterns."（中文：基因型-表型不一致，观察到的严重程度远比 CFTR 基因型预测的更轻（或更重）；摘要指出相关性「并不总是符合预期临床模式」。）
  • modifier gene — a gene other than CFTR whose variation shifts CF severity; in the established literature these cluster in ion transport, inflammation/mucus, and organ-specific-risk pathways (not named in this abstract).（中文：修饰基因，CFTR 之外、其变异会移动囊性纤维化严重程度的基因；在已确立文献中聚集于离子转运、炎症/黏液、器官特异风险等通路（本摘要未点名）。）
  • residual function — a variant leaving some CFTR activity working; the mild-end variants that let disease smolder and present late.（中文：残余功能，仍留下部分 CFTR 活性的变异；让疾病暗燃、晚发的温和端变异。）
  • sweat chloride test — pilocarpine-iontophoresis measurement of salt in sweat, high in CF because the sweat duct can't reclaim chloride through CFTR; the closest thing to a direct in-vivo CFTR functional readout.（中文：汗液氯离子检测，用毛果芸香碱离子电渗测量汗中盐分，囊性纤维化中偏高，因汗腺导管无法经 CFTR 回收氯离子；最接近活体 CFTR 功能直接读数。）

Full citation. Johnson J, Ahmad N. Cystic fibrosis in a septuagenarian. BMJ Case Reports. 2026;19(6):e271848. PMID 42373200; DOI 10.1136/bcr-2025-271848. According to PubMed. Source: abstract-only (not OA, no PMCID). Specific CFTR variants redacted in the abstract.

Methods / tools to lift.

  • Two-step diagnostic logic — a functional screen (sweat chloride) first, then CFTR genotyping to confirm and name the variants; the workflow any downstream discordance/modifier study depends on.
  • Extreme-phenotype anchoring — a late-diagnosed adult is a rare data point from the under-sampled mild tail; extreme phenotypes are the efficient starting points for modifier discovery.
  • Residual-before-modifier discipline — rule out that the CFTR genotype itself is simply mild before invoking a trans-acting modifier.

ALEX Today's Track C paper is a short one with a long shadow: "Cystic fibrosis in a septuagenarian," a 2026 case report by Jessie Johnson and Nawaid Ahmad in BMJ Case Reports. 今天的 C 轨论文篇幅短、影响却长：《一位七旬老人的囊性纤维化》，是 Jessie Johnson 与 Nawaid Ahmad 于 2026 年发表在《BMJ Case Reports》上的一份病例报告。 One flag up front: this paper is not open access and has no PMC full-text ID, so everything I say comes from the abstract only — and the abstract, frustratingly, redacts the two specific CFTR variants, printing "compound heterozygosity for" and then nothing. 先声明一点：这篇论文非开放获取，也没有 PMC 全文编号，所以我讲的一切都只来自摘要——而且这份摘要恼人地把两个具体的 CFTR 变异删掉了，印着「复合杂合」然后就没了。 We're reading it because a man diagnosed with cystic fibrosis in his seventies is a living question mark hanging over the modifier catalog. 我们读它，是因为一位在七十多岁才确诊囊性纤维化的男性，是悬在修饰基因目录之上的一个活生生的问号。

JORDAN Let me get the biology straight first, since this is the side I'm still learning. 让我先把生物学搞清楚，因为这正是我还在学的一面。 Cystic fibrosis is supposed to be a childhood disease, right? 囊性纤维化本该是一种儿童期疾病，对吧？

ALEX Overwhelmingly — the abstract notes that over 75% of cases are diagnosed before age two, and in countries with newborn screening it's usually caught in the first weeks of life. 绝大多数情况下是的——摘要指出超过 75% 的病例在两岁前确诊，而在有新生儿筛查的国家，通常在出生头几周就被发现。 CF is caused by two broken copies of CFTR, the chloride channel; without working CFTR, secretions get thick, and the lungs and pancreas take the damage. 囊性纤维化由 CFTR（氯离子通道）的两个损坏拷贝引起；没有正常工作的 CFTR，分泌物变得黏稠，肺和胰腺首当其冲受损。 A few terms you'll hear tonight. 今晚你会听到几个术语。 Bronchiectasis is permanent widening and scarring of the airways from repeated infection — it's a common downstream feature of CF, but it has many other causes too, which is exactly why it's an easy clue to miss. 支气管扩张 (bronchiectasis) 是气道因反复感染而永久性扩张并瘢痕化——它是囊性纤维化常见的下游表现，但也有许多别的病因，这正是它容易被当成线索错过的原因。 The sweat chloride test — done by pilocarpine iontophoresis, a small skin patch that stimulates sweating — measures salt in sweat, and it's high in CF because the sweat duct can't reclaim chloride through CFTR; it's the closest thing we have to a direct functional readout of CFTR in a living person. 汗液氯离子检测——通过毛果芸香碱离子电渗（在皮肤上贴一小片刺激出汗）进行——测量汗液中的盐分，在囊性纤维化中它偏高，因为汗腺导管无法通过 CFTR 回收氯离子；它是我们对活人体内 CFTR 功能最接近直接的读数。

JORDAN And "compound heterozygosity" — what does that mean here? 那「复合杂合」在这里是什么意思？

ALEX It means the two CFTR copies carry two different variants, one inherited from each parent, rather than the same variant twice. 意思是两个 CFTR 拷贝携带两个不同的变异，从每位父母各遗传一个，而不是同一个变异出现两次。 The other two ideas are the ones that make this case matter. 另外两个概念才是让这个病例有意义的地方。 Residual function means a variant that leaves some CFTR activity working — those are the mild-end variants, and they're why a person can reach adulthood before disease declares itself. 残余功能 (residual function) 指的是一个仍留下部分 CFTR 活性的变异——那些就是温和端的变异，也是一个人能在疾病显现之前活到成年的原因。 A modifier gene is a gene other than CFTR whose own variation nudges how severe the CF phenotype turns out — same CFTR genotype, different outcome, because of the genetic background. 修饰基因 (modifier gene) 指的是 CFTR 之外的一个基因，它自身的变异会推动囊性纤维化表型最终有多严重——相同的 CFTR 基因型，因为遗传背景不同而有不同结局。 When the observed severity is far milder than the CFTR genotype would predict, we call that genotype-phenotype discordance — and the abstract explicitly says these correlations "do not always align with expected clinical patterns." 当观察到的严重程度远比 CFTR 基因型所预测的更轻时，我们称之为基因型-表型不一致 (genotype-phenotype discordance)——而摘要明确指出这些相关性「并不总是符合预期的临床模式」。

JORDAN Help me understand the background here — how does someone with two CFTR variants go seventy years without anyone noticing? 帮我理解一下背景——一个带着两个 CFTR 变异的人，怎么能过七十年都没人察觉？

ALEX Two forces are at work, and they compound. 有两股力量在起作用，而且彼此叠加。 The first is biological: mild, residual-function variants — the classic examples in the established literature are things like R117H paired with certain poly-T backgrounds — can leave enough CFTR working that the disease smolders instead of blazing, sometimes presenting only as sinus disease, pancreatitis, or the infertility we discussed last episode. 第一股是生物学的：温和的残余功能变异——已确立文献中的经典例子，比如 R117H 搭配某些多聚 T 背景——能留下足够工作的 CFTR，使疾病是在暗燃而非烈焰，有时只表现为鼻窦疾病、胰腺炎，或我们上一期讲过的不育。 That's the CFTR-related-disorder end of the spectrum shading into mild classic CF. 那就是 CFTR 相关疾病一端向温和经典囊性纤维化过渡的地带。 The second force is systemic: CF was a childhood diagnosis for so long that the diagnostic reflex in adult medicine simply isn't there, and adults with bronchiectasis are often worked up for everything except CF. 第二股是系统性的：囊性纤维化长期以来是一个儿童期诊断，以至于成人医学里根本没有形成这种诊断反射，患支气管扩张的成年人常常被检查了除囊性纤维化以外的一切。 So a mild genotype and an under-alert system multiply together into a seventy-year gap. 于是一个温和的基因型和一个警觉不足的系统相乘，就成了七十年的空白。

JORDAN And that gap isn't just a curiosity — it skews what we think we know about CF genetics, doesn't it? 而这个空白不只是一件趣事——它扭曲了我们自以为了解的囊性纤维化遗传学，对吧？

ALEX Exactly, and it's a thread we've pulled on all week. 正是如此，而这也是我们整周都在扯的一条线。 If the mildest people are systematically diagnosed late or never, then the catalog of CF genotypes is biased toward severe, early-presenting cases, and the mild-variant and modifier end is under-sampled. 如果最温和的人系统性地被晚诊断或从不诊断，那么囊性纤维化基因型目录就偏向了严重、早发的病例，而温和变异与修饰基因那一端则被采样不足。 Every late-diagnosed adult is a small correction to that bias — one more data point from the part of the distribution our registries and gene panels see least well. 每一位晚诊断的成年人都是对这种偏倚的一个小修正——来自我们的登记系统和基因面板看得最不清楚的那部分分布的又一个数据点。 That is why a single septuagenarian is worth a journal page: not because one case proves anything, but because the mild tail is exactly where the undiscovered biology lives. 这就是为什么单单一位七旬老人值得占一页期刊：不是因为一个病例能证明什么，而是因为温和的尾巴恰恰是尚未被发现的生物学所栖居的地方。

JORDAN So what actually happened to this patient — how do you even end up diagnosing CF at 70-plus? 那这位患者到底经历了什么——一个人怎么会在七十多岁才被诊断出囊性纤维化？

ALEX It started as an accident, which is the whole clinical lesson. 起因是一次偶然，而这正是整个临床教训所在。 Imaging done for something else turned up a pulmonary nodule — a small spot in the lung, usually chased to rule out cancer — and alongside it, bronchiectasis. 为别的原因做的影像检查发现了一个肺结节——肺里的一个小斑点，通常要追查以排除癌症——旁边还有支气管扩张。 A multidisciplinary team looked at the whole picture, raised the possibility of CF, and confirmed it two ways: an elevated sweat chloride and CFTR genotyping showing the compound heterozygous genotype. 一个多学科团队看了整体情况，提出了囊性纤维化的可能性，并通过两种方式确认：升高的汗液氯离子，以及显示复合杂合基因型的 CFTR 基因分型。 The authors' takeaway is a practical one aimed at non-specialists: consider CF when the constellation fits, regardless of age, because adult CF screening in bronchiectasis varies between centers and that variation drives diagnostic delay. 作者的结论是一个面向非专科医生的实用建议：当症状组合吻合时就要考虑囊性纤维化，无论年龄，因为支气管扩张中成人囊性纤维化筛查在各中心之间做法不一，而这种不一致导致了诊断延误。

JORDAN There's something almost accidental about the whole diagnostic route — a nodule chased for cancer ends in a CF diagnosis. 整个诊断路径几乎带着某种偶然性——为查癌症而追踪的一个结节，最终以囊性纤维化诊断收尾。 Is that just luck, or is there a lesson? 这只是运气，还是有教训？

ALEX Both, and the lesson is the one the authors press. 两者都有，而教训正是作者所强调的那一个。 The nodule was the accident, but the bronchiectasis sitting next to it was the real signal that a sharp reader shouldn't have needed a nodule to notice. 结节是偶然，但紧挨着它的支气管扩张才是真正的信号，一个敏锐的读片者本不该等到有结节才注意到它。 The confirmatory logic is clean and worth internalizing: a suggestive picture triggers a sweat chloride test as the functional screen, and a positive sweat test is then anchored by CFTR genotyping that names the actual variants. 确认的逻辑很干净、值得内化：一个提示性的影像触发汗液氯离子检测作为功能筛查，而阳性的汗液检测再由点明实际变异的 CFTR 基因分型来锚定。 That two-step — functional screen first, genotype to confirm and characterize — is exactly the workflow that a modifier or discordance study downstream depends on, because you can't study why a genotype is mild until you actually know the genotype. 这两步——先做功能筛查、再用基因型确认并刻画——正是下游的修饰基因或不一致研究所依赖的工作流，因为你在真正知道基因型之前，无法研究一个基因型为何温和。

JORDAN I can hear a "but" coming about what this can and can't tell us. 我能听出一个关于「它能告诉我们什么、不能告诉我们什么」的转折要来了。

ALEX Here's the design pushback, and it's inherent to a single case. 设计上的质疑在此，而且这是单个病例本身固有的。 A case report of one person cannot separate three very different explanations for survival to the seventies: a genuinely mild residual-function genotype, a protective modifier acting elsewhere in the genome, or plain ascertainment and survival luck — the mild cases who were never sick enough to get diagnosed young. 一份关于单个人的病例报告，无法区分三种非常不同的「活到七十多岁」的解释：一个真正温和的残余功能基因型、一个作用于基因组别处的保护性修饰基因、或纯粹的确诊与生存运气——那些从来没严重到需要在年轻时被诊断的温和病例。 And because the abstract redacts the variants, we can't even place this genotype on the CFTR2 functional-class map, which is the first thing you'd want — CFTR2 is the curated database that assigns each variant a functional category. 而且由于摘要删掉了变异，我们甚至无法把这个基因型放到 CFTR2 功能分类图上，而那是你最想要的第一样东西——CFTR2 是给每个变异指定功能类别的整理型数据库。 So on the evidence we can actually see, "modifier" is a hypothesis this case raises, not one it supports. 所以就我们真正能看到的证据而言，「修饰基因」是这个病例提出的一个假设，而不是它所支持的结论。

JORDAN That's fair. So what does the case actually establish, and what's it hinting at? 说得对。那这个病例究竟确立了什么，又在暗示什么？

ALEX What it establishes is a documented data point at the extreme mild edge of the CF phenotype range — a genotype compatible with life into the eighth decade, with disease quiet enough to stay hidden for seventy years. 它确立的是囊性纤维化表型范围极端温和一端的一个有记录的数据点——一个能让人活到第八个十年的基因型，其病情安静到能隐藏七十年。 What it hints at is the modifier question in its purest form: given two CFTR variants, what determines whether someone presents as a sick infant or an incidentally-diagnosed septuagenarian? 它暗示的是最纯粹形式的修饰基因问题：在两个 CFTR 变异的前提下，是什么决定了一个人是以患病婴儿的身份出现，还是以偶然确诊的七旬老人的身份出现？ Established CF genetics gives part of the answer — residual-function variants and known trans-acting modifiers at other loci both shift severity — but I want to be careful to attribute that to the broader literature, not to this abstract, which names no modifier at all. 已确立的囊性纤维化遗传学给出了部分答案——残余功能变异和已知的、作用于其他基因座的反式修饰基因都会移动严重程度——但我要谨慎地把这归于更广泛的文献，而不是这份摘要，它根本没有点名任何修饰基因。 The value of the case for us is that it is a clean, extreme instance of the discordance that a modifier search is designed to explain. 这个病例对我们的价值在于，它是一个干净、极端的不一致实例，而修饰基因搜索正是为解释这种不一致而设计的。

JORDAN When you say "modifier," what kind of gene are we even talking about — where do they act? 当你说「修饰基因」时，我们说的到底是哪种基因——它们在哪里起作用？
