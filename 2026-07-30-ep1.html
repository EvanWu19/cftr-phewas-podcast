Show notes — Track A — 2026-07-30

Combining multiplexed assays of variant effect for enhanced BRCA2 variant classification — Hu C, et al., Nature Communications, 2026.

Blurb

Two saturation genome editing (SGE) screens independently measured every single-nucleotide variant in the BRCA2 C-terminal DNA binding domain — but disagreed on 16.3% of them (1,041 of 6,383 variants), and current ClinGen rules force those discordant variants to be discarded with no functional weight. This paper refuses that discard. Instead of combining the two studies' category calls, the authors go back to the raw functional scores and fit one calibrated Bayesian hierarchical mixture model ("Integrated VarCall model") across the pooled data, adjusting for batch and outliers. The result outperforms both individual screens and every simpler combination strategy: 98.9% sensitivity and 100% specificity against independent ClinVar and DNA-repair-assay standards, while evaluating 100% of variants and confidently reclassifying 77.3% of the previously discordant ones as pathogenic or benign. Newly-called pathogenic missense variants carry breast- and ovarian-cancer odds ratios (~5.5–6.8) matching established pathogenic variants across All of Us, gnomAD, and case-control cohorts. Where it lands for us: the same problem is arriving for CFTR as multiple functional assays (theratyping, organoid swelling, Ussing currents, deep mutational scans) accumulate on the same variants. The buildable artifact — cftr-mave-integrator — fits a VarCall-style model across two or more CFTR assays anchored on known-benign/pathogenic CFTR controls and outputs a single calibrated PS3/BS3 functional-evidence code per variant.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP framework — the rule-based point system clinical labs use to classify a variant, where each piece of evidence is a code adding or subtracting points toward pathogenic or benign.（中文：临床实验室用来分类变异的规则打分系统，每条证据是一个加减分的代码。）
  • Bayes factor — the strength of evidence favoring one hypothesis (pathogenic) over another (benign); ACMG/AMP maps Bayes-factor thresholds onto evidence strength tiers.（中文：支持一种假设相对另一种假设的证据比值；ACMG/AMP 把它的阈值映射到证据强度档位。）
  • DNA binding domain (DBD) — the self-contained functional module of BRCA2 (exons 15–26) where most established pathogenic missense variants sit.（中文：BRCA2 中大多数已确认致病错义变异所在的功能模块。）
  • haploid cell line — a cell carrying only one copy of the gene, so a single edited allele shows its effect with nothing to mask it (e.g. HAP1).（中文：只带有基因一个拷贝的细胞，单个被编辑的等位基因效果不被掩盖。）
  • HGVS — the standardized syntax for writing a variant.（中文：HGVS，变异书写的标准化规范。）
  • homology-directed repair (HDR) assay — a functional assay measuring BRCA2's real biochemical DNA-repair activity, used here as an independent variant-classification standard.（中文：测量 BRCA2 真实 DNA 修复生化功能的实验，此处用作独立的变异分类标准。）
  • missense variant — a variant that changes a single amino acid in the protein.（中文：改变一个氨基酸的变异。）
  • MAVE / saturation genome editing (SGE) — a multiplexed assay of variant effect; SGE makes every possible single-nucleotide change across a gene region via CRISPR in the native genome and reads function from cell dropout.（中文：多重变异效应实验；SGE 用 CRISPR 在天然基因组里制造某区域每一种单核苷酸改变，从细胞消失读出功能。）
  • PS3 / BS3 — the ACMG evidence codes for functional-assay evidence supporting pathogenicity (PS3) or benignity (BS3).（中文：功能实验证据的 ACMG 代码，PS3 支持致病、BS3 支持良性。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant found in a patient that can't yet be called benign or pathogenic.（中文：在病人身上发现、尚无法判定良性或致病的变异。）

Full citation

Hu C, et al. Combining multiplexed assays of variant effect for enhanced BRCA2 variant classification. Nature Communications. 2026. PMID: 41957374; PMC: PMC13223280; DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-71393-0. (Source: PubMed / PubMed Central.)

Methods / tools to lift

  • Integrated VarCall model — a Bayesian hierarchical model embedding a two-component Gaussian mixture over functional scores; fit on pooled raw scores from both studies (not on their category calls), with batch/replicate random effects and heavy-tailed error terms; posterior → Bayes factor → ACMG-mapped PS3/BS3 strength.
  • Raw-data pooling with cross-assay rescaling — normalize and rescale one assay's scores onto the other's range before stacking replicates; requires similar score ranges.
  • Domain-specific pathogenicity prior — imported here from AlphaMissense (≈22.7% of DBD missense pathogenic → prior 0.2); robustness re-checked at prior 0.1 (99.5% concordant).
  • Dual independent control sets — ClinVar clinical standards and a well-calibrated HDR functional assay, used to rank competing combination models.
  • Case-control risk validation — logistic regression of newly-classified variant groups against All of Us / gnomAD / CARRIERS-BRIDGES controls to confirm real cancer odds ratios.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation.

Paste-ready MVP — cftr-mave-integrator (difficulty M): input = per-variant raw functional scores from ≥2 CFTR assays (theratyping / organoid FIS / Ussing / DMS); fit a VarCall-style Bayesian mixture anchored on known-benign (synonymous/common) and known-pathogenic (established CF-causing) CFTR controls, with assay+batch adjustment; output = one calibrated PS3/BS3 evidence code + strength tier per variant, plus a flag for variants that remain functionally discordant even after joint modeling. Pairs with the 2026-07-28 cftr-calibration-notebook (in silico PP3/BP4 strengths) to cover both the computational and functional evidence streams.

What to verify first

  • Anchor integrity — are there enough unambiguous benign/pathogenic CFTR controls in the assayed region, and are they independent of the assays being calibrated (no circularity)?
  • Prior availability & robustness — is a CFTR domain-specific pathogenicity prior available, and is the output stable to reasonable prior changes (replicate their 0.1-vs-0.2 check)?
  • Functional ≠ final — a strong functional call is one evidence code, not a classification; 25% of their functionally-strong variants still couldn't be finalized without other ACMG codes. Frame the tool as producing better evidence, not verdicts.
  • No registry internals — operates entirely on public + lab-generated variant evidence; makes no claim about registry genotype/data internals and decides no research design.

ALEX Today we're reading "Combining multiplexed assays of variant effect for enhanced BRCA2 variant classification," by Hu and colleagues, published in Nature Communications in 2026. 今天我们读的是 Hu 等人 2026 年发表在《自然·通讯》(Nature Communications) 上的论文《整合多重变异效应实验以增强 BRCA2 变异分类》。 It's an open-access paper and we read the full text. 这是一篇开放获取的论文，我们读了全文。 This is a Track A episode — variant interpretation and harmonization — and even though the gene is BRCA2, the whole point for us is the method. 这是一期 A 轨节目——变异解读与规范化——虽然论文研究的基因是 BRCA2，但对我们来说重点全在方法上。 The question it answers is: when you have two large functional screens of the same variants and they disagree, how do you combine them instead of throwing the disagreements away. 它回答的问题是：当你有两个针对同一批变异的大型功能筛选实验、而它们的结果不一致时，你该如何整合它们，而不是把这些不一致直接丢掉。

JORDAN Before we get into disagreement, walk me through the vocabulary, because there are a few genomics terms here I want pinned down. 在讲"不一致"之前，先帮我把术语理清楚，因为这里有几个基因组学的词我想弄明白。 What is a "multiplexed assay of variant effect"? 什么叫"多重变异效应实验"？

ALEX A multiplexed assay of variant effect — the field calls it a MAVE — is an experiment that measures the functional consequence of thousands of variants in a gene all at once, in a single pooled experiment, instead of one variant at a time. 多重变异效应实验——领域里称为 MAVE (multiplexed assay of variant effect)——是一种实验，它在一次混合实验里同时测量一个基因中成千上万个变异的功能后果，而不是一次只测一个变异。 The specific flavor here is saturation genome editing, or SGE. 这里用的具体类型叫饱和基因组编辑，即 SGE (saturation genome editing)。 "Saturation" means they make every possible single-nucleotide change across a stretch of the gene — every base, every position — nothing is skipped. "饱和"的意思是，他们在基因的一段区域里制造出每一种可能的单核苷酸改变——每个碱基、每个位置——一个都不漏。 They do this with CRISPR-Cas9 editing directly in the native genome of a cell line, and then they let the cells grow. 他们用 CRISPR-Cas9 编辑，直接在细胞系的天然基因组里做这件事，然后让细胞生长。 If a variant kills the gene's function and the gene is essential, cells carrying that variant drop out of the pool over time. 如果某个变异破坏了基因的功能、而这个基因又是细胞存活必需的，那么携带这个变异的细胞会随时间从混合池中逐渐消失。 So the readout — how fast a variant "drops out" — becomes a functional score for pathogenicity. 于是，读出结果——某个变异"消失"的速度——就成了一个衡量致病性的功能评分。

JORDAN And this is happening in a specific part of BRCA2, right? 而且这是在 BRCA2 的某个特定部位进行的，对吧？

ALEX Yes — the C-terminal DNA binding domain, encoded by exons 15 to 26. 对——是 C 端的 DNA 结合结构域 (DNA binding domain, DBD)，由第 15 到 26 号外显子编码。 A domain is a self-contained functional module of the protein. 结构域是蛋白质中一个相对独立的功能模块。 The DNA binding domain is where most of the established pathogenic missense variants in BRCA2 sit. DNA 结合结构域正是 BRCA2 中大多数已确认致病的错义变异 (missense variant，指改变一个氨基酸的变异) 所在的地方。 So it's the highest-yield region to scan if you want to resolve variants of uncertain significance. 所以如果你想解决意义不明变异的问题，扫描这个区域的回报率最高。 A variant of uncertain significance, or VUS, is a variant we've found in a patient but can't yet call benign or pathogenic — and BRCA2 has over five thousand of them in ClinVar. 意义不明变异，即 VUS (variant of uncertain significance)，是指我们在病人身上发现、但还无法判定为良性还是致病的变异——而 BRCA2 在 ClinVar (公共变异-临床意义数据库) 里就有超过五千个这样的变异。

JORDAN One more — you mentioned functional evidence codes. 还有一个——你提到了功能证据代码。 What's the framework the clinical world uses to turn a lab score into a classification? 临床界用来把实验室评分转化为分类的框架是什么？

ALEX That's the ACMG/AMP framework, refined for each gene by a ClinGen Variant Curation Expert Panel. 那就是 ACMG/AMP 框架，并由 ClinGen 变异审编专家组针对每个基因做出细化。 ACMG/AMP is a rule-based point system — each piece of evidence about a variant is a code that adds or subtracts points toward "pathogenic" or "benign." ACMG/AMP 是一套基于规则的打分系统——关于一个变异的每一条证据都是一个代码，向"致病"或"良性"的方向加分或减分。 Functional-assay evidence gets the code PS3 when it supports pathogenicity and BS3 when it supports benignity. 功能实验的证据，在支持致病时对应代码 PS3、在支持良性时对应代码 BS3。 The strength of that code — strong, moderate, supporting — depends on how well the assay separates known-pathogenic from known-benign controls. 这个代码的强度——强、中、支持——取决于这个实验把已知致病和已知良性的对照分开得有多好。 So a MAVE isn't the final answer; it's one strong input into a larger point-based verdict. 所以一个 MAVE 并不是最终答案；它只是那个更大的、基于打分的判定中的一项强证据输入。

JORDAN So set up the actual problem. 那把真正的问题讲清楚。 What did the two studies do, and where did they clash? 这两项研究各自做了什么，它们在哪里起了冲突？

ALEX Two separate groups each ran a saturation genome editing screen of the BRCA2 DNA binding domain. 两个独立的研究组，各自对 BRCA2 的 DNA 结合结构域做了一次饱和基因组编辑筛选。 Huang and colleagues did it in the haploid human HAP1 cell line; Sahu and colleagues did it in a humanized mouse embryonic stem cell line. Huang 等人在单倍体的人类 HAP1 细胞系里做，Sahu 等人在人源化的小鼠胚胎干细胞系里做。 Haploid, by the way, means the cell carries only one copy of the gene, so a single edited allele shows its effect with nothing to mask it. 顺便说一句，单倍体 (haploid) 指细胞只带有该基因的一个拷贝，所以单个被编辑的等位基因会直接显现效果，没有另一份拷贝去掩盖它。 Both groups sorted every variant into the same seven functional categories, from Pathogenic_Strong down through uncertain to Benign_Strong, matching the ClinGen strength tiers. 两组都把每个变异归入相同的七个功能类别，从"强致病"一路经过"不确定"到"强良性"，对应 ClinGen 的强度分级。 Individually, each study was excellent — sensitivity and specificity in the low-to-mid nineties against ClinVar standards. 单独来看，每项研究都很出色——对照 ClinVar 标准的灵敏度和特异度都在九十几的低到中段。 But here's the clash: of 6,383 variants tested in both studies, 1,041 — that's 16.3% — landed in discordant functional categories. 但冲突在这里：在两项研究都测过的 6,383 个变异中，有 1,041 个——也就是 16.3%——落入了不一致的功能类别。

JORDAN And what happens to a discordant variant under the current rules? 在现行规则下，一个不一致的变异会怎样处理？

ALEX Under current ClinGen specifications, if two assays disagree, the variant can't be assigned any functional weight at all. 按照现行的 ClinGen 规范，如果两个实验结果不一致，这个变异就完全不能被赋予任何功能权重。 So the naive approach — call it the "Concordance model" — is to keep only the variants both studies agree on, and discard the 16%. 所以那种最朴素的做法——称之为"一致性模型"——就是只保留两项研究都认同的变异，把那 16% 丢掉。 That's throwing away real, expensive experimental data precisely on the hardest variants, the ones most likely to still be a VUS. 这等于是把真实的、昂贵的实验数据丢掉——而且恰恰丢在最难的那批变异上，也就是最可能仍是 VUS 的那些。 The paper's core move is to refuse that discard. 这篇论文的核心动作，就是拒绝这种丢弃。

JORDAN So how do they combine two disagreeing assays without just averaging them into mush? 那他们怎么整合两个不一致的实验，而又不至于把它们平均成一团糊？

ALEX They tested four combination strategies, and the winner is worth understanding in detail. 他们测试了四种整合策略，而胜出的那个值得我们仔细理解。 It's called the "Integrated VarCall model." 它叫"整合 VarCall 模型"。 VarCall is a Bayesian hierarchical model — it embeds a two-component Gaussian mixture model, one component for the functional-score distribution of pathogenic variants and one for benign, and it infers the probability that each variant belongs to the pathogenic component. VarCall 是一个贝叶斯分层模型——它内嵌了一个双成分高斯混合模型，一个成分对应致病变异的功能评分分布，另一个对应良性变异，然后它推断每个变异属于致病成分的概率。 The critical design choice is that they don't combine the two studies' *category calls*; they go back to the *raw functional scores* from both experiments and fit one model on the pooled raw data. 关键的设计选择在于：他们整合的不是两项研究的*类别判定*，而是回到两个实验的*原始功能评分*，在合并后的原始数据上拟合一个模型。 Because the two cell lines had similar score ranges, they normalized and rescaled the stem-cell data onto the HAP1 scale, then stacked all the replicates together. 由于两个细胞系的评分范围相近，他们把干细胞数据归一化并重新标定到 HAP1 的量纲上，然后把所有重复实验叠在一起。 The model also adjusts for batch and replicate effects and uses heavy-tailed error terms so a few outliers don't drag a variant's call. 这个模型还校正了批次效应和重复效应，并使用重尾误差项，这样少数离群点就不会把一个变异的判定拖偏。 ALEX pushes on one thing here: they set a prior probability of pathogenicity of 0.2 for the domain, borrowed from an AlphaMissense estimate that about 22.7% of DBD missense variants are pathogenic. 我在这里要追问一点：他们给这个结构域设定了 0.2 的致病先验概率，这个数字借自 AlphaMissense (一个蛋白质组范围的错义变异致病性预测工具) 的估计——约 22.7% 的 DBD 错义变异是致病的。 That's an in silico prior feeding into an experimental calibration, which is defensible but is an assumption worth flagging. 这等于把一个计算机预测的先验喂进了一个实验性的校准里，这么做说得通，但确实是一个值得标注的假设。 They checked robustness by re-running with a prior of 0.1 and got 99.5% concordance, which is reassuring. 他们用 0.1 的先验重跑做了稳健性检查，得到 99.5% 的一致性，这让人比较放心。

JORDAN And out of the model comes what — a probability, or an evidence code? 模型输出的是什么——一个概率，还是一个证据代码？

ALEX Both, chained together. 两者都有，而且是串起来的。 From the model's posterior it computes a Bayes factor in favor of pathogenicity for each variant. 从模型的后验分布中，它为每个变异计算一个支持致病的贝叶斯因子 (Bayes factor，即支持一种假设相对另一种的证据比值)。 A Bayes factor is just the strength of evidence one hypothesis has over another, and the ACMG/AMP guidelines already have a Bayesian interpretation that maps Bayes-factor thresholds onto the strong / moderate / supporting evidence tiers. 贝叶斯因子就是一种假设相对另一种假设的证据强度，而 ACMG/AMP 指南本身就有一个贝叶斯式的解读，把贝叶斯因子的阈值映射到强/中/支持这几个证据档位。 So the raw scores become a calibrated PS3 or BS3 code of a specific strength — exactly the object clinical classification needs. 于是原始评分就变成了一个特定强度的、经过校准的 PS3 或 BS3 代码——正是临床分类所需要的那个东西。

JORDAN Does the integration actually beat the individual studies, or is it just tidier? 这种整合真的胜过了单个研究，还是只是更整洁而已？

ALEX It genuinely beats them. 它是真的胜过了它们。 Against 158 pathogenic and benign missense standards from ClinVar that were not influenced by the screens, the Integrated VarCall model hit 98.9% sensitivity and 100% specificity, with a single discrepant variant. 对照 158 个未受这些筛选影响的 ClinVar 致病和良性错义标准，整合 VarCall 模型达到了 98.9% 的灵敏度和 100% 的特异度，只有一个变异判错。 The naive Concordance model managed 95.6% sensitivity and 97% specificity with six errors — and remember it could only evaluate 80% of variants in the first place. 而朴素的一致性模型是 95.6% 的灵敏度、97% 的特异度、六个错误——而且别忘了，它一开始就只能评估 80% 的变异。 The other combination models, an Integrated GMM and a Secondary concordance model, landed in between. 另外两个整合模型——整合 GMM 和二级一致性模型——则落在中间。 They confirmed the ranking against a second, independent control set from a homology-directed DNA repair assay, where VarCall again led at 97% sensitivity and 100% specificity. 他们又用第二个独立对照集——一个同源定向 DNA 修复实验 (测 BRCA2 真实生化功能的实验) 的结果——验证了这个排名，VarCall 再次领先，97% 灵敏度、100% 特异度。 And here's the payoff number: of the 1,041 originally discordant variants, the Integrated VarCall model confidently classified 805 of them — 77.3% — as pathogenic or benign. 而这就是关键收益：在最初 1,041 个不一致的变异里，整合 VarCall 模型自信地把其中 805 个——77.3%——判定为了致病或良性。 The disagreements weren't noise to discard; most of them were resolvable signal once you modeled the raw data jointly. 这些不一致并不是该丢弃的噪声；一旦你对原始数据做联合建模，其中大多数都是可以解决的信号。

JORDAN They also ran a cancer-risk check on the missense variants they newly called pathogenic. 他们还对新判为致病的错义变异做了一次癌症风险检验。

ALEX Right, and that's the validation that matters clinically. 对，而这正是临床上最要紧的验证。 The strongly-pathogenic missense variants carried a breast-cancer odds ratio of about 5.5, rising to 6.5 when restricted to the highest-confidence calls, and an ovarian-cancer odds ratio near 6.8. 被判为强致病的错义变异，其乳腺癌的比值比 (odds ratio) 约为 5.5，限制到最高置信度的判定时升到 6.5，卵巢癌的比值比接近 6.8。 Those risks match what you'd expect for established pathogenic variants, across several independent control cohorts including All of Us and gnomAD. 这些风险与已确认致病变异的预期相符，而且在多个独立对照队列里都成立，包括 All of Us 和 gnomAD (一个大型公共人群等位基因频率数据库)。 So the functionally-defined pathogenic calls behave like real pathogenic variants in patients, not just in a dish. 所以这些由功能定义出的致病判定，在病人身上的表现确实像真正的致病变异，而不只是在培养皿里。
