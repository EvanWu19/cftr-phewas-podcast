Show notes — Track B — Automated real-world data integration improves cancer outcome prediction (MSK-CHORD; Jee et al., 2024)

Blurb. Jee and the MSK data-science group built MSK-CHORD, a harmonized clinicogenomic dataset of 24,950 patients across five cancers (NSCLC 7,809; breast 5,368; colorectal 5,543; prostate 3,211; pancreatic 3,109), fusing a library of document-type-specific NLP models (radiology / oncology notes / pathology) with structured medication, demographic, registry, and OncoKB-annotated genomic data. Models including NLP-derived features (especially disease sites) beat genomics-only and stage-only baselines for overall survival, validated by cross-validation and an external multi-institution dataset; mining 705,241 radiology reports also surfaced organ-site metastasis predictors (e.g. SETD2 → lower metastatic potential in IO-treated lung adenocarcinoma). Code: github.com/clinical-data-mining; data: cBioPortal (msk_chord_2024). Serves AI-tooling/career (secondary: Liz phenotype & data quality, method only); buildable artifact = an NLP-feature-to-prognostic-baseline harness with an explicit model-vs-human audit loop.

Plain-English glossary

  • Clinicogenomic dataset — links each patient's clinical course (diagnoses, treatments, outcomes) to their tumour's genomic profile.（中文：把患者临床过程与肿瘤基因组特征关联起来的数据集。）
  • Clonal hematopoiesis — age-related mutations arising in blood-cell lineages that can masquerade as tumour mutations unless subtracted.（中文：随年龄在血细胞谱系中产生、若不减去会被误当成肿瘤突变的变异。）
  • Germline variant — inherited DNA present in every cell, distinct from somatic tumour-driving mutations.（中文：每个细胞都携带的遗传性 DNA，区别于驱动肿瘤的体细胞突变。）
  • Metastasis (organ-site label) — spread of a tumour to a distant organ; the structured tag naming which organ a report describes.（中文：肿瘤扩散到远处器官；器官部位标签即指明报告所述器官的结构化标记。）
  • MSK-IMPACT — a targeted tumour-sequencing panel (a few hundred cancer genes, deep) with matched white-blood-cell sequencing.（中文：MSK-IMPACT — 靶向肿瘤测序 panel，配对白细胞测序以过滤胚系与克隆性造血。）
  • OncoKB — curated knowledge base annotating which alterations are oncogenic (cancer-driving).（中文：OncoKB — 标注哪些改变具致癌性的精选知识库。）
  • Overall survival (OS) — time from a defined start until death from any cause; the gold-standard cancer endpoint.（中文：总生存期 — 从定义起点到任何原因死亡的时间，癌症金标准终点。）
  • Tumour registry — structured center record of cancer type, stage, and survival, often for regulatory reporting.（中文：肿瘤登记库 — 记录癌种、分期、生存的结构化中心记录。）
  • Weak supervision — generating large volumes of imperfect training labels cheaply (ontology rules / existing signals) instead of hand-labeling.（中文：弱监督 — 用规则/已有信号廉价生成大量不完美训练标签，替代人工标注。）

Full citation

Jee J, Fong C, Pichotta K, ... Li BT, Riely GJ, Schultz N; MSK Cancer Data Science Initiative Group. Automated real-world data integration improves cancer outcome prediction. Nature. 2024. PMID: 39506116 · PMC: PMC11655358 · DOI: 10.1038/s41586-024-08167-5

Methods / tools to lift

  • Document-type-specific NLP model library — one annotation model per source type (radiology impressions / oncology notes / pathology reports) emitting structured labels (disease site, response).
  • Model-vs-human audit loop — independent reviewers re-read false positives/negatives; in several cases the original human curation label was the error, not the model.
  • Weak supervision — ontology-driven rules + existing structured signals generate cheap imperfect training labels at scale.
  • Dated, versioned snapshot — fixed Sept-2023 cut for reproducibility against an ever-mutating RWD source.
  • External multi-institution validation — guards against single-institution overfitting of both NLP and prognostic models.
  • Open deliverables — analysis code (github.com/clinical-data-mining); MSK-CHORD downloadable on cBioPortal (CC licence).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: AI-tooling / career (secondary: Liz — phenotype & data quality, method only).

Tool sketch (paste-ready MVP): NLP-feature-to-prognostic-baseline harness — input a clinical-note corpus + structured outcome → configurable annotation step emits a tidy feature table → train & compare two survival models (with vs without NLP features) on identical CV splits → report discrimination + calibration gap with bootstrap CIs; include a label-disagreement reviewer that surfaces model-vs-existing-label conflicts. Input: note corpus + outcome table. Difficulty: M (core = annotation + audit loop, not modeling). External method only — no registry internals.

What to verify first

  • Transportability — do annotation models tuned on one institution's note style keep precision/recall on a different style? Clinical language is local.
  • Label noise — measure each extracted feature's FP/FN rate and whether survival estimates are sensitive to it; don't assume labels are clean.
  • Calibration & subgroups — check calibration (not just discrimination) and performance across ancestry/site, not just the majority group.
  • Selection — MSK-CHORD is the intersection of "sequenced" ∩ "registry-documented"; carry that selection into any reuse.
  • No registry-internals claim — external method on an external oncology cohort only.

JORDAN Give me the vocabulary before we go deep. 在深入之前，先给我把词汇过一遍。What is a "clinicogenomic" dataset, and what is a "tumour registry"? 什么是"临床基因组（clinicogenomic）"数据集，什么是"肿瘤登记库（tumour registry）"？

ALEX A clinicogenomic dataset links each patient's clinical course — diagnoses, treatments, outcomes — to their tumour's genomic profile. 临床基因组数据集把每个患者的临床过程——诊断、治疗、结局——和他们肿瘤的基因组特征联系起来。A tumour registry is the structured record cancer centers keep of cancer type, stage, and survival, often for regulatory reporting. 肿瘤登记库是癌症中心保存的结构化记录，包含癌症类型、分期和生存情况，通常用于法规上报。The trick this paper pulls is fusing that structured registry layer with messy free-text notes and with tumour DNA sequencing into one harmonized object. 这篇论文的巧妙之处在于，把结构化的登记层、杂乱的自由文本病历、以及肿瘤 DNA 测序融合成一个统一的对象。

JORDAN And the sequencing — they mention MSK-IMPACT and something about clonal hematopoiesis. 还有那个测序——他们提到了 MSK-IMPACT，以及什么"克隆性造血"。

ALEX MSK-IMPACT is a targeted tumour-sequencing panel — it reads a few hundred cancer-relevant genes deeply rather than the whole genome shallowly. MSK-IMPACT 是一个靶向肿瘤测序 panel——它对几百个癌症相关基因做深度测序，而不是对全基因组做浅层测序。Crucially, it sequences matched white blood cells alongside the tumour, which lets them subtract two confounders. 关键在于，它同时对配对的白细胞和肿瘤测序，这样就能减去两个混杂因素。One is germline variants — the inherited DNA you carry in every cell, which is not what is driving the tumour. 一个是胚系变异（germline variant，你每个细胞里都携带的遗传性 DNA），它不是驱动肿瘤的因素。The other is clonal hematopoiesis — mutations that arise in blood-cell lineages with age and would otherwise masquerade as tumour mutations. 另一个是克隆性造血（clonal hematopoiesis，随年龄在血细胞谱系中产生的突变），否则它会被误当成肿瘤突变。To call a mutation "oncogenic," they annotate against OncoKB, a curated knowledge base of which alterations actually drive cancer. 为了判定某个突变是否"致癌"，他们用 OncoKB 来注释——这是一个关于"哪些改变真正驱动癌症"的精选知识库。

JORDAN Two more terms so I follow the results — "overall survival" and "metastasis." 再两个词，这样我能跟上结果——"总生存期"和"转移"。

ALEX Overall survival is the simplest, hardest outcome — time from a defined start until death from any cause. 总生存期（overall survival）是最简单也最硬的结局——从一个定义的起点到任何原因死亡的时间。It is the gold-standard cancer endpoint because it is unambiguous, but it is slow to accumulate, which is why prognostic models that read it well are valuable. 它是癌症的金标准终点，因为它没有歧义，但它积累得慢，这正是能很好预测它的预后模型有价值的原因。Metastasis is the spread of a tumour to a distant organ — liver, brain, bone — and which organ it lands in carries prognostic and treatment meaning. 转移（metastasis）是肿瘤扩散到远处器官——肝、脑、骨——而它落在哪个器官，带有预后和治疗上的含义。So an "organ-site label" is just a structured tag saying "this report describes disease in the liver," extracted from a radiologist's free-text impression. 所以"器官部位标签"只是一个结构化标签，写着"这份报告描述了肝脏的病灶"，是从放射科医生的自由文本印象里抽提出来的。

JORDAN So what was the actual problem they set out to solve? 那他们着手解决的真正问题是什么？

ALEX The problem is that the richest signal in oncology is locked in free text. 问题在于，肿瘤学中最丰富的信号被锁在自由文本里。Radiology impressions, oncology visit notes, pathology reports — these hold disease sites, response, progression — but historically you needed humans to abstract them into structured fields. 影像学印象、肿瘤科就诊记录、病理报告——这些包含了疾病部位、治疗反应、进展信息——但历史上你需要人工把它们抽提成结构化字段。The flagship effort to do that by hand is the AACR Project GENIE biopharma collaborative, which produced cohorts of a couple thousand patients each through expensive manual curation. 用人工做这件事的旗舰项目是 AACR 的 GENIE 生物制药协作组，它通过昂贵的人工整理，做出了每个几千名患者的队列。The bet here is that natural language processing — transformer models that read clinical text — can do that annotation automatically, at much larger scale and near-zero marginal cost. 这里的赌注是：自然语言处理（natural language processing）——能读临床文本的 transformer 模型——可以自动完成这种注释，规模大得多，而且边际成本接近于零。

JORDAN And the unknown they were testing? 他们要检验的未知是什么？

ALEX Whether automatically-extracted features actually improve outcome prediction over the structured data we already trust, like stage and genomics. 也就是：自动抽提的特征，能否真的比我们已经信任的结构化数据（比如分期和基因组数据）更好地改善结局预测。Stage, in particular, is the incumbent — it already dictates many treatment decisions, so beating it is a meaningful bar. 尤其是分期，它是现有的标杆——它已经决定了许多治疗决策，所以超过它是一个有意义的门槛。If NLP-derived features beat stage, that is an argument that the free text carries prognostic information the structured fields throw away. 如果 NLP 衍生的特征能胜过分期，那就证明自由文本携带了结构化字段丢弃掉的预后信息。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built. 带我过一遍他们到底构建了什么。

ALEX They assembled MSK-CHORD from 24,950 patients at MSK with both tumour sequencing and completed registry entries, spanning five cancers — non-small-cell lung at 7,809, breast at 5,368, colorectal at 5,543, prostate at 3,211, and pancreatic at 3,109. 他们用 MSK 的 24,950 名既有肿瘤测序又有完整登记记录的患者组建了 MSK-CHORD，涵盖五种癌症——非小细胞肺癌 7,809 例、乳腺癌 5,368 例、结直肠癌 5,543 例、前列腺癌 3,211 例、胰腺癌 3,109 例。The core engine is a library of NLP models, each trained to read one document type — radiology impressions, medical oncology notes, or pathology reports — and emit a structured label like a disease site or a response status. 核心引擎是一个 NLP 模型库，每个模型被训练去读一种文档类型——影像学印象、肿瘤内科记录、或病理报告——并输出一个结构化标签，比如疾病部位或治疗反应状态。They annotated 705,241 radiology reports with these models, which is the scale manual curation simply cannot reach. 他们用这些模型注释了 705,241 份影像学报告，这是人工整理根本达不到的规模。Then they layered in structured medication, demographic, registry, and OncoKB-annotated genomic data, and trained machine-learning models to predict overall survival. 然后他们叠加了结构化的用药、人口学、登记和经 OncoKB 注释的基因组数据，并训练机器学习模型来预测总生存期。

JORDAN Where would you push back on the design? 这个设计你会在哪里提出质疑？

ALEX My main pushback is error propagation. 我的主要质疑是误差传播。Every NLP-extracted feature is a model output with its own false-positive and false-negative rate, and you are then feeding those noisy labels into a second predictive model as if they were ground truth. 每一个 NLP 抽提的特征都是一个有自身假阳性和假阴性率的模型输出，而你接着把这些带噪声的标签当作真值，喂给第二个预测模型。To their credit, they audited this — they had independent reviewers re-read false positives and false negatives, and in several cases they found the original human curation labels were actually the ones that were wrong, not the model. 值得肯定的是，他们对此做了审计——他们让独立的审阅者重新读假阳性和假阴性，结果在好几个案例里，他们发现原本出错的是人工整理的标签，而不是模型。That is an honest and important finding, but it also underlines that the "gold standard" is itself noisy. 这是一个诚实而重要的发现，但它也凸显了"金标准"本身就是带噪声的。The second pushback is generalization: these NLP models were trained on MSK's note style, and clinical language is institution-specific, so the transportability claim needs its own evidence. 第二个质疑是泛化性：这些 NLP 模型是在 MSK 的病历书写风格上训练的，而临床语言因机构而异，所以可迁移性的主张需要它自己的证据。

JORDAN Did the integration actually pay off? 这种整合真的有回报吗？

ALEX Yes, and the cleanest result is the head-to-head on survival prediction. 有，而最干净的结果是生存预测上的正面对决。Models that included NLP-derived features — especially sites of disease — outperformed models built on genomic data or stage alone, tested both by cross-validation and on an external, multi-institution dataset. 包含 NLP 衍生特征的模型——尤其是疾病部位——胜过了仅基于基因组数据或仅基于分期的模型，这一点在交叉验证和一个外部的、多机构数据集上都得到了检验。The external validation matters because it argues the gain is not just MSK-specific overfitting. 外部验证很重要，因为它说明这种提升不只是 MSK 特异性的过拟合。For early-stage lung, colorectal, and pancreatic cancer, models trained on single data classes — including the stage incumbent — did worse than models trained on all classes together. 对于早期肺癌、结直肠癌和胰腺癌，仅用单一类别数据训练的模型——包括作为现有标杆的分期——表现都不如用所有类别一起训练的模型。

JORDAN And the NLP-at-scale gave them something extra beyond prediction? NLP 的大规模应用还给了他们预测之外的额外东西吗？

ALEX It did. 是的。By mining those 705,241 radiology reports, they could map predictors of metastasis to specific organ sites. 通过挖掘那 705,241 份影像学报告，他们能够绘制出转移到特定器官部位的预测因子。The standout example is a relationship between SETD2 mutation and lower metastatic potential in immunotherapy-treated lung adenocarcinoma, and they corroborated it in independent datasets. 最突出的例子是 SETD2 突变与免疫治疗的肺腺癌中较低转移潜能之间的关系，他们还在独立数据集中加以佐证。That is a discovery you can only make once you have organ-site labels on hundreds of thousands of reports, which is precisely what the NLP layer manufactured. 这是只有当你在数十万份报告上拥有器官部位标签时才能做出的发现，而这恰恰是 NLP 层制造出来的东西。

JORDAN Before we get to impact — how did they train the annotation models without hand-labeling everything? 在谈影响之前——他们是怎么在不手工标注所有东西的情况下训练注释模型的？

ALEX That is the engineering crux, and it leans on weak supervision. 这正是工程上的关键，它依赖于弱监督（weak supervision）。Instead of a human labeling every report, they use ontology-driven rules and existing structured signals to generate large volumes of imperfect training labels cheaply, then train transformer models on that. 与其让人去标注每一份报告，他们用本体驱动的规则和已有的结构化信号，廉价地生成大量不完美的训练标签，然后在此之上训练 transformer 模型。The models were validated by cross-validation and on held-out cancer types, and the fact that they generalized to cancers not in the training mix is the evidence that the NLP tools transfer across solid tumours. 这些模型通过交叉验证和留出的癌种来验证，而它们能泛化到训练里没有的癌种这一事实，正是 NLP 工具能在实体瘤之间迁移的证据。Two practical details I like: the whole dataset is a dated snapshot — a fixed September 2023 cut — so analyses are reproducible, and they validated the prognostic models against an external multi-institution dataset rather than only their own. 我喜欢两个实操细节：整个数据集是一个带日期的快照——固定为 2023 年 9 月的切片——所以分析是可复现的；而且他们用一个外部的多机构数据集来验证预后模型，而不只是用自己的。And the deliverable is genuinely reusable — all the analysis code is on GitHub, and MSK-CHORD itself is downloadable from cBioPortal under a Creative Commons licence, so the annotation-then-integrate pattern is one you can actually inspect and copy. 而且这个交付物是真正可复用的——所有分析代码都在 GitHub 上，MSK-CHORD 本身可以从 cBioPortal 以知识共享许可下载，所以"先注释再整合"的模式是你真的能检视和照搬的。

JORDAN That dated-snapshot habit is underrated. 那个"带日期快照"的习惯被低估了。

ALEX It is, and it is the cheapest reproducibility win there is — a real-world dataset that keeps mutating under you is unfalsifiable, and a frozen, versioned cut fixes that for almost no cost. 确实，而且它是最廉价的可复现性收益——一个在你脚下不停变动的真实世界数据集是不可证伪的，而一个冻结的、带版本的切片几乎零成本地解决了这个问题。 The cohort definition is also worth stating plainly: every patient had tumour genomic sequencing and a completed registry entry, so MSK-CHORD is intentionally the intersection of "sequenced" and "registry-documented," not a random cancer sample. 队列的定义也值得明说：每个患者都有肿瘤基因组测序和一份完整的登记记录，所以 MSK-CHORD 有意是"已测序"和"已登记记录"两者的交集，而不是一个随机的癌症样本。That intersection is a selection choice you would carry into any reuse of the data. 这个交集是一个选择性偏倚，你在任何对该数据的复用中都会带着它。

JORDAN How much bigger is this than the hand-curated effort it builds on? 这比它所基于的人工整理工作大多少？
