Track A — CF/CFTR Variant Interpretation & Harmonization

Blurb

This episode reads Gattu Linga et al. (2026, Genes), which builds an uncertainty-aware machine learning framework for triaging variants of uncertain significance (VUS) in LDLR, the gene behind most genetically-confirmed familial hypercholesterolemia. The authors pull 3962 ClinVar LDLR variants, train calibrated XGBoost/RandomForest/SVM models on 2870 labeled pathogenic/benign variants, and apply the result to 1092 held-out VUS. The headline isn't the raw discrimination (missense-only ROC-AUC ~0.97–0.98) — it's the explicit handling of disagreement: REVEL and AlphaMissense disagree on direction for ~22% of missense VUS, and rather than averaging that disagreement away, the framework flags it as Predictor_Discordant_VUS and routes it to orthogonal evidence instead of a forced classification. It's not a model to retrain for CFTR; it's a template for the discordance-flagging and calibrated-evidence-mapping logic that generalizes. The buildable artifact is a small script that applies this paper's published REVEL/AlphaMissense concordance thresholds to a CFTR VUS list and outputs a PP3/BP4-style tag per variant for human review.

Glossary

  • ACMG/AMP — The joint American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology framework for classifying variants into five tiers (pathogenic, likely pathogenic, uncertain, likely benign, benign).（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会与分子病理学会联合制定的变异五级分类框架）
  • AlphaMissense — DeepMind's structure- and sequence-based deep learning model that scores missense variants, trained using population-frequency-derived weak labels rather than clinical assertions.（中文：基于结构和序列的深度学习错义变异评分模型，用人群频率弱标签而非临床标签训练）
  • CADD — Combined Annotation Dependent Depletion; integrates many genomic annotations into one deleteriousness score usable across variant types, not just missense.（中文：综合注释依赖消减评分，跨变异类型的综合有害性评分）
  • ClinVar — NCBI's public archive of variant clinical-significance submissions, each carrying a review-status star rating.（中文：NCBI维护的变异临床意义提交公共档案库，带审核星级）
  • FH-VCEP — The ClinGen Familial Hypercholesterolemia Variant Curation Expert Panel, which re-adjudicates LDLR variants using gene-specific ACMG/AMP criteria.（中文：ClinGen家族性高胆固醇血症变异解读专家小组，按基因特异标准复核变异）
  • Grantham distance — A quantitative measure of the physicochemical difference between two amino acids, used as a feature for predicting missense severity.（中文：衡量两种氨基酸物理化学性质差异的量化指标，用作错义变异严重度特征）
  • PP3 / BP4 — ACMG/AMP evidence codes for computational (in silico) support toward pathogenic (PP3) or benign (BP4) classification, each gradable as supporting, moderate, or strong.（中文：ACMG/AMP中代表计算证据支持致病[PP3]或良性[BP4]的证据代码，分支持/中等/强三级）
  • Predictor concordance / discordance — Whether two or more in silico predictors (here REVEL and AlphaMissense) agree or disagree in direction on a given variant.（中文：两个或多个计算预测工具对同一变异的预测方向是否一致）
  • REVEL — A curated ensemble meta-predictor combining several missense-effect tools into one calibrated pathogenicity score.（中文：整合多个错义变异效应工具的经校准集成元预测器）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant that cannot yet be confidently classified as pathogenic or benign.（中文：意义未明变异，尚无法确信判定为致病或良性的变异）
  • VEP (Variant Effect Predictor) — Ensembl's tool for annotating variant consequence and functional-impact predictions.（中文：Ensembl的变异后果与功能影响注释工具）

Methods/tools to lift

  • Predictor-concordance tiering — fixed REVEL/AlphaMissense thresholds (REVEL ≥0.7 & AM ≥0.564 = concordant-pathogenic; REVEL <0.3 & AM <0.34 = concordant-benign; specific crossed thresholds = discordant) that explicitly flag disagreement instead of averaging it away.
  • 18-tier calibrated-probability framework — converts a single calibrated P(pathogenic) into graded confidence tiers (high/moderate/low/unresolved) rather than a binary call.
  • Splice-rescue rule — a VEP SpliceRegion-based post-processing layer that reclassifies otherwise-unresolved non-coding VUS, explicitly separable from the trained model.
  • PP3/BP4 evidence-code mapping — Pejaver-calibrated thresholds that turn model probabilities into standard ACMG/AMP computational evidence strengths.
  • Multi-seed stability check — 10 models (5 seeds × 2 split-halves) scoring every VUS, used to flag a model-instability dimension orthogonal to predictor discordance.
  • Local API-response caching + fixed seeds — a small reproducibility practice worth copying into any annotation pipeline.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands for Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation & harmonization — the discordance-flagging and evidence-mapping logic transfers; the trained model itself does not need to.

MVP: a script that pulls REVEL and AlphaMissense scores for a CFTR VUS list (from ClinVar/dbNSFP), applies this paper's published concordance thresholds, tags each variant concordant-pathogenic / concordant-benign / discordant / insufficient-evidence, and outputs a PP3/BP4-style tier per variant for human review. Input: CFTR VUS list. Difficulty: S.

What to verify first

  • Do REVEL's and AlphaMissense's published thresholds, calibrated across many pooled genes, hold up for CFTR's own missense spectrum, or would CFTR need its own recalibration the way this paper effectively did for LDLR?
  • Is a ClinVar-derived CFTR VUS set large enough to support this kind of tiering, given CFTR's smaller variant catalog and that much of its best evidence already lives in CFTR2 rather than ClinVar alone?
  • Would a model-instability check (this paper's 13.2% direction-flipping finding) be worth building for CFTR, where any retraining sample would be far smaller than LDLR's 2870 labeled variants?
  • Does the paper's missense-only calibration (built on only 54 confirmed benign missense examples) generalize beyond LDLR's particular benign/pathogenic ratio?

JORDAN Before we get into it, let's nail down the vocabulary, because this paper leans hard on it. 在深入之前，我们先把词汇弄清楚，因为这篇论文高度依赖这些术语。 What exactly is a VUS? VUS到底是什么？

ALEX A variant of uncertain significance, VUS for short, is a change in DNA sequence that clinical geneticists can't yet confidently call either disease-causing or harmless. 意义未明变异，简称VUS，是指临床遗传学家目前还无法确信地判定为致病或良性的DNA序列变化。 It sits in the middle of a five-tier ACMG/AMP classification scale — ACMG/AMP is the joint American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and Association for Molecular Pathology framework for calling variants pathogenic, likely pathogenic, uncertain, likely benign, or benign. ACMG/AMP（美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会与分子病理学会联合制定的变异分级框架）将变异分为五级：致病、可能致病、意义未明、可能良性和良性，VUS正处于这个量表的中间位置。 Within that framework, PP3 and BP4 are specific evidence codes: PP3 is "supporting pathogenic" computational evidence, and BP4 is "supporting benign" computational evidence, each gradable as supporting, moderate, or strong. 在这个框架内，PP3和BP4是两个特定的证据代码：PP3代表"支持致病"的计算证据，BP4代表"支持良性"的计算证据，二者都可以分为支持级、中等级或强级。

AlphaMissense（DeepMind开发的基于结构和序列的深度学习模型）利用蛋白质结构信息，再加上基于人群频率得出的弱标签而非临床标签来为错义变异打分。 CADD, or Combined Annotation Dependent Depletion, integrates many genomic annotations into a single deleteriousness score usable across variant types, not just missense. CADD（综合注释依赖消减评分，Combined Annotation Dependent Depletion）把多种基因组注释整合成一个可用于所有变异类型、而不仅限于错义变异的有害性评分。 PolyPhen-2 and SIFT are older structure- and conservation-based missense predictors that still show up as baseline comparators. PolyPhen-2和SIFT是较早的、基于结构和保守性的错义变异预测工具，至今仍常作为基线对照出现。

JORDAN One more — ClinVar review status. 还有一个——ClinVar审核状态。

ALEX ClinVar is the NCBI's public archive of variant-interpretation submissions. ClinVar是NCBI（美国国家生物技术信息中心）维护的变异解读提交记录公共档案库。 Each submission carries a review-status "star rating," and a three-star record means an expert panel — in this case the ClinGen Familial Hypercholesterolemia Variant Curation Expert Panel, or FH-VCEP — formally re-adjudicated the variant using gene-specific criteria, which is a much stronger label than a single lab's one-star submission. 每条提交记录都带有审核状态的"星级"，三星记录意味着某个专家小组——此处是ClinGen家族性高胆固醇血症变异解读专家小组（FH-VCEP）——按照该基因特有的标准对变异进行了正式复核判定，这比单个实验室提交的一星记录可信得多。

JORDAN Okay, so why LDLR, and why is this problem hard? 好，那为什么是LDLR，这个问题难在哪？

ALEX LDLR is the low-density lipoprotein receptor gene, and pathogenic variants in it cause about 85 to 90 percent of genetically confirmed familial hypercholesterolemia, FH, an inherited disorder with a heterozygote prevalence around one in 300 people worldwide. LDLR是低密度脂蛋白受体基因，其致病变异导致约85%到90%经基因确诊的家族性高胆固醇血症（FH），这是一种遗传性疾病，全球异质携带者患病率约为三百分之一。 FH is substantially underdiagnosed, which delays lipid-lowering treatment and raises the risk of premature cardiovascular disease. FH存在严重的诊断不足问题，这会延误降脂治疗，并增加早发心血管疾病的风险。 LDLR itself is structurally complex — multiple ligand-binding repeats, EGF-like domains, and trafficking-regulation regions — so variants across these domains can affect receptor expression, LDL binding, or cellular recycling in very different ways, and ClinVar has thousands of LDLR variants still parked as VUS. LDLR本身结构复杂——包含多个配体结合重复序列、EGF样结构域（表皮生长因子样结构域）以及受体循环调控区域——因此分布在这些结构域中的变异可能以截然不同的方式影响受体表达、LDL结合或细胞内循环，而ClinVar中仍有数千个LDLR变异停留在VUS状态。

JORDAN And just throwing predictors at the problem doesn't fully work? 那直接套用预测工具并不能完全解决问题？

ALEX Right, for three reasons the authors lay out. 对，作者列出了三个原因。 First, performance on heterogeneous benchmarks can be inflated because pathogenicity labels in ClinVar correlate strongly with variant type — a model can look great just by learning that frameshift variants tend to be pathogenic and synonymous variants tend to be benign, without learning anything about missense biology specifically. 第一，在异质基准测试上的表现可能被夸大，因为ClinVar中的致病性标签与变异类型本身高度相关——模型可能仅仅学会了移码变异（frameshift variant）往往致病、同义变异往往良性，而根本没有学到任何关于错义变异生物学本身的东西，就能表现得很出色。 Second, naively averaging multiple predictors when they disagree can manufacture overconfident calls instead of surfacing real uncertainty. 第二，当多个预测工具意见不一致时，简单地取平均值可能制造出过度自信的判定，而不是把真正的不确定性呈现出来。 Third, incomplete annotation coverage — REVEL and AlphaMissense aren't available for every variant — can mean a confident-looking prediction is actually resting on thin evidence. 第三，注释覆盖不完整——REVEL和AlphaMissense并非对每个变异都有数据——可能意味着一个看起来很有把握的预测，实际上建立在薄弱的证据之上。

JORDAN So the framework has to do more than just run the predictors and average them. 所以这个框架必须做的，不只是跑一遍预测工具再取平均。

ALEX Exactly, and that's the design story in this paper. 正是如此，这也正是本文的设计思路所在。 They pulled 3962 LDLR variants from ClinVar, normalized the clinical-significance labels into three buckets — 1880 pathogenic or likely pathogenic, 990 benign or likely benign, and 1092 VUS held out for prediction — and built a 45-feature, later expanded to 68-feature, pipeline covering sequence properties, allele frequency with explicit missingness flags, molecular consequence, the in silico predictor scores, amino-acid biochemistry like Grantham distance and hydrophobicity change, a splice indicator, and interaction terms. 他们从ClinVar中提取了3962个LDLR变异，将临床意义标签归一化为三类——1880个致病或可能致病、990个良性或可能良性，以及留作预测目标的1092个VUS——并构建了一个包含45个特征（后扩展到68个）的流程，涵盖序列属性、带明确缺失标记的等位基因频率、分子后果（molecular consequence）、计算预测工具分数、氨基酸生化特性（如Grantham距离和疏水性变化）、剪接（splicing）指示变量，以及交互项。 They trained RandomForest, XGBoost, and SVM with nested cross-validation, then calibrated the best model with isotonic regression so its output probabilities are actually trustworthy numbers, not just a ranking. 他们用嵌套交叉验证训练了RandomForest、XGBoost和SVM这三类模型，再用等渗回归（isotonic regression）对最佳模型进行校准，使其输出的概率真正成为可信的数值，而不仅仅是一个排序。

JORDAN And then there's the concordance piece. 然后还有一致性（concordance）这部分。

JORDAN One more methods detail before results — the missense-only model's training data sounds thin. 在看结果之前还有一个方法细节——仅针对错义变异的模型，其训练数据听起来比较薄弱。

JORDAN And REVEL or AlphaMissense alone couldn't get there? 单靠REVEL或AlphaMissense自己做不到这个水平？
