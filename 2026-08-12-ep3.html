Ep3 — Track C — A better performing algorithm for identification of implausible growth data (Harrall 2024)

A rule-based, non-parametric challenger to growthcleanr and two other published pediatric growth-cleaning algorithms (Shi et al., Phan et al.). Instead of comparing each measurement to a statistical deviation score (an EWMA-style weighted moving average, or a fitted regression), the Harrall algorithm scans the line segments connecting consecutive measurements and flags "caps" (local peaks) and "cups" (local troughs) using simple ordering and smoothness rules — no manual curation required. Validated entirely through Monte Carlo simulation (9,000 replicates of Preece-Baines-model growth curves with known injected errors, matched to the EPOCH cohort's measurement spacing), it achieves sensitivity comparable to existing algorithms (~0.99) but meaningfully higher specificity — 0.928 vs. 0.400–0.903 for height, and 0.908 vs. 0.127–0.786 for weight, a roughly nine-in-ten vs. one-in-ten true-error catch rate at the low end. It's a direct alternative candidate generator for the outlier-flagging stage of a growth-data cleaning pipeline, with a specificity advantage that would shrink a downstream review queue — but the authors themselves flag that it's unvalidated for scenarios of rapid, biologically real weight change, which is exactly the modulator-era-style stress test worth running before trusting it broadly.

Plain-English glossary

Biologically implausible value (BIV) — A height or weight measurement that falls outside expected population norms or breaks an individual's own growth trajectory.（中文:身高或体重测量值超出人群预期常模范围,或打破个体自身生长轨迹规律。）

Cap / cup — A local peak ("cap") or local trough ("cup") in a plotted growth trajectory, formed where the line segments before and after a point point the "wrong" way for plausible growth.（中文:生长轨迹图中的局部峰值点(cap)或局部谷值点(cup),即该点前后线段的走向不符合合理生长规律的位置。）

Preece-Baines model — A named nonlinear parametric growth curve model used to simulate biologically realistic childhood height/weight trajectories, rather than a straight line or simple polynomial.（中文:一种非线性参数化生长曲线模型,用于模拟符合生物学规律的儿童身高体重轨迹,而非简单的直线或多项式曲线。）

EWMA (exponentially weighted moving average) — A moving-average method that gives more weight to recent data points, used by comparator algorithms like growthcleanr as the reference a measurement is compared against.（中文:一种给近期数据点更高权重的滑动平均方法,growthcleanr等对比算法用它作为测量值比较的参照基准。）

Silver standard — Ground truth derived from expert clinician manual review, distinguished from a true "gold standard" because experienced reviewers can disagree with each other or with their own prior judgment.（中文:由专家临床医生人工复核得出的参照标准,之所以称为"白银标准"而非真正的"金标准",是因为经验丰富的复核者之间、甚至同一人前后判断都可能出现分歧。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Rule-based cap/cup/repeat detection on trajectory line segments — no global parametric growth assumption, no manual curation.
  • CDC-percentile-derived weight-velocity threshold for deciding which flagged weight points to remove.
  • Monte Carlo simulation with fully known ground truth (both correct and erroneous simulated values) as a validation design that sidesteps the silver-standard problem of manual curation.
  • Open-source R and SAS implementations (github.com/HarrallKK/HarrallAlgorithms).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation — a rule-based alternative to EWMA/spline mixed-effects candidate generators, claiming meaningfully higher specificity at comparable sensitivity. Buildable: harrall-caps-cups-stress-test (S) — implement the cap/cup/repeat rule logic on synthetic trajectories, inject a rapid-but-real weight-gain pattern, and test whether the velocity threshold over-flags genuine accelerating growth as implausible.

What to verify first

  • Sensitivity/specificity figures come from simulation matched to one cohort's (EPOCH) measurement spacing — untested on sparser or denser visit schedules.
  • No gold-standard validation exists against real, non-simulated data; the EPOCH demonstration shows smoother trajectories, which the authors call an aesthetic improvement, not proof of correctness.
  • Requires ≥3 data points per participant and cannot flag the first or last point in any trajectory — real coverage gaps.
  • Explicitly not validated for severe illness or expected rapid weight change (e.g., bariatric surgery, GLP1-R agonist treatment) — an open question, not a settled one.

Error types covered / not covered: Covers local trajectory-shape errors (spikes/dips in height and weight) and exact-value repeats/carry-forwards. Does not cover unit-conversion errors, digit transposition, or decimal-shift errors as distinct categories, and is not validated for real physiologically rapid change vs. data-entry error confusion.
