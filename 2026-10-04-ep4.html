Blurb

Bian and colleagues (2020, JAMIA) run a large systematic scoping review — three core review articles, twenty DQ frameworks, 226 individual studies, through February 2020 — that extends the Weiskopf/Kahn lineage from ep3's compact three-construct grid into fourteen named data quality dimensions and ten assessment methods, then maps that taxonomy against the actual data checks run in production across PCORnet, the national Patient-Centered Clinical Research Network. The authors, embedded in OneFlorida (one of PCORnet's nine contributing networks), supply a genuinely concrete real-world anecdote: average patient heights aggregated across the network came out implausibly high because some partner sites recorded height in centimeters instead of the inches the common data model specified — a plain unit-conformance error, not a clinical anomaly, caught only by rigorous data quality assessment. PCORnet's own Data Check 2.02 (flagging extreme height/weight/BP/age categories) maps directly to the paper's own vocabulary as a validity-check-plus-distribution-comparison method addressing atemporal plausibility. The paper also finds PCORnet's practice is lopsided — element presence, validity, and conformance checks dominate, while data-source agreement, gold-standard comparison, and six other dimensions get zero coverage. Today's buildable artifact, directly paired with ep3: a unit-conformance candidate generator for anthropometric fields, motivated by the OneFlorida anecdote.

Plain-English glossary

  • Conformance (check) — whether a value meets a syntactic or structural constraint, such as a field only permitting specified categories or a measurement using the correct unit.（中文：规范性（检查），指某个值是否满足句法或结构性约束，例如某字段只允许特定取值类别，或某项测量是否使用了正确的计量单位。）
  • Atemporal plausibility — whether an observed value, distribution, or density agrees with local/common knowledge or a trusted external reference, independent of time.（中文：非时间性合理性，指某个观测值、分布或密度是否与本地／常识性知识或可信的外部参照一致，与时间无关。）
  • PRISMA — Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses, a standard checklist for transparently documenting a review's search and inclusion process.（中文：PRISMA（系统综述与meta分析优先报告条目），一套用于透明记录综述检索与纳入过程的标准清单。）
  • Common data model (CDM) — a shared data structure and vocabulary that different source sites transform their own data into, enabling consistent analysis across a multi-site network.（中文：通用数据模型（CDM），指不同来源站点将各自数据转换为统一结构和词汇的共享数据模型，从而支持跨多站点网络的一致性分析。）
  • Intrinsic vs. extrinsic data quality — intrinsic quality is independent of context; extrinsic quality (contextual, representational, accessibility) depends on the task, data format, and ease of extraction.（中文：内在与外在数据质量，内在质量独立于使用情境；外在质量（情境性、表征性、可获取性）则取决于具体任务、数据格式和提取的难易程度。）

JORDAN Is PCORnet's own DQ practice actually comprehensive across all fourteen dimensions, or lopsided? PCORnet自身的数据质量实践，真的在全部十四个维度上都做到全面覆盖了吗，还是说存在明显的失衡？

JORDAN What would we need to check before trusting this taxonomy or the anecdote enough to act on either? 在信任到足以据此行动之前，我们需要核实这套分类体系或这个故事中的什么内容？

ALEX First, what we'd need to check is that a bimodal-distribution or conversion-factor heuristic for catching unit mismatches is actually distinguishable from genuine population heterogeneity — a dataset spanning young children through adults will show real, legitimate multimodality in height for entirely clinical reasons, so any such check needs to condition on age the same way a growth-reference-based method already does. 首先，我们需要核实，用双峰分布或转换系数这类启发式方法来捕捉单位不匹配问题，是否真的能与真实的人群异质性区分开来——一个跨越从儿童到成人的数据集，会因为完全合理的临床原因而在身高上呈现真实的多峰性，所以任何这类检查都需要像基于生长参照曲线的方法那样，对年龄加以条件限定。 Second, what we'd need to check is the full list of PCORnet's own data checks and their version history beyond what this paper's Table 3 excerpts, since the paper itself notes the characterization process has evolved substantially since the earlier 2018 description it's updating. 第二，我们需要核实PCORnet自身数据检查的完整清单及其版本演变历史，而不只是这篇论文表3中摘录的部分，因为论文本身也指出，数据特征化流程相较于它所更新的2018年早期描述，已经发生了实质性演变。 Third, this paper's own limitations section is worth repeating rather than glossing over: the search covered only PubMed, so relevant work indexed elsewhere may be missing, and the review stayed at the level of naming dimensions and methods rather than detailing exactly how any one of them should be implemented — which is a reminder that a taxonomy, however complete, is still a map, not the territory. 第三，这篇论文自己的局限性部分值得重复而不是一带而过：检索只覆盖了PubMed，所以其他索引来源中相关的工作可能被遗漏了，而这次综述停留在命名维度和方法的层面，而没有详细说明其中任何一个具体该如何实现——这提醒我们，一套分类体系无论多么完整，终究只是一张地图，不是地形本身。
