Ep1 — DeNovoSeer: a deep learning framework for pathogenicity prediction of De novo mutations

Qiu R, Rao X, Jiang H, Li J, Zhao G (2026), Briefings in Bioinformatics. DeNovoSeer is a semi-supervised deep learning framework purpose-built for scoring the pathogenicity of coding-region de novo mutations (DNMs) — variants that arise spontaneously rather than being inherited. Trained on 70,415 coding DNMs from Gene4Denovo under a hierarchical ClinVar-plus-phenotype labeling scheme, and externally validated on the SPARK autism cohort, the model integrates six categories of functional and clinical evidence and — critically for this podcast's purposes — uses SHAP feature attribution to decompose each prediction back onto named ACMG/AMP evidence categories (PVS1, PM2, PP2/PP3, BS2) rather than returning an opaque score. The buildable idea it leaves behind isn't a new CFTR pathogenicity caller (CFTR disease is recessive and inherited, not de novo, so the PS2/PM6 evidence this model is built around barely applies) — it's the interpretability pattern: an evidence-decomposition layer Karen could read next to a variant of uncertain significance (VUS), showing which ACMG-style buckets are pulling a score up or down.

Plain-English glossary

  • De novo mutation (DNM) — A genetic variant present in a child but absent from both parents, arising spontaneously in a parent's germ cell or the early embryo.（中文：出现在孩子基因组中但父母都没有的变异，是自发产生的新生突变。）
  • ACMG/AMP evidence codes — The standard rulebook codes used to classify a variant's pathogenicity; PS2/PM6 credit de novo status, PVS1 flags predicted loss-of-function, PM2 flags population rarity, PP3 is supportive computational evidence, and BS1/BS2 are benign counter-evidence (population frequency, healthy carriers).（中文：用于变异致病性分类的标准规则代码；PS2/PM6对应新生突变证据，PVS1标记预测功能丧失，PM2标记人群稀有性，PP3是支持性计算证据，BS1/BS2则是良性反证。）
  • ClinVar star rating — NIH's public variant database rates each submitted classification zero to four stars by review stringency; this paper trusts only two-star-and-up entries as clean labels.（中文：NIH的公开变异数据库根据评审严格程度给每条分类打零到四星；本文只信任两星及以上的记录作为干净标签。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant lacking enough evidence to call pathogenic or benign.（中文：证据不足以判定为致病或良性的变异。）
  • Semi-supervised learning — Training that combines a small labeled set with a much larger unlabeled set, here via joint supervised classification, self-supervised reconstruction, and contrastive learning tasks.（中文：结合少量标注数据和大量未标注数据进行训练，本文通过监督分类、自监督重建和对比学习三项任务联合实现。）
  • SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) — A feature-attribution method that decomposes a model's prediction into per-feature contributions, used here to trace scores back to ACMG-style evidence.（中文：一种特征归因方法，把模型预测拆解为各特征的贡献，本文用它把评分追溯回ACMG式证据。）

Full citation

Qiu R, Rao X, Jiang H, Li J, Zhao G. DeNovoSeer: a deep learning framework for pathogenicity prediction of De novo mutations. Briefings in Bioinformatics. 2026. PMID: 42574277 | PMC13455622 | DOI: 10.1093/bib/bbag428

Methods / tools to lift

  • Hierarchical labeling scheme — high-confidence ClinVar first, phenotype-count heuristic second, explicit "unknown" bucket rather than forced classification.
  • Semi-supervised conv/dilated-conv hybrid network — joint supervised classification + self-supervised reconstruction + contrastive learning to exploit large unlabeled variant pools.
  • SHAP-to-ACMG evidence mapping — attributing model output to named evidence categories rather than raw feature weights.
  • MiniBatch K-means feature-similarity partitioning — a robustness check that holds out feature-similar variant clusters rather than random splits.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation priority — method only, not the specific de novo classifier. MVP: pull public CFTR missense/coding variants from ClinVar, CFTR2, and gnomAD; train a modest classifier; wire up SHAP to report, per variant, which of a small set of ACMG-flavored evidence buckets (PVS1-like, PM2-like, PP3-like, BS2-like) are driving the score. Difficulty: S–M.

What to verify first

  • The paper itself notes no systematic comparison yet exists between SHAP-derived evidence attribution and manual ACMG classification by clinical experts.
  • SPARK external-validation AUC (0.656) dropped meaningfully from in-distribution performance — any CFTR adaptation needs its own held-out validation, not training-set metrics.
  • The six feature categories and PS2/PM6 evidence codes are tuned for a de novo, dominant-disease context; whether they even make sense (or need re-weighting) for a recessive, inherited-allele disease like CF is untested.
  • Training data reflects Gene4Denovo's own population composition; the authors flag this as a generalization risk, relevant given CFTR variant frequency varies substantially by ancestry.

JORDAN Before we get into it, let's define a few things for anyone following along. 在深入之前，我们先定义几个概念方便大家跟上。A de novo mutation, DNM for short, is a variant that appears in a child's genome but isn't present in either parent's — it arose spontaneously, either in a parent's germ cell or very early in the embryo. 新生突变，简称DNM，是指出现在孩子基因组中、但父母双方都没有的变异——它是自发产生的，可能发生在父母的生殖细胞中，也可能发生在胚胎发育的极早期。That's clinically important because a de novo variant carries its own kind of evidence: ACMG/AMP, the standard rulebook for classifying variants, has specific codes called PS2 and PM6 that credit a variant for being confirmed or assumed de novo. 这在临床上很重要，因为新生变异自带一种特殊证据：ACMG/AMP是变异分类的标准规则手册，其中有专门的证据代码PS2和PM6，用来因变异被确认或推定为新生而加分。Other ACMG codes you'll hear today: PVS1 flags a variant as predicted loss-of-function, PM2 flags it as rare or absent from population databases, PP3 credits supportive computational evidence, and BS1/BS2 are benign counterweights — common in the population, or seen in healthy carriers. 今天你还会听到其他几个ACMG代码：PVS1标记预测为功能丧失的变异，PM2标记该变异在人群数据库中罕见或缺失，PP3是支持性计算证据加分项，而BS1/BS2则是良性方向的反证——分别对应变异在人群中常见，或者在健康携带者中也能观察到。And a quick refresher on ClinVar: it's the NIH's public variant database, where submissions get a star rating from zero to four depending on how much expert review backs the classification — this paper only trusts two-star-and-up entries as clean labels. ClinVar简单回顾一下：这是NIH维护的公开变异数据库，每条提交记录会根据其专家评审的力度获得零到四星的评级——这篇论文只把两星及以上的记录当作可信的标签使用。A VUS, variant of uncertain significance, is just what it sounds like — a variant nobody has enough evidence to call pathogenic or benign yet, and that's the population this whole enterprise is trying to shrink. VUS，即意义未明变异，顾名思义——就是目前证据不足以判定为致病或良性的变异，而整个这类研究的目标正是缩小这个未知类别的规模。

JORDAN So what problem were they actually trying to solve? 那他们到底想解决什么问题？

ALEX De novo mutations punch way above their weight in disease. 新生突变在疾病中的影响力远超其数量占比。Coding-region DNMs account for roughly 30% of diagnoses in individual cases of severe developmental disorders, and up to 45% in female patients specifically; in autism, they explain something like 52-67% of cases in low-risk families. 编码区新生突变在严重发育障碍的个体诊断中大约占30%，在女性患者中这个比例甚至能到45%；在自闭症中，它们能解释低风险家庭中大约52%到67%的病例。High-throughput sequencing has made these variants easy to find, but that's created a bottleneck downstream: most existing pathogenicity predictors weren't built with DNMs specifically in mind. 高通量测序让发现这些变异变得容易，但这也在下游造成了瓶颈：大多数现有的致病性预测工具并不是专门为新生突变设计的。Tools like CADD or DANN give you a general functional score but don't encode de novo-specific evidence like PS2/PM6 at all. 像CADD或DANN这样的工具能给出一个通用的功能评分，但根本不会编码像PS2/PM6这样的新生突变专属证据。Other tools go the opposite direction and specialize too narrowly — ReVe is built for missense variants, CancerVar is built for somatic cancer mutations — neither fits a germline coding DNM well. 另一些工具则走向另一个极端，专精得过于狭窄——ReVe是为错义变异设计的，CancerVar是为体细胞癌症突变设计的——两者都不太适合胚系编码区新生突变。And DNMs are just statistically awkward to model: they're individually extremely rare, they need parental sequencing to confirm, and there's very little labeled training data to work with. 新生突变本身在统计建模上也很棘手：单个变异极其罕见，需要父母测序才能确认，可用的标注训练数据也非常有限。

ALEX It's also worth naming the recent competitive field they're positioning against, because it shows where the state of the art was actually moving before this paper. 这里也值得说一下他们所对标的近期竞争领域，因为这能说明这篇论文之前该领域的技术前沿实际走向何方。SVPath specializes in exon-level structural variants, V2P adds phenotype-conditioned multi-task prediction so the same model jointly predicts pathogenicity and disease outcome, GNN-MAP uses graph neural networks to fuse multimodal annotations, and popEVE combines deep evolutionary models with population-frequency data to better calibrate its scores. SVPath专门针对外显子层面的结构变异，V2P加入了表型条件下的多任务预测，让同一个模型能同时预测致病性和疾病结局，GNN-MAP用图神经网络融合多模态注释，而popEVE则将深度进化模型与人群频率数据结合以更好地校准评分。None of those four are built for de novo evidence specifically, which is the gap DeNovoSeer is squarely aiming at. 这四个工具都不是专门为新生突变证据设计的，而这正是DeNovoSeer要精准填补的空白。

ALEX There's a second gap the authors call out, which is arguably the more interesting one for us. 作者还指出了第二个缺口，对我们来说这个可能更有意思。Most high-performing pathogenicity models are opaque — they spit out a probability with no traceable link back to the kind of evidence a geneticist actually reasons with. 大多数高性能的致病性预测模型都是黑箱——它们只吐出一个概率数字，却无法追溯到遗传学家实际推理时所依赖的那类证据。So DeNovoSeer's stated goal is threefold: build a labeling system specific to DNMs, integrate functional annotation with ACMG-style clinical evidence rather than treating them as separate universes, and do it with a semi-supervised architecture that can use large pools of unlabeled variants — because as we just said, labeled DNMs are scarce. 因此DeNovoSeer设定的目标有三个：建立专门针对新生突变的标签体系，将功能注释与ACMG式临床证据整合在一起而不是把二者当成两个互不相干的世界，并且用一个半监督架构来实现——因为大量新生突变是没有标签的，这样才能利用起这些未标注数据。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built. 那具体讲讲他们建了什么。

ALEX Training data comes from Gene4Denovo, a database the same group built previously, which pools 741,866 DNM records; after removing unannotated and duplicate entries they're left with 70,415 coding-region variants. 训练数据来自Gene4Denovo，这是同一团队之前构建的数据库，汇集了741,866条新生突变记录；去除无注释和重复条目后，剩下70,415个编码区变异。Labels come from a hierarchy: high-confidence ClinVar entries win first, and where ClinVar is silent, a variant gets called pathogenic only if every reported phenotype is disease-related and it's been seen in at least two independent studies or two affected individuals — otherwise it's left "unknown" rather than forced into a bin. 标签来自一个分层规则：高置信度的ClinVar条目优先采用，在ClinVar没有记录的地方，只有当所有报告的表型都与疾病相关、且该变异至少在两项独立研究或两名患者中被观察到时，才会被标记为致病——否则就被留作"未知"，而不是被强行归类。That leaves a heavily imbalanced set: in Gene4Denovo, 2,965 pathogenic, 3,956 benign, and 63,494 unknown. 这样处理后得到的是一个高度不平衡的数据集：在Gene4Denovo中，致病2,965个，良性3,956个，未知63,494个。The model itself is a semi-supervised hybrid of convolutional and dilated-convolution layers, jointly trained on three objectives — supervised classification on the labeled variants, self-supervised reconstruction, and contrastive learning — so the huge "unknown" pile still contributes signal about the structure of the feature space. 模型本身是卷积层和扩张卷积层结合的半监督混合网络，同时在三个目标上联合训练——对已标注变异的监督分类、自监督重建，以及对比学习——这样一来，庞大的"未知"数据池仍然能为特征空间的结构提供信号。Feature inputs span six categories: evolutionary conservation, coding and cross-region functional prediction, splicing effects, population allele frequency, and ACMG/AMP-derived clinical evidence scores. 输入特征跨越六个类别：进化保守性、编码区及跨区域功能预测、剪接效应、人群等位基因频率，以及来自ACMG/AMP的临床证据评分。External validation uses the SPARK autism cohort, run through a Samocha-style trio-calling pipeline for genotype quality and Mendelian consistency before DNM status is confirmed. 外部验证使用SPARK自闭症队列，数据先经过Samocha式的家系三人组（trio）流程处理，检查基因型质量和孟德尔一致性，然后才确认新生突变状态。

JORDAN What would you push back on in that setup? 这套设计里你会质疑哪一点？

ALEX The label hierarchy is doing a lot of work, and it's built partly on ClinVar and partly on a phenotype-counting heuristic from the same lab's own database — that's not circular exactly, but it does mean the "ground truth" for the harder cases is itself a judgment call, not an independent gold standard. 标签分层体系承担了很重的任务，它一部分建立在ClinVar上，一部分建立在同一实验室自家数据库的表型计数启发式规则上——这不完全算循环论证，但确实意味着较难病例的"金标准"本身也是一种判断，而非独立的黄金标准。And DeNovoSeer is explicitly tuned around de novo evidence — PS2, PM6 — which is a strange fit if you're thinking about porting it anywhere outside of dominant, sporadic-onset disease. 而且DeNovoSeer是明确围绕新生证据——PS2、PM6——来调优的，如果你想把它搬到显性、散发起病疾病以外的场景，这种设计其实并不太合适。

JORDAN How lopsided was the SPARK cohort itself, label-wise? SPARK队列本身在标签分布上有多不平衡？

ALEX After the same trio-calling QC pipeline, SPARK contributed 33,948 coding-region variants: 24,817 unknown, 8,614 benign, and only 517 labeled pathogenic — and note the benign count there is dominated by Gene4Denovo's phenotype-based labeling rule rather than ClinVar, since only 260 of those benign calls actually came from ClinVar itself. 经过同样的家系三人组质控流程后，SPARK贡献了33,948个编码区变异：24,817个未知、8,614个良性，只有517个被标记为致病——注意这里的良性计数主要由Gene4Denovo基于表型的标注规则贡献，而非ClinVar本身，因为这8,614个良性判定中只有260个真正来自ClinVar。That's the imbalance the model has to survive when it leaves its home dataset. 这就是模型离开自己的训练数据集之后必须面对的不平衡程度。

JORDAN So did it actually work? 那它到底有没有效果？

ALEX On the Gene4Denovo test splits it's stable across ten independent runs, and on a fair head-to-head comparison — the "common subset" where every competing tool produces a valid score — it wins on both AUC and average precision. 在Gene4Denovo的测试集划分上，模型在十次独立运行中表现稳定，而在公平的正面对比中——也就是所有竞争工具都能给出有效评分的"共同子集"——它在AUC和平均精确率（AP）上都胜出。The real test is the independent SPARK cohort, where it scores an AUC of 0.656 plus or minus 0.019 and an AP of just 0.259 plus or minus 0.030 — a clear drop from training performance, which the authors attribute to SPARK's extreme class imbalance, since pathogenic variants are genuinely rare in that cohort. 真正的考验是独立的SPARK队列，在这里模型的AUC为0.656±0.019，AP仅为0.259±0.030——相比训练时的表现明显下降，作者将其归因于SPARK的极端类别不平衡，因为致病变异在该队列中确实非常稀少。On a fixed common subset of 3,453 SPARK variants — 72 pathogenic, 3,381 benign — DeNovoSeer still beat every competing method under identical conditions, and crucially it maintained full prediction coverage while some competitors simply didn't score every variant. 在一个固定的3,453个SPARK变异共同子集上——72个致病、3,381个良性——DeNovoSeer在相同条件下依然击败了所有竞争方法，而且关键是它保持了完整的预测覆盖率，而一些竞争工具根本没有给出所有变异的评分。They also stress-tested robustness by clustering variants into ten feature-similarity groups with MiniBatch K-means and holding each one out in turn; performance exceeded an AUC of 0.80 on several clusters, and cluster-level performance correlated strongly with how many pathogenic variants that cluster actually contained — R equals 0.83 for AUC, 0.95 for AP — which the authors read as a property of the data, not a model weakness. 他们还用MiniBatch K-means把变异按特征相似度分成十个组，依次留出一组做测试，以此进行稳健性压力测试；在多个分组上AUC超过0.80，而且分组层面的表现与该分组实际包含的致病变异数量高度相关——AUC的相关系数R为0.83，AP为0.95——作者认为这反映的是数据本身的性质，而不是模型的弱点。SHAP attribution — SHAP（一种将模型预测拆解为各输入特征贡献度的归因方法）— showed the same top features across the training set, the SPARK cohort, and the cluster-holdout setting — dominated by computational scores like MPC, M-CAP, and AlphaMissense plus ACMG-linked features like PVS1, PM2, and PP2 — and in their case studies, borderline predictions decomposed into competing pathogenic and benign evidence in a way that's meant to mirror how a curator actually reasons through a VUS. SHAP归因分析显示，在训练集、SPARK队列以及分组留出测试中，排名靠前的特征都高度一致——主要由MPC、M-CAP、AlphaMissense等计算评分，以及PVS1、PM2、PP2等ACMG关联特征主导——在他们的案例分析中，处于临界值的预测会被分解为相互竞争的致病与良性证据，其设计意图正是模拟一位审核人员在面对VUS时的实际推理过程。

JORDAN Does this map onto anything you and Karen are actually working on? 这跟你和Karen正在做的工作有关联吗？
