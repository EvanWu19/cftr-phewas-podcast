Blurb

PUDU (Medaglia-Mata et al., Frontiers in Bioinformatics, 2026) is a modular Snakemake workflow that consolidates three previously fragmented microbiome-sequencing modalities — short-read 16S amplicon, shotgun metagenomics (WGS), and long-read 16S — into one reproducible pipeline with technology-aware preprocessing, established per-modality classifiers (DADA2, Emu, Kraken2/Bracken, Centrifuger), and a harmonized output layer: standardized count/abundance tables, Krona files, a Phyloseq-compatible R object, and an integrated Shiny app for diversity analysis. Validated on two public datasets (rhizosphere WGS, long-read marine-sediment 16S) with cross-classifier phylum-level agreement (Spearman ρ=0.936) and PERMANOVA R²=0.87–0.95, running under 45GB on a standard workstation. It has no CF-genomics content of its own; it lands on the AI-tooling/career priority as an architecture template — standardize at the output boundary, not the method — directly extending the Nextflow Registry-QC Pipeline Skeleton idea already in the tool backlog.

Plain-English glossary

  • Alpha diversity — A measure of how diverse a single sample is internally (species richness and evenness).（中文:衡量单个样本内部多样性的指标,包括物种丰富度和分布均匀度。）
  • Amplicon (16S rRNA) sequencing — PCR-amplifying one conserved marker gene (16S rRNA) present in all bacteria/archaea, then sequencing just that region as a taxonomic barcode; the cheapest and most common microbiome-profiling approach.（中文:通过PCR扩增所有细菌/古菌中都存在的一个保守标记基因(16S rRNA),再对该区域测序作为分类"条形码";是最便宜、最常用的微生物组分型方法。）
  • Beta diversity — A measure of how different two or more samples are from each other in community composition.（中文:衡量两个或多个样本在群落组成上彼此差异程度的指标。）
  • DADA2 — A widely used tool for denoising short-read amplicon sequencing data into high-resolution amplicon sequence variants (ASVs).（中文:一个广泛使用的工具,用于对短读长扩增子测序数据去噪,得到高分辨率的扩增子序列变体(ASV)。）
  • Kraken2/Bracken, Centrifuger — k-mer-based taxonomic classifiers for shotgun metagenomic (WGS) sequencing data.（中文:基于k-mer的分类工具,用于对散弹枪法元基因组(WGS)测序数据进行物种分类。）
  • Nextflow/Snakemake — Workflow-management systems that define a pipeline as a dependency graph of steps, enabling clean resumption after failure and automatic parallelization; Snakemake is Python-native, Nextflow is Groovy-based and leans more toward container-native cloud/HPC execution.（中文:工作流管理系统,把流程定义为一张由步骤构成的依赖关系图,支持失败后干净恢复以及自动并行化;Snakemake基于Python原生,Nextflow基于Groovy,更强调原生容器化的云端/高性能计算执行。）
  • Ordination — A family of dimensionality-reduction techniques (the ecology analogue of PCA/UMAP) that lays samples out in low-dimensional space so similar communities cluster visually.（中文:一类降维技术(相当于生态学里的PCA/UMAP),把样本投影到低维空间,使组成相似的群落在视觉上聚在一起。）
  • PERMANOVA — A permutation-based statistical test for whether a grouping variable explains a significant fraction of the variance in community composition.（中文:一种基于置换检验的统计方法,用于检验某个分组变量是否能解释群落组成方差中显著的一部分。）
  • Phyloseq — The standard R package the microbial-ecology community uses for diversity analysis and visualization.（中文:微生物生态学界用于多样性分析和可视化的标准R软件包。）
  • Shotgun metagenomics (WGS) — Sequencing essentially all DNA in a sample rather than one marker gene; more expensive and computationally heavier than amplicon sequencing, but can reveal gene function, not just taxonomy.（中文:对样本中几乎所有DNA进行测序,而不只是一个标记基因;比扩增子测序更昂贵、计算量更大,但能揭示基因功能,而不仅仅是物种分类。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • Snakemake — Python-native workflow manager used to express PUDU as a resumable, parallelizable dependency graph.
  • Technology-aware preprocessing + centralized QC — a single entry point that routes each modality to appropriate preprocessing before calling its dedicated classifier.
  • Harmonized output layer (count/abundance tables, Krona, Phyloseq object) — the core transferable idea: standardize outputs regardless of which upstream classifier produced them.
  • Integrated Shiny app — a built-in interactive layer for diversity analysis, reducing the custom-scripting burden the paper names as a problem.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority: AI-tooling / career (architecture pattern, not biology).

Buildable artifact: Harmonized-Output QC Module Wrapper. MVP — run two structurally different anthropometric-flagging approaches (e.g., a bounds-check rule set and a mixed-model residual flagger) independently, then write a thin wrapper normalizing both into one common long-format table (patient, encounter, flag type, flag source, deviation score), mirroring how PUDU normalizes DADA2 and Kraken2/Bracken outputs into one schema. Difficulty: Small (schema design and a thin adapter, not new modeling).

JORDAN Let's get the vocabulary straight first, since none of this is genetics in the CFTR sense. 我们先把术语理清楚,因为这里完全不是CFTR意义上的遗传学内容。 What's the actual difference between amplicon sequencing, shotgun metagenomics, and long-read 16S profiling? 扩增子测序(amplicon sequencing)、散弹枪法元基因组学(shotgun metagenomics)和长读长16S分型,这三者之间到底有什么区别?

ALEX All three are ways of asking "which microbes are in this sample," but they trade off cost, resolution, and what you can say beyond taxonomy. 这三者都是在问"这个样本里有哪些微生物",但在成本、分辨率,以及能否回答超出物种分类的问题上各有取舍。 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing — the cheapest, most common approach — PCR-amplifies one conserved marker gene, the 16S ribosomal RNA gene, that's present in all bacteria and archaea but varies enough between species to act as a barcode; short-read amplicon sequencing reads a small variable region of it. 16S rRNA扩增子测序——最便宜、最常见的方法——用PCR扩增一个在所有细菌和古菌中都存在的保守标记基因,即16S核糖体RNA基因,这个基因在不同物种间的差异足够大,可以当作一种"条形码";短读长扩增子测序读取的是其中一段较短的可变区域。 Long-read 16S instead sequences the entire gene in one pass, which gives much finer species-level resolution than the short-read version, at a higher per-sample cost. 长读长16S则是一次性对整个基因进行测序,相比短读长版本,能获得更精细的物种级分辨率,但单样本成本更高。 Shotgun metagenomics skips the marker gene entirely and sequences essentially all the DNA in a sample — every genome fragment from every organism present — which is the most expensive and computationally heaviest option, but it's the only one of the three that can also tell you about gene content and function, not just "who's there." 散弹枪法元基因组学则完全跳过标记基因,直接对样本中几乎所有DNA进行测序——涵盖样本中每个生物体的每一段基因组片段——这是三者中成本最高、计算量最大的选项,但也是唯一能告诉你基因功能内容、而不只是"谁在这里"的方法。 Each of these modalities has historically needed its own dedicated toolchain, which is exactly the fragmentation problem PUDU is built to address. 这三种技术手段历来都各自需要专属的一整套分析工具链,而这正是PUDU试图解决的碎片化问题。

JORDAN And the other terms we'll hit — alpha/beta diversity, ordination, PERMANOVA? 另外还会碰到的几个术语——α/β多样性、排序分析(ordination)、PERMANOVA?

ALEX Alpha diversity measures how diverse a single sample is internally — how many species, how evenly distributed; beta diversity measures how different two or more samples are from each other in community composition. α多样性衡量的是单个样本内部的多样性有多高——有多少物种、分布有多均匀;β多样性衡量的是两个或多个样本在群落组成上彼此有多不同。 Ordination is a family of dimensionality-reduction techniques — the ecology equivalent of what you'd reach for with PCA or UMAP — that lays samples out in a low-dimensional space so visually similar communities cluster together. 排序分析(ordination)是一类降维技术——相当于生态学里的PCA或UMAP——把样本投影到一个低维空间中,让群落组成相似的样本在图上聚在一起。 PERMANOVA is a permutation-based statistical test for whether a grouping variable — site, treatment, sequencing method — explains a significant fraction of the variance in community composition; the R-squared it reports is the fraction of that variance explained. PERMANOVA是一种基于置换检验的统计方法,用于检验某个分组变量——比如采样点、处理方式、测序方法——是否能解释群落组成方差中显著的一部分;它报告的R方值,就是被该分组变量解释掉的方差占比。

JORDAN Okay, so what was the actual problem motivating PUDU, beyond "fragmentation is annoying"? 好,那除了"碎片化很麻烦"之外,PUDU真正想解决的问题是什么?

ALEX The paper frames it as analytical fragmentation with real consequences, not just inconvenience: each modality is "typically modality-specific," tools "require substantial configuration expertise," and outputs often "need further custom scripting before standard ecological analyses can begin." 论文把这个问题定义为"分析上的碎片化",而且是有实际后果的,不只是不方便:每种技术模式"通常都是各自为战的",工具"需要大量的配置专业知识",而输出结果往往"在能进行标准生态学分析之前,还需要额外的自定义脚本处理"。 That combination — fragmented tools, high configuration burden, non-standardized outputs — introduces what they call avoidable technical variability, meaning two labs analyzing similar data with different tool configurations can get results that differ for reasons that have nothing to do with the biology. 这种组合——碎片化的工具、高昂的配置门槛、非标准化的输出——会带来他们所称的"可避免的技术性变异",也就是说,两个实验室用不同的工具配置分析相似的数据,得出的结果可能存在差异,而这种差异与生物学本身毫无关系。 That directly complicates cross-study reproducibility and comparability, which is the thing ecological meta-analyses actually depend on. 这直接妨碍了跨研究的可复现性和可比较性,而生态学的元分析恰恰依赖于这两点。 PUDU's stated fix is to integrate all three modalities into one reproducible workflow, with simplified configuration, harmonized outputs across different classifier tools, and direct compatibility with the downstream ecological-analysis frameworks researchers already use. PUDU提出的解决方案,是把这三种技术模式整合进一条可复现的流程里,简化配置流程,统一不同分类工具的输出格式,并与研究人员已经在用的下游生态学分析框架直接兼容。 If you squint, this is a tool-development-for-registry-science problem in disguise: swap "sequencing modality" for "flagging method" and "ecological analysis" for "adjudication," and the shape of the complaint is one Runyu would recognize immediately from the registry QC side. 仔细想想,这其实是一个披着微生物组外衣的"为注册库科学做工具开发"的问题:把"测序模式"换成"标记方法",把"生态学分析"换成"审阅裁定",这个问题的形状,Runyu从注册库QC那一侧应该会一下子认出来。

JORDAN Let's get into the architecture. 我们来看看具体的架构。 What's actually inside PUDU? PUDU内部具体是什么样的?

ALEX It's a modular Snakemake workflow — Snakemake being a Python-based workflow-management system that defines a pipeline as a dependency graph of rules, similar in spirit to Nextflow, which lets it resume cleanly after a failure and parallelize independent steps automatically. 这是一个模块化的Snakemake工作流——Snakemake是一个基于Python的工作流管理系统,把流程定义为一张由规则构成的依赖关系图,在设计理念上和Nextflow类似,这让它能在失败后从中断处干净地恢复,并自动并行化那些相互独立的步骤。 For each modality it does technology-aware preprocessing and centralized quality control, then routes to the established tool for that data type: DADA2 for short-read amplicon denoising and ASV inference, Emu for full-length long-read 16S, and both Kraken2/Bracken and Centrifuger as alternative k-mer-based classifiers for shotgun WGS data. 针对每种技术模式,它会先做技术相关的预处理和集中化的质量控制,然后调用该数据类型对应的成熟工具:短读长扩增子用DADA2做去噪和ASV(扩增子序列变体)推断,全长长读长16S用Emu,散弹枪法WGS数据则用Kraken2/Bracken以及Centrifuger这两种基于k-mer的分类器作为可替换选项。 The key design move is harmonization at the output layer: regardless of which upstream classifier ran, PUDU produces the same shape of output — count and relative-abundance tables at user-chosen taxonomic ranks, Krona files for interactive hierarchical visualization, and a standardized Phyloseq-compatible R object, Phyloseq being the standard R package the microbial-ecology community already uses for diversity analysis. 这里的关键设计是在输出层做统一化:不管上游用的是哪个分类器,PUDU产生的输出格式都是一样的——用户自选分类等级下的计数表和相对丰度表、用于交互式层级可视化的Krona文件,以及一个标准化的、与Phyloseq兼容的R对象,而Phyloseq正是微生物生态学界已经普遍使用的多样性分析R包。 On top of that, PUDU ships an integrated Shiny interface — Shiny being an R framework for building interactive web apps directly from R code — for metadata-aware alpha/beta diversity, ordination, community composition, and shared-taxa exploration, with exportable figures and tables. 在此基础上,PUDU还内置了一个Shiny界面——Shiny是一个可以直接用R代码构建交互式网页应用的R框架——用于结合元数据进行α/β多样性分析、排序分析、群落组成分析和共有物种探索,并支持导出图表和数据表。

JORDAN You mentioned Snakemake is similar in spirit to Nextflow — are they actually interchangeable, or does the choice matter here? 你提到Snakemake在理念上和Nextflow类似——它们真的可以互换吗,还是说这个选择在这里有影响?

ALEX They solve the same core problem — expressing a pipeline as a dependency graph so steps run in the right order, resume after failure, and parallelize automatically — but Snakemake is Python-native, with rules defined in a Python-flavored syntax and heavy reuse of the Python data-science ecosystem, which is probably why PUDU leans on it given how R- and Python-heavy the downstream ecological analysis already is. 它们解决的是同一个核心问题——把流程表达成一张依赖关系图,让各步骤按正确顺序运行、能在失败后恢复、并自动并行化——但Snakemake是原生基于Python的,规则用带有Python风格的语法定义,并且大量复用Python的数据科学生态,这也可能是PUDU选择它的原因,因为下游的生态学分析本来就大量依赖R和Python。 Nextflow, by contrast, is Groovy-based and leans harder into container-native, cloud/HPC-scale execution with its own dataflow model; neither is strictly better, but a registry-QC pipeline built mostly by a Python/R-fluent team would likely find Snakemake the lower-friction choice, exactly as PUDU did. 相比之下,Nextflow基于Groovy,更强调原生容器化支持,以及面向云端/高性能计算集群规模的执行,并有自己的数据流模型;两者并没有绝对的优劣,但对于一个主要由熟悉Python/R的团队搭建的注册库QC流程来说,Snakemake大概会是摩擦更小的选择,PUDU正是这么做的。

JORDAN If you had to push back on one design choice here, what would it be? 如果要对其中一个设计选择提出质疑,你会挑哪一个?

ALEX Running three fundamentally different classification algorithms — DADA2, Emu, Kraken2/Bracken, Centrifuger — and then presenting their outputs as directly comparable count tables risks papering over the fact that these methods have genuinely different sensitivity and specificity profiles; "harmonized output format" is not the same claim as "harmonized accuracy." 让三种本质上完全不同的分类算法——DADA2、Emu、Kraken2/Bracken、Centrifuger——各自运行,然后把它们的结果以可以直接比较的计数表形式呈现出来,这样做有一个风险,就是容易掩盖这些方法本身敏感性和特异性存在真实差异的事实;"输出格式统一"并不等同于"准确性统一"。 The paper does report a cross-classifier consistency check — Spearman correlation of 0.936 at the phylum level — but phylum-level agreement is a fairly coarse bar; whether that consistency survives down at genus or species level, where classifier disagreement usually gets worse, isn't something the abstract tells us. 论文确实报告了一个跨分类器一致性检验——门(phylum)水平上的Spearman相关系数为0.936——但门水平的一致性是一个相当粗糙的标准;这种一致性在属或种这种分类器通常更容易出现分歧的更细颗粒度上是否依然成立,摘要并没有告诉我们。
