How did this episode land for you?

Blurb

A 2026 retrospective single-center cohort study (1325 referred, 418 with analyzable CFTR molecular testing, Istanbul, 2016–2022) cross-validates the two leading CFTR locus-specific classification databases, CFTR2 and CFTR-France, against each other and — crucially — against a genetics-independent anchor: measured sweat-chloride test status. The two databases show near-complete agreement on paper (362/395 shared variants, 91.6%, Cohen's κ=0.74; 330/330, 100% on directly comparable binary CF-causing/non-CF-causing labels), but several recurrent "non-CF-causing" variants (L997F, I148T, R75Q, I807M, P1013L, M952I, E217G) show discordant or tied results against real measured sweat-chloride data, and domain-stratified alignment drops sharply outside NBD1/NBD2 (perfect) into MSD2/TMD2 (44.4%) and the R-domain (60%). It lands on Karen's variant-interpretation priority: the buildable artifact is a CFTR Cross-Database Concordance Flagger that applies the paper's three-class crosswalk to flag discordant, coverage-gap, or thin-evidence variants for human review, prioritized by structural domain.

Plain-English Glossary

  • ACMG/AMP tiers — The standard five-level clinical variant classification scale (pathogenic, likely pathogenic, VUS, likely benign, benign) used to grade how confidently a variant is linked to disease.（中文:美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会/分子病理学会制定的标准五级变异分类体系,从致病到良性。）
  • CFTR — The gene (and the chloride-channel protein it encodes) in which biallelic pathogenic variants cause cystic fibrosis.（中文:囊性纤维化跨膜传导调节因子基因及其编码的蛋白,两个拷贝上的致病变异共同导致囊性纤维化。）
  • CFTR2 / CFTR-France — Locus-specific curation databases where expert panels review literature and registry evidence to assign each known CFTR variant a clinical label (e.g., CF-causing, non-CF-causing).（中文:基因位点特异性的变异分类数据库,由专家团队依据文献和注册库证据为每个已知CFTR变异分配临床标签。）
  • Complex allele / cis-acting modifier — A haplotype where a studied variant co-occurs with other nearby sequence changes on the same chromosome copy that can alter its apparent clinical effect.（中文:指被研究的变异与同一条染色体拷贝上其他邻近序列变化共同存在的单体型,可能改变该变异表现出的临床效应。）
  • Consanguinity — Reproductive partnership between close biological relatives, which raises the chance of inheriting two copies of the same recessive variant.（中文:近亲之间的生育关系,会提高遗传到同一种隐性变异两个拷贝的概率。）
  • HGVS notation — The standardized way of writing a variant's location and change, e.g. c.2991G>C at the DNA level or p.Leu997Phe at the protein level.（中文:变异命名的标准化书写规范,例如DNA层面的c.2991G>C或蛋白质层面的p.Leu997Phe。）
  • IRT (immunoreactive trypsinogen) — A blood-based newborn-screening marker used as a first-pass flag for possible CF before molecular testing.（中文:免疫反应性胰蛋白酶原,一种基于血液的新生儿筛查标志物,用于分子检测前对可能的CF进行初筛。）
  • MLPA (multiplex ligation-dependent probe amplification) — A lab method for detecting deletions or duplications of whole exons that standard sequencing can miss.（中文:多重连接依赖探针扩增技术,一种用于检测标准测序可能漏检的整个外显子缺失或重复的实验方法。）
  • MSD1/MSD2 (membrane-spanning domains 1 and 2) — Structural regions of the CFTR protein that span the cell membrane and form the chloride channel pore.（中文:CFTR蛋白中跨越细胞膜并形成氯离子通道孔道的结构区域。）
  • NBD1/NBD2 (nucleotide-binding domains 1 and 2) — Structural regions of the CFTR protein that bind and hydrolyze ATP to power channel gating.（中文:CFTR蛋白中结合并水解ATP、为通道开闭提供能量的结构区域。）
  • R-domain — A regulatory region of the CFTR protein whose phosphorylation state controls channel opening.（中文:CFTR蛋白中的调节性结构域,其磷酸化状态控制通道的开闭。）
  • Sweat chloride test — A clinical test measuring chloride concentration in sweat (≥60 mmol/L confirms CF, 30–59 intermediate, <29 makes CF unlikely) that is genetics-independent and used here as an external validation anchor.（中文:测量汗液中氯离子浓度的临床检测,每升60毫摩尔以上确诊CF,30到59之间为中间值,低于29则CF可能性低,该检测不依赖基因型,本研究用作外部验证锚点。）
  • Three-class crosswalk — This paper's prespecified rule for collapsing two databases' differently-labeled categories onto comparable CF-causing / non-CF-causing / not-evaluable classes.（中文:本文预先设定的规则,将两个数据库标签体系不同的分类折叠成可比的CF-causing、non-CF-causing和not-evaluable三类。）
  • VarSome / Franklin / InterVar — Variant-annotation software tools used to apply ACMG/AMP classification criteria to sequence variants.（中文:用于对序列变异应用ACMG/AMP分类标准的变异注释软件工具。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant for which current evidence is insufficient to call it either pathogenic or benign.（中文:意义不明变异,指目前证据不足以判定其为致病或良性的变异。）

Methods/Tools to Lift

  • Three-class crosswalk (Table 7) — a reusable rule for collapsing two differently-labeled curation databases' categories onto comparable CF-causing / non-CF-causing / not-evaluable classes.
  • Genetics-independent anchor design — using measured sweat-chloride status (not another curated database) to validate variant classification labels, avoiding circularity from shared underlying evidence.
  • Domain-stratified alignment reporting — breaking alignment down by CFTR structural domain (NBD1/NBD2, MSD1/TMD1, MSD2/TMD2, R-domain, splice/intronic) rather than reporting one pooled figure.
  • Carrier-weighted vs. variant-level vs. allele-weighted alignment reporting — three complementary ways of summarizing the same concordance data.
  • Tie handling — treating exact positive/negative sweat-result ties as indeterminate and excluding them from the primary estimate, with a separate conservative sensitivity analysis counting ties as non-aligned.

Where It Lands + Buildable Artifact

Priority served: Karen / variant-interpretation (CFTR variant interpretation, VUS pathogenicity prediction, variant-notation harmonization).

Buildable artifact — CFTR Cross-Database Concordance Flagger. One-line MVP: given a list of CFTR variants in HGVS notation plus current CFTR2 and CFTR-France classification exports, apply this paper's three-class crosswalk and flag any variant that is discordant between databases, has a coverage gap in one database, or has tied/thin supporting evidence, annotated by structural domain so low-alignment domains (MSD2/TMD2, R-domain, MSD1/TMD1) are prioritized for human review. Input: variant list + two database exports. Difficulty: S–M (mostly notation harmonization across transcript builds NM_000492.3 vs. NM_000492.4, plus a lookup/join — no modeling).

What to Verify First

  • Whether the discordant non-CF-causing variants (L997F, I148T, R75Q, I807M, P1013L, M952I, E217G) are population-specific to this Turkish cohort or replicate elsewhere — the paper itself calls these hypothesis-generating, not grounds for reclassification.
  • Selection-bias risk in the measured-sweat comparison: only 148 of 418 individuals had a recorded sweat result, and testing availability differed by variant-detection status.
  • Whether some of these recurrent variants sit on complex alleles or alongside cis-acting modifiers (as prior literature suggests for several of them), which would make single-variant labels the wrong unit of analysis.
  • That direct CFTR2-vs-CFTR-France agreement is not independent validation, since both databases draw on overlapping published evidence and curation frameworks.
