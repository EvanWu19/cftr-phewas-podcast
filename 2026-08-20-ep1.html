MAVISp: A modular structure-based framework for protein variant effects

Arnaudi M, Utichi M, Degn K, et al. (Papaleo group, DTU). Protein Science, 2026.

MAVISp is a modular, structure-based framework that scores protein variant effects across several independent mechanistic axes — folding stability, binding-interface disruption, long-range allosteric communication, and post-translational-modification impact — rather than collapsing everything into one damaging/benign number the way most sequence-based predictors do. It integrates AlphaFold-derived structures, FoldX/Rosetta/RaSP consensus free-energy calculations, and established variant-effect predictors (ClinVar, REVEL, DeMaSk, GEMME, EVE, AlphaMissense) into a public, continuously updated database now covering 10+ million variants across 1,216 proteins, with a Streamlit web server and open GitHub/OSF code and data. The framework was benchmarked against deep mutational scanning data for PTEN and SMPD1, and applied as worked case studies to BRCA2, BLM, ULK1, and MAP1LC3B — all cancer- or disease-related proteins, none CF-modifier genes. It lands as a Karen Raraigh / CFTR-variant-interpretation prospect: CFTR isn't in the database yet, and given MAVISp's explicit exclusion of transmembrane-contacting residues from its stability and binding modules, it likely never will be fully covered — but CFTR's cytoplasmic domains (NBD1, NBD2, R-domain) are plausible territory for a scoped prototype cross-referencing structural ΔΔG calls against CFTR2's curated VUS list.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP — the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology consensus guidelines for classifying variant pathogenicity, including evidence codes like PS3 (strong functional evidence of pathogenicity) and BS3 (strong functional evidence of benign effect).（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学院与分子病理学会联合制定的变异致病性分类共识指南，包含 PS3、BS3 等证据代码。）
  • AlphaFold — a deep-learning system that predicts 3D protein structure from sequence with accuracy often comparable to experimental methods; MAVISp uses AlphaFold2/AlphaFold3 models as its default starting structures.（中文：一种通过深度学习从序列预测蛋白质三维结构的系统，精度常可媲美实验方法；MAVISp 默认使用其模型作为起始结构。）
  • cryo-EM — cryo-electron microscopy, an experimental method for solving protein structures by flash-freezing samples and imaging with an electron microscope; CFTR already has solved cryo-EM structures.（中文：冷冻电镜，一种通过快速冷冻样品并用电子显微镜成像来解析蛋白质结构的实验方法；CFTR 已有已解出的冷冻电镜结构。）
  • deep mutational scanning (DMS) — an experimental method that measures a functional readout (e.g. protein abundance, enzyme activity) for thousands of variants simultaneously in one pooled assay; used here as ground truth via the VAMP-seq assay.（中文：一种在一次汇总实验中同时测量成千上万个变异功能读数的实验方法，本文用 VAMP-seq 检测法作为真实标准。）
  • ΔΔG (delta-delta-G) — the change in free energy caused by an amino-acid substitution; a large positive folding ΔΔG means the mutant protein is markedly less stable than wild-type.（中文：氨基酸替换导致的自由能变化量；折叠 ΔΔG 越大越正，说明突变蛋白比野生型稳定性差得越多。）
  • NBD1 / NBD2 (nucleotide-binding domains) — the two cytoplasmic domains of CFTR that bind and hydrolyze ATP to gate the channel; not membrane-embedded, so usable by MAVISp's stability/binding modules unlike CFTR's transmembrane domains.（中文：CFTR 的两个胞质核苷酸结合域，通过结合和水解 ATP 控制通道开关；不嵌于膜内，因此不同于跨膜结构域，可被 MAVISp 的稳定性/结合模块使用。）
  • pLDDT — predicted local distance difference test; AlphaFold's per-residue confidence score (0–100) for how much to trust that part of the predicted structure.（中文：预测局部距离差异检验，AlphaFold 对预测结构中每个残基给出的 0–100 置信度评分。）
  • R-domain — CFTR's regulatory domain, a cytoplasmic, largely disordered segment that must be phosphorylated for the channel to open; not membrane-embedded.（中文：CFTR 的调节域，一个胞质、结构较松散的区段，需被磷酸化通道才能开启；不嵌于膜内。）
  • VUS (Variant of Uncertain Significance) — a variant for which the evidence is insufficient to classify as pathogenic or benign under ACMG/AMP rules.（中文：意义不明的变异，现有证据不足以按 ACMG/AMP 规则判定为致病或良性。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • FoldX — fast empirical force-field tool for estimating folding/binding free-energy changes from a structure.
  • Rosetta (cartddg2020 / flexddg protocols) — physics-based structure-modeling suite, used here as the higher-cost, higher-fidelity member of the stability/binding consensus pair.
  • RaSP — a fast neural-network approximation to Rosetta-quality folding ΔΔG, now MAVISp's default stability-module partner to FoldX since March 2024.
  • AlloSigMA2 — coarse-grained model estimating allosteric free-energy changes upon substitution, powering the LONG_RANGE module.
  • Snakemake / Dask — workflow-management engines chaining MAVISp's computational modules reproducibly.
  • GEMME, DeMaSk, EVE, REVEL, AlphaMissense — sequence/structure-based variant-effect predictors integrated as comparison/complement layers.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation and harmonization.

Buildable: cftr-mavisp-module-prototype (M–L) — run MAVISp's STABILITY and LONG_RANGE modules (FoldX/Rosetta/RaSP + AlloSigMA2) against CFTR's existing cryo-EM structure, scoped to NBD1, NBD2, and the R-domain (excluding transmembrane-contacting residues per MAVISp's own limitation), and cross-reference the resulting ΔΔG/allosteric calls against CFTR2's curated VUS list. Input: CFTR cryo-EM structure + CFTR2 VUS list. Difficulty is driven mainly by FoldX/Rosetta/RaSP/AlloSigMA2 installation and compute overhead, not by biological ambiguity.

What to verify first

  • Whether a CFTR-specific deep mutational scanning dataset exists with per-replicate granularity comparable to PTEN's VAMP-seq data, to serve as a benchmark.
  • Whether restricting analysis to NBD1/NBD2/R-domain (excluding the transmembrane domains, where many known CF-causing variants sit) yields a genuinely useful subset of CFTR's VUS landscape.
  • How MAVISp's module-by-module output (separate stability/binding/allostery calls) would need to be collapsed or weighted to map onto ACMG/AMP evidence codes like PS3/BS3 — the paper doesn't propose this mapping itself.

ALEX Today we're reading MAVISp, a 2026 Protein Science paper from Arnaudi and colleagues, led by Elena Papaleo's group at DTU. 今天我们要读的是 MAVISp，这是 Arnaudi 等人 2026 年发表在《Protein Science》上的论文，由丹麦技术大学 Elena Papaleo 团队主导。 It's a modular, structure-based framework for scoring the effects of protein variants, with a public web server and over ten million pre-computed variant scores. 这是一个模块化的、基于结构的蛋白质变异效应评分框架，配有公开的网络服务器，已经预先计算了超过一千万个变异的评分。 We're covering it on Track A, the variant-interpretation track, because it's a different flavor of tool than the sequence-based predictors we usually cover — it tries to tell you *why* a variant is damaging, not just whether it is. 我们把它放进 A 赛道——变异解读赛道——是因为它和我们平时讨论的基于序列的预测工具不太一样，它试图告诉你一个变异*为什么*有害，而不只是判断它是否有害。

JORDAN Before we get into it, can you set up the vocabulary? 在深入之前，你能先讲讲相关术语吗？ I know what a VUS is generically, but what's the structural angle here? 我大致知道 VUS 是什么，但这里的"结构"角度具体指什么？

ALEX Sure. Right, so a VUS — Variant of Uncertain Significance — is a variant where the evidence isn't strong enough to call it pathogenic or benign under ACMG/AMP guidelines, the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and Association for Molecular Pathology's consensus rules for variant classification. 好的。VUS，即"意义不明的变异"（Variant of Uncertain Significance），是指现有证据不足以按照 ACMG/AMP 指南——美国医学遗传学与基因组学学院与分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类共识规则——判定其为致病或良性的变异。 Most variant-effect predictors, VEPs for short, work from sequence alone or from a single structure snapshot, and they output one number: probably damaging, probably benign. 大多数变异效应预测工具，简称 VEP，只依赖序列信息或单一结构快照，输出一个数字：大概率有害，或大概率良性。 MAVISp instead runs a whole battery of structure-based calculations — how much does this substitution destabilize the protein's fold, does it break a binding interface, does it disrupt long-range communication to a distant functional site — and reports each one separately. 而 MAVISp 则运行一整套基于结构的计算——这个突变让蛋白质折叠失稳多少、是否破坏了结合界面、是否干扰了到远端功能位点的长程信息传递——并将每一项分开报告。 The core currency for a lot of these modules is ΔΔG, delta-delta-G, the change in free energy caused by the substitution — a big positive ΔΔG for folding means the mutant protein is much less stable than wild-type. 这些模块中很多用的核心指标是 ΔΔG，即突变导致的自由能变化——如果折叠 ΔΔG 是一个很大的正值，就意味着突变蛋白比野生型稳定性差得多。

JORDAN And the structures themselves — where do those come from? 那这些结构本身是从哪里来的？ Solved crystal structures for every protein? 是每个蛋白质都有已解出的晶体结构吗？

ALEX Mostly not — mostly AlphaFold. 大多数不是——大多数用的是 AlphaFold。 MAVISp defaults to AlphaFold2 database models, trimmed to remove low-confidence regions, and falls back to AlphaFold3 for very large proteins. MAVISp 默认使用 AlphaFold2 数据库模型，并裁剪掉低置信度区域，对特别大的蛋白质则改用 AlphaFold3。 Confidence there is scored with pLDDT, predicted local distance difference test, a per-residue 0-to-100 score for how much to trust that piece of the model. 结构置信度用 pLDDT 衡量，全称是"预测局部距离差异检验"（predicted local distance difference test），是每个残基 0 到 100 分的可信度评分。 For a handful of proteins they also generate actual conformational ensembles — multiple structures from molecular dynamics simulation or NMR — rather than trusting one static snapshot. 对少数蛋白质，他们还会生成真正的构象系综——通过分子动力学模拟或 NMR 核磁共振得到多个结构，而不是只信任一个静态快照。 One more term worth flagging: deep mutational scanning, DMS, is an experimental method that measures a functional readout — like protein abundance or enzyme activity — for thousands of variants at once in a pooled assay; MAVISp uses DMS datasets, including one called VAMP-seq, as ground truth to check its own predictions against. 还有一个术语值得一提：深度突变扫描（deep mutational scanning，DMS）是一种实验方法，能在一次汇总实验中同时测量成千上万个变异的功能读数——比如蛋白质丰度或酶活性；MAVISp 用 DMS 数据集，包括一种叫 VAMP-seq 的方法，作为检验自身预测的真实标准。

JORDAN Okay, so why did this framework need building — what wasn't working before? 好，那这个框架为什么需要被搭建出来——之前是什么地方不行？

ALEX The motivating problem is a widening gap. 促使他们做这件事的核心问题是一个不断扩大的缺口。 Sequencing throughput keeps climbing, so the raw count of identified variants keeps climbing too, but functional characterization — actually knowing what a variant does — lags way behind. 测序通量不断提升，被发现的变异数量也随之不断增加，但功能表征——真正搞清楚一个变异做了什么——却远远跟不上。 That gap is exactly where VUS and conflicting-evidence calls pile up. VUS 和证据相互矛盾的判定，正是在这个缺口里不断堆积的。 The paper frames the newer generation of sequence-based predictors — AlphaMissense being the headline example, since it's built on an AlphaFold-like architecture — as a real leap in raw accuracy, but says accuracy alone isn't the whole job. 论文把新一代的序列型预测工具——以 AlphaMissense 为代表，因为它基于类似 AlphaFold 的架构构建——定位为原始准确率上的真正飞跃，但同时指出准确率本身并不是全部任务。 A clinician or a bench scientist deciding whether to trust a "likely pathogenic" call, or deciding what experiment to run next, wants to know the *mechanism*: is this variant unstable, does it lose a binding partner, does it break allosteric signaling to some pocket three domains away? 一位临床医生或实验科学家在决定是否信任一个"可能致病"的判断、或决定下一步做什么实验时，想知道的是*机制*：这个变异是不稳定，还是丢失了一个结合伙伴，还是破坏了到三个结构域之外某个口袋的变构信号传递？ Papaleo's group had already published several one-off, single-protein studies doing exactly this kind of structural dissection — on TP53, on PTEN, on the pro-apoptotic BCL2 family — and MAVISp is explicitly the generalization of that pattern into one standardized, reusable, continuously updated public resource rather than a one-paper-one-protein cottage industry. Papaleo 团队此前已经针对 TP53、PTEN、促凋亡的 BCL2 家族等单个蛋白发表过多篇一次性的结构剖析研究，而 MAVISp 正是把这种模式明确地推广成一个标准化、可复用、持续更新的公开资源，而不再是"一篇论文对应一个蛋白"的手工作坊模式。 The paper is also explicit that MAVISp isn't the only entrant in this space — it names Missense3D, ADDRESS, MUTATIONEXPLORER, VUStruct, the Genomics 2 Proteins portal, and ProtVar as related structure-aggregating resources — and positions its own distinguishing claim as being the first to combine binding free energy, long-range allosteric effects, ensemble-based simulation data, and curated experimental data in one centralized database rather than offering any single one of those in isolation. 论文也明确指出，MAVISp 并不是这个领域唯一的产品——它列举了 Missense3D、ADDRESS、MUTATIONEXPLORER、VUStruct、Genomics 2 Proteins 门户网站，以及 ProtVar 等相关的结构聚合资源——并把自己的独特之处定位为：首个把结合自由能、长程变构效应、基于系综的模拟数据，以及经过整理的实验数据整合进同一个集中数据库，而不是只单独提供其中某一项。

JORDAN Walk me through what's actually inside the pipeline. 带我具体看看这个流程里面到底有什么。

ALEX Structurally, it's organized as independent modules, mostly run through Snakemake or Dask workflow engines — Snakemake being a workflow-management tool popular in bioinformatics for chaining computational steps reproducibly. 从结构上看，它被组织成一系列独立的模块，大多通过 Snakemake 或 Dask 工作流引擎运行——Snakemake 是生物信息学中常用的工作流管理工具，用来可复现地串联计算步骤。 STRUCTURE_SELECTION picks and trims the starting model; INTERACTOME finds binding partners via the Mentha and STRING databases and lines up complex structures. STRUCTURE_SELECTION 模块负责挑选并裁剪起始结构模型；INTERACTOME 模块通过 Mentha 和 STRING 数据库寻找结合伙伴，并准备好复合物结构。 STABILITY computes folding ΔΔG using a consensus of FoldX, Rosetta, and a fast neural-network approximation called RaSP — since March 2024 they default to the RaSP-FoldX pairing specifically because Rosetta is too CPU-expensive to run at their current scale. STABILITY 模块用 FoldX、Rosetta，以及一种叫 RaSP 的快速神经网络近似方法的共识结果，来计算折叠 ΔΔG——从 2024 年 3 月起，他们默认使用 RaSP 与 FoldX 的组合，原因是 Rosetta 在目前的规模下计算成本太高。 LOCAL_INTERACTION does the same consensus math for binding ΔΔG at residues within 10 Ångström of an interface. LOCAL_INTERACTION 模块对界面 10 埃范围内的残基做同样的共识计算，衡量结合 ΔΔG。 LONG_RANGE estimates allosteric effects — perturbations at one site rippling through the structure to change a distant functional region — using a coarse-grained model called AlloSigMA2. LONG_RANGE 模块用一种叫 AlloSigMA2 的粗粒化模型，估算变构效应——即一个位点的扰动如何在结构中传播，改变远端的功能区域。 There's also a PTM module for phosphorylation sites, and an EXPERIMENTAL_DATA module that ingests DMS datasets like VAMP-seq to validate everything else against. 还有一个 PTM 模块用于磷酸化位点，以及一个 EXPERIMENTAL_DATA 模块，用来导入 VAMP-seq 这类 DMS 数据集，对其他模块的结果进行验证。 Everything funnels into a text database, then a Python package, then a Streamlit web app plus GitBook write-ups per protein, and both code and data are openly released on GitHub and OSF. 所有结果最终汇入一个文本数据库，再经过一个 Python 包处理，最后输出到基于 Streamlit 的网页应用，并配有每个蛋白质的 GitBook 说明文档，代码和数据都在 GitHub 和 OSF 上公开发布。

JORDAN That's a lot of moving parts. 这里面的组件真不少。 Where would you push back? 你会在哪里提出质疑？

ALEX Two places. 两个地方。 First, the classification thresholds feel somewhat arbitrary even though they're grounded in prior literature — 3 kcal/mol as the destabilizing cutoff, roughly 1 kcal/mol for binding — and "uncertain" gets assigned whenever two methods disagree, which quietly makes disagreement itself load-bearing without a clean way to say which method was right. 第一，虽然分类阈值有文献依据，但仍显得有些武断——3 千卡/摩尔作为失稳判定阈值，结合能大约用 1 千卡/摩尔——而且只要两种方法结果不一致就被归为"不确定"，这悄悄地让"方法间分歧"本身承担了判断责任，却没有办法说清到底哪个方法是对的。 Second — and this is the one that matters most for our purposes — the STABILITY and LOCAL_INTERACTION modules explicitly don't support variants in transmembrane regions; the protocols were validated for soluble proteins, and residues in contact with the membrane get excluded outright. 第二点——也是对我们目的而言最重要的一点——STABILITY 和 LOCAL_INTERACTION 模块明确不支持跨膜区域的变异；这些计算流程是针对可溶性蛋白验证的，与膜接触的残基会被直接排除在外。 That's a real structural gap for a project thinking about CFTR, which is a multi-domain ABC transporter built substantially out of transmembrane helices. 这对我们考虑 CFTR 相关工作来说是个实实在在的结构性缺口，因为 CFTR 是一个由跨膜螺旋大量构成的多结构域 ABC 转运蛋白。

JORDAN So how well did it actually perform where they could test it? 那在能测试的地方，它的表现究竟如何？

ALEX The cleanest benchmark is PTEN, a tumor suppressor with real VAMP-seq abundance data. 最干净的一个基准测试是 PTEN，一个拥有真实 VAMP-seq 丰度数据的抑癌蛋白。 Against a threshold from the ProteinGym benchmark dataset, the RaSP-FoldX stability consensus hit accuracy 0.814, sensitivity 0.666, specificity 0.954; using an alternative threshold from the original MaveDB deposit — MaveDB being a public repository for DMS datasets — accuracy rose to 0.845, sensitivity 0.707, specificity 0.955. 在 ProteinGym 基准数据集给出的阈值下，RaSP-FoldX 稳定性共识达到了准确率 0.814、灵敏度 0.666、特异度 0.954；而用来自原始 MaveDB 存放数据的另一套阈值——MaveDB 是一个公开的 DMS 数据集库——准确率提高到 0.845，灵敏度 0.707，特异度 0.955。 GEMME, a pure sequence-conservation predictor, actually beat the structural consensus on sensitivity, around 0.72 to 0.76, but trailed badly on specificity and overall accuracy — a genuine precision-recall tradeoff between the two approaches, not a clean win for either. GEMME 是一个纯粹基于序列保守性的预测工具，它在灵敏度上其实超过了结构共识方法，大约 0.72 到 0.76，但在特异度和整体准确率上明显落后——两种方法之间存在真实的精确率-召回率权衡，谁都没有干净地赢。 On a second PTEN readout, phosphatase activity, combining STABILITY with GEMME pushed 左右——是真实但中等程度的相关，算不上完胜。 On the pure cataloging side, BLM turned up 1170 predicted destabilizing variants genome-wide for that protein, of which only 45 are currently in ClinVar at all, and of those 42 8962 possible substitutions across its two modeled domains, and simple mode alone flagged 22 of those 215 as damaging by at least one MAVISp module, concentrated specifically in the catalytic N-terminal domain — a pattern the team then partly confirmed by running a microsecond-scale molecular dynamics ensemble on that domain, which validated five long-range-damaging and two stability-damaging calls from the faster simple-mode pass. 还有一个值得引用的案例，用来说明规模：激酶 ULK1 在两个已建模结构域中共有 8962 种可能的饱和突变背景下，被 ClinVar/COSMIC/cBioPortal 报告过的变异有 215 个，仅在简单模式下就有 22 个被至少一个 MAVISp
