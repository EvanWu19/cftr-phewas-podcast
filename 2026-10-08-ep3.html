Blurb

Today's episode covers a 2010 paper examining anthropometric (height and weight) data quality in the Veterans Health Administration's (VHA) Corporate Data Warehouse (CDW) — note: this paper is abstract-only, genuinely closed access, confirmed via Europe PMC with no PMCID. The study compared record counts and values between the national CDW and several regional VISN (Veterans Integrated Service Network) warehouses, finding fewer recorded heights than weights and 97-99%+ concordance between the two independently-derived data sources, alongside implausible same-day and same-year height/weight variation suggestive of measurement or entry error. This lands primarily on candidate generation (a) in our outlier-pipeline framework, and secondarily on evaluation (d): it offers two ideas worth layering into any registry QC pipeline — a cross-source/cross-extract reconciliation check as a pipeline-health signal distinct from single-value plausibility, and a near-zero-tolerance heuristic that same-day height variation is almost certainly a data-entry error. The buildable artifact for this episode is a Cross-extract concordance check: a simple join-and-compare script that measures per-field agreement between two independently-sourced extracts of the same encounters, flagging disagreement as a pipeline-health issue. No sensitivity, PPV, or flag-rate figures were reported — this is a descriptive, structural data-quality characterization, not a benchmarked detector.

Plain-English glossary

  • Carry-forward / stale value — A last-known value (such as a height) that persists in a record past the point where it is still true; a particular risk for height since it is measured far less often than weight in adults.(中文:延续带入值/陈旧值——一个"最后已知值"在记录中持续存在,超过了它仍然成立的时间点;由于成年人身高测量频率远低于体重,这是身高数据的一个特定风险。)
  • CDW (Corporate Data Warehouse) — VHA's national data warehouse; a downstream, ETL-processed copy of data distinct from the source EHR system where a value is originally entered.(中文:企业数据仓库——VHA的国家级数据仓库,是经ETL处理的下游数据副本,区别于最初录入数据的源头电子病历系统。)
  • Completeness — A standard EHR/registry data-quality dimension measuring how much of the expected data is actually present; illustrated here by heights being recorded far less often than weights.(中文:完整性——EHR/注册库数据质量的标准维度之一,衡量预期应有的数据实际存在多少;本文中以身高记录远少于体重记录为例说明。)
  • Concordance / cross-source reconciliation — Comparing two independently-derived extracts of the same underlying data to see if they agree, as a data-quality check distinct from asking whether any single value is itself plausible.(中文:一致性/跨源对账——比较同一份底层数据的两份独立提取结果是否一致,这是一种与"单个数值本身是否合理"不同的数据质量检查方法。)
  • ETL (Extract-Transform-Load) — The pipeline process that moves and reshapes data from a source system into a downstream warehouse.(中文:抽取-转换-加载——将数据从源系统移动并重塑到下游数据仓库的管道处理流程。)
  • VHA (Veterans Health Administration) — The U.S. agency providing healthcare to military veterans, within the Department of Veterans Affairs.(中文:美国退伍军人事务部卫生署——美国退伍军人事务部下属,为退伍军人提供医疗服务的机构。)
  • VISN (Veterans Integrated Service Network) — A regionally organized grouping of VHA healthcare facilities; some VISNs maintain their own regional data warehouses in parallel with the national CDW.(中文:退伍军人综合服务网络——VHA按区域划分的医疗机构集群;部分VISN维护自己的区域数据仓库,与国家级CDW并行存在。)

Methods / tools to lift

  • Cross-source / cross-extract concordance check: compare two independently-derived extracts of the same underlying encounters (e.g., national warehouse vs. regional warehouse, or two exports taken at different times) and measure agreement rate as a pipeline-health signal, orthogonal to statistical plausibility modeling.
  • Same-day-height-as-near-certain-error heuristic: treat any within-day change in recorded height as near-zero-tolerance evidence of a data-entry error rather than biology, distinct from same-year weight variation, which needs a statistical model to judge.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation [primary] / (d) evaluation [secondary]. Buildable artifact — Cross-extract concordance check (difficulty S): a generic join-and-compare script that takes two independently-derived extracts covering the same patient-encounters, keyed on a shared encounter identifier, and computes per-field agreement (exact or tolerance-based for continuous fields), surfacing disagreement as a candidate pipeline-health flag, independent of and complementary to any statistical outlier model.

What to verify first

  • Whether more than one independently-sourced extract of the same encounters actually exists to compare in any given registry setup — this paper's method requires that precondition, which may or may not hold.
  • Whether a 97-99% concordance bar is even the right target once the underlying base error rate is unknown — two pipelines sharing the same upstream bug could agree highly while both being wrong.
  • Whether building and maintaining a second independent extract pipeline, solely for a reconciliation check, is worth its cost relative to the error rate it would catch.

Pipeline stage

(a) candidate generation [primary] / (d) evaluation [secondary]

Error types covered / not covered

Covered: cross-extract/cross-source disagreement (pipeline-health signal); same-day height implausibility (near-certain entry error). Not covered: unit conversion errors, digit transposition, decimal shift, carry-forward values, real-clinical-event vs error confusion — none of these are addressed by this abstract's described method.
