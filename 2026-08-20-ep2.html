An OMOP-CDM based pharmacovigilance data-processing pipeline (PDP) providing active surveillance for ADR signal detection from real-world data sources

Shin H, Lee S. BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making, 2021.

This paper builds and validates a pharmacovigilance data-processing pipeline (PDP) that converts two heterogeneous real-world data sources — a Korean hospital's electronic health records (Konyang University Hospital, 347,040 patients, 2014–2018) and the FDA's spontaneous Adverse Event Reporting System (FAERS, ~6.95M reports, 2012–2018) — into a shared OMOP common data model, then mines both for drug-induced ear-disorder signals as a worked test case, including manually digitizing ~7,800 unstructured pure-tone-audiometry (PTA) graph images into structured OMOP fields. Six drugs showed significant hazard ratios in the EHR arm (nortriptyline HR 8.06 highest), four showed significant reporting odds ratios in FAERS (nortriptyline ROR 1.94 highest), but only three drugs — doxycycline, nortriptyline, sucralfate — were significant in both raw sources before meta-analysis, and the fixed- vs. random-effects meta-analysis models themselves disagreed on which of five vs. three drugs survived. The pipeline was also stress-tested on neutropenia/thrombocytopenia and acute-hepatitis outcomes, surfacing further candidate signals. It lands as an AI-tooling/career-track piece — the contribution is the reusable two-source-triangulation architecture, not any single drug-safety finding — and the buildable artifact is a Synthea-based OMOP pipeline demonstrating the same EHR+SRS triangulation pattern on synthetic data, decoupled from any real hospital's records.

Plain-English glossary

  • CDM (common data model) — a standardized table schema and vocabulary that lets data from different institutions be reshaped into the same structure so identical analysis code runs unmodified everywhere.（中文：一种标准化的表结构和词汇表，能把不同机构的数据统一转换成同样的结构，使同一套分析代码可在任何地方原样运行。）
  • disproportionality statistic — a class of pharmacovigilance statistics (like ROR) that flag a drug-event pair as over-represented relative to background co-occurrence rates, without establishing causation.（中文：一类药物警戒统计量（如 ROR），用于标记某药物-事件组合相对于背景共现率被过度报告，但并不确立因果关系。）
  • FAERS — the FDA Adverse Event Reporting System, a spontaneous reporting system where patients and clinicians submit suspected adverse-drug-reaction reports.（中文：FDA 不良事件报告系统，一种由患者和临床医生主动提交疑似药物不良反应报告的自发报告系统。）
  • OHDSI — Observational Health Data Sciences and Informatics, the public-private research network that maintains the OMOP CDM standard and builds open-source tooling around it.（中文：观察性健康数据科学与信息学网络，维护 OMOP CDM 标准并构建配套开源工具的公私合作研究网络。）
  • OMOP — Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership, the origin project behind the OMOP common data model used throughout this paper.（中文：观察性医疗结果合作项目，是本文所用 OMOP 通用数据模型的发起项目。）
  • PTA (pure tone audiometry) — a hearing test measuring a patient's hearing threshold at different frequencies against a normal-hearing baseline, typically reported as a graph rather than a structured number.（中文：纯音测听，一种测量患者在不同频率下听力阈值、与正常听力基线对比的听力测试，通常以图表而非结构化数字呈现。）
  • ROR (reporting odds ratio) — the standard disproportionality statistic in pharmacovigilance, comparing how often a drug-event pair co-occurs in reports versus how often each occurs with everything else.（中文：药物警戒领域标准的比例失衡统计量，比较某药物-事件组合在报告中共现的频率，与二者各自和其他情况共现的频率。）
  • SRS (spontaneous reporting system) — a data source built from voluntarily submitted adverse-event reports, as opposed to systematically collected clinical records.（中文：一种由自愿提交的不良事件报告构成的数据源，区别于系统性采集的临床记录。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • AEOLUS (Banda et al.) — public FAERS-to-OMOP preprocessing pipeline (github.com/ltscomputingllc/faersdbstats), reused here on the FAERS side; the only component of this paper's pipeline with a confirmed public code repository.
  • MetaNurse drug-selection algorithm — filters candidate drugs via Beers criteria, pediatric-precaution lists, and UN prohibited-substance lists.
  • 1:1 propensity score matching + Cox proportional hazards — standard causal-comparison scaffold applied to the EHR arm.
  • metabin (R) — fixed- and random-effects meta-analysis function combining EHR and FAERS estimates.

Where it lands + buildable artifact — tool sketch

Serves AI-tooling / career priority.

Tool sketch: omop-pv-signal-harness (S–M) — a minimal, from-scratch OMOP-CDM pipeline ingesting Synthea-generated synthetic OMOP data, computing both a FAERS-style ROR disproportionality statistic and a matched-cohort EHR-style hazard-ratio statistic on the same synthetic drug-event pairs, and reporting where the two agree or disagree. The point is demonstrating the two-source-triangulation pattern as reusable, portfolio-grade tooling, not reproducing this paper's specific clinical findings.

What to verify first

  • Whether the actual EHR-to-OMOP conversion code (beyond the FAERS/AEOLUS preprocessing step) is available anywhere, or whether reproduction means starting from general-purpose OHDSI tooling (Achilles, WhiteRabbit) instead.
  • How much the manual PTA-digitization step would need to be replaced by automated image parsing before this pattern generalizes past a single hand-curated proof of concept.
  • Whether the fixed- vs. random-effects disagreement (five vs. three significant drugs) reflects genuine EHR/FAERS heterogeneity or is mechanically driven by FAERS's much larger sample size.
  • Whether the borrowed MetaNurse drug-selection filter (Beers criteria + pediatric-precaution + UN-prohibited lists) is an appropriate candidate filter outside geriatric/pediatric safety monitoring.

OMOP 全称是 Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership（观察性医疗结果合作项目），CDM 是通用数据模型——一套标准化的表结构和词汇表，能把来自完全不同医院、甚至完全不同国家的电子健康记录数据，统一转换成同样的结构，这样同一套分析代码就能在任何地方原样运行。 OMOP is maintained by OHDSI, Observational Health Data Sciences and Informatics, a public-private research network that also builds the open-source tooling around the model. OMOP 由 OHDSI 维护，全称是 Observational Health Data Sciences and Informatics（观察性健康数据科学与信息学），这是一个公私合作的研究网络，也负责构建围绕该模型的开源工具。 The other data source here, FAERS, is the FDA Adverse Event Reporting System — a spontaneous reporting system, SRS for short, meaning people, not an algorithm, actively submit reports when they suspect a drug caused a problem. 这里用到的另一个数据源 FAERS，是 FDA 不良事件报告系统——一种自发报告系统（spontaneous reporting system，SRS），意思是由人主动提交报告，而不是靠算法自动发现，当他们怀疑某种药物导致了问题时就会上报。 EHR data and SRS data have complementary weaknesses: EHR has rich, time-stamped clinical detail but ambiguous outcome definitions, while SRS has an unambiguous "this was reported as an adverse event" label but no denominator and no timeline. EHR 数据和 SRS 数据的弱点正好互补：EHR 有丰富的、带时间戳的临床细节，但结局定义比较模糊；而 SRS 有明确的"这被报告为一次不良事件"标签，但没有分母，也没有时间线。 One more clinical term: PTA, pure tone audiometry, is a hearing test that measures a patient's hearing threshold at different frequencies against a normal-hearing baseline — normally it's read off a graph, not stored as a clean number. 还有一个临床术语：PTA，纯音测听（pure tone audiometry），是一种听力测试，测量患者在不同频率下的听力阈值，与正常听力基线对比——通常是从一张图上读出来的，而不是存成一个干净的数字。 And since we'll use it later: a reporting odds ratio, ROR, is a disproportionality statistic — it compares how often a drug and an adverse event co-occur in reports against how often each shows up with everything else, so a ROR above 1 means that pairing is over-represented relative to background, not that the drug necessarily caused the event. 我们后面会用到这个词：报告比值比（reporting odds ratio，ROR）是一种比例失衡统计量——它比较某种药物和某个不良事件在报告中同时出现的频率，与它们各自和其他所有情况同时出现的频率，ROR 大于 1 意味着这个组合相对于背景水平被过度报告了，但并不代表这种药物一定导致了这个事件。

JORDAN Walk me through the actual pipeline and the drug-safety math. 带我具体过一遍这条流水线，以及药物安全性方面的统计方法。

ALEX On the EHR side, they pulled five years — 2014 through 2018 — of records from Konyang University Hospital in Korea, 347,040 patients total, converted into OMOP tables. 在 EHR 一侧，他们提取了韩国建阳大学医院五年——2014 到 2018 年——的记录，共 347,040 名患者，转换成 OMOP 表格式。 PTA reports specifically were still image files, so roughly 7,800 patients' worth were manually read off the graph and converted to a normal-or-abnormal category using a fixed 20-decibel-per-frequency cutoff — that manual step is worth flagging up front, it's not automated. PTA 报告本身仍是图像文件，所以大约 7800 名患者的数据是人工从图上读出来，并按每个频率固定 20 分贝的阈值转换成"正常/异常"类别——这一步是人工完成的，值得先说明清楚，并没有自动化。 Candidate drugs were selected using an existing algorithm called MetaNurse, screening against Beers-criteria medications, pediatric-precaution drug lists, and a UN prohibited-substances list, narrowing down to nine drugs prescribed to over 100 patients each and already flagged as significantly associated with ear disorders. 候选药物通过一个已有的算法 MetaNurse 筛选，对照 Beers 标准用药清单、儿科慎用药物清单，以及联合国禁用物质清单，最终筛出九种处方超过 100 名患者、且已被标记为与耳部疾病显著相关的药物。 They then ran 1:1 propensity score matching on age, sex, visit count, and hospitalization length, followed by Cox proportional hazards regression, to get a hazard ratio for each drug in the EHR cohort. 随后他们对年龄、性别、就诊次数和住院时长做了 1:1 倾向评分匹配，再用 Cox 比例风险回归，得到 EHR 队列中每种药物的风险比（hazard ratio）。 On the FAERS side, with nearly 6.95 million reports from 2012 to 2018, they built simple 2-by-2 contingency tables per drug-ADR pair and computed a reporting odds ratio, ROR — the standard disproportionality statistic in pharmacovigilance, essentially asking whether a drug shows up disproportionately often alongside a given adverse event compared to all other drug-event pairs in the database. 在 FAERS 一侧，凭借 2012 到 2018 年将近 695 万份报告，他们为每个药物-不良反应组合建立了简单的 2×2 列联表，并计算出报告比值比（reporting odds ratio，ROR）——这是药物警戒领域标准的比例失衡统计量，本质上是判断某种药物在数据库中，与某个不良事件同时出现的频率，是否相对于所有其他药物-事件组合明显偏高。 Finally, they combined both sources with a random-effects and fixed-effects meta-analysis using R's metabin function. 最后，他们用 R 的 metabin 函数，对两个数据源做了随机效应和固定效应的元分析，把结果合并起来。
