How did this episode land for you?

Track A — Cross-protein transfer learning for missense pathogenicity (Jagota et al., 2023)

Blurb. Jagota, Song, and colleagues (Genome Biology, 2023) train CPT-1, a missense-pathogenicity predictor, on deep mutational scanning (DMS) data from only five human proteins measured with one uniform yeast-fitness assay. By fusing protein-language-model scores (EVE, ESM-1v), shallow vertebrate conservation (~100 genomes), and AlphaFold2 structural features — and deliberately not training on clinical labels — CPT-1 transfers to unseen genes and reaches 68% specificity at 95% sensitivity on ClinVar (vs 27% ESM-1v, 55% EVE), while comparing favorably to the widely used supervised predictor REVEL on genes REVEL never saw. Predictions are released for ~90% of human genes. Where it lands: the Karen / CFTR variant-interpretation move. Buildable artifact: a CFTR missense feature-fusion and calibration notebook that assembles the same feature stack for CFTR variants and benchmarks it against CFTR2 and CFTR functional data to pick and calibrate the predictor feeding the PP3/BP4 evidence lane.

Plain-English glossary

  • AlphaFold2 structural features — properties read off a predicted 3D protein structure, e.g. whether a residue is buried in the core or surface-exposed.（中文:从 AlphaFold2 预测的三维蛋白质结构中读出的性质,例如某残基是埋在内核还是暴露在表面。)
  • ClinVar — a public archive of variants with clinical significance classifications; widely used as ground truth, which creates circularity when tools both train and test on it.（中文:ClinVar 是变异临床意义分类的公共档案库;常被当作真值,当工具既在它上面训练又在它上面测试时会产生循环性。)
  • Conservation / multiple sequence alignment (MSA) — stacking the same protein across many species to see which positions never change; unchanged positions are usually functionally critical.（中文:多序列比对把多物种的同一蛋白质叠在一起,看哪些位点从不改变;不变的位点通常对功能至关重要。)
  • Deep mutational scanning (DMS) / saturation mutagenesis — an experiment that makes every possible single amino-acid change in a protein and measures each one's effect on function at once.（中文:深度突变扫描/饱和突变一次性制造蛋白质里每一种可能的单氨基酸改变并测量其对功能的影响。)
  • Missense variant — a single DNA change that swaps one amino acid for another, leaving a full-length protein of uncertain function.（中文:错义变异是一个改变一个氨基酸的单碱基突变,产生功能不确定的全长蛋白质。)
  • Pathogenicity — how likely a variant is to cause disease.（中文:致病性,即某变异导致疾病的可能性。)
  • Protein language model (EVE, ESM-1v) — a model trained on millions of natural protein sequences that learns which amino-acid patterns are plausible, without seeing clinical labels.（中文:蛋白质语言模型在数百万天然蛋白质序列上训练,学习哪些氨基酸模式合理,不接触临床标签。)
  • REVEL — a widely used supervised ensemble missense predictor (kept in English).（中文:REVEL 是一个被广泛使用的监督式集成错义预测器。)

Full citation

Jagota M, Ye C, Albors C, Rastogi R, Koehl A, Ioannidis N, Song YS. Cross-protein transfer learning substantially improves disease variant prediction. Genome Biology. 2023;24:182. PMID: 37550700 · PMC: PMC10408151 · DOI: 10.1186/s13059-023-03024-6

Methods / tools to lift

  • Cross-protein transfer (CPT): train on DMS from a few well-controlled proteins, predict for the whole proteome — escapes clinical-label circularity.
  • Feature fusion: combine EVE + ESM-1v (protein language models) + vertebrate conservation + AlphaFold2 burial/structure features, then feature-select down to a small model (9 features here).
  • High-sensitivity evaluation: report specificity at fixed 95% sensitivity, the clinically decisive operating point, not just AUROC.
  • Fair-comparison design: to compare against a supervised tool (REVEL), restrict to genes whose labels the tool never trained on.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

  • Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation.
  • MVP (paste-ready): cftr-missense-feature-fusion-notebook — input a list of CFTR missense variants (HGVS); pull each variant's EVE, ESM-1v, CPT-1 released score, vertebrate conservation, and AlphaFold-derived burial features; benchmark against CFTR2 classifications + published CFTR functional assays; report which predictor/fusion best separates pathogenic from benign at 95% sensitivity and output calibrated PP3/BP4 thresholds. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • Whether CPT-1's proteome-wide accuracy actually holds for CFTR, a large multi-domain membrane transporter unlike the five small soluble training proteins — check calibration on CFTR variants specifically.
  • Residual ClinVar circularity: validate CFTR calls against orthogonal truth (functional assays, CFTR2), not ClinVar alone.
  • Vertebrate-alignment depth and AlphaFold confidence at the specific CFTR positions where clinically important variants sit.
  • Calibration to ACMG evidence strength (PP3/BP4 tiers), not just score ranking. No registry-internal data is involved — external variant-level method only.

ALEX Today we are reading "Cross-protein transfer learning substantially improves disease variant prediction," by Milind Jagota and colleagues in Yun Song's group, published in Genome Biology in 2023. 今天我们读的是 Milind Jagota 及其合作者(资深作者 Yun Song)2023 年发表在《Genome Biology》上的论文《跨蛋白质迁移学习大幅提升疾病变异预测》。 It is a Track A paper, squarely in variant interpretation. 这是一篇 Track A 论文,正好属于变异解读这一主题。 We are reading it because it proposes a way to train an accurate missense-pathogenicity predictor from a surprisingly tiny amount of experimental data, and that recipe maps directly onto CFTR. 我们读它,是因为它提出了一种方法:用出奇少量的实验数据就能训练出准确的错义变异 (missense variant) 致病性 (pathogenicity) 预测器,而这个思路可以直接迁移到 CFTR 上。 The claim I want to test on air is whether five proteins can really teach a model about the whole proteome. 我想在节目里检验的核心主张是:仅仅五个蛋白质,真的能教会模型理解整个蛋白质组吗?

JORDAN Before we get into the model, I need the biology vocabulary. 在进入模型之前,我需要先搞清楚生物学词汇。 What exactly is a missense variant, and why is it the hard case? 到底什么是错义变异,为什么它是最难的情形?

ALEX A missense variant is a single-letter DNA change that swaps one amino acid for another in the protein the gene encodes. 错义变异是指一个单碱基的 DNA 改变,使基因所编码蛋白质中的一个氨基酸被替换成另一个。 Unlike a nonsense variant that truncates the protein, a missense change leaves a full-length protein that might work fine, work a little worse, or be completely broken, and you cannot tell which from the sequence alone. 与会截断蛋白质的无义变异 (nonsense variant) 不同,错义变异保留了全长蛋白质,它可能功能正常、稍差、或完全失效,而你无法仅凭序列判断是哪一种。 That ambiguity is why tens of millions of possible missense variants sit in databases labeled "of uncertain significance." 正是这种模糊性,使得数据库里数以千万计的可能错义变异被标注为"意义不明" (variant of uncertain significance)。

JORDAN And the experimental data they train on — deep mutational scanning — what is that? 那他们用来训练的实验数据——深度突变扫描——又是什么?

ALEX Deep mutational scanning, or DMS, is an experiment where you make every possible single amino-acid change in a protein and measure how each one affects function, all at once. 深度突变扫描 (DMS,deep mutational scanning) 是一种实验:你在一个蛋白质里制造出每一种可能的单氨基酸改变,并一次性测量每一种改变对功能的影响。 People also call this saturation mutagenesis, because you saturate every position with every substitution. 人们也把它叫做饱和突变 (saturation mutagenesis),因为你在每个位点都用尽了所有替换。 In this paper the readout is yeast fitness: the human protein is expressed in yeast, and how well the yeast grows tells you how functional that variant is. 在这篇论文里,读数是酵母的生长适合度:把人类蛋白质在酵母中表达,酵母长得好不好就反映了该变异的功能高低。 It is powerful because it gives thousands of graded functional measurements per protein, but it does not scale to all twenty-thousand human proteins, and you have to design a working assay for each one. 它很强大,因为每个蛋白质能给出成千上万个分级的功能测量值,但它无法扩展到全部两万个人类蛋白质,而且你得为每一个蛋白质单独设计一个可行的实验。

JORDAN So the whole game is: use DMS on a few proteins, then predict for the ones you never measured. 所以整个游戏就是:在少数几个蛋白质上做 DMS,然后去预测那些你从未测量过的蛋白质。 What signals does the model actually look at? 模型实际上依赖哪些信号?

ALEX Three complementary sources, and the combination is the interesting part. 三种互补的信号来源,而它们的组合正是有意思之处。 First, general protein language models — EVE and ESM-1v — which learn the statistical grammar of proteins from millions of natural sequences without ever seeing a clinical label. 第一,通用的蛋白质语言模型 (protein language model)——EVE 和 ESM-1v——它们从数百万条天然序列中学习蛋白质的统计"语法",而从未见过任何临床标签。 EVE (an unsupervised model of evolutionary sequence variation) 和 ESM-1v (a protein language model trained on raw sequence) are the two here. EVE(一个对进化序列变异建模的无监督模型)和 ESM-1v(一个在原始序列上训练的蛋白质语言模型)是这里用到的两个。 Second, conservation from vertebrate multiple sequence alignments. 第二,来自脊椎动物多序列比对 (multiple sequence alignment) 的保守性 (conservation)。 A multiple sequence alignment stacks the same protein from many species so you can see which positions never change over evolution — those tend to be functionally critical, so a variant there is more suspicious. 多序列比对把来自许多物种的同一个蛋白质叠在一起,让你看到哪些位点在进化中从不改变——这些位点往往对功能至关重要,因此该处的变异更值得怀疑。 Third, structural features computed from AlphaFold2 predicted structures — things like whether the residue is buried in the protein core or exposed on the surface. 第三,从 AlphaFold2 预测结构中计算出的结构特征——比如某个残基是埋在蛋白质内核里还是暴露在表面。

JORDAN Why would a hundred-genome alignment matter when a language model has already read millions of sequences? 当一个语言模型已经读过数百万条序列时,一个仅一百个基因组的比对为什么还重要? ALEX: Because they capture different things: a language model learns broad protein grammar across all life, while a focused vertebrate alignment captures which positions are locked down specifically in animals close to humans. 因为它们捕捉的是不同的东西:语言模型学习的是横跨所有生命的宽泛蛋白质语法,而一个聚焦的脊椎动物比对捕捉的是——在与人类相近的动物中——哪些位点被特别地锁定。 A position can look tolerant across bacteria and fungi but be strictly conserved across mammals, and that mammal-specific constraint is exactly the clinically relevant signal. 一个位点在细菌和真菌里可能看起来可变,但在哺乳动物中却严格保守,而这种哺乳动物特异的约束,恰恰就是与临床相关的信号。

JORDAN What problem were they trying to fix that the existing predictors could not? 他们想解决的、现有预测器解决不了的问题是什么?

ALEX The deep worry in this field is circularity in the training data. 这个领域深层的担忧是训练数据的循环性。 Many popular clinical predictors are supervised on ClinVar labels, and then they get evaluated on ClinVar, so they can look great partly because they memorized the answer key. 许多流行的临床预测器是在 ClinVar 标签上做监督训练的,然后又在 ClinVar 上评估,所以它们看起来很好,部分原因是记住了答案。 ClinVar (a public archive of clinically classified variants) is both the training set and the exam for those tools. ClinVar(一个临床已分类变异的公共档案库)对这些工具而言,既是训练集又是考卷。 The authors wanted a predictor whose signal comes from functional experiments and evolution rather than from clinical labels, so its accuracy would transfer honestly to genes it had never seen. 作者们想要一个信号来自功能实验和进化、而非临床标签的预测器,这样它的准确性才能诚实地迁移到从未见过的基因上。 The other unsolved piece was clinical usability: a tool has to catch almost every true disease variant, and many predictors sacrifice that high-sensitivity regime. 另一个未解决的问题是临床可用性:一个工具必须抓住几乎每一个真正的致病变异,而许多预测器在这个高敏感度区间表现不佳。

JORDAN Why is high sensitivity the metric they keep returning to instead of overall accuracy? 为什么他们反复强调的是高敏感度,而不是整体准确率? ALEX: Because in a clinic the cost of the two error types is wildly asymmetric. 因为在临床上,两类错误的代价极不对称。 Missing a true pathogenic variant can mean a missed diagnosis, so you tune the tool to catch nearly all of them, and then the only question that matters is how many false positives you eat to get there. 漏掉一个真正的致病变异可能意味着漏诊,所以你把工具调到几乎能抓住全部致病变异,而此时唯一重要的问题就是:为了做到这一点,你要吞下多少误报。 That is why a jump from 27% to 68% specificity at fixed 95% sensitivity is a genuinely clinical improvement, not just a prettier curve. 这就是为什么在固定 95% 敏感度下、特异度从 27% 跃升到 68% 是一个真正意义上的临床改进,而不只是曲线更好看。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built. 带我过一遍他们到底构建了什么。

ALEX They trained a model called CPT-1 on DMS data from just five human proteins — CALM1, MTHFR, SUMO1, UBE2I, and TPK1 — all measured with the same yeast-fitness assay by the same lab. 他们训练了一个叫 CPT-1 的模型,只用了五个人类蛋白质的 DMS 数据——CALM1、MTHFR、SUMO1、UBE2I 和 TPK1——全部由同一个实验室用同一套酵母适合度实验测得。 That homogeneity is a deliberate choice: one clean assay avoids mixing incompatible measurement scales. 这种同质性是刻意的选择:单一干净的实验避免了混入互不兼容的测量尺度。 They fed the three feature sources into a feature-selection step and kept nine features for the final model. 他们把三类特征来源送入一个特征选择步骤,最终模型保留了九个特征。 One design choice I would push on is training on only five proteins that are all small, soluble, and from one assay. 我想质疑的一个设计选择是:只在五个都很小、可溶、且来自同一实验的蛋白质上训练。 They actually tested adding thirteen more DMS datasets from ProteinGym and found it did not improve CPT-1, which is reassuring, but it still leaves open how well the recipe covers exotic proteins like large membrane transporters. 他们其实测试过再加入 ProteinGym 里的十三个 DMS 数据集,发现并没有提升 CPT-1,这让人稍感安心,但仍然留下一个问题:这套配方对像大型膜转运蛋白这类特殊蛋白质覆盖得如何。

ALEX The reason the yeast-fitness assay transfers well is subtle: it measures overall organismal fitness with the human protein swapped in, not one narrow biophysical property, so it is closer to "does this variant break the protein in a way that matters for the cell." 酵母适合度实验之所以迁移得好,原因很微妙:它测量的是换入人类蛋白质后整个生物体的适合度,而不是某一个狭窄的生物物理性质,因此它更接近"这个变异是否以一种对细胞有影响的方式破坏了蛋白质"。 That framing is why a model trained on yeast growth can say something useful about human disease. 正是这种设定,使得一个在酵母生长上训练的模型能够对人类疾病说出有用的话。

JORDAN And does it actually work on the clinical test? 那它在临床测试上真的有效吗?

ALEX On a high-quality set of 24,155 ClinVar missense variants across 1,298 genes, released from 2017 onward with at least a one-star annotation, CPT-1 beat both EVE and ESM-1v on the overall ROC curve. 在一个高质量的测试集上——24,155 个 ClinVar 错义变异,横跨 1,298 个基因,均为 2017 年以后发布、至少一星注释——CPT-1 在整体 ROC 曲线上击败了 EVE 和 ESM-1v。 The headline number is at the clinically important operating point: at 95% sensitivity for detecting known disease variants, CPT-1 reaches 68% specificity, versus 27% for ESM-1v and 55% for EVE. 最重要的数字出现在临床关键工作点上:在检测已知致病变异达到 95% 敏感度时,CPT-1 的特异度达到 68%,而 ESM-1v 为 27%、EVE 为 55%。 In plain terms, when you insist on catching almost all the real disease variants, CPT-1 raises far fewer false alarms. 说白了,当你要求抓住几乎所有真正的致病变异时,CPT-1 产生的误报要少得多。 They also compared against REVEL, a supervised ensemble that clinicians use widely, on a fair subset of 3,754 variants in 407 genes whose labels REVEL never trained on, and CPT-1 held up favorably. 他们还与 REVEL(一个临床广泛使用的监督集成模型)在一个公平子集上比较——3,754 个变异、407 个基因,这些标签 REVEL 从未训练过——CPT-1 表现依然占优。 REVEL is an opaque acronym I will keep in English; it is just a widely-used supervised missense predictor. REVEL 是一个我会保留英文的缩写;它就是一个被广泛使用的监督式错义预测器。 And a finding I did not expect: their vertebrate alignments used only about 100 genomes, yet that shallow conservation signal was strongly complementary to the deep-learning language models trained on massive sequence data. 还有一个我没料到的发现:他们的脊椎动物比对只用了大约 100 个基因组,但这种"浅"的保守性信号,与在海量序列上训练的深度学习语言模型高度互补。 They released predictions for every possible missense variant in about 90% of human genes. 他们公开了约 90% 人类基因中每一个可能错义变异的预测结果。

JORDAN Quick practical question first — for a given CFTR variant, could we just look up their published number? 先问个实际问题——对于某个给定的 CFTR 变异,我们能不能直接查他们发布的分数? ALEX: Sometimes, if CFTR falls in that 90% and the exact position is covered, but the released score is only a starting point. 有时可以,如果 CFTR 落在那 90% 里、且确切位点被覆盖到,但已发布的分数只是一个起点。 The real value for us is the recipe, not the lookup table: rebuilding the feature stack for CFTR lets us recalibrate thresholds against CFTR-specific truth, which a one-size-fits-all proteome score cannot do. 对我们真正的价值在于配方,而不是查找表:为 CFTR 重建特征堆栈,让我们能够针对 CFTR 特异的真值重新校准阈值,而一个"一刀切"的全蛋白质组分数做不到。

JORDAN This is where I want to know where it lands for us. 我最想知道的是这对我们意味着什么。

ALEX This lands squarely on the Karen collaboration — CFTR variant interpretation. 这正好落在 Karen 的合作方向上——CFTR 变异解读。 The transferable idea is feature fusion plus honest transfer: you do not need a CFTR-specific supervised model, you assemble EVE, ESM-1v, vertebrate conservation, and AlphaFold structural features and let a light model combine them. 可迁移的思想是特征融合加上诚实的迁移:你不需要一个 CFTR 专属的监督模型,而是把 EVE、ESM-1v、脊椎动物保守性和 AlphaFold 结构特征组装起来,让一个轻量模型去组合它们。 The concrete buildable artifact is a CFTR missense feature-fusion and calibration notebook. 具体可构建的产物是一个 CFTR 错义特征融合与校准笔记本 (notebook)。 The MVP: input a list of CFTR missense variants in HGVS notation; pull each variant's EVE, ESM-1v, CPT-1 released score, vertebrate conservation, and AlphaFold-derived burial features; then benchmark them against CFTR's own published functional-assay readouts and CFTR2 classifications to see which predictor, or which fusion, best separates known-pathogenic from known-benign at high sensitivity. MVP 是这样:输入一批用 HGVS(变异命名的标准化书写规范,HGVS)表示的 CFTR 错义变异;为每个变异提取 EVE、ESM-1v、CPT-1 已发布分数、脊椎动物保守性,以及 AlphaFold 推导的埋藏度特征;然后用 CFTR 自己已发表的功能实验读数和 CFTR2 分类作为基准,看哪个预测器、或哪种融合,能在高敏感度下最好地把已知致病与已知良性分开。 HGVS is the standardized way variants are written, and CFTR2 is the curated CFTR variant database. HGVS 是变异书写的标准化规范,而 CFTR2 是经过人工审编的 CFTR 变异数据库。 Difficulty is medium: the features are all public, the labels come from CFTR2 and functional papers, and the output feeds directly into the PP3/BP4 evidence lane we have been building. 难度中等:特征全是公开的,标签来自 CFTR2 和功能学论文,而输出直接接入我们一直在搭建的 PP3/BP4 证据线。 PP3 and BP4 are the ACMG evidence codes for computational pathogenic and benign support. PP3 和 BP4 是 ACMG(变异分类的标准规则集,ACMG)里表示计算证据支持致病或支持良性的两个代码。

JORDAN What would we need to check before trusting this on CFTR? 在把它用到 CFTR 上并信任它之前,我们需要核实什么?
