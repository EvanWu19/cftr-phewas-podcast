Ep2 — LLM-AIx: an open-source, local LLM pipeline for medical information extraction

Wiest, Wolf, Leßmann, and colleagues from Kather's lab (NPJ Precision Oncology, 2025) ship LLM-AIx, a documented, Dockerized, open-source pipeline (github.com/KatherLab/LLMAIx) that extracts structured fields from unstructured clinical text using in-context learning rather than task-specific fine-tuning. It runs entirely locally via llama.cpp on GGUF-quantized models (Llama-2/3, Phi-3, Mistral) on consumer-grade hardware (single GPU, 48GB VRAM, or an RTX 4090/Apple Silicon M2), removing the data-governance burden of a cloud API. Validated across anonymization (99.9% specificity/100% sensitivity on fictitious pulmonary-embolism letters) and information extraction (TNM staging on 100 real TCGA colorectal-cancer pathology reports: 87% overall accuracy, with per-category F1 revealing T-stage's real weakness — 89% accuracy but only 0.57 F1 — hidden by the aggregate number). The buildable takeaway is a portfolio-grade local demonstration of the pipeline's design pattern: local open-weight models, in-context field definitions, and a built-in ground-truth-vs-extraction confusion-matrix evaluation.

Plain-English glossary

  • In-context learning — Using examples/instructions within a prompt to steer a pretrained LLM's output, without updating any model weights.（中文：在提示内容中使用示例或指令来引导预训练LLM的输出，不更新任何模型权重。）
  • TNM staging — The standard cancer-staging system: T (primary tumor size/local spread), N (regional lymph node involvement), M (distant metastasis).（中文：标准的癌症分期体系：T（原发肿瘤大小/局部扩散）、N（区域淋巴结受累）、M（远处转移）。）
  • GGUF — A file format used by llama.cpp for storing (typically quantized) model weights for efficient local inference.（中文：llama.cpp用于存储（通常经过量化的）模型权重、以实现高效本地推理的一种文件格式。）
  • Quantization — Compressing model weights from full precision (16/32-bit) to lower precision (4/8-bit) to reduce memory and compute cost, at some accuracy trade-off.（中文：将模型权重从全精度（16/32位）压缩到低精度（4/8位），以降低内存和算力成本，但会带来一定的准确率损失。）
  • llama.cpp — A C++ inference engine for running large language models efficiently on consumer hardware.（中文：一个用于在消费级硬件上高效运行大语言模型的C++推理引擎。）
  • Coverage rate — In this context, the fraction of documents for which a model returns a usable field value rather than "unknown"/blank. (Distinct from Track C's use of the term.)（中文：在此语境下，指模型对文档实际返回可用字段值而非"未知"/空值的比例。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • LLM-AIx (github.com/KatherLab/LLMAIx) — open-source, Docker-or-manual-install, locally-runnable LLM information-extraction pipeline.
  • llama.cpp + GGUF quantized models — local inference stack requiring no cloud API and no institutional compute cluster.
  • Four-stage design — (1) problem definition/data prep, (2) preprocessing (OCR, format normalization), (3) LLM-based extraction, (4) automated confusion-matrix evaluation against ground truth.
  • In-context field definitions — plain-language extraction-field specification instead of task-specific fine-tuning.

What to verify first

  • Whether accuracy numbers hold on real (non-fictitious, non-pre-corrected) clinical text at deployment scale.
  • Actual compute cost/latency at a research-team-relevant document volume (dozens-hundreds vs. the small demo sets shown).
  • Whether in-context field definitions generalize as well to a genuinely different document type/vocabulary than pathology reports or clinical letters.
  • How much manual ground-truth-curation effort the evaluation mode assumes for any new extraction task.

JORDAN Before the architecture — what's actually hard about pulling structured data out of a pathology report, and what's TNM staging, since that's the running example? 在讲架构之前——从病理报告里提取结构化数据，实际难在哪里？他们用作示例的TNM分期又是什么？

ALEX Pathology and clinical reports are free text written by clinicians for other clinicians, full of abbreviations, negations, and institution-specific phrasing, so a rule-based parser or a narrowly fine-tuned classifier breaks the moment the phrasing shifts even slightly. 病理报告和临床报告是临床医生写给其他临床医生看的自由文本，充满缩写、否定表达和机构特有的措辞，所以一旦措辞稍有变化，基于规则的解析器或狭窄微调的分类器就会失效。 TNM staging is the standard cancer-staging system: T describes the primary tumor's size and local spread, N describes how many nearby lymph nodes are involved, and M describes whether the cancer has metastasized to distant sites — each gets its own category, like T1 or N2, and the three combine into an overall stage that drives treatment decisions. TNM分期是癌症分期的标准体系：T描述原发肿瘤的大小及局部扩散情况，N描述邻近淋巴结的受累数量，M描述癌症是否已转移到远处部位——每一项都有自己的分类，比如T1或N2，三者组合起来构成一个总体分期，用来指导治疗决策。

JORDAN And that staging information just lives buried in narrative text? 而这些分期信息就这样埋藏在叙述性文本里？

ALEX Right, usually written out in prose within the pathology report's free-text impression section rather than as a discrete structured field, which is exactly the kind of unstructured-to-structured conversion problem this pipeline targets — and it's structurally the same problem as pulling a clean phenotype flag or a lab value out of a free-text clinical note in any registry context, CF included. 没错，通常是以散文形式写在病理报告的自由文本印象（impression）部分，而不是作为独立的结构化字段存在，这正是这个流水线要解决的"非结构化转结构化"问题——而且从结构上看，这和从任何登记数据库背景下的自由文本临床笔记里提取一个干净的表型标记或化验值，是同一类问题，CF登记数据库也不例外。

JORDAN What was the actual gap before this pipeline — hadn't people already tried LLMs for medical text extraction? 在这个流水线出现之前，实际存在什么空白——难道之前没人尝试过用LLM做医学文本提取吗？

ALEX Plenty had, but the authors point to two specific gaps. First, most prior medical NLP, rule-based or the earlier generation of fine-tuned transformers like BERT, needs task-specific annotation and retraining for every new extraction task, which is exactly the cost structure that makes this kind of work slow and expensive for any individual clinical or research group. 很多人已经尝试过，但作者指出了两个具体的空白。第一，大多数以往的医学NLP方法，无论是基于规则的还是早一代的微调式Transformer（比如BERT），都需要针对每一个新的提取任务进行专门标注和重新训练，这正是让这类工作对任何一个具体的临床或研究团队来说都显得缓慢且昂贵的成本结构。 Second, medical free text shows up in wildly different source formats — PDF exports from the clinical information system, scanned documents needing OCR, plain text from a secondary system — and there's no ubiquitous harmonization layer turning all of that into one clean input format before any NLP even starts. 第二，医学自由文本会以千差万别的源格式出现——来自临床信息系统的PDF导出文件、需要OCR的扫描文档、来自次级系统的纯文本——而在任何NLP工作真正开始之前，并没有一个普遍适用的协调层，把这些格式统一转换成一种干净的输入格式。

ALEX There's also a privacy dimension driving the local-deployment choice: sending patient-level clinical text to a cloud API like OpenAI's isn't something most European hospitals or US health systems can do without a substantial data-governance lift, so a pipeline that runs entirely on local hardware, with no data ever leaving the institution, removes that barrier entirely rather than working around it. 还有一个隐私维度推动了"本地部署"这一选择：把病人层面的临床文本发送到像OpenAI这样的云端API，对大多数欧洲医院或美国医疗系统来说，如果没有大量的数据治理工作，是做不到的，所以一个完全在本地硬件上运行、数据永远不离开机构的流水线，是彻底消除了这个障碍，而不是绕开它。

JORDAN So the two design pressures are: no task-specific retraining, and no data leaving the building. 所以两个设计压力是：不需要针对任务重新训练，以及数据不能离开机构。

ALEX That's the whole thesis, and it shapes every downstream design choice — in-context learning instead of fine-tuning, and open-weight local models instead of an API call. 这就是整篇论文的核心论点，它塑造了后续的每一个设计选择——用上下文学习（in-context learning）取代微调，用开放权重的本地模型取代API调用。

JORDAN Where has this actually been used before this paper — is it brand new, or has it been road-tested? 在这篇论文之前，这个工具实际上被用在哪些地方——是全新的，还是已经经过实战检验？

ALEX It's been road-tested across several prior use cases from the same group: extracting suicidality mentions from psychiatric admission notes, parsing endoscopy reports, and processing the MIMIC critical-care dataset, each of which fed lessons back into the current pipeline design before this paper packaged it as a general-purpose, documented release. 它已经在同一团队之前的几个用例中经过了实战检验：从精神科入院记录中提取自杀倾向的相关记述、解析内镜检查报告、以及处理MIMIC重症监护数据集，每一个用例都把经验反馈回了当前的流水线设计，最终这篇论文才把它打包成一个通用的、有完整文档的正式发布版本。 That history matters for trust: this isn't a one-off demo built specifically to look good in a paper, it's a tool that's already absorbed several rounds of real deployment friction across genuinely different note types, psychiatric, gastroenterology, oncology, before the version being benchmarked here. 这段历史对建立信任很重要：这不是一个专门为了在论文里好看而做的一次性演示，而是一个已经在真正不同的笔记类型——精神科、消化科、肿瘤科——上，吸收过好几轮实际部署摩擦之后，才形成这篇论文所基准测试的版本的工具。

JORDAN And the fictitious pulmonary-embolism letters used for the anonymization benchmark — why fictitious rather than real, redacted clinical letters? 用于匿名化基准测试的虚构肺栓塞信件——为什么用虚构的，而不是真实的、已脱敏的临床信件？

ALEX Because you can't ethically publish a benchmark built on real patient letters even after redaction, since the whole point of the anonymization test is checking whether the pipeline successfully strips identifying information, and a leftover identifier in a published benchmark dataset would defeat the purpose. Fictitious letters, written to mimic real clinical structure and phrasing, let them publish a fully reproducible, shareable test set without that risk, at the cost of not knowing for certain whether real clinical letters' messier, more varied phrasing would perform identically. 因为即使经过脱敏处理，你也不能在伦理上把基于真实病人信件构建的基准测试公开发布，因为匿名化测试的整个意义就在于检验流水线是否成功剥离了身份识别信息，而一个公开发布的基准数据集里如果还残留着某个身份标识，就违背了这个测试的初衷。虚构信件模仿了真实临床文本的结构和措辞，让他们能够发布一个完全可复现、可共享的测试集而不承担这个风险，代价是无法百分之百确定，真实临床信件那种更混乱、更多样化的措辞，是否会得到完全相同的表现。

JORDAN Walk me through the pipeline architecture. 跟我讲讲这个流水线的架构。

ALEX Four stages. One, problem definition and data preparation, where the user specifies what fields they want extracted, in plain language, no code. Two, preprocessing, which handles OCR for scanned pages, splits multi-report documents, and normalizes input across CSV, Excel, PDF, and TXT formats. 四个阶段。第一，问题定义与数据准备，用户用自然语言指定想要提取哪些字段，不需要写代码。第二，预处理，负责对扫描页面做OCR，拆分多份报告合并成的文档，并把CSV、Excel、PDF和TXT等不同格式的输入统一规范化。 Three, the actual LLM-based extraction step, running any GGUF-format model through llama.cpp — that's a C++ inference engine for running large language models efficiently on consumer hardware — with models ranging from Llama-2 7B up to Llama-3 70B, plus Phi-3 and Mistral 7B. Four, output evaluation, which compares the extracted CSV against a ground-truth CSV and generates confusion matrices automatically. 第三，实际的基于LLM的提取步骤，通过llama.cpp运行任何GGUF格式的模型——llama.cpp是一个C++推理引擎，用于在消费级硬件上高效运行大语言模型——可选模型从Llama-2 7B到Llama-3 70B不等，外加Phi-3和Mistral 7B。第四，输出评估，把提取出的CSV与真值（ground truth）CSV进行比较，自动生成混淆矩阵。

ALEX The whole thing is open source on GitHub under KatherLab/LLMAIx, runs via Docker or a manual Python virtual-environment setup, and the hardware bar is genuinely low — a single consumer GPU with 48 gigabytes of VRAM, or even an RTX 4090 or Apple Silicon M2 for smaller models, no institutional compute cluster required. 整个工具在GitHub上以KatherLab/LLMAIx这个仓库名开源，可以通过Docker运行，也可以手动搭建Python虚拟环境，硬件门槛确实很低——一块拥有48GB显存的消费级GPU，甚至一块RTX 4090或Apple Silicon M2就能跑较小的模型，不需要机构级计算集群。

JORDAN Quick clarification — GGUF, is that a quantization format specifically, or something broader? 简单问一下——GGUF具体是一种量化格式，还是更广泛意义上的东西？

ALEX GGUF is a file format used by llama.cpp for storing model weights, and in practice it's almost always paired with quantization, compressing a model's weights from full 16- or 32-bit precision down to 4- or 8-bit representations to shrink both memory footprint and inference cost, which is exactly what makes running a 70-billion-parameter model on a single consumer GPU with 48 gigabytes of VRAM feasible at all. GGUF是llama.cpp用来存储模型权重的一种文件格式，在实践中它几乎总是和量化（quantization）搭配使用——把模型权重从完整的16位或32位精度压缩到4位或8位表示，从而同时减少内存占用和推理成本，这正是让在一块拥有48GB显存的单块消费级GPU上运行一个七百亿参数模型成为可能的原因。 That quantization step does trade off some accuracy for that memory savings, which is worth keeping in mind whenever comparing this pipeline's local-model results against a full-precision cloud API result on the same task. 这个量化步骤确实会用一定的准确率损失来换取内存节省，每当把这个流水线的本地模型结果，与云端API在同一任务上的全精度结果做比较时，这一点都值得留意。

JORDAN Where would you push back? 你会在哪里提出质疑？

ALEX The evaluation mode compares against a ground-truth CSV the user has to already have — which is fine for validating the pipeline on a known dataset, but it quietly assumes someone has already done the expensive part, curating that ground truth, for any new extraction task, and the paper doesn't really engage with how much manual annotation effort that step requires before you can trust a new field's extraction accuracy on a dataset without one. 评估模式需要用户已经拥有一份真值CSV来做对比——这对在已知数据集上验证流水线是没问题的，但它悄悄假设了，对于任何一个新的提取任务，昂贵的部分——整理出这份真值——已经有人做完了，而这篇论文并没有真正讨论清楚，在一个没有真值的数据集上，要建立起对某个新字段提取准确率的信任，需要多少人工标注工作量。

JORDAN Did it actually work well? 它实际效果如何？

ALEX Across use cases, yes, with real variation by task difficulty. On anonymization of eight fictitious pulmonary-embolism clinical letters using Llama-3.1 70B, character-wise evaluation hit 99.9% specificity and 100% sensitivity overall, with precision ranging from 100% for full patient-ID redaction down to 90% for last-name redaction specifically — that drop came from one fictitious patient's last name matching the treating provider's surname, 'Miller,' which the model then redacted inconsistently. 在不同用例中，效果确实不错，但会随任务难度真实波动。在使用Llama-3.1 70B对八封虚构的肺栓塞临床信件做匿名化处理时，逐字符评估显示总体特异性达到99.9%，敏感性达到100%，精确率则从"完整患者ID"脱敏的100%，到"姓氏"脱敏的90%不等——这个下降是因为其中一位虚构病人的姓氏恰好和主治医生的姓氏"Miller"相同，导致模型对这个姓氏的脱敏处理出现了不一致。

ALEX For information extraction on the same fictitious pulmonary-embolism letters, most symptom fields — shortness of breath, chest pain, cough, dizziness — hit perfect 100% accuracy and F1, but leg pain or swelling dropped to 88% accuracy and an F1 of 0.8, with a recall of only 67%, meaning the model missed about a third of the actual leg-symptom mentions. 在对同一批虚构肺栓塞信件做信息提取时，大多数症状字段——呼吸急促、胸痛、咳嗽、头晕——都达到了100%的准确率和F1分数，但"腿部疼痛或肿胀"这一项准确率降到88%，F1为0.8，召回率（recall）只有67%，也就是说模型漏掉了大约三分之一真实存在的腿部症状记录。 The oncology-specific demonstration used 100 TCGA colorectal cancer pathology reports and Llama-3.1 70B to extract TNM stage: overall accuracy across all extracted variables was 87%, but that average hides real spread — N-stage extraction hit 92% accuracy with F1 of 0.86, while T-stage hit 89% accuracy but only 0.57 F1, because T-stage has more sub-categories and more room for a close-but-wrong classification. 面向肿瘤学的具体演示使用了100份TCGA结直肠癌病理报告，用Llama-3.1 70B来提取TNM分期：所有提取变量的总体准确率为87%，但这个平均值掩盖了真实的差异——N分期提取准确率达到92%，F1为0.86，而T分期虽然准确率达到89%，但F1只有0.57，因为T分期的子类别更多，更容易出现"接近但不完全正确"的分类错误。

JORDAN So accuracy alone is a misleading headline number here. 所以单看准确率这个数字，在这里是有误导性的。

ALEX Right, and the paper itself demonstrates that by digging into F1 and finding T-stage's real weakness despite a respectable-looking 89% accuracy — a useful reminder to always ask for the per-class breakdown, not just the aggregate, whenever someone reports a single accuracy number for a multi-category clinical extraction task. 没错，论文本身也通过深入分析F1分数，发现了T分期在看似不错的89%准确率背后真实存在的弱点——这提醒我们，每当有人为一个多类别临床提取任务只报告一个准确率数字时，一定要追问按类别拆分的结果，而不是只看聚合数字。
