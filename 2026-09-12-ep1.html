Ep1 (Track A) — MOSAIC: multimodal feature fusion for noncanonical splice-altering variant pathogenicity prediction

Blurb. Most splice-variant predictors are tuned for the canonical +/-1/+/-2 GT-AG dinucleotides, where a hit is strong evidence of pathogenicity almost by definition. The much harder and more common problem is the noncanonical splice-altering variant (SAV) — a deep-intronic or exonic change, like CFTR's own 3849+10kbC>T, that disrupts a weaker signal (a branch point, an exonic splicing enhancer) without touching the GT-AG at all. MOSAIC, published in Briefings in Bioinformatics, fuses three feature streams — DNA2vec local k-mer embeddings, GPN-MSA evolutionary-conservation embeddings, and 47 hand-crafted functional annotation features — through a multi-scale CNN, a transformer encoder, and a gated fusion module, and beats six established splicing predictors (CADD, MMSplice, SpliceAI, SQUIRLS, Pangolin, DeltaSplice) on every benchmark tested, including a gene-independent holdout that rules out label leakage. It lands squarely in the Karen Raraigh collaboration lane: the buildable artifact is a scored shortlist of CFTR's deep-intronic and noncanonical VUS, built either from MOSAIC directly (if code/weights turn out to be public) or from a replicated version of its feature recipe.

Plain-English glossary

  • Branch point — a short, weakly conserved sequence motif deep in an intron that the spliceosome uses to loop the intron out during splicing; variants here can disrupt splicing without touching the canonical GT-AG.（中文：内含子深处一段保守性较弱的短序列，剪接体用它来完成内含子的环出和去除，此处的变异可在不涉及GT-AG位点的情况下破坏剪接。）
  • Canonical splice site — the nearly invariant GT...AG dinucleotides marking the start and end of an intron; mutating these is treated as strong evidence of pathogenicity under ACMG guidelines.（中文：标志内含子起止、几乎不变的GT...AG二核苷酸序列；根据ACMG指南，破坏这两个位点被视为致病性的有力证据。）
  • ACMG (American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics) — the professional body whose joint guidelines with AMP define the evidence framework used to classify variants as benign, likely benign, VUS, likely pathogenic, or pathogenic.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会，其与AMP联合制定的指南是目前对变异进行良性/致病性分级的权威证据框架。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant that current evidence cannot confidently classify as benign or pathogenic.（中文：意义未明变异，指现有证据尚不足以将其明确归类为良性或致病性的变异。）
  • Deep-intronic variant — a variant located far from the exon-intron boundary (not in the canonical +/-1/+/-2 positions) that can still disrupt splicing, e.g. by creating a cryptic exon.（中文：位于外显子-内含子边界较远处（不在典型+/-1、+/-2位置）的变异，仍可能通过制造隐蔽外显子等机制破坏剪接。）
  • Cryptic exon — a segment of intronic sequence that gets mistakenly spliced into the mature transcript as if it were an exon, usually because a deep-intronic variant creates a new splice site.（中文：内含子中的一段序列被错误地当作外显子拼接进成熟转录本，通常是深内含子变异制造了新的剪接位点所致。）
  • HGMD (Human Gene Mutation Database) — a curated, license-gated database of disease-associated gene mutations used here as a training-data source.（中文：人类基因突变数据库，一个需要许可证访问的疾病相关基因突变精选数据库，本文用作训练数据来源之一。）
  • ClinVar — a free, NIH-maintained public archive of variant-to-phenotype relationships and their clinical significance classifications.（中文：美国国立卫生研究院维护的免费公开数据库，收录变异与表型的关系及其临床意义分级。）
  • DNA2vec — a k-mer embedding method (inspired by word2vec) that converts short DNA subsequences into dense vectors capturing local sequence similarity.（中文：受word2vec启发的k-mer嵌入方法，将短DNA子序列转化为能够捕捉局部序列相似性的稠密向量。）
  • GPN-MSA — a DNA language model pretrained on multi-species whole-genome alignments, used to encode evolutionary conservation and functional constraint around a variant.（中文：基于多物种全基因组比对预训练的DNA语言模型，用于编码变异周围的进化保守性和功能限制信息。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • DNA2vec — dense k-mer embeddings for local DNA sequence context.
  • GPN-MSA — pretrained multi-species-alignment DNA language model for long-range conservation signal.
  • Multi-scale CNN + squeeze-and-excitation blocks — local pattern extraction at multiple receptive fields with channel-wise recalibration.
  • Gated fusion module — adaptive blending of sequence-embedding and hand-crafted functional-annotation streams.
  • Gene-independent holdout design (HDGTesting) — a reusable evaluation pattern: withhold entire genes, not just variants, to rule out gene-level label leakage.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation collaboration. MVP: pull CFTR's current CFTR2 + ClinVar VUS and unclassified-variant list restricted to noncanonical positions (outside +/-1/+/-2), score them with MOSAIC (if code/weights are public) or a replicated feature pipeline, and produce a ranked shortlist for discussion with Karen on functional follow-up or reclassification. Difficulty: M (replicating the fusion architecture is a multi-week effort if weights aren't released; a scaled-down DNA2vec-only version is a faster first pass).

What to verify first

  • Whether MOSAIC's code, trained weights, or an inference endpoint are actually publicly available — not stated either way in the full text retrieved.
  • How well CFTR and CF-relevant variant classes are represented in the HGMD/ClinVar training distribution (the gene-independent test set covered 159 variants from unspecified genes, not confirmed to include CFTR).
  • Per-variant score calibration, not just aggregate AUC/AUPR — the paper doesn't report a calibration analysis.
  • Any CFTR VUS flagged by MOSAIC would need orthogonal functional evidence (e.g., minigene assay, RNA study) before supporting a classification change.
