Blurb

This episode takes yesterday's conclusion — that a long-term cancer PASS needs both an ITT-aligned and a per-protocol estimand — as its question, and pairs it with Hernán and Hernández-Díaz's 2012 paper, retrieved in full text, on why intention-to-treat is not the safe default it's often assumed to be. The paper's core move is to separate two arguments that get conflated: ITT is conservative in placebo-controlled efficacy trials because nonadherence dilutes the contrast toward the null, but in a safety study that same dilution is bias toward declaring a drug safe, which makes ITT anti-conservative for harm. The episode works through the mechanics of that dilution, why the direction and size of the bias depends on the adherence pattern rather than the drug itself, why naive as-treated and per-protocol analyses just relocate the bias into confounding or selection, and why a defensible per-protocol estimate needs inverse probability of censoring weighting or another g-method to handle time-varying confounding that is itself affected by prior treatment. It closes on the practical asymmetry in the team's own design: the ITT-aligned trial is cheap and regulator-friendly, the per-protocol trial is the one that could actually answer the clinical question but carries assumptions that are hard to verify in a registry with frequent biologic switching.

Plain-English glossary

As-treated analysis — A comparison that classifies people by the treatment they actually received rather than the one they were assigned, which turns a randomized comparison into an observational one and inherits confounding by indication from whatever drove the decision to stay on or switch off treatment.（中文：按实际治疗分析，即按患者实际接受的治疗而非最初分配的治疗进行比较，这会使随机对照比较变成观察性比较，并因"是否继续用药"背后的临床决策而产生适应症混杂。)

Confounding by indication — Bias that arises when the clinical reasoning behind a treatment decision (such as staying on or switching off a drug) is itself related to prognosis, so the treated and untreated groups differ in ways connected to the outcome.（中文：适应症混杂，指治疗决策背后的临床考量（如是否继续或停用某药物）本身与预后相关，导致治疗组与非治疗组之间存在与结局相关的系统性差异。)

G-methods — A family of causal-inference techniques, including inverse probability weighting, the g-formula, and g-estimation, designed to correctly adjust for time-varying confounders that are themselves affected by prior treatment, a setting where conventional stratification, regression, or matching gives biased answers.（中文：g方法，一类因果推断技术，包括逆概率加权、g公式与g估计，专用于正确调整"本身受既往治疗影响的时变混杂因素"，在此类情形下传统的分层、回归或匹配方法会产生偏倚结果。)

Intention-to-treat (ITT) analysis — A comparison of outcomes by assigned treatment strategy, regardless of what people actually did afterward; correctly estimates the null when the true treatment effect is null, but is diluted toward the null when the effect is non-null and adherence is imperfect.（中文：意向性治疗分析，按最初分配的治疗策略比较结局，不论受试者后续实际采取了什么行为；当真实治疗效应为零时能正确估计出零效应，但当效应非零且依从性不完美时，估计值会被稀释而偏向零。)

Inverse probability of censoring weighting (IPCW) — A reweighting technique that corrects for dependent censoring in a per-protocol analysis by giving more weight to people who remain uncensored but resemble, on measured time-varying confounders, those who were censored, so the weighted sample stands in for the full population that would have stayed on protocol.（中文：删失逆概率加权法，一种加权方法，用于纠正按方案分析中的依赖性删失问题，通过对未被删失但在已测量的时变混杂因素上与被删失者相似的个体赋予更高权重，使加权样本能够代表若全部人群都维持方案时的情形。)

Per-protocol analysis — A comparison restricted to people who actually followed their assigned treatment strategy; for a time-varying treatment this usually means censoring someone's follow-up when they deviate, which introduces dependent censoring unless properly adjusted.（中文：按方案分析，仅纳入实际遵循其分配治疗策略的患者进行比较；对于时变治疗而言，通常意味着在患者偏离方案时对其随访进行删失，若未经恰当调整则会引入依赖性删失偏倚。)

Randomized cynical trial — Hernán and Hernández-Díaz's term for a trial designed to quantify harm whose protocol foresees only an ITT analysis, since that analysis is structurally biased toward not detecting the harm it is meant to find.（中文："愤世嫉俗的随机试验"，Hernán与Hernández-Díaz提出的说法，指一项旨在评估危害却仅规划意向性治疗分析的试验，因为该分析在结构上就偏向于无法检测出其本应发现的危害。)

Selection bias (in per-protocol restriction) — Distortion introduced by restricting analysis to the subset of people who adhered to their assigned strategy, when the reasons for adhering are themselves related to prognosis.（中文：（按方案限制中的）选择偏倚，指将分析限制在遵循其分配治疗策略的人群子集时所引入的偏差，尤其当"是否遵循方案"本身与预后相关时。)

Time-varying confounder affected by prior treatment — A variable, such as disease activity, that both predicts future treatment decisions and the outcome, and is itself changed by past treatment, which is exactly the setting where standard regression adjustment can be provably wrong and g-methods are required.（中文：受既往治疗影响的时变混杂因素，例如疾病活动度，它既能预测未来的治疗决策与结局，本身又会被既往治疗所改变；在这种情形下，标准回归调整可被证明是错误的，必须使用g方法。)
