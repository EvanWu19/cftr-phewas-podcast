Ep1 — Track A — Variant characterization in the intrinsically disordered human proteome (Hubrich 2026)

Roughly 37% of all annotated missense variants sit inside intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs) — the quarter of the human proteome that doesn't fold into a stable shape — and that's exactly where general-purpose predictors like AlphaMissense are weakest, because they lean on evolutionary conservation and structural context that IDRs don't reliably provide. This paper combines sequence-pattern searches for short linear motifs (SLiMs) with AlphaFold-based local docking models to structurally annotate 1,300 protein-protein interactions selected because they overlap variants of uncertain significance, producing structural models for 1,187 IDR variants. Extensive experimental validation confirmed a meaningful set of interfaces and deleterious effects that AlphaMissense had scored as benign — a direct rescue of the tool's blind spot. It's not a CFTR paper, but CFTR has its own large IDR, the R-domain, sitting at the center of the channel's regulatory machinery, and this method maps directly onto the Karen Raraigh variant-interpretation collaboration as a way to triage AlphaMissense-benign CFTR variants for a second look.

Plain-English glossary

Intrinsically disordered region (IDR) — A stretch of a protein that does not fold into a stable three-dimensional structure on its own, lacking the fixed shape most structural predictors rely on.（中文:蛋白质中不会自行折叠成稳定三维结构的一段序列,缺少大多数结构预测工具所依赖的固定形状。）

Missense variant — A single DNA change that alters one amino acid in the resulting protein.（中文:导致所编码蛋白质中一个氨基酸发生改变的单个DNA变化。）

Short linear motif (SLiM) — A short, few-residue sequence pattern within a disordered region that folds transiently against a partner protein's surface to form a real binding interface.（中文:无序区域内一小段仅由几个氨基酸组成的序列模式,在与配偶蛋白对接时会短暂折叠,形成真实的结合界面。）

Protein-protein interaction interface — The specific contact surface where two proteins physically bind.（中文:两个蛋白质发生物理结合的具体接触面。）

R-domain — CFTR's large intrinsically disordered regulatory segment, which must be phosphorylated and interact with the rest of the channel before the channel opens.（中文:CFTR中一段较大的内在无序调控片段,必须被磷酸化并与通道其余部分相互作用,通道才能打开。）

Variant of uncertain significance (VUS) — A genetic variant whose effect on protein function or disease risk has not been conclusively classified as benign or pathogenic.（中文:对蛋白质功能或疾病风险的影响尚未被明确归类为良性或致病性的遗传变异。）

Evolutionary conservation — The degree to which a sequence position has remained unchanged across species, used by many predictors as a proxy for functional importance.（中文:某个序列位置在物种间保持不变的程度,许多预测工具用它作为功能重要性的代理指标。）

Full citation

Hubrich D, et al. (2026). Variant characterization in the intrinsically disordered human proteome. Nature Structural & Molecular Biology. PMID: 42538434 · DOI: 10.1038/s41594-026-01846-z. No PMCID on record; abstract-only this run (non-OA per index).

Methods / tools to lift

  • Sequence pattern search against short linear motif (SLiM) consensus catalogs — automated scan, not manual curation.
  • AlphaFold used for local docking models of a candidate motif segment against its predicted partner domain, rather than whole-protein fold prediction.
  • Targeted selection of interfaces by overlap with variants of uncertain significance, rather than blind proteome-wide annotation.
  • Experimental validation pipeline specifically targeting AlphaMissense-benign predictions, to surface false negatives.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation & harmonization collaboration. Buildable: cftr-rdomain-interface-scan (S) — pull AlphaMissense-benign CFTR VUS sitting in the R-domain or other annotated disordered stretches; check for overlap with known/predicted SLiMs; run AlphaFold-based local docking on any hits; output a short triage list of variants with a plausible structural reason to be reconsidered.

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR's R-domain has any annotated or predicted SLiMs in public motif databases at all — the plan's second step depends on this.
  • Whether this paper's method, validated on its own chosen 1,300 interfaces, transfers to a domain (CFTR's R-domain) it was never pointed at.
  • That a predicted disrupted contact is a hypothesis, not a functional readout — no equivalent wet-lab follow-up exists yet for a CFTR-scoped version.
  • The actual experimental confirmation rate once the full paper is accessible — today's numbers come from abstract summary language only, not an inspectable table.
