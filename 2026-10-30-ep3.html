Ep3 (Track C 1/2 — registry QC): A completeness score for records that technically arrived

Gesicho, Babic, and Were (2020, BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making) report a systematic, replicable data-cleaning process applied to eight years (2011-2018) of Kenya's HIV-indicator data extracted from DHIS2 (District Health Information Software 2), a national aggregate health-reporting platform used in over 40 LMICs. Because DHIS2 cannot distinguish a true zero from a missing value, and facilities must submit all six programmatic-area reports even for services they don't offer, the authors engineered Cumulative Percent Completion (CPC) — the share of a report's expected indicator fields actually populated — and crossed it with the system's own Reporting Rate (RR) and Reporting Rate on Time (RRT) into an eight-situation (A-H) decision table, adapted from Van den Broeck et al.'s screening/diagnosis/treatment framework. Starting from 93,179 facility-records across 11,446 facilities, 50.23% had no data at all and were removed; final retained shares by programmatic area ranged from 93.98% (HIV Counselling and Testing) down to 0.46% (Blood Safety), the latter driven by non-service-providing facilities rather than genuine errors. Friedman/Wilcoxon tests confirmed the distribution of empty-report and content-missing situations differs significantly by programmatic area (p=0.001). The method is purely structural — submission completeness and rate, not value magnitude — and offers a transferable candidate-generation rule plus a reusable conceptual framework for the CFFPR outlier pipeline, with no claim about CFFPR's own data or current cleaning rules.

Plain-English glossary

  • DHIS2 (District Health Information Software 2) — a national-level aggregate health-data reporting platform used in 40+ LMICs; facilities submit routine indicator counts up through it.（中文：DHIS2（地区卫生信息软件第二版），在40多个低收入和中等收入国家使用的国家级汇总卫生数据报告平台，医疗机构通过它提交常规指标计数。）
  • Reporting Rate (RR) / Reporting Rate on Time (RRT) — DHIS2's automatically-computed percentages of expected monthly reports actually submitted, and submitted by deadline, respectively; both measure whether a report arrived, not whether it contains real content.（中文：报告率（RR）/按时报告率（RRT），DHIS2自动计算的百分比，分别衡量预期月度报告实际提交的比例、以及按截止日期提交的比例；两者都只衡量报告是否送达，不衡量其是否包含真实内容。）
  • Cumulative Percent Completion (CPC) — a derived variable counting how many of a report's expected indicator fields are non-blank, divided by the total number of indicators for that programmatic area; a content-completeness measure, not a submission measure.（中文：累计完成百分比（CPC），一个派生变量，统计一份报告中非空的预期指标字段数量，除以该项目领域的指标总数；这是针对报告内容完整性的度量，而不是针对提交本身的度量。）
  • Van den Broeck screening-diagnosis-treatment framework — a three-phase, cyclical data-cleaning framework (flag suspicious data → classify as erroneous/true-extreme/true-normal/idiopathic → correct/delete/leave unchanged); a direct generic ancestor of EHR-specific frameworks like Kahn's conformance/completeness/plausibility taxonomy.（中文：Van den Broeck筛查-诊断-处理框架，一个三阶段循环式数据清洗框架（标记可疑数据→归类为确为错误/真实极端值/真实正常值/无法确定→纠正/删除/保持不变）；是像Kahn的符合性/完整性/合理性分类体系这类EHR专用框架的直接通用前身。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • CPC x RR x RRT situation table (A-H) — a combinatorial, interpretable decision rule classifying facility-records into excluded vs. retained buckets with an explicit diagnosis per situation.
  • Van den Broeck screening/diagnosis/treatment cycle — the generic three-phase conceptual skeleton underneath the situation table, reusable independent of any specific fields.
  • Duplicate-detection scenarios — two rules for flagging same-facility-year-county duplicates (differing values vs. identical values), with different exclusion actions for each.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation, primary (the situation table); (e) secondary (the reusable screening/diagnosis/treatment framework). Buildable artifact: Completeness-and-Rate Situation Classifier — derive a CPC-style completeness score per record, cross it with two reporting-structure indicators, and produce an explicit situation table sorting records into excluded-with-reason-code vs. retained buckets, generically scoped. Difficulty: S-M.

What to verify first

  • Whether the true-zero-vs-missing ambiguity described for DHIS2 has any analog in a different registry's own field conventions — a general risk pattern to name, not a claim about any specific registry's current behavior.
  • Whether excluding situations B and C outright (rather than flagging separately) loses information a later non-reporting-pattern analysis might want.
  • How this purely structural, submission-level approach would need to be adapted or combined with a magnitude-based method for data where the interesting errors live inside a field's numeric value.
  • What the Friedman/Wilcoxon tests actually support (significant distributional differences across areas/years) versus what they don't (no external gold standard validating any single record's exclusion).

Pipeline stage

(a) Candidate generation, primary (combinatorial situation table). (e) Secondary (reusable screening/diagnosis/treatment framework).

Error types covered / not covered

Covered: empty/blank submitted reports; non-service-providing facility false "completeness"; logically-impossible over-100% rate values; duplicate facility-year-county records (two scenarios). Not covered: any value-magnitude error (unit conversion, digit transposition, decimal shift, carry-forward, real-clinical-event confusion) — this method operates entirely at the submission/completeness level, not inside any continuous measurement's numeric value.

JORDAN Before the numbers, let's get the setting straight. 在看数字之前，我们先把背景搞清楚。 What's DHIS2, and what are "reporting rate" and "reporting rate on time"? DHIS2是什么，"报告率（reporting rate）"和"按时报告率（reporting rate on time）"又是什么？

ALEX DHIS2, the District Health Information Software 2, is a national-level aggregate health-data reporting platform used in over 40 low- and middle-income countries — health facilities submit routine indicator counts up through it, and it's conceptually the registry-system equivalent of what a CFF registry does for CF care, just aggregated at the facility level rather than the patient level and built for a very different resource setting. DHIS2，即地区卫生信息软件第二版（District Health Information Software 2），是一个在40多个低收入和中等收入国家使用的国家级汇总卫生数据报告平台——医疗机构通过它逐级提交常规指标计数，从系统角色上看，它相当于CFF注册库对CF医疗的作用，只不过它是在机构层面而不是患者层面做汇总，并且是为一个非常不同的资源环境而设计的。 Within DHIS2, Reporting Rate, RR, is the percentage of expected monthly reports a facility actually submitted, calculated automatically as submitted reports divided by expected reports; Reporting Rate on Time, RRT, is the same calculation restricted to reports submitted by a Ministry-of-Health-set deadline. 在DHIS2内部，报告率（Reporting Rate，缩写RR）指的是一个机构实际提交的月度报告占预期报告数的百分比，由系统自动计算，即已提交报告数除以预期报告数；按时报告率（Reporting Rate on Time，缩写RRT）是同样的计算方式，但只统计在卫生部设定的截止日期之前提交的报告。 Crucially, both of these only measure whether a report arrived and whether it arrived on time — not whether the report, once submitted, actually contains any real indicator values, which is exactly the gap this paper's cleaning approach is built to close. 关键的是，这两个指标衡量的都只是报告是否送达、以及是否按时送达——而不是报告一旦提交之后，里面是否真的包含有实际意义的指标数值，而这恰恰是这篇论文的清洗方法要弥补的那个缺口。

JORDAN So what problem were they actually trying to solve? 那他们到底要解决什么问题？

ALEX Kenya's Ministry of Health mandates a form called MOH731, the Comprehensive HIV/AIDS Facility Reporting Form, covering six independent programmatic-area reports — HIV Counselling and Testing, Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission, Care and Treatment, Voluntary Medical Male Circumcision, Post-Exposure Prophylaxis, and Blood Safety — submitted monthly by every facility offering any of those services, for all 47 counties, from 2011 to 2018. 肯尼亚卫生部要求使用一种名为MOH731的表格，即《HIV/AIDS综合机构报告表》（Comprehensive HIV/AIDS Facility Reporting Form），涵盖六个独立的项目领域报告——HIV咨询检测（HCT）、母婴传播预防（PMTCT）、关怀与治疗（CrT）、自愿医学男性包皮环切（VMMC）、暴露后预防（PEP）和血液安全（BS）——由提供上述任一服务的每个机构每月提交，覆盖全部47个县，时间跨度为2011年至2018年。 Because a facility offering any HIV service must submit the full form covering all six areas, a facility with no actual male-circumcision or blood-safety services still submits something for those sections — which comes back in DHIS2 as an empty report rather than as an honest "not applicable," and that's the structural root of the whole cleaning problem: DHIS2 itself can't distinguish a true zero from a missing value. 因为任何提供HIV服务的机构都必须提交覆盖全部六个领域的完整表格，一个实际上没有男性包皮环切或血液安全服务的机构，仍然会为这些部分提交内容——这在DHIS2里表现为一份空报告，而不是一句诚实的"不适用"，而这正是整个清洗问题的结构性根源：DHIS2本身无法区分真正的零值和缺失值。 The authors' stated aim was to produce a systematic, transparent, replicable cleaning process for this eight-year extract, for use in a later, separate study on facility reporting performance — not to run that downstream analysis themselves in this paper. 作者明确表示，这篇论文的目标是为这八年的数据提取产出一套系统化、透明、可复现的清洗流程，供后续一项单独研究（关于机构报告表现）使用——而不是在这篇论文里自己去跑那项下游分析。

JORDAN What framework did they use to structure the actual cleaning? 他们用什么框架来组织实际的清洗工作？

ALEX They adapted Van den Broeck et al.'s conceptual data-cleaning framework, which breaks cleaning into repeated cycles of three phases — screening, which flags lacking or excess data, outliers, erroneous inliers, and strange patterns; diagnosis, which sorts each flagged point into erroneous, true extreme, true normal, or idiopathic, meaning no clear diagnosis found despite suspicion; and treatment, which is the actual correct-delete-or-leave-unchanged decision. 他们采用了Van den Broeck等人提出的概念性数据清洗框架，该框架把清洗工作拆分为三个阶段的反复循环——筛查（screening），标记数据缺失或过量、异常值、看似正常但实际有问题的"内点"（erroneous inliers）以及异常的分布模式；诊断（diagnosis），把每一个被标记的点归类为确为错误、真实极端值、真实正常值，或者无法确定（idiopathic），也就是尽管有怀疑但找不到明确诊断依据；以及处理（treatment），即最终的纠正、删除或保持不变的决定。 This is worth flagging on air as a direct generic ancestor of frameworks you likely already know by name, like Kahn's conformance-completeness-plausibility taxonomy — the same three-phase screen-diagnose-treat logic, just predating the EHR-specific vocabulary by over a decade. 值得在节目里指出的是，这是一套可以被看作是你可能已经知道名字的那些框架——比如Kahn的"符合性-完整性-合理性"（conformance-completeness-plausibility）分类体系——的直接通用前身，同样是"筛查-诊断-处理"三阶段循环的逻辑，只是比EHR专用的那套词汇早了十多年。 On top of the raw Reporting Rate and Reporting Rate on Time, the authors engineered one new derived variable specifically to close the "empty-but-submitted" gap: Cumulative Percent Completion, CPC, calculated by counting how many of a report's expected indicator fields are actually non-blank and dividing by the total number of indicators for that programmatic area — a completeness measure on the content of the report itself, not on whether the report merely arrived. 在原始的报告率和按时报告率之上，作者专门设计了一个新的派生变量，用来弥合"已提交但是空的"这个缺口：累计完成百分比（Cumulative Percent Completion，CPC），计算方法是统计一份报告中实际有多少个预期指标字段是非空的，再除以该项目领域的指标总数——这是一个针对报告内容本身的完整性度量，而不是针对报告是否仅仅送达的度量。

JORDAN And then what — how did combining those three numbers actually catch errors? 然后呢——把这三个数字组合起来，具体是怎么抓出错误的？

ALEX They built a situation table crossing all three indicators — CPC, RR, and RRT, each coded simply as zero, above-zero, or, for RR and RRT specifically, above 100 percent, which is a logical impossibility since a reporting rate can't exceed what was expected — into eight labeled situations, A through H, each with its own diagnosis and prescribed action. 他们构建了一个情形表，把这三个指标——CPC、RR、RRT，每一个都简单编码为零、大于零，或者对RR和RRT而言，大于100%，这在逻辑上是不可能的，因为报告率不可能超过预期值——交叉组合成八种被标记为A到H的情形，每一种都有自己的诊断结论和规定的处理动作。 Situations A through F all got excluded outright: nothing reported at all, likely a non-reporting facility; empty reports submitted on time or late, meaning content-free and therefore useless to a decision-maker regardless of timeliness; and the logically-impossible over-100-percent values in situations E and F. A到F这几种情形全部被直接排除：什么都没报的，很可能是不报告的机构；按时或迟交的空报告，意味着内容为空，因此无论是否及时，对决策者来说都没有用；以及E和F两种情形里逻辑上不可能出现的超过100%的数值。 Only situations G and H — reports submitted with real indicator content, whether on time or not — were kept as the "ideal" retained set, which is a deliberate choice to keep reports that are late but genuinely informative rather than discarding them on timeliness grounds alone. 只有G和H两种情形——提交了真实指标内容的报告，无论是否按时——被保留为"理想"的最终数据集，这是一个有意的选择：保留那些迟交但确实提供了真实信息的报告，而不是仅仅因为不及时就把它们丢弃。 On top of the situation table, they separately flagged two duplicate scenarios for the same facility-year-county combination — one where Reporting Rate and Reporting Rate on Time values genuinely disagreed between duplicate entries, and one where they were identical — and excluded both, dropping all entries in the first case and all but one in the second. 在情形表之外，他们还单独标记了针对同一机构-年份-县组合的两种重复记录情形——一种是重复条目之间报告率和按时报告率的值确实不一致，另一种是完全相同——两种都被排除，第一种情形下所有条目都被删除，第二种情形下只保留一条。 Worth pushing on here: excluding situation B and C outright, on the logic that an empty report is useless "regardless of timeliness," does throw away the information that the facility was at least alive and submitting — a milder treatment, like a separate flag rather than full exclusion, might have preserved that signal for whoever eventually studies non-reporting patterns themselves. 这里值得追问一下：把B和C两种情形直接排除，理由是空报告"无论是否及时"都没用，这确实丢掉了"这个机构至少还活着、还在提交"这条信息——一种更温和的处理方式，比如单独打一个标记而不是彻底排除，或许可以为将来想研究"不报告"这种模式本身的人保留这个信号。
