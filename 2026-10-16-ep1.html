Ep1 (Track A) — Two ultra-rare CFTR variants get a theratyping profile

This episode covers a 2026 Orphanet Journal of Rare Diseases paper from Dousova, Borek-Dohalska, and colleagues at Charles University (Prague) that functionally characterizes two ultra-rare CFTR variants, p.Glu1104Lys (E1104K) and p.Leu999del (L999del), each found in trans with the minimal-function variant G542X in a single pediatric patient. Using patient-derived intestinal organoids (forskolin-induced swelling) and Ussing-chamber electrophysiology on nasal epithelial cells, the authors show both variants retain measurable residual CFTR activity and respond strongly to ELX/TEZ/IVA and VAN/TEZ/IVA — enough to push E1104K to a "likely pathogenic" ACMG/AMP call, while L999del stays a VUS despite clear functional rescue. For the Karen Raraigh CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration, the paper is a worked template for triaging unclassified variants: check ClinVar/CFTR2/gnomAD absence, pull in silico scores, check trans configuration, then route to a functional assay. The buildable artifact proposed is a VUS Evidence Gap Tracker — a medium-difficulty pipeline that normalizes CFTR variant HGVS notation across CFTR1/CFTR2/ClinVar/gnomAD, pulls CADD/REVEL/AlphaMissense scores, and ranks variants by "high in silico score, no classification, no functional data" to prioritize wet-lab testing.

Glossary

  • ACMG/AMP criteria — The American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology evidence framework used to classify a variant as pathogenic, likely pathogenic, VUS, likely benign, or benign.（中文：美国医学遗传学会与分子病理学会制定的变异致病性分类证据标准。）
  • AlphaMissense — A deep-learning model that predicts whether a missense variant is likely pathogenic or benign from protein sequence/structure context.（中文：一种基于深度学习、根据蛋白序列和结构背景预测错义变异致病性的模型。）
  • AUC (area under the curve, FIS context) — Here, the integrated organoid-swelling signal over 60 minutes after forskolin stimulation, used to quantify CFTR functional rescue.（中文：在FIS实验中，福斯高林刺激后60分钟内类器官肿胀信号的积分值，用于量化CFTR功能修复程度。）
  • CADD — Combined Annotation Dependent Depletion; a computational score estimating how deleterious a genetic variant is likely to be.（中文：一种综合多种注释信息来评估变异有害程度的计算评分。）
  • CFTR2 — A curated, expert-reviewed database that links specific CFTR variants to clinical and functional evidence; being absent from it does not mean a variant is benign, only that it hasn't been curated yet.（中文：一个经专家审核、将具体CFTR变异与临床及功能证据相关联的数据库；未被收录不代表变异是良性的，只代表尚未被整理收录。）
  • ClinVar — NCBI's public archive of variant-to-phenotype classifications submitted by labs and clinicians worldwide.（中文：NCBI维护的公共数据库，收录全球实验室和临床机构提交的变异与表型关联分类。）
  • Fsk / FIS assay (forskolin-induced swelling) — A functional test in which forskolin is used to stimulate CFTR-dependent fluid secretion in an intestinal organoid; swelling indicates CFTR channel activity.（中文：一种功能检测方法，用福斯高林刺激肠道类器官中依赖CFTR的液体分泌；肿胀程度反映CFTR通道活性。）
  • gnomAD — The Genome Aggregation Database, a large population reference of allele frequencies used to check how common (or absent) a variant is in the general population.（中文：一个大型人群等位基因频率参考数据库，用于检查某个变异在普通人群中出现的频率，或是否完全未被观察到。）
  • HGVS notation — The standardized system for writing out a genetic variant at the DNA (c.) or protein (p.) level, e.g. c.3310G>A / p.Glu1104Lys.（中文：用于书写基因变异的标准化命名规范，分为DNA层面（c.）和蛋白层面（p.）两种写法。）
  • HNEC (human nasal epithelial cell) — Epithelial cells obtained by nasal brushing and grown at an air-liquid interface, used as a near-native tissue model for testing CFTR function and drug response.（中文：通过鼻腔刷检获得、在气液界面条件下培养的上皮细胞，用作接近天然组织的CFTR功能及药物反应测试模型。）
  • In-frame deletion — A deletion that removes a whole number of codons (here, one leucine), so the reading frame downstream is preserved, unlike a frameshift.（中文：删除整数个密码子的变异（此处为一个亮氨酸），不会打乱下游的阅读框，区别于移码变异。）
  • Minimal function variant — A CFTR variant (like G542X, a nonsense/stop variant) that produces essentially no functional protein, often used as a "null" background to isolate another variant's contribution.（中文：几乎不产生有功能蛋白的CFTR变异（如G542X这种终止/无义变异），常被用作"空白"背景以分离另一个变异的单独贡献。）
  • Missense variant — A single-nucleotide change that alters one amino acid in the protein sequence (as opposed to creating a stop codon or shifting the reading frame).（中文：改变蛋白序列中一个氨基酸的单核苷酸变化，区别于产生终止信号或移码的变异。）
  • MSD2 (membrane-spanning domain 2) — One of CFTR's two transmembrane domains, a structural region critical for forming the channel pore and its gating behavior.（中文：CFTR蛋白两个跨膜结构域之一，是形成通道孔道及其门控行为的关键结构区域。）
  • PDIO (patient-derived intestinal organoid) — A miniature 3D gut-tissue culture grown from a patient's own rectal-biopsy stem cells, preserving that patient's CFTR genotype for functional testing.（中文：由患者自身直肠活检干细胞培养出的微型3D肠道组织培养物，保留该患者的CFTR基因型以供功能测试。）
  • PhyloP score — A cross-species sequence-conservation score; a high value means the nucleotide/residue has been evolutionarily constrained, supporting functional importance.（中文：跨物种序列保守性评分，数值越高说明该核苷酸/氨基酸在进化中受到的约束越强，提示其功能重要性。）
  • REVEL — Rare Exome Variant Ensemble Learner; an ensemble computational score combining multiple sub-predictors to estimate missense variant pathogenicity.（中文：一种整合多种子预测模型的集成计算评分，用于估计错义变异的致病性。）
  • Theranostics — Testing a variant's drug response directly in a patient's own native cells (here, organoids/HNECs) to guide that individual's treatment decision.（中文：直接在患者自身天然细胞（此处为类器官/HNEC）中测试变异的药物反应，用来指导该患者个体的治疗决策。）
  • Theratyping — Testing a variant's drug response in a heterologous expression system (e.g., FRT cells engineered to express one CFTR variant) rather than the patient's own tissue.（中文：在异源表达系统（例如改造为表达单一CFTR变异的FRT细胞）中测试变异的药物反应，而非在患者自身组织中测试。）
  • Trans configuration ("in trans") — Two variants sitting on opposite copies (maternal/paternal) of a gene, so each copy carries only one of them — the standard setup for compound heterozygosity.（中文：两个变异分别位于基因的两个不同拷贝（母源/父源）上，即每个拷贝只带一个变异——复合杂合状态的标准配置。）
  • Ussing chamber — An electrophysiology apparatus that measures short-circuit current across a sheet of epithelial cells, used to quantify ion-channel (including CFTR) activity.（中文：一种电生理装置，测量上皮细胞层的短路电流，用于量化离子通道（包括CFTR）的活性。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant for which current evidence is insufficient to classify it as pathogenic or benign.（中文：现有证据不足以判定为致病性或良性的变异，即意义未明变异。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • FIS (forskolin-induced swelling) assay on PDIOs — organoid-swelling readout of CFTR function, quantified as AUC via confocal imaging + CellProfiler + GraphPad Prism.
  • Ussing chamber electrophysiology on HNECs — short-circuit current measurement isolating CFTRinh-172-sensitive current as a % of wild-type, used as the most trusted functional readout.
  • ACMG/AMP + CFTR-specific variant classification criteria — the rubric used to turn functional/computational evidence into a pathogenic/VUS call.
  • CADD, REVEL, AlphaMissense in silico scoring — three independent computational pathogenicity predictors cross-checked against each other and against functional data.
  • PhyloP100 conservation scoring — cross-species conservation check used as supporting (not standalone) evidence for an in-frame deletion.
  • Cross-database absence-checking (ClinVar, CFTR2, CFTR France, gnomAD) — the triage step that flags a variant as "unclassified" rather than "benign."

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Maps to the Karen Raraigh collaboration priority: CFTR variant interpretation / VUS pathogenicity prediction / variant-notation harmonization.

Buildable artifact — VUS Evidence Gap Tracker. One-line MVP: given a list of CFTR variants in HGVS notation with trans-allele context, normalize naming across CFTR1/CFTR2/ClinVar/gnomAD, pull existing CADD/REVEL/AlphaMissense scores, and rank variants by "high in silico pathogenicity, no database classification, no functional data" to prioritize organoid/HNEC testing. Difficulty: M (medium) — multi-database HGVS normalization is the fiddly part; the ranking logic itself is simple.

What to verify first

  • Whether CADD, REVEL, AlphaMissense, and gnomAD allele-count fields are reliably captured per variant in whatever source feeds a tracker like this, and how often "missing" means "not recorded" versus "genuinely absent."
  • What HGVS normalization tooling (if any) is already in use, given that legacy mutation names, c. vs. p. nomenclature, and differing reference transcripts can collide.
  • What level of functional evidence (single donor vs. several, which assay) internal criteria would consider sufficient to act on, rather than assuming this paper's n-of-1 bar applies.
