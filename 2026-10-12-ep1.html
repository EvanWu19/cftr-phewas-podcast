Track A — Variant Interpretation & Harmonization

Blurb

This episode covers Planté-Bordeneuve et al. (2026, Human Genomics), a case study that resolves a de novo, intronic FOXP1 variant — c.1531-31_1531-15del, found in a child with a neurodevelopmental disorder — from an unresolved VUS to a confidently interpreted variant. The authors combine three escalating layers of evidence: in-silico splice prediction (SpliceAI, SPiP, MaxEntScan), a minigene splicing assay, and Nanopore long-read cDNA sequencing of the patient's own blood RNA, which revealed a five-way mixture of transcript isoforms that no predictor alone anticipated. For us, the paper itself isn't about CFTR — it's about FOXP1 — but the method transfers directly to the Karen Raraigh collaboration on CFTR variant interpretation, where deep-intronic VUS like 3849+10kbC>T raise the exact same adjudication problem. We outline one buildable artifact: a lightweight scoring tool that triages which CFTR deep-intronic VUS are good candidates for minigene/long-read follow-up, based on where SpliceAI, SPiP, and MaxEntScan agree or disagree.

Glossary

  • ACMG evidence codes — The American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics framework for combining evidence types (genetic, population, computational, functional) into a variant classification such as pathogenic, likely pathogenic, or VUS.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会用于综合各类证据给出变异分类的证据条目体系）
  • Cryptic splice site — A sequence that isn't normally used for splicing but becomes activated as a donor or acceptor site when a nearby variant disrupts the canonical site.（中文：平时不被使用、但在邻近变异破坏正常剪接位点后被激活的隐秘剪接位点）
  • De novo variant — A variant present in the child but not inherited from either parent, confirmed by sequencing the family trio.（中文：患儿自身新发生的变异，父母均未携带，需通过家系三人测序确认）
  • Exon / Intron — Exons are the gene segments retained in mature mRNA; introns lie between them and are removed by splicing.（中文：外显子是保留在成熟mRNA中的片段；内含子位于外显子之间，会在剪接中被去除）
  • Haploinsufficiency — A disease mechanism where one functional copy of a gene is not enough, so losing one copy to a loss-of-function variant causes disease even though the other copy is normal.（中文：单倍剂量不足——一个功能性基因拷贝不足以维持正常功能，单拷贝功能丧失即可致病）
  • HGVS notation — The standardized nomenclature for describing variants relative to a reference sequence (e.g., c.1531-31_1531-15del).（中文：用于描述变异相对参考序列位置的标准化命名法）
  • Intron retention — A splicing outcome where part or all of an intron is kept in the mature transcript instead of being removed.（中文：内含子保留——部分或全部内含子未被正常切除，残留在成熟转录本中）
  • MaxEntScan — A maximum-entropy-model-based tool that scores how strong a splice donor or acceptor sequence is.（中文：基于最大熵模型为剪接供体/受体序列强度打分的工具）
  • Minigene assay — A functional test where a genomic fragment of interest is cloned into a reporter plasmid (here, pCAS2) with built-in splice sites, transfected into cells (here, HeLa), and the resulting spliced transcript is read out by RT-PCR and sequencing.（中文：微基因实验——将目标基因组片段克隆入带内置剪接位点的报告质粒并转染细胞，通过RT-PCR和测序读出实际剪接结果的功能实验）
  • Nanopore long-read sequencing — Sequencing technology (e.g., PromethION) that reads full-length individual cDNA or RNA molecules end to end, capturing whole transcript isoforms rather than reassembling short overlapping fragments.（中文：纳米孔长读长测序——端到端读取完整单个cDNA/RNA分子的测序技术，可直接获得完整转录本异构体）
  • Splice donor / acceptor site — The sequence motifs at an intron's 5' end (donor) and 3' end (acceptor) that the spliceosome recognizes to remove the intron.（中文：剪接体识别并用于切除内含子的边界序列motif，分别位于内含子5'端（供体位点）和3'端（受体位点））
  • SpliceAI — A deep-learning model that predicts the probability a variant creates or destroys a splice site.（中文：基于深度学习预测变异是否产生或破坏剪接位点的工具）
  • SPiP — A splicing-prediction tool that scores disruption of splicing-relevant sequence elements, such as the polypyrimidine tract.（中文：评估剪接相关序列元件（如多聚嘧啶区）受扰动程度的剪接预测工具）
  • VUS (Variant of Uncertain Significance) — A variant whose effect on disease risk cannot yet be confidently classified as benign or pathogenic.（中文：意义未明变异——目前尚无法确信判定为良性或致病性的变异）

Methods/tools to lift

  • SpliceAI — deep-learning scoring of splice-site gain/loss probability from sequence alone.
  • SPiP — flags disruption of splicing-regulatory elements (e.g., polypyrimidine tract) with a quantitative risk score and confidence interval.
  • MaxEntScan — maximum-entropy scoring of donor/acceptor site strength, before vs. after a variant.
  • Minigene / pCAS2 assay — cheap, fast first functional readout of splicing in a heterologous (HeLa) cell system.
  • Nanopore long-read cDNA sequencing — full-length, in-vivo transcript isoform calling directly from patient RNA, resolving mixtures short-read RNA-seq can't separate.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority: Karen Raraigh collaboration — CFTR variant interpretation and harmonization.

Why it's relevant: CFTR carries its own well-known deep-intronic VUS (e.g., 3849+10kbC>T) that present the same adjudication problem this paper solves for FOXP1 — splice predictors give a suggestive but insufficient signal, and real splicing outcomes can be a multi-isoform mixture no single predictor anticipates.

Buildable artifact (paste-ready MVP): A lightweight CFTR deep-intronic VUS triage scorer. Input: a CFTR variant's HGVS notation plus its SpliceAI, SPiP, and MaxEntScan scores. Output: a flag for variants where the three predictors disagree or only weakly agree, and a rank of how good a candidate each variant is for follow-up functional work (minigene first, long-read second). Difficulty: S–M (a rules/thresholds scoring script plus a small lookup table — no new modeling). Gated on: whether CFTR-specific functional-assay or RNA data (minigene results, long-read transcript data) is actually available to build or validate against — separate from whatever genotype data CFFPR holds; that availability question is for Karen's side to confirm, not something asserted here.

What to verify first

  • Generalizability beyond n=1 — does this multi-isoform mixing pattern show up for other deep-intronic splice VUS, in FOXP1 or other genes, or was this case unusually messy?
  • Tissue adequacy for CFTR — FOXP1 was only lowly expressed in blood (~4.6 TPM, GTEx) yet still informative for long-read analysis; whether blood or another accessible tissue gives an informative CFTR read needs separate confirmation, not an assumption by analogy.
  • Minigene-only reliability — how often do heterologous minigene results agree or disagree with long-read in-vivo results across other validated cases, to calibrate how much weight minigene-only evidence should carry.
  • Infrastructure availability — whether functional-assay and long-read sequencing capacity for CFTR variants exists for us or collaborators at all; an open resourcing question, not a claim about current CFFPR holdings.

JORDAN The variant in this paper is written c.1531-31_1531-15del — that's HGVS notation, where the "c." prefix means it's numbered from the coding DNA sequence, and the negative number before the dash tells you it sits upstream of a coding exon, inside an intron. 这篇论文里的变异写作c.1531-31_1531-15del——这是HGVS（人类基因组变异命名系统，Human Genome Variation Society nomenclature）命名法，前缀"c."表示编号是按照编码DNA序列来计算的，而横线前的负数则说明这个位置在一个编码外显子的上游，位于内含子（intron）内部。 And just so we're clear — exons are the segments that end up in mature mRNA, separated by introns, which get spliced out. 明确一下——外显子（exon）是最终保留在成熟mRNA中的片段，它们之间被内含子隔开，而内含子会在剪接过程中被去除。

ALEX Each intron has a donor site at its 5' end and an acceptor site at its 3' end, and the spliceosome uses short, fairly degenerate sequence motifs at those boundaries to find and cut there. 每个内含子在5'端都有一个供体位点（splice donor site），在3'端有一个受体位点（splice acceptor site），剪接体会利用这些边界处简短且序列相对不严格的识别motif来定位并切割。 So when a variant falls near one of those motifs but doesn't touch the exon itself, you get a VUS — a Variant of Uncertain Significance — because you can't just read the amino acid change off the codon, you have to ask what it does to splicing. 所以当一个变异恰好落在这些motif附近，却没有直接改变外显子本身时，就会出现VUS（意义未明变异，Variant of Uncertain Significance）——因为你不能简单地从密码子读出氨基酸变化，而必须去追问它对剪接造成了什么影响。

JORDAN Two ways to test that experimentally — a minigene assay, where you clone the genomic region of interest into a reporter plasmid with built-in splice sites, transfect it into cells, and read out what transcript comes out. 有两种实验方法可以验证这一点——第一种是微基因实验（minigene assay），也就是把感兴趣的基因组区域克隆进一个带有内置剪接位点的报告质粒中，转染进细胞，再读出产生的转录本。 The other is long-read sequencing, which sequences individual cDNA molecules end to end on something like a Nanopore platform, so you see each isoform as a whole, intact molecule, rather than reassembling short overlapping reads the way short-read RNA sequencing does. 另一种是长读长测序（long-read sequencing），它在类似Nanopore（纳米孔测序平台）这样的仪器上对单个cDNA分子进行端到端测序，这样你看到的每个转录本异构体都是完整的分子，而不像短读长RNA测序那样需要拼接短的重叠读段。

ACMG（美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会，American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics）证据条目则是临床遗传学家把这些信息正式整合成分类结论的方式——PS2是支持新生变异（de novo variant）的强证据，PM2是变异在人群数据库中缺失的中等证据，PP3是计算预测方面的支持性证据，而PS3则是来自经过验证的功能实验的强证据。 One more term — haploinsufficiency, meaning one functional copy of a gene isn't enough; losing one copy to a loss-of-function variant causes disease even though the other copy is normal. 再补充一个术语——单倍剂量不足（haploinsufficiency），意思是一个有功能的基因拷贝是不够的；即使另一条拷贝正常，失去一个功能性拷贝（由功能丧失性变异导致）也会致病。

ALEX So why is this a hard problem in the first place? 那这个问题到底难在哪里？ Genome sequencing, as opposed to exome sequencing, now routinely picks up thousands of deep-intronic variants per patient, and tools like SpliceAI, SPiP, and MaxEntScan were built to triage which ones are even worth worrying about. 与外显子组测序不同，基因组测序（genome sequencing）现在常规就能在每个患者身上发现成千上万个深度内含子变异，而SpliceAI（一种基于深度学习的剪接预测工具）、SPiP（一种剪接预测软件）和MaxEntScan（一种基于最大熵模型的剪接位点评分工具）这类工具正是为了筛选出哪些变异值得进一步关注而被开发出来的。

JORDAN But triage isn't the same as resolution — what these predictors are good at is flagging that a splice site's strength has probably changed; what they're much weaker at is telling you the actual downstream consequence, because a disrupted acceptor can be compensated by a nearby cryptic site, or by partial intron retention, or by exon skipping, and real cells sometimes produce several of these outcomes at once, in different proportions. 但筛选不等于最终解析——这些预测工具擅长的是提示某个剪接位点的强度可能发生了变化；它们相对薄弱的地方在于告诉你真正的下游后果，因为一个被破坏的受体位点可能被附近的隐秘剪接位点（cryptic splice site）、部分内含子保留（intron retention），或者外显子跳跃（exon skipping）所代偿，而真实的细胞有时会同时按不同比例产生好几种这样的结果。

ALEX That's exactly the gap this paper is going after, using FOXP1 as the test case. 这正是这篇论文想要填补的空白，他们用FOXP1（一个对神经发育至关重要的转录因子基因）作为测试案例。 FOXP1 is a forkhead-box transcription factor that's essential for neurodevelopment — it helps shape neural circuit formation and synaptic plasticity, and it regulates gene expression programs in the developing brain. FOXP1是一种forkhead-box转录因子（transcription factor），对神经发育至关重要——它参与塑造神经环路的形成和突触可塑性，并调控发育中大脑的基因表达程序。

FOXP1的单等位基因功能丧失性变异会导致一种已知的常染色体显性（autosomal dominant）神经发育障碍，编号为MIM #613670，表现为智力障碍、言语语言障碍和行为特征。 So the mechanism is haploinsufficiency, which is why the central question for any FOXP1 variant is simply: does it actually knock down function, yes or no? 所以其机制就是单倍剂量不足，这也是为什么对任何FOXP1变异来说，核心问题就是：它到底有没有真正敲低基因功能？
