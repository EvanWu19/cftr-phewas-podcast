Show notes — Track C — SLC26A9 variant in two CF siblings (2026-07-05)

Blurb

A Lublin (Poland) CF group screened exons 3 and 17 of SLC26A9 by Sanger sequencing in 20 adult CF patients, targeting the spots where Luo et al. (2024) reported severe-CF variants. They did not find Luo's variants; instead they found a new, essentially undescribed change — c.1847C>T (p.Pro616Leu) in exon 17 — in exactly two patients, who were siblings both carrying CFTR p.Phe508del + c.3140-26A>G (a mild class V splicing genotype). p.Pro616Leu sits in the STAS domain's disordered S/T-rich loop (~620–628 aa) that regulates transporter activity and mediates the SLC26A9–CFTR interaction. In-silico calls are split (PolyPhen-2 benign, MetaLR damaging); it's absent from ClinVar and ultra-rare in gnomAD (~2e-5). The siblings' FEV1 (82%, 84%) exceeded the cohort mean (54%), but n=2 and the mild CFTR genotype confounds any SLC26A9 attribution — so it stays a VUS with no functional assay. Where it lands: Liz/CFFPR phenotype & data-quality (method only). Buildable artifact: a modifier-stratification analysis template that conditions on CFTR severity before estimating a modifier's FEV1 association.

Plain-English glossary

  • Compensation (in CF) — a second transporter (here SLC26A9) carrying part of the anion load CFTR can't, potentially softening disease.（中文：代偿，另一转运体（此处为 SLC26A9）承担 CFTR 无法完成的部分阴离子负荷，可能减轻病情。）
  • Modifier gene — a gene that isn't the primary cause of a disease but tunes its severity.（中文：修饰基因，非疾病主因、但调节其严重程度的基因。）
  • Pleiotropy — one gene influencing multiple distinct phenotypes (SLC26A9 → lung function, modulator response, CFRD).（中文：多效性，一个基因影响多个不同表型（SLC26A9 → 肺功能、调节剂应答、CFRD）。）
  • Sanger sequencing — classic single-fragment, high-accuracy sequencing, vs next-generation sequencing's millions of parallel reads.（中文：Sanger 测序，经典的单片段高准确度测序，与二代测序的数百万并行读取相对。）
  • SNP — single-nucleotide polymorphism, a common single-letter difference in the population (e.g. rs7512462).（中文：SNP，单核苷酸多态性，人群中常见的单字母差异，如 rs7512462。）
  • STAS domain — sulfate transporter and anti-sigma factor antagonist; the cytoplasmic SLC26A9 domain that regulates transport and physically contacts CFTR.（中文：STAS 结构域，"硫酸盐转运蛋白与抗 sigma 因子拮抗剂"，SLC26A9 的胞质结构域，调控转运并与 CFTR 物理接触。）
  • VUS — variant of uncertain significance; seen but not yet classifiable as benign or pathogenic.（中文：VUS，意义未明变异，已观察到但尚无法归类为良性或致病。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Targeted-exon Sanger screen — cheap hypothesis test: amplify only the exons where prior variants were reported, rather than full-gene NGS.
  • Condition on CFTR genotype — the design lesson: modifier effects are only interpretable within a fixed CFTR severity stratum.
  • Multi-predictor triangulation — PolyPhen-2 / SIFT / CADD / MetaLR / PhyloP disagreement is itself signal that a variant is genuinely uncertain.
  • Domain-anchored interpretation — map the residue to a functional sub-region (STAS disordered S/T-rich loop) to reason about plausible direction of effect.

Difficulty: S/M.

What to verify first

  • Functional evidence — p.Pro616Leu has no chloride-transport assay; it can't enter the catalog as more than a VUS until one exists.
  • Confounding — the mild phenotype is fully explained by CFTR p.Phe508del + c.3140-26A>G; a real SLC26A9 claim needs modifier variation within a fixed CFTR stratum.
  • Unsettled literature — Strug (2016) vs Eastman (2021) disagree on rs7512462; check which phenotype and CFTR background any association was measured in.
  • Power — n=2 siblings (correlated genomes) cannot support an association claim.

Gap-map / catalog

  • Closes: adds a specific new STAS-domain SLC26A9 variant (p.Pro616Leu) and consolidates the SLC26A9 modifier landscape (Luo 2024 severe-CF variants; Strug 2016 rs7512462 lung/ivacaftor; Eastman 2021 null; rs4077468/rs4077469 CFRD).
  • Leaves open: functional direction of p.Pro616Leu (STAS disordered-loop deletions can raise Cl− transport); whether STAS variants act via CFTR-interaction disruption vs intrinsic transport change; any registry-scale, CFTR-stratified association.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到读书间。 Today's Track C paper is "Nucleotide Variant in the SLC26A9 Gene in Two Siblings with Cystic Fibrosis," from a cystic fibrosis group at the University Clinical Hospital in Lublin, Poland, published in the Journal of Clinical Medicine in 2026. 今天 Track C（CF 修饰基因专题）的论文是《两名囊性纤维化同胞中 SLC26A9 基因的核苷酸变异》，来自波兰卢布林大学临床医院的一个囊性纤维化研究组，2026 年发表在《Journal of Clinical Medicine》上。 It's a small case-plus-molecular study, and I'll flag up front that it's a 20-patient screen that found the variant in just two people, so we read it for the modifier gene it puts on the table, not for statistical power. 这是一项小型的"病例加分子"研究，我先说清楚：它是一个 20 人的筛查，只在两个人身上发现了该变异，所以我们读它是为了它端上桌的那个修饰基因，而不是为了统计效力。 That modifier gene is SLC26A9, and it feeds the Track C catalog. 那个修饰基因是 SLC26A9，它为 Track C 目录添砖加瓦。

JORDAN Let's build up the biology. 我们先把生物学背景搭起来。 What is SLC26A9, and why would a gene that isn't CFTR change how CF plays out? SLC26A9 是什么，为什么一个不是 CFTR 的基因会改变 CF 的走向？

ALEX SLC26A9 is solute carrier family 26 member 9, a multifunctional anion transporter in the same epithelial cell membranes where CFTR lives — airway, gut, kidney. SLC26A9 是溶质载体家族 26 成员 9（solute carrier family 26 member 9），一种多功能阴离子转运蛋白，它就位于 CFTR 所在的那些上皮细胞膜上——气道、肠道、肾脏。 It moves chloride, and it also swaps chloride for bicarbonate. 它转运氯离子，也做氯离子与碳酸氢根的交换。 The key idea is compensation: when CFTR is broken, SLC26A9 can carry some of the anion transport load, so a person with more SLC26A9 activity may have milder disease. 关键概念是"代偿"：当 CFTR 损坏时，SLC26A9 能承担一部分阴离子转运的负荷，因此 SLC26A9 活性更强的人可能病情更轻。 That's what makes it a modifier gene — a gene that isn't the primary cause of a disease but tunes its severity. 这就是它成为修饰基因（modifier gene）的原因——一个并非疾病主因、却调节其严重程度的基因。 The term you need next is the STAS domain. 你接下来需要的术语是 STAS 结构域。 STAS stands for sulfate transporter and anti-sigma factor antagonist; it's a cytoplasmic domain of SLC26A9 that regulates how well the transporter works and, importantly, is the region that physically talks to CFTR. STAS 是"硫酸盐转运蛋白与抗 sigma 因子拮抗剂"（sulfate transporter and anti-sigma factor antagonist）的缩写；它是 SLC26A9 的一个胞质结构域，调控转运体的工作效率，而且重要的是，它正是与 CFTR 发生物理相互作用的区域。 The variant in this paper sits right in that STAS domain. 本文的变异恰好就落在这个 STAS 结构域里。 One more term: a variant of uncertain significance, a VUS, is a change we've seen but can't yet classify as benign or disease-relevant. 还有一个术语：意义未明变异（VUS），是我们观察到、但尚无法归类为良性或与疾病相关的改变。

JORDAN What was actually known about SLC26A9 as a CF modifier before this paper? 在这篇论文之前，关于 SLC26A9 作为 CF 修饰基因，实际上已经知道些什么？

ALEX Quite a lot, and the paper does a nice job laying out the landscape. 相当多，而且这篇论文把整个格局梳理得很好。 In 2024, Shiyu Luo and colleagues found two SLC26A9 variants — p.Gly77Ser and p.Pro629Ser — in patients with severe, rapidly progressive CF, and argued that impaired SLC26A9 worsens the course by knocking out that compensatory anion transport. 2024 年，Shiyu Luo 及同事在重症、快速进展的 CF 患者中发现了两个 SLC26A9 变异——p.Gly77Ser 和 p.Pro629Ser——并提出 SLC26A9 功能受损会通过削弱那条代偿性阴离子转运通路而加重病程。 On the common-variant side, an intronic SNP called rs7512462 is the famous one. 在常见变异这一侧，一个叫 rs7512462 的内含子 SNP 是最著名的。 A SNP is a single-nucleotide polymorphism, a common single-letter difference in the population. SNP 是单核苷酸多态性（single-nucleotide polymorphism），指人群中常见的单个字母差异。 Strug and colleagues in 2016 reported that the C allele of rs7512462 tracks with better baseline lung function in CF patients carrying gating variants like Gly551Asp, and with a roughly 25 percent better response to the modulator ivacaftor. Strug 及同事在 2016 年报告，rs7512462 的 C 等位基因与携带门控变异（如 Gly551Asp）的 CF 患者更好的基线肺功能相关，也与对调节剂 ivacaftor 约 25% 更好的应答相关。 But Eastman and colleagues in 2021 found no such association with severity or ivacaftor response in Gly551Asp patients, so that one is genuinely contested. 但 Eastman 及同事在 2021 年在 Gly551Asp 患者中未发现与严重程度或 ivacaftor 应答的这种关联，所以这一点确实存在争议。 And two 5-prime SNPs, rs4077468 and rs4077469, are associated with the risk of cystic-fibrosis-related diabetes. 另外两个位于 5' 端的 SNP，rs4077468 和 rs4077469，与囊性纤维化相关糖尿病（CFRD）的风险相关。 So SLC26A9 already reaches across lung function, modulator response, and CFRD — genuine pleiotropy. 所以 SLC26A9 已经横跨肺功能、调节剂应答和 CFRD——真正的多效性（pleiotropy，即一个基因影响多个表型）。

JORDAN You mentioned the siblings' CFTR genotype includes a class V splicing variant. 你提到同胞的 CFTR 基因型里包含一个 V 类剪接变异。 Unpack that, because it turns out to matter for the whole interpretation. 展开讲讲，因为事实证明它对整个解读都很关键。

ALEX The siblings carry p.Phe508del on one allele and c.3140-26A>G on the other. 两名同胞一条等位基因上是 p.Phe508del，另一条上是 c.3140-26A>G。 p.Phe508del is the classic class II variant: the protein misfolds and never reaches the cell membrane, which usually means severe disease with pancreatic insufficiency. p.Phe508del 是经典的 II 类变异：蛋白质错误折叠，永远到不了细胞膜，这通常意味着伴有胰腺功能不全的重症。 The other variant, c.3140-26A>G, is a class V variant. 另一个变异 c.3140-26A>G 是一个 V 类变异。 Class V means it's a splicing variant that reduces, but doesn't abolish, the amount of normal CFTR made. V 类意味着它是一个剪接变异，减少但不消除正常 CFTR 的产量。 Splicing is the step where the cell cuts introns out of the RNA and stitches exons together; a variant near a splice site can make that machinery misfire part of the time, so some normal protein still gets through. 剪接是细胞把内含子从 RNA 中切除、再把外显子缝合在一起的步骤；剪接位点附近的变异会让这套机器部分时候出错，因此仍有一些正常蛋白质得以产生。 That residual normal CFTR is why class V genotypes usually give a milder course with preserved pancreatic function. 正是这些残余的正常 CFTR，使得 V 类基因型通常表现为较轻的病程且保留胰腺功能。 And that's exactly what these siblings showed: late diagnosis in their late thirties, no pancreatic insufficiency, nasal polyps. 而这恰恰是这两名同胞所表现的：将近四十岁才确诊、无胰腺功能不全、有鼻息肉。 So their mild phenotype is already well explained before SLC26A9 even enters the picture. 所以在 SLC26A9 尚未登场之前，他们较轻的表型就已经得到了很好的解释。

JORDAN Which is the whole problem with attributing anything to the new variant. 而这正是把任何东西归因于新变异的全部问题所在。 The mild explanation is already spoken for. 那个"较轻"的解释已经名花有主了。

ALEX Precisely, and the authors are refreshingly honest about it. 正是如此，而作者对此诚实得令人耳目一新。 You have two people, who share half their genome by being siblings, both with a CFTR genotype that predicts mild disease, and both mild. 你有两个人，作为同胞共享一半基因组，两人都带着预示轻症的 CFTR 基因型，两人也都轻症。 There is simply no statistical room to separate the SLC26A9 variant's contribution from the CFTR genotype's. 根本没有统计空间去把 SLC26A9 变异的贡献与 CFTR 基因型的贡献分开。 That's not a flaw in the lab work; it's an inherent limit of a two-person observation. 这不是实验工作的缺陷；这是两人观察的固有局限。 It's the reason the design lesson — condition on CFTR genotype in a properly powered cohort — is the real takeaway, more than the variant itself. 这正是为什么"在有足够效力的队列中对 CFTR 基因型做条件化"这个设计教训，才是真正的收获，胜过变异本身。

JORDAN So what did this group do, and what did they find? 那这个研究组做了什么，发现了什么？

ALEX The method is deliberately narrow. 方法是刻意收窄的。 They took 20 adult CF patients in Lublin, all with pathogenic variants on both CFTR alleles — twelve homozygous for p.Phe508del, the rest compound heterozygotes. 他们纳入了卢布林的 20 名成年 CF 患者，全部在 CFTR 两条等位基因上都携带致病变异——十二名为 p.Phe508del 纯合子，其余为复合杂合子。 Rather than sequence the whole SLC26A9 gene, they PCR-amplified just exons 3 and 17 — the exact spots where Luo had found the severe-CF variants — and read them with Sanger sequencing. 他们没有测整个 SLC26A9 基因，而是只用 PCR 扩增了外显子 3 和 17——正是 Luo 发现重症 CF 变异的那两个位置——然后用 Sanger 测序读出。 Sanger sequencing is the classic single-fragment, high-accuracy method, as opposed to next-generation sequencing that reads millions of fragments at once. Sanger 测序是经典的单片段、高准确度方法，与一次读取数百万片段的二代测序（NGS）相对。 They did not find Luo's two variants. 他们没有找到 Luo 的那两个变异。 Instead, in exon 17, they found a different, essentially undescribed change: c.1847C>T, which swaps proline to leucine at position 616, p.Pro616Leu. 相反，他们在外显子 17 里发现了一个不同的、基本未被描述过的改变：c.1847C>T，即在第 616 位把脯氨酸换成亮氨酸，p.Pro616Leu。 It showed up in exactly two patients, who turned out to be siblings, both carrying the CFTR genotype p.Phe508del plus c.3140-26A>G. 它恰好出现在两名患者身上，他们其实是同胞，两人都携带 CFTR 基因型 p.Phe508del 加 c.3140-26A>G。 Here's the design limit I'd push on: because their CFTR genotype already predicts a mild course, you cannot cleanly attribute the mild phenotype to the SLC26A9 variant. 这里有一个我要追问的设计局限：由于他们的 CFTR 基因型本就预示较轻的病程，你无法干净地把这个较轻的表型归因于 SLC26A9 变异。 The two explanations are confounded. 这两种解释相互混杂。

JORDAN What do we actually know about whether p.Pro616Leu matters? 关于 p.Pro616Leu 到底重不重要，我们实际上知道什么？

ALEX Honestly, not much yet, and the paper is candid about it. 说实话，目前还不多，论文对此很坦诚。 The in-silico predictors disagree in the usual way: PolyPhen-2 calls it benign at 0, SIFT tolerated at 0.11, CADD 16 which is borderline-benign, but MetaLR flags it damaging at 0.6. 计算机预测器像往常一样意见不一：PolyPhen-2 判良性为 0，SIFT 判可耐受为 0.11，CADD 为 16 属于良性边缘，但 MetaLR 却标为有害 0.6。 It's absent from ClinVar, and in gnomAD it's extraordinarily rare at an allele frequency around 0.00002. 它未被 ClinVar 收录，在 gnomAD 中极其罕见，等位基因频率约 0.00002。 What makes it biologically interesting is location, not evidence. 让它在生物学上有意思的是位置，而非证据。 Position 616 sits inside a disordered loop of the STAS domain, roughly residues 620 to 628, a serine- and threonine-rich motif that regulates the transporter. 616 位处于 STAS 结构域一个无序环内，大约在第 620 到 628 残基之间，一个富含丝氨酸和苏氨酸、调控转运体的基序。 The paper cites bioinformatic work by Chen and colleagues in 2025 showing that deleting the whole STAS domain kills chloride channel function, but — counterintuitively — deleting just the disordered loop increases chloride transport. 论文引用了 Chen 及同事 2025 年的生物信息学工作，显示删除整个 STAS 结构域会摧毁氯通道功能，但——反直觉地——只删除那个无序环反而增加氯离子转运。 So a variant in that loop could plausibly go either way: reduce or enhance transport. 因此这个环里的一个变异，合理地说可能走向任一方向：减少或增强转运。 And there's prior functional precedent: Chen in 2012 showed p.Val622Leu cut channel activity by about 50 percent, and a p.Arg575Trp variant may disrupt the SLC26A9–CFTR interaction directly. 而且已有功能先例：Chen 在 2012 年显示 p.Val622Leu 把通道活性削减了约 50%，一个 p.Arg575Trp 变异则可能直接破坏 SLC26A9 与 CFTR 的相互作用。 The honest bottom line from the authors: no functional assay was done here, so p.Pro616Leu stays a VUS, possibly benign, possibly a protective modifier. 作者诚实的结论是：这里没有做任何功能实验，所以 p.Pro616Leu 仍是 VUS，可能良性，也可能是一个保护性修饰因子。 The one quantitative hint is that the two siblings had FEV1 at 82 and 84 percent predicted, versus a mean of 54 percent in the other patients — suggestive, but n equals two. 唯一的定量线索是，两名同胞的 FEV1 分别为预测值的 82% 和 84%，而其他患者的均值为 54%——有提示性，但 n 等于二。

JORDAN I want to sit with the STAS-domain biology a moment longer, because it's the part I find genuinely surprising. 我想在 STAS 结构域的生物学上多停留一会儿，因为这是我觉得真正令人意外的部分。 Why would deleting part of a functional domain increase transport? 为什么删除一个功能结构域的一部分反而会增加转运？

ALEX It's counterintuitive, and it tells you the STAS domain isn't just structural scaffolding — it's regulatory. 这确实反直觉，而它告诉你 STAS 结构域不只是结构性的支架——它是调控性的。 Think of the disordered loop as a brake rather than an engine. 把这个无序环想象成一个刹车，而不是引擎。 Deleting the whole STAS domain removes machinery the channel needs, so chloride transport collapses. 删除整个 STAS 结构域会移除通道所需的机器，于是氯离子转运崩溃。 But deleting just the disordered loop appears to release a built-in inhibition, so transport goes up. 但只删除那个无序环，似乎解除了一种内建的抑制，于是转运上升。 A disordered region, by the way, is a stretch of protein that doesn't fold into a fixed shape; it stays floppy, and that flexibility is often where regulation and protein-protein contacts happen. 顺带一提，无序区（disordered region）是一段不折叠成固定形状的蛋白质；它保持松软，而这种柔性往往正是调控和蛋白质间接触发生的地方。 So a single amino-acid change inside that loop, like proline to leucine, could stiffen or reshape it in a way that nudges the brake. 因此那个环内单个氨基酸的改变，比如脯氨酸变亮氨酸，可能以某种方式使它变硬或改变形状，从而轻推那个刹车。 Proline is special here because it's the most rigid, kink-inducing amino acid, so losing a proline can meaningfully change a loop's geometry. 脯氨酸在这里很特殊，因为它是最刚性、最能诱导折角的氨基酸，所以失去一个脯氨酸可能显著改变一个环的几何形状。 That's the biophysical reason this particular variant is at least worth a functional look, even though the population evidence is thin. 这正是为什么这个特定变异至少值得做一次功能检测的生物物理学理由，尽管人群证据很薄弱。

JORDAN And that connects to why SLC26A9 matters therapeutically, not just as a marker. 而这也连到了为什么 SLC26A9 在治疗上有意义，而不仅仅是作为一个标记物。 Is that the gene-therapy angle the paper raised? 这就是论文提出的基因治疗角度吗？
