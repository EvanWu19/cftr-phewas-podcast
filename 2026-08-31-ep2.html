Ep2 — Track B: ViroProfiler: a containerized bioinformatics pipeline for viral metagenomic data analysis

ViroProfiler is an nf-core-based Nextflow pipeline for characterizing bacteriophage (viral) communities from shotgun metagenomic data — quality control, assembly, dereplication, optional binning, five-tool viral detection with confidence-tiered scoring, functional/AMG annotation, taxonomy, host and replication-cycle prediction, and abundance estimation, all containerized (Docker/Singularity, or Conda for HPC) and version-pinned for reproducibility. On a 13-sample mock benchmark it had the best F1 score at phylum/order rank versus Kraken2+Bracken, especially at low abundance, though sensitivity dropped sharply at genus/species; on a real ulcerative-colitis fecal-virome reanalysis it recovered 4x more viral contigs than the original study and produced materially different composition and host-prediction findings. The phage biology has no CF relevance, but the architecture does: ViroProfiler's rule for calling a candidate "high confidence" — agreement across at least two independently-built detection method families (homology-based tools plus a separately-trained ML detector), rather than one tool's score crossing a threshold — is a directly portable software pattern for combining multiple anthropometric outlier detectors into a single confidence-tiered flag.

Plain-English glossary

  • Bacteriophage (phage) — a virus that infects bacteria; thought to be the most abundant biological entity on Earth and a major driver of which bacteria dominate an ecosystem.（中文：噬菌体，感染细菌的病毒；被认为是地球上数量最多的生物实体，也是决定生态系统中优势细菌种类的主要驱动因素之一。）
  • Shotgun metagenomics — sequencing all DNA in a sample at once without isolating or culturing organisms first; reads are computationally assembled into longer contigs.（中文：鸟枪法宏基因组测序，在不预先分离或培养生物体的情况下，一次性对样本中所有DNA测序；读段通过计算方法被拼接成更长的contig。）
  • Contig — a contiguous stretch of assembled sequence built from overlapping short sequencing reads.（中文：contig，由相互重叠的短测序读段拼接而成的连续序列片段。）
  • Auxiliary metabolic gene (AMG) — a gene a phage carries that can reprogram its bacterial host's metabolism during infection.（中文：辅助代谢基因，噬菌体携带的、能在感染过程中重编程其细菌宿主代谢的基因。）
  • MIUViG dereplication (95% ANI / 85% AF) — community-standard thresholds (average nucleotide identity / aligned fraction) for merging near-duplicate viral contigs into one non-redundant sequence.（中文：MIUViG去冗余标准（95%平均核苷酸一致性/85%比对覆盖比例），用于合并近乎重复的病毒contig为单一非冗余序列的社区标准阈值。）
  • Confidence-tiered detection — ViroProfiler's approach of calling a candidate "high confidence" when ≥2 independently-built detection tools (homology-based or ML-based) agree, versus "low confidence" for single-method-only calls.（中文：置信度分级检测，ViroProfiler的做法：当≥2个独立构建的检测工具（基于同源性或基于机器学习）达成一致时判定为"高置信度"，仅有单一方法支持则判定为"低置信度"。）
  • vMAG (viral metagenome-assembled genome) — a "bin" of contigs grouped by statistical co-occurrence and inferred to belong to the same viral genome, distinct from a confirmed physically-linked genome assembly.（中文：病毒宏基因组组装基因组，由统计共现模式归并、推断属于同一病毒基因组的contig"箱"，区别于经过物理连锁确认的基因组组装。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • nf-core/Nextflow containerized architecture — per-tool versioned Docker/Singularity containers, auto-managed by Nextflow, with a Conda fallback for container-restricted HPC environments.
  • Deliberate optionality design — distinguishing steps made optional because a method is genuinely uncertain (binning) from steps made optional purely because they're computationally expensive (host prediction's ~200GB database).

ALEX Today's paper is "ViroProfiler: a containerized bioinformatics pipeline for viral metagenomic data analysis," by Ru and colleagues, published in Gut Microbes in 2023. 今天的论文是Ru等人2023年发表在《Gut Microbes》期刊上的《ViroProfiler：一个用于病毒宏基因组数据分析的容器化生物信息学流程》。This is Track B — AI tooling with a tool-development lens, the track that serves the career-positioning priority. 这是Track B——带有工具开发视角的AI工具选题，服务于职业定位这一优先方向。The biology here is bacteriophages in gut samples, which has nothing directly to do with CF or CFTR — what's worth stealing is the pipeline architecture, specifically how it aggregates several independent tools into one confidence-scored call. 这里的生物学内容是肠道样本中的噬菌体，跟CF或CFTR没有直接关系——真正值得借鉴的是流程架构，尤其是它如何把多个独立工具的结果整合成一个带置信度评分的判定。

JORDAN Before the pipeline — what's a bacteriophage, and what makes finding them in sequencing data hard? 在讲流程之前——什么是噬菌体？在测序数据里找到它们为什么这么难？

ALEX A bacteriophage, or phage, is a virus that infects bacteria — they're thought to be the most abundant biological entities on Earth, and they shape which bacteria thrive in an ecosystem, including the human gut. 噬菌体（bacteriophage，简称phage）是感染细菌的病毒——它们被认为是地球上数量最多的生物实体，并且塑造着一个生态系统（包括人体肠道）中哪些细菌能够繁盛。Shotgun metagenomics sequences all the DNA in a sample at once, without first isolating or culturing anything, and the raw reads get computationally stitched into longer stretches called contigs — short for contiguous sequences. 鸟枪法宏基因组测序（shotgun metagenomics）一次性对样本中所有DNA进行测序，事先不做任何分离或培养，得到的原始读段（read）会通过计算方法拼接成更长的片段，称为contig（重叠群，即连续序列的缩写）。For bacteria, there are standardized profiling pipelines, QIIME and Mothur, that most labs use. 对细菌而言，已经有QIIME和Mothur这类大多数实验室都在用的标准化分析流程。No equivalent standard exists for viruses, partly because viral reference databases are far sparser than bacterial ones — most phages in a given sample have no close match in any database — so you can't just look them up, you have to infer that a contig is viral at all before you can say anything else about it. 而病毒领域还没有对应的标准，部分原因是病毒参考数据库比细菌参考数据库要稀疏得多——一个样本中的大多数噬菌体在任何数据库里都找不到近似匹配——所以根本无法直接查表确认，必须先推断出某个contig是不是病毒来源的，才能进一步讨论其他问题。

JORDAN So what was actually missing before this paper — why did they think a new pipeline was needed? 那么在这篇论文之前，到底缺了什么——他们为什么认为需要一个新的流程？

ALEX Existing tools split cleanly into three groups that didn't talk to each other. 现有的工具明显分成三类，彼此之间互不联通。One group does viral discovery — deciding whether a contig is viral at all, tools like VirSorter2 and DeepVirFinder — mostly using homology searches against reference databases, or in DeepVirFinder's case, patterns learned from known viral sequences. 第一类做病毒发现（viral discovery）——判断某个contig到底是不是病毒来源，比如VirSorter2和DeepVirFinder这类工具——大多依靠与参考数据库的同源性搜索，而DeepVirFinder则是依靠从已知病毒序列中学到的模式。A second group profiles overall virome composition, tools like MetaVir and FastViromeExplorer. 第二类工具用于描绘整体病毒组（virome）构成，比如MetaVir和FastViromeExplorer。A third group handles taxonomy and functional annotation once you already have confirmed viral sequences, such as vConTACT2. 第三类工具则在已经确认是病毒序列之后，负责分类学归属和功能注释，比如vConTACT2。Stitching those together into one coherent analysis meant a researcher had to install, configure, and reconcile the outputs of several separate tools by hand, and the paper notes plainly that several of these tools are difficult to install or use for anyone without deep bioinformatics experience. 要把这些工具串联成一套连贯的分析流程，研究者就得手动安装、配置并协调好几个独立工具的输出结果，而论文也直言不讳地指出，其中不少工具对没有深厚生物信息学背景的人来说很难安装或使用。That's the gap ViroProfiler is built to close: one containerized workflow that runs the whole chain — discovery through functional annotation, taxonomy, host prediction, and abundance — with reproducible, version-pinned software at every step, runnable on a local Linux machine or in the cloud. ViroProfiler正是为填补这个空缺而搭建的：一个容器化的工作流程，跑通整个链条——从发现到功能注释、分类学、宿主预测和丰度估算——每一步都使用可复现、版本锁定的软件，可以在本地Linux机器或云端运行。

JORDAN Walk me through the actual pipeline shape, then — what happens step by step? 那带我过一遍具体的流程结构——每一步都发生了什么？

ALEX It's built as an nf-core-based Nextflow pipeline, meaning every tool runs inside its own versioned Docker or Singularity container that Nextflow downloads and manages automatically, with a Conda-based alternative for HPC systems that don't support containers. 它是基于nf-core构建的Nextflow流程，也就是说每个工具都运行在自己版本锁定的Docker或Singularity容器里，由Nextflow自动下载和管理，同时为不支持容器的高性能计算（HPC）系统提供了基于Conda的替代方案。Reads are quality-controlled with fastp — adapter trimming, low-quality read removal, end-trimming below a quality threshold, length filtering, and optional removal of reads matching a host genome — then each sample is assembled individually with metaSPAdes, and contigs are dereplicated at 95% average nucleotide identity and 85% aligned fraction, thresholds set by the MIUViG community guidelines for viral genomes, to build a non-redundant contig library. 读段先用fastp做质控——包括去除接头、剔除低质量读段、修剪质量分数低于阈值的末端碱基、按长度过滤，以及可选地去除匹配宿主基因组的读段——然后每个样本分别用metaSPAdes进行装配，再按95%的平均核苷酸一致性（ANI）和85%的比对覆盖比例（AF）——这是病毒基因组数据的社区指南MIUViG设定的阈值——对contig去冗余，构建一个非冗余contig文库。An optional binning step groups fragmented contigs believed to belong to the same genome into a single "bin," or viral metagenome-assembled genome, vMAG, using either Phamb or vRhyme. 一个可选的分箱（binning）步骤，会用Phamb或vRhyme把认为属于同一基因组的碎片化contig归并成一个"箱（bin）"，即病毒宏基因组组装基因组（vMAG）。For viral detection itself, five tools run in parallel — VirSorter2, MMseqs2's taxonomy module, CheckV, and VIBRANT, which are homology-based, plus DeepVirFinder, which is the one machine-learning-based detector in the group — and ViroProfiler assigns each candidate a confidence tier: "high confidence" if it's flagged by VIBRANT, by category 1 or 2 in VirSorter2, by the MMseqs2 taxonomy module, or rated complete-to-medium-quality by CheckV, versus "low confidence" if the only support is DeepVirFinder's machine-learning call or an "unclassified"/"low quality" rating elsewhere. 病毒检测本身则由五款工具并行完成——同源性方法的VirSorter2、MMseqs2的分类学模块、CheckV和VIBRANT，再加上组内唯一基于机器学习的检测器DeepVirFinder——ViroProfiler会给每个候选序列分配一个置信度等级："高置信度"是指被VIBRANT标记、被VirSorter2归为1类或2类、被MMseqs2分类学模块识别，或在CheckV中被评为完整到中等质量；"低置信度"则是指唯一支持来自DeepVirFinder的机器学习判定，或在别处被标为"未分类"/"低质量"。Functional annotation defaults to DRAM-v, which searches multiple databases and specifically flags auxiliary metabolic genes, AMGs — genes a phage carries that can reprogram its bacterial host's metabolism — with eggNOG-mapper offered as a faster alternative when AMG detection isn't the goal. 功能注释默认使用DRAM-v，它会检索多个数据库，并专门标记辅助代谢基因（auxiliary metabolic gene，AMG）——噬菌体携带的、能够重编程其细菌宿主代谢的基因——当不需要检测AMG时，也提供eggNOG-mapper作为更快的替代方案。Taxonomy combines vConTACT2's gene-sharing-network clustering with an MMseqs2 lowest-common-ancestor search against NCBI's viral RefSeq, host prediction uses iPHoP with a reported under-10% false-discovery rate, and replication-cycle prediction — lytic, lysogenic, or chronic — combines BACPHLIP with an in-house tool called Replidec for over 90% combined accuracy. 分类学部分结合了vConTACT2基于基因共享网络的聚类方法，以及针对NCBI病毒RefSeq数据库的MMseqs2最近共同祖先（LCA）搜索；宿主预测使用iPHoP，报告的假发现率低于10%；复制周期预测——裂解型、溶原型或慢性型——则结合了BACPHLIP和一款名为Replidec的自研工具，综合准确率超过90%。Abundance can be estimated two ways — Kraken2-plus-Bracken against a reference database, or read-mapping directly to the assembled contigs via bowtie2 and CoverM — and results feed into an R package, vpfkit, with an interactive Shiny app for filtering and visualization. 丰度可以用两种方式估算——通过Kraken2加Bracken对照参考数据库，或通过bowtie2和CoverM把读段直接比对回已装配的contig——结果最终导入一个R包vpfkit，并配有一个交互式Shiny应用用于筛选和可视化。

ALEX pushes back here on one claim: the paper repeatedly frames ViroProfiler as "database-independent," but that's only true for detection sensitivity — DeepVirFinder can flag a contig as viral with no database match at all — while the taxonomy step still ultimately falls back on NCBI's viral RefSeq for naming anything. 这里ALEX要对一个说法提出质疑：论文反复强调ViroProfiler是"不依赖数据库的（database-independent）"，但这只在检测灵敏度上成立——DeepVirFinder确实可以在没有任何数据库匹配的情况下把一个contig标记为病毒——但分类学这一步最终仍然依赖NCBI病毒RefSeq数据库才能给出具体名称。So a genuinely novel phage with no relatives in any database can still be detected and flagged high-confidence, but it comes out the other end essentially unnamed — "database-independent" describes finding it, not classifying it. 所以一个数据库中确实没有近亲的全新噬菌体，仍然可以被检测到并标记为高置信度，但走完流程之后基本上还是无法命名——"不依赖数据库"描述的是发现它的能力，而不是给它分类的能力。

JORDAN Did the benchmarking back up the pipeline's claims? 基准测试是否支撑了这套流程的说法？

ALEX On the mock dataset — 13 simulated Illumina sample sets, each seeded with 500 to 1,000 known viral genomes drawn from NCBI RefSeq — ViroProfiler had the best F1 score, the harmonic mean of precision and sensitivity, at the phylum and order ranks, especially at low abundance thresholds. 在模拟数据集上——13组模拟的Illumina样本集，每组都掺入了从NCBI RefSeq中抽取的500到1000个已知病毒基因组——ViroProfiler在门（phylum）和目（order）这两个分类级别上取得了最佳的F1分数（精确率和敏感度的调和平均值），在低丰度阈值下表现尤为突出。Comparing it against Kraken2-plus-Bracken run two ways — against Kraken2's standard database, and against a custom database built only from ViroProfiler-annotated contigs — the standard-database version had the highest raw sensitivity but lower precision at phylum and order; performance converged around the family level; and at genus and species level, ViroProfiler's sensitivity dropped sharply, while the custom-database version was worst across every rank, only about 50% sensitivity at phylum where ViroProfiler exceeded 95%. 与用两种方式跑的Kraken2加Bracken对比——一种对照Kraken2标准数据库，另一种对照仅由ViroProfiler注释的contig构建的自定义数据库——标准数据库版本的原始敏感度最高，但在门和目级别精确率较低；到科（family）一级性能趋于接近；而到属（genus）和种（species）级别，ViroProfiler的敏感度明显下降，自定义数据库版本则在所有级别上表现最差，在门级别的敏感度只有约50%，而ViroProfiler同一级别超过了95%。For abundance estimation against a known-true composition, ViroProfiler tracked the standard-database Bracken result closely at phylum and order, while Bracken with the standard database pulled ahead at family, genus, and species. 在丰度估算方面，对照已知真实组成，ViroProfiler在门和目级别与标准数据库版Bracken结果高度接近，而标准数据库版Bracken在科、属、种级别则更胜一筹。On a real dataset — a re-analysis of 20 of 266 fecal virome samples from a published ulcerative colitis study — ViroProfiler recovered 761 viral contigs versus the original study's 183, more than four times as many, and produced a materially different biological picture: a higher proportion of Podoviridae in healthy samples than UC samples, 34.6% versus 12.3%, the opposite direction from the original paper's finding, no significant diversity-score difference where the original study had reported one, and a host-prediction finding that UC patients carried fewer phages predicted to infect Bacteroidia. 在真实数据集上——对一项已发表的溃疡性结肠炎（UC）病毒组研究中266个粪便样本里的20个样本重新分析——ViroProfiler找到了761个病毒contig，而原研究只有183个，超过四倍，并且得出了实质上不同的生物学图景：健康样本中Podoviridae科病毒的比例高于UC样本（34.6%对12.3%），方向恰好与原论文的发现相反；原研究报告的多样性评分差异在此未见显著；宿主预测方面还发现，UC患者体内预测能感染Bacteroidia纲细菌的噬菌体更少。The compute footprint is real — roughly 80GB minimum storage, and the 13-mock-dataset benchmark took 12 hours on an HPC cluster with 1 to 20 CPUs and up to 120GB RAM per process, with host prediction alone taking 10 of those hours. 计算资源开销是实打实的——最低存储需求约80GB，13个模拟数据集的基准测试在一个每进程使用1到20个CPU、最多120GB内存的HPC集群上耗时12小时，其中仅宿主预测这一步就占了10小时。

JORDAN One more thing on the architecture before we move on — you mentioned binning and host prediction are optional. Why build in optionality at all, instead of just running everything every time? 在往下讲之前，再问一个架构上的问题——你提到分箱和宿主预测都是可选步骤。为什么要专门设计成可选，而不是每次都跑全套流程？
