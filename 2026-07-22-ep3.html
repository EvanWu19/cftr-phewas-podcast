Show notes — Track C — 2026-07-22

Single-cell RNA-sequencing co-expression analysis with CFTR and CF modifier genes in lung tissue — Wang C, Kanagarajah K, Wong A, Ratjen F, Strug LJ, Scientific Reports (2025).

Blurb

CF severity varies among people with the same CFTR genotype, and modifier genes explain part of that variance — but GWAS names a locus without saying through which cell or mechanism it acts. This paper maps CFTR co-expression at single-cell resolution in lung. Using Smart-Seq2 data from non-CF lung (Travaglini atlas; 3 donors, ~9,400 cells, 44 cell types) with a zero-inflated negative binomial co-expression test (bidirectional, FDR within cell type, Cauchy/ACAT pooling), and 10X Chromium data (Travaglini, GTEx, and CF lung) as proportion-based replication, they find CFTR sits in the richest modifier network in AT2 cells (also differentiating basal and club cells) — even though ionocytes carry the most CFTR per cell but were too rare (n=2) to test. The cleanest result is a CFTR–SLC6A14–SLC26A9 co-expression trio specific to AT2 cells (p ~10⁻³⁶, survives correction; SLC9A3 excluded), replicated by proportion in non-CF 10X but unconfirmable in CF 10X (no AT2 cells captured). Three independent gene sets — GWAS modifiers, 156 apical-membrane genes, 638 F508del interactors — all converge on AT2/basal/club cells, and the authors read this as a cell-type shortlist for gene-therapy targeting. Where it lands: Track C modifier catalog / WGS long game, via a cell-type-weighted modifier prioritizer that up-ranks modifiers co-expressed with CFTR in CFTR-relevant cells.

Plain-English glossary

  • Modifier gene — a gene, separate from CFTR, whose variation shifts CF disease severity.（中文：不同于CFTR、其变异会改变CF疾病严重程度的基因。）
  • Co-expression — two genes whose transcript levels rise and fall together across single cells, hinting at a functional partnership.（中文：在单个细胞间转录本水平同升同降的两个基因，暗示功能上的伙伴关系。）
  • scRNA-seq (single-cell RNA sequencing) — reads each cell's transcripts separately, so cell types aren't averaged together.（中文：分别读取每个细胞转录本的测序，避免把不同细胞类型平均掉。）
  • Smart-Seq2 (SS2) vs 10X Chromium — SS2 reads more genes per cell but fewer cells; 10X reads many cells but shallower per cell.（中文：SS2每细胞读到的基因多但细胞少；10X细胞多但每细胞更浅。）
  • Dropout — a scRNA-seq zero caused by the method missing a transcript, not by the gene being off.（中文：因方法漏检而非基因关闭造成的scRNA-seq零值。）
  • Zero-inflated negative binomial (ZINB) — a count model that separates true zeros from dropout zeros.（中文：把真实零值与脱落零值区分开的计数模型。）
  • Apical membrane — the airway-facing surface of an epithelial cell, where CFTR and several ion transporters sit.（中文：上皮细胞朝向气道的表面，CFTR和多个离子转运体所在处。）
  • AT2 cell (alveolar type 2) — an alveolar epithelial cell type; here, the cell with the richest CFTR-modifier co-expression network.（中文：II型肺泡上皮细胞；本文中CFTR-修饰基因共表达网络最丰富的细胞。）
  • Ionocyte — a rare airway cell carrying a high proportion of CFTR; too rare here (n=2) to test.（中文：携带高比例CFTR的稀有气道细胞；本文数量太少无法检验。）
  • SLC6A14 / SLC26A9 / SLC9A3 — apical ion transporters/channels reported as CF modifiers; the first two form the AT2 trio with CFTR, SLC9A3 does not.（中文：作为CF修饰基因的顶膜离子转运体/通道；前两者与CFTR构成AT2三元组，SLC9A3不属于。）
  • F508del — the most common CFTR variant; the 638-gene interactor set is built around the F508del protein.（中文：最常见的CFTR变异；638基因相互作用集围绕F508del蛋白构建。）

Full citation

Wang C, Kanagarajah K, Wong A, Ratjen F, Strug LJ. Single-cell RNA-sequencing co-expression analysis with CFTR and CF modifier genes in lung tissue. Sci Rep 2025. PMID 41330982 · PMC PMC12673106 · DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-27111-9

Methods / tools to lift

  • Zero-inflated negative binomial co-expression test (R package pscl, log link) — regress gene A on gene B and vice versa per cell type, adjusting for the per-cell proportion of non-zero genes as a background/dropout control.
  • Within-cell-type FDR + Cauchy combination (ACAT) — control multiple testing per cell type and pool the two regression directions into one p-value.
  • Proportion-based replication for underpowered platforms — where 10X lacks power for the formal test, count the proportion of cells co-expressing each pair as concordance evidence.
  • Convergent gene sets — cross-check the same cell types against three independent gene lists (GWAS modifiers, apical-membrane constituents, F508del interactors) rather than one.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: modifier / WGS long game (Track C).

Paste-ready MVP — cell-type-weighted modifier prioritizer (S–M): input a list of candidate CF modifier genes; from a public lung scRNA atlas, attach each gene's CFTR co-expression status and cell type; emit a prior weight that up-ranks modifiers co-expressed with CFTR in AT2/basal/club cells and flags those with no CFTR-relevant co-expression. Output: a re-ranked gene list with a cell-type tag and co-expression score per gene. Gives a variant-prioritization pipeline a biological reason to rank one modifier above another instead of treating GWAS hits as interchangeable. Atlas and ZINB model are already public.

What to verify first

  • Correlational, not causal: co-expression ranks hypotheses; it is never evidence a gene causally modifies disease.
  • Non-CF tissue: the signal came almost entirely from non-CF lung, and the one CF dataset lacked AT2 cells — check whether the trio/co-expression holds in diseased tissue before trusting it as a CF-specific prior.
  • Low power: 3 donors, tiny effect sizes, ionocytes untestable (n=2) — "no co-expression" may mean under-sampled; never down-rank a gene to zero on absence.
  • Annotation dependency: the prior inherits any cell-type mislabeling or merging in the source atlas.

Gap-map / catalog: closes / leaves open

Closes: adds cell-type-resolution evidence to the modifier catalog for SLC6A14 and SLC26A9 (AT2-specific co-expression trio with CFTR) and a negative for SLC9A3 (not in the trio); establishes AT2/basal/club as the CFTR-modifier-network cell types and a gene-therapy target shortlist; supplies a reusable co-expression-as-prior method.

Leaves open: no CF-tissue confirmation of the AT2 trio (AT2 absent from CF 10X); no causal or variant-level test — expression co-occurrence ≠ genetic modification; ionocyte co-expression untestable; effect sizes biologically subtle; whether the prior improves real modifier-variant prioritization is unmeasured.

ALEX Today's Track C paper is "Single-cell RNA-sequencing co-expression analysis with CFTR and CF modifier genes in lung tissue," by Cheng Wang and Lisa Strug's group in Toronto, published in Scientific Reports in 2025. 今天的Track C论文是"肺组织中CFTR与CF修饰基因的单细胞RNA测序共表达分析"，作者是多伦多Cheng Wang与Lisa Strug团队，2025年发表于《科学报告》。 Strug's lab is one of the central groups in CF modifier genetics, so this is a modifier-catalog paper with real pedigree. Strug实验室是CF修饰基因遗传学的核心团队之一，所以这是一篇有真正来历的修饰基因目录论文。 The question it asks is deceptively simple: in which lung cell types is CFTR turned on at the same time as the genes that modify CF severity? 它提出的问题看似简单：在哪些肺细胞类型里，CFTR与那些调节CF严重程度的基因是同时开启的？

JORDAN Start me at the beginning — what's a modifier gene, and why do we care where it's expressed? 从头讲给我听——什么是修饰基因，我们又为什么在意它在哪里表达？

ALEX Cystic fibrosis is caused by loss-of-function variants in one gene, CFTR. 囊性纤维化是由单个基因CFTR的功能缺失变异引起的。 But two people with the exact same CFTR genotype can have very different lung disease, and a lot of that difference traces to other genes called modifier genes. 但两个拥有完全相同CFTR基因型的人，肺部疾病可能天差地别，而这种差异很大程度上要归因于其他被称为修饰基因 (modifier gene) 的基因。 Genome-wide association studies have named several of these modifiers. 全基因组关联研究 (GWAS) 已经命名了其中几个修饰基因。 The trouble is that GWAS tells you a locus matters statistically, but not through which cell or which mechanism. 麻烦在于，GWAS告诉你某个位点在统计上重要，却没告诉你是通过哪种细胞、哪种机制起作用。 A GWAS hit is a genomic coordinate with a p-value attached; it doesn't come with a biological story. 一个GWAS命中是一个附着了p值的基因组坐标；它并不自带一个生物学故事。 The gap between "this locus associates with lung function" and "here is how it works" is where most of the real work in modifier genetics still sits. "这个位点与肺功能相关"和"这就是它的作用方式"之间的鸿沟，是修饰基因遗传学里大部分真正的工作至今仍然所在之处。 Knowing the cell type matters for two reasons: it constrains the biological mechanism, and it tells a gene-therapy designer which cell to actually target. 知道细胞类型有两个意义：它约束了生物学机制，也告诉基因治疗的设计者到底该瞄准哪种细胞。

JORDAN And "co-expression" — what does it mean for two genes to be co-expressed? "共表达"——两个基因共表达是什么意思？

ALEX It means that across single cells, when one gene's transcript goes up, the other's tends to go up too. 它的意思是：在单个细胞之间，当一个基因的转录本升高时，另一个也倾向于升高。 The biological bet is that genes switched on together in the same cell are more likely to be working together. 生物学上的赌注是：在同一个细胞里被一起开启的基因，更可能在协同工作。 For CF specifically, several modifiers — SLC9A3, SLC6A14, SLC26A9 — encode alternative ion channels and transporters that sit at the same apical membrane as CFTR. 具体到CF，好几个修饰基因——SLC9A3、SLC6A14、SLC26A9——编码替代性的离子通道和转运体，它们与CFTR位于同一个顶膜 (apical membrane) 上。 If one of those can move chloride when CFTR can't, it might compensate — and co-expression in normal tissue is the first hint that such a partnership exists. 如果其中一个能在CFTR失效时搬运氯离子，它或许能补偿——而在正常组织里的共表达，正是这种伙伴关系存在的第一个线索。

JORDAN You mentioned single cells — why not just measure the whole lung at once? 你提到单细胞——为什么不干脆一次测量整个肺？

ALEX Because the lung is a mix of cell types, and bulk measurement averages them into mush. 因为肺是多种细胞类型的混合体，而整体测量会把它们平均成一团糊。 Single-cell RNA sequencing, or scRNA-seq, reads each cell separately, so you can ask which specific cell type carries the co-expression. 单细胞RNA测序，即scRNA-seq，分别读取每个细胞，于是你可以追问是哪种具体的细胞类型承载了共表达。 The airway has ciliated cells, goblet cells, basal cells, rare ionocytes, and down in the gas-exchange region, alveolar type 1 and type 2 cells — AT1 and AT2. 气道里有纤毛细胞、杯状细胞、基底细胞、稀有的离子细胞 (ionocyte)，再往下到气体交换区，还有I型和II型肺泡上皮细胞——AT1和AT2。 The catch with scRNA-seq is that it's noisy and full of zeros — a gene can read zero not because it's off but because the method missed it, an artifact called dropout. scRNA-seq的问题在于它噪声大、充满零值——一个基因读到零可能不是因为它关闭了，而是因为方法漏检了，这种假象叫做脱落 (dropout)。 That's why they used a zero-inflated negative binomial model, which explicitly separates true zeros from dropout zeros. 这就是他们为什么用了零膨胀负二项 (zero-inflated negative binomial) 模型，它明确地把真实的零与脱落的零区分开。

JORDAN Good — I know the statistics, so tell me what they actually did. 好——统计我懂，所以告诉我他们究竟做了什么。

ALEX The primary dataset was Smart-Seq2 single-cell data from non-CF lung — the Travaglini atlas — three donors, about 9,400 cells, 44 cell types after filtering. 主数据集是来自非CF肺的Smart-Seq2单细胞数据——Travaglini图谱——三个供体，约9,400个细胞，过滤后44种细胞类型。 Smart-Seq2, abbreviated SS2, reads more genes per cell but covers fewer cells; the 10X Chromium platform is the opposite — many cells, shallower per cell. Smart-Seq2缩写SS2，每个细胞读到的基因更多但覆盖的细胞更少；10X Chromium平台正相反——细胞多，但每个细胞更浅。 They ran the formal co-expression test only on the SS2 data, using the negative binomial model in both directions — gene A on gene B and B on A — with false-discovery correction within each cell type and a Cauchy combination to pool the two directions. 他们只在SS2数据上跑了正式的共表达检验，用负二项模型双向进行——基因A对基因B、以及B对A——在每种细胞类型内做假发现率 (FDR) 校正，再用Cauchy组合把两个方向合并。 The 10X datasets — including actual CF lung — were used only as replication, by counting the proportion of cells co-expressing each pair, because the 10X design didn't have the power for the formal test. 10X数据集——包括真正的CF肺——只被用作重复验证，方法是统计共表达每一对基因的细胞比例，因为10X的设计没有足够的功效做正式检验。 And they tested CFTR against the GWAS modifiers, a set of 156 apical-membrane genes, and 638 genes reported to interact with F508del CFTR. 他们把CFTR对照GWAS修饰基因、一组156个顶膜基因、以及638个被报告与F508del CFTR相互作用的基因进行了检验。

JORDAN Where would you push on that design? 你会在这个设计上质疑哪里？

ALEX The load-bearing caveat is that co-expression is a correlation, not a mechanism. 最吃劲的一条警告是：共表达是相关，不是机制。 Two genes turning on in the same cell is consistent with them cooperating, but also with them simply responding to the same upstream signal — it's a prior, not proof of a functional partnership. 两个基因在同一个细胞里开启，既可能意味着它们在协作，也可能只是它们对同一个上游信号做出反应——它是一个先验，不是功能伙伴关系的证明。 Second, the formal test ran on three donors and about 9,400 cells, which is thin — the authors are candid that power for co-expression detection is low. 第二，正式检验只跑在三个供体、约9,400个细胞上，这很单薄——作者坦承共表达检测的功效很低。 Ionocytes are the extreme case: they carry the most CFTR of any cell type, but there were only two of them in the SS2 data, so they couldn't be tested at all. 离子细胞是极端的例子：它们携带的CFTR比任何细胞类型都多，但SS2数据里只有两个，所以根本无法检验。 So the cell that looks most CFTR-relevant is exactly the one the study is blind to. 于是那个看起来与CFTR最相关的细胞，恰恰是这项研究看不见的。

JORDAN So what did they find in the cells they could test? 那在他们能检验的细胞里，他们发现了什么？

ALEX CFTR itself was detectable in 25 cell types, but concentrated in a few. CFTR本身在25种细胞类型里可检测到，但集中在少数几种里。 By proportion of cells expressing it: ionocytes at 100%, goblet cells 59%, AT2 cells 41%, club cells 34%, and differentiating basal cells 21%. 按表达它的细胞比例：离子细胞100%、杯状细胞59%、AT2细胞41%、纤毛前体的club细胞34%、以及分化中的基底细胞21%。 But raw CFTR abundance isn't the story — the co-expression is. 但CFTR的原始丰度不是重点——共表达才是。 The cell types with the greatest number of CFTR-co-expressing modifier genes were differentiating basal, AT2, and club cells, and AT2 cells had the single highest proportion of significant co-expression with the modifier set. 拥有最多与CFTR共表达的修饰基因的细胞类型是分化中的基底细胞、AT2和club细胞，而AT2细胞与修饰基因集的显著共表达比例是单项最高的。 So ionocytes carry the most CFTR, but AT2 cells are where CFTR sits inside the richest modifier network. 所以离子细胞携带最多CFTR，但AT2细胞才是CFTR身处最丰富修饰网络之中的地方。

JORDAN And there was a specific three-gene result — walk me through it. 还有一个具体的三基因结果——带我过一遍。

ALEX This is the cleanest finding. 这是最干净的发现。 CFTR was significantly co-expressed with both SLC6A14 and SLC26A9, and those two were co-expressed with each other — a co-expression trio. CFTR与SLC6A14和SLC26A9都显著共表达，而这两者彼此之间也共表达——一个共表达三元组。 That trio appeared only in AT2 cells, and it survived multiple-testing correction, with p-values as small as around ten-to-the-minus-thirty-six. 这个三元组只出现在AT2细胞里，并且通过了多重检验校正，p值小到约10的负36次方。 Notably, SLC9A3 — another apical transporter modifier — was not part of the trio, so this isn't just "all transporters cluster together." 值得注意的是，SLC9A3——另一个顶膜转运体修饰基因——不属于这个三元组，所以这不是简单的"所有转运体都聚在一起"。 That exclusion is quietly important, because it's the kind of negative result that tells you the signal is specific rather than an artifact of the gene set. 这个排除悄悄地很重要，因为它是那种能告诉你信号是特异的、而非基因集假象的阴性结果。 If every apical transporter had co-expressed with CFTR, you'd suspect the finding was just "genes expressed in the same membrane look correlated." 如果每个顶膜转运体都与CFTR共表达，你会怀疑这个发现只是"同一个膜上表达的基因看起来相关"。 SLC6A14 and SLC26A9 being in and SLC9A3 being out is what makes the trio look like biology instead of bookkeeping. SLC6A14和SLC26A9在内、SLC9A3在外，正是这一点让这个三元组看起来像生物学，而不是记账。 The trio was supported in the non-CF 10X data by the proportion counts, so it replicated across platforms. 这个三元组在非CF的10X数据里由细胞比例统计所支持，因此跨平台得到了重复。 One honest gap: AT2 cells weren't captured in the CF 10X data, so the one dataset from actual CF lung couldn't confirm the trio in the cell type that matters. 一个诚实的缺口：CF的10X数据里没有采到AT2细胞，所以那份来自真正CF肺的数据，无法在那个关键细胞类型里证实这个三元组。 And the effect sizes are tiny in absolute terms — this is a statistically strong but biologically subtle signal. 而且效应量在绝对值上很小——这是一个统计上很强、但生物学上很微妙的信号。

JORDAN They also tested two other gene sets — the apical-membrane list and the 638 F508del interactors. 他们还检验了另外两个基因集——顶膜基因列表和638个F508del相互作用基因。 Did those tell a different story? 那些讲了一个不同的故事吗？

ALEX They told the same story from a different angle, which is what you want from a replication. 它们从不同的角度讲了同一个故事，而这正是你希望从重复验证中看到的。 The 156 apical-membrane genes had previously been shown, as a set, to associate with CF disease severity, so they're a biologically motivated group, not a random panel. 那156个顶膜基因此前已被证明作为一个整体与CF疾病严重程度相关，所以它们是一个有生物学动机的群体，不是随机的面板。 The 638 genes come from Pankow and colleagues, who mapped high-confidence physical interactors of F508del CFTR — the mutant protein's neighborhood. 那638个基因来自Pankow等人，他们绘制了F508del CFTR的高置信度物理相互作用基因——也就是突变蛋白的"邻里"。 For both sets, the same cell types lit up: AT2, club, and differentiating basal cells carried the most CFTR co-expression. 对两个基因集来说，亮起来的是同样的细胞类型：AT2、club和分化中的基底细胞承载了最多的CFTR共表达。 So three different gene lists — GWAS modifiers, apical constituents, and F508del interactors — all point at the same handful of cells. 所以三份不同的基因列表——GWAS修饰基因、顶膜组成成分、和F508del相互作用基因——全都指向同一小撮细胞。 That convergence is more persuasive than any single co-expression p-value. 这种趋同比任何单个共表达的p值都更有说服力。

JORDAN And you said this feeds gene-therapy targeting — how directly? 你说这为基因治疗的靶向提供依据——有多直接？

ALEX The authors are explicit that they're after cellular targets for next-generation therapies. 作者明确表示他们在寻找下一代疗法的细胞靶点。 The naive assumption would be to target ionocytes, since they carry the most CFTR per cell. 天真的假设会是瞄准离子细胞，因为它们每个细胞携带的CFTR最多。 But ionocytes are vanishingly rare and, as prior work noted, probably not the main source of CFTR function in the lung. 但离子细胞极其稀少，而且正如先前的工作指出，很可能不是肺中CFTR功能的主要来源。 The co-expression map argues that AT2 and basal cells — abundant, and networked with modifiers — may be the more sensible delivery targets. 这张共表达图谱主张，AT2和基底细胞——数量丰富、又与修饰基因相互连接——可能是更合理的递送靶点。 That's a genuinely actionable output: a cell-type shortlist backed by convergent expression evidence. 这是一个真正可执行的产出：一份由趋同表达证据支撑的细胞类型候选名单。
