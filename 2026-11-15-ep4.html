Ep4 (Track C — registry QC, 2/2): Modelling childhood growth using fractional polynomials and linear splines

Tilling, Macdonald-Wallis, Lawlor, Hughes, and Howe lay out, side by side on the same ALSPAC birth-cohort data (5,588 girls, height from birth to age 10), the two standard ways to model a nonlinear growth trajectory inside a multilevel model: fractional polynomials (an automated-selection family of flexible curve shapes, smooth but hard to interpret directly) and linear splines (a "broken-stick" piecewise-linear model with no consensus knot-selection method, biologically implausible at its corners but directly interpretable in cm/month). In their worked example the 3-knot linear spline (knots at 3, 12, and 36 months) fit slightly better by AIC than the best 2-degree fractional polynomial, despite the latter's "automated" selection process actually ending in a human rounding deviance-optimal knots (2, 11, 32 months) to convenient round numbers. This is the trajectory-modeling substrate that any outlier-flagging approach needs before it can define what "expected" even means — and it pairs directly with ep3's warning that the measurement timing feeding into that substrate can itself be biased. The buildable artifact is a model-comparison harness that fits both approaches to a toy dataset and surfaces where — not just whether — they disagree on which points are outliers.

Plain-English glossary

  • Fractional polynomial — a flexible family of curve shapes built from a fixed menu of allowed age powers (e.g. square root, log, squared), with a model-selection procedure choosing the best one or two.（中文：由一组预先设定好的年龄幂次(如平方根、对数、平方)构成的灵活曲线族，通过模型选择程序挑出最优的一个或两个幂次。）
  • Knot (in a spline model) — a specific age at which a linear spline's slope is allowed to change abruptly, joining one linear segment to the next.（中文：线性样条模型中，允许斜率发生突变、连接两段直线的特定年龄点。）
  • Linear spline / broken-stick / piecewise model — a trajectory model built from connected straight-line segments joined at knots, each segment with its own (possibly random-effect) slope.（中文：由若干在结点处相连的直线段构成的轨迹模型，每一段都有自己的(可能带随机效应的)斜率，也叫"折线模型"或分段模型。）
  • Multilevel model (MLM) — a regression framework that accounts for repeated measurements being correlated within the same individual, rather than treating every measurement as independent.（中文：一种考虑到同一个体的重复测量彼此相关、而非把每次测量当作独立观测的回归建模框架。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Multilevel fractional polynomial modelling — fit via deviance comparison across a fixed menu of age powers (-2, -1, -0.5, 0 [log], 0.5, 1, 2, 3), selecting the best degree-1 or degree-2 combination.
  • Multilevel linear spline (broken-stick) modelling — fit by comparing model deviance across a grid of candidate knot positions, then (per this paper's own example) rounding to interpretable round numbers.
  • AIC-based model comparison — used throughout to compare fractional-polynomial vs. linear-spline fit on the same data.

JORDAN Let's get the two competing approaches straight before anything else. 先把这两种相互竞争的方法讲清楚，再说别的。 A fractional polynomial is a flexible family of curve shapes built from a small menu of allowed powers of age — things like the square root of age, the log of age, age squared — and a model-selection procedure picks the best one or two powers from that menu based on how much they improve model fit. 分数多项式 (fractional polynomial) 是一种灵活的曲线族，由一小组预先设定好的年龄幂次菜单构成——比如年龄的平方根、年龄的对数、年龄的平方等等——然后通过一个模型选择程序，根据能在多大程度上改善模型拟合，从这个菜单中挑出最好的一个或两个幂次。 A linear spline — also called a broken-stick or piecewise model — instead chops the age range into connected straight-line segments, joined at specific ages called knots, so growth rate is allowed to change abruptly at each knot but is linear within each segment. 线性样条 (linear spline)——也叫"折线模型" (broken-stick) 或分段模型——则是把年龄范围切割成若干段相连的直线段，在称为"结点" (knot) 的特定年龄处连接起来，这样生长速率可以在每个结点处发生突变，但在每一段内部是线性的。 Both get fit inside a multilevel model, or MLM, which just means the model accounts for the fact that repeated height measurements on the same child are correlated with each other, rather than treating every measurement as independent. 两者都是在多水平模型 (multilevel model, MLM) 内部拟合的，这只是意味着模型考虑到了同一个孩子的多次重复身高测量彼此相关，而不是把每一次测量都当作互相独立的。

ALEX One mechanical detail worth knowing because it trips people up: fractional polynomials require age to be strictly greater than zero, since some of the allowed powers are things like the log of age or negative powers, which blow up at exactly zero. 有个机械性的细节值得了解，因为它经常让人踩坑：分数多项式要求年龄严格大于零，因为允许使用的幂次里有一些是年龄的对数或负数次幂，在年龄恰好为零时会直接发散。 If your trajectory starts at birth, you have to add a small constant to every age value to push it above zero, and the paper flags — almost as an aside — that this constant isn't free to choose arbitrarily, because fractional polynomials are sensitive to additive shifts in a way they aren't sensitive to multiplicative ones. 如果你的轨迹是从出生那一刻开始算的，就必须给每个年龄值加上一个小常数，让它大于零，而论文里几乎是以一句带过的方式指出，这个常数不能随意选取，因为分数多项式对加法平移很敏感，而对乘法变换却不敏感。 They used 0.01 months here, following a stated recommendation to use the smallest gap between successive time measurements, but say further sensitivity analysis on that choice would be worthwhile — which is a small thing, but it's exactly the kind of modeling decision that's invisible unless you've read the methods closely. 他们在这里用的是0.01个月，依据的是"用相邻两次测量之间最小的时间间隔"这一建议，但他们也说明，针对这个选择做进一步的敏感性分析是值得的——这是个小细节，但恰恰是那种除非仔细读过方法学部分，否则根本不会察觉到的建模决策。
