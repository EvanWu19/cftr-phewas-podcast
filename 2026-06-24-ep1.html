Show notes — ClinGen PP3/BP4 calibration / Pejaver 2022 (Track A, variant interpretation)

Blurb. Pejaver et al. (Am J Hum Genet, 2022), writing for the ClinGen Sequence Variant Interpretation Working Group, supply the missing manual for every missense predictor this track has covered. The 2015 ACMG/AMP rules capped computational evidence at "supporting" (codes PP3 for pathogenic, BP4 for benign) and told labs to require consensus across multiple tools — a practice that is statistically miscalibrated because most predictors are correlated. Building on the Tavtigian 2018 Bayesian reframing (where supporting/moderate/strong/very-strong map to a geometric ladder of positive likelihood ratios), the authors estimate, via a local positive-predictive-value sliding window, the score thresholds at which each of 13 tools (BayesDel, CADD, EA, FATHMM, GERP++, MPC, MutPred2, PhyloP, PolyPhen-2, PrimateAI, REVEL, SIFT, VEST4) earns each evidence strength. Datasets were aggressively scrubbed of training-set overlap (including constituent-tool training variants for meta-predictors). Key findings: several tools reach strong evidence (BayesDel, VEST4 for pathogenic; CADD for benign; MutPred2, REVEL both) — far beyond the 2015 "supporting" cap — and these intervals are stringent (only 1.4–1.7% of gnomAD variants qualify for strong-pathogenic, below the 4.41% prior). Crucially, developer-recommended defaults are wrong: SIFT/PolyPhen-2/CADD at default cutoffs don't even reach PP3-supporting, and CADD=20 sits in the benign-moderate interval. Recommendation: use one calibrated tool genome-wide, chosen before seeing results. Where it lands: Karen / variant interpretation. Buildable artifact: a CFTR PP3/BP4 evidence-assignment table, with a higher-value CFTR-specific recalibration variant.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP guidelines — the 2015 rulebook (American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics + Association for Molecular Pathology) that clinical labs use to classify a variant as pathogenic, benign, or uncertain, via weighted evidence codes.（中文：ACMG/AMP 指南，2015 年发布的变异分类规则手册，临床实验室据此用加权证据代码判定变异为致病、良性或意义未明。）
  • BP4 / PP3 — the specific ACMG evidence codes for computational predictions: BP4 = in silico evidence the variant is benign; PP3 = in silico evidence it is pathogenic.（中文：BP4 / PP3，ACMG 中专给计算预测的证据代码；BP4 表示计算证据提示良性，PP3 表示提示致病。）
  • CFTR2 — the curated database of CFTR variant functional/clinical classifications; the deepest gene-specific variant resource for CF.（中文：CFTR2，CFTR 变异功能/临床分类的专家整理数据库，是 CF 领域最深入的基因专属变异资源。）
  • ClinVar — the NIH public archive of variants with submitted clinical interpretations.（中文：ClinVar，NIH 的公开数据库，汇集了带有临床解读判定的变异。）
  • Evidence strength (supporting/moderate/strong/very strong) — ACMG's four-rung ladder for how much weight a single line of evidence carries; under the Bayesian model the rungs are a geometric series of odds.（中文：证据强度（支持性/中等/强/非常强），ACMG 衡量单条证据分量的四级阶梯；在贝叶斯模型下各级是比值的等比数列。）
  • gnomAD — the Genome Aggregation Database, the standard population reference for how often a variant is seen across large sequencing cohorts; used here as a benign-enriched / population background set.（中文：gnomAD，基因组聚合数据库，衡量变异在大型测序队列中出现频率的标准人群参考；此处用作富集良性的人群背景集。）
  • Missense variant — a single-nucleotide change that swaps one amino acid for another; the protein stays full-length but with one substitution.（中文：错义变异，把一个氨基酸换成另一个的单核苷酸改变；蛋白质仍为全长，只是替换了一个氨基酸。）
  • positive likelihood ratio (LR+) — in this clinical-genetics sense, how much more often a given score is produced by truly pathogenic vs truly benign variants; the currency the evidence ladder is denominated in.（中文：阳性似然比 (LR+)，在此临床遗传学语境中指：某分数由真正致病变异产生的频率，相对真正良性变异高多少倍；它是证据阶梯的计价货币。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant that current evidence can't classify as pathogenic or benign; the backlog this whole framework is trying to shrink.（中文：VUS（意义未明变异），现有证据无法判定为致病或良性的变异；正是这整套框架想要缩小的积压。）

Full citation. Pejaver V, Byrne AB, Feng BJ, et al.; ClinGen Sequence Variant Interpretation Working Group. Methods / tools to lift

  • Local positive-predictive-value / local-likelihood-ratio sliding window to convert any continuous predictor score into a calibrated ACMG evidence strength — tool-agnostic, works on RF, evolutionary, or transformer-based scores.
  • Tavtigian Bayesian points framework: supporting/moderate/strong/very-strong as a geometric ladder of odds, enabling a single tool to provide more-than-supporting evidence.
  • Aggressive training-set scrubbing (including constituent-tool training variants for meta-predictors) to estimate generalization, not memorization.
  • gnomAD-fraction stringency check: a calibrated strong-pathogenic interval should flag only a small fraction (here 1.4–1.7%) of population variants, below the estimated prior (4.41%).
  • Validation on a held-out, time-separated ClinVar set (2020) to confirm thresholds don't drift.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Karen / variant interpretation. MVP (paste-ready): for every CFTR missense variant, join its precomputed score from one chosen calibrated tool (e.g., REVEL) against the Pejaver PP3/BP4 threshold table and emit per-variant {ACMG strength, direction} ∈ {PP3_Strong, PP3_Moderate, PP3_Supporting, indeterminate, BP4_Supporting, BP4_Moderate, ...}. Input: CFTR missense variant list with build-tagged coords. Difficulty S. Higher-value extension (difficulty M, publishable): run the local-LR calibration on CFTR variants alone using CFTR2 labels to test whether CFTR-specific thresholds differ from genome-wide. Pairs with the existing CFTR-missense-REVEL-triage-table (discordance) — this one assigns formal evidence weight.

What to verify first

  • Circularity for the CFTR-specific version: confirm CFTR2-labeled variants used for recalibration weren't in the chosen tool's training set (or constituent-tool training sets for meta-predictors).
  • Variant count: gene-specific local-LR windows need enough pathogenic AND benign CFTR variants to be stable — verify the benign side has the numbers.
  • BP4_Moderate handling: this benign-moderate tier isn't natively compatible with the 2015 combining-rules table; downstream classification logic must use the points-based adaptation.
  • Tool/version pinning: thresholds are specific to a tool version and score build; pin the exact REVEL/AlphaMissense version and genome build to the threshold table used.
  • "What we'd need to check," not a settled design — no claim made here about CFFPR internals or any decided research plan.

JORDAN That mapping sounds like the heart of it. 那个映射听起来才是核心。Where did it come from? 它是从哪里来的？

ALEX That's the right place to dig, so let me give you the background. 这正是该深挖的地方，让我把背景讲一下。Back in 2018, Sean Tavtigian and colleagues showed that the 2015 ACMG/AMP rules — which read like an arbitrary committee checklist — actually fit a clean Bayesian model. 早在 2018 年，Sean Tavtigian 等人就证明了：2015 年的 ACMG/AMP 规则——读起来像是一份随意的委员会清单——其实贴合一个干净的贝叶斯模型。If you assign "supporting" a certain odds of pathogenicity, then "moderate," "strong," and "very strong" come out as that odds raised to escalating powers — the strengths form a geometric ladder. 如果你给"支持性"赋予一个确定的致病比值 (odds)，那么"中等""强""非常强"就表现为那个比值的逐级乘方——这些强度构成了一个等比阶梯。That reframing is what made calibration even thinkable, because now each strength tier corresponds to a precise likelihood-ratio threshold instead of a vibe. 正是这个重新表述让"校准"这件事变得可想象，因为现在每个强度等级都对应一个精确的似然比阈值，而不是一种感觉。The problem Pejaver tackles is that tool developers had never calibrated their score cutoffs to that ladder.
