Show notes — Track A — 2026-07-22

When splicing is not all or none: GT>GC 5' splice-site variants as a model for intermediate effects and challenges in variant classification — Lin JH, Chen JM, Férec C et al., HGG Advances (2026).

Blurb

Canonical dogma says any change to the invariant GT dinucleotide at a 5' splice site abolishes splicing. This paper shows GT>GC (+2T>C) variants are the systematic exception: roughly 15–18% retain measurable residual wild-type transcript, placing them in a functional "gray zone" that binary pathogenic/benign frameworks can't hold. Using CFTR as a single-locus model, the authors scored all 26 possible GT>GC substitutions with SpliceAI's delta-donor-loss (ΔDL) score, cross-checked CFTR2 and CFTR-France classifications, and ran minigene assays on four CFTR variants plus a contested BAP1 variant. The headline: there is no consistent quantitative relationship between predicted ΔDL and measured residual transcript — SPINK1 retains ~10% at ΔDL 0.33, MGP retains ~80% at ΔDL 0.97 — and two minigene vectors (pET01, pSPL3) disagreed with each other, exposing assay instability as much as variant ambiguity. Where it lands: the Karen / CFTR variant-interpretation priority, via a buildable CFTR splice-variant gray-zone triage sheet that flags variants where the SpliceAI score and the functional/database evidence contradict each other.

Plain-English glossary

  • 5' splice site (donor site) — the exon-end/intron-start boundary where splicing cuts; almost all introns begin with an invariant "GT".（中文：外显子结束、内含子开始的剪接边界，几乎所有内含子都以不变的"GT"开头。）
  • GT>GC (+2T>C) variant — a change of the second invariant base (T→C) at the donor site; unusually, can leave partial splicing intact.（中文：供体位点第二个不变碱基由T变C的变异，罕见地可能保留部分正常剪接。）
  • ΔDL (SpliceAI delta donor-loss) — SpliceAI's 0–1 score for how much a variant weakens the donor site.（中文：SpliceAI给出的0到1分值，衡量变异对供体位点的削弱程度。）
  • Gray zone / intermediate effect — a variant with real, partial loss of function — too weak to call pathogenic, too strong to call benign.（中文：具有真实的部分功能缺失的变异，弱到不能判致病、强到不能判良性。）
  • Intron / exon — introns are cut out during splicing; exons are kept and joined.（中文：内含子在剪接时被剪除，外显子被保留并连接。）
  • Loss-of-function (LoF) — a variant that reduces or removes the normal activity of the gene product.（中文：降低或消除基因产物正常功能的变异。）
  • Minigene assay — a lab construct cloning an exon plus flanking intron into a plasmid to read out splicing in cells.（中文：把外显子及两侧内含子克隆进质粒、在细胞中读出剪接结果的实验室方法。）
  • Residual wild-type transcript — the fraction of normal mRNA still produced from a variant allele.（中文：变异等位基因仍产生的正常mRNA的比例。）
  • U1 snRNA — the small RNA that base-pairs with the 9-base donor motif to define the splice site.（中文：与9碱基供体序列配对、界定剪接位点的小RNA。）
  • HGVS — the standardized nomenclature for writing variants (e.g. c.194+2T>C).（中文：书写变异的标准化命名规范，如c.194+2T>C。）
  • CFTR2 / CFTR-France — expert-curated CFTR variant classification databases.（中文：专家审编的CFTR变异分类数据库。）
  • ClinVar — NCBI's public archive of variant interpretations and supporting evidence.（中文：NCBI收录变异解读及支持证据的公共数据库。）
  • VUS — variant of uncertain significance; the holding category in ACMG/AMP classification.（中文：意义未明变异，ACMG/AMP分类中的待定类别。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • SpliceAI Lookup (Broad) — web/API tool returning ΔDL and the other three delta scores (hg38, GENCODE, max distance 10,000 bp).
  • Residual-transcript-as-quantitative-readout — treat splicing outcome as a continuous dose (% wild-type transcript), not a binary call.
  • Dual-vector minigene design (pET01 + pSPL3) — using vector disagreement as a diagnostic of assay context-dependence, not just of variant effect.
  • Locus-wide enumeration — score every possible variant of one class in one gene (all 26 CFTR GT>GC donors) rather than only observed variants.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation.

Paste-ready MVP — CFTR splice-variant gray-zone triage sheet (S–M): input a list of CFTR splice variants in HGVS; join each variant's SpliceAI ΔDL (via SpliceAI Lookup API), its CFTR2 and CFTR-France classification, and any published residual-transcript/minigene evidence; output a ranked "needs RNA follow-up" queue flagging variants where a high or low ΔDL sits next to contradictory functional or database evidence. Scope to +2 and non-invariant positions only (exclude +1/−1/−2). Hard part is coordinate/API wiring, not logic.

What to verify first

  • Minigene instability: pET01 and pSPL3 disagreed — treat any single-vector result as provisional, not ground truth.
  • Clinical ascertainment bias: disease-associated variants skew toward low residual transcript because they were found in sick people; not a true population distribution.
  • No validated threshold: no residual-transcript cutoff separating pathogenic from benign exists yet — "80% = benign" is not an applicable rule.
  • Extrapolation scope: findings apply to non-canonical/+2 positions, not the invariant +1/−1/−2 sites; scope any tool accordingly.
  • Assay ≠ native tissue: minigene and full-length assays do not fully recapitulate splicing in disease-relevant tissue; RNA from affected individuals remains the most reliable readout when available.

ALEX Today we're reading a 2026 paper from HGG Advances called "When splicing is not all or none: GT>GC 5' splice-site variants as a model for intermediate effects and challenges in variant classification." 今天我们读的是2026年发表在《HGG Advances》上的一篇论文，题目是"当剪接不是全有或全无：GT>GC 5'剪接位点变异作为中间效应与变异分类难题的模型"。 It's by Jin-Huan Lin, Jian-Min Chen, Claude Férec and colleagues, a French–Chinese group that has spent years on splice-site biology. 作者是Jin-Huan Lin、Jian-Min Chen、Claude Férec等人，一个多年研究剪接位点生物学的中法团队。 This is a Track A episode, so it's aimed straight at variant interpretation. 这是一期Track A节目，所以它直接指向变异解读。 And the reason we're reading it is that it uses CFTR as its main worked example — all 26 possible GT>GC donor variants in the gene. 我们读它的原因是它把CFTR作为主要的实例——基因里全部26个可能的GT>GC供体变异。

JORDAN Before we go further, I need the vocabulary. 在继续之前，我需要先搞清楚词汇。 What is a 5' splice site, and what does "GT>GC" actually mean? 什么是5'剪接位点，"GT>GC"到底是什么意思？

ALEX A gene is read in pieces: exons are kept, introns are cut out, and the cutting is called splicing. 一个基因是分段读取的：外显子被保留，内含子被剪掉，这个剪切过程叫做剪接 (splicing)。 The 5' splice site, or donor site, is the boundary where an exon ends and an intron begins. 5'剪接位点，也就是供体位点 (donor site)，是外显子结束、内含子开始的边界。 Almost every intron starts with the two bases G and T, and that "GT" is so conserved it's called the invariant dinucleotide. 几乎每个内含子都以G和T这两个碱基开头，这个"GT"极其保守，被称为不变二核苷酸。 A GT>GC variant changes just the second of those two bases, T to C — in HGVS notation that's the "+2T>C" change. GT>GC变异只改变这两个碱基中的第二个，把T换成C——用HGVS（变异命名的标准化书写规范）写就是"+2T>C"。 The old rule of thumb was that touching the invariant GT abolishes splicing entirely. 过去的经验法则是：只要动了这个不变的GT，剪接就彻底失效。 This paper's whole point is that GT>GC is the exception. 这篇论文的全部要点就是：GT>GC是个例外。

JORDAN Why would swapping T for C be the exception when the other changes at that spot break everything? 为什么在这个位置上别的改变都会破坏一切，唯独T换成C是例外？

ALEX Because the spliceosome doesn't read that one dinucleotide alone — it base-pairs a 9-base motif at the splice site against the end of a small RNA called U1. 因为剪接体不是单看那一个二核苷酸——它是把剪接位点上一段9个碱基的序列与一个叫U1的小RNA末端进行碱基配对。 GC still forms a weaker but workable pairing, so if the surrounding motif is strong, some correct splicing survives. GC仍然能形成一个较弱但可用的配对，所以如果周围的序列足够强，一部分正确的剪接就能存活下来。 What survives is called residual wild-type transcript — some fraction of normal messenger RNA still gets made from the variant allele. 存活下来的部分叫残余野生型转录本 (residual wild-type transcript)——变异等位基因仍然产生一部分正常的信使RNA。 The authors' earlier work found that roughly 15% to 18% of GT>GC variants retain some of this residual transcript. 作者早前的研究发现，大约15%到18%的GT>GC变异保留了一部分这种残余转录本。 So instead of "broken" or "fine," you get a dial somewhere in the middle. 所以你得到的不是"坏了"或"正常"，而是一个停在中间某处的旋钮。

JORDAN And that middle is the "gray zone" in the title. 而那个中间地带就是标题里的"灰区"。 Why is the middle so hard for classification? 为什么中间地带对分类这么难？

ALEX Because our entire clinical framework is binary. 因为我们整个临床框架都是二元的。 The ACMG/AMP guidelines sort variants toward pathogenic or benign, with "uncertain significance," or VUS, as the holding pen. ACMG/AMP指南把变异归向致病或良性，"意义未明"，也就是VUS（意义未明变异），是那个待定的中转站。 A variant that reproducibly makes, say, 10% or 30% or 80% of normal transcript doesn't fit either bucket. 一个可重复地产生比如10%、30%或80%正常转录本的变异，两个桶都装不下。 It has a real, measurable, partial loss of function — too weak to call pathogenic, too strong to call benign. 它有一个真实、可测量的部分功能缺失 (partial loss-of-function)——弱到不能叫致病，强到不能叫良性。 The authors argue this is an under-recognized class of alleles, and they use residual transcript level as a quantitative readout to expose it. 作者认为这是一类被低估的等位基因，他们用残余转录本的水平作为定量读数来揭示它。

ALEX Let me set up what motivated the work before the methods. 在讲方法之前，我先说说这项工作的动机。 The move to genome-first sequencing means we now find splice variants faster than we can functionally test them. 转向基因组优先测序意味着我们发现剪接变异的速度，已经快过我们对它们做功能测试的速度。 So the field leans on in silico predictors, and for splicing the dominant tool is SpliceAI. 于是这个领域依赖计算机预测工具，而对剪接来说，主导工具是SpliceAI。 SpliceAI outputs a delta donor-loss score, abbreviated ΔDL — a number from 0 to 1 estimating how much the donor site is weakened. SpliceAI输出一个delta供体丢失分数，缩写为ΔDL（衡量供体位点被削弱程度的0到1的分值）。 The unspoken assumption is that a high ΔDL means the splice site is dead and a low ΔDL means it's fine. 一个没说出口的假设是：高ΔDL意味着剪接位点死了，低ΔDL意味着它没事。 This paper asks whether that number actually tracks the residual transcript we can measure in the lab. 这篇论文要问的是：这个数字是否真的与我们在实验室能测到的残余转录本相符。

JORDAN So what did they actually do? 那他们究竟做了什么？

ALEX Three layers. 三个层次。 First, they revisited disease-associated GT>GC variants across many genes that were already known to keep residual transcript — SPINK1 in pancreatitis, HBB in thalassemia, BRCA2 in cancer risk. 第一，他们重新审视了多个基因中已知保留残余转录本的、与疾病相关的GT>GC变异——胰腺炎里的SPINK1、地中海贫血里的HBB、癌症风险里的BRCA2。 Second, they took CFTR as a single-locus model and evaluated all 26 theoretically possible GT>GC substitutions in the gene. 第二，他们把CFTR作为单一位点模型，评估了基因里全部26个理论上可能的GT>GC替换。 Third, they picked four CFTR variants for wet-lab minigene assays and added one contested BAP1 variant. 第三，他们挑了四个CFTR变异做湿实验小基因 (minigene) 检测，还加了一个有争议的BAP1变异。 A minigene is a lab construct: you clone the exon and its flanking intron into a plasmid, put it in cells, and read out how it splices. 小基因是一种实验室构建体：你把外显子和它两侧的内含子克隆进一个质粒，放进细胞，然后读出它是怎么剪接的。

JORDAN How did they choose which four CFTR variants to test? 他们是怎么选出要测试的那四个CFTR变异的？

ALEX They used SpliceAI ΔDL as a triage filter. 他们用SpliceAI ΔDL作为分诊过滤器。 Of the 26 CFTR variants, only three had a low-ish score at or below 0.56 — c.2490+2T>C, c.3717+2T>C, and c.4242+2T>C — while every other one scored above 0.80. 26个CFTR变异中，只有三个分数偏低，在0.56或以下——c.2490+2T>C、c.3717+2T>C和c.4242+2T>C——其余每一个都在0.80以上。 They added c.3873+2T>C, which scores a very high 0.98 but had been reported to make residual transcript, as a deliberate stress test of the score. 他们又加了c.3873+2T>C，它分数高达0.98却曾被报告产生残余转录本，作为对该分数的一次刻意的压力测试。 Here's where I'd push on the design: they ran two different minigene vectors, pET01 and pSPL3, and treated agreement between them as the signal. 我要对设计提出质疑的地方在这里：他们用了两个不同的小基因载体，pET01和pSPL3，并把两者之间的一致性当作信号。 The trouble is both vectors put the exon into an artificial sequence context that isn't the native gene. 问题是这两个载体都把外显子放进了一个非天然基因的人工序列环境。 If they disagree, you can't tell whether the variant is ambiguous or whether the assay is the thing that's unstable. 如果它们不一致，你无法判断是变异本身模棱两可，还是检测系统本身不稳定。

JORDAN And did they disagree? 那它们不一致了吗？

ALEX They did, and that's the most honest result in the paper. 不一致了，而这是论文里最诚实的结果。 Take c.2490+2T>C: residual wild-type transcript showed up in the pET01 vector but not in pSPL3, matching its modest ΔDL of 0.35. 拿c.2490+2T>C来说：残余野生型转录本在pET01载体里出现了，但在pSPL3里没有，这与它0.35的中等ΔDL相符。 Now the two that break the score in opposite directions. 再看那两个从相反方向打破分数的例子。 c.3873+2T>C, with a near-maximal ΔDL of 0.98, made no residual transcript in either vector — the high score was right, and it's a known CF-causing variant. c.3873+2T>C，ΔDL接近满分0.98，在两个载体里都没有产生残余转录本——高分是对的，它是已知的CF致病变异。 But c.4242+2T>C, with a very low ΔDL of 0.15, also made no residual transcript in the minigenes — even though an earlier assay with more native context had reported that it does. 但c.4242+2T>C，ΔDL极低只有0.15，在小基因里同样没有产生残余转录本——尽管一个保留了更多天然序列环境的早期检测曾报告它会产生。 So a low score didn't guarantee residual transcript, and a different assay format flipped the answer. 所以低分并不保证有残余转录本，而换一种检测格式就把答案翻了过来。

JORDAN There was also an NF1 example in the paper — what did that one add? 论文里还有一个NF1的例子——那个又补充了什么？

ALEX NF1 is a nice contrast because they had two variants at the very same donor site. NF1是一个很好的对照，因为他们在同一个供体位点上有两个变异。 One was c.888+1G>A, hitting the invariant +1 position, and the other was c.888+2T>C, the GT>GC change. 一个是c.888+1G>A，击中不变的+1位置，另一个是c.888+2T>C，也就是GT>GC改变。 RNA sequencing showed the +1 variant produced clear aberrant transcripts, exactly as you'd expect for a broken donor. RNA测序显示+1变异产生了明显的异常转录本，正如你对一个坏掉的供体所预期的那样。 The +2T>C variant, by contrast, showed only wild-type transcript, with no aberrant splicing above a 2% threshold. 相比之下，+2T>C变异只显示野生型转录本，没有超过2%阈值的异常剪接。 And SpliceAI rated the +2 variant's donor-loss at only 0.18 versus 0.95 for the +1 variant — so here the low score and the biology actually agreed. 而SpliceAI给+2变异的供体丢失评分只有0.18，相比+1变异的0.95——所以在这里低分和生物学倒是一致的。 ClinVar, meanwhile, lists the +2 variant with conflicting classifications, one lab calling it pathogenic and another uncertain. 而与此同时，ClinVar把+2变异列为分类冲突，一个实验室称它致病，另一个称意义未明。 So even when the assay is clean, the databases haven't caught up. 所以即便检测是干净的，数据库也还没跟上。

JORDAN And the BAP1 variant they threw in — why include a fifth gene at all? 还有他们塞进来的那个BAP1变异——为什么要额外加第五个基因？

ALEX BAP1 c.783+2T>C had conflicting clinical interpretations, so it was a test case for whether their assays could resolve a real-world dispute. BAP1 c.783+2T>C有相互冲突的临床解读，所以它是一个测试案例，看他们的检测能否解决一个真实世界里的争议。 The honest answer is that it exposed the assay's limits instead. 诚实的答案是，它反而暴露了检测的局限。 Their full-length gene assay failed to give clean bands even for the wild-type construct, and in their earlier work only about a quarter of full-length constructs produced usable readouts. 他们的全长基因检测连野生型构建体都没能给出干净的条带，而在他们早前的工作里，只有约四分之一的全长构建体产生了可用的读数。 So the paper doesn't end with "we solved BAP1" — it ends with "the tools we have are shakier than the field pretends." 所以论文的结尾不是"我们解决了BAP1"——而是"我们手上的工具比这个领域假装的要不牢靠得多"。

JORDAN Let me make sure I have the cross-gene numbers too, because those felt like the punchline. 我也想确认一下跨基因的那些数字，因为它们感觉才是重点。

ALEX The cross-gene panel is where the quantitative dial is clearest. 跨基因的这组数据最能看清那个定量旋钮。 SPINK1 c.194+2T>C retains about 10% residual transcript, and its ΔDL is only 0.33. SPINK1 c.194+2T>C保留约10%的残余转录本，它的ΔDL只有0.33。 HBB c.315+2T>C retains around 30% and is a mild beta-plus thalassemia allele, currently listed in ClinVar as pathogenic. HBB c.315+2T>C保留约30%，是一个轻型的β+地中海贫血等位基因，目前在ClinVar（变异解读的公共数据库）里列为致病。 MGP c.94+2T>C is the striking one: about 80% residual transcript, yet SpliceAI predicts a high ΔDL of 0.97 and ClinVar calls it likely pathogenic. MGP c.94+2T>C是最惊人的：约80%残余转录本，然而SpliceAI预测出0.97的高ΔDL，ClinVar还称它可能致病。 Across the whole set there was no consistent quantitative relationship between the predicted score and the measured residual transcript. 在整组数据里，预测分数与实测残余转录本之间没有一致的定量关系。 That's the core finding — SpliceAI can tell you a site is weakened, but not how much normal message survives. 这就是核心发现——SpliceAI能告诉你一个位点被削弱了，但说不出还有多少正常信息存活下来。

JORDAN So where does this land for our actual priorities? 那这对我们真正的优先事项落在哪里？

ALEX The clinical stakes here are concrete, not abstract. 这里的临床利害是具体的，不是抽象的。 A variant misfiled as benign gets a patient told they're not a carrier when they carry a partial-function allele. 一个被错误归档为良性的变异，会让一个携带部分功能等位基因的患者被告知自己不是携带者。 A variant overcalled as pathogenic can steer a couple's reproductive decisions on evidence that a 30%-transcript allele doesn't support. 一个被过度判定为致病的变异，会在一个30%转录本等位基因根本支撑不了的证据上，左右一对夫妇的生育决定。 The gray zone isn't a curiosity — it's where the interpretation actually changes what happens to someone. 灰区不是一个奇观——它是解读真正改变一个人命运的地方。
