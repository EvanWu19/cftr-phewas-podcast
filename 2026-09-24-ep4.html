Plain-English glossary

Benchmarking / trial-replication study — An observational study designed to reproduce a completed randomized trial as closely as possible, using the trial's result as the reference standard against which the observational estimate is scored.（中文：以已完成的随机对照试验结果为基准,尽可能复现其设计的观察性研究,并据此评分观察性估计。）

Efficacy-effectiveness gap — The difference between how well a treatment performs under trial conditions (efficacy) and how it performs in ordinary clinical practice (effectiveness).（中文：治疗在试验理想条件下的疗效（efficacy）与在真实临床实践中的效果（effectiveness）之间的差距。）

Estimate agreement — A benchmarking metric asking whether the observational study's point estimate falls within the randomized trial's 95% confidence interval.（中文：一项基准评估指标,判断观察性研究的点估计是否落在随机对照试验95%置信区间内。）

New-user active-comparator design — A study design that restricts the cohort to people newly initiating one of two (or more) active treatments, comparing them from a common time zero, rather than comparing new users to nonusers.（中文：仅纳入新开始使用某一种（或多种）在用治疗药物的人群,以共同的起点时间进行比较的设计,而非将新使用者与未使用者比较。）

Regulatory agreement — A benchmarking metric asking whether the observational study replicates the randomized trial's direction of effect and statistical significance.（中文：一项基准评估指标,判断观察性研究是否复现了随机对照试验的效应方向与统计学显著性。）

Standardized difference (benchmarking sense) — A metric quantifying the magnitude and direction of the gap between an observational study's effect estimate and the corresponding randomized trial estimate.（中文：用于量化观察性研究效应估计与对应随机对照试验估计之间差距大小与方向的指标。）

Strong ignorability — The propensity-score assumption that, conditional on measured covariates, there is no unmeasured confounding driving treatment assignment.（中文：倾向评分方法的假设,即在已测量协变量条件下,不存在驱动治疗分配的未测量混杂因素。）

Stable unit treatment value assumption (SUTVA) — The assumption that one person's treatment does not affect another person's outcome, and that a given treatment has the same meaning regardless of how a person came to receive it.（中文：稳定个体处理值假设,即个体接受的治疗不影响他人的结局,且同一治疗对不同获得途径的个体含义相同。）
