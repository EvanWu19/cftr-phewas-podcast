Track A — Distinct CFTR Mutation Spectrum in Chinese Patients with CF

Blurb. This 2026 review (Wang et al., Int. J. Mol. Sci.) maps how the CFTR variant spectrum in Chinese patients diverges sharply from the European-ancestry model that our prediction tools, databases, and modulator drugs were built on. p.Phe508del — two thirds of Western CF alleles — is a minor player; instead, region-specific variants (p.Gly970Asp, p.Arg553Ter, and the southern-Han founder variant p.Ile1023Arg), splicing variants, and complex rearrangements predominate, and many patients present atypically (CBAVD, pseudo-Bartter syndrome) rather than with classic respiratory disease. The authors argue China's low reported CF incidence (~1/128,000) reflects underrecognition, not true rarity, driven by diagnostic pathways tuned to the wrong variants and phenotypes. Where it lands: Karen / variant interpretation — the transferable point is that a variant-classification pipeline is only as trustworthy as its population coverage. Buildable artifact: a population-stratified CFTR variant annotation + theratyping-gap tracker that normalizes China-enriched variants to HGVS and joins CFTR2/ClinVar/gnomAD to surface high-frequency-but-low-evidence variants as an annotation-gap worklist.

Plain-English glossary

  • CBAVD (congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens) — a cause of male infertility that can be a single-organ CFTR-related presentation without classic lung disease.（中文：先天性双侧输精管缺如，一种可作为单器官 CFTR 相关表现的男性不育原因，可不伴典型肺病。）
  • Founder mutation — a variant common in a population because it descends from a shared ancestor, e.g. p.Ile1023Arg in southern Han Chinese.（中文：奠基者突变，因源自共同祖先而在某人群中常见，如中国南方汉族的 p.Ile1023Arg。）
  • HGVS — the standardized rules for writing variant names, e.g. p.Gly970Asp = position 970, glycine→aspartate.（中文：HGVS 是变异命名的标准化书写规范，如 p.Gly970Asp 表示第 970 位甘氨酸变为天冬氨酸。）
  • Missense variant — one amino acid substituted for another (e.g. p.Gly970Asp).（中文：错义变异，一个氨基酸被另一个替换。）
  • Nonsense variant — introduces a premature stop codon ("Ter"), usually no full-length protein (e.g. p.Arg553Ter, CFTR class I).（中文：无义变异，引入提前终止密码子，通常无全长蛋白，如 p.Arg553Ter，CFTR 一类。）
  • Pseudo-Bartter syndrome — a salt-wasting presentation mimicking a renal tubular disorder that can be a CFTR-related phenotype.（中文：假性 Bartter 综合征，一种类似肾小管疾病的失盐表现，可为 CFTR 相关表型。）
  • Splice variant — disrupts the signals directing where RNA is cut and joined; common and functionally important in the Chinese spectrum.（中文：剪接变异，破坏 RNA 剪切拼接位点的信号；在中国的变异谱中常见且功能重要。）
  • Theratyping — matching a variant to a predicted therapeutic strategy based on its functional class rather than its name.（中文：theratyping，按变异的功能类别（而非名称）来匹配预测的治疗策略。）
  • CFTR mutation classes (I–VI/VII) — a framework grouping variants by what breaks in the protein's life cycle (I = no protein; II = misfolding/ER retention, e.g. p.Phe508del; III = defective gating).（中文：CFTR 突变分类（I–VI/VII），按蛋白质生命周期中出问题的环节给变异分组（I 类无蛋白；II 类错折叠/内质网滞留，如 p.Phe508del；III 类门控缺陷）。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • CFTR class framework (I–VI/VII) — maps a variant's molecular defect to a predicted modulator strategy (theratyping); the backbone for genotype→therapy reasoning.
  • Population-stratified allele-frequency framing — treat allele frequency as a per-population quantity, not a global constant.
  • HGVS normalization — canonicalize legacy CF variant names to the current CFTR reference transcript before any database join.
  • Public variant databases — CFTR2 (CF-specific curated classifications), ClinVar (clinical assertions), gnomAD (population allele frequencies).

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读paper时间。 Today's Track A paper is a 2026 review in the International Journal of Molecular Sciences, first author Wang Z and colleagues, titled "Distinct CFTR Mutation Spectrum and Atypical Clinical Presentations in Chinese Patients with Cystic Fibrosis." 今天 Track A 的文章是一篇 2026 年发表在《International Journal of Molecular Sciences》上的综述，第一作者是 Wang Z 等人，题目是《中国囊性纤维化患者中独特的 CFTR 突变谱与非典型临床表现》。 We're reading it because it is the cleanest recent map of how a population's variant spectrum can diverge from the one all our prediction tools and drugs were built on. 我们读它，是因为它是近期最清晰的一份地图，展示了一个人群的变异谱可以如何偏离我们所有预测工具和药物赖以建立的那个变异谱。 It sits squarely in the variant-interpretation track and points straight at the Karen collaboration. 它正好落在变异解读这条线上，并且直接指向与 Karen 的合作。

JORDAN Before we go further, give me the vocabulary. 在往下讲之前，先给我把词汇过一遍。 What does a name like p.Gly970Asp actually encode? 像 p.Gly970Asp 这样的名字到底编码了什么信息？

ALEX That's an HGVS protein-level variant name. 那是一个 HGVS（变异命名的标准化书写规范）在蛋白质层面的写法。 It reads "at protein position 970, the amino acid glycine is replaced by aspartate." 它读作"在蛋白质第 970 位，甘氨酸被天冬氨酸取代"。 That is a missense variant — one amino acid swapped for another. 那是一个错义变异 (missense variant) —— 一个氨基酸被换成了另一个。 Contrast that with p.Arg553Ter, where "Ter" means a premature stop codon. 与之对照的是 p.Arg553Ter，其中 "Ter" 表示一个提前出现的终止密码子。 That is a nonsense variant, and it usually means no full-length protein gets made. 那是一个无义变异 (nonsense variant)，通常意味着无法产生全长的蛋白质。 A third big category is splice variants, which disrupt the signals that tell the cell where to cut and join the RNA transcript. 第三个大类是剪接变异 (splice variant)，它破坏了那些告诉细胞在哪里剪切、拼接 RNA 转录本的信号。 CF genetics also uses a class system, Classes one through six or seven, that groups variants by what breaks in the protein's life cycle. CF 遗传学还用一套分类系统，从一类到六类或七类，按蛋白质生命周期中"哪一环出问题"来给变异分组。 Class one is no protein, class two is misfolding and getting stuck in the endoplasmic reticulum — that's where p.Phe508del lives — and class three is a channel that reaches the surface but won't gate open. 一类是不产生蛋白，二类是错误折叠并滞留在内质网 —— p.Phe508del 就属于这一类 —— 三类是通道能到达细胞表面却无法正常开门。 Two clinical terms you'll hear: CBAVD, congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens, a cause of male infertility; and pseudo-Bartter syndrome, a salt-wasting picture that mimics a kidney disorder. 你会听到两个临床术语：CBAVD（先天性双侧输精管缺如），一种男性不育的原因；以及假性 Bartter 综合征，一种模仿肾脏疾病的失盐表现。

JORDAN So the whole paper hangs on the idea that "the variant spectrum" is not one fixed thing worldwide. 所以整篇文章都建立在这样一个观点上：所谓"变异谱"在全球并不是一个固定不变的东西。 What was the state of belief before this review? 在这篇综述之前，大家原本的认知是什么？

ALEX The default mental model, built on European-ancestry cohorts, is that CF equals p.Phe508del. 建立在欧洲血统队列上的默认心智模型是：CF 约等于 p.Phe508del。 In those populations, that one variant accounts for roughly two thirds of CF alleles, and the newborn-screening and modulator ecosystem was optimized around it. 在那些人群里，仅这一个变异就占了约三分之二的 CF 等位基因，而新生儿筛查和调节剂 (modulator) 的整个生态系统都是围绕它优化的。 The review argues that in China this model quietly breaks. 这篇综述认为，在中国，这个模型悄悄地失效了。 Reported CF incidence in China is about one in 128,000, versus one in 2,500 to 3,500 in European-ancestry populations. 中国报告的 CF 发病率约为十二万八千分之一，而欧洲血统人群约为二千五百到三千五百分之一。 But the authors' central claim is that this low number is not true rarity — it is substantial underrecognition. 但作者的核心论点是：这个低数字并不代表真正的罕见 —— 而是大量的漏诊与识别不足。 The reasons are diagnostic pathways tuned to the wrong phenotype and the wrong variants. 原因在于：诊断路径是按错误的表型、错误的变异来调校的。 Chinese CF patients show an extremely low frequency of p.Phe508del, and instead carry region-specific variants like p.Gly970Asp, p.Ile1023Arg, and p.Arg553Ter, plus a high proportion of splicing variants and complex rearrangements. 中国 CF 患者中 p.Phe508del 的频率极低，取而代之的是区域特异性变异，如 p.Gly970Asp、p.Ile1023Arg 和 p.Arg553Ter，还有很高比例的剪接变异和复杂重排。 p.Ile1023Arg is described as a founder mutation among Han Chinese in southern China. p.Ile1023Arg 被描述为中国南方汉族人群中的一个奠基者突变 (founder mutation)。 And clinically, many patients present not with the classic respiratory-dominant picture but with CBAVD or pseudo-Bartter syndrome — CF-like phenotypes inside the broader CFTR-related-disorder spectrum. 在临床上，许多患者并非以典型的呼吸系统为主的表现就诊，而是表现为 CBAVD 或假性 Bartter 综合征 —— 属于更广义的 CFTR 相关疾病谱内的"类 CF"表型。

JORDAN Hold on the class framework for a second, because it seems to be doing a lot of work. 在分类框架上再停一下，因为它似乎承担了很多分量。 Why does grouping variants into classes matter more than just knowing the name? 为什么把变异归入分类，比仅仅知道名字更重要？

ALEX Because the class is what predicts whether a drug can help. 因为分类才是预测"某种药物能否起效"的东西。 The modulator drugs come in two mechanistic flavors: correctors, which help a misfolded protein reach the cell surface, and potentiators, which help a surface channel actually open. 调节剂药物有两种机制类型：矫正剂 (corrector)，帮助错误折叠的蛋白到达细胞表面；以及增效剂 (potentiator)，帮助已在表面的通道真正打开。 A class two variant like p.Phe508del is a folding problem, so it needs a corrector, usually plus a potentiator. 像 p.Phe508del 这样的二类变异是折叠问题，所以它需要一个矫正剂，通常再加一个增效剂。 A class three variant reaches the surface but won't gate, so a potentiator alone can rescue it. 三类变异能到达表面却无法门控，所以单用增效剂就能挽救它。 But a class one nonsense variant like p.Arg553Ter makes essentially no protein at all, so there's nothing for a corrector or potentiator to bind. 但像 p.Arg553Ter 这样的一类无义变异几乎完全不产生蛋白，所以矫正剂或增效剂无处可结合。 That's the whole logic of theratyping: the molecular defect class, not the variant's identity per se, tells you the therapeutic strategy. 这就是 theratyping 的全部逻辑：分子缺陷的类别，而非变异本身的身份，告诉你治疗策略。 And it's why a spectrum dominated by class one and splicing variants is a genuinely different therapeutic problem, not just a different list of names. 这也是为什么一个由一类变异和剪接变异主导的谱系，是一个真正不同的治疗问题，而不仅仅是一份不同的名字清单。

JORDAN And the low incidence number — one in 128,000 — how confident should we be in that? 那个低发病率数字 —— 十二万八千分之一 —— 我们该有多大把握？

ALEX Not very, and the authors are the ones telling us so. 不太有把握，而且是作者自己在告诉我们这一点。 That figure comes from clinical reporting in a system where CF was long considered a Western disease, so testing wasn't reflexively ordered for the atypical presentations. 那个数字来自一个长期把 CF 视为西方疾病的系统里的临床报告，所以对那些非典型表现并不会条件反射式地开检测。 If your diagnostic pathway only recognizes classic respiratory CF, then the CBAVD-only and pseudo-Bartter-only patients never get counted, and the incidence looks artificially low. 如果你的诊断路径只识别典型的呼吸系统 CF，那么那些只有 CBAVD、只有假性 Bartter 的患者就永远不会被计入，发病率看起来就被人为地压低了。 The authors invoke population genetics to explain the spectrum differences — drift, migration history, and selection shaping which variants became common where. 作者援引群体遗传学来解释谱系差异 —— 遗传漂变、迁徙历史和选择塑造了哪些变异在哪里变得常见。 That's a mechanistic story for why p.Ile1023Arg can be a southern-Han founder variant while being essentially invisible in European cohorts. 这为"为什么 p.Ile1023Arg 可以是南方汉族的奠基者变异、却在欧洲队列中几乎不可见"提供了一个机制层面的解释。

JORDAN This is a review, not a new cohort. 这是一篇综述，不是一个新队列。 So what exactly did they do — how do you evaluate the method when the method is synthesis? 那他们究竟做了什么 —— 当方法本身就是"综合梳理"时，你怎么评价这个方法？

ALEX Right, so the "method" here is a structured literature synthesis of Chinese CF research spanning case reports, pedigree studies, multicenter cohorts, and functional work. 对，所以这里的"方法"是对中国 CF 研究的一次结构化文献综合，涵盖病例报告、家系研究、多中心队列和功能学工作。 They organize the evidence along three axes: the mutation spectrum, the atypical phenotype spectrum, and the functional-mechanism work that assigns variants to classes. 他们沿三条主线组织证据：突变谱、非典型表型谱，以及把变异归入分类的功能机制研究。 The class framework I mentioned is their backbone for connecting genotype to a predicted therapeutic strategy — what people call theratyping. 我前面提到的分类框架是他们把基因型与预测的治疗策略连接起来的骨架 —— 也就是人们说的 theratyping（按变异功能类型来匹配药物的方法）。 Here's where I'd push back on the design. 这里我想对这个设计提出一点质疑。 A narrative review that pools single-center case series inherits every selection bias of those sources. 一篇把单中心病例系列汇总起来的叙述性综述，会继承这些来源的每一种选择偏倚。 The authors are honest about this: cohorts concentrate in tertiary pediatric centers, which overrepresents severe disease and undercaptures the mild, single-system CFTR-related disorders. 作者对此很坦诚：队列集中在三级儿科中心，这会高估重症、而漏掉那些轻症的、单系统的 CFTR 相关疾病。 So the reported allele frequencies are best read as "what shows up in tertiary care," not clean population frequencies. 因此报告的等位基因频率最好被理解为"在三级医疗中出现的东西"，而不是干净的人群频率。 That caveat matters enormously for anyone trying to reuse these numbers as priors. 对于任何想把这些数字当作先验来复用的人来说，这个警告极其重要。

JORDAN Fair. 有道理。 What are the concrete findings a reader should walk away with? 读者应该带走哪些具体的发现？

ALEX First, the spectrum inversion is real and named. 第一，这种谱系的"翻转"是真实存在的，而且有具体名字。 p.Phe508del, the Western workhorse, is a minor player; p.Gly970Asp, p.Arg553Ter, and p.Ile1023Arg recur across Chinese pedigrees. 西方的"主力"p.Phe508del 在这里只是配角；p.Gly970Asp、p.Arg553Ter 和 p.Ile1023Arg 反复出现在中国的家系里。 Second, the class assignment carries a direct drug consequence. 第二，分类归属直接关系到用药。 p.Arg553Ter is class one, a nonsense variant that makes no protein, so the standard corrector–potentiator modulators that rescue p.Phe508del folding have little to grab onto. p.Arg553Ter 属于一类，是一个不产生蛋白的无义变异，所以那些能挽救 p.Phe508del 折叠的标准"矫正剂—增效剂"调节剂几乎没有着力点。 That's a mechanistic reason a China-enriched genotype could be modulator-unresponsive even where the drug exists. 这就是一个机制层面的原因：即便药物存在，某些在中国富集的基因型也可能对调节剂无反应。 Third, the phenotype story reframes who should even be tested. 第三，表型的这条线重新界定了"谁该做检测"。 The authors argue that unexplained bronchiectasis with malnutrition or steatorrhea, idiopathic pseudo-Bartter syndrome, or CBAVD-related male infertility should all trigger CFTR testing. 作者主张：不明原因的支气管扩张合并营养不良或脂肪泻、特发性假性 Bartter 综合征、或与 CBAVD 相关的男性不育，都应触发 CFTR 检测。 The honest limitation they flag is that functional validation is uneven — splicing, protein expression, and channel function have been measured for only selected variants, and many are still classified by inference rather than by assay. 他们坦诚指出的局限是：功能验证参差不齐 —— 剪接、蛋白表达和通道功能只对部分变异做过测量，许多变异仍然是靠推断而非实测来分类的。 So the catalog they present is real but partly provisional. 因此他们呈现的这份目录是真实的，但部分是暂定的。

JORDAN You keep coming back to functional validation. 你一直绕回到功能验证上。 Why is that the bottleneck rather than just running more predictors? 为什么那是瓶颈，而不是干脆多跑几个预测器？

ALEX Because predictors are calibrated against known answers, and for these variants the known answers are thin. 因为预测器是针对已知答案来校准的，而对这些变异来说，已知答案很稀少。 A tool like a missense predictor learns from labeled pathogenic and benign variants, most of which are European-ancestry alleles in public databases. 一个像错义预测器这样的工具是从有标签的致病和良性变异中学习的，而其中大多数是公开数据库里的欧洲血统等位基因。 When a China-enriched variant has never been through a functional assay — a splicing minigene, a protein-expression western, or a channel-conductance measurement — it sits in an evidence limbo. 当一个在中国富集的变异从未经过功能实验 —— 一个剪接微基因、一个蛋白表达的免疫印迹、或一次通道电导测量 —— 它就悬在证据的空白地带。 The review is explicit that many Chinese variants are classified by inference from computational tools rather than by direct assay, which means the classification can be circular: a predictor trained on one population is used to label a variant from another. 这篇综述明确指出，许多中国变异是靠计算工具推断、而非直接实验来分类的，这意味着分类可能是循环的：一个在某人群上训练的预测器，被用来给另一人群的变异贴标签。 So the bottleneck isn't compute — it's ground-truth functional data for the variants that matter locally. 所以瓶颈不是算力 —— 而是那些在本地重要的变异所缺的功能学真值数据。 That's exactly the gap a harmonization layer can make visible, even if it can't fill it. 这恰恰是一个协调层能让其变得可见的空白，即便它无法填补这个空白。

JORDAN Now the part I care about — where does this land for the work, and what could Runyu actually build? 现在到了我关心的部分 —— 这对我们的工作意味着什么，Runyu 实际上能造出什么？
