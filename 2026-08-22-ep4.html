Ep4 — Track C — Registry Data Quality (2/2)

Blurb

Ng, Patel & Cox (2023, Annals of Epidemiology) propose conditional studentized residuals (CSRs) from linear mixed-effects models as an individual-level longitudinal outlier-detection layer, applied to glomerular filtration rate (GFR) data from the Chronic Kidney Disease in Children (CKiD) cohort — 1,096 participants, 6,881 annual observations, 56 pediatric nephrology programs across the US/Canada. Two mixed models (an "enriched" pre-transplant model with a quadratic time term and a urine-protein-ratio risk factor, and a simpler post-transplant linear model) generate conditional residuals — incorporating both population fixed effects and each patient's own predicted random-effect trajectory — flagged beyond ±5 SDs. Flag rates were low (0.5-0.7%) but far exceeded what normality alone predicts; of 38 flagged records sent to clinical coordinating centers for human review, 8 (~21%) were confirmed errors and corrected, plus 2 records with wrong transplant dates caught incidentally. The paper explicitly names its own key gap: no randomly-sampled unflagged stratum was reviewed alongside the flagged ones, so precision is reported but not sensitivity/specificity — the authors propose this as future work. This is the closest structural analogue this track has found yet to the Liz-assigned CFFPR height/weight adjudication task: (a) candidate generator (the CSR method itself, portable to a height/weight stratified mixed-model design) and (d) evaluation (the named random-unflagged-sample gap is a direct methodological lesson for any CFFPR evaluation design).

Plain-English glossary

  • Conditional residual — the difference between an observed value and a prediction that incorporates both population-level fixed effects and an individual's own predicted random-effect trajectory (vs. a marginal residual, which only uses the population average).（中文：条件残差指观测值与一个同时纳入总体固定效应和个体自身随机效应预测轨迹的预测值之间的差值；相对地，边际残差仅使用总体平均预测值。）
  • eGFR (estimated glomerular filtration rate) — the standard measure of kidney filtration function, estimated from serum creatinine using age-appropriate validated equations.（中文：估算肾小球滤过率，是衡量肾脏滤过功能的标准指标，通过血清肌酐并使用适龄的经验证公式估算得出。）
  • Empirical Bayes prediction — how a mixed model estimates each individual's specific random-effect values, shrunk toward the population average based on how much data that individual contributed.（中文：混合模型估计每个个体特有随机效应值的方式，根据该个体贡献的数据量，向总体平均值方向"收缩"。）
  • Studentized residual — a residual standardized by its estimated standard deviation, expressed in SD units so extreme values are comparable across different models/scales.（中文：用其估计标准差进行标准化后的残差，以标准差为单位表示，便于在不同模型或量表之间比较极端值。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • Conditional studentized residuals (CSR) — observed-minus-predicted from full conditional prediction, standardized by estimated SD; computable via PROC MIXED (SAS) or the redres package (R).
  • Stratified linear mixed-effects models — enriched (quadratic time + risk-factor interaction) vs. simple (linear time) model specifications for different data-richness regimes.
  • Human-adjudication loop — flagged records routed to clinical coordinating centers with original-form/lab-output review and written findings per record.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: primarily (a) candidate generation, also (d) evaluation. Buildable artifact: csr-vs-marginal-flag-comparator — generate synthetic longitudinal height/weight data with a mixed-model trajectory plus injected errors of known types, fit CSR alongside a simpler marginal-residual/fixed-threshold approach, report precision across threshold choices plus a recall-estimation design sketch (what a randomly-sampled unflagged stratum would need to look like). Difficulty: M (needs a credible synthetic-error generator plus modeling code).

What to verify first

  • Whether height/weight in a registry setting have the same progressive, roughly-monotonic trajectory shape that makes conditional residuals well-behaved for GFR — growth curves have their own nonlinear shape with spurts, and modulator-era weight changes complicate "expected trajectory."
  • What threshold (4, 5, 6 SDs, or stratum-specific) would be appropriate outside the CKD context — this paper's ±5 was an explicit judgment call tied to GFR's known heterogeneity, not a universal constant.
  • What a randomly-sampled unflagged review stratum would cost in reviewer time before proposing one.
  • Nothing here claims anything about what CFFPR's current height/weight cleaning rules do or don't already catch — separate, unverified question.

Track C fields

Error types covered/not covered: The paper doesn't classify flagged errors by type in detail — it reports 8/38 confirmed as corrected (unspecified error type) plus 2 transplant-date errors caught incidentally (a different-field error, not the modeled outcome itself). Not explicitly assessed: unit conversion, digit transposition, decimal shift, or carry-forward errors as distinct categories; the method is agnostic to error mechanism and only flags magnitude-of-deviation from individual trajectory, so it would need external validation for which specific error types it best captures.

matters a lot for a registry setting: marginal residuals versus conditional residuals. 对于纵向重复测量数据而言，用于检验模型假设的标准工具——残差分析，即比较观测值与预测值——其实还有一个未被充分利用的第二种用途，即作为错误检测工具，而作者们专门针对混合模型对这一工具做了扩展，并提出了一个在注册系统场景中格外重要的区分：边际残差（marginal residuals）与条件残差（conditional residuals）。 A marginal residual compares
