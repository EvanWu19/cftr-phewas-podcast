How did this episode land for you?

Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): A second opinion for the variants AlphaMissense can't call

Gereben and colleagues (2026, Bioinformatics Advances) build pLM-SAV, a lightweight convolutional classifier trained on the difference ("Δ-embedding") between wild-type and mutant protein-language-model embeddings over a seven-residue window. Benchmarked on Eff10k folds, independent ClinVar test sets, the PMD4k benchmark, and ProteinGym, pLM-SAV never beats AlphaMissense or REVEL on broad benchmarks — but on the specific subset of variants where AlphaMissense or REVEL's own scores land in their self-declared "ambiguous" band, pLM-SAV significantly outperforms whichever tool was unsure. The transfer to Karen's CFTR variant-interpretation work is narrow and concrete: a cheap, locally runnable tie-breaker for exactly the missense VUS where the leading tools admit they don't know. Buildable artifact: a CFTR Ambiguous-VUS Second-Opinion Scorer.

Plain-English glossary

  • ClinVar — the public NIH archive of variant-to-phenotype claims, where each entry carries a star rating (0–4) reflecting how much independent clinical evidence backs its classification.（中文：美国国立卫生研究院建立的公共数据库，收录变异与表型的关联声称，每条记录带有0至4星的评分，反映支持该分类的独立临床证据量。）
  • Δ-embedding (delta-embedding) — the per-residue difference between a wild-type protein's embedding and its mutant counterpart's embedding, used here as the input signal for classification instead of either embedding alone.（中文：野生型蛋白质嵌入与突变型蛋白质嵌入在每个残基位置上的差值，本文用它（而非单独使用任一嵌入）作为分类模型的输入信号。）
  • Embedding — the numerical vector a protein language model assigns to each amino acid position, capturing its sequence context; 1024 numbers per position for ProtT5, 1536 for Ankh.（中文：蛋白质语言模型为每个氨基酸位置分配的数值向量，用以捕捉其序列上下文；ProtT5为每个位置生成1024个数字，Ankh则生成1536个。）
  • Protein language model (pLM) — a neural network trained on millions of unlabeled protein sequences to learn contextual embeddings, without ever being told what any protein does.（中文：在数百万未标注蛋白质序列上训练的神经网络，用以学习上下文相关的嵌入，训练过程中从未被告知任何蛋白质的具体功能。）
  • PSI-BLAST — a classic bioinformatics sequence-alignment search tool, used here to cluster proteins by similarity so homologous sequences never span both training and test folds.（中文：一种经典的生物信息学序列比对检索工具，本文用它按相似性对蛋白质聚类，确保同源序列不会同时出现在训练折和测试折中。）
  • REVEL (Rare Exome Variant Ensemble Learner) — an ensemble predictor combining multiple earlier tools into one pathogenicity score, widely adopted in clinical genomics pipelines.（中文：一种集成预测工具，将多个早期工具组合成单一致病性评分，在临床基因组学流程中被广泛采用。）
  • Single amino acid variant (SAV) — one amino acid in a protein sequence swapped for another due to a missense mutation; whether the swap breaks protein function is the pathogenicity question.（中文：由于错义突变导致蛋白质序列中一个氨基酸被替换为另一个氨基酸；该替换是否破坏蛋白质功能，正是致病性问题的核心。）
  • Variant of uncertain significance (VUS) — the clinical label for a variant that current evidence can't confidently call either benign or pathogenic.（中文：对于现有证据既不能确信判断为良性、也不能确信判断为致病性的变异所使用的临床标签。）

Full citation

Gereben AB, et al. "pLM-SAV: a Δ-embedding approach for predicting pathogenic single amino acid variants." Bioinformatics Advances. 2026. PMID: 42755479 | PMC13581460 | DOI: 10.1093/bioadv/vbag195

Methods / tools to lift

  • pLM-SAV (code + trained CNN weights on Zenodo) — lightweight CNN (CNN1c) trained on 7-residue-window Δ-embeddings from ProtT5 or Ankh, no handcrafted features.
  • ProtT5 / Ankh — the two public, pretrained protein language models used as embedding sources; no CFTR-specific retraining needed to reuse them.
  • Eff10k / PMD4k — the Rost-lab labeled SAV-effect datasets (neutral vs. effect, not benign vs. pathogenic) used for training and cross-method benchmarking.
  • Per-fold MCC-threshold sweep — instead of a fixed 0.5 cutoff, each of the 90 (method A) / 72 (method B) fold-models gets its own threshold chosen by maximizing MCC on validation data.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / CFTR variant interpretation, specifically as a tie-breaker for missense VUS where AlphaMissense or REVEL's own score already lands in that tool's self-declared ambiguous band. MVP: generate wild-type/mutant embeddings for a CFTR VUS list, compute the Δ-embedding, run the released CNN, and flag a second-opinion score only for AlphaMissense/REVEL-ambiguous variants.

What to verify first

  • Whether Eff10k's ~9,600 training proteins included CFTR or anything in its structural family — if not, local reliability on CFTR is undemonstrated, not just uncertain.
  • Whether the Zenodo release ships usable pretrained weights or only training code requiring the original folds to reproduce.
  • Whether AlphaMissense's and REVEL's published ambiguous-score thresholds, tuned on broad ClinVar populations, are appropriate to apply gene-by-gene to CFTR specifically.
  • That any pLM-SAV score is read as weak supporting evidence at most, given the neutral/effect-vs-pathogenic label mismatch between its training data and the clinical question being asked.

ALEX Today's paper is from Bioinformatics Advances, 2026 — Gereben and colleagues, "pLM-SAV: a Δ-embedding approach for predicting pathogenic single amino acid variants." 今天要聊的论文发表在2026年的《Bioinformatics Advances》上——作者是Gereben等人，题目是《pLM-SAV：一种用于预测致病性单氨基酸变异的Δ嵌入方法》。 This is a Track A pick, landing straight in variant interpretation, which is Karen's lane. 这是Track A的选题，直接落在变异解读这条线上，也就是Karen的方向。 The pitch is narrow but sharp: AlphaMissense and REVEL dominate missense pathogenicity prediction, but AlphaMissense is hard to run locally and both tools still produce a large pile of ambiguous, un-actionable scores, so the authors built a lightweight model that tries to be useful specifically where those two leaders are not confident. AlphaMissense和REVEL是错义变异致病性预测领域的两个主导工具，但AlphaMissense很难在本地部署运行，而且这两个工具都会产生大量模糊不清、无法直接采纳的评分；于是作者构建了一个轻量级模型，专门针对这两个领先工具信心不足的情况提供帮助。

JORDAN Let's get the vocabulary straight first. 我们先把术语理清楚。 A single amino acid variant, or SAV (单氨基酸变异), is exactly what it sounds like — one amino acid in a protein sequence is swapped for another because of a missense mutation in the underlying gene; whether that swap breaks the protein's function is the pathogenicity question. 单氨基酸变异（single amino acid variant，缩写SAV）顾名思义，就是蛋白质序列中的一个氨基酸因为基因中的错义突变而被替换成另一个氨基酸；这一替换是否会破坏蛋白质的功能，就是致病性问题的核心。 A protein language model, or pLM (蛋白质语言模型), is a neural network trained on millions of unlabeled protein sequences, the same way a text language model learns from unlabeled sentences, so that it learns a numerical representation — an embedding — capturing each amino acid's context within the sequence without ever being told what any protein does. 蛋白质语言模型（protein language model，缩写pLM）是一种在数百万个未标注蛋白质序列上训练出来的神经网络，训练方式与文本语言模型从未标注句子中学习的方式类似，从而学会一种数值化的表示方式——也就是嵌入（embedding）——用来捕捉序列中每个氨基酸所处的上下文，而完全不需要被告知任何蛋白质的具体功能。 An embedding (嵌入) is simply that numerical vector: for ProtT5, one of the two pLMs used here, it's 1024 numbers per amino acid position; for Ankh, the other, it's 1536.嵌入（embedding）说白了就是这样一个数值向量：本文用到的两个蛋白质语言模型中，ProtT5为每个氨基酸位置生成1024个数字，另一个模型Ankh则生成1536个数字。 The paper's central trick is the Δ-embedding (Δ嵌入，差值嵌入): take the embedding of the wild-type, unmutated sequence, subtract the embedding of the mutant sequence at the same positions, and use that difference vector — rather than either embedding alone — as the input signal for classification. 这篇论文的核心技巧是Δ嵌入（Δ-embedding，差值嵌入）：取野生型（未突变）序列在某些位置的嵌入，减去突变序列在相同位置的嵌入，用这个差值向量——而不是单独使用任一嵌入——作为分类模型的输入信号。

ALEX One more term worth planting before we go further: ClinVar (ClinVar数据库) is the public NIH archive of variant-to-phenotype claims, where each entry carries a "star rating" from zero to four reflecting how much independent clinical evidence backs the classification — a one-star-or-better entry is the baseline bar most benchmarking studies use, this one included. 再补充一个术语：ClinVar是美国国立卫生研究院建立的公共数据库，收录变异与表型之间的关联声称，每条记录都带有从零星到四星的星级评分，反映该分类背后有多少独立临床证据支持——一星及以上的记录是大多数基准测试研究（包括本文）通常采用的最低门槛。 And variant of uncertain significance, or VUS (意义不明的变异), is the clinical label for a variant that current evidence can't confidently call either benign or pathogenic — resolving VUS calls is the whole reason Karen's collaboration exists, and it's the backdrop against which every method in this episode should be judged. 意义不明的变异（variant of uncertain significance，缩写VUS）是临床上对那些现有证据既不能确信判断为良性、也不能确信判断为致病性变异所使用的标签——解决VUS判读问题正是与Karen合作这条线存在的全部意义，也是本集中每一种方法都应被放在这个背景下评判的参照点。

JORDAN So why build yet another predictor when AlphaMissense already covers 216 million possible human amino acid substitutions? 既然AlphaMissense已经覆盖了2.16亿个可能的人类氨基酸替换，为什么还要再造一个预测工具呢? Two practical gaps, the authors say. 作者给出了两个实际存在的缺口。 First, deployability: AlphaMissense builds on the full AlphaFold2 structure-prediction framework, its model weights aren't easily retrained or run outside its own published score tables, and it only covers human proteins — so a lab wanting to batch-score, say, a non-human ortholog or simply wanting a retrainable local model is out of luck. 第一是可部署性：AlphaMissense建立在完整的AlphaFold2结构预测框架之上，其模型权重并不容易在其自身发布的评分表之外重新训练或运行，而且它只覆盖人类蛋白质——因此，如果某个实验室想要批量评分非人类的同源蛋白，或者单纯想要一个可以本地重新训练的模型，就会发现无从下手。 Second, and more interesting for us, both AlphaMissense and REVEL define their own "ambiguous zone" — a score band near the decision threshold where the tool itself is essentially saying "I don't know" — and that zone is exactly where a clinical lab is stuck without additional evidence. 第二点，对我们来说更有意思：AlphaMissense和REVEL都各自定义了一个"模糊区"——也就是评分恰好落在判定阈值附近的区间，工具本身实际上就是在说"我不确定"——而这正是临床实验室在缺乏额外证据时卡住的地方。 REVEL, by the way, stands for Rare Exome Variant Ensemble Learner (罕见外显子变异集成学习器) — it's an ensemble method that combines multiple earlier predictors into one score, and it's been widely adopted in clinical genomics pipelines precisely because ensembling tends to smooth out any single tool's blind spots. 顺便说一句，REVEL全称是Rare Exome Variant Ensemble Learner（罕见外显子变异集成学习器）——这是一种集成方法，将多个早期的预测工具组合成一个评分，之所以在临床基因组学流程中被广泛采用，正是因为集成方法往往能够弥补单一工具的盲区。 The authors' bet is that pLM-SAV, built on a completely different signal — embedding differences rather than evolutionary conservation or structure — might be complementary precisely in that ambiguous zone, even if it never beats the leaders on a broad benchmark. 作者的赌注是，pLM-SAV建立在一种完全不同的信号之上——嵌入差异，而非进化保守性或结构信息——因此恰恰有可能在这个模糊区内起到互补作用，即便它在广泛基准测试中永远无法超越领先工具。

JORDAN Walk me through how they actually built it. 具体说说他们是怎么构建这个模型的。 The training data is called Eff10k (Eff10k数据集), a dataset the Rost lab originally assembled for two earlier predictors, SNAP2 and VESPA, combining three sources — the Protein Mutant Database, protein-specific curated studies, and OMIM disease variants — into just over 100,000 labeled variants from about 9,600 proteins, each one labeled simply "neutral" or "effect," not "benign" or "pathogenic." 训练数据叫做Eff10k数据集（Eff10k dataset），最初是Rost实验室为两个早期预测工具SNAP2和VESPA整理的数据集，综合了三个来源——蛋白质突变数据库（Protein Mutant Database）、特定蛋白的精选研究，以及OMIM疾病变异数据库——总共包含约10万个来自约9600个蛋白质的标注变异，每个变异仅被标注为"中性（neutral）"或"有效应（effect）"，而不是"良性"或"致病性"。 That label distinction matters, and ALEX will come back to it. 这个标签区分很重要，ALEX后面会再提到。 Crucially, the authors clustered all proteins by sequence similarity using PSI-BLAST — a classic bioinformatics alignment-search tool — and split those clusters into ten folds so that no two sequence-similar proteins ever landed in both a training fold and a test fold, which is the standard way to stop a model from just memorizing near-duplicate sequences instead of learning the general signal. 关键的一点是，作者使用PSI-BLAST——一种经典的生物信息学序列比对检索工具——按序列相似性对所有蛋白质进行聚类，并将这些聚类划分到十个折（fold）中，确保序列相似的蛋白质永远不会同时出现在训练折和测试折中，这是防止模型只是记住几乎重复的序列、而不是学习到通用信号的标准做法。 For each variant, they computed the Δ-embedding across a seven-residue window — three amino acids before the mutation site, three after, plus the mutation site itself — because the signal turned out to extend measurably beyond the single mutated residue into its immediate neighbors. 对每一个变异，他们计算了一个跨越七个残基窗口的Δ嵌入——突变位点前三个氨基酸，后三个氨基酸，再加上突变位点本身——因为研究发现这个信号确实会可测地延伸到突变残基本身之外，波及其紧邻的相邻位点。 That seven-position array then feeds a small, purpose-built convolutional neural network, nicknamed CNN1c, which was chosen over larger architectures specifically because it trained faster and performed just as well. 这个七位置的数组随后被输入到一个小型、专门构建的卷积神经网络中，昵称为CNN1c，之所以选择它而非更大的架构，正是因为它训练速度更快，而性能同样出色。

JORDAN Any training details worth flagging before we get to numbers? 在看结果之前，还有什么训练细节值得一提吗? A couple. 有几点。 They trained with BCEWithLogitsLoss, standard binary cross-entropy with a built-in sigmoid for numerical stability, applied per-fold weighting to correct for Eff10k's uneven fold sizes, and used early stopping triggered by the gap between training and validation loss rather than a fixed epoch count, which is a reasonable way to avoid overfitting a dataset this heterogeneous. 他们使用了BCEWithLogitsLoss，也就是带有内置sigmoid以保证数值稳定性的标准二元交叉熵损失函数，并对每个折应用了权重调整，以纠正Eff10k各折大小不均的问题，还采用了早停策略，依据训练损失与验证损失之间的差距触发，而不是固定的训练轮数，这对于应对如此异构的数据集来说是一种合理的防止过拟合的做法。 The classification threshold itself wasn't fixed at the usual 0.5 — for every fold they swept the Matthews correlation coefficient across thresholds from 0.1 to 0.9 in steps of 0.01 on the validation set and picked whichever threshold maximized it, meaning every one of the ninety method-A models and seventy-two method-B models carries its own calibrated cutoff rather than a single global one. 分类阈值本身并没有固定在通常的0.5——对于每一个折，他们都在验证集上以0.01为步长，在0.1到0.9之间扫描马修斯相关系数（MCC），并选择使其最大化的阈值，这意味着90个方法A模型和72个方法B模型中的每一个，都带有各自校准出来的截断值，而不是统一使用一个全局阈值。

ALEX I want to push back on one choice here before we move to results. 在进入结果之前，我想对这里的一个选择提出质疑。 The training labels — neutral versus effect in Eff10k — measure general functional disruption, not clinical pathogenicity, while the evaluation sets drawn from ClinVar measure benign versus pathogenic, a related but genuinely different question; a variant can wreck a protein's biochemical function in an assay and still be clinically irrelevant if that protein's disruption doesn't cause disease, or vice versa. 这里的训练标签——Eff10k中的中性与有效应——衡量的是一般性的功能破坏，而不是临床致病性，而从ClinVar抽取的评估集衡量的是良性与致病性，这是一个相关但确实不同的问题；一个变异可能在实验检测中严重破坏了蛋白质的生化功能，但如果该蛋白质的功能破坏并不会导致疾病，那么它在临床上可能毫不相关，反之亦然。 The authors are upfront about this mismatch and even point to it as a partial explanation for pLM-SAV's weaker performance on some ClinVar subsets, which is the right way to handle it, but it also means any downstream use of this tool for clinical variant classification is borrowing a model trained on a subtly different target than the one being asked. 作者对这种标签不匹配是坦率承认的，甚至将其作为pLM-SAV在某些ClinVar子集上表现较弱的部分解释，这是正确的处理方式，但这也意味着，任何将这个工具用于下游临床变异分类的做法，实际上都是在借用一个训练目标与实际提问目标存在微妙差异的模型。 Worth remembering before anyone treats its score as a clinical verdict. 在有人把它的评分当作临床判定结论之前，这一点值得牢记。

JORDAN So does it actually work? 那它到底管不管用? On its own home turf — the Eff10k test folds — yes, comfortably: the best configuration, using the Ankh embeddings with what the authors call "method B" training, which drops one oversized, disproportionate fold from the training process, cleared the standard significance bars for both AUC and MCC, and Ankh consistently beat ProtT5 as the embedding source across every comparison. 在它的"主场"——Eff10k测试折——答案是肯定的，而且表现相当不错：最佳配置使用Ankh嵌入，并采用作者称为"方法B"的训练方式，即从训练过程中剔除一个过大、不成比例的折，这一配置在AUC和MCC指标上都清除了标准的显著性门槛，而且在每一次比较中，Ankh作为嵌入来源都持续优于ProtT5。 On the independent, sequence-filtered ClinVar test sets — proteins with no overlap to anything seen in training — only that same Ankh-method-B combination held up with statistical significance across the board, which already tells you the signal is real but fragile to configuration choices. 在独立的、经过序列过滤的ClinVar测试集上——这些蛋白质与训练中见过的任何内容都没有重叠——只有同样的Ankh-方法B组合在所有指标上保持了统计显著性，这已经说明这个信号是真实存在的，但对配置选择相当敏感。 Against the broad PMD4k benchmark, used originally to evaluate SNAP2 and VESPA, pLM-SAV landed right in the pack: SNAP2 scored the best MCC at 0.280, pLM-SAV's Ankh models scored 0.274 and 0.271, essentially tied with VESPA — meaning on a head-to-head, feature-rich-versus-lightweight comparison, nobody won decisively. 在广泛使用、原本用于评估SNAP2和VESPA的PMD4k基准测试上，pLM-SAV的表现恰好处于同一水平：SNAP2取得了最佳MCC值0.280，pLM-SAV的Ankh模型则分别取得0.274和0.271，基本上与VESPA持平——这意味着在特征丰富型方法与轻量级方法的正面对比中，没有谁能明显占据上风。
