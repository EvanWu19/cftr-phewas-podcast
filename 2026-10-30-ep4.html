How did this episode land for you?

Ep4 (Track C 2/2 — registry QC): The one Track C paper with an actual right answer

Kontopantelis and colleagues (2017, BMC Research Notes) present mibmi, a Stata command for cleaning and longitudinally multiply-imputing body mass index (BMI) or other variables with very low individual-level variability, built on the insight that such variables vary far more between people than within a single person's own time series. The command's regression-based cleaning (xclean) fits a per-individual OLS trend of the variable on time (≥3 observations required) and flags points whose |residual|/observation ratio exceeds a threshold (default 50%) as implausible; its interpolation step uses Stata's ipolate to quantify per-gap-width prediction error on held-out real observations, then draws sequential imputed values from a normal distribution centered on that prediction. Validated against real, masked CPRD (Clinical Practice Research Datalink) diabetes-patient data rather than simulation — a genuine ground-truth comparison — mibmi matched the comparator "twofold" algorithm for interpolation (mean absolute error ~0.81 vs. ~0.80) and beat it on a harder 3-sequential-missing-value test, but lost clearly on extrapolation (1.614 vs. 1.179), where twofold's use of correlated covariates (HbA1c, blood pressure, cholesterol) pays off. A worked example showed uncleaned BMI data masking a real BMI-polypharmacy association entirely, which simple range-based cleaning alone restored. The authors' own recommendation: use mibmi for cleaning and interpolation; feed its output into twofold specifically for extrapolation when correlated covariates exist.

Methods / tools to lift

  • mibmi (Stata command) — per-individual regression-residual cleaning plus sequential, gap-width-aware multiple imputation for low-individual-variability longitudinal variables.
  • xclean (regression-based cleaning) — the per-person OLS-residual-ratio implausible-value flag inside mibmi.
  • Masked-real-value validation design — mask a genuinely observed value, impute it, compare to the known true value; a reusable evaluation technique for any imputation/correction step.
  • twofold algorithm (comparator, not from this paper) — fully-conditional-specification longitudinal imputation using correlated covariates, the better choice for extrapolation.

Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • How the per-individual OLS flag's blind spots compare to a population-level mixed-effects model's — a carry-forward value would look perfectly consistent with a flat per-person trend and pass through unflagged, a real limitation.
  • Whether the default 50% residual-ratio threshold and the 8-210/0.81-2.3m range bounds, tuned on a UK adult primary-care population, need re-derivation for a different age distribution or clinical context.
  • How the method needs to change for a much sparser or more irregular visit schedule than the roughly-annual CPRD measurements used here, since per-gap-width RMSD estimates need enough examples per gap width to be reliable.
  • Whether the upstream xclean flagging rule's own sensitivity/false-positive rate needs a separately-designed validation, since this paper's masked-value evaluation validates imputation, not detection.

Pipeline stage

(a) Candidate generation, primary (per-individual OLS-residual-ratio flag). (d) Evaluation, secondary (masked-real-value validation design, with the imputation-vs-detection caveat noted above).

Error types covered / not covered

Covered: out-of-logical-range BMI/height/weight values (standard clean); implausible within-person deviations from an individual's own linear trend (xclean, regression-residual). Not covered, and an explicit blind spot worth flagging on-air: carry-forward values (a flat, unchanged trend passes through undetected), digit transposition or decimal-shift errors that happen to still fall within range and near the person's own trend, and any distinction between a genuine extreme clinical event and a data-entry error.

JORDAN Before the method, let's place the data source. 在讲方法之前，我们先说清楚数据来源。 What's CPRD, and why would blood pressure be well-recorded for diabetes patients but not others? CPRD是什么，为什么糖尿病患者的血压记录会比其他人完整得多？

ALEX CPRD, the Clinical Practice Research Datalink, is one of several UK primary-care electronic-medical-record databases this paper draws on, alongside THIN and QResearch — large repositories of routinely-collected GP consultation data covering millions of patients, the UK's closest analog to the kind of longitudinal real-world clinical data a registry like this project's accumulates, just sourced from primary care rather than a disease-specific specialty clinic. CPRD，即临床实践研究数据链（Clinical Practice Research Datalink），是这篇论文所使用的几个英国初级医疗电子病历数据库之一，另外还有THIN和QResearch——这些是覆盖数百万患者的常规收集全科医生（GP）问诊数据的大型数据库，是英国最接近这个项目所积累的那种纵向真实世界临床数据的资源，只不过它的数据来源是初级医疗，而不是某个疾病专科诊所。 The reason blood pressure specifically is well recorded for diabetes patients traces to the Quality and Outcomes Framework, QOF, a UK pay-for-performance scheme introduced in 2004 that financially incentivizes GPs to record specific measurements for specific chronic-condition patient groups. 血压数据对糖尿病患者来说记录特别完整的原因，可以追溯到质量与结果框架（Quality and Outcomes Framework，QOF），这是英国2004年推出的一项按绩效付费机制，用经济激励的方式鼓励全科医生为特定慢性病患者群体记录特定的测量指标。 That's a concrete illustration of a general registry-provenance point worth internalizing: a field's completeness pattern is often an artifact of some external incentive or workflow structure, not of the underlying clinical reality, and that structure is exactly what determines whether missingness is closer to random or informative. 这正好具体说明了一个值得记住的、通用的注册库数据来源问题：一个字段的完整性模式，往往是某种外部激励或工作流程结构造成的产物，而不是底层临床真实情况的反映，而正是这种结构，决定了缺失数据更接近随机缺失，还是带有信息性的缺失。

JORDAN So why does BMI specifically need its own imputation command instead of just using an existing one? 那为什么BMI具体需要一个专门的插补命令，而不是直接用现成的方法？

ALEX The paper's argument is that BMI, and variables like it, has an unusual statistical property: very low within-person variability over time compared to its between-person variability, meaning a single individual's BMI trajectory is close to a smooth, slowly-drifting line, while BMI varies enormously across different people. 这篇论文的论点是，BMI以及与它类似的变量，具有一个不寻常的统计特性：相对于人与人之间的差异，同一个人在不同时间点上的变异非常小，也就是说，一个人自己的BMI轨迹接近一条平滑、缓慢漂移的曲线，而不同人之间的BMI却可能差异巨大。 Standard cross-sectional multiple imputation, and even existing longitudinal extensions that lean on other correlated biological covariates, treat each missing value as something to predict mainly from other variables measured at the same or nearby time points — which, for a variable this stable within a person, throws away the single strongest available signal: that person's own prior and subsequent BMI values. 标准的横断面多重插补，甚至是依赖其他相关生物学协变量的现有纵向扩展方法，都倾向于把每一个缺失值主要当作需要从同一时间点或相邻时间点的其他变量来预测的东西——而对于这样一个在个体内部如此稳定的变量来说，这恰恰丢掉了手头最强的信号：这个人自己之前和之后的BMI数值本身。 So the authors built mibmi specifically to lean on each individual's own time series, interpolating between that person's own observations rather than borrowing primarily from population averages or other covariates. 所以作者专门构建了mibmi，让它依赖每个个体自己的时间序列，在这个人自己的观测值之间进行插值，而不是主要借用人群平均水平或其他协变量。

JORDAN Walk through what mibmi actually does, step by step. 一步一步讲讲mibmi具体做了什么。

ALEX There are really two layers: cleaning, and then imputation. 这里其实有两层：清洗，然后插补。 Standard cleaning is the blunt version — it sets BMI to missing if it falls outside 8 to 210, and, when height and weight are supplied, applies analogous hard range limits to those, 0.81 to 2.3 meters and 15 to 500 kilograms, with age-conditional lower bounds. 标准清洗是比较粗暴的版本——如果BMI落在8到210这个区间之外，就把它设为缺失，如果提供了身高和体重，也对它们应用类似的硬性范围限制，身高0.81到2.3米、体重15到500公斤，并附带根据年龄调整的下限。 The more interesting layer is regression-based cleaning, xclean: for each individual with at least three observations, the command fits a separate ordinary-least-squares regression of the variable on time, computes the ratio of the absolute residual, meaning observed minus predicted, to the observed value itself, and drops — sets to missing — any point where that ratio exceeds a threshold, 50 percent by default and user-adjustable. 更有意思的是回归式清洗（xclean）：对于每一个至少有三次观测的个体，这个命令会单独拟合一条该变量对时间的普通最小二乘回归，计算绝对残差（即观测值减去预测值）与观测值本身的比值，并把任何比值超过某个阈值（默认50%，可由用户调整）的点删除，设为缺失。 That's structurally the same move as the prior-wave-anchored residual flag this podcast covered a few days back — a per-person model predicting what a value should be, then a threshold on how far reality deviates from that prediction — except here the model is purely a per-individual linear trend over time, with no population-level mixed-effects borrowing across people at all. 这在结构上和这个节目几天前介绍过的"锁定前几次访视的残差标记法"是同一招——用一个针对个体的模型预测某个值应该是什么样，然后对现实与这个预测之间的偏离程度设定一个阈值——不同的是，这里的模型纯粹是针对每个个体自己随时间变化的线性趋势，完全没有借用跨个体的人群层面混合效应信息。 Once cleaned, interpolation works by first using Stata's ipolate command to quantify, for every possible gap width between observations, the root mean square deviation of what ipolate would have predicted versus what was actually observed — treating real observations as if they were temporarily missing, purely to measure prediction error — and then drawing each actual imputed value from a normal distribution centered on ipolate's point estimate, with that gap-width-specific RMSD as the standard deviation, imputed sequentially so each new draw conditions on the immediately preceding value, observed or imputed, rather than fluctuating independently around a flat mean. 清洗完成之后，插值的做法是先用Stata的ipolate命令，针对观测值之间每一种可能的间隔宽度，量化ipolate本来会预测出的值与实际观测值之间的均方根偏差（RMSD）——也就是把真实观测值暂时当作缺失来处理，纯粹是为了衡量预测误差——然后把每一个真正要插补的值，从一个以ipolate点估计为中心的正态分布里抽取，用该间隔宽度对应的RMSD作为标准差，依次顺序插补，这样每一个新抽取的值都以紧邻的前一个值（无论是观测值还是已插补值）为条件，而不是围绕一个固定均值独立波动。 Extrapolation, for values before the first or after the last observation, follows the same logic but with notably larger RMSD estimates, since it has less information to work with — and the authors are explicit that it's computationally far more expensive and should be used with care.外推，即对第一个观测值之前或最后一个观测值之后缺失的值，遵循同样的逻辑，但RMSD估计值明显更大，因为可用的信息更少——作者也明确指出，这一步计算成本要高得多，使用时应当谨慎。

JORDAN ALEX, is there a design choice here worth pushing on? ALEX，这里有什么设计选择值得追问一下吗？

ALEX Yes — building the entire cleaning-and-imputation pipeline around a per-individual model with no population-level pooling is a deliberate bet that, for a stable variable like BMI, a person's own trajectory beats any amount of population or covariate information, and the paper's own benchmark bears that out specifically for interpolation but not for extrapolation. 有——把整条清洗加插补流程都建立在一个不做任何人群层面汇总的、针对个体的模型之上，这是一个有意为之的赌注：对于像BMI这样稳定的变量，一个人自己的轨迹能胜过任何数量的人群或协变量信息，而论文自己的基准测试恰恰证实了这一点，但仅限于插值，不适用于外推。 That's a real, honestly-reported limitation rather than something glossed over, and it tells you exactly when to reach for this tool and when to reach for something else. 这是一个真实的、被诚实报告出来的局限，而不是被一笔带过的问题，它恰好告诉了我们什么时候该用这个工具，什么时候该换别的工具。

benchmark, HbA1c, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, and total cholesterol — rather than only the target variable's own time series. 对比方法是"twofold"，一种现有的纵向多重插补算法，采用完全条件规范（fully conditional specification）方法，依赖其他相关的生物学协变量——在这次基准测试里是HbA1c（糖化血红蛋白）、收缩压和舒张压、以及总胆固醇——而不仅仅依赖目标变量自身的时间序列。 Rather than simulating data, the authors deliberately chose to validate against real observations: they took 10,000 people with at least three BMI measurements, randomly masked one true value per person, imputed it with both methods, and compared each method's estimate back to the actual, known value — genuine ground truth, which is exactly why this paper stands out in this track. 作者没有选择模拟数据，而是刻意选择对照真实观测值进行验证：他们选取10,000名至少有三次BMI测量记录的人，随机遮盖每人一个真实数值，用两种方法分别进行插补，再把每种方法的估计值与实际的、已知的真实值进行比较——这是真正的金标准，正是这一点让这篇论文在这个赛道里显得格外突出。 For interpolation specifically, mean absolute error was people with at least five observations, interpolation only — mibmi actually beat twofold, and the gap widened at the middle time point, the one furthest from any real observation: mibmi's error there is refreshingly specific rather than a blanket recommendation: use mibmi's cleaning and interpolation when only the target variable's own time series is available, and feed its cleaned output into twofold specifically when extrapolation is needed and correlated covariates exist. 作者自己给出的结论令人耳目一新，是一个具体的分层建议，而不是笼统的推荐：当只有目标变量自身的时间序列可用时，使用mibmi的清洗和插值；当确实需要外推、并且有相关协变量可用时，把mibmi清洗后的输出接入twofold。

JORDAN There's a demonstration with real diabetes patients and drug prescriptions — what did that actually show? 论文里还用真实的糖尿病患者和用药处方做了一个演示——那部分实际说明了什么？

ALEX On the raw, uncleaned dataset, a multi-level Poisson regression of one-year drug-prescription count on BMI found a weak, non-significant relationship. 在未经清洗的原始数据集上，一个针对一年用药处方数量对BMI的多层泊松回归（multi-level Poisson regression）发现两者之间的关系很弱，且不显著。 After applying just the simple range-based cleaning — no imputation yet — the same analysis found a statistically significant association. 在仅应用了简单的基于范围的清洗之后——还没有进行任何插补——同样的分析发现了一个统计上显著的关联。 That's a concrete, worked demonstration of a principle worth sitting with: a handful of implausible outlier values, left uncorrected, can mask a real underlying signal entirely, not just add noise around it — which is a sharper and more consequential failure mode than "slightly biased estimate." 这是一个具体、完整演示出来的原则，值得我们细细体会：一小撮未经校正的不合理异常值，可能会把一个真实存在的底层信号完全遮蔽掉，而不仅仅是在它周围添加一些噪声——这是一种比"估计值略有偏差"更尖锐、后果也更严重的失败模式。 Full cleaning plus multiple imputation then reproduced results similar to the simple-cleaning-only analysis — reassuring, since it means the heavier imputation machinery isn't distorting the substantive conclusion relative to the lighter cleaning-only pass, just filling in the gaps it leaves behind. 完整清洗加多重插补之后，得到的结果与只做简单清洗的分析结果相近——这令人安心，因为这说明更重的插补机制并没有扭曲相对于轻量级"只清洗"分析得出的实质性结论，它只是在填补清洗之后留下的空白。

JORDAN How does this change what we'd think about for the CFFPR outlier pipeline — which pipeline stage does it touch? 这篇论文对我们思考CFFPR异常值流程有什么改变——它触及哪个流程阶段？

主要是流程阶段（a），候选生成——其个体回归残差阈值方法（xclean）是一个真正的不合理数值标记规则，在结构上与几天前介绍的"锁定前几次访视的残差标记法"接近，但明显更简单：只是一条针对单个个体的普通最小二乘直线，而不是一个人群层面的混合效应模型，这样的方法理论上可以捕捉到一个人群模型会被平滑掉的个体特异性偏离，代价是每个人至少需要三次观测才能拟合。 There's a genuine secondary touch on pipeline stage (d), evaluation — but with an important caveat worth stating precisely: the masked-real-value comparison this paper uses validates imputation accuracy against a known true value, which is a different and in some ways easier problem than validating detection sensitivity against a labeled true error, since here the "true answer" is simply the value that was deliberately hidden, not a judgment call about whether something was ever actually wrong. 还有一个真正的次要触及点是流程阶段（d），评估——但有一个值得精确说明的重要限定：这篇论文使用的"遮盖真实值再对比"方法，验证的是插补准确性相对于一个已知真实值的表现，这在某种意义上是一个比验证检测敏感度相对于一个带标签的真实错误更容易的问题，因为这里的"正确答案"仅仅是被故意隐藏起来的那个数值本身，并不涉及对某个东西是否曾经真的出错做出判断。 Still, the reusable design
