Ep3 (Track C) — Calibration to reduce EHR measurement error in prevalence estimates

Blurb. Chan, Zhao, Lim, Perlman, and McVeigh (Prev Chronic Dis, 2018) calibrate six health indicators from the NYC Macroscope, an EHR-based public-health surveillance system, against the 2013-2014 NYC Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NYC HANES) gold standard, using a 190-person chart-reviewed subsample linked at the individual level to both data sources. A Firth logistic regression trained on that small subsample — predicting true (survey-measured) status from EHR status plus covariates — is applied to the full 716,076-person EHR population to produce a corrected prevalence estimate. For the two indicators with high baseline measurement error (depression, influenza vaccination), calibration cut bias substantially (10.8→2.5 and 26.7→8.4 percentage points respectively); for four low-error indicators (obesity, smoking, diabetes, hypertension), it changed essentially nothing. The transferable idea for CFFPR: a small adjudicated sample can do more than grade a detector after the fact — used as a calibration set, it can correct an aggregate estimate computed from the whole dataset, but the value only shows up where the underlying error rate clears a threshold worth correcting.

Plain-English glossary

  • NYC Macroscope — An EHR-based public-health surveillance system that aggregates indicator counts (e.g., hypertension prevalence) from primary care practices meeting federal documentation-quality standards.（中文：一个基于电子健康档案(EHR)的公共卫生监测系统，从符合联邦文书质量标准的初级保健诊所汇总指标计数，如高血压患病率。）
  • Meaningful use — The US federal incentive program that set documentation and data-quality benchmarks for EHR-using providers.（中文：美国联邦激励项目，为使用电子健康档案的医疗机构设定文书和数据质量基准。）
  • Chart review — Manually abstracting a patient's actual medical record to verify or compare against another data source.（中文：人工提取患者实际病历记录，用以核实或与其他数据来源进行比较。）
  • Gold standard — A reference measurement or dataset assumed to reflect the true value, against which other measures are validated.（中文：一种被假定能反映真实值的参考测量或数据集，其他测量方法据此进行验证。）
  • Firth logistic regression — A bias-corrected variant of logistic regression that performs better than standard logistic regression on small or separated samples.（中文：一种经过偏差校正的逻辑回归变体，在小样本或存在完全分离问题的样本上表现优于标准逻辑回归。）
  • Youden index — A statistic (sensitivity + specificity − 1) used to select an optimal classification probability cutoff.（中文：一种统计量（灵敏度+特异度−1），用于选择最优的分类概率切点。）
  • Poststratification — A survey-adjustment technique that applies weights to correct for sampling imbalances relative to a target population.（中文：一种调查数据调整技术，通过施加权重来修正样本相对于目标人群的抽样不平衡。）
  • Misclassification / measurement error — The proportion of false-positive plus false-negative cases relative to a gold standard, used here to categorize indicators as high vs. low error.（中文：相对于金标准的假阳性加假阴性比例，本文用它把各项指标分为高误差和低误差两类。）
  • PHQ-9 — A standardized 9-item questionnaire used to screen for depression severity, referenced here as part of the NYC HANES depression definition.（中文：一份标准化的9题问卷，用于筛查抑郁症严重程度，本文中作为NYC HANES抑郁症定义的一部分被引用。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • Firth logistic regression for calibration modeling on small, individually-linked chart-review samples.
  • Youden-index-based probability cutoff selection for reclassifying indicator status.
  • A high-vs-low measurement-error triage rule (15% misclassification threshold) to decide where calibration effort is worth spending.
  • Sensitivity analysis excluding overlap between calibration and validation samples, as a check on non-independence.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Serves Liz / CFFPR data quality (method only — no claims about CFFPR's current flag rate or rules). MVP: adjudication-calibration-corrector — given a small adjudicated height/weight sample with candidate-generator flag status, fit a Firth-logistic-style correction model on registry-wide covariates, apply it to the full population, and report a corrected aggregate implausible-value rate alongside the raw flag rate. Input: adjudicated sample + candidate-generator flags + covariates. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • Calibration and validation samples here overlapped (not fully independent) — the paper's own sensitivity analysis found only small shifts, but a cleaner split would be preferable in a fresh build.
  • The method assumes the covariate-to-true-status relationship learned on the small sample generalizes to the full population; the two samples here weren't perfectly matched on all covariates (e.g., neighborhood wealth).
  • This paper corrects binary yes/no indicators; extending the same logic to continuous or graded anthropometric outlier scores is untested here and would need its own methodological work.
  • No claims here about CFFPR's own current implausible-value flag rate or existing cleaning rules.

Pipeline stage. (d) Evaluation — primary: turns a small adjudicated sample into a correction on an aggregate estimate, not just a detector scorecard.

Error types covered / not covered. Covered: general misclassification (false-positive and false-negative indicator status) at the aggregate-estimate level. Not covered: no distinction between error subtypes (e.g., unit conversion, digit transposition, decimal shift, carry-forward) — the method treats "wrong" as a single binary category, not a taxonomy of error mechanisms.
