How did this episode land for you?

Track C — registry data quality. Boone-Heinonen and the ADVANCE Clinical Data Research Network compare two ways of defining anthropometric outliers in 1.27 million pediatric EHR encounters (147,375 children, ages 10-18): a fixed CDC modified-z-score population cutoff versus a longitudinal definition using studentized residuals (>|6|) from a sex-stratified linear-spline mixed-effects growth model. Overall agreement was extremely high (PABAK ≥0.995), but the two definitions diverged sharply for children at the high end of the growth distribution — annualized height/weight/BMI change among children above the 85th percentile was consistently and substantially larger when longitudinal outliers were excluded versus population outliers, suggesting the fixed cutoff was disproportionately excluding real, fast-growing children. Because there's no gold-standard error label in this data, the paper's real contribution is a downstream-impact evaluation strategy: rather than asking which method is "more correct," it measures how much the choice of outlier definition moves the scientific conclusion — directly useful when no gold standard exists, as is the case for most registry QC decisions.

Plain-English glossary

CDC 2000 growth curves / modified z-score — the standard US pediatric growth reference; the CDC's modified z-score convention flags population outliers at fixed thresholds (e.g., weight z <-5 or >8).（中文:美国标准的儿科生长参照标准;CDC的修正z分数惯例用固定阈值标记群体异常值(例如体重z分数低于-5或高于8)。）

Linear-spline mixed-effects model / knot — a regression allowing a growth trajectory to bend at a specific age (the knot), rather than one straight line across the age range, with random effects capturing individual variation.（中文:一种允许生长轨迹在特定年龄(节点)发生弯折的回归模型,而非用一条直线拟合整个年龄区间,并用随机效应捕捉个体差异。）

Studentized residual — the gap between an observed measurement and a patient's own fitted trajectory, standardized by estimated error; the longitudinal-outlier score in this paper.（中文:观测测量值与患者自身拟合轨迹之间的差距,经估计误差标准化后得到的值;本文用作纵向异常值分数。）

PABAK (prevalence-adjusted bias-adjusted kappa) — an agreement statistic that corrects for the paradox where plain Cohen's kappa gives low scores despite high raw agreement when an outcome is rare.（中文:一种一致性统计量,纠正了当结局罕见时,普通Cohen's kappa即使在原始一致率很高时也可能给出偏低分数的悖论。）
