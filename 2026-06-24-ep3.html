How did this episode land for you?

Show notes — SLC26A9 / ivacaftor pharmacogenomics — Corvol 2018 (Track C, CF modifier)

Blurb. Corvol et al. (Front Pharmacol, 2018) test whether the SLC26A9 modifier locus predicts ivacaftor lung response in the French CF Gene Modifier Study (n=4,840). Two clean results: SLC26A9 variants (rs7512462 + six others) were NOT associated with lung-function variability in untreated patients (consistent with international GWAS, where lung-modifier hits sit at MUC4/MUC20, SLC9A3, SLC6A14, HLA — not SLC26A9); and treated patients improved in line with trials (+11.7% ppFEV1 early for gating-mutation carriers, +14.4% for G551D). The notable result: rs7512462 was associated with ivacaftor response, but in the OPPOSITE direction from the 2014 Strug discovery — each C allele = ~−7.7% ppFEV1 improvement in French G551D carriers (p≈0.0007), where Strug reported ~+8.5% per C allele. The pharmacogenomic analysis rests on only 30 of 4,840 patients (gating mutations are ~4% of CF; funnel laid out in a flowchart). The authors attribute the sign flip to ancestry-dependent LD: rs7512462 is a non-functional tag SNP, North American cohorts are admixed, the French cohort is predominantly European, so the tag's LD with the unidentified causal variant can differ in direction. Context: Eastman 2021 (already covered) later found a well-powered (>97%) NULL for this association — today's paper is one of the conflicting positive-but-inverse signals that null had to reconcile. Where it lands: summary-stats colocalization / fine-mapping. Buildable artifact: a cross-cohort direction-of-effect & LD-divergence checker for modifier tag SNPs.

Plain-English glossary

  • Admixture (population) — when a cohort's members have ancestry from multiple source populations, which changes local LD patterns relative to a single-ancestry cohort.（中文：人群混合 (admixture)，队列成员的血统来自多个源人群，会使局部 LD 模式相对单一血统队列发生改变。）
  • FEV1pp / SaKnorm — CF lung-severity measures: percent-predicted forced expiratory volume in 1 s, and a normalized CF-specific Z-score.（中文：FEV1pp / SaKnorm，CF 肺部严重程度度量；前者为占预计值百分比的一秒用力呼气量，后者为标准化的 CF 专用 Z 分数。）
  • Gating mutation — a CFTR variant (classically G551D) that reaches the cell surface and folds, but whose channel gate barely opens; the target class for ivacaftor.（中文：门控突变 (gating mutation)，一种能到达细胞表面并折叠、但通道门几乎不开的 CFTR 变异（经典为 G551D）；是 ivacaftor 的靶向类别。）
  • Haplotype — a set of variants inherited together on the same chromosome segment; analyzed here to corroborate the single-SNP results.（中文：单倍型 (haplotype)，在同一染色体片段上一起遗传的一组变异；此处用于佐证单位点结果。）
  • Ivacaftor / potentiator — a CFTR drug that binds the channel and raises its open probability; first CFTR modulator approved (2012).（中文：ivacaftor / 增效剂 (potentiator)，结合 CFTR 通道并提高其开放概率的药物；最早获批（2012）的 CFTR 调节剂。）
  • KASP genotyping — a cheap PCR-based allele-specific assay for typing a handful of known SNPs (not array or sequencing).（中文：KASP 基因分型，一种廉价的、基于 PCR 的等位基因特异性检测，用于对少数已知 SNP 分型（非芯片或测序）。）
  • Linkage disequilibrium (LD) — non-random co-inheritance of nearby variants on the same haplotype block, which lets a tag SNP stand in for a correlated causal variant.（中文：连锁不平衡 (LD)，同一单倍型区块上邻近变异的非随机共同遗传，使标签位点能替代一个与之相关的致病变异。）
  • Pharmacogenomics — using a patient's genotype to predict drug response; the goal a marker like rs7512462 would serve if it replicated.（中文：药物基因组学，用患者基因型预测药物反应；若 rs7512462 这样的标记能被重复验证，它正可服务于此。）
  • Pleiotropy — one gene affecting multiple distinct traits; SLC26A9 touches CFRD, meconium ileus, pancreas, and (here) drug response.（中文：多效性 (pleiotropy)，一个基因影响多个不同性状；SLC26A9 涉及 CFRD、胎粪性肠梗阻、胰腺，以及（此处）药物反应。）
  • Tag SNP — a genotyped marker that is not itself causal but is in LD with the true causal variant and represents it statistically.（中文：标签位点 (tag SNP)，一个本身非致病、但与真正致病变异处于 LD 并在统计上代表它的被分型标记。）

Full citation. Corvol H, Mésinèle J, Douksieh IH, Strug LJ, Boëlle PY, Guillot L. SLC26A9 Gene Is Associated With Lung Function Response to Ivacaftor in Patients With Cystic Fibrosis. Front Pharmacol. 2018;9:828. PMID 30140228 · PMC PMC6095007 · DOI 10.3389/fphar.2018.00828. According to Europe PMC; full text via BioC PMC-OA route.

Methods / tools to lift

  • Targeted KASP genotyping of a small SNP panel across a large cohort — cheap way to test a specific modifier hypothesis without array/WGS.
  • Transparent attrition flowchart from full cohort (4,840) to analyzable pharmacogenomic subset (30) — the right way to expose a small-n drug-response analysis.
  • Baseline-adjusted linear regression on FEV1pp change, with both an early (15–75 day) and 1-year averaged endpoint (trapezoidal rule for irregular measurements).
  • Haplotype reconstruction (EM algorithm, haplo.stats) to corroborate single-SNP direction of effect.
  • Explicit ancestry/LD reasoning to explain a between-cohort direction flip rather than dismissing it as contradiction.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Summary-stats colocalization / fine-mapping. MVP (paste-ready): for each catalogued modifier locus, take the lead SNP → flag whether it's a non-functional tag → pull its LD with nearby candidate causal variants from a multi-ancestry reference panel → emit per-locus flags where the tag↔causal LD diverges by ancestry (predicting a direction flip when cohorts of different ancestry are pooled). Input: catalogued modifier lead-SNP list. Difficulty S–M. Complements the tissue-aware-eQTL-coverage-checker already in the backlog: that asks "is the right tissue sampled?"; this asks "does my lead SNP mean the same thing across ancestries I'm pooling?" — both are pre-flight checks before trusting a pooled modifier signal.

What to verify first

  • n=30 fragility: the French effect's confidence interval is wide; the direction (not just magnitude) needs independent replication before it's trusted as LD-driven rather than noise.
  • Causal variant unidentified: any LD-divergence flag is only as good as the candidate-causal set fed to it — fine-mapping must come first.
  • Don't conflate questions: the lung-modifier null in untreated patients is consistent across studies; the pharmacogenomic signal must not leak into a baseline-lung-modifier claim.
  • Field context: reconcile against Eastman 2021's well-powered null before treating rs7512462 as an actionable ivacaftor-response marker.
  • "What we'd need to check," not a settled design; nothing here describes CFFPR internals or any decided analysis.

Gap-map / catalog

  • Closes: adds a large French-cohort confirmation that SLC26A9 is NOT a baseline-lung modifier, and a worked example of an ancestry/LD-driven direction flip for the non-functional tag rs7512462 in ivacaftor pharmacogenomics.
  • Leaves open: the causal variant at the locus (rs7512462 is only a tag); whether the pharmacogenomic effect is real at all given Eastman 2021's null; the mechanism by which SLC26A9 variants would modulate ivacaftor response (hypothesized via SLC26A9–CFTR-G551D interaction / trafficking); transferability of any effect to the modulator-combination era (e.g., elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor).

Read full transcript (28 turns · ~1654 words)

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读paper时间。Today's Track C paper is "SLC26A9 Gene Is Associated With Lung Function Response to Ivacaftor in Patients With Cystic Fibrosis," by Harriet Corvol and colleagues, published in Frontiers in Pharmacology in 2018. 今天 Track C 的论文是 Harriet Corvol 等人 2018 年发表在《药理学前沿》(Frontiers in Pharmacology) 上的"SLC26A9 基因与囊性纤维化患者对 ivacaftor 的肺功能反应相关"。It's open access and we have the full text. 它是开放获取的，我们拿到了全文。We're reading it because SLC26A9 is one of the most pleiotropic genes in our modifier catalog, and this paper is a pharmacogenomic test of it — does a modifier SNP predict how well a CFTR drug works? 我们读它，是因为 SLC26A9 是我们修饰基因目录里最具多效性 (pleiotropic) 的基因之一，而这篇论文是对它的一次药物基因组学检验——一个修饰位点能否预测一种 CFTR 药物的疗效？

JORDAN Let me prime the biology before we get into it. 在深入之前，让我先把生物学背景铺垫一下。What is ivacaftor actually doing, and what's a "gating mutation"? ivacaftor 到底在做什么，什么又是"门控突变"(gating mutation)？

ALEX CFTR is a chloride channel that sits on the apical surface of epithelial cells and opens and closes to let chloride out. CFTR 是一个氯离子通道，位于上皮细胞的顶端表面，通过开合让氯离子流出。A gating mutation — the classic one is G551D — lets the channel reach the surface and fold correctly, but it barely opens; the gate is stuck. 一个门控突变——经典的那个是 G551D——能让通道到达细胞表面并正确折叠，但它几乎不开；那扇门卡住了。Ivacaftor is a "potentiator": it binds CFTR directly and increases the probability that the gate is open. ivacaftor 是一种"增效剂"(potentiator)：它直接结合 CFTR，提高那扇门处于打开状态的概率。So for gating mutations it can produce large gains in lung function, which is why it was the first CFTR modulator approved, back in 2012. 所以对门控突变，它能带来肺功能的大幅改善，这就是为什么它是 2012 年最早获批的 CFTR 调节剂。The catch is that gating mutations are rare — only about 4% of people with CF carry one. 问题在于门控突变很罕见——只有约 4% 的 CF 患者携带。

JORDAN And SLC26A9 — remind me where it fits? 那 SLC26A9——提醒我一下它在哪里？

ALEX SLC26A9 is itself a chloride channel, expressed in the airway, that physically interacts with CFTR and is thought to provide an alternative or supporting chloride conductance. SLC26A9 本身是一个氯离子通道，表达于气道，它与 CFTR 有物理相互作用，被认为提供一种替代或支持性的氯离子传导。In our catalog it's the gene with a CFRD signal, a meconium-ileus signal, and a prenatal-pancreas signal — genuinely pleiotropic across CF organs. 在我们的目录里，它是同时带有 CFRD（囊性纤维化相关糖尿病）信号、胎粪性肠梗阻信号、和产前胰腺信号的基因——在 CF 各器官间是真正多效的。The lead variant here is rs7512462, and the key genomics point is that it is not presumed to be functional itself. 这里的主导变异是 rs7512462，关键的基因组学要点是：它本身并不被假定是功能性的。It's a tag SNP — a marker in linkage disequilibrium with whatever the true causal variant is. 它是一个标签位点 (tag SNP)——一个与真正致病变异处于连锁不平衡 (linkage disequilibrium, LD) 状态的标记。

JORDAN Linkage disequilibrium meaning it's inherited together with the real culprit? 连锁不平衡的意思是它和真正的元凶被一起遗传？

ALEX Exactly — nearby variants travel together on the same haplotype block, so a tag SNP stands in for an unmeasured causal allele it's correlated with. 正是——邻近的变异在同一个单倍型区块 (haplotype) 上一起传递，所以标签位点替代了一个与它相关、却未被测量的致病等位基因。Hold onto that, because it's the crux of the whole paper's puzzle. 记住这一点，因为它是整篇论文谜题的关键。

JORDAN So what was known going in, and what were they testing? 那么开始之前已知什么，他们又在检验什么？

ALEX The background is a 2014 study by Strug and colleagues that made two claims. 背景是 Strug 等人 2014 年的一项研究，提出了两个论断。First, that rs7512462 in SLC26A9 was associated with lung-function variability in people carrying a gating mutation. 第一，SLC26A9 中的 rs7512462 与携带门控突变的人群的肺功能变异相关。Second, and more striking, that the same SNP explained about 28% of the variability in how well patients responded to ivacaftor. 第二，更引人注目的是，同一个位点解释了患者对 ivacaftor 反应好坏约 28% 的变异。If that held, it would be a real pharmacogenomic marker — a cheap genotype that predicts drug response. 如果这成立，那将是一个真实的药物基因组学标记——一个能预测药物反应的廉价基因型。Corvol's group set out to test it in an independent and larger cohort: the French CF Gene Modifier Study, with 4,840 patients. Corvol 的团队着手在一个独立且更大的队列中检验它：法国 CF 修饰基因研究，共 4,840 名患者。

JORDAN Walk me through the cohort and how they measured response. 给我讲讲队列，以及他们如何测量反应。

ALEX They genotyped seven SLC26A9 SNPs across the whole 4,840 using KASP, a PCR-based allele-specific assay — cheap, targeted, not array or sequencing. 他们用 KASP——一种基于 PCR 的等位基因特异性检测，便宜、靶向、不是芯片也不是测序——对全部 4,840 人的七个 SLC26A9 位点做了基因分型。Lung function was the percent-predicted FEV1, the standard CF lung measure, also expressed as a normalized CF-specific Z-score they call SaKnorm. 肺功能用的是占预计值百分比的一秒用力呼气量 (FEV1pp)，CF 的标准肺功能指标，也表达为他们称作 SaKnorm 的标准化 CF 专用 Z 分数。But the drug-response analysis is where the numbers collapse. 但药物反应分析正是数字坍缩的地方。Of 4,840 patients, only 119 carried an ivacaftor-approved gating mutation, 81 were actually prescribed the drug, 60 had before-and-after lung measurements, and only 30 met the inclusion criteria — older than six, baseline FEV1 between 40 and 90%. 在 4,840 人中，只有 119 人携带 ivacaftor 获批的门控突变，81 人实际被处方了该药，60 人有用药前后的肺功能测量，而只有 30 人符合纳入标准——大于六岁、基线 FEV1 在 40% 到 90% 之间。

JORDAN Thirty. 三十个。That's the design choice I'd push on. 这就是我想深究的设计选择。

ALEX It's the right thing to push on, and to their credit they're transparent about it — the funnel from 4,840 down to 30 is laid out explicitly in a flowchart. 这正是该深究的，而值得肯定的是他们对此很透明——从 4,840 漏到 30 的整个漏斗在一张流程图里被明确画出。The reason is structural: gating mutations are rare, ivacaftor was new in this window, and a third of treated patients were missing a clean baseline. 原因是结构性的：门控突变罕见，ivacaftor 在这个时间窗里是新药，而三分之一被治疗的患者缺少一个干净的基线。So this is a real, larger cohort for the lung-phenotype question — thousands of patients — but the pharmacogenomic question rests on dozens. 所以对肺表型问题这是一个真实、更大的队列——数千名患者——但药物基因组学问题却建立在几十人之上。That asymmetry is the thing to carry through the results. 这个不对称是要贯穿到结果里去看的东西。

JORDAN Okay — what did they find? 好——他们发现了什么？

ALEX Two clean results and one strange one. 两个干净的结果，一个奇怪的结果。Clean result one: in untreated patients, SLC26A9 variants were not associated with lung-function variability — for any CFTR genotype group, rs7512462 and the other six SNPs all came back null. 干净结果之一：在未治疗的患者中，SLC26A9 变异与肺功能变异不相关——对任何 CFTR 基因型组，rs7512462 和其余六个位点全部为零结果。That agrees with the big international GWAS, where the lung-modifier hits are at other loci — the mucins, SLC9A3, SLC6A14, the HLA region — not SLC26A9. 这与大型国际 GWAS 一致，其中肺修饰基因的命中位于其他位点——黏蛋白基因、SLC9A3、SLC6A14、HLA 区域——而不是 SLC26A9。Clean result two: the patients on ivacaftor did improve, right in line with the trials — about +11.7% FEV1 at the early timepoint for gating-mutation carriers, and +14.4% for the G551D subset. 干净结果之二：用 ivacaftor 的患者确实改善了，与临床试验吻合——门控突变携带者在早期时间点约 +11.7% FEV1，G551D 亚组 +14.4%。

JORDAN And the strange one? 那个奇怪的呢？

ALEX They confirmed that rs7512462 was associated with ivacaftor response — but in the opposite direction from Strug. 他们确认了 rs7512462 与 ivacaftor 反应相关——但方向与 Strug 相反。In the French G551D carriers, each copy of the C allele was associated with about 7.7% less FEV1 improvement, both early and over the first year, with p around 0.0007. 在法国的 G551D 携带者中，每多一个 C 等位基因，与 FEV1 改善减少约 7.7% 相关，早期和第一年都如此，p 约为 0.0007。In the original study, the C allele had been associated with more improvement, roughly +8.5% per allele. 而在最初的研究里，C 等位基因曾与改善增多相关，约 +8.5% 每个等位基因。Same SNP, same drug, opposite sign. 同一个位点，同一种药，相反的符号。Other SLC26A9 SNPs and a haplotype analysis pointed the same way as the French rs7512462 result. 其他 SLC26A9 位点和一个单倍型分析都指向与法国 rs7512462 结果相同的方向。

JORDAN How can the same variant flip direction between two cohorts? 同一个变异怎么会在两个队列之间方向翻转？

ALEX This is the genomics lesson of the paper, and it comes straight back to the tag-SNP point. 这是这篇论文的基因组学教益，它直接回到标签位点那个要点。Because rs7512462 isn't functional, its association is borrowed entirely from the causal variant it's in LD with. 因为 rs7512462 不是功能性的，它的关联完全是从与它处于 LD 的致病变异那里借来的。If the LD relationship — which allele of the tag travels with which allele of the causal variant — differs between populations, the tag can point the opposite way even when the underlying biology is identical. 如果 LD 关系——标签的哪个等位基因与致病变异的哪个等位基因一起传递——在不同人群间不同，那么即使底层生物学完全相同，标签也可能指向相反的方向。The authors note the North American CF cohorts had substantial population admixture, while the French cohort is predominantly of European ancestry. 作者指出北美 CF 队列有显著的人群混合 (admixture)，而法国队列以欧洲血统为主。So an ancestry-dependent LD difference is the leading explanation for the sign flip. 所以血统依赖的 LD 差异是这个符号翻转的首要解释。

JORDAN So the direction flip isn't necessarily a contradiction — it could be two different tags of the same truth. 所以方向翻转不一定是矛盾——可能是同一个真相的两个不同标签。

ALEX Right, and that's exactly why a non-functional lead variant is fragile as a clinical marker. 没错，这正是为什么一个非功能性的主导变异作为临床标记是脆弱的。It's also worth saying where the field landed after this. 也值得说说这之后这个领域走到了哪里。We covered Eastman 2021 a few episodes back — a well-powered, multi-cohort study that found no association between rs7512462 and ivacaftor response, with over 97% power to detect the originally reported effect. 我们几期前讲过 Eastman 2021——一项有充分检验力、多队列的研究，发现 rs7512462 与 ivacaftor 反应之间没有关联，且有超过 97% 的检验力去检测最初报告的效应。So today's paper is one of the conflicting positive-but-inverse signals that the later, larger null had to reconcile. 所以今天这篇论文，是后来那个更大的零结果必须去调和的、相互冲突的"阳性但方向相反"信号之一。

JORDAN So where does this land for us, and what could we build? 那么这对我们落在哪里，我们能造点什么？

ALEX This lands on summary-stats colocalization and fine-mapping. 这落在汇总统计共定位 (colocalization) 与精细定位 (fine-mapping) 上。The buildable artifact is a cross-cohort direction-of-effect and LD-divergence checker for modifier tag SNPs. 可造的成品是一个面向修饰基因标签位点的"跨队列效应方向与 LD 散度检查器"。The MVP: for each catalogued modifier locus, take the lead SNP, check whether it's a non-functional tag, and pull its LD pattern with nearby candidate causal variants from a reference panel across multiple ancestries — then flag loci where the tag's LD with the likely causal allele diverges by ancestry, which predicts exactly this kind of direction flip before you ever pool cohorts. MVP：对每个已编目的修饰位点，取其主导位点，检查它是否为非功能性标签，再从一个跨多血统的参考面板中拉取它与邻近候选致病变异的 LD 模式——然后标出那些"标签与可能致病等位基因之间的 LD 因血统而散度大"的位点，这恰恰能在你合并队列之前就预测出这类方向翻转。Input is the catalogued modifier lead-SNP list; difficulty small-to-medium. 输入是已编目的修饰位点主导 SNP 列表；难度为小到中 (S–M)。

JORDAN How does that sit next to the eQTL-coverage checker we already logged? 这和我们已经记录的 eQTL 覆盖检查器是什么关系？

ALEX They're complementary guardrails for the same colocalization workflow. 它们是同一个共定位工作流的互补护栏。The eQTL-coverage checker asks "is the right tissue even sampled?"; this one asks "does my lead SNP mean the same thing across the ancestries I'm pooling?" eQTL 覆盖检查器问的是"正确的组织到底有没有被采样？"；这个问的是"我的主导位点在我要合并的各血统间是否含义一致？"Both are pre-flight checks you run before trusting a pooled modifier signal — and to be careful, the transfer here is methodological; nothing in this episode describes any decided analysis or anything about CFFPR internals. 两者都是在你信任一个合并的修饰信号之前要做的飞行前检查——而要谨慎说明，这里的迁移是方法学层面的；本期没有任何内容描述任何已定的分析，也不涉及 CFFPR 内部情况。
