Blurb

The direct quantitative follow-on to yesterday's PAPSS2 reclassification episode: Nikolovski et al. (2026, J Biol Chem) built a high-throughput functional screen — cell-based transport assays paired with laser-ablation mass spectrometry (LA-ICP-TOF-MS), plus flow cytometry for protein localization — and systematically tested 33 naturally occurring missense variants in the extracellular domain of ZIP8 (SLC39A8), a divalent metal transporter unrelated to CFTR. Fourteen of the 33 variants (over 40%) showed significantly reduced transport activity. Comparing measured activity against AlphaMissense's computational pathogenicity scores gave only a moderate correlation (R² = 0.423) and a roughly 20% outright error rate, and most loss-of-function variants turned out to be trafficking defects — the protein never reaching the cell surface — rather than transporters that reached the membrane but failed to move metal. ZIP8 causes a congenital disorder of glycosylation and is separately linked to schizophrenia and blood-pressure loci; CFTR and cystic fibrosis are never mentioned in the paper. The transfer to Karen Raraigh's CFTR VUS work — that a single computational score, even a strong one, is not a substitute for functional evidence — is this podcast's own framing, built around a proposed AlphaMissense-discordance flag list for CFTR VUS. Abstract-only: no PMCID on record, article subscription-gated.

Plain-English glossary

  • AlphaMissense — A deep learning model built on the AlphaFold2 architecture that scores missense variant pathogenicity on a 0–1 scale.（中文：一个建立在AlphaFold2架构上的深度学习模型，以0到1量表给错义变异的致病性打分。）
  • Extracellular domain (ECD) — The part of a membrane protein that sits outside the cell, facing the surrounding fluid, as opposed to segments buried in or inside the membrane.（中文：胞外结构域，膜蛋白中位于细胞外部、面向周围液体的部分，与埋藏在膜内或细胞内的部分相对。）
  • LA-ICP-TOF-MS (laser ablation inductively coupled plasma time-of-flight mass spectrometry) — A technique that vaporizes a tiny sample spot with a laser, ionizes it in a plasma torch, and weighs the resulting ions by time-of-flight, giving a fast, direct readout of metal content inside cells.（中文：激光剥蚀电感耦合等离子体飞行时间质谱，用激光气化样品微小斑点、在等离子体炬中电离，再通过飞行时间称量离子，快速直接读出细胞内的金属含量。）
  • Missense variant — A DNA change that substitutes one amino acid for another in the resulting protein, without truncating it.（中文：错义变异，指DNA变化导致蛋白质中一个氨基酸被替换为另一个，但蛋白质本身不被截断。）
  • Trafficking defect — When a protein is synthesized correctly at the sequence level but fails to reach its proper working location (e.g. the cell surface), often because a misfolded protein is retained at an internal quality-control checkpoint.（中文：转运缺陷，指蛋白质在序列层面被正确合成，却未能到达其应发挥作用的位置（如细胞表面），常因折叠错误的蛋白质被内部质量控制机制截留所致。）
  • ZIP8 / SLC39A8 — A divalent metal transporter (gene symbol SLC39A8) that moves manganese, zinc, and iron across the cell membrane; loss of function causes a congenital disorder of glycosylation.（中文：一种二价金属转运蛋白（基因符号SLC39A8），负责将锰、锌、铁运送穿过细胞膜；功能丧失会导致先天性糖基化障碍。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Pairing a scalable cell-based functional assay with LA-ICP-TOF-MS for direct, quantitative readout of transporter activity, at a throughput (~150 samples/hour) that supports systematic variant screening rather than one-off case studies.
  • Flow cytometry as a companion assay to separate "transporter doesn't work" from "transporter never reached the membrane" — a mechanistic split that changes the interpretation of a loss-of-function call.
  • Computing an outright error rate between computational score and measured function, not just a correlation coefficient — a more actionable number for deciding how much to trust a tool.
  • Scoping a functional screen to one structurally accessible protein domain first, as a way to make systematic testing tractable — with the caveat of checking whether findings generalize beyond that domain.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority served: Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation (one only).

Artifact: AlphaMissense-discordance flag list for CFTR VUS. Difficulty M.

MVP line: pull AlphaMissense's bulk precomputed scores for every CFTR missense variant currently classified as VUS in ClinVar, join against existing CFTR functional-assay data (CFTR2's own functional classifications, published Sosnay-era functional datasets), and flag variants where a confident AlphaMissense call and the available functional data disagree — prioritizing the discordant tail for human review rather than treating it as computationally settled.

The AlphaMissense and ClinVar pulls are both straightforward bulk downloads; the real work is cleanly joining them against heterogeneous, differently-formatted CFTR functional-assay sources.

What to verify first

  • Is the 0.423 R² / ~20% error rate specific to ZIP8's extracellular domain, or would it hold for variants elsewhere in the protein — and how does CFTR's own structural class (an ABC-transporter chloride channel) compare to a divalent metal transporter for AlphaMissense reliability?
  • Does AlphaMissense's own training/validation data already include enough transporter-family variants that this result is confirmatory rather than novel?
  • Does AlphaMissense's error run in one direction more than the other (true-pathogenic-called-benign vs. true-benign-called-pathogenic) — the paper's abstract doesn't say, and the two failure modes have very different consequences for a CFTR flag list.
  • Is CFTR2's existing functional-assay dataset granular and structured enough to join against a bulk AlphaMissense pull without substantial manual harmonization first?
