Track C (2/2) — Automated Data Cleaning of Paediatric Anthropometric Data (Southampton Protocol)

Blurb

Phan, Borca, Cable, Batchelor, Davies, and Ennis (University Hospital Southampton / University of Southampton, Scientific Reports, 2020) — the most-cited reference across this literature's seed anchors — built a four-tier decision cascade for cleaning longitudinal paediatric height and weight data: WHO biologically-implausible-value (BIV) thresholds, sparse-data implausible-increment/decrement rules, robust-regression outlier flagging (Cook's distance/DFFITS/DFBETAS) for patients with ≥4 datapoints, and an adult-height stability check. Tuned against a physician-curated gold standard (6,279 weight patients, 4,396 height patients, ≥7 datapoints each), the protocol hit sensitivity >0.9 at κ=2 (height) and κ=2.9 (weight), passing 92%/97.4% of height/weight data automatically and leaving 5.8%/2.6% for manual review — with an explicitly reported false-negative rate (0.06% height, 0.02% weight) at the chosen operating point. Applied to 68,595 patients / ~360,000 measurements, the paper also breaks error rates down by data-entry staff occupation and department. Code and a Docker container are public. This paper's central contribution — an honest sensitivity-vs-review-queue-size tuning curve — is a template for the Liz assignment's review-budget problem. Buildable artifact: cascade-review-budget-simulator (M-difficulty).

Plain-English glossary

  • WHO biologically-implausible-value (BIV) thresholds — fixed z-score cutoffs (e.g., height-for-age outside [−6,6]) published by WHO to exclude physiologically impossible values before any further modeling.（中文：WHO发布的固定z评分截断线（如身高年龄别z评分超出[−6,6]）,用于在进一步建模之前排除生理上不可能的数值。）
  • Robust regression via influence diagnostics (Cook's distance, DFFITS, DFBETAS) — statistics that flag individual datapoints whose removal would substantially change a fitted regression line; used here to iteratively re-fit a within-child growth trajectory robust to the outliers it's detecting.（中文：用于标记"剔除后会显著改变拟合回归线"的个别数据点的统计量;此处用于对同一儿童的生长轨迹做迭代重新拟合,使其对正在检测的离群点具有稳健性。）
  • Kappa (κ, SD-fold threshold) — the tunable standard-deviation-multiple cutoff used to call a regression residual an outlier; tuning κ trades sensitivity against the size of the flagged-for-review set.（中文：用于判定回归残差是否为离群点的、可调节的标准差倍数阈值;调节κ值会在灵敏度和待复核数据量之间做权衡。）
  • Adult-height stability check — since final adult height is reached by ~18 years, any post-18 measurement deviating >1cm from a patient's median adult-era height is flagged, applied regardless of datapoint count.（中文：由于成年身高约在18岁达到,18岁后任何偏离该患者成年期身高中位数超过1厘米的测量值都会被标记,不论数据点数量。）
  • Gold-standard tuning curve (sensitivity vs. PPV / review burden) — plotting sensitivity and positive predictive value across a range of threshold settings against manually-curated ground truth, to choose an operating point deliberately rather than by convention.（中文：在一系列阈值设置下,针对人工审核的真值数据绘制灵敏度与阳性预测值曲线,以便有意识地选择操作点,而非凭惯例设定。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • The four-tier decision cascade (WHO BIV → sparse-data rules → robust-regression outlier flags → adult-height stability check) as a general pipeline architecture.
  • The κ-sweep tuning methodology against a gold-standard set, reporting sensitivity, PPV, and the explicit false-negative rate at the chosen operating point.
  • The public code + Docker container (referenced in the paper) implementing the full protocol.
  • Error-rate breakdown by data-entry staff occupation/department, as a distinct data-quality audit angle.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (c) the human review budget. The paper's central contribution is a tunable threshold that trades sensitivity against manual-review queue size, with a documented false-negative cost at the chosen setting — directly the shape of the ~0.05%-needing-adjudication framing. Buildable artifact: cascade-review-budget-simulator — implement the multi-tier cascade against synthetic longitudinal height/weight data and produce a sensitivity-vs-review-queue-size curve like the paper's own tuning figure, so a chosen κ can be justified against an explicit sensitivity floor. Difficulty: M (lowered by the paper's public code/Docker container).

What to verify first

  • What fraction of a given population actually clears the ≥4 (or ≥7, for the richest gold-standard tuning) datapoint bar for the robust-regression tier, versus falling back to the cruder sparse-data rules.
  • Which specific Cook's-distance/DFFITS/DFBETAS cutoffs the paper's public code actually uses, since suggested thresholds vary across sources.
  • Whether an equivalent "who entered this measurement, in what role" field is even captured in a way that would support the same occupation/department audit angle.
  • This paper says nothing about CFFPR's current review capacity, existing thresholds, or how many records need adjudication today — not assumed either way.

Error types covered / not covered

Covered: outright BIV violations; sparse-data implausible increments/decrements (weight/height change over fixed time windows); within-child regression outliers (general implausible trajectory deviation); adult-height instability/decrease. Not explicitly covered: unit-conversion errors, digit transposition, or decimal-place shifts as distinct error categories (these may be captured incidentally by the BIV/regression tiers but aren't separately modeled); explicit real-clinical-event-vs-error discrimination beyond the >18-year adult-height logic.

ALEX A couple of things worth naming. 有几个概念值得点出来。This paper leans on WHO Child Growth Standards biologically-implausible-value, or BIV, thresholds as its first-pass filter — fixed cutoffs on the standard deviation score, like a height-for-age z-score outside minus-six to six, that the WHO itself publishes as a floor for excluding physiologically impossible values before any more sophisticated modeling happens. 这篇论文用WHO儿童生长标准里的"生物学上不合理值"(biologically implausible value,BIV)阈值作为第一道筛选——这是标准差评分上的固定截断线,比如身高年龄别z评分落在负六到六之外,由WHO官方发布,作为在任何更复杂的建模之前先排除生理上不可能数值的底线。The other new piece is robust regression, applied here specifically through influence diagnostics — Cook's distance, DFFITS, and DFBETAS — which flag individual datapoints whose removal would substantially change the fitted regression line; the paper temporarily drops high-influence points, re-estimates the line from the remaining data, and uses that re-fit line as the reference for the standard-deviation-fold outlier threshold. 另一个新概念是稳健回归,这里具体是通过影响力诊断量来实现的——Cook距离、DFFITS和DFBETAS——它们标记出那些一旦被剔除就会显著改变拟合回归线的个别数据点;论文的做法是先临时剔除高影响力的点,用剩下的数据重新估计回归线,再用这条重新拟合的线作为标准差倍数离群阈值的参照基准。That's a meaningfully different design philosophy from this morning's within-child moving average — this is a within-child linear regression across that child's own longitudinal series, robustified against the outliers it's trying to detect. 这和今天早上那种个体内部移动平均的设计思路有明显不同——这里是对同一个孩子自己的纵向序列做个体内部线性回归,并对它正试图检测的那些离群点做了稳健化处理。

ALEX The authors frame it around a specific institutional need: University Hospital Southampton rolled out an Electronic Growth Chart across departments starting in 2013, which meant a sudden, sustained increase in the volume of routinely-collected anthropometric data available for research — but data entered for routine clinical care by busy end-users is prone to typographical and default-value entry errors, and there wasn't yet a published, general protocol for periodic (not perfectly regular) longitudinal anthropometric cleaning that handled the full range of visit frequencies a real clinical population produces. 作者是围绕一个具体的机构需求来展开的:南安普顿大学医院从2013年起在各科室推行电子生长曲线图,这意味着可用于研究的常规采集人体测量数据量突然持续增长——但由繁忙的一线工作人员为常规临床护理录入的数据,容易出现打字错误和默认值录入错误,而当时还没有一个已发表的、通用的周期性(而非完全规律的)纵向人体测量数据清洗协议,能够处理真实临床人群所产生的各种就诊频率。They're explicit that prior methods either only worked for annual, regularly-spaced measurements, or used the jack-knife residual method, which needs at least four datapoints per patient but can be too strict — rejecting real, pronounced fluctuations that are clinically normal, like a child gaining or losing significant weight around a treatment period. 他们明确指出,此前的方法要么只适用于每年一次、间隔规律的测量,要么使用jack-knife残差法——这种方法需要每位患者至少四个数据点,但可能过于严格——会拒绝掉那些临床上正常、但波动明显的真实变化,比如一个孩子在治疗前后体重出现明显增减。So the gap wasn't "no automated method exists," it was "no automated method handles the messy, non-periodic, variable-frequency reality of routine clinical data without being either too lax or too strict." 所以这里的缺口不是"没有自动化方法",而是"没有一种自动化方法能在不过松或过严的前提下,处理常规临床数据里那种杂乱、非周期、就诊频率不固定的真实情况"。
