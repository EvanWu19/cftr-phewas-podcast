How did this episode land for you?

AI-CURA — an automated LLM workflow for high-accuracy variant classification (Track A)

Blurb. Ma et al. (Science Translational Medicine, 2026) present AI-CURA, a workflow that nearly fully automates ACMG/AMP/ClinGen variant classification by splitting the evidence into two streams: non-literature criteria (absence from population databases, splice/null signals) computed deterministically with standard bioinformatic tools, and literature-based criteria assessed by a reasoning LLM. Careful prompt engineering plus ACMG-rule-specific knowledgebases let DeepSeek-R1 outperform o3-mini-high, reaching high sensitivity and 100% specificity on literature-dependent rules; on 150 ClinGen-curated variants the full pipeline showed high concordance with human curators, and it was also used to reanalyze 150 ClinVar variants with conflicting interpretations. Read closed-access, so abstract-only, flagged on-air. Where it lands: the Karen collaboration / CFTR variant interpretation — the buildable artifact is a cftr-vus-acmg-agent that produces an auditable, cited evidence table per ACMG code rather than a black-box verdict.

Plain-English glossary.

  • ACMG/AMP framework — the standard rulebook of ~28 evidence codes whose combination classifies a variant as pathogenic, likely pathogenic, VUS, likely benign, or benign.（中文：ACMG/AMP框架是一套约28条证据代码的规则手册，其组合把变异判定为致病/可能致病/VUS/可能良性/良性五级。）
  • ClinGen — NIH-funded consortium that refines and gene-specifies the ACMG rules.（中文：ClinGen是NIH资助的联盟，负责细化并按基因特化ACMG规则。）
  • Co-segregation — a variant tracking with disease across affected family members, evidence used by codes like PP1.（中文：共分离指变异在家系中与疾病一起遗传，是PP1等代码所用的证据。）
  • HGVS — the standardized nomenclature for writing variants, e.g. c.1521_1523delCTT for F508del.（中文：HGVS是变异命名的标准化书写规范，例如F508del写作c.1521_1523delCTT。）
  • Missense / nonsense (null) variant — missense swaps one amino acid; nonsense inserts a premature stop, typically abolishing the protein.（中文：错义变异替换一个氨基酸；无义变异插入提前终止密码子，通常使蛋白无法产生。）
  • PVS1 / PM2 / PS3 / PP1 — example ACMG codes: null variant in a LOF gene / absence from population databases / functional-assay evidence / family co-segregation.（中文：这些是ACMG代码示例：功能缺失基因中的无效变异／人群数据库中缺失／功能实验证据／家系共分离。）
  • VUS — variant of uncertain significance; cannot currently be called pathogenic or benign.（中文：VUS即意义未明变异，目前无法判定致病或良性。）

Full citation. Ma W, Fong G, Lai J, Wu H, Hue SPY, Ying D, Chen L, Tang W, Preusch C, Chu ATW, Chung BHY. AI-CURA, an automated LLM workflow for high-accuracy genetic variant classification. Science Translational Medicine, 2026. PMID: 42341082 · PMC: n/a (closed access) · DOI: 10.1126/scitranslmed.adz4172

Methods / tools to lift.

  • Split ACMG evidence into a deterministic stream (bioinformatic tools: gnomAD frequency for PM2, SpliceAI/null signals for PVS1) and a literature stream (LLM-assessed).
  • Prompt the LLM one rule at a time with a defined evidence question, never "classify this variant."
  • Build ACMG-rule-specific knowledgebases so the model reasons against a grounded corpus, not open-ended memory.
  • Use reasoning-style LLMs (DeepSeek-R1 > o3-mini-high here) whose intermediate steps are auditable.
  • Reuse the pipeline for reanalysis of conflicting-interpretation variants, not just fresh classification.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Serves Karen / CFTR variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP: cftr-vus-acmg-agent (M–L) — input a CFTR variant in HGVS → split ACMG criteria into deterministic (PM2 from gnomAD, PVS1/splice from SpliceAI, etc.) vs literature; a reasoning LLM answers one CFTR-specific rule at a time against a curated CFTR knowledgebase (CFTR2 + ClinVar + primary functional studies), returning a source citation per claim → output an evidence table (one row per ACMG code: value, source, confidence flag) + provisional classification. Pairs with the CFTR-calibrated PP3/BP4 thresholds from the 2026-07-28 calibration episode.

What to verify first.

  • Knowledgebase freshness — how it is refreshed and whether variants with new functional evidence get stale answers.
  • CFTR-specific calibration — 100% specificity on a defined set is not out-of-distribution guarantee; a genome-wide benchmark can hide gene-specific failure.
  • Citation faithfulness + human sign-off — every literature claim must carry a real, checkable citation, with a human in the loop before clinical use.
  • No claim about any registry's internal genotype storage — external method mapped onto public variant evidence only.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读paper时间。 Today's Track A paper is "AI-CURA, an automated LLM workflow for high-accuracy genetic variant classification," by Ma, Fong, Lai, and colleagues out of Hong Kong, published in Science Translational Medicine in 2026. 今天Track A的论文是《AI-CURA：一套用于高准确度遗传变异分类的自动化大语言模型工作流》，作者是来自香港的Ma、Fong、Lai等人，2026年发表于《Science Translational Medicine》。 One honest caveat up front: this paper is closed-access, so we are working from the structured abstract only, and I will flag anywhere I am inferring beyond it. 先坦白一个前提：这篇论文不是开放获取的，所以我们只能依据结构化摘要来讲，凡是我超出摘要去推断的地方，我都会明确标注。 We are reading it because it is the most direct template yet for the Karen collaboration: an auditable pipeline that automates ACMG variant classification while keeping the evidence chain reviewable. 我们读它，是因为它是目前对Karen合作最直接的一个模板：一套可审计的流水线，能自动完成ACMG变异分类，同时让整条证据链保持可复核。

JORDAN Before we go further, walk me through the vocabulary, because this is the genomics layer I am still ramping on. 在往下讲之前，先带我过一遍术语，因为这正是我还在补的基因组学部分。 What is the ACMG framework, and what does it mean to "classify" a variant? ACMG框架是什么？给一个变异"分类"又是什么意思？

ALEX The ACMG/AMP framework is a rulebook for deciding whether a genetic variant is pathogenic, benign, or somewhere in between. ACMG/AMP框架是一套规则手册，用来判定一个遗传变异是致病的、良性的、还是介于两者之间。 ACMG is the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics, and AMP is the Association for Molecular Pathology. ACMG是美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会，AMP是分子病理学协会。 The rulebook lists roughly 28 evidence codes — things like PVS1 for a null variant in a gene where loss of function causes disease, PM2 for absence from population databases, PS3 for functional-assay evidence, PP1 for co-segregation in families. 这套手册列出了大约28条证据代码——比如PVS1表示"在一个功能缺失即致病的基因中出现的无效变异"，PM2表示"在人群数据库中不存在"，PS3表示"功能实验提供的证据"，PP1表示"在家系中的共分离"。 You tally the codes that apply, and their combination maps to a five-tier call: pathogenic, likely pathogenic, VUS, likely benign, benign. 你把适用的代码逐条累加，它们的组合再映射到五级判定：致病、可能致病、VUS、可能良性、良性。 VUS means "variant of uncertain significance" — the clinic cannot currently call it either way, and it is the pile this whole field is trying to shrink. VUS指"意义未明变异 (variant of uncertain significance)"，也就是临床目前无法判定方向的那一堆，而缩小这一堆正是整个领域努力的方向。 ClinGen is the NIH-funded consortium that refines and specifies these rules gene by gene. ClinGen是由NIH资助的联盟，负责逐个基因地细化和特化这些规则。

JORDAN So why is this a hard thing to automate — isn't it just applying a checklist? 那这为什么难以自动化——不就是套一张清单吗？

ALEX That is the key insight of the paper, and it is worth sitting with. 这正是这篇论文的关键洞见，值得停下来体会。 The codes split into two very different kinds of work. 这些代码分成两类截然不同的工作。 Some are essentially database lookups: is the variant absent from gnomAD, does a splice predictor flag it, is it a nonsense change in a critical exon? 有些本质上是数据库查询：这个变异在gnomAD里是否缺失、剪接预测工具是否标记它、它是否是关键外显子里的无义 (nonsense) 改变？ Those are non-literature criteria, and standard bioinformatic tools already produce them deterministically. 这些是"非文献类"标准，标准的生物信息学工具已经能确定性地给出结果。 But other codes require reading the published literature — has a functional study shown this variant abolishes protein function, has it been reported to co-segregate with disease across families? 但另一些代码需要阅读已发表文献——是否有功能研究显示该变异会破坏蛋白功能、是否有报道显示它在多个家系中与疾病共分离？ That reading step is unstructured, judgment-heavy, and exactly where human curators spend their hours. 这个"读文献"的步骤是非结构化的、高度依赖判断的，也正是人类审阅者耗费大量时间的地方。 AI-CURA's whole architecture is built around that split. AI-CURA的整个架构就是围绕这一分割搭建的。

JORDAN Give me the background — what was unknown or contested before this? 给我讲讲背景——在此之前，有什么是未知或有争议的？

ALEX Large language models have been thrown at all sorts of medical tasks, but clinical genetics — and rare-disease diagnosis specifically — stayed underexplored. 大语言模型已经被用于各种医疗任务，但临床遗传学——尤其是罕见病诊断——一直探索得不够。 There is a good reason for the caution: classification errors here change a diagnosis, so a black box that outputs "likely pathogenic" with no traceable reasoning is clinically useless, no matter how accurate on average. 谨慎是有道理的：这里的分类错误会改变一个诊断，所以一个只输出"可能致病"却没有可追溯推理的黑箱，无论平均准确率多高，在临床上都是无用的。 What changed recently is that reasoning-style LLMs — models that expose an explicit chain of thought — became good enough that their intermediate steps can be audited. 最近发生变化的是，推理型大语言模型——那些会展开明确思维链的模型——变得足够好，以至于它们的中间步骤可以被审计。 That reopens the door: you are no longer asking the model for a verdict, you are asking it to summarize specific literature evidence against a specific rule, and a human can check that summary. 这重新打开了一扇门：你不再是向模型索要一个裁决，而是让它针对某一条具体规则去归纳具体的文献证据，然后由人来核查这份归纳。 The contested question the paper enters is whether an LLM can do the literature-reading part reliably enough to trust, and which model. 这篇论文切入的争议问题是：大语言模型能否把"读文献"这部分做得可靠到值得信任，以及该用哪个模型。

JORDAN How big is the VUS problem in practice — is this a marginal cleanup or a real bottleneck? VUS问题在实践中有多大——这是边角料的清理，还是真正的瓶颈？

ALEX It is a real bottleneck, and it is the reason this paper matters at all. 它是一个真正的瓶颈，也正是这篇论文之所以重要的原因。 Public variant databases hold hundreds of thousands of entries filed as uncertain, and every one is a place where a clinical answer stalls. 公开的变异数据库里存着数十万条被标为"不确定"的记录，每一条都是一个临床答案卡住的地方。 Expert panels curate them by hand, which is careful but slow, and it does not scale to the volume of sequencing now happening. 专家小组靠人工整理，这很严谨但很慢，跟不上如今测序产生的数据量。 Each hand-curated variant can consume real reading hours, and the queue only grows as more people get sequenced. 每一个人工整理的变异都可能消耗大量阅读时间，而随着更多人接受测序，这个队列只会越来越长。 So a tool that automates the reading-heavy criteria while staying auditable does not just save minutes — it changes whether the backlog is tractable at all. 所以一个既能自动化那些"读文献"密集的标准、又保持可审计的工具，省下的不只是几分钟——它改变的是这个积压是否根本可解。 That is the framing to hold: this is throughput on a problem where throughput has been the wall. 记住这个视角：在一个"吞吐量一直是那堵墙"的问题上，这做的正是吞吐量。

JORDAN And what does a "reasoning" LLM buy you over an ordinary one here? 在这里，"推理型"大语言模型相比普通模型能带来什么？

ALEX The difference is that a reasoning model exposes intermediate steps you can inspect, rather than jumping straight to an answer. 区别在于，推理型模型会展开可供你检查的中间步骤，而不是直接跳到答案。 For a clinical task, that transparency is not a nicety — it is the whole point, because a curator has to be able to see why a criterion was called met and check it against the source. 对临床任务而言，这种透明不是锦上添花——它就是关键，因为审阅者必须能看到某条标准为什么被判为"成立"，并对照来源去核查。 An opaque model that is right 95% of the time is still unusable if you cannot tell which 5% it got wrong. 一个不透明、95%的时候正确的模型，如果你无法分辨它错在哪5%，那它依然不可用。 So the reasoning capability is what makes the literature stream auditable at all. 所以正是这种推理能力，才让"文献流"变得可审计。

JORDAN So how did they actually build it? 那他们到底是怎么搭的？

ALEX AI-CURA nearly fully automates classification by handling the two evidence streams separately and then combining them. AI-CURA通过分别处理两条证据流、再加以合并，几乎完全自动化了分类。 The non-literature criteria are computed with standard bioinformatic tools — the deterministic, reproducible part. 非文献类标准用标准生物信息学工具计算——这是确定性、可复现的部分。 The literature-based criteria are handed to an LLM, but not naively. 文献类标准交给大语言模型，但不是简单粗暴地交出去。 They did two things that matter. 他们做了两件关键的事。 First, careful prompt engineering, so the model is asked one rule at a time with a defined evidence question rather than "classify this variant." 第一，精细的提示工程，让模型一次只针对一条规则、带着一个明确的证据问题被提问，而不是"给这个变异分类"。 Second, they built ACMG-rule-specific knowledgebases — curated reference material tied to each rule — so the model reasons against a grounded corpus instead of its open-ended memory. 第二，他们构建了针对每条ACMG规则的知识库——与每条规则绑定的、经过整理的参考资料——让模型基于一个有依据的语料去推理，而不是依赖它开放式的记忆。 They tested two reasoning models head to head: DeepSeek-R1 and o3-mini-high. 他们让两个推理模型正面对决：DeepSeek-R1和o3-mini-high。 The design choice I would push on is the knowledgebase itself — its coverage and freshness silently bound the whole system, and the abstract does not tell us how it was assembled or maintained. 我想质疑的设计选择是知识库本身——它的覆盖面和时效性会悄悄地为整个系统设上限，而摘要并没有告诉我们它是如何组装或维护的。

JORDAN Why does asking one rule at a time matter so much — couldn't you just ask for the whole classification and save steps? 为什么"一次问一条规则"这么重要——难道不能直接要整个分类结果、省去这些步骤吗？

ALEX You could, and that is exactly the design they avoided, for a good reason. 你可以那样做，而这恰恰是他们刻意避开的设计，理由很充分。 If you ask a model to classify a variant end to end, its mistakes become entangled — you cannot tell whether it misread a paper, misapplied a rule, or mis-tallied the codes. 如果你让模型端到端地分类一个变异，它的错误就会纠缠在一起——你分不清它是误读了论文、误用了规则、还是把代码累加错了。 By decomposing into one evidence question per rule, each answer is small, scoped, and independently checkable, and the deterministic tally happens outside the model. 通过拆解成"每条规则一个证据问题"，每个答案都很小、范围明确、可独立核查，而确定性的累加则在模型之外完成。 That is the same discipline you would use in any pipeline where you want to localize failures rather than debug a monolith. 这与你在任何"想要定位失效、而不是去调试一个庞然大物"的流水线里会采用的纪律是一样的。 It also means the deterministic codes never depend on the LLM at all — the model only ever touches the literature questions, which shrinks its blast radius. 它还意味着确定性的代码根本不依赖大语言模型——模型只碰文献类问题，这就缩小了它的"爆炸半径"。

JORDAN And the results — does it work? 结果呢——它有效吗？

ALEX On the literature-reading task, DeepSeek-R1 outperformed o3-mini-high and, in interpreting the ACMG rules that hinge on literature evidence, achieved high sensitivity and 100% specificity. 在读文献这一任务上，DeepSeek-R1胜过o3-mini-high，并且在解读那些依赖文献证据的ACMG规则时，达到了高灵敏度和100%的特异度。 The specificity number is the one to hold onto: it means when the model asserted a literature-based criterion was met, it was not inventing support. 特异度这个数字值得记住：它意味着当模型断言某条文献类标准成立时，它并没有在凭空捏造依据。 That is the failure mode everyone fears with LLMs on evidence tasks, and it is the one they measured against directly. 这正是所有人对"让大语言模型做证据类任务"最担心的失效模式，而他们正是直接冲着它去做的衡量。 Then, on 150 variants that ClinGen experts had already curated, the full pipeline showed high concordance with the human curators on the final call. 接着，在150个已由ClinGen专家整理过的变异上，整套流水线在最终判定上与人类审阅者高度一致。 And in a third test, they pointed it at 150 ClinVar variants that carry conflicting interpretations and used it for reanalysis. 在第三项测试中，他们把它对准了150个在ClinVar中存在相互冲突解读的变异，用它来做重新分析。 That last one is the quietly important use case — the conflicting-interpretation pile is a real, growing problem, and an auditable reanalysis engine is exactly what it needs. 最后这一项是低调却重要的用例——"解读冲突"的那一堆是一个真实且在增长的问题，而一台可审计的重分析引擎正是它所需要的。

JORDAN Why is reanalysis a distinct problem from first-pass classification? 为什么"重新分析"和"初次分类"是两个不同的问题？

ALEX Because conflicting-interpretation variants already have submissions pointing in different directions, so the task is not to reach a call from scratch — it is to adjudicate. 因为"解读冲突"的变异已经有指向不同方向的提交记录了，所以任务不是从零得出一个判定——而是去裁决。 That is harder in a way that rewards exactly this architecture: you need to see which evidence codes each submitter likely relied on and re-derive them transparently, so a reviewer can locate where the disagreement lives. 这在某种意义上更难，而它恰恰奖励这种架构：你需要看出每个提交者可能依据了哪些证据代码，并透明地重新推导它们，好让审阅者能定位分歧究竟在哪。 A black-box reclassifier that just outputs a new label gives you a third opinion with no way to reconcile the first two; an auditable one shows its work and lets you resolve the conflict on the evidence. 一个只输出新标签的黑箱重分类器，只是给了你第三种意见，却无法调和前两种；而一个可审计的重分类器会展示它的推导过程，让你在证据层面解决冲突。 For CFTR specifically, the conflicting pile is where a lot of the clinically consequential ambiguity actually sits, so this is not a side feature — it may be the most useful mode. 具体到CFTR，"解读冲突"的那一堆正是大量具有临床后果的模糊性真正所在，所以这不是一个附带功能——它可能是最有用的模式。

JORDAN So where does this land for us, concretely? 那么对我们来说，这具体落在哪里？

ALEX This lands squarely on the Karen collaboration and CFTR variant interpretation. 这正好落在Karen合作和CFTR变异解读上。 CFTR has a long tail of variants sitting as VUS, and the bottleneck is precisely the literature-reading labor AI-CURA automates. CFTR有一长串以VUS状态存在的变异，而瓶颈恰恰就是AI-CURA所自动化的那部分"读文献"的劳动。 The buildable artifact I would prototype is a cftr-vus-acmg-agent. 我想做出来的可搭建产物是一个 cftr-vus-acmg-agent。 The MVP: input a single CFTR variant in HGVS notation; the tool splits the ACMG criteria into the deterministic set — pulling PM2 from gnomAD frequency, PVS1/splice signals from SpliceAI, and so on with standard tools — and the literature set. 最小可行版本：输入一个用HGVS书写的CFTR变异；工具把ACMG标准拆成确定性那一组——从gnomAD频率取PM2、从SpliceAI取PVS1/剪接信号，等等，都用标准工具——以及文献那一组。 HGVS is the standardized nomenclature for writing variants — for example c.1521_1523delCTT for F508del.
