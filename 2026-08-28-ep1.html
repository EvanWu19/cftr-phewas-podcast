ClinGen gives risk alleles their own vocabulary

The ClinGen Low Penetrance/Risk Allele Working Group's 2023 recommendations paper formally splits "risk allele" and "low-penetrance variant" off from the highly penetrant Mendelian variants that ACMG/AMP was built for — variants where segregation logic breaks down because plenty of carriers stay healthy. Built through internal and community Likert-scaled surveys plus expert consensus (not a benchmark study), the paper delivers preferred terminology, a framework for reviewing and classifying risk-allele evidence, and guidance for reporting these variants clinically so a "risk allele, moderate evidence" label doesn't get misread as a yes/no diagnosis. This maps directly onto CFTR2's "varying clinical consequence" genotypes, which are functionally risk alleles in a recessive-disease context and currently lack a shared curation vocabulary. The buildable artifact is a structured evidence-intake template — not a scoring classifier, since the point-value rubric isn't visible at abstract length — that operationalizes the paper's evidence categories into a spreadsheet, seeded first against CFTR2's existing varying-clinical-consequence list.

Plain-English glossary

  • Penetrance — the probability that a person with a given genotype actually develops the associated phenotype.（中文：携带特定基因型的人实际表现出相应表型的概率。）
  • Risk allele — a genetic variant defined by its association with elevated disease risk at the population level, typically via case-control or cohort data, rather than by causing disease outright.（中文：一种通过在人群层面与疾病风险升高相关联而定义的遗传变异，通常来自病例对照或队列数据，而非直接致病。）
  • Low-penetrance variant — a variant where only a fraction of carriers develop the associated phenotype; related to but distinct from "risk allele."（中文：只有部分携带者会表现出相应表型的变异；与"风险等位基因"相关但不完全相同。）
  • Segregation (analysis) — evidence that a variant travels together with disease status through a family pedigree; the classic evidence type for high-penetrance Mendelian variants, which mostly breaks down for risk alleles.（中文：变异在家系谱系中与疾病状态共同遗传的证据；是高外显率孟德尔变异的经典证据类型，但对风险等位基因大多不适用。）
  • Odds ratio (OR) — the case-control measure of how much more common a variant is in affected individuals versus unaffected controls; the primary quantitative evidence type for risk alleles.（中文：病例对照研究中衡量某变异在患病者中出现频率相对未患病对照组高出多少的指标；是风险等位基因的主要定量证据类型。）
  • ACMG/AMP — the 2015 American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology framework for classifying variants as pathogenic through benign; scoped to highly penetrant, largely monogenic disease.（中文：2015年由美国医学遗传学与基因组学学院/分子病理学会发布的变异分类框架（致病至良性），适用范围限定于高外显率、基本单基因的疾病。）
  • ClinGen — the Clinical Genome Resource, the field's NIH-funded consortium that develops consensus standards for variant curation and classification.（中文：Clinical Genome Resource，由美国NIH资助的联盟，负责制定变异整理与分类的共识标准。）
  • Varying clinical consequence (CFTR2 category) — a CFTR2 classification for genotypes that sometimes cause CF and sometimes don't, functionally analogous to a risk allele in a recessive-disease context.（中文：CFTR2数据库中的一个分类，指有时导致囊性纤维化、有时不导致的基因型，在隐性遗传病背景下功能上类似于风险等位基因。）
