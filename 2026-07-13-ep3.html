How did this episode land for you?

Blurb

Shyr and colleagues build a generalizable EHR-based framework for growth modeling across genetic disorders: 22 years of Vanderbilt records, 452,470 pediatric patients, 2,253,058 height measurements, 15 genetically confirmed conditions. GAMLSS produces condition-specific population centile charts; SITAR decomposes each individual's messy longitudinal trajectory into exactly three interpretable scalars — size, timing (age at peak height velocity), and intensity (peak height velocity). The CF-relevant result: stratifying 553 CF patients by CFTR functional class, boys with M/M (homozygous minimal-function) genotypes were 5.2 cm shorter at maximum height (95% CI −9.7, −0.8) with 1.1 cm/year lower growth intensity (95% CI −2.7, −0.1) than M/R (minimal/residual) boys — and, critically, the two groups tracked together through childhood and diverged only in adolescence. A genotype effect that lives in an age window is invisible to any analysis that collapses a trajectory into one number. The framework was validated by treating OMIM/Orphanet HPO annotations as a labelled benchmark (sensitivity 0.89; 16/18 positive controls recovered; 22 undocumented patterns surfaced). Lands on Liz / phenotype characterization & data quality (external method only). Buildable artifact: sitar-phenotyper.

Plain-English glossary

CFTR functional class (minimal vs residual function) — CFTR variants are graded by how much working protein reaches the cell surface. Minimal function (M) leaves essentially none (nonsense, frameshift, severe splicing defects); residual function (R) leaves some. M/M = both inherited copies minimal-function; M/R = one of each. This is a severity axis — distinct from homozygous-vs-compound-heterozygous, which is not.（中文：CFTR 变异按“最终有多少有功能的蛋白到达细胞表面”分级。最小功能 (M) 基本不留下有功能的蛋白；残余功能 (R) 留下一些。M/M 表示两个拷贝都是最小功能，M/R 表示一个最小功能加一个残余功能。这是一个严重程度的轴——与“纯合 vs 复合杂合”不同。）

GAMLSS (Generalized Additive Models for Location, Scale and Shape) — regression that models the mean, the variance, and the skewness as smooth functions of age; the machinery behind population centile charts.（中文：把均值、方差和偏度都建模为年龄的平滑函数的回归方法；构建人群百分位图的底层机器。）

Growth centile chart — describes the distribution of a measurement in a population at each age (the CDC/WHO charts on a pediatrician's wall).（中文：描述某测量值在人群中每个年龄上的分布，即儿科诊室墙上的 CDC/WHO 图表。）

Growth curve — describes how one individual's measurements change with age. Not the same object as a centile chart.（中文：描述单个个体的测量值如何随年龄变化。与百分位图不是同一个东西。）

HPO (Human Phenotype Ontology) — the standardized vocabulary of clinical phenotype terms ("short stature," "delayed puberty") used to annotate conditions in OMIM and Orphanet. Categorical, not quantitative — which is the gap this paper attacks.（中文：用于在 OMIM 和 Orphanet 中标注疾病的标准化临床表型术语表，如“矮身材”“青春期延迟”。它是分类式的、非定量的——这正是本文攻击的空白。）

Peak height velocity (intensity) / age at peak height velocity (timing) — the fastest growth rate during the pubertal spurt (cm/year), and the age at which it occurs.（中文：青春期猛长阶段的最快生长速率（厘米/年）称为强度；达到该速率的年龄称为时机。）

SITAR (SuperImposition by Translation And Rotation) — assumes everyone shares one underlying growth-curve shape; each person differs from the mean curve by exactly three random effects — a vertical shift (size), a horizontal shift (timing), and a time-axis stretch (intensity). Turns an irregular trajectory into three interpretable scalars you can regress on.（中文：假设所有人共享同一条底层生长曲线的形状；每个人与平均曲线的差别恰由三个随机效应决定——上下平移（尺寸）、左右平移（时机）、时间轴拉伸（强度）。它把一条不规则轨迹变成三个可回归的可解释标量。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • SITAR as a phenotype-derivation layer — decompose a longitudinal trajectory into (size, timing, intensity) per person, then run the association on the three scalars rather than on a collapsed z-score. This is what makes an adolescence-only genotype effect visible instead of averaging it into a null.
  • Genotype as a fixed effect on all three SITAR parameters — a single model, with M/M vs M/R entered on size, timing, and intensity simultaneously, gives you three interpretable contrasts with CIs from one fit.
  • GAMLSS for centiles — models variance and skewness as functions of age, not just the mean; required when the spread of a trait changes with age.
  • Knowledge base as a labelled benchmark (the most portable idea in the paper) — treat OMIM/Orphanet HPO annotations as positive controls, score your data-driven method's sensitivity against them (here: 0.89, 16/18), and read the annotation-negative disagreements as a discovery list. One table yields both a validation metric and results. It also surfaces knowledge-base self-contradictions (NF1 annotated as both "short stature" and "tall stature").
  • Iterative, re-derivable references — charts regenerated as EHR data accrue, rather than frozen historical cohorts. Directly relevant to CF, where pre-modulator growth charts describe a different disease than children have today.

Ship it with a knowledge-base benchmark harness bolted on: pull the HPO growth annotations for the condition under study, score the method's sensitivity against them as positive controls, and only then read the disagreements as candidate findings. The benchmark is what converts a plotting exercise into a validated instrument.

What to verify first

  • Who the "unaffected" cohort actually is. 449,513 people with ≥3 encounters at an academic medical centre is not a healthy population — it is an ascertained one, with a median of only 4 height measurements each (vs 28 for CF). Every condition-vs-unaffected contrast inherits that reference group's selection and its measurement sparsity.
  • Treatment era is unmodelled. 22 years of CF care spans the arrival of CFTR modulators. The authors flag stratification by treatment exposure as future work — so the M/M vs M/R contrast today mixes biology with who received which drug when, and this design cannot separate them.
  • The sex difference may be power, not biology. No significant finding in girls ≠ no effect in girls. Both significant CIs in boys nearly touch zero (−0.8 cm; −0.1 cm/year). Group sizes and a power calculation are needed before interpreting the asymmetry.
  • Multiplicity. 15 conditions × 3 parameters × 2 sexes, with no correction described. The 22 "new growth patterns" should be read as a hypothesis list, not a discovery list.
  • SITAR's core assumption is stretched hardest exactly where we care most. The authors concede SITAR "may not be well suited to capture highly irregular or non-monotonic growth patterns that result from episodic illness" — and episodic illness is cystic fibrosis (exacerbations, hospitalizations, nutritional crises).
  • Single centre. Everything here requires external replication.

Gap-map / catalog

Closes:

  • A quantified, EHR-derived CF growth phenotype stratified by CFTR functional class (M/M vs M/R), with effect sizes and CIs on three separate axes (size, timing, intensity) rather than a single stature deficit.
  • A documented instance of a genotype effect confined to an age window (childhood convergence → adolescent divergence in boys), demonstrating the failure mode of trajectory-collapsing analyses.
  • A demonstration that CF carries no growth HPO annotation in either OMIM or Orphanet — a knowledge-base gap the data fills.

Leaves open:

  • Whether the M/M vs M/R growth gap survives adjustment for CFTR modulator exposure and treatment era — the single largest confounder, explicitly unmodelled.
  • Whether the female null is a real sex difference or a power artifact.
  • Whether SITAR's single-shape assumption is defensible in CF at all, given exacerbation-driven trajectory interruptions; a CF-specific model comparison against a more flexible spline/mixed-effects alternative is needed.
  • No new modifier gene — this paper is CFTR-intrinsic (functional class), not a modifier-locus finding. Growth remains a modifier-relevant trait with no modifier locus implicated here.

JORDAN Prime me. 给我打个底。

ALEX Three biology terms and two methods terms. 三个生物学词，两个方法学词。 Start with **CFTR functional class**. 先说 **CFTR 功能分类** (CFTR functional class)。 CFTR variants are sorted by how much working protein they leave behind. CFTR 变异是按“最后还剩下多少能干活的蛋白”来分类的。 **Minimal function** variants — abbreviated M — leave essentially no functioning CFTR at the cell surface: nonsense variants, frameshifts, severe splicing defects. **最小功能** (minimal function) 变异——缩写为 M——在细胞表面基本不留下任何有功能的 CFTR：包括无义变异、移码变异、严重的剪接缺陷。 **Residual function** variants — R — leave some. **残余功能** (residual function) 变异——R——则还留下一些。 So an **M/M genotype** means both inherited copies are minimal-function; **M/R** means one of each. 因此 **M/M 基因型**意味着遗传到的两个拷贝都是最小功能；**M/R** 则是一个最小功能加一个残余功能。 This is the axis that actually tracks CF severity — and note it is *not* the same as homozygous-versus-compound-heterozygous, which we took apart on this show last week. 这才是真正与 CF 严重程度相关的那个轴——请注意它*不同于*“纯合 vs 复合杂合”，那个我们上周在节目里已经拆解过了。 Two copies of two *different* minimal-function variants is still M/M. 携带两个*不同的*最小功能变异，依然是 M/M。

JORDAN Good — that distinction stuck. 好——那个区别我记住了。 And the growth terms? 那生长方面的词呢？

ALEX **Growth curve** versus **growth centile chart** — the paper is careful about this and you should be too. **生长曲线** (growth curve) 与**生长百分位图** (growth centile chart)——这篇论文对二者分得很清楚，你也应该。 A growth curve describes how *an individual's* measurements change with age. 生长曲线描述的是*一个个体*的测量值如何随年龄变化。 A centile chart describes the *distribution* of measurements in a population at each age — the CDC and WHO charts on your pediatrician's wall are centile charts. 百分位图描述的是*一个人群*在每个年龄上的测量值*分布*——你儿科医生墙上贴的 CDC 和 WHO 图表就是百分位图。 Then two derived quantities: **peak height velocity**, the fastest rate of growth during the pubertal spurt, in centimetres per year — that's growth **intensity** — and the **age at** peak height velocity — that's growth **timing**. 然后是两个派生量：**身高峰值速度** (peak height velocity)，即青春期猛长阶段的最快生长速率，单位厘米每年——这就是生长的**强度**；以及达到身高峰值速度的**年龄**——这就是生长的**时机**。 Size, timing, intensity. 尺寸、时机、强度。 Hold those three words; they are the whole paper. 记住这三个词；它们就是整篇论文。

JORDAN So what was broken that needed fixing? 那原本有什么问题需要解决？

ALEX Two things, and they compound. 两件事，而且它们相互叠加。 The first is that the *knowledge* about growth in genetic disease is written in adjectives. 第一，关于遗传病中生长异常的*知识*，是用形容词写的。 OMIM and Orphanet — the two canonical genetic-disease knowledge bases — annotate conditions with Human Phenotype Ontology terms like "short stature," "tall stature," "delayed puberty." OMIM 和 Orphanet——两大权威的遗传病知识库——用人类表型本体 (Human Phenotype Ontology, HPO) 的术语来标注疾病，比如“矮身材”“高身材”“青春期延迟”。 Those are categorical. 这些都是分类式的。 Short by how much? 矮多少？ Starting when? 从什么时候开始？ Growing how fast? 长得多快？ The annotations don't say. 标注里没说。

JORDAN And the second? 第二件呢？

ALEX The second is that where condition-specific growth charts *do* exist — Down syndrome, Turner, Prader-Willi — they were mostly built from historical cohorts, before the treatments that reshaped the natural history of these diseases, and mostly from patients with severe, classic presentations. 第二，即使某些疾病*确实*有专属的生长图表——唐氏综合征、特纳综合征、普拉德-威利综合征——它们大多是从历史队列建立的，早于那些重塑疾病自然史的治疗手段出现之前，而且大多来自表现严重、典型的病人。 For CF that critique lands hard. 对 CF 来说，这个批评格外扎心。 A growth chart built before CFTR modulators describes a different disease than the one children have now. 一张在 CFTR 调节剂 (CFTR modulator) 出现之前建立的生长图表，描述的是一种与今天孩子们所患的疾病不同的病。

JORDAN So what did they build? 那他们建了什么？

ALEX A six-step framework over 22 years of electronic health records at a single academic medical centre — Vanderbilt. 一个建立在单一学术医疗中心（范德堡）22 年电子病历之上的六步框架。 452,470 pediatric patients with at least three encounters between 2002 and 2023; 2,253,058 height measurements between ages 2 and 20. 452,470 名儿科病人，2002 到 2023 年间至少有三次就诊；年龄 2 到 20 岁之间共 2,253,058 条身高测量。 Fifteen genetic conditions with genetically confirmed diagnoses, plus everybody else as the unaffected comparison group. 十五种经遗传学确诊的疾病，其余所有人作为未受累对照组。 Cystic fibrosis: 553 patients, 291 boys and 262 girls, with a median of 28 height measurements each. 囊性纤维化：553 名病人，291 名男孩、262 名女孩，每人中位数 28 次身高测量。 Note that number against the overall cohort median of four. 请把这个数字和整体队列中位数 4 次做个对比。 CF kids are measured constantly; that density is what makes the CF analysis possible. CF 患儿被反复测量；正是这种密度让 CF 的分析成为可能。

JORDAN And the two methods? 那两个方法呢？

ALEX **GAMLSS** — Generalized Additive Models for Location, Scale, and Shape — builds the population centile charts. **GAMLSS**（位置、尺度与形状的广义可加模型）用来构建人群百分位图。 The key idea is that it models not just the mean as a smooth function of age, but the *variance* and the *skewness* too, which is what you need if the spread of heights changes with age — and it does. 关键思想是：它建模的不只是均值随年龄的平滑变化，还包括*方差*和*偏度*，而当身高的离散程度随年龄变化时（事实确实如此），你正需要这一点。 That's the centile machinery. 这就是造百分位图的机器。 The interesting one is the other one. 有意思的是另一个。

JORDAN **SITAR.** **SITAR**。

ALEX SITAR — SuperImposition by Translation And Rotation. SITAR——通过平移与旋转实现的曲线叠合 (SuperImposition by Translation And Rotation)。 The premise: everybody's growth curve is the *same shape*. 它的前提是：每个人的生长曲线都是*同一个形状*。 There is one mean curve, and each individual differs from it by exactly three numbers — shift it up or down (**size**), shift it left or right in time (**timing**), and stretch or compress its time axis (**intensity**). 存在一条平均曲线，而每个个体与它的差别恰好由三个数字决定——上下平移（**尺寸**）、在时间上左右平移（**时机**）、以及拉伸或压缩时间轴（**强度**）。 Three random effects per person. 每人三个随机效应。 That is the whole trick, and it is a genuinely elegant one: it takes a messy, irregularly sampled longitudinal trajectory and turns it into three interpretable scalars. 这就是全部的技巧，而且它确实优雅：它把一条杂乱、采样不规则的纵向轨迹，变成了三个可解释的标量。 Then you can regress *on* those scalars. 然后你可以*对这三个标量*做回归。

JORDAN Push back on it. 挑刺。

ALEX The premise is the weakness. 前提就是它的弱点。 SITAR assumes one underlying curve shape for everyone, and the authors concede this directly: it "may not be well suited to capture highly irregular or non-monotonic growth patterns that result from episodic illness." SITAR 假设所有人共享同一条底层曲线形状，而作者自己直接承认了这一点：它“可能不适合刻画由间歇性疾病导致的高度不规则或非单调的生长模式”。 Read that sentence again with CF in mind. 带着 CF 再读一遍这句话。 Episodic illness *is* cystic fibrosis. 间歇性疾病*就是*囊性纤维化。 Pulmonary exacerbations, hospitalizations, nutritional crises — a CF growth trajectory is precisely the kind of stepwise, interrupted curve SITAR is least equipped to model. 肺部急性加重、住院、营养危机——CF 的生长轨迹恰恰是那种阶梯式、被打断的曲线，也正是 SITAR 最不擅长建模的类型。 They apply the method to CF anyway, and they are right to — you go with the tool you have — but the assumption is being stretched hardest exactly where we care most. 他们还是把这个方法用到了 CF 上，这也没错——有什么工具就用什么工具——但这个假设被拉伸得最厉害的地方，恰恰是我们最在意的地方。

JORDAN Results. Give me the CF one. 结果。先给我 CF 那个。

ALEX They fitted a single SITAR model to CF patients with genotype — M/M versus M/R — entered as a fixed effect on all three parameters: size, timing, and intensity. 他们对有基因型的 CF 病人拟合了单一的 SITAR 模型，把 M/M 与 M/R 作为固定效应加在全部三个参数上：尺寸、时机、强度。 Among boys, M/M genotypes were associated with **5.2 centimetres shorter maximum height** — 95% confidence interval negative 9.7 to negative 0.8 — and **1.1 centimetres per year lower growth intensity**, CI negative 2.7 to negative 0.1. 在男孩中，M/M 基因型与**最大身高矮 5.2 厘米**相关——95% 置信区间为 −9.7 到 −0.8——以及**生长强度低 1.1 厘米/年**，置信区间 −2.7 到 −0.1。 Among girls, no significant differences in any of the three. 在女孩中，三个参数均无显著差异。 And the shape of the effect matters: M/M and M/R boys tracked similarly through childhood and **diverged during adolescence**, at the higher centiles. 而效应的*形状*也很重要：M/M 与 M/R 的男孩在整个童年期轨迹相近，却在**青春期发生分离**，且分离出现在较高百分位上。

JORDAN An age window. 一个年龄窗口。

ALEX An age window. 一个年龄窗口。 That's the sentence I want you to take from this episode. 这就是我希望你从这一集里带走的那句话。 A genetic effect on a quantitative trait need not be constant across life. 遗传因素对一个数量性状的效应，未必在整个生命过程中恒定。 If you collapse a CF child's twenty-eight height measurements into one number — a height-for-age z-score, say — and regress genotype on it, you will *average away* an effect that only exists in adolescence, and you will report a null. 如果你把一个 CF 患儿的二十八次身高测量压缩成一个数字——比如身高别 z 分数——然后拿基因型去回归它，你会把一个只在青春期存在的效应*平均掉*，然后报告一个阴性结果。 The three-parameter decomposition is what makes the window visible. 正是这个三参数分解，让那个窗口变得可见。

JORDAN What about the other fourteen conditions — do they look right? 那另外十四种疾病呢——结果看起来对吗？
