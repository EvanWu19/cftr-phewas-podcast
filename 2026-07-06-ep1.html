How did this episode land for you?

Show notes — Track A — Sequence UNET (Dunham et al. 2023)

Blurb. Sequence UNET is a fully convolutional, U-shaped deep-learning variant effect predictor (VEP) that scores variants from the wild-type amino acid sequence alone — no per-protein multiple sequence alignment or model retraining at inference. It is trained cheaply on a proxy task (predict the PSSM / classify whether a variant is rare, using ProteinNet's 104,059 PDB structures and frequencies from >332M sequences), then fine-tuned on ClinVar pathogenicity. It scores all 24,187 SARS-CoV-2 Spike variants in 420 ms (vs SIFT4G 22 min, FoldX 232 h), reaches ClinVar AUC 0.73 at a 1% frequency threshold, and lands just behind the much larger ESM-1v protein language model — trading a little accuracy for one-to-three orders of magnitude in speed. Where it lands: the Karen CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration, as the cheap first pass in a VUS triage funnel. Buildable artifact: a CFTR whole-protein pre-annotation scorer that returns a per-position deleteriousness matrix for every possible missense substitution, used to rank CFTR2 VUS before spending EVE/ESM compute on the shortlist.

Plain-English glossary

  • Allele frequency (as a deleteriousness proxy) — how common a variant is in a population; rare variants are more likely to be harmful, so rarity substitutes for a damage label.（中文：某变异在人群中的常见程度；越罕见越可能有害，故以罕见性替代"危害"标签。）
  • CASP — a blind protein-structure-prediction competition; its targets are used here as a hard, low-homology test set.（中文：CASP —— 蛋白质结构预测的盲测竞赛，其目标蛋白被用作低同源性的严苛测试集。）
  • Conservation (evolutionary) — positions unchanged across many species over evolutionary time are inferred to be functionally important.（中文：进化保守性 —— 在许多物种中长期不变的位点被推断为功能上重要。）
  • Deep mutational scanning (DMS) — an assay that experimentally measures the functional effect of thousands of a protein's variants at once.（中文：深度突变扫描 —— 一次性实验测量一个蛋白质数千种变异功能效应的方法。）
  • Missense variant — a single-nucleotide change that swaps one amino acid for another.（中文：错义变异 —— 使一个氨基酸被替换为另一个的单核苷酸改变。）
  • Multiple sequence alignment (MSA) — the same protein from many species aligned column-by-column to read off per-position variability.（中文：多序列比对 —— 把同一蛋白质在多物种中逐列对齐，读出各位点的变异程度。）
  • PSSM (position-specific scoring matrix) — a per-position table of how favored each amino acid is at that spot, derived from an alignment.（中文：PSSM —— 由比对得出、逐位点记录每种氨基酸受偏好程度的打分矩阵。）
  • VEP (variant effect predictor) — a tool that estimates how damaging an amino-acid change is likely to be.（中文：VEP —— 估计某氨基酸改变可能有多大危害的工具。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant with insufficient evidence to call pathogenic or benign.（中文：VUS —— 证据不足、无法判定致病或良性的变异。）

Full citation

Dunham AS, Beltrao P, AlQuraishi M. High-throughput deep learning variant effect prediction with Sequence UNET. Genome Biology 2023;24:110. PMID 37161576 · PMC PMC10169183 · DOI 10.1186/s13059-023-02948-3

Methods / tools to lift

  • Fully convolutional U-Net for sequence — compression/expansion CNN with skip connections; wide receptive field captures long-range interactions without an MSA at inference.
  • Proxy-task pre-training + fine-tuning — learn general sequence grammar cheaply (PSSM / rare-variant classification on ProteinNet), then fine-tune on scarce ClinVar labels.
  • Frequency-threshold tuning — the deleteriousness cutoff matters; 1% allele frequency was optimal for ClinVar (AUC 0.73), consistent with benign≈0.112 / pathogenic≈0.008 mean frequencies.
  • Optional GraphCNN structural module — residues within 10 Å treated as connected; opt-in, and for pathogenicity it slightly hurt — test before adding.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / CFTR variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP: CFTR whole-protein pre-annotation scorer — input a single CFTR FASTA sequence → output a per-position × per-amino-acid deleteriousness matrix plus a ranked list of highest predicted-damage positions; use to triage CFTR2 VUS before running EVE/ESM. Difficulty: S. Feeds the calibrated PP3/BP4 missense lane assembled from the earlier VariPred and feature-fusion episodes.

What to verify first

  • Structural/training bias — model trained on PDB-solved proteins; CFTR is a large multidomain membrane transporter in the underrepresented tail. Validate CFTR scores against CFTR-specific functional data.
  • Calibration vs ranking — AUC 0.73 orders variants but is not a probability calibrated to an ACMG evidence strength; treat output as triage rank.
  • ClinVar leakage — some CFTR variants are in ClinVar; test on post-training-cutoff variants or orthogonal functional assays.
  • No registry internals — external sequence-model method mapped onto CFTR only.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读书室。 Today's Track A paper is "High-throughput deep learning variant effect prediction with Sequence UNET," by Alistair Dunham and colleagues, published in Genome Biology in 2023. 今天 Track A 这一篇是 Alistair Dunham 等人 2023 年发表在《Genome Biology》上的《用 Sequence UNET 做高通量深度学习变异效应预测》。 We're reading it because it is a variant effect predictor built for speed and scale — fast enough to score every possible variant of a protein on a laptop. 我们读它，是因为它是一个为速度和规模而设计的变异效应预测器 (variant effect predictor)，快到可以在一台笔记本电脑上给一个蛋白质的每一种可能变异打分。 That scalability is exactly the property you want when the protein in question is CFTR and the goal is annotating the whole variant space. 当目标蛋白正是 CFTR、而任务是注释整个变异空间时，这种可扩展性正是你想要的性质。

JORDAN Before we go further, can we ground the vocabulary? 在往下讲之前，我们能先把词汇打好基础吗？ What exactly is a variant effect predictor doing? 变异效应预测器到底在做什么？

ALEX A variant effect predictor, or VEP, takes a change to a protein's amino acid sequence and estimates how damaging that change is likely to be. 变异效应预测器，也就是 VEP（根据序列或结构预测某个氨基酸改变有多大危害的工具），接收蛋白质氨基酸序列上的一个改变，估计这个改变可能有多大破坏性。 Most classic VEPs lean on evolutionary conservation, which is the idea that if a position has stayed the same across many species for millions of years, it is probably functionally important. 大多数经典 VEP 依赖进化保守性 (evolutionary conservation)，也就是说：如果某个位点在许多物种中数百万年保持不变，它很可能在功能上很重要。 They measure conservation with a multiple sequence alignment, or MSA — you line up the same protein from hundreds of species and read off how variable each column is. 它们用多序列比对 (multiple sequence alignment)，即 MSA（把同一蛋白质在数百个物种里对齐、逐列读出变异程度的方法），来度量保守性。 From that alignment you can build a PSSM, a position-specific scoring matrix, which is just a per-position table of how favored each amino acid is at that spot. 从这个比对可以构建一个 PSSM（position-specific scoring matrix，逐位点记录每种氨基酸在该处受偏好程度的打分矩阵）。 A rare or disfavored amino acid at a conserved position is a red flag. 在一个保守位点上出现罕见或不受偏好的氨基酸，就是一个危险信号。

JORDAN And where does the experimental ground truth come from? 那实验上的金标准从哪里来？

ALEX Two main places. 主要有两处。 One is deep mutational scanning, or DMS, an assay that experimentally measures the functional effect of thousands of variants of a protein at once. 一个是深度突变扫描 (deep mutational scanning)，即 DMS（一次性实验测量一个蛋白质数千种变异功能效应的实验方法）。 The other is clinical databases like ClinVar, where variants are labeled pathogenic or benign from patient evidence. 另一个是像 ClinVar 这样的临床数据库，其中变异根据患者证据被标注为致病或良性。 A third useful signal is population allele frequency — a variant that is common in healthy people is probably tolerated, so rarity becomes a rough proxy for deleteriousness. 第三个有用的信号是人群等位基因频率 (allele frequency)——在健康人群中常见的变异大概率是可耐受的，所以罕见性成了危害性的一个粗略替代指标。 That proxy is the clever hinge this whole paper turns on. 这个替代指标正是整篇论文巧妙的支点。

JORDAN Why not just always use the strongest model and wait? 为什么不干脆总是用最强的模型、然后等着呢？ ALEX: Because waiting does not scale to the phenome. 因为等待无法扩展到整个表型组。 If you want to score every missense variant across every gene in a registry-linked analysis, per-protein alignment-plus-training turns a weekend into a quarter. 如果你想在一个与登记库关联的分析里，给每个基因的每种错义变异都打分，逐蛋白的比对加训练会把一个周末拖成一个季度。 A model that amortizes all its cost into one training run and then answers in milliseconds is what makes exhaustive, genome-wide annotation even thinkable. 一个把全部成本摊进一次训练、之后毫秒级作答的模型，才让穷举式、全基因组范围的注释变得可想象。 That is the strategic reason speed is not a luxury here — it is what changes which questions you are allowed to ask. 这就是速度在这里不是奢侈品的战略原因——它决定了你被允许提出哪些问题。

JORDAN So what problem were the authors actually trying to solve? 那作者到底想解决什么问题？

ALEX The honest bottleneck in this field is not accuracy — it is cost. 这个领域真正的瓶颈不是准确率，而是成本。 The best neural VEPs at the time, models like EVE and DeepSequence, are genuinely strong, but they require building a multiple sequence alignment and training a separate latent-variable model for every single protein you want to score. 当时最好的神经网络 VEP，比如 EVE 和 DeepSequence，确实很强，但它们要求为你想打分的每一个蛋白质都单独构建一次多序列比对、并训练一个独立的潜变量模型。 That is fine for one protein and crippling for a proteome. 对一个蛋白质来说没问题，对整个蛋白质组来说就是灾难。 The authors quote concrete numbers: scoring all 24,187 possible variants of the SARS-CoV-2 Spike protein took SIFT4G twenty-two minutes and FoldX two hundred and thirty-two hours. 作者给出了具体数字：给 SARS-CoV-2 刺突蛋白全部 24,187 种可能变异打分，SIFT4G 用了二十二分钟，FoldX 用了二百三十二小时。 FoldX is a structure-based tool that estimates how a variant destabilizes the folded protein, and that physical modeling is expensive. FoldX 是一个基于结构的工具，估计变异如何破坏折叠蛋白的稳定性，而这种物理建模非常昂贵。 So the question was: can a single neural network, trained once, produce comparable predictions for any protein in milliseconds? 所以问题是：一个只训练一次的神经网络，能否在毫秒级为任意蛋白质给出可比的预测？

ALEX The reason this matters beyond convenience is that pre-computation breaks down when the space is too big. 这件事之所以不只是图方便，是因为当空间太大时预先计算就行不通了。 Precomputed databases like dbNSFP exist for human proteins, but the moment you want a non-model organism, a novel construct, or an exhaustive scan the databases do not cover, you are stuck regenerating everything. 像 dbNSFP 这样的预计算数据库对人类蛋白质是存在的，但一旦你想要一个非模式生物、一个新构建体、或数据库未覆盖的穷举扫描，你就得重新生成一切。 A lightweight model that runs anywhere removes that wall. 一个能在任何地方运行的轻量模型，拆掉了这堵墙。

JORDAN Okay, so what did they actually build? 好，那他们究竟造了什么？

ALEX The core is a fully convolutional neural network shaped like a U — hence "UNET." 核心是一个形状像字母 U 的全卷积神经网络 (fully convolutional neural network)，所以叫 "UNET"。 The architecture is borrowed from image segmentation: a compression path uses max pooling to shrink the sequence into successively coarser representations, then an expansion path rebuilds position-level detail, with skip connections carrying information across. 这个架构借自图像分割：压缩路径用最大池化把序列逐步缩成越来越粗的表示，然后扩展路径重建逐位点的细节，并用跳跃连接把信息横向传递过来。 The point of the U-shape is receptive field — neurons deep in the compression path see wide stretches of the protein at once, which lets a convolutional model capture the long-range interactions that normally need an alignment. U 形的意义在于感受野——压缩路径深处的神经元能一次看到蛋白质很宽的一段，这让一个卷积模型也能捕捉通常需要比对才能得到的长程相互作用。 Crucially it operates on the one-hot-encoded wild-type sequence alone, with no MSA at inference time. 关键在于，它只在独热编码 (one-hot) 的野生型序列上运行，推理时不需要 MSA。 There is also an optional graph convolutional module that folds in protein structure — treating residues within ten angstroms as connected — but structure is opt-in, not required. 还有一个可选的图卷积模块把蛋白质结构折进来——把十埃 (angstrom) 以内的残基视为相连——但结构是可选项，不是必需。 The whole thing is about 128 million parameters in TensorFlow. 整个模型在 TensorFlow 里大约有一亿两千八百万个参数。

ALEX The training trick is the interesting part. 训练的巧思才是有意思的地方。 Because there is nowhere near enough labeled pathogenic data to train a 128-million-parameter network directly, they first train it on a task where labels are effectively free — predicting the PSSM and classifying whether a variant is rare — using ProteinNet, which packages 104,059 PDB structures alongside variant frequencies distilled from over 332 million sequences. 因为标注过的致病数据远不足以直接训练一个一亿两千八百万参数的网络，他们先在一个标签几乎免费的任务上训练它——预测 PSSM、并分类某个变异是否罕见——用的是 ProteinNet，它把 104,059 个 PDB 结构与从三亿三千两百多万条序列提炼出的变异频率打包在一起。 Then they fine-tune that pre-trained backbone on ClinVar pathogenicity. 然后他们在 ClinVar 致病性上微调这个预训练主干。 It is transfer learning: learn general sequence grammar cheaply, then adapt to the scarce clinical labels. 这就是迁移学习：先便宜地学会通用的序列语法，再适配稀缺的临床标签。

JORDAN If you were going to push back on one design choice, what would it be? 如果要对某个设计选择提出质疑，你会挑哪一个？

ALEX The training set is drawn from ProteinNet, which is built from proteins that have solved PDB structures. 训练集取自 ProteinNet，而它是由已解出 PDB 结构的蛋白质构建的。 That is a biased sample — proteins that crystallize well are not representative of hard cases like large membrane transporters, and the authors themselves see performance drop on proteins without structural information. 这是一个有偏样本——容易结晶的蛋白质并不能代表像大型膜转运体这样的难例，而作者自己也看到在没有结构信息的蛋白质上性能下降。 CFTR is exactly the kind of awkward, multidomain membrane protein that sits in the underrepresented tail of that distribution, so I would not assume laptop-speed convenience comes for free on our target. CFTR 恰恰是那种别扭的、多结构域的膜蛋白，落在那个分布中被低估的尾部，所以我不会假设笔记本级的便利在我们的目标蛋白上是免费的。

JORDAN So how well does it actually work? 那它实际效果如何？

ALEX On raw speed, it is a different category — 420 milliseconds to score that entire Spike variant set after a one-time 85-second initialization, versus SIFT4G's twenty-two minutes and FoldX's two hundred-plus hours. 在纯速度上，它是另一个量级——一次性 85 秒初始化后，用 420 毫秒给整套刺突蛋白变异打分，而 SIFT4G 要二十二分钟、FoldX 要两百多小时。 On accuracy, the fine-tuned model reaches a ClinVar AUC of 0.73 at the best frequency threshold, which was a variant-frequency cutoff of one percent. 在准确率上，微调后的模型在最佳频率阈值下达到 ClinVar AUC 0.73，那个阈值是百分之一的变异频率界值。 They tested several thresholds — 0.1 gave 0.67, 0.01 gave 0.73, 0.001 gave 0.65, 0.0001 gave 0.66 — and one percent won. 他们测了几个阈值——0.1 得 0.67，0.01 得 0.73，0.001 得 0.65，0.0001 得 0.66——百分之一胜出。 That is a nice sanity check, because ClinVar benign variants average an allele frequency around 0.112 and pathogenic ones around 0.008, so the winning cutoff sits right between them. 这是个漂亮的自洽检验，因为 ClinVar 良性变异的平均等位基因频率约 0.112、致病的约 0.008，胜出的界值正好落在两者之间。

ALEX The comparative headline is that the fine-tuned Sequence UNET lands only slightly behind ESM-1v — a much larger protein language model — and actually does better on proteins that have structural data. 对比的重点是，微调后的 Sequence UNET 只比 ESM-1v（一个大得多的蛋白质语言模型）略差一点，而且在有结构数据的蛋白质上反而更好。 A protein language model, for the record, is a network trained to predict masked amino acids across millions of sequences, so it learns general protein grammar much the way a text model learns language. 顺带说明，蛋白质语言模型 (protein language model) 是一个被训练去预测数百万条序列中被遮盖氨基酸的网络，因此它像文本模型学语言那样学会了通用的蛋白质语法。 The models that clearly beat ESM-1v are all trained directly on human pathogenic variation and many are ensembles, so the authors caution their scores are probably inflated on data that overlaps their training. 那些明显胜过 ESM-1v 的模型都直接在人类致病变异上训练、且很多是集成模型，所以作者提醒它们在与训练重叠的数据上的分数很可能被高估了。 On deep mutational scanning correlations and a yeast gold-standard set, Sequence UNET generalizes but sits a notch below the top specialized predictors. 在深度突变扫描的相关性和一套酵母金标准上，Sequence UNET 能泛化，但比顶尖的专门预测器低一档。 The honest summary: it trades a small amount of accuracy for one to three orders of magnitude in speed. 诚实的总结是：它用一点点准确率，换来了一到三个数量级的速度。

JORDAN Does adding the structural module actually help? 加上结构模块真的有帮助吗？ ALEX: This is the counterintuitive result — for pathogenicity, turning on the structure input slightly reduced the fine-tuned model's performance. 这是个反直觉的结果——对致病性预测，打开结构输入反而让微调模型的表现略微下降。 The authors read that as structure being less directly related to pathogenicity than raw sequence conservation for this task, and they flag the graph module as one of the least-explored parts of the network. 作者把这解读为：对这个任务而言，结构比原始序列保守性与致病性的关联更弱，而且他们指出图模块是网络中被探索得最少的部分之一。 For CFTR that is a useful warning — do not assume bolting on an AlphaFold structure buys you accuracy; test it, because it might cost you. 对 CFTR 来说这是个有用的提醒——别以为接上一个 AlphaFold 结构就能买到准确率；要测试，因为它可能反而让你损失。

JORDAN So where does this land for us, and what could Runyu actually build? 那这对我们落在哪里，Runyu 具体能造什么？

ALEX This lands squarely on the Karen collaboration — CFTR variant interpretation. 这稳稳落在与 Karen 的合作上——CFTR 变异解读。 The transferable idea is using a fast, sequence-only model as the first pass in a triage funnel, not as the final arbiter. 可迁移的想法是：把一个快速、只需序列的模型当作分诊漏斗的第一道，而不是最终裁判。 The buildable artifact is a CFTR whole-protein pre-annotation scorer: feed the canonical CFTR amino acid sequence to Sequence UNET, get back a full matrix of deleteriousness scores for every possible missense substitution at every position, and use it to rank and bucket the CFTR2 variants of uncertain significance before you spend expensive EVE or ESM compute on the shortlist. 可造的产物是一个 CFTR 全蛋白预注释打分器：把标准 CFTR 氨基酸序列喂给 Sequence UNET，取回每个位点上每种可能错义替换 (missense) 的完整危害性打分矩阵，用它在你把昂贵的 EVE 或 ESM 算力花在候选名单之前，先对 CFTR2 里的意义未明变异 (variants of uncertain significance) 排序分桶。 The MVP is a script that takes one FASTA sequence and returns a per-position score table plus a flagged list of positions where predicted-damaging density is highest; input is just the CFTR sequence; difficulty is small. MVP 是一个脚本：输入一条 FASTA 序列，返回一张逐位点的打分表，外加一份标注了预测危害密度最高位点的清单；输入只是 CFTR 序列；难度是小 (S)。 It becomes the cheap wide funnel that feeds the calibrated PP3/BP4 missense lane we have been assembling from the earlier VariPred and feature-fusion episodes. 它就成了那个便宜的宽口漏斗，为我们从前面 VariPred 和特征融合几集里一直在组装的、经过校准的 PP3/BP4 错义证据通道 (变异致病性评级中的计算证据码) 供料。
