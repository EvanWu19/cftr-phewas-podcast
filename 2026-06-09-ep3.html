Blurb (~150w)

Schamschula and colleagues (J Cyst Fibros, 2021) used identical (monozygotic) twins who share the same severe CF genotype — p.Phe508del homozygous — but diverge clinically, drawn from the European Cystic Fibrosis Twin and Sibling Study. They selected the 3 most clinically concordant and 3 most discordant pairs and examined two postzygotic (after-conception) attributes that can differ even between identical twins: the CDR3 region of the T-cell receptor beta chain (immunotyping) and DNA methylation at 450,000 CpG sites. They found cell-type-independent methylation changes at selected positions and individual T-cell-receptor-repertoire profiles, interpreted as immune-system plasticity in genetically identical CF patients adapting to their environment. Proposed CF direction: within a genetically fixed stratum (e.g., F508del homozygotes), estimate the residual outcome variation left unexplained by all inherited variants as the quantitative "budget" for non-inherited factors. Open question: whether differences are causal vs incidental, and whether the cohort has the data layers and homozygote count to support this.

Plain-English glossary

  • 450K array: a chip measuring DNA methylation at ~450,000 CpG positions.
  • CDR3 region: the most variable part of the T-cell receptor, used as an immune "fingerprint."
  • CFTR: the gene whose protein forms a chloride channel; mutations cause cystic fibrosis.
  • Concordant / discordant (twins): pairs where both twins are similarly affected (concordant) vs one much sicker than the other (discordant).
  • CpG site: a specific DNA position where a methylation tag can attach.
  • Epigenetics: heritable-through-cell-division marks layered on top of the DNA sequence without changing the letters.
  • F508del / p.Phe508del: the most common CF mutation; the protein is missing the phenylalanine at position 508.
  • Homozygous: carrying the same variant on both copies of a gene.
  • Immunotyping: cataloguing a person's T-cell-receptor repertoire.
  • Methylation: a chemical (methyl) tag that switches genes on/off without changing DNA letters.
  • Modifier genes: other genes that tweak CF severity beyond CFTR itself.
  • Monozygotic (MZ) / identical twins: twins from one fertilized egg, sharing essentially the same inherited DNA.
  • Postzygotic: arising after the egg is fertilized — can differ even between identical twins.
  • T-cell receptor repertoire: the diverse collection of receptors across a person's T-cells, shaped by their infection history.
