Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): Does 3D structural context predict mutation effect?

Green AG, Tasmin M, Vargas R, and Farhat MR (eLife, 2026) ask a question that sits directly under every structure-aware CFTR variant-effect predictor, but they ask it in a different organism entirely: across the Mycobacterium tuberculosis proteome, does a mutation's 3D structural context — not just its position in the raw amino acid sequence — predict whether it confers antibiotic resistance? They curate high-confidence structural annotations for 1,350 crystallography structures and 2,337 AlphaFold-predicted structures, and match them against mutations drawn from more than 31,000 clinical M. tuberculosis isolates. A classifier built on a single feature — 3D distance from a given residue to the nearest known resistance site — reaches an F1 score of 96.5% on a held-out test set, and more than 450 proteins across the proteome show a clear signal of 3D mutation clustering, many with an independently known link to resistance. This episode maps the method onto the Karen/CFTR variant-interpretation priority: a cheap, interpretable "3D-proximity-to-known-pathogenic" score computed from CFTR's own structural models and the CFTR2 classified-variant list, layered on top of AlphaMissense/REVEL/CADD rather than replacing them — with an explicit built-in flag for CFTR domains too sparsely characterized for the distance feature to mean anything.

Plain-English glossary

  • X-ray crystallography — The traditional gold-standard method for determining a protein's real 3D atomic structure, by crystallizing the purified protein and diffracting X-rays through it.（中文：测定蛋白质真实三维原子结构的传统金标准方法，通过将纯化蛋白结晶后用X射线衍射成像来重建结构。）
  • AlphaFold — A deep-learning model that predicts a protein's 3D structure directly from its amino acid sequence, without needing an experimental crystal structure.（中文：一种深度学习模型，可直接从蛋白质的氨基酸序列预测其三维结构，不需要实验测定的晶体结构。）
  • Non-synonymous mutation — A DNA mutation that actually changes the resulting amino acid, as opposed to a silent mutation that leaves the protein sequence unchanged.（中文：实际改变了氨基酸的DNA突变，区别于不改变蛋白质序列的沉默突变。）
  • 3D mutation clustering — The phenomenon where mutations that are far apart in the linear amino acid sequence turn out to sit close together once the protein folds into its 3D shape, often inside a functionally important pocket or surface.（中文：指在线性氨基酸序列上相距很远的突变，在蛋白质折叠成三维结构后却彼此靠近，常常位于功能上重要的口袋或表面区域的现象。）
  • CFTR domain architecture (NBD1, NBD2, TMD, R domain) — CFTR's modular structure: two transmembrane domains (TMDs) that form the channel pore, two nucleotide-binding domains (NBD1, NBD2) that bind and hydrolyze ATP, and a regulatory (R) domain; pathogenic variants are unevenly characterized across these modules, with NBD1 the most densely studied.（中文：CFTR的模块化结构，包括构成通道孔的两个跨膜结构域（TMD）、结合并水解ATP的两个核苷酸结合结构域（NBD1、NBD2），以及一个调控（R）结构域；致病变异在这些模块间的研究深度并不均衡，其中NBD1被研究得最透彻。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Residue-to-nearest-known-site 3D distance — a single, cheap, interpretable geometric feature computed from a protein structure (crystallography or AlphaFold) plus a list of already-classified functional sites.
  • Proteome-wide structural annotation at AlphaFold scale — curating confident structural models (crystallography + AlphaFold) across an entire proteome rather than a handful of well-studied proteins, enabling population-level structural questions.
  • Clustering-signal test on "should-fail" cases — deliberately testing the 3D-clustering hypothesis even in proteins where the textbook expectation is that any inactivating mutation anywhere should cause the phenotype, as the hardest and most convincing test of the idea.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation (method only). Buildable artifact — CFTR 3D-Proximity VUS Score: for each CFTR missense VUS, compute minimum 3D distance to the nearest CFTR2-classified pathogenic residue using CFTR's cryo-EM structure plus AlphaFold-modeled regions, stratified by domain (NBD1/NBD2/TMD/R domain), with an explicit flag for domains too sparse in classified neighbors for the distance feature to be meaningful. Layer the score on top of AlphaMissense/REVEL/CADD rather than replacing them. Difficulty: M (medium) — the geometry is simple; the real work is a clean, versioned CFTR2-to-structure residue mapping.

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR pathogenicity mechanisms (often global fold-stability or trafficking defects) transfer from the localized drug-binding-site disruption mechanism that dominates M. tuberculosis antibiotic resistance.
  • Whether the CFTR2 classified-pathogenic-variant set is dense enough, domain by domain, for a proximity feature to carry real signal — plausible for NBD1, open for the R domain and NBD2.
  • Whether AlphaFold-modeled CFTR regions carry the same clustering-signal reliability as the solved cryo-EM core, a crystallography-vs-AlphaFold comparison this paper's abstract does not itself report.
  • Whether the reported 96.5% F1 and the "450+ proteins" clustering count hold up once the full methods (not just the abstract) are read — this episode draws on the Europe PMC abstract only; the paper was not retrievable in full text.

ALEX Sure. JORDAN So what's the actual gap this paper is filling? Sequence-based variant-effect predictors already exist and work reasonably well — why isn't that enough? 那这篇论文到底填补了什么空白？基于序列的变异效应预测模型已经存在，而且效果还不错——为什么这还不够？

ALEX Sequence-based methods — think conservation scores, or amino-acid substitution matrices, or even a sequence-only AlphaMissense-style model — treat each residue mostly as a 1D position in a chain. 基于序列的方法——比如保守性评分、氨基酸替换矩阵，甚至是纯序列版本的AlphaMissense式模型——基本上把每个氨基酸残基当作链条上的一维位置来处理。 They can pick up "this position is conserved, so a change here is probably bad," but they systematically miss a different kind of signal: positions that are far apart in the linear sequence can sit right next to each other once the protein folds, inside a binding pocket or a drug-contact surface. 它们能捕捉到"这个位置很保守，所以这里的变化大概不太好"这类信号，但它们系统性地漏掉了另一种信号：在一维序列上相距很远的两个位置，一旦蛋白质折叠完成，可能在三维空间中紧挨在一起，共同构成一个结合口袋或药物接触面。 The authors' hypothesis is explicit: integrating that 3D structural context should improve prediction of a mutation's effect on function and phenotype, over and above whatever a sequence-only model already captures. 作者的假设很明确：把这种三维结构背景信息整合进来，应该能在纯序列模型已经捕捉到的信息之上，进一步提升对突变功能和表型效应的预测能力。

Structure-aware variant-effect work has mostly focused on a handful of well-studied proteins with abundant crystal structures and abundant known mutations — think kinases, or specific drug targets. 不是以他们这里的方式。此前结构感知的变异效应研究主要集中在少数研究得很透彻的蛋白质上，这些蛋白拥有丰富的晶体结构和已知突变——比如各种激酶，或特定的药物靶点。 What's new here is doing it across the whole M. tuberculosis proteome — more than 31,000 clinical isolates' worth of mutations, mapped onto structural annotations for essentially every protein they could get a confident structure for, crystallography or AlphaFold. 这里的新意在于把这件事做到了结核分枝杆菌整个蛋白质组的规模——超过31000株临床分离株的突变数据，被映射到几乎每一个他们能获得可信结构（无论是晶体学还是AlphaFold）的蛋白质上。 That scale is what lets them ask a population-level question: across the whole proteome, do resistance-conferring mutations cluster in 3D space, even in proteins where the textbook story is "any inactivating mutation anywhere causes resistance"? 正是这种规模让他们能提出一个群体层面的问题：在整个蛋白质组范围内，耐药性相关突变是否会在三维空间中聚集，即便对于那些"教科书式认为任何位置的失活突变都会导致耐药"的蛋白质也是如此？ That second clause matters — it's testing the hypothesis even in the cases where it's supposed to fail. 后半句很关键——他们是在那些理论上这个假设"应该"失效的情况下去检验它的，这才有说服力。

JORDAN Why does that matter for CFTR, where we already have a fairly mature set of structure-informed tools? 这对CFTR有什么意义？我们现在不是已经有一套相对成熟的结构信息辅助工具了吗？

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built. 具体说说他们实际构建了什么。
