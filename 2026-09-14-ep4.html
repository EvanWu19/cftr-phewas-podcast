Ep4 (Track C, 2 of 2) — AI-aided clinical data management: applications, human-in-the-loop workflows, and regulatory considerations

Blurb. This narrative review from Japan's academic research organization (ARO) context surveys domestic and international examples of AI use in clinical data management (CDM) — data cleaning, medical coding, query generation — motivated by a chronic shortage of qualified CDM personnel relative to growing data volume and complexity. Its synthesized finding: across the cases reviewed, a common design principle emerged, AI performs initial processing/detection while final judgment remains with human personnel — a human-in-the-loop pattern, not full automation, in every case described. The review connects this to regulatory frameworks (ICH E6(R3), the FDA-EMA Guiding Principles) that are only beginning to address how AI-aided processes fit under Good Clinical Practice (GCP) obligations.
