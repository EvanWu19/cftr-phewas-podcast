How did this episode land for you?

Interpretable distillation reveals that deep learning splicing models suffer from pervasive confounders and blind spots

Liu, Zhang and Regev (Genome Biology, 2026) build a technique called "interpretable distillation": fit a simple, additive surrogate model made of known sequence-motif scores to reproduce a deep learning splicing predictor's output across many sequences, then use the places where the surrogate matches versus diverges to map out what the deep model is really doing. Where the surrogate tracks well, known splicing regulatory elements explain the prediction — the model is behaving the way a biologist would expect. Where it diverges, the paper reports two failure modes: "pervasive" genomic confounders unrelated to splicing biology, and a blind spot around RNA secondary structure that produces systematic errors specifically on non-reference sequence — the exact scenario of scoring a rare variant. For Runyu, this is a reliability audit on the SpliceAI-style tools used in CFTR splice-region VUS calls, and the buildable artifact is a surrogate-based "confound-and-blind-spot" auditor that flags which CFTR variant predictions are motif-grounded versus structurally suspect. The paper is very recent and not yet indexed in full text anywhere Europe PMC could reach, so everything here is abstract-level and flagged as such.

Plain-English glossary

  • Delta score — the output of a splicing variant-effect predictor (e.g. SpliceAI): how much a variant is predicted to change the probability that a position is a splice site, relative to baseline.（中文:剪接变异效应预测工具(如SpliceAI)的输出值,表示相对于基线,该变异预测会使某位置成为剪接位点的概率改变多少。）
  • Exon — a segment of a gene's transcript that is retained in the mature mRNA after splicing.（中文:基因转录本中,经过剪接后仍保留在成熟mRNA中的片段。）
  • Intron — a segment of a gene's transcript that is cut out during splicing and does not appear in the mature mRNA.（中文:基因转录本中,在剪接过程中被切除、不会出现在成熟mRNA里的片段。）
  • Non-reference sequence — a sequence that differs from the reference genome, such as one carrying a rare or novel variant; the scenario a variant-interpretation tool actually has to score.（中文:与参考基因组不同的序列,例如携带罕见或新发变异的序列;也就是变异解读工具实际需要打分的场景。）
  • Reference sequence — the standard genome sequence a model is predominantly trained and benchmarked on.（中文:模型主要用于训练和基准测试的标准基因组序列。）
  • RNA secondary structure — the way a single RNA strand folds back on itself through base pairing, which can physically affect whether a splice site is accessible to the spliceosome.（中文:单条RNA链通过碱基配对自身折叠形成的结构,这种折叠可能从物理上影响剪接位点是否能被剪接体接触到。）
  • Splice acceptor site — the short sequence motif at the 3' end of an intron that marks where splicing reconnects to the next exon.（中文:内含子3'端的短序列motif,标记剪接重新连接到下一个外显子的位置。）
  • Splice donor site — the short sequence motif at the 5' end of an intron that marks where splicing begins cutting.（中文:内含子5'端的短序列motif,标记剪接开始切割的位置。）
  • Splicing regulatory element (SRE) — an auxiliary sequence motif near a splice site that enhances or silences splicing there, beyond the core donor/acceptor motifs.（中文:剪接位点附近的辅助性序列motif,在核心供体/受体motif之外,进一步增强或抑制该位点的剪接。）
  • Spliceosome — the cellular machinery that recognizes splice sites and physically removes introns from pre-mRNA.（中文:识别剪接位点并从前体mRNA中实际去除内含子的细胞机器。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Interpretable distillation — fit a simple, additive surrogate (sum of known motif scores) to approximate a deep model's output and read off where they agree vs. disagree.
  • Additive SRE-motif scoring as a baseline "explainable" splicing model, usable as a comparison layer against any black-box splice predictor.
  • Agreement/divergence mapping as a cheap, model-agnostic way to flag where a black-box score is likely confounded.
