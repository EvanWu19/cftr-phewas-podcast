Ep3 (Track C — registry QC, slot 1/2): Mapping the EHR data-quality vocabulary

Penev and colleagues (JMIR Medical Informatics, 2024) run a PRISMA-ScR scoping review of PubMed literature (inception through May 2023) on electronic health record data-quality (DQ) and data-performance (DPA) assessment, narrowing 154 search hits down to 26 original research articles. Rather than proposing a new flagging algorithm, the review maps out what the field actually measures: a four-term DQ taxonomy (completeness, conformance, plausibility, uniqueness) and a six-term performance taxonomy (correctness/accuracy, currency/recency, fairness/bias, stability, shareability, robustness), each with its own definition and typical mitigation strategy. Completeness, conformance, and plausibility dominate usage (81%, 69%, 62% of articles respectively), while uniqueness, stability, and shareability are rarely assessed at all. More than half the reviewed articles (54%) had at least one reporting limitation, and even the most-used performance metric, correctness/accuracy, was validated inconsistently across studies — manual review in some, external gold-standard comparison in others. This episode treats the paper as vocabulary and framework to borrow (pipeline stage (e)), with a secondary candidate-generation angle buried in its finding that height values are especially prone to plausibility errors because of competing "estimated/reported/measured" EHR flowsheet fields — a provenance signal, not a measurement signal. Buildable artifact: a DQ/Performance Taxonomy Tagger, a small worksheet exercise to spot coverage gaps in existing or candidate checks.

Plain-English glossary

  • Completeness — Whether a data point is present at all, independent of whether it would be plausible or correctly formatted.（中文：某个数据点是否存在，与它是否合理或格式是否正确无关。）
  • Conformance — Whether data complies with expected formatting, relational, or absolute definitions (e.g. a diagnosis code using the expected coding system).（中文：数据是否符合预期的格式、关联或绝对定义，例如诊断代码是否使用了预期的编码体系。）
  • Plausibility — Whether a value is believable given the context of other variables or temporal sequence, such as a birth date needing to precede a treatment date.（中文：结合其他变量或时间顺序来看，某个值是否可信，例如出生日期必须早于治疗日期。）
  • Uniqueness — The absence of duplicate patient records, often arising from erroneous record merging or reprocessing.（中文：病人记录中不存在重复项，重复常常源于记录合并或重新处理过程中的错误。）
  • Correctness/accuracy, currency/recency, fairness/bias, stability, shareability, robustness — The six "data performance" dimensions this review distinguishes from data quality: whether records are error-free (correctness); entered within a clinically relevant time window (currency); free of unwarranted over/under-representation (fairness); stable against unexpected drift over time (stability); shareable without information loss (shareability); and the overall ratio of tolerable to intolerable DQ problems (robustness).（中文：本综述区分出的六个"数据表现"维度：记录是否无误（正确性）；是否在临床相关的时间窗口内录入（时效性）；是否存在不当的过度或不足代表性（公平性）；随时间是否保持稳定、未出现意外漂移（稳定性）；能否无损共享（可共享性）；以及可容忍与不可容忍数据质量问题的总体比例（稳健性）。）
  • EHR flowsheet — The structured, repeated-measurement interface clinical staff use to record vitals at the bedside (e.g. height, weight, temperature), often with multiple competing fields (estimated/reported/measured) for the same variable.（中文：临床人员在床边记录生命体征时使用的结构化、重复测量录入界面，同一变量常有多个相互竞争的字段，如"估计值""自报值""实测值"。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Ten-term DQ/performance taxonomy (Table 1) — a reusable checklist for naming exactly which dimension of data quality or performance a given check is meant to address.
  • PRISMA-ScR scoping-review structure — a standardized way to systematically map what a literature actually does, as opposed to synthesizing a single pooled estimate.
  • Four-axis article-quality scoring (data integrity, method clarity, outcome clarity, generalizability) — a lightweight rubric for assessing whether a published DQ study's own methods are trustworthy and replicable.

JORDAN So before the numbers, let's get the vocabulary straight — this paper splits things into "data quality" and "data performance," which sounds like it could just be the same thing twice. 那在讲数字之前，我们先把术语理清楚——这篇论文把问题分成了"数据质量"和"数据表现"两部分，听起来好像是同一件事说了两遍。

ALEX It's a real and useful split. 这是一个真实且有用的区分。 Data quality, in their taxonomy, covers completeness — whether a value is present at all; conformance — whether the data matches expected formatting or relational rules; plausibility — whether a value is believable given other variables, like a patient's date of birth having to come before their date of treatment; and uniqueness — the absence of duplicate records. 在他们的分类体系里，数据质量包括：完整性（completeness）——某个值是否存在；一致性（conformance）——数据是否符合预期的格式或关联规则；合理性（plausibility，中文：某个值在结合其他变量后是否可信，比如病人的出生日期必须早于其治疗日期）；以及唯一性（uniqueness）——记录中是否存在重复。 Data performance is a different layer entirely — it's about whether the data, once it exists and is formatted correctly, actually does what you need it to do: correctness or accuracy, currency or recency, fairness or bias, stability over time, and shareability. 数据表现则完全是另一个层面——它关心的是：一旦数据存在、格式也正确之后，它是否真的能满足你的需求，包括正确性/准确性、时效性（currency）、公平性/偏倚、随时间的稳定性，以及可共享性。 This is the Kahn-style terminology we keep running into on this track, and it's worth having the full ten-term vocabulary in your head, not just completeness/conformance/plausibility, because Liz's assignment will eventually need language for more than just "is this value implausible." 这正是我们在这条赛道上反复遇到的Kahn式术语体系，值得把这十个术语的全貌都记在脑子里，而不只是完整性/一致性/合理性三个词，因为Liz的任务迟早会需要不仅仅是"这个值是否不合理"的语言。

JORDAN There's also this detail about height being flagged more often than weight because of something called a "flowsheet." 还有一个细节提到，身高比体重更容易被标记为有问题，原因是某种叫"流程表"（flowsheet）的东西。

ALEX A flowsheet, in EHR terms, is the structured, repeated-measurement interface clinical staff use to record vitals at the bedside — think a grid of timestamps and values for things like height, weight, temperature, and blood pressure. 在EHR（电子健康记录）的术语里，流程表（flowsheet）是临床人员在床边记录生命体征时使用的一种结构化、重复测量的录入界面——可以想象成一张由时间戳和数值组成的表格，记录身高、体重、体温、血压等等。 The paper notes that height specifically tends to get recorded under multiple competing flowsheet fields — "estimated," "reported," and "measured" — which then get averaged together or one gets dropped, and that handling inconsistency is itself a source of plausibility errors, separate from the actual measurement being wrong. 论文指出，身高这个指标特别容易被记录在多个相互竞争的流程表字段下——"估计值"、"自报值"和"实测值"——而这些值随后会被平均，或者其中一个被丢弃，而这种处理方式本身的不一致，就是合理性错误的一个来源，与测量本身是否准确是两件不同的事。 That's a provenance problem, not a measurement problem — the number that ends up in the record depends on which field won, not just on what the tape measure said. 这是一个数据溯源问题，不是测量本身的问题——最终留在记录里的那个数字，取决于哪个字段"胜出"了，而不仅仅取决于卷尺上读出的数字。

JORDAN So what's the actual gap this review is trying to fill? 那这篇综述到底想填补什么空白？

ALEX The field has a real standardization problem. 这个领域确实存在标准化的问题。 Multiple institutions and consortia — PCORnet, All of Us, MIRACUM, the EHDEN project — have all built their own harmonized databases and their own ad hoc data-quality terminology, and completeness numbers for the "same" kind of EHR parameter have been reported anywhere from 60% to 100% depending on who's measuring and how. 多个机构和联盟——PCORnet、All of Us、MIRACUM、EHDEN项目——都各自建立了自己的协调数据库和各自临时拼凑的数据质量术语，而对于"同一类"EHR参数，完整性数字的报告范围可以从60%跨到100%，具体取决于是谁在测量、怎么测量的。 Earlier data-quality-assessment efforts already flagged inconsistent reporting and called for universal terminology standards, and groups like Kahn and colleagues and Wang and colleagues responded with proposed ontologies — but by 2023, when this review's search cutoff hit, it still wasn't clear how consistently any of that had actually been adopted across the published literature, or how data-quality work and the separate, newer field of data-performance assessment actually relate to each other. 早期的数据质量评估工作已经指出了报告不一致的问题，并呼吁建立统一的术语标准，Kahn及其同事、Wang及其同事等团队也相应提出了本体方案——但到这篇综述2023年的检索截止时间点，仍然不清楚这些方案在已发表文献中究竟被多大程度地一致采用，也不清楚数据质量工作和另一个更新的、独立的数据表现评估领域之间到底是什么关系。 This review set out to combine both strands into one clearer road map. 这篇综述想要把这两条线索合并成一张更清晰的路线图。

JORDAN What did they actually do, mechanically? 他们具体是怎么做的？

ALEX A PRISMA-ScR scoping review — that's the standardized checklist for how to report a scoping review, as opposed to a full systematic review — searching PubMed from inception through May 2023. 这是一篇遵循PRISMA-ScR规范的范围综述（scoping review）——PRISMA-ScR是用来规范"范围综述"报告方式的标准清单，区别于完整的系统综述——检索了PubMed从建库之初到2023年5月的文献。 That search returned 154 records; after removing duplicates and screening out irrelevant ones, 51 went to full-text review, and 25 more were excluded there, leaving 26 original research articles. 这次检索得到154条记录；在去重和筛除不相关文献之后，51篇进入全文评审，其中又有25篇被排除，最终剩下26篇原创研究论文。 For each included article, they scored it against four quality axes — data integrity, method clarity, outcome clarity, and generalizability — and separately tallied which of the ten DQ/performance indicators each article actually used. 对每一篇纳入的文献，他们按照四个质量维度打分——数据完整性、方法清晰度、结果清晰度，以及可推广性——并单独统计了每篇文献实际使用了十个DQ/表现指标中的哪些。

JORDAN Anything concrete on how plausibility checks were actually implemented across these 26 papers? 这26篇论文中，合理性检查具体是怎么实现的，有没有什么具体细节？

ALEX Eighteen of the 26 used logic statements — rules like "a lab result can't be drawn at 10:65 AM" or "death date can't come before birth date" — and eleven of the 26 used straightforward thresholding: fixed value-range checks like BMI between 12 and 90, or logical thresholds like flagging a greater-than-730-day mismatch between a recorded age and a date-of-birth field. 26篇中有18篇使用了逻辑语句规则——比如"化验结果不能在10点65分抽取"，或者"死亡日期不能早于出生日期"——还有11篇使用了简单的阈值判断：固定数值范围检查，比如BMI在12到90之间，或者逻辑阈值检查，比如标记记录年龄与出生日期字段之间相差超过730天的不一致。 I'd push back a little here: a fixed BMI-range threshold like "12 to 90" is crude compared to a spline mixed-effects model that accounts for age and sex, and a straight 730-day cutoff is a blunt instrument next to anything trajectory-aware. 我在这里要稍微提出一点质疑：像"12到90"这样固定的BMI范围阈值，比起一个考虑了年龄和性别的样条混合效应模型，确实显得很粗糙，而一个简单的730天硬性截断，比起任何考虑了轨迹变化的方法，也显得很钝。 But the honest trade-off is that thresholding is cheap, fully transparent, and trivially auditable by a non-statistician reviewer, while a model-based flagger is none of those things without real investment — and eleven out of twenty-six studies choosing the cheap, auditable option tells you something about what's actually sustainable to maintain in practice. 但诚实地说，这里存在一个权衡：阈值判断成本低、完全透明，而且连非统计背景的审核人员也能轻易核查；而一个基于模型的标记器在没有真正投入的情况下，这三点一个都做不到——而26篇里有11篇选择了这种低成本、可审核的方案，这本身就说明了在实践中真正能维护下去的是什么。

JORDAN So what did they actually find about how these indicators get used? 那他们到底发现这些指标实际是怎么被使用的？

ALEX Completeness was the most-cited data-quality indicator, referenced in 81% of the 26 articles, followed by conformance at 69% and plausibility at 62%; uniqueness, by contrast, showed up in only one study, 4%. 完整性是被引用最多的数据质量指标，在26篇文献中有81%提到过，其次是一致性，占69%，合理性占62%；相比之下，唯一性只在一篇研究中出现过，占比4%。 On the performance side, correctness or accuracy led at 54%, then currency at 27%, fairness at 23%, stability at 15%, and shareability at just 8%. 在数据表现方面，正确性/准确性领先，占54%，其次是时效性27%，公平性23%，稳定性15%，可共享性只有8%。 More than half the articles, 54%, had at least one reporting limitation — incomplete methods, incomplete data, or limited generalizability — and the correctness/accuracy metric itself was validated inconsistently: manual review in 57% of the papers that reported it, comparison against an external gold standard like a national registry in 36%, and sometimes both. 超过一半的文献，即54%，至少存在一项报告缺陷——方法不完整、数据不完整，或可推广性有限——而正确性/准确性这个指标本身的验证方式也并不一致：在报告了该指标的论文中，57%采用人工审核，36%与外部金标准（比如国家登记系统）进行比较，有时两者都用。 Did the review deliver on its stated aim? Yes, as a landscape map — it successfully shows the field has converged on completeness/conformance/plausibility as the core trio, while performance assessment is still fragmented and inconsistently validated; what it doesn't do, and doesn't claim to do, is resolve that fragmentation itself. 这篇综述是否实现了自己的目标？作为一张领域地图来说，是的——它成功地说明了这个领域已经在完整性/一致性/合理性这三者上形成了共识核心，而数据表现评估这一块仍然碎片化、验证方式也不统一；它没有做、也没有声称要做的，是去真正解决这种碎片化本身。

JORDAN Did any of these 26 studies actually show that fixing data quality worked, not just measuring it? 这26篇研究里有没有真正证明"修复数据质量确实有效"，而不只是在测量它？

JORDAN What would you want to check before leaning on this? 在依赖这篇论文之前，你会想先核实什么？
