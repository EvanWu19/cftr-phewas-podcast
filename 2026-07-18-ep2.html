How did this episode land for you?

Track B — BEHRT: Transformer for Electronic Health Records (Li et al., 2020)

Blurb. BEHRT ("BERT for EHR") reads a person's coded diagnosis history as if it were a sentence: a transformer pre-trained with a masked-language-model objective on ~1.6M UK primary-care patients (CPRD linked to hospital HES), then fine-tuned to predict which of 301 conditions will appear in a person's future visits. The EHR-specific innovation is summing four embeddings — disease, age, segment, position — where the age embedding encodes irregular inter-visit time as a clinically meaningful clock. BEHRT beat the prior deep-EHR state of the art (Deepr, RETAIN) by 8.0–13.2% average precision, its disease embeddings recover clinical structure and implicitly learn sex, and an audit of apparent errors surfaced a data-quality gem (sex-agnostic Read codes assigning "male infertility" to 1,734 women). It lands on the AI-tooling/career lane (secondary: Liz-style data quality, method only); the buildable artifact is a registry-event-embedder with neighbor-QC whose deliverable is a clinician-readable nearest-neighbor table plus a sex-consistency flag — a representation model that doubles as a data-quality auditor.

Plain-English glossary.

  • Caliber codes — an expert-curated UCL dictionary mapping Read and ICD-10 codes into one harmonized set (301 diagnoses here); the controlled-vocabulary layer BEHRT depends on.（中文：Caliber 编码，伦敦大学学院整理的字典，把 Read 与 ICD-10 编码统一映射为一套（此处 301 个诊断），是 BEHRT 依赖的受控词表层。）
  • CPRD — Clinical Practice Research Datalink, UK primary-care (GP) records, longitudinally linked to other health data.（中文：CPRD，临床实践研究数据链，英国初级医疗（全科）记录，纵向链接其他健康数据。）
  • HES — Hospital Episode Statistics, the England NHS hospital-admissions dataset linked to CPRD for the secondary-care side.（中文：HES，医院事件统计，英国 NHS 住院数据集，与 CPRD 链接以覆盖二级医疗端。）
  • ICD-10 — International Classification of Diseases, 10th revision; a hierarchical diagnosis coding system (coarse chapters to fine sub-chapters).（中文：ICD-10，国际疾病分类第十版，分层诊断编码系统（从粗章节到细子章节）。）
  • multi-label prediction — predicting a probability for each of many labels (301 diseases) simultaneously from one model, vs one model per disease.（中文：多标签预测，用一个模型同时为许多标签（301 种病）各预测一个概率，而非每病一个模型。）
  • primary vs secondary care — GP/family-doctor records vs hospital records; a person's history is split across both and must be linked.（中文：初级 vs 二级医疗，全科/家庭医生记录 vs 医院记录；病史分散于两者，需链接。）
  • Read codes — the UK primary-care clinical coding system; some codes are sex-agnostic, a source of the record errors BEHRT surfaced.（中文：Read 编码，英国初级医疗临床编码系统；部分编码不区分性别，是 BEHRT 暴露出的记录错误来源。）
  • age / segment / position embedding — the three non-disease embeddings summed with the disease embedding; age encodes irregular time, segment marks visit boundaries, position marks sequence order.（中文：年龄/段落/位置嵌入，与疾病嵌入相加的三个非疾病嵌入；年龄编码不规则时间，段落标记就诊边界，位置标记序列顺序。）

Full citation. Li Y, Rao S, Solares JRA, Hassaine A, Ramakrishnan R, Canoy D, Zhu Y, Rahimi K, Salimi-Khorshidi G. BEHRT: Transformer for Electronic Health Records. Scientific Reports. 2020;10(1):7155. PMID 32346050; PMC PMC7189231; DOI 10.1038/s41598-020-62922-y. According to PubMed. Source: OA full text via PubMed MCP.

Methods / tools to lift.

  • Sum-of-embeddings for temporal EHR — disease + age + segment + position; the age embedding turns irregular inter-visit gaps into a clinically meaningful clock.
  • MLM pre-training → cheap fine-tuning — a "universal feature extractor" reusable for small downstream sub-cohort tasks (the transfer-learning regime rare-disease registries live in).
  • Embedding QC as validation — t-SNE against a known code taxonomy, clinician-graded top-k nearest neighbors (0.757 overlap here), and implicit-attribute recovery (sex) as sanity checks.
  • Representation-as-data-auditor — a model trained on real records makes the records' own inconsistencies legible (the sex-agnostic-code find).

Where it lands + buildable artifact (tool sketch). Serves AI-tooling / career (secondary Liz / CFFPR data-quality, method only). Paste-ready MVP: registry-event-embedder + neighbor-QC (M) — input de-identified coded event sequences (one ordered list of diagnosis codes + age-at-event per person); MLM-pretrain summed disease/age/position embeddings à la BEHRT; deliverable = a per-code nearest-neighbor table for clinician plausibility review + a sex-consistency flag that auto-surfaces mislabeled codes. Dogfood first on a coded stream you already understand.

What to verify first.

  • The embedding inherits the data's noise; a clinically-implausible neighbor may be a coding artifact, not a model failure — check the raw records before judging either.
  • Diagnoses-only is a real ceiling; the paper never actually added labs/meds despite advertising the capability — quantify their marginal value before claiming a good representation.
  • UK-primary-care/Read-Caliber model — transportability to another coding system or care setting is unproven; validate the vocabulary layer for your own data first.
  • The retrieved full text has no code-availability statement — confirm the group's public BEHRT implementation, license, and paper-matching version before building.
  • Method on a public dataset only — no claims about any registry's data storage or internals.

ALEX Today's Track B paper is a classic worth revisiting: "BEHRT: Transformer for Electronic Health Records," by Yikuan Li and colleagues at Oxford's Deep Medicine group, published in Scientific Reports in 2020. 今天的 B 轨论文是一篇值得重读的经典：《BEHRT：用于电子健康记录的 Transformer》，由牛津 Deep Medicine 团队的 Yikuan Li 等人于 2020 年发表在《Scientific Reports》上。 The name is a pun — BEHRT is BERT for EHR — and the whole idea is to read a person's coded medical history as if it were a sentence. 这个名字是个双关——BEHRT 就是用于电子健康记录（EHR）的 BERT——其核心思想是把一个人的编码病史当作一句话来读。 We're reading it on the tooling track because it's the template a registry representation-learning effort would start from, and the paper gives enough architectural detail to rebuild it. 我们把它放在工具轨来讲，因为它是一个登记系统表示学习项目会作为起点的模板，而且论文给出了足以重建它的架构细节。 You already know BERT, so tonight we spend our time on what makes EHR different from text, not on re-explaining transformers. 你已经了解 BERT，所以今晚我们把时间花在电子健康记录与文本的不同之处上，而不是重新解释 Transformer。

JORDAN Good, because I don't need the attention-mechanism lecture. 好，因为我不需要那套注意力机制的讲座。 What I do need is the clinical-data vocabulary — where does this data even come from? 我真正需要的是临床数据的词汇——这些数据到底是从哪来的？

ALEX The data is CPRD, the Clinical Practice Research Datalink, which is UK primary-care records — data from general practitioners, the family doctors — linked to HES, Hospital Episode Statistics, which is the hospital side. 数据来自 CPRD（临床实践研究数据链），也就是英国的初级医疗记录——来自全科医生（家庭医生）的数据——再链接到 HES（医院事件统计），也就是医院端的数据。 That primary-versus-secondary-care split matters: a person's story is scattered across the GP office and the hospital, and linking them is what makes the record longitudinal. 初级医疗与二级医疗的这种切分很重要：一个人的病史散落在全科医生诊所和医院之间，把它们链接起来才使记录具有纵向性。 Now the tricky part — the diseases are coded in two different systems: Read codes on the primary-care side and ICD-10 on the hospital side. 棘手的地方来了——疾病用两套不同的系统编码：初级医疗端用 Read 编码，医院端用 ICD-10。 ICD-10 is the International Classification of Diseases, tenth revision, a hierarchical code where you can work at coarse chapters or fine sub-chapters. ICD-10 是《国际疾病分类》第十版，是一种分层编码，你可以在粗的章节层级或细的子章节层级上操作。 To make the two systems talk, they mapped everything to Caliber codes, an expert-curated dictionary from University College London, ending up with 301 diagnosis codes. 为了让两套系统对话，他们把所有编码都映射到 Caliber 编码——一部由伦敦大学学院专家整理的字典——最终得到 301 个诊断编码。

JORDAN So already, before any deep learning, there's a controlled-vocabulary harmonization step doing a lot of quiet work. 所以在任何深度学习之前，就已经有一个受控词表的规范化步骤在默默地做大量工作。 That sounds familiar from our Track B episodes. 这听起来和我们之前的 B 轨节目很像。

ALEX It's the recurring theme, and hold that thought for the tool at the end. 这是反复出现的主题，把这个念头先记住，留到最后讲工具。 The task itself is framed as multi-label prediction: given a person's history, simultaneously predict the probability of each of the 301 conditions appearing in future visits. 任务本身被设定为多标签预测：给定一个人的病史，同时预测这 301 种病症中每一种在未来就诊中出现的概率。 That's the "one model for many diseases" design, instead of training a separate model per disease. 这就是「一个模型预测多种疾病」的设计，而不是为每种疾病训练一个单独的模型。

JORDAN Before we get to how, why did this need a transformer at all? 在讲「怎么做」之前，这为什么非得用 Transformer 不可？ People had been putting deep learning on EHR for years by 2020. 到 2020 年，人们已经把深度学习用在电子健康记录上好几年了。

ALEX They had, and the paper is honest about the lineage. 确实如此，论文对这个谱系也很坦诚。 The early work — restricted Boltzmann machines, denoising autoencoders — just learned a patient representation and beat manual feature engineering, but ignored time and order. 早期工作——受限玻尔兹曼机、去噪自编码器——只是学到一个患者表示并击败了手工特征工程，却忽略了时间和顺序。 Then Deepr wrapped a convolutional net around visit sequences and inserted special tokens for the gap between visits, and RETAIN used a reverse-time attention RNN for heart-failure prediction — both good, both the state of the art BEHRT is measured against. 之后 Deepr 用卷积网络包住就诊序列，并为就诊间隔插入特殊标记，RETAIN 则用一个逆时间注意力的 RNN 做心衰预测——两者都不错，也都是 BEHRT 用来对标的当时最先进方法。 The unmet needs were the ones EHR shares with language but pushes harder: long-range dependencies, where a diabetes diagnosis stays a risk factor for decades; heterogeneous concepts of different sizes; and above all, irregular intervals between visits. 那些未被满足的需求，正是电子健康记录与语言共有、但更极端的问题：长程依赖，一个糖尿病诊断在几十年里都是风险因素；大小不一的异质概念；以及最重要的，就诊之间不规则的时间间隔。 Text tokens arrive one after another at an even cadence; visits don't. 文本的词元以均匀的节奏一个接一个到来；就诊却不是。 So the interesting question isn't "can we use BERT," it's "how do you tell BERT that six years passed between these two events." 所以有意思的问题不是「我们能不能用 BERT」，而是「你怎么告诉 BERT 这两个事件之间过了六年」。

JORDAN And their answer? 那他们的答案是什么？

ALEX Four embeddings, summed. 四个嵌入 (embedding) 相加。 On top of the disease embedding — the code itself — they add a position embedding for order within the sequence, a segment embedding to mark visit boundaries, and, the EHR-specific one, an age embedding: the patient's age at each event. 在疾病嵌入——也就是编码本身——之上，他们再加上一个位置嵌入表示序列内的顺序、一个段落嵌入标记就诊边界、以及一个电子健康记录专用的年龄嵌入：患者在每个事件时的年龄。 Age is how irregular time sneaks back in — instead of encoding the literal gap, they anchor every event to how old the person was, which is a clean, clinically meaningful clock. 年龄就是不规则时间重新溜进来的方式——他们不去编码字面上的间隔，而是把每个事件锚定在这个人当时多大，这是一个干净、有临床意义的时钟。 They pre-train with a masked-language-model objective on the diagnosis sequences, then fine-tune to the multi-label prediction task in three flavors: next visit, next six months, and next twelve months. 他们先用掩码语言模型 (masked language model，MLM) 目标在诊断序列上做预训练，再微调到多标签预测任务的三个版本：下一次就诊、未来六个月、未来十二个月。 The final architecture is small by today's standards — six layers, twelve attention heads, hidden size 288 — trained on about 1.6 million patients who had at least five visits. 最终架构以今天的标准看很小——六层、十二个注意力头、隐藏维度 288——在约 160 万名至少有五次就诊的患者上训练。

JORDAN You've got a design objection queued up, I can tell. 我看得出来，你已经准备好一个设计上的质疑了。

ALEX One big one. 一个大的。 The paper repeatedly sells BEHRT's ability to fold in heterogeneous concepts — diagnoses, medications, measurements — but the actual model in this paper uses diagnoses only. 论文反复推销 BEHRT 融合异质概念（诊断、用药、检测）的能力，但本文中真正的模型只用了诊断。 So the flexibility is architectural potential, not a demonstrated result, and a lot of the predictive signal in real records lives in the labs and prescriptions they left out. 所以那种灵活性是架构上的潜力，而非已证实的结果，而真实记录中大量的预测信号恰恰藏在他们略去的化验和处方里。 The second, quieter issue is that the whole thing rests on the Caliber mapping — a UK-specific, hand-curated dictionary — so "reproduce BEHRT elsewhere" secretly means "first rebuild that vocabulary layer for your own coding system." 第二个更隐蔽的问题是，整套东西都建立在 Caliber 映射之上——一部英国特有、手工整理的字典——所以「在别处复现 BEHRT」暗地里意味着「先为你自己的编码系统重建那一层词表」。

ALEX Now the results, because they earn the paper its status. 现在讲结果，因为正是它们奠定了这篇论文的地位。 Across the three tasks, BEHRT improved average precision score by 8.0 to 13.2 percent over Deepr and RETAIN. 在三个任务上，BEHRT 的平均精度分数 (average precision score) 比 Deepr 和 RETAIN 提高了 8.0 到 13.2 个百分点。 They report average precision alongside AUROC precisely because the labels are rare and multi-label, where a ranking metric that respects precision at low prevalence is more honest than AUROC alone — you know why that matters, so I won't belabor it. 他们同时报告平均精度和 AUROC，正是因为标签稀有且是多标签的，这种在低患病率下尊重精度的排序指标，比单看 AUROC 更诚实——你懂这为什么重要，所以我不多讲。 The part I find more interesting is the embedding validation. 我觉得更有意思的部分是嵌入的验证。 They reduced the 288-dimensional disease vectors with t-SNE and the clusters recovered the Caliber disease chapters; then a clinician graded the top-ten nearest neighbors for 87 common diseases and agreed with 75.7 percent of them. 他们用 t-SNE 把 288 维的疾病向量降维，聚类结果重现了 Caliber 疾病章节；然后一位临床医生对 87 种常见病的前十个最近邻打分，认可了其中 75.7%。 And without ever being fed sex, the model separated sex-specific diseases — endometriosis far from prostate malignancy — meaning it inferred sex from context. 而在从未被输入性别的情况下，模型把性别特异的疾病分开了——子宫内膜异位症离前列腺恶性肿瘤很远——意味着它从上下文推断出了性别。

JORDAN That last bit is a nice sanity check. 最后那一点是个很好的合理性检验。 Did anything surprising fall out of it? 从中有没有掉出什么让人意外的东西？

ALEX Before the surprise, two more results worth having in hand. 在讲那个意外之前，还有两个值得记住的结果。 They ran an ablation that selectively switched off the age, segment, and position embeddings, and performance dropped when each was removed — which is the evidence that the age embedding, their headline EHR-specific trick, is actually pulling weight rather than being decorative. 他们做了一个消融实验，选择性地关掉年龄、段落和位置嵌入，去掉每一个时性能都会下降——这就是证据，说明年龄嵌入这个他们主打的电子健康记录专用技巧确实在发挥作用，而不只是装饰。 And for reproducibility they report the concrete numbers: hyperparameters found by Bayesian optimization, the masked-language-model pre-training run for 100 epochs to a precision of 0.6597 before any fine-tuning. 而为了可复现性，他们报告了具体数字：超参数由贝叶斯优化搜得，掩码语言模型预训练跑了 100 个周期，在任何微调之前达到 0.6597 的精度。 That precision isn't the product — it's the pre-training checkpoint quality, the thing you'd watch to know your embeddings are learning at all. 那个精度不是最终产品——它是预训练检查点的质量，是你用来判断嵌入到底有没有在学习的指标。

JORDAN And the attention maps — did they show anything beyond "recent visits matter"? 那些注意力图——除了「近期就诊很重要」之外，有没有显示出别的东西？

ALEX They did, and it's the payoff of the long-range-dependency design. 有，而这正是长程依赖设计的回报。 For one example patient, self-attention linked rheumatoid arthritis to enthesopathies and synovial disorders that appeared far later in the record — a connection across many years, not just between adjacent visits. 对一个示例患者，自注意力把类风湿关节炎与病历中晚得多才出现的附着点病和滑膜疾病联系起来——这是一条跨越多年的关联，而不只是相邻就诊之间的关联。 Because the model is bidirectional, that link is a statement of correspondence, not of one disease causing the other — a distinction worth keeping straight before anyone over-reads an attention weight as a causal claim. 由于模型是双向的，这条关联表达的是彼此对应关系，而不是一种疾病导致另一种——在有人把注意力权重过度解读为因果论断之前，这个区分值得记清楚。 So the interpretability is real but modest: it's a lens on associations the representation captured, not a mechanism. 所以这种可解释性是真实但有限的：它是一面观察表示所捕捉到的关联的透镜，而不是机制。

ALEX Yes, and it's a data-quality gem. 有，而且是一颗数据质量的宝石。 When they audited the apparent errors, they found "male infertility" recorded for 1,734 female patients and "female infertility" recorded for 192 male patients — because some Read codes are sex-agnostic and get applied loosely. 当他们审查那些表面上的错误时，发现「男性不育」被记录在 1734 名女性患者身上，「女性不育」被记录在 192 名男性患者身上——因为某些 Read 编码不区分性别，被松散地套用了。 The model looked wrong, but the record was wrong, and the embedding surfaced it. 模型看起来错了，其实是记录错了，而嵌入把它暴露了出来。 That is the transferable insight hiding inside a prediction paper: a representation trained on real records makes the records' own inconsistencies legible. 这就是藏在一篇预测论文里的可迁移洞见：在真实记录上训练出的表示，让记录自身的矛盾变得可读。

JORDAN There's one framing in the paper I want you to pin down — they call the pre-trained model a "universal feature extractor." 论文里有一个说法我想请你说清楚——他们把预训练后的模型称为「通用特征提取器」。 What does that buy someone practically? 这对使用者实际上意味着什么好处？

ALEX It's the transfer-learning argument, and it's the real reason a registry team would care. 这是迁移学习的论点，也是一个登记系统团队真正会关心的原因。 Once you've paid the one-time cost of masked-language-model pre-training on the full population, the model has absorbed the statistical shape of how diagnoses co-occur and follow one another, and that knowledge lives in the embeddings. 一旦你付出了在全人群上做掩码语言模型预训练的一次性成本，模型就吸收了诊断如何共现、如何前后相随的统计形状，而这种知识就存在于嵌入之中。 After that, a downstream task — predict this one outcome, in this small sub-cohort — needs only a little additional fine-tuning, because it starts from a representation that already understands the domain rather than from scratch. 之后，一个下游任务——在这个小亚队列里预测某一个结局——只需要少量额外的微调，因为它从一个已经理解该领域的表示出发，而不是从零开始。 That's exactly the regime a rare-disease registry lives in: the whole registry is large, but any specific clinically-interesting question often has few labeled cases. 这恰恰是一个罕见病登记系统所处的情境：整个登记系统很大，但任何一个具体的、临床上有意思的问题往往只有很少的有标签病例。 So the pre-train-once, fine-tune-cheaply pattern is the part of BEHRT most worth stealing, independent of whether you ever reproduce their exact prediction numbers. 所以「预训练一次、廉价微调」这个模式，才是 BEHRT 最值得借鉴的部分，无论你是否复现他们那些确切的预测数字。
