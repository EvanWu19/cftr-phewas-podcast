How did this episode land for you?

Show notes — Track A (variant interpretation & harmonization)

Blurb. Schulze & Lindorff-Larsen (Protein Science, 2026) tackle a problem that blocks any attempt to pool functional-assay data for variant scoring: the number a multiplexed assay of variant effects (MAVE) reports is shaped by the experimental protocol, so the same score means different real protein levels in different experiments. Working with VAMP-seq abundance data for six soluble proteins (PTEN, TPMT, CYP2C9, NUDT15, ASPA, PRKN), they build a modular framework — an ESM-IF-based feature extractor, a dense "abundance network" predicting interpretable low-throughput abundance, and a dataset-specific standard-curve module that converts that prediction into each experiment's VAMP-seq score so training can exploit the abundant high-throughput data. At inference the standard curve is dropped, leaving variant effects on an interpretable, experiment-independent scale. Accuracy gains over ESM-IF zero-shot are modest and honestly reported; the contribution is the method for learning across heterogeneous assays. Where it lands: the Karen / CFTR variant-interpretation move — a CFTR functional-assay harmonizer with a per-assay calibration layer that puts organoid-swelling, conductance, and maturation readouts on one common scale.

Plain-English glossary.

  • Abundance (cellular abundance) — how much of a protein variant remains in the cell at steady state; a proxy for its stability, since unstable variants get degraded.（中文：细胞丰度——稳态下细胞里某个蛋白变异残留的量，是其稳定性的代理指标，因为不稳定的变异会被降解。）
  • ESM-IF — an "inverse folding" model: given a 3D protein structure, it predicts how likely each of the 20 amino acids is at each position; the variant-vs-wild-type likelihood ratio serves as a variant-effect signal.（中文：ESM-IF——一种"反向折叠"模型，给定三维结构反过来预测每个位置上 20 种氨基酸的可能性；变异与野生型的可能性之比可作为变异效应信号。）
  • GFP / mCherry — green and red fluorescent protein tags; in VAMP-seq the GFP:mCherry ratio reports variant protein level normalized to expression.（中文：GFP/mCherry——绿色和红色荧光蛋白标签；VAMP-seq 用 GFP 与 mCherry 的比值来报告经表达量归一化后的变异蛋白水平。）
  • MAVE — multiplexed assay of variant effects; measures the functional consequence of thousands of variants in one pooled experiment.（中文：MAVE——多重变异效应实验，在一次混合实验里测量成千上万个变异的功能后果。）
  • Missense variant — a single-nucleotide change that swaps one amino acid for another in the protein.（中文：错义变异——一个把蛋白里某个氨基酸换成另一个的单碱基改变。）
  • Standard curve — an empirically fit curve converting a score on one scale (interpretable low-throughput abundance) to another (a specific experiment's VAMP-seq readout).（中文：标准曲线——经验拟合的曲线，把一个尺度上的分数（可解释的低通量丰度）转换到另一个尺度（某个具体实验的 VAMP-seq 读数）。）
  • VAMP-seq — a MAVE that reads out variant cellular abundance/stability via fluorescence sorting and sequencing.（中文：VAMP-seq——一种通过荧光分选加测序读出变异细胞丰度/稳定性的 MAVE。）
  • ΔΔG (folding stability predictor) — tools estimating the change in protein folding free energy caused by a variant; a classic stability-based variant-effect signal.（中文：ΔΔG——估计变异引起的蛋白折叠自由能变化的工具，是一类经典的基于稳定性的变异效应信号。）

Full citation. Schulze TK, Lindorff-Larsen K. Supervised learning of protein variant effects across large-scale mutagenesis datasets. Protein Science. 2026. PMID 41795207; PMC PMC12967566; DOI 10.1002/pro.70526.

Methods / tools to lift.

  • Per-experiment readout modeling — fit a dataset-specific standard curve mapping an interpretable score to each experiment's raw readout, so heterogeneous assays can be pooled for supervised training without averaging away their idiosyncratic scaling.
  • ESM-IF inverse-folding features — variant/wild-type sequence-likelihood ratio given the wild-type structure as a strong zero-shot abundance signal to build on.
  • Structural-context stability features — buried-vs-surface, packing, and local-environment descriptors handed to the network alongside ESM-IF.
  • Leave-one-protein-out cross-validation + MAE — honest generalization test across proteins, plus Spearman on the network output (standard-curve-independent).

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Serves Karen / CFTR variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP: cftr-assay-harmonizer (M) — collect published CFTR variant readouts across assay types (organoid forskolin swelling, Ussing-chamber conductance, band-C maturation), fit a per-assay standard curve to a shared set of well-characterized anchor variants (F508del, G551D, wild-type), and output each variant's effect on one harmonized scale with an assay-of-origin tag. That harmonized table is the substrate for any cross-assay CFTR pathogenicity model.

What to verify first.

  • Anchor coverage: the calibration only works where enough shared, well-measured reference variants exist across assays; sparse anchors make the standard curve a guess.
  • Mechanism scope: VAMP-seq/abundance captures stability only — gating/conductance mutants (e.g., G551D) fold fine and would look near-normal, so keep gating and conductance as a separate axis, never collapsed onto abundance.
  • Generalization: six-protein leave-one-out is a thin base; sanity-check the calibration idea on a held-out CFTR assay not used to fit any curve before trusting it.
  • Scope of claims: external, public-data method only — no assumptions about registry internals.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读paper时间。Today's Track A paper is "Supervised learning of protein variant effects across large-scale mutagenesis datasets," by Tobias Schulze and Kresten Lindorff-Larsen, published in Protein Science in 2026. 今天 Track A 的文章是《跨大规模突变扫描数据集的蛋白质变异效应监督学习》，作者是 Tobias Schulze 和 Kresten Lindorff-Larsen，2026 年发表在 Protein Science 上。We're reading it because it tackles a problem you hit the moment you try to pool functional data from different experiments to score variants. 我们读它，是因为它正面处理了一个你一旦想把不同实验的功能数据汇总起来给变异打分就会撞上的问题。And that pooling problem is exactly what stands between us and a harmonized CFTR variant table. 而这个汇总问题，正是横在我们和一张标准化的 CFTR 变异表之间的障碍。

JORDAN Before we get into it, give me the vocabulary. 在深入之前，先给我讲讲术语。What is a MAVE, and what does "variant effect" actually mean here? 什么是 MAVE，这里说的"变异效应"到底指什么？

ALEX A MAVE is a multiplexed assay of variant effects — an experiment that measures the functional consequence of thousands of protein variants in a single pooled experiment instead of one at a time. MAVE（多重变异效应实验，指在一次混合实验里同时测量成千上万个蛋白质变异功能后果的方法，而不是一个一个地测）。A variant here is a missense change — one amino acid in the protein swapped for another. 这里的变异指的是错义变异 (missense variant)，也就是蛋白质里的一个氨基酸被换成了另一个。The specific MAVE in this paper is VAMP-seq, which reads out one particular consequence: cellular abundance, meaning how much of that protein variant is left standing in the cell at steady state. 这篇文章用的具体 MAVE 是 VAMP-seq，它读出的是一个特定后果——细胞丰度 (cellular abundance)，也就是在稳态下这个蛋白质变异在细胞里还剩多少。If a variant destabilizes the fold, the cell degrades it, and its abundance drops. 如果一个变异破坏了蛋白质的折叠，细胞就会把它降解掉，它的丰度就会下降。So VAMP-seq is really a stability readout dressed up as an abundance number. 所以 VAMP-seq 本质上是一个披着丰度数字外衣的稳定性读数。

JORDAN And how does one VAMP-seq experiment physically produce a number per variant? 那一次 VAMP-seq 实验在物理上是怎么给每个变异产出一个数字的？

ALEX Each cell expresses a single variant tagged with green fluorescent protein, GFP, and co-expresses mCherry, a red fluorescent protein, as an internal loading control. 每个细胞表达一个带绿色荧光蛋白 GFP（green fluorescent protein，一种发绿光的标记蛋白）标签的单一变异，同时共表达 mCherry（一种发红光的红色荧光蛋白）作为内部对照。The GFP-to-mCherry ratio tells you how much variant protein survived, normalized to how hard that cell was expressing. GFP 与 mCherry 的比值告诉你有多少变异蛋白存活了下来，并且对该细胞的表达强度做了归一化。Cells get sorted into bins by that ratio and sequenced, and the bin distribution becomes each variant's score. 细胞按这个比值被分选到不同的区间里再测序，区间分布就成了每个变异的分数。That is the high-throughput score. 这就是所谓的高通量分数。

JORDAN So what's the actual problem they're solving? 那他们真正要解决的问题是什么？

ALEX Here's the catch. 关键问题在这里。The number a VAMP-seq experiment spits out is not a clean physical quantity — it's shaped by the experimental protocol and the composition of the variant library that was sorted. VAMP-seq 实验吐出来的数字并不是一个干净的物理量，它被实验方案和被分选的变异文库的组成所塑造。That means the mapping from a variant's true molecular effect to the VAMP-seq readout is different from one experiment to the next. 这意味着从一个变异的真实分子效应到 VAMP-seq 读数之间的映射，在不同实验之间是不一样的。Schulze and Lindorff-Larsen show this concretely. Schulze 和 Lindorff-Larsen 用具体数据展示了这一点。They combined VAMP-seq data for six soluble proteins — PTEN, TPMT, CYP2C9, NUDT15, ASPA, and PRKN — each measured in its own experiment. 他们把六个可溶性蛋白的 VAMP-seq 数据合在一起——PTEN、TPMT、CYP2C9、NUDT15、ASPA 和 PRKN——每一个都是在各自的实验里测的。For a handful of variants per protein, between eleven and twenty-four, someone had also measured the GFP-to-mCherry ratio the slow way, one variant at a time, in low throughput. 对于每个蛋白里的少数几个变异，从 11 个到 24 个不等，还有人用慢办法一个一个地在低通量下测过 GFP 与 mCherry 的比值。

ALEX When you plot the low-throughput measurements against the high-throughput scores, the rank correlation, Spearman's, is high. 当你把低通量测量值对着高通量分数画出来，秩相关，也就是 Spearman 相关，是很高的。But the linear correlation is much weaker, and it differs by protein. 但线性相关就弱得多，而且每个蛋白都不一样。For ASPA and PRKN, variants spanning a wide range of real protein levels all get squashed to VAMP-seq scores near zero. 对于 ASPA 和 PRKN，真实蛋白水平跨度很大的变异，其 VAMP-seq 分数都被压到了接近零的地方。For PTEN, variants with quite different intracellular concentrations get near-wild-type scores. 而对于 PTEN，胞内浓度相当不同的变异却拿到了接近野生型的分数。So the same VAMP-seq score in two experiments can mean two very different actual protein levels. 所以同一个 VAMP-seq 分数在两个实验里可能代表两个非常不同的真实蛋白水平。That is the wall that stops you from just pooling everyone's scores and training one model. 这就是那堵墙，它拦住你，让你没法把所有人的分数一汇总就训练一个模型。

JORDAN Why even bother pooling across proteins? 那为什么非要跨蛋白汇总不可？

ALEX Because the whole appeal of MAVEs is scale, and that scale is only useful if it can teach a model that generalizes. 因为 MAVE 的全部吸引力就在于规模，而这个规模只有在它能教出一个可泛化的模型时才有用。Any single VAMP-seq experiment covers one protein, but a model trained across many proteins could, in principle, predict abundance effects for variants and even proteins nobody has assayed yet. 任何单个 VAMP-seq 实验只覆盖一个蛋白，但一个跨多个蛋白训练出来的模型，原则上能预测那些还没有人测过的变异、甚至还没测过的蛋白的丰度效应。That generalization is the prize — it's how you go from a lookup table of measured variants to an actual predictor. 这个泛化能力才是奖品——它是你从一张"已测变异查询表"迈向一个真正的预测器的方式。But you only get there if the pooling doesn't poison the signal, which is why the readout problem has to be solved first. 但你只有在汇总不毒害信号的前提下才能到达那里，这正是为什么读数问题必须先被解决。

JORDAN I see the danger. 我明白危险在哪了。If I concatenate six datasets and train, the model spends half its capacity learning each experiment's idiosyncratic squashing instead of biology. 如果我把六个数据集拼起来训练，模型会把一半的容量花在学每个实验特有的压缩效应上，而不是学生物学。So how do they get around it? 那他们是怎么绕过去的？

ALEX They model the experiment explicitly, as a separate module. 他们把实验本身显式地建模，作为一个独立的模块。The framework has three parts. 这个框架有三个部分。First, a feature extractor reads the protein's structure and sequence, mainly using ESM-IF, an "inverse folding" model. 第一，一个特征提取器读取蛋白质的结构和序列，主要用的是 ESM-IF，一个"反向折叠"模型 (inverse folding，指给定三维结构、反过来预测每个位置上 20 种氨基酸出现可能性的模型)。Given a structure, ESM-IF estimates how likely each of the twenty amino acids is at each position, and the ratio of variant to wild-type likelihood already tracks the VAMP-seq score reasonably well. 给定一个结构，ESM-IF 会估计每个位置上 20 种氨基酸各自出现的可能性，而变异与野生型可能性的比值本身就已经跟 VAMP-seq 分数对得相当好了。Second, an "abundance network," a dense neural net, takes those ESM-IF scores plus one-hot encodings of the wild-type and variant residues plus engineered stability features, and predicts abundance on the interpretable low-throughput scale. 第二，一个"丰度网络"，一个全连接神经网络，把这些 ESM-IF 分数、加上野生型和变异残基的独热编码、再加上人工设计的稳定性特征拿进来，预测出可解释的低通量尺度上的丰度。

ALEX Third — and this is the clever bit — the low-throughput prediction is passed through a dataset-specific "standard curve" that transforms it into that experiment's VAMP-seq score. 第三——这是聪明的地方——低通量预测会经过一条针对每个数据集的"标准曲线" (standard curve，指把一个尺度上的分数转换到另一个尺度的经验拟合曲线)，把它转换成那个实验的 VAMP-seq 分数。That standard curve is fit per experiment from the paired low- and high-throughput data. 这条标准曲线是用每个实验里配对的低通量和高通量数据分别拟合出来的。So during training, the loss is computed in VAMP-seq space, where the abundant high-throughput data lives, but the abundance network itself is forced to speak the interpretable low-throughput language. 所以在训练时，损失是在 VAMP-seq 空间里算的，那里有大量的高通量数据，但丰度网络本身被强制去讲那种可解释的低通量语言。And here's the payoff: at inference you delete the standard-curve module and keep only the abundance network, so the model outputs variant effects on a physically interpretable, experiment-independent scale. 而回报在这里：在推理时你把标准曲线模块删掉，只留下丰度网络，于是模型输出的是一个物理上可解释、且与具体实验无关的尺度上的变异效应。ALEX pushes on one choice: this whole thing leans on having paired low- and high-throughput data for every protein, and that anchor is only eleven to twenty-four variants per system. 我要对一个设计选择提出质疑：整套东西都依赖于每个蛋白都有配对的低通量和高通量数据，而那个锚点每个系统只有 11 到 24 个变异。That is a thin thread to hang a calibration on. 用这么细的一根线去挂一整条校准，实在有点悬。

JORDAN What are those engineered stability features doing on top of ESM-IF? 那些加在 ESM-IF 之上的、人工设计的稳定性特征是干什么用的？

ALEX They give the network information ESM-IF alone doesn't foreground. 它们给网络提供了 ESM-IF 本身不会突出的信息。The features report on the local and more global structural and sequence environment of the substituted residue — is it buried in the hydrophobic core or sitting on the surface, is it in a tightly packed region, what does the neighborhood look like. 这些特征报告的是被替换残基所处的局部乃至更全局的结构与序列环境——它是埋在疏水核心里还是暴露在表面，是否处于一个紧密堆积的区域，周围的邻居长什么样。The idea is that thermodynamic and cellular stability both depend on that context, and a variant deep in the core is far more likely to destabilize than one on the surface. 想法是热力学稳定性和细胞稳定性都取决于这个环境，一个深埋在核心里的变异比一个在表面上的变异更可能造成失稳。So the abundance network is being handed both an evolutionary-likelihood signal from ESM-IF and an explicit structural-context signal, and asked to fuse them into an abundance prediction. 所以丰度网络同时拿到了来自 ESM-IF 的进化可能性信号，和一个显式的结构环境信号，然后被要求把它们融合成一个丰度预测。

JORDAN And concretely, what does the standard-curve trick fix in the predictions? 那具体来说，标准曲线这个技巧在预测里修好了什么？

ALEX Look at ASPA, NUDT15, and PRKN. 看 ASPA、NUDT15 和 PRKN 这三个。When the network is trained to hit the high-throughput scores directly, without the standard curve, it does fine on wild-type-like variants but systematically over-predicts abundance for variants whose experimental VAMP-seq score sits near zero. 当网络被直接训练去命中高通量分数、没有标准曲线时，它在野生型样的变异上表现不错，但对那些实验 VAMP-seq 分数接近零的变异，会系统性地高估它们的丰度。That's because, for those three proteins, the low-to-high-throughput mapping squashes a wide band of real protein levels down toward zero, so "score near zero" doesn't actually mean "no protein." 这是因为对这三个蛋白，低通量到高通量的映射把一大段真实蛋白水平都压向了零，所以"分数接近零"其实并不代表"没有蛋白"。The standard curve encodes that squashing, so the network stops being punished for predicting the true intermediate abundance and learns the right physical values. 标准曲线把这种压缩编码了进去，于是网络不再因为预测出真实的中间丰度而被惩罚，从而学到了正确的物理数值。

JORDAN Does it actually beat just using ESM-IF straight? 那它真的比直接用 ESM-IF 更强吗？

ALEX Honestly, only modestly, and the authors are candid about it. 说实话，只强了一点点，而且作者对此很坦诚。They evaluate with leave-one-protein-out cross-validation and mean absolute error, MAE, between predicted and experimental VAMP-seq scores. 他们用留一蛋白交叉验证，以及预测与实验 VAMP-seq 分数之间的平均绝对误差 MAE（mean absolute error）来评估。Including the standard curves during training improves the high-throughput predictions for several proteins — except PTEN, where it actually hurt, so they end up retraining with PTEN left out entirely. 在训练中加入标准曲线改善了好几个蛋白的高通量预测——唯独 PTEN 例外，在它身上反而变差了，所以他们最后干脆把 PTEN 剔出训练重新训。When they benchmark against established predictors, including ΔΔG folding-stability tools, ESM-IF zero-shot is the single best of the benchmark set. 当他们对着已有的预测器做基准测试，包括 ΔΔG（预测变异引起的折叠自由能变化的工具）稳定性工具时，ESM-IF 的零样本预测是基准集里单个最强的。Their supervised model gives a small MAE improvement over ESM-IF but not a clear win in correlation, and with data this sparse they refuse to over-claim. 他们的监督模型相比 ESM-IF 在 MAE 上有小幅改善，但在相关性上没有明显胜出，而且在数据这么稀疏的情况下，他们拒绝夸大结论。

JORDAN So the headline isn't "we beat ESM-IF." 所以重点不是"我们打败了 ESM-IF"。

ALEX Right. 对。The real contribution is the framework — a principled way to learn across heterogeneous functional datasets by modeling each experiment's readout transformation explicitly, and to recover variant effects on an interpretable absolute scale rather than an arbitrary per-experiment one. 真正的贡献是这个框架——一种有原则的方法，通过显式地建模每个实验的读数转换，来跨异质功能数据集学习，并且把变异效应还原到一个可解释的绝对尺度上，而不是每个实验各自随意的尺度。That idea is worth more than the incremental accuracy number. 这个思想比那个渐进的准确率数字更值钱。

JORDAN Why does the interpretable absolute scale matter so much to you? 为什么这个可解释的绝对尺度对你这么重要？
