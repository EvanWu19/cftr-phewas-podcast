Ep4 (Track C) — Using linear and natural cubic splines, SITAR, and latent trajectory models to characterise nonlinear longitudinal growth trajectories in cohort studies

Blurb. A companion to today's ep3: if the Carsley paper quantified how much anthropometric data goes missing before you can even start, this Elhakeem et al. tutorial addresses the modeling choice you face once you have data and need an expected-value curve to compare it against. It's an expository (not discovery) paper comparing four approaches to nonlinear longitudinal growth-trajectory modeling -- linear spline LME, natural cubic spline LME, SITAR (a shape-invariant nonlinear mixed-effects model), and latent trajectory (growth-mixture) models -- illustrated on repeated bone-mineral-content (BMC) DXA measurements from three cohorts (ALSPAC, BMDCS, PBMAS; ~8,500 individuals, 37,000 measurements, ages 5-40). Linear spline, natural cubic spline, and SITAR gave similar mean-trajectory and velocity summaries (peak BMC accrual age: 12.2y females, 13.9y males, pooled SITAR); latent trajectory models instead identified up to four distinct trajectory subgroups. Full R code and synthetic data are public (github.com/aelhak/nltmr/). This lands on pipeline stage (a), candidate generation: any residual-based flagging system needs an expected-value model first, and this paper is a decision guide for that choice, with practical notes on knot count/placement and random-effects complexity. The buildable artifact is a small model-comparison harness fitting at least two of these approaches to public/synthetic longitudinal anthropometric data and comparing fit statistics and resulting predictions/residuals.

Plain-English glossary

  • Spline / knot — a spline is a set of piecewise polynomial segments joined at points called knots, used to flexibly approximate a curve too complex for a single polynomial; knot placement and count are user choices that affect the fit.（中文：样条/结——样条是一组在称为"结"的点处连接的分段多项式，用于灵活逼近单一多项式难以良好拟合的复杂曲线；结的位置和数量是用户需要选择的参数，会影响拟合效果。）
  • Linear spline vs. natural cubic spline — linear splines join straight-line segments (slope changes abruptly at knots); natural cubic splines join smooth cubic segments with continuous 1st/2nd derivatives (curve and its rate of change stay smooth, enabling peak/trough velocity identification).（中文：线性样条与自然三次样条——线性样条连接直线段（斜率在结点处突变）；自然三次样条连接一阶二阶导数连续的平滑三次曲线段（曲线及其变化率保持平滑，可识别速度峰谷）。）
  • SITAR (Super Imposition by Translation and Rotation) — a nonlinear mixed-effects model built on a natural cubic spline with a "shape invariant" assumption: one average curve shape for the population, with each individual represented as that shape shifted/stretched by size, timing, and intensity parameters.（中文：SITAR（平移旋转叠加法）——建立在自然三次样条基础上的非线性混合效应模型，采用"形状不变"假设：为人群拟合一条平均曲线形状，每个个体表示为该形状经大小、时间、强度参数变换后的结果。）
  • Latent trajectory (growth-mixture) model — a model that, instead of assuming one shared population-average trajectory, tries to discover unobserved subgroups of individuals following distinctly different trajectory shapes.（中文：潜在轨迹（生长混合）模型——不假设人群共享一条平均轨迹，而是试图发现遵循明显不同轨迹形状的未观测个体亚群。）
  • DXA (dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry) / BMC (bone mineral content) — DXA is the imaging method used to measure bone density/content; BMC is the specific quantity (grams) measured this way and used as this paper's example outcome.（中文：DXA（双能X射线吸收测定法）/BMC（骨矿物质含量）——DXA是用于测量骨密度/骨含量的成像方法；BMC是通过该方法测量的具体量（克），是本文示例结局指标。）
  • Missing at random (MAR) / missing not at random (MNAR) — MAR means the probability an outcome value is missing depends only on observed data; MNAR means it depends on the missing value itself. Mixed-effects models are unbiased under MAR but biased under MNAR.（中文：随机缺失（MAR）/非随机缺失（MNAR）——MAR指某结局值缺失的概率仅取决于已观测数据；MNAR指其取决于缺失值本身。混合效应模型在MAR下无偏，在MNAR下有偏。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • Side-by-side four-method comparison table (Table 1) — description, advantages, limitations, and R package for each of linear spline LME, natural cubic spline LME, SITAR, and latent trajectory models, a reusable model-selection reference.
  • Knot-count/random-effects-complexity sensitivity reporting — supplementary BIC/fit statistics across 2-6 knots, and a worked demonstration that random-effects spline complexity (not just fixed-effects) needs to be rich enough for individual-level variability.
  • Cohort-specific SD-unit harmonization — modeling an outcome in cohort-specific standardized units (rather than raw values) plus a cohort fixed effect, to pool across cohorts using non-interchangeable measurement devices.
  • Full public R code + synthetic datasets (github.com/aelhak/nltmr/) and companion R package spluti (github.com/ZheyuanLi/spluti) for post-processing spline outputs.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation. This is the upstream modeling decision any residual-based flagging approach depends on: what expected-value curve is a deviation computed against. Doesn't touch adjudication, review budget, or evaluation directly (a modeling-choice paper, not a flagging-performance paper), but an upstream model error shows up downstream as bad candidates regardless of adjudication/review-budget design quality.

MVP: a model-comparison harness — fit at least two of these four approaches (e.g. natural cubic spline LME and SITAR) to a public/synthetic longitudinal anthropometric dataset across a small grid of knot counts, and report BIC/fit statistics plus how much the resulting expected-value predictions (and therefore downstream flagging residuals) differ between model choices. Difficulty: M (fitting SITAR correctly and extracting comparable predictions across model types takes care).

Error types covered / not covered

Not applicable in the usual sense -- this paper is a modeling-choice tutorial, not an error-detection method, so it doesn't classify or flag specific error types (unit conversion, digit transposition, decimal shift, carry-forward, real-clinical-event confusion). Its relevance is entirely upstream of error detection: it determines what "expected value" a future flagging step would measure deviation from.

What to verify first

  • Whether the same relative method rankings and practical guidance transfer from bone mineral content (smooth, research-grade DXA schedule) to height/weight measured through irregular routine clinical visits with potentially more measurement noise -- not tested in this paper.
  • Whether the no-autocorrelation assumption holds for any new dataset with different (likely closer) visit spacing than this paper's cohorts -- stated as reasonable for their data, not proven as a general property.
  • Whether the MAR assumption underlying all four models holds in a real registry setting -- the paper is explicit that MNAR or covariate-missingness-related-to-outcome causes bias, and today's companion ep3 paper suggests real-world primary-care missingness may not be purely random.
  • Whether SITAR's shape-invariant assumption, validated here for bone mineral content, holds equally well for a trajectory shape like weight that can plateau, dip, or show multiple inflection points differently than growing BMC does.

JORDAN Before the four methods, walk me through the vocabulary -- what's a spline, a knot, and what does SITAR's "shape invariant" model actually mean? 在讲这四种方法之前，先带我过一遍术语——什么是样条（spline）？什么是结（knot）？SITAR的"形状不变"模型究竟是什么意思？

ALEX A spline is a set of piecewise polynomial segments joined together at specific points called knots, used to approximate a curve that's too complex for a single polynomial to fit well without either being too rigid or producing artificial wiggles at the extremes -- growth over childhood and adolescence is exactly that kind of curve, with distinct velocity phases that a straight line or a single quadratic can't capture. 样条是一组分段多项式，在被称为"结"（knot）的特定点上连接在一起，用于逼近那些过于复杂、单一多项式要么过于僵硬、要么在极端处产生人为波动而难以良好拟合的曲线——儿童和青少年期的生长曲线正是这样一种曲线，具有明显不同的速度阶段，单一直线或单一二次曲线都无法很好地捕捉。 A linear spline joins straight-line segments at the knots, so the slope can change abruptly at each knot; a natural cubic spline joins smooth cubic segments with continuous first and second derivatives at the knots, so the curve and its rate of change both stay smooth throughout, which is what lets you actually identify a peak or trough in growth velocity rather than just an abrupt slope change. 线性样条在结点处连接直线段，因此斜率可以在每个结点处发生突然变化；自然三次样条在结点处连接平滑的三次曲线段，一阶和二阶导数都是连续的，因此曲线本身及其变化速率始终保持平滑，这正是能够真正识别出生长速度峰值或谷值——而不仅仅是斜率突变——的原因所在。 SITAR, Super Imposition by Translation and Rotation, is built on a natural cubic spline but adds a "shape invariant" assumption -- it fits one average curve shape for the whole population, then represents each individual as that same shape shifted and stretched by a size, a timing, and an intensity parameter, so an individual's growth is modeled as a transformed version of a single template curve rather than as its own independent shape. SITAR，全称Super Imposition by Translation and Rotation（平移旋转叠加法），建立在自然三次样条的基础上，但加入了一个"形状不变"的假设——它为整个人群拟合出一条平均曲线形状，然后将每个个体表示为该同一形状经过大小、时间和强度三个参数进行平移与伸缩后的结果，也就是说，个体的生长被建模为对同一条模板曲线的变换，而不是各自独立的形状。 That assumption is what makes it easy to extract a clean individual feature like age at peak growth velocity, but it's also why it can struggle with trajectories that have multiple peaks or don't plateau the way the template curve does. 正是这一假设，使得提取诸如"生长速度峰值年龄"这类简洁的个体特征变得容易，但也正因如此，它在处理具有多个峰值、或不像模板曲线那样趋于平台期的轨迹时会遇到困难。

JORDAN And latent trajectory models -- how are those different from the other three? 那潜在轨迹模型呢——它与另外三种方法有什么不同？

ALEX The first three methods -- linear spline, natural cubic spline, and SITAR -- all assume the whole study population shares one common average trajectory shape, with individuals varying around it. 前三种方法——线性样条、自然三次样条和SITAR——都假设整个研究人群共享同一条平均轨迹形状，个体只是围绕它有所变化。 Latent trajectory models drop that assumption and instead try to discover unobserved subgroups of individuals who follow distinctly different trajectory shapes -- the trajectory shape itself, here estimated using a natural cubic spline, becomes a way of clustering people rather than a single population-average curve. 潜在轨迹模型则放弃了这一假设，转而试图发现未被观测到的、遵循明显不同轨迹形状的个体亚群——轨迹形状本身（此处用自然三次样条估计）成为了一种对人群进行聚类的方式，而不是单一的人群平均曲线。 That's a fundamentally different question -- "does everyone follow roughly the same curve at different speeds" versus "are there genuinely different curve shapes in this population" -- and the paper is careful to frame it as a different modeling goal, not just a fourth way to answer the same question as the first three. 这是一个根本不同的问题——"每个人是否大致遵循同一条曲线，只是速度不同"，还是"这个人群中是否真的存在不同形状的曲线"——论文特别强调，这是一个不同的建模目标，而不只是回答与前三种方法相同问题的第四种方式。

ALEX The authors' starting point is that appropriately modeling repeated measures in cohort studies improves understanding of developmental trajectories, what influences them, and how variation in those trajectories relates to later health -- but many developmental processes are genuinely nonlinear, especially during growth, and there's a documented lack of accessible, practical guidance, which the authors say discourages researchers from using more sophisticated methods even when they're the right tool for the job. 作者的出发点是，恰当地对队列研究中的重复测量数据建模，有助于理解发育轨迹本身、影响它的因素，以及这些轨迹变化与后续健康之间的关系——但许多发育过程本质上是非线性的，尤其是在生长发育期间，而目前确实缺乏易于理解的实用指导，作者表示，这会使研究者望而却步，即便更复杂的方法才是正确的工具，也不敢使用。 So rather than proposing a new method, this paper's contribution is comparative and pedagogical: lay out all four approaches side by side, with their underlying models, similarities, differences, advantages, and limitations made explicit, and then demonstrate correct application and correct interpretation on a real, nontrivial dataset. 因此，这篇论文的贡献不在于提出一种新方法，而在于比较和教学层面：把这四种方法并排列出，把它们各自的底层模型、相似之处、差异、优势和局限性都讲清楚，然后在一个真实、有一定复杂度的数据集上演示正确的应用和正确的解读。 That's the whole reason it belongs in a methods primer rather than a discovery paper -- the value is in the decision framework, not in a novel biological finding.这正是它属于方法学入门读物而非发现性论文的全部原因——其价值在于决策框架本身，而不在于某项新颖的生物学发现。
