Track C — SLC26A9 modifies rapidly progressing cystic fibrosis in F508del-homozygous patients

Blurb

Luo, Rollins, Schmitz-Abe, and colleagues (Clinica Chimica Acta, 2024) used an extreme-phenotype design — exome sequencing six F508del-homozygous patients classified as rapidly progressing CF (RPCF) — plus an unbiased genome-wide genetic burden test against 3,076 controls to identify SLC26A9 as a candidate rare-variant modifier of CF lung severity. Two rare missense variants, p.Gly77Ser (2/6 patients) and p.Pro629Ser (1/6), were found in structurally critical protein domains predicted to impair anion-transport function; single-cell RNA-seq (LungMAP) showed SLC26A9/CFTR co-expression concentrated in alveolar type 2 (AT2) cells (6.3% human, 9.0% mouse). This is the primary source behind two variants that a 2026-07-05 episode (Lublin-CF-group) tried and failed to re-find in an independent 20-patient screen — a concrete replication cautionary tale. The transferable method here is the extreme-phenotype study design itself, which maps onto Liz's CFFPR phenotype-characterization work. Buildable artifact: cf-extreme-phenotype-flagger, a trajectory-based tool for systematically identifying severe-outlier phenotype subgroups from longitudinal lung-function data.

Plain-English glossary

  • Genetic burden test — a test aggregating all rare variants within a gene to check whether the gene, as a whole, is enriched in cases vs. controls, used when individual rare variants are too infrequent to test alone.（中文:基因负荷检验,汇总某基因上所有稀有变异、检验该基因整体是否在病例组富集,适用于单个稀有变异过于罕见无法单独检验的情况。）
  • LungMAP — a public single-cell atlas of the developing and adult lung cataloging gene expression by specific lung cell type.（中文:一个公开的肺部单细胞图谱,记录基因在具体肺细胞类型中的表达情况,涵盖发育中及成年肺。）
  • AT2 cells (alveolar type 2 cells) — lung cells lining the air sacs that produce surfactant and serve as the lung's stem-cell-like repair population.（中文:II型肺泡上皮细胞,排列在肺泡内壁,负责分泌表面活性物质,并具有干细胞样的肺部修复能力。）
  • Missense variant — a single-amino-acid substitution in a protein; its functional impact depends heavily on whether it falls in a structurally/functionally critical domain.（中文:蛋白质中单个氨基酸的替换;其功能影响很大程度上取决于是否发生在结构或功能关键的结构域。）
  • Extreme-phenotype (outlier) study design — deliberately selecting patients from the most severe end of a phenotype distribution to maximize power for detecting rare, large-effect variants, at the cost of power for common, modest-effect variants.（中文:极端表型研究设计,刻意挑选表型分布中最严重的患者,以最大化发现稀有大效应变异的统计效力,但代价是降低了发现常见、效应较小变异的效力。）
  • Rapidly progressing CF (RPCF) — a clinically defined severe-outlier subgroup of CF patients whose lung function declines much faster than expected for their genotype.（中文:快速进展型囊性纤维化,一个临床定义的严重异常表型亚组,指肺功能下降速度远超其基因型预期的CF患者。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • Extreme-phenotype exome sequencing design — hand-select severe-outlier patients (RPCF) within a fixed genotype stratum (F508del homozygous) to maximize power for rare, large-effect modifier discovery with a small n.
  • Unbiased genome-wide genetic burden test — aggregate rare variants per gene and compare enrichment against a control population, without a candidate-gene starting hypothesis.
  • Multi-line-of-evidence triangulation — pairing a genetic burden signal with orthogonal single-cell co-expression data (LungMAP) and structural modeling before naming a candidate modifier gene.

Method only; makes no claim about how any specific registry stores its longitudinal data internally.

What to verify first

  • A later, independent 20-patient screen (Lublin-CF-group, covered 2026-07-05) looked for these exact two variants (p.Gly77Ser, p.Pro629Ser) and found neither — not a full refutation given the small re-screen size and variant rarity, but a concrete replication caution.
  • Neither variant has functional (e.g., electrophysiological/patch-clamp) validation in this paper — "structural modeling suggests a critical domain" is a computational prediction, not proof of impaired channel function.
  • Ancestry composition of the 6 RPCF patients vs. the 3,076 controls is unstated in the abstract — an ancestry mismatch could inflate or deflate the burden signal independent of real biology.
  • A genome-wide burden scan across ~20,000 genes with only 6 discovery patients operates near the statistical floor of what such a test can responsibly claim — the finding leans heavily on the co-expression and structural evidence to be convincing.

Gap-map / catalog

  • CLOSES: primary-source detail for the SLC26A9 p.Gly77Ser/p.Pro629Ser rare-variant discovery (previously only referenced secondhand via the 2026-07-05 Lublin-CF-group replication-attempt episode) — burden-test design, control-cohort size, AT2 co-expression percentages, and the structural-domain rationale are now catalogued directly from the primary source.
  • LEAVES OPEN: independent replication in an adequately powered cohort (the one replication attempt to date found neither variant); functional validation of anion-transport impairment; ancestry-matching adequacy of the burden test; and whether SLC26A9's rare-variant lung-severity axis and its common-variant CFRD/meconium-ileus axis reflect one pleiotropic biology or two independent findings on a heavily-studied gene.
