Clinician Diagnoses of Failure to Thrive Before and After Switch to World Health Organization Growth Curves

Daymont C, et al. Academic Pediatrics, 2020.

Blurb

When the Children's Hospital of Philadelphia primary care network switched its EHR's default growth chart for children under two from CDC to WHO in July 2012, the authors used an interrupted time series design (83,229 children, 12 quarters before/after) to compare growth-percentile-defined failure to thrive (FTT) against clinician-diagnosed FTT (ICD-9 codes). In infants six months and younger, both measures jumped upward at the switch, tracking the known chart geometry. In infants six to 24 months, growth-percentile FTT dropped at the switch as expected — but clinician-diagnosed FTT showed no significant change at all, implying clinicians in that age group lean on more than percentile numbers. The paper is a clean demonstration that a reference-standard change alone, with zero real change in patients, can manufacture a registry-data discontinuity — and a caution against treating clinician diagnosis codes as an automatic gold standard when validating a numeric flagging rule. The buildable artifact is a reference-version-drift scanner that recomputes z-scores under multiple reference charts to separate artifact from signal.

Plain-English glossary

  • CDC growth charts vs WHO growth standards — CDC charts describe how a U.S. population actually grew (descriptive); WHO standards describe growth under near-optimal, predominantly breastfed conditions across six countries (prescriptive). Crossover in the 5th-percentile line for weight-for-age/weight-for-length occurs around 6 months.（中文:CDC生长曲线图描述美国人群实际的生长情况(描述性);WHO生长标准描述六国健康、以母乳喂养为主儿童在接近最佳条件下的生长情况(规范性)。两者体重相关的第5百分位线在约6月龄处发生交叉。）
  • Failure to thrive (FTT) — a clinical sign, not a single disease, indicating inadequate weight gain in infants/young children; no single universally agreed numeric definition.（中文:一种临床征象而非单一疾病,指婴幼儿体重增长不足;目前没有统一公认的数值定义。）
  • Weight-for-age (WFA) / weight-for-length (WFL) — standard pediatric growth-percentile metrics comparing a child's measurement to a reference distribution for age or length.（中文:标准儿科生长百分位指标,将儿童的测量值与按年龄或身长划分的参照分布进行比较。）
  • Major percentile line (MPL) crossing — a trajectory-based FTT criterion: a child's weight-for-age percentile crossing two major reference lines downward over time, capturing concerning trends even without an absolute cutoff being breached.（中文:一种基于生长轨迹的生长迟缓判定标准:儿童的年龄别体重百分位随时间向下跨越两条主要参照线,即使未跌破绝对临界值也能捕捉到令人担忧的趋势。）
  • Interrupted time series (ITS) design — a quasi-experimental design comparing trends before and after a known intervention or change point (here, the CDC-to-WHO switch date).（中文:一种准实验设计,比较已知干预或变化点前后的趋势(此处为CDC切换至WHO的日期)。）
