How did this episode land for you?

Variant-specific RNA testing resolves variants of uncertain significance in exome testing

O'Neill AK, VanNoy GE, Gasser B, et al. (Ambry Genetics). BMC Genomics 2026. A four-case clinical case series showing how targeted RNA testing — specifically RT-PCRseq, a custom reverse-transcription-PCR-sequencing assay — can reclassify splice-suspect variants of uncertain significance (VUS) to Likely Pathogenic when exome sequencing alone leaves the splicing impact unresolved. The four genes (ATP6V0A2, SLC20A2, CSF1R, COL1A1) span homozygous and heterozygous variants, recessive and dominant inheritance, and loss-of-function and non-loss-of-function disease mechanisms — and one case (CSF1R) reclassified a variant that SpliceAI had scored as essentially negligible, a sharp reminder that splice predictors can false-negative. The paper's real contribution for CFTR work isn't a new assay; it's an explicit four-criterion eligibility rule (splice-impact score or position, tissue expression, gene-disease validity, mechanism fit) for deciding which VUS are worth sending to RNA testing in the first place. Lands on Karen's CFTR variant-interpretation work as a triage method — buildable artifact: a CFTR RNA-Testing Eligibility Screener that scores a CFTR VUS list against this same four-part rule to shortlist strong candidates for an actual RNA follow-up assay, before any wet-lab time is spent.

Plain-English glossary

  • Canonical splice site — The near-invariant two-base sequence right at an exon-intron boundary; disrupting it is almost always pathogenic.（中文：外显子与内含子交界处几乎恒定不变的两个碱基序列，破坏它几乎总是致病的。）
  • Cryptic exon — A stretch of intron that looks exon-like and gets spliced in as a new, abnormal exon when splicing is disrupted.（中文：一段看起来像外显子的内含子序列，在剪接异常时被错误地拼接成一个新的外显子。）
  • Exon / intron — Exons are the protein-coding chunks of a gene; introns are the non-coding chunks removed during splicing.（中文：外显子是基因中编码蛋白质的片段；内含子是剪接过程中被去除的非编码片段。）
  • Exon skipping — A splicing error in which an entire exon is left out of the final transcript.（中文：一种剪接错误，整个外显子在最终转录本中被遗漏。）
  • Gene-disease validity (GDV) — A curated assessment of how well-established the link is between a given gene and a given disease.（中文：对某个基因与某种疾病之间关联可信程度的系统评估。）
  • HGVS notation — The Human Genome Variation Society's standard for writing variants: c. for the DNA coding sequence, r. for the RNA transcript, p. for the predicted protein change.（中文：人类基因组变异协会制定的变异命名标准：c.表示DNA编码序列层面，r.表示RNA转录本层面，p.表示预测的蛋白质层面变化。）
  • In-frame vs. frameshift — An in-frame change removes or adds a multiple of three bases, preserving the reading frame; a frameshift does not, usually garbling everything downstream.（中文：框内变化增减的碱基数是三的倍数，不改变阅读框；移码变化则会打乱其后所有的阅读框。）
  • Intron retention — A splicing error in which a segment of intron that should be removed is instead kept in the mature transcript.（中文：一种剪接错误，本该被去除的内含子片段被保留在成熟转录本中。）
  • Nonsense-mediated decay (NMD) — The cell's quality-control system that degrades transcripts carrying a premature stop codon.（中文：细胞用来降解带有提前终止信号转录本的质量控制系统。）
  • Percent Splicing Index (PSI) — The fraction of RNA reads at a given position that show an aberrant splicing event versus the normal one; the core quantitative readout of RT-PCRseq.（中文：在某个位置上显示异常剪接事件的RNA读段占全部读段的比例，是RT-PCRseq的核心定量指标。）
  • RT-PCRseq — Reverse-transcription PCR followed by sequencing; a targeted assay that measures splicing directly from a patient's RNA rather than predicting it from DNA.（中文：逆转录PCR测序，一种直接从患者RNA中测量剪接情况的靶向检测方法，而不是从DNA预测剪接。）
  • SpliceAI — A deep-learning tool that predicts a variant's impact on splicing from DNA sequence alone, producing donor/acceptor loss and gain scores.（中文：一种基于深度学习的工具，仅根据DNA序列预测变异对剪接的影响，给出供体/受体位点丧失或获得的分数。）
  • Variant of uncertain significance (VUS) — A DNA change identified by testing that cannot yet be classified as pathogenic or benign.（中文：检测中发现的一种DNA变化，目前尚无法判定为致病或良性，即意义未明变异。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • RT-PCRseq — targeted reverse-transcription PCR with custom primers spanning the exon-exon junction flanking a suspect variant, sequenced on an Illumina MiSeq.
  • Four-criterion RNA-testing eligibility rule — splice-impact score/position, tissue expression of the gene, gene-disease validity, and mechanism-of-disease fit, used to decide which VUS get RNA follow-up.
  • PSI calculation against same-run matched controls — a practical, non-population-based comparator for a single-patient diagnostic assay.
  • SpliceAI scoring — used as one input into eligibility, explicitly not as a standalone verdict (see the CSF1R false-negative case).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation (method only — no claim made about CFFPR's own data internals). Buildable artifact: CFTR RNA-Testing Eligibility Screener — input a CFTR VUS list with SpliceAI donor/acceptor scores and splice-site position; score each variant against the paper's four criteria (splice-impact score or consensus-site position, CF-relevant tissue expression, CFTR's established gene-disease validity, and mechanistic fit with the predicted splicing event); output a ranked shortlist of strong RNA-testing candidates. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • CFTR's actual expression level in whole blood versus a more CF-relevant accessible tissue (e.g., nasal epithelial brushings) — all four genes in this paper happen to be blood-expressed, which CFTR is not, at a comparable level.
  • Any PSI threshold borrowed from this paper's numbers needs its own calibration; their n=3 control design is explicitly not statistically powered.
  • Don't gate purely on SpliceAI score — the CSF1R case shows a near-zero score sitting alongside a strongly pathogenic splicing event.
  • No assumption made here about what RNA or expression data CFFPR itself holds — that remains a separate, registry-side question.

ALEX Today we're looking at a short, sharp case series out of Ambry Genetics — O'Neill and colleagues, published this year in BMC Genomics, "Variant-specific RNA testing resolves variants of uncertain significance in exome testing." 今天我们来看一篇来自Ambry Genetics实验室的简短但扎实的病例系列研究——O'Neill及其同事今年发表在《BMC Genomics》上的论文，题为《变异特异性RNA检测解决了外显子组检测中的意义未明变异》。

JORDAN Four patients, four genes, zero CFTR — so why are we reading it? 四位患者，四个基因，一个CFTR都没有——那我们为什么要读这篇论文呢？

ALEX Because it's Track A, the Karen track, and it's basically a worked tutorial in exactly the decision Karen's team makes every week — when does a splice-suspect VUS（意义未明变异，variant of uncertain significance）deserve an RNA follow-up assay instead of just sitting in limbo. 因为这是A线，也就是服务于Karen工作的那条线，而这篇论文基本上就是一份实操教程，示范了Karen团队每周都要做的那个判断——一个疑似影响剪接的VUS，到底什么时候值得做RNA后续检测，而不是一直悬而未决。

JORDAN Before we get into cases, let's nail down vocabulary — first, VUS. 在进入具体病例之前，先把词汇术语搞清楚——首先是VUS。

ALEX A variant of uncertain significance — a DNA change you've found but can't yet call pathogenic or benign; in exome sequencing, twenty to twenty-five percent of patients still end up with one as their best answer. 意义未明变异指的是你已经找到的一个DNA变化，但目前还无法判定它是致病的还是良性的；在外显子组测序（exome sequencing）中，百分之二十到二十五的患者最终拿到的"最佳答案"仍然是这样一个未明变异。

JORDAN And "splice-suspect" specifically? 那"疑似影响剪接"具体是什么意思？

ALEX Genes are read out in exons, the coding chunks, stitched together after introns — the non-coding chunks in between — are cut out; a splice site is the sequence right at an exon-intron boundary that tells the cell's splicing machinery where to cut. 基因的编码片段叫外显子（exon），这些外显子之间是非编码的内含子（intron），细胞会把内含子剪掉，再把外显子拼接起来；剪接位点（splice site）就是外显子和内含子交界处的那段序列，它告诉细胞的剪接机器该在哪里下刀。

ALEX The canonical splice site is the near-invariant dinucleotide right at that boundary — break that, and you're almost always pathogenic; move even a few bases away and prediction gets much less certain, but the variant can still wreck splicing. 典型剪接位点（canonical splice site）是交界处几乎恒定不变的两个碱基——破坏它，几乎肯定是致病的；哪怕只偏离几个碱基，预测的确定性就会大大下降，但那个变异仍然可能破坏剪接。

JORDAN What does "wreck splicing" actually produce? "破坏剪接"具体会产生什么结果？

ALEX A few flavors — the whole exon gets skipped, that's exon skipping（外显子跳脱）; a chunk of intron that's normally cut out gets kept in, that's intron retention（内含子滞留）; or a stretch of intron that looks exon-like gets spliced in as a new "cryptic exon"（隐匿外显子）. 结果大概有这么几种——整个外显子被跳过，这叫外显子跳脱；原本该被剪掉的一段内含子被保留下来，这叫内含子滞留；或者一段看起来像外显子的内含子序列被拼接进去，形成一个新的"隐匿外显子"。

ALEX Any of those can shift the reading frame, which usually triggers nonsense-mediated decay — NMD（无义介导的mRNA降解）, the cell's quality-control system degrading a transcript with a premature stop — or, if it's a clean multiple-of-three deletion, leaves an in-frame but shortened protein. 这些变化都可能移动阅读框，通常会触发NMD——也就是无义介导的mRNA降解，是细胞用来降解带有提前终止信号转录本的质量控制系统；但如果缺失的碱基数恰好是三的倍数，就会留下一个框内（in-frame）但变短的蛋白质。

JORDAN And how do you actually measure "wrecked"? 那到底怎么衡量"被破坏"的程度？

ALEX Percent Splicing Index — PSI（剪接百分比指数）— the fraction of RNA reads at that spot that show the aberrant splicing event versus the normal one; that's the number this whole paper runs on. 剪接百分比指数，也就是PSI，指的是在那个位置上，显示异常剪接事件的RNA读段占全部读段的比例；这篇论文的全部结论都建立在这个数字上。

JORDAN One more notation thing before we move on — the paper writes variants three different ways, c-dot, r-dot, p-dot. 在往下讲之前再问一个符号问题——论文里用了三种不同的写法来表示变异，c.、r.和p.。

ALEX That's HGVS（人类基因组变异协会制定的变异命名标准，Human Genome Variation Society nomenclature）notation — c. describes the change at the DNA coding-sequence level, like c.2055+4A>C; r. describes what actually shows up in the RNA transcript after splicing, like r.1936_2055del; and p. describes the predicted effect on the protein, like p.E646_R685del. 那是HGVS命名法——c.表示在DNA编码序列层面的变化，比如c.2055+4A>C；r.表示剪接之后RNA转录本上实际出现的变化，比如r.1936_2055del；而p.表示对蛋白质层面的预测影响，比如p.E646_R685del。

ALEX The reason all three show up together in every case is exactly why RNA testing matters — the DNA notation alone tells you where the variant sits, but only the RNA notation tells you what it actually did to the transcript, and that's the evidence gap this whole paper is closing. 这三种写法在每个病例里同时出现，原因正是RNA检测的意义所在——单看DNA层面的写法只能告诉你变异在哪里，只有RNA层面的写法才能告诉你它对转录本到底造成了什么影响，而这正是整篇论文想要填补的证据缺口。

JORDAN So what was actually broken before this paper — what problem were they solving? 那在这篇论文之前，到底存在什么没解决的问题？

ALEX The core problem is that splice predictors like SpliceAI（基于深度学习预测剪接影响的工具）only give you a probability, not a verdict; a high score flags a variant as worth worrying about, it doesn't tell you what actually happens to the transcript, how big the effect is, or whether it's even pathogenic once you measure it — and a low score doesn't rule splicing disruption out either, which the CSF1R case later nails hard. 核心问题在于，像SpliceAI这样的剪接预测工具只能给出一个概率，而不是一个确定的判断；高分只是提示这个变异值得警惕，并不能告诉你转录本到底发生了什么变化、影响有多大，或者测出来之后它到底是不是致病的——而低分也不能排除剪接被破坏的可能，后面的CSF1R病例就狠狠证明了这一点。

ALEX Formal evidence frameworks like ACMG/AMP（美国医学遗传学学院与分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类证据框架）are explicit that in-silico splice scores alone can't carry a pathogenic call — you need direct functional evidence; but applying weight for an RNA functional study is comparatively new and still not fully standardized across labs. 像ACMG/AMP这样的正式证据框架明确指出，单靠计算机预测的剪接分数不足以支撑致病性判断——你需要直接的功能证据；但如何为RNA功能研究的证据赋予权重，这件事相对较新，在不同实验室之间还没有完全统一的标准。

JORDAN Had anyone shown RNA follow-up actually moves the needle before this? 在这之前有没有人证明过RNA后续检测真的能起作用？

ALEX Yes, piecemeal — one US cohort reported roughly fifteen percent of previously inconclusive exome or genome cases got a molecular diagnosis after RNA follow-up. 有的，但比较零散——一项美国队列研究报告，大约百分之十五原本在外显子组或全基因组测序中没有结论的病例，在做了RNA后续检测之后拿到了分子诊断。

ALEX A primary immunodeficiency study found RNA evidence supported pathogenicity in over half its cohort — and notably, every variant in that cohort was exonic, not intronic, which matters because it means RNA testing isn't just a trick for splice-site variants narrowly defined; coding variants can quietly wreck splicing too. 一项原发性免疫缺陷病（primary immunodeficiency）研究发现，RNA证据在超过一半的队列中支持了致病性判断——而且值得注意的是，那个队列里所有的变异都位于外显子内，而不是内含子，这一点很重要，因为它说明RNA检测并不只是对狭义剪接位点变异才有用；编码区变异也可能悄悄破坏剪接。

ALEX There's also a quieter advantage worth naming — because RNA evidence comes from the patient's own transcript, it doesn't depend on population allele-frequency databases at all, which makes it especially useful for variants seen in populations that are thin in those databases to begin with. 还有一个不太起眼但同样重要的优势——因为RNA证据来自患者自己的转录本，它完全不依赖人群等位基因频率数据库，这使得它对那些在数据库里本来就记录稀少的人群中出现的变异尤其有用。

JORDAN So what's actually new here, if the approach already existed? 既然这种方法早就存在，这篇论文到底新在哪里？

ALEX This paper isn't claiming a new assay — RT-PCRseq（逆转录PCR测序，reverse transcription PCR sequencing）is their existing in-house pipeline; what's useful is that they lay out, explicitly, their eligibility rule for who gets tested. 这篇论文并不是在宣称一种全新的检测方法——RT-PCRseq，也就是逆转录PCR测序，是他们实验室原本就在用的内部流程；真正有价值的是，他们明确地给出了"谁够资格做这项检测"的判定规则。

ALEX The variant has to be predicted spliceogenic by position or SpliceAI score; the gene has to be expressed in whatever sample type is available, in their case whole blood; the gene-disease relationship has to be at least moderately well established by their internal curation; and the disease mechanism has to be plausibly consistent with a splicing hit, which usually means loss of function. 这个变异必须根据位置或SpliceAI分数被预测为可能影响剪接；相关基因必须在可获取的样本类型中有表达，在他们这里就是全血；基因与疾病之间的关联必须经由他们内部的基因-疾病有效性（gene-disease validity）评估，达到中等以上的可信度；而且疾病机制必须合理地与剪接受损相符，通常意味着功能丧失（loss of function）。

ALEX Then they walk through four cases — four different genes, four different inheritance patterns — where that rule worked, plus one case where the splice predictor itself was wrong and they tested anyway because the biology argued for it. 然后他们详细介绍了四个病例——四个不同的基因，四种不同的遗传模式——展示这套规则是如何奏效的；其中还有一个病例，剪接预测工具本身给出了错误的判断，但他们依然坚持检测，因为生物学逻辑支持这么做。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually did in the lab. 带我了解一下他们在实验室里具体是怎么操作的。

ALEX All four probands came through one clinical diagnostic lab's exome pipeline — trio exome for the pediatric case, singleton for two adult cases, duo exome for the osteogenesis imperfecta family. 四位先证者都是经过同一家临床诊断实验室的外显子组检测流程——儿科病例用的是三人家系外显子测序（trio exome），两个成人病例用的是单人外显子测序（singleton exome），成骨不全症（osteogenesis imperfecta）家庭用的是二人外显子测序（duo exome）。

ALEX Once a candidate spliceogenic VUS cleared their four-part eligibility filter, they drew whole blood, extracted RNA from a PAXgene tube using a MagMAX kit, then ran targeted RT-PCR — reverse transcription into cDNA（互补DNA，由RNA经逆转录合成）followed by PCR — using custom primers designed to span the exon-exon junction on either side of the suspect variant. 一旦一个候选的疑似剪接VUS通过了他们那四项资格筛选，他们就会采集全血，用MagMAX试剂盒从PAXgene采血管中提取RNA，然后做靶向RT-PCR——也就是先逆转录生成cDNA，再进行PCR扩增——所用的引物是专门设计的，跨越疑似变异两侧的外显子-外显子交界处。

ALEX The amplicons went onto an Illumina MiSeq, and a custom bioinformatics pipeline aligned the reads to the hg19 reference genome and computed PSI at each splice junction; critically, every proband's PSI gets compared against three same-run、age-matched control samples with no variant in that gene — not a population database, a same-day, same-protocol control. 扩增产物上机做Illumina MiSeq测序，再用一套内部生物信息学流程把读段比对到hg19参考基因组，并计算每个剪接位点的PSI；关键的是，每位先证者的PSI都会和三个同批次、年龄匹配、且该基因无变异的对照样本进行比较——不是和人群数据库比较，而是和同一天、同一流程跑出来的对照样本比较。

JORDAN Three controls feels thin for a statistics-literate audience. 对于熟悉统计学的听众来说，三个对照样本似乎有点单薄。

ALEX It is, and the authors say so themselves — with n equals three, they explicitly can't claim statistical significance, only that the proband's PSI sits well outside the control range. 确实单薄，作者自己也这么说——在n等于三的情况下，他们明确表示无法声称统计学显著性，只能说先证者的PSI明显落在对照范围之外。

ALEX That's a real design trade-off — in a clinical diagnostic lab, you're not running a discovery study, you're running a decision-support assay for one patient at a time, so the bar is "is this aberrant splicing event clearly abnormal relative to a handful of matched controls, consistent with other biological evidence," not "is this a well-powered case-control comparison." 这是一个真实存在的设计取舍——在临床诊断实验室里，你不是在做发现型研究，而是在为每一位患者做决策支持型检测，所以衡量标准是"这个异常剪接事件相对于少量匹配对照是否明显异常，并且与其他生物学证据一致"，而不是"这是否是一个统计功效充足的病例对照研究"。

ALEX It works for a yes-or-no clinical call; it would not survive peer review as a quantitative effect-size claim, and it's worth remembering that distinction before porting PSI thresholds anywhere else. 这种设计对于一个"是或否"的临床判断是可行的；但如果拿去当作一个定量的效应量结论投稿，是过不了同行评审的，在把PSI阈值挪用到别的场景之前，这个区别值得牢记。

JORDAN Give me the numbers. 给我说说具体数字。
