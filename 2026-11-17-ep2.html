PolyGenie: a reproducible Nextflow pipeline for phenome-wide association studies using polygenic risk scores

Farré, Gasco, Blay, and de Cid ship PolyGenie, an open-source Nextflow DSL2 pipeline that takes precomputed polygenic risk scores (PRS) and a cohort's phenotype data, runs a systematic phenome-wide association study (PheWAS) across binary and continuous outcomes, and surfaces the results through a SQLite-backed, Plotly Dash web app with scatter, prevalence-by-percentile, and exportable top-hits views. The pipeline deliberately hands PRS construction off to the user (MegaPRS, PRSice-2, PRScs) and focuses its reproducibility guarantee on the downstream analysis and visualization steps, configured entirely through a YAML file plus two metadata CSVs rather than code changes. The worked example deploys it on GCAT, a ~20,000-person Catalan population cohort with a ~5,000-person genotyped subset, running 135 externally-computed PRS against 1,483 quality-filtered traits for over 200,000 association tests, and surfaces a sex-differentiated frailty-PRS-to-depression signal as a demonstration of hypothesis generation without custom analysis code. For the AI-tooling/career track, the buildable move isn't pointing this at CFTR data yet — it's replicating PolyGenie's own architecture end to end on its public demo dataset to build direct fluency with Nextflow DSL2 and metadata-driven pipeline design.

Plain-English glossary

  • PRS (polygenic risk score) — A single number aggregating the small effects of thousands of common genetic variants, meant to summarize inherited risk for a complex trait.（中文：多基因风险评分，把成千上万个效应微小的常见遗传变异汇总成一个数值，用来概括一个人对某个复杂性状的遗传风险。）
  • PheWAS (phenome-wide association study) — A systematic scan testing one genetic measure (a variant or a PRS) against as many phenotypes as possible at once, to detect pleiotropy.（中文：表型组范围关联分析，把一个遗传指标同时拿去和尽可能多的表型做关联检验，用来发现基因多效性。）
  • Pleiotropy — One genetic factor influencing multiple, seemingly unrelated phenotypes.（中文：基因多效性，指一个遗传因素同时影响多个看似不相关的表型。）
  • Phecode — A clinically-coherent grouping of raw ICD diagnosis codes, designed to be better suited to statistical association testing than billing-code-level ICD data.（中文：表型编码，把原始ICD诊断编码归并整理为更适合统计关联分析的临床分组，而非直接使用零散的账单编码。）
  • Genotype imputation — Statistically inferring genotypes at genomic positions not directly measured, using a large reference panel such as TOPMed.（中文：基因型填补，借助像TOPMed这样的大型参考面板，对未被直接检测的基因组位点进行统计学推断。）
  • Nextflow (DSL2) — A workflow-management framework for defining multi-step, parallelizable, resumable data pipelines that run unchanged on a laptop, an HPC cluster, or the cloud.（中文：一个工作流管理框架，用于定义可并行、可断点续跑的多步骤数据分析流程，可在笔记本电脑、高性能计算集群或云端以同样方式运行。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • PolyGenie (Nextflow DSL2 pipeline) — metadata-driven PRS-to-PheWAS pipeline: validation, percentile-binning, and regression stages, parallelized per PRS.
  • YAML + dual metadata CSV configuration pattern — adapts the whole pipeline to a new cohort without touching code.
  • SQLite + Plotly Dash results layer — stores regression and percentile results, serves an interactive, exportable web interface.
  • Selectable multiple-testing correction (domain-wise Bonferroni, global Bonferroni, Benjamini-Hochberg FDR) — user-chosen at the visualization layer rather than baked in upstream.
  • GenoPred + MegaPRS — the external PRS-construction pipeline used for the GCAT worked example (PolyGenie itself accepts PRS from any method).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands on AI-tooling / career (not Karen/Liz — the paper makes no claim about CFFPR structure or mapping, and none should be assumed). Buildable artifact: PolyGenie Architecture Replication — fork the open repository (MIT license, Zenodo-archived), swap in its own public/simulated demo PRS-plus-phenotype dataset, and rebuild the metadata-driven YAML+CSV configuration pattern and the two-stage Nextflow DSL2 structure end to end, before pointing any of it at CFTR-modifier or CFFPR-adjacent data. Input: the repository's own demo dataset. Difficulty: S–M (following, then deliberately rebuilding, a well-documented existing pipeline rather than designing one from scratch).

What to verify first

  • Whether the PRS-percentile-binning design, built for a continuous genome-wide aggregate score, can be meaningfully adapted to a small number of discrete genotype categories at one or a few candidate modifier loci, rather than a true polygenic score.
  • Whether PolyGenie's frequentist-regression-only analysis engine (no Mendelian randomization or colocalization, by the authors' own stated limitation) is the right tool for a summary-stats colocalization/MR question, versus just borrowing its configuration and visualization patterns.
  • Whether the single-ancestry caveat the authors flag for GCAT (Iberian White Western European, European-derived PRS) would need fresh, independent scrutiny — not inherited by assumption — for whatever cohort this approach eventually gets pointed at.

JORDAN Before the methods — what's a PRS, and why scan it against a whole phenome? 在讲方法之前——先说说PRS是什么，为什么要把它拿去跟整个表型组做扫描? ALEX: A polygenic risk score — PRS（多基因风险评分，把成千上万个小效应常见变异汇总成一个单一数值的评分） — aggregates the small effects of thousands of common genetic variants into one number meant to summarize someone's inherited risk for a trait. 全称polygenic risk score，中文是多基因风险评分，它把成千上万个效应微小的常见遗传变异汇总成一个数值，用来概括一个人在某个性状上的遗传风险。 A single GWAS hit rarely moves the needle much for a complex trait, but summing many small effects across the genome does. 对于复杂性状来说，单个GWAS显著位点往往影响有限，但把基因组里成百上千个小效应加总起来，影响就显著了。

JORDAN And "phenome-wide" — PheWAS? 那"表型组范围"——也就是PheWAS——又是什么意思? ALEX: Phenome-wide association study — PheWAS（表型组范围关联分析，把一个遗传指标拿去和一整套临床表型做系统性关联扫描）. 全称phenome-wide association study，中文是表型组范围关联分析，它把一个遗传指标——可以是单个变异，也可以是一个PRS——拿去和尽可能多的临床表型逐一做关联检验。 Instead of testing one gene against one disease, you run the same PRS against hundreds or thousands of phenotypes at once, looking for pleiotropy — pleiotropy（基因多效性，指一个遗传因素同时影响多个看似不相关的表型） — meaning one genetic factor quietly influencing several seemingly unrelated outcomes. 它不是只拿一个基因去测一种疾病，而是把同一个PRS同时拿去和成百上千个表型做检验，寻找基因多效性（pleiotropy），也就是一个遗传因素同时悄悄影响多个看似不相关的结局。

JORDAN How do you even standardize "phenotype" across that many outcomes? 面对这么多不同的结局，到底怎么把"表型"标准化呢? ALEX: A lot of PheWAS work maps raw ICD-10 diagnosis codes onto Phecodes — Phecode（表型编码，把ICD诊断编码整理归并成更适合做统计关联分析的临床分组） — which group and clean up ICD codes into clinically coherent categories better suited to statistical testing than raw billing codes. 很多PheWAS工作会把原始的ICD-10诊断编码映射成Phecode，中文可以理解为"表型编码"，它把ICD编码归并整理成更适合做统计关联分析的、临床上更连贯的分组，而不是直接用零散的账单编码。 And one more piece worth flagging: genotype imputation. 还有一个值得提一下的概念：基因型填补（imputation）。 Most cohorts only directly genotype a subset of genetic positions; imputation uses a large reference panel, like TOPMed, to statistically infer the genotype at millions of additional positions that weren't directly measured. 大多数队列只会直接检测一部分遗传位点；基因型填补会借助像TOPMed这样的大型参考面板，对数百万个未被直接检测的位点进行统计学推断。

ALEX So here's the gap the authors are pointing at. 接下来说说作者指出的这个缺口。 GWAS have found thousands of loci associated with complex traits, but any single variant's effect is usually too small to be clinically useful on its own — that's exactly why PRS exist, to aggregate across the genome. GWAS已经发现了数千个与复杂性状相关的位点，但单个变异的效应通常太小，单独拿来用在临床上意义有限——这正是PRS存在的原因，它把整个基因组的效应汇总起来。 Running a PRS against many phenotypes at once can reveal shared genetic architecture that a single disease-focused study would never surface. 把一个PRS同时拿去和许多表型做检验，能揭示出单一疾病导向的研究根本不会发现的共享遗传结构。

JORDAN So what's stopping everyone from just doing that already? 那是什么阻碍了大家直接去做这件事呢? ALEX: Standardization. 标准化问题。 GWAS summary statistics and PRS-construction methods are increasingly available, but the actual analysis steps — handling wildly heterogeneous phenotype types, choosing which regression model fits each one, presenting hundreds of thousands of results in a way anyone can actually use — are rarely standardized, which hurts reproducibility badly. GWAS汇总统计数据和PRS构建方法现在越来越容易获取，但真正的分析步骤——处理千差万别的表型类型、为每一种选择合适的回归模型、把数十万条结果以别人真能用上的方式呈现出来——却很少被标准化，这严重损害了可复现性。 The authors survey what already exists and find each piece lacking: PRS Atlas and a Spanish reference called SpPGS are static, fixed references, not tools you can point at a new cohort. 作者梳理了现有的工具，发现每一个都有缺陷：PRS Atlas和一个叫SpPGS的西班牙参考资源都是静态、固定的参考数据，不是能直接用在新队列上的工具。 R PheWAS and pyPheWAS are flexible but require real command-line skills and don't come with integrated visualization. R PheWAS和pyPheWAS确实灵活，但需要真正的命令行操作能力，也没有内置的可视化功能。 PheWAS-ME and pyPheWAS Explorer are nice dashboards, but they assume you've already run a separate statistical analysis elsewhere and just want to look at it. PheWAS-ME和pyPheWAS Explorer是不错的可视化面板，但前提是你已经在别的地方做好了统计分析，只是想拿来查看结果。 And the PheWAS Catalog is a reference of published results, not something redeployable on your own data. 而PheWAS Catalog则是一份已发表结果的参考资料，并不是可以在你自己的数据上重新部署运行的东西。

JORDAN So nothing covers the whole pipeline end to end, on arbitrary new data. 所以没有一个工具能端到端地覆盖整个流程，还能直接用在任意新数据上。 ALEX: Right — and that's the specific slot PolyGenie is built to fill: take precomputed PRS plus a new cohort's phenotype data, run the whole PheWAS systematically, and hand back both numbers and an interactive way to explore them. 没错——这恰恰是PolyGenie要填补的那个具体空缺：拿来预先计算好的PRS，加上一个新队列的表型数据，系统性地跑完整个PheWAS流程，最后同时给出数字结果和一个可交互的探索界面。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built. 具体讲讲他们到底搭建了什么。 ALEX: It's a Nextflow pipeline — Nextflow（一个工作流管理框架，能让多步骤的数据分析流程在笔记本电脑、高性能计算集群或云端以同样的方式可复现地运行） — a workflow-management framework that lets a multi-step analysis run unchanged on a laptop, an HPC cluster, or the cloud, with built-in parallelization and resumability. 这是一个用Nextflow搭建的流程，Nextflow是一个工作流管理框架，能让一个多步骤的分析流程在笔记本电脑、高性能计算集群或云端都以同样的方式可复现地运行，并自带并行化和断点续跑能力。 PolyGenie splits into two Nextflow stages. PolyGenie分为两个Nextflow阶段。 Preprocessing validates every input file — one process checks each PRS file against its metadata for existence and correct column structure, another checks each phenotype file and drops any binary trait with fewer than ten cases by default. 预处理阶段负责验证每一个输入文件——一个流程会检查每个PRS文件是否存在、列结构是否正确并与其元数据匹配，另一个流程会检查每个表型文件，默认情况下会剔除病例数不足十例的二分类表型。 Analysis then runs two things per PRS, in parallel: percentile binning, which computes prevalence or mean outcome within each of a default 100 percentile bins of the score, and regression, which runs linear or logistic models comparing a top quantile against a bottom quantile or the rest of the cohort — with several quantile and reference-group configurations runnable at once. 分析阶段随后针对每个PRS并行运行两件事：百分位分箱，计算评分的每个百分位区间（默认分100个区间）内的患病率或结局均值；以及回归分析，运行线性或逻辑回归模型，将评分最高的分位组与最低分位组或队列其余部分进行比较——而且可以同时运行多种不同的分位数设置和参照组定义。 Regression itself runs in Python with statsmodels, parallelized across phenotypes via joblib. 回归本身用Python的statsmodels库实现，通过joblib在不同表型之间并行执行。

JORDAN And everything's configured without touching code? 而所有的配置都不需要动代码? ALEX: That's the design intent — one YAML file sets the overall parameters, and two metadata CSVs, one for PRS and one for phenotypes, define file paths, variable types, sex stratification, and any phenotype-specific covariates. 这正是设计意图所在——一个YAML文件设置整体参数，另外两个元数据CSV文件，一个对应PRS、一个对应表型，定义文件路径、变量类型、是否按性别分层，以及任何特定于某个表型的协变量。 Adapting to a brand-new cohort is supposed to need only reformatting input files and editing that configuration, no code changes. 迁移到一个全新的队列理论上只需要重新格式化输入文件、修改配置，不需要改动任何代码。 Results land in a SQLite database and get visualized through a Plotly Dash web app with three views: a PheWAS scatter plot, a sex-stratified prevalence-by-percentile plot for any chosen PRS-phenotype pair, and a sortable, exportable top-hits table. 结果会存入一个SQLite数据库，并通过一个Plotly Dash网页应用进行可视化，提供三种视图：PheWAS散点图、针对任意选定PRS-表型组合的按性别分层的"患病率-百分位"曲线图，以及一个可排序、可导出的"重要结果"表格。

JORDAN And how do they handle the multiple-testing problem, running hundreds of thousands of tests at once? 那在同时跑几十万次检验的情况下，他们是怎么处理多重检验问题的? ALEX: The web interface lets the user pick between three corrections on the fly: Bonferroni applied within each phenotype domain separately, Bonferroni applied across every phenotype at once, or a Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate correction across the whole set. 网页界面让用户可以随时在三种校正方式之间切换：在每个表型领域内部单独做Bonferroni校正、对所有表型一次性做Bonferroni校正，或者对整个结果集做Benjamini-Hochberg假发现率（FDR）校正。 That's a small design choice, but it matters — the domain-wise threshold is what the authors actually use as their primary cutoff in the GCAT deployment, shown as dashed lines directly on the scatter plot, rather than leaving users to silently default to whichever correction happens to be least conservative. 这是一个不起眼的设计选择，但很重要——在GCAT实际部署中，作者真正采用的主要判定阈值正是领域内校正，并直接以虚线的形式画在散点图上，而不是任由用户在不知不觉中默认选用最宽松的那种校正方式。

JORDAN What's the catch? 有什么问题吗? ALEX: My pushback is about where the pipeline draws its reproducibility boundary. 我的质疑在于这个流程把"可复现性"的边界划在了哪里。 PolyGenie deliberately doesn't compute PRS itself — it hands that off to the user, pointing at tools like MegaPRS, PRSice-2, or PRScs. PolyGenie故意不自己计算PRS——而是把这一步交给用户，建议使用MegaPRS、PRSice-2或PRScs之类的工具。 That's a reasonable separation of concerns, but it means the pipeline's reproducibility guarantee only covers the downstream half of the workflow. 这是一种合理的职责划分，但也意味着这个流程的可复现性保证只覆盖了整个工作流的下游那一半。 Two groups could both say "we ran it through PolyGenie" while using completely different PRS-construction methods upstream, and their results still wouldn't be comparable — the reproducibility is real, but it's partial, and that's easy to miss if you only read the abstract. 两个团队都可以说"我们用PolyGenie跑的"，但如果上游使用的PRS构建方法完全不同，两边的结果依然无法直接比较——可复现性是真实存在的，但只是部分的，如果只读摘要很容易忽略这一点。

ALEX On the worked example — the authors deployed PolyGenie on GCAT, a living population-based cohort of about 20,000 middle-aged Catalan adults aged 40 to 65, recruited between 2014 and 2018, with ongoing follow-up through linkage to Catalonia's public healthcare records spanning over 15 years of longitudinal data. 关于实际应用案例——作者把PolyGenie部署在了GCAT队列上，这是一个纳入约2万名40到65岁加泰罗尼亚中年成年人的在役人群队列，于2014到2018年间招募，并通过与加泰罗尼亚公共医疗记录系统的关联持续随访，拥有超过15年的纵向数据。 About 5,000 of those participants have genotype data, genotyped on the Illumina MEGAEx array, all of Iberian White Western European ancestry confirmed by self-report and principal-component analysis, then imputed via the TOPMed reference panel out to roughly 20 million autosomal variants. 其中约5000名参与者拥有基因型数据，使用Illumina MEGAEx芯片进行基因型检测，所有人均为通过自报与主成分分析确认的伊比利亚白人西欧血统，随后通过TOPMed参考面板进行填补，扩展到约2000万个常染色体变异位点。 After quality control, 4,988 participants and 17,203,152 variants were retained, filtered to minor allele frequency above 0.001 and imputation R-squared above 0.3. 经过质控后，保留了4,988名参与者和17,203,152个变异位点，筛选标准为最小等位基因频率高于0.001、填补R平方高于0.3。

JORDAN And the PRS side? 那PRS那一侧呢? ALEX: 135 PRS were computed externally using the GenoPred pipeline and MegaPRS, built from public GWAS summary statistics. 135个PRS是在外部使用GenoPred流程和MegaPRS方法计算出来的，基于公开的GWAS汇总统计数据构建。 After quality filtering, 1,483 traits remained, yielding more than 200,000 individual PRS-phenotype association tests, adjusted for age, sex, and the first ten principal components, with metabolite models also including chylomicrons to account for non-fasting blood draws. 经过质量筛选后保留了1,483个性状，产生了超过20万个独立的PRS-表型关联检验，调整了年龄、性别和前十个主成分，代谢物相关模型还额外纳入了乳糜微粒这一变量，以校正非空腹采血的影响。

JORDAN Give me one concrete result. 给我讲一个具体的结果。 ALEX: The authors ran a frailty PRS, built from a published frailty GWAS, against two GCAT outcomes: overweight/obesity and major depressive disorder. 作者把一个基于已发表虚弱表型GWAS构建的虚弱PRS，拿去和GCAT里的两个结局做了检验：超重/肥胖，以及重度抑郁症。 Higher frailty PRS tracked with higher prevalence of both outcomes in a clear dose-response pattern. 更高的虚弱PRS与这两个结局更高的患病率都呈现出清晰的剂量反应关系。 Obesity prevalence rose roughly linearly across percentile bins, similarly in males and females. 肥胖患病率在各百分位区间之间大致呈线性上升，在男性和女性中趋势相似。 Depression showed a similar gradient, but with markedly higher prevalence in females at every single percentile — consistent with the roughly two-fold higher prevalence of depression in women seen across the broader epidemiological literature, and with prior genetic-correlation work linking frailty, adiposity, and neuropsychiatric traits. 抑郁症呈现出类似的梯度，但在每一个百分位上女性的患病率都明显更高——这与更广泛流行病学文献中女性抑郁症患病率大约是男性两倍的观察一致，也与此前关于虚弱、肥胖和神经精神性状之间存在遗传相关性的研究结果相符。
