Blurb

Livesey & Marsh (Molecular Systems Biology 2023) re-run their variant-effect-predictor benchmark, now ranking 55 VEPs against deep mutational scanning (DMS) data for 26 human proteins. The point of using DMS is to escape data circularity: because DMS functional scores come from a wet-lab assay rather than clinical records, they are independent of the labels predictors trained on. Ranking is done by pairwise Spearman-correlation "wins" against the DMS data, over shared variants only, with bootstrap significance and coverage/windowing robustness checks. Headline: the protein language model ESM-1v ranks first overall (by zero-shot inference, no per-protein training), statistically tied with EVE; the top supervised method VARITY is beaten significantly only by ESM-1v and survives removal of its 10 overlapping DMS training sets. A second, independent test — AUROC on ClinVar/HGMD pathogenic vs gnomAD putatively-benign variants — correlates strikingly with DMS agreement, cross-validating the rankings. Where it lands: the Karen / variant-interpretation priority — a CFTR VEP calibration-and-agreement notebook that treats CFTR's own published functional readouts as the DMS-style yardstick and picks the missense predictor to trust for CFTR specifically.

Plain-English glossary

  • AUROC — area under the ROC curve, a threshold-free summary of how well a score separates two classes.（中文：AUROC——ROC曲线下面积，衡量一个分数把两类分开的能力，不依赖具体阈值。）
  • Deep mutational scanning (DMS) — a massively parallel experiment that measures a functional score for thousands of protein variants at once, independent of any clinical label.（中文：深度突变扫描——一种大规模并行实验，一次性测量成千上万个蛋白质变异的功能分数，独立于任何临床标签。）
  • Data circularity — inflated benchmark performance caused by testing a predictor on data related to what it was trained on (Type 1: same variants; Type 2: gene-level pathogenic/benign skew).（中文：数据循环论证——用与训练数据相关的数据去测试预测器，导致基准表现虚高（第一类：同一批变异；第二类：基因层面致病/良性的偏斜）。）
  • ESM-1v — a protein language model trained on ~98M protein sequences that scores variants by zero-shot inference, no per-protein training or alignment needed.（中文：ESM-1v——在约9800万条蛋白质序列上训练的蛋白质语言模型，用零样本推断给变异打分，无需针对每个蛋白质训练或比对。）
  • EVE / DeepSequence — variational-autoencoder VEPs trained on a per-protein multiple sequence alignment.（中文：EVE／DeepSequence——基于每个蛋白质多序列比对训练的变分自编码器类预测器。）
  • Missense variant — a single-nucleotide change that swaps one amino acid for another.（中文：错义变异——把一个氨基酸换成另一个的单核苷酸改变。）
  • MSA (multiple sequence alignment) — the same protein stacked across many species, lined up position by position, used to read evolutionary constraint.（中文：MSA（多序列比对）——把同一蛋白质在许多物种间逐位置对齐堆叠，用于读取进化约束。）
  • Protein language model — a model trained on large protein-sequence corpora that learns which amino acids "belong" at each position.（中文：蛋白质语言模型——在大规模蛋白质序列上训练、学会每个位置"应该"出现哪些氨基酸的模型。）
  • Putatively benign (gnomAD) — population-database variants treated as benign but which still contain some recessive/low-penetrance/late-onset pathogenic variants.（中文：推定良性（gnomAD）——被当作良性的人群数据库变异，但仍含有一些隐性/低外显率/迟发的致病变异。）
  • VARITY — a gradient-boosted-tree supervised VEP that weights training data by predicted quality.（中文：VARITY——一种梯度提升树的有监督预测器，按预测质量对训练数据加权。）
  • VEP (variant effect predictor) — an algorithm that scores how damaging a variant is likely to be.（中文：VEP（变异效应预测器）——给变异的可能危害程度打分的算法。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant not yet classifiable as pathogenic or benign.（中文：VUS（意义未明变异）——尚无法判定为致病或良性的变异。）
  • Zero-shot inference — applying a trained model to a new task with no task-specific training.（中文：零样本推断——把已训练的模型直接用于新任务，不做针对性训练。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • DMS-as-independent-benchmark — use experimentally measured functional scores (never in training data) as the gold standard, avoiding both types of circularity.
  • Pairwise-win ranking over shared variants — score each predictor by the fraction of head-to-head Spearman comparisons it wins, computed only over variants both tools scored; bootstrap (1,000×) for significance.
  • Coverage correction — refill missing scores with each tool's most-benign value so low-coverage tools can't win by declining hard regions.
  • Two-benchmark cross-validation — correlate DMS-agreement ranking with an independent AUROC classification of ClinVar/HGMD vs gnomAD variants.
  • Named tools: ESM-1v (protein language model), EVE, DeepSequence (VAE on MSA), VARITY (gradient-boosted trees), MaveDB (DMS dataset repository).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP: CFTR VEP calibration-and-agreement notebook — input a table of CFTR missense variants × {published functional measurement (e.g. forskolin-stimulated chloride current, CFTR2 functional annotations), each predictor's score for ESM-1v / EVE / AlphaMissense / REVEL / VARITY}; treat the functional data as a DMS-style independent yardstick; output per-region agreement so you pick the missense predictor to trust for CFTR that feeds the PP3/BP4 lane of the curation stack. Difficulty: M. Public CFTR variant/functional data only; no registry internals.

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR functional assays are truly independent of predictor training labels (back-door circularity if clinical labels derive from the same assays).
  • Per-region variant counts — CFTR functional data is patchy and domain-concentrated; region-level conclusions need adequate n.
  • Which functional axis is measured (chloride conductance vs processing/trafficking vs expression) — different failure modes; a predictor strong on one may miss another.
  • Keep this paper's putatively-benign caveat in mind if a classification step is added.
  • Tooling exercise on public data only — decides no research design and says nothing about any registry's internal genotype data.

ALEX Good morning — today we're reading a 2023 paper from Molecular Systems Biology. 早上好，今天我们要读的是一篇2023年发表在《分子系统生物学》(Molecular Systems Biology)上的论文。The title is "Updated benchmarking of variant effect predictors using deep mutational scanning," by Benjamin Livesey and Joseph Marsh at the University of Edinburgh. 标题是《用深度突变扫描对变异效应预测器进行的最新基准评测》，作者是爱丁堡大学的Benjamin Livesey和Joseph Marsh。Good news first: this one is open access, so we have the full text, tables and all. 先说好消息：这篇是开放获取(OA)的，所以我们拿到了全文，包括所有表格。This is Track A — variant interpretation — and it's the missense counterpart to the splice paper we read yesterday. 这是Track A——变异解读，它是我们昨天读的剪接论文在错义方向上的对应篇。The one-line reason we're reading it: it ranks 55 variant effect predictors using a gold standard that sidesteps the circular-reasoning trap that makes most predictor rankings untrustworthy. 我们读它的一句话理由是：它用一个能绕开"循环论证"陷阱的金标准，对55个变异效应预测器做了排名，而那个陷阱正是让大多数预测器排名不可信的原因。

JORDAN Before the rankings, ground me in the biology. 在讲排名之前，先帮我把生物学背景铺清楚。What is a "variant effect predictor," and what does "missense" mean again? "变异效应预测器"是什么，"错义"又是什么意思来着？

ALEX A missense variant is a single-letter DNA change that swaps one amino acid in the protein for a different one. 错义变异(missense variant)是一个单字母的DNA改变，它把蛋白质里的一个氨基酸换成了另一个。Most of them are harmless; a few break the protein and cause disease; and the hard clinical problem is telling those two apart. 它们大多数是无害的；少数会破坏蛋白质、导致疾病；而临床上的难题就是把这两类区分开。A variant effect predictor, or VEP, is an algorithm that takes a variant and outputs a score for how damaging it's likely to be. 变异效应预测器，也就是VEP，是一种算法，它接收一个变异，然后输出一个分数，表示该变异有多大可能是有害的。These predictors lean on things like evolutionary conservation — if a position is the same amino acid across a thousand species, changing it is probably bad — plus physicochemical differences and structural context. 这些预测器依赖的东西包括进化保守性——如果某个位置在一千个物种里都是同一个氨基酸，那么改变它多半是坏事——再加上理化性质差异和结构背景。The reason this matters for us is VUS. 这对我们之所以重要，是因为VUS。A VUS (variant of uncertain significance) is a variant we've found in a patient but can't yet call pathogenic or benign, and VEP scores are one of the evidence types used to tip that call. VUS（意义未明变异，variant of uncertain significance）是我们在患者身上发现、但还无法判定为致病或良性的变异，而VEP分数是用来推动这个判定的证据类型之一。

JORDAN And "deep mutational scanning"? 那"深度突变扫描"呢？That's the gold standard in the title. 这就是标题里的那个金标准。

ALEX Right — this is the key term. 对，这是关键词。Deep mutational scanning, or DMS, is an experiment where you make a huge library of protein variants — ideally every possible amino acid substitution at every position — and then measure, in one massively parallel assay, how well each variant still performs some function. 深度突变扫描(deep mutational scanning，DMS)是一种实验：你构造一个庞大的蛋白质变异文库——理想情况下是每个位置上每一种可能的氨基酸替换——然后用一个大规模并行的实验，测量每个变异还能多好地执行某项功能。The functional readout varies by protein: yeast growth rate when the human protein has to rescue a defect, antibody binding on a cell surface, drug-resistance, protein stability by a fluorescence sort. 功能读出因蛋白质而异：当人类蛋白质需要挽救某个缺陷时用酵母的生长速率，细胞表面的抗体结合，耐药性，或用荧光分选测蛋白质稳定性。The output is a functional score for thousands of variants at once. 输出是同时对成千上万个变异给出的功能分数。The crucial property is that these scores come from a wet-lab experiment, not from clinical records — so they're independent of the clinical labels the predictors were trained on. 关键的性质在于，这些分数来自湿实验室的实验，而不是临床记录——所以它们独立于那些用来训练预测器的临床标签。

JORDAN Why is that independence such a big deal? 这个独立性为什么这么重要？

ALEX Because of circularity, which is the whole motivation of the paper. 因为循环论证(circularity)，这正是整篇论文的动机所在。The traditional way to benchmark a VEP is to take a set of known pathogenic and known benign variants from a clinical database and see if the predictor sorts them correctly. 给VEP做基准评测的传统做法，是从临床数据库里取一组已知致病和已知良性的变异，看预测器能否把它们正确分开。The problem is that many predictors were trained on those exact same clinical variants. 问题在于，很多预测器本身就是用这些同样的临床变异训练出来的。The authors name two kinds. 作者点出了两种类型。Type 1 circularity is literally testing a predictor on data it was trained on, which inflates its score. 第一类循环(Type 1 circularity)就是字面意义上用训练过的数据去测试预测器，从而虚高它的分数。Type 2 is subtler: if a gene's variants in the database are almost all pathogenic, a predictor can learn to just call everything in that gene "bad" and look accurate without understanding anything. 第二类更隐蔽：如果某个基因在数据库里的变异几乎全是致病的，预测器就能学会把该基因里的一切都判成"坏"，从而在不理解任何东西的情况下看起来很准。DMS breaks both, because the functional scores are continuous, per-variant, and were never in anyone's training set. DMS能打破这两种，因为功能分数是连续的、逐变异的，而且从来不在任何人的训练集里。

JORDAN Why has this been such a stubborn problem — surely we have millions of sequenced variants by now? 这个问题为什么这么顽固——到现在我们不是已经有几百万个测序过的变异了吗？

ALEX We do, but the labeled ones are the bottleneck. 我们是有，但有标签的那些才是瓶颈。Pathogenic variants are enriched among the very rarest variants in the population, so gathering enough well-classified examples to judge a predictor is genuinely hard. 致病变异集中在人群中最罕见的那一小撮变异里，所以要凑齐足够多、分类可靠的例子来评判一个预测器，是真的很难。And confidently benign rare variants are arguably harder still — absence of a variant from disease records isn't proof it's harmless. 而有把握判为良性的罕见变异可以说更难——某个变异没出现在疾病记录里，并不能证明它无害。So for two decades the field has leaned on the same small, reused clinical sets, which is exactly what breeds circularity. 于是二十年来，这个领域一直依赖同样一小批被反复使用的临床集合，而这恰恰是滋生循环论证的温床。DMS sidesteps the scarcity entirely: a single experiment can score thousands of variants that were never seen in any patient, so you're no longer starved for independent test data. DMS彻底绕开了这种稀缺：单个实验就能给成千上万个从未在任何患者身上见过的变异打分，于是你不再为独立的测试数据发愁。That abundance is really why this benchmarking strategy became possible at all. 正是这种丰富度，才让这套评测策略成为可能。

JORDAN So what did they actually do this time that was "updated"? 那这次他们究竟做了什么"更新"？

ALEX This builds on their earlier benchmark. 这是在他们早前的基准评测上做的扩展。They increased the DMS datasets from 13 to 26 human proteins — things like MSH2, TP53, CYP2C9, VKORC1, CBS, MTHFR — pulled from the MaveDB repository and the recent literature. 他们把DMS数据集从13个增加到26个人类蛋白质——像MSH2、TP53、CYP2C9、VKORC1、CBS、MTHFR这些——来自MaveDB数据库和近期文献。Against those, they benchmarked 55 different VEPs. 用这些数据，他们对55个不同的VEP做了评测。The scoring method is worth one sentence because it's clever: for each protein they take every pair of predictors and ask which one's scores correlate better with the DMS functional data, using Spearman correlation only over the variants both predictors actually scored. 评分方法值得说一句，因为它很巧妙：对每个蛋白质，他们取每一对预测器，用Spearman相关系数、只在两个预测器都实际打了分的变异上，问哪个的分数与DMS功能数据相关得更好。A predictor gets a point for every pairwise "win," and its final rank score is the fraction of other predictors it beats, averaged across all proteins. 一个预测器每"赢"一次成对比较就得一分，它最终的排名分数就是它击败的其他预测器所占的比例，在所有蛋白质上取平均。And here's the design choice I'd push on: comparing only over shared variants is fair, but it means a low-coverage predictor that only scores the easy, well-conserved positions could look artificially good. 这里有个我想追问的设计选择：只在共有变异上比较是公平的，但这意味着一个覆盖率低、只给容易的、高度保守的位置打分的预测器，可能会显得虚假地好。To their credit they test exactly this by filling missing scores with each tool's most-benign value, and re-rank. 值得肯定的是，他们正是通过用每个工具最"良性"的分数填补缺失值、然后重新排名来检验了这一点。

JORDAN And who won? 那谁赢了？

ALEX The top-ranked predictor overall was ESM-1v. 综合排名第一的预测器是ESM-1v。ESM-1v is a protein language model — a model trained on 98 million protein sequences the way a text language model is trained on sentences, learning which amino acids "belong" at each position. ESM-1v是一个蛋白质语言模型(protein language model)——它像文本语言模型学句子那样，在9800万条蛋白质序列上训练，学会哪些氨基酸"应该"出现在每个位置。The striking part is that it does this by zero-shot inference: no per-protein training, no alignment to build, you just hand it a sequence and it scores variants. 令人瞩目的是它靠零样本推断(zero-shot inference，即模型不经针对性训练直接完成新任务)做到这一点：不需要针对每个蛋白质训练，不需要构建比对，你只要把序列交给它，它就给变异打分。That makes it dramatically faster and easier to run than the other top methods. 这让它比其他顶尖方法快得多、也好用得多。Its lead over the second-place method, EVE, wasn't statistically significant, so read it as "ESM-1v is in the top tier," not "ESM-1v is uniquely best." 它领先第二名EVE的幅度在统计上并不显著，所以应把它读作"ESM-1v处在第一梯队"，而不是"ESM-1v独一无二地最好"。The best supervised predictor was VARITY, a gradient-boosted tree model that weights its training data by predicted quality; it was significantly beaten only by ESM-1v. 表现最好的有监督预测器是VARITY，一个梯度提升树模型，它按预测质量对训练数据加权；只有ESM-1v显著地击败了它。Then EVE and DeepSequence, both variational autoencoders trained on a multiple sequence alignment — an MSA, meaning a stack of the same protein across many species lined up position by position. 接着是EVE和DeepSequence，两者都是在多序列比对(MSA，multiple sequence alignment，即把同一蛋白质在许多物种间逐位置对齐的堆叠)上训练的变分自编码器。

JORDAN There's a subtlety I want to catch — VARITY used some DMS data in its training, right? 我想抓住一个微妙之处——VARITY在训练里用过一些DMS数据，对吧？Isn't that the circularity trap again? 这不又是循环论证陷阱吗？

ALEX Sharp catch, and the authors handle it head-on. 抓得准，作者正面处理了这一点。VARITY had used 10 of the same DMS datasets they benchmark with. VARITY用过其中10个和他们评测所用相同的DMS数据集。So they simply dropped those 10 and re-ranked — and VARITY held at 4th and 5th place, its scores even improving slightly. 于是他们干脆去掉那10个再重新排名——结果VARITY仍稳居第四和第五，分数甚至还略有提升。So its strength isn't a circularity artifact. 所以它的强势并不是循环论证的产物。And they stress-test robustness in two more ways worth knowing. 他们还用另外两种值得了解的方式对稳健性做了压力测试。First, the coverage correction I mentioned: when they fill each tool's missing scores with its most-benign value so low-coverage tools can't hide in hard regions, ESM-1v keeps first place, EVE drops from 2nd to 4th because it has the lowest coverage of the top group at 84%, and mutationTCN — the lowest-coverage tool overall at 64.5% — collapses from 9th to 20th, exposing that much of its apparent skill was just declining to guess where guessing is hard. 第一，是我提到的覆盖度校正：当他们用每个工具最"良性"的分数填补缺失值、让低覆盖的工具无法躲在难预测的区域里时，ESM-1v仍居第一，EVE因为在顶尖组里覆盖度最低（84%）而从第二跌到第四，而mutationTCN——总体覆盖度最低（64.5%）——从第九崩到第二十，暴露出它看似的本领很大程度上只是回避在难猜处下注。Second, they slide a 20-amino-acid window along each protein and drop regions where every predictor correlates poorly with the DMS data, on the logic that comparisons are meaningless where no tool works; the rankings barely move. 第二，他们沿每个蛋白质滑动一个20氨基酸的窗口，删掉所有预测器都与DMS数据相关很差的区段，理由是在没有工具管用的地方比较毫无意义；结果排名几乎不动。That stability across re-slicings is itself reassuring. 这种在多次重新切分下的稳定性本身就令人安心。

JORDAN You said there's a second, independent test. Walk me through it. 你说还有第二个独立的检验，给我讲讲。

ALEX This is the part that most validates the whole approach. 这是最能验证整个方法的部分。They asked a different question: can these tools, or the DMS data directly, separate genuinely pathogenic variants from benign ones? 他们问了一个不同的问题：这些工具、或者DMS数据本身，能不能把真正致病的变异和良性的分开？They pulled pathogenic and likely-pathogenic variants from ClinVar and HGMD, and putatively benign variants from gnomAD — the population database of people without severe pediatric disease — and computed AUROC. 他们从ClinVar和HGMD取致病和疑似致病的变异，从gnomAD——即没有严重儿童期疾病的人群数据库——取推定良性的变异，然后计算AUROC。The finding that matters: there's a striking correlation between how well a predictor agrees with DMS data and how well it classifies real clinical variants. 关键发现是：一个预测器与DMS数据的吻合程度，和它对真实临床变异的分类能力之间，存在惊人的相关性。Two completely independent benchmarks agreeing is strong evidence the rankings are real, not artifacts. 两个完全独立的基准评测互相吻合，是这些排名真实、而非虚象的有力证据。They also note the mixed side: some DMS datasets classify clinical variants superbly, others poorly, so a functional assay is only as good as its relevance to the disease mechanism. 他们也指出了参差的一面：有些DMS数据集对临床变异分类得极好，有些则很差，所以一个功能实验的价值，取决于它与疾病机制的相关性。
