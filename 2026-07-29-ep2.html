An open data model for pharmacogenetics — semantic interoperability with openEHR + FHIR (Track B)

Blurb. Sharma et al. (The Pharmacogenomics Journal, 2026, open access) develop and validate an open data model for pharmacogenetic test results, built as an openEHR archetype and mapped to HL7 FHIR. Its central design move is separation of concerns: the genetic test result (permanent, universal) is modeled and stored apart from the therapeutic implication (contingent, evolving with evidence and drug labels), linked but independently updatable. The model binds to SNOMED CT, HGNC, LOINC, and CPIC terms; it was refined through two Delphi-inspired review rounds with 24 experts across 10 countries, published on the openEHR Clinical Knowledge Manager (v1.0, 22 Nov 2024), and mapped to FHIR both manually and automatically via FHIR-Connect with demonstrated bidirectional flow. Validation was consensus- and interview-based, not a live deployment. Where it lands: AI-tooling / career (secondary: Liz-style phenotype & data-quality, method only) — the buildable artifact is a cftr-modulator-indicator-model that stores a CFTR genotype once as a stable "genomic indicator" and keeps modulator eligibility in a version-stamped therapeutic-implication layer.

Plain-English glossary.

  • Archetype (openEHR) — a reusable, agreed content model of one clinical concept (e.g. a pharmacogenetic result).（中文：archetype是openEHR中对某一临床概念的可复用、经共识的内容模型。）
  • CPIC — Clinical Pharmacogenetics Implementation Consortium; writes guidelines translating genotype into prescribing recommendations.（中文：CPIC是临床药物基因组学实施联盟，撰写把基因型翻译成处方建议的指南。）
  • Diplotype — the pair of star-alleles a person carries (one per chromosome), which imply the metaboliser phenotype.（中文：diplotype是一个人携带的一对星号等位基因（每条染色体一个），可推断代谢型表型。）
  • FHIR — Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources; a standard for exchanging health data between systems as messages.（中文：FHIR是一套以消息形式在系统间交换健康数据的标准。）
  • Genomic indicator — a dedicated record "home" for a lifelong-reused genomic result, instead of forcing it into an observation/diagnosis slot.（中文：基因组指标是为终身反复使用的基因组结果设立的专属存放位，而非硬塞进"观察"或"诊断"字段。）
  • HGNC — HUGO Gene Nomenclature Committee; assigns official gene symbols (CFTR is always "CFTR").（中文：HGNC是人类基因命名委员会，分配官方基因符号，使CFTR恒为"CFTR"。）
  • openEHR — an open standard for modeling and storing clinical data in full detail ("maximum dataset" philosophy).（中文：openEHR是以完整细节建模和存储临床数据的开放标准，奉行"最大数据集"哲学。）
  • Semantic interoperability — the receiving system understands the meaning identically, achieved by binding data elements to shared terminologies.（中文：语义互操作性指接收系统对含义的理解完全一致，靠把数据元素绑定到共享术语实现。）
  • SNOMED CT / LOINC — controlled clinical / laboratory terminologies with stable codes.（中文：SNOMED CT与LOINC是带稳定代码的受控临床／实验室术语。）

Full citation. Sharma V, McDermott JH, Keen J, Koikkalainen H, McNicoll I, Davies A, Newman WG. Development and validation of an open data model for pharmacogenetics to enable semantic interoperability in clinical practice. The Pharmacogenomics Journal, 2026. PMID: 42285967 · PMC: PMC13263137 · DOI: 10.1038/s41397-026-00418-0

Methods / tools to lift.

  • Separation of concerns — store the permanent genetic result apart from the mutable therapeutic implication; link, don't merge.
  • openEHR-first, FHIR-second — model rich (maximum dataset), then map down to FHIR's 80/20 core; the reverse loses detail.
  • Terminology binding — HGNC (gene), HGVS (variant), SNOMED CT/LOINC/CPIC (results, guidance) turn strings into computable facts.
  • FHIR-Connect — tool for automated, reusable openEHR↔FHIR mappings instead of hand-coding each transformation.
  • Delphi-inspired review on openEHR CKM — structured asynchronous expert consensus as a governance/validation pattern.
  • Genomic indicator — a reusable record slot pattern that generalizes to PRS and single-gene disease markers.

Where it lands + buildable artifact (tool sketch). Serves AI-tooling / career (secondary: Liz phenotype & data-quality, method only). Paste-ready MVP: cftr-modulator-indicator-model (M) — define two linked-but-separate structures: (1) a stable "genomic indicator" holding the CFTR genotype in HGVS + variant class/function annotation; (2) a version-stamped therapeutic-implication layer holding modulator eligibility for that genotype, stamped with label version + guideline date. Leaf terms bind HGNC/HGVS + a controlled eligibility vocabulary; ship as JSON Schema + FHIR mapping. Payoff: an FDA label expansion becomes a one-table update and every stored genotype's eligibility re-derives with no data migration.

What to verify first.

  • The separation holds for CFTR — confirm no therapeutic logic leaks into the "permanent" genetic structure (eligibility sometimes depends on functional evidence).
  • Terminology coverage — HGNC/HGVS + eligibility vocabulary must cover CFTR complex alleles and compound-het genotypes without lossy encoding.
  • Scalability / real-world fit is open — validated by consensus + interviews, not deployment; test the FHIR round-trip on real CFTR records.
  • External data-modeling method only — no claim about any registry's internal genotype storage; decides no research design.

ALEX Welcome back. 欢迎回来。 Today's Track B paper is "Development and validation of an open data model for pharmacogenetics to enable semantic interoperability in clinical practice," by Sharma, McDermott, Keen, and colleagues from Manchester, published in The Pharmacogenomics Journal in 2026. 今天Track B的论文是《开发并验证一套用于药物基因组学的开放数据模型，以在临床实践中实现语义互操作性》，作者是来自曼彻斯特的Sharma、McDermott、Keen等人，2026年发表于《The Pharmacogenomics Journal》。 It is open access, so we read the full text. 它是开放获取的，所以我们读了全文。 It is a Track B pick because it ships a concrete, published, machine-readable artifact — an openEHR archetype on a public platform, plus a FHIR mapping — and the whole thing is a template for structuring genotype-to-therapy data for reuse. 它入选Track B，是因为它交付了一个具体、已发表、机器可读的产物——一个发布在公开平台上的openEHR原型 (archetype)，外加一套FHIR映射——而整件事是一个把"基因型到治疗"数据结构化以便复用的模板。

JORDAN This one is more informatics than biology, so define the standards for me. 这一篇更偏信息学而不是生物学，所以先给我把这些标准讲清楚。 What are openEHR and FHIR, and why would you need both? openEHR和FHIR是什么，为什么两个都要？

ALEX Both are open standards for health data, but they solve different halves of the problem. 两者都是开放的健康数据标准，但它们解决的是问题的不同半边。 openEHR is a standard for modeling and storing clinical information in detail — you build reusable content models called "archetypes," each one a rich, agreed definition of a clinical concept like a lab result. openEHR（一套用于对临床信息进行建模与存储的开放标准）是用来详细建模和存储临床信息的——你构建可复用的内容模型，称为"原型 (archetype)"，每个原型都是对某个临床概念（例如一项化验结果）的丰富且经过共识的定义。 FHIR — Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources — is a standard for exchanging data between systems as messages over the wire. FHIR（Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources，快速医疗互操作资源）是一套用于在系统之间以消息形式传输数据的标准。 The paper's mental model is: openEHR is how you store the source of truth in full detail; FHIR is how you ship a slice of it to another system. 论文的思路是：openEHR用于以完整细节存储"真相之源"，FHIR用于把其中一部分传送到另一个系统。 openEHR follows a "maximum dataset" philosophy — capture everything — while FHIR follows an 80/20 philosophy — carry the common core. openEHR遵循"最大数据集"哲学——尽量把一切都捕获——而FHIR遵循80/20哲学——只承载常用的核心部分。 That asymmetry is why they start with openEHR: it is easy to map a rich model down to FHIR, but you cannot recover detail you never modeled if you start narrow. 这种不对称正是他们从openEHR起步的原因：把一个丰富的模型向下映射到FHIR很容易，但如果你一开始就建得很窄，就无法找回你从未建模的细节。

JORDAN And "semantic interoperability" — how is that different from just sending a file? "语义互操作性"——这和单纯发一个文件有什么区别？

ALEX Sending a file gets bytes across; semantic interoperability means the receiving system understands the meaning identically. 发文件只是把字节传过去；语义互操作性意味着接收系统对含义的理解完全一致。 The way you get there is by binding each data element to a shared terminology — a controlled vocabulary with stable codes. 达到这一点的办法是把每个数据元素绑定到一套共享术语——一套带有稳定代码的受控词表。 This model binds to SNOMED CT for clinical concepts, HGNC for gene symbols, and LOINC plus the CPIC-recommended terms for pharmacogenetic results. 这个模型绑定了SNOMED CT用于临床概念、HGNC（人类基因命名委员会，规范基因符号）用于基因符号、以及LOINC加上CPIC推荐的术语用于药物基因组学结果。 HGNC is the committee that assigns official gene symbols so that CFTR is always "CFTR" and never a synonym. HGNC负责分配官方基因符号，让CFTR永远是"CFTR"，而不会变成某个同义写法。 CPIC — the Clinical Pharmacogenetics Implementation Consortium — is the group that writes the guidelines translating a genotype into a prescribing recommendation. CPIC（Clinical Pharmacogenetics Implementation Consortium，临床药物基因组学实施联盟）是撰写指南、把基因型翻译成处方建议的机构。 Without those bindings, "CYP2C19 intermediate metaboliser" is just a string; with them, it is a computable fact two systems agree on. 没有这些绑定，"CYP2C19中间代谢型"只是一串字符；有了它们，它就成了两个系统达成一致的可计算事实。

JORDAN Why not just standardize on FHIR alone — isn't one standard simpler than two? 为什么不干脆只标准化到FHIR——一个标准不是比两个更简单吗？

ALEX Simpler to operate, but it would lock in a loss you can never undo, and that is the argument the paper makes explicitly. 操作上更简单，但它会锁死一个你永远无法挽回的损失，而这正是论文明确提出的论点。 FHIR is built to move a common core efficiently, so it deliberately does not carry every detail; if you author your source of truth in FHIR, the details it omits were never captured. FHIR是为高效搬运常用核心而设计的，所以它有意不承载每一个细节；如果你把"真相之源"直接写成FHIR，它省略掉的细节就从未被捕获过。 openEHR's maximum-dataset philosophy captures everything first, and then you project the relevant slice into FHIR for exchange. openEHR的"最大数据集"哲学先把一切都捕获下来，然后你再把相关的切片投射成FHIR用于交换。 The direction of the mapping is the whole point: rich-to-lean is a projection you can always redo differently later; lean-to-rich is guessing at information you threw away. 映射的方向就是关键：从丰富到精简是一个你日后总能以不同方式重做的投射；从精简到丰富则是在猜测你已经丢弃的信息。 It also keeps you free to map to other targets — they note that starting from openEHR, mapping onward to OMOP stays possible, whereas starting from FHIR would have foreclosed it. 它还让你保有映射到其他目标的自由——他们指出，从openEHR起步，向下映射到OMOP仍然可行，而从FHIR起步就会把这条路封死。

JORDAN What problem were they actually trying to fix? 他们到底想解决什么问题？

ALEX Pharmacogenetics has strong clinical and economic evidence, but it barely gets used at scale, and the bottleneck is not the science — it is the plumbing. 药物基因组学有很强的临床和经济学证据，却几乎没能大规模落地，而瓶颈不在科学，而在"管道"。 Historically the output of genetic testing is a report — a PDF, not machine-readable, written in specialist language, and easy to lose. 历史上，基因检测的产物是一份报告——一个PDF，不是机器可读的，用专家语言写成，还容易丢失。 That works for rare-disease clinics, but pharmacogenetics is different: prescribing happens everywhere, including general practice, and a genetic result is lifelong, so the same result needs to resurface at every prescribing moment for decades. 这对罕见病门诊行得通，但药物基因组学不一样：开药到处都在发生，包括全科诊疗，而且一个基因结果是终身有效的，所以同一个结果需要在往后几十年的每一个处方时刻重新浮现。 Point-to-point messaging between systems does not scale to that; you need something interoperable by design. 系统之间点对点的消息传递撑不起这种规模；你需要一种"设计上即可互操作"的东西。 They also flag a subtler conceptual bug: prior work jammed two different things into one model. 他们还指出了一个更微妙的概念性缺陷：以往的工作把两个不同的东西塞进了同一个模型。

JORDAN Which two things? 哪两个东西？

ALEX The genetic test result and the therapeutic implication. 基因检测结果，和治疗上的含义。 The result — "this person has reduced function in a drug-processing enzyme" — is permanent and universal; it does not change over a lifetime. 结果——"此人某个药物代谢酶功能降低"——是永久且普适的，一辈子不会变。 The therapeutic implication — "therefore adjust the dose of drug X" — is contingent: it shifts as evidence evolves, as new drugs appear, as guidelines update. 治疗含义——"因此调整药物X的剂量"——是有条件的：它会随证据演进、新药出现、指南更新而改变。 The paper's core design move is to model these as two separate concepts, linked but stored apart. 论文的核心设计动作，是把这两者建模为两个独立的概念，彼此关联但分开存储。 The stable genetic fact is recorded once as a pharmacogenetic test result; the mutable guidance lives separately as a therapeutic implication in a knowledge base that can be updated without touching the genetic record. 稳定的基因事实作为"药物基因检测结果"只记录一次；可变的指导则作为"治疗含义"单独存放在一个可以更新、而无需触动基因记录的知识库里。 If I push on one thing, it is that the paper validates through stakeholder interviews and a Delphi-style expert review rather than a live deployment — the model is consensus-strong but not yet field-tested. 如果要我质疑一点，那就是这篇论文是通过利益相关者访谈和德尔菲式专家评审来验证的，而不是真实上线部署——这个模型在共识层面很强，但还没经过现场检验。

JORDAN Why does that separation actually help — it sounds tidy, but what breaks if you don't do it? 这种分离到底有什么用——听起来很整洁，但如果不这么做会坏在哪？

ALEX What breaks is every future update. 会坏的是未来的每一次更新。 If the genetic result and the prescribing guidance live in one record, then when the guidance changes — a new drug, revised evidence, an expanded label — you have to reach into and rewrite records that also hold the permanent genetic fact. 如果基因结果和处方指导住在同一条记录里，那么当指导发生变化时——新药、修订的证据、扩展的标签——你就得伸手去改写那些同时保存着永久基因事实的记录。 That is error-prone, it risks corrupting the stable part, and it does not scale across millions of records. 这容易出错，可能污染那个稳定的部分，而且在数百万条记录上无法扩展。 Separate them, and a guidance change touches only the therapeutic-implication layer while every genetic record sits untouched and simply re-derives its implication on read. 把它们分开，一次指导变更就只触及治疗含义层，而每一条基因记录原封不动，只是在读取时重新推导它的含义。 It is the same instinct as separating slowly-changing reference data from immutable facts in any well-designed store — you never rewrite history to reflect a new interpretation. 这与在任何设计良好的存储中"把缓慢变化的参考数据和不可变的事实分开"是同一种直觉——你绝不为了反映新的解读而去改写历史。

JORDAN Walk me through how they built and reviewed it. 带我过一遍他们怎么构建和评审的。

ALEX They started from a baseline model synthesized from genomic sequencing outputs, the literature, and a prior 2022 FHIR pharmacogenetic specification, drawn out first as a mind map. 他们从一个基线模型出发，这个模型综合了基因组测序的输出、文献、以及此前2022年的一份FHIR药物基因组学规范，最初以思维导图的形式画出来。 Then they ran it through two rounds of Delphi-inspired peer review on the openEHR Clinical Knowledge Manager platform — 14 reviewers in the first round, 16 in the second, spanning 10 countries and a mix of doctors, pharmacists, nurses, lab staff, and health-IT professionals. 然后他们在openEHR临床知识管理平台 (Clinical Knowledge Manager) 上进行了两轮德尔菲式同行评审——第一轮14名评审者，第二轮16名，横跨10个国家，涵盖医生、药师、护士、实验室人员和健康信息技术专业人士。 Delphi review just means structured, asynchronous rounds of expert feedback that converge toward consensus. 德尔菲评审指的就是结构化、异步的多轮专家反馈，逐步收敛到共识。 The finalized archetype, version 1.0, was published on 22 November 2024 and is openly viewable. 最终定稿的原型，版本1.0，于2024年11月22日发布，且公开可查。 The model's core elements are the gene symbol, the diplotype, and the inferred phenotype, plus allele/haplotype detail with per-allele activity values and an aggregated activity score, plus the genomic region studied so the scope and limits of the test are explicit. 模型的核心元素是基因符号、双倍型 (diplotype) 和推断出的表型，再加上等位基因/单倍型细节及每个等位基因的活性值和一个汇总的活性分数，还有所检测的基因组区域，好让检测的范围和局限一目了然。 A diplotype is the pair of star-alleles a person carries — one per chromosome — which together imply the metaboliser phenotype. 双倍型 (diplotype) 是一个人携带的一对星号等位基因——每条染色体一个——它们共同推断出代谢型表型。 For FHIR they tested mapping both manually and automatically with a tool called FHIR-Connect, and showed bidirectional flow. 在FHIR方面，他们既手动、也用一个叫FHIR-Connect的工具自动测试了映射，并展示了双向的信息流动。

JORDAN Results — what did it deliver? 结果——它交付了什么？

ALEX The concrete deliverable is the separation-of-concerns data model itself, published, consensus-approved across 10 countries, and terminology-bound. 具体的交付物就是这个"关注点分离"的数据模型本身，已发布、经10个国家共识批准、且已绑定术语。 The FHIR mapping demonstrated bidirectional information flow, and the automated route via FHIR-Connect showed that the transformations can be scalable and reusable rather than hand-coded each time. FHIR映射展示了双向的信息流动，而经由FHIR-Connect的自动化路径表明，这些转换可以是可扩展、可复用的，而不必每次手工编码。 The stakeholder interviews surfaced the real adoption levers: alignment with existing prescribing infrastructure, results presented concisely inside prescribing workflows with decision support to cut cognitive load, and governance plus training as gating factors. 利益相关者访谈揭示了真正的采纳杠杆：与既有开药基础设施对齐、在开药流程内以简洁方式呈现结果并配以决策支持以降低认知负荷、以及把治理和培训作为关键前提。 They also promote a broader idea worth stealing: the "genomic indicator" — a dedicated home in the record for a genomic result that is used repeatedly across a lifetime, rather than forcing it into an "observation" or "diagnosis" slot that does not fit its clinical use. 他们还倡导一个值得借鉴的更宽泛的想法："基因组指标 (genomic indicator)"——在病历中为一个会在一生中被反复使用的基因组结果设立一个专属的存放位置，而不是硬塞进"观察"或"诊断"这类并不契合其临床用法的字段里。 The genomic indicator concept extends beyond pharmacogenetics to polygenic risk scores and single-gene disease markers. "基因组指标"这个概念可以从药物基因组学延伸到多基因风险评分和单基因疾病标记。

JORDAN Why is "genomic indicator" a better home than the observation or diagnosis slots systems already have? 为什么"基因组指标"比系统里已有的"观察"或"诊断"字段是更好的存放位？
