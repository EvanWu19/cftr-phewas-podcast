Blurb

The European label for elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor (ETI) excludes anyone carrying two Class I CFTR variants -- the class defined by a complete absence of CFTR protein, which leaves modulators with nothing to fix. This 2025 EClinicalMedicine paper from the French compassionate ETI programme puts that exclusion to a real-world test: of 163 people with two Class I variants who received ETI anyway, 155 (95%) were non-responders as expected, but 8 (5%) responded clinically, with meaningful drops in sweat chloride and gains in lung function. Tracing the responders' genotypes back to a predictive "exceptional Class I variant" list -- built from splice-site logic, exon position, and known residual-function evidence across CFTR2 and CFTR-France -- the authors show the response tracks to specific, identifiable variants rather than being random noise. It's a working example of exactly the kind of clinical-evidence-driven variant reclassification loop the Karen Raraigh collaboration angle is built around: a curated database (CFTR2-style) plus a stream of real outcome data, continuously reconciled by human experts. Buildable artifact: a variant-evidence tracker that flags disagreements between a variant's current structural classification and its accumulating clinical-outcome evidence, for expert review -- not automated reclassification.

Plain-English glossary

  • Class I variant -- a CFTR variant causing complete absence of functional protein at the cell surface (nonsense, canonical splice-site, frameshift, start-loss, or large exon deletion).（中文：导致细胞表面完全没有功能性CFTR蛋白的一类变异，包括无义变异、经典剪接位点变异、移码变异、起始密码子缺失或大片段外显子缺失。）
  • CFTR2 / CFTR-France -- expert-curated reference databases that classify CFTR variants by functional class and clinical evidence, used by clinicians and regulators to determine modulator eligibility.（中文：由专家审核维护的CFTR变异参考数据库，根据功能分类和临床证据对变异分类，供临床医生和监管机构判断调节剂适用性。）
  • ETI (elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor) -- the triple-combination CFTR modulator drug at the center of this study; a corrector-plus-potentiator combination designed to help an existing CFTR protein fold and open correctly.（中文：本研究核心的三联CFTR调节剂药物，属于矫正剂加增效剂组合，用于帮助已合成的CFTR蛋白正确折叠和开放。）
  • sweat chloride concentration (SCC) -- a direct functional readout of CFTR channel activity measured in sweat gland fluid; lower values after treatment indicate more channel activity.（中文：通过汗腺液测量的CFTR通道活性的直接功能指标，治疗后数值下降代表通道活性增加。）
  • ppFEV1 -- percent-predicted forced expiratory volume in one second, the standard lung-function metric in CF trials, expressed relative to the expected value for a person's age, sex, and height.（中文：一秒用力呼气容积占预计值的百分比，是CF临床试验中的标准肺功能指标，以相对于同龄同性别同身高人群预期值的百分比表示。）
  • adjudication committee -- a panel that reviews all available clinical data together (not a single lab value in isolation) to make a final responder/non-responder determination.（中文：综合评估全部临床数据（而非单一化验值）以做出最终响应/无响应判定的专家小组。）
  • "exceptional" Class I variant -- a variant structurally classified as Class I but flagged, by predictive logic (splice leakage, exon position, documented residual function), as potentially still producing some CFTR protein.（中文：结构上归类为I类，但根据预测逻辑（剪接漏读、外显子位置、已知残留功能证据）被标记为仍可能产生部分CFTR蛋白的变异。）
  • theratype -- a CF genotype grouping based on predicted or observed response to a specific class of modulator therapy, distinct from the structural variant class itself.（中文：根据对特定类别调节剂治疗的预测或实际反应对CF基因型进行的分组，区别于变异本身的结构分类。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • CFTR2 / CFTR-France cross-referencing -- pooling two independently curated variant databases to build a comprehensive structural-Class-I variant list (1,033 variants) before applying finer-grained predictive logic.
  • Predictive "exceptional variant" flagging rules -- a reusable structural checklist (splice-leakage plausibility, terminal exon/intron position, canonical splice T>C substitutions, documented residual function, modulator-binding-region-sparing in-frame deletions) for triaging which nominally-null variants deserve closer scrutiny.
  • Centralized adjudication committee model -- multi-signal (SCC + ppFEV1 + clinical/imaging) responder determination rather than a single-threshold rule.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen Raraigh / CFTR2-style variant curation and reclassification. Buildable artifact: a variant-evidence tracker joining (1) a variant table keyed on HGVS notation with current classification + source database/version, and (2) a per-variant outcome-event table (SCC delta, ppFEV1 delta, adjudicated status, n observed) -- flagging variants where accumulated evidence crosses a threshold and disagrees with the current label, for human curator review. Difficulty: S.

What to verify first

  • Most "probably non-responsive" (85) and "probably responsive" (6) exceptional-variant calls rest on evidence from just one or two patients each -- only 23 variants had ≥3 cases backing the classification.
  • Whether the French programme's adjudication methodology and responder threshold would transfer cleanly to a different clinical/registry context.
  • This paper says nothing about CFFPR's own genotype or modulator-response data structure, or whether a comparable adjudication process exists there -- open question, not something this paper can answer.

ALEX Today's paper is a 2025 EClinicalMedicine study from the French compassionate ETI programme, led by Pierre-Regis Burgel's group, and it's Track A -- variant interpretation and harmonization, feeding the Karen Raraigh collaboration angle. 今天这篇论文发表于2025年的《EClinicalMedicine》，来自法国elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor同情用药项目（compassionate programme），由Pierre-Regis Burgel团队主导，属于今天的A轨——变异解读与协调统一，这条线直接服务于与Karen Raraigh合作的方向。 It asks a narrow, high-stakes question: what happens when you give the triple-combination CFTR modulator to people whose genotype the drug label says shouldn't respond at all? 这篇论文提出了一个范围很窄但意义重大的问题：当把三联CFTR调节剂用于说明书上明确写着"不应有反应"的基因型患者身上时，会发生什么? We're reading it because it's a live example of the exact kind of variant-classification revision work the Karen conversation is about -- not a new predictive model, but a clinical dataset forcing a curated database to update itself. 我们选读这篇，是因为它正是Karen那次对话所指向的那类变异分类修订工作的一个真实案例——不是一个新的预测模型，而是一份临床数据集，迫使一个经过人工审核的数据库进行自我更新。

JORDAN Let's start with the vocabulary. What's a "Class I" CFTR variant, and why does that class matter so much for treatment decisions? 我们先从术语讲起。什么是CFTR基因的"I类"变异（Class I variant），这个分类为什么对治疗决策如此重要？

ALEX CFTR variants are historically sorted into six functional classes based on what goes wrong with the protein. CFTR变异历来按照蛋白质出问题的方式被分为六个功能类别。 Class I means defective protein synthesis -- essentially, no CFTR protein reaches the cell surface at all, because the variant is a nonsense variant creating a premature stop codon, a canonical splice-site variant, a frameshift, a start-loss, or a large exon deletion. I类（Class I）指的是蛋白质合成缺陷——本质上就是根本没有CFTR蛋白能到达细胞表面，原因可能是无义变异（nonsense variant，产生提前终止密码子）、经典剪接位点变异（splice-site variant）、移码变异（frameshift）、起始密码子缺失变异（start-loss），或大片段外显子缺失。 That matters enormously for the CFTR modulators -- elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor, or ETI, the triple-combination drug at the center of this paper -- because modulators work by helping an already-made CFTR protein fold correctly and open properly. 这一点对CFTR调节剂——本文核心药物、三联复方药elexacaftor-tezacaftor-ivacaftor（简称ETI）——极为重要，因为调节剂的作用机制，是帮助一个已经被合成出来的CFTR蛋白正确折叠、正常开放。 If there's no protein to begin with, there's nothing for a modulator to fix, which is exactly why the current European label excludes people with two Class I variants from ETI eligibility. 如果一开始就没有蛋白质，调节剂就无从"修复"，这正是目前欧洲药品说明书将携带两个I类变异的患者排除在ETI适应症之外的原因。 The two reference databases that classify these variants -- CFTR2, curated in part at CHOP, and CFTR-France -- are the ground truth clinicians and regulators use to make that eligibility call. 用来对这些变异进行分类的两个参考数据库——部分由费城儿童医院（CHOP）参与整理的CFTR2，以及CFTR-France——正是临床医生和监管机构据以做出这一适应症判定的"金标准"。

JORDAN And just so I've got the full picture -- there are six of these classes total? What are the other five, roughly? 那让我把整个图景补全一下——总共有六个这样的类别？其余五类大致是什么？

ALEX Roughly: Class II is a folding/trafficking defect -- the protein is made but gets stuck and degraded before reaching the cell surface, which is the F508del situation and the single most common CF genotype worldwide. 大致来说：II类是折叠/转运缺陷——蛋白质被合成出来，但在到达细胞表面之前就被卡住并降解，这正是F508del的情形，也是全球最常见的CF基因型。 Classes III through VI describe progressively milder problems -- a channel that reaches the surface but won't open (gating), one that opens too briefly or conducts poorly, one made in reduced quantity, and one that's structurally normal but functionally unstable. III类到VI类描述的是程度依次减轻的问题——通道到达了细胞表面但打不开（门控缺陷）、打开时间过短或传导能力差、合成数量减少，以及结构正常但功能不稳定。 ETI, as a triple-combination corrector-plus-potentiator, was designed for exactly those classes where some protein exists to be corrected and potentiated -- which is why Class I, with no protein at all, sits outside the intended mechanism, and why any responder found there is scientifically interesting rather than just an edge case to shrug off. ETI作为一种矫正剂加增效剂的三联复方药，正是为那些"存在一定量蛋白质可供矫正和增效"的分类而设计的——这正是为何完全没有蛋白质的I类被排除在其预期作用机制之外，也正是为何在I类中发现有反应者具有科学意义，而不只是一个可以忽略的边缘案例。

ALEX Here's the wrinkle this paper is built around. 而这篇论文正是围绕这个问题展开的。 Not every variant that structurally looks like Class I -- say, a splice-site change or an in-frame exon deletion -- actually behaves like Class I in a real patient. 并非每一个结构上看起来像I类的变异——比如剪接位点改变，或整码框内的外显子缺失——在真实患者体内实际表现得都像I类。 Some splice variants leak a little residual, correctly spliced transcript through. 有些剪接变异会"漏出"少量经过正确剪接的转录本。 Some in-frame deletions remove a chunk of protein that isn't where modulators bind. 有些整码框内缺失切除的那部分蛋白，恰好不在调节剂结合的位置。 The authors call these "exceptional" Class I variants -- structurally Class I, but potentially still producing some CFTR protein, which means a modulator might have something to work with after all. 作者将这类变异称为"例外"I类变异（exceptional Class I variant）——结构上属于I类，但仍可能产生一定量的CFTR蛋白，这意味着调节剂或许仍有可作用的对象。

JORDAN Before we get to what they found -- how do you even measure whether ETI is "working" in a given patient? 在讲结果之前——你们究竟怎么衡量ETI在某位患者身上"起效"了没有？

ALEX Two main readouts. 主要靠两个指标。 Sweat chloride concentration, or SCC, is a direct functional readout of CFTR channel activity at the sweat gland -- lower chloride after starting a modulator means the channel is doing more of its job. 汗氯浓度（sweat chloride concentration，简称SCC）是CFTR通道在汗腺处活性的直接功能读数——开始使用调节剂后氯离子浓度下降，说明该通道确实在发挥更多功能。 And ppFEV1, percent-predicted forced expiratory volume in one second, is the standard lung-function metric in CF trials -- how much air you can forcefully exhale in a second, expressed as a percentage of what's expected for someone of your age, sex, and height. ppFEV1（一秒用力呼气容积占预计值的百分比）是CF临床试验中的标准肺功能指标——指一秒钟内用力呼出的气体量，以相对于同龄、同性别、同身高健康人群预期值的百分比表示。 In this study, both were tracked before ETI and again after a 4-6 week trial period, and a centralized adjudication committee -- a panel reviewing all the clinical data together, not just one lab value in isolation -- made the final call on responder status. 在本研究中，这两项指标都在开始ETI前和4至6周试验期后分别测量，并由一个统一裁定委员会（centralized adjudication committee，即综合评估全部临床数据、而非仅凭单一化验值做判断的专家小组）对是否"有反应"做出最终裁定。

JORDAN So walk me through the actual design -- who got enrolled, and how did they build these "true" versus "exceptional" variant lists in the first place? 那具体说说研究设计——都有哪些人入组，"真"I类变异与"例外"I类变异这两份名单最初是怎么建立起来的？

ALEX This runs on the French compassionate programme, which since May 2022 has offered anyone with CF who lacks the common F508del variant and hasn't had a lung transplant a 4-6 week individual trial of ETI, regardless of genotype -- a much broader eligibility net than the approved label. 这项研究依托的是法国同情用药项目，该项目自2022年5月起，向所有不携带常见F508del变异、且未接受过肺移植的CF患者提供为期4至6周的ETI个体化试用，不论基因型如何——入组条件比正式获批适应症宽松得多。 Of 652 people screened by March 2025, 209 carried two Class I variants; 163 of those actually received the drug for the trial period. 截至2025年3月，共652人接受筛查，其中209人携带两个I类变异；实际接受药物试用的有163人。 To build the reference lists, the authors first pooled every variant classified as leading to absent CFTR protein across CFTR2 and CFTR-France -- 1,033 variants total -- then flagged a subset as "exceptional" using structural logic: known or predicted splicing defects that might leave residual transcript, location in the very last exon or intron, T-to-C substitutions at a specific splice position, documented residual clinical function, or in-frame deletions and duplications that spare the modulator-binding region. 为建立参考名单，作者首先汇总了CFTR2和CFTR-France中所有被归类为导致CFTR蛋白完全缺失的变异——共计1033个——随后依据结构逻辑，将其中一部分标记为"例外"：已知或预测可能残留部分转录本的剪接缺陷、位于最末一个外显子或内含子、特定剪接位点上的T到C替换、有文献记载的残留临床功能，以及不影响调节剂结合区域的整码框内缺失或重复。 That yielded 955 "true" Class I variants and 78 "exceptional" ones.由此得出955个"真"I类变异和78个"例外"I类变异。

ALEX I'll push back on one thing here, though. 不过这里我要提出一点质疑。 The "exceptional" flagging criteria are structural and predictive -- inferred from where a variant sits and what it's expected to do to the transcript -- not functionally validated for most of these 78 variants before this study. 这78个变异中大多数的"例外"标记标准，本质上是结构性和预测性的——根据变异在基因上的位置以及对转录本的预期影响推断出来的——在本研究之前，绝大多数并未经过功能验证。 That's a reasonable starting hypothesis, but it means the "exceptional" list was itself an unvalidated prediction being tested by this very cohort, which is worth keeping in mind when we get to how confident the responder counts really are. 这是一个合理的出发假设，但也意味着"例外"名单本身就是一个未经验证的预测，恰恰是靠这批队列的数据来接受检验的——这一点在我们接下来讨论"响应者"计数到底有多可靠时值得留意。

JORDAN Before the results -- why now? What changed to make this question worth asking at scale? 在讲结果之前——为什么是现在？是什么让这个问题变得值得大规模研究？

ALEX The European Medicines Agency expanded the ETI label in April 2025 to cover anyone with at least one non-Class I variant, aged two and up. 欧洲药品管理局（European Medicines Agency，EMA）在2025年4月将ETI的适应症扩展到了所有携带至少一个非I类变异、年满两岁的患者。 That's a big expansion, but it explicitly leaves out the two-Class-I-variant group, and until this paper, there was no large-scale clinical data on what actually happens if you give ETI to that excluded group anyway. 这是一次很大的适应症扩展，但明确排除了携带两个I类变异的患者群体，而在本文之前，如果真的给这部分被排除的患者用上ETI，会发生什么，一直没有大规模临床数据能够说明。 The French compassionate programme's genotype-agnostic eligibility -- anyone without F508del, regardless of what their other variant is -- made it possible to actually observe that excluded group at scale instead of just predicting their response from protein structure. 法国同情用药项目"不看基因型、只要不携带F508del即可入组"的准入方式，使得研究者第一次有可能在真实世界中大规模观察这一被排除群体的实际反应，而不只是从蛋白质结构去预测。 Before this study, the evidence for E831X responding to ETI was cell-culture data plus a handful of case reports -- this is the first time it and several other variants get a systematic clinical test in this large a cohort. 在本研究之前，关于E831X对ETI有反应的证据仅限于细胞培养实验数据加上零星的个案报告——这是这类变异首次在如此大规模的队列中接受系统性临床检验。

JORDAN Okay, so what did they actually find? 那么，他们究竟发现了什么？

ALEX Of the 163 people with two Class I variants who received ETI, 155 -- 95 percent -- were classified as non-responders by the adjudication committee, and 8 -- 5 percent -- were classified as responders. 在163名携带两个I类变异并接受ETI的患者中，155人（95%）被裁定委员会判定为无反应者，8人（5%）被判定为有反应者。 Responders showed a mean SCC drop of 18.6 mmol/L and a mean ppFEV1 gain of 11.5 points; non-responders showed essentially no SCC change and only a small, though still statistically significant, 1.7-point ppFEV1 bump. 有反应者的平均汗氯浓度下降了18.6 mmol/L，平均ppFEV1提高了11.5个百分点；无反应者的汗氯浓度基本没有变化，ppFEV1仅有小幅但仍具统计学意义的1.7个百分点提升。 Critically, when the authors traced which specific variants showed up in the 8 responders, six were found in variants the "exceptional" logic had already flagged -- E831X, 4374+1G>A, 1716+2T>C, 4382delA, 875+1G>A, and a duplication spanning exons 1-3 -- while the other variants those same responders carried had already been shown non-responsive elsewhere in the programme, meaning the response tracked back to the exceptional partner allele. 关键的是，当作者追溯这8名有反应者携带的具体变异时，发现其中六个正是"例外"逻辑已经标记出的变异——E831X、4374+1G>A、1716+2T>C、4382delA、875+1G>A，以及一个跨越1至3号外显子的重复变异——而这些患者携带的另一个等位基因变异，在项目中的其他病例里已被证实无反应，也就是说，响应可以追溯到"例外"那一侧的等位基因。 So the paper isn't just confirming Class I means non-responsive on average -- it's using clinical outcome data to sharpen exactly which structurally-Class-I variants deserve to be pulled out of that bucket. 所以这篇论文做的不仅是从平均意义上确认"I类=无反应"，而是用临床结局数据，精确地划出哪些结构上属于I类的变异，理应被从这个大类里单独摘出来。
