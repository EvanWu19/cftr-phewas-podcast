Track C — CF genetic heterogeneity in Torbat Jam (Ghaeninejhad 2026)

Abstract-only — no open full text; all figures below are from the abstract, flagged on-air.

Blurb. A small, clean CFTR variant-spectrum study in an under-described consanguineous Iranian population (Torbat Jam, Khorasan). Seven unrelated sweat-chloride-diagnosed patients were worked up on a Sanger → MLPA → WES ladder. Solve rate 57.1% (4/7): F508del (c.1521_1523delCTT / p.Phe508del) homozygous in one and heterozygous in another; the poly-T splice variant c.1210-11T>G (5T; TG12) in two compound heterozygotes; a missense c.547C>A (p.Leu183Ile); and — caught only by MLPA — a homozygous whole-deletion of exon 2 in a patient with no sequence variant. 42.9% (3/7) stayed unresolved after WES, which the authors read as deep-intronic or extra-genic contributions and a case for WGS. Where it lands: the modifier catalog + the missing-heritability long game. Buildable artifact: a CFTR intragenic-modifier-and-CNV-aware annotation notebook — annotate poly-T/TG status, flag genotypes whose interpretation changes once the tract or a large deletion is accounted for, and mark sequence-only-unsolvable cases. WGS-free first step.

Plain-English glossary.

  • Autosomal recessive — needs two damaged gene copies (one per parent) to cause disease.（中文：常染色体隐性遗传，需父母各传一份受损拷贝才致病。）
  • cis-modifier — a variant on the same DNA molecule as the gene that tunes another variant's penetrance (e.g. the poly-T/TG tract).（中文：顺式修饰因子，与基因位于同一条 DNA 分子上、调节另一变异外显率的变异。）
  • CNV — copy-number variation; whole exons/regions deleted or duplicated (invisible to sequencing, seen by MLPA).（中文：拷贝数变异，整段外显子/区域的缺失或重复；测序看不见，MLPA 可见。）
  • Compound heterozygous — two different damaging variants, one on each gene copy (vs homozygous = same variant on both).（中文：复合杂合，两份拷贝上各带一个不同的有害变异；纯合则两份相同。）
  • Consanguinity — mating between relatives; raises homozygosity and clusters recessive disease + rare local alleles.（中文：近亲通婚，提高纯合度，使隐性疾病与罕见地方性等位基因聚集。）
  • Deep-intronic variant — a variant far inside an intron that can create a cryptic splice site; missed by exome sequencing.（中文：深内含子变异，藏于内含子深处、可造隐蔽剪接位点；外显子组测序会漏。）
  • MLPA — Multiplex Ligation-dependent Probe Amplification; dosage-based assay that detects exon deletions/duplications.（中文：多重连接依赖探针扩增，基于剂量检测外显子缺失/重复。）
  • Missing heritability — known variants don't fully account for observed disease; here, unsolved cases despite full workup.（中文：缺失遗传力，已知变异无法完全解释所见疾病；此处即检查完备后仍未解决的病例。）
  • poly-T / TG tract — a variable thymine stretch + adjacent TG repeat in a CFTR intron; short 5T (esp. with long TG) worsens splicing and modifies penetrance (classic R117H example).（中文：CFTR 内含子中可变的胸腺嘧啶段加相邻 TG 重复；短 5T（尤其配长 TG）损害剪接、改变外显率，R117H 为经典例。）
  • Sweat chloride test — CF diagnostic; >60 mmol/L is diagnostic (CF disrupts Cl- transport → salty sweat).（中文：CF 诊断检测，>60 mmol/L 即诊断；CF 破坏氯离子转运使汗液偏咸。）
  • WES / WGS — whole-exome (protein-coding only) vs whole-genome (introns + intergenic too) sequencing.（中文：全外显子组测序（仅编码区）对全基因组测序（含内含子与基因间区）。）

Full citation. Ghaeninejhad H, DinMohammadi H, Tavakoli S, Hamzehloei T. Unmasking the genetic heterogeneity of cystic fibrosis in the Torbat Jam region: From common variants to large deletions and the challenge of missing heritability. Respir Med. 2026;260:108907. PMID 42208689 · DOI 10.1016/j.rmed.2026.108907. (Abstract only; no PMC full text.)

Methods / tools to lift.

  • Sanger → MLPA → WES escalation — sequence first, then dosage/CNV, then genome-wide coding; any genotype with ≤1 sequence hit is a CNV candidate.
  • Treat the poly-T/TG tract as first-class annotation, encoding the pairing (tract length + the other allele), not tract length alone.
  • Separate intragenic-completeness (sequencing/CNV, addressable now) from extra-genic modifiers (association/summary-stats question) when accounting for unsolved cases.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Serves the modifier catalog (+ missing-heritability long game). MVP: cftr-cismodifier-cnv-annotator — input = CFTR variant call list + CFTR2 poly-T/TG annotations; output = poly-T/TG-annotated genotypes, a flag for genotypes whose interpretation changes with tract/deletion status, and a list of sequence-only-unsolvable cases (structural / deep-intronic follow-up candidates). Difficulty: S–M.

What to verify first.

  • n=7, one region — allele frequencies, 57% solve rate, and the exon-2 deletion are a local snapshot, not a population estimate.
  • poly-T/TG interpretation is pairing-dependent (5T penetrance depends on TG length + the opposite allele).
  • "Unresolved after WES ⇒ deep-intronic / extra-genic" is the authors' inference, not a demonstrated finding — hold as hypothesis (could be technical misses or off-panel genes).
  • All external published-variant reasoning; nothing about or assuming registry genotype internals.

Gap-map / catalog.

  • Closes: a concrete reminder that CFTR interpretation needs poly-T/TG cis-modifier annotation + a CNV step; a clean local example of severe-allele + large-deletion spectrum in a consanguineous population.
  • Leaves open: the identity of any extra-genic modifiers behind the 42.9% unsolved; whether those cases are truly deep-intronic/extra-genic vs technical; population-transferable allele frequencies.

JORDAN Give me the genomics vocabulary before the results. 在讲结果之前，先给我把基因组学词汇过一遍。 What's consanguinity doing here, and what's this poly-T thing? 近亲通婚在这里起什么作用，还有那个"poly-T"是什么？

ALEX Start with the disease frame. 先从疾病的框架说起。 CF is autosomal recessive, meaning you need two damaged CFTR copies — one from each parent — to have the disease. CF 是常染色体隐性遗传 (autosomal recessive)，意思是你需要两份受损的 CFTR 拷贝——父母各一份——才会患病。 Consanguinity — marriage between relatives — raises the chance both copies are identical by descent, so recessive disease clusters and you see more homozygotes and rarer local alleles. 近亲通婚 (consanguinity)——亲属间的婚配——提高了两份拷贝因同源而相同的概率，于是隐性疾病聚集，你会看到更多纯合子和更罕见的地方性等位基因。 Diagnosis here anchors on the sweat chloride test — CF disrupts chloride transport, so sweat is salty, and above 60 millimoles per liter is diagnostic. 这里的诊断以汗液氯离子检测 (sweat chloride test) 为锚——CF 破坏氯离子转运，所以汗液偏咸，高于每升 60 毫摩尔即为诊断标准。 Now the poly-T: in one CFTR intron there's a stretch of thymine bases whose length varies between people — 5T, 7T, or 9T — and it's followed by a TG repeat tract. 现在说 poly-T：在 CFTR 的一个内含子 (intron) 里，有一段胸腺嘧啶碱基，其长度因人而异——5T、7T 或 9T——后面还跟着一段 TG 重复序列。 The shorter the T tract, especially 5T paired with a long TG tract, the worse the splicing — the cell more often skips an exon and makes less working CFTR. JORDAN You mentioned the poly-T tract shows up in real variant-interpretation decisions — give me the classic case. 你提到 poly-T 段会出现在真实的变异解读决策中——给我讲那个经典例子。

R117H 是一个 CFTR 变异，其临床后果会随同一条染色体上的 poly-T 段而翻转——与 5T 处于顺式 (cis) 时它可导致经典型 CF，但与 7T 顺式时往往产生轻得多、甚至非经典的表现。 That's the whole reason labs report the poly-T status alongside R117H — the same coding change is a different clinical entity depending on its cis-modifier. 这正是实验室在报告 R117H 时会一并报告 poly-T 状态的全部原因——同一个编码改变，会因其顺式修饰因子不同而成为不同的临床实体。 So when this paper finds 5T with TG12 in compound heterozygotes, it's not a footnote; it's the kind of within-gene modifier that changes how you'd counsel and classify. 所以当这篇论文在复合杂合子中发现 5T 搭配 TG12 时，那不是脚注；它正是那种会改变你如何咨询和分类的基因内修饰因子。 That's exactly why an annotation tool has to treat the tract as first-class, not as an afterthought bolted onto the coding call. 这正是为什么注释工具必须把这个段作为一等公民对待，而不是当作贴在编码判读之后的补充。

JORDAN Let me define one term you slipped in — compound heterozygous? 让我澄清一个你刚带过的术语——复合杂合是什么？

ALEX Good catch. 问得好。 Homozygous means the same variant on both gene copies; compound heterozygous means two different damaging variants, one on each copy. 纯合 (homozygous) 指两份基因拷贝上是同一个变异；复合杂合指两个不同的有害变异，每份拷贝各一个。 Both satisfy the recessive requirement of two hits, but compound heterozygotes are genetically more varied and often harder to interpret, because now the phenotype depends on the specific pair. 两者都满足隐性遗传"需要两次打击"的要求，但复合杂合子在遗传上更多样、也往往更难解读，因为此时表型取决于那一对具体的变异。 In CF that pairing matters enormously — the poly-T 5T allele, for instance, is only weakly penetrant on its own but becomes clinically meaningful opposite a severe allele like F508del. 在 CF 里，这种配对极其重要——比如 poly-T 的 5T 等位基因单独存在时外显率很弱，但当它与像 F508del 这样的严重等位基因配对时，就变得有临床意义。

JORDAN And the headline was the unsolved patients, not the solved ones. 而标题讲的是未解决的病人，不是已解决的。

ALEX Exactly, and that's the scientific hook. 正是如此，这就是科学上的钩子。 Three of seven — 42.9% — stayed genetically unresolved even after whole-exome sequencing. 七人中的三人——42.9%——即便做了全外显子组测序也仍未获遗传学解决。 The authors read that as pointing to two things exome sequencing can't see: deep intronic variants, which sit far inside introns and can create cryptic splice sites, and extra-genic modifiers, meaning contributors outside CFTR's coding sequence entirely. 作者把这解读为指向外显子组测序看不到的两样东西：深内含子变异 (deep intronic variants)，它们藏在内含子深处、可能造出隐蔽的剪接位点；以及基因外修饰因子 (extra-genic modifiers)，即完全位于 CFTR 编码序列之外的影响因素。 That's the missing-heritability problem in miniature — the known variants don't fully account for the disease you can see in the clinic. 这就是缩微版的"缺失遗传力 (missing heritability)"问题——已知变异无法完全解释你在临床上能看到的疾病。 And their prescription is whole-genome sequencing, WGS, which reads introns and intergenic regions that exome sequencing skips. 他们开出的处方是全基因组测序 (WGS)，它读取外显子组测序略过的内含子和基因间区。

ALEX One more concept, because the abstract leans on it and the catalog cares about it — missing heritability. 再讲一个概念，因为摘要依赖它、目录也在意它——缺失的遗传力。 In a monogenic disease like CF, "missing heritability" shows up as unsolved cases: the patient clearly has the disease, but the two expected causal hits aren't both found. 在像 CF 这样的单基因病里，"缺失遗传力"表现为未解决的病例：病人明显患病，但预期的两次致病打击并未双双找到。 The candidates for the gap are ordered by how far you have to look: an undetected variant still inside CFTR — deep-intronic or a structural rearrangement — then a true extra-genic modifier that pushes a borderline genotype into disease. 填补这个缺口的候选，按你需要找多远来排序：一个仍在 CFTR 内部却未被检出的变异——深内含子或结构重排——再往外是一个真正的基因外修饰因子，把一个临界基因型推入疾病。 For the modifier catalog, the useful move is to separate those two: intragenic completeness is a sequencing-and-CNV problem you can chip at now, while extra-genic modifiers are the summary-stats and association question the catalog is being built to hold. 对修饰基因目录而言，有用的做法是把这两者分开：基因内的完整性是一个测序与 CNV 问题，你现在就能着手；而基因外修饰因子是关联与汇总统计的问题，正是这个目录被建来承载的。 The Torbat Jam paper is a clean reminder that you should exhaust the intragenic explanation — poly-T, CNV, deep-intronic — before you attribute an unsolved case to a novel modifier. Torbat Jam 这篇论文清楚地提醒我们：在把一个未解决的病例归因于新修饰因子之前，应先穷尽基因内的解释——poly-T、CNV、深内含子。
