Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): A domain-specific classifier for BRCA1 BRCT VUS

Torretto and colleagues (Current Oncology, 2026) build a three-layer pipeline — computational, functional, and structural — to resolve variants of uncertain significance (VUS) within BRCA1's BRCT domain, a region they first show is a VUS hotspot by plotting ClinVar missense-variant density across the whole gene. They assemble 50 in silico tools via Ensembl's Variant Effect Predictor, use an ensemble feature-selection application (MolecularFeaST) to rank them, and train an Ensemble Subspace k-nearest-neighbors classifier on the top nine — CADD, MetaRNN, ClinPred, VEST4, BayesDel AD, EVE, Eigen PC, gMVP, and PolyPhen2 — reaching 87.9% test-set accuracy, matching AlphaMissense and beating ESM and CPT-1 on the same variants. Twenty-two VUS spanning the score range are then run through a phosphopeptide-binding pull-down assay and PyMOL structural modeling, letting the authors reclassify 12 of 22 under formal ACMG-AMP rules. This episode maps the method onto the Karen/CFTR variant-interpretation priority: a CFTR NBD1 Domain-Specific VUS Classifier, restricting a feature-selected ensemble to one structural domain at a time rather than pooling across the whole gene, with an explicit check for whether a given domain has enough CFTR2-classified variants to support its own classifier.

Plain-English glossary

  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A DNA variant whose effect on gene function and disease risk is genuinely unresolved, due to insufficient or conflicting evidence.（中文：一种DNA变异，由于证据不足或相互矛盾，其对基因功能和疾病风险的影响目前无法确定。）
  • ACMG-AMP — The rule system jointly developed by the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology for classifying variants, combining weighted evidence codes (e.g. PS3, BS3, PM5, BP4) under fixed rules.（中文：由美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会和分子病理学会联合制定的变异分类规则体系，通过固定规则组合带权重的证据代码（如PS3、BS3、PM5、BP4）得出分类结论。）
  • RING domain / BRCT domain — BRCA1's two most functionally critical structural regions: the N-terminal RING domain (E3 ubiquitin ligase activity) and the C-terminal BRCT domain (forms phosphopeptide-binding complexes with DNA-repair partner proteins); both are pathogenic-variant and VUS hotspots.（中文：BRCA1两个功能最关键的结构区域：N端的RING结构域（负责E3泛素连接酶活性）和C端的BRCT结构域（与DNA修复伙伴蛋白形成磷酸肽结合复合物）；两者都是致病变异和VUS的高发区。）
  • Phosphopeptide-binding motif — The specific phosphorylated peptide pattern (a phosphoserine, then any two residues, then a phenylalanine anchor) that the BRCT domain's binding cleft recognizes.（中文：BRCT结构域结合凹槛所识别的特定磷酸化肽段模式——一个磷酸化丝氨酸，接两个任意残基，再接一个苯丙氨酸锚定位点。）
  • CFTR domain architecture (NBD1, NBD2, R domain) — CFTR's own modular structure, with pathogenic variants and VUS almost certainly unevenly distributed across domains, NBD1 being by far the most densely characterized.（中文：CFTR自身的模块化结构，致病变异和VUS在各结构域之间的分布几乎肯定不均衡，其中NBD1是迄今研究最透彻的。）

Full citation

Torretto GC, et al. "Domain-Specific Computational, Functional and Structural Methods Enable Interpretation of BRCA1 BRCT Variants of Uncertain Significance." Current Oncology (Toronto, Ont.). 2026. PMID: 42346254 | PMCID: PMC13298341 | DOI: 10.3390/curroncol33060354

Methods / tools to lift

  • MolecularFeaST — an ensemble feature-selection application ranking in silico tools by combining results from 14 different feature-selection algorithms, used to pick which predictors actually discriminate within one structural domain.
  • Domain-restricted ensemble classifier (Ensemble Subspace kNN) — incrementally adding feature-selected predictors to train 32 candidate model types, stopping once additional tools start hurting accuracy, then selecting a kNN ensemble trained on random tool subspaces.
  • Phosphopeptide-binding pull-down assay (FLAG-tagged co-immunoprecipitation) — orthogonal functional evidence independent of any in silico score, measuring whether a variant disrupts a BRCT-domain protein's actual molecular interaction with its binding partners.
  • Single-reference-variant threshold calibration — using one already-classified benign variant's own assay result to set the significance bar (p<0.01 vs p<0.05) separating "uncertain functional effect" from "sufficient evidence of loss of function."

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation (method only). Buildable artifact — CFTR NBD1 Domain-Specific VUS Classifier: restrict a feature-selected ensemble classifier to CFTR2-classified pathogenic/benign variants within one structural domain at a time (starting with NBD1), score that domain's VUS with it, and explicitly flag domains (R domain, NBD2) too sparse in classified variants to support a domain-specific model. Difficulty: M (medium) — classifier training is routine; the real work is domain-boundary variant curation and wiring a functional-assay-review step for mid-confidence VUS.

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR2 has enough classified variants within each domain — not just NBD1 — to make a domain-restricted classifier statistically meaningful rather than a smaller, noisier version of a whole-gene one.
  • Whether a single benign or pathogenic reference variant per CFTR functional assay is a defensible way to calibrate a loss-of-function threshold, the way T1720A did here for BRCT, or whether CFTR's own assays already use a broader calibration panel.
  • Whether this paper's small, imbalanced test set (23 pathogenic / 10 benign held out, from a training set of only 92/43) signals a real overfitting risk that would bite just as hard for a CFTR domain-specific classifier outside NBD1.

VUS是指一种DNA变异，其对基因功能和疾病风险的影响目前确实无法确定——证据不足，或者证据相互矛盾，无法将其归类为致病或良性。 ACMG-AMP is the rule system the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology jointly developed for classifying variants: you accumulate weighted evidence codes — population frequency, computational predictions, functional assay data, segregation in families, de novo occurrence — each tagged with a strength (supporting, moderate, strong, very strong) and combine them under fixed rules to land on pathogenic, likely pathogenic, VUS, likely benign, or benign.
