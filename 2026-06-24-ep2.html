How did this episode land for you?

Show notes — GatorTron / Yang 2022 (Track B, AI tooling)

Blurb. Yang et al. (npj Digital Medicine, 2022) build GatorTron, the first large language model trained from scratch on real clinical narratives at scale — >90 billion words total, of which >82 billion are de-identified notes from University of Florida Health (290M notes, ~2M patients, 2011–2021), topped up with PubMed, Wikipedia, and MIMIC-III. They train three BERT-style sizes (345M, 3.9B, 8.9B parameters) using Megatron-LM on 992 A100 GPUs (~6 days for the largest), with masked-language-modeling + sentence-order-prediction objectives. Evaluated on five clinical NLP tasks against BioBERT, BioMegatron, and ClinicalBERT: GatorTron-large wins all five, with monotonic gains by parameter count on four (NLI accuracy 90.20%, +9.6%/+7.5% over BioBERT/ClinicalBERT; concept-extraction F1 up to 0.90; relation-extraction F1 0.9627; MQA exact-match +6.8–9.5%). The exception is semantic textual similarity, where the medium model essentially ties the large one — an honest "scale stops paying" signal. Weights are public on NVIDIA NGC. Where it lands: AI-tooling / career. Buildable artifact: a clinical-narrative information-extraction service built on the runnable 345M model, complementing the structured-code embedding modules already in the backlog.

Plain-English glossary

  • Clinical concept extraction / clinical NER — finding and labeling the spans in a clinical note that are problems, treatments, drugs, labs, or adverse events.（中文：临床概念抽取 / 临床命名实体识别，在病历中找出并标注属于疾病问题、治疗、药物、化验或不良事件的文本片段。）
  • Computable phenotype — a reproducible rule or model that identifies a patient cohort from records (e.g., "all patients meeting criteria X").（中文：可计算表型，一种从病历中识别患者队列的可复现规则或模型。）
  • De-identification / PHI / HIPAA safe harbor — removing the 18 categories of protected health information (names, dates, IDs...) before clinical text can be reused; "safe harbor" is the HIPAA method for doing so.（中文：去标识化 / PHI / HIPAA 安全港，在临床文本可被复用前去除 18 类受保护健康信息（姓名、日期、编号等）；"安全港"是 HIPAA 规定的去除方法。）
  • i2b2 / n2c2 / MedSTS / MedNLI / emrQA — the public benchmark datasets for, respectively, concept+relation extraction, semantic similarity, natural language inference, and medical question answering.（中文：i2b2 / n2c2 / MedSTS / MedNLI / emrQA，分别是概念与关系抽取、语义相似度、自然语言推理、医学问答的公开基准数据集。）
  • Medical relation extraction — deciding whether (and how) two extracted concepts are related, e.g., drug-causes-adverse-event.（中文：医学关系抽取，判断两个已抽取的概念之间是否（及如何）相关，例如药物引起不良事件。）
  • MIMIC-III — the widely used public corpus of de-identified ICU notes; the training base for most prior clinical models.（中文：MIMIC-III，被广泛使用的去标识化 ICU 病历公开语料库，是大多数先前临床模型的训练基础。）
  • Natural language inference (NLI) — deciding whether a hypothesis can be inferred from a premise sentence (entailment / contradiction / neutral).（中文：自然语言推理 (NLI)，判断一个假设是否能从前提句推出（蕴含 / 矛盾 / 中性）。）
  • Sentence-order prediction (SOP) — a self-supervised pretraining task: predict whether two consecutive text segments are in the correct order.（中文：句序预测 (SOP)，一种自监督预训练任务：预测两个连续文本段是否顺序正确。）

Full citation. Yang X, Chen A, PourNejatian N, et al. A large language model for electronic health records. npj Digit Med. 2022;5:194. PMID 36572766 · PMC PMC9792464 · DOI 10.1038/s41746-022-00742-2. Weights: NVIDIA NGC (clara/gatortron_og). According to Europe PMC; full text via BioC PMC-OA route.

Methods / tools to lift

  • Domain-specific pretraining from a very large de-identified clinical-note corpus → strong label-efficiency on downstream extraction tasks (fewer hand-labeled notes to reach usable F1).
  • Megatron-LM model/data parallelism for training/serving multi-billion-parameter encoders; the 345M variant is single-GPU-runnable for prototyping.
  • Standard fine-tuning recipe: BIO sequence-labeling head for NER, entity-marker + [CLS] concatenation for relation extraction, span start/end heads for QA.
  • Error-analysis-against-baseline as a qualitative complement to F1 (long nested phrases, adjacent-category disambiguation).
  • Per-task benchmarking before committing scale: the STS tie shows raw parameter count is not universally worth the GPU spend.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. AI-tooling / career (method also serves Liz-style phenotype work, methodologically only). MVP (paste-ready): pull GatorTron-base (345M) weights from NGC → fine-tune for one clinical concept-extraction task on a small in-house labeled set → expose a REST endpoint that turns a free-text note into structured {concept, semantic-type, span} tuples + drug–adverse-event relations, with per-extraction confidence. Input: raw clinical narrative. Output: structured features. Difficulty M. Complements the registry-foundation-embedding-module and skip-gram baseline already in the backlog — this is the narrative→structured front end; those are the representation back end (a two-stage feature factory).

What to verify first

  • De-identification governance: any deployment on institutional notes needs its own validated de-id pipeline first (HIPAA safe-harbor); this is a prerequisite, not an afterthought.
  • Domain shift: published i2b2/n2c2 F1 reflects UF Health note style; evaluate on a held-out set of your own annotated notes, expect lower F1 pre-fine-tuning.
  • Scaling is task-dependent: the STS result shows bigger isn't always better — benchmark per task before spending GPU budget; the 345M model may suffice.
  • Confidence calibration: if any downstream cohort selection leans on the extractor, calibrate its output probabilities.
  • Encoder, not generative: GatorTron needs a fine-tuned task head per task; it is not a zero-shot chat model.
  • "What we'd need to check," not a decided plan; nothing here describes or assumes CFFPR internal text or schema.

ALEX Welcome back. 欢迎回来。Today's Track B paper is "A large language model for electronic health records," by Xi Yang and colleagues at the University of Florida, published in npj Digital Medicine in 2022 — the model is called GatorTron. 今天 Track B 的论文是佛罗里达大学 Xi Yang 等人 2022 年发表在《npj 数字医学》(npj Digital Medicine) 上的"一个面向电子健康记录的大语言模型"——这个模型叫 GatorTron。It's fully open access, we have the complete full text, and — important for this track — it ships: the trained weights are public on NVIDIA's NGC catalog. 它是完全开放获取的，我们拿到了完整全文，而且——这对本条线很重要——它是交付了东西的：训练好的权重在 NVIDIA 的 NGC 目录上公开。We're reading it because it's the clinical-text counterpart to the structured-EHR models we've covered, and it's a clean case study in how far domain-specific scaling actually moves the needle. 我们读它，是因为它是我们讲过的结构化 EHR 模型在"临床文本"一侧的对应物，也是一个干净的案例研究：领域专属的规模化到底能把指针推动多远。

JORDAN Let me prime the domain vocabulary first, because the data-science machinery here you already know — it's BERT-style transformers — but the clinical-NLP tasks are their own world. 让我先把领域词汇铺垫一下，因为这里的数据科学机制你已经懂了——就是 BERT 式的 Transformer——但临床自然语言处理 (clinical NLP) 的那些任务是它们自己的一个世界。What are the actual tasks they test on? 他们到底在哪些任务上测试？

ALEX Five of them, and they map onto real registry-and-record chores. 五个任务，而且它们对应着真实的"病历与登记"杂活。First, clinical concept extraction, also called clinical named-entity recognition: find the spans in a note that are problems, treatments, lab tests, drugs, adverse events. 第一，临床概念抽取 (clinical concept extraction)，也叫临床命名实体识别 (clinical NER)：在病历里找出哪些片段是疾病问题、治疗、化验、药物、不良事件。Second, medical relation extraction: given two concepts, decide whether there's a relation — for example, this drug caused that adverse event. 第二，医学关系抽取 (medical relation extraction)：给定两个概念，判断它们之间是否存在关系——比如这个药物引起了那个不良事件。Third, semantic textual similarity: score how close in meaning two clinical sentences are, which matters for de-duplicating copy-pasted note bloat. 第三，语义文本相似度 (semantic textual similarity)：给两句临床句子的语义接近程度打分，这对去重那些反复复制粘贴的病历冗余很重要。Fourth, natural language inference: decide whether a conclusion follows from a sentence. 第四，自然语言推理 (natural language inference, NLI)：判断某个结论是否能从一句话中推出。Fifth, medical question answering: pull the answer span out of a document for a clinical question. 第五，医学问答 (medical question answering, MQA)：针对一个临床问题，从文档中抽出答案片段。

JORDAN And there are named benchmarks behind each of those? 这每一个背后都有具名的基准数据集？

ALEX Yes, and they're worth knowing because they're the public yardsticks. 是的，值得知道，因为它们是公开的标尺。Concept extraction uses the 2010 and 2012 i2b2 and 2018 n2c2 datasets; similarity uses MedSTS; inference uses MedNLI; question answering uses emrQA. 概念抽取用 2010 和 2012 年的 i2b2 以及 2018 年的 n2c2 数据集；相似度用 MedSTS；推理用 MedNLI；问答用 emrQA。Two more domain terms: MIMIC-III is the public ICU-notes corpus that almost every prior clinical model trained on, and "de-identification" means stripping the 18 HIPAA protected-health-information categories — names, dates, record numbers — before text can be used. 还有两个领域词：MIMIC-III 是几乎每个先前临床模型都拿来训练的公开 ICU 病历语料库，而"去标识化"(de-identification) 指的是在文本可被使用前，去除 HIPAA 规定的 18 类受保护健康信息 (PHI)——姓名、日期、病历号等。And "computable phenotype" — a term you'll hear in Beat 6 — is a reproducible rule or model that identifies a patient cohort from records. 还有"可计算表型"(computable phenotype)——你在第六拍会听到——是一种从病历中识别患者队列的可复现规则或模型。

JORDAN Okay, so what was the actual gap before this? 那么，在这之前真正的空白是什么？

ALEX The gap was scale, specifically in the clinical-text domain. 空白在于规模，特别是在临床文本这个领域里。In the general domain, language models had blown past a hundred billion parameters — GPT-3 was 175 billion. 在通用领域，语言模型早已突破千亿参数——GPT-3 有 1750 亿。In the biomedical literature domain, BioBERT and PubMedBERT existed at 110 million parameters, and NVIDIA's BioMegatron pushed to 1.2 billion using PubMed text. 在生物医学文献领域，BioBERT 和 PubMedBERT 是 1.1 亿参数级别，NVIDIA 的 BioMegatron 用 PubMed 文本推到了 12 亿。But clinical narratives — the actual notes clinicians type — are different from journal articles, and they're locked behind privacy walls. 但临床叙述——临床医生真正敲进去的病历——和期刊文章不一样，而且它们被锁在隐私墙后面。The largest model trained on real clinical text was ClinicalBERT, still only 110 million parameters, trained on half a billion words from MIMIC-III. 在真实临床文本上训练的最大模型是 ClinicalBERT，仍只有 1.1 亿参数，用 MIMIC-III 的 5 亿词训练。

JORDAN So why hadn't anyone gone bigger on clinical text? 那为什么没人在临床文本上做得更大？

ALEX Two reasons, and both are instructive for anyone trying to do this inside an institution. 两个原因，对任何想在机构内部做这件事的人都有启发。One, clinical text is full of protected health information, so you can't just scrape it; you need a health system willing to expose its warehouse plus a de-identification pipeline you trust. 第一，临床文本满是受保护健康信息，所以你不能随便爬取；你需要一个愿意开放其数据仓库的医疗系统，外加一条你信得过的去标识化管线。Two, the compute is brutal. 第二，算力是残酷的。This is where UF had an unusual advantage — they had an in-house NVIDIA supercomputer. 这正是佛罗里达大学有个不寻常优势的地方——他们有一台自有的 NVIDIA 超级计算机。So the paper is partly a demonstration of what becomes possible when a single academic medical center owns both the data and the GPUs. 所以这篇论文在某种程度上是一个演示：当一个学术医疗中心同时拥有数据和 GPU 时，什么会变得可能。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built and trained. 给我讲讲他们到底建了什么、训了什么。

ALEX The corpus is the headline asset: over 90 billion words total, of which more than 82 billion are de-identified clinical notes from UF Health. 语料库是头号资产：总共超过 900 亿词，其中超过 820 亿是来自 UF Health 的去标识化临床病历。That's 290 million notes from about 2 million patients over 2011 to 2021, across 126 departments — then topped up with PubMed, Wikipedia, and MIMIC-III. 那是 2011 到 2021 年间、约 200 万患者的 2.9 亿份病历，覆盖 126 个科室——再用 PubMed、Wikipedia 和 MIMIC-III 补足。The architecture is BERT-style, trained from scratch — not fine-tuned from an existing model — using the Megatron-LM framework. 架构是 BERT 式的，从零训练——不是在现有模型上微调——用的是 Megatron-LM 框架。They built three sizes: 345 million, 3.9 billion, and 8.9 billion parameters. 他们做了三个尺寸：3.45 亿、39 亿、和 89 亿参数。Pretraining used masked language modeling plus sentence-order prediction — you know both objectives, so I'll just flag that they kept SOP rather than dropping it the way some BERT successors did. 预训练用了掩码语言建模 (masked language modeling) 加句序预测 (sentence-order prediction)——这两个目标你都懂，所以我只标一句：他们保留了 SOP，而没像某些 BERT 后继者那样丢掉它。

JORDAN And the compute bill for the big one? 那最大那个的算力账单呢？

ALEX The 8.9-billion model took about six days on 992 A100 GPUs across 124 DGX nodes. 89 亿那个模型，在 124 个 DGX 节点上的 992 块 A100 GPU 上跑了大约六天。And here's the design choice I'd push on for our purposes. 这里有个为我们的目的我想深究的设计选择。They trained the largest model from scratch rather than continuing from a general-domain checkpoint — which is defensible at 90 billion words, but it's also the most expensive possible path. 他们让最大的模型从零训练，而不是从一个通用领域的检查点继续训练——在 900 亿词的体量下这是说得过去的，但它也是最昂贵的那条路。For almost anyone who isn't UF, continued pretraining from an open clinical or general model would get most of the benefit at a tiny fraction of the cost, and the paper doesn't give us that ablation. 对几乎所有不是 UF 的人来说，从一个开放的临床或通用模型继续预训练，能以极小的成本拿到大部分收益，而论文没有给我们这个消融对比。

JORDAN So did the scaling actually pay off? 那么规模化真的有回报吗？

ALEX Mostly yes, and they report it cleanly. 大体上是的，而且他们报告得很干净。On natural language inference, GatorTron-large hit 90.20% accuracy, beating BioBERT and ClinicalBERT by 9.6% and 7.5%. 在自然语言推理上，GatorTron-large 达到 90.20% 准确率，比 BioBERT 和 ClinicalBERT 高出 9.6% 和 7.5%。On concept extraction it reached F1 scores up to 0.90 on the benchmark sets; on relation extraction, 0.9627 for drug-cause-adverse-event relations. 在概念抽取上，它在各基准集上的 F1 分数高达 0.90；在关系抽取上，药物-引起-不良事件关系的 F1 是 0.9627。On medical question answering, it improved exact-match scores by 6.8 to 9.5% over the smaller models. 在医学问答上，它把精确匹配分数比那些更小的模型提高了 6.8% 到 9.5%。And critically, performance rose monotonically with parameter count on four of the five tasks — they explicitly say they hadn't hit the ceiling. 而关键在于，在五个任务中的四个上，性能随参数量单调上升——他们明确说他们还没碰到天花板。

JORDAN Did they look at WHY the bigger model wins, not just that it does? 他们有没有去看为什么更大的模型赢，而不只是它赢了？

ALEX They did an error analysis against ClinicalBERT, and it's the most concrete part of the paper. 他们做了一个对比 ClinicalBERT 的错误分析，这是论文里最具体的部分。Two examples stuck with me. 有两个例子让我印象深刻。On a radiology phrase, GatorTron correctly tagged the whole span "a mildly dilated ascending aorta" as a problem, where ClinicalBERT grabbed only "mildly dilated" and dropped the anatomy. 在一个放射学短语上，GatorTron 正确地把整个片段"a mildly dilated ascending aorta"（轻度扩张的升主动脉）标为一个问题，而 ClinicalBERT 只抓住了"mildly dilated"，丢掉了解剖部位。And it correctly categorized "kidney protective effects" as a treatment, which ClinicalBERT misread as a problem. 它还正确地把"kidney protective effects"（肾脏保护作用）归类为一种治疗，而 ClinicalBERT 误读成了一个问题。So the gain isn't abstract — the larger domain-specific model is better at long, nested clinical phrases and at telling apart semantically adjacent categories. 所以这个收益不是抽象的——更大的领域专属模型更擅长处理长而嵌套的临床短语，也更擅长区分语义上相邻的类别。

JORDAN That long-phrase point matters for messy real notes. 这个长短语的要点对凌乱的真实病历很重要。

ALEX It does, and the authors are candid that even GatorTron still struggles on the document-level tasks — pulling the right span out of a long paragraph for question answering is where the headroom remains. 确实如此，而作者也坦白，即便是 GatorTron 在文档级任务上仍然吃力——为问答从一大段里抽出正确的片段，正是仍有提升空间的地方。Clinical notes are not like journal prose: heavy abbreviation, copy-forward boilerplate, negation, and telegraphic shorthand. 临床病历不像期刊文章：大量缩写、复制延续的套话、否定语气，以及电报式的速记。That's exactly why a model trained on 82 billion words of actual notes beats one trained on journal articles or ICU notes alone. 这正是为什么一个在 820 亿词真实病历上训练的模型，会胜过仅在期刊文章或 ICU 病历上训练的模型。

JORDAN You said four of five — what broke the pattern? 你说五个里的四个——是什么打破了这个规律？

ALEX Semantic textual similarity. 语义文本相似度。There, the medium 3.9-billion model actually edged out the large one — Pearson 0.8903 versus 0.8896 — essentially a tie, but not the clean monotonic climb. 在那个任务上，39 亿的中型模型其实略微胜过大型的——皮尔逊相关 0.8903 对 0.8896——本质上是平手，但不是那种干净的单调攀升。That's an honest and useful negative: it tells you that for sentence-level regression on a small benchmark like MedSTS, raw scale stops buying you accuracy, and you're better off spending elsewhere. 这是个诚实又有用的负面结果：它告诉你，对于像 MedSTS 这样小基准上的句级回归，纯粹的规模不再为你买到准确度，你不如把资源花在别处。They also showed data scale matters: the full-corpus base model beat a base model trained on a quarter of the corpus on four of five tasks. 他们还表明数据规模也重要：用完整语料训练的 base 模型，在五个任务中的四个上胜过只用四分之一语料训练的 base 模型。

JORDAN So where does this land for us, and what's the buildable thing? 那么这对我们落在哪里，可造的东西又是什么？

ALEX This lands on the AI-tooling and career lane, and the buildable artifact is a clinical-narrative information-extraction service. 这落在 AI 工具与职业那条线上，可造的成品是一个临床叙述信息抽取服务。The MVP: take GatorTron-base — the 345-million model, which actually fits on a single workstation GPU, unlike the 8.9-billion monster — pull the public weights off NGC, and fine-tune it for one concept-extraction task on a small in-house labeled set, exposing a thin REST endpoint that turns a free-text note into structured {concept, semantic type, span} tuples plus drug-adverse-event relations. MVP：取 GatorTron-base——那个 3.45 亿的模型，它真能装进单块工作站 GPU，不像 89 亿那个巨兽——从 NGC 拉取公开权重，在一个小型自有标注集上为某一个概念抽取任务微调它，并暴露一个轻量 REST 端点，把一份自由文本病历转换成结构化的 {概念, 语义类型, 片段} 元组外加药物-不良事件关系。Input is raw clinical narrative; output is structured features. 输入是原始临床叙述；输出是结构化特征。Difficulty M. 难度为中 (M)。

JORDAN Why fine-tune at all — couldn't you just use the big model zero-shot? 为什么还要微调——你不能直接零样本用那个大模型吗？

ALEX For this architecture, no — GatorTron is an encoder, a BERT descendant, not a generative chat model. 对这个架构来说，不行——GatorTron 是一个编码器 (encoder)，是 BERT 的后代，不是一个生成式聊天模型。It produces representations; you bolt a small task head on top and fine-tune on labeled examples for each task. 它产生的是表示；你在上面装一个小的任务头，再对每个任务用标注样例去微调。That's actually a feature for our setting, because the fine-tuning is where the label-efficiency shows up. 这在我们的场景里其实是个优点，因为微调正是标签效率显现的地方。The whole pitch of a domain-pretrained model is that you reach a usable F1 with far fewer hand-labeled notes than training from scratch would need. 一个领域预训练模型的全部卖点就是：你能用远少于从零训练所需的人工标注病历，达到一个可用的 F1。For an institution that can only afford to annotate a few hundred notes, that gap is the whole game. 对一个只负担得起标注几百份病历的机构来说，那个差距就是全部胜负所在。
