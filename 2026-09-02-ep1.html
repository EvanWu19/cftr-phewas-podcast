Ep1 — Track A: a protein language model for ion-channel GoF/LoF direction

Gies S, Alishbayli A, Tiesinga PHE, Martens MB. (2026). Functional effect predictions for ion channel missense variants using a protein language model. Journal of Human Genetics.

Abstract-only (subscription required; no PMCID, not in Europe PMC). MissION is a protein-language-model classifier trained on 1996 experimentally labeled gain-of-function (GoF) or loss-of-function (LoF) ion-channel variants — the largest such curated set to date — built to predict functional direction, not just pathogenicity, for channelopathy variants that will likely never get a patch-clamp measurement. It reaches ROC-AUC 0.918 versus 0.884 and 0.779 for two unnamed "leading" comparator models, generalizes well to channel genes with little or no electrophysiological training data, and is deployed via an online interface scoring over 600,000 ion-channel variants. CFTR is structurally an ion channel (an ABC-transporter-family anion channel, atypical next to the voltage-/ligand-gated channels that likely dominate the training set), making this a genetics-first idea for the Karen Raraigh collaboration — pending confirmation that CFTR is actually within the tool's gene coverage.

Plain-English glossary

  • Channelopathy — a disease caused by a variant that disrupts ion channel function.（中文：由破坏离子通道功能的变异所引起的疾病。）
  • Gain-of-function (GoF) / loss-of-function (LoF) — the two directions a channel-disrupting variant can push function: too much/too-easy conduction (GoF) versus too little or none (LoF); the same gene can have both, sometimes needing opposite treatments.（中文：通道破坏性变异可能推动功能变化的两个方向：传导过多或过于容易开放（功能获得），或传导过少甚至完全没有（功能丧失）；同一个基因可能同时存在两种变异，有时需要相反的治疗方案。）
  • Protein language model (PLM) — a model trained on large protein sequence databases to learn generalizable amino-acid substitution patterns, without needing a dedicated multiple-sequence alignment for one specific gene.（中文：在大规模蛋白质序列数据库上训练、学习具有泛化性的氨基酸替换模式的模型，不需要针对某一特定基因专门构建多序列比对。）
  • Alignment-free — a scoring approach that does not depend on building a multiple-sequence alignment across species for the gene being scored, useful for genes with sparse evolutionary/orthology data.（中文：一种不依赖为待评分基因跨物种构建多序列比对的打分方法，对进化/同源数据稀疏的基因尤其有用。）
  • Heterologous expression system / patch-clamp recording — the gold-standard wet-lab method for measuring a channel variant's function: expressing the mutant channel in a non-native cell system (e.g. HEK293, Xenopus oocyte) and directly recording the resulting current.（中文：测量通道变异功能的金标准湿实验室方法：在非天然细胞系统（如 HEK293 细胞、非洲爪蟾卵母细胞）中表达突变通道，并直接记录产生的电流。）
  • CFTR channel architecture (TMD/NBD/R domain) — CFTR is an ABC-transporter-family anion channel with two transmembrane domains (TMD) forming the pore, two nucleotide-binding domains (NBD) gating it via ATP binding/hydrolysis, and a regulatory (R) domain controlled by PKA phosphorylation — structurally distinct from the voltage-/ligand-gated channels common in channelopathy research.（中文：CFTR 是 ABC 转运蛋白家族的一种阴离子通道，由两个形成孔道的跨膜结构域（TMD）、两个通过结合/水解 ATP 控制通道开闭的核苷酸结合结构域（NBD），以及一个受 PKA 磷酸化调控的调节结构域（R 结构域）组成——在结构上与通道病研究中常见的电压门控/配体门控通道明显不同。）

ALEX Today's Track A pick is a 2026 paper in the Journal of Human Genetics — Gies, Alishbayli, Tiesinga, and Martens, "Functional effect predictions for ion channel missense variants using a protein language model." 今天 Track A 选的是一篇 2026 年发表在《人类遗传学杂志》（Journal of Human Genetics）上的论文——作者是 Gies、Alishbayli、Tiesinga 和 Martens，题为《利用蛋白质语言模型预测离子通道错义变异的功能效应》。 We're reading it because CFTR is, structurally, an ion channel, and this paper tackles a prediction problem that generic variant-effect predictors handle badly: not just "pathogenic or not," but which direction a variant pushes channel function. 我们选读这篇论文，是因为 CFTR 本质上也是一种离子通道，而这篇论文要解决的正是通用变异效应预测工具处理得很差的一个问题：不只是判断"致病还是不致病"，而是判断一个变异把通道功能推向了哪个方向。 It's only available to us as an abstract — the full text sits behind a subscription and Europe PMC has no open-access copy — so we'll flag that up front and be precise about what the abstract actually supports. 我们目前只能拿到摘要——全文在订阅付费墙后面，Europe PMC 上也没有开放获取版本——所以我们先把这一点说清楚，并且在讨论时严格限定在摘要实际支持的范围内。

JORDAN Let's set up the vocabulary first, since "channelopathy" and "gain-of-function versus loss-of-function" aren't things we've defined on this show yet. 我们先把词汇讲清楚，因为"通道病"（channelopathy）以及"功能获得性与功能丧失性"（gain-of-function versus loss-of-function）这两个概念我们节目之前还没讲过。 What actually is a channelopathy? 通道病到底是什么？

ALEX An ion channel is a membrane protein that opens a pore to let specific ions — sodium, potassium, calcium, chloride — flow across the cell membrane, and a channelopathy is any disease caused by a variant that disrupts that channel's function. 离子通道是一种膜蛋白，它打开一个孔道，让特定的离子——钠、钾、钙、氯——跨细胞膜流动，而通道病就是任何由破坏通道功能的变异所引起的疾病。 The key clinical wrinkle is that disruption isn't one-directional: a variant can cause loss-of-function, where the channel conducts too little current or none at all, or gain-of-function, where the channel conducts too much, opens too easily, or fails to close properly. 关键的临床复杂之处在于，功能破坏并不是单一方向的：一个变异可能导致功能丧失（loss-of-function），即通道传导的电流过少甚至完全没有；也可能导致功能获得（gain-of-function），即通道传导过多、过于容易开放，或者无法正常关闭。 In cardiac sodium and potassium channel genes, for example, loss-of-function and gain-of-function variants in the very same gene can cause opposite arrhythmia syndromes, and the two directions sometimes call for opposite treatments — a sodium-channel blocker helps one and can worsen the other. 举个例子，在心脏钠通道和钾通道基因中，同一个基因里的功能丧失性变异和功能获得性变异可能引起完全相反的心律失常综合征，而这两种方向有时需要相反的治疗方案——一种钠通道阻滞剂可能对一种情况有帮助，却会让另一种情况恶化。 CFTR itself is a chloride and bicarbonate channel, but it's an unusual one: it's built from the ABC-transporter protein family, with two transmembrane domains forming the pore and two nucleotide-binding domains that gate the channel by binding and hydrolyzing ATP, plus a regulatory domain controlled by PKA-mediated phosphorylation. CFTR 本身是一种氯离子和碳酸氢根离子通道，但它是一种不寻常的通道：它由 ABC 转运蛋白家族构建而成，由两个跨膜结构域（transmembrane domain）组成孔道，两个核苷酸结合结构域（nucleotide-binding domain）通过结合和水解 ATP 来控制通道的开闭，此外还有一个受 PKA 介导的磷酸化调控的调节结构域（regulatory domain）。 That's structurally distinct from the voltage-gated and ligand-gated channels — like the cardiac sodium channel Nav1.5 or the potassium channels behind long QT syndrome — that dominate most channelopathy research and training data. 这在结构上与大多数通道病研究和训练数据中占主导地位的电压门控（voltage-gated）和配体门控（ligand-gated）通道有明显区别，比如心脏钠通道 Nav1.5，或者与长 QT 综合征相关的钾通道。

JORDAN And the "protein language model" part — what is that, one clause? 那"蛋白质语言模型"（protein language model）又是什么，一句话说清楚？

ALEX It's a model trained on large databases of protein sequences to learn which amino acids tend to appear where, purely from sequence patterns across many proteins, so it can score how unusual or destabilizing a substitution looks without needing a multiple-sequence alignment built specifically for that one protein. 这是一种在大规模蛋白质序列数据库上训练出来的模型，纯粹从许多蛋白质的序列模式中学习哪些氨基酸倾向于出现在什么位置，因此它可以在不需要专门为某个蛋白质构建多序列比对（multiple-sequence alignment）的情况下，判断一个氨基酸替换看起来有多不寻常或多具破坏性。 That alignment-free property matters most for genes with sparse evolutionary conservation data, which is exactly the situation for a lot of less-studied ion channel genes. 这种不依赖比对的特性，对那些进化保守性数据稀疏的基因尤其重要，而这正是许多研究较少的离子通道基因所面临的情况。

JORDAN Why does "alignment-free" matter so much here — isn't a multiple-sequence alignment across species usually the whole basis for conservation-based variant scoring? 为什么"不依赖比对"（alignment-free）在这里这么重要——跨物种的多序列比对不一直都是基于保守性的变异打分方法的核心基础吗？

ALEX It usually is, and that's exactly the weak point for a lot of ion channel genes: classic conservation scoring needs enough related sequences across enough species to build a statistically meaningful alignment column at every position, and that works beautifully for something like a widely conserved metabolic enzyme but breaks down for channel genes with fewer well-annotated orthologs, unusual domain architectures, or long, poorly conserved loop regions between transmembrane segments. 通常确实如此，而这正是许多离子通道基因的薄弱环节：经典的保守性打分方法需要足够多跨物种的同源序列，才能在每个位置上构建出具有统计意义的比对列，这对广泛保守的代谢酶这类蛋白质效果很好，但对那些同源基因注释不足、结构域排布特殊，或跨膜片段之间存在较长、保守性差的环区的通道基因来说就会失效。 A protein language model pretrained across the whole universe of known protein sequences learns more generalizable patterns about what makes an amino acid substitution look structurally or biophysically unusual, so it doesn't need a dedicated, high-quality alignment for the one specific gene you're scoring. 一个在已知蛋白质序列的整个宇宙中预训练出的蛋白质语言模型，学到的是更具泛化性的模式，用来判断一个氨基酸替换在结构或生物物理上看起来有多不寻常，因此它不需要针对你正在打分的这一个特定基因专门构建高质量的比对。 That's the specific property the authors are leaning on to claim good performance even on channel genes with sparse electrophysiological and evolutionary data. 这正是作者所依赖的特定属性，用以证明即便在电生理数据和进化数据都稀疏的通道基因上，模型也能取得良好表现。

JORDAN So what was actually missing before this paper — why build a new tool instead of using something like AlphaMissense or REVEL that already exists? 那么在这篇论文之前到底缺了什么——为什么要造一个新工具，而不是直接用像 AlphaMissense 或 REVEL 这样已有的工具？

ALEX Generic pathogenicity predictors are trained to answer one question — is this variant disease-causing or not — and for most clinical purposes that's the right question. 通用的致病性预测工具是为了回答一个问题而训练的——这个变异是否致病——对大多数临床用途来说，这确实是需要回答的问题。 But for channelopathies specifically, "disease-causing" isn't enough information to act on, because the functional direction changes both the mechanism and, in some genes, the treatment. 但对于通道病来说，"致病"这一个信息还不够用，因为功能方向不仅决定了致病机制，在某些基因里还直接决定了治疗方案。 The gold-standard way to get that direction has always been electrophysiology — expressing the mutant channel in a heterologous system like a HEK293 cell line or a Xenopus oocyte and measuring the resulting current directly with patch-clamp recording. 获得这个功能方向信息的金标准方法，一直都是电生理学——在异源表达系统中表达突变通道，比如 HEK293 细胞系或非洲爪蟾卵母细胞，然后通过膜片钳记录（patch-clamp recording）直接测量产生的电流。 That's slow, requires specialized wet-lab infrastructure most labs don't have, and simply cannot keep pace with the number of ion channel missense variants being discovered by exome and genome sequencing. 这种方法速度慢，需要大多数实验室都不具备的专门湿实验室基础设施，根本无法跟上外显子组和基因组测序不断发现新离子通道错义变异的速度。 So the gap the authors are targeting is specifically the GoF/LoF direction call for variants that will likely never get a patch-clamp measurement, not the initial pathogenic/benign triage that existing tools already do reasonably well. 所以作者瞄准的缺口，正是那些很可能永远不会被膜片钳测量到的变异的功能获得/丧失方向判断，而不是现有工具已经做得相当不错的初步致病/良性分类。

JORDAN What did they actually build, and what did they train it on? 他们究竟造了什么，又是用什么数据训练的？

ALEX They built a classifier called MissION — Missense ION channel variant classifier — on top of a protein language model backbone, and trained it on 1996 variants across a broad range of ion channel genes that had each been experimentally labeled as gain-of-function or loss-of-function, which the authors describe as the largest such labeled set assembled to date. 他们构建了一个名为 MissION（Missense ION channel variant classifier，即错义离子通道变异分类器）的分类器，建立在一个蛋白质语言模型骨干之上，并用 1996 个变异进行训练，这些变异分布在广泛的离子通道基因中，每一个都经过实验标记为功能获得性或功能丧失性，作者称这是迄今为止组建的最大规模同类标注数据集。 That's a real methodological choice worth pausing on: 1996 labeled variants is a genuinely large curated functional dataset by the standards of electrophysiology, where every label requires an actual wet-lab experiment, but it's tiny compared to the genome-wide scale a protein language model backbone is typically pretrained on, and it's spread thin across what's presumably dozens of different channel genes with very different structures. 这里有一个真正值得停下来讨论的方法学选择：按电生理学的标准，1996 个标注变异确实是一个规模很大的功能数据集，因为每一个标签都需要一次真实的湿实验室实验；但相比蛋白质语言模型骨干通常预训练所依赖的全基因组规模，这个数字其实很小，而且很可能分散在几十个结构迥异的通道基因之中，显得比较稀薄。 If I'm pushing on the design here, it's that a binary GoF/LoF label per variant is a clean training target, but it also discards any variant with no functional effect at all or a mixed, partial, or complex phenotype — which the abstract doesn't say how they handled, and which matters a lot for a gene family where "how much residual function" is often more clinically relevant than a clean binary direction. 如果要对这个设计提出质疑的话，那就是每个变异一个二元的功能获得/丧失标签虽然是一个干净的训练目标，但它也丢弃了任何完全没有功能影响的变异，以及混合型、部分型或复杂表型的变异——摘要里没有说明他们是如何处理这些情况的，而这一点对于一个"剩余功能有多少"往往比干净的二元方向更具临床意义的基因家族来说，影响不小。

JORDAN And how well did it actually perform? 它实际表现到底如何？

ALEX MissION reached a ROC-AUC of 0.918, compared to 0.884 and 0.779 for what the abstract calls the two current leading models — it doesn't name them in the abstract, so we can't say yet whether that's AlphaMissense, a structure-based method, or something else, and we can't confirm the comparison ran on a held-out set the leading models had never seen. MissION 达到了 0.918 的 ROC-AUC（受试者工作特征曲线下面积），而摘要中所说的两个"当前领先模型"分别是 0.884 和 0.779——摘要里没有点明这两个模型具体是什么，所以我们目前还不能确定这是不是 AlphaMissense、某种基于结构的方法，还是别的工具，也无法确认这次比较是否是在领先模型从未见过的留出测试集上进行的。 The second finding is arguably the more interesting one for our purposes: the model generalizes well to ion channel genes that have little or no electrophysiological data of their own, meaning it isn't just memorizing gene-specific patterns from well-studied channels like the cardiac sodium and potassium channels that dominate the training labels. 从我们的角度来看，第二个发现或许更有意思：这个模型在那些自身几乎没有或完全没有电生理数据的离子通道基因上也表现良好，这说明它并不只是在记忆那些在训练标签中占主导地位、研究充分的通道——比如心脏钠通道和钾通道——所特有的基因模式。 That cross-gene transfer is exactly the property you'd want if you were hoping to apply a model like this to a channel gene, like CFTR, that wasn't part of the original training set. 这种跨基因的迁移能力，正是我们希望把这样一个模型应用到像 CFTR 这样并非原始训练集一部分的通道基因上时所需要的特性。 The model is deployed through an online interface that scores over 600,000 ion channel variants, which is a real deliverable, not just a benchmark number in a table. 这个模型通过一个在线界面部署，为超过 60 万个离子通道变异提供打分，这是一个真正可用的产出，而不只是表格里的一个基准数字。 Whether that deliverable did what it set out to do depends on your yardstick: against the stated aim of improving functional-direction prediction beyond existing leading models, yes, on the AUC metric reported; against the harder bar of a fully external, independently curated test set with transparent comparator identities, we simply can't tell from the abstract alone. 这个产出是否真正达成了目标，取决于你用什么标准来衡量：如果对照的是"在功能方向预测上超越现有领先模型"这个既定目标，那么按照报告的 AUC 指标来看，答案是肯定的；但如果对照的是一个完全独立、经过独立整理、比较对象透明公开的外部测试集这一更严格的标准，那么仅凭摘要本身，我们无法判断。

JORDAN Does this map onto anything Runyu's actually working on? 这和 Runyu 目前实际在做的事情有关联吗？

ALEX This lands squarely on the Karen Raraigh collaboration — CFTR variant interpretation and VUS pathogenicity classification — and it's the one priority worth naming here, not the registry data-quality work or the AI-tooling career track. 这正好落在 Karen Raraigh 的合作方向上——CFTR 变异解读和意义未明变异（VUS）致病性分类——这是这里唯一值得点名的优先事项，而不是登记处数据质量工作，也不是 AI 工具开发的职业方向。 CFTR VUS classification cares about more than a clean gain/loss binary — mechanism class matters, whether a variant disrupts folding and trafficking, gating kinetics, conductance, or synthesis, because that mechanism class is exactly what determines which CFTR modulator drug class, if any, a variant might respond to.
