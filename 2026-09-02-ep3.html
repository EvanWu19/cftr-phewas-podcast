Ep3 — Track C: the WHO-to-CDC growth-chart discontinuity at age 2

Daymont C, Hwang W, Paul IM, Shur N, Freedman DS. (2025). Creation and Evaluation of New Growth Charts With a Gradual Transition From WHO to CDC Values. Pediatrics.

Abstract-only (no PMCID, not open access). CDC recommends switching from WHO Growth Standards to the CDC 2000 Growth Reference at exactly age 2 — an abrupt switch between two structurally different statistical constructs (a prescriptive optimal-growth standard vs. a descriptive US population reference) that manufactures an artifactual z-score drop even in children growing normally. The authors built new charts blending WHO and CDC values via a weighted average across ages 2–5. In a retrospective cohort of 7429 children, the CDC-recommended abrupt-switch charts showed a mean BMI-for-age z-score (BMIz) drop of -0.59 SD and weight-for-age z-score (WTz) drop of -0.35 SD between ages 1.5–2y, versus -0.09 and -0.01 SD respectively under the gradual charts — and the proportion of children crossing a "large drop" (z<-1) threshold fell from 28.3%→11.6% (BMIz) and 6.0%→0.85% (WTz). Same author as the growthcleanr algorithm already in the QC catalog, but this paper targets a chart artifact, not a data-entry error.

Plain-English glossary

  • WHO Growth Standards vs. CDC Growth Reference — WHO Standards (birth–5y, breastfed multi-country cohort raised under optimal conditions) are prescriptive ("how children should grow"); the CDC 2000 Reference (2–19y, cross-sectional US survey data) is descriptive ("how children in this population did grow").（中文：WHO 生长标准（出生至 5 岁，跨国母乳喂养队列，理想条件下成长）是规范性的（"儿童应该如何生长"）；CDC 2000 生长参考（2 至 19 岁，美国横断面调查数据）是描述性的（"该人群儿童实际如何生长"）。）
  • LMS method — the statistical technique underlying both chart families: for each age/sex, three fitted parameters (L = skewness/Box-Cox power, M = median, S = coefficient of variation) define the distribution used to compute z-scores and percentiles.（中文：两套曲线图体系背后共同的统计技术：针对每个年龄/性别组合，拟合出三个参数（L=偏度/Box-Cox 幂参数，M=中位数，S=变异系数），共同定义用于计算 z 分数和百分位数的分布。）
  • Reference-chart discontinuity artifact — a jump in a child's computed z-score caused purely by switching which chart's L/M/S parameters are applied to the same raw measurement, not by any real change in the child.（中文：仅因为对同一原始测量值切换使用不同曲线图的 L/M/S 参数，而产生的儿童计算 z 分数跳变，并非儿童本身发生了真实变化。）
  • Weighted-average gradual transition — blending two reference charts' values across a multi-year age window instead of switching abruptly at one age point.（中文：在一个跨越多年的年龄窗口内混合两套参考曲线图的数值，而非在某一个年龄点骤然切换。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • Gradual WHO/CDC transition charts — new BMI-, weight-, and length/height-for-age charts blending WHO and CDC values via weighted average across ages 2–5, replacing an abrupt switch at exactly age 2.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation, primary — changes the reference chart underlying any z-score-based flag at the WHO/CDC age-2 boundary; an abrupt-switch-based candidate generator systematically over-flags children in this age band. Buildable artifact: diagnostic script computing flag outcomes under current (abrupt-switch) vs. reconstructed gradual-blend methodology for encounters straddling age 2, quantifying how many boundary-adjacent flags disappear. Difficulty: Small-to-Medium (WHO/CDC LMS parameter tables are public; blending logic approximable without the paper's exact formula).

What to verify first

  • How CFFPR's own height/weight z-score computation currently handles the WHO-to-CDC age-2 transition, if at all.
  • The exact weighted-average blending formula and whether the new gradual charts are publicly released as downloadable LMS tables (not available from the abstract alone).
  • Whether CF-specific growth patterns or modulator-era weight trajectories interact with this general-population artifact in ways this study (apparently a general pediatric cohort) wouldn't capture.
  • Whether the age 2–5 window this artifact concentrates in overlaps meaningfully with ages CFFPR tracks most closely for nutritional status.

Error types covered / not covered

Covered: a reference-chart-switching artifact producing spurious large z-score changes at the WHO/CDC age-2 boundary (a confound in the reference chart, not the individual record). Not covered: unit conversion errors, digit transposition, decimal-shift errors, carry-forward errors, or discriminating a genuine clinical event from a data-entry error at the individual-encounter level — this paper operates entirely at the reference-chart-construction layer, upstream of individual-record adjudication, and provides no error-detection sensitivity/PPV figures (evaluation is a before/after distributional comparison, not against an adjudicated ground-truth error set).

ALEX Our first Track C episode today is Daymont and colleagues, published in Pediatrics in 2025: "Creation and Evaluation of New Growth Charts With a Gradual Transition From WHO to CDC Values." 今天第一集 Track C 讲的是 Daymont 及其同事在 2025 年发表于《儿科学》（Pediatrics）期刊上的论文：《创建并评估从 WHO 到 CDC 值渐进过渡的新生长曲线图》。 Carrie Daymont is the same author behind the growthcleanr algorithm already in our QC catalog, and this paper attacks a completely different piece of the same territory — not a data-entry error, but an artifact baked directly into the reference chart itself. Carrie Daymont 正是我们质控方法目录中已收录的 growthcleanr 算法的作者，而这篇论文攻克的是同一领域中一个完全不同的问题——不是数据录入错误，而是直接嵌入参考生长曲线图本身的一种伪影（artifact）。 We only have the abstract for this one — no PMCID, not open access — so we'll be explicit about that limit throughout. 这一篇我们只拿到了摘要——没有 PMCID，也不是开放获取——所以我们全程都会明确标注这一限制。

JORDAN Let's start with the basics, since we haven't covered growth-chart conventions on this show before. 我们先从基础讲起，因为这个节目之前还没讲过生长曲线图（growth chart）的相关惯例。 What's the actual difference between the WHO charts and the CDC charts, and why does anyone switch between them at all? WHO 曲线图和 CDC 曲线图之间到底有什么区别，为什么会有人在两者之间切换？

ALEX The World Health Organization Growth Standards are built from a multi-country cohort of breastfed infants raised under optimal conditions — feeding, healthcare access, no maternal smoking — so they're explicitly meant to describe how children *should* grow, which is why they're called a "standard" rather than a "reference." 世界卫生组织生长标准（WHO Growth Standards）是基于一个跨国队列构建的，队列中的婴儿都是母乳喂养，并且在最理想的条件下成长——良好的喂养、可及的医疗保健、母亲不吸烟——所以它明确是用来描述儿童"应该"如何生长的，这也是为什么它被称为"标准"（standard）而不是"参考"（reference）。 They cover birth to age five, using recumbent length for the youngest children. 它覆盖从出生到五岁的年龄段，对最小的儿童使用卧位测量的身长（recumbent length）。 The CDC 2000 Growth Reference, by contrast, is built from cross-sectional survey data on the actual US population — historically including formula-fed infants — so it describes how children in that population actually grew, not a prescriptive ideal, which is why it's a "reference." 相比之下，CDC 2000 生长参考（CDC 2000 Growth Reference）是基于对美国实际人群的横断面调查数据构建的——历史上也包括配方奶喂养的婴儿——因此它描述的是那个人群实际的生长情况，而不是一个规范性的理想标准，这也是为什么它被称为"参考"（reference）。 It covers ages 2 through 19, using standing height. 它覆盖 2 到 19 岁的年龄段，使用站立身高（standing height）。 The CDC recommends switching from the WHO standard to the CDC reference at exactly age 2, since that's where the WHO charts stop and the CDC charts begin their coverage. CDC 建议恰好在 2 岁时从 WHO 标准切换到 CDC 参考，因为这正是 WHO 曲线图覆盖范围结束、CDC 曲线图覆盖范围开始的年龄点。

JORDAN And the "z-score" mechanics underneath all of this — how does a chart actually turn a raw measurement into a number you can flag as abnormal? 而这背后的 z 分数（z-score）机制——一个曲线图究竟是如何把一个原始测量值转换成一个可以被标记为异常的数字的？

ALEX Both chart families use something called the LMS method: for every age and sex, three parameters are fit from population data — L, a Box-Cox power parameter capturing skewness, M, the median value, and S, a coefficient of variation — and together those three numbers define a specific statistical distribution for that age and sex. 两个曲线图体系都使用一种叫做 LMS 方法的技术：针对每一个年龄和性别组合，从人群数据中拟合出三个参数——L，一个描述偏度的 Box-Cox 幂参数；M，中位数；以及 S，变异系数——这三个数字共同定义了该年龄和性别组合下的一个特定统计分布。 A z-score is just how many of those S-based standard deviations a child's raw measurement sits away from that age- and sex-specific M, after adjusting for the L-based skewness. z 分数就是在根据 L 参数调整偏度之后，一个儿童的原始测量值偏离该年龄和性别特定的 M 值有多少个基于 S 的标准差。 The catch here is that WHO and CDC each fit their own separate L, M, and S curves from their own separate populations, so the exact same raw measurement — say, a specific weight in kilograms for a 23-month-old — can convert to two meaningfully different z-scores depending purely on which chart's L, M, S parameters you plug it into. 这里的问题在于，WHO 和 CDC 分别从各自独立的人群中拟合出各自独立的 L、M、S 曲线，所以完全相同的原始测量值——比如一个 23 个月大孩子的某个具体体重公斤数——仅仅因为你代入的是哪一套曲线图的 L、M、S 参数不同，就可能转换出两个有意义差异的 z 分数。 That's a pure artifact of which reference chart you're using, with nothing biological happening in the child at all. 这纯粹是所使用参考曲线图不同所造成的伪影，孩子本身并没有发生任何生理上的变化。

JORDAN So what actually motivated this paper — was the WHO-to-CDC discontinuity a known problem before this? 那这篇论文的动机到底是什么——WHO 到 CDC 的不连续性在此之前是已知问题吗？

ALEX Yes, the discontinuity itself has been documented before as a real clinical nuisance, but it was mostly treated as an acknowledged quirk rather than something anyone had built a practical fix for. 是的，这种不连续性本身此前就已经被记录为一个真实存在的临床困扰，但它大多被当作一个公认的怪癖来对待，而不是有人真正为之构建了实用的解决方案。 Institutions typically implement the CDC's recommendation as a hard, abrupt switch: a child's chart uses WHO parameters right up through their second birthday and CDC parameters starting the very next measurement, with no blending in between. 各机构通常将 CDC 的这一建议实施为一次生硬、突然的切换：孩子的曲线图在满两岁生日之前一直使用 WHO 参数，从下一次测量开始就立刻使用 CDC 参数，中间没有任何过渡混合。 Because the underlying populations and statistical constructs differ — a prescriptive optimal-growth standard versus a descriptive population reference — that abrupt switch can produce a real jump in a child's plotted z-score trajectory even when nothing about the child's actual growth has changed, and clinicians reviewing that trajectory, or any automated system built on top of it, has no easy way to tell a real growth deceleration from a chart-switching artifact. 由于两套曲线图背后的人群和统计构造不同——一个是规范性的最优生长标准，一个是描述性的人群参考——这种生硬的切换可能会在孩子绘制出的 z 分数轨迹上产生一次真实的跳变，即使孩子本身的实际生长完全没有变化，而审阅这条轨迹的临床医生，或者建立在其之上的任何自动化系统，都没有简单的办法区分真正的生长减速和曲线图切换造成的伪影。 That's exactly the gap this paper set out to close: build a chart that removes the discontinuity itself, rather than just documenting that it exists. 这正是这篇论文要填补的缺口：构建一个能消除不连续性本身的曲线图，而不仅仅是记录这种不连续性的存在。

JORDAN What did they actually build, and what's the concrete decision rule they used to evaluate it? 他们究竟构建了什么，评估时使用的具体判定规则又是什么？

ALEX They iteratively developed new charts for three parameters — BMI-for-age, weight-for-age, and length/height-for-age — that blend WHO and CDC values using a weighted average spanning ages 2 through 5, rather than switching all at once exactly at the second birthday. 他们针对三个参数——年龄别体质指数（BMI-for-age）、年龄别体重（weight-for-age）、以及年龄别身长/身高（length/height-for-age）——迭代开发出了新的曲线图，使用一个跨越 2 岁到 5 岁的加权平均法来混合 WHO 和 CDC 的数值，而不是在两岁生日那一刻一次性完全切换。 They then ran a retrospective cohort study on 7429 children, comparing the mean change in z-score between ages 1.5 and 2 years — a window chosen specifically to straddle the transition — under the CDC-recommended abrupt-switch charts versus their new gradual charts, and also tracked what proportion of children crossed a concrete threshold: a z-score drop below -1 or a rise above +1 between those two ages, counted as a "large change." 他们随后对 7429 名儿童进行了一项回顾性队列研究，比较了 1.5 岁到 2 岁之间 z 分数平均变化——这个年龄窗口是特意选来跨越过渡期的——在 CDC 推荐的骤然切换曲线图与他们新的渐进曲线图下的差异，并且还追踪了跨越一个具体阈值的儿童比例：两岁之间 z 分数下降超过 -1 或上升超过 +1，被计为一次"大幅变化"。 If I'm pushing on this design choice, it's whether a weighted-average blend across ages 2 to 5 is a principled correction or more of a cosmetic smoothing patch — WHO and CDC aren't just offset versions of the same underlying distribution, one is a prescriptive optimal-growth standard and the other a descriptive population reference, so linearly blending two structurally different statistical constructs could, in principle, introduce its own new distortions somewhere in that three-year blending window even as it removes the single-point discontinuity at exactly age 2. 如果要对这个设计选择提出质疑，那就是：跨越 2 岁到 5 岁的加权平均混合，究竟是一次有原则依据的校正，还是更像是一种表面上的平滑修补——WHO 和 CDC 并不只是同一个底层分布的两个偏移版本，一个是规范性的最优生长标准，另一个是描述性的人群参考，因此对两个结构上不同的统计构造做线性混合，原则上有可能在这个长达三年的混合窗口的某个位置引入自己新的扭曲，即便它消除了恰好在两岁那一点的单点不连续性。

JORDAN And did it actually work — did the gradual charts remove the artifact? 那它实际效果如何——渐进曲线图真的消除了这种伪影吗？

ALEX Under the CDC-recommended abrupt-switch charts, the cohort showed a large mean drop between 1.5 and 2 years — BMI-for-age z-score down 0.59 SDs on average, weight-for-age z-score down 0.35 SDs — even though nothing suggests these children were actually failing to gain weight as a population. 在 CDC 推荐的骤然切换曲线图下，该队列在 1.5 岁到 2 岁之间显示出较大的平均下降——年龄别体质指数 z 分数平均下降了 0.59 个标准差，年龄别体重 z 分数平均下降了 0.35 个标准差——尽管没有任何迹象表明这批儿童作为一个群体真的存在体重增长不足的问题。 Under the new gradual charts, that mean drop nearly disappeared — BMI-for-age z-score down only 0.09 SDs, weight-for-age z-score down just 0.01 SDs, essentially flat. 而在新的渐进曲线图下，这种平均下降几乎消失了——年龄别体质指数 z 分数只下降了 0.09 个标准差，年龄别体重 z 分数仅下降了 0.01 个标准差，基本持平。 The proportion of children crossing the "large drop" threshold told the same story at the individual level: 28.3% of children showed a large BMI-for-age z-score drop under the CDC-recommended charts versus 11.6% under the gradual charts, and 6.0% showed a large weight-for-age drop under the CDC-recommended charts versus 0.85% under the gradual charts. 跨越"大幅下降"阈值的儿童比例，在个体层面讲述了同样的故事：在 CDC 推荐曲线图下，28.3% 的儿童出现了大幅的年龄别体质指数 z 分数下降，而在渐进曲线图下这一比例是 11.6%；年龄别体重出现大幅下降的比例，CDC 推荐曲线图下是 6.0%，渐进曲线图下是 0.85%。 That's a real, substantial reduction in an artifact-driven false-positive-style flag, and on the paper's own stated aim — building a chart that reduces overidentification of slow weight gain — it delivered. 这是对一种由伪影驱动的、类似假阳性的标记的一次真实且显著的削减，而按照论文自己设定的目标——构建一个能够减少"体重增长缓慢"被过度识别的曲线图——它确实做到了。 What we can't assess from the abstract is whether there's any independent, adjudicated ground truth here at all — this is a before-versus-after distributional comparison under an assumption of stable growth, not a comparison against a labeled set of true errors versus true growth changes, so we should think of it as strong artifact-reduction evidence rather than a sensitivity/specificity result. 从摘要中我们无法评估的是，这里是否存在任何独立的、经过判定的真值——这是一次基于"生长稳定"假设的前后分布比较，而不是与一组已标注的"真实错误 vs. 真实生长变化"数据的对照，所以我们应该把它理解为强有力的伪影削减证据，而不是一个灵敏度/特异度结果。

JORDAN Does this change anything about how a candidate-flagging system for implausible values should be built? 这是否会改变一个用来标记不合理数值的候选筛查系统应该如何构建？

ALEX This lands squarely on pipeline stage (a), the candidate generator — it changes the reference chart underlying any z-score-based flag, specifically right at the WHO-to-CDC boundary that most implementations hit at exactly age 2. 这正好落在流程的第 (a) 环节——候选生成器（candidate generator）——它改变了任何基于 z 分数的标记规则所依赖的参考曲线图，特别是在大多数实现方案恰好在两岁时触及的 WHO 到 CDC 边界上。 A candidate generator built on the abrupt-switch charts would systematically over-flag children in this age band as having implausible drops in weight or BMI, and that's a false-positive rate baked directly into the flagging threshold's reference chart, not a problem with the underlying data at all. 一个建立在骤然切换曲线图之上的候选生成器，会系统性地过度标记这个年龄段的儿童存在不合理的体重或体质指数下降，而这是一种直接嵌入标记阈值所依赖的参考曲线图中的假阳性率，与底层数据本身完全无关。 The buildable artifact: a diagnostic script that, for any registry variable with encounters straddling age 2, computes the flag outcome under the current abrupt-switch methodology versus a reconstructed WHO/CDC gradual-blend methodology, and quantifies how many age-2-boundary-adjacent flags disappear under the gradual approach — a scoping tool to size the problem before deciding whether adopting gradual charts is worth the engineering effort. 可搭建的原型是一个诊断脚本：对于任何在两岁前后有就诊记录的登记处变量，分别计算在当前骤然切换方法与重建的 WHO/CDC 渐进混合方法下的标记结果，并量化在渐进方法下有多少邻近两岁边界的标记会消失——这是一个在决定是否值得投入工程精力采用渐进曲线图之前，先量化问题规模的评估工具。 Difficulty: Small-to-Medium, since both the WHO and CDC LMS parameter tables are publicly available, and the blending logic can be approximated even without the full paper's exact formula. 难度评估：小到中等，因为 WHO 和 CDC 的 LMS 参数表都是公开可获取的，即便没有论文中完整精确的公式，混合逻辑也可以近似实现。

JORDAN What would we need to check before trusting this for CFFPR height and weight data specifically? 在真正把这个方法用于 CFFPR 的身高体重数据之前，我们需要检查些什么？
