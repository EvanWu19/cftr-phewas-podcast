Ep1 (Track A) -- Deep Learning and Experimental Validation of BAP1 Missense Variant Pathogenicity in Uveal Melanoma

Dave N, Taylor Gonzalez DJ, Djulbegovic MB, Cernichiaro-Espinosa LA, King BA, Shields CL, Wilson MW. 2026. Ophthalmology Science. PMID 42199731 / PMC13199758 / DOI 10.1016/j.xops.2026.101199.

A University of Tennessee ophthalmology group benchmarks AlphaMissense, REVEL, MetaRNN, and CADD against a genuine experimental ground truth -- Waters et al's saturation genome editing (SGE) map of 4,619 BAP1 missense variants -- for the single gene that drives metastatic risk in uveal melanoma. AlphaMissense wins on both ROC (AUROC 0.837) and precision-recall (AUPRC 0.627), but the real finding is that its built-in genome-wide threshold is badly miscalibrated for BAP1 specifically: the default cutoff gives 91.5% sensitivity but only 49.6% specificity, while a Youden-index-optimized, BAP1-specific threshold (0.952, versus the default 0.564) trades some sensitivity for much better specificity and lifts overall accuracy from 59.0% to 77.7%. This is a direct methodological cousin of the McDonald 2024 CFTR-AlphaMissense calibration paper already in this podcast's catalog, and the buildable artifact below runs the same recipe on CFTR's own functional data.

Plain-English glossary

  • AlphaFold2 -- DeepMind's protein structure prediction model, used here to map pathogenicity scores onto BAP1's 3D structure.（中文：DeepMind开发的蛋白质结构预测模型，本文用它把致病性分数映射到BAP1的三维结构上。）
  • AUPRC (area under the precision-recall curve) -- a single number summarizing precision-recall performance across all thresholds; more informative than AUROC when the positive class is rare, as pathogenic variants are here (21%).（中文：概括在所有阈值下精确率-召回率表现的单一数值；当阳性类别较少见时（此处致病变异占21%），比AUROC更具信息量。）
  • BAP1 (BRCA1-associated protein 1) -- a deubiquitinating enzyme involved in chromatin remodeling and DNA-damage repair; its loss is the biggest driver of metastatic risk in uveal melanoma and causes a germline tumor-predisposition syndrome.（中文：一种去泛素化酶，参与染色质重塑和DNA损伤修复；其功能丧失是葡萄膜黑色素瘤转移风险的最大驱动因素，生殖细胞系突变还会导致肿瘤易感综合征。）
  • Deubiquitinating enzyme -- an enzyme that removes the small protein tag ubiquitin from other proteins, reversing a common regulatory modification.（中文：从其他蛋白上移除泛素这一小蛋白标签的酶，逆转一种常见的调控修饰。）
  • Saturation genome editing (SGE) -- a CRISPR-Cas9 method that installs nearly every possible point mutation in a gene at its native genomic location, then reads out a functional consequence (here, cell fitness) at scale.（中文：一种CRISPR-Cas9方法，在基因原本所在的基因组位置上安装几乎所有可能的点突变，然后大规模读出功能后果（此处为细胞适应性）。）
  • Uveal melanoma (UM) -- the most common primary intraocular cancer in adults, arising from melanocytes in the iris, ciliary body, or choroid.（中文：成年人中最常见的原发性眼内恶性肿瘤，起源于虹膜、睫状体或脉络膜的黑色素细胞。）
  • Youden index -- a threshold-selection criterion that maximizes sensitivity plus specificity minus one, used here to find a BAP1-specific AlphaMissense cutoff rather than trusting the genome-wide default.（中文：一种阈值选择准则，最大化敏感性与特异性之和减一，本文用它为BAP1找到专属的AlphaMissense切点，而不是沿用全基因组默认值。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • AlphaMissense -- DeepMind's proteome-wide missense pathogenicity predictor (structure + conservation + population frequency), scored 0-1.
  • REVEL / MetaRNN / CADD -- three comparator pathogenicity predictors, retrieved here via the Ensembl Variant Effect Predictor's dbNSFP plugin.
  • Youden-index threshold optimization -- a one-line statistical recipe for turning "does this score correlate with truth" into "here is the specific cutoff to use for this gene."
  • ROC / precision-recall benchmarking against SGE -- the general pattern of using a large functional-genomics dataset as ground truth for a computational predictor, rather than relying on ClinVar alone (which is 87% unannotated here).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation. Buildable: CFTR AlphaMissense Youden-threshold recalibrator (S-M) -- pull the McDonald 2024 CFTR functional dataset's forskolin-induced chloride-current readouts (already AlphaMissense's best-correlating functional axis for CFTR), dichotomize at a defensible clinical cutoff, run the same ROC/Youden-index search this paper ran for BAP1, and report CFTR's own optimal threshold against the 0.564 global default. Extends the existing cftr-predictor-calibration-report backlog item (2026-08-23/ep1) with an explicit threshold-recalibration step.

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR has anything approaching BAP1's SGE-scale, single-experiment functional map, or only a patchwork of smaller, differently-designed assays.
  • Whether cell-fitness-style ground truth (viability) is the right analog for CFTR's clinically relevant function (chloride-channel conductance) -- McDonald 2024 found AlphaMissense correlates far better with direct channel current than with trafficking.
  • Whether a Youden-optimized threshold, checked against only 12 ClinVar-definitive variants in this paper, risks overfitting -- any CFTR version needs a genuinely held-out check.
  • That none of this speaks to how CFFPR's own genotype data is structured, curated, or classified -- this is an external, public-data recalibration exercise only.

SGE（饱和基因组编辑）用CRISPR-Cas9技术，把一个基因几乎所有可能的单核苷酸变化逐一直接安装到细胞中该基因原本所在的基因组位置上，然后读出功能后果——通常是通过追踪被编辑的细胞群体生长或死亡的速度。For BAP1, the source dataset here -- from a different group, Waters et al -- profiled 4,619 missense variants this way in a near-haploid human cell line, so each edit shows its effect without a second, unedited copy of the gene to mask it. 对于BAP1，这里用作数据来源的——来自另一个团队Waters等人的研究——用这种方法在一种近单倍体（near-haploid）人类细胞系中检测了4619个错义变异，因为细胞里几乎没有第二个未编辑的基因拷贝来掩盖效果，每个编辑的效应都会直接显现出来。

JORDAN So it's a functional readout you can scale to thousands of variants at once, instead of testing one variant in a dish by hand. 所以这是一种可以一次性扩展到成千上万个变异的功能读出方法，而不是一个一个手工在培养皿里测试。
