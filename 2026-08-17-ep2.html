Ep2 (Track B) — nf-core/metatdenovo: What a Community-Standard Nextflow Pipeline Looks Like

Di Leo et al. (2025, PeerJ) introduce nf-core/metatdenovo, a Nextflow pipeline for reference-genome-free assembly and annotation of metatranscriptomes (community RNA-seq data). Not human genomics, and not new science we'd cite for CF — the value is entirely in the tooling reference. The pipeline chains five stages (pre-processing, assembly/gene-calling, quantification, functional annotation, taxonomy) with configurable tool choices at each step (SPAdes vs. MEGAHIT; Prodigal/Prokka vs. TransDecoder; eggNOG-mapper vs. KofamScan; EUKulele vs. DIAMOND), runs identically on laptop/HPC/cloud via Nextflow + containers, and follows nf-core's community code/documentation/testing standards. Validated with a direct reanalysis of a 2016 published dataset — broad ecological conclusions held up, though specific taxonomic abundance numbers shifted with the change from per-sample to combined assembly. Code and every analysis figure's Quarto/R source live in a public repo. The buildable artifact: an nf-core-style structural audit of existing CFTR-WGS Nextflow tooling — module boundaries, config-driven tool swapping, a wired-in MultiQC report, and a companion reproducible-analysis notebook.

Plain-English glossary

  • DIAMOND — a fast sequence-alignment tool used here for taxonomic classification of assembled genes against reference databases like GTDB. (中文：DIAMOND，一种快速序列比对工具，本文中用于将组装出的基因与GTDB等参考数据库比对以进行物种分类。)
  • eggNOG / KEGG — two general-purpose gene-function annotation/classification systems used by different annotation tools in the pipeline. (中文：eggNOG和KEGG，两套通用的基因功能注释/分类体系，流程中不同的注释工具分别基于它们进行注释。)
  • MAG (metagenome-assembled genome) — a genome-scale sequence reconstructed computationally from mixed-community sequencing data rather than from a single cultured organism. (中文：宏基因组组装基因组，一种从混合群落测序数据中通过计算重建出的基因组规模序列，而非来自单一培养生物体。)
  • Metatranscriptomics — sequencing all expressed mRNA from an entire microbial community at once, showing which genes are actively transcribed rather than merely present. (中文：宏转录组学，一次性测序整个微生物群落表达的所有mRNA，反映的是哪些基因正在被主动转录，而不仅仅是哪些基因存在。)
  • MultiQC — a tool that aggregates logs/statistics from many pipeline steps into one consolidated HTML report. (中文：MultiQC，一种将流程中多个步骤的日志/统计数据汇总成一份统一HTML报告的工具。)
  • nf-core — a community collaboration enforcing shared code/testing/documentation standards across a library of Nextflow pipelines, aimed at long-term maintainability beyond any one lab. (中文：nf-core，一个在众多Nextflow流程之间推行统一代码/测试/文档标准的社区协作项目，目标是实现超越单个实验室的长期可维护性。)
  • Nextflow — a workflow-manager domain-specific language that lets a multi-step pipeline run unchanged across laptop, HPC, and cloud platforms, with software delivered via containers. (中文：Nextflow，一种工作流管理器领域特定语言，能让一个多步骤流程在笔记本电脑、高性能计算集群和云平台上不加修改地运行，软件通过容器分发。)

  • Reanalysis-as-validation pattern — validating a re-architected pipeline by direct reanalysis of a previously published dataset and comparing conclusions, not just raw numbers.

ALEX Today's Track B pick is a 2025 paper in PeerJ by Di Leo and colleagues, "The Nextflow nf-core/metatdenovo pipeline for reproducible annotation of metatranscriptomes, and more." 今天B轨道的内容来自2025年发表在PeerJ上的一篇论文，作者是Di Leo等人，题目是《用于宏转录组可重复注释的Nextflow nf-core/metatdenovo流程及其扩展应用》。 It's not about human genomics at all — it's a microbial-ecology tool — but we picked it because it's a clean, recent example of what a pipeline built to nf-core community standards actually looks like end to end, which is exactly the reference point worth having on hand while maintaining CFTR-WGS tooling. 它跟人类基因组学完全无关——这是一个微生物生态学的工具——但我们选它是因为它清楚展示了一个按照nf-core社区标准构建的流程从头到尾应该是什么样子，而这正是在维护CFTR-WGS工具链时值得随手参考的一个范例。 The pipeline is called nf-core/metatdenovo, and it handles de novo assembly and annotation of metatranscriptomes — community RNA sequencing data — without needing a reference genome. 这个流程叫做nf-core/metatdenovo，它负责宏转录组（metatranscriptome，也就是群落RNA测序数据）的从头组装（de novo assembly）和注释，不需要参考基因组。

JORDAN Before the pipeline itself, what's metatranscriptomics actually measuring, and why no reference genome? 在讲流程本身之前，宏转录组学到底测量的是什么，为什么不需要参考基因组？

ALEX Metatranscriptomics sequences all the expressed mRNA from an entire microbial community at once, rather than one organism at a time. 宏转录组学一次性测序整个微生物群落表达的所有mRNA，而不是一次只测一个物种。 That gives you a readout of what the community is actually doing — which genes are being transcribed right now — as opposed to metagenomics, which sequences DNA and tells you what genes are present but not whether they're active. 这样得到的结果反映的是整个群落此刻正在做什么——哪些基因正在被转录——而宏基因组学（metagenomics）测序的是DNA，只能告诉你哪些基因存在，却不能告诉你它们是否活跃。 The reference-genome problem is that most environmental microbes have never been cultured or sequenced, so for many samples there's no catalog of "known genomes" to align reads against. 参考基因组的问题在于，大多数环境微生物从未被培养或测序过，所以对很多样本来说，根本没有一份"已知基因组"目录可以用来比对测序读段（read）。 De novo assembly sidesteps that by reconstructing longer sequences directly from the overlapping short reads themselves, genome-independent. 从头组装（de novo assembly）绕过了这个问题，它直接从重叠的短读段本身重建出更长的序列，完全不依赖已知基因组。 One term worth flagging since it recurs constantly in this space: MAGs, metagenome-assembled genomes — genome-scale sequences reconstructed computationally from mixed-community data rather than from a single cultured organism. 有一个术语值得先说明，因为它在这个领域反复出现：MAGs，即宏基因组组装基因组（metagenome-assembled genomes）——这是从混合群落数据中通过计算重建出的基因组规模序列，而不是来自单一培养的生物体。

JORDAN Okay, walk me through the pipeline stages. 好，跟我讲讲这个流程的各个阶段。

ALEX Five stages, chained together. 五个阶段，串联在一起。 Pre-processing first: raw reads go through Trim Galore! for quality trimming, optionally BBDuk to filter out unwanted sequences like host genome contamination, Seqtk to interleave paired reads, and BBNorm to normalize read depth — flattening out the natural imbalance where some organisms or genes are wildly overrepresented, which both speeds up assembly and improves its quality. 首先是预处理：原始读段先经过Trim Galore!进行质量修剪，可选地用BBDuk过滤掉不需要的序列，比如宿主基因组污染，再用Seqtk把成对读段交织排列，最后用BBNorm对测序深度进行归一化——抹平某些物种或基因被过度代表的天然不平衡，这样既能加快组装速度，又能提升组装质量。 Second, assembly and gene calling: reads get assembled with either SPAdes — in its rnaspades mode by default — or MEGAHIT, and then genes are called three ways depending on what's in the sample — Prodigal and Prokka for prokaryotes, TransDecoder for eukaryotes — and since real communities usually have both, the pipeline can run all three gene-calling paths on the same assembly. 第二，组装与基因预测（gene calling）：读段用SPAdes（默认使用rnaspades模式）或MEGAHIT进行组装，然后基因预测有三条路径，取决于样本里有什么——原核生物用Prodigal和Prokka，真核生物用TransDecoder——由于真实的群落通常两者都有，流程可以在同一个组装结果上并行运行全部三条基因预测路径。 Third, quantification: the pre-processed reads get mapped back to the assembly with BBMap, and featureCounts, from the Subread package, tallies up expression counts per gene per sample. 第三，定量：预处理后的读段用BBMap重新比对回组装结果上，然后用Subread软件包中的featureCounts工具，统计每个样本中每个基因的表达计数。 Fourth, functional annotation: Prokka does its own similarity-based annotation for prokaryotes, and on top of that the pipeline offers eggNOG-mapper or KofamScan, which annotate against the eggNOG or KEGG systems respectively — both are general-purpose gene-function classification schemes — plus an option to supply custom HMMER protein profiles for genes of specific interest that the general annotators miss. 第四，功能注释：Prokka本身会对原核生物做基于相似性的注释，在此基础上，流程还提供eggNOG-mapper或KofamScan，分别针对eggNOG或KEGG系统进行注释——两者都是通用的基因功能分类体系——此外还可以提供自定义的HMMER蛋白质谱（profile），用于捕捉通用注释工具遗漏的特定关注基因。 Fifth, taxonomy: EUKulele and DIAMOND both assign taxonomic identity to the called genes against reference databases like GTDB, and the paper found EUKulele achieved much higher species-level assignment precision than DIAMOND on the same data. 第五，物种分类（taxonomy）：EUKulele和DIAMOND都会把预测出的基因与GTDB等参考数据库比对，赋予其物种分类身份，论文发现在同一份数据上，EUKulele的物种级别分配精度明显高于DIAMOND。 Everything wraps up in MultiQC, which produces one consolidated HTML report summarizing trimming, assembly, mapping, and annotation statistics across the whole run. 整个流程最后由MultiQC收尾，它会生成一份统一的HTML报告，汇总整个运行过程中修剪、组装、比对和注释的各项统计数据。

JORDAN And this all runs through Nextflow specifically because — what, portability? 这一切都跑在Nextflow上，是因为——可移植性吗？

ALEX That's the core of it. 这正是核心所在。 Nextflow is a workflow-manager domain-specific language: it lets you describe a multi-step pipeline once and then run it unchanged on a laptop, an HPC cluster, or a cloud platform like AWS or Google Cloud, with the actual software delivered through containers — Docker or Singularity — so nobody has to manually install fifteen different bioinformatics tools with fifteen different dependency trees. Nextflow是一种工作流管理器领域特定语言（domain-specific language）：你只需要描述一次多步骤流程，之后就可以在笔记本电脑、高性能计算集群，或者AWS、谷歌云这样的云平台上不加修改地运行，实际用到的软件通过容器（Docker或Singularity）分发，这样就不需要有人手动安装十五种不同的生物信息学工具，还要处理十五套不同的依赖关系。 The authors also mention the Seqera platform, a web-based graphical interface for launching and monitoring Nextflow runs without touching the command line, which matters for the biologist-facing side of adoption even if it's less relevant to how we'd actually run it. 作者们还提到了Seqera平台，这是一个基于网页的图形界面，可以不用命令行就启动和监控Nextflow运行，这对面向生物学家的推广采纳很重要，尽管对我们实际使用方式的相关性较小。 nf-core is the community layer on top of Nextflow specifically: a collaboration that enforces relatively strict code standards — consistent module structure, required tests, documentation conventions — across a large shared library of community-maintained pipelines, precisely to solve the problem that a single-lab pipeline tends to stop being maintained once the postdoc who wrote it leaves. nf-core是专门建立在Nextflow之上的社区层：这是一个执行相对严格代码标准的协作项目——统一的模块结构、必需的测试、文档规范——覆盖一个庞大的、由社区共同维护的流程库，目的正是解决一个常见问题：一个单一实验室写的流程，往往在写它的博士后离开之后就不再有人维护了。

JORDAN You mentioned SPAdes and MEGAHIT as interchangeable assembler choices — do they actually behave the same in practice? SPAdes和MEGAHIT你说是可以互换的组装工具选项——它们在实际使用中表现真的一样吗？

ALEX Not at all, and the paper actually benchmarks the trade-off directly on the marine bacterioplankton dataset. 完全不一样，而且论文确实直接在海洋细菌浮游生物数据集上对这个权衡做了基准测试。 About a third of total runtime went to early quality-trimming and filtering steps, another third to the quantification step of mapping reads back to the assembly, and assembly itself was a comparatively small share of total compute time — but memory told a different story: MEGAHIT used less than half the memory SPAdes required, so much less that MEGAHIT on the full unnormalized read set still used less memory than SPAdes did even after depth normalization had shrunk its input. 大约三分之一的总运行时间花在早期的质量修剪和过滤步骤上，另外三分之一花在把读段重新比对回组装结果的定量步骤上，而组装本身占用的计算时间比例相对较小——但内存使用情况则完全不同：MEGAHIT使用的内存不到SPAdes的一半，少到即便在未经归一化的完整读段集上运行，MEGAHIT所用的内存仍然低于SPAdes在深度归一化、输入已经缩小之后所用的内存。 Gene-calling choice mattered even more for the actual output: Prodigal returned roughly twice as many ORFs as Prokka on the same assembly — 130,146 versus 62,769 on the MEGAHIT assembly, and an even wider gap on the SPAdes assembly — because Prokka is deliberately more conservative, and Prodigal's looser threshold specifically picked up thousands of very short ORFs, under 90 amino acids, that Prokka rejected outright as likely false positives. 基因预测方式的选择对实际输出的影响甚至更大：在同一个组装结果上，Prodigal预测出的ORF数量大约是Prokka的两倍——在MEGAHIT组装结果上是130146对62769，在SPAdes组装结果上差距更大——因为Prokka刻意更保守，而Prodigal较松的阈值恰恰捕捉到了数千个非常短的ORF，短于90个氨基酸，而Prokka直接把它们当作可能的假阳性拒绝了。 That's a concrete illustration of what "config-driven tool swapping" actually buys you: the same input data, run through two legitimate but philosophically different gene-calling defaults, can produce a two-fold difference in how many genes you think you found. 这具体展示了"配置驱动的工具切换"到底能带来什么：同样的输入数据，经过两种合理但理念不同的基因预测默认设置处理后，得到的基因数量结果可以相差两倍。

JORDAN Did they validate it against anything, or is this purely a tooling description? 他们有没有拿它去和什么东西做验证，还是纯粹是工具描述？

ALEX They ran it on three real datasets and, notably, re-ran one of them as a direct reanalysis of a previously published study. 他们在三个真实数据集上运行了这个流程，值得注意的是，其中一个是对之前已发表研究的直接重新分析。 The marine bacterioplankton dataset from Bunse et al. 2016 is the reanalysis case: the original study built two separate per-sample assemblies with different assemblers and annotated against NCBI taxonomy, while nf-core/metatdenovo built a single combined assembly across all samples and annotated against GTDB using EUKulele. 来自Bunse等人2016年研究的海洋细菌浮游生物（bacterioplankton）数据集是重新分析的案例：原始研究用不同的组装工具分别为每个样本单独构建了两个组装结果，并用NCBI分类法做注释，而nf-core/metatdenovo则跨所有样本构建了一个统一的组装结果，并用EUKulele基于GTDB数据库做注释。 The two approaches diverged in specifics — Flavobacteriaceae, Alteromonadaceae, and Rhodobacteraceae reached notably higher relative transcript abundances in the nf-core/metatdenovo reanalysis than in the original paper — but the broad ecological picture, which taxa dominate, held up across both methodologies. 两种方法在具体细节上有分歧——黄杆菌科（Flavobacteriaceae）、交替单胞菌科（Alteromonadaceae）和红杆菌科（Rhodobacteraceae）在nf-core/metatdenovo的重新分析中达到的相对转录本丰度，明显高于原始论文中的结果——但从更宏观的生态学图景来看，哪些类群占主导地位，这一点在两种方法下是一致的。 They also ran a Kimchi fermentation microbiome dataset and a eukaryotic dataset from Narragansett Bay to demonstrate the pipeline handles both prokaryote-dominated and eukaryote-inclusive communities. 他们还运行了一个泡菜发酵微生物组数据集，以及一个来自纳拉甘西特湾（Narragansett Bay）的真核生物数据集，以此证明这个流程既能处理以原核生物为主的群落，也能处理包含真核生物的群落。 Code and analysis are fully open: the R and Quarto scripts used to generate every figure in the paper live in a public GitHub repository alongside the pipeline itself, which is the reproducibility standard worth noting, not just the pipeline's existence. 代码和分析完全公开：论文中生成每一张图所用的R和Quarto脚本，都和流程本身一起放在一个公开的GitHub仓库里，这种可重复性标准本身值得关注，而不仅仅是这个流程的存在。

JORDAN You said three datasets — was the third comparison as direct as the marine reanalysis? 你说是三个数据集——第三个比较和海洋数据集的重新分析一样直接吗？

ALEX Slightly different shape, but still a real cross-pipeline check. 形式略有不同，但仍然是一次真正的跨流程验证。 For the eukaryotic Narragansett Bay dataset, they compared nf-core/metatdenovo's output against results from a different existing pipeline called Eukrhythmic, rather than against the original raw-mapping study alone. 对于真核生物纳拉甘西特湾数据集，他们把nf-core/metatdenovo的输出结果，与一个叫Eukrhythmic的现有流程的结果做了比较，而不仅仅是和最初的原始比对研究做比较。 Read-mapping counts agreed in rank order between the two pipelines, major taxonomic groups showed high relative-abundance correlation between the two, and functional annotation — read counts summed by KEGG orthology group — produced a regression line with an R-squared substantially above zero when compared across pipelines, meaning the two independently engineered pipelines converged on largely the same functional picture despite different internal choices. 两条流程在读段比对计数的排序上是一致的，主要分类群之间的相对丰度相关性很高，而功能注释——按KEGG直系同源组汇总的读段计数——在跨流程比较时得到的回归线R平方值明显大于零，也就是说，两条独立设计的流程，尽管内部实现选择不同，最终却在很大程度上得出了相近的功能图景。 The one notable discrepancy was in an "other," unassigned taxonomic category, where the two pipelines diverged more than they did on the named major groups — a reminder that agreement between independent pipelines tends to be strongest on the well-characterized majority and weakest on whatever falls outside clean classification. 唯一值得注意的分歧出现在一个"其他"、未分类的类群上，两条流程在这里的分歧比在已命名的主要类群上更大——这提醒我们，独立流程之间的一致性往往在已被充分表征的主体部分最强，而在任何落在清晰分类之外的部分最弱。

JORDAN So where does this actually land for the work you're doing? 那这对你实际在做的工作来说，落地点在哪里？
