How did this episode land for you?

Ep1 (Track A) — Minigene-based characterization and classification of splice-associated variants in succinate dehydrogenase B

Blurb. SDHB causes hereditary paraganglioma/pheochromocytoma, and roughly 40% of its reported variants sit as VUS in ClinVar -- intronic and synonymous ones especially, since functional readouts and patient RNA are rarely available. Köhler et al. built a partial SDHB minigene (exons 2-5, cloned into pcDNA3.1), prioritized 48 variants by SpliceAI delta score, transfected 50 constructs (48 variants + 2 negative controls) into HEK293T cells, and read out splicing with targeted NGS sensitive to 1% transcript abundance. 19 of 48 variants (40% by unmasked score) spliced essentially normally despite high in silico scores -- masking dropped that false-positive-looking rate to 29%, still a meaningful residual gap. They then converted each variant's transcript mixture into ACMG/AMP evidence strength (PVS1 for aberrant/PTC-producing fractions, BP7 for wild-type-dominant fractions), reclassifying 13 of 26 codeable variants (50%) -- 12 VUS to likely benign, 1 likely pathogenic to VUS. CFTR is never mentioned in the paper; the connection is this podcast's own framing, since SpliceAI-flagged CFTR intronic variants commonly lack RNA evidence and would follow the same triage-then-assay shape. The buildable artifact is a computational (not wet-lab) shortlist builder: score CFTR2/ClinVar CFTR variants with both masked and unmasked SpliceAI, and split into a concordant-high priority shortlist versus a discordant likely-normal-splicing group for future minigene-collaboration planning.

Plain-English glossary

  • Minigene assay — a technique that clones a gene's exons of interest plus flanking intronic sequence into a plasmid, introduces a candidate variant, transfects it into a cell line, and reads out how the cell actually splices it -- a controlled proxy for splicing effect without needing patient tissue.（中文：微基因实验——将目标外显子连同侧翼内含子序列克隆进质粒，引入候选变异后转染进细胞系，读出细胞实际的剪接结果，是一种无需患者组织即可评估剪接效应的可控替代方法。）
  • SpliceAI — a deep-learning tool that predicts, on a 0-1 scale, how much a variant changes local splicing probability across four categories (acceptor/donor gain/loss); the largest value is usually reported as the delta score.（中文：SpliceAI——一款深度学习工具，在0到1的量表上预测变异对局部剪接概率的改变程度，分为受体/供体位点获得或丢失四类，通常取最大值作为delta分数。）
  • Masked vs. unmasked SpliceAI score — unmasked reports the raw prediction; masked suppresses predictions where a plausible alternative splicing pattern is already common in the population, reducing (but not eliminating) false-positive-looking high scores.（中文：掩蔽与未掩蔽SpliceAI分数——未掩蔽版本报告原始预测；掩蔽版本会抑制那些在人群中已存在常见备选剪接模式位置上的预测，从而降低（但不能消除）"看似假阳性"的高分情况。）
  • Premature termination codon (PTC) / nonsense-mediated decay (NMD) — a PTC is a stop codon appearing earlier than normal due to a frameshift or in-frame deletion; transcripts carrying one are frequently degraded by NMD before translation into a truncated protein.（中文：提前终止密码子/无义介导降解——PTC是因移码或框内缺失而提前出现的终止密码子；携带PTC的转录本常在翻译前被NMD降解。）
  • PVS1 / BP7 — ACMG/AMP evidence codes: PVS1 (predicted null variant where loss of function is a known disease mechanism); BP7 (benign code for synonymous/intronic variants that computational and RNA evidence suggest don't actually affect splicing).（中文：ACMG/AMP证据代码——PVS1（在已知功能丧失致病机制的基因中预测为无功能变异）；BP7（适用于同义/内含子变异的良性证据，当计算与RNA证据均表明其并不真正影响剪接时使用）。）
  • Targeted NGS splice-junction quantification — next-generation sequencing focused on a specific region, here used to quantify the relative abundance of each splice junction/transcript down to 1%, far more sensitive than conventional gel/Sanger-based minigene readouts.（中文：靶向二代测序剪接位点定量——聚焦特定区域的二代测序，此处用于量化每个剪接位点/转录本的相对丰度，精度可达1%，远超传统凝胶电泳/Sanger测序的微基因读出方式。）
  • Iron-sulfur cluster domain — a structurally and functionally critical protein region (here in SDHB) that binds iron-sulfur cofactors essential for electron transfer in mitochondrial complex II.（中文：铁硫簇结构域——一个在结构和功能上至关重要的蛋白质区域（此处指SDHB中的），结合线粒体复合体II电子传递所必需的铁硫辅因子。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • SpliceAI masked-vs-unmasked scoring as an upstream triage signal — not just a single delta score but the discordance between the two versions, shown here to be enriched for likely-false-positive high scorers.
  • Targeted NGS splice-junction quantification — a scalable, high-sensitivity (1% abundance) alternative to gel/Sanger-based minigene readout, catching roughly twice the transcript complexity per the authors' comparison.
  • Transcript-fraction-to-ACMG-strength decision framework — a reusable template for converting a continuous RNA readout (% aberrant vs. % wild-type transcript) into discrete, codeable PVS1/BP7 strength tiers.
  • Endogenous-splicing sanity check before trusting a minigene — validating the construct against the cell line's own native splicing of the same gene before layering variants on top.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation. SpliceAI-flagged CFTR intronic variants commonly lack any RNA evidence (per this podcast's own framing; not discussed in the paper, which never mentions CFTR).

MVP: a computational shortlist builder — pull CFTR2/ClinVar-listed CFTR variants flagged by SpliceAI, compute both masked and unmasked delta scores for each, and split into a concordant-high priority group (both scores agree on likely splice disruption) versus a discordant group (high unmasked, substantially lower masked — this paper's own signature of a likely-normal-splicing variant), producing a ranked candidate list for future CFTR minigene-collaboration planning. Difficulty: S (scoring and sorting two existing metrics, no new model).

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR-specific minigene, blood-RNA, or nasal-brushing splicing data already exists for candidate shortlist variants — check before treating this as a from-scratch problem.
  • Whether HEK293T-style heterologous-cell splicing generalizes to CFTR's airway/epithelial-tissue splicing regulatory environment — this paper's endogenous-splicing validation only established baseline fidelity for SDHB in a kidney-derived line, not tissue-specific regulation for any gene.
  • Whether the paper's conservative "Strong"-ceiling and combined-strength thresholds (calibrated for SDHB against BRCA1/2 precedent) transfer to CFTR, or whether CFTR needs its own calibration.
  • The minigene's limited flanking intronic sequence left 3 low-abundance whole-intron-retention transcripts unevaluable here — any CFTR minigene design faces the same construct-size-vs-intron-depth tradeoff, which matters specifically for CFTR's known deep-intronic pathogenic-variant class.

JORDAN Before the details, walk me through the vocabulary -- what's a minigene assay actually doing, and what is SpliceAI scoring? 在讲细节之前，先带我过一遍术语——微基因实验到底在做什么？SpliceAI又是在给什么打分？

ALEX A minigene assay clones a gene's exons of interest, plus enough flanking intronic sequence to preserve the splice sites, into a plasmid, introduces a candidate variant into that construct, transfects it into a cell line, and reads out what the cell's own splicing machinery actually does with it. 微基因实验会把目标外显子连同足够的侧翼内含子序列一起克隆进一个质粒中，以保留剪接位点，然后把候选变异引入这个构建体，转染进细胞系，读出细胞自身的剪接机制对它的实际处理结果。 It's a controlled, scalable proxy for asking "does this variant actually break splicing" without needing patient tissue. 这是一种可控、可规模化的替代方法，用来回答"这个变异是否真的破坏了剪接"，而不需要患者组织样本。 SpliceAI is a deep-learning tool that takes a variant's genomic context and predicts, on a zero-to-one scale, how much it changes the probability of splicing at nearby positions, broken into four categories -- acceptor gain, acceptor loss, donor gain, donor loss -- and the largest of those four is usually reported as the delta score. SpliceAI是一个深度学习工具，它读取变异的基因组上下文，在0到1的量表上预测该变异对附近位点剪接概率的改变程度，分为四类——受体位点获得、受体位点丢失、供体位点获得、供体位点丢失——通常取这四者中最大值作为delta分数进行报告。 A higher delta score means the model thinks splicing is more likely to be disrupted, but it's still a prediction, not a measurement. delta分数越高，代表模型认为剪接越可能被破坏，但这终究只是一个预测，而不是实测。

JORDAN The paper keeps mentioning "masked" versus "unmasked" SpliceAI scores -- what's that distinction? 论文里反复提到"masked（掩蔽）"和"unmasked（未掩蔽）"版本的SpliceAI分数——这个区别是什么？

ALEX SpliceAI can be run two ways. SpliceAI可以用两种方式运行。 The unmasked score reports the model's raw prediction regardless of whether the variant creates a splice signal that already looks similar to something common in the population; the masked score suppresses predictions at positions where a plausible alternative splicing pattern is already frequently used, which tends to reduce false-positive-looking predictions for variants that are near, but don't actually disrupt, an already-flexible splice region. 未掩蔽分数报告的是模型的原始预测，无论该变异产生的剪接信号是否与人群中已经常见的模式相似；掩蔽分数会抑制那些在某个位置上、已经存在一种常被使用的备选剪接模式的预测，这往往能降低那些"看起来像假阳性"的预测——也就是那些位于某个本来就有弹性的剪接区域附近、但实际并不会真正破坏它的变异。 In this paper, switching from unmasked to masked scores reduced the rate of variants that scored high but actually spliced normally from 40% down to 29% -- better, but still a meaningful residual gap between prediction and reality. 在这篇论文中，从未掩蔽分数切换到掩蔽分数，把那些打分很高但实际剪接正常的变异比例，从40%降到了29%——有所改善，但预测与实际之间仍然存在不小的差距。 One more piece of vocabulary: a premature termination codon, PTC, is a stop codon that appears earlier than the normal one because of a frameshift or an in-frame deletion that introduces one, and transcripts carrying a PTC are frequently degraded by nonsense-mediated decay, NMD, before they can be translated into a truncated protein. 再补充一个术语：提前终止密码子（premature termination codon，PTC）是指因移码突变或引入终止密码子的框内缺失而提前出现的终止密码子，携带PTC的转录本通常会在被翻译成截短蛋白之前，被无义介导降解（nonsense-mediated decay，NMD）清除掉。 PVS1 and BP7 are two ACMG/AMP evidence codes relevant here -- PVS1 is "predicted null variant in a gene where loss of function is a known disease mechanism," which applies when a variant looks like it destroys the protein; BP7 is a benign code applied to synonymous or intronic variants that computational and now RNA evidence suggests don't actually affect splicing after all. PVS1和BP7是这里涉及的两个ACMG/AMP证据代码——PVS1是"在已知功能丧失为致病机制的基因中，预测为无功能变异"，适用于看起来会破坏蛋白质的变异；BP7则是一个良性证据代码，适用于同义或内含子变异，当计算证据、现在加上RNA证据都表明它实际上并不影响剪接时使用。

ALEX SDHB encodes a subunit of mitochondrial complex II, succinate dehydrogenase, which sits at the junction of the TCA cycle and the electron transport chain. JORDAN What would you push back on here? 你会对这里的哪些地方提出质疑？

ALEX Two things. 两点。 First, HEK293T is a human embryonic kidney-derived line, not paraganglion or adrenal medullary tissue, and it's not disease-relevant tissue at all -- the authors validated that it reproduces the correct baseline MANE-transcript splicing for endogenous SDHB, which is reassuring, but baseline fidelity for one gene's constitutive splicing doesn't guarantee that every tissue-specific or developmentally regulated splicing event would look identical in the actual tumor-relevant cell type, and the paper doesn't test that directly. HEK293T是人胚胎肾来源的细胞系，不是副神经节或肾上腺髓质组织，完全不是疾病相关组织——作者验证了它能够重现内源性SDHB正确的基线MANE转录本剪接，这一点令人安心，但某个基因组成性剪接的基线保真度，并不能保证每一个组织特异性或受发育调控的剪接事件，在真正与肿瘤相关的细胞类型中也会表现得完全一致，而论文并没有直接检验这一点。 Second, even after masking, 29% of high-scoring variants still spliced essentially normally -- SpliceAI triage clearly narrows the search, but it's still generating a substantial false-positive-looking rate that only the minigene resolves, so this is not a case where the in silico score becomes unnecessary once you have the assay; it's a case where you need both, and the assay is doing real work, not just confirming what the score already told you. 第二，即便使用了掩蔽分数，仍有29%的高分变异剪接基本正常——SpliceAI初筛显然缩小了搜索范围，但它仍然产生了相当比例"看起来像假阳性"的结果，只有微基因实验才能解决，所以这并不是那种"一旦有了实验手段，计算分数就变得没必要"的情形；而是两者都需要，实验确实在做实质性的工作，而不只是验证计算分数已经告诉你的结论。
