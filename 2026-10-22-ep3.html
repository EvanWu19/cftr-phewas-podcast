Ep3 (Track C — registry QC, 1 of 2): Per-subject growth curves as a flagging substrate

Cole, Pan, and Butler (2014, Annals of Human Biology) extend SITAR — a shape-invariant model that aligns individual growth curves via size, timing, and intensity random effects — from height alone to Tanner stage, testis volume, and menarche, using 20 years of data from 103 boys and 74 girls in the Edinburgh Longitudinal Growth Study. SITAR itself fit height beautifully (98.4% of variance explained) but failed to converge on most of the Tanner-stage data, forcing a bespoke logistic mixed model instead — a telling sign about how coarse, step-function clinical scales break spline-based models. This is a measurement paper, not a QC paper, but the transfer is direct: a per-subject fitted growth curve is a personalized expected trajectory that candidate-generation rules could compare each new visit against, instead of only a cross-sectional population reference. Buildable artifact: a SITAR Residual Flagging Prototype.

Plain-English glossary

  • Age at peak height velocity (APHV) — the age at which a child's height is increasing fastest during the pubertal growth spurt.（中文：青春期生长加速阶段身高增长速度最快时的年龄。）
  • Peak height velocity (PHV) — the fastest rate of height gain during the pubertal growth spurt.（中文：青春期生长加速阶段身高增长的最快速率。）
  • Prader orchidometer — a string of graduated beads a clinician compares against by feel to estimate testis volume without imaging.（中文：一串按体积分级的珠子，临床医生通过触摸比对来估算睾丸体积，无需借助影像检查。）
  • Shape-invariant model — the principle underlying SITAR: fit one mean curve shape, then let each individual's curve be a shifted and rescaled version of it, captured by a few numbers (size, timing, intensity).（中文：SITAR方法背后的原理：拟合一条共同的均值曲线形状，再让每个个体的曲线成为该曲线经过平移和缩放后的版本，用大小、时序、强度几个数字即可捕捉。）
  • SITAR (SuperImposition by Translation And Rotation) — a mixed-effects growth-curve model with subject-specific random effects for size, timing (tempo), and intensity (velocity), aligning individual curves onto a common mean shape.（中文：一种混合效应生长曲线模型，为每个受试者设定大小、时序和强度的随机效应，将个体曲线对齐到一条共同的均值曲线形状上。）
  • Tanner stage — a five-point clinical scale (1 = prepubertal, 5 = adult) grading pubertal development of genitalia and pubic hair in boys, breast and pubic hair in girls.（中文：一套五级临床评分标准（1期为青春期前，5期为成人期），用于评估男孩的生殖器和阴毛发育、女孩的乳房和阴毛发育。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • SITAR (R sitar package) — the mixed-effects shape-invariant growth-curve model, here fit to height with a cubic spline mean curve and size/timing/intensity random effects.
  • Bespoke logistic mixed model — a bounded-growth alternative built specifically because SITAR failed to converge on most Tanner-stage data.
  • BIC-based model comparison — used throughout to choose spline degrees of freedom and to compare SITAR versus the logistic alternative.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation, primary — a per-subject fitted curve is a personalized expected trajectory, giving a residual-based flagging rule that doesn't depend only on a cross-sectional population reference. (e) framework/vocabulary, secondary — size/tempo/intensity decomposition and the shape-invariant-model framing. Tool sketch: a SITAR Residual Flagging Prototype — fit a per-subject growth curve from longitudinal height data, extract random effects, and flag any visit whose residual from that subject's own curve crosses a threshold.

Error types covered / not covered: Potentially covers a within-subject implausible deviation that looks normal against a population reference (not covered by cross-sectional LMS methods alone); does not address unit conversion, digit transposition, decimal-shift, or carry-forward errors directly, since the paper never tests any flagging rule at all — this is an inferred methods transfer, not a validated application.

What to verify first

  • Convergence rate of SITAR (or the logistic alternative) on real, irregularly spaced registry visit data, given how often it failed to converge even on a clean 20-year research cohort.
  • Generalizability of the modelling approach (not the specific numeric estimates) beyond a small, historical, single-country 1970s–80s cohort.
  • That any residual-based flagging rule derived from this substrate gets its own separate validation against labeled implausible values — this paper provides goodness-of-fit, not a sensitivity or false-positive rate.

JORDAN Let's set up the clinical vocabulary, since this one's dense with paediatric growth terms. 我们先把临床术语讲清楚，因为这篇论文里有不少儿科生长相关的术语。 Peak height velocity, or PHV (身高增长速度峰值), is the fastest rate of height gain during the adolescent growth spurt, and age at peak height velocity, APHV (达到身高增长速度峰值时的年龄), is when that fastest rate occurs — together they describe how intensely and when a child's pubertal growth spurt happens. 身高增长速度峰值（PHV）是青春期生长加速阶段身高增长最快的速率，而达到该峰值时的年龄（APHV）则是这一最快速率出现的时间点——两者共同描述了一个孩子青春期生长加速的强度和时间点。 Tanner stage (坦纳分期) is a five-point clinical scale, developed by Marshall and Tanner, used to grade pubertal development of genitalia and pubic hair in boys, and breast and pubic hair in girls, from stage 1 (prepubertal) to stage 5 (adult). 坦纳分期（Tanner stage）是由Marshall和Tanner提出的一套五级临床评分标准，用于评估男孩的生殖器和阴毛发育、女孩的乳房和阴毛发育，从1期（青春期前）到5期（成人期）。 Testis volume is measured clinically with a Prader orchidometer (普拉德睾丸测量器), a string of graduated beads a clinician compares against by feel to estimate volume without imaging. 睾丸体积在临床上使用普拉德睾丸测量器（Prader orchidometer）测量，这是一串按体积分级的珠子，临床医生通过触摸比对来估算体积，而不需要借助影像检查。 And menarche (月经初潮) is simply the age at first menstrual period, a single binary event rather than a continuous trajectory.

ALEX One more term worth flagging: a shape-invariant model (形状不变模型) is the general idea underlying SITAR — instead of fitting a separate curve shape to every individual, you fit one mean curve shape and let each person's curve be a shifted and rescaled version of it, with just a few numbers — size, timing, and intensity — capturing all of their individual variation. 还有一个值得提一下的术语：形状不变模型（shape-invariant model）是SITAR背后的核心思想——不是为每个个体单独拟合一条曲线形状，而是拟合一条共同的均值曲线形状，再让每个人的曲线成为这条均值曲线经过平移和缩放后的版本，只用几个数字——即大小、时序和强度——就能捕捉到个体间的全部差异。 That's a much more data-efficient way to describe a whole population's growth patterns than fitting each child's trajectory from scratch.

JORDAN So what motivated this particular extension of SITAR? 那这篇论文为什么要对SITAR做这样的扩展呢？ There's long-standing interest in the life-course epidemiology literature in whether the pattern of a child's growth — not just its endpoint — predicts adult disease risk, things like cardiovascular disease or diabetes, which makes precisely characterizing pubertal timing and intensity valuable research currency, not just a curiosity. 在生命历程流行病学文献中，人们一直对一个问题很感兴趣：孩子生长的模式——而不仅仅是最终结果——是否能预测成年后的疾病风险，比如心血管疾病或糖尿病，这使得精确刻画青春期的时序和强度成为有价值的研究资产，而不仅仅是一种学术兴趣。 The problem was that prior approaches to Tanner-stage-based markers only used individual stage transitions or the gap between a pair of stages — say, stage 2 to stage 5 — throwing away all the repeated measurements in between, which wastes information and makes individual-level estimates noisy. 过去的问题在于，以往基于坦纳分期的方法只利用了单个分期的转变，或者某一对分期之间的间隔——比如从第2期到第5期——而把其间所有的重复测量都丢弃掉了，这浪费了信息，也让个体层面的估计变得不精确。 The authors set out to build a toolbox that uses every available longitudinal measurement per subject, for height, Tanner stages, testis volume, and menarche alike, so that timing and intensity could be estimated efficiently and compared directly across markers within the same individual. 作者想要构建的是一套工具箱，能够利用每个受试者所有可用的纵向测量数据——身高、坦纳分期、睾丸体积和月经初潮都一样——从而高效地估计时序和强度，并在同一个体内直接比较不同标志物之间的关系。

ALEX The data came from the Edinburgh Longitudinal Growth Study — 103 boys and 74 girls followed from birth through age 20, with height measured quarterly or half-yearly from age 5, plus Tanner stages, Prader-orchidometer testis volume, and recorded menarche age. 数据来自爱丁堡纵向生长研究——103名男孩和74名女孩，从出生追踪到20岁，身高从5岁起每季度或每半年测量一次，同时还记录了坦纳分期、普拉德睾丸测量器测得的睾丸体积，以及月经初潮年龄。 For height, they fit the standard SITAR model: a mean cubic spline curve plus three subject-specific random effects — size, timing, and intensity — with the spline's degrees of freedom chosen by minimizing BIC. 对于身高，他们采用标准的SITAR模型：一条均值三次样条曲线，加上三个受试者特异的随机效应——大小、时序和强度——样条的自由度通过最小化BIC来选择。 But for Tanner stage and testis volume, SITAR itself turned out to be the wrong tool: when the authors tried fitting SITAR to the Tanner stage data, only the breast-stage model converged at all, and even that one fit 18 BIC units worse than a simpler alternative — every other SITAR attempt on Tanner data simply failed to converge. 但对于坦纳分期和睾丸体积，SITAR本身反而成了一个不合适的工具：作者尝试把SITAR套用到坦纳分期数据上时，只有乳房分期模型能够收敛，而且即便如此，其拟合效果也比一个更简单的替代模型差了18个BIC单位——其他所有SITAR在坦纳分期数据上的尝试都根本无法收敛。 So they built a bespoke mixed-effects logistic model instead, bounded between the stage-1 and stage-5 asymptotes, with random timing and intensity effects analogous to SITAR's, and extended it further for testis volume with two additional random effects for the child and adult asymptotes.
