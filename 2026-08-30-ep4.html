Ep4 — Track C (2/2): Quality of anthropometric data of children under 5 years in the Brazilian National Food and Nutrition Surveillance System (SISVAN), 2008–2020

Full text is in Portuguese; no English translation was available at the source, so this episode is drawn directly from the original. Santos and colleagues analyzed SISVAN, Brazil's national administrative food/nutrition surveillance system, for 23,453,620 children under 5 (cleaned from 141,688,094 raw records, 2008–2020), computing missing-value rates, out-of-equipment-range flags, a digit-preference dissimilarity index, and biologically implausible value (BIV) frequency against WHO 2006 growth-standard cutoffs. Missing data was low (<0.5%), but digit preference for height exceeded 80 (of a 0–90 scale, threshold ≤20) in every state, and BIV frequency was 4.14% for height-for-age nationally — over four times the WHO's 1% ceiling — though both measures improved substantially from 2008 to 2020. Overdispersion in the nutritional indices persisted even after excluding flagged implausible values, worse for height than weight, and some municipalities had near-normal means sitting on top of double the expected standard deviation. This is a concrete, numbers-heavy demonstration of a two-part detection method — per-record BIV flags plus a site-level digit-preference monitor — the second part of which is a genuine evaluation trick: a population-level distributional signature that needs no per-record ground truth.

Plain-English glossary

  • Growth reference / z-score — a child's height or weight expressed as standard deviations (SD) from the median for their age and sex, per a reference population; this study uses the WHO 2006 growth standard (HAZ = height-for-age, WAZ = weight-for-age, BMIZ = BMI-for-age).（中文：将儿童的身高或体重换算为相对于某参考人群同龄同性别中位数的标准差（SD）数值；本研究采用WHO 2006年生长标准（HAZ=年龄别身高，WAZ=年龄别体重，BMIZ=年龄别体质指数）。）
  • Biologically implausible value (BIV) — a value so extreme relative to the growth reference that it is incompatible with life, essentially certain to be a measurement or entry error (here: HAZ outside −6/+6 SD, WAZ outside −6/+5 SD, BMIZ outside −5/+5 SD; WHO guidance recommends BIV frequency <1% in a well-measured sample).（中文：相对生长参考标准极端到与生存不相容的数值，基本可确定为测量或录入错误（本研究中：HAZ超出−6/+6 SD、WAZ超出−6/+5 SD、BMIZ超出−5/+5 SD；WHO建议测量良好样本的BIV频率应低于1%）。）
  • Digit preference / dissimilarity index — a measurement artifact where terminal decimal digits cluster on convenient values (e.g. 0, 5) instead of being uniformly distributed; the dissimilarity index (0–90) measures the percentage of observations that would need to be redistributed to reach uniformity; ≤20 is considered acceptable.（中文：一种测量伪影，末位小数位聚集在便于凑整的数值（如0、5）而非均匀分布；差异指数（0–90）衡量需要重新分配多少比例的观测值才能达到均匀分布；≤20被视为可接受。）
  • Recumbent vs. standing measurement (Frankfurt plane) — WHO protocol measures children under 24 months lying down and 24 months or older standing; a 0.7 cm correction is conventionally applied when a child under 24 months is measured standing instead.（中文：WHO规范要求24个月以下儿童采用卧位测量、24个月及以上采用立位测量；若24个月以下儿童被误以站立姿势测量，惯例上会加回0.7厘米予以校正。）
  • Overdispersion — excess variability (standard deviation) in a distribution beyond what the reference predicts, which can persist even after removing individually flagged implausible values.（中文：分布中超出参考标准预期的多余变异性（标准差），即便在剔除单独标记的不合理值之后仍可能持续存在。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Digit-preference dissimilarity index — a cheap, ground-truth-free, site/period-level signal of measurement-process quality, complementary to per-record outlier detection.
  • BIV cutoffs relative to WHO 2006 growth standard — a population-survey-derived plausibility rule, applied here at administrative-system scale.
  • Municipality-level mean/SD funnel plot vs. survey-derived adequacy bands — a way to spot sites with hidden overdispersion even when the mean looks normal.
  • Strict multi-record identity check — excluding all of a child's records (not just the conflicting one) when birthdate/sex disagree across visits.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Squarely (a) candidate generation, with a strong secondary hit on (d) evaluation. MVP: a lightweight digit-preference monitoring script — compute the terminal-decimal-digit dissimilarity index for height/weight aggregated by site and quarter, flag any site whose index exceeds ~20 or shows a sudden trend reversal. Runs as an orthogonal signal alongside any per-record outlier or mixed-model residual detector. Difficulty: S.

What to verify first

  • Whether BIV cutoffs, the digit-preference threshold of 20, and the SD-adequacy bands (built for population-survey contexts with trained enumerators) transfer validly to data collected during routine clinical care by variably trained staff.
  • That even the WHO's own SD-adequacy reference bands carry some uncertainty (not always from fully representative samples; partly based on the older 1978 NCHS/OMS reference).
  • Whether the clear 2008–2020 improvement in these metrics reflects real measurement-practice change versus underlying data-system changes — the paper doesn't fully disentangle the two.
  • This paper's numbers are about SISVAN, not CFFPR — no assumption is made about what CFFPR's own current measurement quality looks like.

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation (BIV flags) + (d) evaluation (digit-preference index as a ground-truth-free quality signal).

Error types covered / not covered: covers biologically implausible extreme values and rounding/digit-preference artifacts (likely a data-entry/measurement-habit signature, not fabrication, per the authors); does not directly address unit conversion errors, decimal shift, carry-forward errors, or confusion between a data-entry error and a real clinical event — those remain separate error classes this method doesn't disambiguate.

ALEX Today's second Track C paper is "Quality of anthropometric data of children under 5 years in the Brazilian National Food and Nutrition Surveillance System, 2008–2020," by Santos and colleagues, published in Cadernos de Saúde Pública in 2024. 今天Track C的第二篇论文是Santos等人2024年发表在《Cadernos de Saúde Pública》期刊上的《巴西国家食品与营养监测系统（SISVAN）中5岁以下儿童人体测量数据质量研究，2008-2020》。One thing worth flagging up front: the full text is in Portuguese, and there's no English translation at the source — everything tonight is drawn directly from the original. 有一点需要事先说明：全文是葡萄牙语写成的，原文没有提供英文译本——今晚的所有内容都直接来自原始葡语文献。Where ep3 gave us vocabulary, this one gives us numbers: exactly what implausible-value and digit-preference rates look like in a real, national-scale administrative health system. 如果说第三集给了我们词汇，这一集给我们的就是数字：一个真实的、全国规模的行政卫生系统中，不合理值和末位数偏好的比率究竟是什么样子。

JORDAN Set up the growth-reference terms for me first. 先给我讲讲生长参考相关的术语。

ALEX Every child's height and weight gets converted into a z-score relative to a growth reference — how many standard deviations away from the median for their age and sex. 每个孩子的身高和体重都会被换算成相对于某个生长参考标准的z-score——也就是相对于其年龄和性别对应的中位数，偏离了多少个标准差。This study uses the WHO's 2006 growth standard, which is height-for-age, HAZ, weight-for-age, WAZ, and BMI-for-age, BMIZ. 这项研究使用的是世界卫生组织（WHO）2006年的生长标准，计算的是年龄别身高（HAZ）、年龄别体重（WAZ）和年龄别体质指数（BMIZ）。That's a meaningfully different reference than the older NCHS/OMS 1978 standard some prior guidance was built on, and switching references shifts which raw values get excluded as implausible, since the reference itself defines the median and spread everything else is measured against. 这与此前部分指导意见所依据的更早的1978年NCHS/OMS标准存在实质性差异，而更换参考标准会改变哪些原始数值被判定为不合理值并被排除，因为参考标准本身定义了衡量一切的中位数和离散程度。A biologically implausible value, BIV, is a value so extreme relative to that reference that it's incompatible with life — not just unusual, but essentially certain to be a measurement or entry error. 生物学上不合理值（biologically implausible value，BIV）是指相对于该参考标准极端到与生存不相容的数值——不只是不寻常，而是基本可以确定为测量或录入错误。And digit preference is a specific, well-known measurement artifact: when people round measurements to convenient endings — a lot of 0s and 5s — the terminal decimal digit stops being uniformly distributed across 0 through 9, which is itself a signal of measurement sloppiness even before any single value looks implausible. 末位数偏好（digit preference）则是一种具体而众所周知的测量伪影：当人们把测量值凑整到某些方便的结尾——比如大量出现0和5——末位小数位就不再均匀分布在0到9之间，这本身就是测量草率的一个信号，甚至在任何单个数值看起来还不算不合理之前就已经存在。

JORDAN Why look specifically at SISVAN, and why does data quality here matter beyond this one study? 为什么专门研究SISVAN，为什么这里的数据质量问题不仅仅关系到这一项研究本身？

ALEX SISVAN is Brazil's main administrative food and nutrition surveillance system — it compiles anthropometric measurements taken during routine primary care visits across the whole public health system, and its aggregated statistics directly feed national nutrition policy planning and monitoring. SISVAN是巴西主要的行政性食品与营养监测系统——它汇集了整个公共卫生系统中常规初级保健就诊时采集的人体测量数据，其汇总统计数据直接用于国家营养政策的制定与监测。The authors point out two assumptions worth challenging directly: first, that a bigger sample size automatically improves the precision of an estimate, and second, that random measurement errors are neutral because overestimates and underestimates should cancel out. 作者明确指出了两个值得质疑的假设：第一，样本量越大，估计精度就自动越高；第二，随机测量误差是中性的，因为高估和低估应该会相互抵消。Neither holds up well when the errors aren't actually random — measurement error in anthropometry comes from four components: the person being measured, their life stage and body structure; the instrument, whether it's properly calibrated; standardization of the measurement technique itself; and the performance of the professional doing the measuring. 当误差实际上并非随机时，这两个假设都站不住脚——人体测量中的误差来自四个组成部分：被测量者本身，其所处的生命阶段和体型结构；测量仪器，是否经过适当校准；测量技术本身是否标准化；以及负责测量的专业人员的操作水平。Studies of SISVAN's coverage and nutritional trends are common, but studies of its underlying measurement reliability are much rarer — this paper is trying to fill that specific gap, at a scale of over 23 million children. 关于SISVAN覆盖率和营养趋势的研究并不少见，但针对其底层测量可靠性的研究却少得多——这篇论文正是要填补这个具体的空白，而且是在超过2300万儿童这样的规模上进行的。

JORDAN Walk me through how they built the dataset and what they actually measured. 给我讲讲他们是怎么构建数据集的，具体又测量了什么。

ALEX They started from 141,688,094 raw records of children under five, then removed duplicates by municipality, reference year, child ID, and visit date, dropped records where the same child was measured on the same date in two different municipalities, and — this is the strict part — where a child's birthdate or sex disagreed across visits, they excluded every single record for that child rather than trying to guess which one was right. 他们从1.41688094亿条5岁以下儿童的原始记录出发，先按市镇、参照年份、儿童ID和就诊日期去除重复记录，再剔除同一儿童在同一天于两个不同市镇被测量的记录——而这里比较严格的一点是：如果某个儿童的出生日期或性别在不同就诊记录之间不一致，他们会直接剔除该儿童的全部记录，而不是去猜哪一条才是对的。That left 23,453,620 children. 最终留下了2345.362万名儿童。For each child they computed four quality indicators: the missing-value rate for height and weight, recoding a raw value of zero as missing rather than a real zero; an out-of-equipment-range flag for heights above 200 centimeters or weights above 200 kilograms, excluded before anything else was calculated; the digit-preference dissimilarity index, a 0-to-90 scale representing the percentage of observations that would need to be redistributed to reach a uniform terminal-digit distribution, with 20 or below considered acceptable; and the BIV frequency itself, using cutoffs of below negative 6 or above positive 6 standard deviations for HAZ, below negative 6 or above positive 5 for WAZ, and below negative 5 or above positive 5 for BMIZ, with the WHO's own guidance being that a well-measured sample should have a BIV frequency under 1%. 他们为每个儿童计算了四项质量指标：身高和体重的缺失值率，其中原始值为0会被重新编码为缺失而非真实的零值；超出仪器量程标记，针对超过200厘米的身高或超过200公斤的体重，在其他任何计算之前就先予以排除；末位数偏好差异指数，取值范围0到90，代表需要重新分配多少比例的观测值才能达到末位数均匀分布，20及以下被视为可接受；以及BIV频率本身，其判定标准是HAZ低于负6或高于正6个标准差、WAZ低于负6或高于正5个标准差、BMIZ低于负5或高于正5个标准差，而WHO官方指导意见认为，测量良好的样本其BIV频率应低于1%。They also plotted each municipality's mean and standard deviation for HAZ and WAZ against adequacy bands derived from 158 nationally representative nutrition surveys, after excluding municipalities with fewer than 100 children — leaving 5,543 municipalities in that part of the analysis. 他们还把每个市镇的HAZ和WAZ均值与标准差，与基于158项具有全国代表性的营养调查得出的合格区间做了比较，并在此之前剔除了儿童数少于100人的市镇——该部分分析最终保留了5543个市镇。ALEX pushes back here: the digit-preference index and BIV cutoffs, and the SD-adequacy bands they're compared against, were originally developed for population survey contexts with trained enumerators doing dedicated measurement visits, not for routine clinical encounters where anthropometry is one rushed task among many — whether that transfer is fully valid is worth treating as an open question, not a given. 这里ALEX要提出一点质疑：末位数偏好指数、BIV判定标准，以及用来比对的标准差合格区间，最初都是为有专职测量人员进行专门测量走访的人口调查场景所开发的，而不是为常规临床就诊中——人体测量只是众多任务中被匆忙完成的一项——所设计的，这种迁移是否完全站得住脚，值得当作一个开放问题来看待，而不是想当然地接受。

JORDAN What did the numbers actually show? 数字实际显示了什么？

ALEX Missing data was genuinely low — under 0.5% for both height and weight — so completeness isn't the problem here. 缺失数据其实很低——身高和体重都低于0.5%——所以完整性在这里不是问题所在。Digit preference for height was over 80 on that 0-to-90 scale in every single Brazilian state, four times the acceptable threshold of 20, with a strong preference for the terminal digit zero, consistent with rounding rather than fabrication. 身高的末位数偏好指数在0到90的量表上，在巴西每一个州都超过了80，是可接受阈值20的四倍，而且强烈偏好末位数字0，这与凑整而非编造数据的解释是一致的。Weight's digit preference was lower but still above the threshold everywhere, with the Federal District at the low end at 20.91 — and nationally it fell substantially over time, from 50.4 in 2008 to 18.9 in 2020, a real improvement. 体重的末位数偏好指数虽然更低，但在各地仍高于阈值，联邦区（Distrito Federal）以20.91位居最低——而且从全国层面看，这一指数随时间显著下降，从2008年的50.4降到2020年的18.9，是一个真实的改善。BIV frequency for height-for-age was 4.14% nationally, more than four times the WHO's 1% ceiling; weight-for-age BIV was 2.07%. 年龄别身高（HAZ）的BIV频率在全国范围内为4.14%，超过WHO 1%上限的四倍以上；年龄别体重（WAZ）的BIV频率为2.07%。Both improved over the study period — HAZ BIV fell from 4.5% in 2008 to 2.5% in 2020, WAZ from 2.2% to 0.7% — but height was still running well above the 1% recommendation even at the end of the series. 两者在研究期间都有所改善——HAZ的BIV从2008年的4.5%降至2020年的2.5%，WAZ从2.2%降至0.7%——但即便到了序列末期，身高指标仍远高于1%的建议上限。BIV was consistently more common in children under 2, and — this is the part that stands out most — overdispersion, meaning excess variability beyond what the growth reference predicts, persisted even after excluding all the flagged implausible values, and was worse for height than for weight. BIV在2岁以下儿童中始终更常见——而最突出的一点是——即便在剔除了所有被标记的不合理值之后，过度离散（即超出生长参考标准预期的多余变异性）依然存在，并且在身高指标上比体重指标更严重。Some municipalities had a mean close to zero — looking perfectly normal on average — while their standard deviation ran double the expected reference band, meaning an average that looks fine can still be sitting on top of a lot of hidden noise. 有些市镇的均值接近于零——看起来完全正常——但其标准差却达到了预期参考区间的两倍，这说明一个看起来正常的平均值之下，仍可能潜藏着大量隐藏的噪声。

JORDAN You mentioned BIV was worse in younger children and varied by region — any sense of why? 你提到BIV在年龄较小的儿童中更严重，而且因地区而异——有没有讲原因？

ALEX Partly measurement mechanics, partly context. 部分是测量本身的机制问题，部分是背景条件问题。Under the WHO protocol, children younger than 24 months should be measured lying down, recumbent, while children 24 months or older should be measured standing — and in international surveys, if a child under 24 months is measured standing instead, 0.7 centimeters is added back to correct for the difference, since a standing measurement of the same child tends to read shorter. 按照WHO的操作规范，24个月以下的儿童应采用卧位测量身长，而24个月及以上的儿童应采用立位测量身高——在国际调查中，如果一名24个月以下的儿童被误以站立姿势测量，通常会加回0.7厘米予以校正，因为同一儿童以站立姿势测得的数值往往偏短。That protocol switch around the 24-month mark is a plausible mechanical contributor to the age pattern the authors saw. 这个大约在24个月这个节点上的测量姿势切换，是作者观察到的年龄分布模式的一个合理的机制性解释。On the regional side, the Northeast showed the highest BIV frequency for height-for-age, while the South and Southeast showed the lowest, echoing a citation the authors bring in from a separate study in Alagoas state, where 60% of health facilities reportedly had no appropriate dedicated space for anthropometry — measurements were sometimes taken in dressing rooms, vaccination rooms, waiting rooms, even hallways. 在地区方面，东北部地区的年龄别身高（HAZ）BIV频率最高，而南部和东南部最低，这与作者引用的另一项针对阿拉戈阿斯州（Alagoas）的研究结果相呼应，该研究报告称，60%的卫生机构没有适合的专门空间用于人体测量——测量有时是在换药室、接种室、候诊室甚至走廊里进行的。That's exactly the kind of "instrument and environment" component of measurement error mentioned earlier, showing up as a real regional gradient rather than staying an abstract category. 这正是前面提到的测量误差中"仪器和环境"这一组成部分，以真实的地区梯度呈现出来，而不再只是一个抽象的分类。

JORDAN Where does this land on the CFFPR outlier pipeline? 这项研究落在CFFPR异常值检测流程的哪个环节？

ALEX Squarely (a), candidate generation, plus (d), evaluation. 主要落在(a)候选生成环节，同时兼顾(d)评估环节。The BIV cutoffs and the digit-preference index together are, functionally, a two-part detection method — one flags individual extreme records, the other flags entire sites or time periods where the underlying measurement process itself looks suspect, independent of whether any single record crosses a threshold.
