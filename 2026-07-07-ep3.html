Show notes — Track C — CFRD ECFS viewpoints (Snowball, Salem, Wu, Blackman, 2026)

Sourcing flag: ABSTRACT-ONLY. Not open-access, no PMC identifier; sourced from the Europe PMC core abstract. No per-topic numbers or specific loci are claimed beyond the abstract (which names none).

Blurb. A conference viewpoint from the 20th ECFS Basic Science meeting laying out three research directions for cystic-fibrosis-related diabetes (CFRD): (1) genetic and non-genetic risk factors and their potential to sharpen pathophysiology and clinical risk prediction, (2) diet and body composition in clinical management, and (3) importing type-1-diabetes tools — stem-cell-derived islets and organ-on-chip — to model CFRD. CFRD is distinct from type 1 and type 2 diabetes: the dominant lesion is insulin insufficiency from islet loss secondary to exocrine pancreatic damage, though it has overlapping pathophysiology and some overlapping genetic risk with type 2. The organizing genetic idea is a shared-versus-unique split — some CFRD risk overlaps type 2 diabetes (shared pathophysiology), some is CFRD-unique, and some variants act pleiotropically through the CF disease process itself. As a viewpoint it sets an agenda rather than reporting loci, so for the catalog it is hypothesis, not finding. Where it lands: summary-stats colocalization. Buildable artifact: a CFRD shared-versus-unique genetic-architecture quantifier (LDSC genetic correlation + coloc vs type 2 diabetes summary statistics), using public data only.

Plain-English glossary

  • Body composition — the ratio of fat to lean mass (not just total weight); a heritable metabolic-risk trait re-entering CF care in the modulator era.（中文：身体成分，脂肪与瘦体重的比例，可遗传的代谢风险表型，在调节剂时代重回 CF 照护视野。）
  • CFRD — cystic-fibrosis-related diabetes; insulin insufficiency from islet loss secondary to exocrine pancreatic damage, clinically distinct from type 1 and type 2.（中文：CFRD，囊性纤维化相关糖尿病，因外分泌胰腺损伤继发胰岛丢失导致的胰岛素不足，临床上不同于 1 型和 2 型。）
  • Colocalization — tests whether a specific associated locus is driven by the same causal variant in two traits.（中文：共定位，检验某关联位点在两个表型中是否由同一因果变异驱动。）
  • Endocrine vs exocrine pancreas — endocrine = the islets, which secrete insulin; exocrine = enzyme-secreting tissue damaged by CF.（中文：内分泌 vs 外分泌胰腺，内分泌即分泌胰岛素的胰岛，外分泌为分泌消化酶、在 CF 中受损的组织。）
  • Genetic correlation — whether two traits share their genetic influences overall.（中文：遗传相关，两个表型总体上是否共享遗传影响。）
  • GWAS — genome-wide scan for common variants associated with a trait.（中文：GWAS，全基因组关联研究，扫描与表型相关的常见变异。）
  • Heritability — fraction of trait variation attributable to genetic differences in a population.（中文：遗传力，表型变异中可归因于人群遗传差异的比例。）
  • Insulin / islet / beta cell — islets are the endocrine pancreas; beta cells within them secrete insulin, an anabolic hormone driving growth and weight.（中文：胰岛素/胰岛/β 细胞，胰岛是内分泌胰腺，其中的 β 细胞分泌胰岛素，一种驱动生长与体重的合成代谢激素。）
  • LDSC — LD-score regression; estimates heritability and cross-trait genetic correlation from summary statistics without individual genotypes.（中文：LDSC，连锁不平衡分数回归，无需个体基因型即可从汇总统计量估计遗传力与跨表型遗传相关。）
  • Modifier gene — a gene outside CFTR whose variants shift a CF trait (here, CFRD onset/risk).（中文：修饰基因，CFTR 之外、其变异改变某 CF 表型（此处为 CFRD 发生/风险）的基因。）
  • Organ-on-chip — a microfluidic device co-culturing cell types under flow to mimic an organ's microenvironment.（中文：器官芯片，在流体下共培养多种细胞、模拟器官微环境的微流控装置。）
  • Pleiotropy — one variant affecting multiple traits (here, multiple CF manifestations).（中文：多效性，一个变异影响多个表型（此处指多个 CF 表现）。）
  • Polygenic score — a weighted sum of many risk alleles into a single risk number.（中文：多基因评分，把许多风险等位基因加权求和成一个风险数值。）
  • Stem-cell-derived islets — beta-like cells grown from human stem cells to study islet biology in a dish with a defined genetic background.（中文：干细胞来源胰岛，由人干细胞培养出的类 β 细胞，用于在培养皿中以明确遗传背景研究胰岛生物学。）

Full citation

Snowball J, Salem V, Wu M, Blackman SM. Cystic fibrosis related diabetes - viewpoints on three research areas presented at the 20th ECFS basic science conference. Journal of Cystic Fibrosis. 2026. PMID 42156290. Not OA; Europe PMC core abstract only.

Methods / tools to lift

  • Shared-versus-unique framing — partition CFRD genetic risk into shared-with-type-2, CFRD-unique, and pleiotropic-through-CF buckets.
  • LDSC (LD-score regression) — genome-wide genetic correlation from summary statistics, no individual genotypes.
  • Colocalization — per-locus test for a shared causal variant between CFRD and type 2 diabetes.
  • Borrowed type 2 polygenic scores — reuse large existing T2D PGS for the shared component; reserve a CF-specific score for the unique/pleiotropic components.
  • Stem-cell-derived islets / organ-on-chip — adjudicate whether the CFRD islet defect is intrinsic to beta-cell CFTR loss or secondary to exocrine damage (informs which loci a CFRD GWAS should prioritize).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

  • Serves: summary-stats colocalization.
  • MVP (paste-ready): cfrd-shared-vs-unique-quantifier — input public CFRD/glycemic-trait GWAS summary statistics + type 2 diabetes summary statistics; estimate genome-wide genetic correlation with LDSC; run coloc at overlapping loci; output a genetic-correlation estimate + a per-locus shared-vs-unique table. Difficulty: M (LDSC/coloc mature; work is sourcing well-powered CFRD sumstats + ancestry-matched LD reference). No controlled genotype data needed.

What to verify first

  • Power — CFRD GWAS are small (cases are a subset of a rare disease); a null genetic correlation may mean underpowered, not truly unshared.
  • Data availability — confirm usable public CFRD summary statistics exist at scale (a go/no-go before building).
  • LDSC assumptions — ancestry-matched LD reference, reasonably powered traits both sides; coloc's single-causal-variant assumption fails in multi-signal regions.
  • Modulator-era drift — if CFTR modulators are shifting CFRD incidence, phenotypes behind older sumstats may not match today's patients.
  • External-methods analysis on public data only — no registry genotype/data-internal claims.

Gap-map / catalog

  • Closes: a clean three-bucket conceptual frame for CFRD genetic risk (shared-with-T2D / CF-unique / pleiotropic-through-CF) + an explicit, publicly answerable shared-vs-unique test; adds the translational-model angle (stem-cell islets / organ-on-chip) as the intrinsic-vs-secondary adjudicator.
  • Leaves open: the specific CFRD modifier loci (none named in the abstract), variant-level resolution of T2D-shared vs CF-unique, whether the islet defect is intrinsic vs secondary, and CF bone/growth endocrine modifiers.
  • Catalog action: no new genes logged (abstract-only, names no specific loci); TCF7L2 (shared) and SLC26A9 (pleiotropic) referenced from the existing catalog only, as illustrations of the framing — no new citations appended.

ALEX Our Track C paper today is "Cystic fibrosis related diabetes: viewpoints on three research areas presented at the 20th ECFS Basic Science conference," by Snowball, Salem, Wu, and Blackman, in the Journal of Cystic Fibrosis, 2026. 今天 Track C 的论文是 Snowball、Salem、Wu 和 Blackman 于 2026 年发表在《Journal of Cystic Fibrosis》上的《囊性纤维化相关糖尿病：第 20 届 ECFS 基础科学大会上提出的三个研究方向的观点》。 One flag right away: this one is abstract-only for us. 先说明一点：这篇对我们而言只有摘要。 It's not open-access and has no PMC identifier, so I'm working from the Europe PMC core abstract, and I'll claim no per-topic numbers or loci beyond what the abstract states. 它不是开放获取，也没有 PMC 标识符，所以我只依据 Europe PMC 的核心摘要，不会声称任何超出摘要所述的分主题数字或位点。 It's in Track C because it maps the genetic risk architecture of CF-related diabetes, which feeds our modifier catalog. 它属于 Track C，因为它勾画了囊性纤维化相关糖尿病的遗传风险结构，这为我们的修饰基因目录提供素材。

JORDAN Start me at the biology. 从生物学开始讲。 What is CF-related diabetes, and why is it its own category? 什么是囊性纤维化相关糖尿病，它为什么自成一类？

ALEX CF-related diabetes — CFRD — is diabetes that arises in people with cystic fibrosis, and it's clinically distinct from both type 1 and type 2. 囊性纤维化相关糖尿病 (CFRD，CF-related diabetes) 是发生在囊性纤维化患者身上的糖尿病，它在临床上既不同于 1 型也不同于 2 型。 The pancreas has two jobs: an exocrine part that secretes digestive enzymes, and an endocrine part — the islets — that secretes insulin. 胰腺有两项工作：分泌消化酶的外分泌部分，以及分泌胰岛素 (insulin) 的内分泌部分——即胰岛 (islet)。 In CF, thick secretions and progressive fibrosis damage the exocrine pancreas, and that damage spills over onto the islets, so beta-cell mass and insulin secretion fall. 在囊性纤维化中，黏稠分泌物和进行性纤维化损伤外分泌胰腺，而这种损伤波及胰岛，于是 β 细胞数量和胰岛素分泌下降。 So the dominant lesion is insulin insufficiency from islet loss, not the autoimmune beta-cell destruction of type 1 or the insulin resistance that headlines type 2 — although the abstract notes CFRD has overlapping pathophysiology and some overlapping genetic risk with those. 所以主导病变是胰岛丢失导致的胰岛素不足，而不是 1 型的自身免疫性 β 细胞破坏，也不是 2 型以之为主角的胰岛素抵抗——尽管摘要指出 CFRD 与它们存在重叠的病理生理和部分重叠的遗传风险。

JORDAN And the genetics vocabulary — modifier gene, heritability, the rest? 那遗传学的词汇——修饰基因、遗传力，等等？

ALEX A modifier gene is a gene outside CFTR whose variants shift a CF trait — here, whether and when someone develops CFRD. 修饰基因 (modifier gene) 是 CFTR 之外的基因，其变异会改变某个 CF 表型——这里指的是某人是否以及何时患上 CFRD。 Heritability is the fraction of trait variation attributable to genetic differences in a population. 遗传力 (heritability) 是某表型的变异中可归因于人群遗传差异的比例。 A GWAS scans the genome for common variants associated with a trait. GWAS（全基因组关联研究）在全基因组范围内扫描与某表型相关的常见变异。 Two more that matter for today: genetic correlation asks whether two traits share the same genetic influences overall, and colocalization asks whether a specific associated locus is driven by the same causal variant in two traits. 还有两个今天很关键：遗传相关 (genetic correlation) 问的是两个表型总体上是否共享相同的遗传影响，而共定位 (colocalization) 问的是某个特定关联位点在两个表型中是否由同一个因果变异驱动。 And pleiotropy is when one variant affects multiple traits. 而多效性 (pleiotropy) 指一个变异影响多个表型。

JORDAN Why write a viewpoint on this now? 为什么现在写这样一篇观点文章？

JORDAN Why does CFRD get its own screening and its own worry, clinically? 从临床上看，CFRD 为什么要有自己的筛查、自己的担忧？ ALEX: Because it tracks with worse outcomes in CF specifically. CFRD 在 CF 中与更差的结局相关。 Insulin is anabolic — it drives growth and helps maintain weight — so insulin insufficiency in CF hits nutrition and lung function, not just blood sugar. 胰岛素是合成代谢性的——它驱动生长、帮助维持体重——所以 CF 中的胰岛素不足打击的是营养和肺功能，而不只是血糖。 A decline in weight or lung function can precede the formal diabetes diagnosis by years, which is why guidelines push annual screening with an oral glucose tolerance test from age ten. 体重或肺功能的下降可能比正式的糖尿病诊断早数年出现，这正是指南推动从十岁起用口服葡萄糖耐量试验 (OGTT，oral glucose tolerance test) 每年筛查的原因。 So better genetic risk prediction — topic one's promise — would let you watch the high-risk patients more closely before glucose ever drifts. 所以更好的遗传风险预测——主题一的许诺——能让你在血糖尚未漂移之前，就更密切地盯住高风险患者。

ALEX Because the clinical pressure is rising. 因为临床压力正在上升。 The abstract notes CFRD prevalence is expected to grow as the CF population ages — people are living long enough to develop it. 摘要指出，随着 CF 人群老龄化，CFRD 患病率预计将上升——患者活得足够久，久到会患上它。 And there's a live question about the highly effective CFTR modulators — the drugs that rescue CFTR protein function. 还有一个悬而未决的问题，关于高效 CFTR 调节剂 (highly effective CFTR modulators)——那些挽救 CFTR 蛋白功能的药物。 The abstract says their impact on CFRD is unknown; it's possible that sufficiently early treatment could delay or prevent CFRD, but studies so far show limited change in incidence and complexity in the post-modulator era. 摘要说它们对 CFRD 的影响尚不清楚；有可能足够早的治疗能延缓或预防 CFRD，但迄今研究显示，在"后调节剂时代"其发病率和复杂性变化有限。 So the authors argue for accelerating research with direct patient benefit, and they organize it around three emerging topics from the conference. 因此作者主张加速能直接惠及患者的研究，并把它组织成大会上的三个新兴主题。

JORDAN What are the three, as the abstract frames them? 按摘要的说法，这三个是什么？

ALEX Topic one: genetic and non-genetic risk factors for CFRD, and their potential to inform pathophysiology and improve clinical risk prediction. 主题一：CFRD 的遗传与非遗传风险因素，及其在阐明病理生理、改善临床风险预测方面的潜力。 Topic two: the role of optimizing dietary intake and body composition in clinical management. 主题二：优化膳食摄入和身体成分 (body composition) 在临床管理中的作用。 Topic three: importing advances from type 1 diabetes research — specifically stem cells and organ-on-chip modeling — into CFRD. 主题三：把 1 型糖尿病研究的进展——具体是干细胞和器官芯片 (organ-on-chip) 建模——引入 CFRD。 For our modifier catalog, topic one is the payload; topics two and three are context. 对我们的修饰基因目录而言，主题一是核心载荷；主题二和三是背景。

JORDAN Dig into topic one — what's the genetic structure they're pointing at? 深入主题一——他们指向的遗传结构是什么？

ALEX The organizing idea from the abstract is a shared-versus-unique split. 摘要给出的组织性思路是"共享 vs 独有"的二分。 Some CFRD genetic risk factors overlap with type 2 diabetes, implying shared pathophysiology. 一些 CFRD 遗传风险因素与 2 型糖尿病重叠，暗示共享的病理生理。 Some appear CFRD-unique. 一些则似乎是 CFRD 独有的。 And some influence multiple CF manifestations at once, which the abstract reads as those variants acting through the CF disease process itself rather than through a generic diabetes pathway. 还有一些同时影响多个 CF 表型，摘要将其解读为这些变异是通过 CF 疾病过程本身、而非通过通用的糖尿病通路起作用。 That third bucket is the pleiotropic one, and it's the most CF-specific and the most interesting. 第三类就是多效性的那一类，它最具 CF 特异性，也最有意思。 The non-genetic side — topic two — folds in body composition and diet as modifiable risk, which matters because it means CFRD risk isn't purely genetic destiny. 非遗传的一面——主题二——把身体成分和饮食纳入作为可干预的风险，这很重要，因为它意味着 CFRD 风险并非纯粹的遗传宿命。

JORDAN Push back on the paper for me. 帮我质疑一下这篇论文。

TCF7L2 是教科书式的"共享"类——它是最强的常见 2 型糖尿病位点，而它也与 CFRD 相关，这正是"与 2 型共享"的样子。 SLC26A9 sits in the pleiotropic bucket — in our catalog it turns up across meconium ileus, CFRD, and modulator response, which is the signature of a variant acting through the CF process rather than a generic diabetes pathway. JORDAN Why does that fork matter for the genetics side? 这个岔路为什么对遗传学那一面重要？ ALEX: Because it tells you where to expect the modifier signal to live. 因为它告诉你该在哪里预期修饰基因信号的存在。 If the islet defect is intrinsic to CFTR loss in the beta cell, modifiers of CFTR itself and of beta-cell biology should dominate. 如果胰岛缺陷是 β 细胞内 CFTR 丢失所固有的，那么 CFTR 本身及 β 细胞生物学的修饰基因应占主导。 If it's secondary to exocrine destruction, then modifiers of pancreatic fibrosis and inflammation move to the foreground instead. 如果它继发于外分泌破坏，那么胰腺纤维化和炎症的修饰基因就会转到前台。 The stem-cell and chip models can adjudicate that, and the answer reshapes which loci a CFRD GWAS should even prioritize. 干细胞和芯片模型能裁决这一点，而答案会重塑一项 CFRD GWAS 究竟该优先关注哪些位点。
