Blurb

Sarafoglou and colleagues (2026, Molecular Genetics and Metabolism) build disease-specific height, weight, and BMI-for-age growth charts for PMM2-CDG, a rare congenital disorder of glycosylation in which 96% of children show growth faltering — a rate so high that general-population CDC reference charts systematically mislabel real, disease-driven short stature and low weight as anomalous. Using pooled de-identified longitudinal data from four sources (156 children, 1,614 visits, median follow-up 6.8-8.5 years), the team fit semi-parametric (LMS-family) disease-specific charts and ran one-sided quantile nodal-point tests against the CDC reference at fixed ages. At age 20, PMM2-CDG females were 13 cm shorter than CDC-reference peers (150 vs 163 cm) and males 16 cm shorter (160 vs 176 cm), with every tested nodal point (ages 4, 8, 12, 16, 20) significantly different at p<0.05. PMM2-CDG is not CF; the transfer to the CF/modulator-era growth-reference-mismatch problem is this podcast's own framing, landing as pipeline stage (e): vocabulary/framework rather than a direct pipeline change, built around a proposed CF-modulator-era reference-shift literature scan.

Plain-English glossary

  • CDC vs. WHO growth charts — Two general-population paediatric reference chart families built from different reference samples and age ranges (WHO: global breastfed infants, birth-2y; CDC: US sample, 2y+); using the wrong one for a population or age is a known source of spurious implausible-value flags.（中文：两套通用人群儿科参考生长曲线图，分别基于不同的参考样本和年龄范围；用错年龄段或用错人群会导致虚假的"不合理值"标记。）
  • Disease-specific growth chart — A growth chart built using the same LMS statistical machinery as general-population charts, but fit to a reference sample of children who actually have the disease in question.（中文：疾病特异性生长曲线图，采用与通用人群曲线图相同的LMS统计方法，但拟合对象是真正患有该疾病的儿童样本。）
  • LMS method — The standard technique behind most growth charts: three smoothly age-varying curves (L=skewness, M=median, S=coefficient of variation) that convert any raw measurement into a z-score.（中文：大多数生长曲线图背后的标准方法：用三条随年龄平滑变化的曲线（L=偏度、M=中位数、S=变异系数）把任意原始测量值换算成z分数。）
  • Nodal-point analysis — Formally comparing two growth distributions at fixed ages (here 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 years) rather than across the whole continuous curve.（中文：节点分析，在固定年龄点上比较两个生长分布，而不是在整条连续曲线上比较。）
  • One-sided quantile test — A directional hypothesis test (here: does the disease population sit below the reference at a given percentile) rather than a two-sided test of whether two distributions merely differ.（中文：单侧分位数检验，检验一个有方向性的假设——例如疾病人群是否在某百分位上低于参考标准——而不是双侧检验两个分布是否存在任何差异。）
  • Z-score (in growth-chart context) — A standardized measure of how many population standard deviations a measurement sits from the median for that exact age and sex.（中文：z分数，衡量某一测量值相对于该年龄和性别中位数的标准化偏离量，以人群标准差为单位。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • The core reframe: a flagged "implausible" value may indicate the wrong reference population, not a data error — check the reference before trusting the flag.
  • Semi-parametric (LMS-family) disease-specific chart fitting as the general pattern for any rare-disease or treatment-era population whose growth shape genuinely differs from the general population.
  • One-sided quantile nodal-point testing at fixed ages as a lightweight way to formally quantify a disease population's divergence from a reference, without needing a full new chart if only a directional comparison is needed.
  • Multi-source data pooling (four organizations here) as the necessary strategy for any rare-population reference chart that needs sample size no single site can provide alone.
