Episode blurb

Abstract-only episode (no PMCID, closed access). Van Camp, Mahdi, Liu, Ni, Spooner, and Wu (Studies in Health Technology and Informatics, 2019) describe the design of a visual annotation tool, built using Agile development, that lets clinicians rapidly flag weight errors by looking at a child's weight plotted as a trajectory over time rather than judging bare numbers in isolation — the design premise is that a genuine error visually "breaks" the pattern the rest of a child's own trajectory establishes. The stated motivation is collecting labeled training data for a future supervised weight-error-detection algorithm; weight-based pediatric dosing makes incorrect EHR weight values a real patient-safety concern, and manual chart review doesn't scale. The abstract reports only qualitative, preliminary results — positive user feedback, framed as preparation for a formal evaluation still to come — with no quantitative performance figures available. Buildable artifact: a generic, lightweight visual trajectory-annotation app for rapidly collecting labeled examples for any anthropometric candidate-generation method, serving the Liz/CFFPR data-quality priority (method only).

Plain-English glossary

CDC growth charts — US pediatric growth reference curves built from a cross-sectional population sample, typically used for children roughly age two and older.（中文：基于横断面人群抽样构建的美国儿科生长参考曲线，通常用于约两岁及以上的儿童。）

LMS method (Lambda-Mu-Sigma) — Cole's statistical technique for building smooth percentile/z-score growth curves from skewed raw measurement distributions, using three age-varying parameters: skewness (L), median (M), and coefficient of variation (S).（中文：Cole提出的统计方法，用三个随年龄变化的参数——偏度L、中位数M和变异系数S——把偏态的原始测量值分布转换成平滑的百分位数/z分数生长曲线。）

Weak supervision — a machine-learning training approach that combines several noisy or imperfect labeling signals instead of relying on one clean, fully-reviewed label set.（中文：一种机器学习训练方法，组合多个带噪声或不完美的标注信号，而不是依赖单一干净、经过完整审查的标注集。）

WHO growth standards — international pediatric growth reference curves built from healthy, breastfed infants across multiple countries, typically used for children under age two.（中文：基于多国健康、母乳喂养婴儿样本构建的国际儿科生长参考曲线，通常用于两岁以下的儿童。）

Z-score (growth context) — a child's measurement expressed as standard deviations from the age- and sex-matched reference median, computed via the LMS method.（中文：通过LMS法计算得出的，表示孩子的测量值相对于同年龄同性别参考中位数偏离多少个标准差的分数。）
