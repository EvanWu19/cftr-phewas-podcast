Genetic background shapes AI-predicted variant effects

Nayar G, et al. bioRxiv (preprint), 2026.

Blurb

This preprint introduces pVEP (personalized variant effect predictor), a framework that reruns standard deep-learning variant-effect predictors — ESM for protein fitness, SpliceAI for splicing, Flashzoi for regulatory DNA — not just against the single human reference genome, but across thousands of realistic personal haplotypes drawn from the 1000 Genomes Project and Human Genome Diversity Project. Across ClinVar missense, splice-altering, and UTR variants, more than 95% of clinical variants showed multimodal (not single-peaked) score distributions across backgrounds, meaning a given variant's predicted effect can look pathogenic on one haplotype and benign on another — most dramatically for VHL 121C>F, which ranged from benign (reference score) to the single most pathogenic-looking prediction in the dataset (one African-ancestry haplotype). A BRCA1 sensitization analysis found background-dependent interactions concentrated up to 351-fold at AlphaFold2-predicted structural contacts, including a previously unreported variant pair. CFTR wasn't studied here, but the implication transfers directly to any off-the-shelf VEP score run on a CFTR VUS: it's a reference-genome-only estimate, not necessarily representative for patients whose background differs from the reference. Buildable artifact: a CFTR haplotype-sensitivity checker that reruns a public VEP model across sampled population haplotypes for a variant of interest and reports the resulting score range.

Plain-English glossary

  • Haplotype — a contiguous stretch of DNA or protein sequence inherited together on one chromosome copy; two people's genomes differ by roughly 4–5 million variants, so the sequence context around any given variant differs by carrier.（中文:同一条染色体拷贝上一起遗传下来的一段连续DNA或蛋白质序列;任意两人的基因组约有400万到500万个变异位点的差异,因此任何变异周围的序列背景在不同携带者之间都不同。）
  • VEP (variant effect predictor) — a computational model (often deep learning) that scores how damaging a genetic variant is, typically by comparing predicted molecular function with vs. without the variant introduced into a reference sequence.（中文:一种计算模型(通常基于深度学习),通过比较在参考序列中引入变异前后的预测分子功能差异,来评估该变异的致病性。）
  • ESM — a protein language model (Evolutionary Scale Modeling) trained on protein sequences the way large language models are trained on text, learning which amino acid patterns look evolutionarily plausible.（中文:一种蛋白质语言模型(Evolutionary Scale Modeling),训练方式类似大语言模型学习文本,学习哪些氨基酸组合在进化上看起来合理。）
  • SpliceAI — a deep neural network that predicts splice-site usage (donor/acceptor probability at each position) from DNA sequence, and how a variant shifts it.（中文:一种深度神经网络,根据DNA序列预测每个位置作为剪接供体/受体的概率,以及变异如何改变这一概率。）
  • Flashzoi / Borzoi — Flashzoi is an efficient version of Borzoi, a model predicting regulatory activity (transcription, chromatin accessibility) from noncoding DNA sequence; used here to score UTR variants.（中文:Flashzoi是Borzoi的高效版本,Borzoi是一种从非编码DNA序列预测调控活性(转录、染色质可及性)的模型,本文用它给UTR变异打分。）
  • pLDDT — AlphaFold2's per-residue confidence score for its predicted structure, used as a check that background-dependent structural differences weren't just prediction noise.（中文:AlphaFold2对其预测结构的逐残基置信度评分,本文用它检验背景依赖的结构差异是否只是预测噪音。）
  • Non-additive interaction / epistasis (structural) — when two variants on the same haplotype combine to produce an effect on predicted pathogenicity that isn't simply the sum of their individual effects.（中文:同一单倍型上的两个变异组合后,对预测致病性产生的效应并非二者单独效应的简单相加。）
  • UTR (untranslated region) — the non-protein-coding 5' or 3' ends of a gene's mRNA, which can still carry regulatory function affecting how much protein gets made.（中文:基因mRNA两端不编码蛋白质的区域(5'或3'),但仍可能具有影响蛋白质产量的调控功能。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • pVEP framework: rerun any reference-based VEP model across sampled personal haplotypes rather than the reference alone.
  • Variant-sensitization analysis: interpretable linear model quantifying how strongly a background variant shifts a clinical variant's predicted effect.
  • Gaussian mixture modeling to detect multimodal (background-dependent) vs. unimodal score distributions.
  • Population-averaged VEP scoring as a more representative alternative to single reference-based scoring.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation. Buildable: a CFTR haplotype-sensitivity checker — for a CFTR variant of interest, sample phased population haplotypes (gnomAD/1000 Genomes) spanning the local region, rerun a public VEP (AlphaMissense/SpliceAI) on the variant embedded in each haplotype instead of just the reference, and report the score range plus any haplotypes crossing a clinically meaningful threshold. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • No population-scale experimental ground truth exists yet to confirm which background-dependent predicted effect is molecularly/clinically correct for a given haplotype — this paper describes model behavior, not confirmed biology.
  • Strongest evidence here is for protein (ESM) and splicing (SpliceAI) predictions specifically; newer models (Evo 2, AlphaGenome) were not evaluated.
  • Whether phased, ancestrally diverse haplotype data exists at sufficient density specifically around the CFTR locus.
  • This paper says nothing about CFFPR's own genotype data structure or curation practices — that remains a separate, internal question.

ALEX Today we're reading "Genetic background shapes AI-predicted variant effects," a 2026 bioRxiv preprint from Nayar and colleagues. 今天我们要读的是奈亚尔(Nayar)等人2026年发表在bioRxiv上的预印本论文,题目是《遗传背景如何影响AI预测的变异效应》。 It's Track A, our variant-interpretation lane, and it's not about any one disease gene — it's about the tool we increasingly lean on to interpret every gene, including CFTR: the deep-learning variant effect predictor, or VEP. 这篇属于Track A,也就是我们的变异解读赛道,内容不针对某一个特定疾病基因——而是关于我们越来越依赖的、用来解读包括CFTR在内所有基因变异的工具:深度学习变异效应预测器,简称VEP。 Good news on access: this one is fully open, so everything below is drawn from the actual results, not an abstract. 访问方面是个好消息:这篇论文是完全开放获取的,所以下面的内容都来自论文的实际结果,而不是仅凭摘要推测。

JORDAN Let's start with the basic object here — what is a "haplotype," and why does it matter for a variant score? 我们先从一个基本概念说起——什么是"单倍型"(haplotype),它为什么会影响变异评分?

ALEX A haplotype is a specific, contiguous stretch of DNA or protein sequence inherited together on one chromosome copy. 单倍型是指在同一条染色体拷贝上、一起遗传下来的一段连续DNA或蛋白质序列。 Any two people's genomes differ by roughly four to five million variants, so the actual sequence context surrounding any given clinical variant — the other nearby variants riding along on the same chromosome — is different in almost every person who carries it. 任意两个人的基因组之间大约有四到五百万个变异位点的差异,所以围绕任何一个临床变异的实际序列背景——也就是同一条染色体上携带的其他邻近变异——在几乎每一个携带者身上都是不同的。 A VEP score, though, is almost always computed against a single human reference genome: you take the variant, drop it into the reference sequence, and ask the model how much worse the mutant looks than the unmodified reference. 但一个VEP评分几乎总是只针对单一的人类参考基因组来计算:把变异放进参考序列里,然后问模型突变体比未改动的参考序列"差"多少。 That implicitly assumes the reference is a representative stand-in for everyone's genome, which this paper sets out to test directly. 这就隐含地假设参考基因组能够代表所有人的基因组,而这篇论文正是要直接检验这个假设是否成立。

JORDAN And the three model types they used — ESM, SpliceAI, Flashzoi? 他们用的三种模型——ESM、SpliceAI和Flashzoi——分别是什么?

ALEX ESM is a protein language model — trained the way a large language model learns English, but on protein sequences, so it learns which amino acids tend to appear together in functional proteins and flags substitutions that look evolutionarily implausible. ESM是一种蛋白质语言模型——它的训练方式和大语言模型学习英语类似,只不过学习对象是蛋白质序列,因此它学会了哪些氨基酸倾向于在功能性蛋白质中共同出现,并能标记出在进化上看起来不合理的氨基酸替换。 SpliceAI is a deep neural network trained specifically to predict splice-site usage from DNA sequence — given a stretch of exon and intron, it scores how likely each position is to be used as a splice donor or acceptor, and how a variant shifts that. SpliceAI是一个专门训练用来从DNA序列预测剪接位点使用情况的深度神经网络——给定一段外显子和内含子序列,它会为每个位置打分,预测其被用作剪接供体或受体的可能性,以及某个变异会如何改变这一可能性。 Flashzoi is an efficient version of Borzoi, a model that predicts regulatory activity — things like transcription and chromatin accessibility — from a long stretch of noncoding DNA sequence, useful here for scoring variants in untranslated regions, or UTRs. Flashzoi是Borzoi的高效版本,Borzoi是一种从一长段非编码DNA序列预测调控活性——比如转录水平和染色质可及性——的模型,在这里被用来给非翻译区(UTR)中的变异打分。 All three follow the same reference-versus-mutant scoring logic; the paper's contribution is running that scoring across thousands of different genetic backgrounds instead of just the one reference. 这三个模型都遵循同样的"参考序列对突变序列"打分逻辑;这篇论文的贡献在于,把这套打分方法应用到成千上万个不同的遗传背景上,而不仅仅是那一份参考序列。

ALEX So here's why this study exists. 这项研究的出发点是这样的。 Over half of all ClinVar entries are still variants of uncertain significance or carry conflicting interpretations, and even confidently-labeled pathogenic variants often show incomplete penetrance in large population studies — carriers who, for reasons not fully understood, don't get the disease. ClinVar数据库中超过一半的条目仍然是意义未明的变异,或者带有相互矛盾的解读,而即使是被自信标注为致病的变异,在大型人群研究中也常常表现出外显不全——也就是说,携带者出于目前尚未完全理解的原因并未发病。 The authors' hypothesis is that part of this mess traces back to a shared blind spot in essentially every VEP: they score a variant against one reference sequence and implicitly treat that score as if it generalizes to everyone, when each person's own genetic background could be quietly amplifying or damping the same variant's real effect. 作者的假设是,这种混乱局面部分要归咎于几乎所有VEP共有的一个盲点:它们只针对一份参考序列给变异打分,并隐含地认为这个分数适用于所有人,而实际上每个人自身的遗传背景都可能在悄悄放大或削弱同一个变异的真实效应。 To test this, they built the personalized variant effect predictor framework, pVEP, which reconstructs realistic personal haplotypes from population sequencing data and reruns the same VEP models across each one. 为了检验这一点,他们构建了"个体化变异效应预测器"框架,简称pVEP,该框架从人群测序数据中重建出真实的个体单倍型,并在每一个单倍型背景下重新运行同样的VEP模型。

JORDAN How diverse was the background data they actually used? 他们实际使用的背景数据有多大的多样性?

ALEX They assembled personalized haplotypes from roughly 3,800 to 3,900 genomes spanning the 1000 Genomes Project and the Human Genome Diversity Project — two of the standard population-sequencing resources that sample globally diverse ancestries, not just European. 他们从1000人基因组计划和人类基因组多样性计划中大约3800到3900个基因组里,重建出了个体化单倍型——这两个资源都是标准的人群测序数据来源,取样覆盖全球多样化的族群,而不仅仅是欧洲人群。 At the protein level alone, that yielded 132,721 distinct haplotypes across 2,847 transcripts; 59% differed from the reference by a single variant, and haplotype diversity was highest in African-ancestry genomes — 27% of protein haplotypes were unique to a single genome there, versus 17% in East Asian and 11% in European genomes, which tracks with the well-established fact that African populations carry the deepest and most diverse branch of human genetic variation. 仅在蛋白质层面,这就产生了跨2847个转录本的132,721个不同单倍型;其中59%与参考序列只相差一个变异,而单倍型多样性在非洲血统基因组中最高——在那里27%的蛋白质单倍型是某个基因组独有的,相比之下东亚血统为17%,欧洲血统为11%,这与"非洲人群携带着人类遗传变异中最深、最多样的分支"这一已被充分证实的事实相吻合。 They then applied pVEP to three classes of real clinical variants: 62,727 ClinVar missense variants via the ProteinGym benchmark, 8,490 splice-altering variants from SpliceVarDB, and 13,771 ClinVar UTR variants — introducing each one into every compatible personal haplotype and comparing the resulting score distribution to the single reference-based score. 随后他们把pVEP应用到三类真实的临床变异上:通过ProteinGym基准集获得的62,727个ClinVar错义变异、来自SpliceVarDB的8,490个剪接改变变异,以及13,771个ClinVar UTR变异——将每一个变异分别引入所有与之兼容的个体单倍型,并将由此得到的评分分布与单一的参考序列评分进行比较。

ALEX I'd flag one design choice here worth pushing on. 这里有一个设计选择,我觉得值得追问一下。 This is explicitly framed by the authors themselves as a proof of concept — a few thousand genomes is a real but still limited sample of global human diversity, and the paper has no independent experimental ground truth confirming which of the many background-dependent scores is actually correct for any given haplotype. 作者自己明确地把这项研究定位为概念验证——几千个基因组虽然是真实数据,但相对于全球人类多样性而言样本量仍然有限,而且这篇论文并没有独立的实验金标准,能够确认对于某个具体单倍型来说,众多背景依赖评分中到底哪一个才是"正确"的。 So everything downstream is a statement about model behavior — how much these AI predictors' outputs move when you change the genetic context — not yet a validated statement about biological truth. 所以后面所有的结果都只是在描述模型行为——也就是改变遗传背景后,这些AI预测器的输出会发生多大变化——而还不能算是经过验证的生物学事实陈述。

JORDAN So does the score actually move much in practice? 那么在实际操作中,评分到底会有多大变化?

ALEX A lot, and in a way that's hard to write off as noise. 变化很大,而且很难简单归结为噪音。 Across thousands of clinical missense variants observed in at least 20 different haplotypes, only about 5% of the resulting score distributions were a simple, single-peaked normal curve — fitting a mixture model showed more than 95% of variants had two or more distinct peaks, meaning the "typical" background-dependent variant doesn't have one true effect size, it has several depending on what else is riding along on that chromosome. 在至少观察到20种不同单倍型的成千上万个临床错义变异中,只有大约5%的评分分布呈现简单的单峰正态曲线——用混合模型拟合后发现,超过95%的变异都存在两个或更多明显的峰值,这意味着一个"典型的"背景依赖变异并没有单一的真实效应大小,而是根据同一条染色体上携带的其他变异,呈现出多种不同的效应。 The clearest single example is VHL 121C>F, a ClinVar-pathogenic variant in the Von Hippel-Lindau disease gene: across different haplotypes its predicted effect ranged from clearly pathogenic to essentially benign, and one African-ancestry haplotype produced the single most pathogenic-looking prediction in the whole dataset — while the reference-genome-only score alone would have called that same variant benign. 最清晰的一个例子是VHL基因中的121C>F变异,这是ClinVar标注为致病的、位于希佩尔-林道病(Von Hippel-Lindau disease)基因中的变异:在不同单倍型背景下,其预测效应从明显致病一直到基本良性都有分布,其中一个非洲血统单倍型给出了整个数据集中致病性看起来最强的预测结果——而如果只用参考基因组打分,这同一个变异反而会被判定为良性。

ALEX The mechanistic dissection is where it gets genuinely interesting for interpretation work. 从解读工作的角度来看,真正有意思的是他们对机制的拆解分析。 Using BRCA1 as a model system, they fit an interpretable linear model — what they call a variant-sensitization analysis — quantifying how strongly each background variant shifts the predicted effect of each clinical variant, across 132 background variants and 456 clinical variants in 144 haplotypes; the fit explained 83% of the variance. 他们以BRCA1作为模型系统,拟合了一个可解释的线性模型——称为"变异敏化分析"——用来量化每一个背景变异对每一个临床变异预测效应的影响强度,分析覆盖了144种单倍型中的132个背景变异和456个临床变异;该模型解释了83%的方差。 About 1% of background-clinical variant pairs showed statistically significant non-additive interaction, and those interactions were dramatically concentrated — up to 351-fold enriched — at residue pairs AlphaFold2 predicts to be in direct three-dimensional contact, especially within BRCA1's RING and BRCT domains, exactly where most pathogenic BRCA1 variants cluster. 大约1%的背景变异-临床变异组合表现出统计学显著的非加性交互作用,而这些交互作用高度集中——富集程度最高可达351倍——出现在AlphaFold2预测为直接三维空间接触的残基对上,尤其集中在BRCA1的RING结构域和BRCT结构域内,而这恰好是大多数致病性BRCA1变异聚集的区域。 They report one previously undescribed pairwise interaction: a BRCT substitution, 1729L>Q, is benign on its own but synergistically amplifies the pathogenicity of a separate breast-cancer-risk variant, 1722S>F, when both sit on the same haplotype. 他们报告了一个此前未被描述过的成对交互作用:一个BRCT结构域的替换1729L>Q单独存在时是良性的,但当它与另一个乳腺癌风险变异1722S>F同时出现在同一单倍型上时,会协同放大后者的致病性。 Parallel analysis in TP53 found the same general pattern — pathogenic variants show stronger background sensitivity than benign ones, and interactions cluster at structural contacts — with about 2.3% of pairs showing significant non-additivity there.在TP53基因中进行的平行分析发现了同样的总体模式——致病性变异比良性变异表现出更强的背景敏感性,交互作用也同样集中在结构接触位点——那里约2.3%的变异对表现出显著的非加性效应。

ALEX Yes, with one finding I think is the single most important one in the paper for how we think about splice-variant scoring specifically. 是的,而且其中有一个发现,我认为是这篇论文里对我们思考剪接变异评分方式最重要的一条。 Across roughly JORDAN So where does this land for the work we're actually doing? 那这对我们实际在做的工作意味着什么?

ALEX One priority: Karen's CFTR variant-interpretation work. 对应一个优先事项:凯伦(Karen)的CFTR变异解读工作。 CFTR itself wasn't one of the genes studied here — BRCA1, TP53, and VHL were the worked examples — but the reference. 这篇论文本身研究的基因并不包括CFTR——BRCA1、TP53和VHL是他们的工作案例——但其含义可以直接迁移过来:目前CFTR2分类以及任何基于预测器的VUS初筛工作中,对CFTR变异运行的AlphaMissense、SpliceAI或CADD评分,本质上都是仅基于参考基因组的评分,而这篇论文的核心发现是,这类评分可能存在系统性的不具代表性问题,对于那些遗传背景与参考基因组差异最大的人群尤其如此。
