Ep4 (Track C — registry QC, 2 of 2): Optimal design for longitudinal studies to estimate pubertal height growth in individuals

Tim Cole (2018, Annals of Human Biology) — the creator of the SITAR growth-curve model — asks a study-design question with real data instead of simulation: how infrequently can you measure height during puberty and still get a trustworthy SITAR estimate of pubertal timing and intensity? Using a purpose-built timegap algorithm to extract evenly-spaced sub-sets from one enormous dataset (3,172 boys, 128,679 measurements, Christ's Hospital School, 1939-1968), he fit separate SITAR models at 2, 3, 4, 6, 12, and 24-month intervals. Models from 2 to 12 months were essentially indistinguishable (residual SD 0.74-0.80cm, random-effect correlations >0.9 for size/timing); the 2-year model fit with only slightly worse precision (residual SD 0.92cm) and needed a median of just 4 measurements per boy — but models with only 2-3 measurements per boy were unreliable or failed to converge entirely. For the Liz assignment, this establishes a concrete visit-density floor (roughly 4+ reasonably-spaced measurements) below which a SITAR-based flagger's per-individual residual shouldn't be trusted as a real signal. The buildable artifact: a Visit-Density Confidence Gate.

Plain-English glossary

  • Borrowing strength — a mixed-effects model's ability to stabilize an individual's sparse-data estimate by pooling information from the whole cohort's shared curve shape, rather than relying on that individual's data in isolation.（中文：混合效应模型利用整个队列共享的曲线形状来稳定某个数据稀疏个体的估计，而不是孤立地只依赖该个体自身数据的能力。）
  • Degrees of freedom (spline curve) — the number of free parameters controlling a spline's flexibility; fewer degrees of freedom means a simpler, less wiggly mean curve.（中文：控制样条曲线灵活度的自由参数数量；自由度越少，平均曲线越简单、波动越少。）
  • SITAR random effects (size, timing, intensity) — the three subject-specific parameters SITAR estimates per person: size shifts height up/down, timing shifts the growth spurt earlier/later in age, intensity stretches/compresses the age axis to change the spurt's steepness.（中文：SITAR为每个人估计的三个个体专属参数——大小让身高上下移动，时相让生长突增在年龄上提前或推后，强度拉伸或压缩年龄轴以改变突增的陡度。）
  • timegap algorithm — a purpose-built function (in the R sitar package) that extracts evenly time-spaced sub-sets of measurements per individual from a denser dataset, at a specified target interval and tolerance.（中文：一个专门编写的函数（在R的sitar包中），按指定的目标间隔和容差，从更密集的数据集中为每个个体抽取出时间间隔均匀的测量子集。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • timegap — a reusable technique for simulating sparser visit schedules from a dense dataset to test model robustness, applicable to any longitudinal design question, not just height.

Error types covered / not covered: Not applicable in the usual sense — this is a study-design/precision paper, not a per-record error detector; it says nothing about which error types (unit conversion, transcription, carry-forward, real clinical events) a SITAR-based flagger built on top of its findings would or wouldn't catch.

What to verify first

  • This is an all-boys, single historical UK cohort measured 50-80 years ago; the exact "4 measurements" floor would need re-deriving, not assumed, for a contemporary, demographically mixed population — girls' pubertal timing differs on average by about 2 years.
  • This paper's reliability metric is about accurate timing/intensity estimation, not about outlier-detection sensitivity or specificity for a flagger built on those estimates — the two floors aren't guaranteed to be the same number.
  • How a visit-density gate built this way interacts with yesterday's finding that growth-model choice itself changes flagging behavior — this specific numeric floor was derived for SITAR and may not transfer unchanged to a polynomial or spline substrate.
