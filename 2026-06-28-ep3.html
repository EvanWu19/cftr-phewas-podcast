Show notes — Track C — Residual function of CF mutants predicts response to small molecule CFTR modulators (Han et al., 2018)

Blurb. Han, Raraigh, Cutting and colleagues stably expressed 43 rare missense CFTR variants associated with moderate CF from a single genomic site in human CF bronchial epithelial (CFBE41o-) cells, then measured each variant's residual CFTR function and its response to the potentiator ivacaftor, the corrector lumacaftor, and the combination. Response magnitude was highly correlated with residual function across all three treatments; a second set of 16 variants in Fischer rat thyroid (FRT) cells replicated the correlations nearly identically. Combination beat monotherapy for almost all variants (40/43 CFBE; 13/16 FRT), and the panel represents 87% of CFTR2 individuals carrying ≥1 missense variant. It lands on the Karen Raraigh variant-interpretation priority via theratype; buildable artifact = a CFTR missense theratype map testing where a computational predictor reproduces residual-function-and-response ordering. Plain-English glossary

  • CFBE41o- cells — a human CF bronchial epithelial cell line (airway-derived) used to express and test CFTR variants.（中文：人源 CF 支气管上皮细胞系，气道来源，用于表达检测 CFTR 变异。）
  • CFTR2 — CF-specific variant database classifying disease liability from clinical + functional evidence.（中文：结合临床与功能证据给出 CF 致病性判断的 CF 专属变异库。）
  • Corrector — a modulator (e.g. lumacaftor) that helps misfolded CFTR fold and traffic to the cell surface.（中文：校正剂 — 帮助错误折叠的 CFTR 正确折叠并运输到细胞表面的调节剂。）
  • FRT cells — Fischer rat thyroid cells, a standard non-human system for clean CFTR-variant expression.（中文：Fischer 大鼠甲状腺细胞，用于干净表达 CFTR 变异的标准非人源系统。）
  • HGVS — standardized rules for writing a variant relative to a named reference sequence.（中文：相对于指定参考序列书写变异的标准化命名规范。）
  • Missense variant — a single-base change swapping one amino acid for another.（中文：使一个氨基酸被替换的单碱基改变。）
  • Moderate CF (variant) — variants at a milder end of the disease spectrum (often pancreatic-sufficient, later onset) with meaningful residual function.（中文：疾病谱较轻一端的变异，常胰腺功能保留、起病较晚，具可观残余功能。）
  • Potentiator — a modulator (e.g. ivacaftor) that increases gating of CFTR channels already at the surface.（中文：增效剂 — 提高已到达表面的 CFTR 通道门控效率的调节剂。）
  • Residual function — how much CFTR channel activity a variant retains, as a percentage of wild-type.（中文：残余功能 — 变异保留的 CFTR 通道活性，以野生型百分比表示。）
  • Theratype — grouping variants by predicted treatment response rather than only by pathogenicity.（中文：治疗型别 — 按预测的治疗反应而非仅按致病性对变异分组。）

  • CFTR2-coverage framing — express results as the % of real missense carriers the panel represents.

ALEX Good morning. 早上好。Today's Track C paper is Han and colleagues, "Residual function of cystic fibrosis mutants predicts response to small molecule CFTR modulators," published in JCI Insight in 2018, out of Garry Cutting's lab — and Karen Raraigh is on the author list again. 今天 C 轨道的论文是 Han 等人的《囊性纤维化突变体的残余功能可预测其对小分子 CFTR 调节剂的反应》，2018 年发表在《JCI Insight》，来自 Garry Cutting 实验室——而且作者名单里又有 Karen Raraigh。We read it in Track C, the modifier and genotype-function catalog, because it builds a quantitative bridge between a CFTR variant's residual function and how well a drug rescues it. 我们把它放在 C 轨道——修饰基因与基因型-功能目录——因为它在一个 CFTR 变异的残余功能和药物对它的挽救程度之间，搭起了一座定量的桥梁。Honest flag: this one is abstract-only — the full text would not resolve through any open route — so I will stick to what the abstract states and not invent figure-level detail. 老实说明：这篇也是只有摘要——全文通过任何开放渠道都取不到——所以我会严格依据摘要所述，不去编造图表层面的细节。

JORDAN Let me get the biology straight first. 我先把生物学搞清楚。What does "residual function" of a CFTR variant actually mean? 一个 CFTR 变异的"残余功能"到底是什么意思？

ALEX CFTR is a chloride channel at the cell surface, and a pathogenic variant can reduce how much working channel reaches the membrane and how well it gates. CFTR 是细胞表面的一个氯离子通道，一个致病变异会减少到达细胞膜的有功能通道的数量，以及它的门控（开关）效率。Residual function is simply how much channel activity is left, usually expressed as a percentage of wild-type CFTR. 残余功能就是剩下多少通道活性，通常用占野生型（wild-type，正常未突变）CFTR 的百分比来表示。A variant with near-zero residual function is severe; one with, say, twenty percent of normal is "moderate," and that middle band is exactly where this paper lives. 残余功能接近零的变异是严重的；而比如保留正常水平 20% 的，是"中等"的，而这个中间带正是这篇论文所关注的。

JORDAN And the two drug types — potentiator and corrector? 那两类药物——增效剂和校正剂？

ALEX Two different repair mechanisms. 两种不同的修复机制。A potentiator, like ivacaftor, acts on channels that already reach the surface and holds the gate open longer, increasing chloride flow. 增效剂（potentiator），比如 ivacaftor，作用于已经到达细胞表面的通道，让门控保持开放更久，增加氯离子流动。A corrector, like lumacaftor, helps the misfolded protein fold and traffic to the surface in the first place, so there is more channel there to potentiate. 校正剂（corrector），比如 lumacaftor，帮助错误折叠的蛋白先正确折叠并运输到表面，这样那里才有更多通道可供增效。Combination therapy uses both — correct the trafficking, then potentiate the gating. 联合治疗两者并用——先校正运输，再增效门控。These are the "small molecule modulators" in the title — drugs that tune the defective protein rather than treating downstream symptoms. 这些就是标题里的"小分子调节剂"——调校缺陷蛋白本身、而非治疗下游症状的药物。

JORDAN And these are tested in cell lines, not patients? 这些是在细胞系里测试，而不是在患者身上？

ALEX Right, and the cell systems matter for how we read the results. 对，而细胞系统对我们如何解读结果很重要。They used CFBE41o- cells — a human CF bronchial epithelial cell line, so airway-derived — and, for a second set, FRT cells, Fischer rat thyroid cells, a standard non-human workhorse for expressing CFTR variants cleanly. 他们用了 CFBE41o- 细胞——一种人源 CF 支气管上皮细胞系，所以是气道来源的——以及第二组用的 FRT 细胞，即 Fischer 大鼠甲状腺细胞，是一种用于干净表达 CFTR 变异的标准非人源工具细胞。

JORDAN So what gap motivated the study? 那是什么缺口促成了这项研究？

ALEX Modulator eligibility has historically been variant-by-variant and label-based — a drug gets approved for a specific list of mutations, and if your variant is not on the list, you are out, even if it is biologically similar. 调节剂的用药资格历来是逐变异、基于标签的——一种药被批准用于一份特定的突变清单，如果你的变异不在清单上，你就被排除在外，即便它在生物学上很相似。Rare missense variants are the ones most often left off those lists, simply because nobody tested them. 罕见错义变异（missense variant，使一个氨基酸被替换的变异）是最常被漏在清单外的，仅仅因为没人测过它们。The motivating question is whether a single underlying property — residual function — predicts drug response across many variants at once, which would let you reason about variants no trial ever enrolled. 驱动性的问题是：一个单一的底层属性——残余功能——能否一次性预测许多变异的药物反应，这样你就能对任何试验从未纳入的变异进行推断。If that holds, residual function becomes a unifying axis instead of a long lookup table of variant-specific results. 如果这成立，残余功能就成了一个统一的坐标轴，而不是一张冗长的、逐变异结果的查找表。

JORDAN You keep saying "moderate CF" and "CFTR2" — pin those down for me. 你一直说"中等 CF"和"CFTR2"——帮我把这两个定准。

ALEX CFTR2 is the CF-specific variant database that classifies each CFTR variant by combining clinical data and functional evidence into a disease-liability call. CFTR2 是 CF 专属的变异数据库，它把临床数据和功能证据结合起来，给每个 CFTR 变异一个疾病致病性判断。"Moderate CF" here refers to variants associated with a milder end of the disease spectrum — typically pancreatic-sufficient, later-onset, less severe than two minimal-function alleles. 这里的"中等 CF"指的是与疾病谱较轻一端相关的变异——通常是胰腺功能保留、起病较晚、比两个最低功能等位基因要轻。Those are exactly the variants with meaningful residual function, which is why they are the informative test set for a function-versus-response correlation. 这些恰恰是有可观残余功能的变异，这也是为什么它们是"功能对反应"相关性的信息量最大的测试集。If you only studied minimal-function variants near zero, you would have no dynamic range to detect a correlation at all. 如果你只研究接近零的最低功能变异，你根本没有动态范围去检测出相关性。

JORDAN So the moderate band is where the signal can even exist. 所以中等带正是信号能存在的地方。

ALEX Exactly — it is both the clinically ambiguous group and the statistically informative one, which is a nice alignment of need and design. 正是如此——它既是临床上含糊的那一组，也是统计上信息量大的那一组，需求和设计在这里很好地对齐了。

JORDAN How did they set it up? 他们是怎么搭建实验的？

ALEX They stably expressed 43 rare missense CFTR variants associated with moderate CF from a single site in the genome of CFBE41o- cells. 他们把 43 个与中等 CF 相关的罕见错义 CFTR 变异，从 CFBE41o- 细胞基因组的单一位点稳定表达出来。The "single site in the genome" detail is the clever control — every variant goes into the same genomic location, so they all sit under the same regulatory context and you are comparing the variant, not random integration effects. "基因组单一位点"这个细节是个巧妙的对照——每个变异都插入同一个基因组位置，所以它们都处在相同的调控背景下，你比较的是变异本身，而不是随机插入带来的效应。Then for each variant they measured residual CFTR function and the magnitude of response to ivacaftor, to lumacaftor, and to the ivacaftor-lumacaftor combination. 然后对每个变异，他们测量了残余 CFTR 功能，以及对 ivacaftor、对 lumacaftor、以及对 ivacaftor-lumacaftor 联合用药的反应幅度。To test robustness they expressed a second set of 16 variants in FRT cells and repeated the correlation. 为了检验稳健性，他们在 FRT 细胞里表达了第二组 16 个变异，并重复了相关性分析。

JORDAN Where would you push on that design? 这个设计你会在哪里施压？

ALEX The biggest caveat is that this is heterologous expression — you are putting a CFTR variant into a cell line under an artificial single-copy construct, not observing it in a patient's own airway epithelium at native dosage. 最大的告诫是这属于异源表达——你是把一个 CFTR 变异放进一个细胞系、在一个人工的单拷贝构建体下，而不是在患者自己的气道上皮里以天然剂量观察它。Single-site integration controls expression beautifully, but it also fixes expression at one level, which can mask variants whose real-world problem is altered splicing or messenger-RNA stability rather than channel gating. 单位点插入对表达水平控制得很漂亮，但它也把表达固定在一个水平上，这会掩盖那些真实问题在于剪接改变或信使 RNA 稳定性、而非通道门控的变异。And a cell line is not a primary cell or a patient-derived organoid, so the absolute numbers are a model of function, not a clinical readout. 而且细胞系不是原代细胞，也不是患者来源的类器官，所以那些绝对数值是功能的一个模型，而非临床读数。Those are not reasons to dismiss it — they are the boundary conditions for how far you can carry the prediction. 这些不是否定它的理由——而是这个预测能推到多远的边界条件。

JORDAN And the headline result? 那核心结果呢？

ALEX The central finding is that the magnitude of drug response was highly correlated with residual CFTR function — for the potentiator ivacaftor, for the corrector lumacaftor, and for the combination. 核心发现是：药物反应的幅度与残余 CFTR 功能高度相关——对增效剂 ivacaftor、对校正剂 lumacaftor、以及对联合用药都是如此。And the second set of 16 variants in FRT cells showed nearly identical correlations, which is the cross-system replication that makes the relationship credible rather than a cell-line artifact. 而 FRT 细胞里的第二组 16 个变异显示出几乎相同的相关性，这种跨系统的重复正是让这种关系可信、而非细胞系假象的关键。They also identified subsets of variants with statistically significantly higher responses to specific treatments, so it is not a flat one-size-fits-all story. 他们还识别出一些对特定治疗有统计学上显著更高反应的变异亚组，所以这并不是一个平淡的"一刀切"的故事。

JORDAN And the combination-versus-single-drug question? 那联合用药对单药的问题呢？

ALEX Nearly all variants did better on combination therapy than on either modulator alone — 40 of 43 in the CFBE cells and 13 of 16 in the FRT cells. 几乎所有变异在联合治疗下都比单用任一调节剂表现更好——CFBE 细胞里 43 个中的 40 个，FRT 细胞里 16 个中的 13 个。And the coverage claim is what makes this clinically pointed: these variants represent 87 percent of individuals in the CFTR2 database who carry at least one missense variant. 而覆盖率的说法正是这一点在临床上很有针对性的原因：这些变异代表了 CFTR2 数据库中携带至少一个错义变异的个体的 87%。So the practical conclusion the authors draw is that most people with CF carrying missense variants are likely to respond modestly to currently available modulators, a small fraction will respond pronouncedly, and most derive the greatest benefit from combination therapy. 所以作者得出的实际结论是：大多数携带错义变异的 CF 患者，可能对当前可用的调节剂有中等程度的反应，一小部分会有显著反应，而大多数从联合治疗中获益最大。

JORDAN Is it surprising that combination beat single drugs for almost everything? 联合用药对几乎所有变异都胜过单药，这令人意外吗？

ALEX Mechanistically, no — and that is part of why the result is reassuring rather than shocking. 从机制上看，不意外——这也是为什么这个结果令人安心、而非令人震惊的原因之一。A corrector increases how much channel reaches the surface, and a potentiator increases how well that channel gates, so they act on two different bottlenecks and would be expected to combine. 校正剂增加到达表面的通道数量，增效剂提高那些通道的门控效率，所以它们作用于两个不同的瓶颈，本就预期能叠加。What the data add is the magnitude and the consistency — that the additivity holds for 40 of 43 and 13 of 16 variants, across the whole moderate-function band, rather than only for a favored few. 数据增添的是幅度和一致性——这种叠加性在 43 个中的 40 个、16 个中的 13 个变异上都成立，覆盖了整个中等功能带，而不只是少数受偏爱的变异。The surprise, if any, is how cleanly one number — residual function — orders the whole response landscape. 如果说有什么意外，那就是单一一个数字——残余功能——竟能如此干净地为整个反应图景排序。

JORDAN Before we map it onto your work — what does this paper actually add to a modifier catalog? 在把它映射到你的工作之前——这篇论文究竟为一个修饰基因目录增添了什么？

ALEX It is worth being precise here: the gene under study is CFTR itself, not a separate modifier gene, so this does not add a new modifier locus to the catalog. 这里值得说精确：被研究的基因是 CFTR 本身，而不是一个独立的修饰基因，所以它不会给目录增加一个新的修饰位点。What it adds is an axis — residual function as a continuous, measurable property that modifies drug response across many CFTR alleles. 它增添的是一个坐标轴——残余功能，作为一个连续的、可测量的属性，在许多 CFTR 等位基因上修饰药物反应。That is a genotype-to-treatment-response relationship, which is the pharmacogenetic edge of the same genotype-phenotype map the catalog tracks. 这是一种"基因型到治疗反应"的关系，也就是目录所追踪的同一张基因型-表型图的药物遗传学边缘。And it connects cleanly to the variant-interpretation work, because residual function is one of the functional evidence types that feeds a CFTR2 or ACMG classification in the first place. 它还与变异解读工作干净地衔接，因为残余功能正是最初喂给 CFTR2 或 ACMG 分类的功能证据类型之一。So the catalog entry is conceptual: a residual-function-predicts-modulator-response axis, anchored on this 43-plus-16-variant dataset, not a new gene symbol. 所以这个目录条目是概念性的：一个"残余功能预测调节剂反应"的坐标轴，锚定在这个 43 加 16 变异的数据集上，而不是一个新的基因符号。

JORDAN Where does this land for what you are working on? 那这对你正在做的事情落到哪里？
