Ep4 (Track C, #2) — A 2010 Baseline: How Much Noise Is in Routine-Care Height/Weight Data?

Abstract-only (predates PMC; also, our own paper index had this title mismatched to the wrong PMID — corrected below by exact DOI match). Smith et al. (2010, International Journal of Pediatric Obesity) evaluate weight/height data quality at Kaiser Permanente Southern California in children aged 0-18, using the simplest possible candidate-generation method: comparing a measurement against the same child's sequential nearby encounters — the conceptual ancestor of the within-child trajectory methods (EWMA, jackknife residuals, mixed-effects splines) already catalogued from this track. Error rates by age band (<2, 2-5, 6-9, 10-13, 14-18y) were 0.4%, 0.7%, 1.0%, 1.0%, 0.7%, dropping to 0.4%, 0.4%, 0.6%, 0.4%, 0.1% after exclusion. The most striking finding is the age-stratified sensitivity of this naive method: 10.9%, 36.6%, 32.9%, 59.2%, 82.5% — a near-monotonic climb, meaning the method caught barely one in ten true errors in children under two while catching four in five in teenagers, likely because young children's naturally higher visit-to-visit growth variability makes a real change harder to distinguish from an error. Errors were also directionally asymmetric — implausibly low values dominated for height, implausibly high for weight — consistent with (though not confirmed as) unit-confusion or digit-transposition error types. As one of the earliest empirical floor measurements in this literature, it's a useful "naive baseline" number to beat when evaluating the more sophisticated candidate generators already covered.

Plain-English glossary

  • Directional error asymmetry — when a measurement's errors skew consistently toward one direction (e.g., height errors skewing low, weight errors skewing high) rather than being evenly split. （中文：方向性错误不对称，指某测量指标的错误持续偏向某一方向（例如身高错误偏低、体重错误偏高），而不是均匀分布在两个方向。）
  • Sequential-encounter comparison — judging a measurement's plausibility by comparing it to the same patient's other nearby visits, rather than to a population reference chart. （中文：连续就诊比较，指通过将某次测量值与同一患者其他相近就诊记录比较来判断其合理性，而不是对照人群参考图表。）
  • Sensitivity (error detection) — here, the fraction of true errors a detection method successfully flags; note this paper's sensitivity is self-referential (relative to the same method's own exclusion logic), not validated against an independent physician-adjudicated gold standard. （中文：敏感度（错误检测），此处指检测方法成功标记出的真实错误比例；需注意本文的敏感度是自指的（相对于同一方法自身的排除逻辑），并未针对独立的、经医生裁定的金标准进行验证。）

JORDAN Before the numbers — what does "based on sequential health care encounters" actually mean as a detection method? 在讲数字之前——"基于连续就诊记录"（sequential health care encounters）作为一种检测方法，具体是什么意思？

ALEX In plain terms, it means comparing a given height or weight measurement against the same child's other nearby visits, rather than checking it against a population reference chart in isolation. 简单来说，就是把某次身高或体重测量值，拿来和同一个孩子其他相近就诊记录做比较，而不是孤立地对照人群参考图表来检查。 That's conceptually the ancestor of the within-child trajectory methods we've covered on this podcast — the EWMA logic in growthcleanr, the jackknife residuals, the mixed-effects spline approaches — all of which formalize the same basic intuition: a value that doesn't fit this particular child's own pattern over time is more suspicious than one that merely sits far from the population average. 这在概念上是我们这档播客讲过的那些"个体轨迹内部"方法的祖先——growthcleanr里的EWMA逻辑、jackknife残差、混合效应样条方法——所有这些方法都把同一个基本直觉正式化：一个不符合这个孩子自己长期模式的数值，比一个仅仅是偏离人群平均值的数值更可疑。 The abstract doesn't spell out the exact rule used to call a value "implausible" — whether it's a fixed threshold, a percent-change rule, or something else — so today we're working with the headline numbers, not the underlying decision rule. 摘要并没有说明具体用来判定某个值"不合理"的确切规则——是固定阈值、百分比变化规则，还是别的方式——所以今天我们能核实的是结论性数字，而不是背后的判定规则本身。

JORDAN What was this paper actually trying to establish? 这篇论文实际上想要确立什么？

ALEX Context matters here: this is 2010, right as electronic medical records were starting to get used seriously for population-level care management and obesity surveillance, and nobody had a solid empirical number for how much routine-care height and weight data could actually be trusted. 这里的背景很重要：这是2010年，正是电子病历开始被认真用于人群层面的健康管理和肥胖监测的时候，当时没有人拥有一个扎实的实证数字，来说明常规医疗中记录的身高体重数据究竟有多可信。 The stated goal was to evaluate the quality of body weight and height measures for children based on those sequential encounters, at Kaiser Permanente Southern California medical centers, across children aged 0 to 18. 论文明确的目标是，基于这些连续就诊记录，评估南加州凯撒医疗集团（Kaiser Permanente Southern California）在0至18岁儿童中记录的体重和身高测量数据的质量。 It's worth placing this paper in the timeline of everything else we've covered in this track: growthcleanr, the Southampton cascade, the All of Us adult reference chart, all of that later methodological machinery was built to improve on exactly the kind of baseline number this paper set out to measure in the first place. 值得把这篇论文放进我们这个赛道之前讲过的所有内容的时间线里：growthcleanr、南安普顿级联方法、All of Us成人参考图表，所有那些后来出现的方法学机制，本质上都是为了改进这篇论文最初想要测量的那种基线数字。 This is, in other words, one of the earliest empirical floor measurements in a literature that today includes flag rates ranging from under 1% to over 20% depending on the method and population. 换句话说，这是这个研究领域里最早的一批经验性"基线"测量之一，而今天这个领域里，根据方法和人群的不同，标记率可以从不到1%一直到超过20%不等。

JORDAN What were the actual error rates they found? 他们实际发现的错误率是多少？

ALEX Broken out by age band — under 2, 2 to 5, 6 to 9, 10 to 13, and 14 to 18 years — the error rates in weight and height data were 0.4%, 0.7%, 1.0%, 1.0%, and 0.7% respectively. 按年龄段划分——2岁以下、2至5岁、6至9岁、10至13岁、14至18岁——体重和身高数据的错误率分别为0.4%、0.7%、1.0%、1.0%和0.7%。 The most frequently identified errors weren't symmetric: implausibly low values were the dominant error type for height, while implausibly high values dominated for weight — a directional asymmetry worth remembering, since it means "implausible" doesn't mean "randomly wrong in either direction," it tends to mean a specific, characteristic failure mode per measurement type. 最常见的错误类型并不是对称的：身高方面以不合理的偏低值为主，而体重方面则以不合理的偏高值为主——这种方向性的不对称值得记住，因为这意味着"不合理"并不是"随机偏向任一方向的错误"，而往往对应着每种测量类型各自特有的一种典型失败模式。 After excluding those implausible values, the residual error rates dropped to 0.4%, 0.4%, 0.6%, 0.4%, and 0.1% across the same five age bands — a real but modest improvement, and notably the error rate never gets fully to zero even after exclusion, since some noise is presumably irreducible measurement variability rather than a data-entry mistake. 排除这些不合理值之后，剩余错误率在同样五个年龄段中降到了0.4%、0.4%、0.6%、0.4%和0.1%——这是一个真实但幅度有限的改善，值得注意的是，即便在排除之后，错误率也从未完全降到零，这大概是因为部分噪声本身就是不可消除的测量变异，而不是数据录入错误。

JORDAN What about the sensitivity numbers — how good was this sequential-encounter approach at actually catching the errors that were there? 那敏感度数字呢——这种基于连续就诊记录的方法，在实际捕捉存在的错误方面表现如何？

ALEX This is the number I'd flag hardest: sensitivity to detect errors, again by the same five age bands, was 10.9%, 36.6%, 32.9%, 59.2%, and 82.5%. 这是我要特别强调的一组数字：按同样五个年龄段划分，检测错误的敏感度分别为10.9%、36.6%、32.9%、59.2%和82.5%。 That's a striking, near-monotonic climb with age, and it means the sequential-encounter method caught barely one in ten true errors in children under two, while catching more than four in five in teenagers. 这是一个显著的、随年龄近乎单调上升的趋势，也就是说，这种基于连续就诊记录的方法在2岁以下儿童中大约只能捕捉到十分之一的真实错误，而在青少年中却能捕捉到超过五分之四。 The likely mechanism is straightforward: young children grow fast and their trajectories are naturally more variable visit to visit, so a method that leans on "does this look consistent with this child's nearby measurements" has a much harder time telling real rapid growth apart from a genuine data error, precisely when both are large relative to the child's size. 背后的机制大概很直接：幼儿生长速度快，就诊之间的轨迹本身自然更具变异性，因此一种依赖"这个数值是否和这个孩子附近的测量值一致"的方法，在区分真实的快速生长和真正的数据错误时会困难得多，而这恰恰发生在两者相对于孩子体型都显得很大的时候。 That's a genuinely uncomfortable finding for any registry tracking a condition like CF, where the youngest years are exactly when growth failure and nutritional status carry the most clinical weight — a naive sequential-comparison method is least able to catch errors in precisely the age band where getting it right matters most. 这对于任何追踪像CF这样疾病的登记系统来说，都是一个相当令人不安的发现，因为最年幼的这几年，恰恰是生长发育不良和营养状况在临床上最为重要的时期——而一种朴素的连续对比方法，恰恰在这个最需要判断准确的年龄段，最难捕捉到错误。

JORDAN You flagged that low-height, high-weight asymmetry as worth remembering — do we have any sense of the mechanism behind it? 你提到"身高偏低、体重偏高"这种不对称值得记住——我们对背后的机制有什么了解吗？

ALEX The abstract doesn't give a mechanism directly, but the direction is consistent with the kind of data-entry error taxonomy later papers in this catalog spell out explicitly — unit confusion between inches and centimeters would make a height read implausibly low if a centimeter value got entered into a field expecting inches, and a decimal-point shift or a units mix-up between pounds and kilograms would make a weight read implausibly high in the equivalent way. 摘要没有直接给出机制，但这个方向与本目录中后续论文明确列出的数据录入错误分类相符——如果厘米单位的数值被填入了预期为英寸的字段，就会导致身高读数不合理地偏低；同样地，小数点位置错误，或磅与千克之间的单位混淆，也会以类似的方式导致体重读数不合理地偏高。 A digit-transposition error, say entering 51 instead of 15 for an inches-based height, would also skew toward implausibly low rather than high given how such transpositions interact with typical child height ranges. 数字换位错误，比如把身高的英寸数15错误录入成51，鉴于这种换位方式与儿童身高典型范围的相互作用方式，同样会偏向导致不合理的偏低值而不是偏高值。 None of that is confirmed by this paper specifically, but it's a reasonable, testable hypothesis for why the error direction wasn't symmetric, and it's exactly the kind of question a synthetic-data replication could investigate directly by injecting each candidate error type and checking which one reproduces this paper's specific low-height / high-weight skew. 这些都不是这篇论文本身证实的，但这是一个合理、可检验的假设，用来解释为什么错误方向不是对称的，而这正是一个合成数据复现实验可以直接研究的问题——通过注入每一种候选错误类型，检验哪一种能重现这篇论文中"身高偏低/体重偏高"这一具体的偏斜模式。

JORDAN Is there anything specific to a modulator-treated CF population that would make this particular naive method even harder to trust? 对于接受调节剂治疗的CF人群来说，有没有什么特别之处会让这种朴素方法更加难以信赖？

ALEX Speaking only about the general mechanism this paper describes, not about any specific registry's data — a sequential-encounter comparison method's whole premise is that a child's nearby measurements form a stable-enough baseline to judge a new one against, and any population undergoing a real, clinically meaningful shift in growth trajectory around a specific point in time is exactly the scenario where that premise gets stressed hardest, for the same reason young children stress it: the method can't easily tell a genuine trajectory change apart from an error when both look like a sudden jump relative to recent visits. 只谈这篇论文所描述的一般机制，不涉及任何具体登记系统的数据——连续就诊比较方法的整个前提，是孩子近期的测量值能构成一个足够稳定的基线，用来判断新的测量值，而任何在某个特定时间点前后经历真实的、具有临床意义的生长轨迹转变的人群，恰恰是这个前提被最严峻考验的场景，原因和幼儿的情况相同：当真实的轨迹变化和错误在相对于近期就诊记录上都表现为突然的跳跃时，这种方法很难将两者区分开来。 That's a general methodological point worth carrying into any evaluation of this kind of method, not a claim about what actually happens in any specific dataset we haven't looked at. 这是一个值得带入对这类方法的任何评估中的一般方法论要点，而不是对我们没有看过的任何具体数据集实际情况的断言。
