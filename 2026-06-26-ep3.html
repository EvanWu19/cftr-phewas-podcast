Show Notes — Track C — Variant Classifications, Databases and Genotype-Phenotype Correlations (Raynal & Corvol, 2020)

Blurb. Raynal and Corvol review the CFTR variant-database landscape and the genotype-phenotype problem underlying it. As CFTR sequencing became one of the world's most-ordered genetic tests, rare variants of uncertain impact keep accumulating, and three complementary locus-specific databases — CFTR1 (the raw mutation registry), CFTR2 (clinical + functional disease-liability calls), and CFTR-France (national-cohort annotations) — exist to characterize them. The review's deeper point: even a well-classified CFTR variant doesn't fully predict phenotype, because severity is shaped by candidate modifier genes, regulatory loci, epigenetic profiles, and trans-regulators — driving variability in lung function, meconium ileus susceptibility, CF-related diabetes, and liver disease. It closes on pharmacogenetics: with CFTR modulators now in use, patients split into "good" and "poor" responders, adding a new layer of complexity. Where it lands: Karen / variant interpretation (notation-and-classification harmonization). Buildable artifact: a CFTR cross-database classification concordance table that normalizes variants to HGVS and flags where CFTR1/CFTR2/CFTR-France disagree. Fetched abstract-only (no PMC, no open full text); flagged on-air.

Plain-English glossary

  • Epigenetic profile — chemical marks on DNA (e.g., methylation) that change gene activity without changing the DNA sequence.（中文：DNA 上的化学标记如甲基化，不改变序列却改变基因活性。）
  • Genotype-phenotype correlation — the mapping between the variants a person carries and their observable disease; in CF it is notoriously loose.（中文：携带的变异与可观察疾病之间的对应关系；在 CF 中非常松散。）
  • Locus-specific database (LSDB) — a database cataloging variants in a single gene (here CFTR) rather than genome-wide.（中文：只收录单个基因变异的数据库，此处为 CFTR。）
  • Meconium ileus — newborn bowel obstruction from abnormally thick intestinal contents; one of the earliest, most heritable CF complications.（中文：新生儿因肠内容物异常黏稠导致的肠梗阻，CF 最早、遗传性最强的并发症之一。）
  • Modifier gene — a gene other than CFTR whose variants nudge CF disease severity.（中文：CFTR 以外、其变异会左右 CF 严重程度的基因。）
  • Pharmacogenetics — genetic variation that influences how well a drug works (e.g., CFTR modulator "good" vs "poor" responders).（中文：影响药物疗效的遗传变异，如调节剂的良好/不良应答者。）
  • Regulatory locus — a stretch of DNA controlling how much a gene is expressed, without changing the protein sequence.（中文：控制基因表达量、不改变蛋白序列的 DNA 区段。）
  • Trans-regulator — a factor acting on a gene from a distance (often a different chromosome), usually by controlling its expression.（中文：从远处（常是另一条染色体）作用于某基因的调控因子，通常通过控制表达。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • Three complementary CFTR LSDBs — CFTR1 (mutation registry), CFTR2 (clinical/functional disease liability), CFTR-France (national-cohort annotations) — overlapping but non-identical sources of truth.
  • Four non-CFTR severity axes — modifier genes, regulatory loci, epigenetic profiles, trans-regulators — the framework for why genotype underdetermines phenotype.
  • Pharmacogenetic responder stratification — "good" vs "poor" modulator responders as a phenotype layer.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / variant interpretation (harmonization). Paste-ready MVP: Build a CFTR cross-database classification concordance table — a script that pulls the public CFTR2 variant list, normalizes every variant to canonical HGVS, joins to accessible CFTR1 and CFTR-France identifiers, and flags variants whose disease-liability classification disagrees across sources. Input: public CFTR2 + other LSDB exports. Difficulty: M (bulk of work is HGVS normalization, not the comparison). Output: a ranked list of variants the databases disagree about — a natural deliverable to a CFTR geneticist.

What to verify first

  • What fraction of CFTR variants round-trip cleanly through HGVS normalization (large deletions, deep-intronic variants fail silently) before quoting any concordance rate.
  • That CFTR2/CFTR1/CFTR-France snapshots being compared are current and license-compatible.
  • A careful definition of "disagreement" — a real classification conflict vs. a difference in how each database bins severity.

Gap-map / catalog: closes / leaves open

  • Closes: indexes the three CFTR LSDBs and the four non-CFTR severity axes; confirms the modifier phenotype axes (lung function, meconium ileus, CFRD, liver disease) already in the catalog.
  • Leaves open: which specific modifier loci the authors judge well-replicated vs. speculative (not visible in the abstract); the specific pharmacogenetic variants behind modulator response.
  • Catalog: no new genes — review is abstract-only and names no specific gene symbols in scientific substance.

JORDAN Let me get the foundational terms first. 我先把基础术语搞清楚。 What's a genotype-phenotype correlation, and why is it a "problem"? 什么是基因型-表型相关性，它为什么是个"问题"？ ALEX: Genotype is the set of variants a person carries; phenotype is the observable disease — lung function, nutrition, and so on. 基因型 (genotype) 是一个人携带的变异集合；表型 (phenotype) 是可观察到的疾病——肺功能、营养状况等等。 A genotype-phenotype correlation is the mapping between them, and in CF it's notoriously loose: two people with identical CFTR variants can have very different disease courses. 基因型-表型相关性就是这两者之间的对应关系，而在囊性纤维化 (cystic fibrosis) 里，这种关系出了名地松散：两个带着完全相同 CFTR 变异的人，病程可能差别很大。 JORDAN: And the paper leans on three databases — CFTR1, CFTR2, and CFTR-France. 论文依赖三个数据库——CFTR1、CFTR2 和 CFTR-France。 What's the distinction? 它们有什么区别？ ALEX: These are all locus-specific databases, meaning each one catalogs variants in a single gene — CFTR — rather than across the whole genome. 它们都是位点特异性数据库 (locus-specific database)，意思是每一个都只收录单个基因——CFTR——里的变异，而不是覆盖整个基因组。 CFTR1 is the original mutation list, a registry of every variant ever reported in the gene. CFTR1 是最初的突变列表，是这个基因里报告过的每一个变异的登记册。 CFTR2 (一个把 CFTR 变异和临床及功能数据关联起来、给出致病性判定的数据库) layers in clinical and functional data to assign disease liability. CFTR2（一个把 CFTR 变异和临床及功能数据关联起来、给出致病性判定的数据库）在此之上叠加临床和功能数据，来判定致病性。 CFTR-France adds another national cohort's clinical annotations. CFTR-France 则加入了另一个国家队列的临床注释。 JORDAN: So one is a raw list, and the others add clinical meaning. 所以一个是原始列表，另外两个加上了临床意义。 ALEX: Right, and they're complementary rather than identical, which is exactly where the interesting friction lives. 对，而且它们是互补的、而非完全一致的，这正是有意思的摩擦所在之处。

JORDAN When CFTR2 says a variant has "disease liability," what does that phrase actually mean? CFTR2 说一个变异有"致病性"时，这个说法到底是什么意思？ ALEX: It means the database has assembled enough evidence — clinical features in people who carry it, plus functional measurements of the protein — to assign whether, and how strongly, the variant causes CF. 意思是这个数据库已经汇集了足够的证据——携带它的人的临床特征，加上蛋白质的功能测量——来判定这个变异是否致病、以及致病有多强。 CFTR1, by contrast, was built simply to record that a variant exists, without that clinical adjudication. 相比之下，CFTR1 当初只是为了记录一个变异的存在，并不带这种临床裁定。 JORDAN: So if I see the same variant in all three, I might get a bare entry in one and a graded clinical call in another. 所以如果我在三个里都看到同一个变异，在一个里可能只是一条干巴巴的记录，在另一个里却是一个分级的临床判定。 ALEX: Exactly, and they were curated by different groups, at different times, under different criteria — which is precisely why reconciling them is non-trivial and worth doing. 没错，而且它们是由不同的团队、在不同的时间、用不同的标准整理的——这正是为什么把它们对齐既不简单、又值得去做。

每次一个临床实验室发现一个新的罕见变异，就得有人回答：它会致病吗，如果会，有多严重，我们该怎么跟家属说？ JORDAN: And the databases are how the community pools that answer. 而数据库就是整个社区汇集这个答案的方式。 ALEX: Exactly, but the abstract makes a second, deeper point. 没错，但摘要还提出了第二个更深的观点。 Even when you've nailed the CFTR variant itself, that doesn't fully predict the phenotype, because disease severity is shaped by things outside CFTR. 即使你把 CFTR 变异本身钉死了，也不能完全预测表型，因为疾病严重程度受到 CFTR 之外因素的影响。 The review names four such classes: candidate modifier genes, regulatory loci, epigenetic profiles, and trans-regulators. 这篇综述点出了四类这样的因素：候选修饰基因、调控位点、表观遗传图谱，以及反式调控因子。 JORDAN: Define those for me — modifier gene, regulatory locus, trans-regulator, epigenetic profile. 给我定义一下——修饰基因、调控位点、反式调控因子、表观遗传图谱。 ALEX: A modifier gene is a gene other than CFTR whose variants nudge how severe the CF phenotype is. 修饰基因 (modifier gene) 是 CFTR 以外的基因，它的变异会左右 CF 表型的严重程度。 A regulatory locus is a stretch of DNA that controls how much a gene is expressed rather than changing the protein's sequence. 调控位点 (regulatory locus) 是一段控制基因表达多少、而不改变蛋白质序列的 DNA。 A trans-regulator is a factor that acts on a gene from a distance — often a different chromosome — typically by controlling its expression. 反式调控因子 (trans-regulator) 是从远处——常常是另一条染色体——作用于某个基因的因子，通常是通过控制它的表达。 And an epigenetic profile is the layer of chemical marks on DNA, like methylation, that changes gene activity without changing the underlying sequence. 表观遗传图谱 (epigenetic profile) 是 DNA 上的一层化学标记，比如甲基化，它在不改变底层序列的情况下改变基因活性。 JORDAN: So beyond "which CFTR variant," there are at least four other dials that turn the severity up or down. 所以除了"是哪个 CFTR 变异"之外，至少还有另外四个旋钮在上下调节严重程度。

ALEX And it's worth being precise about why a modifier even has room to act. 而且值得精确说明，修饰因子为什么会有发挥作用的空间。 CFTR sets the baseline defect, but the downstream consequences — how much inflammation, how much mucus, how the gut and pancreas respond — run through other pathways, and those pathways carry their own genetic variation. CFTR 定下了基础缺陷，但下游后果——有多少炎症、有多少黏液、肠道和胰腺如何反应——是经由其他通路实现的，而那些通路带着它们自己的遗传变异。 JORDAN: So two people can have the same broken gate but different plumbing around it. 所以两个人可以有同样坏掉的闸门，但闸门周围的管路不同。 ALEX: That's a fair picture, and it's why a CFTR-only view systematically under-predicts how a given person will actually do. 这是个贴切的比喻，也是为什么只看 CFTR 会系统性地低估一个具体的人实际会怎样。

在这四个表型轴里，哪一个对修饰因子研究最干净？ ALEX: Meconium ileus is often singled out because it's binary, present at birth, and highly heritable — there's less environmental noise than something like adult lung function, which accumulates decades of infection and treatment history. 胎粪性肠梗阻常常被单独挑出来，因为它是二元的、出生时就有、而且遗传性很高——它的环境噪声比成人肺功能那样的东西少，后者积累了几十年的感染和治疗史。 JORDAN: So the trait's own structure decides how easy the modifier signal is to find. 所以性状本身的结构决定了修饰信号有多容易找到。 ALEX: Right, and the review listing all four together is a quiet reminder that each axis needs its own phenotype definition before you go hunting modifiers. 对，而这篇综述把四个一起列出来，是在悄悄提醒：在你去搜寻修饰因子之前，每个轴都需要它自己的表型定义。 Then there's the last and most clinically live point: pharmacogenetics. 然后是最后一个、临床上最活跃的点：药物遗传学。 With CFTR modulator drugs now available, the abstract notes that patients split into "good" and "poor" responders, and it raises pharmacogenetic factors — genetic variation that influences how well a drug works — as a new layer of complexity in managing CF. 随着 CFTR 调节剂 (modulator) 药物的出现，摘要指出病人分成了"良好应答者"和"不良应答者"，并提出药物遗传学因素——影响药物疗效的遗传变异——作为 CF 管理中新的一层复杂性。 JORDAN: The responder split is striking — is that thought to be CFTR variation or modifier variation?
