How did this episode land for you?

Evolutionary Constraints Improve Protein Large Language Model Predictions for Protein Stability, Binding Regions, and Epistasis

Tzavella K, et al. introduce D2D, a method that fuses a protein language model's embeddings with protein-specific evolutionary information from multiple sequence alignments to score mutational effects with little to no task-specific labeled data. Rather than treating structure-based and sequence-based approaches as competitors, D2D combines them: at each alignment position, it fits a Gaussian mixture model over species-level embeddings and scores a mutation as the gap between wild-type and mutant log-probability under that model. The payoff shows up exactly where most predictors struggle — residues exposed to solvent, and intrinsically disordered binding regions — where D2D and GEMME are the only methods in the comparison that don't degrade with increasing solvent exposure. A cancer-specific extension, D2Deep, performs comparably to AlphaMissense on 18 benchmark genes while being fully open and independently reproducible, unlike AlphaMissense's non-public weights. CFTR itself is never discussed in the paper, so everything here lands as a transferable method for the Karen collaboration, not a CF-specific result — particularly D2D's compensatory-mutation framework, which maps naturally onto CFTR's biallelic genotypes and rare second-site variants. The buildable artifact is a CFTR D2D-Style Compensatory-Pair Screen scoring known CFTR variant pairs against distance-matched controls.

Plain-English glossary

Epistasis — The effect of one mutation depends on what else is happening elsewhere in the same protein; a second mutation can worsen or rescue the first.（中文：上位效应，指一个突变的效果取决于同一蛋白质其他位置发生的变化，第二个突变可能使第一个突变的效果加重或得到补偿。）

Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) — A statistical model describing a distribution as a combination of one or more Gaussian (normal) components; here, used to model the range of evolutionarily tolerated embeddings at a single alignment position.（中文：高斯混合模型，一种用一个或多个高斯分布组合来描述数据分布形态的统计模型，本文用它描述某个比对位置在进化上可容忍的嵌入向量分布范围。）

Intrinsically Disordered Region (IDR) — A stretch of a protein with no fixed three-dimensional fold, often poorly conserved across species and invisible to structure-based predictors.（中文：内在无序区域，指蛋白质中没有固定三维折叠结构的片段，通常跨物种保守性较低，基于结构的预测工具难以处理。）

Linear Interacting Peptide (LIP) — A short, often disordered motif that mediates a protein-protein interaction, frequently found within IDRs.（中文：线性互作肽段，一种通常位于无序区域内、介导蛋白质相互作用的短序列模块。）

Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA) — An alignment of a protein's sequence across many species, used to extract evolutionary conservation and variation signal at each position.（中文：多序列比对，将一个蛋白质在多个物种间的序列对齐排列，用于提取每个位置的进化保守性与变异信号。）

Oncogene / Tumor Suppressor Gene (OG/TSG) — An oncogene promotes cancer when activated (gain-of-function); a tumor suppressor gene normally restrains cancer and promotes it when inactivated (loss-of-function).（中文：癌基因在被激活（功能获得）时促进癌症发生；肿瘤抑制基因通常抑制癌症，在失活（功能丧失）时反而促进癌症发生。）

Relative Solvent Accessibility (RSA) — How exposed a given amino acid residue is to the surrounding solvent, versus buried within the protein's core; many predictors perform worse on high-RSA (exposed) residues.（中文：相对溶剂可及性，衡量某个氨基酸残基暴露在周围溶剂中的程度，相对于被埋藏在蛋白质核心内部而言；许多预测工具在RSA较高（暴露）的残基上表现较差。）

ΔΔG — The change in free energy between a protein's folded and unfolded states caused by a mutation; the standard quantitative measure of how destabilizing or stabilizing a substitution is.（中文：自由能变化差值，指突变导致蛋白质折叠态与去折叠态之间自由能的变化，是衡量一个突变"使蛋白质失稳或稳定程度"的标准定量指标。）

Full citation

Tzavella K, et al. "Evolutionary Constraints Improve Protein Large Language Model Predictions for Protein Stability, Binding Regions, and Epistasis." Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal. 2026. PMID: 42992201 | PMC13738275 | DOI: 10.34133/csbj.0226

Methods / tools to lift

  • D2D — unsupervised/minimally-supervised mutation-effect scorer combining pLM embeddings with per-position MSA-derived GMMs; needs little to no labeled data.
  • D2Deep — cancer-driver-mutation classifier extension of D2D, trained on latent-driver and gnomAD control mutations.
  • ProtT5 — the protein language model supplying the ~1,000-dimension per-residue embeddings that D2D max-pools down to 20 dimensions.
  • GEMME — an evolutionary-information-based mutation-effect predictor used as a comparator; the other RSA-robust method in the benchmark.
  • ESM-Effect — a fine-tuned ESM-based supervised predictor that substantially outperforms D2D+MLP under in-protein cross-validation.
  • ScanNet / P2Rank — structure-based binding-site predictors that D2D outperforms in both ordered regions and IDRs.
  • CAID-3 Challenge — the binding-in-disorder benchmark (52 sequences) used to validate D2D's IDR binding-site predictions.
  • AlphaMissense / EVE / FATHMM-cancer — comparator predictors in the D2Deep cancer-driver benchmark; AlphaMissense's weights are not public.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands on the Karen collaboration — CFTR variant interpretation (method only; CFTR is never discussed in the paper itself).

Buildable artifact — CFTR D2D-Style Compensatory-Pair Screen (M): Take a list of known CFTR complex alleles or co-occurring rare-variant pairs, build a CFTR cross-species MSA, pull ProtT5 embeddings, fit per-position GMMs, and score each pair against a distance-matched control the way the paper's TP53 analysis did it, flagging pairs that look like real epistatic interactions rather than two independent hits.

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR's own cross-species MSA is deep enough — the paper needed a minimum MSA depth of 24 species to justify its embedding dimensionality, and membrane proteins don't always match cytosolic genes' sequence coverage.
  • Whether the RSA-robustness result, demonstrated on globular/soluble proteins, generalizes to a multi-pass transmembrane protein with distinct topological domains like CFTR.
  • What fraction of CFTR's own alignment positions would clear the paper's high-confidence filtering bar, given that its best reported number came from a filtered 24-study subset, not the full benchmark.
  • No claim is made, or should be assumed, about how any of this maps onto CFFPR's own genotype data structures.

JORDAN Last one — ΔΔG. 最后一个——ΔΔG。

ALEX That's the change in free energy between a protein's folded and unfolded states caused by a mutation — the standard unit for how destabilizing a substitution is. 这是突变导致蛋白质折叠态与去折叠态之间自由能变化的差值，英文记作ΔΔG（ΔΔG，衡量一个突变"有多大破坏稳定性"的标准单位）。Positive and negative signs flip depending on convention, so the paper is careful about which direction counts as destabilizing versus stabilizing. 正负号会因惯例不同而翻转，所以论文在"哪个方向代表失稳、哪个方向代表稳定"这件事上格外谨慎。

ALEX The motivating problem is that most computational predictors of mutation effects assume additivity — that if you know what one mutation does and what another does, you can just add their effects together to predict what both do at once. 这项研究的出发点是，大多数计算预测突变效应的工具都假设"可加性"——也就是说，只要知道单个突变各自的效果，把它们加起来就能预测两个突变同时出现时的效果。

JORDAN Which breaks down exactly when epistasis is in play. 而这恰恰在存在上位效应时会失效。

ALEX Right, and the deeper issue is upstream of that: most existing approaches split into two camps. 对，而更深层的问题出现在这之前：现有方法大致分成两个阵营。Structure-based predictors, like mCSM and PoPMusic, lean on a protein's 3D fold, but they carry a well-documented bias — they tend to call buried mutations pathogenic just because packing changes there look dramatic, and they get worse as a residue's solvent exposure increases. 基于结构的预测工具，比如mCSM（mCSM，一种基于蛋白质三维结构预测突变对稳定性影响的工具）和PoPMusic，依赖蛋白质的三维折叠结构，但它们有一个被反复记录的偏差——它们往往仅因为埋藏位点处的紧密堆积变化看起来剧烈，就把埋藏突变判定为致病性，而且随着残基溶剂暴露程度增加，表现也会变差。Sequence-based protein language models, trained purely on statistical patterns across huge numbers of protein sequences, don't need structure at all, but the paper shows they inherit a similar RSA-dependent blind spot — ESM-1v and MSA_Transformer both degrade on exposed residues too, just for different underlying reasons. 基于序列的蛋白质语言模型完全依赖海量蛋白质序列中的统计模式训练而成，根本不需要结构信息，但论文表明它们也继承了类似的、依赖RSA的盲点——ESM-1v（ESM-1v，一种基于序列训练的蛋白质语言模型）和MSA_Transformer（MSA_Transformer，一种基于多序列比对的蛋白质语言模型）在暴露残基上的表现同样会变差，只是背后原因不同。

ALEX For a given protein, you build a multiple sequence alignment, or MSA, across many species — that's the raw evolutionary signal. 对于某个给定的蛋白质，你先构建一个跨多个物种的多序列比对，英文缩写MSA（MSA，捕捉蛋白质家族跨物种保守性模式的序列比对方法）——这是原始的进化信号。Each amino acid at each alignment position gets mapped to a high-dimensional protein language model embedding — specifically ProtT5 — and then max-pooled down from roughly a thousand dimensions to twenty, using a pooling kernel and stride both set to fifty. 比对中每个位置的每个氨基酸都会被映射到一个高维的蛋白质语言模型嵌入向量——具体用的是ProtT5（ProtT5，一种生成蛋白质序列嵌入向量的蛋白质语言模型）——然后通过最大池化操作，把大约一千维压缩到二十维，池化核大小和步幅都设为五十。

JORDAN Why max-pooling instead of just averaging? 为什么用最大池化而不是直接取平均？

ALEX The authors argue averaging would dilute the sparse, high-magnitude dimensions that actually carry the mutational signal, and could even cancel out signed values within a pooling block — max-pooling keeps the highest-variance coordinates, at the cost of being noisier. 作者认为取平均会稀释那些真正承载突变信号的稀疏、高强度维度，甚至可能在一个池化块内部把带符号的数值相互抵消——最大池化保留了方差最高的坐标，代价是噪声更大。Then, at each alignment position, they fit a single-component Gaussian mixture model, or GMM, across all the species in that position's column, and a mutation's score is just the gap between the wild-type's log-probability under that distribution and the mutant's. 接着，在每个比对位置上，他们对该位置列中所有物种的数据拟合一个单组分的高斯混合模型，英文缩写GMM（GMM，一种用若干高斯概率分布描述数据分布形态的统计模型）——而一个突变的分数，就是野生型在该分布下的对数概率，与突变体对数概率之间的差值。
