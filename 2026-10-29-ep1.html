Ep1 (Track A — variant interpretation): Benchmark before you build

Arda İnan and colleagues (2026, Genetics in Medicine) present AAVC, a publicly hosted, automated ACMG/AMP variant classifier that computationally scores sequence variants against ACMG guidelines, ClinGen specifications, and an unspecified "novel framework," drawing on large public databases and in silico prediction tools. Against a reference set of FDA-recognized variant classifications, AAVC reports 94.39% concordance, which the authors describe as outperforming currently available tools (comparator tools and margins not named in the retrievable abstract). It also reclassified 55% of ClinVar-style variants of uncertain significance into clinically significant categories, and when run across the entire Turkish Variome population biobank it flagged 215 novel pathogenic/likely-pathogenic/VUS-high variants in ACMG secondary-finding genes, with roughly 1 in 10 individuals carrying an actionable genotype — a notably higher rate than typically reported in other biobanks, worth independent scrutiny. Only the abstract is retrievable for this paper; every number above is abstract-only and flagged as such on-air. The transferable idea for Karen's CFTR variant-interpretation work isn't AAVC's code, which isn't available to us, but its benchmark methodology: run CFTR2/ClinVar's already-expert-classified CFTR variants through AAVC's public tool and measure concordance and VUS-resolution before committing to a custom classifier build.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP variant classification — the formal five-tier system (Pathogenic, Likely Pathogenic, Uncertain Significance, Likely Benign, Benign) assigned by combining weighted evidence codes such as population frequency, computational predictors, and functional data.（中文：ACMG/AMP变异分类，一套正式的五级体系（致病、可能致病、意义不明、可能良性、良性），通过组合人群频率、计算预测、功能实验等带权重的证据代码得出。）
  • ClinGen Variant Curation Expert Panel (VCEP) — a gene- or gene-family-specific expert group that issues specifications overriding or sharpening the generic ACMG rules (CFTR has its own VCEP).（中文：ClinGen变异审定专家小组，针对特定基因或基因家族发布细则，用以覆盖或收紧通用ACMG规则（CFTR有自己的专家小组）。）
  • Secondary finding gene — one of roughly 80 genes on the ACMG's recommended list (e.g. BRCA1) that should be reported back to a patient if incidentally found pathogenic during unrelated sequencing.（中文：次要发现基因，ACMG推荐名单上约80个基因之一（如BRCA1），若在不相关的测序中偶然发现致病变异，应反馈给患者。）
  • Turkish Variome — a national-population genomic variant catalog built from Turkish genomes, the same category of resource as gnomAD but population-specific.（中文：Turkish Variome（土耳其变异组），基于土耳其人群基因组构建的国家级变异目录，与gnomAD同类但为人群特异版本。）
  • Evidence code (e.g. PM1, PP3, BS1) — a standardized label for one piece of ACMG evidence (hotspot location, computational prediction, population frequency, etc.) combined under formal rules to reach a final classification.（中文：证据代码（如PM1、PP3、BS1），标准化标注的一种ACMG证据（热点位置、计算预测、人群频率等），按正式规则组合得出最终分类。）
  • In silico prediction tools — computational variant-effect predictors (e.g. AlphaMissense, REVEL, CADD, SpliceAI) that output a continuous pathogenicity score later converted into a discrete evidence strength.（中文：计算机预测工具，即计算性变异效应预测模型（如AlphaMissense、REVEL、CADD、SpliceAI），输出连续的致病性评分，再转换为离散的证据强度等级。）
