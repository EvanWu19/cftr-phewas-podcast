**DNABERT (Ji, Zhou, Liu, Davuluri; Bioinformatics, 2021) is one of the first BERT-style pretrained language models applied directly to genomic DNA sequence, and it's widely regarded as a founding point in the lineage that later produced DNABERT-2, Nucleotide Transformer, HyenaDNA, and Evo. We're covering it for its methodological lineage, not as new work. Per the abstract, a single pretrained model, fine-tuned on small labeled datasets, achieved state-of-the-art performance on promoter, splice-site, and transcription-factor-binding-site prediction, offered nucleotide-level interpretability, and transferred with strong performance to other organisms after pretraining only on the human genome. Note: this episode is abstract-only** — full text could not be retrieved, so no specific benchmark numbers, architecture dimensions, or dataset details are reported beyond what the abstract states. The episode lands on an AI-tooling buildable artifact: a CFTR intronic splice-impact scorer built by fine-tuning an open DNA-language-model checkpoint.

Plain-English glossary

  • Enhancer — A regulatory DNA element, sometimes far from the gene it controls, that increases transcription when bound by the right proteins.（中文:一种调控DNA元件,有时离它调控的基因很远,被正确的蛋白结合后会增强转录。）
  • Exon skipping — A splicing error where an exon is left out of the mature RNA because its splice site signal is disrupted.（中文:一种剪接错误,因剪接位点信号被破坏,导致某个外显子没有被保留在成熟RNA中。）
  • Intron retention — A splicing error where an intron is mistakenly kept in the mature RNA instead of being removed.（中文:一种剪接错误,内含子本应被去除,却被错误地保留在成熟RNA中。）
  • k-mer tokenization — Splitting a DNA sequence into overlapping fixed-length chunks of k nucleotides, used as the input units for a DNA language model.（中文:把DNA序列切分成重叠的、固定长度为k个核苷酸的片段,作为DNA语言模型的输入单元。）
  • Motif — A short, recurring DNA sequence pattern that proteins recognize and bind to.（中文:一段短而重复出现的DNA序列模式,蛋白质能够识别并结合它。）
  • Non-coding DNA — The majority of the genome that doesn't code for proteins but can still regulate gene activity.（中文:基因组中不编码蛋白质、但仍可能调控基因活动的大部分区域。）
  • Promoter — The DNA region just upstream of a gene's transcription start site where transcription machinery assembles.（中文:基因转录起始位点上游的DNA区域,转录机器在此组装。）
  • Regulatory element — A DNA sequence that controls when, where, or how much a gene is expressed.（中文:一段控制基因何时、何处、以多大程度表达的DNA序列。）
  • Silencer — A regulatory element that represses transcription when bound by the right proteins.（中文:一种调控元件,被正确的蛋白结合后会抑制转录。）
  • Spliceosome — The molecular machine that removes introns and joins exons to produce mature messenger RNA.（中文:负责去除内含子并连接外显子、生成成熟信使RNA的分子机器。）
  • Splice site — A short sequence signal at an intron-exon boundary that tells the spliceosome where to cut and join.（中文:内含子-外显子边界处的短序列信号,告诉剪接体在哪里切割和连接。）
  • Transcription factor binding site (TFBS) — A short motif where a specific transcription factor protein docks to regulate a gene.（中文:特定转录因子蛋白结合、以调控基因的一段短基序。）
