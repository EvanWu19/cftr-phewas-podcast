A tissue-specific splice model beats generic SpliceAI by a wide margin

Lesurf et al. (Genome Medicine, 2024) combined genome sequencing with myocardial RNA-sequencing in 1101 congenital heart disease (CHD) probands to build a heart-specific machine-learning model for splice-disrupting variants — including deep-intronic, "cryptic" ones invisible to panel or exome sequencing. Using FRASER to call confirmed splicing-outlier events in patient heart tissue as ground truth, a random forest combining SpliceAI scores, DNA positional features, and myocardial gene expression reached AUC 0.94 on an independent validation cohort, versus 0.67 for SpliceAI alone. Applied to 947 additional CHD patients with DNA only, the model found non-canonical splice-disrupting variants in 11% of them, nearly half of them more than 10 base pairs into an intron. The design — generic sequence predictor to generate candidates, tissue-matched RNA evidence to validate and train a gene-specific refinement model — is a direct template for a CFTR-specific splice-variant tool, and CFTR's airway epithelium is an easier tissue source than pediatric cardiac surgery. Serves the Karen Raraigh collaboration on CFTR variant interpretation.

Plain-English glossary

  • Splice-disrupting variant — a DNA variant that alters normal RNA splicing, either at a canonical splice site or elsewhere in the gene.（中文：一种改变正常RNA剪接过程的DNA变异，可能位于经典剪接位点，也可能位于基因的其他位置。）
  • Canonical vs. non-canonical (cryptic) splice site — a canonical site is the normal, annotated exon-intron boundary; a cryptic site is a new splice site created or activated by a variant elsewhere in the gene, sometimes deep in an intron.（中文：经典剪接位点是正常、已知注释的外显子-内含子边界；隐蔽剪接位点是由基因其他位置的变异新产生或激活的剪接位点，有时深藏于内含子内部。）
  • SpliceAI — a deep-learning tool that scores any genomic position for its predicted probability of creating or disrupting a splice site, trained genome-wide without tissue-specific information.（中文：一款深度学习工具，可为基因组上任意位置打分，预测其产生或破坏剪接位点的概率，训练时未使用组织特异性信息。）
  • Minigene assay — a lab technique that clones a small gene fragment around a variant into a plasmid, expresses it in cultured cells, and observes the resulting splicing pattern; the traditional functional-validation method for splice variants.（中文：一种实验技术，将变异周围的一小段基因片段克隆入质粒并在培养细胞中表达，观察其剪接结果；是剪接变异传统的功能验证方法。）
  • FRASER — a statistical tool that detects "outlier" splicing events in RNA-sequencing data by comparing a sample's splicing pattern against a reference set.（中文：一款统计学工具，通过将某样本的剪接模式与参考样本集比较，检测RNA测序数据中的"异常"剪接事件。）
  • ACMG/AMP — the standard framework (American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology) for classifying variants from pathogenic to benign.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学院/分子病理学会制定的变异分类标准框架，从致病到良性。）
  • MANE transcript — the single, community-agreed "Matched Annotation from NCBI and EMBL-EBI" reference transcript per gene, used to standardize which transcript a variant's effect is reported against.（中文："NCBI与EMBL-EBI匹配注释"参考转录本，每个基因一个，由学界共同认定，用于统一变异效应所参照的转录本。）
  • gnomAD / PopMax allele frequency — gnomAD is a large population reference database of DNA variant frequencies; PopMax is the highest allele frequency observed in any single population subgroup, used to filter out common, likely-benign variants.（中文：gnomAD是一个大型人群变异频率参考数据库；PopMax指在任一单一人群亚组中观察到的最高等位基因频率，用于过滤掉常见的、大概率良性的变异。）
  • pLI (probability of loss-of-function intolerance) — a gnomAD-derived score estimating how intolerant a gene is to loss-of-function variants; high pLI genes are more likely to cause disease via haploinsufficiency.（中文：源自gnomAD的评分，估计某基因对功能缺失型变异的耐受程度；高pLI基因更可能通过单倍剂量不足机制致病。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Two-stage design: SpliceAI to generate genome-wide candidates, then tissue-matched RNA-Seq + FRASER as functional ground truth to train a gene-specific refinement model.
  • Random forest with progressively richer feature sets (SpliceAI-only → DNA positional features → + tissue gene expression) to isolate which feature types actually add value.
  • Rarity filtering against gnomAD PopMax allele frequency and restriction to MANE transcripts before treating a candidate as clinically relevant.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh collaboration (CFTR variant interpretation / harmonization). Buildable artifact: a CFTR-specific splice-candidate scorer — pull rare CFTR variants with nontrivial SpliceAI scores from gnomAD/ClinVar, train a lightweight DNA-feature model against published CFTR splicing functional data (minigene results, existing CF airway RNA-Seq studies). Difficulty: M — the hard part is assembling functional training labels from scattered literature, not the modeling. Stretch goal: the full two-stage RNA-Seq pipeline if CF airway tissue data access becomes available.

What to verify first

  • Whether this model's feature set (myocardial expression + DNA position) transfers to CFTR/airway tissue without retraining, or whether CF-specific data is required — not established in this paper.
  • How sensitive the reported AUC is to the 100-bp window used to define a "confirmed" FRASER splicing event.
  • What CFTR-specific functional splicing data (minigene, RNA-Seq) is actually publicly available before assuming there's enough to train even a lightweight model.

ALEX Today's paper is "A validated heart-specific model for splice-disrupting variants in childhood heart disease," Lesurf and colleagues, published in Genome Medicine in 2024. 今天这篇论文题为《一个经过验证的、针对儿童心脏病剪接破坏变异的心脏特异性模型》，作者是Lesurf及其同事，2024年发表于《Genome Medicine》期刊。 It's Track A, variant interpretation, and we picked it up because it's not about CFTR at all — it's about congenital heart disease — but the machine-learning design pattern it uses is exactly the kind of gene-specific splice-effect model a CFTR-focused version would need. 这是我们的A线——变异解读——我们选它并不是因为它跟CFTR有关，它讲的其实是先天性心脏病，而是因为它所用的机器学习设计模式，正是构建一个CFTR专用剪接效应模型所需要的那种范式。 The full text was retrievable, so we have real numbers today, not just an abstract. 今天全文是可以获取的，所以我们有真实的数据可讲，而不只是一篇摘要。

JORDAN Congenital heart disease and CFTR are pretty different diseases. 先天性心脏病和CFTR相关疾病是两种很不一样的病。 What's the actual transferable idea? 这里真正可以迁移的想法是什么？

ALEX The core idea is that a generic splice-effect predictor, trained on nothing but DNA sequence, misses a lot — and a tissue-specific model, trained on that same DNA sequence plus real RNA evidence from the relevant organ, catches what the generic model misses. 核心想法是：一个仅基于DNA序列训练的通用剪接效应预测模型会漏掉很多变异；而一个组织特异性模型——在同样的DNA序列基础上，再加入来自相关器官的真实RNA证据——就能捕捉到通用模型遗漏的部分。 That's a pattern that generalizes to any single-organ Mendelian disease with accessible patient tissue, CF included, since CFTR is highly expressed in airway epithelium. 这种模式可以推广到任何有可获取患者组织的单器官孟德尔遗传病，包括CF在内，因为CFTR基因在气道上皮组织中高表达。

JORDAN Let's start with the vocabulary. 我们先从术语讲起。 What's a "splice-disrupting variant," and why isn't SpliceAI alone good enough? 什么是"剪接破坏变异"（splice-disrupting variant）？为什么单靠SpliceAI还不够？

ALEX Genes are transcribed as pre-mRNA containing exons and introns, and splicing is the process that cuts out the introns and joins the exons together, guided by short sequence signals at each exon-intron boundary — the splice junctions. 基因被转录为含有外显子（exon）和内含子（intron）的前体mRNA，剪接（splicing）就是把内含子切除、把外显子拼接起来的过程，这个过程由每个外显子-内含子边界处的短序列信号——即剪接位点（splice junction）——来引导。 A canonical splice-site variant sits right at one of those boundary signals and can break splicing outright. 一个位于经典剪接位点（canonical splice site）的变异，恰好落在这些边界信号上，可能直接破坏剪接过程。 A non-canonical, or cryptic, splice-disrupting variant is different — it sits elsewhere, sometimes deep inside an intron, and either weakens an existing junction or creates a brand-new, "cryptic" splice site that the spliceosome starts using instead, producing an abnormal transcript. 而非经典的、或称隐蔽的（cryptic）剪接破坏变异则不同——它位于其他位置，有时深藏在内含子内部，会削弱现有的剪接位点，或者制造出一个全新的"隐蔽剪接位点"，剪接体（spliceosome）转而使用这个新位点，从而产生异常转录本。 SpliceAI is a deep-learning tool that scores any DNA position for its predicted probability of gaining or losing a splice site, and it's genuinely good at flagging candidates — but it was trained on a tissue-agnostic, genome-wide basis, so it has no way of knowing whether a candidate site actually gets used in heart tissue specifically. SpliceAI是一个深度学习工具，它会为基因组上的任意位置打分，预测该位置获得或失去剪接位点的概率，在筛选候选变异方面确实表现不错——但它是在与组织无关（tissue-agnostic）、全基因组的基础上训练出来的，因此它没有办法判断某个候选位点是否真的会在心脏组织中被实际使用。

JORDAN So SpliceAI gives you candidates, but not confirmation. 所以SpliceAI给你的是候选名单，而不是确认结果。 How do you confirm a candidate actually does something in real tissue? 那要怎么确认一个候选变异真的在实际组织中产生了作用？

ALEX That's where RNA-sequencing of patient tissue comes in — instead of just reading the DNA, you sequence the actual messenger RNA transcripts present in a sample, which tells you what splicing decisions the cell made. 这就要靠对患者组织进行RNA测序（RNA-sequencing）——不只是读取DNA序列，而是对样本中实际存在的信使RNA转录本进行测序，这样就能知道细胞实际做出了怎样的剪接选择。 This group had access to myocardium — actual heart muscle tissue, typically collected during corrective cardiac surgery — from a subset of their patients, and ran RNA-Seq on it. 这个研究团队获得了部分患者的心肌组织（myocardium）——通常是在心脏矫正手术中采集的实际心肌样本——并对其进行了RNA测序。 They used a tool called FRASER, which statistically detects "outlier" splicing events in RNA-Seq data — places where a sample's splicing pattern deviates significantly from what's typical across a reference set of samples — to call which DNA variants were actually associated with abnormal splicing in that patient's own heart. 他们使用了一个名为FRASER的工具，它能够对RNA测序数据中的"异常"剪接事件进行统计学检测——即某个样本的剪接模式与参考样本集中的典型模式发生显著偏离的位置——从而判断哪些DNA变异真正与该患者自身心脏中的异常剪接相关联。 So the pipeline is: call candidate variants genome-wide with SpliceAI, then use myocardial RNA-Seq and FRASER as the functional truth set to see which candidates actually validate. 所以整个流程是：先用SpliceAI在全基因组范围内呼叫候选变异，再用心肌RNA测序和FRASER作为功能层面的真值集，来判断哪些候选变异真正得到了验证。

JORDAN And the "minigene assay" that keeps coming up in this literature — how does that compare? 这类文献中经常提到的"微基因实验"（minigene assay），跟这个方法相比如何？

ALEX A minigene assay is a lab construct — you clone a small piece of a gene, including the region around the variant of interest, into a plasmid, transfect it into cultured cells, and see how it splices. 微基因实验是一种实验室构建方法——研究者克隆出基因的一小段片段，包括感兴趣变异周围的区域，将其插入质粒（plasmid）中，转染进培养的细胞，然后观察其剪接情况。 It's the traditional functional validation method for splice variants, but it has real limitations: the cells used are rarely cardiac-specific, it can't easily test variants that sit in long repetitive regions, and it sometimes gives an indeterminate result. 这是剪接变异传统的功能验证方法，但存在明显的局限性：所用的细胞很少是心脏特异性的，难以检测位于长重复区域中的变异，而且有时会得出不确定的结果。 Using actual patient myocardium sidesteps all three problems at once, at the cost of needing access to that tissue in the first place — which is the whole reason this approach isn't more widespread. 使用真实的患者心肌组织可以同时避开这三个问题，但代价是首先需要能够获取到这类组织——这也正是这种方法尚未被广泛采用的根本原因。

JORDAN Okay — so what was the actual scientific question driving this, beyond "let's build a better splice model"? 好——那么除了"造一个更好的剪接模型"之外，驱动这项研究的实际科学问题是什么？

ALEX Congenital heart disease, or CHD, is the most common congenital anomaly, occurring in roughly one percent of live births, and it clearly has a strong genetic component — but around ninety percent of sporadic, isolated cases still have no explained genetic cause after standard clinical testing. 先天性心脏病（congenital heart disease，CHD）是最常见的先天性畸形，发生率约占活产儿的百分之一，它显然具有很强的遗传成分——但大约百分之九十的散发性、孤立性病例，在经过标准临床检测后仍然找不到明确的遗传病因。 Standard clinical testing typically means panel or exome sequencing, both of which are built around protein-coding exons and can't reliably see deep intronic variants — anything more than roughly fifty base pairs into an intron falls outside their effective range. 标准临床检测通常指的是基因panel或外显子组测序，两者都是围绕蛋白编码外显子设计的，无法可靠地发现深内含子变异——任何距离内含子边界超过约50个碱基对的位置，都超出了它们的有效检测范围。 So the working hypothesis was that some meaningful fraction of that ninety percent "unexplained" pool is sitting in the introns, invisible to conventional testing but potentially visible through genome sequencing plus RNA evidence. 因此该研究的工作假设是：这"90%未解释"的病例中，有相当一部分变异实际上就藏在内含子里——常规检测看不见，但通过基因组测序加RNA证据是有可能被发现的。

JORDAN What was the study actually built on — cohort size, data types? 这项研究的实际数据基础是什么——队列规模、数据类型？

ALEX The overall cohort was 1101 CHD probands, mostly tetralogy of Fallot and dextro-transposition of the great arteries, drawn from three centers — Toronto, Sydney, and Amsterdam. 总队列包含1101名CHD先证者，主要为法洛四联症（tetralogy of Fallot）和大动脉转位（dextro-transposition of the great arteries）患者，来自三个中心——多伦多、悉尼和阿姆斯特丹。 Of those, 154 patients had both genome sequencing and myocardial RNA-Seq available — 106 formed a Discovery cohort used to train the model, and 48 formed a held-out Validation cohort used purely for testing. 其中154名患者同时具备基因组测序和心肌RNA测序数据——106名构成用于训练模型的"发现队列"（Discovery cohort），48名构成完全用于测试的独立"验证队列"（Validation cohort）。 A second validation set of 43 unrelated cardiomyopathy patients tested whether the model generalized to a related but distinct cardiac disease. 另一组由43名无关的心肌病（cardiomyopathy）患者组成的第二验证集，用于测试该模型是否能推广到一种相关但不同的心脏疾病。 The remaining 947 CHD probands — the Extension cohort — had genome sequencing but no myocardial RNA-Seq, so the trained model was applied to them blind, as the real test of clinical utility. 剩余的947名CHD先证者——"扩展队列"（Extension cohort）——只有基因组测序数据、没有心肌RNA测序数据，因此训练好的模型是在完全不知道RNA证据的情况下应用到他们身上的，这才是对其临床实用性的真正检验。 Controls came from 2570 genome sequences in the Medical Genome Reference Bank, an Australian population reference cohort. 对照组来自澳大利亚人群参考队列——医学基因组参考库（Medical Genome Reference Bank）中的2570例基因组测序数据。

JORDAN How did they actually build the model — what features, what algorithm? 他们具体是怎么构建这个模型的——用了哪些特征、哪种算法？

ALEX They used a random forest, R's randomForest package with default parameters, which is a deliberately simple, interpretable choice rather than a deep architecture — reasonable, given the training set was only 106 patients. 他们使用了随机森林（random forest），也就是R语言中默认参数的randomForest包，这是一个刻意选择的简单、可解释的模型，而非深度架构——考虑到训练集只有106名患者，这个选择是合理的。 They built four progressively richer models to isolate which feature types actually mattered. 他们构建了四个特征逐渐丰富的模型，用来分离出究竟是哪类特征真正起作用。 Model 1 used only the SpliceAI delta score. 模型1仅使用SpliceAI的delta分数。 Model 2 dropped SpliceAI entirely and used DNA-only features instead — distance to the nearest annotated splice junction, variant type, and whether the variant sat in a branchpoint, low-complexity, or repetitive region. 模型2完全弃用SpliceAI，改用纯DNA特征——距离最近已知剪接位点的距离、变异类型，以及该变异是否位于分支点（branchpoint）、低复杂度或重复区域。 Model 3 added the gene's median myocardial expression level, measured in RNA-Seq as TPM, on top of the model 2 features. 模型3在模型2特征的基础上，加入了该基因在心肌中的中位表达水平——以RNA测序中的TPM值衡量。 Model 4, the one they ultimately used, combined everything: SpliceAI score, the DNA positional features, and myocardial gene expression together. 模型4，也就是他们最终采用的模型，把所有信息都结合了起来：SpliceAI分数、DNA位置特征，以及心肌基因表达水平。 The ground truth for training and testing was whether a candidate variant's gene showed a matching FRASER-confirmed outlier splicing event within a 100-base-pair window of the predicted disruption site — variants near an event but outside that window were labeled indeterminate and excluded rather than forced into either class. 训练和测试所用的真值标签，是该候选变异所在的基因是否在预测破坏位点±100碱基对的窗口内出现了经FRASER确认的异常剪接事件——落在事件附近但窗口之外的变异被标记为"不确定"并被排除，而不是被强行归入某一类别。

JORDAN I'd push back on the random forest choice a little — with only 106 training patients and presumably a very imbalanced positive class, doesn't that risk overfitting or just noisy feature-importance estimates? 我对随机森林这个选择有点保留意见——只有106名训练患者，而且推测正类样本严重不平衡，这难道不会有过拟合的风险，或者导致特征重要性估计很不稳定吗？

ALEX That's a fair concern, and it's exactly the kind of thing worth checking before reusing this design — the paper does mention weighting for class imbalance, and the independent 48-patient validation cohort is really doing the heavy lifting to show the model isn't just overfit noise, since performance held up on data the model never touched during training. 这个担心很合理，也正是在借鉴这套设计之前值得核查的地方——论文中确实提到了针对类别不平衡进行了加权处理，而独立的48名患者验证队列在证明模型不是单纯过拟合噪声这件事上起到了关键作用，因为模型在训练阶段从未接触过的数据上依然保持了良好表现。 A simple, low-variance model like a random forest is also arguably the right conservative choice at this sample size — a bigger neural architecture would likely overfit even harder on 106 patients. 在这样的样本规模下，像随机森林这样简单、低方差的模型，其实也可以说是更稳妥的保守选择——一个更大的神经网络架构在106名患者身上大概率会过拟合得更严重。

JORDAN Let's get to results. 我们来看结果。 Did the heart-specific model actually beat SpliceAI alone? 心脏特异性模型真的比单独使用SpliceAI表现更好吗？
