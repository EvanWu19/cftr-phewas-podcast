Track C — MagicalRsq: Machine-learning-based genotype imputation quality calibration

Blurb. Sun et al. (Am J Hum Genet, 2022) tackle a sharp corner of imputation: the standard per-variant quality metric (minimac's Rsq / IMPUTE's INFO) is well-calibrated for common variants but drifts badly for rare and low-frequency variants — exactly the ones that matter for CF. MagicalRsq is an XGBoost model that predicts true R² from standard Rsq plus minor-allele-frequency and population-genetics summary statistics, trained in three frequency-stratified models. Built on WGS from 1,992 Cystic Fibrosis Genome Project (CFGP) samples, it beat standard Rsq by net gains of 1.4M rare, 117k low-frequency, and 18k common variants when applied to an independent 3,103-sample set; gains held for European and African ancestry and replicated on UK Biobank exomes. Notably stress-tested in the ±10 Mb region around CFTR. Code: github.com/quansun98/MagicalRsq. Where it lands: summary-stats colocalization / modifier catalog (long-game WGS). Buildable artifact: a rare-variant imputation-quality re-scoring filter for known CF modifier loci.

Plain-English glossary

  • Calibration — whether a quality score's claim ("well-imputed") matches reality (the true R²). (kept in English for the metric)（中文：校准，质量分数所声称的"填补得好"是否与真实情况一致。）
  • Genotype imputation — statistically inferring unmeasured genotypes by matching measured variants against a dense reference panel.（中文：基因型填补，通过把已测变异与稠密参考面板比对来统计推断未测基因型。）
  • Haplotype — a stretch of DNA variants inherited together.（中文：单倍型，一起遗传的一段 DNA 变异。）
  • Linkage disequilibrium (LD) — the correlation between nearby variants that makes imputation possible.（中文：连锁不平衡，使填补成为可能的邻近变异间的相关性。）
  • Minor allele frequency (MAF) — frequency of the less common allele; defines common (>5%), low-frequency (0.5–5%), and rare (<0.5%) bins here.（中文：次要等位基因频率，较少见等位基因的频率，用于划分常见/低频/罕见变异。）
  • Reference panel — a large set of fully sequenced genomes used as the template for imputation (e.g., TOPMed, 1000 Genomes).（中文：参考面板，用作填补模板的大量完整测序基因组，如 TOPMed、1000 Genomes。）
  • Rsq / INFO — the standard built-in per-variant imputation quality metric from minimac / IMPUTE.（中文：Rsq / INFO，minimac / IMPUTE 软件内置的标准每变异填补质量指标。）
  • True R² — the actual squared correlation between imputed and real sequenced genotypes; the supervised label MagicalRsq predicts.（中文：真实 R²，填补基因型与真实测序基因型间的真正平方相关性，是 MagicalRsq 要预测的监督标签。）
  • WGS / WES — whole-genome / whole-exome sequencing.（中文：WGS/WES，全基因组/全外显子测序。）

Full citation

Sun Q, Yang Y, Rosen JD, et al. MagicalRsq: Machine-learning-based genotype imputation quality calibration. Am J Hum Genet. 2022;109(11):1986–1997. PMID: 36198314 · PMC: PMC9674945 · DOI: 10.1016/j.ajhg.2022.09.009

Methods / tools to lift

  • MagicalRsq — XGBoost regression predicting true R² from standard Rsq + estimated MAF + population-genetics summary statistics; three frequency-stratified models (common / low-frequency / rare).
  • MagicalRsq-cross — cross-population variant designed to transfer the calibration across ancestry groups.
  • Net-gain evaluation — count variants correctly re-classified (well- vs poorly-imputed) by the new score minus those it gets wrong relative to the baseline.
  • Locus stress test — restrict to a region (±10 Mb around CFTR) and train region-only models to probe local performance.
  • Repo — github.com/quansun98/MagicalRsq.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves summary-stats colocalization / modifier catalog (long-game WGS). Paste-ready MVP: Notebook that runs MagicalRsq over imputed dosages at a curated list of CF modifier regions (e.g., SLC26A9, SLC9A3 neighborhoods, CFTR locus) and outputs per variant {standard Rsq, MagicalRsq score, keep/drop flag}, so downstream modifier analyses start from a calibrated quality filter rather than a blanket threshold. Input: imputed dosage files + per-variant summary statistics. Difficulty: M (model is published/forkable; effort is feature assembly + wiring onto the region list).

What to verify first

  • Training labels are CFGP-derived and ancestry-skewed — check calibration for the ancestry composition of your target sample; consider MagicalRsq-cross if mismatched.
  • Score depends on the imputation reference panel — a keep-list built under one panel may not hold under another (reference-panel-specific variants hinder transfer).
  • MagicalRsq predicts imputation quality, not biological effect — a well-imputed variant is not a real modifier; this is upstream plumbing, never association evidence.
  • Confirm per-variant summary-statistic features are computed identically to the paper — a feature-definition mismatch silently miscalibrates everything.

Gap-map / catalog

  • Closes: the "which rare imputed variants can I trust at a modifier locus" gap — provides a calibrated filter, demonstrated in the CFGP cohort and around CFTR itself.
  • Leaves open: no new modifier gene–phenotype finding (purely methodological); reference-panel and ancestry transferability for any specific downstream cohort; bridge from imputation quality to biological effect.
  • catalog: no new genes (methodological paper; SLC26A9/SLC9A3/CFTR appear as application targets, not new findings).

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到读paper时间。 Today's Track C paper is "MagicalRsq: Machine-learning-based genotype imputation quality calibration," by Quan Sun and colleagues, with Yun Li as senior author, published in the American Journal of Human Genetics in 2022. 今天 Track C 的论文是 Quan Sun 等人、Yun Li 为通讯作者、2022 年发表在《美国人类遗传学杂志》上的《MagicalRsq：基于机器学习的基因型填补质量校准》。 It is open access, the code is on GitHub, and here is why it earns a Track C slot even though it is a methods paper: it is built on whole-genome sequencing from the Cystic Fibrosis Genome Project. 它是开放获取的，代码在 GitHub 上，而它之所以能占一个 Track C 名额——尽管是篇方法论文——原因在这里：它建立在囊性纤维化基因组计划 (CFGP) 的全基因组测序数据之上。 That is the same CF cohort whose modifier biology this track tracks. 那正是本 track 一直在追踪其修饰基因生物学的同一个 CF 队列。

JORDAN I'm going to need the genomics vocabulary before the machine learning. 我需要先把基因组学词汇搞清楚，再谈机器学习。 What is genotype imputation, and what is this "Rsq" thing we're trying to fix? 什么是基因型填补 (imputation)，我们要修的这个"Rsq"又是什么？

ALEX Let's build it up. 我们一步步来。 Most studies don't sequence everyone's whole genome — it's too expensive — so they genotype a sparse array of common variants and then impute the rest. 大多数研究不会给每个人做全基因组测序——太贵了——所以他们用基因分型芯片测一组稀疏的常见变异，然后填补其余的。 Imputation means statistically inferring the genotypes you didn't measure, by matching each person's measured variants against a dense reference panel of fully sequenced genomes. 填补的意思是：通过把每个人测到的变异和一个稠密的、完整测序的参考面板 (reference panel) 比对，统计性地推断你没测到的基因型。 Think of it as pattern-completion over haplotypes — stretches of DNA inherited together because of linkage disequilibrium, the correlation between nearby variants. 可以把它想成对单倍型 (haplotype，因连锁不平衡而一起遗传的 DNA 片段) 的模式补全；连锁不平衡 (linkage disequilibrium) 就是邻近变异之间的相关性。 Now, not every imputed genotype is trustworthy, so you need a per-variant quality score to decide which ones to keep. 然而，不是每个填补出来的基因型都可信，所以你需要一个每个变异的质量分数来决定保留哪些。 The field's standard score is called Rsq — minimac's Rsq, or IMPUTE's INFO — and it estimates quality by comparing the observed variation in the imputed dosages to what you'd expect. 这个领域的标准分数叫 Rsq——minimac 的 Rsq，或 IMPUTE 的 INFO（都是填补软件的内置质量指标）——它通过比较填补剂量的观测变异和期望变异来估计质量。 The whole problem this paper attacks: standard Rsq works fine for common variants above 5 percent frequency, but it is poorly calibrated for rare and low-frequency variants — exactly the variants that modern, ever-growing reference panels let you impute, and exactly the ones that matter for a disease like CF. 这篇论文要解决的核心问题是：标准 Rsq 对频率高于 5% 的常见变异工作良好，但对罕见和低频变异校准很差——而这些恰恰是现代不断扩张的参考面板让你能填补的变异，也恰恰是对 CF 这类疾病最关键的变异。

JORDAN So the metric you'd use to filter the most interesting variants is the metric that's least reliable there. 所以你用来过滤最有趣变异的指标，恰恰在那里最不可靠。 That's a nasty corner. 这是个讨厌的死角。

ALEX It is, and it's the motivating gap. 正是，这就是研究动机所在。 "Calibration" here means: when the score says a variant is well-imputed, is it actually well-imputed? 这里的"校准"意思是：当分数说一个变异填补得好，它是不是真的填补得好？ The ground truth is true R-squared — the actual squared correlation between imputed genotypes and the real sequenced genotypes, which you can only compute when you happen to have both. 真值是真实 R 平方 (true R²)——填补基因型和真实测序基因型之间真正的平方相关性，只有当你恰好两者都有时才能算出来。 Standard Rsq is supposed to estimate that true R-squared, but for rare variants it drifts. 标准 Rsq 本应估计这个真实 R 平方，但对罕见变异它会偏离。 Before this paper, the contested practice was just to apply a blanket Rsq threshold, say 0.3, and accept that you were throwing away good rare variants and keeping bad ones. 在这篇论文之前，有争议的做法就是套一个一刀切的 Rsq 阈值，比如 0.3，然后接受你在丢掉好的罕见变异、留下坏的罕见变异这一现实。 The authors' bet was that you can predict true R-squared much better by feeding a machine-learning model not just standard Rsq but a bundle of population-genetics features about each variant. 作者的押注是：你可以更好地预测真实 R 平方——只要给一个机器学习模型喂的不只是标准 Rsq，还有一束关于每个变异的群体遗传学特征。 The deeper idea is that imputation quality is not a single-number property; it depends on the variant's frequency, its local haplotype context, and how the reference panel represents it. 更深的想法是：填补质量不是一个单一数值的属性；它取决于变异的频率、它的局部单倍型背景，以及参考面板如何表示它。

JORDAN Why is the rare-variant corner the one that actually matters for CF? 为什么罕见变异这个死角，对 CF 才是真正要紧的？

ALEX Because CF genetics lives in the rare end of the spectrum. 因为 CF 遗传学就活在频谱的罕见那一端。 CFTR itself has hundreds of disease-causing variants, many of them individually rare, and the modifier story — the genes beyond CFTR that tune disease severity — increasingly points to low-frequency and rare variation too. CFTR 本身有数百个致病变异，其中许多单个看是罕见的，而修饰基因的故事——CFTR 之外、调节疾病严重度的基因——也越来越指向低频和罕见变异。 If your quality metric is only reliable above 5 percent frequency, you are well-calibrated precisely where the biology is least interesting and miscalibrated precisely where it's most interesting. 如果你的质量指标只在 5% 频率以上可靠，那你恰好在生物学最无趣的地方校准良好，在最有趣的地方失校准。 And the cost of miscalibration cuts both ways: a falsely optimistic Rsq lets a badly-imputed variant into your analysis and creates spurious associations, while a falsely pessimistic Rsq throws away a real signal you paid to collect. 而失校准的代价是双向的：一个虚高乐观的 Rsq 让填补很差的变异混进你的分析、制造虚假关联，而一个虚低悲观的 Rsq 则丢掉一个你花钱采集来的真实信号。 In a disease with a modest cohort size, throwing away real rare variants is especially painful, because you can't easily recover power by adding samples. 在一个队列规模不大的疾病里，丢掉真实的罕见变异尤其痛苦，因为你没法靠加样本轻松找回统计功效。 So a better-calibrated rare-variant quality score isn't a nicety; it directly changes how many usable variants survive into a modifier analysis. 所以一个校准更好的罕见变异质量分数不是锦上添花；它直接改变有多少可用变异能存活进入修饰基因分析。 That's the practical stake, and it's why a CF group built this rather than, say, a general population-genetics lab. 这就是实际的利害关系，也是为什么是一个 CF 团队、而不是比如一个普通群体遗传学实验室建了它。

JORDAN Walk me through what they built. 带我过一遍他们建了什么。

ALEX MagicalRsq is a supervised gradient-boosted-trees model — they use XGBoost — that predicts true R-squared for each variant from a set of variant-level features. MagicalRsq 是一个有监督的梯度提升树模型——他们用 XGBoost——从一组变异层面的特征预测每个变异的真实 R 平方。 The features include standard Rsq itself, the estimated minor allele frequency, and additional population-genetics summary statistics for the variant. 这些特征包括标准 Rsq 本身、估计的次要等位基因频率 (minor allele frequency)，以及该变异的其他群体遗传学汇总统计量。 To train it, you need true R-squared for a subset — meaning a subset of individuals who actually have sequencing, so you can compute the real correlation as a label. 要训练它，你需要一个子集的真实 R 平方——也就是一个真正有测序数据的个体子集，这样你才能算出真实相关性作为标签。 They train three separate models, split by frequency: one for common variants above 5 percent, one for low-frequency between half a percent and 5 percent, and one for rare below half a percent. 他们按频率训练三个独立的模型：一个用于 5% 以上的常见变异，一个用于 0.5% 到 5% 的低频变异，一个用于 0.5% 以下的罕见变异。 That stratification matters because the failure mode is frequency-dependent, so one model would smear across regimes. 这个分层很重要，因为失效模式是依赖频率的，一个模型会把不同情形糊在一起。 The CF connection is concrete: they leverage WGS from 1,992 CFGP sequenced samples to derive training labels. CF 的关联很具体：他们利用 1,992 个 CFGP 已测序样本的全基因组测序数据来构建训练标签。 ALEX's pushback on a design choice: training labels come from a CF cohort, which is enriched for European ancestry and for a specific disease, so the learned mapping from features to true R-squared could carry cohort-specific quirks. 我对一个设计选择的质疑：训练标签来自一个 CF 队列，它在欧洲血统和某一特定疾病上是富集的，所以从特征到真实 R 平方学到的映射，可能带有队列特有的怪癖。 They partly anticipate this with a cross-population variant called MagicalRsq-cross, designed to transfer across ancestry groups. 他们部分预见到了这点，做了一个叫 MagicalRsq-cross 的跨群体变体，设计用来在不同祖源群体间迁移。

JORDAN Help me understand why population-genetics features would even help here. 帮我理解为什么群体遗传学特征在这里会有帮助。 What does a variant's frequency or its neighbors tell you about whether it was imputed well? 一个变异的频率或它的邻居，能告诉你它填补得好不好吗？

ALEX It tells you a surprising amount, and this is the conceptual heart of the paper. 能告诉你的出乎意料地多，这正是这篇论文的概念核心。 Imputation works by borrowing information from haplotypes in the reference panel, so a variant's imputability depends on how well its local haplotype is represented there. 填补的原理是从参考面板的单倍型借信息，所以一个变异能否被填补好，取决于它的局部单倍型在面板里被表示得有多好。 A rare variant sitting on a haplotype that the panel barely captures will get a deceptively confident standard Rsq, because that metric only looks at the dosage variance, not at whether the underlying haplotype support is thin. 一个坐落在面板几乎没捕捉到的单倍型上的罕见变异，会得到一个虚高自信的标准 Rsq，因为那个指标只看剂量方差，不看底层单倍型支持是否单薄。 Population-genetics summary statistics are proxies for exactly that hidden context — they carry signal about a variant's evolutionary age, its local diversity, and how it sits relative to neighbors. 群体遗传学汇总统计量正是那种隐藏上下文的代理——它们携带关于变异的演化年龄、局部多样性，以及它相对邻居位置的信号。 So the model isn't doing magic; it's reading side-channels that correlate with the true haplotype support that standard Rsq is blind to. 所以模型不是在变魔术；它在读那些与真实单倍型支持相关、而标准 Rsq 看不见的旁路信号。 That's also why frequency stratification helps — the relationship between those side-channels and true R-squared is shaped differently for rare versus common variants. 这也是为什么频率分层有帮助——那些旁路信号和真实 R 平方的关系，对罕见变异和常见变异是不同形状的。

JORDAN And does the machine-learned score actually beat the standard one? 那这个机器学习分数真的打败了标准分数吗？

ALEX It does, and the flagship number is striking. 是的，而且旗舰数字很惊人。 When they trained models from the 1,992 CFGP sequenced samples and applied them to an independent 3,103 samples that had no sequencing — only TOPMed imputation from array genotypes — MagicalRsq beat standard Rsq by net gains of 1.4 million rare, 117 thousand low-frequency, and 18 thousand common variants. 当他们用 1,992 个 CFGP 已测序样本训练模型、再应用到一个独立的、没有测序——只有从芯片基因型做 TOPMed 填补——的 3,103 个样本上时，MagicalRsq 比标准 Rsq 净多正确区分了 140 万个罕见、11.7 万个低频、1.8 万个常见变异。 "Net gain" here means the number of variants MagicalRsq correctly classified as well- or poorly-imputed that standard Rsq got wrong, minus the reverse. 这里"净增益"指的是 MagicalRsq 正确分类（为填补好或差）而标准 Rsq 弄错的变异数，减去反方向的情况。 So 1.4 million rare variants is the headline: that is an enormous recovery of usable rare-variant calls, the exact currency of CF genetics. 所以 140 万个罕见变异是重点：这是对可用罕见变异判定的巨大回收，而罕见变异正是 CF 遗传学的硬通货。 The gains held for both European and African ancestry samples, and they replicated the approach on UK Biobank whole-exome data, so it isn't a CF-only fluke. 收益在欧洲和非洲血统样本上都成立，而且他们在 UK Biobank 全外显子数据上复现了这个方法，所以不是 CF 独有的偶然。 There's one detail Runyu will love: in one experiment they restricted to the ±10 megabase region around CFTR on chromosome 7 and trained models using only variants in that region. 有一个细节 Runyu 会喜欢：在一个实验里他们限定在 7 号染色体 CFTR 周围 ±10 兆碱基的区域，只用该区域的变异训练模型。 They explicitly note that CFTR mutations are the molecular basis of CF, making that locus a region of high imputation interest. 他们明确指出 CFTR 突变是 CF 的分子基础，使该基因座成为填补价值很高的区域。 So the method was stress-tested in exactly the genomic neighborhood that matters most for this disease. 所以这个方法恰恰是在对这个疾病最重要的那段基因组邻域里做了压力测试。

JORDAN One more on the method — why does it matter so much that they tested it across reference panels and ancestries? 关于方法再问一个——他们跨参考面板和跨祖源测试，为什么这么重要？
