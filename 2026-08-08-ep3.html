Track C — TAS2R38 genotype and CRS severity in children with cystic fibrosis

Blurb

Cantone and colleagues (Heliyon, 2025), from the University of Naples "Federico II," report a genuine new pediatric modifier-gene finding rather than a method paper. In a retrospective case-control study of 69 CF children (vs. 270 healthy controls), TAS2R38 — a bitter-taste receptor also expressed in sinonasal epithelium, where it triggers a nitric-oxide antibacterial response — showed no altered genotype frequency in CF versus controls (it's not a CF-susceptibility gene), but within the CF group, non-functional AVI/AVI children had significantly worse chronic rhinosinusitis (CRS) severity on both endoscopic (p=0.031) and CT (p=0.03) scoring than PAV-carrying children. The study also reports, for the first time in a pediatric CF population, an inverse correlation between age at first Pseudomonas aeruginosa infection and CRS severity (r=−0.34, p=0.03). TAS2R38 genotype correlated strongly with the directly measured PROP bitter-taste phenotype (p=0.0001), and CFTR genotype itself showed no relationship to TAS2R38 distribution, confirming the two act independently. This is a single-locus candidate-gene result, not a genome-wide signal — the natural next move is checking whether it has any life at the summary-statistics level. Buildable artifact: tas2r38-crs-signal-scan, a lookup-and-flag tool querying GWAS Catalog/Open Targets for any CF sinonasal-outcome association signal at the TAS2R38 locus.

Plain-English glossary

  • Chronic rhinosinusitis (CRS) — persistent inflammation of the sinuses and nasal passages; in CF, driven by the same thick, poorly cleared mucus that causes lung disease.（中文:鼻窦和鼻腔的持续性炎症;在CF患者中,由导致肺部疾病的同一种粘稠、难以清除的黏液所驱动。）
  • Unified airway hypothesis — the idea that the upper and lower airways share one continuous mucosal surface and therefore share disease mechanisms.（中文:上呼吸道和下呼吸道共享同一连续黏膜表面、因此共享疾病机制的假说。）
  • TAS2R38 — a bitter-taste receptor gene expressed in sinonasal epithelial cells, where it senses bacterial quorum-sensing molecules and triggers a nitric-oxide antibacterial/mucociliary-clearance response.（中文:一种苦味受体基因,表达于鼻窦上皮细胞,能感知细菌群体感应分子并触发一氧化氮抗菌/纤毛清除反应。）
  • PAV / AVI haplotypes — the two common functional variants of TAS2R38; PAV is functional (bitter-tasting), AVI is non-functional (non-tasting), with PAV/PAV "supertasters," PAV/AVI intermediate, and AVI/AVI "non-tasters."（中文:TAS2R38的两种常见功能变异单倍型;PAV为功能性(能尝出苦味),AVI为非功能性(尝不出苦味)。）
  • PROP (propylthiouracil) taste test — a cheap, direct bitterness-rating test used as an indirect but strongly correlated proxy for TAS2R38 genotype.（中文:一种廉价、直接的苦味评分测试,作为TAS2R38基因型的间接但高度相关的替代指标。）
  • Gene modifier — a gene separate from CFTR that doesn't cause CF by itself but shifts how severe the disease turns out to be once present.（中文:一个独立于CFTR的基因,本身不致病,但会影响已患病个体的疾病严重程度。）
  • Lund-Kennedy (LK) / Lund-Mackay (LM) scores — standardized severity-scoring systems for CRS, LK via nasal endoscopy and LM via CT imaging.（中文:CRS的标准化严重程度评分系统,LK通过鼻内镜评分,LM通过CT影像评分。）

Full citation

Cantone E, Tosco A, Sepe A, Raia V, Negri R, Castaldo A, Cimbalo C, Pezzella P, Russo MB, Grimaldi G, Di Nola C, Greco L. (2025). TAS2R38 genotype and CRS severity in children with cystic fibrosis. Heliyon. PMID: 39866409 | PMC11761314 | DOI: 10.1016/j.heliyon.2025.e41716

Methods / tools to lift

  • Dual severity scoring (endoscopic Lund-Kennedy + CT Lund-Mackay) paired with a cheap, validated behavioral proxy (PROP taste test) for a receptor genotype that's otherwise expensive to assay directly.
  • Power calculation grounded in known population diplotype frequencies (~20% AVI/AVI, 52% PAV/AVI, 28% PAV/PAV) before enrollment, rather than an arbitrary sample-size target.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Summary-stats colocalization at known modifier loci. Buildable: tas2r38-crs-signal-scan (S) — query GWAS Catalog / Open Targets Genetics for any published CF sinonasal/CRS/Pseudomonas-colonization association signal at or near the TAS2R38 locus (chr7q34); if a signal exists, escalate to formal colocalization against nasal/sinonasal epithelium eQTL data; a null result is itself informative, flagging TAS2R38 as a candidate for future modifier-locus panel design pending independent replication.

What to verify first

  • Single center, 69 cases — replication in an independent, adequately powered cohort needed, especially for the underpowered 17-child modulator-therapy subgroup.
  • Multiple outcomes tested (LK, LM, two QoL instruments, PA-colonization timing) without an explicit multiple-comparisons correction reported — read marginal p-values skeptically.
  • PROP taste test is an indirect behavioral proxy for genotype (p=0.0001 correlation, but not identity) — default to direct TAS2R38 genotyping where available.
  • The age-at-first-PA-infection/CRS-severity correlation (r=−0.34) is modest — explains only part of the variance, not a strong standalone predictor.

Gap-map / catalog

  • CLOSES: first pediatric-specific (age-stratified) CF cohort demonstrating TAS2R38 as a CRS-severity modifier, and the first report in pediatric CF of an inverse correlation between age at first Pseudomonas infection and CRS severity.
  • LEAVES OPEN: whether TAS2R38 carries independent genetic-association evidence at the GWAS/summary-statistics level; replication in a larger, ideally multicenter, pediatric cohort; whether the modulator-therapy subgroup null result reflects a real attenuated effect or is simply underpowered (n=17).
