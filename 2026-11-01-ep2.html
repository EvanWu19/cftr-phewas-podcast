Ep2 (Track B — AI tooling): A Foundation Model of Cancer Genotype (MutationProjector)

Kong et al.'s 2026 Cancer Discovery paper introduces MutationProjector, a foundation model pretrained on genomic alteration data from more than 30,000 solid tumors across ten cancer types (AACR Project GENIE + TCGA), using a graph attention network with eight interaction-specific attention heads drawing on eight molecular networks (physical binding, transcriptional regulation, phosphorylation, ubiquitination, genetic interactions, DNA damage repair, STRING, PCNet). Training is purely self-supervised — masked gene prediction, reconstructing a gene's hidden alteration status from the rest of the tumor's genotype and the network structure, with no outcome labels involved — and the resulting embedding, with no fine-tuning, already recovers tissue type, known driver subtypes, HPV status, and basal/luminal bladder subtypes without ever being given those labels. Fed into a plain random forest, the same pretrained embedding transferred to three separate small-cohort clinical tasks — immunotherapy response, chemotherapy response, metastasis prediction — beating current single-gene biomarkers (PD-L1, TMB, microsatellite status) and classical ML baselines, using cohorts of under 250 patients for training in each case. The transferable idea for Runyu isn't the oncology finding — it's the pretrain-once/fine-tune-many architecture itself, which the episode maps to a CF-registry genotype embedding prototype. Code ships at github.com/idekerlab/MutationProjector.

Plain-English glossary

Aneuploidy — Whole chromosome arms gained or lost in a tumor genome, a coarser genomic-instability signal than individual point mutations.（中文：肿瘤基因组中整条染色体臂的增加或丢失，是比单个点突变更粗粒度的基因组不稳定性信号。）

Co-occurrence (gene pairs) — Two genes' disruptive mutations appearing together in the same tumor more often than chance would predict, suggesting a cooperative relationship.（中文：两个基因的破坏性突变在同一肿瘤中共同出现的频率高于随机预期，提示两者之间存在协同关系。）

Copy-number alteration (CNA) — A gene being amplified (extra copies) or deleted (lost) rather than point-mutated.（中文：基因被扩增（多出拷贝）或缺失（丢失），而非点突变。）

Graph attention network (GAT) — A neural network architecture that applies attention mechanisms over graph-structured data, here one attention head per molecular interaction network.（中文：一种对图结构数据使用注意力机制的神经网络架构，此处每个相互作用网络对应一个注意力头。）

Masked gene prediction — A self-supervised training task where the model reconstructs a gene's hidden alteration status from the rest of the genotype, with no outcome label required.（中文：一种自监督训练任务，模型仅凭基因型其余部分重建某个被隐藏基因的改变状态，不需要任何结局标签。）

Mutational signature — A characteristic pattern of mutation types that points back to the biological process that caused them (e.g., APOBEC enzyme activity, mismatch-repair deficiency).（中文：一种特征性的突变类型模式，能反推出造成这些突变的生物学过程，如APOBEC酶活性或错配修复缺陷。）

Mutual exclusivity (gene pairs) — Two genes whose disruptive mutations almost never co-occur in the same tumor, suggesting redundant pathway membership.（中文：两个基因的破坏性突变几乎从不在同一肿瘤中共同出现，提示两者在同一通路中功能冗余。）

RECIST — The standard clinical criteria (Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors) for scoring whether a tumor shrank, stayed stable, or grew on imaging after treatment.（中文：实体瘤疗效评价标准，评判治疗后肿瘤在影像上缩小、稳定还是增大的标准临床标准。）

Tumor mutational burden (TMB) — The count of mutations per megabase of sequenced tumor DNA; higher TMB correlates with immune visibility and immunotherapy response.（中文：每百万碱基测序肿瘤DNA中的突变计数，TMB越高通常与免疫系统可见度和免疫治疗反应相关。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • MutationProjector (GAT architecture) — graph attention network, one head per molecular interaction network type, pretrained via masked gene prediction.
  • Molecular interaction network resources — BioPlex, SIGNOR, SignaLink, TRRUST, PhosphoSitePlus, UbiNet/UbiBrowser, STRING, PCNet — the network layer that makes the attention mechanism biologically grounded.
  • Attention-based feature importance — linear probing on attention weights to identify which genes/gene-pairs drove a given prediction, for interpretability.
  • Code: github.com/idekerlab/MutationProjector

Rough difficulty: L.

What to verify first

  • Whether an analogous gene-gene relationship structure (physical interaction, functional interaction) exists — or would need building from scratch — for CFTR-adjacent/modifier genes.
  • Whether a pretrain-then-transfer approach holds up on downstream cohorts much smaller or more heterogeneous than the hundreds-to-low-thousands-patient cohorts used here.
  • Independent replication of the chromatin-remodeler (ARID1A/SMARCA4/KMT2D) immunotherapy-response finding before treating it as established, despite STK11's agreement with prior literature lending it some credibility.
  • A documented, deliberate justification for whatever gene set any CF-adjacent pretraining effort chose to use, mirroring this paper's own panel-gene-only limitation.
