Track A — Making sense of missense (Molotkov 2024)

Blurb. A short, opinionated review of missense variant pathogenicity prediction that steps back from leaderboards to ask what these tools deliver in the clinic. It splits predictors into supervised (trained on ClinVar/HGMD labels — accurate to target but bias-inheriting) and unsupervised (AlphaMissense and CPT — learn from sequence/structure, dodging label bias). AlphaMissense benchmarks strongly but not uniquely (2nd on 36-protein MAVE behind CPT; 3rd on clinical data behind SNPred and MetaRNN), and the review documents its failure mode: it conflates damages the protein with causes disease — 15/18 confirmed-benign variants misclassified in one gene (83% FPR), and CPA1 loss-of-function (benign) repeatedly called pathogenic. The recommended 0.56 threshold was calibrated on bulk ClinVar with no per-gene guarantee. Where it lands: the Karen variant-interpretation collaboration. Buildable artifact: a CFTR-specific AlphaMissense audit-and-recalibration notebook that finds the CFTR operating threshold against CFTR2 + ClinVar and flags model-vs-function discordances as the functional-work shortlist.

Plain-English glossary.

  • AlphaMissense — Google DeepMind's unsupervised missense pathogenicity predictor, built on AlphaFold2's structure model.（中文：基于 AlphaFold2 结构模型的无监督错义变异致病性预测工具。）
  • Conservation (evolutionary) — how unchanged a protein position has stayed across related species; high conservation implies a change there is more likely to matter.（中文：某蛋白质位置在相关物种间的保守程度；越保守，该处变异越可能有害。）
  • Gain-of-function / loss-of-function — a variant that adds/increases activity vs one that reduces/removes it; disease direction can differ (CPA1: gain causes pancreatitis, loss is benign).（中文：功能获得型变异增强活性、功能丧失型降低或消除活性；致病方向可能相反。）
  • HGMD — Human Gene Mutation Database, a curated store of disease-associated mutations used to train supervised predictors.（中文：人类基因突变数据库，用于训练有监督预测器的疾病相关突变库。）
  • MAVE — multiplexed assay of variant effect; a high-throughput lab experiment measuring the functional impact of thousands of variants at once.（中文：变异效应多重实验，一次高通量测量数千个变异的功能影响。）
  • Missense / nonsense / synonymous variant — amino-acid swap / premature stop / silent change, respectively; missense is the ambiguous case.（中文：错义=氨基酸替换、无义=提前终止、同义=沉默改变；错义最难判断。）
  • VUS — variant of uncertain significance; a variant that can't yet be called pathogenic or benign.（中文：意义未明变异，暂时无法判定致病或良性。）

Full citation. Molotkov I, et al. Making sense of missense: challenges and opportunities in variant pathogenicity prediction. Dis Model Mech. 2024. PMID 39676521 · PMC PMC11683568 · DOI 10.1242/dmm.052218. (Full text via PubMed / PMC.)

Methods / tools to lift.

  • Per-gene threshold recalibration — don't trust the bulk-ClinVar 0.56 AlphaMissense cutoff; find the CFTR-specific operating point on a held-out set.
  • Discordance mapping — cross a predictor's calls against orthogonal functional labels (CFTR2) and clinical labels (ClinVar); treat disagreements as the uncertain set.
  • Tool-disagreement-as-signal — where two predictors using different information disagree, route the variant to functional work.
  • Leakage-safe evaluation — test on variants classified after the model's training cutoff.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Serves Karen / variant interpretation. MVP: cftr-alphamissense-audit-notebook — input = public AlphaMissense CFTR missense score table + CFTR2 functional classes + ClinVar; output = CFTR-specific threshold, calibration curve, and a ranked model-vs-function discordance list. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first.

  • Calibration honesty — CFTR threshold found on held-out data, not refit on the same variants.
  • Leakage — restrict the test set to post-cutoff ClinVar classifications.
  • Pathogenicity ≠ mechanism — the score won't distinguish gating / conductance / trafficking / splice; the notebook outputs triage + discordance, not a verdict.
  • Phenotype-specificity — "pathogenic for which manifestation?" (pancreatic / pulmonary / sweat / CFTR-related vs classic CF) stays a human call.
  • Review is secondary — spot-check the 83% FPR and benchmark ranks against the cited primary studies before quoting.

ALEX Your DNA codes for protein in three-letter words called codons, and each codon specifies one amino acid. 你的 DNA 以三个字母为一组的"密码子 (codon)"来编码蛋白质，每个密码子对应一个氨基酸 (amino acid)。 A synonymous variant changes a letter but lands on the same amino acid, so the protein is unchanged and you usually shrug. 同义变异 (synonymous variant) 改变了一个字母，但对应的仍是同一个氨基酸，所以蛋白质没变，通常你可以不理会它。 A nonsense variant inserts a premature stop, truncating the protein, and that's often clearly damaging. 无义变异 (nonsense variant) 插入了一个提前终止信号，把蛋白质截断，这通常是明显有害的。 A missense variant swaps one amino acid for another, and that's the genuinely ambiguous middle. 错义变异则是把一个氨基酸替换成另一个，这才是真正模棱两可的中间地带。 It might do nothing, or it might wreck folding, stability, or catalytic activity — same class of change, wildly different consequences. 它可能什么都不影响，也可能破坏蛋白质的折叠、稳定性或催化活性——同一类改变，后果却天差地别。 That unpredictability is why missense is the prime target for prediction tools, and the prime source of the "variant of uncertain significance," the VUS. 正是这种不可预测性，使错义变异成为预测工具的主要攻克对象，也是"意义未明变异 (VUS，variant of uncertain significance)"的主要来源。
