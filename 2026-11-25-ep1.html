How did this episode land for you?

Highly Accurate Protein Structure Prediction with AlphaFold

Jumper and the DeepMind team's 2021 Nature paper is the structural-biology founding document underlying nearly every structure-based CFTR variant tool this show has covered: it is the original AlphaFold, the network that essentially closed CASP14's fifty-year "protein folding problem" by predicting atomic-accuracy 3D structures even for proteins with no close homolog already solved in the PDB. We read it today as infrastructure for Karen's CFTR variant-interpretation work — not as a new finding, but as the backbone every AlphaMissense-style or structure-based stability tool already assumes is sitting there, precomputed. The buildable artifact: a CFTR Structural-Confidence Mapper that pulls CFTR's existing AlphaFold DB structure (UniProt P13569), parses per-residue pLDDT confidence from the B-factor column, and flags whether a given CFTR VUS position sits in a high- or low-confidence region before anyone trusts a structure-based pathogenicity call on it.

Plain-English glossary

CASP — Critical Assessment of Structure Prediction: a blind, biennial competition in which organizers withhold recently solved, unpublished protein structures so every method is tested without having seen the answer.（中文：蛋白质结构预测关键评估竞赛，每两年一次的盲测比赛，组织者扣留尚未发表的新解析结构，确保所有方法都在盲测条件下接受检验。）

lDDT (local distance difference test) — a metric scoring how well a predicted structure's local atomic geometry matches the true structure, without requiring whole-chain alignment first.（中文：局部距离差异检验，一种无需先对齐整条链、就能评估预测结构局部原子几何与真实结构吻合程度的指标。）

MSA (multiple sequence alignment) — a column-by-column alignment of the same protein's sequence across many species or strains, used to read evolutionary conservation directly as a structural signal.（中文：多序列比对，把同一蛋白质在多个物种或菌株中的序列逐列对齐，用演化保守性来推断结构信号。）

PDB (Protein Data Bank) — the global public archive of experimentally solved protein structures, roughly 100,000 unique entries as of this paper, against billions of known sequences.（中文：蛋白质数据库，全球性的实验解析蛋白质结构公开档案库，本文发表时约有十万个独特条目，相对于数十亿条已知序列而言。）

pLDDT — AlphaFold's own per-residue self-estimated confidence score, correlated with (Pearson's r = 0.76) but not identical to true structural accuracy.（中文：AlphaFold对每个残基给出的自我评估置信度分数，与真实结构准确度存在相关性（皮尔逊相关系数r=0.76），但并不等同。）

TM-score / pTM — TM-score measures whole-chain global fold correctness against the true structure; pTM is AlphaFold's own predicted estimate of that score (r = 0.85 against true TM-score).（中文：TM-score衡量整条链相对真实结构的整体折叠正确性；pTM则是AlphaFold对这一分数的自我预测值（与真实TM-score的相关系数r=0.85）。）

Full citation

Jumper J, Evans R, Pritzel A, et al. Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold. Nature. 2021;596(7873):583-589. PMID: 34265844 | PMC8371605 | DOI: 10.1038/s41586-021-03819-2

Methods / tools to lift

  • AlphaFold DB — public, precomputed structure predictions for UniProt proteins, including CFTR (P13569); no local GPU or re-run required.
  • pLDDT via B-factor column parsing — a free, per-residue structural-confidence readout usable as a pre-filter before trusting any structure-based variant claim.
  • Evoformer / dual MSA + residue-pair representation architecture — the general "two representations, repeatedly cross-informing each other, refined under geometric consistency constraints" pattern that recurs conceptually across later variant-effect models (reference only, not for reimplementation).
  • Open-source code and trained weights: github.com/deepmind/alphafold.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation (method/infrastructure only — no claim about CFFPR's own data holdings).

Buildable artifact — CFTR Structural-Confidence Mapper (difficulty: S). Pull CFTR's existing precomputed AlphaFold DB structure file (UniProt P13569); parse per-residue pLDDT values from the B-factor column; build a small lookup that takes any CFTR VUS position (from CFTR2, ClinVar, or any other list) and returns its structural confidence tier plus whether it falls within one of CFTR's transmembrane-spanning segments — a pre-filter before trusting any downstream AlphaFold-structure-based pathogenicity call, not a verdict on its own.

What to verify first

  • pLDDT measures structural confidence, not functional or pathogenic consequence — a confidently folded residue can still be catalytically or functionally critical; high pLDDT is never a "benign" signal.
  • This training set is PDB-derived, and the PDB is historically skewed toward soluble, crystallizable, globular proteins; whether CFTR's multi-pass ABC-transporter transmembrane fold gets the same confidence-calibration quality as a typical soluble PDB protein needs checking directly against CFTR's own structure, not assumed from the paper's aggregate numbers.
  • The pLDDT-to-true-accuracy correlation (r = 0.76) leaves real scatter at the single-residue level; a borderline pLDDT score at one specific CFTR position deserves a second look rather than a single threshold treated as decisive.

ALEX Today's Track A pick is about as close to a founding document as this field has: "Highly Accurate Protein Structure Prediction with AlphaFold," John Jumper and the DeepMind team, published in Nature in 2021. 今天A赛道选的这篇论文，可以说是这个领域最接近"奠基性文献"的存在：《AlphaFold实现高精度蛋白质结构预测》（Highly Accurate Protein Structure Prediction with AlphaFold），作者是John Jumper和DeepMind团队，2021年发表于《自然》（Nature）杂志。

JORDAN The paper that won CASP14. 就是那篇赢得CASP14（蛋白质结构预测关键评估竞赛第14届）的论文。

ALEX Exactly — the one that effectively closed out the fifty-year "protein folding problem" as an open competition. 没错——就是那篇基本终结了长达五十年的"蛋白质折叠问题"（protein folding problem）这一公开竞赛难题的论文。 We're reading it today because every CFTR structure-based tool you've touched this year, including the AlphaFold-structure stability work in last week's episode, sits directly on top of what this paper built. 我们今天读它，是因为你今年接触过的每一个基于结构的CFTR工具——包括上周那期关于AlphaFold结构稳定性分析的节目——都直接建立在这篇论文的基础之上。

JORDAN Before we get into it, give me the vocabulary. 进入正题之前，先给我讲讲词汇。 What's a multiple sequence alignment? 什么是多序列比对（multiple sequence alignment）？

ALEX An MSA — multiple sequence alignment — is a stack of the same protein's sequence from many different species or strains, lined up column by column. MSA——也就是多序列比对——是把同一种蛋白质在许多不同物种或菌株中的序列叠放在一起，逐列对齐排列。 Columns that stay identical across a billion years of evolution are usually structurally or functionally load-bearing; columns that drift freely usually aren't. 在数十亿年演化中始终保持不变的那些列，通常在结构或功能上承担着关键作用；而自由漂变的列通常并不承担这种作用。 AlphaFold reads that conservation pattern directly as a structural signal. AlphaFold直接把这种保守性模式当作结构信号来读取。

JORDAN And the PDB? 那PDB呢？

ALEX Protein Data Bank — the global archive of experimentally solved structures. 全称是蛋白质数据库（Protein Data Bank），是全球实验解析结构的档案库。 Around 100,000 unique proteins, each one taking months to years of crystallography, cryo-EM, or NMR to solve. 大约收录了十万种独特的蛋白质，每一种都要耗费数月到数年时间，通过X射线晶体学、冷冻电镜（cryo-EM）或核磁共振（NMR）才能解析出来。 That's the entire labeled training set this field had, against billions of known protein sequences. 相对于数十亿条已知蛋白质序列而言，这就是这个领域当时所拥有的全部带标签训练数据。

JORDAN CASP? CASP呢？

ALEX Critical Assessment of Structure Prediction — a blind, biennial competition. 全称是"蛋白质结构预测关键评估"（Critical Assessment of Structure Prediction），是一项每两年举办一次的盲测竞赛。 Organizers hold back recently solved, unpublished structures, and every lab predicts them blind, so nobody can cheat by having already seen the answer. 组织者会扣留最近解析出但尚未发表的结构，让每个实验室都在盲测状态下进行预测，这样就没有人能靠提前看到答案来作弊。 CASP14, in 2020, is the one AlphaFold entered and essentially broke. 2020年的CASP14，正是AlphaFold参赛并基本上"打破"了的那一届。

JORDAN And the accuracy metrics — lDDT, pLDDT, TM-score? 那准确度指标呢——lDDT、pLDDT、TM-score？

ALEX lDDT, the local distance difference test, scores how well a predicted structure's local atomic geometry matches the true one, without needing to align whole chains first. lDDT，全称局部距离差异检验（local distance difference test），用来衡量预测结构的局部原子几何形状与真实结构的吻合程度，而不需要先把整条链对齐。 pLDDT is AlphaFold's own per-residue self-estimate of that same score, its built-in confidence output. pLDDT则是AlphaFold针对每个残基、对这同一个分数做出的自我评估，是它内置的置信度输出。 TM-score does the equivalent job at the whole-chain level, for global fold correctness, and pTM is AlphaFold's own prediction of that number. TM-score在整条链的层面上做同样的事，衡量整体折叠的正确性，而pTM就是AlphaFold对这个数值自己做出的预测。

ALEX So why did this matter? 那这篇论文为什么重要？ For about fifty years, predicting 3D structure from sequence alone was one of biology's grand open problems, split into two rival camps. 大约五十年来，仅凭序列预测三维结构一直是生物学中悬而未决的重大难题之一，分裂成两大对立阵营。

JORDAN Two camps meaning what? 两大阵营具体指什么？

ALEX One camp tried to simulate the actual physics directly — electrostatics, van der Waals forces, folding kinetics. 一个阵营试图直接模拟真实的物理过程——静电作用、范德华力、折叠动力学。 Theoretically clean, but computationally hopeless at protein scale; you can't brute-force simulate a protein folding in real time even on a supercomputer. 这在理论上很干净，但在蛋白质尺度上计算量根本无法承受；哪怕用超级计算机，也无法用暴力模拟的方式实时重现一个蛋白质的折叠过程。 The other camp went evolutionary: use MSAs and homology to already-solved structures to infer constraints statistically, without simulating any physics at all. 另一个阵营走演化路线：利用MSA以及与已解析结构的同源性，通过统计方法推断约束条件，完全不模拟任何物理过程。

JORDAN And neither camp was winning before this? 在这篇论文之前，这两个阵营都没能真正取得突破？

ALEX Not at the accuracy this paper claims. 至少没能达到这篇论文所宣称的精度。 Evolutionary and co-evolution methods — contact-map predictors, earlier deep-learning approaches — had improved steadily through CASP12 and CASP13, but still fell well short of atomic accuracy whenever no close homologous structure existed in the PDB to lean on. 演化类与共演化类方法——比如接触图（contact-map）预测器、早期的深度学习方法——在CASP12和CASP13期间稳步进步，但只要PDB中没有可依赖的近缘同源结构，精度就始终远达不到原子级别。 That is exactly the gap this paper targets: regularly predicting atomic-accuracy structures even when no similar solved structure is available. 而这正是这篇论文瞄准的空白：即便没有任何相似的已解析结构可用，也能稳定地做出原子级精度的预测。

JORDAN Why does "no close homolog" matter so much? 为什么"没有近缘同源结构"这件事这么重要？

ALEX Because that's most of biology. 因为这是生物学里的大多数情况。 The PDB has maybe 100,000 solved structures against billions of sequenced proteins, and solving one more by crystallography or cryo-EM is still a months-to-years undertaking. PDB大约有十万个已解析结构，而已测序的蛋白质却有数十亿条，用晶体学或冷冻电镜再多解析一个结构，仍然是一项耗时数月到数年的工程。 If a method only works when a near-identical structure has already been solved by someone else, it hasn't actually solved structure prediction, it's built a lookup table with extra steps. 如果一种方法只有在别人已经解析出几乎相同的结构时才管用，那它其实并没有真正解决结构预测问题，只是做了一张带了些额外步骤的查找表而已。 The real test is generalizing to sequences structurally unlike anything in the training set. 真正的考验在于，能否泛化到那些结构上与训练集中任何已知结构都不相似的序列。

JORDAN So this is squarely a generalization claim. 所以这本质上是一个关于泛化能力的主张。

ALEX Right, and specifically a blind one, validated under CASP's no-cheating rules rather than the authors' own benchmark choices. 没错，而且是严格意义上的盲测泛化，是在CASP"不许作弊"的规则下验证的，而不是作者自己挑选的基准测试。 That's part of why this result landed as hard as it did across the field — structural biologists, including plenty of skeptics, watched it happen in real time against structures nobody on the AlphaFold team could possibly have seen in advance. 这也是这个结果在整个领域引起如此强烈反响的部分原因——结构生物学家们，包括不少怀疑论者，都亲眼看着它在那些AlphaFold团队不可能提前见过的结构上实时完成预测。

JORDAN What did the immediately-prior generation of methods actually look like, mechanically? 在这之前那一代方法，具体的运作机制是什么样的？

ALEX Mostly a two-step heuristic pipeline, and the paper's own related-work section is candid about this. 基本上是一个两步式的启发式流程，论文自己的"相关工作"部分对此说得很坦白。 You'd convert the evolutionary-coupling signal from an MSA into something like an "image" of a predicted residue-distance matrix using earlier deep-learning contact predictors, then feed that distance matrix into a separate, hand-built optimization routine to actually produce 3D coordinates. 先用早期的深度学习接触预测器，把MSA中的演化共变信号转换成某种类似"图像"的预测残基距离矩阵，然后再把这个距离矩阵输入一个单独的、手工搭建的优化程序，最终生成三维坐标。 A few groups tried predicting coordinates directly end-to-end before AlphaFold, but none of them matched the accuracy of the traditional, hand-crafted multi-step pipelines. 在AlphaFold之前，也有少数团队尝试过直接端到端地预测坐标，但精度都比不上传统的手工多步骤流程。 That's the architectural gap AlphaFold closes: one trained network going straight from sequence input to atomic coordinates, with no separate heuristic assembly step bolted on afterward. 而这正是AlphaFold在架构上填补的空白：用一个训练好的网络，直接从序列输入走到原子坐标，后面不再附加任何单独的启发式拼装步骤。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built. 具体说说他们到底构建了什么。

ALEX A two-stage network. 这是一个两阶段网络。 The first stage, the Evoformer, takes the MSA and an all-by-all residue-pair representation and repeatedly exchanges information between them across forty-eight stacked blocks, each one refining both representations a little further. 第一阶段叫Evoformer，它接收MSA以及一个"残基两两配对"的表示，在四十八个堆叠模块中反复在两者之间交换信息，每个模块都对这两种表示做一点点进一步精炼。 The key trick is treating pairs of residues as edges in a graph and enforcing triangle-consistency constraints between them: distances between three residues have to be mutually compatible in 3D space, so the network bakes that geometric constraint directly into its attention mechanism. 关键技巧是把残基对当作图中的边，并在它们之间强制施加"三角一致性"约束：任意三个残基之间的距离在三维空间里必须相互兼容，于是网络就把这种几何约束直接烘焙进了它的注意力机制里。

JORDAN And the second stage? 第二阶段呢？

ALEX The structure module. 第二阶段是"结构模块"（structure module）。 It takes what the Evoformer produced and turns it into actual 3D coordinates, literally a rotation and translation for every residue, initialized at the origin and then iteratively refined using what they call invariant point attention, which lets the network reason in 3D space without caring which way the whole protein happens to be oriented. 它把Evoformer输出的内容转化成真正的三维坐标，具体做法是给每个残基分配一个旋转和平移，初始时都设在原点，然后用他们称为"不变点注意力"（invariant point attention）的机制反复迭代精炼，这让网络能够在三维空间里进行推理，而不用在意整个蛋白质恰好是朝哪个方向摆放的。 They also "recycle": feed the whole network's own output back in as a new input, repeatedly, which cheaply buys a surprising amount of extra accuracy. 他们还会做"循环"（recycle）：把整个网络自己的输出重新当作新的输入反复送回去，这种做法成本很低，却能换来惊人的额外精度提升。

JORDAN Any part of this you'd push back on? 这里面有哪部分是你想提出质疑的？

ALEX Two things, actually. 其实有两点。 First, their self-distillation step: after initial training, they use the network itself to predict structures for about 350,000 unlabeled sequences, filter to a high-confidence subset, then retrain from scratch on a mix of that self-generated data and the real PDB. 第一，是他们的自蒸馏（self-distillation）步骤：初步训练完成后，他们用网络自己去预测大约三十五万条无标签序列的结构，筛选出高置信度的子集，再用这批自生成数据和真实PDB数据混合起来，从头重新训练一遍。 That's a network partly teaching itself from its own predictions. 这相当于网络在一定程度上用自己的预测结果来教自己。 They guard against pure circularity with confidence filtering and heavy data augmentation, but it's still the kind of self-referential training loop I'd want independently stress-tested on anything structurally unusual rather than accepted as a free accuracy win. 他们用置信度过滤和大量数据增强来防止纯粹的循环论证，但这仍然是一种自我指涉的训练循环，在应用到任何结构上不寻常的蛋白质时，我更想看到独立的压力测试，而不是直接当作一次"白捡"的精度提升来接受。 Second, the compute itself: full, ensembled inference on a single V100 GPU takes about 18 hours for a 2,500-residue protein. 第二，是计算成本本身：在单块V100 GPU上进行完整的集成（ensembled）推理，对一个2500个残基的蛋白质大约需要18个小时。 They later found that switching off ensembling costs almost no accuracy and is eight times faster, but even then you're talking GPU-hours per protein, not something you'd casually rerun yourself for a single CFTR query. 他们后来发现，关掉集成推理几乎不损失精度，速度却能快八倍，但即便如此，每个蛋白质仍然要耗费数小时的GPU时间，并不是你能为了单个CFTR查询就随手重新跑一遍的事情。

JORDAN Did it actually deliver on CASP14? 它在CASP14上真的兑现了吗？

ALEX Decisively. 相当彻底地兑现了。 Median backbone accuracy — Cα r.m.s.d. at 95% residue coverage — was 0.96 angstroms, against 2.8 angstroms for the next-best CASP14 entry. 主链准确度的中位数——以95%残基覆盖率计算的Cα（α碳原子）均方根偏差（r.m.s.d.）——是0.96埃，而CASP14第二名的成绩是2.8埃。 A single carbon atom is about 1.4 angstroms wide, so AlphaFold's typical error sits comfortably inside the width of one atom. 一个碳原子的直径大约是1.4埃，也就是说AlphaFold的典型误差，完全落在一个原子的宽度之内。 All-atom accuracy, including side chains, was 1.5 angstroms versus 3.5 for the best alternative. 包含侧链在内的全原子准确度是1.5埃，而最好的替代方法是3.5埃。

JORDAN Did that hold up outside the CASP14 test set itself? 这个结果在CASP14测试集之外还站得住脚吗？

ALEX Yes, and this is the part I find more convincing than the competition win. 站得住脚，而且这部分恰恰是我觉得比赢得竞赛本身更有说服力的地方。 They ran it against nearly 11,000 recent PDB chains released after the training cutoff, explicitly excluding anything with a close homolog already in training. 他们把它用在了近一万一千条训练截止日期之后新发布的PDB链上进行测试，并明确排除了任何在训练集中已有近缘同源结构的情况。 Median backbone accuracy there was 1.46 angstroms. 在这批数据上，主链准确度的中位数是1.46埃。 Not quite CASP14-level, since this is an uncurated, harder real-world sample, but still far ahead of prior methods. 没有完全达到CASP14那样的水平，因为这是一批未经筛选、更贴近真实世界的更难样本，但仍然远远领先于此前的方法。

JORDAN And the confidence score, pLDDT, is it trustworthy? 那置信度分数pLDDT，靠谱吗？

ALEX Mostly. 大体上靠谱。 pLDDT correlates with true per-residue accuracy at Pearson's r equal to 0.76 across those roughly 11,000 chains, a real signal, not noise, but far from a perfect one-to-one mapping. 在那大约一万一千条链上，pLDDT与真实的逐残基准确度之间的皮尔逊相关系数（Pearson's r）是0.76，这是一个真实的信号，不是噪声，但远远算不上完美的一一对应关系。 Same story for the whole-chain confidence score, pTM, against true TM-score: r equal to 0.85. 整链层面的置信度分数pTM与真实TM-score之间的情况也类似：r值是0.85。 Both numbers say "generally trust the confidence score," not "treat it as ground truth." 这两个数字传达的意思都是"大体上可以信任这个置信度分数"，而不是"把它当作绝对真值来看待"。

JORDAN Any places it clearly struggled? 有没有哪些地方它明显表现不佳？
