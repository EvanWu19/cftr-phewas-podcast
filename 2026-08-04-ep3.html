Show notes — Track C — 2026-08-04

Diagnosis of cystic fibrosis: a high heterogeneity of symptoms and genotypes in a Brazil population — Meneses DG, Dos Santos FR, Botelho AJ, Bispo LM, Matos CG, Propheta VGS, Rodrigues AF, Oliveira GU, da Silva AM, Gurgel RQ. BMC Pediatrics, 2024.

Blurb

A retrospective cross-sectional study of 58 CF patients diagnosed 2007–2021 at a tertiary referral center in Sergipe, in Brazil's admixed Northeast. Faltering growth was the most common presenting sign (62% under age 1); clinical presentation shifted sharply with age (bronchiectasis/pneumonia/upper-airway disease >10yr vs. electrolyte imbalance/thinness/underweight <2yr, all p<0.001–0.03). CFTR sequencing found 23 variants forming 27 genotypes; p.Gly542*, p.Arg334Trp, and p.Ser549Arg were locally enriched relative to Brazil-wide and global frequencies, reflecting Sergipe's specific African/European/Native American admixture. Patients with two class I–III alleles (Group 1) had significantly worse malnutrition and electrolyte markers than milder Group 2/3 patients. Nine patients (15.5%) carried rare, novel, or uncertain-significance variants, including two genuinely new frameshift alleles and a VUS, p.Lys162Glu, that the authors classified "probably pathogenic" using ACMG neighbor-codon (PM5) precedent from two already-CF-associated adjacent residues. For us this lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation: the reusable move isn't the regional allele frequencies, it's the neighbor-precedent reasoning pattern — worth automating as a lookup tool.

Plain-English glossary

  • CFTR variant functional classes (I–V) — a system grouping CFTR mutations by mechanism: I = no protein made (nonsense/frameshift); II = protein misfolds and is degraded (e.g. F508del); III = reaches membrane but gate won't open; IV = reaches membrane, conducts poorly; V = reduced quantity of otherwise-normal protein (often splicing). Classes I–III generally mean little/no function; IV–V leave residual function.（中文：CFTR 变异功能分类（I 到 V 类）——按机制对 CFTR 突变分组：I 类完全不产生蛋白质；II 类蛋白质错误折叠被降解（如 F508del）；III 类到达细胞膜但闸门无法打开；IV 类到达细胞膜但传导差；V 类正常蛋白质数量减少（常因剪接缺陷）。I–III 类通常意味着几乎无功能，IV–V 类保留部分残余功能。）
  • Newborn screening (NBS) / IRT — heel-prick blood test measuring immunoreactive trypsinogen; two elevated readings trigger a sweat chloride test.（中文：新生儿筛查／免疫反应性胰蛋白酶原——通过足跟血检测，两次升高即触发汗液氯化物试验。）
  • Sweat chloride test — measures chloride concentration in sweat after pilocarpine stimulation; ≥60 mmol/L on two samples is diagnostic for CF, 30–59 is indeterminate.（中文：汗液氯化物试验——毛果芸香碱刺激出汗后测定汗液氯离子浓度；两次样本≥60 mmol/L 可确诊 CF，30–59 为意义不明区间。）
  • ACMG criteria (PM2, PM5, PP3, PP4) — American College of Medical Genetics evidence codes combined to classify a variant: PM2 = absent/rare in population databases; PM5 = same or neighboring residue as a known pathogenic variant; PP3 = multiple computational predictors agree it's damaging; PP4 = patient's phenotype is highly specific for the disease.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会证据代码，组合用于变异分类：PM2＝在群体数据库中缺失或罕见；PM5＝与已知致病变异位于相同或相邻残基；PP3＝多个计算预测工具一致判断有害；PP4＝患者表型高度特异地符合该疾病。）
  • HGVS — the standardized nomenclature for describing DNA/protein sequence variants.（中文：变异命名的标准化书写规范。）
  • Segregation study — tracing which parent a variant was inherited from to determine whether two variants sit on the same (cis) or different (trans) chromosome copies.（中文：分离分析——追溯变异遗传自哪一方父母，以判断两个变异位于同一条（顺式）还是不同条（反式）染色体上。）
  • Transmembrane domain (MSD1) — a membrane-spanning region of the CFTR protein that lines the chloride channel; a mutation there can disrupt ion permeation.（中文：跨膜结构域（MSD1）——CFTR 蛋白中横跨细胞膜、构成氯离子通道内壁的区域；该处突变可能破坏离子通透。）
  • Faltering growth — a drop of more than 1 standard deviation in weight-for-age or weight-for-height over the prior three months in children under 1.（中文：生长发育迟滞——一岁以下儿童在此前三个月内体重年龄比或体重身高比下降超过 1 个标准差。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Genotype-class stratification (Group 1/2/3 by variant class pairing) — group patients by the functional-class combination of their two CFTR alleles before comparing phenotype severity.
  • ACMG PM5 neighbor-codon reasoning — check whether residues adjacent to a VUS already carry an established CF-causing classification as supporting pathogenicity evidence.
  • Segregation analysis in relatives — confirm trans (biallelic) configuration of two variants via parental/sibling inheritance tracing.
  • Population-frequency comparison (gnomAD v2/v3 + CFTR2) — cross-check local allele frequencies against population databases to contextualize regional enrichment.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation. MVP (paste-ready): CFTR neighbor-precedent VUS worksheet — given a candidate missense variant's HGVS notation, pull every CFTR2/ClinVar-classified variant within a small window of neighboring codons plus domain annotation, gnomAD population frequency, and an open in-silico consensus score (PP3 stand-in); output assembled evidence, not an automated verdict. Input: CFTR variant (HGVS). Output: structured evidence worksheet. Difficulty: S–M.

What to verify first

  • Proprietary classifier opacity — most variant calls ran through Mendelics' proprietary Abracadabra software, not independently auditable; any buildable tool should run on transparent, reproducible sources only (gnomAD, CFTR2, ClinVar, open predictors).
  • Small-cohort caveat — n=58 with thin genotype-class subgroups (especially Group 3); the malnutrition/electrolyte severity gradient is statistically real here but needs replication in a larger or pooled cohort.
  • Neighbor-precedent is suggestive, not definitive — p.Lys162Glu's own classification remains "probably pathogenic, pending functional study"; any automated tool built on this reasoning should preserve that hedge rather than output a hard verdict.

Gap-map / catalog

  • CLOSES: a concrete regional CFTR variant spectrum (23 variants/27 genotypes) with locally enriched alleles (p.Gly542*, p.Arg334Trp, p.Ser549Arg) explained by admixture; two genuinely novel frameshift alleles; a worked PM5-style VUS classification example (p.Lys162Glu); a genotype-class-to-malnutrition/electrolyte-severity gradient (G1 vs. G2/G3).
  • LEAVES OPEN: no trans-modifier gene findings (this is CFTR-intragenic only); whether the severity gradient replicates outside Sergipe; functional confirmation of p.Lys162Glu still pending; the second pathogenic allele for p.Arg516Leufs*51 remains unidentified (possible deep-intronic/regulatory variant).

ALEX CFTR variants are grouped into six functional classes based on what actually goes wrong with the protein, and this paper leans on classes one through five.
