Track C — Registry Data Quality & Anthropometric Outlier Detection

Blurb

This is the second of today's two Track C episodes. It takes a deep-learning angle on the trajectory-substrate and outlier-detection scope items that episode 3 covered with classical splines. Javidi and colleagues at Cleveland Clinic (npj Digital Medicine, 2022) built 6,336 simulated "magnitude" cohorts and 6,336 "shape" cohorts. They varied effect size, dispersion, missingness, irregular spacing and class imbalance, and compared nine deep-learning architectures on them. Magnitude differences were easy for nearly every model. Shape differences were not. GAF-CNN and TSF-CNN held AUCs of 0.83 and 0.81 at 50% missingness, while the Transformer, MLP and ResNet fell to 0.52–0.59. A real-world test, predicting pediatric T2D from BMI trajectories in 35,056 children, peaked at AUC 0.72, which is a useful reality check. For CFFPR work the paper lands mainly on candidate generation, and its simulation grid could be reused as a stress-test template for any flagging method.

Glossary

  • CDC growth curve — Sex- and age-specific pediatric reference curves (built with the LMS method) that convert a child's BMI to a percentile; by convention ≥95th percentile = obesity, 85th–<95th = overweight.（中文：CDC生长曲线，按性别和年龄将儿童BMI换算为百分位数，用于界定肥胖和超重）
  • Class-imbalance downsampling — In this paper's grid, removing 0/10/25/50% of one class to create imbalance.（中文：类别不平衡下采样，按比例删减某一类别样本）
  • eMERGE criteria — Standardized, rule-based EHR phenotype algorithms from the multi-site eMERGE network, used here to label pediatric T2D.（中文：eMERGE表型标准，多中心网络制定的基于规则的EHR表型定义）
  • FDR correction — False-discovery-rate adjustment, used here to compare observation-window AUCs against the best window.（中文：错误发现率校正）
  • Gramian Angular Field (GAF) — A transform that turns a 1-D time series into a 2-D image-like matrix so an image CNN can classify it.（中文：格拉姆角场，将一维时间序列转为二维类图像矩阵）
  • Irregularity — Uneven measurement spacing, simulated here as none, moderate (0–3 missing measurements, mean 1) or high (2–5, mean 4) via a beta distribution.（中文：不规则性，测量间隔不均匀）
  • LOCF / LOCB — Last observation carried forward, or carried backward when the first point is missing; the imputation used for simulated missingness.（中文：末次观测值向前/向后结转）
  • Observation window — The age span of BMI history fed to the model (51 variants tested) before the fixed T2D outcome window of ages 13–18.（中文：观察窗口，用于预测的历史数据年龄区间）
  • STROBE diagram — The reporting-convention flowchart showing how many patients were included or excluded at each cohort-building step.（中文：STROBE流程图，观察性研究中逐步纳入/排除患者的报告图）
  • Time-series forest (TSF) — A tree-ensemble feature extractor for time series; feeds a CNN in TSF-CNN or an MLP in TSF-MLP.（中文：时间序列森林，基于树集成的时间序列特征提取方法）
  • Trajectory shape vs. magnitude — Magnitude cohorts differ in overall level. Shape cohorts differ in curvature: one polynomial coefficient is varied, isolated from level.（中文：轨迹形状与幅度，前者指弯曲形态差异，后者指整体水平差异）

Full citation

  • PMID 35896817
  • PMCID PMC9329311
  • DOI 10.1038/s41746-022-00651-4

Methods/tools to lift

  • TSF-CNN: time-series-forest features fed into a CNN; robust on shape tasks (AUC ≥0.77 when missingness <25%).
  • GAF-CNN: 1-D trajectory turned into a Gramian Angular Field image, then a CNN; the most robust on shape, but it needs min-max rather than z-score normalization.
  • TSF-MLP as an ablation contrast: the same features with no convolution did notably worse, which suggests the CNN component matters.
  • Magnitude/shape simulation-cohort design: real reference trajectories, median-normalized; level shifts drawn from normal distributions; shape shifts made by varying the most influential coefficient of a 10-coefficient polynomial (found by permutation MSE testing).
  • Parameter grid: effect size 0.25–2.75 (step 0.5), dispersion 0.25–2 (step 0.35), missingness 0/10/25/50%, irregularity none/moderate/high, imbalance 0/10/25/50%. Up to 2,000 trajectories per class, ≤16 timepoints, 70/30 split, 20% of training held out for tuning.
  • eMERGE phenotyping: a reproducible, rule-based outcome label drawn from EHR data.
  • Observation-window sweep with FDR-corrected comparison: a systematic way to choose how much history to use.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands primarily as (a) candidate generation, secondarily (d) evaluation, for Liz/CFFPR data quality (method only).

MVP: a simulation stress-test harness for height/weight trajectories, modeled on this paper's design. It takes a few deidentified, representative registry trajectories as seeds and generates magnitude- and shape-perturbed synthetic cohorts at controlled effect size, missingness, irregularity and imbalance. One existing candidate flag (e.g., a spline or mixed-model residual flag) is then scored across the whole grid. Difficulty: M (porting polynomial shape perturbation plus missingness/irregularity sampling; no DL training needed). Training a TSF-CNN or GAF-CNN on top would be an optional L follow-on.

What to verify first

  • Does the real-world vs. simulated AUC gap (0.72 vs. ~0.89–0.93) reflect a general limit on transferring from simulation to real data, one that would also hit a registry application, or is it specific to T2D's multifactorial etiology?
  • What method and threshold did the paper's "height/weight outliers were removed" step use? It is unspecified, so it can't be reused or audited.
  • Do findings from general-population BMI/glucose/SBP trajectories transfer to CF growth and weight patterns, especially given modulator-era weight-gain trends that may change typical trajectory shapes?
  • Is a CNN, or any DL approach, the right tool for registry-scale anthropometric flagging at all, compared with the classical spline/mixed-model substrates (ep3)? This paper doesn't settle it.

Pipeline stage

Primary: (a) candidate generation — shape/magnitude anomaly detection in longitudinal trajectories.

Secondary: (d) evaluation — the systematic simulation-stress-test design is a reusable validation template independent of model architecture.

Error types covered / not covered

Covered (indirectly, as a detection signal): abrupt shape or magnitude discontinuities in a trajectory, of the kind a real data-entry error (unit-conversion mistake, digit transposition, decimal shift) could produce — though the paper never tests on actual labeled data-entry errors, only on simulated/general-disease-prediction tasks.

Not covered: no direct test on real registry data-entry error types; no detail on how "outliers were removed" in their own real-world preprocessing step; no CF-specific validation; does not address real-clinical-event-vs-error discrimination directly.

JORDAN Before we get into models, I want to pin down three pieces of clinical vocabulary the paper leans on. 在讨论模型之前，我想先弄清楚论文依赖的三个临床术语。 First, they define obesity and overweight using CDC 2000 growth curves. 第一，他们用CDC（美国疾病控制与预防中心）2000年生长曲线 (growth curve) 来定义肥胖和超重。 What does that actually mean for a child's BMI? 这对儿童的BMI（体重指数）实际意味着什么？

ALEX For children, BMI isn't judged against a fixed adult cutoff, because normal BMI changes with age and sex. 对儿童来说，BMI不是用固定的成人阈值来判断的，因为正常BMI会随年龄和性别变化。 Instead, a child's BMI is placed on a sex- and age-specific reference curve and converted to a percentile, using the same LMS machinery that produces pediatric z-scores. 相反，儿童的BMI会被放到特定性别和年龄的参考曲线上并换算成百分位数 (percentile)，所用的正是生成儿科z分数 (z-score) 的同一套LMS（偏度-中位数-变异系数）方法。 By the standard CDC convention, the 95th percentile or above is obesity, and the 85th up to the 95th is overweight. 按照CDC的标准惯例，达到或超过第95百分位为肥胖，第85到第95百分位之间为超重。

JORDAN Second, eMERGE phenotyping criteria. 第二，eMERGE（电子病历与基因组学网络）表型定义标准。

ALEX eMERGE is a multi-site research network linking EHRs to genomics. eMERGE是一个将EHR（电子健康记录）与基因组学相连接的多中心研究网络。 Its phenotype algorithms are standardized, rule-based definitions for deciding from EHR data whether a patient "has T2D." 它的表型算法是标准化的、基于规则的定义，用于根据EHR数据判断一个患者是否"患有T2D（2型糖尿病）"。 The point is that the label is a reproducible rule that travels across sites, not a local ad hoc judgment. 重点在于，这个标签是一条可在不同中心之间复用的可复现规则，而不是某个机构的临时判断。

JORDAN And third, the STROBE diagram. 第三，STROBE（观察性流行病学研究报告规范）流程图。

ALEX STROBE is the reporting convention for observational studies. STROBE是观察性研究的报告规范。 The diagram shows how many patients were included or excluded at each step, so a reader can audit how the analytic cohort was carved out of the raw EHR. 这个流程图展示了每一步纳入或排除了多少患者，让读者能够审核分析队列是如何从原始EHR中划分出来的。 For registry work, that's the provenance trail you'd want behind any cleaned dataset. 对于登记库工作来说，这正是任何清洗后数据集背后都应具备的溯源记录 (provenance trail)。

JORDAN So why build a simulation at all, when Cleveland Clinic has a huge EHR? 既然Cleveland Clinic拥有庞大的EHR，为什么还要专门构建模拟数据？

ALEX Because in real EHR data you never know true class membership. 因为在真实的EHR数据中，你永远无法知道真实的类别归属。 A "false positive" might just be someone not yet diagnosed, which is label noise you can't resolve. 一个"假阳性"可能只是尚未被确诊的人，这是一种你无法消除的标签噪声 (label noise)。 Simulation gives full control over effect size, dispersion, missingness, irregularity, and class imbalance. 模拟则让你完全掌控效应量、离散度、缺失程度 (missingness)、不规则性和类别不平衡 (class imbalance)。 So you can map exactly where each architecture breaks down. 因此你可以准确描绘出每种架构在何处失效。

JORDAN Couldn't they reuse existing time-series benchmarks? 他们难道不能复用现有的时间序列基准吗？

ALEX The authors argue those benchmarks were built for audio and video signal processing. 作者认为，那些基准是为音频和视频信号处理而构建的。 EHR longitudinal data has its own correlation structure, non-random missingness, and other properties those benchmarks don't capture. EHR纵向数据有其自身的相关结构、非随机缺失，以及那些基准没有捕捉到的其他特性。 Think about registry data: visit spacing is uneven, and a child can go a long stretch without a recorded height. 想想登记库数据：就诊间隔并不均匀，一个孩子可能很长一段时间都没有身高记录。 So they needed a clinically realistic simulation instead. 所以他们需要的是一种具有临床真实性的模拟方法。

JORDAN So the core question isn't "which model is most accurate"? 所以核心问题并不是"哪个模型最准确"？

ALEX Right, it's which architecture is most robust across realistic conditions. 对，而是哪种架构在贴近现实的条件下最稳健 (robust)。 Several architectures can look similar on clean data and diverge sharply once data gets messy. 好几种架构在干净数据上可能看起来差不多，但数据一旦变得混乱，就会明显分化。 They also split discrimination into two tasks. 他们还把判别任务分成了两类。 Magnitude means the two classes differ in overall level. 幅度 (magnitude) 指两个类别在整体水平上存在差异。 Shape means the trajectories bend differently, isolated from any level change. 形状 (trajectory shape) 指轨迹的弯曲方式不同，并与任何水平变化分离开来。

JORDAN How does that distinction map onto data-entry errors? 这个区分和数据录入错误 (data-entry error) 有什么对应关系？

ALEX A unit-conversion error or a decimal shift can produce an abrupt level jump, which looks like a magnitude anomaly. 单位换算错误或小数点错位可能造成水平的突然跳变，看起来就像幅度异常。 A transposed digit at one visit creates a local kink, which looks like a shape anomaly. 某次就诊中的数字颠倒会造成一个局部折点，看起来就像形状异常。 But this paper never tests on labeled entry errors. 但这篇论文从未在标注过的录入错误上做过测试。 It's about distinguishing cohorts, so we're borrowing a signal concept, not a validated result. 它讨论的是区分队列，所以我们借用的是一个信号概念，而不是经过验证的结果。

JORDAN Walk me through the simulation. 带我过一遍模拟设计吧。

ALEX They started from six real reference trajectories, drawn from BMI, glucose, and systolic blood pressure, each measure handled separately, from Cleveland Clinic EHR data spanning 2000 to 2018. 他们从Cleveland Clinic 2000年至2018年的EHR数据中选取了六条真实参考轨迹，来自BMI、血糖和收缩压，每种测量分别处理。 Missing yearly points were filled by averaging the neighboring values, and each trajectory was median-normalized. 缺失的年度数据点用前后相邻值的平均来填补，每条轨迹都做了中位数归一化。 Magnitude cohorts sample two classes from normal distributions where the mean sets the effect size and the SD sets the dispersion. 幅度队列从正态分布中抽样两个类别，其均值决定效应量，标准差决定离散度。

JORDAN And the shape cohorts? 那形状队列呢？

ALEX That's the clever part. 这是最巧妙的部分。 They fit a ten-coefficient polynomial to the reference trajectory. 他们对参考轨迹拟合了一个含十个系数的多项式。 Then permutation-based mean-squared-error testing identifies which single coefficient most influences the curve's shape. 然后通过基于置换的均方误差检验，找出对曲线形状影响最大的那一个系数。 Varying only that coefficient between classes isolates shape from magnitude. 只在类别之间改变这一个系数，就能把形状与幅度分离开来。

JORDAN And the parameter grid? 那参数网格呢？

ALEX Effect size runs from 0.25 to 2.75 in steps of 0.5, and dispersion from 0.25 to 2 in steps of 0.35. 效应量从0.25到2.75，步长0.5；离散度从0.25到2，步长0.35。 Missingness is 0, 10, 25, or 50 percent, imputed by LOCF, or carried backward if the first point was missing. 缺失比例为0%、10%、25%或50%，用LOCF（末次观测值结转）填补，如果第一个点缺失则向后结转。 Irregularity has three levels sampled from a beta distribution, and imbalance downsamples one class by 0, 10, 25, or 50 percent. 不规则性分三个等级，通过beta分布抽样；不平衡则对一个类别下采样0%、10%、25%或50%。 That gives 6,336 magnitude cohorts and 6,336 shape cohorts, each with up to 2,000 trajectories per class and at most 16 timepoints. 由此得到6,336个幅度队列和6,336个形状队列，每个类别最多2,000条轨迹，每条最多16个时间点。

JORDAN Then nine architectures. 然后是九种架构。

MLP（多层感知机）作为简单基线，此外还有Transformer（基于注意力机制的模型）、ResNet（残差网络）以及专门的时间序列模型。 The standouts are TSF-CNN, which feeds time-series-forest features into a convolutional network, and GAF-CNN, which turns the one-dimensional trajectory into a two-dimensional Gramian Angular Field and classifies it like an image. 两个突出者是TSF-CNN（将时间序列森林特征输入卷积网络的模型）和GAF-CNN（把一维轨迹转换为二维格拉姆角场、再像图像一样分类的模型）。 There's also TSF-MLP, the same forest features fed into a plain MLP. 还有TSF-MLP（时间序列森林特征加普通MLP），即把同样的森林特征输入普通MLP。

GAF-CNN在最佳条件下的AUC几乎处处超过0.74，只有在50%缺失且效应量最小时才降到0.6到0.7。 TSF-CNN was a close second, at 0.77 or above whenever missingness was under 25 percent. TSF-CNN紧随其后，只要缺失低于25%，AUC就不低于0.77。

ALEX The paper doesn't say. 论文没有说明。 There's no method, no threshold, and no rate. 没有方法，没有阈值，也没有比例。 For us, that's exactly the step that matters, and it's treated as a black box. 对我们来说，这恰恰是最关键的一步，却被当成了黑箱。

JORDAN And the prediction setup? 那预测是怎么设置的？
