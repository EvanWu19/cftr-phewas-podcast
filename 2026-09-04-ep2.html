Ep2 (Track B) — MDKG: an LLM-built knowledge graph, and the recipe behind it

2026-09-04 · Gao et al. (2025) · Nature Communications · Full text (open access)

MDKG, the Mental Disorders Knowledge Graph, is less interesting for its mental-health content than for its construction pipeline — a documented, largely reproducible recipe for turning a focused PubMed corpus into a contextualized, evaluable knowledge graph using LLMs in the loop. Entities come from BERN2 plus GPT-4-assisted noun-phrase extraction and QuickUMLS mapping into UMLS; relationships come from a BERT-based SpERT.PL model refined by a GPT-4o scoring-and-reprocessing pass; every triplet carries conditional statements, cohort baseline characteristics, and source/confidence side information rather than being a bare fact. The literature-only graph reaches 236,542 entities and 1.9 million triplets (10.7 million after database fusion); expert correctness was 0.79, with contextual features cutting expert evaluation time by ~70%. Applied to UK Biobank pre-diagnosis histories as 200-dimensional embeddings, MDKG improved prediction of major depressive disorder, anxiety, and bipolar disorder by 0.02-0.21 AUC. The pipeline — not the mental-health content — is the transferable asset for AI-tooling/career positioning: a scaled-down version could build a literature knowledge graph for any focused biomedical corpus, CF included.

Plain-English glossary

  • Knowledge graph (KG) — a network of entities connected by typed relationships, letting you query and reason across many papers at once.（中文：由实体和类型化关系连接而成的网络，可用于跨大量文献进行查询与推理。）
  • Triplet — the atomic unit of a KG: head entity, relation, tail entity (e.g. orbitofrontal-hippocampal pathway — associated with — depression).（中文：知识图谱的基本单位：头实体—关系—尾实体。）
  • PheCode — a scheme consolidating fine-grained ICD-10 diagnosis codes into clinically coherent phenotype groupings, the same PheWAS-style vocabulary used in registry phenotyping.（中文：将细粒度ICD-10诊断代码归并为临床连贯表型分组的编码体系，与PheWAS所用词汇一致。）
  • UMLS (Unified Medical Language System) — a large standardized biomedical vocabulary linking different terms for the same concept together.（中文：一个庞大的标准化生物医学词表，将指向同一概念的不同术语关联起来。）
  • Active learning (headword kept in English) — here, prioritizing the most informative abstracts for human annotation across ten sequential batches rather than annotating randomly.（中文：本文中指分十个批次优先挑选信息量最大的摘要交由人工标注，而非随机标注。）

Full citation

Gao et al. Large language model powered knowledge graph construction for mental health exploration. Nature Communications. 2025. PMID 40804250 · PMC12350947 · DOI

Methods / tools to lift

  • BERN2 — biomedical named-entity recognition/normalization tool, first-pass entity tagger.
  • SpERT.PL — BERT-based span-based joint entity-relation extraction model.
  • GPT-4/GPT-4o two-step refinement — score every triplet 1-10, reprocess anything under 5, adjudicate contradictions against the full abstract.
  • SapBERT + FAISS entity alignment — cosine-similarity-based linking of extracted entities to UMLS/MONDO/UBERON/GO.
  • OCR + ChatGPT table QA — extracting cohort baseline characteristics from full-text tables without explicit structure parsing.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves AI-tooling / career positioning. MVP: a lightweight literature-KG builder — BERN2 entities, a smaller relation extractor (or well-prompted LLM pass), one contextual layer (cohort characteristics), evaluated against a 50-100-abstract hand-labeled gold set. Difficulty: M-L (entity alignment to an ontology is the fiddly part; a raw-triplet-only version without full database fusion is a smaller, still-demonstrable v1).

What to verify first

  • API cost and reproducibility risk of leaning on GPT-4/GPT-4o across a large corpus — not reported in the paper.
  • The 0.79 correctness figure came from expert review on two conditions (schizophrenia, anxiety) only — doesn't necessarily generalize to all nine entity categories/seven relation types.
  • How BERN2/UMLS mapping performs on a much smaller, narrower corpus than mental health's — untested here.

ALEX: Because the whole value proposition of a KG is letting you query and reason across tens of thousands of papers at once, automatically — and if you strip context to get a clean, queryable graph, you silently convert "true under these conditions" into "true, full stop," which is exactly the kind of error that compounds when someone runs a downstream analysis or trains a model on top of it without ever reading the source sentence. 因为知识图谱真正的价值主张，就在于能让你一次性、自动化地跨越成千上万篇论文进行查询和推理——而如果为了得到一个干净、可查询的图谱就把上下文剥离掉，就等于悄悄把"在特定条件下成立"变成了"无条件成立"，而这正是一种会不断累积放大的错误，一旦有人在此基础上做下游分析或训练模型，却从未回头去读原始那句话，问题就会被继续放大。 That's also why the paper spends real effort on baseline characteristics like age, gender, and sample size as first-class triplet properties rather than an afterthought — a relationship found in 40 elderly inpatients and one found in 4,000 general-population adults are not the same kind of evidence, even if the extracted triplet text looks identical. 这也是为什么这篇论文把年龄、性别、样本量这类基线特征，当作三元组的一等公民属性来认真对待，而不是事后补充——一个在40名老年住院患者中发现的关系，和一个在4000名普通成年人中发现的关系，即便抽取出来的三元组文本看起来一模一样，也不该被当作同等强度的证据。
