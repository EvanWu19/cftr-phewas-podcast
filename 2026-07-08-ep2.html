Track B — MetaGP: a generative medical foundation model (Liu 2025)

Blurb. MetaGP ships an open, concrete recipe: take Qwen-1.5 32B, continually pretrain on ~8M EHRs (14.8B tokens) + 5.4M articles (48B tokens) + 15,731 books (8.6B tokens), then fine-tune (~630k rare-disease/emergency EHRs, 600k+ CXRs, ~24k CTs, medical QA). It reaches senior-clinician-level diagnosis (rare disease 1.57 vs GPT-4 0.93 on a −2..+2 scale; automated accuracy 0.698 vs 0.329; emergency 1.59 vs 1.19) and — the transferable result — lifts clinicians most where experience is thinnest (junior emergency physicians +53%, mid +46%, senior +19%), while reducing harmful diagnoses. Where it lands: the AI-tooling/career lane. Buildable artifact: a clinician-AI diagnostic-uplift eval harness — model-agnostic, runs on de-identified vignettes + paired pre/post clinician diagnoses, scores on the −2..+2 rubric, reports uplift stratified by experience. Secondary reusable asset: the open continual-pretrain-then-fine-tune pattern (curated domain data beat GPT-4 despite far fewer parameters).

Plain-English glossary.

  • Continual pretraining — taking an already-pretrained general model and pretraining it further on a domain corpus before task fine-tuning, to buy domain fluency cheaply.（中文：在已预训练的通用模型上，先用领域语料继续预训练，再做任务微调，以低成本获得领域流畅度。）
  • EHR — electronic health record; the longitudinal clinical text + structured entries a health system keeps per patient.（中文：电子健康档案，医疗系统为每位病人保存的纵向临床文本与结构化条目。）
  • Foundation model — one broad model pretrained on a huge corpus then adapted to many tasks, rather than trained per task.（中文：在巨大语料上预训练、再适配到多任务的宽域模型，而非逐任务训练。）
  • Multimodal — a model that handles more than one data type; here text plus chest X-ray and CT images.（中文：能处理多种数据类型的模型；此处为文本加胸片与 CT 影像。）
  • Rare disease (clinical) — a low-incidence condition with ambiguous presentation; CF itself is one, and its complication long-tail fits this profile.（中文：发病率低、表现模糊的疾病；CF 本身即属此类，其并发症长尾符合这一画像。）

Full citation. Liu et al. (lead contact Kang Zhang). MetaGP: A generative foundation model integrating electronic health records and multimodal imaging for addressing unmet clinical needs. Cell Rep Med. 2025. PMID 40187356 · PMC PMC12047458 · DOI 10.1016/j.xcrm.2025.102056. (Full text via PubMed / PMC.)

Methods / tools to lift.

  • Continual-pretrain-then-fine-tune on an open base (Qwen-1.5 32B) — domain corpus first, tasks second; curated domain data outweighed raw parameter count in-domain.
  • Experience-stratified human-AI uplift eval — measure pre/post clinician diagnosis with the model in the loop, report gains by seniority (the value concentrates where experience is thin).
  • −2..+2 clinical scoring rubric (+2 correct primary dx, −2 potentially harmful) + automated accuracy/F1/precision/recall alongside manual grading.
  • Prioritize precision over raw accuracy in decision support (false alarms are costly).
  • Open weights for data-path control, reproducibility, and inference cost/agility.

Where it lands + buildable artifact. Serves AI-tooling / career (secondary: Liz / registry decision support, method only). MVP: clinician-ai-uplift-harness — input = de-identified case vignettes + each clinician's diagnosis without and with a candidate model's suggestion + the model output; output = uplift stratified by clinician experience (and optionally patient subgroup), on a fixed −2..+2 rubric with inter-rater agreement. Model-agnostic (plug in GPT-4, an open model, a future CF-tuned model). Difficulty: M. Companion sketch: a small domain-continual-pretrain sandbox on public CF text to validate the pattern safely.

What to verify first.

  • Private-data dependence — headline EHR numbers rest on request-only records; treat as promising-but-unreplicated until open benchmarks alone reproduce the gap.
  • Rubric quality — multiple graders, an inter-rater agreement figure, and a pre-registered rubric so uplift isn't a grading artifact.
  • "Minimal structuring" claim — how raw EHRs were actually cleaned/tokenized is where hidden labor and leakage hide.
  • Decision transparency — the model doesn't explain its reasoning; the eval harness, not the black box, is the safer first deliverable.
  • All methods external — nothing here describes or assumes registry data internals.

ALEX Welcome back to Track B, the tooling lane. 欢迎回到 Track B，也就是工具开发这条线。 Today's paper is MetaGP — Meta General Practitioner — a generative medical foundation model from Liu and colleagues, lead contact Kang Zhang, published in Cell Reports Medicine in 2025. 今天的论文是 MetaGP——Meta General Practitioner（元全科医生）——一个生成式医学基础模型，作者是 Liu 及其同事，通讯联系人是 Kang Zhang，2025 年发表在《Cell Reports Medicine》上。 We're reading it because it ships a concrete, open recipe: take an open 32-billion-parameter base model and turn it into a medical decision-support engine through continual pretraining and fine-tuning. 我们读它，是因为它给出了一份具体、开放的配方：拿一个开源的 320 亿参数基座模型，通过持续预训练和微调，把它变成一个医学决策支持引擎。 And it pairs that with an evaluation design that measures something Runyu can reuse directly — how much a model lifts a clinician, stratified by experience. 而且它还配了一套评估设计，测量的正是 Runyu 可以直接复用的东西——模型对临床医生的提升有多大，并按经验分层。

JORDAN You keep saying "foundation model" and "continual pretraining." 你一直说"基础模型"和"持续预训练"。 The ML I know cold, but frame the medical-genomics-specific parts for me. 机器学习本身我很熟，但请把其中医学与基因组学特有的部分给我讲清楚。

ALEX Fair — I'll skip the transformer basics and stay on the domain layer. 好——变换器 (transformer) 的基础我就跳过，只讲领域这一层。 A foundation model here just means one broad model pretrained on a huge corpus, then adapted to many tasks rather than trained from scratch per task. 这里所说的基础模型，就是一个在巨大语料上预训练、然后适配到多个任务的宽域模型，而不是每个任务从头训练一个。 The medically interesting move is what they feed it: EHRs, meaning electronic health records — the longitudinal clinical text and structured entries a health system accumulates per patient. 医学上有意思的地方在于喂给它什么：EHR，即电子健康档案 (electronic health records)——一个医疗系统为每位病人积累的纵向临床文本和结构化条目。 Continual pretraining means they didn't start from nothing; they took an existing general model and kept pretraining it on medical text so it absorbs clinical language before any task tuning. 持续预训练 (continual pretraining) 意味着他们不是从零开始；他们拿一个已有的通用模型，继续在医学文本上预训练，让它在任何任务微调之前先吸收临床语言。 The clinical targets that make this a hard-mode test are rare-disease diagnosis and emergency-condition identification — low incidence, ambiguous presentations, exactly where narrow single-specialty models get tunnel vision. 让这成为高难度测试的临床目标是罕见病诊断和急症识别——发病率低、表现模糊，恰恰是狭窄的单专科模型会陷入"隧道视野"的地方。

JORDAN So what gap is MetaGP claiming to fill that PMC-LLaMA or GatorTron didn't? 那么 MetaGP 宣称填补的、是 PMC-LLaMA 或 GatorTron 没做到的什么缺口？

ALEX Two gaps, and they're worth stating precisely. 两个缺口，值得精确地说清楚。 First, breadth versus tunnel vision: specialized medical AI tends to be excellent on one modality or one specialty and blind outside it, so a cardiology model misses the neurology clue. 第一，广度对隧道视野：专科医学 AI 往往在单一模态或单一专科上很出色，却对其外一无所见，于是一个心脏科模型会漏掉神经科的线索。 Second, the data-structuring tax: before you can feed EHRs to most models you have to convert messy heterogeneous records into clean structured inputs, which is labor-intensive and loses information, and it caps how much data you can scale to. 第二，数据结构化的"税负"：在把 EHR 喂给多数模型之前，你得先把杂乱异质的记录转换成干净的结构化输入，这既费人力又丢信息，还限制了你能扩展到多大的数据量。 MetaGP's pitch is a generative model that eats relatively raw clinical text and spans specialties, so it needs minimal hand-structuring. MetaGP 的卖点是一个能吃相对原始的临床文本、并跨越各专科的生成式模型，因而只需要极少的人工结构化。 That "minimal structuring" claim is the one I'd push on hardest, and we'll come back to it. 这个"极少结构化"的论断，是我会最用力去质疑的，我们稍后会回到它。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built. 带我看看他们到底建了什么。

ALEX The base is Qwen-1.5 32B, an open-source model, and that choice is deliberate — the whole thing is meant to be reproducible and open. 基座是 Qwen-1.5 32B，一个开源模型，这个选择是刻意的——整个工作旨在可复现、可开放。 Pretraining corpus: about 8 million EHRs, which is 14.8 billion tokens; 5.4 million academic articles at 48 billion tokens; and 15,731 medical books at 8.6 billion tokens. 预训练语料：约 800 万份 EHR，合 148 亿个 token；540 万篇学术文章，合 480 亿个 token；以及 15,731 本医学书籍，合 86 亿个 token。 Then fine-tuning on roughly 630,000 EHR records specifically for rare disease and emergency conditions, plus over 600,000 chest X-rays and nearly 24,000 CT scans for the imaging side, plus medical question-answering sets like PubMedQA, MedQA, and MedMCQA. 然后在约 63 万份专门针对罕见病和急症的 EHR 记录上微调，再加上 60 多万张胸部 X 光片和近 2.4 万张 CT 用于影像端，还有 PubMedQA、MedQA、MedMCQA 等医学问答集。 So it's the standard modern pipeline — general base, domain continual-pretrain, then task fine-tune — but executed at real clinical scale and made multimodal. 所以这是标准的现代流程——通用基座、领域持续预训练、再任务微调——但是在真正的临床规模上执行，并做成了多模态。 Here's my design pushback: they lean on public benchmarks like MIMIC and IU-Xray for imaging but the headline EHR performance rests on private records available only on request, so the most impressive numbers are the least independently checkable. 这是我的设计质疑：影像端他们依赖 MIMIC、IU-Xray 等公开基准，但最亮眼的 EHR 性能却建立在只能按需索取的私有记录上，因此最令人印象深刻的数字恰恰是最难被独立核验的。

JORDAN Okay, the numbers — does it work? 好，说数字——它管用吗？

ALEX On rare disease, manual grading of 97 systemic rare-disease case reports from PubMed, scored on a −2 to +2 scale, MetaGP averaged 1.57 against GPT-4's 0.93. 在罕见病上，对来自 PubMed 的 97 个系统性罕见病病例报告做人工评分，采用 −2 到 +2 的量表，MetaGP 平均 1.57，而 GPT-4 是 0.93。 For calibration, junior general practitioners scored 0.94, mid-level 1.02, and senior 1.5 — so the model landed at the senior-clinician level. 作为参照，初级全科医生得 0.94、中级 1.02、高级 1.5——所以这个模型达到了高级临床医生的水平。 On a larger automated set of 958 rare-disease cases, accuracy 0.698 versus GPT-4's 0.329 and BERT's 0.149, with F1 0.754. 在更大的 958 例罕见病自动化测试集上，准确率 0.698，而 GPT-4 是 0.329、BERT 是 0.149，F1 为 0.754。 On emergencies, 109 manual cases: MetaGP 1.59 versus GPT-4 1.19; and on 2,769 automated cases, accuracy 0.702 and F1 0.783. 在急症上，109 个人工病例：MetaGP 1.59，GPT-4 1.19；在 2,769 个自动化病例上，准确率 0.702、F1 0.783。 The multimodal side held up too: against LLaVA-Med and Med-Flamingo on chest X-ray and CT reports, and in a blinded radiologist review its reports were preferred or tied 53.8% of the time on chest X-ray. 多模态端也站得住：在胸片和 CT 报告上对比 LLaVA-Med 和 Med-Flamingo，在放射科医生的盲评中，其胸片报告有 53.8% 的时候被更青睐或打平。

JORDAN Those are strong, but the single number that jumps out at me is the uplift one. 这些都很强，但真正跳出来打动我的是那个"提升"的数字。

ALEX Same for me, and it's the most transferable result in the paper. 我也一样，而且它是这篇论文里最可迁移的结果。 When physicians were shown MetaGP's output before finalizing their own diagnosis, junior emergency physicians improved by 53%, mid-level by 46%, and senior by 19%. 当医生在敲定自己诊断之前先看到 MetaGP 的输出时，初级急诊医生提升了 53%、中级 46%、高级 19%。 On rare disease the average scores rose across the board — junior, mid, senior went from 0.94, 1.02, 1.5 up to 1.52, 1.62, 1.88. 在罕见病上各层级的平均分全面上升——初级、中级、高级从 0.94、1.02、1.5 升到 1.52、1.62、1.88。 The shape of that result is the point: the model helps the least-experienced most, and it reduced the number of outright harmful diagnoses. 这个结果的形状才是重点：模型对经验最少的人帮助最大，并且减少了明显有害诊断的数量。 That's not a leaderboard score, it's a measured human-plus-AI collaboration effect, and it's stratified by expertise. 这不是排行榜分数，而是实测的"人加 AI"协作效应，并且按专业水平做了分层。 As an engineering footnote, they trained this on 120 A100 GPUs over four weeks for pretraining and 48 A100s over five days per fine-tuning round — heavy, but the kind of budget that's plausibly within reach in a year or two. 作为工程注脚，他们用 120 块 A100 GPU 预训练四周、每轮微调用 48 块 A100 五天——很重，但属于一两年内可能触及的预算。

ALEX Let me add one more thing on why the rare-disease framing should catch Runyu's eye. 让我再补一点，说明为什么"罕见病"这个定位应该引起 Runyu 的注意。 CF is itself a rare disease, and its hard problems aren't the CFTR diagnosis — that's well characterized — but the long tail: which patient will develop CF-related diabetes, liver disease, or an atypical complication, and when. CF 本身就是一种罕见病，而它的难题不在 CFTR 的诊断——那已经刻画得很清楚了——而在长尾：哪位病人会发展出 CF 相关糖尿病、肝病，或非典型并发症，以及何时。 MetaGP's result that a broad model helps most on ambiguous, low-incidence presentations is exactly the profile of those complication-prediction questions. MetaGP 的结果——宽域模型在模糊、低发病率的表现上帮助最大——恰好就是这些并发症预测问题的画像。 So the transfer isn't "diagnose CF," it's "surface the rare downstream event in a phenotypically messy longitudinal record," and that's a genuine registry-adjacent problem. 所以迁移的不是"诊断 CF"，而是"在表型杂乱的纵向记录里浮现罕见的下游事件"，而这是一个真正与登记库相邻的问题。

JORDAN And they made a point about bias — does that hold up? 他们还专门谈了偏差——这站得住吗？

ALEX They make the claim and it's worth taking seriously but not at face value. 他们提出了这个主张，值得认真对待，但不能照单全收。 The argument is that mixing public datasets with private EHRs from varied populations, plus physician-curated rare-disease fine-tuning, plus bias audits during evaluation, reduces demographic skew. 论点是：把公开数据集与来自不同人群的私有 EHR 混合、加上医生策划的罕见病微调、再加上评估阶段的偏差审计，能减少人口统计学上的倾斜。 That's a reasonable design, but "we ran bias audits" without the audit tables is a promise, not a demonstration, and it's precisely the kind of claim the uplift harness could actually test by stratifying not just on clinician experience but on patient subgroup. 这是一个合理的设计，但"我们做了偏差审计"却不给出审计表格，只是承诺而非证明；而这恰恰是那套提升评估工具能真正检验的东西——不仅按临床医生经验分层，还按病人亚组分层。

JORDAN So where does this land for Runyu, and what's the buildable thing? 那么这对 Runyu 落在哪里，可落地的东西是什么？

ALEX This is a Track B paper and it serves the AI-tooling and career lane. 这是一篇 Track B 论文，服务于 AI 工具开发与职业发展这条线。 The tempting takeaway is "build a MetaGP for CF," but the honest, high-leverage piece is the evaluation harness, not the 32B model. 诱人的结论是"给 CF 造一个 MetaGP"，但诚实、杠杆最高的部分是那套评估工具，而不是那个 320 亿参数模型。 So the buildable artifact is a clinician-AI diagnostic-uplift eval harness. 所以可落地的产物是一个"临床医生—AI 诊断提升"评估工具。 The MVP: assemble a set of de-identified case vignettes, collect each clinician's diagnosis both without and with a model's suggestion, and score them on a fixed rubric — the −2 to +2 scale, where +2 is a correct primary diagnosis and −2 is potentially harmful — then report the uplift stratified by clinician experience level. 最小可行版本：汇集一组去标识化的病例小档、收集每位临床医生在"无模型建议"和"有模型建议"两种情况下的诊断，用固定评分标准打分——即 −2 到 +2 的量表，+2 是正确的主要诊断、−2 是可能有害——然后按临床医生经验层级报告提升幅度。 Input is vignettes plus paired pre/post diagnoses plus any candidate model's output; difficulty medium; and crucially it's model-agnostic, so you can plug in GPT-4, an open model, or a future CF-tuned one and compare on the same rubric. 输入是病例档加上配对的前后诊断加上任一候选模型的输出；难度中等；关键是它与模型无关，所以你可以接入 GPT-4、开源模型，或未来某个 CF 微调模型，在同一评分标准上比较。 That harness is the thing that makes any later model claim credible, and it never needs to touch registry internals — it runs on vignettes and human judgments. 这个工具正是让日后任何模型主张变得可信的东西，而且它完全不需要触碰登记库内部——它跑在病例档和人类判断之上。

ALEX Two more numbers worth banking before we move on, because they sharpen the "smaller but curated wins" thesis. 在继续之前还有两个数字值得记下，因为它们让"更小但精整取胜"的论点更锋利。 On the automated rare-disease set MetaGP's precision hit 0.867 against GPT-4's 0.651, and on emergencies precision reached 0.889 against 0.521. 在自动化罕见病集上，MetaGP 的精确率达到 0.867，而 GPT-4 是 0.651；在急症上精确率达到 0.889，而 GPT-4 是 0.521。 High precision matters more than raw accuracy in a decision-support setting, because a false alarm that sends a clinician chasing a wrong rare diagnosis carries real cost. 在决策支持场景里，高精确率比原始准确率更重要，因为一次把临床医生引向错误罕见诊断的假警报，代价是实实在在的。 The junior-radiologist detail rhymes with this: juniors preferred the AI report 29.8% of the time versus seniors at 15.9%, which again says the tool's value concentrates where experience is thin. 初级放射科医生那个细节与此呼应：初级医生有 29.8% 的时候更青睐 AI 报告，而高级医生是 15.9%，这再次说明工具的价值集中在经验薄弱之处。 That's a design principle Runyu can carry into any CF tool — target the experience gap, and measure precision, not just hit rate. 这是 Runyu 可以带进任何 CF 工具的设计原则——瞄准经验缺口，并测量精确率，而不只是命中率。

JORDAN And the continual-pretrain recipe itself — is that reusable, or just the eval? 那持续预训练的配方本身呢——是可复用的，还是只有评估可用？

ALEX The recipe is reusable as a pattern, and that's the second thing to bank. 那个配方作为一种模式是可复用的，这是第二个值得记下的东西。 The pattern is: start from a strong open base like Qwen, continually pretrain on your domain corpus — CF literature, guidelines, textbooks — before you ever fine-tune on tasks, because that's what buys domain fluency cheaply. 这个模式是：从像 Qwen 这样强的开源基座出发，先在你的领域语料上持续预训练——CF 文献、指南、教科书——然后才在任务上微调，因为这才是低成本换取领域流畅度的办法。 MetaGP's own numbers argue for this: it beat GPT-4 badly in-domain despite being far smaller, which says curated domain data can outweigh raw parameter count for a narrow problem. MetaGP 自己的数字就支持这一点：尽管它小得多，却在领域内大幅击败 GPT-4，这说明对一个狭窄问题而言，精心整理的领域数据可以压过原始参数量。 So Runyu's realistic first build isn't the model — it's the eval harness plus a small domain-continual-pretraining sandbox on public CF text, to prove the pattern on something safe before anything near real records. 所以 Runyu 现实的第一步不是造模型——而是那套评估工具，加上一个在公开 CF 文本上的小型领域持续预训练沙盒，先在安全的东西上验证这个模式，再谈接近真实记录的任何事。

JORDAN One thing I want to nail down — why is an open base model like Qwen the right call over just calling GPT-4? 有一件事我想弄清楚——为什么用像 Qwen 这样的开源基座是对的，而不是直接调用 GPT-4？

ALEX Three reasons, and they're all practical rather than ideological. 三个理由，都是务实的而非意识形态的。 One, control of the data path: continual pretraining on sensitive clinical text means the weights have to live somewhere you govern, which a closed API can't offer. 一，掌控数据通路：在敏感临床文本上持续预训练，意味着权重必须放在你能治理的地方，而闭源 API 做不到这一点。 Two, reproducibility: an open base plus a published recipe is something another group — or a regulator — can actually re-run, which is the currency of the tooling-and-career lane. 二，可复现性：开源基座加上公开配方，是另一个团队——或监管方——能真正重跑的东西，而这正是工具与职业这条线的硬通货。 Three, cost and agility at inference: a 32B model you host is cheaper and more controllable per query than a frontier API at clinical volume. 三，推理时的成本与灵活度：你自己托管的 320 亿参数模型，在临床规模的每次查询上，比前沿 API 更便宜、更可控。 MetaGP's own framing leans on all three, and its beat-GPT-4-while-smaller result is the empirical argument that you don't need frontier scale to win a narrow domain.
