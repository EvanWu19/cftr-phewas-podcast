Blurb

A structured review of the whole noncoding-variant pathogenicity prediction field, sorted into three paradigms: integrative annotation-based models (CADD, DANN, GWAVA, FINSURF, ReMM, TraP, NCBoost, regBase), context-dependent sequence-feature hybrids (JARVIS), and self-supervised genomic language models (DNABERT-2, Nucleotide Transformer, HyenaDNA, with DYNA as the clinically fine-tuned exemplar). The review's central argument is that these are trade-offs rather than a progression — annotation-based models keep a durable role because they encode prior knowledge, give traceable evidence, and cost little to deploy, while foundation models win mainly where labels are absent. Two sections carry more weight than the taxonomy: a benchmarking critique showing that high reported AUROC coexists with tools that cannot distinguish different alleles at the same locus (GWAVA) and pathogenic-allele scores barely above the scale midpoint (CADD), and a clinical-translation section arguing for a predefined interpretable-region set, version-pinned annotation with element-to-target-gene mapping, stratified region-specific thresholds rather than one global cutoff, and named functional validation as the actual adjudicator. The review also flags a data-recirculation loop: clinically validated variants are the ones models already scored high, those go into curated databases, and those databases train the next model — a closed loop with no random sample in it. CFTR and cystic fibrosis are never mentioned in this paper; all CF framing in the episode is this podcast's own transfer.

Plain-English glossary

  • AUPRC — Area under the precision–recall curve; the review flags it as more informative than AUROC on heavily imbalanced variant sets.（中文：精确率-召回率曲线下面积，在正负样本严重不平衡时比AUROC更有参考价值。）
  • Deep intronic variant — A variant far enough inside an intron that canonical splice-site rules don't reach it, yet which can still create or activate a splice site.（中文：深部内含子变异，位于内含子深处、经典剪接位点规则覆盖不到，但仍可能造出或激活剪接位点。）
  • Foundation model / gLM — A genomic language model pretrained self-supervised on large unlabeled DNA corpora, then either scored by embedding/likelihood difference or fine-tuned on clinical labels.（中文：基因组语言模型，在大规模无标注DNA语料上自监督预训练，之后既可通过嵌入或似然差打分，也可用临床标签微调。）
  • Functional effect vs pathogenic effect — A functional effect is a measurable molecular perturbation; a pathogenic effect additionally requires that it occur in a disease-relevant tissue or developmental window and translate into phenotype.（中文：功能效应是可测量的分子扰动；致病效应还要求它发生在疾病相关组织或发育窗口中，并转化为表型。）
  • Interpretable-region set — A predefined list of noncoding regions a given clinical workflow is willing to interpret, adopted specifically so the interpretation space stays bounded.（中文：可解释区域集合，即某一临床流程愿意解释的非编码区域预定义清单，其目的是让解释空间保持有界。）
  • MPRA — Massively parallel reporter assay; one of the functional-validation options the review names for adjudicating a high-scoring noncoding candidate.（中文：大规模平行报告实验，是综述点名的、可用于裁定高分非编码候选变异的功能验证手段之一。）
  • Noncoding variant — A DNA change that alters no amino acid; it sits in an intron, promoter, UTR, enhancer, or noncoding RNA.（中文：非编码变异，不改变任何氨基酸，位于内含子、启动子、非翻译区、增强子或非编码RNA中。）
  • Region-specific threshold — A score cutoff defined separately per region class (5'UTR, intron, splice-related, 3'UTR) rather than one global cutoff; CADD derives these from ClinVar noncoding score distributions.（中文：区域特异阈值，按区域类别分别定义的分数阈值，而非一个全局阈值；CADD的此类阈值源自ClinVar非编码变异的分数分布。）
  • Self-supervised pretraining — Training on unlabeled sequence by masked or causal language modeling, so every tokenized nucleotide position becomes a training signal.（中文：自监督预训练，通过掩码或因果语言建模在无标注序列上训练，使每个被分词的核苷酸位置都成为训练信号。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • The three-paradigm sorting itself, as a triage question: for a given CFTR noncoding region, is there annotation coverage (paradigm 1), is the mechanism local-sequence-driven (paradigm 2), or are labels simply absent (paradigm 3)?
  • Region-specific rather than global thresholds, following CADD's own practice of deriving separate cutoffs per region class from ClinVar noncoding score distributions.
  • FINSURF's per-feature contribution output as the pattern for any annotation-based score used in a clinical evidence chain — a score with attribution beats a score.
  • Predefining the interpretable-region set before scoring anything, so the interpretation space is bounded and auditable.
  • Version-pinning both the variant-calling pipeline and the annotation layer, with element-to-target-gene mapping treated as a required, versioned artifact rather than a lookup.
  • Negative-class hygiene as an explicit reporting item: state the MAF threshold and region-matching rule used to build the benign set, since the review shows benign-set construction materially drives apparent performance.
  • Training-set overlap checks (independent test sets, leave-one-out, cross-population/cross-platform validation) before believing any reported number on CFTR variants.

Most of the M is the annotation-versioning discipline, not the table. The value is that it converts "score this variant" into "score this variant, in this region class, against this region's threshold, with this named next experiment" — the form a reviewable call has to take.

JORDAN So what's the actual gap this review is written into? 那么这篇综述是为了填补什么实际空白而写的？

ALEX Two forces converging. 两股力量在汇合。First, most disease-associated variants that population genetics turns up are noncoding, and they act by disrupting transcriptional regulation, RNA processing, or chromatin organization rather than by breaking a protein. 第一，群体遗传学发现的大多数与疾病相关的变异都是非编码的，它们的作用方式是破坏转录调控、RNA加工或染色质组织，而不是破坏某个蛋白质。Second, whole-genome sequencing has moved from research into clinical practice, and each genome contains millions of variants, almost all of them noncoding. 第二，全基因组测序 (WGS) 已经从研究走进临床实践，而每个基因组包含数百万个变异，其中几乎全部都是非编码的。So the interpretation problem didn't get harder in kind, it got harder by several orders of magnitude in scale. 所以解释问题的性质没有变难，但规模上难了好几个数量级。Meanwhile the labels needed to train anything supervised are painfully scarce: experimentally validated pathogenic noncoding variants are a tiny population next to benign ones, so class imbalance and overfitting are structural, not incidental. 同时，训练任何有监督模型所需的标签少得令人痛苦：经过实验验证的致病性非编码变异，与良性变异相比是极小的一个群体，所以类别不平衡和过拟合是结构性的，不是偶然的。The review's organizing move is to group all the published methods into three paradigms and then argue — this is the part I found genuinely useful — that the newest paradigm is not simply the best one. 这篇综述的组织思路是把已发表的方法归为三种范式，然后论证——这是我觉得真正有用的部分——最新的范式并不就是最好的那一个。

JORDAN Walk me through the three. 给我讲讲这三种。

ALEX Paradigm one is integrative annotation-based models. 第一种范式是整合注释类模型。Each variant becomes a tabular feature vector — conservation, selective constraint, epigenetic marks, transcription-factor binding sites, DNA methylation, population-genetic information, genomic context — and a classical learner ranks it. 每个变异被编码成一个表格化的特征向量——保守性、选择约束、表观遗传标记、转录因子结合位点、DNA甲基化、群体遗传学信息、基因组背景——然后由一个经典学习器给它打分排序。CADD is the canonical example: over sixty annotations, labels built from observed human derived alleles versus simulated variants, originally a linear support vector machine, now at version 1.7. CADD是典型代表：六十多种注释，标签由人群中观察到的衍生等位基因与模拟变异对比构建，最初用的是线性支持向量机，现在已经到1.7版。DANN swapped CADD's SVM for a deep neural network on the same features. DANN把CADD的SVM换成了在同一组特征上的深度神经网络。GWAVA, FINSURF, ReMM and TraP use random forests; NCBoost and regBase use XGBoost. JORDAN And paradigm two? 那第二种范式呢？

ALEX Context-dependent sequence feature models — a transitional hybrid that feeds raw genomic sequence and structured annotations into one end-to-end network. 情境依赖型序列特征模型——一种过渡性的混合方法，把原始基因组序列和结构化注释一起送进一个端到端网络。JARVIS is the representative: a convolutional branch slides over raw sequence to pick up local features, a dense branch processes the structured annotations, and the two are fused into one pathogenicity probability. JORDAN And paradigm three is the foundation-model wave. 第三种范式就是基础模型 (foundation model) 的浪潮了。
