Ep3 — Cross-registry benchmarking of data quality

Stausberg et al. (Stud Health Technol Inform, 2023) implement a cross-registry data-quality benchmark across six health-services-research registries, comparing 5 (2020) then 6 (2021) quality indicators both against a predefined threshold and against each other. 74% (2020) and 79% (2021) of results fell outside their threshold's 95% confidence interval — a signal that most measured indicators were statistically distinguishable from target, in either direction. Abstract-only (no PMCID, genuinely closed access) — everything here comes from the published abstract; flagged on-air. This is an aggregate-level evaluation design, distinct from the record-level flagging methods most of this track covers — closer in spirit to the NCDR random-audit-alongside-targeted-report pattern (covered 2026-08-24) but pushed across institutions rather than within one registry's random sample.

Plain-English glossary

  • Quality indicator (registry sense) — An aggregate-level measure (e.g., % completeness) tracked over time and reported back to registry sites, distinct from a record-level flagging rule.（中文：一种汇总层面的衡量指标（如完整性百分比），随时间跟踪并反馈给注册库站点，区别于针对单条记录的标记规则。）
  • Confidence interval (in benchmarking context) — The range within which the true indicator value likely falls; used here to test whether a result is statistically distinguishable from a threshold, not just numerically different.（中文：真实指标值可能落入的区间；此处用于检验一个结果在统计上是否与阈值可区分，而不仅仅是数值不同。）
