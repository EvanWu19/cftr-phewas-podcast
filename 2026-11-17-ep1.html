Challenges and approaches to genetic variants of uncertain significance in clinical practice and research

Li, Morton, Luo, Stark, and Agrawal's 2026 Genome Medicine review is less a new method than a map of the entire VUS-resolution landscape: every established and emerging strategy anyone is currently using to turn a variant of uncertain significance into a confident pathogenic or benign call, laid out side by side. The scale is sobering — VUS rose from 8.2% of ClinVar submissions in 2013 to 53.4% in 2025, and missense variants alone account for 90.2% of them — while the review's own framework (population frequency and constraint, computational predictors, curation guidelines, deep phenotyping, reanalysis, functional and multi-omics data, and AI) reads as a comprehensive taxonomy rather than a prioritized protocol. It directly names CFTR once, citing a 48-variant functional study that found some presumed-pathogenic missense variants had no detectable effect on channel function — a small but concrete anchor for Karen's CFTR variant-interpretation work. The buildable artifact is a CFTR VUS Evidence-Completeness Tracker: not a merged pathogenicity score, but a per-variant map of which of the paper's named evidence streams actually have data behind them for a given CFTR VUS, and which are simply missing.

Plain-English glossary

  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — The ACMG/AMP five-tier classification system's holding bucket for a variant whose evidence doesn't clearly land on pathogenic or benign.（中文：ACMG/AMP五级分类体系中的"收容类别"，用来收纳证据既不足以判定致病、也不足以判定良性的变异。）
  • ACMG/AMP evidence codes — The American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology's shared evidence-code framework (e.g., PM2 for population-database rarity, PP3 for computational pathogenic evidence, PS3 for strong functional pathogenic evidence, BS3 for strong functional benign evidence, BA1 for population frequency high enough to call benign outright).（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会与分子病理学会联合制定的证据编码体系，例如PM2代表在人群数据库中罕见，PP3代表计算证据支持致病，PS3代表强功能致病证据，BS3代表强功能良性证据，BA1代表人群频率高到可直接判定良性。）
  • ClinGen — A curation consortium that rates gene-disease validity (Definitive through Disputed) and runs expert panels that write gene-specific rules for applying ACMG/AMP codes to individual variants.（中文：一个变异整理联盟，负责评定基因-疾病关联的有效性等级（从"确定"到"有争议"），并组织专家小组为具体基因制定应用ACMG/AMP编码的规则。）
  • gnomAD (Genome Aggregation Database) — A population-scale database of variant allele frequencies across hundreds of thousands of sequenced individuals, used to judge whether a variant is rare enough to be disease-relevant.（中文：基因组聚合数据库，汇总数十万已测序人群的变异等位基因频率，用于判断变异是否足够罕见、可能与疾病相关。）
  • Constraint — A measure of how intolerant a gene or genomic region is to variation across human populations; a highly constrained region makes a new variant landing there more suspicious.（中文：衡量一个基因或基因组区域对变异的耐受程度；受约束程度高的区域里出现新变异会更加可疑。）
  • HPO (Human Phenotype Ontology) — A standardized vocabulary clinicians use to describe a patient's clinical features precisely enough to compare across cases ("deep phenotyping").（中文：人类表型本体，临床医生用来精确描述患者临床特征、便于跨病例比较的标准化词汇体系（即"深度表型分析"）。）
  • MAVE (multiplexed assay of variant effect) — A functional assay that tests nearly every possible amino-acid change in a gene at once, using a biological readout as a proxy for whether each variant breaks the protein.（中文：多重变异效应测定，一种能同时检测一个基因几乎所有可能氨基酸变化的功能实验方法，用生物学读数判断每个变异是否破坏蛋白质功能。）
  • SGE (saturation genome editing) — A CRISPR-based method that tests nearly every possible variant directly inside its native genomic location, rather than in an artificial assay plasmid.（中文：饱和基因组编辑，一种基于CRISPR的方法，可在基因原生的基因组位置直接检测几乎所有可能的变异，而非借助人工构建的质粒载体。）
  • Homologous missense constraint (HMC) — A newer constraint metric that outperforms older gene- and region-level scores at flagging which missense VUS deserve closer scrutiny.（中文：同源错义约束，一种较新的约束指标，在标记哪些错义VUS值得进一步审视方面，表现优于传统的基因层面和区域层面指标。）

Full citation

Li Q, Morton SU, Luo S, Stark Z, Agrawal PB. "Challenges and approaches to genetic variants of uncertain significance in clinical practice and research." Genome Medicine. 2026;18(1):173. PMID: 42989384 | PMCID: PMC13737733 | DOI: 10.1186/s13073-026-01740-9

Methods / tools to lift

  • gnomAD v4.1.0 — population-frequency and constraint-metric reference (730,947 exomes, 76,215 genomes) for filtering and flagging VUS.
  • AlphaMissense, ESM1b, VARITY — computational variant-effect predictors with calibrated PP3-strength thresholds (0.990, −24.0, and 0.965 respectively).
  • MAVE and MaveDB/ClinMAVE — multiplexed functional assays and their aggregating databases (MaveDB >7M measurements; ClinMAVE >2.1M variants across 821 genes).
  • Saturation genome editing (SGE) — CRISPR-based in-situ variant-effect testing, with worked examples across VHL, BAP1, FKRP, LARGE1, RAD51C, TP53, BRCA2, and RNU4-2.
  • Deep phenotyping via HPO + systematic VUS reanalysis — two low-tech, high-yield resolution strategies with documented reclassification rates.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation (method only — the paper names CFTR just once, via a 48-variant functional study citation, and never touches CFTR2 or registry structure). Buildable artifact: CFTR VUS Evidence-Completeness Tracker — input a CFTR VUS list (HGVS notation or ClinVar IDs), pull gnomAD allele frequency and constraint metrics via API, check whether each variant has an existing calibrated computational predictor score, and flag category-by-category whether functional or multi-omics evidence exists in the literature for that position. Output is a per-variant evidence-gap scorecard, not a merged pathogenicity verdict. Difficulty: M (mostly API wiring and literature-flagging logic, no new statistical modeling).

What to verify first

  • Whether a single gene-level constraint score means much for CFTR specifically, given it's a large multi-domain transporter where missense tolerance likely varies sharply between nucleotide-binding, transmembrane, and regulatory regions.
  • Whether a CFTR-wide MAVE or saturation-genome-editing dataset exists with coverage comparable to BRCA1 or TP53 — if not, the tracker's "functional evidence" category would stay mostly empty, which is itself informative but should be confirmed rather than assumed.
  • Whether gnomAD's ancestry representation is adequate for the populations actually present in the variant backlog, given the paper's own TNNT2 example of a variant misclassified as uncertain purely from thin reference-population sampling.
