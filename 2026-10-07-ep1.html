Blurb

Lindquist et al.'s 2026 Ophthalmology Science paper benchmarks AlphaMissense against ClinVar across 107 inherited-retinal-disease (IRD) genes, using 4204 definitively classified missense variants as ground truth. The headline numbers are lopsided in an informative way: specificity of 94.1% and sensitivity of only 79.4%, with specificity reaching a full 100% in the four most commonly implicated IRD genes (ABCA4, USH2A, RPGR, PRPH2). The weak spot is hypomorphic variants — partial-function changes that produce milder, variable phenotypes — where AlphaMissense correctly called only one of nine well-characterized ABCA4 hypomorphs pathogenic. The authors show that layering allele frequency, homozygote count, and CADD score on top of a raw AlphaMissense call meaningfully helps catch its likely false negatives, and demonstrate the point clinically: targeted long-read sequencing on one patient found a candidate ABCA4 variant AlphaMissense scored as benign sitting in trans with a known pathogenic variant, completing a molecular diagnosis AlphaMissense alone would have missed. This lands on the Karen Raraigh collaboration because CFTR has its own well-documented population of hypomorphic, partial-function variants, and its R domain is a known intrinsically disordered region — the exact structural context where AlphaMissense's AlphaFold2-derived signal is least informative. Buildable: a CFTR hypomorphic-flag cross-check that re-flags AlphaMissense-benign CFTR variants whose population metrics don't match a confident true-negative profile.

Plain-English glossary

  • Inherited retinal disease (IRD) — a group of genetic disorders causing progressive photoreceptor/retinal pigment epithelium failure and vision loss.（中文：遗传性视网膜疾病（IRD），一组导致光感受器细胞和视网膜色素上皮逐渐衰竭、引起视力丧失的遗传性疾病。）
  • Missense variant — a DNA change that substitutes one amino acid for another in the protein, as opposed to a nonsense or frameshift variant that typically abolishes protein function outright.（中文：错义变异，一种使蛋白质中某个氨基酸被替换为另一个氨基酸的DNA变化，区别于通常直接导致蛋白功能完全丧失的无义变异或移码变异。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant a lab cannot confidently call pathogenic or benign, usually producing a nondiagnostic genetic test result.（中文：VUS（意义未明变异），实验室无法确信判定为致病或良性的变异，通常导致基因检测结果无法给出明确诊断。）
  • Hypomorphic variant — a variant that only partially reduces protein function rather than abolishing it, often producing a milder, later-onset, more variable phenotype.（中文：低效变异（hypomorphic variant），只部分降低蛋白功能而非彻底摧毁功能的变异，往往表现为更轻、发病更晚、表型更多变。）
  • AlphaMissense — a deep learning model that scores every possible human missense substitution (0–1) by combining AlphaFold2 structural prediction, evolutionary conservation, and population allele frequency.（中文：AlphaMissense，一种深度学习模型，综合AlphaFold2结构预测、进化保守性和人群等位基因频率，为每一种可能的人类错义替换打分（0到1之间）。）
  • AlphaFold2 — a deep learning system that predicts a protein's 3D structure from its amino-acid sequence.（中文：AlphaFold2，一种根据氨基酸序列预测蛋白质三维结构的深度学习系统。）
  • CADD (Combined Annotation Dependent Depletion) — an older variant-effect score integrating many genomic annotations into a single deleteriousness estimate.（中文：CADD（Combined Annotation Dependent Depletion），一种较早的变异效应评分，整合多种基因组注释信息，得出单一的综合有害性估计值。）
  • gnomAD (Genome Aggregation Database) — the standard public reference for how frequently a variant appears in real, mostly healthy human populations.（中文：gnomAD（基因组聚合数据库），衡量某个变异在真实的、大多健康的人群中出现频率的标准公共参考数据库。）
  • Intrinsically disordered region — a stretch of a protein that doesn't fold into a stable 3D structure, the kind of region where AlphaFold2-derived structural signal is least reliable.（中文：内在无序区（intrinsically disordered region），蛋白质中不形成稳定三维结构的区段，是AlphaFold2结构信号最不可靠的区域类型。）
  • Phased variant / long-read sequencing — sequencing reads long enough to determine whether two variants sit on the same chromosome copy or different ones (in trans), which standard short-read sequencing often cannot resolve.（中文：单体型分析（phasing）／长读长测序，指读长足够长、能判断两个变异是位于同一条染色体拷贝上还是不同拷贝上（即trans关系）的测序技术，这是常规短读长测序常常无法解决的问题。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Gene-panel-scoped predictor benchmarking — restricting a genome-wide predictor's evaluation to a clinically defined gene list (IRD panels) rather than a whole-genome or whole-ClinVar average, which surfaces gene-family-specific failure modes a global benchmark would average away.
  • Three-metric false-negative/false-positive discriminator (allele frequency + homozygote count + CADD) — a lightweight, no-retraining-required way to flag likely predictor mistakes using only public population data.
  • ClinVar star-rating confidence filtering — restricting ground truth to variants with assertion criteria provided (1-star or higher), rather than trusting every ClinVar label equally.
  • Phased long-read sequencing for compound-heterozygote confirmation — using read-length-driven haplotype resolution to confirm two candidate variants sit in trans, completing a molecular diagnosis a predictor alone could not.

Serves Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation and VUS pathogenicity prediction directly — CFTR's own hypomorphic, partial-function variant population and its intrinsically disordered R domain mirror exactly the structural context where this paper found AlphaMissense weakest. What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR's documented hypomorphic/partial-function variants actually cluster in unstructured or low-AlphaFold-confidence regions the way ABCA4's hypomorphs did, rather than assuming the structural analogy holds without checking CFTR's own structural annotation.
  • Whether the specific population-metric thresholds useful for ABCA4 transfer numerically to CFTR, given CFTR's very different carrier-frequency landscape as an autosomal recessive gene compared to most IRD genes.
  • What fraction of CFTR's own ClinVar entries meet the one-star-or-higher confidence bar this paper required, since a thinner evidence base would weaken any CFTR-specific version of this benchmark.
