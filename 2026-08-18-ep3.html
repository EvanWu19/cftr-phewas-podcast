How did this episode land for you?

Ep3 (Track C, #1) — LMSz: Building a Condition-Specific Growth Reference from Small Data

Low et al. (2026, European Journal of Human Genetics) tackle the layer underneath any outlier-flagging rule: the reference itself. Conventional LMS growth charts need many thousands of individuals to fit reliably — exactly what rare-disease cohorts don't have. LMSz works around this by converting rare-disease measurements to z-scores against a large baseline reference (UK90) first, then fitting only two simple straight lines against age — a mean (M) line and a spread (S) line, each optionally sex-specific — via GAMLSS, selecting among ~30-45 candidate models with BIC (97% specificity for the correct null model on synthetic random data, vs. 53% for AIC). Applied to six genes (ANKRD11, ARID1B, ASXL3, DDX3X, KMT2A, SATB2), the resulting charts largely matched published clinical descriptions, and for four of the six genes, no growth chart had existed before this paper at all. Compared head-to-head against conventional LMS-on-raw-data for Mowat-Wilson Syndrome, LMSz avoided a real overfitting failure — raw LMS's post-pubertal centiles kept rising due to sparse older-age data, while LMSz's stayed anchored to the external reference and correctly plateaued — but diverged from raw LMS on BMI's 98th centile, because LMSz inherits UK90's skewness pattern wholesale and BMI's true skew didn't match. The authors plan to automate the method within DECIPHER for continuous updating. The core question this raises for the Liz assignment, without asserting anything about how CFFPR's own rules work: should a flagging reference be general-population or condition-specific, especially for a condition whose growth pattern has shifted across treatment eras?

Plain-English glossary

  • BCCG (Box-Cox Cole-Green) — the three-parameter distribution family (L=skewness, M=median, S=spread) that conventional LMS growth charts are built on. （中文：Box-Cox Cole-Green分布族，传统LMS生长图表所依据的三参数分布族（L=偏度，M=中位数，S=离散度）。）
  • BIC vs. AIC (model selection) — two competing penalties for model complexity; BIC penalizes extra parameters more heavily, favoring simpler models when the true answer is "no real effect." （中文：BIC与AIC，两种对模型复杂度的惩罚方式；当真实答案是"没有真实效应"时，BIC对额外参数的惩罚更重，因此更倾向于选择更简单的模型。）
  • GAMLSS — Generalised Additive Models for Location, Scale and Shape, a regression framework that can fit not just a mean but also spread and skewness as functions of covariates. （中文：位置-尺度-形状广义可加模型（GAMLSS），一种回归框架，不仅能拟合均值，还能将离散度和偏度作为协变量的函数来拟合。）
  • LMS method — the standard way population growth charts are built: three smooth age-varying curves (L=skewness, M=median, S=spread) fit from a large reference sample. （中文：LMS方法，构建人群生长图表的标准方法：从大型参考样本中拟合出三条随年龄变化的平滑曲线（L=偏度，M=中位数，S=离散度）。）
  • UK90 — the UK's official growth reference for birth and ages 4-20, also used by the DECIPHER genomic database. （中文：UK90，英国官方的出生及4至20岁生长参考标准，也是DECIPHER基因组数据库所使用的参考标准。）
  • Z-score (in growth charts) — a raw measurement's standardized deviation from a reference population's median at the same age/sex, convertible back to a centile. （中文：z分数（在生长图表语境下），指原始测量值相对于参考人群同年龄/性别中位数的标准化偏差，可转换回百分位。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • Z-score-scale reference borrowing — convert small-cohort measurements to z-scores against a large external baseline first, so condition-specific deviation reduces to fitting two simple linear (mean/spread) terms rather than a full three-parameter curve.
  • BIC-based small-universe model selection — when the candidate model space is small and enumerable (~30-45 models here), BIC's stronger complexity penalty gives much better-calibrated null-model selection than AIC.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

(a) Candidate generation — LMSz doesn't flag anything itself, but offers a concrete way to build a condition-specific reference (the yardstick a flagging rule's z-score depends on) by borrowing strength from a population baseline, rather than defaulting to a general-population reference. MVP: implement LMSz on synthetic CF-shaped longitudinal height/weight data anchored to a public CDC baseline, and compare the resulting condition-specific z-score reference against plain CDC-referenced z-scores to see how many borderline points flip flagged status — using pre- vs. post-modulator-era growth patterns as a natural test case. Difficulty: S–M.

What to verify first

  • LMSz's sensitivity (whether it captures a genotype's true growth pattern) can't be tested the way its specificity was — true centiles for any genotype are inherently unknown. This is a structural evaluation gap, not something this method resolves.
  • Conditions that attenuate the pubertal growth spurt (achondroplasia named explicitly) produce a spurious pubertal spurt under LMSz; whether CF's own growth pattern, especially modulator-era, has any comparably attenuated feature is an open question, not to be assumed either way.
  • The skewness-transfer assumption already showed real divergence for BMI in the MWS comparison — check whether a given measurement's true skew plausibly matches the chosen baseline before trusting a condition-specific reference built this way.

Pipeline stage

(a) Candidate generation (reference/baseline layer, upstream of any flagging rule).

Error types covered / not covered

Not an error-flagging method — a reference-construction method. Doesn't address any specific error type (unit conversion, digit transposition, carry-forward, etc.) directly; instead determines what counts as "implausible" in the first place by defining the yardstick a flagging rule measures against.

ALEX Track C's first pick today is a 2026 paper in the European Journal of Human Genetics by Low and colleagues, "The LMSz method — an automatable scalable approach to constructing gene-specific growth charts in rare disorders." 今天C轨道的第一篇论文来自2026年发表在《欧洲人类遗传学杂志》（European Journal of Human Genetics）上的研究，作者是Low等人，题目是《LMSz方法——一种可自动化、可扩展的、用于构建罕见病基因特异性生长图表的方法》。 It's not about outlier flagging directly — it's about the reference itself, the yardstick every plausibility check ultimately measures against, which is exactly the layer underneath any flagging rule we've covered so far. 它不是直接讲异常值标记的——而是讲参考标准本身，也就是每一个合理性检验最终都要拿来衡量的那把尺子，这恰恰是我们迄今为止讨论过的所有标记规则底层所依赖的那一层。

JORDAN Before the method — remind me what the LMS method actually is, since this paper is explicitly a variant of it. 在讲这个方法之前——提醒一下我LMS方法到底是什么，因为这篇论文明确是它的一个变体。

ALEX LMS is the conventional way population growth charts get built, and it fits three smooth curves against age from a large reference sample: M is the median, the 50th centile curve; S is the coefficient of variation, essentially how spread out measurements are around that median at each age; and L is a skewness parameter that lets the distribution be asymmetric rather than forcing it to be a plain bell curve. LMS是构建人群生长图表的常规方法，它针对年龄拟合三条平滑曲线，来自一个大型参考样本：M是中位数，即第50百分位曲线；S是变异系数，本质上是每个年龄段测量值围绕中位数的离散程度；L是一个偏度参数，使分布可以呈现非对称形态，而不是被强制成一条普通的钟形曲线。 Any centile curve can be reconstructed from those three, and the process reverses cleanly too — a raw measurement converts to an exact z-score, a standardized deviation from the reference. 任何百分位曲线都可以由这三者重建出来，而且这个过程也可以干净地反向操作——一个原始测量值可以转换为精确的z分数，即相对于参考标准的标准化偏差。 The catch is that LMS needs a lot of data to work well, ideally many thousands of individuals per curve, which is exactly what a rare-disease cohort doesn't have. 问题在于，LMS要想拟合得好，需要大量数据，理想情况下每条曲线需要数千人，而这正是罕见病队列所不具备的。

JORDAN So what's the actual trick that lets LMSz work with a small sample? 那么，能让LMSz在小样本上也能工作的具体窍门是什么？

ALEX It borrows strength from an existing large baseline reference, in this case UK90, the UK's official growth reference for birth and ages four to twenty and the same one used by the DECIPHER genomic database. 它从一个已有的大型基线参考中"借用力量"，本例中用的是UK90，也就是英国官方的出生及4至20岁生长参考标准，也是DECIPHER基因组数据库所使用的同一个参考标准。 Every rare-disease measurement first gets converted to a z-score against UK90. 每一个罕见病测量值，首先都会转换为相对于UK90的z分数。 On the z-score scale, the reference population's centile curves become perfectly flat horizontal lines — the 50th centile is a flat line at z equals zero for every age, since by construction z-scores are already age-and-sex-adjusted. 在z分数尺度上，参考人群的百分位曲线会变成完全水平的直线——第50百分位在所有年龄段都是z等于0的一条水平线，因为z分数本身在构造时就已经对年龄和性别做了调整。 That turns "does this rare-disease group deviate from the reference" into a much simpler question: is the rare-disease group's own z-score mean, and its own z-score spread, still flat at zero and one respectively, or do they trend with age? 这就把"这个罕见病群体是否偏离参考标准"这个问题，转化成了一个简单得多的问题：这个罕见病群体自己的z分数均值，以及自己的z分数离散程度，是否仍然分别在0和1附近保持平坦，还是说它们会随年龄呈现出趋势？ LMSz assumes the answer can be captured with a straight line for the mean, called the M line, and a straight line for the spread, called the S line, each allowed to differ by sex, fit using GAMLSS with a plain Normal distribution rather than the more complex Box-Cox Cole-Green family LMS itself uses. LMSz假设这个答案可以用一条直线来刻画均值（称为M线），再用一条直线来刻画离散程度（称为S线），两者都允许因性别而不同，使用GAMLSS拟合，采用简单的正态分布，而不是LMS本身所使用的更复杂的Box-Cox Cole-Green分布族。 There's no equivalent L, skewness line, at all, because the Normal distribution isn't skewed by definition — the method simply assumes any real skewness in the rare-disease data matches whatever skewness UK90 already has baked in from its own L curve. 这里根本没有对应的L（偏度）线，因为正态分布按定义就是不偏的——这个方法只是假设罕见病数据中真实存在的任何偏度，都与UK90自身L曲线中已经内嵌的偏度相匹配。

JORDAN That's a fair number of assumptions riding on a small dataset. 这对一个小数据集来说，需要承担相当多的假设。

ALEX It is, and I'd flag that directly rather than let it pass. 确实如此，这一点我想直接指出来，而不是含糊带过。 Four assumptions carry real weight here: that the rare-disease median is a straight line against age, that it can differ by sex, that the spread is also a straight line, and that the skewness pattern transfers unchanged from the baseline reference. 这里有四个假设承担着实质性的权重：罕见病群体的中位数相对年龄是一条直线，它可以因性别而不同，离散程度也是一条直线，以及偏度模式从基线参考不变地迁移过来。 The authors do build in a safety valve for the first three — GAMLSS considers the whole universe of simpler models, dropping the sex term, the age term, or the interaction if its coefficient is small enough, and selects among roughly 30 to 45 candidate models total using BIC rather than AIC, specifically because AIC tends to pick overly complex models. 作者们确实为前三个假设内置了一个安全阀——GAMLSS会考虑简化模型的整个空间，如果系数足够小，就去掉性别项、年龄项或交互项，并在总共约30到45个候选模型中，使用BIC而不是AIC来选择，具体原因是AIC倾向于选出过于复杂的模型。 On synthetic random-Normal data where the correct answer is "no real trend at all," the BIC-selected models correctly picked the plain null model 97% of the time, versus only 53% for AIC — real numbers, not a hand-wave, and a meaningfully better calibrated result. 在真实答案是"完全没有真实趋势"的合成随机正态数据上，BIC选出的模型有97%的时候正确地选中了这个纯粹的零模型，而AIC只有53%——这是真实的数字，不是空口白话，是一个明显校准更好的结果。 But the fourth assumption, skewness transferring unchanged from the baseline, has no equivalent safety valve, and it shows up as a real limitation later. 但第四个假设——偏度不变地从基线迁移过来——没有对应的安全阀，而这在后面确实体现为一个真实的局限。

JORDAN Where did that skewness assumption actually break down in the results? 这个偏度假设在结果中具体是在哪里出现问题的？

ALEX In the direct head-to-head comparison against conventional LMS applied straight to raw data, using the Mowat-Wilson Syndrome consortium's own dataset. 在与直接对原始数据应用传统LMS方法进行的正面对比中，使用的是Mowat-Wilson综合征联盟自己的数据集。 For height and weight, the two methods agreed reasonably well. 对于身高和体重，两种方法的一致性相当不错。 For BMI specifically, the 98th centiles diverged more noticeably, because BMI is markedly right-skewed at older ages in a way that doesn't match UK90's own skewness pattern at those ages — since LMSz has no L-line of its own, it just inherits UK90's skew assumption, and that assumption doesn't hold for this particular measurement. 但具体到BMI，第98百分位的分歧就明显得多，因为BMI在较大年龄段呈现出明显的右偏，而这种偏态方式与UK90自身在那些年龄段的偏度模式并不匹配——由于LMSz本身没有L线，它只是继承了UK90的偏度假设，而这个假设在这个特定测量指标上并不成立。 More encouragingly, though, LMSz avoided a specific overfitting failure mode that plain LMS-on-raw-data fell into: with too few data points at older ages, the raw-data LMS centiles kept rising after puberty despite growth having actually ended, while LMSz's centiles correctly plateaued, because they stay anchored to the external UK90 reference rather than being pulled around by whatever sparse data happens to sit at the oldest ages. 不过更令人鼓舞的是，LMSz避开了一个特定的过拟合失败模式，而直接对原始数据应用LMS则掉进了这个陷阱：由于较大年龄段数据点太少，原始数据LMS得出的百分位曲线在青春期结束后依然继续上升，尽管实际生长已经停止，而LMSz的百分位曲线则正确地趋于平稳，因为它始终锚定在外部的UK90参考上，而不会被恰好落在最大年龄段的稀疏数据牵着走。 The method was also stress-tested on a genuinely tiny sample — DDX3X syndrome with only five male cases — and still produced a usable, if appropriately cautious, chart, consistent with the published literature on the phenotype. 这个方法还在一个真正微小的样本上做了压力测试——DDX3X综合征只有5例男性病例——依然产生了一个可用的、虽然需要谨慎对待的图表，与已发表的表型文献一致。

JORDAN Beyond ANKRD11 and Mowat-Wilson, did they test this on other genes, and did the charts actually match what's already known clinically? 除了ANKRD11和Mowat-Wilson之外，他们还在其他基因上测试过这个方法吗？得到的图表是否真的和已知的临床认识相符？

ALEX Yes, five more: ARID1B, ASXL3, DDX3X, KMT2A, and SATB2, and in most cases the charts lined up with what was already reported clinically, which is itself a form of validation even without a formal accuracy metric. 是的，还有五个基因：ARID1B、ASXL3、DDX3X、KMT2A和SATB2，在大多数情况下，生成的图表与已有的临床报道相符，即便没有正式的准确性指标，这本身也是一种验证形式。 For KMT2A, which causes Wiedemann-Steiner Syndrome, the charts landed the median height, weight, and head circumference at roughly the 2nd, 2nd, and 25th centiles respectively, concordant with published descriptions of short stature and microcephaly in about a third of cases. 对于导致Wiedemann-Steiner综合征的KMT2A，图表得出的身高、体重和头围中位数分别大约落在第2、第2和第25百分位，与已发表文献中约三分之一病例出现矮小身材和小头畸形的描述一致。 For ASXL3-related disorder, median height came out around the 12th centile with BMI rising with age, matching the known pattern of poor postnatal growth from feeding issues that stabilizes after intervention, followed later by weight gain. 对于ASXL3相关疾病，身高中位数大约落在第12百分位，BMI随年龄上升，这与已知的模式相符——出生后因喂养问题导致生长不良，经过干预后趋于稳定，之后体重逐渐上升。 For ARID1B, direct comparison against the existing Coffin-Siris Syndrome chart wasn't fully possible since that chart covers a more heterogeneous group of genes and different endpoints, but the overlapping centiles were broadly consistent. 对于ARID1B，由于现有的Coffin-Siris综合征图表覆盖的是一个更异质化的基因组合以及不同的终点指标，直接比较并不完全可行，但重叠部分的百分位大体上是一致的。 None of ANKRD11, KMT2A, DDX3X, or ASXL3 had any published growth chart before this paper at all, so for those genes LMSz isn't just an alternative method, it's the only chart that exists. ANKRD11、KMT2A、DDX3X和ASXL3在本文之前都没有任何已发表的生长图表，所以对这些基因而言，LMSz不仅仅是一种替代方法，而是目前唯一存在的图表。

JORDAN What's the plan for keeping charts like these up to date as more cases accumulate? 随着更多病例的积累，要如何让这类图表保持更新？

ALEX The authors plan to automate LMSz directly within DECIPHER, the same genomic database that already uses UK90 as its reference, drawing on DECIPHER's own open-access data alongside further measurements being collected through the GenROC study, so gene-specific charts become continuously updatable rather than a one-time analysis frozen at publication. 作者计划直接在DECIPHER内部自动化LMSz，而DECIPHER本身就已经使用UK90作为参考标准，同时利用DECIPHER自身的开放获取数据，加上通过GenROC研究持续收集的新测量数据，使得基因特异性图表可以持续更新，而不是一次性分析、发表后就被冻结。 That automatability is arguably the point of the method as much as the statistics are — a rare-disease chart that has to be manually rebuilt every time a handful of new cases turn up isn't nearly as useful as one that can absorb new data on a rolling basis with the same lightweight linear-regression machinery each time. 这种可自动化性，某种程度上和统计方法本身一样，正是这个方法的意义所在——一个每次多出几个新病例就得手动重建的罕见病图表，远不如一个每次都能用同样轻量级线性回归机制、持续吸收新数据的图表来得有用。

JORDAN Does this change anything about the CFFPR outlier pipeline specifically? 那这对CFFPR异常值检测流程具体会带来什么改变吗？

ALEX This touches candidate generation, and it does so at the most upstream layer possible — not a flagging rule itself, but the reference that any flagging rule's z-score depends on in the first place. 这涉及候选生成（candidate generation），而且是在最上游、最靠前的一层——不是一个标记规则本身，而是任何标记规则的z分数从一开始就依赖的那个参考标准。 The open question this method offers a concrete way to explore, without asserting anything about how CFFPR's current rules actually work, is whether a condition-specific reference — built by borrowing strength from a large general population baseline the way LMSz does — would shift which points count as implausible relative to using a general-population reference outright, particularly for a condition where growth patterns are known to differ from the general population and have also shifted meaningfully across treatment eras. 这个方法提供了一种具体的方式来探索这样一个开放问题，同时不对CFFPR现有规则的实际运作方式做任何断言：一个像LMSz那样通过从大型普通人群基线"借用力量"而构建的、针对特定病种的参考标准，是否会改变哪些点被判定为不合理，相较于直接使用一般人群参考标准而言——尤其是对于一种已知生长模式与普通人群不同、并且在不同治疗时代之间也发生了有意义变化的疾病。 The buildable artifact: implement LMSz on synthetic CF-shaped longitudinal height and weight data, anchored to a public CDC baseline rather than UK90, and compare the resulting condition-specific z-score reference lines against plain CDC-referenced z-scores to see, empirically, how many borderline points would flip flagged status depending on which reference is used — pre- and post-modulator-era growth patterns treated as a natural test case for the method's assumptions. 可构建的成果：在合成的、模拟CF形态的纵向身高体重数据上实现LMSz，锚定在一个公开的CDC基线上而不是UK90，然后比较由此得到的特定病种z分数参考线与直接基于CDC参考的z分数，从经验上看有多少临界点会因为使用不同参考标准而改变标记状态——把调节剂时代前后的生长模式，作为检验这个方法假设的一个天然测试案例。 Difficulty S to M — GAMLSS and the Normal-distribution regression machinery are routine, and the CDC baseline is already public; the real work is building realistic synthetic CF-shaped trajectories and being honest about where the method's assumptions might not transfer. 难度S到M——GAMLSS和正态分布回归的计算机制是常规操作，CDC基线也已经公开；真正的工作在于构建真实合理的、模拟CF形态的合成轨迹，并诚实地评估这个方法的假设在哪些地方可能无法迁移。
