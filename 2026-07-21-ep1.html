Ep 1 — Track A (variant interpretation) — 2026-07-21

Blurb

Krusiński and colleagues Sanger-sequenced two exons of SLC26A9 — the exons containing the two variants Luo et al. reported in rapidly progressing CF in 2024 — in 20 adult CF patients in Lublin, Poland. They found neither Luo variant (a null replication), but incidentally picked up a novel exon-17 missense variant, NM_052934.4:c.1847C>T (p.Pro616Leu), in two siblings. The siblings are mild (FEV1 82% and 84% predicted vs a cohort mean of 54%), but they also carry the class-V splicing allele c.3140-26A>G in trans with p.Phe508del, which already predicts exactly that picture — so the phenotype contrast is confounded and the authors say so. The interpretive value is elsewhere: the same variant, the same evidence codes, gets called VUS by Franklin (PM2) and likely benign by GeneBe (PM2 + BP4), while the paper simultaneously notes the residue sits in a never-modelled, compositionally biased stretch of the STAS domain where structure-based prediction is not reliable. That is an applicability-domain condition on a computational score, and no ACMG evidence table records it. Buildable artifact: a disorder-aware predictor-reliability flagger that joins AlphaFold pLDDT and UniProt region annotations to a variant list and red-flags structure-dependent scores where they should not be trusted.

Plain-English glossary

  • Bronchiectasis — permanent widening and scarring of the airways, a structural consequence of repeated infection and inflammation.（中文：支气管扩张——气道永久性扩张并伴瘢痕，是反复感染和炎症的结构性后果。）
  • Class V CFTR variant — a variant that causes a splicing abnormality but allows some correctly spliced, functional CFTR to still be produced ("leaky" splicing); typically milder disease with preserved pancreatic function.（中文：V 类 CFTR 变异——造成剪接异常但仍有部分正确剪接的功能性 CFTR 被合成，即"渗漏型"剪接；通常病情较轻且胰腺功能保留。）
  • Compositional bias — a stretch of protein sequence dominated by a few residue types (here serine/threonine and lysine/arginine); alignment and conservation estimates are less reliable in such regions.（中文：组成偏倚——蛋白质序列中由少数几种残基主导的一段；这类区域的序列比对与保守性估计都不太可靠。）
  • Gating variant — a CFTR variant where the channel reaches the membrane but fails to open properly (e.g. p.Gly551Asp); the target class for the potentiator ivacaftor.（中文：门控型变异——通道能到达细胞膜但无法正常开放的 CFTR 变异，例如 p.Gly551Asp；是增效剂 ivacaftor 的目标类别。）
  • HGVS — the standardised nomenclature for writing variants; c. denotes coding-DNA coordinates, p. protein coordinates, and both are meaningless without a stated reference transcript.（中文：HGVS——变异书写的标准化命名规范；c. 表示编码 DNA 坐标，p. 表示蛋白质坐标，二者脱离指定的参考转录本都没有意义。）
  • Intrinsically disordered region — a protein segment that does not fold into a fixed 3D structure but samples many conformations; often functional (regulatory switches) but unmodellable.（中文：内在无序区——不折叠成固定三维结构、在多种构象间采样的蛋白质区段；常有功能（如调控开关）但无法建模。）
  • Missense variant — a single-base change that swaps one amino acid for another; the hardest class to interpret because the effect ranges from catastrophic to inert.（中文：错义变异——单碱基改变导致一个氨基酸被另一个替换；最难解读的一类，因为效应可以从灾难性到完全无害。）
  • Modifier gene — a gene elsewhere in the genome whose variation shifts disease severity without causing the disease.（中文：修饰基因——基因组中其他位置的基因，其变异会改变疾病严重程度但本身不致病。）
  • PM2 / BP4 (ACMG evidence codes) — PM2 = moderate pathogenic evidence from extreme rarity in population databases; BP4 = supporting benign evidence from concordant computational predictions.（中文：PM2 / BP4（ACMG 证据编码）——PM2 指基于人群数据库中极度罕见的中等致病性证据；BP4 指基于计算预测一致的良性支持性证据。）
  • Potentiator vs corrector — a potentiator (ivacaftor) forces a membrane-resident CFTR channel to open; a corrector (tezacaftor, elexacaftor) helps a misfolded CFTR reach the membrane in the first place.（中文：增效剂与矫正剂——增效剂（ivacaftor）促使已在细胞膜上的 CFTR 通道开放；矫正剂（tezacaftor、elexacaftor）则帮助错误折叠的 CFTR 先到达细胞膜。）
  • SLC26A9 — solute carrier family 26 member 9; an epithelial anion transporter acting both as a chloride channel and a chloride–bicarbonate exchanger, hypothesised to partly compensate for lost CFTR function.（中文：SLC26A9——溶质载体家族 26 成员 9；一种上皮阴离子转运蛋白，兼具氯离子通道与氯离子–碳酸氢根交换体功能，被假设可部分代偿丧失的 CFTR 功能。）
  • STAS domain — sulfate transporter and anti-sigma factor antagonist domain; the cytoplasmic domain of SLC26A9 (~residues 562–654) that physically binds the CFTR regulatory domain, and which contains an inhibitory disordered loop.（中文：STAS 结构域——sulfate transporter and anti-sigma factor antagonist 结构域；SLC26A9 的胞质结构域（约 562–654 残基），与 CFTR 调控结构域直接结合，其中含有一个抑制性的无序环区。）
  • Sweat chloride — the diagnostic measure of CFTR function; the reference range is <30 mmol/L, and CF-range values are typically >60.（中文：汗液氯离子——CFTR 功能的诊断性指标；参考范围为低于 30 mmol/L，CF 范围通常高于 60。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant with insufficient or conflicting evidence to classify as pathogenic or benign.（中文：VUS，意义未明变异——证据不足或互相矛盾，无法判定为致病或良性的变异。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Targeted candidate-region rescreen — take the exact positions reported by a prior rare-variant paper and Sanger-sequence only those exons in a new cohort. Cheap and fast; the cost is that the denominator for any frequency statement is wrong (2 of 21 exons here).
  • Sanger sequencing — capillary electrophoresis read of a PCR amplicon; gold-standard confirmation, but only sees what you aimed at. The authors themselves recommend full-length NGS as the next step.
  • Franklin / GeneBe — variant curation platforms that apply ACMG evidence codes automatically; useful precisely because they disagree here and the disagreement is diagnosable.
  • gnomAD v4 — population allele-frequency reference used to justify PM2; only interpretable alongside its ancestry composition.
  • AlphaFold pLDDT — per-residue model confidence; a ready-made, position-resolved proxy for "is this region structurally modellable at all."
  • UniProt region annotations — domain, disordered region, compositional bias, low-complexity calls, all position-indexed and joinable to a variant table.
  • dbNSFP-style predictor panels (SIFT, PolyPhen-2, CADD, MetaLR, MetaRNN, PhyloP) — the standard evidence panel; note MetaLR and MetaRNN split hard on this variant.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation.

disorder-aware-predictor-reliability-flagger (S–M) — input: a variant list in HGVS plus a UniProt accession. Map each variant to its protein residue; join (a) AlphaFold per-residue pLDDT at that position, (b) UniProt region annotations covering it (domain / disordered / compositional bias / low-complexity), and (c) a small curated table of which predictors in your evidence panel are structure-dependent vs sequence- or conservation-only. Output: a red/amber/green trust flag on the structure-dependent scores per variant, plus a consensus that down-weights them where the flag is red. One CSV in, one CSV out. The fiddly part is transcript ↔ protein ↔ AlphaFold coordinate mapping, not the logic.

What to verify first

  • Sibling non-independence — two carriers who are siblings share haplotypes by descent; this is one independent observation, not two. Unrelated carriers are needed before p.Pro616Leu counts twice.
  • Ancestry behind the allele frequency — AF 0.0000197 in gnomAD v4 is only interpretable if Polish/Slavic samples are meaningfully represented. "Truly rare" and "under-sampled" produce the same empty cell and opposite conclusions.
  • Direction of effect is genuinely open — Chen et al. 2025 report that deleting the whole STAS domain abolishes chloride channel function, while deleting the disordered loop within it increases transport (the loop is inhibitory), and that Ala→Ser/Thr substitutions in the S/T-rich motif at 620–628 reduce activity. A substitution at 616 could be loss, gain, or nothing. The paper's protective interpretation is untested.
  • Confounded phenotype contrast — the FEV1 82%/84% vs 54% gap is against a comparison group of 12 p.Phe508del homozygotes, while the siblings carry the mild class-V allele c.3140-26A>G. The authors acknowledge this; don't let the number travel without the caveat.
  • Predictor version and threshold conventions — before treating MetaLR 0.6 ("damaging") vs MetaRNN 0.041 ("benign") as a substantive disagreement, check tool versions and cutoffs; a threshold mismatch can manufacture a contradiction.
  • Conservation in low-complexity sequence — PhyloP100 = 3.59 is moderate, but conservation evidence is weaker in a compositionally biased stretch because the underlying alignment is less reliable.
  • Coverage — only exons 3 and 17 of 21 were sequenced. No cohort-level frequency claim about SLC26A9 is supportable until the full coding sequence is covered.
  • A functional assay is the only clean exit — and Chen's chloride-transport system already exists to run it in.

ALEX SLC26A9 stands for solute carrier family twenty-six member nine. SLC26A9 的全称是溶质载体家族 26 成员 9 (solute carrier family 26 member 9)。 It is an anion transporter — it moves negatively charged ions across the epithelial cell membrane. 它是一个阴离子转运蛋白，负责把带负电的离子运过上皮细胞膜。 It does two jobs: it acts as a chloride channel, and it acts as a chloride–bicarbonate exchanger. 它有两项功能：既作为氯离子通道，也作为氯离子–碳酸氢根交换体。 That should sound familiar, because that is roughly CFTR's job description too. 这听起来应该很耳熟，因为这基本上也是 CFTR 的工作内容。 SLC26A9 is expressed in respiratory and gastrointestinal epithelium, and the standing hypothesis is that when it is working well it can partly compensate for a broken CFTR. SLC26A9 表达于呼吸道和胃肠道上皮，目前的主流假说是：当它功能正常时，可以部分代偿失灵的 CFTR。 That single sentence is why it has been the most-watched candidate modifier gene in CF for over a decade. 就这一句话，让它成为过去十多年里 CF 领域最受关注的候选修饰基因 (modifier gene)。

JORDAN And a modifier gene is — remind me. 修饰基因是——再帮我确认一下。

ALEX CF is monogenic: two bad CFTR alleles and you have the disease. CF 是单基因病：两个致病的 CFTR 等位基因，你就患病。 But two people with the identical CFTR genotype can have wildly different disease severity, and a large share of that spread is heritable but not explained by CFTR. 但两个 CFTR 基因型完全相同的人，病情严重程度可以差得非常远，而这种差异中很大一部分是可遗传的，却无法用 CFTR 本身解释。 A modifier gene is a gene elsewhere in the genome whose variation shifts the phenotype without causing the disease. 修饰基因就是基因组中其他位置的基因，它的变异会改变表型，但本身不导致这个疾病。

STAS 是 sulfate transporter and anti-sigma factor antagonist 的缩写（一类源自细菌抗σ因子拮抗蛋白的胞内结构域）。 It is a cytoplasmic domain — inside the cell, not in the membrane — that sits at roughly residues five hundred sixty-two to six hundred fifty-four of SLC26A9. 它是一个胞质结构域，位于细胞内而不是膜内，大致对应 SLC26A9 的第 562 到 654 号残基。 Two things make it interesting. 有两点让它变得有意思。 First, the STAS domain is where SLC26A9 physically touches CFTR — it binds CFTR's regulatory domain, so this is a literal protein–protein handshake between the disease gene and the modifier gene. 第一，STAS 结构域正是 SLC26A9 与 CFTR 发生物理接触的地方——它结合 CFTR 的调控结构域，所以这是致病基因与修饰基因之间一次名副其实的蛋白质–蛋白质握手。 Second, part of it is intrinsically disordered. 第二，它的一部分是内在无序的。

JORDAN Disordered meaning what? 无序是什么意思？

ALEX Most of a protein folds into a fixed three-dimensional shape. 蛋白质的大部分区域会折叠成固定的三维结构。 An intrinsically disordered region does not — it stays floppy, sampling many conformations. 而内在无序区 (intrinsically disordered region) 不会，它保持松散摆动，在多种构象之间来回采样。 Disordered regions are frequently functional, often as regulatory switches, but they have no stable structure to model. 无序区往往是有功能的，常常充当调控开关，但它们没有稳定结构可供建模。 Layered on top of that, this stretch has what UniProt calls compositional bias — the sequence is dominated by a few residue types, here serine and threonine, and lysine and arginine. 在这之上，这一段还带有 UniProt 所说的组成偏倚 (compositional bias)——序列由少数几种残基主导，这里是丝氨酸和苏氨酸，以及赖氨酸和精氨酸。 Hold onto that. 记住这一点。 It becomes the whole episode. 它会成为整期节目的核心。

SLC26A9 作为 CF 修饰基因，有一段异常漫长也异常混乱的历史。 The best-known signal is a common intronic variant, rs7512462, in intron five. 最著名的信号是内含子 5 中的一个常见内含子变异 rs7512462。 Strug and colleagues in twenty sixteen reported that the C allele was associated with better baseline lung function in untreated CF patients carrying gating CFTR variants — variants like p.Gly551Asp where the channel reaches the membrane but does not open properly. Strug 及同事在 2016 年报告，携带门控型 CFTR 变异（例如 p.Gly551Asp，这类变异下通道能到达细胞膜但无法正常开放）的未治疗 CF 患者中，C 等位基因与更好的基线肺功能相关。 They also reported a variable response to ivacaftor, the potentiator that forces those gating channels open, on the order of twenty-five percent average improvement. 他们还报告了对增效剂 (potentiator) ivacaftor——也就是强行让这些门控通道打开的药物——的应答存在差异，平均改善幅度约为 25%。 Then Eastman and colleagues in twenty twenty-one looked in p.Gly551Asp patients and found no association with either disease severity or ivacaftor response. 随后 Eastman 及同事在 2021 年针对 p.Gly551Asp 患者进行研究，未发现该基因型与疾病严重程度或 ivacaftor 应答有关联。 So the flagship SLC26A9 pharmacogenetic finding is contested. 所以 SLC26A9 最标志性的那个药物遗传学发现，是有争议的。

SLC26A9 有 21 个外显子，他们只测了两个，所以任何关于该队列中 SLC26A9 变异出现频率的陈述都站不住脚——分母是错的。 There is no control group, which the authors state plainly as a limitation. 没有对照组，作者自己坦率地把这列为局限。 There are no functional assays, which they also state. 没有功能实验，他们也说了。 The load-bearing one is this: they found the variant in two people, and those two people are siblings. 承重的那一点是：他们在两个人身上找到了这个变异，而这两个人是同胞。 Siblings share haplotypes by descent. 同胞之间因共同祖先而共享单倍型。 So this is one independent observation wearing two coats — not two carriers. 所以这是一次独立观察穿了两件外套，而不是两个携带者。 Every downstream comparison inherits that. 后面所有的比较都继承了这个问题。

Luo 的两个变异一个都没找到。 Not p.Gly77Ser, not p.Pro629Ser, in any of the twenty. 二十人中没有一个携带 p.Gly77Ser 或 p.Pro629Ser。 That is the replication result, and it is a null. 这就是复制的结果，是一个阴性结果。 What they found instead, by accident of proximity, was a different exon-seventeen variant: c.1847C>T, p.Pro616Leu. 他们真正找到的，是因为位置相邻而意外发现的另一个外显子 17 变异：c.1847C>T，p.Pro616Leu。 Thirteen codons away from the variant they were hunting. 距离他们要找的那个变异只有 13 个密码子。
