How did this episode land for you?

Blurb

CATVariant is an open-access web server from a UC Davis team (Khoa Ngo, Hajar Amini, Igor Vorobyov, Colleen Clancy) that tries to collapse protein-variant interpretation — normally a manual slog across UniProt, ClinVar, gnomAD, PDB/AlphaFoldDB, MaveDB, and up to fifteen separate variant-effect predictors — into one unified, residue-level report. Given a protein name, sequence, or uploaded structure, it harmonizes sequence, structural, population, clinical, experimental, and literature evidence and produces a tunable "priority score" meant to guide triage, not replace clinical classification. On a 6,388-variant ClinVar benchmark across 22 proteins it edges out REVEL and AlphaMissense on AUROC and AUPRC, though the gap narrows to rough parity on the cleanest, two-star-reviewed subset. The paper's running example is the hERG/KCNH2 cardiac channel, used purely as a UI demo, not a new disease-gene finding. For our work, the transferable piece is the evidence-synthesis pattern, which maps to Karen's variant-interpretation priority: run a curated set of CFTR missense VUS through CATVariant's multi-predictor scoring and tabulate concordance against existing CFTR2/ClinVar calls — a medium-difficulty, paste-ready buildable artifact described below.

Glossary

  • ACMG/AMP criteria — Standardized evidence criteria from the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology used to classify clinical variant pathogenicity.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学会/分子病理学会制定的临床变异致病性分级标准。）
  • AlphaFold / AlphaFoldDB — A predicted-protein-structure model and its public database, used as a fallback structural source when no experimental structure is available.（中文：一种蛋白质结构预测模型及其公开数据库,在没有实验结构时作为备用结构来源。）
  • AlphaMissense — A protein-language-model-based predictor of missense variant pathogenicity.（中文：一种基于蛋白质语言模型的错义变异致病性预测工具。）
  • AUPRC — Area under the precision-recall curve; here, how enriched true pathogenic variants are near the top of a method's ranking.（中文：PR曲线下面积,衡量排名靠前的变异中真正致病变异的富集程度。）
  • AUROC — Area under the receiver operating characteristic curve; here, how well a method separates pathogenic from benign variants overall.（中文：ROC曲线下面积,衡量一个方法整体区分致病与良性变异的能力。）
  • AutoPVS1 — A tool for evaluating predicted loss-of-function variants, including nonsense-mediated-decay context.（中文：用于评估预测功能丧失变异的工具,包括无义介导的mRNA降解相关背景。）
  • CADD — A computational score folding many genomic annotations into one variant deleteriousness estimate.（中文：把多种基因组注释整合成单一有害性评分的计算工具。）
  • ClinGen — An expert curation effort that formally rates the strength of evidence linking a given gene to a given disease, plus inheritance and dosage-sensitivity context.（中文：正式评定基因与疾病关联证据强度,以及遗传方式和剂量敏感性背景的专家组。）
  • ClinVar — A public database of clinical variant classifications (e.g., pathogenic, benign) and their supporting assertion history.（中文：收录临床变异分类及其提交历史的公开数据库。）
  • deep mutational scanning (MaveDB) — A large-scale experimental method that measures the functional effect of many or all possible variants at each position in a protein; MaveDB is a public repository of such datasets.（中文：一种大规模实验方法,测量蛋白质每个位点所有可能变异的功能效应;MaveDB是收录此类数据集的公开数据库。）
  • gnomAD — A population database providing allele frequencies, ancestry distribution, and genetic constraint (how intolerant a gene/region is to variation) from large sequenced cohorts.（中文：提供大规模测序人群中等位基因频率、族群分布和遗传约束信息的人群数据库。）
  • InterVar — A tool providing ACMG/AMP-based clinical variant interpretation support using standardized criteria.（中文：基于ACMG/AMP标准,提供临床变异解读支持的工具。）
  • missense variant — A single DNA change that results in a different amino acid being incorporated into the protein.（中文：导致蛋白质中某个氨基酸被替换为另一个氨基酸的单一变异。）
  • Open Targets — A resource aggregating broad gene-disease association evidence from genetics, literature, and other public sources.（中文：汇总来自遗传学、文献等多来源的基因-疾病关联证据的资源。）
  • PDB (Protein Data Bank) — The public repository of experimentally determined three-dimensional protein structures.（中文：收录实验解出的蛋白质三维结构的公开数据库。）
  • PharmGKB — A resource providing pharmacogenomic context relevant to drug response and drug-associated phenotypes.（中文：提供与药物反应及药物相关表型相关的药物基因组学背景信息的资源。）
  • population constraint — A measure of how depleted a gene or protein region is for variation in large population datasets, used as a proxy for functional importance.（中文：衡量某基因或蛋白质区域在大规模人群数据中变异被耗竭程度的指标,用作功能重要性的替代证据。）
  • protein language model — A model architecture of the same family as large language models, but trained on protein sequences instead of text, used to predict variant effects (e.g., ESM1b, AlphaMissense).（中文：与大语言模型同属一类架构,但训练数据是蛋白质序列而非文本,用于预测变异效应。）
  • REVEL — An ensemble score combining multiple other predictors specifically to estimate missense variant pathogenicity.（中文：整合多种计算工具、专门用于估计错义变异致病性的集成评分。）
  • residue interaction network — A structural analysis identifying positions that may help transmit a variant's effect from one part of a protein to another.（中文：一种结构分析方法,识别可能将变异效应从蛋白质一部分传递到另一部分的关键位点。）
  • selectivity filter — The structural region of an ion channel that determines which ions can pass through the pore.（中文：离子通道中决定哪些离子能通过孔道的结构区域。）
  • SIFT — An early substitution-tolerance score estimating whether an amino-acid change is likely tolerated.（中文：一种评估氨基酸替换是否可能被耐受的较早期预测工具。）
  • structural hotspot analysis — A test of whether residues with elevated variant burden cluster together in 3D structure, revealing vulnerable regions not obvious from the linear sequence.（中文：检验变异负荷较高的残基是否在三维结构中聚集,以揭示从线性序列上不明显的脆弱区域的分析方法。）
  • trafficking (protein trafficking) — The process of a protein being transported to its correct cellular location, such as the cell membrane; impaired trafficking is a common mechanism of channel dysfunction.（中文：蛋白质被运输到其正确细胞位置(如细胞膜)的过程;转运受损是通道功能障碍的常见机制。）
  • UniProt — A curated database of protein sequences, domains, functional annotations, and known variants.（中文：收录蛋白质序列、结构域、功能注释及已知变异的整理数据库。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A variant for which there is not enough evidence to classify it as pathogenic or benign.（中文：证据不足以判定为致病或良性的变异。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • Two-stage ClinVar lookup — queries gene-level records first, then does targeted variant-level lookups only for unmatched substitutions, instead of one-shot variant queries.
  • Structure-selection fallback order — user-uploaded structure, then highest-coverage experimental PDB model, then AlphaFold prediction, as a deterministic way to pick one structural reference per protein.
  • Multi-predictor pooling with tunable weights — combines up to 15 variant-effect predictors (REVEL, AlphaMissense, PolyPhen-2, SIFT, CADD, MutPred, ESM1b, EVE, VARITY, MetaLR, MetaSVM, phastCons, SpliceAI, Pangolin, phyloP) into one adjustable score rather than trusting a single predictor.
  • Leave-one-protein-out thresholding — sets a classification cutoff using all other proteins' variants, then applies it to a held-out protein, to avoid threshold leakage.
  • Separation of clinical-interpretation tools (AutoPVS1, InterVar) from the main computational priority score, keeping ACMG/AMP-based classification support as distinct, non-blended context.
  • Residue interaction-network and 3D hotspot clustering analyses layered on top of linear-sequence variant burden to flag spatially concentrated vulnerable regions.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen's variant-interpretation priority (VUS pathogenicity triage / CFTR variant classification). CATVariant itself contains no CFTR-specific content — the transferable piece is the evidence-synthesis method, not any CFTR result.

Buildable artifact: Submit a curated list of CFTR missense VUS to CATVariant, score them with its default multi-predictor priority framework, and tabulate agreement versus disagreement against existing CFTR2 and ClinVar calls. Input: a CFTR missense VUS list. Output: a concordance table. Difficulty: Medium.

What to verify first

  • Whether CFTR has comparable data richness to the hERG demo case (2,403 variants, 82% deep-mutational-scanning coverage) in CATVariant's underlying sources — unknown from this paper.
  • Whether a CFTR-like transmembrane transporter was represented among the benchmark's 22 proteins — the protein identities beyond hERG aren't given in the text we have.
  • How to treat CFTR2 in any comparison — as the thing being checked against for concordance, not as a target to be overturned; no research design has been decided.
  • Whether the server's 3-month data-retention window and PIN-based job protection meet whatever data-handling expectations would apply to any variant list submitted.
