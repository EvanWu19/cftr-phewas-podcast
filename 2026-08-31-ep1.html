Ep1 — Track A: Large-scale in vitro functional testing and novel variant scoring via protein modeling provide insights into alkaline phosphatase activity in hypophosphatasia

Del Angel and colleagues tested 155 ALPL missense/LoF variants by transient transfection to measure residual TNSALP enzymatic activity and dominant-negative effect (DNE), then used that functional ground truth to build and benchmark a physics-based structural scorer (MM/GBSA stability/binding-affinity prediction, "Prime_S") against six generic in silico predictors (CADD, DANN, MutationTaster2, PolyPhen-2, PROVEAN, SIFT). Prime_S alone reached AUC 0.90 — beating every generic predictor individually — at a specificity-first operating point (TPR 0.63 at FPR 0), and combining it with PolyPhen-2 pushed AUC to 0.945. Active site, crown domain, and homodimer interface positions were strongly enriched for both low activity and DNE; a notable outlier variant (c.571G>A) showed incompletely penetrant pathogenicity despite low measured activity. The gene (ALPL/hypophosphatasia) has no CF relevance, but the recipe — large-scale functional-assay data feeding a structure-based scorer, benchmarked against generic predictors, with DNE explicitly flagged as an unsolved gap — is close to an exact template for what a CFTR2-functional-data-driven, structure-based CFTR variant scorer would need to do, serving the Karen Raraigh collaboration directly.

Plain-English glossary

  • Hypophosphatasia (HPP) / TNSALP — a rare metabolic disorder from ALPL gene variants causing deficient tissue-nonspecific alkaline phosphatase (TNSALP) enzyme activity, ranging from lethal neonatal skeletal disease to adult-onset fractures depending on residual activity.（中文：低磷酸酯酶症，由ALPL基因变异引起的罕见代谢病，导致组织非特异性碱性磷酸酶（TNSALP）活性缺陷，严重程度从新生儿致死性骨骼疾病到成年发病的骨折不等，取决于残余酶活性。）
  • Dominant-negative effect (DNE) — a mechanism where a mutant protein actively interferes with the normal protein's function (e.g. by co-assembling into a dysfunctional dimer), making a heterozygous carrier symptomatic beyond what simple loss-of-function would predict.（中文：显性负效应，指突变蛋白主动干扰正常蛋白功能的机制（如与正常蛋白共同组装成功能异常的二聚体），使杂合携带者出现单纯功能缺失所无法预测的症状。）
  • Homology model — a predicted 3D protein structure built using a related protein's solved crystal structure as a template, used here because no TNSALP crystal structure existed at the time.（中文：同源模型，利用相关蛋白质已解出的晶体结构作为模板，预测目标蛋白三维结构；此处使用是因为当时尚无TNSALP自身的晶体结构。）
  • MM/GBSA (Molecular Mechanics/Generalized Born Surface Area) — a physics-based method estimating how a single amino acid change affects protein structural stability and binding affinity, by simulating physical/chemical consequences rather than pattern-matching known variants.（中文：分子力学/广义玻恩表面积法，一种基于物理原理的方法，通过模拟物理化学后果（而非与已知变异做模式匹配）来估计单个氨基酸变化对蛋白质结构稳定性和结合亲和力的影响。）
  • AUC at a specificity-first operating point — Prime_S's reported performance (TPR 0.63 at FPR 0) means it functions as a high-confidence "rule-in" flag rather than a complete classifier, leaving roughly a third of true low-activity variants unflagged.（中文：以特异性优先的操作点衡量AUC——Prime_S报告的表现（假阳性率为0时真阳性率为0.63）意味着它是一个高置信度的"确认阳性"标记工具，而非完整的分类器，约三分之一真正低活性的变异不会被标记。）
  • Incomplete penetrance — when a variant with a damaging measured effect (here, low enzymatic activity) doesn't reliably cause disease, illustrated by c.571G>A having 5 apparently healthy gnomAD homozygotes.（中文：外显不全，指测得具有损害性效应（此处为低酶活性）的变异并不总是可靠地致病，以c.571G>A在gnomAD中有5名表面健康的纯合个体为例。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Functional-data-driven structural scorer (Prime_S/Prime_A/Prime_max) — MM/GBSA-based stability and binding-affinity prediction, benchmarked against real in vitro functional data rather than clinical classification labels alone.
  • Composite scoring (structural + generic predictor) — taking max(structural rule-in call, normalized generic predictor score) to boost AUC beyond either alone.
  • HGVS-standardized variant nomenclature against a named canonical RefSeq transcript — used throughout, a direct callback to the standard itself (see prior HGVS Nomenclature episode).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves the Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation track. MVP: build a CFTR-specific structural stability/binding-affinity scorer using a real CFTR structural model (cryo-EM, not homology-on-paralog), benchmarked against CFTR2's own published variant-level functional data as ground truth, then test whether combining it with an existing generic predictor (e.g. AlphaMissense/REVEL) boosts performance the way Prime_S + PolyPhen-2 did here. Difficulty: M–L (requires a structural-modeling pipeline).

What to verify first

  • How much CFTR2 functional data is public and variant-level versus aggregated/access-restricted.
  • What CFTR structural resources already exist, at what quality and conformational-state coverage, and whether structure-based CFTR scoring work already exists in the literature.
  • Whether an MM/GBSA-style stability/affinity approach is the right physical model for a channel protein (ion conduction/gating) versus an enzyme like TNSALP — may need real adaptation.
  • This approach, like Prime_S itself, would likely inherit a blind spot for dominant-negative or gating-specific mechanisms that don't show up as a simple stability change — worth naming from the start.

ALEX Today's paper is "Large-scale in vitro functional testing and novel variant scoring via protein modeling provide insights into alkaline phosphatase activity in hypophosphatasia," by Del Angel and colleagues, published in Human Mutation in 2020. 今天的论文是Del Angel等人2020年发表在《Human Mutation》期刊上的《大规模体外功能检测与基于蛋白质建模的新型变异评分为低磷酸酯酶症中碱性磷酸酶活性提供了新见解》。This is a Track A pick, and it's about as close a template as we've found yet for a CFTR-specific variant scorer. 这是Track A的选题，而且到目前为止，这可能是我们找到的、与CFTR专属变异评分工具最接近的一个模板了。The gene here is ALPL, causing a different rare disease, hypophosphatasia, but the recipe — large-scale functional assay data feeding a physics-based structural scoring algorithm, benchmarked against generic predictors — maps almost directly onto what a CFTR2-functional-data-driven scorer would need to do. 这里涉及的基因是ALPL，导致的是另一种罕见病——低磷酸酯酶症（hypophosphatasia），但它的做法——用大规模功能检测数据驱动一个基于物理原理的结构评分算法，并与通用预测器进行基准对比——几乎可以直接映射到一个由CFTR2功能数据驱动的评分工具所需要做的事情上。

JORDAN Set up the disease and the gene for me first. 先给我讲讲这个疾病和这个基因。

ALEX Hypophosphatasia, HPP, is a rare metabolic disorder caused by pathogenic variants in ALPL, the gene encoding tissue-nonspecific alkaline phosphatase, TNSALP — an enzyme that, when deficient, causes anything from lethal skeletal disease at birth to adult-onset fractures and pain, depending on how much residual enzyme activity is left. 低磷酸酯酶症（hypophosphatasia，HPP）是一种由ALPL基因致病变异引起的罕见代谢病，该基因编码组织非特异性碱性磷酸酶（tissue-nonspecific alkaline phosphatase，TNSALP）——这种酶一旦缺乏，轻则导致成年后骨折和疼痛，重则导致出生时致死性骨骼疾病，具体取决于残余的酶活性还剩多少。Over 390 pathogenic ALPL variants have been reported, most of them missense, and they can disrupt the protein through more than one mechanism — reduced folding or trafficking, or something called a dominant-negative effect, DNE. 已报告的ALPL致病变异超过390个，大多数是错义变异，它们可能通过不止一种机制破坏蛋白质功能——折叠或转运受损，或者一种被称为显性负效应（dominant-negative effect，DNE）的现象。

JORDAN What's a dominant-negative effect, exactly — how is that different from a simple loss-of-function variant? 显性负效应到底是什么——它和单纯的功能缺失变异有什么不同？

ALEX TNSALP works as a dimer — two protein copies joined together — so in someone with one normal and one mutant copy of the gene, the mutant protein doesn't just sit out of the reaction, it can pair up with a normal protein and drag the whole dimer's function down, or trap the normal copy so it never reaches the cell membrane where it needs to work. TNSALP以二聚体形式发挥作用——两个蛋白质拷贝结合在一起——所以在一个人体内如果一份基因拷贝正常、一份是突变体，突变蛋白并不会只是袖手旁观，它可能会与正常蛋白结合，把整个二聚体的功能拖下水，或者困住正常拷贝，使其永远无法到达它需要发挥作用的细胞膜上。That's the dominant-negative mechanism: a single mutant allele actively interferes with the product of the normal allele, rather than simply failing to contribute its own. 这就是显性负效应的机制：单个突变等位基因主动干扰正常等位基因的产物，而不仅仅是自身无法作出贡献。It matters clinically because it can make a heterozygous carrier symptomatic in a way a simple loss-of-function variant, where the normal copy just picks up the slack, would not. 这在临床上很重要，因为它可能让一个杂合携带者出现症状，而单纯的功能缺失变异——正常拷贝可以补上缺口——通常不会导致这种情况。

JORDAN And what's the actual computational method here — what's a homology model, and what's this MM/GBSA scoring? 那这里用的具体计算方法是什么——什么是同源建模？MM/GBSA评分又是什么？

ALEX Since no crystal structure of TNSALP itself existed at the time, the authors built a homology model — a predicted 3D structure based on a related protein whose structure is already solved, here human placental alkaline phosphatase, which shares 57% sequence identity with TNSALP and has a high-resolution, 1.8-angstrom crystal structure to build from. 由于当时还没有TNSALP本身的晶体结构，作者构建了一个同源模型——基于一个结构已经解出的相关蛋白质来预测三维结构，这里用的是人胎盘碱性磷酸酶，它与TNSALP有57%的序列一致性，并且有一个高分辨率（1.8埃）的晶体结构可供参考建模。MM/GBSA, Molecular Mechanics with Generalized Born Surface Area, is a physics-based method that estimates how a single amino acid change affects a protein's structural stability and its binding affinity to its dimer partner — not by pattern-matching against known variants, but by actually simulating the physical and chemical consequences of swapping one residue for another. MM/GBSA（分子力学与广义玻恩表面积法）是一种基于物理原理的方法，用来估计单个氨基酸变化对蛋白质结构稳定性、以及对其与二聚体伙伴结合亲和力的影响——它不是通过与已知变异做模式匹配，而是真正模拟替换一个氨基酸残基所带来的物理和化学后果。That's a genuinely different kind of evidence than the sequence-conservation signal most variant-effect predictors rely on. 这与大多数变异效应预测器所依赖的序列保守性信号，是一种截然不同的证据类型。

JORDAN So why did this study need to happen — what was the actual gap? 那这项研究为什么有必要做——实际存在的空白是什么？

ALEX The core problem is that clinical severity in HPP tracks with residual enzyme activity and DNE status, but those two things were known for only a fraction of the reported ALPL variants — most variants had a clinical classification, maybe, but no actual functional measurement behind it. 核心问题在于，HPP的临床严重程度与残余酶活性和DNE状态相关，但这两项数据只有一小部分已报告的ALPL变异是已知的——大多数变异或许有一个临床分类，但背后并没有真正的功能测量数据支撑。The study had two goals tied together: expand the catalog of variants with real in vitro functional data, and then use that expanded catalog as ground truth to build and validate a computational scorer, rather than relying purely on generic predictors trained on cross-gene, cross-protein patterns that may not capture what specifically drives TNSALP dysfunction. 这项研究有两个相互关联的目标：一是扩充拥有真实体外功能数据的变异目录，二是用这个扩充后的目录作为金标准，来构建和验证一个计算评分工具，而不是单纯依赖那些基于跨基因、跨蛋白质模式训练出来的通用预测器——这些通用预测器未必能捕捉到具体驱动TNSALP功能障碍的那些因素。That's precisely the situation CFTR is in too: CFTR2 has accumulated real functional data on many variants through years of theratyping and functional assay work, but a lot of VUS calls for CFTR still lean on generic predictors that were never trained specifically on what makes this particular protein fail. CFTR面临的情况恰恰也是如此：经过多年的theratyping（分型治疗研究）和功能检测工作，CFTR2已经积累了许多变异的真实功能数据，但对CFTR来说，很多VUS的判定仍然依赖那些从未专门针对这个蛋白质的失效机制进行训练的通用预测器。

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built. 那带我过一遍他们具体构建了什么。

ALEX They selected 155 ALPL variants to test — all missense variants above a 1-in-25,000 carrier frequency in the Genome Aggregation Database, gnomAD, plus additional variants pulled from a French clinical cohort of 345 HPP patients genotyped between 1997 and 2016. 他们选取了155个ALPL变异进行检测——包括基因组聚合数据库（gnomAD）中携带频率高于1/25000的所有错义变异，再加上从一个法国临床队列中提取的额外变异，该队列包含345名在1997至2016年间接受基因分型的HPP患者。Each variant was transiently transfected into cells and TNSALP enzymatic activity was measured directly — both as a pure mutant, and as a 50:50 mutant-to-wild-type mixture to detect DNE, using a below-0.4-relative-activity threshold to call DNE present and above 0.45 to call it absent. 每个变异都被瞬时转染到细胞中，直接测量TNSALP的酶活性——既测量纯突变体的活性，也测量突变体与野生型50:50混合后的活性以检测DNE，判定标准是相对活性低于0.4视为存在DNE，高于0.45视为不存在DNE。On the structural side, the homology model was used to computationally score all 8,911 possible missense changes across 469 scoreable amino acid positions, producing a predicted stability change and a predicted binding-affinity change for essentially the complete space of possible ALPL missense variants, not just the 155 actually tested in the lab. 在结构层面，同源模型被用于对469个可评分氨基酸位点上全部8911种可能的错义改变进行计算评分，为几乎整个ALPL错义变异可能性空间——而不仅仅是实验室里实际检测的155个变异——生成了预测的稳定性变化和预测的结合亲和力变化。The 155 lab-measured variants were then used as ground truth to benchmark that structural score, called Prime_S, against six existing in silico predictors — CADD, DANN, MutationTaster2, PolyPhen-2, PROVEAN, and SIFT — using a low-activity threshold of 0.25 or below and a high-activity threshold of 0.5 or above, excluding the ambiguous middle range. 随后，这155个实验室实测的变异被用作金标准，将这个被称为Prime_S的结构评分与六款现有的计算机预测工具——CADD、DANN、MutationTaster2、PolyPhen-2、PROVEAN和SIFT——进行基准对比，采用低于等于0.25作为低活性阈值、高于等于0.5作为高活性阈值，排除掉中间那部分模糊区间。Variant descriptions throughout were standardized to HGVS convention against the RefSeq canonical transcript, which is a small but pointed detail — even a purely functional-genomics paper like this one anchors everything to a specific, named reference transcript before doing anything else. 论文中所有变异描述都统一按照HGVS规范、针对RefSeq标准转录本进行了标准化，这是一个不起眼但很关键的细节——即便是像这样一篇纯功能基因组学论文，在做任何其他事情之前，也要先把一切锚定到一个具体的、明确命名的参考转录本上。

ALEX pushes back here on what the structural score can't do: Prime_S achieved a true-positive rate of 0.63 at a false-positive rate of 0 — meaning when it does flag a variant, it's essentially always right, but it stays silent on the roughly one-third of truly low-activity variants it doesn't confidently flag, so on its own it's a rule-in tool, not a complete classifier. 这里ALEX要指出结构评分做不到的事：Prime_S在假阳性率为0的情况下，真阳性率达到了0.63——也就是说，一旦它标记某个变异，几乎总是正确的，但对于大约三分之一真正低活性、它却没有信心标记的变异，它会保持沉默，所以单独使用时，它是一个"确认阳性"的工具，而不是一个完整的分类器。And more importantly for the CF parallel: this structural score was built entirely around predicting low enzymatic activity, and it does not attempt to predict DNE — the authors say that explicitly and flag it as future work — so a variant that's pathogenic purely through the dominant-negative mechanism, without a clean activity drop, would be invisible to this exact approach. 而对于CF这条平行线来说更重要的是：这个结构评分完全是围绕预测低酶活性构建的，它并没有尝试预测DNE——作者明确说明了这一点，并将其列为未来工作——因此，一个纯粹通过显性负效应机制致病、而活性本身没有明显下降的变异，在这个具体方法下会是不可见的。

JORDAN What did they find, both on the biology side and on how well the scorer worked? 他们在生物学层面和评分工具的表现层面，分别发现了什么？

ALEX On the biology: 58% of the 155 tested variants showed low activity, 34% showed high activity, and 8% fell in the ambiguous middle and were excluded. 在生物学发现方面：155个已检测变异中，58%表现为低活性，34%表现为高活性，8%落在模糊的中间地带，被排除在外。Variants localized to the active site or its immediate vicinity were dramatically more likely to show low activity — 94% versus 53% for variants elsewhere in the protein, an odds ratio of 11.74 — and DNE was significantly enriched at both the active site and the homodimer interface, the physical contact surface between the two protein copies. 位于活性位点或其邻近区域的变异，表现为低活性的可能性明显更高——94%对比蛋白质其他区域的53%，比值比达到11.74——而DNE则在活性位点和同型二聚体界面（两个蛋白质拷贝之间的物理接触面）都显著富集。Variants only found in HPP patients, and never in the general population, had significantly lower activity than variants also seen in gnomAD — the expected signature of purifying selection working against the most damaging alleles. 只在HPP患者中出现、从未在普通人群中出现的变异，其活性显著低于同时也在gnomAD中出现的变异——这正是纯化选择针对最具破坏性等位基因发挥作用时会留下的预期特征。One variant stood out as a genuine outlier: c.571G>A, the single most common HPP variant in patients of European ancestry, had low measured activity, 0.214, right near the pathogenic threshold, yet gnomAD contains five apparently healthy people homozygous for it, and it reaches a carrier frequency above 3% in the Finnish population — a clear signal of incomplete penetrance that a purely functional score alone wouldn't flag as a caveat. 有一个变异格外突出，是个真正的异常值：c.571G>A，是欧洲血统患者中最常见的HPP变异，实测活性较低，为0.214，非常接近致病阈值，但gnomAD中有五名表面健康、对该变异呈纯合状态的个体，而且它在芬兰人群中的携带频率超过3%——这是外显不全的明确信号，而一个纯粹的功能评分本身并不会把这一点标记为需要注意的警示。On predictor performance: Prime_S alone reached an AUC of 0.90, beating every one of the six generic predictors tested individually, and combining Prime_S with the best of those, PolyPhen-2, pushed the AUC up to 0.945, with a 95% confidence interval of 0.907 to 0.976 — a genuinely large jump from adding one structure-based signal on top of a sequence-based one. 在预测器表现方面：Prime_S单独的AUC达到0.90，超过了所有六款单独测试的通用预测器，而把Prime_S与其中表现最好的PolyPhen-2结合后，AUC进一步提升到0.945，95%置信区间为0.907到0.976——在一个基于序列的信号之上加入一个基于结构的信号，带来的提升相当可观。
