How did this episode land for you?

Ep1 (Track A — variant interp): A deep-intronic MYBPC3 variant causes hypertrophic cardiomyopathy by amplifying splicing errors the cell already makes

Gallego-Delgado and colleagues (Circulation, 2026) identify a previously unreported deep-intronic variant, MYBPC3 c.2308+227G>A, in a cohort of genotype-negative hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) patients from Salamanca, Spain — a variant SpliceAI scored as essentially benign (delta 0.03). Despite the low predicted score, its recurrence across 35 unrelated probands prompted deeper investigation: cosegregation analysis across 20 informative families gave a combined LOD score of 5.51, and blood-based splicing assays, confirmed in cardiac tissue, showed the variant amplifies the frequency of two pre-existing cryptic splicing events — already catalogued as rare background "natural missplicing" in population RNA-seq data via SpliceVault — rather than creating a new splice site outright. Penetrance was high (81% in women, 96% in men by age 60) and clinical severity matched other established pathogenic MYBPC3 variants. Only the abstract was available for this episode. The mechanism — a predictor-invisible variant that nudges existing background splicing noise rather than making a novel splice site — is a direct, if gene-agnostic, cautionary case for CFTR deep-intronic variant triage, where low splice-predictor scores are routinely used to deprioritize candidates. The buildable artifact is a CFTR SpliceVault Cross-Reference Screen that flags any low-scoring deep-intronic CFTR VUS whose position already shows detectable background missplicing in reference RNA-seq data.

Plain-English glossary

  • Deep-intronic variant — A variant located well inside an intron, far from the short sequences at the exon-intron boundary that the splicing machinery normally recognizes.（中文：位于内含子深处、远离外显子-内含子交界处那些剪接机制通常识别的短序列的变异。）
  • Canonical splice site — The short, highly conserved sequence motif right at an exon-intron boundary that the spliceosome normally uses to cut and join exons.（中文：紧邻外显子-内含子交界处的高度保守短序列，剪接体通常据此识别并拼接外显子。）
  • Cryptic splice site — A sequence elsewhere in a gene that resembles a splice site closely enough to be used instead of the canonical site under the right conditions, normally at low or zero frequency.（中文：基因中别处与剪接位点足够相似的序列，在特定条件下可能被剪接体转而使用，正常情况下使用频率很低甚至为零。）
  • SpliceAI delta score — A deep-learning splice-effect predictor's estimate (0 to 1) of how much a variant changes the probability that a nearby position is used as a splice site; trained mainly on variants that create or destroy a site outright.（中文：一种深度学习剪接效应预测工具给出的0到1之间的得分，衡量某个变异在多大程度上改变了附近位置被用作剪接位点的概率；该模型主要基于直接制造或破坏剪接位点的变异训练而成。）
  • SpliceVault — A reference database built from population RNA-seq data (e.g. GTEx) cataloguing minor, rare splicing events already detectable at each exon-intron junction, independent of any variant.（中文：基于人群RNA测序数据（如GTEx）构建的参考数据库，收录了每个外显子-内含子交界处本来就可检测到的微小、罕见剪接事件，与任何变异无关。）
  • Cosegregation / LOD score — A test of whether a variant tracks with disease through a family pedigree more often than chance, summarized as a logarithm-of-the-odds (LOD) score; higher values indicate stronger genetic linkage evidence.（中文：检验某个变异是否比随机概率更频繁地在家系中与疾病一起传递，结果以对数概率（LOD）得分表示；数值越高，遗传连锁证据越强。）
  • Founder haplotype — A shared block of linked variants inherited from a single common ancestor, used to show that recurrent detections of a variant trace back to one ancient origin rather than many independent occurrences.（中文：从单一共同祖先继承下来的一组连锁变异，用以证明某个变异的反复检出，源自一个古老的共同起源，而非多次独立发生。）
  • Penetrance — The proportion of people carrying a disease-causing variant who actually go on to show the disease.（中文：携带某个致病变异的人中，实际表现出相应疾病的比例。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • SpliceVault background-missplicing lookup — cross-reference a candidate position's genomic coordinates against a precomputed catalog of minor splicing events already detectable in population RNA-seq, independent of any predictor score.
  • Blood-to-tissue splicing assay confirmation — validate a blood-based splicing-assay result against the actual disease-relevant tissue before treating it as conclusive, given tissue-specific splicing.
  • Founder-haplotype reconstruction — distinguish a single ancient-origin recurrent variant from independent recurrent mutation using shared-haplotype analysis across unrelated carriers.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Lands on Karen / CFTR variant interpretation (method and mechanism only — MYBPC3 is not a CF-relevant gene). Buildable artifact — CFTR SpliceVault Cross-Reference Screen: cross-reference every deep-intronic CFTR VUS with a low SpliceAI delta against SpliceVault's precomputed background-missplicing catalog; a hit flags a position already known to misfire at low frequency in reference tissue, a reason to prioritize it for a wet-lab splicing assay regardless of the delta score alone. Difficulty: S-M (small to medium) — mostly a lookup/prioritization layer over an existing public resource, not a new model.

What to verify first

  • Whether SpliceVault's underlying RNA-seq coverage (largely blood, fibroblasts, and other GTEx tissues) adequately represents CFTR's relevant tissues — airway and intestinal epithelium — since a background event invisible in GTEx's panel could still occur in epithelium, or vice versa.
  • Whether any CFTR deep-intronic candidate has a founder-haplotype pattern analogous to this paper's recurrent Spanish cohort, since a private, non-recurrent variant can't be cosegregation-tested the same way.
  • Whether a minigene or patient-derived-cell splicing assay is actually feasible for a given CFTR candidate position before treating a cross-reference hit as more than a prioritization signal.

ALEX SpliceAI is a deep-learning splice-effect predictor that, for a given variant, estimates how much it changes the probability that a nearby position is used as a splice donor or acceptor, expressed as a delta score between 0 and 1 — the paper reports a delta of just 0.03 here, which reads as essentially no predicted effect. SpliceAI是一个基于深度学习的剪接效应预测工具，针对给定的变异，它会估算附近某个位置被用作剪接供体或受体的概率发生了多大变化，并给出一个介于0到1之间的得分差（delta score）——这篇论文里报告的delta只有0.03，基本等同于"预测没有影响"。 SpliceAI is trained overwhelmingly on variants that create or destroy a splice site outright, so a variant like this one — which doesn't touch the sequence motif of either cryptic site but instead shifts the balance of how often the spliceosome chooses between the correct site and sites it already sometimes uses — sits squarely outside what the model learned to flag. SpliceAI的训练数据绝大多数是那些直接制造或摧毁剪接位点的变异，所以像这个变异这样——它根本没有触碰任何一个隐蔽位点本身的序列模体（sequence motif），而只是改变了剪接体在"正确位点"和"它本来偶尔也会用的位点"之间做选择的概率平衡——这类变异恰恰落在模型学会标记的范围之外。 That's the structural blind spot worth sitting with: a low delta score tells you the variant doesn't look like the training examples, not that the variant has no splicing consequence. 这正是值得认真对待的结构性盲点：低delta得分只说明这个变异不像训练样本里见过的那类变异，并不代表这个变异对剪接真的毫无影响。

ALEX SpliceVault is a reference database built by mining real RNA sequencing data from population datasets like GTEx for every minor, rare splicing event already detectable at each exon-intron junction across many individuals — effectively a catalog of the splicing errors every gene is already making at low frequency, with no variant required to produce them.
