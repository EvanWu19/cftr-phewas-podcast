Blurb

Wu et al.'s 2019 JMIR Medical Informatics paper fills a gap that mattered for years of PheWAS research: the phecode system was built and validated entirely on ICD-9-CM, and there was no validated map to the ICD-10 and ICD-10-CM codes health systems were switching to. The authors built a layered mapping — direct string matching, then a CMS crosswalk (GEMS) into the existing ICD-9-CM phecode map, then a stack of pre-validated UMLS/SNOMED CT/OHDSI semantic resources for whatever was left — and validated it in two real databases (Vanderbilt, UK Biobank). Coverage of codes actually used in practice topped 90%, a deliberate reproducibility check behaved exactly as a sanity check should (70-75% reproduction for chronic diseases, under 10% for an acute negative control), and a head-to-head PheWAS of a Lipoprotein(a) variant replicated the same two associations with similar effect sizes under both the old and new maps. The result shipped as a real artifact: a public map and an update to the PheWAS R package. This lands on AI-tooling and career positioning — it's a clean template for terminology-crosswalk infrastructure that inherits validated work rather than reinventing it, and validates itself with a negative control plus a real replication. Buildable: a reusable phecode-based phenotype-definition reproducibility harness, built and tested on public/synthetic data only.

Plain-English glossary

  • PheWAS (phenome-wide association study) — the mirror image of a GWAS: fix one genetic variant and scan across many phenotypes (drawn from diagnosis codes) to see what it's associated with.（中文：PheWAS（表型组关联研究），GWAS的镜像操作：固定一个遗传变异，在许多表型（来自诊断编码）中扫描其关联性。）
  • Phecode — a clinically curated grouping of related ICD codes into one coherent disease phenotype, with built-in exclusion criteria that keep related conditions out of the control group.（中文：Phecode（表型编码），一种经临床审核的编码分组方式，将相关的ICD编码归并为一个连贯的疾病表型，并内置排除标准，防止相关疾病混入对照组。）
  • ICD-10 vs. ICD-10-CM — ICD-10 is the WHO's international diagnostic classification; ICD-10-CM is the US "clinical modification" built for billing/documentation, with roughly 10x more codes in some categories.（中文：ICD-10与ICD-10-CM，ICD-10是世界卫生组织的国际诊断分类；ICD-10-CM是美国版的"临床修订版"，专为计费和文档记录而制定，部分类别的编码数量约为前者的十倍。）
  • GEMS (General Equivalence Mappings) — the official CMS crosswalk between ICD-9-CM and ICD-10-CM codes, with the majority of its mappings only "approximate," not exact equivalences.（中文：GEMS（通用对等映射），CMS制定的ICD-9-CM与ICD-10-CM官方交叉映射表，其中大部分映射只是"近似"对等，并非精确等价。）
  • UMLS (Unified Medical Language System) / CUI — the National Library of Medicine's meta-thesaurus linking different clinical vocabularies together via shared Concept Unique Identifiers.（中文：UMLS（统一医学语言系统）／CUI，美国国家医学图书馆建立的元词库，通过共享的概念唯一标识符（CUI）将不同的临床术语体系连接起来。）
  • SNOMED CT — a comprehensive clinical terminology (Systematized Nomenclature of Medicine Clinical Terms) used as one of the bridging vocabularies in this mapping.（中文：SNOMED CT（医学系统命名法临床术语），一套全面的临床术语体系，在本文的映射中作为桥接词表之一使用。）
  • OHDSI (Observational Health Data Sciences and Informatics) — an international research network and its common-data-model ecosystem, used here for its own validated concept-relationship mappings.（中文：OHDSI（观察性健康数据科学与信息学），一个国际研究网络及其通用数据模型生态系统，本文借用了其自身已验证的概念关系映射。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Layered terminology-crosswalk architecture — direct string matching first, then an authoritative government crosswalk (GEMS), then a stack of independently pre-validated semantic-mapping resources (UMLS/SNOMED CT/OHDSI) last, minimizing novel mapping-error surface.
  • Two-database coverage evaluation — checking real-world code coverage in two independent EHR systems (Vanderbilt, UK Biobank) rather than only against the full code catalog.
  • Reproducibility analysis with a deliberate negative control — comparing phenotype case status across two time windows for several chronic diseases plus one acute, self-resolving disease, to confirm a map isn't just over-matching everything.
  • End-to-end PheWAS replication as a pipeline sanity check — running a known genetic association through the full phenotyping pipeline with both the old and new maps and comparing effect sizes directly.
  • PheWAS R package — the shipped, still-maintained open-source package incorporating these maps.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves AI-tooling and career positioning — a clean template for reusable, code-shipping terminology-crosswalk infrastructure of exactly the kind registry and RWE work constantly needs. Buildable: a reusable phecode-based phenotype-definition reproducibility harness, built on the public phecode map and a public/synthetic (e.g. Synthea) ICD-coded dataset, replicating this paper's two-window reproducibility-plus-negative-control validation design (see picks.json for the full MVP). Difficulty: S-M.

What to verify first

  • Whether a newer phecode release has superseded the version used in this 2019 paper, and which version any reusable harness should pin to and document.
  • How much of any future mapping-accuracy risk traces back specifically to GEMS's largely approximate (not exact) crosswalk versus the paper's own added logic, since that determines where a reproducibility harness should concentrate its scrutiny.
  • Whether the acute-disease negative-control logic generalizes cleanly to other phenotype domains, or whether a reusable harness needs a freshly chosen, domain-appropriate negative control each time.

JORDAN Before the numbers — what's a phecode, and why does PheWAS need its own code system instead of just using ICD directly? 在进入数字之前——什么是phecode（表型编码），为什么PheWAS需要自己专门的一套编码体系，而不是直接用ICD编码就好?

ALEX PheWAS, phenome-wide association study, is the mirror image of a GWAS: instead of scanning the genome for associations with one phenotype, you fix one genetic variant and scan across hundreds or thousands of phenotypes to see what it's associated with, using diagnosis codes pulled from the electronic health record. PheWAS（表型组关联研究）是GWAS（全基因组关联研究）的镜像操作：不是在整个基因组中扫描某一个表型的关联信号，而是固定一个遗传变异，然后在成百上千个表型中扫描，看它与哪些表型存在关联，这些表型信息来自电子健康记录（EHR）中提取的诊断编码。 Raw ICD codes are a bad substrate for that on their own, because a single clinically meaningful disease can be split across several near-duplicate codes, and a naive case/control split risks "contamination" — control patients who actually have a related, undiagnosed, or differently coded form of the same disease, which quietly destroys statistical power. 原始ICD编码本身并不适合直接拿来做这件事，因为一个在临床上有意义的疾病，可能被拆分成好几个几乎重复的编码，而简单粗暴地划分病例组/对照组，会有"污染"风险——也就是对照组里其实混入了患有相关疾病、未被诊断，或者编码方式不同的同一疾病患者，这会悄悄削弱统计功效。 Phecodes solve this by grouping related ICD codes into a single clinically coherent phenotype and attaching built-in exclusion criteria — a list of related conditions that automatically get removed from the control group — so the case/control contrast stays clean. Phecode正是为解决这个问题而设计的：它把相关的ICD编码归并成一个在临床上连贯的单一表型，并内置了排除标准（exclusion criteria）——一份相关疾病清单，会自动把这些情况从对照组中剔除——从而保持病例组/对照组对比的干净可靠。

JORDAN Got it. 明白了。 So what's the actual mapping problem here — ICD-9, ICD-10, ICD-10-CM, that's three different things, right? 那这里真正的映射问题是什么——ICD-9、ICD-10、ICD-10-CM，这是三套不同的东西，对吧?

ALEX Right, and the "CM" matters a lot. 没错，而"CM"这个后缀很关键。 ICD-10 is the World Health Organization's international diagnostic classification; ICD-10-CM is the US "clinical modification" of it, built by the National Center for Health Statistics specifically for billing and clinical documentation, and it's considerably more granular — about tenfold more diabetes-related codes alone compared to its ICD-9-CM predecessor. ICD-10是世界卫生组织制定的国际疾病诊断分类；ICD-10-CM则是美国版的"临床修订版"（clinical modification），由美国国家卫生统计中心专门为计费和临床文档记录而制定，粒度要细得多——仅糖尿病相关编码就比上一代ICD-9-CM多出大约十倍。 Two other acronyms worth planting here: GEMS, General Equivalence Mappings, is the official CMS crosswalk between ICD-9-CM and ICD-10-CM codes, and UMLS, the Unified Medical Language System, is the National Library of Medicine's giant meta-thesaurus that links different clinical vocabularies — including SNOMED CT, the Systematized Nomenclature of Medicine Clinical Terms — together through shared Concept Unique Identifiers, or CUIs. 这里再埋两个值得记住的术语：GEMS（General Equivalence Mappings，通用对等映射）是CMS（美国医疗保险和医疗补助服务中心）制定的ICD-9-CM与ICD-10-CM之间的官方交叉映射表；UMLS（Unified Medical Language System，统一医学语言系统）则是美国国家医学图书馆建立的一个庞大元词库，通过共享的概念唯一标识符（Concept Unique Identifier，简称CUI）把不同的临床术语体系——包括SNOMED CT（Systematized Nomenclature of Medicine Clinical Terms，医学系统命名法临床术语）——连接在一起。 The original phecode system was built entirely on ICD-9-CM — 1866 hierarchical phenotype codes mapping to over 15,000 ICD-9-CM codes — and as health systems moved to ICD-10 and ICD-10-CM, there was no equivalent direct map, which is the gap this paper fills. 最初的phecode系统完全建立在ICD-9-CM之上——1866个层级化的表型编码，对应映射超过15,000个ICD-9-CM编码——而随着医疗系统转向ICD-10和ICD-10-CM，当时并没有对应的直接映射表，这正是这篇论文要填补的空白。

JORDAN Why did that gap matter enough to need a whole paper — couldn't people just convert ICD-10 back to ICD-9-CM first and use the old map? 为什么这个空白重要到需要专门写一篇论文——大家不能先把ICD-10转换回ICD-9-CM，再用旧的映射表吗?

ALEX A few groups had done exactly that, but only for ICD-10 proper, not ICD-10-CM specifically, and crucially, nobody had evaluated the accuracy of doing it that way. 确实有几个团队这样做过，但只针对ICD-10本身，没有专门针对ICD-10-CM，而且关键是，没有人评估过这种做法的准确性到底如何。 Since PheWAS had been built and validated almost entirely on ICD-9-CM — the authors call it the gold-standard map — the open question was whether a researcher moving to ICD-10-CM-era EHR data could trust that they'd get the same phenotype definitions and the same genotype-phenotype associations, or whether the switch would quietly change their results. 由于PheWAS几乎完全是在ICD-9-CM基础上建立和验证的——作者称其为"金标准"映射——这里悬而未决的问题是：一个使用ICD-10-CM时代电子健康记录数据的研究者，是否能够信任自己得到的表型定义和基因型-表型关联结果与以前一致，还是说这次编码体系的转换会悄悄改变研究结果。 This is exactly the kind of invisible infrastructure risk that matters enormously for reproducibility but is easy to overlook, because the PheWAS paradigm itself — scan a variant across many phenotypes — was already a well-established, widely replicated tool by the time ICD-10-CM adoption became unavoidable. 这正是那种对可重复性影响巨大、却很容易被忽视的隐性基础设施风险，因为到ICD-10-CM不得不被采用的时候，PheWAS这种"用一个变异扫描多个表型"的研究范式本身，早已是一个成熟且被广泛验证过的工具。

JORDAN So walk me through what they actually built. 那跟我讲讲他们具体构建了什么。

ALEX They started from the 2018AA release of UMLS and built the map through a layered set of strategies rather than one single method. 他们以UMLS 2018AA版本为起点，通过一套分层策略而不是单一方法来构建这份映射表。 First, 515 ICD-10-CM codes were mapped directly by matching code descriptions verbatim to an existing phecode's description. 第一步，515个ICD-10-CM编码通过与现有phecode描述逐字匹配，被直接映射。 Second, and this is the bulk of the work, 82,287 ICD-10-CM codes were mapped indirectly by routing through GEMS to ICD-9-CM and then applying the existing, already-validated ICD-9-CM phecode map — so the new map inherits the old map's clinical curation rather than reinventing it. 第二步，也是工作量最大的部分，82,287个ICD-10-CM编码通过GEMS路由到ICD-9-CM，再应用现有的、已经验证过的ICD-9-CM phecode映射表来间接映射——这样新的映射表就继承了旧映射表的临床审核成果，而不是从零重新构建。 Since GEMS doesn't cover every ICD-10-CM code, they layered in three supplementary routes — UMLS CUI semantic equivalence, OHDSI's Observational Health Data Sciences and Informatics concept relationships, and National Library of Medicine SNOMED CT maps — each one manually validated by someone else beforehand, which the authors leaned on rather than inventing their own semantic matching from scratch. 由于GEMS并未覆盖所有ICD-10-CM编码，他们又叠加了三条补充路径——UMLS的CUI语义等价关系、OHDSI（Observational Health Data Sciences and Informatics，观察性健康数据科学与信息学）的概念关系，以及美国国家医学图书馆的SNOMED CT映射——每一条路径此前都已经由其他人手工验证过，作者直接借用这些成果，而不是自己从零构建语义匹配。 ICD-10 proper, lacking its own GEMS crosswalk, was mapped using only string matching plus these same pre-validated UMLS, NLM, and OHDSI resources. 由于没有专门针对ICD-10本身的GEMS交叉映射，ICD-10只能用字符串匹配，再加上同样这些预先验证过的UMLS、NLM和OHDSI资源来完成映射。

ALEX To validate the result, they ran two evaluations in two different databases: coverage was checked at Vanderbilt University Medical Center, using real ICD-10-CM data from over three million patients, and at the UK Biobank, using ICD-10 data from over 500,000 participants. 为了验证结果，他们在两个不同的数据库中做了两项评估：覆盖率评估使用了范德堡大学医学中心（Vanderbilt University Medical Center，VUMC）超过三百万患者的真实ICD-10-CM数据，以及英国生物样本库（UK Biobank）超过50万参与者的ICD-10数据。 Fidelity — whether the new ICD-10-CM map actually produces the same phenotypes as the gold-standard ICD-9-CM map — was tested two ways: a reproducibility analysis comparing the same 357,728 patients' phenotype case status across an ICD-9-CM-era time window and a later ICD-10-CM-era window for four chronic diseases plus one acute-disease negative control, and a head-to-head PheWAS replication using a known Lipoprotein(a) genetic variant, rs10455872, run once with each map. 保真度——即新的ICD-10-CM映射是否真能产出与"金标准"ICD-9-CM映射相同的表型——用了两种方式检验：一是可重复性分析，比较同一批357,728名患者在ICD-9-CM时代窗口和稍后的ICD-10-CM时代窗口中，针对四种慢性病加一种急性病阴性对照的表型病例状态是否一致；二是用一个已知的脂蛋白(a)（Lipoprotein(a)）遗传变异rs10455872，分别用两套映射表各做一次PheWAS，做头对头对比。 Here's where I'd push back a bit: the bulk indirect-mapping route runs through GEMS, and the paper itself cites prior work showing over 90% of GEMS's ICD-10-CM-to-ICD-9-CM mappings are only "approximate," not exact equivalences — so any error GEMS introduces gets silently baked into 82,287 of the new phecode mappings, and the paper's own reproducibility check for root causes of non-reproduction relied on manually reviewing just 30 patient charts out of a 357,728-patient cohort, which is a thin sample for ruling out map-driven error specifically. 这里我想稍微提出一点质疑：这套间接映射方法的主体是通过GEMS完成的，而论文自己也引用了此前的研究，指出GEMS中超过90%的ICD-10-CM到ICD-9-CM映射只是"近似"对等，并非精确等价——所以GEMS引入的任何误差，都会悄无声息地嵌入到82,287个新phecode映射当中，而论文自己为排查"表型未能重现"根本原因所做的核查，只人工审查了357,728名患者中的30份病历，就排除映射本身造成误差这一点而言，这个样本量相当薄弱。

JORDAN Fair. 这个质疑有道理。 So does it actually hold up in practice — what did the coverage and replication numbers look like? 那它在实际应用中表现如何——覆盖率和复现的数字结果怎么样?

ALEX Coverage was strong: over 75% of all possible ICD-10 and ICD-10-CM codes mapped to at least one phecode, and when you look at the codes actually used in real patient data rather than the full code catalog, it jumps to over 90% — 94.40% of the distinct ICD-10-CM codes used at Vanderbilt and 93.24% of the distinct ICD-10 codes used in UK Biobank. 覆盖率表现强劲：所有可能的ICD-10和ICD-10-CM编码中，超过75%被映射到至少一个phecode；而如果只看实际患者数据中真正用到的编码，而不是整个编码目录，这个比例会跳升到90%以上——范德堡实际使用的ICD-10-CM编码中94.40%被映射，英国生物样本库实际使用的ICD-10编码中93.24%被映射。 Most of the unmapped codes turned out to be exactly the kind you'd want excluded anyway — external causes of morbidity like injuries from assault, administrative or encounter codes, and personal or family history concepts that the ICD-9-CM-era phecode system never covered either. 而大部分未被映射的编码，恰恰是那些本来就应该被排除在外的类型——比如外部致病原因（例如袭击导致的伤害）、行政或就诊事件相关的编码，以及个人或家族病史相关的概念，而这些概念在ICD-9-CM时代的phecode系统中本来也从未被覆盖过。 The reproducibility analysis behaved exactly the way you'd want a sanity check to behave: 70 to 75% of patients with a chronic-disease phecode in the ICD-9-CM era still had the same phecode in the ICD-10-CM era, while the acute-disease negative control — intestinal infection, which you'd expect to resolve and not recur identically — reproduced in under 10% of patients, confirming the map isn't just forcing everything to match. 可重复性分析的表现，正是你希望一个合理性检验应该表现的样子：在ICD-9-CM时代带有某个慢性病phecode的患者中，70%到75%在ICD-10-CM时代仍然带有相同的phecode；而作为急性病阴性对照的肠道感染——这类疾病本应痊愈、不会以同样方式反复出现——其复现比例低于10%，这证实了这份映射表并非在强行让一切都对上号。 And the PheWAS replication landed cleanly: both the ICD-9-CM map and the new ICD-10-CM map found the same two associations for the LPA variant — coronary atherosclerosis at an odds ratio of 1.60 under both maps, and chronic ischemic heart disease at 1.56 versus 1.47 — close enough in direction and magnitude to call it a successful replication.
