A Generalized Tool to Assess Algorithmic Fairness in Disease Phenotype Definitions — Zelko JS, Manjourides J. AMIA Joint Summits on Translational Science Proceedings, 2025.

Note: this paper is NOT open access. Everything below is drawn from the published abstract only — no full methods, no full results.

Blurb

This proceedings abstract raises a question that most phenotype-validation work skips: is a disease phenotype definition's accuracy uniform across demographic subgroups, or does one aggregate sensitivity/specificity number hide badly-wrong performance in a specific slice of patients? The authors describe (in abstract only, without naming specific metrics, a disease, or a dataset size) a generalized tool that evaluates an implemented phenotype definition against common fairness metrics and can examine "coarse and strongly intersecting populations across many characteristics," demonstrated with an example OMOP CDM analysis. Because this is abstract-only and published at a proceedings venue rather than a full peer-reviewed journal article, we treat it as a well-motivated problem statement rather than a specification to implement against. For us this lands on AI-tooling/career (with a Liz data-quality tie, method only): the transferable idea is stratifying any phenotype algorithm's accuracy by intersecting demographic subgroups — complementary to, not redundant with, tools like CohortDiagnostics and PheValuator — before trusting its headline number.

Plain-English glossary

  • Disease phenotype definition / phenotype algorithm / case definition — a rule set (diagnosis codes, labs, meds, visit patterns) used to decide who counts as a case in observational data.（中文：疾病表型定义／表型算法／病例定义——用诊断代码、检验、用药、就诊模式等规则来判定观察性数据中谁算病例。）
  • OMOP CDM — the Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership Common Data Model; a standardized table structure letting the same phenotype rule run across different health-data sources.（中文：观察性医学结果伙伴关系通用数据模型——让同一套表型规则能在不同医疗数据源上运行的标准化表结构。）
  • Intersecting / coarse populations — demographic subgroups defined by combining several characteristics at once (e.g., age band × sex × race × insurance), versus single-characteristic breakdowns.（中文：交叉／粗粒度人群——由多个特征同时组合定义的人口学亚群体（如年龄段×性别×种族×保险类型），相对于单一特征的拆分。）
  • Sensitivity / specificity (in phenotyping) — how well a phenotype algorithm's case calls match a validated gold standard; here the concern is whether these differ meaningfully by subgroup.（中文：在表型定义语境下的灵敏度／特异度——表型算法判定的病例与已验证金标准的吻合程度；这里关注的是它是否会因亚群体不同而出现明显差异。）
  • AMIA Joint Summits on Translational Science — an informatics conference whose proceedings publish shorter, earlier-stage methods papers than a full journal article.（中文：AMIA 转化科学联合峰会——一个信息学会议，其论文集发表的方法学论文比完整期刊论文更简短、更早期。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Subgroup-stratified accuracy auditing — compute a phenotype algorithm's sensitivity/specificity/calibration separately per demographic subgroup, not just in aggregate.
  • Intersectional stratification — examine combinations of characteristics (age × sex × race × insurance, etc.) rather than one characteristic at a time, while flagging cells too small to assess reliably.
  • (Complementary prior Track-B tools: CohortDiagnostics — cohort characterization vs. clinical expectation; PheValuator — accuracy estimation without a hand-labeled gold standard.)

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: AI-tooling/career (+ Liz / CFFPR data-quality, method only). MVP (paste-ready): Given an OMOP-style cohort from a candidate phenotype algorithm, demographic covariates, and a small validated case/non-case subsample, stratify into intersecting subgroups and compute per-subgroup sensitivity/specificity/calibration wherever cell counts are large enough; output a one-page scorecard flagging subgroups that diverge from the overall number, or flagging "insufficient data" where cells are too thin. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • Abstract-only limitation — locate the full proceedings paper or a follow-on journal version before implementing anything against this as a specification; the abstract names no specific metrics, disease, or dataset.
  • Clinical vs. algorithmic disparity — a subgroup accuracy gap may reflect a genuine clinical/documentation difference rather than an algorithm bug; any flag needs clinician review before being treated as a fix-it item.
  • Small-cell noise — intersectional subgroups with few patients make any fairness metric unstable; the tool needs an honest "insufficient data" state rather than a confident wrong number.

ALEX In observational research — studies built on electronic health records or claims data rather than a prospective trial — you almost never get a clean, pre-labeled list of "who has the disease." 在观察性研究中——建立在电子健康记录或理赔数据而非前瞻性临床试验之上的研究——你几乎从不会拿到一份干净、预先标注好的“谁得了这个病”名单。 Instead, researchers write a phenotype definition, sometimes called a phenotype algorithm or case definition: a rule set built from diagnosis codes, lab values, medications, and visit patterns that decides, patient by patient, who counts as a case. 相反，研究者要写一个表型定义，有时也叫表型算法 (phenotype algorithm) 或病例定义 (case definition)：一套由诊断代码、检验值、用药记录和就诊模式构成的规则，逐个病人地判定谁算作病例。 OMOP CDM — the Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership Common Data Model — is the standardized table structure many of these definitions are written against, so the same rule can in principle run against many different hospital systems' data. JORDAN How is this different from the general algorithmic-fairness auditing you'd already do for a trained classifier — demographic parity, equalized odds, that whole toolbox? 这跟你原本会对一个训练出来的分类器做的通用算法公平性审计——人口统计学均等 (demographic parity)、机会均等 (equalized odds) 那整套工具箱——有什么不同？

ALEX The metrics themselves can be borrowed wholesale, but the object being audited is different in a way that matters. 指标本身完全可以直接借用，但被审计的对象不同，而这一点很重要。 A trained classifier is optimized against a loss function on labeled data, so you can usually retrain or reweight it once you find a disparity. 一个训练出来的分类器是针对标注数据上的损失函数优化出来的，所以一旦你发现了差异，通常可以重新训练或重新加权它。 A disease phenotype definition is closer to hand-written clinical logic — a chain of diagnosis codes, lab thresholds, and visit-pattern rules that a team of researchers wrote and validated once, often years ago, and then reused across many downstream studies without revisiting it. 而一个疾病表型定义更接近手写的临床逻辑——一串由研究团队编写、往往在多年前验证过一次、此后在许多下游研究中被反复复用却不再重新审视的诊断代码、检验阈值和就诊模式规则链。 So a fairness gap in a phenotype definition isn't a knob you retrain; it's a design flaw baked into a rule set that may already be sitting inside dozens of published studies. 所以表型定义中的公平性缺口不是一个可以重新训练调整的旋钮；它是一个已经嵌入规则集里的设计缺陷，而这个规则集可能早已被用在几十项已发表研究里了。 That's exactly why a generalized auditing tool — something you can point at any existing phenotype definition after the fact — is a genuinely useful idea, even before we know the specific metrics this paper used. 这正是为什么一个通用的审计工具——一个可以事后对准任何现有表型定义使用的工具——是一个确实有用的想法，哪怕我们还不知道这篇论文具体用了哪些指标。

JORDAN Does this overlap with the OHDSI tools we've covered before — CohortDiagnostics, PheValuator? 这跟我们之前讲过的 OHDSI 工具——CohortDiagnostics、PheValuator——有重叠吗？

ALEX It's complementary rather than redundant. 是互补关系，而不是重复。 CohortDiagnostics characterizes a phenotype's output cohort against a clinical-expectation template to catch sensitivity, specificity, or index-date errors, and PheValuator estimates a phenotype algorithm's overall accuracy without needing a hand-labeled gold standard. CohortDiagnostics 把一个表型的输出队列拿去和一个临床预期模板对照，从而捕捉灵敏度、特异度或索引日期方面的错误；PheValuator 则在不需要人工标注金标准的情况下估计一个表型算法的总体准确率。 Neither of those tools, as we covered them, breaks its accuracy number down by intersecting demographic subgroup — they both report toward a population-level or cohort-level answer. 就我们之前讲过的版本来说，这两个工具都没有把准确率数字按交叉人口学亚群体拆开——它们给出的都是面向整个人群或整个队列层面的答案。 A fairness scorecard would sit downstream of either: once you trust that a phenotype is roughly accurate overall, the next question is whether that overall accuracy is evenly spread or concentrated unevenly across who the algorithm is actually being run on. 一个公平性记分卡会位于这两者的下游：一旦你相信一个表型总体上大致准确，下一个问题就是这种总体准确性是均匀分布的，还是在算法实际运行的人群中分布得很不均匀。

JORDAN Where does this land for us specifically? 这对我们具体落在哪里？

ALEX This lands on AI-tooling for career, with a direct secondary tie to Liz's CFFPR phenotype and data-quality work — as a method only, since we're not asserting anything about CFFPR's actual internal phenotype definitions. 这落在服务于职业发展的 AI 工具赛道上，并与 Liz 的 CFFPR 表型和数据质量工作有直接的次要关联——仅作为方法层面的借鉴，因为我们并不对 CFFPR 实际内部的表型定义做任何断言。 The transferable idea, even without the paper's specific metrics, is the workflow shape: stratify a phenotype algorithm's performance by intersecting demographic characteristics, not just overall. 即便没有这篇论文具体的指标，可迁移的想法仍然是这套工作流的形状：按交叉的人口学特征、而不只是整体，来拆分一个表型算法的表现。 Here's the buildable artifact: a phenotype-fairness scorecard. 这里的可构建产物是：一份表型公平性记分卡 (phenotype-fairness scorecard)。 Input is an OMOP-style patient cohort produced by a candidate phenotype algorithm, plus demographic covariates, plus — critically — a small validated subsample with a trusted case/non-case label, even if it's only chart-reviewed for a few hundred patients. 输入是一个由候选表型算法生成的 OMOP 风格患者队列，加上人口学协变量，再加上——关键的一点——一个带有可信病例/非病例标签的小型已验证子样本，哪怕只是对几百名患者做过病历审查。 The tool would stratify patients into intersecting subgroups — age band by sex by race or ethnicity by insurance type, for instance — and compute per-subgroup sensitivity, specificity, and calibration against the validated subsample wherever cell counts are large enough to be meaningful, flagging any subgroup where performance diverges meaningfully from the overall number. 该工具会把患者按交叉亚群体分层——比如年龄段 × 性别 × 种族或族裔 × 保险类型——并在样本量足够大到有意义的每个亚群体上，针对已验证子样本计算灵敏度、特异度和校准情况，标记出任何表现与总体数字有明显偏离的亚群体。 Output is a one-page scorecard: overall accuracy, per-subgroup accuracy where computable, and an explicit flag for any subgroup too small to assess reliably, so the honest answer for a thin cell is "unknown," not a false "fine." 输出是一页记分卡：总体准确率、可计算情况下的各亚群体准确率，以及对任何样本量太小、无法可靠评估的亚群体的明确标记——这样，对于一个样本稀薄的格子，诚实的答案是“未知”，而不是一个虚假的“没问题”。 Difficulty medium: computing the metrics once you have labels is the easy part; assembling even a modest validated per-subgroup gold standard is the actual work. 难度中等：一旦有了标签，计算指标是容易的部分；真正的工作量在于整理出哪怕是一个适度的、按亚群体划分的已验证金标准。

JORDAN What would we need to check before trusting a scorecard like that, or trusting this paper's premise at all? 在信任这样一份记分卡、乃至信任这篇论文的前提之前，我们需要核实什么？
