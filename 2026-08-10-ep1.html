Blurb

Lansdon, Porath, and colleagues (Children's Mercy Kansas City / Labcorp / Emory / Boston Children's / ACMG / University of Manchester, Clinical Chemistry, 2026) turned a widely-suspected problem — that published genomic variant nomenclature is often wrong — into a hard measurement. Using Genetics in Medicine's own internal author-instructions and editorial-review protocol as a standardized rubric with severity scoring, they reviewed manuscripts submitted over a two-year window and found 100% noncompliant with current HGVS/ISCN nomenclature standards, with every manuscript containing at least one error expected to measurably reduce variant findability. A four-manuscript case study testing three open-source lookup tools showed findability degradation is also tool-dependent — the same error can break retrieval in one system and be silently tolerated by another. The transfer to Karen's CFTR variant-harmonization work is close to direct: any CFTR curation pipeline has to assume a meaningful fraction of ingested variant strings carry exactly this kind of error. Buildable artifact: cftr-hgvs-linter, a small rule-based validator for CFTR variant strings, S-difficulty.

Plain-English glossary

  • HGVS nomenclature — the Human Genome Variation Society's standardized syntax for writing a genetic variant (reference sequence, transcript version, and precise c./p./g.-level notation) so it means exactly one thing.（中文：人类基因组变异学会制定的标准化变异书写规范,确保每条变异描述只对应唯一含义。）
  • ISCN — International Standing Committee on Human Cytogenomic Nomenclature; governs chromosome- and cytogenetics-level naming (karyotypes, structural rearrangements), distinct from HGVS's sequence-level syntax.（中文：国际人类细胞基因组学命名常务委员会,负责染色体和细胞遗传学层面的命名规则,与HGVS的序列层面语法不同。）
  • Findability — whether a curator or automated pipeline can actually locate a variant, as printed in a paper, inside a database like ClinVar or gnomAD; a small nomenclature slip can silently break the lookup even if the notation looks superficially valid.（中文：给定论文中印出的变异描述,编目人员或自动化流程能否真正在ClinVar、gnomAD等数据库中定位到该变异；一个微小的命名疏漏可能悄然导致检索失败。）
  • c. / p. / g. notation — HGVS prefixes indicating the level a variant description applies to: coding DNA (c.), protein (p.), or genomic (g.) sequence.（中文：HGVS前缀,分别表示变异描述所对应的层级:编码DNA(c.)、蛋白质(p.)或基因组(g.)序列。）
  • Severity-scoring rubric — a standardized system (here, Genetics in Medicine's internal editorial protocol) for grading how serious a nomenclature deviation is, rather than treating all deviations as equally bad.（中文：一套标准化评分体系（此处为《医学遗传学》内部编辑流程）,用于评估命名偏差的严重程度,而非将所有偏差一视同仁。）
