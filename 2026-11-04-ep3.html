Ep3 (Track C — registry QC, 1 of 2): Estimating peak height velocity in individuals: a comparison of statistical methods

Boeyer, Middleton, Duren, and Leary (2020, Annals of Human Biology) compare three statistical methods for estimating age-at-peak-height-velocity (aPHV) and peak height velocity (PHV) in 686 individuals from the dense, long-running Fels Longitudinal Study (19.6 height measurements per person on average): a 5th-order polynomial fit per individual, natural cubic splines (the same basis used in CDC pediatric growth charts), and SITAR (SuperImposition by Translation And Rotation). All three methods fit raw height almost identically well (leave-one-out cross-validated RMSd ~5.3-5.7cm for every method), but disagreed substantially on the velocity-derived quantities: SITAR consistently produced the latest aPHV and largest PHV estimates, differing from the other two methods by 4+ months and 0.28+ cm/year. The authors recommend the polynomial for modeling individuals (fewest structural constraints) and SITAR for population-level questions (minimizes individual-vs-population bias by design). For the Liz assignment, the headline point is methodological: because velocity is a derivative, model choice is not a neutral preprocessing step — it is itself part of the flagging logic a residual-based outlier detector would be built on. The buildable artifact: a Growth-Model Sensitivity Auditor.

Plain-English glossary

  • aPHV (age at peak height velocity) — the chronological age at which a person's height-growth rate is fastest during the adolescent growth spurt.（中文：青春期生长突增阶段身高增长速率最快时所对应的年龄。）
  • CDC growth charts — the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention's pediatric growth reference charts, built on a natural-cubic-spline basis for children two years and older.（中文：美国疾控中心（CDC）的儿科生长参考曲线图，对两岁以上儿童采用自然三次样条方法构建。）
  • Natural cubic spline — a flexible curve-fitting method built from piecewise polynomial segments joined at "knots," with constraints at the boundaries; the basis of the CDC growth charts.（中文：一种灵活的曲线拟合方法，由若干段分段多项式在"节点"处连接而成，并在边界处有约束条件；是CDC生长曲线图的基础方法。）
  • PHV (peak height velocity) — the fastest rate of height growth during the adolescent growth spurt, in centimeters per year.（中文：青春期生长突增阶段身高增长的最快速率，单位为厘米/年。）
  • SITAR (SuperImposition by Translation And Rotation) — a shape-invariant growth-curve model that fits one population-average curve plus three individual random effects (size, tempo, velocity), so every person's curve is a shifted-and-stretched version of the same shape.（中文：一种形状不变的生长曲线模型，拟合一条群体平均曲线，加上三个个体随机效应——大小、时相、速度——每个人的曲线都是同一形状曲线经过平移和拉伸后的版本。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • SITAR (R sitar package) — shape-invariant mixed-effects growth curve model; best for population-level questions per this paper's own recommendation.
  • 5th-order polynomial / natural cubic spline individual fits — per-individual curve fitting with minimal (polynomial) or knot-constrained (spline) structural assumptions; polynomial recommended here for individual modeling.
  • Leave-one-out cross-validation (RMSd) — a way to separate raw-curve-fit quality from derivative (velocity)-estimate disagreement between model families.

ALEX The motivating problem is squarely clinical: aPHV and PHV estimates feed directly into decisions about skeletal growth and developmental disorders — constitutional growth delay, adolescent idiopathic scoliosis, leg-length discrepancy — where knowing whether a child has already passed their growth spurt, or is about to hit it, changes the treatment plan. 这篇论文要解决的问题直接是临床层面的：aPHV和PHV的估计结果，直接影响骨骼生长和发育障碍相关的临床决策——体质性生长延迟、青少年特发性脊柱侧弯、双下肢不等长——判断一个孩子是已经度过了生长突增期，还是即将迎来它，会改变治疗方案。But as the authors note up front, the most commonly used methodologies for predicting aPHV or PHV produce large differences in estimates even on identical data — citing prior work where, among models tested on the same cohort, population-average aPHV estimates differed by more than a year between methods, and under realistic clinical conditions like unbalanced, biennial visits with real measurement error, a meaningful fraction of model fits, over 11% for one method and over 60% for another, simply failed to converge. 但正如作者在开头指出的那样，最常用的那几种预测aPHV或PHV的方法，即便用完全相同的数据，也会产生很大的估计差异——他们引用的前人研究显示，在同一个队列上测试时，不同方法得出的群体平均aPHV能相差超过一年，而在更贴近临床真实情况的条件下——比如间隔不均、每两年一次、带有真实测量误差的随访——相当一部分模型根本拟合不收敛，一种方法超过11%，另一种方法甚至超过60%。So the open question wasn't "does growth-curve modeling work," it was "which model should a clinician or researcher actually trust, and under what data conditions." 所以悬而未决的问题不是"生长曲线建模到底有没有用"，而是"临床医生或研究者到底该信任哪个模型，以及在什么样的数据条件下可以信任"。
