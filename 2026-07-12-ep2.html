Ep2 — 2026-07-12 — Track B (AI for registry/bioinformatics, tool-development lens)

Blurb

A GRU recurrent network (PyTorch_EHR, Zhi lab, public repo) over a patient's time-ordered sequence of categorical EHR codes — diagnoses, procedures, antibiotics given, prior microbiology, demographics, plus an embedded day-gap between visits — predicting whether a patient will have a MRSA-positive culture within two weeks of an index culture. Developed on 26,233 patients / 56,233 index events at Memorial Hermann (Houston), externally validated on 152,979 patients / 393,713 events in MIMIC-IV (Boston). AUROC 0.911 vs LightGBM 0.892 vs logistic regression 0.857; externally 0.859 / 0.838 / 0.816. The deep model wins — by ~2 points over a well-tuned tabular twin. The transferable asset is not the 2 points; it is the discipline: a label aligned to the clinician's decision horizon rather than the lab event, an explicit index-time leakage guard (only results reported by index; in MIMIC, only codes from previous encounters), a mandatory tabular baseline on the identical index definition, and a voluntary disarming of their own interpretability figure (integrated-gradient attributions are declared patient-level only, not population risk factors). Lands on AI-tooling / career; artifact = event-sequence-registry-baseline.

Plain-English glossary

Antimicrobial stewardship — a hospital program whose job is to get patients off unnecessary broad-spectrum antibiotics.（中文：抗菌药物管理——医院中负责让患者尽早停用不必要广谱抗生素的项目。）

Bacteremia — bacteria present in the bloodstream; the severe end of infection and the case nobody wants to miss.（中文：菌血症——细菌进入血流，属重症一端。）

Beta-lactams — the first-line, cheap, safe antibiotic class (penicillins, cephalosporins) that MRSA is by definition resistant to.（中文：β-内酰胺类——一线、廉价、安全的抗生素类别，而 MRSA 按定义对其耐药。）

Culture — the lab test that grows bacteria from a patient sample to identify the organism; takes days.（中文：培养——从患者样本中培养细菌以鉴定病原体的实验室检查，需数天。）

Empiric therapy — the antibiotic started before the culture result exists, on clinical suspicion alone.（中文：经验性治疗——在培养结果出来之前，仅凭临床怀疑就开始的抗生素治疗。）

Index culture / index time — the anchor event from which the prediction window (here, two weeks) runs; also the cutoff before which all model inputs must be available.（中文：索引培养／索引时刻——预测窗口起算的锚点事件，也是所有模型输入必须已经可得的截止时刻。）

MIMIC-IV — a public, de-identified EHR database from a Boston tertiary academic center; the standard external-validation set for clinical ML.（中文：MIMIC-IV——波士顿一家三级学术医疗中心的公开去标识化电子病历数据库，是临床机器学习的标准外部验证集。）

MRSA — methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus; a common skin/nose bacterium whose resistant strains eliminate the beta-lactam class as a treatment option.（中文：MRSA——耐甲氧西林金黄色葡萄球菌。）

De-escalation — stopping or narrowing broad antibiotic coverage once it's judged unnecessary. This model's honest product.（中文：降阶治疗——在判断不必要后停用或收窄广谱抗生素覆盖。）

Vancomycin — the IV workhorse used when MRSA is suspected; effective but carries kidney toxicity and cost.（中文：万古霉素——怀疑 MRSA 时使用的静脉主力药物，有效但有肾毒性与成本负担。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • PyTorch_EHR — GRU RNN over sequences of categorical clinical codes with an embedded inter-visit day-gap; categorical-only inputs (no labs, notes or images) is a deliberate portability choice, and it is why the model transfers to MIMIC at all.
  • Label aligned to the decision, not the event — predict "MRSA-positive culture within 2 weeks of index," because the clinician's question is "start/continue vancomycin?", not "name today's organism." Zero math, largest single design win.
  • Index-time leakage guard — only results reported (not merely drawn) by index time; in MIMIC, encounter-level codes only from previous encounters, discarding real signal on purpose because encounter codes can be assigned after the outcome is known.
  • The mandatory tabular twin — LightGBM and LR given count-of-code-before-index plus recency-of-last-occurrence, so the temporal information isn't withheld from the baseline. Optuna for all three; DeLong for AUC CIs.
  • Pretrain-then-transfer — a MHHS-pretrained model scores 0.860 on MIMIC, ≈ a model trained on MIMIC directly.
  • Policy-relevant cutoffs, not argmax — thresholds set at 95% spec / 95% sens (Houston), tightened to 99% / 90% in MIMIC because the outcome is rarer. Report PPV/NPV at the cutoff you'd deploy.
  • Integrated gradients, declared patient-level only — the authors explicitly forbid reading their own attributions as population risk factors ("inputs could surrogate other underlying events"). Copy this norm.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Priority served: AI-tooling / career.

event-sequence-registry-baseline (difficulty: M) — Input: any longitudinal coded clinical dataset as (patient, day_offset, code, code_type) + an index-time definition + a label. Does: (1) enforces the leakage guard, dropping codes whose result time falls after index; (2) trains the GRU sequence model with time-gap embeddings (wrapping ZhiGroup/pytorch_ehr); (3) mandatorily trains the tabular twin (LightGBM on count + recency features) on the identical index definition; (4) emits one head-to-head report: DeLong-tested AUCs, calibration curve, PPV/NPV at policy cutoffs. Selling point: it makes the leakage guard and the tabular twin non-optional — the two things sequence-model papers quietly skip. Public repo + public MIMIC-IV as the demo dataset; no registry internals.

What to verify first

  • The Houston base rate is engineered. They randomly selected 8,164 MRSA-positive vs 18,069 other patients "to avoid an imbalanced dataset." Every threshold-dependent number from Houston — PPV 65.6% above all — is a statement about that construction, not a ward. The MIMIC PPV of 22.4%, computed at real imbalance, is the number to carry.
  • The clinical-impact table is a counterfactual simulation, not a trial, and it assumes the clinician would have obeyed the model.
  • The outcome is a positive culture, not an infection. Cultures are ordered by humans → the label inherits the institution's ordering habits; the authors concede MRSA infection can occur without a positive culture.
  • The DL margin is ~2 AUROC points over LightGBM. Before choosing the GRU for anything, ask whether 0.02 justifies the serving and governance cost.
  • Attributions are patient-level only — do not lift the integrated-gradient figure into a risk-factor claim; the authors say so themselves.
  • If ever adapted to a registry setting, the label must be defined by the domain owners, not inferred from the data. Everything here stays external method.

ALEX Today on Track B: Masayuki Nigo, Laila Rasmy, Degui Zhi and colleagues at UTHealth Houston, "Deep learning model for personalized prediction of positive MRSA culture using time-series electronic health records," Nature Communications, March twenty twenty-four. 今天 Track B 的论文来自休斯敦 UTHealth 的 Masayuki Nigo、Laila Rasmy、Degui Zhi 及其同事，题为《使用时间序列电子健康记录对 MRSA 培养阳性进行个体化预测的深度学习模型》，2024 年 3 月发表于《Nature Communications》。 It is open access, it ships public code, and it is the cleanest template I have seen for the thing you would actually do with a longitudinal coded clinical dataset. 它是开放获取的，附带公开代码，而且是我见过的、把一个纵向编码临床数据集真正用起来的最干净的模板。 We are reading it for the harness, not for the bug. 我们读它是为了这套框架，而不是为了那种细菌。

JORDAN Then start with the bug, because I need the clinical layer. 那还是先从细菌讲起，因为我需要临床这一层。

ALEX Staphylococcus aureus is an extremely common bacterium — it lives on skin and in noses and it also causes serious infections. 金黄色葡萄球菌是一种极其常见的细菌——它寄居在皮肤和鼻腔中，同时也会引起严重感染。 MRSA is methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus, meaning the strain has acquired resistance to the beta-lactam class of antibiotics, which is the class doctors reach for first and which is safe, cheap and effective. MRSA 指的是耐甲氧西林金黄色葡萄球菌，也就是说这个菌株获得了对 β-内酰胺类抗生素的耐药性；而这一类抗生素恰恰是医生首选、安全、便宜且有效的药物。 If the organism is MRSA, that whole class is off the table and you have to use something like intravenous vancomycin instead. 如果致病菌是 MRSA，那整类抗生素就都用不了了，你必须改用静脉注射万古霉素这样的药物。 Two more terms: a culture is the lab test where you grow bacteria from a sample and see what comes up, and empiric therapy is the antibiotic you start *before* the culture result exists, on suspicion alone. 还有两个术语：培养 (culture) 是把样本中的细菌培养出来、看长出什么的实验室检查；而经验性治疗 (empiric therapy) 指的是在培养结果出来之前，仅凭怀疑就开始使用的抗生素。

JORDAN So the decision is made in the dark, and the answer arrives days later. 所以决策是在黑暗中做出的，而答案要在几天后才到。

ALEX Precisely, and both errors are expensive. 正是如此，而且两种错误都代价高昂。 If you withhold anti-MRSA coverage and the patient has MRSA, you have under-treated a potentially lethal infection. 如果你不给抗 MRSA 的覆盖，而病人恰好感染了 MRSA，你就漏治了一种可能致命的感染。 If you give vancomycin to everyone, you get kidney toxicity, cost, and — the systemic harm — you breed resistance. 如果你给每个人都用万古霉素，你会得到肾毒性、高成本，以及一种系统性危害——你在培育耐药性。 That is why hospitals run antimicrobial stewardship programs, whose whole job is to get people *off* unnecessary broad antibiotics. 这就是医院设立抗菌药物管理项目 (antimicrobial stewardship) 的原因，它们的全部工作就是让病人尽早停掉不必要的广谱抗生素。 One more term for later: bacteremia, which means the bacterium is in the bloodstream — that is the severe end, and it is the version nobody wants to miss. 还有一个后面会用到的术语：菌血症 (bacteremia)，指细菌进入了血流——那是重症的一端，也是谁都不想漏掉的那一种。

JORDAN What was wrong with the existing prediction models? 现有的预测模型有什么问题？

ALEX Two things, and both are about deployability rather than accuracy. 有两个问题，而且都关乎可部署性，而不是准确率。 First, prior MRSA models tended to be narrow — built for one infection type, usually pneumonia, because narrowing the population lets you simplify the risk factors. 第一，以往的 MRSA 模型往往很窄——只针对一种感染类型，通常是肺炎，因为把人群缩窄可以简化风险因素。 The authors cite one such model at an area under the ROC curve of zero point seven seven five. 作者引用了这样一个模型，其 ROC 曲线下面积为零点七七五。 Second, many of them depend on a specific test — the nasal MRSA PCR swab is the classic — and if your hospital does not run that test, or has not run it yet on this patient, the model is simply unusable at the moment you need it. 第二，很多模型依赖某项特定检查——鼻拭子 MRSA PCR 是最典型的——而如果你的医院不做这项检查，或者还没给这位病人做，那么在你最需要模型的那一刻，它根本用不了。 A model that requires a variable the clinician does not have at decision time is not a model, it is a wish. 一个在决策时刻要求医生提供他手头没有的变量的模型，那不是模型，那是许愿。

JORDAN So they built something that only eats what the chart already contains. 于是他们造了一个只吃病历里已经有的东西的模型。

ALEX That is the design commitment, and it explains everything downstream. 这就是他们的设计承诺，而它解释了后面的一切。 The model is PyTorch underscore EHR, from the Zhi lab, and it is deliberately boring: a gated recurrent unit recurrent network over the patient's sequence of categorical clinical codes — diagnoses, procedures, medications administered, prior microbiology results, demographics — with the time gap between visits fed in as an additional embedded input. 模型叫 PyTorch_EHR，来自 Zhi 实验室，而它刻意做得很平淡：一个门控循环单元 (GRU) 循环网络，作用于病人按时间排列的分类临床编码序列——诊断、操作、给药记录、既往微生物学结果、人口学信息——并把就诊之间的时间间隔作为一个额外的嵌入输入喂进去。 No lab values, no notes, no images — categorical codes only. 没有化验数值，没有病历文本，没有影像——只有分类编码。 The prediction target: given an index culture, will this patient have a MRSA-positive culture within the next two weeks. 预测目标是：给定一次索引培养 (index culture)，这位病人在接下来两周内是否会出现 MRSA 培养阳性。

JORDAN Why a two-week window rather than "is today's culture positive"? 为什么是两周窗口，而不是"今天这次培养是不是阳性"？

ALEX Because the two-week window is the clinician's actual decision horizon, and this is the single sharpest thing in the paper. 因为两周窗口才是医生真正的决策视野，而这是全文最锋利的一点。 The doctor is not deciding whether to name today's organism; the doctor is deciding whether to start vancomycin now and whether to keep it going tomorrow, and cultures drawn after antibiotics have started still matter to that decision. 医生要决定的不是给今天的病原体命名；医生要决定的是现在要不要开万古霉素、明天要不要继续用，而在抗生素开始之后采集的培养，对这个决策依然重要。 Aligning the label to the decision rather than to the lab event is a modeling choice with no math in it, and it is the choice that makes the model clinically usable. 把标签对齐到"决策"而不是"化验事件"，是一个不含任何数学的建模选择，而正是这个选择让模型在临床上真正可用。

JORDAN Data? 数据呢？

ALEX Development at Memorial Hermann Hospital System in Houston: twenty-six thousand two hundred thirty-three patients, fifty-six thousand two hundred thirty-three index culture events, from January twenty eighteen to April twenty twenty-one. 开发数据来自休斯敦的 Memorial Hermann 医院系统：两万六千二百三十三名患者，五万六千二百三十三次索引培养事件，时间跨度为 2018 年 1 月至 2021 年 4 月。 External validation on MIMIC-four version two point one — that is the public Boston intensive-care database — one hundred fifty-two thousand nine hundred seventy-nine patients and three hundred ninety-three thousand seven hundred thirteen events. 外部验证在 MIMIC-IV 2.1 版上进行——那是公开的波士顿重症监护数据库——包含十五万两千九百七十九名患者和三十九万三千七百一十三次事件。 Baselines are logistic regression and LightGBM, both given a fair shake: the tabular models get the count of each code before index time and the recency of its most recent occurrence, so the temporal information is not simply withheld from them. 基线模型是逻辑回归和 LightGBM，两者都得到了公平对待：表格模型拿到了索引时刻之前每个编码的出现次数，以及最近一次出现的时间距离，因此时间信息并没有被简单地对它们隐瞒。 Hyperparameters for all three tuned with Optuna, AUC confidence intervals by DeLong. 三个模型的超参数都用 Optuna 调优，AUC 的置信区间用 DeLong 检验计算。

JORDAN And the leakage guard, because that is where this kind of study usually dies. 还有防泄漏措施，因为这类研究通常就死在这里。

ALEX They are explicit about it twice, which I respect. 他们两次明确写出了这一点，我很敬重这一点。 In the Houston data, only results actually *reported* by the index time are used as input — not results from tests drawn before index time that resulted afterward. 在休斯敦的数据中，只有在索引时刻之前已经"发出报告"的结果才被用作输入——而不是那些在索引时刻之前采样、之后才出结果的检查。 In MIMIC, because codes are aggregated at the encounter level, they use only codes from *previous* encounters, throwing away the current encounter's codes entirely. 在 MIMIC 中，由于编码是按就诊层级汇总的，他们只使用"之前"就诊的编码，把当次就诊的编码全部丢弃。 That second rule is costly — it discards real signal — and they took the cost anyway, because an encounter-level code can be assigned after the outcome is known. 第二条规则代价不小——它丢掉了真实的信号——但他们还是承担了这个代价，因为就诊层级的编码有可能是在结局已知之后才被赋予的。 That is what a defensible index time looks like. 这就是一个站得住脚的索引时刻应有的样子。

JORDAN One architecture question before results. 在讲结果之前问一个架构问题。 Why a gated recurrent unit in twenty twenty-four, when every other paper we read is a transformer? 到了 2024 年为什么还用门控循环单元，而我们读的其他每一篇论文都在用 Transformer？

ALEX Because the sequence here is short and the vocabulary is small. 因为这里的序列很短，词表也很小。 A patient trajectory is tens to low hundreds of coded events, not nine kilobases of nucleotides — attention buys you long-range dependency modeling that this data does not really contain. 一条病人轨迹是几十到一两百个编码事件，而不是九千个碱基的核苷酸——注意力机制带来的是长程依赖建模能力，而这份数据里其实并不存在那样的依赖。 The thing the recurrent unit does need, and gets, is an explicit representation of *irregular time*: two visits three days apart and two visits three years apart are not the same sequence, and they embed the day-gap to say so. 循环单元真正需要、也确实得到的，是对"不规则时间"的显式表示：相隔三天的两次就诊和相隔三年的两次就诊，并不是同一个序列，而他们通过嵌入天数间隔来表达这一点。 The lesson generalizes past this paper: in clinical event sequences the bottleneck is almost never model capacity, it is the fidelity of the timeline you feed it. 这个教训可以推广到这篇论文之外：在临床事件序列中，瓶颈几乎从来不是模型容量，而是你喂给它的那条时间线的保真度。

JORDAN Does the deep model win? 那深度模型赢了吗？

ALEX It wins, and by less than the framing suggests. 它赢了，但赢的幅度比论文的叙述给人的印象要小。 In Houston: PyTorch underscore EHR at zero point nine one one, LightGBM at zero point eight nine two, logistic regression at zero point eight five seven. 在休斯敦：PyTorch_EHR 是零点九一一，LightGBM 是零点八九二，逻辑回归是零点八五七。 On external MIMIC: zero point eight five nine, zero point eight three eight, zero point eight one six — same ordering, everything drops about five points, which is what honest external validation looks like. 在外部的 MIMIC 上：零点八五九、零点八三八、零点八一六——排序相同，所有模型都下降约五个点，这正是诚实的外部验证该有的样子。 And a pretrained-then-transferred version scores zero point eight six zero on MIMIC, essentially matching the model trained on MIMIC directly. 而一个"先预训练再迁移"的版本在 MIMIC 上得到零点八六零，基本追平了直接在 MIMIC 上训练的模型。

JORDAN So the gradient-boosted tree is within two points of the neural network. 也就是说，梯度提升树和神经网络只差两个点。

ALEX Two points, and this is my design objection, and I want to state it as an objection to the *narrative*, not the work. 差两个点——这就是我的设计异议，而且我想说清楚，这是对"叙事"的异议，而不是对工作本身的异议。 The paper is framed as deep learning beating traditional machine learning; the numbers say a well-tuned LightGBM with count-and-recency features is a near-peer, and it is cheaper to train, cheaper to serve, and far easier to defend to a hospital committee. 论文的叙述框架是"深度学习击败传统机器学习"；但数字告诉我们，一个调好的、带计数与近因特征的 LightGBM 是一个接近同级的对手，而且它训练更便宜、部署更便宜，也远远更容易向医院委员会交代。 The paper's real value is not the two points; it is that it *ran the tabular twin at all*, on the same index-time definition, on the same features, and reported it honestly. 这篇论文真正的价值不在那两个点；而在于它"确实跑了那个表格模型的孪生对照"，用的是相同的索引时刻定义、相同的特征，并且如实报告了结果。 That discipline is the artifact. 这种纪律才是真正的产物。

JORDAN What about the clinical-impact numbers? 那临床影响的数字呢？

ALEX This is where it gets interesting and slightly uncomfortable. 这就是它变得有意思、也略微令人不适的地方。 They set risk cutoffs by operating characteristics — ninety-five percent specificity for the high-risk flag and ninety-five percent sensitivity for the low-risk flag in Houston, tightened to ninety-nine and ninety in MIMIC because the outcome is far rarer there. 他们通过操作特性来设定风险阈值——在休斯敦，高风险标记用百分之九十五的特异度，低风险标记用百分之九十五的灵敏度；在 MIMIC 中收紧到百分之九十九和百分之九十，因为那里的结局要罕见得多。 Positive predictive value in the high-risk group: sixty-five point six percent in Houston, but only twenty-two point four percent in MIMIC. 高风险组的阳性预测值：休斯敦是百分之六十五点六，但在 MIMIC 中只有百分之二十二点四。 Negative predictive value in the low-risk group: ninety-eight point six and ninety-nine point eight percent. 低风险组的阴性预测值：百分之九十八点六和百分之九十九点八。 So the honest product here is not "who has MRSA" — it is *de-escalation*: the model is extremely good at telling you who does not need vancomycin, and it identified nearly eight thousand potentially avoidable anti-MRSA doses in the Houston test set alone. 因此这里诚实的产品并不是"谁得了 MRSA"——而是"降阶治疗"：这个模型极其擅长告诉你谁不需要万古霉素，仅在休斯敦的测试集中，它就识别出近八千剂本可避免的抗 MRSA 用药。 And the high-risk group is genuinely sicker: thirty-one point eight percent of high-risk events had MRSA bacteremia versus zero point five percent of low-risk events. 而高风险组确实病得更重：高风险事件中有百分之三十一点八伴有 MRSA 菌血症，而低风险事件中只有百分之零点五。
