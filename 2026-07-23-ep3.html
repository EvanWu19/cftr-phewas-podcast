Show notes — Role of the ADAM33 rs2280091 Variant in Modulating Lung Function in Cystic Fibrosis

Blurb. Dos Santos et al. (2025, Int J Mol Sci) test whether ADAM33 — a metalloproteinase gene first known from asthma, involved in airway remodeling — modifies lung function in cystic fibrosis. In 55 CF patients at one Brazilian center (96% female, F508del/F508del most common), the synonymous variant rs2280091 GG genotype was over-represented vs. controls (OR ~12) but deviated from Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium, which the authors candidly attribute to a possible survivor/recruitment effect rather than a clean disease association. The more interesting signal is functional: G-allele carriers had significantly better peripheral airflow (post-BD FEF25 64% vs 51%, FDR p=0.006; FEF50 adj p=0.015) — the opposite direction to asthma, where the G allele is deleterious. The proposed explanation is divergent lung pathology (asthma = hyperresponsiveness/remodeling; CF = neutrophilic/infective structural damage). Where it lands: the modifier catalog + summary-stats colocalization lane. Buildable artifact: a cross-phenotype modifier direction-check notebook that compares a variant's effect direction across asthma/COPD/lung-function GWAS to catch sign flips before proposing colocalization.

Plain-English glossary

  • ADAM33 — "A Disintegrin and Metalloproteinase Domain 33," a matrix-remodeling enzyme in fibroblasts and bronchial smooth muscle; an asthma-susceptibility and candidate CF-modifier gene.（中文：一个二整合素金属蛋白酶，参与气道重塑，是哮喘易感基因和候选 CF 修饰基因。）
  • Modifier gene — a gene that shifts disease severity/variability without causing the disease; explains why identical CFTR genotypes differ.（中文：修饰基因——不引起疾病但改变其严重程度/变异性的基因，解释相同 CFTR 基因型为何表现不同。）
  • Synonymous variant — a DNA change that does not alter the protein's amino acid sequence; can still act via mRNA stability, codon usage, or as a linkage tag.（中文：同义变异——改变 DNA 但不改变蛋白氨基酸序列的变异，仍可能通过 mRNA 稳定性、密码子使用或作为连锁标签起作用。）
  • rs2280091 — the specific synonymous ADAM33 variant (A>G) studied here.（中文：本研究的具体 ADAM33 同义变异（A>G）。）
  • Linkage disequilibrium (LD) — the tendency of nearby loci to be inherited together, so a tag variant can mark a nearby causal one.（中文：连锁不平衡——邻近位点倾向于一起遗传，因此标签变异可标记附近的因果变异。）
  • Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium (HWE) — the genotype-frequency distribution expected in a stable population; deviation flags genotyping error, stratification, or selection.（中文：哈迪-温伯格平衡——稳定群体中预期的基因型频率分布；偏离提示基因分型错误、群体分层或选择。）
  • Spirometry — a breathing test measuring how much/how fast air is exhaled.（中文：肺量计——测量呼出气量与速度的呼吸测试。）
  • FVC / FEV1 — Forced Vital Capacity (total forced exhale) and Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 second.（中文：用力肺活量 / 一秒用力呼气量。）
  • FEF25 / FEF50 / FEF25-75 / MEF — forced expiratory flow measures reflecting small-airway patency; sensitive to early peripheral obstruction.（中文：用力呼气流量指标，反映小气道通畅度，对早期外周阻塞敏感。）
  • Bronchodilator (BD) — a drug relaxing airway muscle; spirometry pre/post-BD gauges reversibility.（中文：支气管扩张剂——放松气道肌肉的药物；用药前后测肺量计评估可逆性。）
  • PCR-RFLP — polymerase chain reaction–restriction fragment length polymorphism; a low-cost genotyping method (not sequencing).（中文：一种基于限制性酶切的低成本基因分型方法，非测序。）
  • Odds ratio (OR) — ratio of odds of an outcome between genotype groups.（中文：优势比——不同基因型组间结局发生几率之比。）

Full citation. Dos Santos VS, Mello LS, Marques LFA, Silva LR, Bertuzzo CS, Ribeiro JD, Marson FAL. Role of the ADAM33 rs2280091 Variant in Modulating Lung Function in Cystic Fibrosis. Int J Mol Sci. 2025. PMID: 41373738 · PMC: PMC12692732 · DOI: 10.3390/ijms262311583

Methods / tools to lift

  • Candidate-modifier association testing against peripheral-flow spirometry (FEF25/50/25-75, MEF), not just FEV1 — small-airway measures caught the signal.
  • FDR multiple-testing correction across spirometric parameters.
  • Hardy–Weinberg check as a data-quality/selection diagnostic — and honest interpretation of a deviation as possible survivor effect.
  • Cross-disease effect-direction comparison (asthma vs CF) as a sanity check on a modifier's sign.

Difficulty: S–M."

What to verify first

  • Sample: n=55, 96% female, single center, control GG freq 0.02% breaking HWE — replicate in a larger, sex-balanced, multi-center cohort before trusting the direction.
  • Genotyping: PCR-RFLP is error-prone vs sequencing; a genotyping artifact is a standard HWE-deviation explanation — confirm calls.
  • Causality: rs2280091 is a synonymous LD tag — fine-map before treating it as the causal actor.
  • Direction: protective sign in CF contradicts asthma; treat as a hypothesis, not a finding.

Gap-map / catalog

  • Closes: adds ADAM33 as a candidate CF lung-function modifier with a concrete variant (rs2280091), phenotype (peripheral airflow), and effect estimates.
  • Leaves open: whether the association survives replication and HWE concerns; whether rs2280091 or an LD neighbor is causal; mechanism of the asthma↔CF direction flip; any gene–gene interaction with CFTR class or other modifiers.

ALEX Today's Track C paper is "Role of the ADAM33 rs2280091 Variant in Modulating Lung Function in Cystic Fibrosis," by Dos Santos and colleagues, led out of a Brazilian group with Fernando Marson as senior author, published in the International Journal of Molecular Sciences in 2025. 今天 Track C 的论文是 Dos Santos 及其同事的《ADAM33 rs2280091 变异在调节囊性纤维化肺功能中的作用》，由一个巴西团队主导、Fernando Marson 为资深作者，2025 年发表于《International Journal of Molecular Sciences》。 It's a CF modifier-gene study, and it feeds our genes-and-variants catalog. 这是一项 CF 修饰基因研究，为我们的基因与变异目录提供素材。 We're reading it because it's a clean example of a modifier locus outside CFTR that tracks with lung function — and because it comes with a genuinely counterintuitive twist. 我们读它，是因为它是一个 CFTR 之外、与肺功能相关的修饰位点的干净例子——而且它带着一个真正违反直觉的转折。

JORDAN Let's define the pieces. 我们先把各部分定义清楚。 What is a modifier gene, and what is ADAM33? 什么是修饰基因，ADAM33 又是什么？ ALEX: A modifier gene (在主致病基因之外、影响疾病严重程度或表现的基因) doesn't cause cystic fibrosis — CFTR does that — but it shifts how severe or how variable the disease is. 修饰基因不会引起囊性纤维化——那是 CFTR 的事——但它会改变疾病的严重程度或变异性。 Two people with the exact same CFTR genotype can have very different lung function, and modifier genes are part of why. 两个 CFTR 基因型完全相同的人，肺功能可能大不相同，而修饰基因是其中一部分原因。 ADAM33 (一个二整合素金属蛋白酶，参与气道重塑和组织修复) stands for "A Disintegrin and Metalloproteinase Domain 33." ADAM33 全称是"含解整合素和金属蛋白酶结构域 33"。 It sits on chromosome 20 and encodes a metalloproteinase — an enzyme that remodels the extracellular matrix — expressed in fibroblasts and bronchial smooth muscle. 它位于 20 号染色体，编码一种金属蛋白酶——一种重塑细胞外基质的酶——在成纤维细胞和支气管平滑肌中表达。 It was first identified as an asthma susceptibility gene, tied to airway remodeling and bronchial hyperresponsiveness. 它最初被鉴定为一个哮喘易感基因，与气道重塑和支气管高反应性相关。 JORDAN: And the specific variant, rs2280091? 那具体的变异 rs2280091 呢？ ALEX: rs2280091 is a single-nucleotide variant in ADAM33, an A-to-G change. rs2280091 是 ADAM33 中的一个单核苷酸变异，是一个 A 到 G 的改变。 Here's the important subtlety: it's a synonymous variant (改变 DNA 但不改变蛋白氨基酸序列的变异). 这里有个重要的微妙之处：它是一个同义变异。 JORDAN: Synonymous meaning it doesn't change the protein? 同义的意思是它不改变蛋白？ ALEX: Right — it changes the DNA but not the amino acid sequence, so it's easy to dismiss as silent. 对——它改变 DNA 但不改变氨基酸序列，所以很容易被当作"沉默的"而忽略。 But synonymous variants can still matter through messenger-RNA stability, codon usage, or RNA folding, and this one is in linkage disequilibrium (在群体中倾向于一起遗传的位点之间的关联) with other ADAM33 variants that do have functional effects. 但同义变异仍可能通过信使 RNA（mRNA）稳定性、密码子使用或 RNA 折叠而产生影响，而且这一个与 ADAM33 中其他确有功能效应的变异处于连锁不平衡（linkage disequilibrium）状态。 So rs2280091 may be a tag for a nearby causal variant rather than the cause itself. 所以 rs2280091 可能是附近某个因果变异的"标签"，而不是原因本身。

JORDAN Give me the spirometry vocabulary too, because the results hang on it. 也给我讲讲肺量计的词汇，因为结果都系在它上面。 ALEX: Spirometry measures how much and how fast you can breathe out. 肺量计测量你能呼出多少气以及呼得多快。 The headline numbers are FVC (用力肺活量), the total volume you can forcibly exhale, and FEV1 (一秒用力呼气量), the volume in the first second. 主要指标是 FVC（用力肺活量），即你能用力呼出的总体积，和 FEV1（一秒用力呼气量），即第一秒内呼出的体积。 But this paper's signal is mostly in the peripheral-flow measures: FEF25, FEF50, FEF25-75, and MEF. 但这篇论文的信号主要在外周气流指标上：FEF25、FEF50、FEF25-75 和 MEF。 Those (用力呼气流量指标，反映小气道的通畅程度) capture flow through the small airways, and they're sensitive to obstruction deep in the lung, before it shows up in FEV1. 这些用力呼气流量指标反映小气道的气流，它们对肺深处的阻塞很敏感，早在 FEV1 出现变化之前就能捕捉到。 JORDAN: And bronchodilator? 那支气管扩张剂呢？ ALEX: They measured spirometry before and after a bronchodilator, a drug that relaxes airway muscle, to see how much the obstruction is reversible. 他们在使用支气管扩张剂（一种放松气道肌肉的药物）前后各测一次肺量计，以观察阻塞有多少是可逆的。 That's standard per the ATS/ERS guidelines they followed. 这是他们所遵循的 ATS/ERS 指南中的标准做法。

ALEX So the study. 那么来说这项研究。 It's a cross-sectional study of 55 CF patients at a single Brazilian referral center, diagnosis confirmed by sweat test and CFTR genotyping. 这是一项在巴西单一转诊中心对 55 名 CF 患者的横断面研究，诊断经汗液试验和 CFTR 基因型确认。 The cohort skews young and, notably, 96 percent female — hold onto that. 这个队列偏年轻，而且值得注意的是 96% 为女性——记住这一点。 The most common CFTR genotype was F508del homozygous at 53 percent, then F508del/G542X and others. 最常见的 CFTR 基因型是 F508del 纯合，占 53%，其次是 F508del/G542X 及其他。 They genotyped rs2280091 by PCR-RFLP — a low-tech restriction-enzyme method, not sequencing — and compared against controls. 他们用 PCR-RFLP（一种低技术含量的限制性酶切方法，而非测序）对 rs2280091 进行基因分型，并与对照组比较。 JORDAN: What did they find on frequency? 他们在频率上发现了什么？ ALEX: This is where I'd push hard. 这是我会强烈质疑的地方。 The GG genotype was far more common in CF cases, 18 percent, than in controls, where they report 0.02 percent, giving an odds ratio of 12. GG 基因型在 CF 病例中远比对照组常见，18% 对他们报告的 0.02%，得出优势比为 12。 And the variant deviated from Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium (在稳定群体中预期的基因型频率分布) in the cases but not the controls. 而且这个变异在病例中偏离了哈迪-温伯格平衡（Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium，即稳定群体中预期的基因型频率分布），在对照中则没有。 JORDAN: What does a Hardy-Weinberg deviation usually signal? 哈迪-温伯格偏离通常意味着什么？ ALEX: Genotyping error, population stratification, or a real selective effect. 基因分型错误、群体分层，或真实的选择效应。 The authors are commendably honest that a 0.02 percent control frequency is suspicious and that the deviation could be a survivor effect or a recruitment artifact rather than a true disease association. 作者非常值得称赞地坦承，0.02% 的对照频率是可疑的，而这种偏离可能是幸存者效应或招募偏倚，而不是真实的疾病关联。 That's the right framing, but it does undercut the case-control OR quite a bit. 这是恰当的表述，但它确实相当程度地削弱了病例对照的优势比。

ALEX Now the more interesting result, the lung-function association. 现在说更有趣的结果，即肺功能关联。 Among the CF patients, GG carriers had consistently better peripheral airflow than AA or AG carriers. 在 CF 患者中，GG 携带者的外周气流始终优于 AA 或 AG 携带者。 Post-bronchodilator FEF25 was a median of 78 percent predicted in GG versus about 50 percent in AA and 55 in AG. 支气管扩张剂后的 FEF25 中位数在 GG 中为预测值的 78%，而 AA 约为 50%、AG 约为 55%。 FEF50 was 48 percent in GG versus 24 in AA. FEF50 在 GG 中为 48%，AA 为 24%。 On the allele level, G-allele carriers beat A-allele carriers on post-bronchodilator FEF25, 64 versus 51 percent, with an FDR-adjusted p of 0.006, and FEF50 at an adjusted p of 0.015. 在等位基因层面，G 等位基因携带者在支气管扩张剂后 FEF25 上胜过 A 等位基因携带者，64% 对 51%，FDR 校正后 p 值为 0.006，FEF50 的校正 p 值为 0.015。 They properly corrected for multiple testing, which I want to credit. 他们恰当地对多重检验做了校正，这一点我要肯定。 JORDAN: Wait — the G allele is protective here? G 等位基因在这里是保护性的？ ALEX: That's the twist. 这就是那个转折。 In asthma, the same G allele of rs2280091 is associated with worse lung function and greater susceptibility. 在哮喘中，rs2280091 的同一个 G 等位基因与更差的肺功能和更高的易感性相关。 In this CF cohort it points the opposite way — better small-airway flow. 而在这个 CF 队列中它指向相反方向——更好的小气道气流。 JORDAN: How do they explain the flip? 他们如何解释这个翻转？ ALEX: Their argument is that the two diseases have different lung pathology. 他们的论点是这两种疾病有不同的肺部病理。 Asthma obstruction is driven by hyperresponsiveness and excessive remodeling, so more ADAM33 activity makes it worse. 哮喘的阻塞由高反应性和过度重塑驱动，所以更多的 ADAM33 活性会使它恶化。 CF is driven by neutrophilic inflammation and infection-mediated structural damage, and they speculate that ADAM33-mediated matrix turnover might actually help preserve small-airway structure under chronic inflammatory stress. CF 则由中性粒细胞炎症和感染介导的结构损伤驱动，他们推测 ADAM33 介导的基质更新在慢性炎症压力下也许实际上有助于保存小气道结构。 It's biologically plausible, but it's a post-hoc story, and they say so. 这在生物学上说得通，但它是一个事后的解释，他们也这么说了。

JORDAN You mentioned a survivor effect twice — walk me through why that's the honest reading. 你两次提到幸存者效应——请说明为什么那是诚实的解读。 ALEX: Think about what it takes to be a 20-something CF patient recruited into a spirometry study. 想想要成为一个被招募进肺量计研究的二十几岁 CF 患者需要什么。 You had to survive to that age with enough lung function to walk into the clinic and blow into a spirometer. 你得活到那个年龄，并且有足够的肺功能走进诊所、对着肺量计吹气。 If the G allele genuinely preserves small-airway flow, then G-allele carriers are more likely to reach that point — so the study population is enriched for them, not because G causes CF but because G-carriers with CF live longer and get recruited. 如果 G 等位基因确实保存了小气道气流，那么 G 等位基因携带者更可能到达那个节点——所以研究人群会富集他们，不是因为 G 导致 CF，而是因为携带 G 的 CF 患者活得更久、更容易被招募。 That single mechanism explains both the odd case-control frequency and the Hardy-Weinberg deviation and the protective spirometry, all at once. 这一个机制同时解释了异常的病例对照频率、哈迪-温伯格偏离和保护性的肺量计结果。 JORDAN: So the association and the artifact are hard to tell apart here. 所以在这里，真实关联和假象很难区分。 ALEX: Exactly, and the authors say as much — which is why replication in an incident, prospectively-followed cohort matters more than any p-value in this cross-sectional snapshot. 正是如此，作者也是这么说的——这就是为什么在一个新发、前瞻性随访的队列中复现，比这个横断面快照里的任何 p 值都更重要。 A cross-sectional study samples survivors; a longitudinal one watches the decline happen. 横断面研究采样的是幸存者；纵向研究则观察衰退的发生。

JORDAN The paper also spends time on other modifier genes and on environment. 这篇论文也花了篇幅讨论其他修饰基因和环境。 Why does that matter for us? 这对我们为什么重要？ ALEX: Because it frames ADAM33 as one candidate in a crowded field, which is the right humility. 因为它把 ADAM33 定位为一个拥挤领域中的候选者，这是恰当的谦逊。 They list SLC-family transporters, ACE2, glutathione-pathway genes like GSH and GCLC, the beta-2 adrenergic receptor ADRB2, and adiponectin ADIPOQ as other studied CF modifiers. 他们列出了 SLC 家族转运体、ACE2、谷胱甘肽通路基因如 GSH 和 GCLC、β2 肾上腺素受体 ADRB2，以及脂联素 ADIPOQ 作为其他被研究过的 CF 修饰基因。 And they cite Goss 2004, where in a cohort of over 11,000 patients a 10-microgram-per-cubic-meter rise in particulate air pollution was tied to an 8 percent higher chance of frequent pulmonary exacerbations. 他们引用了 Goss 2004，在一个超过 11,000 名患者的队列中，空气中颗粒物每升高 10 微克每立方米，与频繁肺部加重的几率高出 8% 相关。 JORDAN: So lung function in CF is genetics plus environment plus CFTR class, all at once. 所以 CF 的肺功能是遗传加环境加 CFTR 类别，同时作用。 ALEX: Right, and a single-variant, single-center study can't disentangle those — which is a reason the transferable lesson isn't "ADAM33 is protective," it's "any modifier claim needs to survive the confounders." 对，而一个单变异、单中心的研究无法拆解这些——这也是为什么可迁移的教训不是"ADAM33 是保护性的"，而是"任何修饰基因主张都需要经受住混杂因素的考验"。

ALEX One more mechanistic note worth carrying, because it's the reason a synonymous variant isn't automatically noise. 还有一个值得记住的机制性说明，因为它是同义变异不会自动等于噪声的原因。 rs2280091 doesn't change the protein, but the paper points out that a synonymous change can still alter messenger-RNA stability, codon usage efficiency, or RNA secondary structure, any of which can shift how much protein gets made. rs2280091 不改变蛋白，但论文指出，同义改变仍可能改变信使 RNA 稳定性、密码子使用效率或 RNA 二级结构，其中任何一项都可能改变蛋白的产量。 And separately, it's in linkage disequilibrium with promoter and intronic ADAM33 variants that do have recognized functional effects on transcription. 另外，它与 ADAM33 中确有已知转录功能效应的启动子和内含子变异处于连锁不平衡。 So there are two live hypotheses: rs2280091 acts through post-transcriptional regulation, or it's just a marker for a nearby functional variant. 所以有两个尚待验证的假说：rs2280091 通过转录后调控起作用，或者它只是附近某个功能变异的标记。 Distinguishing those is exactly what fine-mapping is for. 区分这两者正是精细定位的用途。

ALEX Where does this land, and I'll pick one priority: the modifier catalog and summary-stats colocalization lane. 这落在哪里，我只选一个优先方向：修饰基因目录与摘要统计共定位方向。 ADAM33 goes into the catalog as a candidate CF lung-function modifier, but flagged as context-dependent and effect-direction-uncertain, with rs2280091 explicitly noted as a synonymous tag variant, not a proven causal one.
