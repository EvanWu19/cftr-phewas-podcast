Ep3 — Capturing infant and child growth dynamics with P-splines mixed effects models

Hernandez M, Li Z, Cole T, Ong Y, Tilling K, Elhakeem A (2026), International Journal of Obesity. This is a methods guide (plus a new R package, psme) for fitting penalized-spline (P-spline) linear mixed-effects models to infant and child height, weight, and BMI trajectories — giving each child both a population-average curve and their own individual-specific expected curve, without the manual knot-placement judgment call classic regression splines require. Demonstrated on the GUSTO Singapore birth cohort (1,014 children, 15,183 observations after growthcleanr-based cleaning removed 346 of 15,554 raw observations), the paper extracts clinically meaningful growth features — peak/rebound BMI, peak height/weight velocity — and reports their distributions, correlations, and associations with maternal/birth factors. For the CFFPR outlier pipeline, this is a candidate-generation substrate paper: a per-child expected trajectory is what turns "is this measurement plausible" into a well-defined residual question.

Plain-English glossary

  • P-splines (penalized B-splines) — B-splines with plenty of evenly-spaced knots plus a penalty term controlling curve smoothness, removing the need to manually choose knot placement.（中文：带惩罚项的B样条，通过施加惩罚项控制曲线平滑度，从而无需手动选择节点位置。）
  • BMI/adiposity rebound — The point in early childhood (often before age 5) where BMI, after falling from its infancy peak, begins rising again; earlier rebound predicts higher later obesity risk.（中文：幼儿期BMI从婴儿期峰值下降后再次开始上升的时间点（通常在5岁前）；反弹越早，日后肥胖风险越高。）
  • growthcleanr — An open-source R package that flags likely-erroneous height/weight measurements by comparing each observation against that child's own trajectory (implausible jumps, duplicates, carried-forward values); a candidate-generation tool, not a ground-truth arbiter.（中文：一个开源R包，通过将每次观测值与该儿童自身生长轨迹比较，标记可能有误的身高体重测量值（不合理跳变、重复值、结转值）；是候选生成工具，不是真值裁决工具。）
  • REML (restricted maximum likelihood) — An estimation method preferred here over standard maximum likelihood because ML tends to bias variance components downward and oversmooth fitted curves.（中文：一种参数估计方法，本文优先选用而非标准最大似然法，因为最大似然法容易低估方差成分并使拟合曲线过度平滑。）
  • Peak height/weight velocity — The maximum rate of growth (cm/month or g/month) during infancy, a clinically tracked growth landmark distinct from the measurement itself.（中文：婴儿期身高/体重增长速率的峰值（厘米/月或克/月），是区别于测量值本身的临床生长里程碑。）

Full citation

Methods / tools to lift

  • psme R package — sparse mixed-model reformulation of P-spline LME curves; fit all six sex-stratified models (height/weight/BMI x boys/girls) in 34 seconds.
  • Individual-specific random-effect spline — a per-child expected trajectory, distinct from the population-average curve, computed via a 1st-order difference penalty to avoid collinearity with the 2nd-order-penalized population spline.
  • Turning-point detection — second-derivative sign change + iterative local quadratic refinement to locate peak/rebound BMI and peak growth velocity (via the sitar package's getPeakTrough()).
  • growthcleanr preprocessing — used upstream of the modeling itself, a directly citable example of candidate-generation-then-modeling pipeline order.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Pipeline stage: (a) candidate generation, primary. A per-child P-spline expected trajectory is a smoother, better-justified substrate for computing flagging residuals than a hand-knotted spline, since the penalty removes the per-field, per-registry knot-placement judgment call. MVP: fit sex-stratified P-spline LME curves (via psme) to a synthetic or public growth dataset, then compute each observation's deviation from its own individual-specific curve as a candidate flagging statistic. Difficulty: M.

What to verify first

  • The basis size (10) and penalty orders were tuned for GUSTO's specific age range (1 week-10 years) and visit density (median 17/child); unclear whether they generalize to a registry with different visit cadence or age range.
  • This model was fit on data already cleaned by growthcleanr — its behavior in the presence of the erroneous values growthcleanr normally removes (i.e., before flagging) is untested here.
  • 10.7% of children had no identifiable peak or rebound; any downstream flagging system needs its own plan for such trajectories rather than silent exclusion.
  • The turning-point detection method (second-derivative sign change + expanding-window quadratic refinement) has its own tuning knobs whose sensitivity isn't explored in this paper.

Error types covered / not covered: This paper's own preprocessing (growthcleanr) targets implausible jumps, duplicates, and carried-forward values upstream of curve fitting; the P-spline model itself does not target any error type directly — it supplies the expected-value substrate a separate residual-based detector would need to define its own error-flagging rule on top of.
