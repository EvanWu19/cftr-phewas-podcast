Show notes — Track A — VariPred (2026-07-05)

Blurb

VariPred (Lin et al., Scientific Reports 2024) predicts whether a missense variant is disease-causing using only the protein sequence — no multiple sequence alignment, no structure. It runs the wild-type and mutant sequences through a frozen protein language model (ESM-1b), pulls the embedding at the mutated position from each, concatenates them, appends the ESM log-likelihood ratio, and feeds that to a shallow feed-forward classifier. On ClinVar it reaches MCC 0.714, beating REVEL, PolyPhen-2, FATHMM, and ESM-variant, and matching the MSA-heavy state of the art (3Cnet) while requiring none of its inputs. Crucially, it resists Type 2 data-circularity bias — it reads the variant, not the gene — and holds up (MCC 0.65) under a strict no-homology split. Where it lands: the Karen Raraigh CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration. Buildable artifact: a CFTR-focused, total-coverage pathogenicity scorer + benchmark harness built on ESM-1b, used to surface CFTR2 VUS and genotype–phenotype discordant cases.

Plain-English glossary

  • Embedding — the numeric vector a protein language model produces per residue, encoding what it implicitly learned about conservation, structure, and biophysics.（中文：蛋白质语言模型为每个残基输出的数值向量，隐含了保守性、结构与生物物理信息。）
  • HGVS — the standardized convention for writing variant names, e.g. p.Gly77Ser.（中文：HGVS 是书写变异名称的标准化规范，例如 p.Gly77Ser。）
  • LLR (log-likelihood ratio) — the pLM's likelihood of the mutant amino acid versus the wild-type at a position; strongly negative flags an unlikely, probably damaging substitution.（中文：LLR 是 pLM 在某位置上突变氨基酸相对野生型氨基酸的似然之比；强负值提示不太可能出现、很可能有害的替换。）
  • Missense variant — a single-base change that swaps one amino acid for another in the protein.（中文：错义变异，单个碱基改变把蛋白质里一个氨基酸换成另一个。）
  • MSA (multiple sequence alignment) — stacking homologous sequences across species to measure evolutionary conservation per position.（中文：多序列比对，把跨物种的同源序列叠起来，逐位衡量进化保守性。）
  • Protein language model (pLM) — a transformer trained on hundreds of millions of protein sequences by masked-residue prediction; ESM-1b/1v/2 are examples.（中文：蛋白质语言模型，通过遮盖残基预测在数亿条蛋白序列上训练的 transformer；ESM-1b/1v/2 即为例。）
  • Type 2 data circularity — a benchmarking trap where a model scores well by memorizing a gene's dominant class label instead of reading the specific variant.（中文：二型数据循环性，一种基准陷阱——模型靠记住基因的主导类别标签、而非读取具体变异来刷分。）
  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — a variant with insufficient evidence to be called benign or pathogenic.（中文：意义未明变异，证据不足以判定良性或致病。）

Full citation

Lin W, Wells J, Wang Z, Orengo C, Martin ACR. Enhancing missense variant pathogenicity prediction with protein language models using VariPred. Sci Rep. 2024;14:8136. PMID: 38584172 · PMC: PMC10999449 · DOI: 10.1038/s41598-024-51489-7

Methods / tools to lift

  • Twin (Siamese) pLM architecture — pass wild-type and mutant sequences through the same frozen pLM; take only the mutated-position embeddings and concatenate (2×1280 = 2560 dims).
  • LLR feature — append ESM's mutant-vs-wildtype log-likelihood ratio as one extra column (2561 dims); cheap and additive to performance.
  • Shallow FNN head — one hidden layer (1280), LeakyReLU, dropout 0.5, softmax; trained on labels while the pLM stays frozen. Pathogenic threshold 0.2 (skew-corrected).
  • ESM-1b > ESM-1v/ESM-2 for human clinical missense — bigger/newer isn't better here.
  • Circularity controls — SwissvarFilteredMix vs VaribenchSelectedPure to detect gene-label memorization; MCC as the primary metric over AUC-ROC under class imbalance.
  • Sequence-truncation trick — keep 1022-residue windows centered on the mutation for length-limited encoders.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves: Karen / CFTR variant interpretation.

Paste-ready MVP: A notebook that ingests the CFTR2 missense list (HGVS-p) + the CFTR reference sequence, runs ESM-1b to extract wild-type/mutant embeddings and the per-position LLR, and outputs a ranked pathogenicity score compared against CFTR2 clinical labels — flagging genotype–phenotype discordant variants for curation. Difficulty: S (public model, single protein, scoring-only).

What to verify first

  • CFTR-specific calibration — does the 0.2 pathogenic cutoff hold on CFTR variants with known labels, or does the per-gene threshold shift?
  • Training-data leakage — ESM-1b pre-training and the ClinVar split almost certainly include CFTR; test on variants curated after the training cutoff for a fair read.
  • Pathogenicity ≠ mechanism — a sequence-only score can't distinguish splicing/trafficking from gating defects; check correlation with functional readouts (deep mutational scanning, organoids).
  • Long-tail coverage — confirm the truncation strategy behaves sanely for mutations far from both termini in CFTR.

ALEX Welcome back to the reading room. 欢迎回到我们的读书间。 Today's Track A paper is "Enhancing missense variant pathogenicity prediction with protein language models using VariPred," by Weining Lin, Jude Wells, Zeyuan Wang, Christine Orengo and Andrew Martin, published in Scientific Reports in 2024. 今天 Track A（变异解读专题）的论文是《用蛋白质语言模型 VariPred 增强错义变异致病性预测》，作者是 Weining Lin、Jude Wells、Zeyuan Wang、Christine Orengo 和 Andrew Martin，2024 年发表在《Scientific Reports》上。 We're reading it because it makes a strong claim: you can predict whether a missense mutation is disease-causing using nothing but the protein sequence. 我们读它是因为它提出了一个很强的主张：仅凭蛋白质序列本身，就能预测一个错义突变是否致病。 That claim lands squarely on the CFTR variant-interpretation work with Karen. 这个主张恰好落在与 Karen 合作的 CFTR 变异解读工作上。

JORDAN Before we go further, give me the vocabulary. 在往下讲之前，先给我把词汇讲清楚。 What exactly is a missense variant? 到底什么是错义变异（missense variant）？

ALEX A missense variant is a single DNA letter change that swaps one amino acid in the protein for a different one. 错义变异是指单个 DNA 字母的改变，把蛋白质里的一个氨基酸换成了另一个氨基酸。 It's the most common kind of coding change that actually alters the protein, as opposed to silent changes that leave the amino acid sequence untouched. 它是最常见的、真正改变蛋白质的编码改变，与不改变氨基酸序列的沉默突变相对。 The clinical question is always the same: does this swap break the protein enough to cause disease, or is it harmless? 临床问题永远是同一个：这个替换是否足以破坏蛋白质而致病，还是无害的？ When we can't answer that, the variant gets labeled a VUS, a variant of uncertain significance. 当我们无法回答时，这个变异就被标为 VUS（意义未明的变异，即临床证据不足以判断良性还是致病）。 The second term you need is a protein language model, or pLM. 你需要的第二个术语是蛋白质语言模型（protein language model, 简称 pLM）。 It's a transformer trained on hundreds of millions of protein sequences by masking out amino acids and learning to predict them from context, exactly like a text language model predicts words. 它是一种 transformer 模型，通过遮盖氨基酸并从上下文预测它们，在数亿条蛋白质序列上训练而成，就像文本语言模型预测单词一样。 The by-product is an embedding: a numeric vector for each residue position that encodes what the model has implicitly learned about conservation, structure, and biophysics. 其副产品是嵌入表示（embedding）：为每个残基位置生成的数值向量，编码了模型隐式学到的关于保守性、结构和生物物理性质的信息。 The third term is MSA, multiple sequence alignment. MSA（多序列比对，multiple sequence alignment）是第三个术语。 That's the classical way to measure evolutionary conservation by stacking up homologous sequences from many species and seeing which positions never change. 那是衡量进化保守性的经典方法：把来自许多物种的同源序列叠在一起，看哪些位置从不改变。 The whole selling point of a pLM is that it captures conservation without you ever building an MSA. pLM 的核心卖点就是：它无需你构建 MSA 就能捕获保守性。

JORDAN So the old tools all needed alignments or structures, and this one just needs the sequence. 所以旧工具都需要比对或结构，而这个只需要序列。 Why does that matter so much? 这为什么这么重要？

ALEX Because coverage is the quiet killer in variant interpretation. 因为覆盖率是变异解读中一个不动声色的杀手。 There are around 200 million protein sequences in UniProt but only about 200,000 experimentally solved structures in the PDB, so only about one protein in a thousand has a reliable experimental structure. UniProt 里大约有两亿条蛋白质序列，但 PDB（蛋白质结构数据库）里只有约二十万个实验解出的结构，所以大约每一千个蛋白质里只有一个有可靠的实验结构。 A structure-based predictor like Missense3D could only annotate a couple thousand variants out of hundreds of thousands. 像 Missense3D 这样基于结构的预测器，在几十万个变异里只能注释区区几千个。 AlphaFold2 fills some of that gap, but its predicted structures are shaky for proteins with shallow alignments, and structure-based predictors may need re-training per confidence tier. AlphaFold2 填补了一部分空缺，但对于比对稀疏的蛋白质，它预测的结构并不稳，而基于结构的预测器可能需要按置信度分层重新训练。 So the field has a long history of hand-crafted features: PolyPhen-2 uses alignments plus structure, FATHMM uses hidden Markov models over conservation, and ensemble tools like REVEL and MetaLR stack the scores of a dozen or more predictors together. 因此该领域长期依赖人工设计的特征：PolyPhen-2 用比对加结构，FATHMM 用基于保守性的隐马尔可夫模型，而像 REVEL 和 MetaLR 这样的集成工具则把十几个甚至更多预测器的分数叠加在一起。 REVEL combines 13 tools; MetaLR combines 10. REVEL 组合了 13 个工具，MetaLR 组合了 10 个。 The state of the art in the CAGI-6 competition was 3Cnet, an LSTM that eats MSAs, physicochemical properties, motifs, and active-site annotations. 在 CAGI-6 竞赛中的最先进方法是 3Cnet，一个吞入 MSA、理化性质、基序和活性位点注释的 LSTM 模型。 Every one of those inputs is a dependency that can be missing, stale, or wrong. 这些输入中的每一个都是一个可能缺失、过时或错误的依赖。 The one prior pLM attempt, called "ESM variant," used ESM-1b with no supervised training at all, just the model's own likelihoods. 此前唯一一次 pLM 尝试叫 "ESM variant"，它完全不做有监督训练，只用模型自身的似然值。 VariPred asks a sharper question: what if we keep the sequence-only input but add a small trained classifier on top? VariPred 提出了一个更犀利的问题：如果我们保留纯序列输入，但在上面加一个小的、经过训练的分类器，会怎样？

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually built. 带我过一遍他们到底搭了什么。

ALEX The architecture is a twin network, sometimes called a Siamese network. 这个架构是孪生网络（twin network），有时也叫 Siamese 网络。 You pass two versions of the protein through the same frozen pLM: the wild-type sequence and the mutant sequence. 你把蛋白质的两个版本送进同一个冻结的 pLM：野生型序列和突变型序列。 For each, you pull out the embedding at just the mutated position, then concatenate the wild-type and mutant embeddings into one feature vector. 对每一个，你只取出突变位置上的嵌入向量，然后把野生型和突变型的嵌入拼接成一个特征向量。 Each residue embedding is 1280 numbers, so the concatenation is 2560. 每个残基嵌入是 1280 维，所以拼接后是 2560 维。 On top of that they train a deliberately shallow feed-forward network, one hidden layer of 1280 nodes, dropout 0.5, and a softmax over benign versus pathogenic. 在其上，他们训练了一个刻意保持浅层的前馈网络：一个 1280 节点的隐藏层，dropout 为 0.5，最后是良性与致病的 softmax。 They also compute the log-likelihood ratio, the LLR, which is the model's likelihood of the mutant amino acid versus the wild-type at that position. 他们还计算对数似然比（log-likelihood ratio, 简称 LLR），即模型在该位置上突变氨基酸相对于野生型氨基酸的似然之比。 A strongly negative LLR means the model finds the substituted amino acid very unlikely, which is a red flag. LLR 强烈为负，意味着模型认为被替换的氨基酸非常不可能出现，这是一个危险信号。 The best VariPred appends that LLR as one extra feature to the embeddings, giving 2561 inputs. 最优的 VariPred 把这个 LLR 作为一个额外特征附加到嵌入上，共 2561 维输入。 One design choice I'd push on: they froze the pLM and never fine-tuned it. 有一个设计选择我想追问：他们冻结了 pLM，从未对它做微调。 That keeps training cheap and avoids overfitting a giant model on a small labeled set, but it also means the embeddings are generic, not pathogenicity-aware. 这让训练成本低，也避免了在小标注集上过拟合一个巨大模型，但也意味着嵌入是通用的，而非面向致病性的。 They tested ESM-1b, ESM-1v, and ESM-2, and interestingly the older, smaller ESM-1b won. 他们测试了 ESM-1b、ESM-1v 和 ESM-2，有趣的是，更老、更小的 ESM-1b 胜出了。 They speculate ESM-1b's training data aligns better with the human-only proteins in ClinVar. 他们推测 ESM-1b 的训练数据与 ClinVar 里只含人类蛋白质的集合更吻合。

JORDAN One thing I want to understand about the architecture: why only the mutated position? 关于架构，我想弄清一件事：为什么只取突变位置？ The mutation might affect residues far away in the folded protein. 突变可能影响折叠蛋白中很远的残基。

ALEX That's the sharpest question about the design, and the answer rests on the transformer's self-attention. 这是关于该设计最犀利的问题，答案落在 transformer 的自注意力机制上。 Because every residue's embedding already incorporates context from the whole 1022-residue window, the embedding at the mutated position is not just about that one spot — it has already absorbed information from the rest of the sequence. 由于每个残基的嵌入已经吸收了整个 1022 残基窗口的上下文，突变位置上的嵌入不只关乎那一个点——它已经纳入了序列其余部分的信息。 So taking the mutated-position embedding gives you a summary that's both mutation-specific and globally informed. 因此取突变位置的嵌入，给你的是一个既针对突变、又具备全局信息的摘要。 That's the elegant part: you focus the classifier on the one position that changed, without throwing away long-range context. 精妙之处正在于此：你把分类器聚焦在那个改变了的位置，同时又不丢弃长程上下文。 The wildtype-versus-mutant concatenation then lets the head see the before-and-after at exactly that position. 然后野生型与突变型的拼接，让头部恰好在那个位置看到"改变前"与"改变后"。 It's a clean way to encode "what specifically changed here, in the context of the whole protein." 这是一种干净的方式来编码"在整个蛋白质的背景下，这里具体改变了什么"。

JORDAN And the numbers? 那数字呢？ Did sequence-only actually beat the tools that use structure and alignments? 纯序列真的打败了那些用结构和比对的工具吗？

ALEX On the ClinVar test set, VariPred scored a Matthews correlation coefficient of 0.714. 在 ClinVar 测试集上，VariPred 的马修斯相关系数（MCC）为 0.714。 The closest competitor, 3Cnet, hit 0.673, then ESM variant at 0.649, then the ensembles REVEL at 0.537 and MetaLR at 0.513, PolyPhen-2 at 0.521, and FATHMM trailing at 0.379. 最接近的竞争者 3Cnet 为 0.673，然后是 ESM variant 的 0.649，接着是集成方法 REVEL 的 0.537 和 MetaLR 的 0.513，PolyPhen-2 是 0.521，FATHMM 垫底为 0.379。 They note a subtlety: 3Cnet had a higher AUC-ROC but a lower MCC, which they attribute to threshold and class-imbalance effects, and they lean on MCC because it evaluates all four confusion-matrix quadrants at the actual operating threshold. 他们指出一个微妙之处：3Cnet 的 AUC-ROC 更高但 MCC 更低，他们把这归因于阈值和类别不平衡效应，并倾向于用 MCC，因为它在实际工作阈值上评估混淆矩阵的全部四个象限。 The result I care about most is the data-circularity test. 我最在意的结果是数据循环性测试。 There's a known trap called Type 2 data circularity: if the same gene appears in train and test, and that gene's variants are almost all one class, a model can score well just by memorizing the gene label and ignoring the specific variant. 有一个已知的陷阱叫二型数据循环性（Type 2 data circularity）：如果同一个基因同时出现在训练集和测试集里，而这个基因的变异几乎全属于一个类别，模型只要记住基因标签、忽略具体变异，就能拿到好分数。 The SwissvarFilteredMix and VaribenchSelectedPure benchmarks are designed to expose that. SwissvarFilteredMix 和 VaribenchSelectedPure 这两个基准就是为了暴露这一点而设计的。 The traditional tools FATHMM and MetaLR did better on the "pure" set than the "mix" set, the signature of circularity bias. 传统工具 FATHMM 和 MetaLR 在 "pure" 集上比在 "mix" 集上表现更好，这正是循环性偏差的特征。 VariPred did the opposite, scoring highest on SwissvarFilteredMix at 0.466, meaning it's actually reading the variant, not the gene. VariPred 则相反，在 SwissvarFilteredMix 上得分最高，为 0.466，说明它真的在读变异，而不是在读基因。 And when they enforced a hard no-homology split, under 30 percent sequence identity between train and test, MCC only dropped from 0.75 to 0.65. 而当他们施加严格的无同源性划分——训练集与测试集之间序列一致性低于 30%——MCC 仅从 0.75 降到 0.65。 That's the number that tells you it might generalize to a gene it has never seen. 正是这个数字告诉你，它有可能泛化到一个从未见过的基因。

JORDAN Before we land it, I want to understand the training data, because that's usually where these models hide their sins. 在落地之前，我想弄清楚训练数据，因为这些模型的问题通常就藏在那里。 What did they actually train on? 他们到底是在什么数据上训练的？

ALEX They reused the 3Cnet train/test split so they wouldn't have to retrain the competitor. 他们复用了 3Cnet 的训练/测试划分，这样就不必重新训练竞争对手。 The training data has two real sources: curated ClinVar variants labeled pathogenic or benign, and common gnomAD variants treated as benign because they're frequent in supposedly healthy people. 训练数据有两个真实来源：ClinVar 中经过审编、标为致病或良性的变异，以及被当作良性的常见 gnomAD 变异——因为它们在假定健康的人群中频繁出现。 The final training set is about 72,000 ClinVar variants plus 59,000 gnomAD variants, and only about 17 percent are pathogenic, so it's heavily skewed toward benign. 最终训练集约有 72,000 个 ClinVar 变异加 59,000 个 gnomAD 变异，只有约 17% 是致病的，因此严重偏向良性。 That skew is why their pathogenic threshold is 0.2, not 0.5. 正是这种偏斜使得他们的致病性阈值是 0.2，而不是 0.5。 One thing I'd flag: treating "common in gnomAD" as "benign" is a useful heuristic but not a truth, because a variant can be common and still carry partial-penetrance or recessive risk. 有一点我要指出：把"gnomAD 中常见"当作"良性"是一个有用的启发式，但并非真理，因为一个变异可以既常见又携带不完全外显或隐性风险。 For a recessive disease like CF, where carriers are common and healthy, that assumption is exactly the kind of thing you'd want to scrutinize. 对于像 CF 这样的隐性疾病——携带者常见且健康——这个假设恰恰是你会想仔细审视的那类东西。

JORDAN And they deliberately excluded the simulated data 3Cnet used, right? 而且他们刻意排除了 3Cnet 用的模拟数据，对吧？ Why does that matter? 这为什么重要？

ALEX Yes, and it's a principled choice. 是的，而且这是一个有原则的选择。 3Cnet manufactured artificial pathogenic-like variants by looking at amino-acid frequencies in alignments, essentially synthesizing training examples. 3Cnet 通过查看比对中的氨基酸频率来制造人工的"类致病"变异，本质上是在合成训练样本。 VariPred dropped those and trained only on real, labeled human variants, which makes its performance claim cleaner: no synthetic data propping up the numbers. VariPred 抛弃了这些，只在真实、有标签的人类变异上训练，这让它的性能主张更干净：没有合成数据在撑着数字。 The flip side is that real labeled data is scarcer, so the model leans harder on what the frozen pLM already knows. 另一面是，真实标注数据更稀缺，所以模型更依赖冻结的 pLM 已经掌握的知识。 That's the whole bet of this architecture: the pre-training did the heavy lifting, and the tiny head just reads it out. 这正是这个架构的全部赌注：预训练完成了繁重的工作，而那个微小的头部只是把它读出来。

JORDAN This is where I want to land it on CFTR. 我想把它落到 CFTR 上。 Does this help the variant-interpretation work with Karen? 这对与 Karen 的变异解读工作有帮助吗？
