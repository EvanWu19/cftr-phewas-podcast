Ep3 (Track C, 1 of 2) -- MediSim: Multi-granular simulation for enriching longitudinal, multi-modal electronic health records

Theodorou B, Xiao C, Glass L, Sun J. 2025. Patterns. PMID 40575129 / PMC12191717 / DOI 10.1016/j.patter.2025.101261.

A generative model from Jimeng Sun's lab that fills in missing visits and missing data modalities (codes, notes, imaging) in electronic health records, formalizing three deficiency types -- longitudinal (missing visits), modality (missing data types), and dual (both). MediSim improves missing-code prediction by over 74% versus deficient baselines, is the only method tested that handles both temporal and modality simulation in one model, and enriched training data delivers up to 65% better downstream predictive performance. It never flags anything and doesn't directly touch outlier detection, but its formalization of missingness types is a clean vocabulary for registry gaps, and its generate-then-corrupt logic -- much simplified -- is a legitimate way to build synthetic ground truth for evaluating an outlier detector when no gold standard exists.

Plain-English glossary

  • Dual deficiency -- a record missing both entire visits and entire data modalities at once, realistically the most common real-world case.（中文：一份记录同时缺失整段就诊记录和整个数据模态，这在现实中往往是最常见的情况。）
  • ICD / CPT / NDC -- standard coding systems for diagnoses (ICD), procedures (CPT), and medications (NDC/similar), turning clinical documentation into discrete, analyzable values.（中文：分别用于诊断（ICD）、操作（CPT）和用药（NDC/类似体系）的标准编码系统，把临床文书转化为离散的、可分析的数值。）
  • Longitudinal deficiency -- a record missing entire visits (e.g., a patient switched clinics), as opposed to missing specific data types within present visits.（中文：一份记录缺失整段就诊事件（例如患者换了诊所），区别于在已有的就诊记录内缺失特定数据类型。）
  • MIMIC-III -- a widely used public inpatient ICU dataset (Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, ~46,000 patients) used here alongside proprietary outpatient claims data.（中文：一个被广泛使用的公开住院ICU数据集（贝斯以色列女执事医疗中心，约4.6万名患者），本文将其与专有的门诊理赔数据一起使用。）
  • Modality deficiency -- a record with all visits present but missing one or more data types (e.g., procedures/medications) at some or all of them.（中文：一份记录所有就诊事件都在，但其中一种或多种数据类型（例如操作/用药）在部分或全部就诊中缺失。）
  • Structural missingness -- data absent for reasons unrelated to a patient's health (e.g., care fragmented across non-data-sharing institutions), as opposed to missingness that reflects the patient's actual condition.（中文：数据缺失是由于与患者健康无关的原因（例如医疗过程分散在互不共享数据的多个机构之间），而不是反映患者实际病情的缺失。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Deficiency-type formalization (longitudinal / modality / dual) -- a clean vocabulary for naming what kind of registry gap you're looking at.
  • Multi-granular autoregressive architecture -- one model handling both temporal extension and modality imputation, rather than a separate model per deficiency type.
  • Iterative RL-based training regime -- a reward model scoring generation realism, used to refine the generator especially in low-data settings.
  • Generate-then-corrupt evaluation logic -- the general pattern of using a generative model of clean data to manufacture synthetic ground truth with known injected errors.

ALEX A modality is a distinct type of clinical data -- structured medical codes (diagnoses, procedures, medications), free-text clinical notes, and medical imaging are the three the paper works with. 模态指的是一种独立的临床数据类型——这篇论文用到的三种是：结构化医疗编码（诊断、操作、用药）、自由文本临床记录，以及医学影像。The codes themselves come from standard coding systems: ICD for diagnoses, CPT for procedures, and NDC or similar systems for medications -- each one a controlled vocabulary that turns "the doctor wrote this down" into a discrete, countable value a model or a query can actually work with. 这些编码本身来自标准编码体系：诊断用ICD，操作用CPT，用药用NDC或类似体系——每一种都是一套受控词表，把"医生写下的内容"转化成一个离散的、可计数的值，供模型或查询实际使用。A single patient record is a sequence of visits, and at each visit you'd ideally have all three modalities recorded; MediSim's whole premise is that real records are frequently missing entire visits, entire modalities, or both at once. 一份患者记录是一系列就诊事件的序列，理想情况下每次就诊都应该同时记录这三种模态；而MediSim的整个出发点在于，真实记录里经常整段就诊记录缺失、整个模态缺失，或者两者同时缺失。

JORDAN Why would a modality just be missing, rather than the value simply being zero or normal? 为什么一个模态会直接缺失，而不是这个值本身就是零或者正常？

ALEX Because patients move between healthcare systems that don't share data -- a patient might get imaging at one hospital and follow-up care at an entirely separate clinic with no access to that imaging system, so from the second clinic's database, the imaging modality isn't "normal," it's simply absent, structurally, for reasons that have nothing to do with the patient's actual health. 因为患者会在互不共享数据的多个医疗系统之间流动——一个患者可能在一家医院做了影像检查，然后在另一家完全独立的诊所接受后续治疗，而那家诊所根本无法访问那套影像系统的数据，所以从第二家诊所的数据库来看，影像这个模态不是"正常"，而是彻彻底底地缺失，而这个缺失和患者实际的健康状况毫无关系。That's a structural missingness problem, and it's exactly the kind of gap that also shows up, in a different shape, inside any single registry that depends on clinics or sites reporting inconsistently over time. 这是一种结构性的缺失问题，而这类缺口——只是换了一种表现形式——同样会出现在任何依赖各个诊所或站点随时间不一致上报的单一注册库内部。

ALEX The paper formalizes this into three deficiency types. 论文把这一点形式化为三种缺陷类型。A longitudinally deficient record is missing entire visits -- you have visit one and visit five, but nothing in between, because the patient, say, switched clinics. 纵向缺陷记录（longitudinally deficient record）是指整段就诊记录缺失——你有第一次和第五次就诊的记录，中间什么都没有，因为患者比如说换了诊所。A modality-deficient record has every visit but is missing one or more data types at each of them -- diagnoses might be present throughout, but procedures and medications are patchy or absent. 模态缺陷记录（modality-deficient record）是指每次就诊都有记录，但其中一种或多种数据类型缺失——诊断可能始终都有，但操作和用药记录却断断续续或者根本没有。And a dually deficient record has both problems layered on top of each other, which is, realistically, the most common real-world case. 双重缺陷记录（dually deficient record）则是两种问题叠加在一起，而这实际上是现实世界中最常见的情况。

JORDAN So the task they're solving is: given a record with holes, generate a more complete version that's still plausible. 所以他们要解决的任务是：给定一份有漏洞的记录，生成一个更完整、但仍然合理的版本。

ALEX Right, framed formally as learning a probability distribution over complete records conditioned on the deficient one you actually have -- and the motivating use case is training better downstream machine-learning models, since a model trained only on the small subset of patients with complete, high-quality records tends to generalize poorly to everyone else. 没错，正式的表述是：学习一个以你手头实际拥有的缺陷记录为条件的、关于完整记录的概率分布——而这样做的动机用例，是训练更好的下游机器学习模型，因为如果一个模型只用那一小部分记录完整、质量高的患者数据来训练，它往往在其他患者身上泛化得很差。

ALEX Mechanically, MediSim has three pieces. 从机制上说，MediSim有三个部分。First, a multi-granular autoregressive architecture -- autoregressive meaning it predicts each new visit's codes conditioned on everything that came before, the way a language model predicts the next word -- adapted from the same author's earlier work, extended here to handle both temporal gaps and modality gaps within one model rather than needing a separate model for each. 首先是一个多粒度自回归架构——自回归（autoregressive）指的是它在预测每次新就诊的编码时，会以之前发生的一切为条件，就像语言模型预测下一个词一样——这个架构改编自同一作者此前的工作，这里进一步扩展，让一个模型就能同时处理时间上的空缺和模态上的空缺，而不需要为每种缺陷单独训练一个模型。Second, an iterative training regime that borrows the reinforcement-learning-from-human-feedback pattern: instead of only maximizing likelihood on the training set, a separate classification reward model scores how realistic a generated record looks, and that reward signal is used to keep refining the generator, which specifically helps in the low-data regime where naive likelihood training alone tends to produce degenerate or repetitive outputs. 其次是一种借鉴了"基于人类反馈的强化学习"（RLHF）模式的迭代训练机制：不只是在训练集上最大化似然，还有一个独立的分类奖励模型，给生成记录的逼真程度打分，这个奖励信号被用来持续优化生成器，这在低数据场景下尤其有帮助，因为单纯的似然训练在那种场景下往往会产生退化或者重复的输出。Third, for the complex modalities -- notes and images -- MediSim pairs an encoder that reads the structured codes with a decoder that generates the corresponding note text or chest X-ray, rather than trying to force free text and pixel data through the same autoregressive code-sequence machinery. 第三，对于文本记录和影像这类复杂模态，MediSim用一个编码器读取结构化编码，再配一个解码器来生成对应的记录文本或胸片影像，而不是硬把自由文本和像素数据塞进同一套自回归编码序列的机制里。
