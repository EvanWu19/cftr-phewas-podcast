Blurb

Pullenayegum EM, Birken C, Maguire J, and the TARGet Kids! Collaboration's 2021 Statistical Methods in Medical Research paper closes a specific gap in the informative-observation-process literature: existing corrections — inverse-intensity-weighted GEEs (IIW-GEE) and semi-parametric joint models — already handle the case where sicker patients get seen more often (individual-level informative observation), but not the case where the centres themselves differ systematically in follow-up frequency. The authors formulate a semi-parametric joint model with random effects at both the centre and subject level, and separately show three ways to adapt IIW-GEEs for centre-level clustering: stratification, frailty models, and covariate adjustment. The paper is abstract-only tonight (genuinely closed access, no PMCID), so specific simulation numbers and a ranking among the three approaches aren't available — but the core finding, that centre-level visit-timing clustering needs its own correction layered on top of individual-level IIW, lands directly on the candidate-generation stage of any flagging pipeline built on residuals from an expected trajectory in multi-centre data. Buildable: a centre-stratified IIW-GEE residual model, compared against a naive unweighted single-level model on simulated multi-centre data, to quantify how much site-level visit-timing confounding alone can manufacture spurious flags.

Plain-English glossary

  • Informative observation process — when the timing or frequency of a patient's visits is itself related to their underlying health status (e.g. sicker patients seen more often), so analyzing the data as if visit timing were random introduces bias.（中文：具有信息量的观察过程，指患者就诊的时间或频率本身与其潜在健康状况相关（例如病情更重者被看诊更频繁），因此若将就诊时间当作随机发生来分析数据，会引入偏倚。）
  • Inverse-intensity weighting (IIW) — a correction method that models how often a patient is likely to be observed given their covariates, then weights each observation by the inverse of that modeled visit intensity, so more-frequently-observed patients don't disproportionately dominate the analysis.（中文：逆强度加权（IIW），一种校正方法，先根据协变量为患者被观察的频率建模，再用该强度模型的倒数对每次观察加权，使被观察更频繁的患者不会在分析中占据不成比例的主导地位。）
  • Frailty model — a biostatistics term for adding an unobserved, multiplicative random effect onto a hazard or intensity model to represent unmeasured heterogeneity at a group level (here, centre).（中文：frailty模型，生物统计学术语，指在风险或强度模型中加入一个未观测到的、乘性的随机效应，用以表示群体层面（此处为中心）的未测量异质性。）
  • Stratification vs. frailty vs. covariate adjustment (for clustering) — three distinct ways to account for centre-level clustering in a visit-intensity model: fitting the model separately per centre (stratification), adding a centre-level random multiplicative effect (frailty), or simply including centre membership as a fixed-effect covariate.（中文：分层／frailty／协变量调整（用于处理聚类），三种在就诊强度模型中处理中心层面聚类的不同方式：按中心分别拟合模型（分层）、加入中心层面的随机乘性效应（frailty），或直接把中心归属作为固定效应协变量纳入模型。）
  • TARGet Kids! — a real-world, practice-based paediatric research network embedded in primary care across the Greater Toronto Area, used here as a genuinely multi-centre illustrative dataset.（中文：TARGet Kids!，一个嵌入于大多伦多地区初级诊疗体系的真实世界、基于诊疗实践的儿科研究网络，此处作为一个真正意义上的多中心示例数据集使用。）

ALEX The clustering itself is the familiar idea — patients nested within centres, correlated outcomes within a centre — but what's specific here is that it has to be modeled twice, once for the actual outcome of interest, and once for the visit-intensity process that determines when and how often each patient even gets observed in the first place, and those two layers of clustering don't have to line up or behave the same way. 聚类本身是熟悉的概念——患者嵌套在中心之下，同一中心内的结局相互关联——但这里的特殊之处在于，它需要被建模两次：一次是针对你真正关心的结局本身，另一次是针对决定每位患者何时、以多高频率被观察到的就诊强度过程本身，而这两层聚类未必对齐，也未必以相同的方式表现。
