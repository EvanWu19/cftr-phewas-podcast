Ep1 — Track A (Variant Interpretation) — 2026-08-21

Performance of a Protein Language Model for Variant Annotation in Cardiac Disease

Hochstadt A, Barbhaiya C, Aizer A, et al. (NYU Langone inherited arrhythmia program). Journal of the American Heart Association, 2024.

A real-world clinical validation of AlphaMissense — DeepMind's protein-language-model-based missense pathogenicity predictor — accessed via VarCard.io, a free, gene-agnostic web tool the same team built. The study ran 339 missense variants from 266 patients in an inherited-arrhythmia and cardiomyopathy program (genes including SCN5A, KCNQ1, KCNH2, RyR2) through VarCard.io and compared its calls against ClinVar, a competing commercial tool (Franklin), and phenotype data assessed blind to the tool's output. VarCard.io reclassified 86.1% of ClinVar-labeled VUS variants to likely pathogenic/benign — more than double Franklin's 34.8% — with 90.5% agreement against variants ClinVar had already classified, 95.9% genotype-phenotype concordance, and a clean functional cross-check on KCNH2 channel-current data. It lands as a Karen Raraigh / CFTR-variant-interpretation template: VarCard.io is public and gene-agnostic, so the exact same reclassification-audit design can be run directly against CFTR2's curated VUS list with no structural-biology pipeline required.

Plain-English glossary

  • ACMG/AMP — the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics / Association for Molecular Pathology consensus guidelines for classifying variant pathogenicity.（中文：美国医学遗传学与基因组学学院与分子病理学会联合制定的变异致病性分类共识指南。）
  • ClinGen — a National Institutes of Health resource that curates and scores how strongly a given gene is actually linked to a given disease (gene-disease validity), used here to define which genes are compatible with which cardiac phenotype.（中文：美国国立卫生研究院提供的资源，评估某基因与某疾病关联证据的强度，本文用它判定基因与心脏表型是否相符。）
  • concordance (genotype-phenotype) — whether a predicted pathogenicity call for a variant actually lines up with whether the patient carrying it shows the matching clinical phenotype.（中文：预测的变异致病性判断，是否与携带者的实际临床表型相符。）
  • HERG channel (KCNH2) — the cardiac potassium channel encoded by KCNH2, used here as an independent functional cross-check via measured channel current relative to wild-type.（中文：由 KCNH2 编码的心脏钾通道，本文用其相对于野生型的实测通道电流作为独立功能学验证。）
  • reclassification rate — the fraction of ClinVar-labeled VUS variants that a tool is willing to call likely pathogenic or likely benign instead of leaving uncertain.（中文：某工具愿意将 ClinVar 标记为 VUS 的变异改判为"可能致病"或"可能良性"、而非维持不确定的比例。）
  • VUS (Variant of Uncertain Significance) — a variant for which the evidence is insufficient to classify as pathogenic or benign under ACMG/AMP rules.（中文：意义不明的变异，现有证据不足以按 ACMG/AMP 规则判定为致病或良性。）

Methods/tools to lift

  • VarCard.io — free, nonprofit, gene-agnostic web engine that queries AlphaMissense by gene name or HGVS variant notation and returns likely-benign/likely-pathogenic/ambiguous calls plus ClinGen gene-disease correlation.
  • ClinGen framework — NIH-curated gene-disease validity scoring, used to define phenotype-compatible genes.
  • Franklin (Genoox) — a second, independent commercial variant-classification engine used as a comparison benchmark.

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen Raraigh / CFTR variant interpretation and harmonization.

Buildable: cftr-varcardio-reclassification-audit (S) — pull CFTR2's curated VUS list, query VarCard.io for each by HGVS notation, and compute the same reclassification-rate and concordance-style comparison this paper ran, but against CFTR2 expert-panel classifications and, where available, sweat-chloride or organoid theratyping phenotype data. Input: CFTR2 VUS list + available phenotype fields per variant. Difficulty S — web queries plus a comparison script, no structural-biology compute pipeline required.

What to verify first

  • Whether AlphaMissense's genome-wide, extreme-frequency-based fine-tuning set carries any CFTR-specific blind spots not visible from its performance on cardiac ion channels.
  • Whether this paper's genotype-phenotype concordance framing (built around dominant-disease, variable-penetrance genes) maps cleanly onto CF's recessive, two-allele disease mechanism.
  • Whether a CFTR-specific benchmark would need its own independent functional cross-check (comparable to KCNH2's channel-current data here), and what CFTR functional assay data might be accessible for that purpose.

ALEX Today we're reading a 2024 paper from the Journal of the American Heart Association: "Performance of a Protein Language Model for Variant Annotation in Cardiac Disease," by Hochstadt and colleagues out of NYU Langone's inherited arrhythmia program. 今天我们要读一篇 2024 年发表在《美国心脏协会杂志》（Journal of the American Heart Association）上的论文：《蛋白质语言模型在心脏病变异注释中的表现》，作者是 Hochstadt 及其同事，来自纽约大学朗格尼医学中心的遗传性心律失常项目组。 It's a real-world clinical validation study of AlphaMissense, accessed through a free web tool called VarCard.io, tested against 339 missense variants from 266 patients with heritable arrhythmia and cardiomyopathy syndromes. 这是一项对 AlphaMissense 的真实世界临床验证研究，通过一个名为 VarCard.io 的免费网络工具访问，在 266 名遗传性心律失常和心肌病患者的 339 个错义变异上进行了检验。 We're covering it on Track A, variant interpretation, because it's not a new predictor — it's a test of how well an existing one performs when a clinical genetics program actually puts it to work, and that's exactly the kind of study we'd want replicated for CFTR. 我们把它放进 A 赛道——变异解读——是因为它不是一个新的预测工具，而是在检验一个已有工具在临床遗传学项目实际使用时的表现，这正是我们希望在 CFTR 上复现的那类研究。

JORDAN Let's set up the vocabulary before we go further. 在深入之前，先把相关术语讲清楚。 AlphaMissense I know — DeepMind's protein-language-model-based pathogenicity predictor. AlphaMissense 我知道——DeepMind 基于蛋白质语言模型的致病性预测工具。 But what's a "reclassification rate," and what is genotype-phenotype concordance measuring exactly? 但"重新分类率"是什么意思，"基因型-表型一致性"具体又是在衡量什么？

ALEX Good starting point. 这是个很好的切入点。 A reclassification rate here is simple: take every variant that ClinVar currently calls a VUS, a variant of uncertain significance under ACMG/AMP guidelines — the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and Association for Molecular Pathology's consensus rules for calling a variant pathogenic or benign — and ask what fraction AlphaMissense, via VarCard.io, is willing to call likely pathogenic or likely benign instead. 这里的重新分类率很简单：取出每一个目前被 ClinVar 判定为 VUS（意义不明变异）的变异——按照 ACMG/AMP 指南，即美国医学遗传学与基因组学学院与分子病理学会联合制定的、用于判定变异致病或良性的共识规则——然后看看 AlphaMissense 通过 VarCard.io 愿意把其中多大比例改判为"可能致病"或"可能良性"。 Genotype-phenotype concordance is a second, independent check: does the predicted pathogenicity call actually line up with what's happening in the patient? 基因型-表型一致性则是第二项独立的检验：预测出的致病性判断，是否真的与患者实际情况相符？ The authors used the ClinGen framework — a National Institutes of Health resource that curates how strongly a gene is actually linked to a given disease — to define which genes are compatible with which cardiac phenotype, and then checked whether a patient carrying a pathogenic-or-likely-pathogenic call in a phenotype-compatible gene actually had that clinical phenotype, and vice versa for benign calls in phenotype-incompatible genes. 作者们使用了 ClinGen 框架——美国国立卫生研究院（NIH）提供的一个资源，用来评估某个基因与某种疾病之间关联的证据强度——来判定哪些基因与哪种心脏表型相符，然后检验携带"致病"或"可能致病"判断、且位于表型相符基因上的患者，是否真的表现出该临床表型，反之，位于表型不符基因上的"良性"判断是否也成立。 One more piece of context: this cohort mostly has long QT syndrome, cardiomyopathy, and other arrhythmia disorders, driven by genes like SCN5A, KCNQ1, KCNH2, and RyR2 — cardiac ion-channel and calcium-handling genes, not CF-relevant, but structurally the same interpretation problem CFTR faces. 还有一点背景：这个队列的患者主要患有长 QT 综合征、心肌病和其他心律失常疾病，涉及 SCN5A、KCNQ1、KCNH2、RyR2 等基因——这些是心脏离子通道和钙处理相关基因，与 CF 无关，但结构上面临的是与 CFTR 完全相同的解读难题。

JORDAN So why did this validation study need doing — what was the actual problem before this? 那这项验证研究为什么需要做——之前存在的具体问题是什么？

ALEX The core problem is that VUS rates in cardiac genetic testing are genuinely high — the paper cites up to 40% of positive genetic tests in cardiac disease genes landing as VUS — and that's not a paperwork inconvenience, it directly blocks clinical decisions: risk stratification, choice of therapy, preventive interventions like implantable defibrillators in some inherited arrhythmia syndromes, cascade family screening, and it has a real anxiety cost for patients living with an unresolved result. 核心问题是心脏基因检测中的 VUS 比例确实很高——论文引用的数据显示，心脏病相关基因的阳性基因检测结果中，最高可达 40% 落入 VUS——这不是文书上的麻烦，而是直接阻碍临床决策：风险分层、治疗方案选择、某些遗传性心律失常综合征中植入式除颤器等预防性干预的决定、家族级联筛查，而且对带着一个悬而未决结果生活的患者来说，也有真实的焦虑成本。 As gene panels keep expanding to cover more genes, the raw count of VUS keeps climbing right along with them. 随着基因检测面板不断扩大、覆盖更多基因，VUS 的绝对数量也随之不断攀升。 AlphaMissense itself is architecturally different from older predictors like SIFT or REVEL, which lean heavily on sequence homology across species and population-frequency data — approaches that carry real biases, since some rare variants really are pathogenic, and many cardiac conditions show age-dependent penetrance that a pure frequency-based model can't capture well. AlphaMissense 在架构上与 SIFT 或 REVEL 等较早的预测工具有本质不同，后者严重依赖跨物种序列同源性和群体频率数据——这类方法带有真实的偏差，因为一些罕见变异确实是致病的，而许多心脏疾病表现出随年龄增长外显率上升的特征，纯频率型模型很难很好地捕捉这一点。 AlphaMissense instead is trained with a self-supervised, language-model architecture: it learns the fundamental compatibility rules of amino acids in context by masking and predicting them across the whole known proteome, then fine-tunes on variants labeled by extreme population frequency — either very common or essentially absent in humans and closely related primates. AlphaMissense 则采用自监督的语言模型架构进行训练：它通过在整个已知蛋白质组范围内对氨基酸进行遮蔽和预测，学习氨基酸在上下文中的基本兼容规律，然后基于按极端群体频率标记的变异——即在人类及近缘灵长类中要么非常常见、要么几乎不存在的变异——进行微调。 That architecture had already shown strong performance genome-wide — reclassifying 88.8% of 69.5 million gnomAD variants — but nobody had checked what happens when you point it at one specific clinical population with real, adjudicated phenotypes attached. 这种架构此前已经在全基因组层面展现出强劲表现——对 gnomAD 中 6950 万个变异，重新分类了其中 88.8%——但此前没有人检验过，把它用在一个拥有真实、经过判定的表型的特定临床人群上会发生什么。 That's the gap VarCard.io and this study fill: is the accuracy real in the hands of an actual cardiac genetics clinic, not just against population databases? VarCard.io 和这项研究正是填补了这个空白：在真正的心脏遗传学门诊手中，这种准确性是否真实存在，而不仅仅体现在群体数据库层面？

JORDAN Walk me through what they actually did. 带我具体看看他们实际做了什么。

ALEX They pulled every missense variant found in patients presenting to NYU's inherited arrhythmia program, in genes with an established cardiac-disease link per ClinGen — 623 probands reviewed, 266 met inclusion, carrying 339 distinct variants. 他们提取了所有出现在纽约大学遗传性心律失常项目患者身上的错义变异，且这些变异所在基因经 ClinGen 确认与心脏病存在明确关联——共审查了 623 名先证者，266 人符合纳入标准，携带 339 个不同的变异。 Each variant was run through VarCard.io, the team's own free, nonprofit web engine, which queries AlphaMissense by gene name or cDNA/amino-acid change and returns a call of likely benign, likely pathogenic, or ambiguous, plus the ClinGen gene-disease correlation for that gene. 每个变异都通过 VarCard.io 进行查询——这是该团队自己开发的免费非营利网络引擎，可以按基因名称或 cDNA/氨基酸改变查询 AlphaMissense，返回"可能良性"、"可能致病"或"不确定"的判断，并附上该基因的 ClinGen 基因-疾病关联评级。 They compared VarCard.io's calls against two references: the existing ClinVar entry for each variant, and a second, independent commercial classification engine called Franklin, from Genoox. 他们把 VarCard.io 的判断结果与两个参照进行比较：每个变异现有的 ClinVar 条目，以及另一个独立的商业分类引擎——来自 Genoox 公司的 Franklin。 Clinical phenotype was assessed separately and blinded to VarCard.io's output — two physicians trained in cardiogenetics plus the senior author reviewed each patient's full clinical picture, including ECG tracings, provocative testing, and imaging, reaching phenotype calls by consensus before ever seeing VarCard.io's results — which is a meaningful design choice against circularity. 临床表型是独立评估的，且对 VarCard.io 的输出结果保持盲态——两名接受过心脏遗传学训练的医生加上通讯作者，审查了每位患者的完整临床情况，包括心电图记录、激发试验和影像学检查，在看到 VarCard.io 的结果之前，通过共识得出表型判断——这是一个针对循环论证风险的有意义的设计选择。 For one gene, KCNH2, which encodes the well-studied HERG potassium channel, they went further and compared VarCard.io's calls against previously published electrophysiology data — actual measured channel current relative to wild-type — as an independent functional check. 对于 KCNH2 这个编码研究充分的 HERG 钾通道的基因，他们更进一步，把 VarCard.io 的判断结果与已发表的电生理数据——相对于野生型的实测通道电流——进行比较，作为一项独立的功能学检验。

JORDAN Where would you push back on the design? 你会在设计上提出哪些质疑？

ALEX Two places. 两个地方。 First, VarCard.io was built by this same team — the senior author leads it — so this isn't a fully independent third-party validation; there's some risk that a tool's own developers are more fluent in interpreting its output favorably than an outside user would be. 第一，VarCard.io 是这个团队自己开发的——通讯作者正是负责人——所以这并不是一次完全独立的第三方验证；工具的开发者本身在解读输出结果时可能存在偏向有利方向的风险，而外部用户未必如此。 Second, the genotype-phenotype concordance metric leans on ClinGen gene-disease validity calls, which themselves get revised over time and aren't independent of the broader literature AlphaMissense's fine-tuning set draws from — so "concordance" here isn't quite a fully external ground truth, it's checking internal consistency across several related but not fully independent classification systems. 第二，基因型-表型一致性这个指标依赖 ClinGen 的基因-疾病有效性判定，而这些判定本身会随时间修订，也并非完全独立于 AlphaMissense 微调数据集所依据的更广泛文献——所以这里的"一致性"并不是完全外部的真实标准，而更像是在检验几个相关但并非完全独立的分类系统之间的内部一致性。 Worth flagging both, but neither invalidates the finding — they just mean "highly efficient tool" in the conclusion should be read as promising, not proven beyond doubt. 这两点都值得指出，但都不会推翻研究结论——只是意味着结论中的"高效工具"这个说法，应被理解为"有前景"，而不是"已被无可置疑地证实"。

JORDAN So what did they actually find? 那他们究竟发现了什么？

ALEX Of the 339 variants, 328 had ClinVar entries, and 230 of those, or 67.8%, were VUS or had no clinically significant classification. 在 339 个变异中，328 个已有 ClinVar 条目，其中 230 个，即 67.8%，属于 VUS 或没有明确的临床分类。 VarCard.io reclassified 198 of those 230 VUS variants as either likely pathogenic or likely benign — a reclassification rate of 86.1%, with 95% confidence interval 80.9 to 90.3 — split into 111 reclassified to likely benign and 87 to likely pathogenic; only 32 variants, 13.9%, stayed ambiguous. VarCard.io 把这 230 个 VUS 变异中的 198 个重新分类为"可能致病"或"可能良性"——重新分类率为 86.1%，95% 置信区间为 80.9 到 90.3——其中 111 个被改判为"可能良性"，87 个改判为"可能致病"；只有 32 个变异（13.9%）仍保持"不确定"。 That reclassification rate was dramatically higher than Franklin's, which only reclassified 80 of the same 230 variants, 34.8%, a statistically significant gap, P less than 0.001. 这个重新分类率远高于 Franklin，后者在同样的 230 个变异中只重新分类了 80 个，即 34.8%，这一差距具有统计学意义，P 值小于 0.001。 For the 109 variants that already had a clinically significant ClinVar call — pathogenic, likely pathogenic, benign, or likely benign, no VUS entries — VarCard.io agreed with ClinVar 90.5% of the time, similarly high whether the ClinVar call was pathogenic-side or benign-side. 对于已有明确临床分类的 109 个变异——致病、可能致病、良性或可能良性，不含 VUS 条目——VarCard.io 与 ClinVar 的一致率达到 90.5%，无论 ClinVar 的判断是致病方向还是良性方向，一致率都同样高。 Genotype-phenotype concordance across 269 variants with both a clear VarCard.io call and a defined patient phenotype came in at 95.9%; for the much smaller subset with a clear ClinVar call and defined phenotype, concordance was lower, 88.4%, hinting that AlphaMissense-based calls may track real clinical phenotype at least as well as, and here numerically better than, existing ClinVar annotations in this cohort. 在 269 个既有明确 VarCard.io 判断、又有明确患者表型的变异中，基因型-表型一致率为 95.9%；而在数量小得多、拥有明确 ClinVar 判断和明确表型的子集中，一致率较低，为 88.4%，这提示基于 AlphaMissense 的判断，其与真实临床表型的吻合程度，在这个队列中至少不逊于、而且从数字上看还略优于现有的 ClinVar 注释。 On the KCNH2 functional check, all 8 variants VarCard.io called likely pathogenic showed less than 50% of wild-type channel current, and 2 of 3 variants it called likely benign had normal current, with the third borderline at 48% — a small but genuinely independent functional cross-check that lined up. 在 KCNH2 功能学检验中，所有 8 个被 VarCard.io 判断为"可能致病"的变异，其通道电流均低于野生型的 50%；被判断为"可能良性"的 3 个变异中，2 个通道电流正常，第三个处于边缘状态，为 48%——这是一项规模虽小但确实独立的功能学交叉验证，结果与预测相符。

JORDAN So where does this land for what we're trying to do? 那这对我们正在做的事情有什么落地意义？
