Ep4 — Unsupervised clustering of longitudinal clinical measurements (pediatric BMI trajectories)

Mariam et al. (PLOS Digital Health, 2024) systematically benchmark 30 unsupervised clustering algorithms (temporal-matching distance measures × partitional/fuzzy clustering methods) across 6,912 simulated clinical datasets with known ground truth, varying trajectory magnitude vs. shape differences and missingness levels. Dynamic time warping (DTW) and its lower-bound variants with PAM centroids won overall (median ARI >0.70); all algorithms did better distinguishing magnitude than shape, and no algorithm handled shape-based clustering well above 10% missingness. Applied to a real pediatric metabolic-syndrome cohort (N=43,426, Cleveland Clinic) with BMI curated via growthcleanr — the same anchor tool already in our qc catalog — the winning algorithm identified 3 BMI trajectory clusters, one carrying OR=4.87 for metabolic syndrome. The standout contribution is methodological: a simulation-based validation framework (known-ground-truth synthetic data → measure recovery accuracy) directly transferable as an evaluation template for any candidate-generation method in this track.

Plain-English glossary

  • Trajectory clustering — Grouping patients by the shape of how a measurement changes over their full follow-up period, not just its value at one point in time.（中文：根据某项测量值在整个随访期内变化的形态、而非单一时间点的数值来给患者分组。）
  • DTW (dynamic time warping) — An algorithm for comparing two time-varying signals by finding the best alignment even if they're stretched or shifted in time; originated in signal processing.（中文：一种比较两个随时间变化信号的算法，即使信号在时间上被拉伸或偏移，也能找到最优对齐方式；起源于信号处理领域。）
  • PAM centroids — A partitioning method (Partitioning Around Medoids) that picks actual data points as cluster centers, rather than an averaged synthetic point.（中文：一种划分方法（围绕中心点划分），选择实际数据点作为簇中心，而不是一个平均合成的点。）
  • ARI (Adjusted Rand Index) — A metric for how well a clustering result recovers a known true grouping; not re-explained on air (statistics fluency assumed).（中文：衡量聚类结果还原已知真实分组好坏程度的指标；因听众已熟悉统计学，节目中未重新讲解。）
  • growthcleanr — An R package for curating pediatric growth (height/weight/BMI) measurements against reference growth charts; the anchor tool in this podcast's qc methods catalog.（中文：一个用于依照参考生长曲线整理儿科生长（身高/体重/BMI）测量值的R软件包；是本播客质量控制方法目录中的锚点工具。）

JORDAN Let's get the domain layer sorted first. 我们先把领域知识层理清楚。 What's the difference between clustering a single measurement and clustering a trajectory? 对单个测量值聚类和对一条轨迹聚类，区别在哪里？

ALEX Clustering a single measurement groups patients by where one number sits at one point in time — say, everyone's BMI at age ten. 对单个测量值聚类，是按照某个数字在某一时间点上的取值来给患者分组——比如所有人十岁时的BMI。 Clustering a trajectory groups patients by the shape of how that number changes over their whole followup period — not just where it ends up, but how it got there: rising early and plateauing, staying flat, rising late, and so on. 对轨迹聚类，则是按照这个数字在整个随访期内变化的形态来给患者分组——不只是它最终落在哪里，而是它是怎么走到那里的：早期上升后趋于平稳、始终持平、后期上升，等等。 That distinction matters a lot for growth data specifically, because two children can have the exact same BMI at eighteen but got there via completely different trajectories, and the trajectory itself can carry clinical information the endpoint alone doesn't. 这个区别对生长发育数据尤其重要，因为两个孩子在十八岁时可能有完全相同的BMI，但达到这个数值的路径可能完全不同，而轨迹本身可能携带着单看终点数值无法体现的临床信息。 The algorithms used for this kind of task were originally developed for signal processing and protein-sequence alignment — comparing two time-varying signals or two sequences and finding the best way to align them even if they're stretched or shifted in time. 用于这类任务的算法最初是为信号处理和蛋白质序列比对开发的——比较两个随时间变化的信号或两条序列，找到即使它们在时间上被拉伸或偏移，也能最优对齐的方式。

JORDAN So what was actually uncertain going into this study? 那么这项研究开始时，实际上有哪些不确定的地方？

ALEX Those temporal-matching algorithms were designed for data that's fairly regular — evenly sampled signals, complete sequences. 那些时序匹配算法最初是为相对规整的数据设计的——均匀采样的信号、完整的序列。 Clinical measurements from electronic health records are the opposite: patients come in for irregular, unscheduled visits, measurements are missing for stretches of time, and the sampling density itself can correlate with how sick someone is. 而来自电子健康记录的临床测量值恰恰相反：患者的就诊是不规律、非预约的，测量值会在一段时间内缺失，而采样密度本身可能与一个人的病情严重程度相关。 Nobody had systematically tested whether these repurposed algorithms actually hold up under that kind of irregularity and missingness, or whether they'd been validated on data that's cleaner than what a real registry or EHR ever looks like. 此前没有人系统地检验过，这些被挪用的算法在这种不规律性和缺失情况下是否真的还站得住脚，还是说它们此前的验证一直是在比任何真实注册库或电子健康记录都要干净得多的数据上进行的。 That's the gap this paper set out to close — a systematic evaluation, not just another application paper. 这正是这篇论文想要弥补的空白——一次系统性的评估，而不只是又一篇应用型论文。

JORDAN How did they actually test that? 他们具体是怎么测试的？

ALEX This is the part I think is most worth stealing, independent of the clustering results themselves. 我认为这部分是最值得借鉴的，甚至独立于聚类结果本身。 They generated 6,912 simulated clinical datasets systematically varying five parameters, including differences in trajectory magnitude versus differences in trajectory shape, and different levels of missingness. 他们系统性地生成了6,912个模拟临床数据集，系统性地改变五个参数，包括轨迹幅度差异与轨迹形状差异的对比，以及不同的缺失程度。 Because the datasets are simulated, the "true" cluster membership is known by construction, so they could measure each algorithm's accuracy directly using the Adjusted Rand Index, or ARI, comparing 30 different unsupervised algorithms — eight temporal-matching distance measures like dynamic time warping and its lower-bound variants, crossed with fourteen partitional and eight fuzzy clustering methods — and ranking them with Nemenyi tests corrected for multiple comparisons. 因为数据集是模拟的，"真实"的簇归属在构造时就是已知的，所以他们可以直接用调整兰德指数（ARI）来衡量每种算法的准确率，比较了30种不同的无监督算法——包括动态时间规整及其下界变体在内的8种时序匹配距离度量，与14种划分式聚类方法和8种模糊聚类方法两两组合——并用经过多重比较校正的Nemenyi检验对它们排序。 That's a validation-by-simulation design: build synthetic data with known ground truth baked in by construction, then measure whether your method recovers that known truth, rather than needing real labeled data you don't have. 这是一种"模拟验证"设计：构建在设计时就已知真实答案的合成数据，然后测量你的方法能否还原这个已知真相，而不需要你手头并不存在的真实标注数据。
