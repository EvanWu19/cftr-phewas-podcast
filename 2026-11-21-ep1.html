Plain-English glossary

  • VUS (variant of uncertain significance) — A genetic variant for which there isn't yet enough evidence to classify it as disease-causing or harmless.（中文：意义未明变异，指目前证据不足以判断是致病还是无害的基因变异。）
  • ACMG/AMP guidelines — The evidence-category framework clinical labs use to classify variants; PS3/BS3 are its strong-pathogenic/strong-benign criteria specifically from functional-assay evidence.（中文：临床实验室用于变异分类的证据分级指南；PS3/BS3分别是来自功能实验的强致病/强良性证据等级。）
  • eQTL (expression quantitative trait locus) — A genomic location where a variant measurably shifts a nearby gene's expression level.（中文：表达数量性状位点，指基因组中某变异会可测量地改变邻近基因表达水平的位置。）
  • mQTL (methylation quantitative trait locus) — The same concept as an eQTL, but for DNA methylation level instead of expression.（中文：甲基化数量性状位点，与eQTL概念相同，但对应的是DNA甲基化水平而非表达水平。）
  • ATAC-seq — A sequencing assay measuring chromatin accessibility: how open or closed a stretch of DNA is to the cell's transcription machinery.（中文：一种检测染色质可及性的测序技术，衡量DNA某段区域对转录机器的开放程度。）
  • Exon skipping — An aberrant splicing event where a whole exon is dropped from the final mRNA.（中文：外显子跳跃，一种异常剪接事件，整段外显子被从最终mRNA中剔除。）
  • Intron retention — An aberrant splicing event where an intron that should have been removed is kept in the final mRNA.（中文：内含子滞留，本应被剔除的内含子片段保留在了最终mRNA中。）
  • Cryptic splice site — A new, unintended splicing cut point created by a variant, not present in the normal sequence.（中文：隐秘剪接位点，由变异凭空产生的、正常序列中不存在的意外剪切点。）
  • ASE (allele-specific expression) — Unequal expression between a gene's two parental copies; a skewed ratio can signal nonsense-mediated decay.（中文：等位基因特异性表达，指基因两个亲本拷贝表达量不均衡；比例失衡可能提示无义介导的mRNA降解。）
  • NMD (nonsense-mediated decay) — A cellular quality-control mechanism that destroys an mRNA carrying a premature stop signal before it is translated into protein.（中文：无义介导的mRNA降解，细胞的一种质量控制机制，会在带有提前终止信号的mRNA被翻译成蛋白质之前将其降解。）

Methods / tools to lift

  • Integration-strategy taxonomy (early/intermediate/late/network/hybrid) — a decision framework for choosing how to fuse heterogeneous omics layers before modeling.
  • SpliceAI — a 32-layer dilated residual CNN that predicts splice-site usage and splice-impact scores from sequence.
  • AlphaMissense — an AlphaFold-derived model producing missense-variant pathogenicity scores from protein sequence plus structural/evolutionary context.
  • MOFA+ (Multi-Omics Factor Analysis) — a Bayesian factor-analysis framework for unsupervised discovery of shared latent factors across omics modalities.
  • Variational autoencoders (OmiVAE, scMDC) — intermediate-integration architectures that compress multi-omics matrices into a shared latent space for subtype/cluster discovery.
  • Network/graph integrators (MOGONET, netDx) — GCN- and patient-similarity-network-based tools for biomarker sub-network discovery and outcome prediction.
