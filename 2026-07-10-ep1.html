SHINE — protein language model pathogenicity prediction for in-frame indels (Track A, ep1, 2026-07-10)

Blurb. Fan et al. (2023, Briefings in Bioinformatics) built SHINE, a pathogenicity predictor for short in-frame insertion and deletion variants — the one variant class that mainstream missense predictors (AlphaMissense, REVEL, etc.) skip, because indels change protein length. SHINE runs the variant plus its protein context through pretrained protein language models, combining a single-sequence embedding with a multiple-sequence-alignment (evolutionary) view, then feeds that latent representation to a supervised classifier trained on ClinVar (pathogenic) and gnomAD (presumed-benign) labels. On two independent test sets it reported better performance than existing methods for both deletions and insertions. Where it lands: the Karen / CFTR variant-interpretation collaboration — CFTR's most common variant, F508del, is itself a 3-bp in-frame deletion, and the gene carries a tail of rarer in-frame indels no missense tool scores. Buildable artifact: a CFTR in-frame-indel pathogenicity annotator notebook. Note: the OA full text was not retrievable via BioC or Europe PMC fullTextXML this morning; the episode is built from the detailed abstract and flags that no benchmark numbers are quoted.

Plain-English glossary

  • Codon — a three-nucleotide DNA "word" that specifies one amino acid.（中文：三个核苷酸组成的 DNA“词”，指定一个氨基酸。）
  • Conservation — how unchanged a sequence position has stayed across related species; strong conservation implies functional importance.（中文：某位置在相关物种间保持不变的程度，高保守性通常意味着功能重要。）
  • In-frame indel — an insertion/deletion of a multiple of three nucleotides, so the reading frame is preserved and only one or a few amino acids are added/removed (e.g. F508del).（中文：碱基数为三的倍数的插入或缺失，阅读框不变，只增删一个到几个氨基酸，如 F508del。）
  • Frameshift — an insertion/deletion NOT a multiple of three, scrambling every downstream codon, usually causing a premature stop.（中文：碱基数不是三的倍数的插入或缺失，打乱下游所有密码子，通常产生提前终止。）
  • Multiple sequence alignment (MSA) — homologous protein sequences stacked and aligned position-by-position to reveal per-position conservation.（中文：MSA，把同源蛋白序列逐位对齐，显示每个位置的保守程度。）
  • Ortholog — the same gene in a different species, descended from a common ancestor; orthologs fill the columns of an MSA.（中文：不同物种中源自共同祖先的同一个基因，它们填满 MSA 的每一列。）
  • Protein language model — a model pretrained on tens of millions of natural protein sequences (no disease labels) that learns protein "grammar" and yields context-aware residue representations.（中文：在数千万条天然蛋白序列上预训练、不使用疾病标签的模型，学习蛋白“语法”，给出结合上下文的残基表示。）
  • HGVS — the standardized syntax for naming sequence variants, e.g. p.Phe508del.（中文：HGVS，变异命名的标准化书写规范，如 p.Phe508del。）
  • ClinVar — a public database of clinically submitted variant classifications (pathogenic/benign).（中文：ClinVar，公开的临床变异分类数据库。）
  • gnomAD — a large population allele-frequency reference; presence/high frequency is used as a benign proxy.（中文：gnomAD，大规模人群等位基因频率参考库，出现或高频常被当作良性替代标签。）
  • CFTR2 — the expert-curated clinical database of CFTR variant classifications.（中文：CFTR2，CFTR 变异的专家审定临床分类数据库。）
  • In trans — two variants on opposite chromosome copies (relevant to recessive CF: two hits needed).（中文：反式，两个变异分别位于两条染色体拷贝上；隐性 CF 需要两个致病打击。）

Full citation

Fan Z, et al. SHINE: protein language model-based pathogenicity prediction for short inframe insertion and deletion variants. Briefings in Bioinformatics. 2023. PMID 36575831 · PMC PMC9851320 · DOI 10.1093/bib/bbac584. (According to PubMed.)

Methods / tools to lift

  • Dual-source protein representation — combine a single-sequence protein-language-model embedding (general grammar) with an MSA-derived evolutionary view (family-specific conservation).
  • Language-model backbone → light supervised head — pretrain-free-of-labels representation, then a small classifier on ClinVar/gnomAD labels; keeps the representation uncontaminated by disease-label circularity even though the head still learns from ClinVar.
  • Independent test-set design — grade on datasets drawn from different sources than training, for a modest external-validation check.
  • Insertion+deletion symmetry — evaluate both classes explicitly (insertions are rarer/harder).

Where it lands + buildable artifact

Serves Karen / CFTR variant interpretation. Paste-ready MVP: CFTR in-frame-indel pathogenicity annotator notebook — input a list of CFTR in-frame indels in HGVS (e.g. p.Phe508del); output a per-variant pathogenicity score plus a discordance shortlist flagging disagreement with CFTR2. Use SHINE directly if weights are released, else reproduce the recipe (protein-LM embedding of variant context → small classifier). Difficulty M — the modeling is light; curating a clean CFTR in-frame-indel truth set from CFTR2/ClinVar is the real work. Architecturally this is one member of a class-aware ensemble (missense predictor + in-frame-indel predictor + splice predictor), not a single magic score.

What to verify first

  • CFTR-specific calibration — the global decision threshold may not transfer; test whether a gene-specific operating point is needed.
  • Benign-label leakage — gnomAD presence stands in for benign, so mild-but-disease-relevant-in-trans CFTR alleles may be systematically underscored; sanity-check known mild variants.
  • ClinVar circularity — the supervised head learns from labels that older tools helped set; caveat carried, not eliminated.
  • Small evaluation sets — in-frame indels are rare; expect wide confidence intervals; don't over-read a handful of cases.
  • F508del is a positive control — value is in the rarer variants, not re-confirming the obvious.
  • Full text not retrieved — abstract-level sourcing; no benchmark numbers claimed. External public variant data only; no registry internals.

ALEX Good morning — today's Track A paper is SHINE, from Fan and colleagues, published in Briefings in Bioinformatics in 2023. 早上好，今天 Track A 这篇论文是 Fan 等人 2023 年发表在《Briefings in Bioinformatics》上的 SHINE。 The full name is "SHINE: protein language model-based pathogenicity prediction for short inframe insertion and deletion variants." 全名是《SHINE：基于蛋白质语言模型 (protein language model) 对短的框内插入与缺失变异进行致病性预测》。 We're reading it because it tackles the one variant class that almost every CFTR predictor quietly skips — in-frame insertions and deletions. 我们读它，是因为它专门处理几乎所有 CFTR 预测工具都悄悄跳过的那一类变异——框内插入与缺失变异 (in-frame indel)。 And that matters directly for Karen's variant-interpretation collaboration, because the single most common CF variant, F508del, is itself an in-frame deletion. 这一点和 Karen 的变异解读合作直接相关，因为最常见的 CF 变异 F508del 本身就是一个框内缺失。

JORDAN Okay, before we go further, I need the vocabulary. 好，在继续之前，我需要先搞清楚术语。 What exactly is an in-frame indel, and how is it different from the frameshift kind? 到底什么是框内插入缺失变异，它和移码 (frameshift) 那一类有什么区别？

ALEX Great place to start. 这是个很好的起点。 "Indel" is just shorthand for an insertion or deletion of nucleotides in the DNA. “Indel”只是 DNA 中核苷酸插入或缺失的简称。 The genetic code reads DNA in three-letter words called codons, each specifying one amino acid. 遗传密码以三个字母为一组来读取 DNA，这样的一组叫密码子 (codon)，每个密码子指定一个氨基酸。 If you add or remove a number of bases that is a multiple of three, the downstream reading frame stays intact — that's "in-frame." 如果你增删的碱基数是三的倍数，下游的阅读框架 (reading frame) 保持不变——这就是“框内”。 The protein just gains or loses one or a few amino acids, but the rest of the sequence is preserved. 蛋白质只是多出或少了一个到几个氨基酸，但其余序列都保留下来。 F508del, for example, deletes exactly three bases and removes a single phenylalanine at position 508. 比如 F508del 恰好缺失三个碱基，去掉了第 508 位的一个苯丙氨酸 (phenylalanine)。 A frameshift, by contrast, adds or removes a non-multiple of three, which scrambles every codon downstream and usually produces a premature stop. 相比之下，移码增删的碱基数不是三的倍数，会打乱下游的每一个密码子，通常会产生一个提前终止密码子。 Frameshifts are almost always severely damaging and easy to call; in-frame indels are the subtle, ambiguous ones. 移码几乎总是严重有害、容易判断；框内插入缺失变异才是那些微妙、模糊的类型。

JORDAN And why do the popular predictors skip them? 那为什么主流的预测工具会跳过它们？

ALEX Mostly because the big-name tools — the missense predictors — are built to score single amino-acid substitutions, one residue swapped for another. 主要是因为那些大牌工具——错义 (missense) 预测器——是为给单个氨基酸替换打分而设计的，也就是一个残基换成另一个。 An in-frame indel changes the length of the protein, so those models don't have a natural slot for it. 而框内插入缺失变异改变了蛋白质的长度，所以这些模型没有一个天然的位置来处理它。 The result is that a clinically crucial class of variants falls into a scoring blind spot. 结果就是，一类临床上至关重要的变异掉进了打分的盲区。

JORDAN So what was the state of play before SHINE — what made in-frame indels so hard? 那么在 SHINE 之前是什么状况——是什么让框内插入缺失变异这么难？

ALEX Two things, really. 其实是两点。 First, data scarcity: there are far fewer confidently classified in-frame indels than there are missense variants, so there just isn't much labeled training material. 第一，数据稀缺：被自信分类的框内插入缺失变异远少于错义变异，所以可用的带标签训练材料本来就不多。 Supervised models starve without enough known-pathogenic and known-benign examples. 监督模型在没有足够的已知致病和已知良性例子时会“挨饿”。 Second, feature engineering: older methods leaned on hand-crafted features — evolutionary conservation, protein structure and function annotations, and allele frequency. 第二，特征工程：较老的方法依赖人工设计的特征——进化保守性 (conservation)、蛋白质结构和功能注释，以及等位基因频率。 Conservation here means how unchanged a position has stayed across related species — a position that never varies across evolution is probably doing something essential. 这里的保守性是指某个位置在相关物种间保持不变的程度——一个在进化中从不改变的位置，很可能承担着某种关键功能。 Those features are informative but shallow, and they have to be assembled by hand for each variant. 这些特征有信息量但比较浅，而且每个变异都得手工去拼装。 The bet SHINE makes is that a protein language model can learn a richer representation straight from raw sequence, and do it without hand engineering. SHINE 押的赌注是：蛋白质语言模型可以直接从原始序列学到更丰富的表示，而且无需人工设计特征。

JORDAN Let's define that. 我们来定义一下这个。 What is a protein language model, in genomics terms? 从基因组学的角度讲，蛋白质语言模型是什么？

ALEX Think of it as a model trained on tens of millions of natural protein sequences, learning which amino acids tend to go where. 你可以把它想成一个在数千万条天然蛋白序列上训练的模型，学习哪些氨基酸倾向于出现在哪些位置。 It never sees disease labels during that pretraining — it just learns the "grammar" of real proteins by predicting masked-out residues. 在这种预训练中它从不看疾病标签——它只是通过预测被遮盖的残基，学会真实蛋白质的“语法”。 The payoff is that its internal representation of a position implicitly encodes conservation, structural context, and biochemical plausibility, all at once. 好处是，它对某个位置的内部表示会同时隐含地编码保守性、结构背景和生化上的合理性。 You know embeddings well, so I won't belabor that — the genomics point is that the learned signal replaces the hand-built conservation and structure features. 你对嵌入 (embedding) 很熟，所以我不多讲——从基因组学角度看，关键是这种学到的信号替代了人工搭建的保守性和结构特征。

JORDAN Got it. 明白了。 So concretely, what did they build? 那么具体来说，他们造了什么？

ALEX SHINE takes the variant and its surrounding protein context and passes them through pretrained protein language models to build a latent representation of the indel. SHINE 把变异及其周围的蛋白质背景一起送入预训练的蛋白质语言模型，为这个插入缺失变异构建一个隐向量表示。 Crucially, it uses two sources of sequence information: the single protein sequence, and multiple sequence alignments. 关键在于它用了两种序列信息来源：单条蛋白序列，以及多序列比对 (MSA，把同源蛋白序列逐位对齐以显示每个位置的保守程度)。 The alignment gives the model an evolutionary view — which positions tolerate change across homologs and which never budge. 比对给了模型一个进化视角——哪些位置在同源物之间能容忍改变，哪些位置从不动摇。 That latent representation is then fed into a supervised machine-learning classifier that outputs a pathogenicity score. 这个隐向量表示随后被送入一个监督式机器学习分类器，输出一个致病性分数。 For training labels, they curated pathogenic variants from ClinVar and presumed-benign variants from gnomAD. 训练标签方面，他们从 ClinVar（一个公开的临床变异注释数据库）整理出致病变异，从 gnomAD（一个大规模人群等位基因频率数据库）整理出推定良性的变异。 The logic is standard: variants recurrent in a large healthy-ish population are enriched for benign, curated clinical submissions supply the pathogenic side. 逻辑很常规：在大规模、相对健康人群中反复出现的变异富集于良性，而经过整理的临床提交则提供致病的一侧。 One design choice I'd push on: using gnomAD presence as a benign proxy is convenient but leaky — a recessive CF allele like a mild CFTR variant can sit at appreciable frequency in gnomAD yet still be disease-relevant in trans. 有一个设计选择我想质疑：用在 gnomAD 中出现作为良性的替代标签很方便，但会有渗漏——像某个温和的 CFTR 变异这样的隐性 CF 等位基因，可以在 gnomAD 中有相当的频率，却在反式 (in trans) 组合下仍与疾病相关。 So the benign label is really "common," which is not quite the same thing. 所以这个良性标签实际上是“常见”，而这两者并不完全等同。 I should flag: the open-access full text wasn't retrievable through the usual routes this morning, so I'm working from the paper's detailed abstract, and I won't quote benchmark numbers it doesn't state. 我需要说明：今早通过常规途径无法获取这篇论文的开放全文，所以我依据的是这篇论文详细的摘要，我不会引用摘要里没有给出的具体基准数字。

JORDAN That's fair — so what can we say about whether it works? 这很公道——那么关于它是否有效，我们能说什么？

ALEX What the authors report at the abstract level is that they built two independent test datasets from different sources, to avoid grading the model on data too close to its training set. 作者在摘要层面报告的是，他们从不同来源构建了两个独立的测试数据集，以避免用与训练集太接近的数据来评判模型。 On both test sets, SHINE achieved better prediction performance than existing methods, and — importantly — for both deletion and insertion variants. 在这两个测试集上，SHINE 都取得了优于现有方法的预测性能，而且——很重要地——对缺失变异和插入变异都是如此。 That insertion-and-deletion symmetry matters, because insertions are even rarer and often handled worse than deletions. 缺失和插入两方面都好这一点很重要，因为插入更罕见，通常被处理得比缺失更差。 The headline claim is a method-level one: an unsupervised protein language model carries enough signal that, plugged into a modest supervised classifier, it beats the hand-engineered predictors on the hardest, data-poorest variant class. 核心主张是方法层面的：一个无监督的蛋白质语言模型携带了足够的信号，把它接入一个不算复杂的监督分类器，就能在最难、数据最稀缺的变异类别上胜过人工设计特征的预测器。 I can't independently verify the exact margins from the abstract, so treat "better than existing methods" as the claim, not a number I'm vouching for. There's also a subtle generalization point worth stating: because the two test sets were drawn from different sources than the training data, the reported edge is at least not pure memorization. 还有一个值得点明的微妙泛化要点：因为两个测试集取自与训练数据不同的来源，所报告的优势至少不是纯粹的记忆。 That's a modest but real form of external validation, and it's more than a lot of variant tools bother to do. 这是一种不大但真实的外部验证，而且比很多变异工具愿意做的都要多。 我无法从摘要独立核实确切的优势幅度，所以把“优于现有方法”当作一个主张，而不是我在担保的某个数字。

JORDAN Before we get to our work — why feed the model both a single sequence and a multiple sequence alignment? 在讲到我们的工作之前——为什么要同时给模型喂入单条序列和一份多序列比对？ Isn't the alignment redundant if the language model already learned from millions of proteins? 如果语言模型已经从数百万条蛋白学习过了，那比对不是多余的吗？

ALEX They're capturing different things, and that's the elegant part. 它们捕捉的是不同的东西，这正是精巧之处。 The single-sequence embedding encodes what the model has generalized about protein grammar overall — biochemistry, local structure, global plausibility. 单序列嵌入编码的是模型对蛋白“语法”整体归纳出的东西——生化性质、局部结构、全局合理性。 The alignment, on the other hand, is specific to this protein family — it says how much this exact position has actually varied across the real evolutionary history of homologs. 而比对则是这个蛋白家族特有的——它告诉你这个具体位置在同源物真实的进化史中实际变异了多少。 A position can look tolerant in the abstract grammar but be frozen solid in this particular family, or vice versa. 一个位置在抽象的语法里可能看起来能容忍改变，但在这个特定家族里其实被牢牢冻结，反之亦然。 Combining both is a way of hedging: the general prior from the language model, and the family-specific evidence from the alignment. 把两者结合是一种对冲：来自语言模型的通用先验，加上来自比对的家族特异证据。 For a gene like CFTR, whose orthologs span a lot of vertebrates, the alignment signal is genuinely rich. 对像 CFTR 这样在很多脊椎动物中都有直系同源物 (ortholog) 的基因，比对信号确实很丰富。 An ortholog, quickly, is the same gene in a different species, descended from a common ancestor — that's what fills the columns of the alignment. 简单说，直系同源物就是不同物种里的同一个基因，源自共同祖先——正是它们填满了比对的每一列。

JORDAN You raised a leakage worry about the benign side. 你提到了良性一侧的渗漏担忧。 Is there a matching worry on the pathogenic side, from ClinVar? 致病一侧、来自 ClinVar 的，有没有对应的担忧？

ALEX Yes, and it's the quiet issue behind almost every supervised pathogenicity predictor. 有，而且这是几乎每个监督致病性预测器背后那个不被谈及的问题。 ClinVar's pathogenic labels are submitted by clinical labs, and those labs sometimes used older prediction tools when they made the call. ClinVar 的致病标签是临床实验室提交的，而这些实验室在下判断时有时用的就是较老的预测工具。 So a new model trained on those labels can inherit the biases of the very tools it's trying to beat — a mild form of circularity. 于是一个在这些标签上训练的新模型，可能继承它正想超越的那些工具的偏差——一种轻度的循环论证。 SHINE's partial defense is the protein language model backbone, which was pretrained with no disease labels at all, so at least the representation isn't contaminated. SHINE 的部分防御是那个蛋白质语言模型骨干，它在预训练时完全没有用疾病标签，所以至少表示层没有被污染。 But the supervised classifier on top still learns from ClinVar, so the circularity isn't fully gone — just diluted. 但顶层的监督分类器仍然从 ClinVar 学习，所以循环并没有完全消除——只是被稀释了。 That's the honest caveat to carry into any CFTR application. 这是在任何 CFTR 应用中都要带着的一个诚实的注意事项。

JORDAN And how does this sit next to the missense predictors we've covered, like AlphaMissense? 那它和我们讲过的错义预测器，比如 AlphaMissense，是什么关系？

ALEX They're complementary, not competitors — that's the key mental model. 它们是互补而非竞争关系——这是关键的思维模型。 AlphaMissense and its cousins score single-residue substitutions and leave a hole exactly where indels live. AlphaMissense 及其同类给单残基替换打分，恰恰在插入缺失变异所在的地方留了个洞。 SHINE fills that hole with the same underlying philosophy — learn from protein sequence, not just hand-built features. SHINE 用同样的底层理念填上这个洞——从蛋白序列学习，而不只是人工搭建的特征。 So a complete CFTR annotation layer isn't one tool; it's a missense predictor plus an in-frame-indel predictor plus a splice predictor, each covering its own variant class. 所以一个完整的 CFTR 注释层不是单一工具；它是错义预测器加上框内插入缺失变异预测器再加上剪接预测器，各自覆盖自己那一类变异。 That's actually the architecture the Karen collaboration needs — a class-aware ensemble, not a single magic score. 这其实正是 Karen 合作所需要的架构——一个按变异类别分工的组合，而不是一个万能的单一分数。
